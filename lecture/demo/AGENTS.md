# AGENTS.md — Partner-Candidate Shortlist for the Marketing Chair

## Goal

Build a **ranked shortlist of 10 B2B companies** as partner candidates for the
marketing chair (research co-operations, teaching cases, funded projects).

## Criteria (score each 1–5, with evidence)

1. **Industry fit** — the company's business connects to marketing research
   (consumer data, retail, media, MarTech, analytics).
2. **Innovation agenda** — visible AI / data / digitalisation initiatives
   (press releases, job postings, product pages).
3. **University links** — existing co-operations with universities, research
   funding, doctoral programmes, guest lectures.

## Sources

- Company websites and newsrooms
- Press coverage (search engine news)
- Funding registries and university press releases

## Output

- One markdown table: `Rank | Company | Fit signal (1 sentence) | Score | Source URL(s)`
- Sorted by total score, best first.
- **Every claim carries a URL that resolves.**

## Rules

- No invented facts. If a criterion cannot be verified, write `unverified`.
- 2–3 companies are enough for this demo — depth beats breadth.
- If a source contradicts another, flag it instead of averaging.
