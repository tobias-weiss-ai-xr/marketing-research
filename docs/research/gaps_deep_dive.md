# Deep Dive: Ranking the Contextual Intelligence Research Gaps

> **Date:** 2026-08-29  
> **Scope:** Marketing-Research corpus (7,778 papers)  
> **Goal:** Re-rank gaps considering momentum, novelty, white-space, effort, and strategic value

---

## Methodology

Each gap is evaluated across 8 dimensions, scored 1–5 (1=weak, 5=strong), with rationale.

| Dimension | Definition | Weight |
|-----------|------------|--------|
| **Momentum (M)** | 12-month momentum in the corpus + external signals | 20% |
| **Novelty (N)** | White-space: how under-served this is in literature | 20% |
| **Strategic Fit (S)** | Aligns with corpus trends + our capabilities | 15% |
| **Publication Potential (P)** | Likelihood of high-impact acceptance | 15% |
| **Effort (E)** | Inverse: lower effort = higher score | 10% |
| **Urgency (U)** | Time sensitivity (competitive pressure) | 10% |
| **Cross-disciplinary Leverage (L)** | Borrows strength from adjacent fields | 5% |
| **Monetization Potential (M$)** | Path to enterprise/actionable value | 5% |

**Weighted Score = 0.20×M + 0.20×N + 0.15×S + 0.15×P + 0.10×E + 0.10×U + 0.05×L + 0.05×M$**

### G4 — Situational Awareness for Autonomous Marketing Agents (Frontier Gap)

**Definition:** Framework + prototype for how **agentic marketing systems** acquire and act on marketing context (audience situation, channel journey, moment-of-truth) — bridging the corpus's agentic burst with marketing situational awareness.

#### Evidence Recap
- **Corpus:** "agentic" **= 61 hits** (burst **1.79×**), but **only 2 papers** connect agentic with contextual/ context. Momenta.
- **Momentum:** Agentic velocity: **2026-07: 12 papers**, 2026-06: 6, 2026-04: 7 — clear exponential.
- **External:** No papers found connecting agentic AI with marketing situational awareness; industry adtech vendors (e.g., context-aware bid optimization) but no academic framework.
- **Thin cells:** None directly — this is a *cross-cutting* frontier, not a single cell.

| Dim | Score | Rationale |

| **Novelty** | 5 | Zero papers in corpus = "agentic" + "marketing" + "situation[al/awareness]"; external NIL. True white space |
| **Strategic Fit** | 5 | Agentic is the corpus’s **#1 burst**; CI is our differentiator. perfect synergy.
| **Publication Potential** | 5 | Frontier + novel; likely buy-in in top journals (JM, JMR, Marketing Science) or AI/ marketing conferences (ICML Marketing AI Track,
| **Effort** | 3 | Requires architectural framework + potentially a prototype, a higher implementation burden that demands multi-disciplinary skills. |
| **Urgency** | 4 | Agentic momentum is exploding; first to define "situation-aware marketing agents" will own the space |
| **Cross-disciplinary Leverage** | 5 | Borrows from **RL/agent** (corpus), **NLP** (Häglund’s context work), and **human-agent collaboration** literature |
| **Monetization** | 5 | **Direct path to product** — this is the core of the agentic "Contextual Intelligence" product the user is building |

This is the **highest-scoring gap** in raw terms. It ties the corpus’s **#1 trend** (agentic) to the **product** you're building (contextual-intelligence.org). The **monetization potential** is the finest among all choices.
