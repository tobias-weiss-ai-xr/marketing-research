## New-Attacks

**New-Attack 1: The flagship "fair" contrast is classifier-family-confounded — and the paper's deferral standard is selectively applied. MAJOR.** The learned-vs-learned flagship compares `cam_learned` (greedy **interval** rule on one feature, 87.5%) against `cam_multisignal_learned` (**nearest-centroid** on three features, 98.7%). Signal count and classifier family co-vary. §9.2 #13–14 admit exactly this ("a matched-family ablation … is needed to decouple classifier capacity from signal structure") and §10.3 #11 defers it. Fine — except the *same paper* declines to state the per-environment learned-vs-learned comparison (F9: "not reported per-environment") on the ground that it is confounded, while claiming the default-environment version as its core empirical result with a p = 8.5e-32. Either the family confound taints both claims or neither. It cannot be disqualifying in §8.4/F9 and invisible in the abstract. (For the record: NCC-on-intent vs. NCC-on-3-signals is a one-day experiment; if intent-NCC reaches ~95%, the +$50 collapses toward feature-geometry artifact, since 3-feature NCC at SD 0.10–0.15 on hand-separated Gaussian means is the generator's own decoding problem.)

**New-Attack 2: The honest universality claim is sitting in their own table, verified, and still unstated. MAJOR (incoherence; the claim itself would help them).** From the paper's own §8.4 columns, which my sweep reproduces exactly: `cam_multisignal_recalibrated` beats `cam_recalibrated` in **9/9 environments** — +$50.0 default, +$127.8 uniform, +$103.3 decision, +$114.6 crisis, +$72.6 retention, +$59.3 high-costs, +$42.6 weak-bonus, +$51.2 budget, +$91.1 concave (all p < 1e-10 or smaller on my paired tests). The paper defers "learned-vs-learned per-environment (§10.3 #11)" while printing all eighteen numbers of it two pages earlier. Yes, this pair is also family-confounded (interval vs. NCC — see New-Attack 1), which is presumably why it is not claimed; but then the abstract's universality-by-ρ (a 13-agent ranking whose top agents are hand-set) is a *weaker* claim than the printed-but-unstated one. The paper systematically quotes its weakest honest evidence where its strongest is on the same page.

**New-Attack 3: The abstract is now the un-retracted sales section. MAJOR (collectively).** Five concrete instances: (a) "is confirmed by bootstrap BCa CIs (200 resamples)" vs. §10.2 #5 "demoted from confirmatory to diagnostic" and §9.2 #15's demonstration that B = 200's Monte-Carlo noise exceeds the reported agreement; (b) "We contribute … dose-response evidence" vs. #5 "an environment-consistency check, not a dose-response law"; (c) "calibrated bidding nearly doubles flat bidding (+$530 vs +$294)" with no omniscience caveat, vs. §9.2 #20 "an omniscience no practitioner has"; (d) §8.3 "leaves roughly half the deployable value on the table (+$104.26 vs +$206.13)" — the handicapped pair §9.1 explicitly demotes; the learned pair is +$155 vs. +$205, i.e., 24%, a quarter, not "roughly half"; (e) §9.4 still cites ρ ≥ 0.93 as support for Endsley's model *applying* to marketing, omitting §9.1 H4's mechanical caveat. In Round 2 the contradiction ran between §10.2 and §9.2; v0.7 reconciled those two and the asymmetry re-formed one level up. A parity pass is an afternoon.

**New-Attack 4: The one-line baseline was declined twice. MAJOR (governance of evidence, not statistics).** §7.1 labels `baseline` the "context-blind floor." It is not the floor: a constant always-EDUCATIONAL agent with flat bid — six lines against their API — earns −$15.38 vs. baseline's −$170.03, i.e., **+$154.65/episode of a context-*free* policy**. Every "vs. baseline" delta in §8.2 and every d_pooled = 9–35 rides that artificial headroom; §9.2 #3 concedes the effects "reflect the design." I ran this in Round 2 and published the numbers in the review; v0.7's response was to add §10.3 #19, "run an always-majority-action agent … as a trivial floor." When an author's future-work list contains an experiment a reviewer has already executed and reported to them, the future-work list is functioning as a filing cabinet for inconvenient results. This is the smallest of the paper's problems and the most diagnostic of how it handles the rest.

**New-Attack 5 (carried, now bounded): the §8 banner "No hand-typed values" overstates the mechanism. MINOR.** The corrected p/d_z pairs were computed in a separate verification run and inserted by hand (the v0.7 commit message says as much). They are now *correct* — I verified — but "auto-generated, no hand-typed numbers" is a claim about process that is false as stated; the honest banner is "all numbers regenerate from cam_sim.py." Minor, and noted only because the false version of this claim is what licensed the v0.6 error.

## Circularity: Disclosed or Fatal?

**Both, cleanly split by venue.** §9.2 #1 is an adequate disclosure of construction-authorship, and my Round-2 decomposition stands unrebutted and unremedied: zeroing the match bonus removes 42–53% of every classifier agent's profit, and `BASE_REWARDS` encodes the answer key a second time (ideal action maximal in every situation). For a **simulation/benchmark venue**, disclosure plus the robustness sweep is now borderline-adequate *if* the claims are re-worded as environment diagnostics — the paper is one wording pass from that. For **JM/Marketing Science**, the claim "context-aware agents outperform" is still true by construction in the only environment tested: the environment pays agents for agreeing with its authors, twice. No amount of disclosure converts that into evidence about marketing. The adversarial-mapping condition (§10.3 #3) has been future work for three rounds; it is the only remedy, and it has not been run. As an empirical marketing claim: still FATAL. As a benchmark artifact: disclosed.

## Minimum-Viable-Experiments

Ordered by cost; 1–3 are the minimum to change my verdict on the science, 4 to make the flagship claim clean, 5 for the claimed venue.

1. **Majority-action floor + macro-F1 (hours; I have run the floor twice).** One agent, six lines; macro-F1/per-class recall per §10.3 #6. Re-anchors every delta and retires "floor" from baseline. If the authors run nothing else, run this: its continued absence is the review.
2. **Matched-family ablation (a day): NCC on intent-only vs. NCC on 3 signals, default + 9 presets.** Decisive for New-Attack 1. If intent-NCC ≈ 87–90%, the +$50 is signal-driven and the claim survives de-confounding; if ≈ 95%+, the flagship is feature-geometry. Either result is publishable; the current ambiguity is not.
3. **Adversarial-mapping / independently-sourced reward condition (≈ a week): the circularity remedy.** Expert-panel or published-campaign-sourced reward table; test whether multi-signal classification still pays when the designers' preferred action is not the reward maximizer. The single experiment that addresses the FATAL issue; nothing else in the paper does.
4. **One contextual bandit (ε-greedy or LinUCB) on the same 3 signals (a day).** The deployable-policy comparator §5.4 promises; also the cheapest external check that the 3-signal advantage survives a policy that pays exploration costs.
5. **Registered field pilot (§10.3 #1).** ≥ 40 campaigns/arm with a human-coded match-rate audit. Mandatory, not optional, for JM/Marketing Science; no simulation result substitutes.

**Is there a version that survives my review? Yes.** Retarget to a simulation/benchmark venue (datasets-and-benchmarks track or an analytics-oriented marketing journal), run experiments 1–2, do the abstract parity pass (New-Attack 3), re-word all performance claims as environment diagnostics, and either run #3 or move the framework-validity claim explicitly to future work. That paper — reproducible to the cent, honestly bounded, family-deconfounded — I would rate major-revision-toward-accept. The v0.5→v0.7 trajectory shows the authors will do the wording; three rounds have not yet produced the experiments.

## VERDICT

**REJECT at the claimed venue (Journal of Marketing / Marketing Science) — final, unchanged across three rounds on the dispositive point.**

**Justification.** v0.7 fixed the worst concrete error of v0.6, and I verified the fix to the decimal: the flagship contrast now carries its true p = 8.45e-32 and d_z = 3.96, every table reproduces from the authors' unmodified code, and the Contributions section no longer trades on retracted numbers. That is genuine, creditable convergence. But the verdict was never about the arithmetic — it was about what the arithmetic is evidence *of*, and that is unchanged: an environment authored by the paper's designers pays agents twice for agreeing with them (42–53% of classifier profit is the agreement bonus; the base-reward diagonal is the answer key again), the one experiment that could break the loop (§10.3 #3) enters its third round as future work, and the flagship contrast that v0.7 just repaired is family-confounded by the paper's own §9.2 #13 admission — confounded enough to defer the per-environment claim, not enough to caveat the abstract. The honesty architecture remains selective: caveats land in §9 and §10.2; the abstract, §8.3, and §9.4 still sell "confirmed," "dose-response evidence," "roughly half," and "+$530 vs +$294" against the body's own retractions. And the majority floor — run by this reviewer, reported in Round 2, six lines of code — is item #19 of future work, which tells me everything about the ratio of candor to remedy this manuscript maintains. What survives is a genuinely reproducible, now-honestly-numbered simulation showing that features designed to be separable are separable, plus one real, robust, useful engineering finding (miscalibrated bid modulation is value-destroying; calibrated bidding dominates — but only with the omniscience no practitioner has, as the paper itself says). At a benchmark venue, after experiments 1–3 and a parity pass, this becomes a paper worth publishing. At *Journal of Marketing* or *Marketing Science*, where field evidence or validated measurement is the entry price and this manuscript's empirical law is its authors' own scoring rubric, it is not close, and three rounds of increasingly beautiful disclosure have not moved it closer. Reject.
