# 📈 Latest Marketing Trends — Evidence Brief

**Source:** corpus of 5,068 papers (2024–2026, 1,600+ in the last 12 months)
fetched from OpenAlex (via 3 proxy hosts), CrossRef, dblp & Europe PMC. Statistics
in `statistics.json`, full review in `docs/research/trends.md` +
`literature_review.md`.

## Headline findings

1. **Generative AI has moved from "will it change marketing?" to "how does it change outcomes?"**
   The 2025–2026 literature is dominated by quasi-experiments and effect studies:
   GenAI's impact on consumer reviews, AI-powered personalized advertising
   systematic reviews, chatbot persona effects (e.g., the Chinese *Xiehouyu*
   double-edged-sword study, 2026-04), and AI transforming influencer marketing
   & the creator economy (2026-08). Practical implementation, not adoption
   advocacy, is the research frontier.

2. **B2B is the fastest-growing domain in the corpus**
   `b2b/case-study` grew 80% within 12 months; ABM / account-based marketing and
   demand generation are now mainstream topics with their own case literature
   (e.g., industrial demand generation, marketing–sales alignment as a dynamic
   capability, 2026-07). B2B is absorbing consumer techniques (CX, storytelling)
   while ABM tooling matures.

3. **Privacy & "cookieless" advertising became a first-class research theme**
   Privacy–personalization trade-off studies (2026 preprints), first-party data,
   deterministic/telco-based targeting for programmatic, tech-law trends for the
   digital economy (2026-07). The third-party cookie deprecation has triggered a
   wave of alternatives research: contextual, first-party, deterministic.

4. **Measurement renaissance: MMM + attribution + experimentation**
   Marketing mix modeling and attribution are the most measured terms after
   search/purpose; design-science demonstrations of big-data marketing
   analytics (2026-07), data-driven programmatic evaluation. CMO pressure for
   ROI + cookie deprecation → incrementality, MMM, and causal experimentation.

5. **Creator & influencer economy is being institutionalized**
   Influencer persona → path-to-purchase (SEM, 2026-08), content quality,
   AI influencers, creator economy transformation papers (2026-08), social
   commerce and short-form video research.

6. **Brand purpose & storytelling as strategy**
   "Purpose" is the #2 term in recent abstracts; brand purpose/theory cell grew
   57% in 12 months; CSR narrative frameworks for social media engagement
   (2026-07), circular-economy marketing and consumer advocacy.

7. **Omnichannel CX matured into measurement & alignment work**
   Omnichannel, customer journey, CX–marketing alignment, retail media
   (still emerging, small but present), online retail sales events.

8. **Content marketing is going systematic**
   Framework/theory cells growing 67%/43% in 12 months; digital content effect
   studies; multilayered frameworks for CSR/social content.

9. **Digitalized consumer behavior is the empirical backbone**
   Online reviews → purchase intention dominates empirical work; online
   experience sharing; UGC as primary evidence base.

10. **Programmatic advertising in flux**
    In-house programmatic cases (Vidverto, 2026-08), emotions in programmatic
    adoption (2026-07), deterministic targeting — the field is renegotiating
    itself under the privacy regime.

## Where the frontier is (most cited emerging themes)

- **Generative AI & agentic systems** in marketing operations and CX
- **Deterministic / first-party targeting** replacing cookie-based targeting
- **Incrementality & MMM** as the measurement standard
- **AI influencers & virtual brand personas**
- **Retail media** (still early, watch this space)
- **CX–marketing alignment** as an organizational capability (B2B)
