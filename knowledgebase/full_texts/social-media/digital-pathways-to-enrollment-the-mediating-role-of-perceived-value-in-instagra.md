ABSTRACT
As higher education institutions increasingly rely on digital channels to attract prospective students, understanding
how social media activities translate into enrollment becomes critical. This study investigates the impact of
Instagram Social Media Marke ting Activities (SMMA) on prospective students' electronic word -of-mouth
(EWOM) and enrollment intentions, with a focus on the mediating role of perceived value. Using an online survey,
data were collected from 138 valid high school student respondents who  interacted with the official Instagram
account of a private university in Gresik, Indonesia, and were analyzed using Partial Least Squares Structural
Equation Modeling (PLS -SEM). The empirical results demonstrate that Instagram SMMA has a significant
positive effect on perceived value, EWOM, and enrollment intentions. In addition, mediation analysis reveals that
perceived value partially mediates the relationship between social media marketing and both behavioral outcomes.
Theoretically, this study advances consumer behavior literature by validating that digital stimuli shape cognitive-
affective internal states before prompting distinct behavioral responses. These insights offer higher education
marketers a strategic framework to move beyond vanity metrics,  demonstrating how value -driven digital
communication actively fosters institutional advocacy and channels prospective student interest into enrollment.
Keywords: Social media marketing activities, Perceived value, Electronic word -of-mouth (EWOM), Enrollment
intention, Higher education institution
1. INTRODUCTION
The rapid advancement of digital technologies has fundamentally transformed the way
organizations communicate with their stakeholders. In the higher education sector, digital
platforms have become increasingly important for attracting and engaging prospect ive
students. As competition among universities intensifies, higher education institutions are no
longer relying solely on traditional promotional channels such as educational fairs, brochures,
and institutional websites. Instead, social media platforms ha ve emerged as strategic tools for
building institutional visibility, fostering engagement, and influencing prospective students’
decision-making processes (Widodo & Maylina, 2022).
Among various social media platforms, Instagram has gained particular prominence due to
its visually oriented features and widespread adoption among younger generations. According
to recent digital usage reports, Instagram remains one of the most popular s ocial networking
platforms among high school students and prospective university applicants. Through images,
videos, stories, reels, and interactive features, universities can communicate their academic
offerings, campus culture, student achievements, and institutional values in a more engaging
and accessible manner. Consequently, Instagram has evolved beyond a communication

platform into a critical marketing channel capable of shaping perceptions and influencing
educational choices.
Social Media Marketing Activities (SMMA) have been widely recognized as an effective
approach for enhancing consumer engagement and strengthening brand relationships. Previous
studies suggest that social media marketing activities characterized by entertai nment,
interaction, trendiness, customization, and informativeness can positively influence
consumers’ perceptions and behavioral responses (Wahab et al., 2023). In the context of higher
education, effective social media marketing may encourage prospective students to develop
favorable attitudes toward an institution, share positive information with others, and ultimately
express intentions to enroll (Fitri & Othman, 2026; Nafisa et al., 2025) . However, the
mechanisms through which social media marketing activities translate into enrollment-related
behaviors remain insufficiently understood.
Therefore, this study aims to investigate the influence of Instagram Social Media Marketing
Activities on electronic word-of-mouth and enrollment intention among prospective students,
and to examine the mediating role of perceived value in these relationships. The findings are
expected to enrich theoretical understanding of consumer behavior in higher education contexts
and provide actionable recommendations for institutions seeking to enh ance student
recruitment through social media platforms.

2.2. Research Design
This study employed a quantitative research approach to examine the relationships among
Instagram Social Media Marketing Activities (SMMA), perceived value, electronic word -of-
mouth (EWOM), and enrollment intention in the context of higher education. A cross-sectional
survey design was utilized to collect data from prospective university students. The proposed
research model was tested using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS -
SEM), which is well -suited to predictive research and mediati on analyses involving latent
constructs.
2.3. Population and Sample
The target population consisted of senior high school students who were prospective
university applicants and had interacted with the official Instagram account of a private
university located in Gresik, Indonesia. Purposive sampling was employed to ensure  that
respondents possessed relevant experience with the university’s Instagram content.
To be eligible for participation, respondents had to meet the following criteria: (1) be
enrolled in senior high school, (2) follow or have viewed the university’s official Instagram
account, and (3) be considering higher education options. Data were colle cted through an
online questionnaire distributed via social media platforms and educational networks. After
data screening and validation, a total of 138 usable responses were retained for analysis.

2.4. Questionnaire Development
The questionnaire consisted of two sections. The first section collected demographic
information, including gender, age, grade level, and frequency of Instagram use. The second
section measured the study constructs using previously validated scales adapted from the social
media marketing and consumer behavior literature. All measurement items were assessed using
a five-point Likert scale ranging from 1 (“strongly disagree”) to 5 (“strongly agree”).
Instagram Social Media Marketing Activities (SMMA) were measured through four
dimensions: entertainment, interaction, trendiness, and informativeness. Perceived value

2.5. Data Analysis
Data analysis was conducted using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling
(PLS-SEM) with SmartPLS 4 software (Ringle et al., 2024). PLS -SEM is a variance -based
structural equation modeling technique suitable for predictive research, complex stru ctural
models, and mediation analysis involving latent constructs (Hair et al., 2022). The analysis
followed a two-stage approach, assessing the measurement and structural models.
The measurement model evaluation included indicator reliability, internal consistency
reliability, convergent validity, and discriminant validity. Indicator reliability was assessed
using factor loadings, with values above 0.70 considered acceptable. Inter nal consistency
reliability was evaluated using Cronbach’s Alpha and Composite Reliability (CR), with values
above 0.70 indicating  satisfactory reliability. Convergent validity was examined using the
Average Variance Extracted (AVE), with a threshold of 0.50. Discriminant validity was
assessed using the Fornell-Larcker criterion and Heterotrait-Monotrait Ratio (HTMT).
The structural model was evaluated by examining path coefficients, coefficient of
determination (R²), effect size (f²), predictive relevance (Q²), and hypothesis testing.
Bootstrapping with 5,000 subsamples was conducted to determine the significance of di rect
and indirect effects. A p-value below 0.05 was considered statistically significant.

3.3 Discussion
The findings of this study demonstrate that Instagram Social Media Marketing Activities
(SMMA) significantly enhance prospective students' perceived value, supporting H1. This
finding indicates that Instagram content characterized by informativeness, inter action,
entertainment, and trendiness enable prospective students to perceive greater educational value
offered by the university. As students are increasingly exposed to engaging digital content, they
develop more favorable evaluations regarding academic quality, campus life, and future
opportunities. This result is consistent with Perera et al. (2023), who argued that social media
marketing activities positively influence consumers' perceptions of value by delivering relevant
and engaging information. Similarly, Hartini  et al. (2025) emphasized that effective social
media marketing enhances users' perceived value, which in turn affects their behavioral
responses. These findings support the Stimulus –Organism–Response (S-O-R) framework, in
which social media marketing serv es as an external stimulus that shapes individuals' internal
cognitive evaluations.
The results also support H2, indicating that Instagram Social Media Marketing Activities
have a significant positive effect on electronic word -of-mouth (EWOM) intention. This
suggests that attractive and interactive Instagram content encourages prospective students to
voluntarily share positive information and recommend the university through online platforms.
Social media not only functions as an information ch annel but also facilitates interaction and
digital advocacy among users. This finding is consistent with Wahab et al. (2023), who reported
that social media marketing activities significantly stimulate positive electronic word-of-mouth
by increasing user e ngagement. Likewise, Ruangkanjanases and Sivarak (2022) found that
social media marketing activities encourage prospective students to actively recommend higher
education institutions through online communication. Therefore, universities should
continuously develop engaging In stagram content that motivates prospective students to
become active advocates of the institution.
Furthermore, H3 is supported, demonstrating that Instagram Social Media Marketing
Activities significantly influence enrollment intention. This finding indicates that well -

designed Instagram marketing activities directly encourage prospective students to consider
enrolling at the university. Through visual storytelling, academic information, student

The analysis further confirms H4, showing that Perceived Value has a significant positive

educational expectations.
Finally, the results support H5, indicating that Perceived Value significantly influences
enrollment intention. This finding suggests that prospective students' overall evaluation of the
university's educational benefits, affordability, and future career prospects plays a crucial role
in shaping their intention to enroll. When prospective students perceive that the institution
offers superior value compared with other universities, they are more likely to choose it  for
their higher education. This finding supports the argument of Perera et al. (2023), who
identified perceived value as a key determinant of behavioral intention because it reflects
individuals' overall assessment of the benefits obtained relative to the costs incurred. Therefore,
universities should emphasize value -oriented communication strategies that clearly convey
academic excellence, student achievements, campus facilities, and career opportunities to
strengthen prospective students' intentions to enroll.

4. CONCLUSION
This study examined the influence of Instagram Social Media Marketing Activities
(SMMA) on electronic word -of-mouth (EWOM ) and enrollment intention, as well as the
mediating role of perceived value among prospective students in the higher education context.
The findings provide empirical evidence that social media marketing not only directly
influences prospective students' behavioral intentions but also creates value perceptions that
strengthen these relationships.

First, the results confirm that Instagram Social Media Marketing Activities (SMMA) have
a significant positive influence on perceived value, electronic word -of-mouth (EWOM), and
enrollment intention. Furthermore, perceived value has a significant positive effect on both
EWOM and enrollment intention, indicating that prospective students' evaluations of a
university's value play  an important role in shaping their willingness to recommend the
institution and their intention to enroll.
Second, the findings demonstrate that perceived value partially mediates the relationships
between SMMA and both EWOM  and enrollment intention. These results support the
Stimulus–Organism–Response (S -O-R) framework, suggesting that social media marketing
activities function as external stimuli that shape prospective students' cognitive evaluations
before influencing thei r behavioral responses. Consequently, Instagram serves not only as a
promotional platform but also as a value -creation mechanism that encourages positive
advocacy and strengthens enrollment intentions.
Third, this study has several limitations. The research was conducted using respondents
from a single private university and employed a cross -sectional research design, which may
limit the generalizability of the findings and the ability to establish causal relationships. Future
research is encouraged to include multiple higher education institutions, adopt longitudinal
research designs, and incorporate additional variables, such as university brand image,
institutional trust, perceived credibility, and social influence, to provide a more comprehensive
understanding of prospective students' enrollment decisions.
