Abstract
The development of digital technology has encouraged the growth of e -commerce in Indonesia and
intensified competition among digital platforms in maintaining consumer usage. Tokopedia, as an e -
commerce platform in Indonesia, faces challenges in maintaining  sustainability amid increasing
competition. This condition is reflected in Tokopedia’s stagnant access rate compared to other e
commerce platforms. This study aims to analyze the role of brand image in mediating the effect of
social media marketing on reu se intention among Tokopedia consumers in Badung Regency. This
research employed a quantitative approach with a causal associative design. Data were collected
through questionnaires distributed to 120 Tokopedia users in Badung Regency selected using
purposive sampling. The data analysis techniques used were path analysis and the Sobel test with the
assistance of IBM SPSS version 26. The results showed that social media marketing had a positive and
significant effect on both brand image and reuse intention. Brand image also had a positive and
significant effect on reuse intention and was proven to mediate the influence of social media
marketing on reuse intention. These findings support the Stimulus-Organism-Response (S O-R) theory,
which explains that social  media marketing as a stimulus shapes positive consumer perceptions of a
brand as an internal process, thereby encouraging reuse intention. The implications of this study
highlight the importance of managing interactive, relevant, trend oriented social med ia content and
fostering user engagement to strengthen Tokopedia’s brand image and increase platform usage
among consumers.

Keywords: social media marketing; brand image; reuse intention; e-commerce; Tokopedia

INTRODUCTION

behavior, particularly in how individuals seek information, engage with brands, and make
purchasing decisions online (Putra et al., 2025). This transformation has accelerated the shift from
conventional to digital transactions, positioning e-commerce as the primary medium through which
consumers fulfill their needs for convenience, speed, and efficiency (Verhoef et al., 2021; Pantano
et al., 2020) . E -commerce itself encompasses all forms of digital buying and selling activities
conducted via the internet, including both physical and digital goods transacted through websites,
applications, and electronic platforms (Sukma et al., 2025) . In Indonesia, the e -commerce sector
has grown substantially, driven by widespread digital platform adoption, flexible transaction
systems, and an expanding range of product offerings (Mira Yuli & Siti Aisah, 2025) . The sector's
dominance within Indonesia's digital economy is reflected in its estimated economic value by sector

Based on Table 3, Badung Regency recorded a high internet usage rate of 87.48 percent,
ranking second in Bali Province after Denpasar City. This condition reflects the high level of digital
activity among the community, which is closely associated with the use of e-commerce platforms.
In addition, the selection of Badung Regency was based on the replication of previous studies that
primarily focused on Tokopedia consumer behavior in Denpasar City (Ramadhan & Jatra, 2017; Sari
& Fiolita, 2021 ; Negara et al., 2025 ). Therefore, this study shifts the research context to Badung
Regency to broaden the empirical scope while maintaining a focus on the phenomena of social
media marketing, brand image, and reuse intention among Tokopedia users.
Reuse intention refers to consumers’ tendency to continue using a digital platform after
prior usage experience (Nugroho et al., 2025) . In the context of e -commerce, reuse intention is
considered an important indicator in maintaining platform sustainability, as it reflects consumers’
positive evaluation of the benefits and performance of the service used (Amelia et al., 2025; Putra
et al., 2023). The continuity of platform usage is also influenced by consumers’ perceptions of brand
image, where a positive brand image can strengthen consumers’ confidence in reusing the same
platform (Bargandini & Suparna, 2025).
One of the strategies employed by e-commerce companies to encourage reuse intention is
social media marketing. Social media marketing refers to marketing activities conducted through
social media platforms by distributing digital content, online advertisem ents, and building
interactions with consumers to achieve marketing objectives (Ramadevi, 2024); Laoli et al., 2025).
This strategy is considered effective in shaping positive consumer perceptions toward a brand and
encouraging the continued use of digital services (Dewi & Sukawati, 2025) . However, studies
examining the effect of social media marketing on reuse intention have produced inconsistent
findings. Several studies found that social media marketing can enhance consumers’ intention to
reuse a platform ( Subawa et al., 2020 ; Fabiola & Rachmawati, 2022 ; Rizky et al., 2023) , whereas
other studies reported that social media marketing does not significantly affect repurchase
intention or reuse intention (Tong & Subagio, 2020; Nathalia & Satya Indriyanti, 2022; Pakpahan &
Prameswari, 2022) . These inconsistent findings indicate that the influence of social media
marketing on reuse intention may be affected by other variables, one of which is brand image (Putra
et al., 2023; Syifa et al., 2024).
Brand image refers to consumers’ perceptions and beliefs regarding a brand that are
formed through consumers’ experiences and the information they receive (N. K. A. D. Putri et al.,
2023). Taan et al. (2021) found that social media marketing positively influences brand image, while
Rahmanda and Farida (2021) demonstrated that brand image plays an important role in increasing
consumers’ intention to reuse a platform. Therefore, brand image is considered to have a mediating
role in the relationship between social media marketing and reuse intention on e -commerce
platforms. The Stimulus -Organism-Response (S-O-R) theory developed by Mehrabian and Russell
explains that consumer behavior is formed through the relationship between stimulus, individuals’
internal processes (organism), and behavioral responses (respons e) (Alam & Noor, 2020) . In the
context of e -commerce, this theory is used to explain how digital stimuli influence consumers’
decisions to use online services (Lee & Chen, 2021) . In this study, social media marketing is
positioned as the stimulus, brand image as the organism, and reuse intention as the response. Thus,
the S-O-R theory serves as the theoretical foundation for understanding the effect of social media
marketing on Tokopedia consumers’ reuse intention through the formation of brand image. Based
on the phenomenon and research gap described above, this study is important to be conducted in
order to analyze the role of brand image in mediating the effect of social media marketing on reuse
intention.

Various empirical studies have indicated that social media marketing positively affects
brand image. Oktavia and Mariam (2024) found that marketing through social media is capable of
strengthening consumers’ perceptions of a brand. Narayana and Rahanatha (2020)  also showed
that increased interaction and content exposure on social media can enhance consumers’ brand

building a positive brand image in the fashion industry. Based on the theoretical foundation and
previous empirical findings, the following hypothesis is formulated:
H2: Social media marketing has a positive and significant effect on brand image.
Brand image has also been widely recognized as an important factor influencing consumers’
reuse intention in digital services. A positive brand image can strengthen consumer trust and
encourage continued platform usage. Putra et al. (2023)  found that a favorable brand image
motivates consumers to reuse the same e -commerce platform. Similar results were reported by
Syifa et al. (2024) on e-wallet users, Bargandini and Suparna (2025) on e-commerce consumers, and
Suarantalla et al. (2025) , who emphasized that positive brand perceptions increase consumers’
intention to continue using digital services. Based on these findings, the following hypothesis is
proposed:
H3: Brand image has a positive and significant effect on reuse intention.
Several previous studies have shown that brand image serves as a mediating variable in the
relationship between social media marketing and repurchase intention, which conceptually shares
similarities with reuse intention because both describe consumers’ in tention to continue usage
based on prior experience (Amelia et al., 2025) . Putri dan Jatra (2024)  found that brand image is
able to mediate the effect of social media marketing on repurchase intention. Similar findings were
also reported by Ade et al. (2024), Dewi and Sukawati (2025), and Jamila and Rubedo (2025), who
demonstrated that marketing activities through social media can build a positive brand image,
which subsequently encourages consumers’ intention to reuse or repurchase. Based on these
previous findings, the following hypothesis is formulated:
H4: Brand image significantly mediates the effect of social media marketing on reuse intention.
RESEARCH METHODS
This study employed a quantitative approach with a causal associative research design
aimed at analyzing the effect of social media marketing on reuse intention, with brand image
serving as a mediating variable among Tokopedia consumers. The study was conducted in Badung
Regency, Bali Province, due to its high internet usage rate of 87.48 percent, making it relevant for
examining e-commerce consumer behavior. The selection of the research location was also based
on the replication of previous studies conducted in Denpasar City, which found that digital activities
and online marketing influence Tokopedia consumer behavior (Ramadhan & Jatra, 2017; Sari &
Fiolita, 2021; Negara et al., 2025).  The study involved 120 respondents selected using a non -
probability sampling technique with purposive sampling. The respondents were Tokopedia users
residing in Badung Regency, aged at least 17 years, had conducted transactions within the last three
months, actively used social media, and were not affiliated with Tokopedia’s internal parties.
Primary data were collected through online questionnaires distributed via Google Forms, while

secondary data were obtained from scientific journals, books, and other literature relevant to the

The research instrument consisted of a questionnaire using a five -point Likert scale to
measure respondents’ levels of agreement with each statement related to the research variables.
The social media marketing variable was measured using the indicators of  entertainment,
interaction, trendiness, customization, and electronic word-of-mouth (e-WOM) (Yunishafira et al.,
2025), while brand image was measured using the indicators of recognition, reputation, affinity,
and loyalty (Shintiani et al., 2022) . The reuse intention variable was measured through the
indicators of intention to continue reusing, intention to use as often as possible, and intention to
recommend the platform to others (Febrian et al., 2021).
Prior to data collection, the instrument was tested for validity and reliability. The results
indicated that all items met the required criteria, with corrected item -total correlation values
exceeding the r -table value (0.361) and Cronbach’s Alpha values a bove 0.60, indicating that the
instrument was reliable and suitable for data collection. Data analysis was conducted in two stages:
descriptive analysis to describe respondent characteristics and response tendencies, and inferential
analysis consisting of classical assumption tests, path analysis, model fit testing, and the Sobel test
to examine both direct and indirect effects among variables. All statistical analyses were performed
using IBM SPSS version 26 with a significance level of 5 percent. The classical assumption tests were
conducted to ensure that the regression model met the analytical requirements through
examinations of normality, multicollinearity, and heteroscedasticity. The research model was
developed based on the framework proposed  by Baron and Kenny (1986), which includes three
structural relationships: the effect of social media marketing on reuse intention prior to mediation,
the effect of social media marketing on brand image, and the effects of both social media marketing
and brand image on reuse intention. The significance of the indirect effect was subsequently tested
using the Sobel test.

Tanuwijaya and Leontinus Alpha Edison. Tokopedia operates a marketplace business model that

digital services. In maintaining its competitiveness, Tokopedia actively utilizes social media
marketing to enhance user interaction and build a positive brand image. Such positive perceptions
play an important role in encouraging users’ reuse intention to continue using the Tokopedia
platform.

background, the majority of respondents were senior high school/vocational high school graduates,
accounting for 74 individuals or 61.7 percent, and were predominantly university students, totaling
78 individuals or 65 percent. This re flects user characteristics that are closely associated with
internet development, social media usage, and online shopping activities. Meanwhile, based on
average monthly income, most respondents earned less than IDR 1,000,000 per month, totaling 48

From a theoretical perspective, this study reinforces the relevance of the Stimulus-Organism-
Response (S-O-R) theory in explaining the relationship among social media marketing, brand image,
and reuse intention within the e-commerce context. Social media marketing functions as a stimulus
that shapes brand image as an internal consumer process, which subsequently encourages reuse
intention as a behavioral response. Practical implications of the study suggest that Tokopedia
should optimize social media marketing strategies that are trend-oriented, relevant, and interactive
in order to strengthen brand image and enhance user loyalty. The findings further highlight that the
trendiness aspect of social media marketing and the loyalty aspect of brand image are the most
dominant factors influencing Tokopedia users’ reuse intention in Badung Regency.
CONCLUSION
This study demonstrates that social media marketing has a positive and significant effect
on reuse intention among Tokopedia consumers in Badung Regency, indicating that more engaging,
relevant, and informative content encourages users to return to the pla tform for online
transactions. A consistent pattern was also observed in the relationship between social media
marketing and brand image, as effective social media activities strengthen Tokopedia's brand image
in the minds of its users. Furthermore, brand image has a positive and significant effect on reuse
intention, suggesting that a favorable brand image increases users' confidence to continue using
Tokopedia. Brand image also proved to significantly mediate the effect of social media marketing
on reuse intention, demonstrating that social media marketing influences reuse intention not only
directly but also indirectly through the formation of positive brand perception. These findings are
further supported by the fact that the trendiness indicator obtained the highest mean score within
the social media marketing variable, while the loyalty indicator obtained the highest mean score
within the brand image variable.
RECOMMENDATION
For Tokopedia's management, strengthening reuse intention can be achieved by
intensifying the use of engaging and trend -adaptive content, as trendiness serves as the primary
driver within social media marketing. Tokopedia should also maintain elements of e ntertainment
and interaction so that content is not only informative but also closely aligned with users' needs.
Regarding brand image, the company is advised to maintain consistent brand identity and reinforce
positive user experiences to sustain high loyalty levels and attract more users to adopt Tokopedia
as their primary platform. These efforts can be further supported through relevant promotions,
loyalty programs, and continuous brand communication.
For future researchers, this study can be extended by incorporating additional variables
that may influence reuse intention, such as trust, electronic word of mouth, or price perception.
Subsequent studies may also broaden the research scope to other e -commerce platforms or
conduct cross-regional comparisons to yield more comprehensive findings and enrich the empirical
literature on digital consumer reuse behavior.
