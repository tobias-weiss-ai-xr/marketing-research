ABSTRACT:
The fusion of social media and e-commerce, known as shoppertainment, has transformed the shopping experience for consumers, especially in the beauty
industry. Uncertainty about product performance and consumerperceived risk are major challenges for new cosmetic brands launching on platforms like TikTok
Shop.Thisresearch explorestheeffectsofKOC(KeyOpinionConsumer)ExpertiseSignalsandCommunityReviewsonPerceivedQualityandsubsequentTrial

sensitive to Community Reviews (HCM=0.436 vs. HN =0.248). These insights provide practical strategies for managers of cosmetic brands to maximise their

1.INTRODUCTION
1.1.ResearchContext:

2.1.IntegratedTheoreticalFramework
To elucidate the underlying transmissionmechanisms connecting digital communication strategies toactual consumer behavior, this studysynthesizes
twofoundationaltheoreticalperspectives:SignalingTheory(Spence,1973)andtheNewProductAdoptionModel(Rogers,1995;Wasson,1975).This

2.1.1.SignalingTheory
Pioneered by Spence (1973), Signaling Theory provides a foundational framework for explaining how transaction participants navigate and mitigate
conditions of information asymmetry. When buyers are unable to directly verify the unobservable quality of a product prior to consumption, they
actively seek and rely on external "signals" emitted by sellers or intermediary third parties to infer underlying product value. Within the context of
TikTok Shop, the market entry of new cosmetic products is characterized by severe information asymmetry and heightened perceived risk, given the
product category's direct physical interaction with personal health and dermatological well-being (Bae & Chang, 2021; Rizomyliotis et al., 2024). To
counteractthisuncertainty,GenZconsumerssimultaneouslyevaluatetwodistinctsignalingchannels:
1. Personalized Domain-Expertise Signals (KOC Expertise Signals): Technical and highly specific in nature, these signals are
operationalized through a KOC's demonstrated mastery of active chemical formulations, mechanistic analysis of biological skin processes,
and transparent experiential evaluations (Phan & Nguyen, 2023). These signals enable consumers to systematically evaluate the utilitarian
andfunctionalattributesoftheproduct.
2. Macro-Social Proof Signals (Community Reviews): Functioning as aggregate social proof, these signals are operationalized through
cumulativesales volumes,real-timeinteractivecommentstreams,andcommunityfeedbackloops(Yiin,2024).Thissignalingstreamoffers
collectiveassurance,effectivelyalleviatingconsumerpsychologicalhesitationregardingqualityvarianceandperformancefailure.

2.2.2.CommunityReviewsandPerceivedQuality
Community Reviews on TikTok Shop represent aggregate social proof signals (Sun et al., 2019). When a newly launched product accumulates a
substantial volume of positive comments, high unit sales, and active real-time feedback during livestream broadcasts, consumers activate a cognitive
risk-reduction mechanism anchored in "strength-in-numbers" psychology (Yiin, 2024). These macro-community signals demonstrate that the product
hasbeenvalidatedbypeerconsumersinreal-worldsettings,directlyelevatingitsPerceivedQuality(Yiin,2024).
 Hypothesis H2: Community Reviews on TikTok Shop positively influence the Perceived Quality of new cosmetic products among
GenerationZconsumers.
2.2.3.PerceivedQualityandTrialBehavior
Perceived Quality reflects the consumer's overall cognitive evaluation regarding a product's superiority relative to expectations (Zeithaml, 1988). For
newly launched cosmetics, when perceived risk barriers are dismantled by high Perceived Quality, Gen Z consumers become increasingly willing to
allocate financial resources toward Trial Behavior for real-world experiential evaluation (Truong, 2024). Furthermore, Perceived Quality serves as a
criticaltransmittingmechanism(mediator),convertingexternalcommunicationsignalsintoactionablephysicaltrialexecution(Yeetal.,2022).
 Hypothesis H3: Perceived Quality positively influences the Trial Behavior of new cosmetic products among Generation Z consumers on
TikTokShop.

andCommunityReviews)andtheTrialBehaviorofnewcosmeticproducts.

5.DISCUSSION
5.1.TheoreticalContributions
Thisstudyoffersthreecorecontributionstothesocialcommerceanddigitalmarketingliterature:
First,itframes SignallingTheory (Spence, 1973)withinthe constantly changing, multi-sensory environmentof TikTok Shop’s Shoppertainment. This
papershows howdigital consumers overcome acuteinformationasymmetry aboutnew high-involvement cosmeticproducts bybreaking down signals
intotwochannels:personalisedhigh-expertisemicro-signals(KOC)andaggregatesocialproofmacro-signals(CommunityReviews).
Second,theresultsclosethelong-lasting“intention-behaviorgap”bysubstitutinggenericpurchaseintentionswithrealTrialBehaviour(Rogers,2003).
The empirical validation of Perceived Quality as an important partial mediator reveals that in the absence of any contextual anchors, digital signals
cannotdirectlycausephysicaltrial.Instead,digitalsignalsmustfirstdevelopcognitiveconfidenceaboutproductsafetyandfunctionalperformance.
Third,thisstudyprovidesempiricalevidenceofregionaldivergenceinsignalprocessinginanemergingeconomy.Thecontrastbetween Hanoi(where
domain expertise governs consumer evaluation) and Ho Chi Minh City (where social proof and crowd consensus govern) reveals that Gen Z digital
natives are not a monolithic cohort. The processing of digital marketing stimuli byyoung consumers is fundamentally driven by geographical cultural
traits.
5.2.PracticalImplications
The empirical results of this study provide actionable strategic insights for marketing executives, brand managers and digital strategists, especially
thoseofforeignoremergingbeautybrandsventuringintothedynamicsocialcommerceecosystemsofSoutheastAsia.
5.2.1.AdaptingDual-SignalingStrategiesforRegionalSub-Cultures
Instead of running standardised, one-size-fits-all digital campaigns across Vietnam, cosmetics brand managers should use a geographically segmented
signalling framework that is aligned with regional sub-cultural cognitive orientations:Northern Market Strategy (Hanoi – Tighter Sub-Culture):
Educational & Diagnostic Marketing should be the priority of marketing managers. Northern Gen Z consumers tend to avoid uncertainty and rely on
cognitive deliberation. Campaigns in this region should therefore work with KOCs that have verifiable technical credentials or deep product domain
expertise(e.g.,adermatologicalbreakdownofactiveingredientslikeRetinol,Niacinamide,orBHA). Live-streamscriptsandshort-formvideocontent
should frame as scientific formulation mechanisms, safety certifications and objective ingredient transparency rather than superficial lifestyle appeal.
Southern Market Strategy (Ho Chi MinhCity - Looser Sub-Culture) Marketers need totransition toViral & Social Proof-Driven Marketing. Southern
Gen Z consumers are very sensitive to collective social proof and trend dynamics. Therefore, it is important to direct brand resources in HCMC to
maximiseaggregatevisibilityandvolumeindicators.Strategicmovesincludetherolloutofhighfrequencyusercontentcreation(UGC)campaigns,the
demonstrationofrealtimepurchasetickers,thepromotionoflivecommentinteractionandtheutilisationofmass-micro-KOCseedingtobuildobvious
communityendorsement.
5.2.2.Risk-ReducingTrialMechanicstoClosetheIntention-BehaviorGap
Perceived Quality has a strong impact on actual Trial Behaviour, so managers need to create low-friction entry points that lower psychological and
financial barriers for first-time buyers. Brand awareness is not sufficient for newly launched cosmetic brands with a structural 'liability of newness'.
TikTok Shop Brand Trial-Rich Tactics Brand strategists should use the following: Launching discovery sets in mini-size or sample bundles featured

5.2.3.KOCPartnershipManagement–StrategicPortfolioAlignment
Brands need toreimagine their Influencer Relationship Management (IRM) framework bysegmentingKOCs based on their functional utility, not just
follower counts. Strategists should develop a dynamic portfolio of “Authority KOCs” (deep auditing of ingredients and functional quality for risk-
averse segments) and “Relational KOCs” (lifestyle integration, trend creation and instant crowd resonance). This dual-portfolio approach optimises
returnonmarketinginvestment(ROMI)acrossheterogeneousconsumerbasesinemergingmarkets.
5.3.Limitations&FutureResearch
Providing useful empirical evidence and practical managerial implications for [the topic/area of your research, e.g. Gen Z’s purchase behaviour in
socialcommerce],thisstudyisnotdevoidofitsowninherentlimitationsthatpavethewayforfuturescholarlyinquiry.
First, regarding sampling and geographic coverage, the empirical data was strictly gathered from urban Generation Z consumers living in major
metropolitan hubs, namely Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City. Despite urban Gen Z being a very active and profitable segment in the digital commerce,
restricting the sample to this specific demographic limits the generalisability of the empirical findings. The socio-demographic profile, purchasing
power and digital proficiency of consumers in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities and rural areas could be very different. Moreover, there are also differences in
risk perceptions, brand loyalty frameworks and decision-making strategies between older generational groups such as Generation Y (Millennials) and
Generation X. Future research should seek to validate and extend the proposed theoretical framework to broader geographical areas and generational
groupstoimproveexternalvalidityandcomparativeknowledge.
Second, from a methodological perspective, this study employed a cross-sectional survey design, examining consumer perceptions and behavioural
intentions at one point in time. The use of structural equation modelling (PLS-SEM)is a robust way to establish statistically significant relationships;

however,thecross-sectionalframeworkdoes notallowfordefinitivecausalinferencestobemadeortoobservechanges inbehaviourovertime.Social
commerce environments, particularly live selling events, are highly atmospheric and emotionally charged, where consumers’ cognitive and affective
statesfluctuaterapidly. Thislimitationcouldbeaddressedinfuturestudiesbyusinglongitudinaldesignstoobserve long-term behaviouralchanges, or
by using experimental design methods (e.g., scenario-based or field experiments) to measure real-time psychological responses and actual impulse
buyingactionsduringlivestreamingsessions.
Third,thestudyrelied on non-probability conveniencesampling. Althoughstrict screeningfilters were applied, future studies should employstratified
randomsamplingorexperimentaldesigns(e.g.,A/BtestingonTikToklivestreams)toestablishdirectcausalityandminimizeself-selectionbias.
Finally, regarding theoretical boundary conditions, the research was primarily directed toward a core set of psychological and transactional variables.
Buttheunderlyingmechanismsmaybeboundaryconditionedbyexternalcontextualfactors andindividualpsychologicaltraits.Futureresearchers are
encouraged toincorporate criticalmoderating variables toenrichthe conceptualmodel. Specifically, anexamination of thestructural variations across
different brand origins (e.g. local domestic vs. international beautybrands) could reveal how brand equityand perceived origin impact consumer trust
and purchase friction. Moreover, individual dispositional factors such as risk aversion, price sensitivity or domain-specific locus of control could be
consideredtoilluminatetheheterogeneousboundaryconditionsunderwhichlivesellingmechanismscaneffectivelydriveactualsalesconversions.
6.CONCLUSION
To summarise, this study empirically confirms the dual-signaling framework in which Key Opinion Consumer (KOC) Expertise Signals and
CommunityReviewsjointlydrivePerceivedQualityandinitialTrialBehaviourforemergingcosmeticproductsonTikTokShop.Theempiricalresults
combining the signalling theory and social commerce paradigms highlight the essential function of Perceived Qualityas a significant cognitive bridge
totranslatetheonlinedigitalengagementtothereal-lifeproducttrial.
Crucially, this study uncovers a nuanced regional variation in consumer signal receptiveness: consumers in Hanoi show a heavier reliance on deep,
technical domain expertise, whereas consumers in Ho Chi Minh City are more agile in their responses to collective social proof and community
consensus. This geographical dichotomy provides a better theoretical lens to comprehend the regional consumer psychographics in emerging social
commerce markets. These findings offer strategic guidancefor brand managers and livestream merchants to transition from generic marketing models
tolocalised,signal-alignedconversionstrategies,fosteringsustainablebrandgrowthandcustomeracquisitioninthechangingdigitalenvironment.
