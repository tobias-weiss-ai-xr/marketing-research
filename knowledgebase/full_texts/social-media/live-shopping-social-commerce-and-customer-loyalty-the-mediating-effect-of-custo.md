ABSTRACT
The rapid growth of social commerce and live shopping has transformed how consumers interact
with brands and make purchasing decisions. This study analyzes the influence of live shopping and
social commerce on customer loyalty, with customer engagement as a mediating variable, among
TikTok Shop users in Indonesia. This quantitative study employed SEM-PLS with 200 purposively
sampled TikTok Shop users who had transacted and participated in live shopping. Data were
collected through an online Google Forms questionnaire. The results show that live shopping has a
significant effect on customer loyalty and customer engagement, while social commerce also has a
significant effect on customer engagement and customer loyalty. In addition, customer engagement
is proven to have a significant effect on customer loyalty and is able to mediate the influence of live
shopping and social commerce on customer loyalty. These findings indicate that increasing customer
loyalty on the TikTok Shop platform is not only directly influenced by digital features, but also
through customer engagement formed from social interactions and live shopping experiences. This
study contributes in the development of digital consumer behavior models and offers  practical
implications for social commerce-based marketing strategies in Indonesia.

INTRODUCTION
The development of management science has rapidly transformed alongside advances
in digital technology and changes in consumer behavior. Management is no longer limited
to managing organizational resources but increasingly emphasizes value creation, long-
term customer relationships, and competitive advantage. In modern marketing
management, customers are strategic assets whose satisfaction and loyalty can be
strengthened through positive shopping experiences and meaningful digital interactions
(Rasul et al., 2025). Therefore, companies need to utilize digital technology to increase
interaction, foster customer engagement, and build sustainable relationships with
consumers (Auliarahman, 2024; Nugroho & Sugiarto, 2024).
Customer loyalty is a key objective of marketing management because loyal customers
are more likely to repurchase, recommend products, and resist switching to competitors
(Santoso et al., 2025). In the digital era, loyalty is influenced not only by product quality
and price but also by consumers’ experiences during information search, interaction, and
purchasing processes. Positive digital experiences can encourage continued usage and
repeat purchasing, which are important foundations of loyalty. Auliarahma n (2024)
found that interactive experiences in live commerce increase continuous purchase
intentions, while Ritonga and Simbolon (2025) demonstrated that customer relationship
quality positively affects customer loyalty among TikTok Shop users.

repeat purchases, as some consumers continue comparing prices and promotions with
competitors such as Shopee, Tokopedia, and Lazada. Thus, high platform usage does not
automatically indicate strong customer loyalty. Furthermore, previous findings on the
effect of live shopping on loyalty remain inconsistent, while research on social commerce
and customer loyalty in the Indonesian TikTok Shop context remains limited. Wijayanti
et al. (2025) demonstrated that live shopping positively affects TikTok Shop customer
loyalty, whereas Ahza et al. (2026) highlighted the important role of customer

Therefore, the novelty of this study lies in developing an integrated model that

LITERATURE REVIEW & HYPOTHESIS DEVELOPMENT
The Influence of Live Shopping and Social Commerce on Customer Engagement
Live shopping creates an interactive shopping environment through real -time
interactions, product demonstrations, and two-way communication. These features
encourage consumers to actively participate, respond, and interact during the shopping
process, thereby increasing customer engagement. Direct interaction between sellers and
consumers also lets users ask questions, get immediate product information, and receive
personalized responses, making the shopping experience more dynamic and engaging.
Xue et al. (2020) confirmed that live interactions positively promote social commerce
engagement. Nugroho and Sugiarto (2024) foun d that live commerce features
significantly increase customer engagement and actual purchases, indicating that the
interactive nature of live shopping strengthens consumers’ active involvement with the
platform. Furthermore, the combination of entertainment, information, and real-time
communication can encourage consumers to spend more time interacting with live
shopping content. Yan et al. (2023) similarly found that live st reaming affordances
enhance customer engagement through perceived enjoyment and social relationships.
Social commerce integrates social media with trading activities, enabling consumers
to interact, share experiences, and obtain recommendations before making purchasing
decisions. Features such as customer reviews, comments, influencers, and digital
communities facilitate information exchange and create opportunities for consumers to
participate in online discussions. Positive social interactions can strengthen trust and
encourage consumers to become more involved with the platform and other users.
Busalim and Ghabban (2021) found that social interaction, interactivity, and perceived
value significantly determine customer engagement in social commerce. Sidik and
Roostika (2024) found that perceived value in live social commerce increases customer
trust and engagement, demonstrating that valuable social interactions can encourage
consumers to participate more actively in the shopping process and develop stronger
connections with the platform.

The Influence of Live Shopping and Social Commerce on Customer Loyalty
Live shopping is a social commerce innovation that integrates live broadcasts with
transactional activities, creating an interactive and engaging shopping experience. Real-
time interactions, product demonstrations, and two -way communication enable
consumers to obtain product information directly while interacting with sellers and other
viewers. These features can increase perceived value, trust, and customer experience,
encouraging repeat purchases and stronger relationships with the platform. Bao and Zhu
(2023) found that interaction and vividness in live streaming commerce increase customer
satisfaction and platform stickiness. Auliarahman (2024) found that live shopping
increases continuous purchase intention, while Nugraha et al. (2024) showed that
engagement during live streaming strengthens customer relationships with the platform
and contributes to user loyalty. Ye et al. (2023) further confirmed that livestreaming
services enhance customer loyalty through engagement and value co-creation. The
interactive nature of live shopping can therefore encourage consumers to remain active
and loyal to TikTok Shop.
Social commerce also contributes to customer loyalty by providing opportunities for
social interaction, information exchange, and trust development during online shopping.
Consumers can communicate with sellers and other users, read opinions, obtain
recommendations, and evaluate products through social interactions. These activities can
reduce uncertainty and increase confidence in purchasing decisions. Joo and Jin (2021)

The Influence of Customer Engagement on Customer Loyalty
Customer engagement refers to the level of customers’ cognitive, emotional, and
behavioral involvement with a brand or digital platform. Within Relationship Marketing
Theory, customer engagement is a key foundation for developing and maintaining long-
term relationships between customers and companies. On digital commerce platforms
such as TikTok Shop, engagement is reflected through customers’ active participation in
live streaming, interactions with sellers and other users, responses to promotional content,
and willingness to explore product information. These interactions can build stronger
psychological connections and positive experiences, encouraging customers to become
more involved with the platform (Li et al., 2023). These interactions can build stronger
psychological connections and positive experiences, encouraging customers to become
more involved with the platform (Li et al., 2023). Nugraha et al. (2024) found that positive
live streaming experiences can increase customer engagement by encouraging consumers
to participate actively and interact with sellers during the shopping process. Rudy et al.
(2020) further showed that cognitive, emotional, and behavioral engagement are
important dimensions of customer engagement in Indonesian e-commerce.
Furthermore, high customer engagement can contribute to the development of
customer loyalty. Engaged customers are more likely to interact repeatedly with a
platform, provide recommendations, make repeat purchases, and maintain their
relationship with a brand despite the availability of alternative platforms. This suggests
that engagement goes beyond temporary purchasing behavior and reflects  a deeper
relationship between customers and the platform. Nugroho and Sugiarto (2024)
demonstrated that customer engagement significantly influences customer loyalty, while
engagement can also encourage actual purchasing behavior. Hapsari et al. (2020) found
that customer engagement has a direct positive effect on customer loyalty, while Islam
and Balqiah (2021) demonstrated that customer engagement contributes to repurchase
intention and positive word-of-mouth in online shopping. Gao and Huang (2021) also
confirmed that customer engagement plays a mediating role in strengthening customer
loyalty in omnichannel retailing. Therefore, customer engagement serves as an important
mechanism for strengthening customer relationships and fostering loyalty among TikTok
Shop users.

RESEARCH METHODS
This quantitative explanatory study examines the causal relationships between live
shopping, social commerce, customer engagement, and customer loyalty among TikTok
Shop users in Indonesia. The quantitative approach was chosen to objectively measure
relationships between variables through statistical analysis, with live shopping and social
commerce as independent variables, customer engagement as the mediating variable, and
customer loyalty as the dependent variable. The study used SEM-PLS with SmartPLS to
test direct and indirect relationships in the theoretically developed model, as it suits
complex latent constructs, does not require strict normality assumptions, and offers strong
predictive capability in digital marketing research (Nabila et al., 2023; Hair et al., 2024).
The study included TikTok Shop users in Indonesia who had made purchases through
live shopping and social commerce features. Respondents came from various regions in
Indonesia, and the questionnaire was distributed online using Google Forms. Data
collection was conducted from July to December 2025, encompassing instrument
development, questionnaire trials, questionnaire distribution, data processing, research
analysis, and report preparation. The study population was all TikTok Shop users in
Indonesia who had made at least one purchase through the platform. Given the unknown
population, the sampling technique used was purposive sampling with the criteria of
respondents being at least 17 years old, having an active TikTok account, having made a
transaction through TikTok Shop, and having watched live shopping. The sample size
was determined based on SEM-PLS recommendations, which require at least 10 times
the number of the largest structural paths leading to a construct; thus, the minimum
number of respondents is estimated at 200, which is considered adequate to produce stable
model estimates.
Primary data were obtained of distributing a questionnaire using a five-point Likert
scale, ranging from 1 = strongly disagree to 5 = strongly agree. The research instrument
was developed based on indicators adopted from previous research that have been tested
for validity and reliability. Before widespread distribution, a pilot test of the instrument
was conducted with a sample of respondents to ensure the  clarity of the questions.
Secondary data were obtained from reputable scientific journals, books, proceedings, and
other scientific publications relevant to research on live shopping, social commerce,
customer engagement, and customer loyalty. Data analysis was carried out using
Structural Equation Modeling-Partial Least Squares (SEM-PLS) through SmartPLS 4
software. The analysis stages include descriptive statistics, evaluation of the outer model,
which includes convergent validity, discriminant validity, and composite reliability, then
evaluation of the inner model through the R² value, f², Q² predictive relevance, SRMR,
and path coefficient testing using a bootstrapping procedure with a 5% significance level.
Tests also examined the direct and indirect (mediation) effects of customer engagement
on the relationship between live shopping and social commerce and customer loyalty. The
SEM-PLS approach was chosen because it effectively tests complex, prediction-oriented
models and is suitable for use in digital marketing research.

DISCUSSION
The results show that live shopping has a significant positive effect on customer loyalty
among TikTok Shop users in Indonesia. Real-time interaction, product demonstrations,
and direct communication can create more engaging shopping experiences and strengthen
consumers’ intention to remain with the platform. This finding is consistent with
Auliarahman (2024), who found that live shopping increases continuous purchase
intention through interactive experiences. Nugraha et al. (2024) similarly emphasized that
live streaming commerce strengthens consumers’ emotional attachment to digital
platforms, while Wijayanti et al. (2025) found that live shopping contributes to repurchase
intention. Zhang et al. (2022) found that live-streaming interactivity strengthens trust and
continuance intention, while Wongkitrungrueng and Assarut (2020) demonstrated that
live-streaming value and trust enhance consumer engagement with social commerce
sellers. These findings indicate that interactive live shopping experiences can encourage
stronger and more sustainable customer loyalty.
Social commerce also significantly influences customer loyalty, indicating that
reviews, recommendations, influencer content, and other forms of social interaction can
strengthen consumer trust and relationships with the platform. This result is supported by
Sidik and Roostika (2024), who found that social commerce increases trust and purchase
intention, and Burhanudin and Puspita (2023), who showed that social interaction on
digital platforms contributes to customer loyalty. Alfarisi (2024) and Layli et al. (2025)
further found that e-WOM strengthens repurchase decisions. Prestyasih and Hati (2025)
further found that social support and customer review quality significantly strengthen
social commerce trust among TikTok users, supporting the importance of social
interactions and user-generated information in shaping consumer behavior. The findings
therefore demonstrate that social elements within TikTok Shop can encourage consumers
to maintain their relationship with the platform.
The results further show that live shopping significantly increases customer
engagement. Real-time communication, visual demonstrations, and direct seller–buyer
interaction encourage consumers to participate actively in the shopping process. This
finding aligns with Nugroho and Sugiarto (2024), who found that live commerce increases
engagement through real-time interactions. Auliarahman (2024) also highlighted the
importance of visual experiences and direct communication, while Hartono et al. (2023)
found that live streaming marketing increases customer attention and participation.
Similarly, social commerce significantly influences customer engagement, as comments,
reviews, content sharing, and social recommendations encourage consumers to interact
more actively. This result is consistent with Kurniawan and Sunitiyoso (2024), Nugraha
et al. (2024), and Busalim and Asadi (2025), who emphasized the role of social
interaction, digital communities, and social influence in increasing consumer
engagement.
Furthermore, customer engagement significantly influences customer loyalty.
Consumers with higher levels of cognitive, emotional, and behavioral involvement are
more likely to interact repeatedly, make purchases, provide recommendations, and
maintain relationships with the platform. This finding supports Nugroho and Sugiarto
(2024), who linked engagement with customer retention, and Auliarahman (2024), who
identified engagement as an important factor in developing long-term loyalty. Rasool et
al. (2021) further found that customer engagement contributes to the development of a
sustained loyal customer base.
The mediation results indicate that customer engagement significantly mediates the
relationships between live shopping and customer loyalty and between social commerce

CONCLUSION
This study concludes that live shopping and social commerce significantly influence
customer loyalty among TikTok Shop users in Indonesia, both directly and indirectly
through customer engagement. Based on SEM-PLS analysis of 200 respondents, all
proposed hypotheses were supported. Live shopping and social commerce significantly
increase customer engagement, while customer engagement significantly strengthens
customer loyalty. The significant indirect effects further indicate that customer
engagement functions as a partial mediator, demonstrating that interactive and socially
oriented shopping experiences can strengthen loyalty both directly and through greater
consumer involvement.
The findings provide theoretical implications by supporting the SOR Theory, in which
live shopping and social commerce represent stimuli, customer engagement represents the
internal psychological process, and customer loyalty represents the behavioral response.
Practically, marketplace managers and sellers can strengthen loyalty by improving real-
time interaction, product demonstrations, social features, customer reviews,
recommendations, and other activities that encourage active customer engagement.
This study has several limitations. The sample consisted of only 200 TikTok Shop users
in Indonesia, which may limit the generalizability of the findings to other platforms,
countries, or consumer groups. In addition, the cross-sectional design captures consumer
perceptions at a particular point in time and therefore does not reflect changes in
engagement and loyalty over time. Future research could use larger and more diverse
samples, compare different social commerce platforms, and apply longitudinal or mixed-
method approaches. Future studies could also incorporate other relevant variables, such
as customer trust, perceived value, e-WOM, FoMO, or purchase intention, to provide a
more comprehensive understanding of customer loyalty in digital commerce.
