Abstract
The coordination and effective utilization of  customer feedback in e nhancing corporate
marketing decisions has become critical factor in shaping effective marketing decisions strategy
in today’s highly volatile and complex business arena . This paper examines the relationship
between customer feedback and strategic decision making among manufacturing companies in
Ibadan, Oyo State.  A survey research design was employed.  115 respondents from various
manufacturing companies in Ibadan served as sample size of the study . The study notes that
implementing customer feedback in businesses can tailor their marketing efforts to better meet
customer needs, improve product development, and enhance overall customer satisfaction.
Through a data -driven approach to marketing decisions, businesses can strengthen customer
relationships, drive business growth, and achieve long -term success in the competitive
marketplace. In conclusion, Customer feedback mechanism plays a crucial role in shaping
marketing decisions strategy for businesses. Through various channels such as surveys, reviews,
and social media, businesses can gather valuable insights from customers regarding their
experiences and preferences. Thus, the study recommends that feedback should be analyzed and
used to make informed decisions on marketing campaigns, product development, and overall
business strategies. By listening to and acting upon customer feedback, businesses can improve
customer satisfaction, loyalty, and ultimately achieve greater success in the marketplace.

Keywords: Customer Feedback, Marketing, Decisions, Strategy, Business Success

Concept of Customer Feedback
Customer feedback is regarded as customer communication concerning a product or a service
Customer feedback can be either solicited or unsolicited. Solicited feedback is encouraged by the
company through the usage of tools such as surveys and focus groups that inv ite customers to
give feedback . In contrast, unsolicited customer feedback relies on the customer’s own desire
and will to c ommunicate his/her experiences (Cerutti & Piva, 2016).  The mechanisms for
customer feedback include social media, customer forums and survey (Gupta et al., 2018). In

Customer feedback and marketing strategies
According to Gupta, et. al, (2018) u sing customer feedback to identify new market growth
opportunities is a powerful way to drive business growth. Listening to customers and incorporate
their feedback into marketing decision-making process, can gain valuable insights into what they
want and need from business. This can help identify new market growth opportunities that may
not have been considered otherwise. Steps to achieve market growth opportunities using
customer feedback mechanism are:
1. Identify unmet customer needs: By listening to customers and gathering their feedback,
one can identify unmet needs or pain points that they are facing. This can give new ideas for
products or services that can meet those needs and drive growth for business.
2. Expand into new markets: Customer feedback can also help identify new markets to
expand into. For example, the feedback from customers in a specific geographic region, can be
used to consider expanding business into that area.
3. Develop new products or services: Customer feedback can also help identify new product
or service ideas. That is, the feedback received from customers may suggest adding feature to the
existing product, this can help to develop a new product that meets their needs and drives growth
for business.
4. Improve customer satisfaction: Finally, customer feedback can help improve customer
satisfaction and drive growth by providing information to make changes that enhance the
customer experience. For example, if there is a feedback from  customers that are having trouble
navigating the company  website, this information can be used  to improve the user experience
and drive growth by retaining and attracting new customers.
Marketing strategies can provide advantages for businesses seeking better understanding of
current and potential consumers' needs (Ritter, 2016). They help managers in evaluating business
needs and influence purchases of consumers (Woo, Ahn, Lee, & Koo, 2015). To develop a
successful marketing strategy, managers should focus on developing knowledge of consumer
demands and lifestyles (Nica, 2013). The purpose of a marketing strategy is to assess the
essential needs of businesses by evaluating consumer demands and developing relevant
procedures to positively influence consumer purchasing decisions (Woo et al., 2015).
Marketers use strategies to address business goals and concerns by surveying and assessing
organizational knowledge, effectiveness, threats, and opportunities (Lipnická & Ďaďo, 2013). A
marketing strategy should assist businesses in achieving brand awareness, promoting business
trust, and improving profits and investor wealth (Ritter, 2016). In addition, marketing strategies
are pivotal for discovering potential risks within organizational operations, increasing knowledge
of consumers, and competitors (Lipnická & Ďaďo, 2013).
Successful marketing strategies start with an examination and cognizance of consumer behavior
and variables that influence consumer behavior (Sargunani & Bruce, 2015). Establishing an

Theoretical Framework
Attribution Theory
The attribution theory was developed in the domain of social psychology by Fritz Heider,
Dorwin Cartwright, and Leonard Bostwick in a publication entitled “The Psychology of
Judgment”. In this publication, the authors pointed out that people are rational in their judgment
processes and that there are conditions under which their judgment is rational. These researchers
argued that there are three criteria for attribution to be made: consistency, consensus, and
coherence or correspondence.

Conclusion
Customer feedback mechanism is a crucial aspect of any business as it helps in understanding
customer needs and preferences. It involves collecting feedback from customers through various
channels like surveys, reviews, and social media platforms to gather insights on their experiences
with the product or service. Marketing decisions strategy, on the other hand, involves analyzing
customer feedback and using it to make informed decisions on marketing campaigns, product
development, and overall business strategies. By listening to customer feedback, businesses can
tailor their marketing efforts to better meet customer needs and improve customer satisfaction.
In summary, a strong customer feedback mechanism combined with a data -driven marketing
decisions strategy can help businesses improve their products, services, and overall customer
experience, leading to increased customer loyalty and business success.
