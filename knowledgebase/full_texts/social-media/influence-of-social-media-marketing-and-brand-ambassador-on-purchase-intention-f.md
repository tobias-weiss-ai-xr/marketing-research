ABSTRACT

This study aims to determine the influence of social media marketing and brand ambassadors
on purchase intention for Rucas products. The rapid growth of social media as a primary
marketing tool has encouraged many companies to utilize robust digital marketing strategies
and strategic brand  ambassador partnerships to effectively incr ease consumer purchase
intention. This study employed a quantitative method with a descriptive approach to analyze
these variables. The sampling technique utilized non-probability sampling with a specific
purposive sampling method. Data were obtained by distributing online questionnaires to
targeted respondents who either knew or actively followed Rucas' of ficial social media
accounts. Data analysis was performed using mult iple linear regression analysis to
rigorously test the influence of social media marketing and brand ambassadors on purchase
intention. The results demonstrate that social media marketing has a positive and significant
effect on purchase intention for Rucas products. Furthermore, brand ambassadors also exert
a positive and significant effect on the purchase intention for Rucas products. Simultaneously,
social media marketing and brand ambassadors significantly influence consumer purchase
intention for Rucas products. These comprehensive findings suggest that an effective social
media marketing st rategy, combined with the appropr iate s election and use of  brand
ambassadors, can substantially increase consumer purchase intention for Rucas products in
the competitive modern market

Keywords: Social Media Marketing, Brand Ambassador, Purchase Intention

Consumer Purchase Intention
Definition of Purchase Intention
Purchase intention is an i mportant constru ct in consumer b ehavior that  reflects an
individual’s tendency to consider and eventually purchase a product or brand. Sinaga and

were seeking regarding the product they want.
In the context of digital marketing, consumer purchase intention is heavily influenced
by exposure to digital content and recommendations from trusted individuals. Yani et al.
(2022) found that so cial media m arketing h as a positive effect on brand image, whi ch
ultimately increases consumer purchase intention. Punyatoya (in Effendi & Defrizal, 2024)
affirms that brand trust built through effective marketing communication directly drives the
growth of higher purchase intention.

Conceptual Framework
Based on the th eoretical review and previous research, the conceptual framework of
this study describes the relationship between three main variables. Social media marketing
(X1) and brand ambassador (X2), as independent variables, are presumed to have a positive
and signifi cant effect on RUCAS consu mer pu rchase intention (Y)  as the d ependent

consumer b ehavior theo ry—purchase intention is  formed from the int eraction b etween
