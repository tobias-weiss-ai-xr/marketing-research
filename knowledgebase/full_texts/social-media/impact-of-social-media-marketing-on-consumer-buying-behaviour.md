ABSTRACT
Social media marketing has become a significant component of contemporary marketing as customers increasingly
rely on digital media to research, compare, review and buy products. The current study focuses on the influence of
social media marketing on consumer buying behaviour wi th particular reference to Plant Green Inertia Private
Limited, Maduravoyal, Chennai. The key aspects explored include advertisement exposure, brand information,
influencer marketing, reviews, creative content, product comparison, customer engagement and promotional offers.
A descriptive research design was adopted and primary research data were collected using a structured
questionnaire. Five-point Likert scales and convenience sampling were employed in the process. The report presents
demographic statisti cs, analytical results and recommendations. Percentage analysis, Chi -square and one -way
ANOVA tests were performed to reveal the impact of social media marketing on customer buying behaviour. The
study found that respondents actively used social media and that reviews, brand information, offers, product
comparison and engaging content could influence buying behaviour. A Chi -square test result of 42.286 against a
critical value of 9.488 showed that exposure to social media marketing influenced consumer behaviour. An ANOVA
result of 209.304 against a critical value of 3.89 showed significant differences in the buying behaviour of
respondents. The study recommended improved trust -building, engaging content, authentic reviews, judicious
influencer selection, personalisation and regular monitoring.
Keywords: Social Media Marketing, Consumer Buying Behaviour, Digital Marketing, Online Reviews, Influencer
Marketing, Customer Engagement, Purchase Decision.
1. INTRODUCTION
Social media marketing has revolutionised bus iness communication and marketing activities. Smartphone
proliferation, internet penetration and digital media developments have enabled companies to network with
consumers interactively, advertise products and services, create brand image and build relati onships. Facebook,
Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn and X offer businesses opportunities to market their products and services
effectively. As a result, companies have opened accounts on social media networks to gain more customers and
boost sales. Customers increasingly turn to social media to search for products, compare prices, read reviews, watch
demonstrations and engage with businesses before purchasing decisions. Therefore, businesses must understand
online consumer expectations and develop attractive, authentic and engaging content to meet demand.
Plant Green Inertia Private Limited is a professional training and coaching institution that provides training and
placement assistance to learners. It offers training courses on digital marketing, communication skills, soft skills,
personality development, career counselling, and employability skills. It uses LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook to
educate prospects about the benefits of training, share blog posts and articles, post success stories and testimonials,
upload training videos and encourage applica tions. This paper sought to establish the impact of social media
marketing on the buying behaviour of customers of Plant Green Inertia Private Limited, Maduravoyal, Chennai. It
evaluated advertisement exposure, trust, influencer marketing, customer reviews, engaging content, and promotion
offers.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
Modern customers are exposed to a high volume of promotional content and advertisements daily, which makes it
difficult for businesses to gain consumer attention and stand out in the social media crowd. Contemporary customers
are fickle-hearted and can change their buying behaviour depending on digital media trends. Moreover, reviews and
ratings on online platforms can mislead consumers, whereas negative feedback can damage the brand image of a
business. Therefore, businesses must invest in social media strategically to maximise profits. Other issues include
identifying the best social media networks for marketing, analysing customer engagement data to gain insights,
determining the return on in vestment of digital marketing efforts and balancing consumer trust. Businesses must
also note that social media algorithms are continuously evolving, thus impacting the visibility of a company’s
content. Consequently, the current study sought to establish the influence of social media marketing on consumer
buying behaviour and explore effective marketing strategies for businesses.

5. RESEARCH METHODOLOGY
The current study employed a descriptive research design. Both primary and secondary data were used to reveal the
influence of social media marketing on consumer behaviour. Convenience sampling was used to select participants
from the target population. A structured questionnaire was used to collect research data from respondents. The
questionnaire comprised demographic questions a nd statements regarding social media marketing and buying
behaviour. A five -point Likert scale was used to enable respondents to indicate their views on issues raised.
Percentage analysis and statistical tests, including Chi-square and one-way ANOVA, were performed to analyse the
data.
NOTE: Please refer to the appended section for the research data used in the current study. While the report states
that 100 respondents were used, some sections of the document, including the demographics, state that the sample
size was 35. Similarly, the Chi-Square table has a hundred percentages, whereas the ANOVA section notes that the
score was indicative. Therefore, this article has adopted the figures as reported in the research paper without making
adjustments to align the numbers.

The Chi-square test was used to determine whether there is a significant association between social media marketing
exposure and consumer buying behaviour. The calculated Chi-square value of 42.286 is greater than the critical
value of 9.488 at the 5% significance level. Therefore, the null hypothesis is rejected. The reported result indicates
a significant association between social media marketing exposure and consumer buying behaviour.

The ANOVA examined whether mean consumer buying behaviour differs across low, moderate and high social

8. SUGGESTIONS
• Create engaging content using creative pictures, short videos, reels, demonstrations and interactive material.
• Provide transparent, accurate and easily verifiable brand information to strengthen trust.
• Encourage genuine customer reviews and testimonials.
• Use discounts and promotional offers strategically and communicate them clearly.
• Select influencers according to relevance and audience fit rather than popularity alone.
• Use polls, quizzes, contests, live chats, comments and Q&A sessions to increase engagement.
• Present product features, benefits, price and value clearly to support comparison.
• Personalize social media campaigns around customer interests, age, preferences and online habits.
• Monitor engagement, reach, sales, website visits and other social media metrics.
9. LIMITATIONS OF THE STUDY
• The study is focused on Plant Green Inertia Private Limited, Maduravoyal, Chennai s o the findings may not be
generalizable to all organizations or consumer groups.
• Convenience sampling was used so every member of the wider population did not have an equal probability of
selection.
• The project report contains a sample-size inconsistency; this article preserves the source figures.
• The study is based on respondents’ reported perceptions and opinions.
• Social media platforms, algorithms, trends and consumer preferences change rapidly.
• The study does not establish that social media ma rketing is the only factor responsible for consumer buying
behaviour.
10. CONCLUSION
The study concludes that social media marketing is an important element of modern marketing and is meaningfully
associated with consumer buying behaviour. Consumers use so cial media to obtain product information, read
reviews, compare alternatives, view promotional content and learn from other customers’ experiences. The findings
indicate that reviews, product information, promotional offers, product comparison, creative content and customer
engagement can contribute to consumer decision-making.
The statistical analysis reported in the project supports this conclusion. The Chi-square result indicates a significant
association between social media marketing exposure and consu mer buying behaviour while the ANOVA result
indicates significant differences in buying behaviour across levels of exposure. The reported mean scores increase
from 55.0 for low exposure to 67.2 for moderate exposure and 80.0 for high exposure.
However, advertisement exposure alone does not guarantee consumer attention or trust. Businesses need to combine
attractive and relevant content with credible information, genuine reviews, suitable influencers, personalized
communication and interactive engagement. For Plant Green Inertia Private Limited, strengthening these areas can
support brand awareness, customer engagement and purchasing decisions. Continuous monitoring of social media
