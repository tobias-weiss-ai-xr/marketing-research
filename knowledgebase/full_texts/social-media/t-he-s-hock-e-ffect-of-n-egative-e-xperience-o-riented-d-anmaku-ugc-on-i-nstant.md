The Shock Effect of Negative Experience-Oriented Danmaku
UGC on Instant Sales in Douyin Foundation Live Streaming
Rooms
Miaoyan Yu
Beijing 21st Century School, Beijing, China
yumiaoyan529@outlook.com
Abstract. Danmaku in beauty live-streaming serves as a core real-time reference for
consumers to judge the practical application effect of foundation before making purchase
decisions. For such highly experience-dependent cosmetic products that cannot be
physically tested in online shopping scenarios, negative user-generated comments
embedded in live-stream danmaku exert far-reaching and significant impacts on real-time
transactions and instant conversion within live-stream rooms. Against the backdrop of
booming social commerce live-streaming, a peculiar and counter-intuitive phenomenon has
been widely observed in Douyin foundation live-stream rooms: rather than merely
suppressing product sales, negative experience-related comments can reversely drive sales
growth under certain conditions. Drawing on the classical SOR (Stimulus-Organism-
Response), ELM (Elaboration Likelihood Model) and HSM (Heuristic-Systematic Model)
theories of consumer behavior, this paper systematically sorts out domestic and international
relevant literature in the field of e-commerce live-streaming. It further summarizes four
critical action mechanisms behind this counter-intuitive effect, including platform
algorithmic logic, user trust perception, streamer on-site response and audience screening.
Meanwhile, this study points out prominent deficiencies embedded in existing research,
such as over-generalized product category selection, excessive reliance on static aggregate
sales indicators, as well as theoretical frameworks and empirical designs that fail to match
Douyin's unique content-driven traffic logic. This review clarifies existing research gaps in
vertical beauty live-streaming scenarios and provides solid theoretical support for
subsequent empirical research on the two-way impact of negative danmaku, as well as
practical guidance for public opinion monitoring and management of beauty live-stream
operations.
Keywords: Danmaku UGC, negative experience-oriented comments, foundation, Douyin
live streaming room, instant sales

2. Literature review
With the rapid development of live streaming e-commerce, Douyin has become an important
marketing and trading platform for beauty categories. User-generated content (UGC) in live
streaming rooms breaks through the one-way information barrier of streamer marketing, and has
become an important reference factor for consumers' current purchase decisions. Among various
beauty categories, foundation is a representative highly experience-dependent beauty product. When
shopping online, consumers cannot try foundation on their face. Practical usage experiences such as
oxidation, caking, color difference and makeup lasting effect cannot be obtained from product
displays, so consumers rely heavily on UGC feedback from viewers in the live streaming room to
make judgments. Therefore, compared with conventional search products such as clothing and daily
necessities, negative user-generated content (UGC) has a much stronger interference on consumers'
order willingness and real-time decision-making [1].
Traditional academic views and general industry cognition believe that negative comments will
reduce consumers' trust, inhibit their purchase behavior, and lead to a decline in product sales.
However, in the context of Douyin foundation live streaming, there is an industry contradictory
phenomenon: some negative experience-oriented user-generated content (UGC) can improve the
exposure heat of the live streaming room and users' dwell time through Douyin's controversial
traffic push mechanism, and instead make the conversion rate and sales volume rise against the
trend. Existing studies show that negative danmaku has a two-way impact on sales, and the overall
action path can be summarized into four mechanisms: platform algorithm, consumers' trust
perception, streamers' on-site response, and audience screening. The platform algorithm will tilt
traffic to support controversial danmaku, expand the public domain exposure of the live streaming
room, and bring incremental potential customers [2]; moderately scattered experience negative
comments can enhance the authenticity of word-of-mouth, change consumers' trust judgment, and
form the driving force for positive conversion [3]; streamers' on-site responses such as real-time
Q&A and product testing for negative comments can buffer the negative impact brought by negative
information, and differentiated negative experience danmaku will automatically complete audience
stratification, filter out people with mismatched skin types, and retain more suitable target
consumers, so as to optimize the overall conversion efficiency [4, 5]. These four mechanisms work
together, resulting in the anti-intuitive result that negative comments both inhibit and boost instant
sales. Existing reviews point out that social e-commerce algorithms naturally tilt towards

At this stage, most domestic studies discuss the relationship between UGC and consumer
behavior based on classical theories, but their research scenarios are mostly general live streaming
platforms, and their research objects are mostly broad-category products, which do not fit Douyin's
unique algorithm distribution mechanism and the high-experience product characteristics of
foundation. Moreover, existing studies lack the capture of dynamic fluctuations of instant sales in
live streaming rooms, and lack in-depth analysis of segmented experience-oriented negative
comments. It is difficult to explain the two-way shock effect of "simultaneous inhibition and
promotion" of negative comments on sales. It can be seen that there is an obvious research gap in
this segmented field. Against this background, this paper systematically sorts out relevant domestic
and foreign literature, summarizes the advantages and disadvantages of existing research, and
launches a review focusing on the shock effect of negative experience-oriented UGC on real-time
sales in Douyin foundation live streaming rooms. It intends to fill the existing research gaps and
provide theoretical reference for public opinion management and sales regulation of beauty live
streaming.
Existing studies have limitations in sample scenarios and product categories: most domestic
empirical studies take maternal and infant live streaming rooms as core samples, most questionnaire
studies do not limit live streaming platforms and product tracks, and there are few in-depth analyses
focusing on vertical beauty categories. Relevant foreign studies mostly conduct experiments based
on general fashion categories, with insufficient category pertinence [7]. Meanwhile, most domestic
and foreign literature takes Taobao live streaming as the core research scenario, and only a few focus
on Douyin live streaming, nor do they further refine to the specific beauty product of foundation [8].
Although current research can distinguish the difference of danmaku impact between search
products and experience products, and verify that experience products are more susceptible to
danmaku sentiment, relevant results are derived based on Taobao live streaming data, which does
not conform to Douyin's platform traffic rules. Douyin has a unique algorithm system, which will
actively push and amplify controversial danmaku in live streaming rooms. Its traffic communication
logic is completely different from that of traditional e-commerce live streaming. Research
conclusions on general categories such as maternal and infant products and clothing cannot explain
the special contradiction in Douyin foundation live streaming rooms, that is, negative evaluations
can both inhibit product sales and increase transaction volume. In addition, previous negative public
opinion studies mostly focus on serious brand scandals such as streamers selling counterfeits and
false propaganda [9]. Such events are highly sudden and destructive, which are significantly
different from the scattered product experience negative comments common in foundation live
streaming rooms, with limited reference value. At present, refined special research on the scenario of
Douyin foundation is still blank.
Existing research mainly focuses on two directions: the overall impact of danmaku on sales, and
the classification of negative danmaku, with a broad research perspective and insufficient
refinement. Sales-related research mostly takes the cumulative sales of the whole live broadcast and
consumers' subjective purchase intention as core variables, focusing on static result analysis,
ignoring dynamic consumer behavior in the live broadcast process, and cannot accurately capture
sales fluctuations and short-term change trends during the outbreak period of negative danmaku.
Although some studies use minute-level high-frequency data analysis, they have not carried out
targeted research on experience-oriented negative comments such as foundation oxidation, caking,
color difference and poor makeup lasting effect [2, 5].

The existing classification methods of negative danmaku classification research are relatively
rough. Most of them only simply distinguish streamer negative comments from product negative
comments, or only make general judgments based on the number of danmaku and emotional
intensity [2, 7]. They do not subdivide dimensions combined with foundation product characteristics
and consumers' actual demands, so they cannot distinguish the differentiated impact of different
types of experience negative comments on real-time sales in live streaming rooms, and lack targeted
discussion on segmented beauty categories.
Existing danmaku consumption research has formed a mature theoretical framework and
analytical methods, but the adaptation effect is poor for Douyin's vertical beauty scenarios. The
academic community mostly adopts three classical frameworks: SOR (Stimulus-Organism-
Response), ELM (Elaboration Likelihood Model) and HSM (Heuristic-Systematic Model) to explain
the influence process of danmaku on consumer decision-making [10]. At the same time, combined
with text recognition, data modeling, questionnaire experiments, public opinion coding and other
methods, a complete research paradigm has been formed.
Existing research methods have obvious defects when adapting to the scenario of Douyin
foundation live streaming. First, the research perspective is relatively static. Few studies use short-
term event analysis to capture short-term sales fluctuations after the outbreak of negative comments,
making it difficult to observe the instant impact of negative user-generated content (UGC) on sales.
Second, the adaptability of analysis tools is insufficient. The traditional public opinion coding
system is mainly designed for major brand negative scandals, which is not suitable for the trivial and
normalized daily experience negative comments in foundation live streaming rooms [9]. There is an
obvious lack of logic in existing research: most of them do not analyze combined with Douyin's
algorithm traffic mechanism, ignoring the platform's amplification and drainage effect on
controversial danmaku. Such studies can neither explain the two-way shock effect of negative
danmaku on sales, nor clarify the occurrence scope and applicable conditions of this effect.
According to the comprehensive literature review, existing research on live streaming danmaku
and consumer behavior has formed a complete research framework with solid theoretical foundation
and mature research methods. Existing studies have confirmed that factors such as danmaku
sentiment, streamer-product fit and product attributes have a significant impact on live streaming
purchase behavior, which can provide a solid literature basis and theoretical support for this study
[11].
There are still three key deficiencies in current relevant research. First, the research objects are
insufficiently segmented. Most focus on non-beauty categories and traditional e-commerce
platforms, lacking special research on the vertical category of foundation on Douyin platform.
Second, the research topics are one-sided, mostly focusing on static cumulative sales and subjective
purchase intention, lacking exploration of instant dynamic sales, and the classification of negative
danmaku is not fine enough. Third, the scenario adaptability of research methods is poor, which does
not conform to the characteristics of Douyin platform traffic communication, and it is difficult to
reasonably explain the special phenomenon of two-way shock of negative comments on sales. The
existing research gap is the core value of this review, and also clarifies the direction for subsequent
empirical research.
Based on the research deficiencies sorted out above, follow-up targeted empirical research can be
carried out based on the literature research results of this paper, and the existing research
shortcomings can be made up through refined, contextualized and dynamic empirical design. The
specific implementation paths and research methods are as follows:

First, clearly define the research samples and scenarios. Abandon the previous research ideas of

characteristics of foundation, and solve the problem of insufficient adaptability of previous research

Second, optimize the data collection method. Break through the limitations of traditional static
cumulative sales indicators, adopt high-frequency short-term data collection mode, obtain panel data
such as minute-level instant sales, real-time online number, danmaku release time and text content in
the live streaming room, accurately match the time node of negative danmaku outbreak with the
sales fluctuation of the corresponding period, and capture the instant shock effect of negative user-
generated content (UGC) on sales.
Third, refine the variable quantification and classification standards. Abandon the traditional
simple positive and negative sentiment dichotomy. Combined with the core usage pain points of
foundation, manually divide negative experience-oriented user-generated content (UGC) into four

realize the refined processing of negative comment data.

SOR and ELM, adopt the short-term event study method combined with multiple regression models
to empirically test the impact direction and degree of different types of negative experience
danmaku on instant sales. At the same time, introduce platform algorithm exposure, streamer fan
level and product price gradient as moderating variables to explore the moderating effect of internal
and external factors on the two-way effect, and clarify the boundary conditions of negative
comments "inhibiting sales" and "boosting sales".
3. Discussion
Based on the comprehensive literature review, existing studies in the field of live streaming user-
generated content have confirmed that negative danmaku has a dual effect on consumer behavior,
but relevant results are difficult to be directly applied to the segmented scenario of Douyin
foundation. From the perspective of external traffic operation logic, Douyin's unique algorithm
distribution mechanism will expand the visibility of controversial negative danmaku, which is rarely
included in the analytical framework in relevant studies of Taobao live streaming [11]. At the
consumer psychological level, the two-way changes of trust perception and the automatic audience
screening mechanism are only involved in some dynamic sales studies, and have not been discussed
in detail for the differences of foundation skin type adaptation [2, 10]. From the perspective of live
streaming interaction, streamers' on-site response as a key moderating variable has been empirically
supported [4]. However, existing studies have not integrated it with the four dimensions of platform
algorithm, audience stratification and consumer perception, so they cannot fully clarify the
transmission chain of negative comments' two-way shock on instant sales.
Existing literature generally has three types of deficiencies: generalized scenarios, static
indicators, and rough negative comment classification. Most studies take maternal and infant or
general fashion categories as objects, lacking special analysis of foundation, a highly experience-
dependent beauty product. Most mainstream variables adopt the cumulative sales or purchase
intention of the whole live broadcast, and do not make full use of minute-level instant dynamic data.
Negative danmaku is only roughly divided into content targeting streamers or products, and is not
subdivided and coded combined with specific usage pain points of foundation such as oxidation,

The four action dimensions do not play their effects in isolation, but link with each other to form

conversion ratio of the two types of audiences, leading to differentiated results of instant sales rise or
fall. This linkage logic is a theoretical perspective that has not been systematically constructed in
existing research, and is also the core innovation entry point of this study.
4. Conclusion
First of all, foundation is a beauty product highly dependent on usage experience. The interference
intensity of negative experience danmaku in the live streaming room on consumers' real-time order
decision is significantly higher than that of search products such as clothing and daily necessities.
Supported by Douyin's algorithm, such negative danmaku will simultaneously trigger two reverse
shocks: sales inhibition and sales boost. This anti-intuitive phenomenon cannot be fully explained by
the conclusions of traditional Taobao live streaming or broad-category research. Secondly, the two-
way shock effect of negative danmaku is driven by four paths: platform algorithm, consumers' trust
perception, streamers' on-site response, and audience screening. The four mechanisms are linked
with each other to jointly determine the fluctuation range and change direction of instant sales. Most
existing literature separately verifies the role of a single mechanism, and has not yet formed an
analytical framework integrating the four-dimensional mechanisms.
There are three core deficiencies in current relevant research: generalized research objects,
lacking special discussion on the vertical category of Douyin foundation; static research indicators,
lacking minute-level instant dynamic sales observation; rough research classification, which neither
subdivides experience-oriented negative comments combined with foundation product pain points,
nor incorporates Douyin's unique controversial traffic algorithm mechanism into the research model,
making it difficult to clarify the occurrence boundary of the two-way effect.
Subsequent empirical research can select Douyin foundation live streaming sessions as samples,
collect minute-level instant sales panel data, subdivide four types of experience negative comments:
oxidation, caking, color difference and poor makeup lasting effect. Adopt the short-term event study
method, set platform exposure, streamer response and user skin type heterogeneity as moderating
variables, quantitatively test the transmission effect of the four mechanisms, clarify the applicable
boundary of negative comments promoting or inhibiting sales, and provide empirical basis and
theoretical support for public opinion management and real-time sales regulation of beauty live
streaming rooms.
