ABSTRACT
Buyingasmartphoneinthecurrentenvironmentmostlyrequiresextensiveonlinesurveys,comparisonsintermsofpriceandreadingofotherconsumers’reviews.
This study aims to investigate the relationship between four factors, including price, brand image, product quality and social media reviews, and consumers’
buyingbehaviourtowardssmartphones. Thedescriptive researchdesign wasutilised,andtheprimarydata weregathered from 80participants,whowere selected
employing a convenience sampling method. The data were collected using a Google Forms questionnaire and analysed using percentage analysis, Pearson
correlation, one-way ANOVA and the weighted average method. The results revealed that there was a strong positive correlation between smartphone price and
consumerbuyingbehaviour(r=0.785, p=0.000),whereasthebuyingbehaviordifferedsignificantlybetween thebrand-imagecategories(F=34.17, p=0.000).
Regarding the analysis of social media reviews, comparing smartphones on the basis of reviews appeared to be the most common activity (weighted average
3.36), followed by taking reviews into account when choosing a smartphone (3.30). Overall, the results suggest that the participants take all factors into account
when buy smartphones. Smartphone companies can benefit from the results of the study when determining their product’s price, branding, quality and social
mediamarketingstrategy.
