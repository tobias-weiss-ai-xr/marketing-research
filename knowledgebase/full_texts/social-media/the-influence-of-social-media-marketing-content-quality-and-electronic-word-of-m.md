ABSTRACT: This study examines the influence of Social Media Marketing, Content Quality, and Electronic Word of Mouth (eWOM)
on Repurchasing Intention with Brand Trust as a mediating variable in the context of the digital news portal Okezone.com. In
response to increasing competition in digital media, news platforms are required to strengthen audience trust and engagement
to encourage repeated access behavior. This research adopts a quantitative approach with a descriptive-explanatory design, using
data collected from  200 active readers in the Jabodetabek area through purposive sampling, and analyzed using Structural
Equation Modeling (SEM) with LISREL 8.80. The results show that Brand Trust has a positive and significant effect on Repurchasing
Intention (β = 0.215, t = 2.922), while Social Media Marketing (β = 0.208, t = 2.803), Content Quality (β = 0.212, t = 2.992), and
eWOM (β = 0.280, t = 4.123) also have positive and significant effects on Repurchasing Intention. Furthermore, Social Media
Marketing (β = 0.306, t =  3.777), Content Quality (β = 0.260, t = 3.346), and eWOM (β = 0.231, t = 3.114) significantly influence
Brand Trust. Mediation analysis using the Sobel test confirms that Brand Trust significantly mediates the relationship betwee n
Social Media Marketing (indirect effect = 0.066, Z = 2.311), Content Quality (indirect effect = 0.056, Z = 2.201), and eWOM (indirect
effect = 0.050, Z = 2.131) on Repurchasing Intention, with the model explaining 63.3% of Brand Trust and 51.3% of Repurchasin g
Intention, indicating moderate explanatory power.
KEYWORDS: Social Media Marketing; Content Quality; Electronic Word of Mouth (eWOM); Brand Trust; Repurchasing Intention.
I. INTRODUCTION
Okezone.com is one of the leading digital news portals under PT Media Nusantara Citra Tbk (MNC), the largest integrated media
group in Indonesia and Southeast Asia. Since its establishment in 2007, Okezone has positioned itself as a pioneer of interne t-
based media by offering fast, real -time, and easily accessible information through digital devices. Over time, Okezone has
expanded its content coverage across various categories, including national, politics, economy, lifestyle, entertainment, spo rts,
technology, and religion, to meet the diverse needs of its audience and strengthen its position as a reliable news portal. In response
to the increasingly competitive digital media landscape, Okezone continues to innovate by adopting multiplatform strategies such
as Okezone TV and Okezone Radio, as well as actively utilizing social media platforms including Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X
(Twitter), and YouTube to enhance information dissemination and audience engagement (Okezone.com, 2025).
Preliminary research involving 40 respondents indicates that Social Media Marketing activities are generally perceived
positively, with 82.5% of respondents considering the content relevant and up -to-date, 10% perceiving it as moderately relevant,
and 7.5% expressing critical concerns such as sensational headlines and inconsistency. In terms of content sharing, 70% expressed
willingness to share content when it is relevant and impactful, 20% were reluctant due to perceived clickbait, and 10% rarely  or
never shared content. Interaction aspects show that 57.5% perceived engagement as active, 27.5% were passive users, and 15%
considered it suboptimal, while community building was rated positively by 67.5%, moderately by 17.5%, and negatively by 15%.
Regarding Content Quality, most respondents rated performance at 72.5%, reliability at 75%, and conformance to journalistic
standards at 82.5% as positive, although some highlighted limitations related to depth, visual quality, and consistency. For eWOM,
75% of respondents reported sharing or discussing content under certain conditions, 15% occasionally, and 10% never, with 60%
expressing positive opinions, 32.5% neutral, and 7.5% non -evaluative. These findings are reflected in Brand Trust, where 70% of
respondents perceive Okezone as credible, although varying levels of skepticism remain regarding neutrality and framing, while

Previous studies have shown inconsistent findings regarding the relationships among Social Media Marketing, Content Quality,
eWOM, Brand Trust, and Repurchase Intention. Social Media Marketing has been found to have a positive and significant effect
on Brand Trust and Repurchase Intention through interactive and transparent communication ( Ali et al., 2025; Haudi et al., 2022;
Ibrahim et al., 2021). However, several studies report that this effect becomes insignificant when the quality of interaction is low
or not well managed (Fauzi & Evanita, 2024; Permataningrum & Vania, 2024; Tong & Subagio, 2020) . Similarly, Content Quality
plays an important role in building Brand Trust when content is relevant, informative, and consistent (Ningrum et al., 2021; Sultoni
et al., 2019; Tunsakul, 2024). However other studies show that its influence may not be significant when consumers rely more on
direct experience than online information (Abbas & Thahir, 2025; Nasta’in et al., 2023; Prista et al., 2025). In addition, eWOM has
been shown to positively influence Brand Trust and Repurchase Intention through recommendations and shared experiences
(Bhandari & Rodgers, 2018; Bulut & Karabulut, 2018; Matute et al., 2016) , although its effect may weaken when personal
experience dominates or when online information is perceived as less relevant (Fatimah & Widodo, 2023; Haeruddin, 2025;
Ismunandar & Ernawati, 2020).
Furthermore, Brand Trust is widely recognized as an important factor that encourages Repurchase Intention because it reduces
perceived risk and strengthens long -term relationships between consumers and brands (Anaya-Sánchez et al., 2020; Subawa et
al., 2020; Sutanto & Kussudyarsana, 2024) . However, several studies also report that its effect on Repurchase Intention is not
always significant or may vary depending on the context (Angelina & Supriyono, 2024; Aprilia & Andarini, 2023; Lestari &
Ratnawati, 2025) . These inconsistent findings indicate a research gap in understanding how Social Media Marketing, Content
Quality, and eWOM jointly influence Repurchase Intention through Brand Trust, particularly within the context of digital news
consumption on platforms such as Okezone.com.
Moreover, based on the findings from the preliminary study and previous literature, there is a clear inconsistency in researc h
results regarding the influence of Social Media Marketing, Content Quality, and eWOM on both Brand Trust and Repurchase
Intention. Some studies report positive and significant effects, while others find weak, insignificant, or even negative relationships
in certain contexts. In addition, the mediating role of Brand Trust also shows mixed results, indicating that there is still no
consistent conclusion regarding the mechanism among these variables. This research gap becomes more relevant when aligned
with the preliminary findings from Okezone.com, which show that positive perceptions of digital marketing activities and content
quality have not fully translated into strong audience preference. Therefore, further empirical investigation is needed to re -
examine these relationships in the context of Okezone.com in order to provide a more comprehensive understanding of the
factors influencing digital audience repurchase intention.

II. THEORETICAL AND LITERATURE REVIEW
A. Theoretical Review
Social Media Marketing
Social media marketing refers to marketing activities that utilize social media platforms to create content, build interactio ns, and
encourage consumer engagement in order to achieve organizational marketing objectives (Priansa, 2017). It emphasizes two-way
communication between brands and consumers through digital channels such as Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube, enabling
real-time feedback and the development of long -term relationships with audiences (Li et al., 2021) . Social media marketing also
plays an important role in expanding market reach and improving marketing effectiveness through creative and interactive
content strategies (Khraiwish & Alsharif, 2024).
Content Quality
Content quality refers to consumers’ perception of the quality of information provided by a brand on digital platforms, inclu ding
completeness, accuracy, relevance, and timeliness of information (Carlson et al., 2018) . It is not only determined by the
informational content itself but also by user engagement and audience responses within digital platforms (Agichtein et al., 2008).
In addition, attractive and emotionally engaging content has greater potential to capture consumer attention and increase
engagement with the brand (Berger & Milkman, 2012).
Electronic Word of Mouth (eWOM)
Electronic word of mouth (eWOM) is a form of consumer -to-consumer communication through digital media that includes
reviews, recommendations, and experiences regarding a product or brand (Kotler & Keller, 2016). eWOM has developed as part
of viral digital marketing, where information spreads rapidly through social media and online communities (Babić Rosario et al.,

Brand Trust
Brand trust is defined as consumers’ belief in a brand’s ability to fulfill expectations and provide consistent and reliable experiences
(Aaker, 1996). It is formed through consumers’ perceptions of a brand’s reliability, competence, and consistency in delivering
information and value (Chan-Olmsted & Kim, 2023). Brand trust reflects consumers’ expectations that a brand will deliver benefits
and value that meet or exceed their expectations (Nasib et al., 2024).
Repurchase Intention
Repurchase intention refers to consumers’ tendency to repurchase a product or service based on previous satisfactory
consumption experiences (Tjiptono, 2014). It reflects the level of satisfaction and confidence consumers have toward a brand that
has provided positive experiences (Ajzen, 1991). Repurchase intention is also influenced by functional value, emotional value, and
social experiences obtained during the consumption process (Kim et al., 2012).
B. Literature Review and Hypotheses
Brand trust is an important determinant of repurchase intention as it reduces perceived risk and strengthens long-term customer–
brand relationships (Anaya-Sánchez et al., 2020). Several studies show that higher brand trust increases consumer willingness to
repurchase due to perceived reliability and consistency of the brand (Subawa et al., 2020) . Trust also enhances emotional
attachment that supports repeat purchasing behavior (Sutanto & Kussudyarsana, 2024) . However, other findings indicate that
brand trust does not always significantly influence repurchase intention because consumer decisions may be driven by other
factors such as price or switching behavior (Lestari & Ratnawati, 2025) . In some cases, brand trust has been found to have no
direct effect on repurchase intention (Aprilia & Andarini, 2023) . Additionally, trust may not automatically translate into repeat
purchase behavior when alternative choices are more attractive (Angelina & Supriyono, 2024) . Therefore, brand trust plays a
crucial role in shaping consumers’ intention to repurchase a brand.
H1: Brand Trust has a significant effect on repurchase intention.
Social media marketing enhances consumer engagement and strengthens emotional connections with brands, which positively
influences repurchase intention (Cheung et al., 2021) . It also improves consumer awareness and interaction that encourages
repeat purchasing behavior (ElSayad & Md Saad, 2024) . Furthermore, effective social media marketing increases consumer
attachment and loyalty, leading to higher repurchase intention (Tarabieh et al., 2024) . However, some studies show that social
media marketing may not significantly affect repurchase intention due to low engagement quality or irrelevant content (Tong &
Subagio, 2020). In certain cases, its effect is even negative when consumers perceive excessive promotional content (Leksono &
Prasetyaningtyas, 2021). Other findings also show no significant relationship when consumer trust is not established (Lestari &
Ratnawati, 2025). Therefore, social media marketing is an important factor that can influence consumers’ repurchase intention.
H2: Social media marketing has a significant effect on repurchase intention.
High-quality content improves consumer perception and strengthens purchase intention through better information value (Wilson
et al., 2019) . It also enhances consumer satisfaction and encourages repeat usage or purchase behavior (Ibrahim et al., 2021) .
Engaging and relevant content further strengthens consumer decision -making (Dinar, 2025). However, some studies report that
content quality does not significantly influence repurchase intention due to stronger influence of external factors such as price or
experience (Prista et al., 2025). In other cases, content marketing fails to directly affect repeat purchase behavior (Nasta’in et al.,
2023). Additionally, content quality alone may not be sufficient to drive repurchase intention without trust or satisfaction
(Pangestika et al., 2022). Therefore, content quality may contribute to consumers’ intention to repurchase.
H3: Content quality has a significant effect on repurchase intention.
eWOM plays an important role in influencing consumer purchase decisions through peer -generated information (Matute et al.,
2016). Positive reviews and recommendations strengthen consumer confidence in repurchasing (Rachbini et al., 2021) . It also
reduces uncertainty in online purchasing decisions (Maduretno & Junaedi, 2021) . However, eWOM may not always significantly
affect repurchase intention due to differences in consumer preferences and reliance on personal experience (Haeruddin, 2025).
In some cases, eWOM has no significant effect despite positive reviews (Fitri & Nisa, 2022) . Furthermore, changing consumer
needs can weaken the influence of eWOM on repurchase decisions (Sya’roni & Fikriah, 2024). Therefore, electronic word of mouth
is an influential factor in shaping consumers’ repurchase intention.

Social media marketing increases brand trust through interactive communication and transparent engagement (Haudi et al.,

H5: Social media marketing has a significant effect on brand trust.
Content quality strengthens brand trust by providing accurate and reliable information (Tunsakul, 2024) . It also enhances
perceived credibility and brand reliability (Sultoni et al., 2019) . Informative content improves consumer confidence in brands
(Fauzi & Evanita, 2024) . However, some studies show that content quality alone does not always build trust without supporting
factors (Nugraha & Wilujeng, 2025) . In certain cases, content quality has no significant effect on brand trust (Abbas & Thahir,
2025). Additionally, promotional content without credibility fails to build trust effectively (Sanjani & Karmila, 2025) . Therefore,
content quality is an important determinant in developing consumer trust toward a brand.
H6: Content quality has a significant effect on brand trust.
eWOM enhances brand trust through credible peer reviews and shared experiences (Bhandari & Rodgers, 2018) . It strengthens
perceived reliability of brands in digital environments (Seifert & Kwon, 2019). Positive online discussions improve consumer trust
(Abd Elsalam & Ezzat, 2022) . However, eWOM may not always significantly influence trust due to skepticism toward online
information (Ismunandar & Ernawati, 2020) . In some cases, eWOM does not affect trust when interaction quality is low (Fadzila
et al., 2025) . It may also fail to build trust without proper brand response management (Fatimah & Widodo, 2023) . Therefore,
electronic word of mouth contributes significantly to the formation of brand trust.
H7: Electronic word of mouth has a significant effect on brand trust.
Social media marketing increases repurchase intention through brand trust by creating interactive and meaningful engagement
(Ibrahim et al., 2021). It strengthens trust which leads to repeat purchase behavior (Tarabieh et al., 2024). Community building in
social media also supports long -term loyalty (Salhab et al., 2023) . However, the effect becomes weak when brand trust is not
established (Menhard et al., 2025) . Low-quality interaction reduces the effectiveness of this relationship (Lestari & Ratnawati,
2025). Additionally, lack of engagement weakens indirect influence on repurchase intention (Sumira et al., 2024). Therefore, brand
trust acts as an important mechanism that strengthens the influence of social media marketing on repurchase intention.
H8: Social media marketing has a significant effect on repurchase intention through brand trust.
Content quality enhances repurchase intention through brand trust by improving credibility perception (Alfraihat et al., 2024). It
also strengthens long-term consumer relationships (Ningrum et al., 2021). Consistent content increases trust and loyalty (Naily et
al., 2025). However, the mediation effect is sometimes insignificant (Prista et al., 2025). Brand trust does not always translate into
repurchase intention (Sari & Darma, 2022). In some cases, content quality fails to influence purchase behavior indirectly (Amaris
& Ekawati, 2024) . Therefore, brand trust serves as a key mediating factor in the relationship between content quality and
repurchase intention.
H9: Content quality has a significant effect on repurchase intention through brand trust.
eWOM influences repurchase intention through brand trust by providing credible peer information (Bulut & Karabulut, 2018) .
Trust strengthens the impact of online reviews on purchase decisions (Firdaus & Firdaus, 2024) . It also enhances consumer
confidence in digital environments (Hasan & Elviana, 2023). However, trust does not always mediate this relationship effectively
(Raka & Abror, 2025) . In some contexts, eWOM has no indirect effect through trust (Kadang & Berlianto, 2022) . Consumer
evaluation of risk can weaken mediation effects (Bogdan et al., 2025). Therefore, brand trust plays a crucial mediating role in the
effect of electronic word of mouth on repurchase intention.

III. RESEARCH METHODS
This study employs a quantitative approach with a descriptive -explanatory design aimed at systematically examining and
explaining the relationships among variables based on numerical data. The research was conducted in December 2025 in the
Greater Jakarta area (Jabodetabek), consisting of Jakarta, Bogor, Depok, Tangerang, and Bekasi, which represents the largest
metropolitan region in Indonesia with high digital media consumption activity. The population of this study is active readers  of
Okezone.com in Indonesia, while the sample consists of respondents residing in Jabodetabek who are at least 18 years old, have
accessed Okezone.com within the last three months, and have interacted with its content on social media. The sampling technique
used is purposive s ampling, and the sample size is determined using SEM requirements by multiplying the number of indicators
by 10. This study includes 19 indicators across all constructs, resulting in a minimum sample size of 190 respondents based on the
10-times rule in SE M (J. F. Hair et al., 2021) . A total of 200 respondents were collected to improve data accuracy. Data were
collected using a structured online questionnaire distributed via Google Forms and measured using a 6 -point Likert scale to avoid
neutral responses and improve response consist ency (Chomeya, 2010) . The use of a six -point Likert scale is also considered
effective in eliminating midpoint bias and encouraging more decisive responses from respondents (Okunbanjo et al., 2024).
Data analysis in this study uses Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) with LISREL 8.80 to examine complex causal relationships
between latent variables, including social media marketing, content quality, electronic word of mouth (eWOM), brand trust, an d
repurchase intention. SEM is applied because it allows simultaneous testing of measurement and structural models with high
accuracy in evaluating theoretical constructs (Riadi, 2024). The analysis process includes measurement model testing (validity and
reliability), model fit evaluation using Goodness of Fit indices, and structural model testing to examine direct and indirect  effects
among variables. Convergent validity is assessed u sing standardized loading factor with a minimum value of 0.5 (J. Hair et al.,
2010). Construct reliability is considered adequate when the value is ≥ 0.70, while Average Variance Extracted must be > 0.50
(Qomusuddin & Romlah, 2023). Model fit is evaluated using several indices such as RMSEA, CFI, NFI, and others (Suharto & Ligery,
2018). Path analysis is used to test direct and mediated relationships among variables in the research model (Trianto, 2015).

The findings of this study demonstrate that all proposed hypotheses are supported. Brand Trust (BT), Social Media Marketing

SMM emerging as the strongest predictor of trust, suggesting that effective social media activities contribute substantially to
strengthening audience confidence in the news portal.

SMM, CQ, and eWOM and Repurchasing Intention. These findings indicate that digital marketing activities and content -related
strategies are more effective in increasing audiences' intention to revisit Okezone.com when they simultaneously strengthen trust
in the brand. In addition, the structural model demonstrates satisfactory explanatory power, indicating that the proposed variables
collectively explain a substantial proportion of the variance in both Brand Trust and Repurchasing Intention.

improving content quality, and encouraging positive electronic word of mouth to enhance audience engagement and revisit
