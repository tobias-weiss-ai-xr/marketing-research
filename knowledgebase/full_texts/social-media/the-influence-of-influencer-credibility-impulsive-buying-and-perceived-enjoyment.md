Abstract

The purpose of this study is to analyze the effect of Influencer Credibility, Impulsive Buying
and Perceived Enjoyment on Social Commerce Impulse Buying of food and beverage goods.
The study was conducted quantitatively through the survey of respondents who purchased
food and beverage goods through social commerce platforms. Data collection was
conducted using online questionnaires with the purposive sampling method. The data was
analyzed using multiple linear regression. The findings indicate that Influencer Credibility,
Impulsive Buying, and Perceived Enjoyment have a partial and simultaneous positive and
significant impact on Social Commerce Impulse Buying. The most important variable to affect
Social Commerce Impulse Buying is Impulsive Buying. The results of the study show that the
motives of impulse buying on social commerce platforms are affected by the credibility of
influencers, impulsive behavior tendencies and the joyful purchasing experience. This
research adds to the literature on digital marketing development, social commerce, and
consumer behavior. It also provides practical implications for food and beverage businesses
to develop better digital marketing strategies.

Social media has changed the way marketing is done, and consumers’ behavior,
including in the food and beverage industry. Social commerce is the buying and selling

(Fauzan et al., 2023) . It is a concept born out of the convergence of social media and e -
commerce activities. Unlike traditional e -commerce, social commerce enables customers to
view product information, interact with merchants, read customer reviews, and purchase
products on a single platform  (Sudirjo et al., 2023) . This can make the shopping experience
more exciting and may increase the chance of people making impulse buys. In this sense,
social commerce impulsive buying has become an important phenomenon because it is a
buying behavior that occurs quickly and without prior preparation as a result of triggers that
develop during social media interactions  (Lutfi et al., 2022) . This is particularly true of food
and beverages, which are fast -moving, often low -cost and easily marketed using attractive
visual content (Kartika et al., 2020).
One of the elements which influence impulsive purchase of food and beverage goods
in social commerce is suggested to be influencer credibility. The credibility of an influencer is
the extent to which an influencer is perceived to be trustworthy, knowledgeable and attractive
in presenting information about a product to his or her audience  (Solehati et al., 2022) .
Influencers are the opinion leaders of the social media age and they help shape consumers’
perceptions, attitudes and purchase choices. The findings also reveal that impulsive buying
is highly related to social commerce impulse buying for food and beverage products  (Fitri et
al., 2023; Sunarso & Mustafa, 2023 ). Impulsive buying is a characteristic of consumer
behavior, which refers to the spontaneous buying behavior of a person, and social commerce
impulse buying is a kind of impulsive buying that takes place in social commerce
environment (Mustafa et al., 2023; Anita et al., 2023 ). Highly impulsive consumers are more
susceptible to marketing cues (e.g., promotion, discount, influencer suggestion, attractive
product images)  (Dewianawati et al., 2023) . Perceived pleasure is another element
hypothesized to influence impulsive purchase of food and beverage goods in social
commerce (Sudirjo et al., 2024; Sriyanti et al., 2023) . Perceived enjoyment is the degree of
pleasure, comfort and pleasant experience perceived by customers while using social
commerce platforms. Shopping activities via social media are no longer just driven by
necessity, but have become a part of digital pleasure  (Payangan & Mustafa, 2023 ;
Parawansa et al., 2023 ). Short films, live streaming, gamification, interactive reviews and
creative material are some things that may make the buying experience fun for consumers.
The phenomenon of social commerce cannot be separated from the shift in social
media algorithms, which now heavily prioritize content personalization for their users.
Platforms like TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts utilize user behavior data to present
culinary content recommendations that are highly relevant in real-time. When mouth-watering
food content appears on a user's timeline at the perfect moment—such as during dinner time
or late -night relaxation —it creates a powerful psychological urge to make an immediate
transaction. Consequently, the integration of in -app purchase features bypasses the
consumer's rational deliberation process, ultimately triggering impulsive buying.
Product visualization plays a crucial role in the food and beverage industry within the
digital era. The presence of high -quality short videos using dramatic angles, crispy food

visual and auditory stimulus creates instant cravings that are difficult to resist. In the social

external trigger that radically transforms passive browsing into an active purchase decision

The credibility of influencers or culinary content creators (food vloggers) serves as an
anchor of trust amid the overwhelming abundance of product choices on social media. When
an influencer, known for their honesty and culinary expertise, gives a positive review, the
consumer's perceived risk drops drastically. Consumers tend to transfer the responsibility of
product evaluation to the digital authority figures they trust. As a result, recommendations
from these influencers do not merely serve as information sources, but rather as a form of
social validation that legitimizes the consumer's spontaneous purchase without hesitation.
On the other hand, the internal characteristics of consumers in the form of impulsive
buying traits act as a primary catalyst in this ecosystem. Every individual possesses a
different level of self -control when faced with marketing stimuli. Consumers with a high level
of trait impulsivity are generally more sensitive to limited -time offers, flash sales, or urgent
calls-to-action frequently voiced during live -streaming sessions. This inherent trait interacts
synergistically with the social commerce environmental stimuli, creating a psychological
vulnerability that accelerates unplanned buying decisions.
In addition to influencer credibility and consumer traits, the emotional dimension of
perceived enjoyment plays an equally significant role. The process of scrolling through
product catalogs, watching humorous interactions by merchants during live broadcasts, and
reading witty comments from fellow users creates a unique digital recreational experience.
When consumers feel entertained and happy, their cognitive capacity for analytical thinking
tends to decrease, replaced by the dominance of positive emotions. This feeling of pleasure
triggers the pleasure principle, where making a purchase is viewed as an instant gratification
to maintain that positive mood.
Although research on impulsive buying has been widely conducted within the context
of traditional e -commerce, the interaction dynamics within social commerce for food and
beverage products possess unique characteristics that have not been fully explored. In
conventional e -commerce, consumer motivation is largely utilitarian or based on specific
needs. Conversely, social commerce fuses complex hedonic and social motives. An
empirical gap remains regarding how the simultaneous interaction between an influencer's
personal credibility, the consumer's inherent trait vulnerability, and the platform's entertaining
atmosphere collectively drive purchase decisions for fast-moving, perishable food products.
The importance of this research is also driven by the rapid growth of micro, small, and
medium enterprises (MSMEs) in the food and beverage sector migrating to digital platforms.
Many business practitioners still struggle to formulate efficient promotional strategies due to
a limited understanding of digital consumer behavior. Without a comprehensive grasp of the
psychological and environmental factors influencing consumer shopping decisions,
marketing budgets allocated for influencer collaborations or creative content creation often
miss the mark and fail to generate optimal sales conversions.

Theoretically, this study attempts to integrate the Stimulus-Organism-Response (S-O-
R) framework to explain modern consumer behavior. Influencer credibility and the platform's
interactive features act as the Stimulus (S); the internal impulsive tendency and perceived
enjoyment serve as the emotional and cognitive states within the consumer, or the Organism
(O); while the act of social commerce impulse buying represents the Response (R). Through
this comprehensive framework, the research aims to provide a complete visualization of the
consumer's psychological journey from the moment of content exposure to hitting the
checkout button.
For marketing practitioners and food and beverage brand owners, the results of this
study are expected to serve as a strategic guideline for designing digital campaigns.
Businesses can identify which influencer credibility indicators are most sensitive to
consumers, whether it is their expertise or their visual attractiveness. Furthermore, corporate
management can design promotional interfaces and live -stream content that maximize the
element of perceived enjoyment, thereby creating a digital shopping environment that is not
just transactional, but also highly recreational.
Overall, a profound understanding of the interconnectedness among these variables
will contribute significantly to the development of marketing management studies, particularly
digital consumer behavior. Amid the intense competition in the culinary industry and the
dynamic shifts in social media trends, the ability to ethically and effectively predict and trigger
impulsive buying is the key to business sustainability. Based on these premises, this study is
conducted to empirically test the effects of influencer credibility, impulsive buying tendencies,
and perceived enjoyment on the impulse buying behavior of food and beverage goods within
the social commerce ecosystem.
This research aims to investigate the effect of Influencer Credibility, Impulsive Buying
and Perceived Enjoyment on Impulse Buying of food and beverage goods in Social
Commerce. The research is expected to contribute to the literature on social commerce,
digital marketing and consumer behavior. The research aims to understand in theory the
elements that encourage impulsive purchase through social media platforms. The research
also aims to broaden the understanding of the influence of the influencer credibility, impulsive
buying tendencies and perceived satisfaction on the influencing of customer purchase
behaviour in the social commerce environment. In practice, the results of this study are
expected to give inputs to food and beverage organizations in making more successful digital
marketing strategies.

METHOD
This research uses quantitative method with survey technique to study the effect of
Influencer Credibility, Impulsive Buying and Perceived Enjoyment on Social Commerce
Impulse Food and beverage customers purchase. The population of this research is the
customers who purchase food and beverage products by utilizing the social commerce
platform, such as social media that has integrated digital transaction capabilities. The
sampling technique used in this study was purposive sampling with the criteria of the
respondents being at least 18 years old, actively using social commerce platforms for at least

least twice in the last three months. Sample size was calculated as 200 responders. Data
collection used survey sent by google form. The Influencer Credibility variable was measured
by aspects of trustworthiness, competence, attractiveness and authenticity . Impulsive
Buying was measured by aspects of spontaneity, want to buy, lack of preparation and

entertainment value, pleasure and intrinsic motivation. Meanwhile, Social Commerce Impulse

was tested using t -test. Instrument reliability was tested by using Cronbach's Alpha with
minimum value of 0.70. The hypotheses were tested using multiple linear regression analysis
at 5% level of significance.

RESULT AND DISCUSSION
The results of the validity test show that all research indicators have a calculated t
value greater than the t table at a significance level of 5% so that all statement items are
declared legitimate. The reliability test results show a very high degree of internal
consistency. The classical assumption test results also show that the research model has
met all the requirements of regression analysis. The results of multiple linear regression
analysis show that Influencer Credibility, Impulsive Buying and Perceived Enjoyment
simultaneously have a positive and significant effect on Social Commerce Impulse Buying.
The results show the value of coefficient of determination is 0.573 which means 57.3% of the
variance of Social Commerce Impulse Buying can be explained by the three independent
variables in this study while the remaining 42.7% are influenced by other factors outside the
research model. Partial test results also indicate that all independent variables have a
positive and significant effect on Social Commerce Impulse Buying so that all research
hypotheses are accepted.
Based on the research results Influencer Credibility has a positive and significant effect
on Social Commerce Impulse Customer purchases of food and beverage products. The
result indicates that the higher the credibility of the influencer, the more likely customers are
to make impulse purchases through social commerce platforms. The credibility of the
influencer, based on honesty, knowledge, attractiveness, authenticity in the transmission of
information, can strengthen the confidence of the customer in the marketed product. When
customers see influencers as good sources of advice, the process of evaluating the product
is reduced, leading to impulsive choices of purchase. Also, interesting and real material can
create emotional intimacy between influencers and their followers, which can increase the
effectiveness of marketing communications. In the food and beverage product marketing,
attractive visual materials and convincing evaluations are often the key factors that lead to
impulse buying. Hence, the reputation of influencers is an important feature to enhance
impulsive purchasing in social commerce.
The research findings showed that impulsive purchasing has a favorable and
substantial effect on social commerce impulse buying among consumers of food and

The research results indicate that Perceived Enjoyment has a positive and significant

commerce platform, the more likely they will be to make an impulsive buy. A nice shopping
experience through a variety of interactive elements like short movies, live streaming,
gamification and creative material can create an ambiance that stimulates emotional
involvement. Consumers looking for food and beverage products are likely to spend more
time on the site, increasing the chances of being exposed to a variety of marketing stimuli.
This frame of mind is more open to spur -of-the-moment purchase decisions. Also, the fun
derived from interaction with the platform might reduce the logical assessment process, so
that the purchase choice is influenced more by the emotional considerations. So, perceived
pleasure is one of the main variables that drive the development in social commerce
impulsive purchasing for food and beverage goods.

CONCLUSION
Based on the results of the study that has been conducted, it can be concluded that
Influencer Credibility, Impulsive Buying and Perceived Enjoyment have positive and
significant effect on Social Commerce Impulse. Buy food and drink goods, part and at the
same time. At the same time, the three independent variables are capable of explaining most
of the variance of the Social Commerce Impulse purchasing. This indicates that impulsive
buying behavior in social commerce is driven by a combination of personal, social and digital
experience elements. However, there are some limitations to this research such as: cross -
sectional design, data based on the respondents' views and only food and beverage goods
were taken into consideration. Therefore, future research should be longitudinal in nature
and should examine mediators such as purchase intention or emotional connection and
moderators such as perceived risk or digital trust. Food and beverage businesses should
work with credible influencers, create more fun shopping experiences with interactive
features, and develop marketing strategies that can generate positive emotional engagement
with consumers in order to increase sales via social commerce platforms.
