Abstract. This quantitative research aims to empirically analyze the influence of Digital public
relations activities focusing on the Instagram account @jajananhafiz on the level of Brand
Awareness of the Jajanan Hafiz MSME products in Bengkulu City. The research approach is based
on the framework of Social Media Marketing Theory to evaluate how digital communication
dimensions affect consumer cognition. The data collection method was conducted through the
distribution of structured questionnaires to a sample of 92 respondents drawn from a total
population of 1,074 followers using probability sampling with a simple random sampling technique
based on the Slovin Formula. Statistical data analysis in this study uses the Partial Least Squares
Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) method with the assistance of SmartPLS 4 software. The
structural model evaluation (inner model) shows a coefficient of determination (R -Square) value of
0.714, meaning that the variability of Brand Awareness can be explained by Digital public relations
activities by 71.4% (strong influence category). The hypothesis testing results show a path
coefficient (beta) value of 0.845 with a TStatistic achievement of 14.235 (> 1.96) and an empirical
significance P-value of 0.000 (< 0.05). These results absolutely prove that Digital public relations
has a positive and significant effect on the Brand Awareness of Jajanan Hafiz MSME products. The
implications of this research emphasize the importance for MSME actors to optimize interactivity
and digital content quality to solidify brand position in the local culinary market.

Keywords: Digital Public Relations, Brand Awareness, Social Media Marketing, Smartpls 4,
MSMEs.

INTRODUCTION
The development of information and communication technology in the contemporary era
has disrupted the global business landscape, p articularly within the Micro, Small, and Medium
Enterprises (MSME) sector. Indonesia’s culinary industry is currently required to adopt digital
platforms in order to maintain market relevance and respond to increasingly intense competition
(Kotler, Kartajaya, & Setiawan, 2021). This transformation compels business actors to focus not
only on the quality of physical products, but also on the management of online brand
communication. Interactive communication strategies have become a vital instrument for
MSMEs to reach dynamic, digitally connected urban market segments (Sari & Wijaya, 2022).
Amid this shift in marketing communication paradigms, conventional public relations
functions have evolved into digital public relations. This digital approach enables organizations
to build strategic, two-way relationships with audiences through the optimization of social media
(Hapsari & Pratama, 2023). For MSME actors, digital public relations offers an efficient
marketing solution with broad reach without requiring large -scale corporate budget allocations.
Structured communication via social media has proven effective in reducing geographic and
psychological barriers between local culinary producers and their potential target consumers
(Wardani, 2024).

Jajanan Hafiz MSME is one of the culinary business entities in Bengkulu City that actively
utilizes Instagram as its primary public communication channel under the official account name
@jajananhafiz. Based on preliminary observations, this account has accumulated a significant
follower base, reaching more than 1,074 followers. Nevertheless, a high number of followers
does not theoretically guarantee a linear increase in the depth of brand recognition if it is not
supported by credible and consistent digital public relations communication management on the
Instagram account (Gunawan & Lestari, 2025). Therefore, empirical testing is required to
measure the extent to which the operationalization of social media contributes to audience
cognition.One of the fundamental outcomes of a successful digital public relations strategy is
the formation of brand awareness in the minds of audiences. Brand awareness plays a crucial
role as the initial stage in the consumer adoption hierarchy before purchase decisions are made
(Keller & Swaminathan, 2021). Many local MSMEs experience stagnation due to their inability to
build strong brand recognition in the digital sphere, causing their visual identities and product
messages to be submerged amid the dense flow of online information.

LITERATURE REVIEW
The concept of digital public relations represents an extension of traditional public
relations functions by leveraging internet -based media and digital applications to manage
reputation and maintain positive relationships between organizations and their publics (Hapsari
& Pratama, 2023). These activities no longer rely on conventional mass media, but instead shift
toward owned media channels such as websites and official corporate social media accounts.
Within the context of modern public relations, the success of digital public relations is
determined by practitioners’ ability to produce communicative messages that are interactive,
informative, and responsive (Sari & Wijaya, 2022).
Brand awareness reflects the ability of prospective buyers or consumers to recognize a
brand (brand recognition) or to recall it (brand recall) as part of a specific product category
(Keller & Swaminathan, 2021). This level of brand awareness serves as a fundamental
foundation in the formation of brand equity, as consumers tend to choose products whose visual
identity or brand name is already familiar to them. In a highly competitive digital environment,
achieving the highest level of brand awareness —namely, top of mind —requires consistent and
sustained exposure of brand information messages to audiences (Wardani, 2024).
Instagram content is defined as visual and textual communication materials —including
photos, short videos (reels), and stories —produced and uploaded by account owners to convey
specific messages to their followers (Lamberton & Stephen, 2022). Within the digital marketing
landscape, the characteristics of uploaded content must embody aesthetic value, informational
relevance, and uniqueness in order to capture audience attention amid the abundance of digital
information flows. The strategic management of graphic elements and narratives on an
Instagram feed serves as an essential external stimulus in professionally representing a
product’s visual image (Evans, Phua, Lim, & Jun, 2023).
This quantitative study fully adopts Social Media Marketing Theory as its primary
conceptual framework for explaining the causal relationships among variables.  Social Media
Marketing Theory posits that the use of social networking media by business entities is centered
on the creation of collective value through the dimensions of interactivity, collaboration,
community, and intensive two -way communication between organizations and consumers
(Tuten & Solomon, 2021). Through the lens of this theory, the digital public relations activities
carried out on the @jajananhafiz account are analyzed as a practical application of the digital
communication mix aimed at converting audience engagement into measurable brand
awareness.

METHODS
This study employs a quantitative research design using a causal associative method to
examine the cause -and-effect relationship between the independent variable (digital public
relations) and the dependent variable (brand awareness). The population of this study
comprises all active followers of the Instagram account @jajananhafiz, totaling 1,074 followers.
The determination of the sample size was conducted rigorously by applying probability sampling
through a simple random sampling technique to ensure that each member of the population had
an equal and fair chance of being selected (Sugiyono, 2022).
To calculate an accurate and representative sample size from the total population of 1,074
followers, the researchers applied the Slovin formula with a margin of error of 5% (error = 0.05).
The mathematical formulation of the Slovin formula is as follows: n = N / (1 + N(error)²), where N
represents the population size and n denotes the minimum sample size. Based on this
calculation, the actual computation yielded: n = 1074 / (1 + 1074 (0.05)²) = 1074 / (1 + 1074 ×
0.0025) = 1074 / (1 + 2.685) = 1074 / 3.685 = 291.45. Accordingly, the sample size determined
and utilized in this quantitative study consisted of 92 respondents (Gunawan & Lestari, 2025).
The statistical data analysis process was carried out using variance -based Structural
Equation Modeling (SEM) with the SmartPLS 4 software. The stages of data analysis
encompassed two main tests, namely the evaluation of the measurement model (outer model)
and the evaluation of the structural model (inner model). The outer model evaluation was
conducted to assess the validity and reliability of the research instruments, sequentially
examining outer loadings, Average Variance Extracted (AVE), and the Heterotrait –Monotrait
Ratio (HTMT) for validity, followed by tests of Cronbach’s alpha and Composite Reliability
(rho_c) for reliability (Hair, Hult, Ringle, & Sarstedt, 2022). Subsequently, the structural model
(inner model) assessment was applied to evaluate the coefficient of determination (R -square)
and to test the hypotheses through the bootstrapping procedure.

DISCUSSION
The results of the data analysis presented in Table 1 indicate that the outer loading values
for the Digital Public Relations (X) variable are as follows: X.1 = 0.794, X.2 = 0.812, X.3 = 0.841,
X.4 = 0.827, and X.5 = 0.863. Meanwhile, the outer loading values for the Brand Awareness (Y)
variable are Y.1 = 0.789, Y.2 = 0.845, Y.3 = 0.852, and Y.4 = 0.836. All of these indicators
exceed the minimum critical threshold of 0.70 (Hair et al., 2022).  The validity testing stage was
subsequently continued by examining the Average Variance Extracted (AVE) values to ensure
the convergent validity of the latent constructs. The actual calculated AVE values show figures
of 0.685 for the Digital Public Relations (X) variable and 0.690 for the Brand Awareness (Y)
variable. As both AVE values clearly surpass the minimum criterion standard of 0.50, all
constructs are declared to possess strong and acceptable convergent validity (Hair et al., 2022).
Finally, discriminant validity was evaluated using the Heterotrait –Monotrait Ratio (HTMT)
parameter, which produced an inter -construct correlation value of 0.845. An HTMT index below
the maximum threshold of 0.90 confirms that the two latent variables are empirically distinct and
demonstrate adequate discriminant validity.
Based on the statistical calculations shown in Table 2, the observed reliability indicators
include Cronbach’s alpha and Composite Reliability (rho_c). The statistical results indicate that
Cronbach’s alpha for the Digital Public Relations (X) variable is 0.885, while for the Brand
Awareness (Y) variable it is 0.854, with both values meeting the ideal criterion above 0.60
(Sugiyono, 2022). In addition, the Composite Reliability (rho_c) values are recorded at 0.915 for
the Digital Public Relations (X) variable and 0.898 for the Brand Awareness (Y) variable. As
both rho_c values are well above the threshold of 0.70, the research instruments are proven to
exhibit a very high and consistent level of reliability.
Referring to the statistical data in Table 3, the testing of the simple linear regression model
begins with an evaluation of the coefficient of determination (R -square or R²) to assess the
predictive capacity of the model. The R² value obtained for the Brand Awareness (Y) latent
construct is 0.714. This empirical finding indicates that 71.4% of the variability or fluctuation in
brand awareness levels for UMKM Jajanan Hafiz products can be explained by Digital Public
Relations (X) activities, which is methodologically classified as a strong model. Meanwhile, the
remaining 28.6% of variance is explained by other exogenous variables outside the linear model
tested in this study, such as price perception, product quality, or other elements of the marketing
mix.Furthermore, hypothesis testing was conducted using the bootstrapping algorithm to assess
the significance of the path coefficient. The analysis results show a path coefficient (β) value of
0.845 with a T -statistic of 14.235. This value significantly exceeds the critical T -table threshold
of 1.96 for a two-tailed test at a 5% significance level. In addition, the empirical probability value
(p-value) obtained is 0.000, which is far below the significance level of α = 0.05. Based on these
statistical parameters, the null hypothesis (H ₀) is decisively rejected, and the alternative
hypothesis (Hₐ) is accepted. Thus, this study empirically proves that digital public relations has a
positive and significant effect on the brand awareness of consumers or followers of the
Instagram account @jajananhafiz.  Mathematically, the direction and functional contribution
between the variables can be converted into a simple linear regression equation, namely: Y =

CONCLUSION
Based on the entire sequence of statistical data analysis and theoretical discussion
referring to variance -based Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) assisted by the SmartPLS 4
software, this quantitative study concludes that Digital Public Relations activities focused on the
Instagram account @jajananhafiz have a positive and statistically significant empirical effect on
the Brand Awareness of UMKM Jajanan Hafiz products in Bengkulu City. This conclusion is
grounded in the obtained path coefficient (beta) value of 0.845, a T -statistic of 14.235 that
exceeds the critical threshold of 1.96, and a significance value of P = 0.000, which is far below
the alpha threshold of 0.05. The coefficient of determination test further demonstrates that the
contribution capacity of Digital Public Relations in explaining variance in brand awareness
reaches 71.4%, positioning it as a highly powerful predictive marketing instrument (Sari &
Wijaya, 2022).
Viewed from the perspective of the Social Media Marketing Theory applied in this study,
these findings conceptually confirm that the utilization of interactivity, community, and intensive
two-way communication dimensions on social media is optimally converted into tangible brand
awareness enhancement among local culinary consumers. Adaptive digital public relations
activities function as the primary cognitive stimulus channel that transforms digital audience
engagement into solid brand recognition in the marketplace (Tuten & Solomon, 2021)..

LIMITATION
As a practical recommendation for the management of UMKM Jajanan Hafiz, it is advised
to consistently enhance two -way communication services, maintain administrative
responsiveness, and design diversified educational and interactive content (such as Instagram
Reels or question -and-answer sessions). These tactical steps are crucial for sustaining
consumers’ top -of-mind positioning and stimulating interest among potential new customers
amid the intense competition of the online culinary industry in Bengkulu City (Hapsari &
Pratama, 2023). For academics and future researchers, it is recommended to expand the scope
of the structural model by exploring other exogenous variables, such as influencer marketing or
service quality, to account for the remaining 28.6% of unexplained model variance.
