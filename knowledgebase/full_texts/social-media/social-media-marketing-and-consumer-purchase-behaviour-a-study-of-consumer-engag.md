ABSTRACT
Social media has become an influential platform for product promotion, information
sharing, and c ustomer decision-making. This study examines the role of social media
marketing in shaping consum er buying behaviour in Tirupattur District. Th is study covers
consumers' demographic profiles, social media usage and engagement, preferences
between social media and traditional shopping,  and consumer trust and satisfaction. The
study collected primary data  from 120 respondents through a structured Google Form
questionnaire using convenience sampling.  The collected data were analysed using
simple percentage analysis. The results indicate that Instagram is the leading platform,
used by 66.66 percent of respondents, while 82.50 percent access social media daily.
Smartphones are the primary access device  for all respondents. Social media
advertisements, influencer content, and product reviews contribute significantly to product
awareness and evaluation. A majority (83.33 percent)  of respondents believe social
media offers better deal s than traditional s hopping. However, concerns about payment
security, counterfeit products,  and advertisement authenticity remain. Despite these
issues, 88.33 percent are willing to continue purchasing products promoted through social
media. The study recommends greater transparency, secure payment systems, authentic
promotional content, and effective after-sales support to improve consumer confidence.
Keywords: Social Media Marketing, Buying Behaviour, Consumer Engagement,
Social Media Shopping, Consumer Trust, and Customer Satisfaction.

Introduction
The rapid development of digital
technology has significantly transformed
how businesses communicate and build
relationships with  customers. Social
media platforms such as Instagram,
Facebook, and YouTube have created
convenient channels for businesses to
showcase products, communicate with
customers, share product  information,
and respond to customer s’ needs in real
time. At the same time, consumers have
gained greater access to information,
allowing them to explore pr oducts,
compare prices, read customer reviews,
and consider alternatives before making
a purchase.
As a result, social media has
become an integral par t of contemporary
marketing practic es. Different forms of
online content, including promotional
advertisements, influencer
endorsements, customer reviews,
special offers  and discounts , and
interactive posts, may shape consumers’
perceptions and influence their
purchasing behaviour.  However,
consumers may respond differently to
various forms of social media marketing ,
and the extent to which these online
marketing activities influence customers'
attitudes and purchase decisions
requires further investigation. Therefore,
this research study s eeks to provide
empirical evidence on how social media
marketing influences consumer attitudes
and purchase decisions, thereby
contributing to a clearer understanding of
consumer responses in the digital
marketplace. In this context, the present
study examines how social media
marketing influences consumer attitudes
and purchase decisions.

Research Methodology
This research study uses both
primary data and secondary data.
Primary data was collected through a
well-structured questionnair e using
Google Form s. Secondary data was
collected from e-articles, research
journals, and online sources.  A sample
of 120 respondents was chosen using
the convenience sampling  method.
Simple Percentage Analysis was used to
analyze and interpret  the collected data.
Because convenience sampling relies on
the accessibility and availabilit y of
respondents, the selected sample may
not adequately reflect the characteristics
of the overall consumer population.
Consequently, the findings should be
interpreted within the scope of the
selected respondents and may not be
broadly applicable to all consumers.

Conclusion
This research study examined how
social media marketing meaningfully
influences consumer buying behav iour in
Tirupattur District. It has developed beyond
its traditional role as an easy
communication and entertainment medium
and now supports product discovery,
evaluation, and purchasing  behaviour of
consumers. Instagram, influencer
recommendations, advertisements,
customer reviews, and promotional offers
play a vital role in attracting most
consumers to purchase products via social
media platforms . Most respondents also
view social media as a convenient
shopping channel that offers competitive
deals on their products. At the same time,
concerns about misleading
advertisements, counterfeit products,
payment security, and product satisfaction
indicate that consumer trust cannot be
taken for granted. Therefore, businesses
must combine effective promotion with high
product quality, transparency, responsive
customer service, and secure transactions.
