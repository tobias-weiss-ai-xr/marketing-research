Abstract
This research aims to identify the impact of social media marketing strategies on achieving
institutional excellence at Ashur Hotel Company in Iraq. The researcher reviewed prior studies
on social media marketing strategies — particularly precise target -audience identification,
influencer marketing, and user marketing — as well as prior studies on institutional
excellence. A questionnaire was designed to test the study hypothesis and distributed to 51
employees at Ashur Hotel in Iraq across three categories: management staff, front-line staff,
and back-line staff, yielding 47 valid responses for analysis. The hypothesis was tested using
SPSS, applying descriptive statistics, the Kruskal –Wallis test, correlation analysis, and simple
regression. Results showed  a strong, positive, statistically significant correlation (R = 0.739)
between social media marketing strategies and institutional excellence, with the independent
variable explaining 54.6% of the variance in institutional excellence (R² = 0.546). The research
concluded that a positive, direct relationship exists between social media marketing
strategies and institutional excellence at Ashur Hotel Company in Iraq, and recommended
integrating social media management into a dedicated organizational unit suppo rting
institutional excellence.

Introduction
The importance of social media marketing strategies lies in their necessity for building brand
awareness, increasing direct engagement with the audience, and achieving higher sales at a
lower cost compared to traditional marketing. Social media marketing s trategies enable
precise targeting of potential guests, analysis of their behavior, and the building of long-term
relationships that enhance guest loyalty.

Despite the importance of social media marketing strategies, they face numerous challenges
that hinder the achievement of institutional excellence.

Institutional excellence refers to a comprehensive management approach that seeks to
achieve superior and sustainable performance exceeding the expectations of guests and
stakeholders, through the application of best practices, innovation, and continuous p rocess
improvement, as well as the creation of a unique competitive advantage and the achievement
of distinguished results by adopting an organizational culture that encourages effective
leadership and teamwork. This research focuses on analyzing the relationship between social
media marketing strategies and the achievement of institutional excellence at Ashur Hotel
Company in Iraq.

Research Problem
Despite the importance and spread of marketing operations via social media at the level of
organizations and guests, there remain numerous problems that need solutions before
institutional excellence can be achieved. These problems include: declining organ ic reach —
meaning it is difficult for posts to reach followers without paid promotion, which reduces free
engagement; the constant change of algorithms, as platforms such as Facebook and TikTok
regularly update their algorithms, meaning that today’s succe ssful strategies may not work

linking direct engagement (such as likes) to actual sales or precisely achieving business
objectives; the need for continuous, attractive content, meaning the constant need to create
creative content suited to different platforms amid intense digital clutter; and intense
competition and rising advertising costs, as increased competition for user attention has
driven up the cost of clicks and conversion campaigns. Achieving institutional excellence
requires the organization to find solutions and mechanisms for these problems so that social
media marketing can help the hotel organization achieve its institutional -excellence goals.
The main research problem can be formulated in the following question: Does eliminating the
problems of social media marketing help achieve instit utional excellence at Ashur Hotel
Company in Iraq?

Research Objective
The primary objective of the research is to determine the relationship between social media
marketing strategies and the achievement of institutional excellence, which requires the
scientific grounding of the conceptual framework for social media marketing  strategies and
the concepts of institutional excellence. The research also aims to analyze and evaluate the
extent to which social media marketing strategies, in their various dimensions, affect the
achievement of institutional excellence at Ashur Hotel C ompany in Iraq; to present some
social media marketing strategies and their effect on achieving institutional excellence — the
precise target -audience identification strategy, with a focus on the influencer marketing
strategy and the user marketing strateg y — and the theoretical framework of institutional
excellence; and to present the relationship between social media marketing strategies and
the achievement of institutional excellence, along with conducting field research based on a
questionnaire covering the research variables, and statistically analyzing the results using the
SPSS statistical program to verify the validity or invalidity of the research hypothesis.

Research Importance
The research derives its scientific importance from studying and analyzing social media
marketing strategies and their impact on achieving institutional excellence, and from finding
solutions to the challenges they face. The research also derives its appli ed importance from
its application at Ashur Hotel Company in Iraq. The scientific importance appears through
enriching administrative literature, supporting Arabic academic literature with a modern
theoretical framework linking digital marketing thought wi th administrative excellence
thought, and contributing to bridging the research gap regarding the scarcity of Arabic studies
that integrate social media as an enabling factor for institutional excellence — along with
grounding the study’s dimensions by dev eloping a theoretical model linking the dimensions
of digital marketing with the axes of institutional excellence (such as leadership and
operational excellence), and providing a measurement tool (questionnaire) and scientific
indicators that researchers and students may rely on in the future. The practical importance
appears through providing the administrative leadership of Ashur Hotel Company in Iraq with
clear mechanisms for allocating marketing resources toward the most influential digital
platforms, thereby raising spending efficiency, reducing traditional promotion costs,
achieving the maximum return on digital investment, and improving service quality by
enabling employees at Ashur Hotel Company in Iraq to use communication channels as a tool
for monitoring guest complaints and immediately developing operational performance.

The Concept of Strategy and Marketing Strategies
Sir Lawrence Freedman broadly defined strategy as the art of creating power; nevertheless,
he emphasizes that strategy is far more than a mere plan, affirming that a good strategy takes
into account the potential effects of unforeseen factors and the role of others who may
disrupt the strategy due to conflicting interests (Al -Qulaiby, 2019, p. 7). The researcher
believes that strategy represents a comprehensive, future-oriented plan designed to achieve
specific, long -term objectives. It is akin to a “road map” that determines the desired
destination and directs available resources and capabilities with high efficiency to overcome
challenges and competitors.

The concept of strategy varies according to the type and field of the plan; marketing strategy
is linked to marketing plans, while political marketing strategy is linked to political -plan
marketing (Al-Nadawi, 2017, p. 3).

Marketing strategy is also linked to the field of marketing, so social media marketing strategy
is linked to a comprehensive plan that defines social media marketing objectives, the target
audience, the type of content offered, and the digital platforms used to reach customers.
The theoretical grounding of the components that form the process of developing social
media marketing strategies can be established within a framework consisting of a set of
processes, components, concepts, and applied theories representing the driving factors. The
components include: company objectives in social media marketing — guests’ motives for
using social media — company engagement initiatives — guest behaviors on social media —
social connectedness — social interaction — customer participation. This is examined in light
of several theories including: resource dependence theory — uses and gratifications theory
— resource dependence theory — uses and gratifications theory — social exchange theory —
social exchange theory — dominant service logic, as illustrated in Figure 1 (theoretical
foundation table).

The researcher believes that this framework illustrates multiple dimensions of social media
marketing, helping organizations develop the marketing strategies most suited to their

Precise Target-Audience Identification Strategy
In today’s fast-changing business environment, attracting and retaining the target audience
has become an essential element for success. Traditional marketing strategies that rely on
mass marketing are losing their effectiveness in today’s market, as consu mers enjoy wider
choices and greater decision-making power (Chen, 2023, p. 3). A precise understanding of the
target audience and its segmentation has also become an essential element in marketing
strategies, as identifying and segmenting the target audien ce enhances the accuracy of
marketing campaigns and enables marketers to gain a deeper understanding of consumer
behavior and needs (Bogacki et al., 2024, p. 2). Analysis of the key strategies used to attract
target guests includes: mechanisms for understanding the target audience — mechanisms for
selecting appropriate channels — mechanisms for delivering value (Chen, 2023, p. 3). Some
mechanisms for applying the precise target-audience identification strategy can be identified
based on customer information , including: name and contact information — transaction
history (what products they buy, in what quantity, and how often) — communication and
response history — demographic information (age, location, education, etc.) —
psychographic information (consumer interests, hobbies, and concerns regarding health,
food safety, and the environment) — spending habits (deliberate shoppers, impulsive
shoppers, and price-comparing shoppers) (Curtis & Allen, 2018, p. 2).

Based on the foregoing, the researcher believes that the precise target -audience
identification strategy forms the basis of any successful marketing campaign; without it,
advertising spend turns into random waste. However, excessive narrowing of targeting and
confining the audience to an overly narrow segment carries a double-edged risk. The precise-
targeting strategy is characterized by raising the efficiency of advertising spend by directing
every dinar of the budget toward the people most inclined toward and in need of the product,
which minimizes financial waste, increases conversion rates, and tailors the advertising
message and content to address the specific pain points of this segment.

The researcher also believes there are numerous challenges to the target -audience
identification strategy, including: the challenge of privacy laws and restricted data tracking;
the challenge of continuous privacy updates, which requires building private g uest lists by
collecting emails and phone numbers through the website and uploading them to advertising
platforms to create “custom audiences”; the challenge of rapid change in consumer behavior
and interests; and the challenge of artificial intelligence and broad targeting algorithms.

The influence of social media influencers is considered one of the decisive factors driving

Online influencer marketing strategy is defined as a strategy in which companies select online

the ultimate goal of improving the company’s performance (Leung et al., 2022, p. 7).
Online influencer marketing is distinguished from other marketing strategies in that (Leung

• Companies select online influencers and motivate them to help market their products.
• Online marketing strategy seeks to urge online influencers to engage with their
followers with the aim of promoting the company’s products and influencing their
attitudes and behaviors.
• Companies benefit from influencers’ unique resources, including their follower
networks, personal standing, communication content, and followers’ trust.

Influencers can be classified into two types: situational influencers, referring to the influence

influencers, referring to the extension of influence across the en tire community. Marketing
through social media influencers relies on four elements (Al-Zayat, 2025, p. 22):
• Identifying and cultivating influencers on social media platforms.
• Building and managing a strong relationship with social media influencers.
• Monitoring the influencer marketing campaign on social media platforms.
• Measuring the influencer marketing campaign on social media platforms.

The researcher believes that influencer marketing strategy has become one of the most
decisive and impactful tools today. However, this strategy carries operational complexities
and high managerial risks. Influencer marketing is characterized by its abilit y to build instant
trust by transferring the influencer’s credibility with their followers directly to the product.
The disadvantages of influencer marketing include high cost, in addition to the difficulty of
predicting and replicating results, with no gu arantee that the strategy will achieve steady
sales. The success of the influencer marketing strategy requires overcoming current
challenges, including: the challenge of fake accounts and fake followers, the challenge of
measuring genuine return on investment, and the challenge of risking brand reputation.

The Concept of Institutional Excellence
The concept of institutional excellence emerged to express the need for a comprehensive
approach that combines the elements and foundations of building organizations on superior
bases that give them competitive capabilities in facing external changes and c onditions
surrounding them (Ahmed, 2022, p. 11). Institutional excellence is also defined as all the
procedures and activities undertaken by employees in organizations that develop and
improve work in a way that enables them to compete with other organizat ions in delivering
services and achieving the best tangible results and meeting the desires and expectations of
beneficiaries in the future (Abu Bakr & Awad, 2023, p. 8). The American Society for Quality

and processes aimed at engaging and motivating employees to deliver products and services
that meet guest requirements. Furthermore, according to the European Foun dation for

outstanding performance levels or exceed the expectations of stakeholders (Hijazi, 2024, p.

by anticipating and meeting their ne eds, with the organization exerting every effort to
maintain the positive competitive performance that achieves institutional excellence (Al -
Baladi et al., 2026, p. 10).

The Importance of Institutional Excellence
The importance of institutional excellence is evident in providing the organization’s
management with the means and methods to identify obstacles as they arise, serving as a
means of gathering information for making important decisions regarding employee
management — such as who should be promoted and who demonstrates qualities such as
altruism, initiative, and performance excellence — and providing the organization with
opportunities for continuous development of its managers and employees, enabling the
organization to stand out as one of the best -performing organizations in service delivery,
while supporting the skills needed by decision -makers, whether individual or collective, and
encouraging reflection on the sensitivity and importance of their role in achieving creativity
and institutional excellence ( Al-Shayeb et al., 2025, p. 14). The importance of institutional
excellence also lies in enabling individuals to be creative so they can adapt to circumstances
and changes, which in turn requires the organization to possess a high degree of efficiency
and effectiveness — attainable only through ethical, creative efforts led by a distinguished,
innovative leader. Institutional excellence also poses challenges for organizations to seek the
best means of building and continuously developing their strategy towar d maximizing the
value of the organization’s work (Al-Baladi et al., 2026, p. 11).

Foundations and Objectives of Institutional Excellence
Among the most important foundations of institutional excellence is continuous
development, as it allows the organization to remain always in a better competitive position
in terms of excellence, and to be ahead in developing services and performance systems in a
way that ensures faster access to beneficiaries and provides them with benefits and
advantages that competitors cannot match. It also establishes the building and development
of relationships with the various parties and stakeholders connected to t he organization.
Among the hallmarks of excellence is the ability to invest in and leverage these relationships
to maximize the organization’s opportunities to reach its goals and objectives (Al -Shayeb et
al., 2025, p. 14).

Characteristics and Models of Institutional Excellence
A number of characteristics distinguish excellent organizations from other organizations,

Study Limitations
These findings should be interpreted in light of several limitations. First, the study relies on a
cross-sectional questionnaire design administered at a single point in time, which establishes
association rather than causal impact between social media mar keting strategies and
institutional excellence; the statistical results (R = 0.739, R² = 0.546) should therefore be read
as evidence of a strong, statistically significant relationship rather than proof that social media
marketing strategies cause institut ional excellence. Second, the sample consists of only 47
valid respondents drawn from a single hotel company in Iraq, which limits the statistical
power of the analysis and the extent to which the findings can be generalized to other hotel
organizations or  sectors. Future research employing larger samples across multiple
organizations, and longitudinal or experimental designs capable of supporting causal
inference, would help address these limitations.
