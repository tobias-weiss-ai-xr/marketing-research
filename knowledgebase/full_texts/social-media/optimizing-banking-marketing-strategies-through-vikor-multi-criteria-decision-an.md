ABSTRACT
This study uses the VIKOR decision-making framework to better integrate institutional data bases in the bank,
while at the same time analyzing marketing strategies in detail. The study addresses the critical issue of
determining effective marketing strategies in the rapidly growing competitive and digital finance sector, where
organizations must simultaneously juggle multiple competing forces. Seven distinct marketing channels were
examined: Creatingvaluablecontent,buildingasocialmediapresence,growingemailsubscribers,sendingpush
notifications, cultivating online communities, leveraging partner networks, and utilizing traditional advertising
outlets. These options were evaluated against four essential performance metrics: market penetration, financial
performance, user interaction quality, and regulatory compliance. The VIKOR approach was used to establish
balanced solutions that maximize stakeholder benefits while minimizing deviations from optimal outcomes.
The research results indicated that social media marketing produced the most effective strategy, achieving a
VIKOR compromise value (Qj) of 0, which reflects the best fit across all measurement parameters. Email
marketing came in second, confirming its continued relevance in banking communications. Meanwhile, SMS
& app notifications, while showing strength in financial performance, recorded a weak comprehensive design
(Qj = 1), highlighting the framework’s ability to recognize preferences that are not entirely adequate. The
results suggest that banking institutions should focus on integrated social media tactics while maintaining
effective email marketing systems.

2. MATERIALS AND METHODS
Alternative
• Content Marketing:Content marketing involves strategically creating and distributing valuable, relevant material
like blogs, videos, and podcasts to attract and engage a defined target audience. The goal is to encourage profitable
behaviors from consumers, thereby establishing trust, strengthening connections, and supporting prospects as they
progress through the buying process. This is accomplished by providing valuable insights and problem-solving
resources, which ultimately builds customer loyalty and increases revenue.
• Social Media Marketing:Social media marketing (SMM) involves using social networking sites to improve
brand visibility, establish audience connections, generate website traffic, and increase revenue through strategic
contentdevelopment,socialinteraction,andadvertisingcampaigns. Essentialelementsincludecreatingcompelling,
viral-worthy content, monitoring and responding to community members, evaluating metrics and analytics, and
executing focused advertising campaigns on platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn.
• Email Marketing:Email marketing is a digital advertising technique that uses electronic messaging to deliver
business communications to targeted groups of recipients, with the aim of promoting offers, improving brand
recognition, cultivating prospects, and generating revenue. This approach serves as an efficient and budget-friendly
method for maintaining direct contact with existing and prospective customers and building lasting connections
through personalized and relevant messaging that supports customers at various stages of their buying experience.
• SMS & App Notifications:SMS alerts are simple text communications sent over cellular networks that provide
users with important updates, marketing offers, or urgent notifications, serving as a reliable and widely accessible

Vikor Method:The VIKOR methodology emerged as a multi-criteria decision-making framework designed to address
complex decision scenarios involving incompatible and conflicting objectives (Opricovic and Tzeng, 2004). This technique
specializes in ranking and selecting from multiple alternatives while identifying compromise solutions for problems
with competing criteria, ultimately supporting decision-makers in reaching conclusive determinations. A compromise
solution represents a viable option that approximates the optimal outcome most closely, with compromise defined as
consensus achieved through mutual accommodations. Various extensions and improvements of the VIKOR method
have been developed to address computational limitations and enhance practical applications. These include adapted

fuzzy implementations for linguistic decision-making scenarios. The method’s strength lies in its balanced approach to
multi-criteria situations, utilizing linear normalization that remains independent of measurement units, unlike alternative
methods such as TOPSIS which employs vector normalization.

4. CONCLUSION
The VIKOR-based analysis provides a systematic evaluation of seven bank marketing strategies for optimizing One Data
Platform (ODP) implementation across target audience reach, cost-effectiveness, engagement, and regulatory compliance.
The results demonstrate the usefulness of VIKOR in addressing trade-offs among competing criteria. Social Media
Marketing obtained the lowest𝑄 𝑗 value of 0, indicating the closest alignment with the ideal solution, while Email
Marketing ranked second based on the reported results. SMS & App Notifications recorded the highest𝑄 𝑗 value of 1,
demonstrating comparatively greater deviation from the ideal solution despite performing well on selected criteria. The
findings highlight the importance of evaluating marketing strategies through multiple dimensions rather than relying on
individual performance indicators. Banks may benefit from integrating digital channels while maintaining complementary
communication approaches. Future research could apply dynamic VIKOR models and fuzzy extensions to accommodate
changing market conditions, regulatory requirements, and uncertainty in marketing performance assessments.
