# From memes to motivation: understanding how social media influencers drive youth consumption

ARTICLE
From memes to motivation: understanding how
social media in ﬂuencers drive youth consumption
Shabahat Ali 1, Rao Bakhat Yawar 2, Sadaqat Ali 2, Naveed Saif 3 ✉, Gerald Guan Gan Goh 4 ✉ & Faheem Khan 5
This study explores the relationship between the attributes of Social Media In ﬂuencers
(SMIs) and consumer purchase intentions, focusing on the mediating roles of SMI authen-
ticity and individual self-expansion, framed within the Value-Attitude-Behavior (VAB) model.
Data were collected from university students who follow SMIs promoting products and
services through meme marketing. A total of 210 responses were analyzed using structural
equation modeling, speci ﬁcally through measurement and structural analysis with Smart PLS.
The ﬁndings reveal that SMI attributes, particularly attitude homophily and social attrac-
tiveness, positively in ﬂuence individuals ’ self-expansion, while physical attractiveness does
not exhibit a signi ﬁcant relationship. Additionally, the results indicate that both self-
expansion and perceived authenticity of SMIs mediate the relationship between attitude
homophily and brand attitude. Furthermore, positive brand attitudes are shown to enhance
consumer purchase intentions. This study contributes to the understanding of how SMI
characteristics impact consumer behavior in the context of digital marketing.

Introduction
M
arketers are increasingly leveraging social media plat-
forms to disseminate product-related information,
given their remarkable consumer outreach. According
to recent data, more than 62.6% of the global population, or 5.07
billion people, now use social media, with 259 million new users
coming online in the past year, and the average daily time spent
on social media being 2 hours and 20 minutes (Kemp, 2024).
With consumers often skipping or scrolling past commercials to
avoid them, marketers face the challenge of capturing their
attention. Consequently, marketers have turned their focus
towards strategies aimed at engaging customers and mitigating ad
avoidance. Humor has historically been a popular advertising
strategy across various mediums, including print, television, and
radio (Cantor & Venus, 1980; Woltman Elpers et al., 2004; Cat-
anescu & Tom, 2001).
Recently, humor has found widespread use in social media
marketing. Memes marketing has emerged as a strategy aimed at
attracting more internet consumers through amusing content
(Lee et al., 2019). Memes marketing refers to the practice of using
popular internet memes as a basis for promotional content. A
recent report estimated that the annual investment in in ﬂuencer
marketing reached $15 billion in 2022 (Schomer, 2019). Due to
the rapid promotion of in ﬂuential marketing techniques and wide
audience attraction companies are now focusing on promoting
their products/services through SMI. The term SMI typically
refers to individuals who exert a signi ﬁcant impact on digital
community behavior through their actions, as highlighted in
studies by Freberg et al., 2011; Khamis et al. 2017; and Lim et al.
2017. Companies and brands have recognized the potential of
social media in ﬂuencers not only as valuable marketing channels
but also as a means to foster social relationships and strengthen
their connection with their target customers (Cheung et al., 2025).
This emphasis on building relationships can lead to marketing
and sales strategies based on sustainable and enduring connec-
tions, as demonstrated in the research by Augustine ( 2019).
Business Insider has estimated that during 2022, the annual
investment in in ﬂuencer marketing reached up to $15 billion
(Schomer, 2019). More and more brands are now recognizing
inﬂuencer marketing in order to develop a connection with their
target markets (Cheung et al., 2022). According to Golan and
Zaidner ( 2008), content analysis, almost 90% of internet ads in
their research employed funny messaging to persuade customers.
Humor in advertising has been proven to increase customer
attention (Spotts et al., 1997), develop favorable message attitudes
(Barry & Grac a, 2018), and increase sharing intention (e.g.,
Brown et al., 2010).
The use of Internet memes in advertising has become prevalent
among businesses worldwide. While traditional celebrity endor-
sements in mass media resemble in ﬂuencer marketing on social
platforms, the focus shifts more towards the content itself (Lou
and Kim, 2019). In comparison to typical celebrity endorsements,
internet memes encourage greater audience participation (Arora
et al., 2019). With the rise of social media usage and the com-
plexities of the digital consumer landscape, the role of in ﬂuencers
is evolving (Koay et al., 2023). Businesses must adapt to new
marketing strategies and consider consumer value propositions
alongside traditional key performance indicators (KPIs) such as
sales, pro ﬁtability, and customer satisfaction (Masuda et al.,
2022a). Although numerous studies have explored the impact of
memes marketing on consumer purchase intention (Koay et al.,
2022; Hu et al., 2020; Ki et al., 2020). The present study aims to
examine the dynamic relationship between social media in ﬂu-
encers’ (SMIs) attributes (homophily, physical & social attrac-
tiveness) based on the theory of persuasion and followers ’
purchase intentions by integrating the value-attitude-behavior
(VAB) model along with self-expansion and perceived authenti-
city among higher education youth of the district Bannu in
Khyber Pakthunkawa (KP) province of Pakistan through memes
marketing. Stemming from self-expansion theory as applied to
close personal relationships highlights one fundamental motiva-
tion where individuals use their partners to expand their iden-
tities through acquiring new knowledge, assets, and views from
their loved ones (Nikolinakou, Phua, & Kwon, 2021). On the
other hand, the ﬁndings of Wellman et al. 2020 indicate that the
SMIs use their subjective understanding of ‘authenticity’ as an
ethical code when producing sponsored or paid posts for
inﬂuencers.
This study contends that SMI attributes (homophily, physical
& social attractiveness), alone do not solely in ﬂuence consumer
behavioral intentions but interconnected through follower ’s self-
expansion stem from SMI ’s authenticity, which subsequently
shapes attitudinal responses to meme marketing strategies.
Consequently, this interplay between individual values (Self-
Expansion), SMI attributes and authenticity, as well as attitudinal
responses, ultimately determines behavioral intentions. Thus, the
current study seeks to address the following research questions:
RQ1: Does SMI attributes (homophily, physical & social
attractiveness) and perceived authenticity enhance followers
purchase intention via memes marketing?
RQ2: Does Value, attitude and behavioral (VAB) model
intervene the relationship between SMI ’s characteristics and fol-
lowers’ purchase intention via memes marketing?
RQ3: Does SMI authenticity and consumer self-perception
mediate the relationship between brand homophily and brand
attitude via memes marketing?
The current study aims to link the proposed relationship
between social media in ﬂuencer (SMI) attributes, speci ﬁcally
homophily, physical attractiveness, and social attractiveness, and
perceived authenticity to enhance followers ’ purchase intentions
through memes marketing. Grounded in theoretical frameworks
such as Persuasion Theory (Masuda et al., 2022), the VAB Model,
and the Self-Expansion approach (Nikolinakou et al., 2021), this
research explores how SMI characteristics in ﬂuence their trust-
worthiness and promote followers ’ self-expansion by improving
brand attitudes and purchase intentions for products and services.
Inﬂuencer characteristics are viewed as peripheral cues within the
persuasion paradigm (Gupta et al., 2023), with followers assessing
these traits to identify credible in ﬂuencers. Notably, physical
attractiveness, social attractiveness, and homophily are identi ﬁed
as critical criteria for in ﬂuencer effectiveness (Sokolova & Ke ﬁ,
2020).
From a practical standpoint, it becomes evident that concise
messages and subtle cues are more effective tools when compared
to argumentative forms of communication. This underscores the
importance of subtle and persuasive messaging in memes mar-
keting for in ﬂuencing consumer behavior and purchase
intentions.

Methodology
Participants and procedure . In the current study, the researcher
uses the positivist approach to provide a better understanding of
the impact of using memes in the marketing of brands by com-
panies and social media in ﬂuencers in Pakistan (Irshaidat, 2022).
The population of the study comprises students of higher edu-
cation institutions who are familiar with meme marketing as well
as social media marketing. The sample frame consisted of the
students currently enrolled in the University of Science and
Technology, Bannu.
In the ﬁrst phase, participants are presented with various
memes related to Pakistani cricket. They are then asked to
provide their understanding of the product promotion depicted
in the memes (see Fig. 2) and how these relate to the key variables
of the study. This phase aims to gather insights into the
effectiveness of meme-based marketing strategies and their
impact on consumer perceptions and engagement.
For this purpose, we use the snowball sampling technique,
and targeted to get responses from 300 respondents via the on-
campus construct distribution technique. The application of
snowball sampling is appropriat e for meme marketing research
with social media in ﬂuencers because the studied topic remains
speciﬁc and targets a particular group of social media users
(Leighton et al., 2021). Because students display different levels
of knowledge about memes and SMI content, it becomes
necessary to focus research on respondents who already
understand these concepts. The survey begins with multiple
initial followers of SMIs who wil l direct us toward others who
follow these in ﬂuencers because of their shared interests. By
using this method, we increase access to many potential
participants while collecting meaningful information from

Discussion
The study explored the impact of attitude homophily, physical
attractiveness, and social attractiveness (Pramudito et al., 2022)
on self-expansion (Sohail, 2023), subsequently in ﬂuencing cus-
tomers’ brand attitudes and purchase intentions. It was found
that these factors signi ﬁcantly affect self-expansion, revealing
insights into the intricate nature of consumer behavior and
underlying psychological mechanisms.
Attitude homophily, de ﬁned as individuals ’ inclination to
associate with those sharing similar beliefs and attitudes (Pra-
mudito et al., 2022), emerged as a crucial determinant of custo-
mer self-expansion experiences (Michel et al., 2022). This
phenomenon aligns with the principle of “like” supported by
empirical regularities in social environments (Goel, Mason, &
Watts, 2010). Interactions with like-minded individuals validate
and reinforce personal beliefs, fostering a sense of connection and
contributing to self-concept expansion.
Contrastingly, research on physical attractiveness, extensively
studied in social science, presents mixed ﬁndings. While con-
ventionally attractive sources typically facilitate attitude change,
unconventional appearances have proven persuasive in certain
contexts (Bersheid and Walster, 1974; Cooper, Darley, and
Henderson 1974; Maddux and Rogers, 1980). Notably, the study
revealed an inverse relationship between physical attractiveness
and self-expansion, challenging the universal in ﬂuence of physical
attractiveness on attitudes.
These ﬁndings underscore the complex interplay between
attitude homophily (Gupta et al., 2023), physical attractiveness
(Pramudito et al., 2022), and self-expansion (Sohail, 2023),
emphasizing the need for a nuanced understanding of consumer
behavior and its driving factors.
Social attractiveness plays a signi ﬁcant role in in ﬂuencing self-
expansion, particularly evident in the realm of social media where
inﬂuencers with high social beauty often evoke strong emotional
responses from their followers, transcending mere “likes”
(Masuda et al., 2022). This concept, akin to “brand love, ” holds
the potential to enhance customer loyalty and credibility, acting
as an intermediary factor. Research on social networking sites has

The halo effect, as posited by Noor et al. ( 2023), further

attractiveness and interpersonal relationships. In this area,
appearances of physical attractiveness might not count that much
compared to personal qualities such as character, integrity, and
common beliefs that are usually focused on to create a signi ﬁcant
relationship.
Moreover, cultural expectations also make individuals perceive
physical beauty as a super ﬁcial concept that undermines its
purpose of enhancing stronger emotional connections. Rather,
other traits that are more related to self-expansion would include
homophily, whereby individuals pursue similarity in beliefs and
way of life. This is in line with what is believed that, in closely knit
communities, the establishment of relations lays more on shared
experiences and mutual affections as compared to physical looks.
In addition, the growing popularity of authenticity and genuine
relationships as mentioned in the VAB model, followers in Bannu
would be more indulged to get attached to in ﬂuencers with
similar characteristics who are authentic and realistic, other than
the ones who just focus on physical appearance. Accordingly,
notwithstanding the apparent similarity between physical
appearance and satisfying self-expansion, the former does not
directly reﬂect the latter in a culture-enriched climate that sets the
tone of interpersonal relations in the ways that go deeper than
transactional cost and emotional appeal.
Similarly, social attractiveness, encompassing factors like social
status and popularity (Pramudito et al., 2022), can contribute to
self-expansion when customers identify with brands associated
with these qualities. The allure of social attractiveness enhances
customers’ emotional connections with brands, fostering a sense
of belonging and contributing to the expansion of their self-
concept (Sohail, 2023). Thus, both physical and social attrac-
tiveness wield signi ﬁcant in ﬂuence in shaping customer percep-
tions and self-expansion experiences within the realm of
marketing and branding.
The ﬁndings further con ﬁrm that the perceived authenticity of
social media in ﬂuencers (SMIs) plays a crucial role in shaping
consumer brand attitudes (Shoenberger, & Kim, 2023), particu-
larly through memes-based marketing channels. Using
value-attitude and behavioral modeling (VAB), it is evident that
perceived authenticity and homophily are key factors driving
individuals’ self-expansion for purchasing decisions (Michel et al.,
2022). Self-expansion, de ﬁned as the desire to broaden one ’s self-
concept through engaging in novel activities or adopting traits of
others, signi ﬁcantly in ﬂuences brand attitudes (Hughes et al.,
2020).
According to the self-expansion theory (Sohail, 2023), indivi-
duals seek to enhance their self-concept, and brands that facilitate
this process are viewed more favorably. Brands that resonate with
customers’ values, offer opportunities for personal growth, and
foster a sense of belonging tend to receive positive evaluations.
Consequently, as individuals expand their self-concept through
interactions with like-minded SMIs or memers, they develop
positive attitudes toward the brands promoted by these
inﬂuencers.
Furthermore, positive brand attitudes have a direct impact on
purchase intentions (Y. S. Chen et al., 2020). Customers are
more inclined to purchase products or services from brands
they hold favorable attitudes toward. A positive brand attitude
reﬂects trust, satisfaction, and loyalty, all of which contribute to
the likelihood of consumers translating their intentions into
actual purchases. Thus, the interplay between perceived
authenticity, self-expansion, brand attitudes, and purchase
intentions highlights the intricate dynamics of consumer
behavior in the context of social media marketing. This
research analysis, conducted among higher education students
in the Bannu district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province,
demonstrates that the features of social media in ﬂuencers have
a direct and strong impact on youth consumer behavior. People
in this demographic demonstrate a strong preference for atti-
tude congruence when forming connections, which enhances
their self-expansion. Among higher education youth in the
Bannu district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province, there is a
signiﬁcant perception of connection with how social media
inﬂuencers present themselves as socially attractive, fostering
emotional bonds and a sense of belonging.
Most importantly the male-dominated sample size from the
University of Science and Technology (UST) Bannu in this study
on memes and social media in ﬂuencers (SMIs) is justi ﬁed by
several factors. First, the demographic composition of the uni-
versity re ﬂects a larger enrollment of male students in certain
disciplines, which in ﬂuences the representation in research
samples. Furthermore, cultural norms in the region may lead to
higher male engagement with social media platforms, particularly
in contexts involving humor and in ﬂuence. This focus on male
participants allows for a deeper exploration of how memes and
SMIs resonate within this speci ﬁc demographic, providing
insights that can inform marketing strategies and content creation
aimed at male audiences. Additionally, understanding the
dynamics of male interactions with memes and SMIs can con-
tribute to broader discussions about gender representation in
digital spaces, highlighting the need for future research that
includes diverse perspectives.
Physical attractiveness in consumer behavior does not operate
monolithically according to traditional beliefs because the
research study presents multifaceted behavioral patterns. Uni-
versity research ﬁndings demonstrate how Pakistani youth's
brand perceptions interact with psychological factors as their
behavior evolves in the modern digital environment.
Practical implications . The current study highlights the impor-
tance of meme marketing as an innovative advertising approach
that challenges traditional methods through the integration of
internet technology. In today ’s digital landscape, organizations
are increasingly focused on reaching consumers through diverse
channels, capitalizing on the vast opportunities that online
marketing presents. Short-form advertising via Social Media
Inﬂuencers (SMIs) has proven to be one of the most effective
strategies for capturing consumer attention.
This study establishes a connection between three key
attributes of SMIs —homophily, social attractiveness, and physical
attractiveness—with the aim of enhancing consumer self-
actualization. This approach, akin to the concept of “brand love,”
not only seeks to elevate brand awareness but also promotes
products and services by positioning consumers as relatable
inﬂuencers themselves. By leveraging these attributes, brands can
foster deeper customer loyalty and credibility, acting as a bridge
between the in ﬂuencer and their audience.
