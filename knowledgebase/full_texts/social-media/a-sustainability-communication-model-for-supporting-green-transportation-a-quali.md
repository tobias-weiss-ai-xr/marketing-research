ABSTRACT
The low adoption of electric vehicles in Indonesia (1 –1.2%) indicates a gap in public communication narratives
that have not been able to build a sense of environmental  "security" for the public. Amid the climate crisis and
the 2060 Net Zero Emission targ et, the TikTok account @eos_indonesia plays a role as a communication actor
actively  promoting electric vehicles with a safe and comfortable narrative through digital content, in contrast to
competitors who generally only focus on the technical aspects of  the  product. This study aims to identify how
sustainability messages are constructed through visual elements, language, and narrative as a form of marketing
in sustainability. This  study uses a content analysis method, combining a sustainability communication approach
and securitization theory. This study uses securitization theory which refers to the  process by which certain issues
are portrayed as threats that require action. This process includes efforts to convince the public that issues (such
as terrorism, immigration, or  climate change) pose a serious risk of existential threat to security. The focus of
this research is on the framing strategies used by TikTok content creator @eos_indonesia in  marketing electric
vehicles on social media. This stu dy uses a qualitative research method with a content analysis approach. To
ensure the depth of this data, several methods  are used to collect information. These methods consist of
interviews, digital participatory observation and documentation. The data collection is an analysis of the TikTok
account @eos_indonesia content data that will be taken with a period of January - June 2026 according to the
classification that has been made, the data collected includes:  video content (visual, audio and narrative ). and
content analysis has stages, starting from determining the unit of information, data sampling, recoding or recoding
data (coding),  grouping based on certain categories until finally interpreting the meaning contained in the
message content. The results of this study, as presented on the TikTok account  @eos_indonesia, describe electric
vehicles as the newest and most environmentally friendly transportation solution, which is conveyed through
educational content narratives  and highlights the benefit s of electric vehicles in everyday life. The published
content consistently builds the view that the use of electric vehicles is safe, comfortable, and  beneficial for
environmental sustainability. This strengthens public acceptance of the transition to mo re sustainable
transportation.
Keywords: Sustainability Communication, Electric Vehicles, Content Analysis, TikTok, EOS Indonesia.

1. INTRODUCTION
The global environmental crisis poses a major threat that endangers the balance of
ecosystems and human survival. Climate change, deforestation, pollution, and species
extinction are accelerating the problem of environmental pollution, which continues to increase
and become increasingly difficult to control. The environmental crisis is not only a technical
and scientific problem, but also reflects a crisis of values and community involvement
(Febryanti, 2025) . In response to the ongoing climate and environmental crisis, the Indonesian

government has created the Net Zero Emission (NZE) program by 2060. Net Zero Emission
(NZE) or zero carbon emissions is a condition in which the amount of carbon released into the
atmosphere is no greater than the amount of carbon absorbed by the earth. To achieve this
condition, a shift from the current energy system to a cleaner energy system i s necessary to
achieve a balance between human activities and the natural environment (Zahira & Fadillah,
2022).
Indonesia has declared its readiness to enter the electric vehicle era and emphasized
that building a national electric vehicle ecosystem is part of Indonesia's contribution to ASEAN
economic cooperation. This plan is further strengthened by the issuance o f Presidential
Regulation Number 55 of 2019 concerning the acceleration of the battery-based electric motor
vehicle (BEV) program for road transportation (Prasetyo & Rukmana, 2025). Electric vehicles
also make a positive contribution to achieving sustainable economic goals. Green economy
initiatives began in 1992, at the UN Conference in Rio de Janeiro, which served as the
foundation for the concept of sustainable development. The U N Environment Program
explains that a green economy focuses on growth that improves welfare and social equity, while
reducing the risk of environmental damage. The electric vehicles currently being developed are
one innovation in achieving the green economy program in the trans portation sector (Zola et
al., 2023).
One way to reduce the negative effects of disasters, including climate change caused
by increased greenhouse gas emissions in the atmosphere, is to encourage sustainability in the
transportation sector due to high carbon gas emissions, because our governme nt has a
sustainability strategy or sustainability communication (SC) is a newly used term. In general,
Sustainability Communication is described as a global social process consisting of a series of
recurring contributions and conversations, with the aim of achieving a better life in the
ecological, economic, and social fields (Golob et al., 2023).
A number of studies in sustainability communication on social media according to
(Dharmajaya & Minangkabawi, 2024) , building continuous trust in the field of disruptive
communication is an ongoing challenge. Transparency, accountability, education, and
cooperation are important ways to maintain credibility and trust. This study aims to determine
the benefits provided in green technology for safer and more environmentally friendly daily
life with it can support the Indonesian government's zero emission program, encourage
sustainability in the transportation sector due to high carbon gas emissions, therefore EOS
Indonesia supports the government's zero carbon emission program in championing Electric
vehicles, not only having products but EOS Indonesia has the challenge to support government
programs.
Supporting the government's zero-emission program can be considered green
marketing. Green marketing itself is believed to play a crucial role when companies want to
launch environmentally friendly products, so implementing green marketing is a must. Proper
implementation will create consumer desire to purchase the product and recognize the
importance of environmentally friendly products. This aligns with the concept of green brand
positioning, which encompasses brand identity and customer perception of a company's values.
Customer perception of a company's values depends on how the company's environmental

2. METHODS
This research was conducted using a qualitative content analysis approach research
method, which explains how qualitative research according to According to Cresswell (2009),
,qualitative research is a method used to explore and understand the meanings given by
individuals or groups regarding specific social or humanitarian issues. This study uses a
qualitative content analysis method. It combines a sustainability communication point of view
with securitization theory. The goal is to look at how environmental sustainability messagesre
made through TikTok marketing content shared by @eos_indonesia.
The unit of analysis was videos uploaded between January and June 2026. Ten videos
were chosen on purpose. They showed themes about electric vehicles. These themes included
messages about sustainability protecting the environment, money benefits, safety and how the
audience interacts. Along with content analysis data was made stronger by doing in -depth
interviews with the EOS Indonesia Operations Manager, a Social Media Administrator and one
TikTok follower. This helped check and confirm the meanings of the  content. Data was
collected in three ways. First by recording videos, their captions and the pictures. Second, by

Through a qualitative content analysis of social media accounts focused on
sustainability communication over a six -month period, from January to June 2026, this study
identified a systematic integration between sustainability communication approaches and
digital marketing strategies. This phenomenon is based on a securitization theoretical
framework, where environmental and energy efficiency issues are positioned as public issues
through discursive actions (i.e., speech acts). Data was coded on seven key videos representing
the core pillars of @eos_indonesia's content messaging. These seven pieces of content were
coded using key aspects of securitization: Securitization Actor (SA), Referent Object (OR),
Existential/Technical Thr eat (AE), Sustainability Communication (SC), Securitization
Step/Speech Act (SS), Call to Action (CTA), and Audience (AU). These elements are further
discussed in the narrative, and their manifestation in the visualization is listed below:
To increase the validity of the findings based on the content analysis above, this study
combines data obtained from content analysis, interviews involving internal informants, and
external factors such as the audience as the message recipients. The operat ional flow of EOS
Indonesia's digital communication strategy is mapped to three informants based on the
reduction and condensation of interview data.
1) Content planning perspective (social media team – Yunia) the selection of TikTok as
the main communication channel is based on the large number of TikTok users in
Indonesia which has reached 169 million users. The EOS Indonesia social media
admin team creates content that not only emphasizes product aspects but also forms
social meaning with educational and informative messages or content narratives, so
that it can be easily understood by the public.
2) The Operations Manager (Fitri) explained that TikTok EOS Indonesia is not just
about content about product aspects, specifications, and technical aspects, but also
serves as an educational and informative platform that can shape social meaning for
the community. The content selection process follows a well-established oversight
system. The decision-making process is bottom-up , with the social media team
drafting creative drafts, then reviewing the documents, and all decisions (final
approval) being made by a director and manager who ensure the theme, narrative,
brand specifications, and model are perfect before broadcast.
3) Meanwhile, the perspective of TikTok follower @eos_indonesia (Azwalita),
emphasizes more on the marketing narrative that lies in the efficiency of delivering
content messages, the narrative of TikTok content @eos_indonesia and the technical
educational and informative videos may be very effective in attracting public
attention. The creative message used to package this content is the reason behind the
strong appeal (high engagement rate) on likes, comments, and shares. With that, there
is a desire to switch to electric vehicles with the content created by @eos_indonesia.

3.2.1 Deconstructing Product Stigma and Reorienting Perceived Risk Through
Securitization Strategies
In green marketing communications and consumer behavior, the perceived risk of
sustainable technological innovation can also be seen as a driver that makes its adoption very
difficult. This encompasses not only economic risks but also functional and psychological risks
that give rise to collective resistance through product stigma. Based on the findings of content
analysis on the TikTok account @eos_indonesia, the process of deconstructing this stigma is
not carried out through traditional sales persuasion, but rather through a mechanism that tends
towards Securitization Theory, following the steps of a speech act.
According to Buzan and Wæver, (1998) , an issue enters the realm of securitization
when the issue is spread through discourse by an actor (Securitizing Actor) into the realm of
ordinary politics by raising capital arguments, so that the issue becomes an existential
emergency that deserves immediate action. In this study, @eos_indonesia acts as a Securitizing
Actor . The issue that has been securitized has shifted from simply: “global climate campaign”,
to “threats to microeconomic sovereignty and personal safety of consumers” due to extreme
dependence on gasoline-fueled transportation.

Content analysis of the videos “Gen Z rides electric motorbikes so they can drink coffee
every day” and “electric motorbike maintenance costs” relate to operational expenses (the
amount spent compared to the amount earned) arising from owning conventional gasoline-
powered vehicles (fuel components, oil, and radiators). Financial risk , however, through the
securitization process, these expenditure factors become Existential Threats (AE), but the lack
of financial stability of the community. To address this, EOS Indonesia offers aggressive
economic rationalization through low -cost battery charging, with an excess charging cost of
more than IDR 1,000 for a single charger. This is a financial comparison, Reference Object
(OR), or good value under the threat of gasoline price increases, which is intended to be the
consumer's microeconomic sovereignty (consumer financial well-being). This supports Yunia's
argument (Social Media Division), that linking product use to consumers' economic lifestyles
is far more effective in triggering purchasing interest than simply educating the technical
aspects of electric vehicles.
However, the main obstacle to marketing electric vehicles in Indonesia can be traced to
psychological resistance (in the form of product stigma) from the public. This stigma emerges
as functional consumer anxiety, such as the expectation that the vehicle's electrical components
will short out and die when exposed to flooding (technical stigma), that electric motors will not
be able to climb steep hills, or how small items will fit into the trunk of the product unit. In
securitization theory, this public fea r is a Perceived Threat that hinders the switch to electric
vehicles. To address this perceived risk, EOS Indonesia conducted product resilience testing in
the form of content as a securitization calming measure (desecuritization/guarantee action).

3.2.2 The Synergy of Experiential Marketing and Social Influence as Speech Acts in
Converting Brand Trust into Purchase Intention
In the context of sustainable marketing communications, it is not only determined by
the company's ability to eliminate negative views or prejudices, but also by how well they are
able to arouse consumer interest without being trapped in monotonous content  narratives or
aggressively providing commercial coercion (hard selling) . In line with the explanation of Mrs.
Fitri Wulandari as Operational Manager, @eos_indonesia deliberately conveys messages
subliminally (soft-selling) in line with its strategic orientation on the issue of ecological
education amidst the commercial target activities of unit sales. The marketing conversion of
this is explained in securitization theory as Audience Acceptance (AU), where emergency
claims made by Securitization Actors are officially authorized by the public.

4. CONCLUSION
This study shows that the TikTok account @eos_indonesia uses stories that focus on
sustainability to promote vehicles as a clean way to travel. By looking at their content and using
ideas from sustainability communication and securitization theory the resu lts show that their
storytelling does more than just sell a product. They create stories that link benefits, cost -
effectiveness, safety and how electric vehicles fit into daily life in a strong way to share their
message online. They use videos to tell their content showing how electric vehicles work how
they work with others and how they talk to others. All of this helps worries about the vehicles
and makes more people, like them and want to buy them. The study also makes a model that
shows how storytelling about sustainability, combined with comfort-building methods through
marketing can change how people act and encourage them to use safer and easier electric
vehicles. These results help people understand sustainability communication and securitization
in areas. They also help groups and people who make rules learn how to plan for more people
to use vehicles and cut down on pollution leading to better and safer lives.
