ABSTRACT
Social media has become an important part of the daily life of young consumers. Many young people follow social media influencers on platforms such as
Instagram and YouTube andregularly watch their product reviews, recommendations, and promotional content. Influencers can create awareness about different
brands and may also affect the purchase decisions of consumers. This study examines how social media influencers influence the brand purchase behaviour of
youngconsumersinHyderabad,andhowfactorssuchastrust,contenttype,recommendations,andinfluencercredibilityshapetheirbuyingdecisions.
A descriptive, quantitative survey design was used. Primary data were collected through a structured 20-item questionnaire administered online to 73 young
consumersaged18–30between 25Augustand3October2026. The data were analysed using frequencies, means, Spearmanrankcorrelation, chi-square,Mann–
WhitneyUandKruskal–Wallistests,andmultipleregression.
The results show that Instagram is the dominant platform (64.4%) and that 64.4% of respondents have bought a product because of an influencer’s
recommendation, although only 47.9% do so “sometimes” or“often”. Overall influence on brand purchase decisions is moderate (mean 3.14 on a 5-point scale).
Trust in influencer recommendations is the strongest predictor of influence (ρ = .43, p < .001; β = .41 in regression) and of purchase frequency. Honesty and
positive reviews are the most cited trust builders, while follower count is the least cited. Gender, age, occupation, and time spent on social media were not
significantly related to purchase behaviour or overall influence. The findings suggest that influencer marketing works for young consumers in Hyderabad mainly
whenitisperceivedastrustworthy, ratherthansimplybecauseaninfluencerispopular.

2.REVIEWOFLITERATURE
2.1 Prasad and Reddy (2021)
Prasad and Reddy (2021) conducted a study titled “Influence of Social Media Influencers on Consumer Buying Behavior: A Study of Hyderabad
Youth.” The study examined how social media influencers affect the buying behaviour of young consumers in Hyderabad. The researchers mainly
focused on the credibility and relatability of influencers as important factors influencing consumer behaviour. The study found that influencers on
platforms such as YouTube and Instagram had a noticeable influence on the trends and buying decisions of young consumers. The study provided an
earlyunderstandingofinfluencermarketingamongHyderabadyouth,althoughitwasmainlylimitedtourbanrespondents.

understandhowinfluencermarketingisadoptedbyyoungconsumersandhowitaffectstheirpurchasebehaviour.Thestudyconsideredfactorssuchas

2.3 Madhavi and Ramesh (2023)
Madhavi and Ramesh (2023) conducted research on “Consumer Attitudes Towards Influencer Marketing in Hyderabad.” The study focused on
understanding consumer perceptions regarding the credibility and expertise of social media influencers. According to the findings, the expertise of an
influencer played a more important role indeveloping consumertrust thansimply having a large number of followers. The study mainly concentrated
onattitudeformationandpurchaseintentionbutdidnotexamineactualpurchasebehaviourindetail.
2.4 Satish Kumar, Ganeshdas and Khaleeq ur Raheman (2024)
Satish Kumar, Ganeshdas and Khaleeq ur Raheman (2024) examined “The Rise of Social Commerce: A Study of Consumer Buying Behavior
Among Youth in Hyderabad.” The study explored the growing adoption of social commerce and its relationship with consumer buying behaviour.
Factors such as peer validation and influencer posts were considered in the study. The researchers found that Instagram played a major role in the
adoption of social commerce among young consumers. However, the study was based on cross-sectional data, which provides information only for a
particularperiod.
2.5 Kothapally Susmitha and Nazia Sultana (2025)
Kothapally Susmitha andNazia Sultana (2025) conducted a study titled “Effectiveness of Influencer Marketing on Customer Purchase Intention of
Cosmetics Among Women in Hyderabad.”The researchexaminedhowinfluencermarketingaffects women’s intentiontopurchasecosmeticproducts.
The study considered influencer credibility, engagement, and brand alignment as important factors. The findings showed that credibility was the
strongest factor influencing purchase intention. However, the study was specifically focused on women consumers and the cosmetics industry,
creatingscopeforresearchcoveringyoungconsumersanddifferentproductcategories.

5.3 Conclusion
This study examined the influence of social media influencers on brand purchase behaviour among young consumers in Hyderabad. The evidence
shows that influencers are an established part of young consumers’ information environment: most respondents watch influencer content, most have
bought a product on an influencer’s advice, and Instagram is the main channel. However, the overall influence is moderate, purchases on
recommendationareoccasional,andtrustiscautious.
Among the factors studied, trust stands out as the main difference between respondents who are more influenced and those who are less influenced.
Honesty and authenticity, not follower count, are what young consumers say builds that trust, and reviews and short videos are the formats that work
best.Demographiccharacteristicsandtheamountoftimespentonlinedonotappeartohavemucheffectinthissample.Forbrands,themaintakeaway
is that influencer marketing is more useful when the influencer is credible, honest, and suitable for the product, rather than being selected only for a
largereach.Furtherstudieswithlargerandmorerepresentativesampleswouldhelpconfirmthesefindings.
