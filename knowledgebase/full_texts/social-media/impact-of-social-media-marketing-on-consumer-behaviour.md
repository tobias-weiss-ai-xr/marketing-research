ABSTRACT
Social media is where the customers lo ok for products, reviews and are influenced for their final
purchase decision. This study analyses the impact of social media marketing on consumer

1. INTRODUCTION
Social media has become an important marketing tool that helps businesses connect with
customers, promote products and build relationships. Consumers use platforms such as Facebook,
Instagram and YouTube to discover products, read reviews and make purchase decisions.Social
media influences consumer behaviour by creati ng awareness, providing product information and
encouraging customer engagement. This is especially useful for interior businesses because
customers often prefer to view designs, completed projects and product images before
purchasing.The present study foc uses on, a Chennai -based interior solutions company offering
products and services such as wallpapers, blinds, ceilings, flooring, painting and modular interiors.
The study examines how social media marketing influences consumer awareness, engagement,
trust and purchasing decisions in relation to.
2.  Objectives of the Study
1. To study about the impact of social media marketing on consumer buying behaviour.
2. To understand how social media platforms influence consumer behaviour.
3. To identify the factors affecting purchasing decisions through social media.
4. To examine the impact of social media advertisements on consumer preferences.

 Need of the Study
1. The rapid rise of social media has fundamentally changed the ways business and customers
interact with each other. Customers are now better informed than ever before; hence, the need to
understand the impact of digital media on their choices and behaviors.
2. This study is important for several reasons.
3. First, the business needs to meet the changing demands of i ts customers in the face of social
media’s rising prominence.
Social media contains a wealth of information that can help the business understand the likes and
dislikes of its customers and target its marketing efforts more precisely.
4. Second, social media has become an important tool for marketing and is used by many
businesses to reach out to their target customers.
Therefore, it is necessary for the business to grasp the power of social media in order to market its
products more efficiently and effectively.
5. Third, customers are increasingly relying on customer reviews and ratings, recommendations,
and influencers’ opinions when buying a product.
As such, the business must familiarize itself with the way these aspects affect customers’ buying
behaviors.
6. Fourth, social media facilitates direct engagement with customers.
This means that customers can message or comment on the business’s posts and ask questions that
the company can respond to. In this way, the business can promote its products and services and
gain customer trust and loyalty.
7. Last but not least, the business has the opportunity to enhance its marketing efforts on social
media since many people share pictures and videos and post comments about their experiences
with the product or service. Moreover, the study is of particular importance to the business because
its products are highly visual. The business offers interior design services and solutions and can
benefit immensely from showcasing customer reviews and photos of projects c ompleted by the
company.
4. Scope of the Study
1. The scope of the research is limited to studying the relationship between social media marketing
and consumer behaviour.
2. The research covers the most popular social media platforms such as Facebook, Inst agram,
YouTube and Twitter. Therefore, the impact of various forms of social media marketing, such as
advertisements, influencer marketing, online reviews, recommendations, user -generated content
and customer engagement will be analysed.

5. Review of Literature
Gajashree and Anand (2021)
Gajashree and Anand have studied the effect of social media on the consumer purchase decision
process considering mobile phone purchases in Chennai city. From the study  it can be observed
that consumers make use of social media for information search and evaluation purposes prior to
purchase. This research is especially significant as it was done in Chennai.
Upadhyay, Paul and Baber (2022)
The research analyzed the link between social media marketing initiatives and customer reaction
through brand equity and brand trust. The research emphasized that trust and brand perceptions
play an important role in making consumers respond to social media activities.
Shrestha et al. (2023)
This research investigated the impact of social media marketing on customer buying behavior and
stressed the significance of social media involvement in making decisions.
Ehsan, Yousaf and Usman (2024)
This research focused on social media marketing practices, product quality perceptions, usability
of social media platforms, and social influence. The study established that social media marketing
practices can affect purchase intentions through product quality perceptions and platform usability.

6. Research Gap
Past literature has demonstrated the impact of social media marketing on consumer attitudes,
engagement, trust and purchase intentions. However, there is a need for exploring these concepts
in the industry context.Most past researches have been carried out on sectors such as mobile
phones, cosmetic products and general online purchase. Fewer studies have taken into account
industries like interior solutions and wall decoration, which are more visually oriented.Also, there
is a need for investigat ing the different aspects of social media marketing as opposed to
consideringsocial media as one variable. In this regard, this current research has studied the impact
of social media engagement, social media advertisements, influencer marketing and online reviews
on consumer purchases.One more gap identified is that of the consumer context in Chennai. While
there are studies on the social media behavior of consumers in Chennai, this research study is
limited to the context of the , which is an interior sol utions company.Thus, there is an attempt to
fill the gap between social media marketing and real marketing situations in this research study.
7. Research Design
This research utilizes a descriptive, quantitative, and cross-sectional research design. The primary
data will be obtained through the administration of structured questionnaires to 100 respondents
who use social media.
7.1 Sample Size and Sampling Technique
The total sample size for this research is 100 respondents. Convenience sampling technique w as
used in sampling the respondents depending on their availability and willingness to participate in
the study.
7.2 Questionnaire and Data Collection
A structured questionnaire based on the research objectives will be utilized. The questionnaire will
have a five-point Likert Scale including strongly disagree, disagree, neither agree nor disagree,
agree, and strongly agree. Primary data will be gathered through the use of the questionnaire, while
secondary data will be retrieved from books, journals, articles, and websites.
7.3 Variables of the Study
1. Independent Variables
2. Social Media Engagement
3. Social Media Advertising
4. Influencer Marketing
5. Online Reviews
7.4 Dependent Variable
Purchase Decision / Consumer Buying Behaviour

9. Findings of the Study
1. 65% of respondents belong to the 21 –30 age group, showing that most respondents are
young social media users.
2. The respondents were predominantly male (89%), while 11% were female.
3. 63% of respondents are postgraduates, indicating a highly educated sample.
4. 67% of respondents are students, making them the largest occupational group.
5. Social media advertisements easily capture consumer attention, with the highest mean
score of 3.67.
6. Personalised and targeted advertisements are considered more relevant by consumers, with
a mean score of 3.66.
7. Influencer Marketing has the strongest relationship with Purchase Decision (r = 0.565).
8. Social Media Engagement (r = 0.547) and Social Media Advertising (r = 0.505) also show
strong positive relationships with purchase decisions.
9. Online Reviews (r = 0.248) have a positive but comparatively weaker relationship with
purchase decisions.
10. Overall, the study finds that social media marketing has a positive impact o n consumer
buying behaviour, particularly through influencer marketing, engagement and advertising.
10. Suggestions
1. Create high-quality visual content:  should focus on attractive photographs, before -and-
after images, short videos, reels and completed project showcases.
2. Improve customer interaction: Comments, messages and enquiries should be answered
promptly to improve customer relationships and satisfaction.
3. Use local influencers: Collaboration with relevant local influencers and interior designers
can improve brand visibility and credibility.
4. Encourage customer reviews: Customers should be encouraged to provide ratings,
testimonials and reviews after completing projects.
5. Use targeted advertisements: Social media advertising should be customised according to
customer interests, demographics and location.
6. Maintain posting consistency: Regular and consistent posting can help the brand remain
visible and relevant.
7. Increase a udience participation: Polls, Q&A sessions, contests and live sessions can
encourage customers to interact with the brand.
8. Improve transparency: Clear pricing information, authentic project photographs and
genuine customer experiences can improve trust.
9. Use analytics: Social media analytics should be used to understand audience behaviour,
preferences and engagement patterns.

11. Limitations of the Study
1. Firstly, the study has limited scope due to the fact that the number of respondents is relatively
small (100 respondents).
2. Secondly, the study is not generalizable to the whole population because it was used
convenience sampling.
3. Thirdly, the study has limited scope because it was conducted only among respondents in one
consumer environment. It cannot be generalized to other geographic areas.
4. Fourthly, the study has limited scope because it was conducted in a short time frame. In social
media, things are changing rapidly; therefore, it is unclear how relevant the study results will be
in future.
5. Fifthly, the study relies on the respondents’ answers to the research questions. Therefore, one
should be careful about the validity of this study.
6. Sixthly, the study has a limited scope because it focuses on social media only. Although most
of the respondents’ answers were related to Facebook, Instagram and You Tube, there are many
other platforms of social media.
7. Seventhly, the study focuses on social media users only. The study does not consider
respondents who prefer conventional forms of marketing communication.
8. Lastly, consumer behaviour is determined by various factors such as income, tastes and
preferences, lifestyle, culture, product quality and price among others. Therefore, it is not easy to
identify the extent to which social media influences consumer behaviour.

The study demonstrates that social media has become an integral source of information for
consumers and are used to discover produc ts, obtain information, compare alternatives, read
reviews and consider recommendations prior to making purchase decisions.

media advertisements were especiall y successful in capturing respondents' attention while the
personalization of advertising and usage of social media also recorded relatively high mean scores.
