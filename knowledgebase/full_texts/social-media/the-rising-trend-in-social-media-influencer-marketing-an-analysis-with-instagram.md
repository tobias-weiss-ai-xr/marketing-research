ABSTRACT
The integration of social media into consumers' daily lives presents businesses with new opportunities for
promoting and marketing their products. One such opportunity is influencer marketing. Brands now collaborate
with influencers to generate positive pu blicity about themselves among consumers. Although “ influencer status”
encompasses a wide range in today's social media, this study limits its scope to influencer fathers. With the rapid
changes in technology and social life, parents who have made social media use a routine part of their daily lives
have begun sharing many moments of their children's lives on social media, just as they share many of their own
moments. Based on this, this study aims to investigate the potential role of fathers, who are becoming increasingly
active in the digital environment, in influencer marketing, which has become a new marketing field in the changing
and evolving context of social media. Therefore, the study first reviewed domestic and international literature on
social media, influencer marketing, and influencer concepts . Subsequently, the accounts of six influencer fathers
who actively shared content on Instagram and were selected based on their follower counts were analyzed using
content analysis between November 10 and December 10, within the defined categories. These categories were
defined as follower -follower counts, number of posts, product placement categories, product -story integration -
brand categories, text language, and emoji usage. As a result of the research, it was found that influencer fathers
have a high number of followers; however, the number of people they follow is not proportional to this ratio. It was
observed that they keep up with current trends, and the main message in their posts is that the father, together with
the mother, is also the person who cares for the child, shows interest, and is responsible for their education. Similarly,
in posts examined from the perspective of masculine and feminine roles, it was determined that fathers also share
content in roles such as childcare, which are traditionally considered to belong to women in society. Additionally,
the findings show that fathers also take on roles in their content such as valuing their spouse/family and dedicating
special time to them. This study is significant in that it addresses the changing and evolving face of social media
and influencer marketing in the context of the changing and evolving perspective of parenthood.

1. “Social Media”: As A Marketing Arena
Social media, which offers ease of access and empowers every individual to
create content, also has many other advantages. It brings together both consumers and
brands in a more interactive environment and enables the inclusion of new areas of use
in brand communication. Before social media, brand communication activities were
largely controlled by brands, but with the rise of the internet and social media,
consumers have increasingly taken control of these activities. Thanks to social media's
structure, which highlights user -generated content, individuals now have a space
where they can freely share their thoughts and create their own content. Brands that
recognized the importance and power of this space began to take advantag e of it to
promote their products and services easily, quickly, and at low cost; to build close
relationships with consumers; and to receive feedback in a short period of time. Social
media has also come to the fore in terms of offering the opportunities provided by

1.1. Social Media Marketing: Conceptual Framework
The impact of the internet and social media, which is felt not only nationally
but also internationally, has made concepts such as globalism, globalization, and the
disappearance of borders, as well as their consequences, more visible and open to
discussion. Social media has introduced people to a communication environment free
from all constraints such as time and space; it offers people a digital home where they
can communicate with each other, share important moments and information in their
lives, and even trade, through classifications such as blogs, microblogs, wikis, online
communities, social bookmarking/tagging sites, location -based services, content
sharing sites, and social networks, as well as social media marketing  (Sönmez ve
Taşkıran, 2019, p.129) offers people a digital home where they can communicate with
each other, share important moments and information in their lives, and even conduct
business. Since the main focus of this study is social media marketing, the study will
be structured around this axis.
According to Weinberg (2009, p.3) , social media marketing refers to “the
promotion of products and services through online social channels and
communicating with consumer groups that are too large to reach through traditional
retention methods.” Another definition describes social media marketing as a process
that empowers individuals to promote their websites, products, or services through
online social channels and to communicate and connect with very large communities
that would not be possible through traditional advertising channels (Tuten, 2008, p.19).
Businesses' inclusion of their Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, or other social
media platforms in their advertising and promotion through traditional media channels
such as radio, television, or magazines can be seen as an example of social media
marketing.  In addition, all marketing activities carried out by businesses through these
social media platforms also fall within the scope of social media marketing (Kotler
and Armstrong, 2011, p.20) . At this point, Handley and Chapman (2013, p.37) ,
Mohammadian and Mohammadreza (2012, p.59), and Weinberg (2009, p.6) list the
fundamental objectives of social media marketing as follows:

As a result of technological developments in our globalized world, brands have
been utilizing social media phenomena in recent years to reach their target audiences
through social media platforms. In fact, using phenomena to promote products has
become one of today's most important marketing and brand communication strategies.
As Yaylagül (2017, p.223)  points out, influencers, who can reach large audiences
through their follower counts, are positioning themselves as a new strategy within
brands' diverse advertising pursuits.

3. “Influencer Marketing”: As A New Marketing Technique
As traditional methods were adapted to the digital world, marketers sought new
approaches, leading to the reformulation of marketing techniques. In this context, the
digital world's offering of new communication channels has taken marketers'
relationships with consumers to new dimensions (Ayaz and Aytekin, 2021, p.210), and
the concept of influencer marketing has emerged as a new trend.
Influencer marketing refers to a marketing method whereby companies invest
in selected influencers to create content for their brands or promote their products or
brands to their followers (Global Yodel, 2017). It stems from the idea that influencers
can influence consumers' purchasing decisions through their authority, position,
relationships, and knowledge. Considered a form of electronic word -of-mouth
(eWOM) marketing, influencer marketing is defined as a ma rketing activity that
encompasses all actions of promoting or selling a brand's products and services
through individuals who have the capacity to influence that brand (HuffPost, 2016).
The concept emerged in the 2000s. In the 2000s, individuals with high
recognition and visibility, known as influencers, began communicating with various

budgets to pay these individuals to advertise a specific brand or product, opening the
doors to a new era in the world of marketing. Beyond recognition and awareness, the
foundations were l aid for a different approach to advertising products and brands,
reaching many people through new communication channels in the digital world, such
as Facebook, Twitter, YouTube, and Instagram. Therefore, marketers have also begun

strategies (Tabellion and Esch, 2019, p.1) . Indeed, since influencer marketing is an
online marketing method, it has become a new and hot topic for marketers (Brown and
Hayes, 2015, p.7). Social media channels such as Facebook, YouTube, and Instagram
have become integral parts of consumer communication for many companies due to

CONCLUSION and EVALUATION
Developments in information and communication technologies have led to many
innovations in the way we do business and communicate. Among these innovations,
social media has come to the fore with its distinctive features. Facebook, a social
networking site that is particularly heavily used today, content sharing sites such as
Instagram and YouTube, microblogging sites such as Twitter, and forums and platforms
have become part of the production and consumption sector, especially marketing. So
much so that changing consumer trends, greatly influenced by digital marketing efforts,
have made new promotion and marketing methods necessary. One such method is
influencer marketing, where opinion leaders play a significant role in spreading word-
of-mouth marketing communication to large audiences. These influencers have become
“phenomena” on online channels. At this point, the need to reach young consumers who
do not trust traditional advertising has led brand managers to prefer  “Influencer
Marketing.”
Social media influencers are a third party involved in the relationship between
consumers and brands. They use blogs, tweets, and other social media platforms to
influence consumer attitudes and brand preferences, even though they have no direct
connection to the brand itself. In this form of marketing, which has entered marketing

effective in spreading content related to the brand like a virus on social media. Based on
this point, the conclusions reached in the study and the issues that should be considered
in the sector going forward can be listed as follows:
⚫ The purpose of content tailored to the target audience should be defined, and how it
will be implemented, where and when it will be done, and which measurement
criteria will be used to monitor whether the goal has been achieved should be
determined.
⚫ Content management should be initiated in order to be able to present original
content, to succeed in being different from all the content presented by competitors,
to be strategic while doing so, and at the same time to be able to empathize with the
target audience.
⚫ It is important to determine the measurement criteria that will monitor whether the
created content has reached the intended point.
⚫ The way to gain a more in-depth view of brand communication is to understand the
message strategies used by influencers.
⚫ Propering content management, presenting original content, making this content
interesting, and convincing customers to spend time in these environments are
important elements.
⚫ Being transparent to connect with customers on social media.
⚫ It is necessary to approach negative content with empathy and be able to respond
positively to it.
⚫ It is extremely important to remember that visual and video content makes a
difference, to listen to customers, and to respond to them in a timely manner. It is
necessary to be able to speak the language of social media and to be prepared for
complaints and questions.
