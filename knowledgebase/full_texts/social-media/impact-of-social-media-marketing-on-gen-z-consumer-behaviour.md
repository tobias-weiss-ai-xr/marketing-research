ABSTRACT
Social media has become an important part of everyday life and has changed the way young consumers interact with businesses and brands. For Generation Z,
social networking platforms are not limited to communication and entertainment. They are also used to discover products, read reviews, follow influencers,
compare alternatives and make purchase decisions. This changing pattern of consumer behaviour has created new opportunities as well as challenges for
marketers.
The present study examines the role of social media marketing in influencing the consumer behaviour of Generation Z. Particular attention is given to social
media engagement,content quality, influencercredibility, electronicword-of-mouth(eWOM),promotionaloffersandinteractionwith brands.The studyadopts a
quantitative research approach and proposes the use of a structured questionnaire among Gen Z consumers. Descriptive statistics, reliability analysis, correlation
andmultipleregressioncanbeusedtoexamine therelationshipbetweensocialmediamarketingactivitiesandpurchaseintention.
The study is based on the view that social media can influence different stages of the consumer decision-making process, beginning with product awareness and
information search and extending to brand evaluation and purchase intention. Previous research suggests that factors such as authenticity, useful content, online
reviews,influencercredibilityandconsumerengagement canshape thewayGen Zresponds tosocialmedia marketing. Thestudytherefore attemptstoprovide a
betterunderstandingofhowbusinessescancommunicatewithGenZconsumersinanincreasinglydigitalmarketplace.

1.Introduction
The growth of the internet and social networking platforms has changed the relationship between businesses and consumers. In the past, marketing
communication was largely controlled by businesses through newspapers, television, radio and other traditional media. Consumers generally received
information from these sources and had limited opportunities to respond publicly. Social media has changed this situation considerably. Today,
consumerscancommentonadvertisements,sharetheirexperiences,reviewproducts,recommendbrandsandcommunicatedirectlywithcompanies.
This change is particularly significant among Generation Z. Young consumers have grown up in an environment where smartphones, internet
connectivityand social networking platforms are part of everyday life. Social media therefore plays a role not only intheir social interactions but also
inthewaytheysearchforinformationandevaluateproducts.
A consumer may see a product on Instagram, watch a review on YouTube, check comments from other users, look at an influencer's experience and
thencomparepricesbeforedecidingwhethertomakeapurchase.In thissense,theconsumerjourneyisnolongerasimplemovementfromadvertising
topurchase.Itinvolvesseveraldigitalinteractionsalongtheway.
Social media marketing refers to the use of social networking platforms to communicate with consumers, promote products, build brand relationships
and encourage consumer engagement. Its effectiveness, however, depends on more than simply posting advertisements. Consumers may respond
differentlydependingonthequalityofthecontent,thecredibilityofthepersonpromotingtheproduct,theopinionsofotherconsumersandthelevelof
interactionofferedbythebrand.
Indiaprovides aninterestingsettingfor studyingthese developmentsbecauseof therapidexpansion ofdigitalandsocial-mediause. At thesametime,
thegrowingnumberofmarketingmessagesonsocialplatformshasmadeconsumersmoreselectiveabouttheinformationtheytrust.
For Gen Z consumers, factors such as authenticity, relevance and peer opinion may therefore be as important as promotional messages. This study
focusesontheseaspectsandexamineshowsocialmediamarketinginfluencestheconsumerbehaviourofGenZ.

The changing behaviour of Gen Z demonstrates that social media is more than another advertising channel. It has become part of the consumer
decision-makingenvironment.
A young consumer may discover a product through a short video, visit the brand's profile, read comments, watch an independent review and then
comparetheproductwithalternatives.Thedecisionisthereforeinfluencedbyacombinationofbrand-createdandconsumer-createdinformation.
ThisisparticularlyimportantbecauseGen Zconsumers aresimultaneouslyconsumersandcontentcreators.Afterpurchasingaproduct,theymaypost
their own review, share photographs, create videos or recommend the product to friends. Their experiences can then become information for other
potentialconsumers.
