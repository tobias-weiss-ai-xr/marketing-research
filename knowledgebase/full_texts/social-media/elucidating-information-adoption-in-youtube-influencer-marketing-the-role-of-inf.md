Abstract
Consumers are increasingly gravitating toward social media influencers as credible information sources for
products and services, owing to the authenticity, domain knowledge, and relatability inherent in their
experiential narratives. In Malawi, beauty inf luencers on YouTube significantly influence consumers' buying
choices, especially in the fashion, fitness, beauty, and food industries. This research analyzes  the impact of
influencer characteristics on perceived information credibility and investigates how this credibility influences
information adoption and buying intentions among consumer in Malawi's cosmetics sector. Data were
gathered using an online surve y that utilized purposive and snowball sampling methods, yielding 358 valid
responses for analysis. The proposed relationships were evaluated through Partial Least Squares Structural
Equation Modelling (PLS -SEM). The results show that the characteristics o f influencers greatly improve the
perceived trustworthiness of information created by them. Moreover, the credibility of information has a
positive impact on both the adoption of information and intentions to make a purchase, while the adoption of
information significantly enhances purchase intentions. These results emphasize the key importance of
information credibility in converting influencer traits into consumers' behaviours regarding information
adoption and their purchasing choices. The research provi des significant theoretical and managerial insights
for marketers and cosmetic brands looking to improve the efficacy of influencer marketing techniques and
strengthen consumer engagement via trustworthy social media endorsements.
Keywords: YouTube, information credibility, purchase intention, influencer attributes.

overwhelming amount of information regarding the products and services present in the market
Andoy, et al., (2023). With the growth of e-commerce, it has transformed consumer purchasing habits,
substituting numerous conventional face -to-face transactions with online platforms (Bakar et al.,

 Related works

2.1 User-Generated Content as a Form of Electronic Word of Mouth

User-created content is identical to digital word of mouth, operating just like conventional word of
mouth (De Costa & Abd Aziz, 2021). but the information disseminates via social media platforms like
Facebook, Twitter, YouTube, and Instagram. Traditional media, like television and magazines, is
increasingly being supplanted by social media, which has emerged as a crucial platform for marketers
to advertise brands and products. Individuals generate and upload multimedia content, sharing their
opinions on br ands and products, which can then be distributed to others (Sokolova & Kefi, 2020).
User-generated content allows online users to articulate their thoughts, opinions, and feedback
regarding the products and services they have utilized or experienced (Intansari & Roostika, 2022).
User-created content signifies an array of digital information resources that can be produced,
started, and disseminated for consumers to access (De Costa & Abd Aziz, 2021). Content created by
users can take various forms, and it often includes images, vid eos, reviews, or testimonials shared on
social media. Utilizing user -generated content stands out as one of the most powerful and delicate
digital marketing techniques in use today, given that the information disseminates rapidly, broadly,
and successfully (Al-Rawabdeh et al., 2021). Social media marketing relates to the use of content created
by users (Sokolova & Kefi, 2020). The majority of social media users are the consumers often possess a
substantial follower count and post original content related to subjects like beauty, fashion, food, and
fitness (Bakar, Fauziyah  & Rahmat, 2025). Such social media users are generally referred to as
influencers, and they gain fame by effectively establishing themselves as authorities on particular
subjects within social media channels (Andoy et al., 2023).

2.2 Purchase Intention

It is widely recognized that purchase intention serves as an indicator of buying behavior (Chakraborty,
2019). Intent to buy has demonstrated its significance as a crucial element in primary market research,
as it can underpin the buying behaviour of consumers (Bakar, Fauziyah  & Rahmat, 2025). Purchase
intention denotes a consumer's intentional strategy to obtain a product, influenced by a personal
decision-making approach that includes both evaluative and normative considerations (Visentin et al.,
2019). The goal is to examine  the fundamental influences that impact this behaviour to comprehend
how these elements interact and influence individual reactions (Shaari et al., 2022).
When users seek more details about a product, they will consult the virtual community for help.
The likelihood that online users will buy products from a specific brand due to online reviews is referred
to as the purchase intention of online users (Hsu, 20 21). The attitudes of consumers toward a product
can also be taken into account when assessing their likelihood of making a purchase (Sun, 2021).
For example, buyers will examine the reviews and feedback of other users prior to making a
purchase decision. When the information's content is pertinent to the consumer, it positively
influences their intention to buy (Weismueller et al., 2020). Marketers  are paying more attention to
social networking platforms as a way to leverage the chance to affect consumers' purchase intentions
(Weismueller et al., 2020). Customers may exhibit varying views on whether the perspectives shared
on networking platforms might help them make improved buying choices (Jiang et al., 2021).

2.3 Hypotheses development and conceptual framework

2.4 Authenticity

Authenticity is a crucial trait of influencers that affects how consumers receive information in YouTube
influencer marketing (AlFarraj , et al., 2021). Influencers are frequently viewed as genuine when they
showcase real-life experiences, such as videos demonstrating their use or testing of the products they
endorse AlFarraj et al., (2021). This type of content fosters emotional bonds with followers, enhancing
audience receptiveness to their suggestions and boosting the chances of product adoption (Ki et al.,
2020). Genuineness also enhances trust and allegiance between influencers and their followers, which
can improve brand performance and purchasing outcomes (AlFarraj et al., 2021).
When influencers showcase products in familiar, daily situations, consumers are more inclined
to see their messages as authentic and trustworthy. As purchase decisions frequently rely on online
information from credible sources, the perceived authenticity significantly influences behavioural
intentions (Yuan & Lou, 2020). In general, influencers who show honesty, transparency, and
impartiality are seen as more genuine and trustworthy, enhancing consumers’ readiness to accept and
follow their suggestions (Ismagilova et al., 2020). Thus, this study hypothesised that:

H1: Information authenticity has a influence impact on information credibility.

2.5 Similarity

Similarity signifies the extent to which consumers see an influencer as having traits like values,
interests, lifestyle, demographics, or experiences in common with them (Andoy et al., 2023). Based on
the homophily principle, people tend to trust and be swayed by those they see as resembling themselves
(Intansari & Roostika , 2022). In YouTube influencer marketing, perceived likeness decreases
psychological distance between influencers and their audience, increasing the credibility and
persuasive impact of the content presented (Wiedmann & Von Mettenheim, 2020). Previous rese arch
has consistently shown the significance of similarity in influencing consumers' reactions to influencer
content.
For example, AlFarraj et al., (2021) discovered that the perceived similarity between influencers
and their followers greatly boosted trust and credibility perceptions, leading to an increase in
consumers' intentions to purchase. In the same vein, Hsu, (2021) found that consum ers who viewed
influencers as resembling themselves were more inclined to trust and adopt their suggestions. In the
beauty sector, Intansari and Roostika , (2022) found that similarity had a positive effect on trust in
influencers and the buying intentions of consumers. These results indicate that consumers tend to view
information from comparable influencers as trustworthy and deserving of acceptance. In t he context
of YouTube cosmetics, women who see beauty influencers as having comparable traits are likely to
view the information given as more trustworthy (Andoy et al., 2023). Thus, the research proposes that:

H2: Similarity positively influences the perceived credibility of influencer-generated information

2.6 Informational usefulness

Informational usefulness denotes the degree to which online material is viewed as pertinent, correct,
and beneficial for consumers' choices (Leong, Loi & Woon, 2021). In YouTube influencer marketing,
consumers are relying more on online reviews and content  created by influencers for product
information, since the internet offers quick and easy access to extensive content (Astarini  & Sumardi,
2022).  The general quality of this type of information is typically evaluated based on its practicality
and capacity to satisfy users' informational requirements (Jiang et al., 2021). Social media sites allow
users to exchange experiences and product assessments, especially in comment sections and influencer

2.7 Likeability

Likeability is a key influencer characteristic that impacts consumers' acceptance of information and
intentions to buy in YouTube influencer marketing (Ismagilova  et al., 2020). Consumers are more
inclined to embrace product suggestions from influencers they respect or consider attractive (Leong,
Loi & Woon, 2021). Likeability is frequently associated with perceived attractiveness, which
encompasses physical looks and general presentation style (Balabanis & Chatzopoulou, 2019). In beauty
and lifestyle content, influencers with physical attractiveness often have greater persuasive power,
particularly when showcasing products in relatable everyday situations (Pinda et al., 2021).
 Skincare influencers demonstrating visibly healthy skin are frequently viewed as more
trustworthy, raising the chances that their followers will believe and embrace their advice (Wiedmann
& Von Mettenheim, 2020).
Accordingly, relatable influencers typically establish greater trust and interaction with their
followers, which boosts the reliability of their communications and raises the chances of buying
behaviour (Lou & Yuan, 2019). Previous research also indicates that the appeal and likability of
influencers greatly impact the perceived credibility of information in social media contexts (Sokolova
& Kefi, 2020). Thus, this study hypothesised that:

H4: Likeability has a significant and positive impact on information credibility.

2.8 Knowledgeability

Knowledgeability pertains to how much YouTube influencers are viewed as knowledgeable, adept, and
capable in a particular product area (Astarini & Sumardi, 2022).  Consumers evaluate this by observing
influencers’ shown comprehension of products, which includes their capacity to clarify features,
advantages, and drawbacks derived from real usage (Schouten et al., 2020). Influencers who regularly
share knowledgeable, experience-driven content tend to be seen as trustworthy sources of information
(Chih et al., 2020).
Consumers commonly depend more on informed influencers, especially when they have no
previous experience with a product or service (Leong, Loi & Woon, 2021). In these situations, perceived
expertise serves as a crucial factor in establishing trust and adop ting information, which ultimately
affects purchasing intentions (Ismagilova et al., 2020). When influencers display evident knowledge of
products through thorough demonstrations, evaluations, and comparisons followers are more inclined
to trust their suggestions and contemplate buying the recommended items (Pinda et al., 2021).
Accordingly, expertise enhances perceived trustworthiness as information from experts is seen as more

2.9 Information Adoption

Information adoption signifies how much individuals embrace and plan to utilize information acquired
from online sources when making decisions (Luo et al., 2019). In contemporary digital environments,
particularly social media, consumers often depend on us er-generated content like reviews and
influencer suggestions when deciding on purchases (Chih et al., 2020). The voluntary acceptance of
this information relies on users' perceptions of its usefulness, credibility, and relevance (Nik Mud et
al., 2020).
The procedure includes internal acknowledgment, in which information is initially assessed and
subsequently incorporated into the person's consciousness and decision-making (Astarini & Sumardi,
2022). Consumers tend to adopt information when they view it a s credible and significant, which
facilitates improved decision-making by selecting and utilizing beneficial content (Leong, Loi & Woon,
2021).
In online forums, individuals frequently look for direction based on others' experiences,
particularly when assessing products and services (Pinda et al., 202). The acceptance of information is
crucial in influencing consumer mindset and buying intentions, as choices are significantly affected by
how social media information is embraced and utilized (Daowd et al., 2020). Thus, this study
hypothesised that:

H6: Information adoption has a significant and positive impact on purchase intention.
H7: Information adoption mediates the relationship between information credibility and purchase
intention.

 Methodology

The target population included online shoppers who have viewed beauty and fashion products
reviewed by beauty influencers on YouTube. Individuals on social media tend to retain advertisement
information better and engage with the content more deeply (Tor -Kadioglu & Bozyigit, 2025.) The
cosmetics and fashion items in Malawi have consistently experienced promising growth, attributed to
a rise in smartphone users and heightened self-awareness (Nik Mud et al., 2020).
Earlier research has shown that videos produced by beauty and fashion vloggers and shared on
YouTube represent a novel trend that has become increasingly popular among smartphone users
(Afifah, 2019). Consequently, the participants selected for this research were online shoppers in Malawi
who had utilized YouTube as an informational resource for cosmetic and fashion items. This choice
was made because cosmetic and fashion items typically utilize social media more and are therefore
more inclined to impact their behaviour.

3.1 Research design, data collection

This research employed a mix of purposive sampling methods. Earlier research has mainly utilized the
purposive sampling technique (AlFarraj et al., 2021; Andoy et al., 2022; Astarini & Sumardi, 2022;
Roostika, 2022), whereas Daowd  et al. (2020) utilized a combination of purposive and snowball
sampling methods. The exact count of women who have viewed beauty influencers' makeup product
review videos on YouTube is still unclear. Beauty influencers on YouTube review cosmetic products,
often focusing on a particular niche audience. For purposive sampling, screening questions were
included to guarantee the data came from the appropriate target respondents.
The inquiry is: "How regularly do you view product review videos made by beauty or fashion
influencers on YouTube?" If a respondent replied “No” or “Never” to the question, the online
questionnaire was disregarded. The researchers could not influence the individuals who opted to take
part in this study since the data was gathered online. The questionnaire was created using Google
Forms, and links to it were distributed on Facebook, WhatsApp, and Telegram. While recruitment was

based on a population of 4.5 million users of YouTube in Malawi. To establish the suitable sample size,
as indicated by Zeithaml et al. (2020), the research employed the G*Power sample size calculator,
frequently utilized in social science studies for preparing survey-oriented research.
 Using G*Power (Faul et al., 2007), a widely recognized power analysis tool, the minimum sample
size was calculated to ensure adequate statistical power and accuracy. The package reduces the
likelihood of underpowered or inflated studies by calculating sample sizes based on effect sizes, desired
power, and significance levels.

 Discussion

Interestingly, against expectations, authenticity showed no substantial impact on information
credibility; therefore, the hypothesis was not validated. This implies that consumers might not view
information as trustworthy just because an influencer seems g enuine. Within the realm of cosmetic
products, consumers might prioritize the quality, knowledge, and practicality of information over its
perceived authenticity. This discovery opposes earlier research by Saima and Khan (2020), which
indicated that influencer authenticity greatly improved credibility. In a similar vein, Chih et al. (2020)
discovered that trustworthiness had a positive effect on the credibility of information, whereas
Ismagilova et al. (2020) indicated that information conveyed by trustworthy and sincere sources was
regarded as more credible.
The inconsistency might be due to contextual variations, cultural influences, or growing
consumer doubt regarding sponsored influencer material. The results indicated that similarity has a
significant positive effect on information credibility, implying th at consumers view information as
more credible when they relate to or see themselves as similar to the influencer. Common values, ways
of life, and preferences can enhance trust and reinforce perceptions of credibility. This result aligns

5.1 Theoretical contribution

This research enhances the fields of digital marketing and influencer studies by merging well -
established theories with empirical results to elucidate how influencer characteristics influence
consumer behaviour regarding YouTube beauty influencers. Based o n the Source Credibility Model

message persuasiveness.
 The findings can also be understood via the Elaboration Likelihood Model (ELM) (1981), where
traits of influencers serve as peripheral signals impacting perceptions of credibility, especially in low -
involvement scenarios like cosmetic use. Moreover, Signalling Theory illustrates how expertise and the
quality of information act as signals that diminish uncertainty and information imbalance,
consequently enhancing credibility perceptions in online settings. In agreement with previous studies,
expertise was shown to greatly improve credibility, in line with Andoy et al. (2022) and Ismagilova et
al. (2020), who highlight the significance of informed sources in lessening uncertainty.
Likewise, likeability had a positive effect on credibility, corroborating the results of Pinda et al.
(2021), Astarini and Sumardi (2022), and Sokolova and Kefi (2020), while reaffirming ELM's claim that
attractive and appealing sources boost persuasion. T he important role of similarity corresponds with

This research provides significant practical value by converting recognized theoretical ideas into
practical strategies for marketers, influencers, and companies. It highlights essential factors influencing

These results align with earlier studies indicating that trustworthy and relatable influencers

 Limitation and direction for future Research studies

Although this research enhances our comprehension of information adoption within YouTube
influencer marketing, it faces multiple limitations. Initially, the research utilized cross -sectional data,
which reflects participants' views at one specific moment and restricts the capacity to determine causal
connections between influencer traits, credibility of information, acceptance of information, and
purchasing intentions. Secondly, the research concentrated solely on YouTube influencers, which
restricts the ap plicability of the results to other platforms like Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, or X,
where the dynamics of influencer marketing might vary. Third, while crucial influencer-related aspects
were analysed, additional possibly significant variables like consu mer trust, engagement rates,
parasocial connections, type of product, and cultural factors were excluded from the model. Ultimately,
the traits of the sample and its geographical context may limit the generalizability of the results to
wider populations and varying market settings.
Future research should utilize longitudinal or experimental design methods to more accurately
determine causal links and track shifts in consumer behaviour over time. Researchers might also
enhance the model by integrating extra variables, including trust,  consumer engagement, perceived
value, brand attitude, and parasocial interaction, to offer a more thorough insight into information
adoption in influencer marketing. Comparative analyses across various social media platforms like
Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook would assist in identifying if the identified relationships are specific
to particular platforms. Additionally, subsequent studies ought to explore the moderating impacts of
demographic variables, cultural variations, product types, and types of inf luencers to improve the
applicability of the results.

 Conclusion

This study explored how the traits of influencers impact the credibility of information, the acceptance
of information, and purchasing intentions in YouTube influencer marketing. The results indicate that
similarity, likeability, the usefulness of information, and knowledgeability greatly improve information
credibility, with similarity and likeability being the most powerful predictors. Conversely, authenticity
had little impact on credibility, indicating that consumers might prioritize expertise, relevanc e, and
content quality more than on perceived honesty. The research adds to both theory and practice by
showing that consumer assessments of influencer content are more and more influenced by
knowledge, utility, and relatability, underscoring the changing dynamics of credibility evaluation in
influencer marketing.
