Abstract
The rapid growth of artificial intelligence (AI) in social commerce has resulted in shifts when it comes to
consumer purchasing behaviour. Along with allowing for personalized recommendations, intelligent
product recommendations and an ability to talk to customers through chatbots the introduction of AI
technologies has reached up to a new level leading to better customer engagement, trust in the brand and
social interaction. The present research is aimed an understanding the impact of AI-powered social
commerce on consumer purchasing habits by focusing on the mediating roles of social presence and trust
in Bangalore.
Its specific objectives include determining consumer preferences regarding apparel and cosmetics on
social commerce websites, assessing the influence of AI technologies on social presence and trust, and
examining how they affect purchase intention. The further objective is to study the influence of online
reviews, influencer marketers’ communication and consumer engagement on the purchase behaviour of
customers online.
I did a study where I gave a questionnaire to people who buy clothes and makeup using media in
Bangalore. I looked at the answers to see how people feel about buying things on media when companies
use artificial intelligence. I wanted to know if artificial intelligence helps companies seem friendly and
trustworthy and if that makes people want to buy more things.
The study shows that when companies use intelligence on social media it helps them seem more friendly
and that makes people trust them more. When people trust a company they are more likely to buy things
from them. Artificial intelligence also helps companies show people things they really like and that makes
people happy and more likely to keep buying from them. This study helps us understand how new
technology affects what people buy on media.

Introduction
The quick growth of technology and the easy access to the internet along with the use of smartphones has
changed how people shop. Shopping in stores is now like a support for online shopping because of the
convenience the fast service and the wide range of choices. Also the involvement of media in shopping has
led to social commerce using sites like Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp, Pinterest and Snapchat.
Unlike online shopping social commerce focuses on interaction, communication and connection, between
people who buy and people who sell through reviews, ratings, messages and other ways of talking and
sharing.
Among  various  product  categories,  apparel  and  cosmetic  products  are  very  appropriate  for  social
commerce since they have a high dependence on trends, visual content and recommendations from
customers. The role of social presence is very significant in influencing consumer behaviour since
comments, likes, shares, reviews and feedback enable social connection and make people feel more
comfortable shopping online. Trust is a very important factor since consumers cannot see and touch
products that they will buy ahead of time. It helps ease their worries and concerns regarding the quality of
products, authenticity, safety of payment and delivery reliability. All of these factors, as well as social
interaction, engagement of customers on the internet, online reviews and recommendation made by an
influencer affect consumers’ intention to buy the product.
Bangalore is a suitable area to research social commerce because of the advancement of technology that
makes it more appealing to consumers. Social media assists people, particularly young ones, in finding
clothes and makeup to wear and following trends in the fashion industry. As such, this research investigates
social presence, trust and purchase intention with regard to social commerce and online selling of clothing
and cosmetics. The goal is to find out how social interaction via social media can create trust and impact
consumer behaviour. The results obtained can be used to attract consumers and create the trust necessary
for sales.
Research Objective
1. To identify consumers preferences and behaviour towards purchase of apparel and cosmetic products
through social commerce platforms in Bangalore.
2. To assess the relationship between social presence and trust among consumers using social commerce

Review of Literature
1. Lu, Fan and Zhou (2016) looked at how social presence, trust and the desire to buyer connected. They
said that social presence that comes from using technologies makes up for the lack of contact with other
people that is found in traditional e-commerce. The study found that different types of presence
increased peoples trust in sellers, which affected how they shopped online. The study offers reasons for
future research on how social presence and trust are related in social commerce.
2. Ahmad and Laroche (2017)  looked at how electronic word-of-mouth's an important part of social
commerce by looking at different reviews. They found that online reviews help customers by giving
them feelings and information when they need to make a decision. Positive reviews were seen as
helpful because they had details about how a product works, its technical features and how it looks,
while negative reviews were seen as important because they showed problems with the service. The
study is important for the research done in this project because looking at the reviews shows that
customer choices and their actions are affected by the reviews especially when it comes to commerce.
3. Cheng, Gu and Shen (2019) looked into how trust is formed in the context of social commerce by
differentiating  between  particularized  trust  of  individuals  and  system  trust  in  social  commerce
platforms. The study relied on the answers from 614 social commerce users and came to the conclusion
that  trust  disposition,  quality-attributed  information  shared  with  members,  knowledge  of  other
members and their recommendations altogether help to form the feeling of particularized trust. The
research also suggested that trust could be passed from members to social commerce system. This
means that the existence of reliable  information, knowledge about the community and positive
recommendations given by consumers can help build trust in social commerce.
4. Zhou (2019) investigated how social interaction influences users’ behaviour in social commerce. The
study  analysed  human-to-human  and  human-to-computer  interactions,  including  informational
interaction,  emotional  interaction,  experience  of  control,  responsiveness  and  personalisation  of
interaction. The findings showed that social interaction has an impact on trust in other members and
trust in the community, hence having a positive effect on intention to participate in social commerce.
The study recommends businesses to create opportunities for real social interaction in order to help
consumers gain more trust in one another and develop.
5. Yin, Wang, Xia and Gu 2019 about how people talk to each other when they buy things on social
commerce websites. They wanted to know how it affects what people buy. The study looked at people
in China and France who had bought things before. What they found out is that talking to people is a big
part of what makes people want to buy things. This study is helpful for what we're doing now because
we need to know about talking to people trusting them and feeling safe when we buy things on social
commerce websites.

commerce websites. They asked 206 people who sell things and people who buy things what they

Conclusion
The research shows that social commerce has changed the way people look for and buy clothes and
makeup. People can use media to find out about products and get opinions from others. They can also talk
to stores and famous people who promote products. The research says that trust is very important when
people decide to buy something. When people get information and have nice interactions with stores and
other customers, they are more likely to buy things on the internet. So companies should try to make people
trust them talk to their customers and be honest, about what people think of their products. They should
also let people communicate with them easily.
Limitation of the Study
1. The limitations of this study included that it only focused on Bangalore and cannot be applied to
consumers in other areas.
2. The data for this study was based on 200 respondents, which may not be sufficient to represent this type
of population.
3. The use of convenience sampling influenced the ability to generalise the results.
4. The research was solely done on apparel and cosmetics products, thus presenting data only on these
two categories.
5. The research depended on the self-response of participant questionnaires and thus reflects the opinions
and previous experiences of the respondents.
6. Social commerce is in a constant state of change because of new technologies and consumer trends,
resulting in the possibility of changes and updates in findings.
