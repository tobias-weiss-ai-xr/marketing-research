Abstract:
The development of information technology has changed many aspects of life, including education.
One significant impact is the use of social media and websites as the primary means of marketing
various educational programs. Although MTS N 2 Pangandaran alr eady has various accounts on
various social media platforms such as Facebook with the account name "MTsN 2 Pangandaran" with
1248 followers, Instagram with the account name "mtsn2pangandaran" with 1749 followers, TikTok
with the account name "mtsn2pangandaran" with 673 followers and the website
"mtsn2pangandaran.sch.id", its implementation in social media -based marketing strategies is still
considered less than optimal. This is evidenced by the decline in the number of new students from 125
students in 2022, 118 students in 2023, and 110 students in 2024. The problem formulation in this thesis
is how social media and website-based marketing strategies can increase the interest of new students
at MTS N 2 Pangandaran. The research conducted at MTS N 2 Pangandaran aims to analyze how
social media and website-based marketing strategies can increase the interest of new students at MTS
N 2 Pangandaran. The type of research used is qualitative research. Data collection techniques used
include observation, interviews, and documentation. Meanwhile, to analyze the data obtained, The
author conducted the research by collecting all data, reducing data, presenting data, and verifying
data. The results of the study, which refer to Philip Kotler's 4P marketing mix theory, show that the
marketing strategy for educational products offered by MTsN 2 Pangandaran is considered adequate,
based on appearance, photos, videos, pamphlets, live and live streaming. The marketing financing
strategy implemented by MTs N 2 Pangandaran has not been well organized, as evidenced by the
absence of specific financing details in product marketing.
Keywords: Marketing Strategy, Education, Social Media

1. INTRODUCTION

The development of information technology has changed many aspects of life,
including in the field of education. One significant impact is the use of social media and
websites as the main means in marketing various education programs. MTs N 2
Pangandaran is one of the schools that uses social media as one of the means of education
marketing. As expressed by Prasetyo, who found that social media has proven effective in
increasing community awareness and the attractiveness of new students to educational
institutions (Prasetyo, 2020). In addition, Sihombing also revealed that the school website
plays an important role in providing complete information regarding the school profile,
programs offered, facilities, and education costs (Sihombing, D. A., Pertiwi, 2021). In this
regard, the website functio ns as the main portal that can assist prospective students and
parents in making decisions related to education choices.
Gunawan also revealed that although many schools use social media to promote their
programs, not all institutions use a directed strategy or have consistent and attractive
content for prospective students (Gunawan, A. I., Amalia, F., 2021). This leads to an inability
to communicate school advantages effectively, so that the attractiveness for prospective
students is not optimal. Many aspects need to be analyzed more deeply, such as the selection
of the right social media platform, the type of content published, and how often and effective
the communication conducted between the school party and prospective students and
parents through these digital platforms.
To resolve this problem, the researcher uses three main theories. First, the theory of
Marketing Management put forward by Philip Kotler, in which he states, "The major
marketing mix tools are classified into four broad groupc  called the four Ps of marketing:
product, price, place, and promotion, that the firm blends to produce the response it wants
in the target market" (Kotler, P. T., & Armstrong, G. M. (2019). The marketing mix is a series
of controllable and tactical marketing tools—product, price, place, and promotion—that are
blended by the company to produce the desired response in the target market. These
indicators can be used in supporting the education marketing process based on social media
and websites in increasing the interest of new students.
Second, the theory of Educational Marketing put forward by Kacung Wahyudi:
educational marketing, namely market identification, segmentation, positioning or
differentiation, marketing communication, and educational institution services. Social
media such a s Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok provide opportunities for schools to
introduce flagship programs, various student activities, and facilities available at MTs N 2
Pangandaran. The school website, on the other hand, can be used as an information center
that provides easy access for parents and prospective students to know everything about
MTs N 2 Pangandaran, from vision and mission, curriculum, to online registration.
However, based on initial observation, MTs N 2 Pangandaran has not yet fully utilized the
potential of social media and websites in marketing their education programs. This is
evident from the lack of variation and creativity in the posted content, minimal interaction
with the audience, the absence of online student admissions, and the lack of  structured
management in digital marketing. This causes many prospective students and parents to
lack the information needed or even not know about the existence of flagship programs
offered by the school.
Third, John Dewey's theory in Siregar states that interest is the tendency to pay more
attention to or be involved in an activity. Interest indicators include greater focus on sports
activities, emotional involvement, and the desire to learn more about religion. The objective
of this research is to analyze the marketing mix elements (product, price, place, promotion)

how this marketing strategy can increase the attractiveness of prospective new students.
This research is expected to provide useful insights for MTs N 2 Pangandaran, a s well as
other educational institutions, in formulating more effective social media and website-based
marketing strategies to attract new students in the future.

2. RESEARCH METHOD

The approach used in this research is a qualitative approach. The qualitative approach
is an approach that uses a descriptive, scientific description mechanism, arranged in an
orderly and systematic manner from data collection to interpreting and presenting research
report results (Ibrahim 2015). While at MTsN  2 Pangandaran, the researcher conducted
direct observation of promotional activities carried out by the school party through social
media such as Instagram, Facebook, and the school's official website.
During the data collection process, the researcher also observed various school digital
documents and archives, such as social media uploads, electronic brochures, and statistical
data on new student admissions from previous years. Through this qualitative  approach,
the data obtained are not only in the form of what is done by the school party, but also reveal
the reasons behind each strategy, challenges faced, and the perceptions of related parties
regarding the effectiveness of social media and website use.
The research object is the issue being researched, namely the lack of management in
using social media and websites as means of Education marketing that results in the decline
in the number of new students. Meanwhile, the research subject is the researcher's limitation
where the researcher determines objects or people who can answer the problem
formulation; in this research, the subjects are the Head of Madrasah, Vice Principal for
Curriculum, Education Marketing Management Staff, Parents, and several students at MTs
N 2 Pangandaran.
This research was conducted at MTs N 2 Pangandaran located in Cimerak Village,
Cimerak District, Pangandaran Regency. This research was conducted over three months,
starting from February 2025.

3. RESULTS
A. Product (Product Strategy)
MTsN 2 Pangandaran actively utilizes various forms of digital content to introduce
the school to the wider community through social media and the official website. The
uploaded contents consist of photo documentation, videos, pamphlets, and live streaming
displaying various student activities, both academic and non -academic. Among them are
extracurricular activities such as English Club, music arts, pencak silat, as well as sports
such as volleyball and football. Documentation videos of these activities not onl y display
students' abilities in creating and competing but also show the spirit of togetherness,
discipline, and character values built in the school environment.
B. Price (Financing)
The school financing policy at MTsN 2 Pangandaran is compiled with an efficiency
approach and utilization of internal resources so that it remains affordable, particularly
regarding social media and website-based marketing strategies. The school does not rely on
large budgets, but rather focuses more on optimizing educators and students who have
interests and abilities in the fields of design, photography, videography, and information

C. Place (Place/Distribution)
MTsN 2 Pangandaran uses several channels to convey information related to new
student admissions, both digitally and directly. Digitally, the school utilizes official social
media such as Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok to disseminate information related to
registration time, requirements, quotas, and the advantages of the education programs
offered. In addition, information is also published through the school's official website in
the form of digital banners, news articles, and announcements that can be acces sed by the
community at any time.
Based on interview results with Mr. Okib, according to him, Facebook, as a platform
with a wider age reach, becomes the right means to convey formal and detailed information,
such as registration schedules, madrasah advantages, and testimonials from alumni.
Content placement on Facebook can be f ocused on the official page feature, educational
community groups. Instagram, on the other hand, is more effective for attracting visual
attention by displaying student activities, madrasah facilities, and academic and non -
academic achievements through att ractively packaged photos and videos. The use of
Instagram Story and Reels features enables rapid and wide dissemination of information,
while simultaneously building emotional closeness with prospective students. TikTok
provides creative space to build a positive madrasah image through short content that is
entertaining yet informative. Content placement on TikTok enables promotional messages
to be packaged in the form of popular trends, challenges, and easily shareable educational
content, so that the dis tribution reach can expand organically. The madrasah's official
website remains the main information center that is formal and comprehensive.
D. Promotion (Promotion Strategy)
The promotion strategy used by MTsN 2 Pangandaran to attract the interest of
prospective students is carried out in various ways that adapt to environmental conditions
and current technological developments. One of the main strategies is utilizing the
momentum of student activities as promotional material. Every extracurricular activity,

4. DISCUSSION
A. Product
According to Philip Kotler & Gary Armstrong, a product is defined as anything
offered to the market to satisfy consumer needs and wants (Kotler, P. T., & Armstrong, G.
M. 2019). Philip Kotler & Keller define a product broadly, encompassing physical goods,
services, experiences, events, people, places, properties, organizations, information, and
ideas (Kotler, P., Keller, K. L. 2016). William J. Stanton in Mohammad: A product is a set of
attributes that have form or not, including packaging, price, color, and services that buyers
can accept as the fulfillment of wants and needs (Mohamad Gita Indrawan, 2023). MTs
Negeri 2 Pangandaran conducts product creation in its education marketing strategy,
among others, posted on social media and websites, such as photos of extracurricular
activities, school facilities, to classroom atmospheres, creating a visual representation of the
learning experience. This attracts the attention of students and parents because it displays
emotional and informational appeal.
There are also products in the form of short videos (reels) that display the dynamics
of school life in a short duration, such as OSIS activities, extracurriculars, achievement
accomplishments, as well as annual activity highlights. Video content is able t o arouse
emotions, curiosity, and emotional closeness so that it is very effective in attracting the
interest of prospective new students. According to Irpan  Ilmi (2024), education marketing
in the digital era is no longer merely conveying information, but must be able to present
authentic visual experiences, build trust, and strengthen the positive image of educational
institutions in the public mind. In addi tion, there are also products in the form of digital
pamphlets used to convey various information, such as New Student Admission (PPDB)
announcements, selection results, and school agendas. This digital information -based
service makes it easier for the com munity to obtain information quickly, easily, and
efficiently.
According to Fitriani and Rahmawati, photos and videos of learning activities
published on social media platforms are able to provide a real visual picture of the learning
atmosphere and student activities, so that they can increase the trust of prospective students
and parents in choosing educational institutions (Fitriani, N., & Rahmawati, L. 2020). This
becomes very relevant when seeing how MTs N 2 Pangandaran has begun to utilize social

B. Price
Price, according to Philip Kotler, is "Price is the amount of money charged for a
product or service, or the sum of the values that consumers exchange for the benefits of
having or using the product or service" (Kotler, P., & Keller, K. L. 2016). Price acc ording to
Kotler is not merely a nominal amount of money, but reflects the exchange value that
consumers pay for the benefits or satisfaction from the product/service. Price becomes an
indicator of consumer perception of the quality and value of a product.
In the context of social media and website -based education marketing strategy at
MTs Negeri 2 Pangandaran, the price element referred to is not merely in the sense of costs
paid by students or parents, but rather refers to internal financing expended by th e school

design software, and other supporting devices needed to produce attractiv e and
professional promotional material on social media and the school's official website. In
addition, price also includes salaries or honorariums for digital media management staff,
including the team responsible for content planning, social media accoun t management,
article writing on the website, to marketing campaign performance analysis. This workforce
plays an important role in forming a positive school image in digital space, so that their
presence needs to be calculated as part of the investment in marketing strategy.

account. Because design settings are done by respective branch teachers; for example, when
there is a tahfidz activity, documentation is done by the accompanying tahfidz teacher.
When there are activities commemorating major Islamic days, they are done by students
assigned to the documentation section, followed by editing also done by students.
MTs N 2 Pangandaran is beginning to understand that the success of digital

However, MTs Negeri 2 Pangandaran has not yet provided specific incentives for social
media managers but still combines them with teacher honorariums.

According to Fandy Tjiptono, "Promotion is a form of marketing communication that
attempts to disseminate information, influence or persuade, and remind the target market
about the company and its products so that they are willing to accept, buy, and be lo yal to
the products offered by the company" (Tjiptono, F. 2020). Tjiptono emphasizes that
promotion is not only about conveying information, but also attempts to influence and

5. CONCLUSION

Based on the research results, it can be concluded that the decline in the number of
new students at MTsN 2 Pangandaran over the last three years is influenced by the not yet
optimal education marketing strategy, particularly in the utilization of social media. Viewed
from Philip Kotler's marketing mix (4P), the product aspect has been well managed through
the presentation of photos, videos, pamphlets, live broadcasts (live and live streaming),
while the price aspect has not yet been supported by structured marketing budget planning.
In the place aspect, social media utilization has been adapted to target char acteristics,
namely Facebook to reach parents, and Instagram and TikTok to attract the interest of
prospective students through visual content. However, the promotion aspect remains the
main weakness because of less varied content, inconsistent upload sche dules, and low
interaction with prospective students and parents. In addition, based on Fandy Tjiptono's
education marketing perspective, the marketing communication strategy has not yet been
supported by clear market segmentation, strong institutional positioning, and digital
marketing optimization. Therefore, MTsN 2 Pangandaran needs to develop a more planned,
integrated, and target-needs-oriented social media marketing strategy in order to be able to
improve competitiveness and the number of new students sustainably.
