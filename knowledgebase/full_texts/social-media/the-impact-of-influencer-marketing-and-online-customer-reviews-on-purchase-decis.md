ABSTRACT
The rapid growth of social media has changed consumer behavior in searching for information and making purchasing decisions. TikTok has
become one of the most popular platforms for product promotion through influencer marketing and online customer reviews. This study aims to
analyze the influence of Influencer Marketing and Online Customer Reviews on the Purchase Decisions of Facetology Sunscreen products
among TikTok users. This research employs a quantitative approach using a survey method. Data are collected through questionnaires
distributed to TikTok users who are familiar with Facetology Sunscreen products. The data are analyzed using multiple linear regression to
examine the partial and simultaneous effects of the independent variables on purchase decisions. The study is expected to provide empirical
evidence regarding the role of influencer marketing and online customer reviews in shaping consumer purchase decisions. The findings are
expected to contribute to the development of digital marketing strategies, particularly for skincare brands that utilize social media platforms to
increase consumer engagement and purchasing behavior.

Keywords: Influencer Marketing, Online Customer Review, Purchase Decision, TikTok, Facetology Sunscreen.

INTRODUCTION
The beauty industry in Indonesia has experienced significant growth, particularly in the
skincare sector, driven by changing consumer trends and increasing awareness of skincare as part of
a modern lifestyle (Nawiyah et al., 2023). Among skincare products, sunscreen has gained considerable
attention due to its role in protecting the skin from ultraviolet (UV) radiation. In tropical countries
such as Indonesia, sunscreen products with SPF 30 or higher have become an essential part of daily
skincare routines (Sulistiyowati et al., 2022).
As shown in Figure 1, the sunscreen category recorded the highest growth among beauty and
personal care products, reaching 99% during 2023–2025. This growth reflects increasing consumer
awareness regarding skin protection and demonstrates the strong market potential of sunscreen
products in Indonesia.
The rapid development of information technology and social media has also transformed
consumer purchasing behavior. Social media platforms are no longer used solely for communication
and entertainment but have become important channels for product promotio n and online
transactions (Nur Zahra et al., 2023). One of the most influential platforms is TikTok, which
integrates entertainment content with e-commerce features through TikTok Shop, enabling users to
discover products and make purchases within a single  application (Rahmat et al., 2024). This
development has contributed to the rapid growth of social commerce in Indonesia (Deni et al., 2025).

According to Figure 2, Indonesia ranks first globally in the number of TikTok users, reaching
approximately 194.37 million users in July 2025. This indicates the platform's substantial influence on
consumer behavior and purchasing decisions. In the beauty industry, TikTok has become an effective
marketing platform due to its large user base and the strong influence of digital content creators on
consumer perceptions (Fatimah et al., 2025).
In social commerce, purchasing decisions are influenced not only by product attributes but
also by digital marketing factors, particularly influencer marketing and online customer reviews.
Influencers with expertise, attractiveness, and credibility can build positive perceptions, increase trust,
and encourage consumers to purchase products (Irrawati et al., 2025; Rumopa et al., 2026). Previous
studies have also found that influencer marketing significantly affects purchasing decisions on TikTok
(Yuliawan et al., 2025).
Similarly, online customer reviews serve as an important source of information that can reduce
perceived risk and increase consumer confidence before purchasing a product (Putri et al., 2025).
Reviews provide insights into product quality, advantages, disa dvantages, and user experiences,
thereby influencing purchase decisions (Andini & Wirsa, 2026). Positive and credible reviews have
been shown to strengthen trust and motivate purchasing behavior (Ananda et al., 2025). However,
previous studies have reported inconsistent findings regarding the influence of online customer
reviews on purchase decisions (Kamila & Pratita, 2026; Karinda & Ariandi, 2024; Zakiyah &
Hariyasih, 2023).
One sunscreen brand actively promoted through TikTok Shop is Facetology Sunscreen.
According to Figure 3, Facetology ranked third among the best -selling sunscreen brands in
Indonesia's e-commerce market during January–September 2025, with a market share of 6.7%. This
achievement reflects the effectiveness of its digital marketing strategy and the competitiveness of local
skincare brands.
To obtain preliminary insights, a pre-survey involving 30 TikTok users was conducted. The
results showed that although respondents generally had positive perceptions of Facetology Sunscreen,
many selected “moderately agree” and “disagree” responses regarding purchase decisions, influencer
marketing, and online customer reviews. These findings indicate that consumers still have doubts
about influencer credibility, online review reliability, and their purchasing decisions.
Based on these conditions and the inconsistent findings of previous studies, further research
is needed to examine the influence of Influencer Marketing and Online Customer Reviews on
Purchase Decisions for Facetology Sunscreen among TikTok users.

METHOD
This study employed a quantitative research approach with a survey design to examine the
influence of Influencer Marketing and Online Customer Reviews on the Purchase Decisions of
Facetology Sunscreen products among TikTok users. A quantitative approach was selected because
it enables the measurement and analysis of relationships among variables objectively through
statistical techniques.
The target population consisted of TikTok users who were familiar with Facetology Sunscreen
and had been exposed to promotional content, influencer endorsements, or customer reviews related
to the product. The sample was determined using a purposive sampli ng technique, in which
respondents were selected based on specific criteria, namely active TikTok users who had knowledge
of Facetology Sunscreen products. Data were collected through an online questionnaire distributed
using Google Forms.
The study involved two independent variables, namely Influencer Marketing (X₁) and Online
Customer Reviews (X₂), and one dependent variable, Purchase Decision (Y). Influencer Marketing
was measured through indicators of attractiveness, expertise, and credibility, while Online Customer
Reviews were measured through review quality, usefulness, and credibility. Purchase Decision was
assessed through indicators related to product selection, purchase intention, and repurchase intention.
All measurement items were evaluated using a five-point Likert scale ranging from strongly disagree
(1) to strongly agree (5).
Data analysis was conducted using IBM SPSS Statistics Version 25. Descriptive analysis was
used to describe respondents’ perceptions of each variable. Prior to hypothesis testing, validity,
reliability, and classical assumption tests were performed to ensu re the quality of the research
instrument and data. Furthermore, multiple linear regression analysis was applied to examine the
partial and simultaneous effects of Influencer Marketing and Online Customer Reviews on Purchase
Decisions. The hypotheses were tested using t-tests and F-tests at a significance level of 0.05, while
the coefficient of determination (R²) was used to evaluate the explanatory power of the proposed
model.

DISCUSSION
The findings indicate that Influencer Marketing has a positive and significant effect on
Purchase Decision among TikTok users. The path coefficient value (β = 0.454) demonstrates that
influencer marketing contributes substantially to consumers’ purchasing decisions regarding
Facetology Sunscreen. This result suggests that influencers play an important role in attracting
consumer attention, building trust, and increasing confidence in the promoted products. Consumers
tend to rely on influencers who are perce ived as attractive, credible, and knowledgeable when
evaluating skincare products. These findings support the study of Irrawati et al. (2025), which found
that influencer credibility and attractiveness positively influence consumers’ purchase intentions.
Similarly, Rumopa et al. (2026) argued that influencers are capable of shaping consumer perceptions
and encouraging purchasing behavior through creative and persuasive content on social media
platforms.
The results also reveal that Online Customer Review has a positive and significant effect on
Purchase Decision, with a higher path coefficient (β = 0.547) than Influencer Marketing. This finding
indicates that online customer reviews exert a stronger influence on consumers’ purchasing decisions.
Before purchasing skincare products, consumers generally seek information from previous buyers
regarding product quality, effectiveness, advantages, and potential drawbacks. Reviews are perceived
as more objective because they reflect actual user experiences. Consequently, positive and credible
reviews can reduce perceived risk and strengthen consumer confidence in making purchase decisions.
This finding is consistent with previous studies conducted by Putri et al. (2025), Andini and Wirsa
(2026), and Ananda et al. (2025), which concluded that online customer reviews serve as an important
source of information that significantly influences consumer purchasing behavior.
The stronger effect of Online Customer Review compared to Influencer Marketing suggests
that consumers tend to place greater trust in information provided by fellow users than in promotional
messages delivered by influencers. This is particularly relevant in the skincare industry, where
consumers are highly concerned about product suitability, effectiveness, and safety. As a result,
authentic experiences shared by other consumers become a critical consideration before making a
purchase.
Furthermore, the coefficient of determination (R² = 0.836) indicates that Influencer
Marketing and Online Customer Review jointly explain 83.6% of the variation in Purchase Decision.
This demonstrates that both variables are highly relevant in influencing consumer purchasing
behavior on TikTok. The remaining 16.4% may be explained by other factors not examined in this

study, such as brand image, product quality, price perception, electronic word-of-mouth, consumer
trust, and personal preferences.
Overall, the findings confirm that digital marketing strategies implemented through influencer
marketing and online customer reviews play an important role in shaping consumer purchase
decisions for Facetology Sunscreen products. Therefore, companies should not only collaborate with
credible influencers but also encourage positive customer reviews and maintain product quality to
enhance consumer trust and purchasing behavior. This study contributes to the growing literature on
social commerce by providing empirical evidence of the importance of influencer marketing and
online customer reviews in influencing purchase decisions among TikTok users.
Managerial Implications
The results of this study provide several managerial implications for skincare companies,
particularly Facetology. First, companies should collaborate with influencers who possess high
credibility, expertise, and strong engagement with their audiences. Second, businesses should actively
encourage customers to share honest product reviews and testimonials on TikTok and e-commerce
platforms. Third, maintaining product quality and customer satisfaction is essential to generate
positive reviews that can strengthen consumer trust. By optimizing both influencer marketing and
online customer reviews, companies can improve their marketing effectiveness and increase consumer
purchase decisions in the competitive skincare market.

CONCLUSIONS
This study examined the influence of Influencer Marketing and Online Customer Reviews on
the Purchase Decisions of Facetology Sunscreen products among TikTok users. The findings reveal
that Influencer Marketing has a positive and significant effect on Purchase Decision, indicating that
influencers with attractive characteristics, credibility, and expertise can enhance consumers’ trust and
encourage purchasing behavior.
The results also demonstrate that Online Customer Reviews have a positive and significant
effect on Purchase Decision. Compared to Influencer Marketing, Online Customer Reviews exert a
stronger influence, suggesting that consumers place considerable importance on the experiences and
opinions shared by other users before making purchasing decisions.
Furthermore, Influencer Marketing and Online Customer Reviews simultaneously have a
significant effect on Purchase Decision. The coefficient of determination shows that both variables
explain 83.6% of the variation in Purchase Decision, indicating that the y are important factors
influencing consumer behavior in the context of social commerce.
These findings highlight the importance of integrating influencer -based promotion and
customer-generated reviews into digital marketing strategies. For skincare companies, particularly
Facetology, collaborating with credible influencers and encouraging positive customer reviews can
strengthen consumer trust and increase purchase decisions. Future research is recommended to
include additional variables, such as brand image, product quality, consumer trust, and electronic
word-of-mouth, to provide a more comprehensive understanding of consumer purchasing behavior
in digital environments.
