The rapid shift from traditional media to digital platforms has
transformed how tourists access information and make travel decisions,
highlighting the need for rural tourism destinations to strengthen their
digital presence. Desa Pakisan in Buleleng Regency, despite its rich natural
and cultural resources, has limited online visibility and inactive digital
platforms, resulting in weak destination branding and low tourist
awareness. This condition is further influenced by low digital literacy,
limited techn ical skills in content production, and the absence of a
structured digital promotion system. This community service program
aimed to address these challenges through an integrated approach
combining digital marketing training, content creation, and the
application of digital tools. The program adopted participatory and
experiential learning methods implemented in four stages: needs
assessment, development of a technology -supported solution, intensive
training, and post- training mentoring. Ten participants, consisting of
Pokdarwis members and village youth, were trained to use practical
digital tools such as mobile -based photography and videography
applications, video editing software, content design platforms, and basic
social media analytics for performance  monitoring. They were also
introduced to the 4A destination framework to structure promotional
narratives. The results show significant improvements in participants’
digital competencies. Digital literacy increased by 75%, photography skills
by 86%, video graphy skills by 79%, and content editing skills by 88%.
Participants were able to produce higher- quality and more consistent
visual content. The village’s social media accounts, previously inactive
since 2019, were successfully reactivated and managed using a structured
content calendar and basic analytics to guide posting strategies. A Village
Creative Team was established and supported by a simple digital workflow
system to ensure sustainable content production and distribution. The
program also produced  a promotional video and a practical handbook
titled “Digital Marketing Success Tips” as long -term digital resources.
Overall, this program not only enhanced Desa Pakisan’s digital visibility
but also introduced a technology -based promotional model that
integrates digital tools, technical skills, and community -based
management to support sustainable tourism development.
