Abstract
Digital technology has made social media one of the main tools in tourism
marketing. Social media not only serves as a promotional tool but also plays a
role in shaping the image of destinations and influencing tourist behavior.
Although research on social media marketing in the tourism sector continues to
grow, studies that integrate research themes, applications, impacts, and future
research trends are still limited. Therefore, this study aims to analyze the
development of social media marketing research in the tourism sector through
the Systematic Literature Review (SLR) approach. The process of identifying and
selecting articles was carried out based on the PRISMA guidelines, while
VOSviewer was used to analyze the emergence of keywords and research trends.
Based on the selection process, 13 articles published between 2020 – 2026 were
included in the literature synthesis. The results showed that the main themes that
dominated the research included tourism behavior, destination marketing,
destination image, platforms, and influencers. In addition to serving as a
promotional medium, social media also influences the intentions and behavior of
tourist visits. The findings of this study provide a more comprehensive
understanding of the role of social media in tourism and identify opportunities
for future research.
Keywords: destination images; influencer marketing; social media marketing;
tourist behavior

INTRODUCTION
Digital transformation has
profoundly changed the tourism
marketing landscape in recent years.
The growth of the internet and social
media allows tourist destinations to
communicate directly with travelers
without time or place restrictions (Gui
et al., 2025). Social media platforms
such as Instagram, Facebook, TikTok,
and YouTube have become the
primary ways to disseminate travel
information because they offer
interactive, visual, and accessible
content for potential travelers. In this

context, social media is no longer just a
communication tool, but also a
strategic instrument that helps build a
destination's image, promote tourism,
and engage travelers in the destination
marketing process (Zhu et al., 2022).
Previous studies have shown that
digital marketing strategies and social
media utilization play a significant
role in increasing market reach,
customer engagement, and business
competitiveness in the digital age
(Ibad & Susilaningsih, 2019; Rahman
et al., 2025). Additionally, social media
platforms have become an effective
marketing tool due to their ability to
facilitate consumer interaction,
promotion, and decision -making
processes.
The increasing use of social
media has also changed the way
travelers search for and evaluate travel
information. Before traveling, tourists
typically use social media to get
information about a destination's
attractions, other travelers'
experiences, and travel
recommendations shared through
different types of digital content. A
study by Nofrizal, N., & Arizal (2024),
found that information from electronic
recommendations, user reviews, and
user-generated content is often
considered more trustworthy than
official promotions conducted by
destination marketing organizations.
This situation suggests that social
media plays a large role in shaping the
way travelers view, choose, and decide
on travel destinations (Orranti et al.,
2025).
The importance of social media
in tourism marketing is demonstrated
by the increasing academic interest in
this topic in recent years. Studies show
that social media helps shape how a
place is branded, improves the image
of the destination, and strengthens the
relationship between the place and its
visitors. Mandagi et al. (2024), in their
systematic review of destination
branding, found that social media has
become a key part of building the
identity and image of the destination
through continuous digital interaction
between stakeholders and tourists.
As the situation evolves, research
on social media marketing in the
tourism sector has seen a significant
increase. Studies show that social
media helps shape the way people
perceive destinations, increase tourist
engagement, strengthen destination
brands, and increase visitor intent to
come (Tran & Rudolf, 2022; Saputra &
Nugraha, 2024). In addition, the rise of
influencer marketing has introduced a
new approach in tourism promotion,
where influencers act as a source of
information that can shape travelers'
perceptions and influence their travel
decisions through content that is
considered more authentic and
personalized (John & Supramaniam,
2026; Akram & Majeed, 2026). This
suggests that tourism marketing
strategies are moving away from one-
way communication towards more
interactive and experience -based
digital engagement.
Several previous systematic
reviews have examined social media in
the context of destination branding
(Tran & Rudolf, 2022; Mandagi et al.,
2024), social media marketing in the
tourism industry (Solanki & Thomas,
2025), and storytelling in destination
marketing (Zhang & Ramayah, 2024).

These studies provide an important
foundation for understanding the role
of social media in tourism. However,
each has a different focus and scope of
research. Therefore, this study does
not claim to be the first review to link
social media marketing to destination
imagery and tourist behavior, but
rather focuses on a synthesis of the
current literature based on a clearly
defined period and scope of selection.
Based on this, this study
conducted a Systematic Literature
Review (SLR) of open access journal
articles indexed by Scopus and
published in the period 2020 –2026,
with bibliometric mapping using
VOSviewer as a supporting analysis.
This research is expected to contribute
to the development of tourism
marketing literature th eory and
become a reference for practitioners in
creating more effective digital
marketing strategies. To achieve this
goal, the research is formulated into
several research questions as follows:
RQ1. What are the main research
themes in social media marketing in
the tourism sector?
RQ2. How is social media marketing
used in tourism destination
marketing?
RQ3. What impact does social media
marketing have on tourist behavior
and destination image?
RQ4. What are the emerging trends
and future research directions in social
media marketing and tourism?

METHODS
This study employed a
Systematic Literature Review (SLR)
approach to identify, evaluate, and
synthesize research related to social
media marketing in the tourism sector.
The literature search was conducted
using the Scopus database on April 13,
2026. The search strategy used the
keywords "digital marketing" AND
"social media" AND "tourism" in the
title, abstract, and keyword fields. The
initial search yielded 2,211 records.
Following the application of the
publication period (2020 –2026),
document type (journal articles), and
English-language criteria, 2,100
records remained. The open -access
filter further reduced the records to
483. These records were then assessed
for relevance to the research topic and
research questions, resulting in 13
articles included in the final literature
synthesis. The selection process
followed the PRISMA framework,
comprising identification, screening,
eligibility, and inclusion stages (Page
et al., 2021), as illustrated in Figure 1

Using the PRISMA guidelines for
the selection process, 13 articles that
met the inclusion criteria were

In addition to the systematic
literature review, bibliometric analysis
was conducted using VOSviewer to
map keyword co-occurrence patterns
and identify the main research themes.
Scopus metadata served as the data
source, and the minimum keyword
occurrence threshold was set at two.
The resulting network visualization
illustrates the relationships among
keywords in research on social media
marketing in tourism. The results of
the analysis were then used to answer
research questions regarding the main
theme, utilization, impact, and future
direction of social media marketing in
the tourism sector.

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
RQ 1. Key Research Themes in Social
Media Marketing
To identify key themes in social
media marketing research in the
tourism sector, keyword co-occurrence
analysis was performed using the
VOSviewer software. This analysis
aims to show how often a particular
keyword appears together in the
article being studied. The larger the
node, the more often the keyword
appears, and the lines connecting them
show how closely related the keyword
is. By looking at this network
visualization, you can see the main
research themes and conceptual
relationships between various topics
in the literature on social media
marketing and tourism.

RQ 3. The impact of social media
marketing on tourist behavior and
destination image
Impact on Tourist Behavior
The synthesis results show that
social media has a strong influence on
travelers' decision-making processes.
Before traveling, travelers use various
social media platforms to get
information about their destinations,
compare travel options, and examine
other travelers' experiences through
reviews, photos, or videos. These
findings are in line with research by
Gaffar et al. (2022), which showed that
social media marketing activities can
increase tourists' intention to visit
natural tourist destinations. Soci al
media not only influences intentions to
visit a place, but it also helps shape
travelers' perceptions and expectations
of the travel experience they will have.
A study by Lusianingrum and Pertiwi
in 2023 found that exposure to digital
information through social media
influences Generation Z's interest in
visiting tourist destinations. In a
broader sense, social media has
become one of the main sources of
information that travelers use during
the information-seeking phase, travel
planning, and even after a visit for
evaluation.
Another impact can be seen from
tourists' behavior after their visit.
Experiences shared on social media
can encourage tourists to recommend
destinations to others, generate
electronic word -of-mouth
recommendations (e-WOM), and
increase the likelihood of revisits
(revisit intent). Findings by Yuliviona
et al. (2025), show that positive travel
experiences contribute to the
formation of revisit intent, which can
ultimately strengthen tourists' loyalty
to a destination.
Impact on Destination Images
In addition to influencing
travelers' behavior, social media also
plays a key role in shaping the image
of a destination. Content shared on
social media platforms allows
travelers to form opinions about the
attractiveness, quality, and uniqueness
of a destination even before visiting it
in person. Therefore, social media has
become one of the main sources in the
process of shaping the image of a
destination. The results of the
synthesis show that visual content,
travel reviews, and digital marketing
activities carried out by destination
managers can create a positive or
negative image of a destination.
Ramadania et al. (2025), stated that the
identity of a city and its brand
influences tourists' intention to visit by
increasing the perceived value of the
destination. These findings suggest
that using the right communication
strategies on social media can help
build a better image of a destination
and make it more appealing to
tourists. In addition, Japutra et al.
(2025) found that the messages
conveyed in des tination
advertisements through social media
can affect tourists' perception of the

CONCLUSION
This study shows that social
media marketing has become one of
the main focuses in tourism marketing
research. The results of the synthesis
show that social media is not only a
way to promote tourist destinations,
but also a medium that influences the
perception, decision-making, and
overall travel experience of tourists.
So, social media marketing plays an
important role in helping destinations
compete better in the digital age. In
addition to identifying key themes, the
study also found a shift in the study's
focus to more specific issues, such as
influencer marketing, Instagram
marketing, and travel behavior.
Therefore, future research should
explore the role of emerging platforms
like TikTok and the integration of
digital technologies to support more
effective destination marketing
strategies.
This study has limitations
because it only uses the Scopus
database and limits articles to open
access and English -language
publications. In addition, the analysis
only includes 13 articles. Further
research is recommended to use
multiple databases, expand the scope
of language and access publications,
and involve more articles to obtain a
more comprehensive literature
synthesis and bibliometric mapping.
