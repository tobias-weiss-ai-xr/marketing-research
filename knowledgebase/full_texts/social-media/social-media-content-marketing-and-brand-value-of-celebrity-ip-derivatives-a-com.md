Abstract. In recent years, digital entertainment and fan‑driven consumption have gained
rapid popularity worldwide. With the rapid development and widespread use of diverse
social media platforms, celebrity IP derivatives have become a vital and fast‑expanding
segment of the fan‑economy market; mainstream platforms such as TikTok and
Xiaohongshu play a decisive role in shaping and sustaining the long‑term brand value of
celebrity‑related products. This essay combines literature analysis with practical case
review, exploring how social‑media‑based content marketing constructs fan engagement and
deepens emotional connection between audiences and idols, so as to generate and enhance
the brand value of celebrity IP derivatives. This paper adopts a comparative case analysis
approach, selecting two representative idol‑operation enterprises, TF Entertainment and
HYBE with its Weverse ecosystem, to systematically compare their content output
strategies, community‑building modes, and commercial transformation paths. It further
discusses prominent potential industry risks, including excessive commercialization, fan
emotional burnout caused by repetitive content and high‑intensity fan labor, as well as
product quality mismatches. The conclusion offers targeted suggestions for sustainable
celebrity IP management and practical references for marketing contemporary cultural and
fan‑oriented products.
Keywords: Celebrity IP, Social media, Content marketing, IP derivatives
1. Introduction
1.1. Research background
Social media platforms have seriously changed the way entertainment companies operate star-
related businesses. In the past, most stars relied on film and television works, songs, and offline
activities to connect with the audience. Now, many stars can interact with fans around the world on
Xiaohongshu, TikTok, Weibo, and other platforms. This high-frequency online interaction has
created a close fan base, and celebrity peripheral derivatives have gradually become an important
source of income for brokerage companies.

Brokerage companies will often publish daily trends of stars, behind-the-scenes tidbits, and
content specially produced for fans online, so as to deepen the emotional bond between fans and

think about is how brokerages should transform the emotional loyalty of fans into the long-term
brand value of star-related products, not just a surge in short-term product profits.
1.2. Research significance
This article refers to past research on celebrity endorsement, online content marketing, and fan

Theoretically, this research can enrich the brand-related research results in the background of the
fan economy and extend the marketing theory to the subdivision of celebrity peripheral goods.
At the practical level, the conclusions of the research can provide entertainment brokerage

1.3. Research content and research method
On the basis of sorting out relevant theories and research results, this article builds an analytical
framework for the influence of star IP content marketing on brand value and conducts comparative
analysis through cases.
Research method:
(1) Literature research: summarize the existing theories and research conclusions.
(2) Case analysis: Choose Weverse and other fan interaction platforms as examples to analyze the
impact of different content operation strategies on the brand value of IP derivatives.
2. Literature review and theoretical basis
2.1. Star IP and IP derivatives
Star IP is a set of industries created around the external image, business strengths, personality traits,
and personal life of the star himself as the main body.
Unlike fictional IPs such as novels and animations, star IP has its own strong personality
attributes. Its commercial value largely depends on the exposure of stars in front of the public,
public favorability, and the real emotional bond between fans and stars.
And star IP derivatives are all kinds of goods developed by celebrities as a series. They include
physical albums, support props, and such peripherals. At the same time, experiential consumption
such as offline meetings and concerts also belongs to the category of derivatives. Entering the era of
social media, derivatives have long been not only a subsidiary of stars. It has slowly become an
important carrier for fans and idols to communicate emotions, and it is also an important way for the
industry to achieve commercial realization.
2.2. Social media content marketing
Content marketing refers to the marketing method for creators to output content with practical value
to attract the target audience and finally complete the audience conversion [1]. In the current
environment, content marketing attaches more importance to two-way interaction and instant

information updates. Enterprises are no longer just one-way indoctrination advertisements, but
produce interesting and emotional content to stimulate potential users to participate and forward
relevant product content.
Therefore, social media content marketing plays a particularly crucial role in the actual operation
of celebrity IP. With the help of Weibo, TikTok, Xiaohongshu, Weverse, and other platforms, stars
and brokerage teams share daily clips and behind-the-scenes tidbits, carry out live interaction, and
release exclusive graphic videos. The essence of these behaviors is content marketing with people as
the core. The continuously output content not only maintains the public exposure of the stars, but
also provides fans with emotional support and topics for chat and discussion, realizing the
interaction between fans and artists, thus accumulating the trust of fans and laying the foundation for
the subsequent sale of peripheral products.
2.3. Fan economy and consumer participation
In the past, in the traditional fan economy, fan consumption was mostly concentrated on core
products such as albums and concert tickets. However, after the popularization of social media, the
boundaries of the fan economy have been continuously broadened, and the purchase of derivatives,
paid digital content, and members who open exclusive applications have become very common
forms of consumption. Nowadays, the construction of virtual brand communities generally improves
the participation enthusiasm of fan groups through the process of information interaction of topics,
fan identity expression, and community cultural norms [2].
Consumer participation is a key concept to understand the fan economy. In the research on social
media brand communities, consumer participation is the process of cognitive input, emotional input,
and practical action input in the process of interaction between users and other members of the brand
and community [3]. There is a wide range of star-chasing scenes, for example, browsing, liking, and
commenting on pictures and texts or videos related to peripherals; editing or creating direct shooting
of concerts; spending money on limited peripherals. Fan participation not only represents the loyalty
of fans, but also an important driving force for the brand to achieve value co-creation.
2.4. Brand value
Brand value can be used to measure the status and actual influence of the brand in the hearts of
consumers. This is based on the brand asset theory (CBBE) from the consumer's perspective that
brand value comes from multiple dimensions such as consumers' perception of the brand,
association, judgment of quality, and brand loyalty [4, 5].
For star IP derivatives, its brand value generation logic is relatively special. The practical
function of the derivative itself is not the main source of value. It relies more on the meaning of star
IP and the subjective emotional identity of the fan group. Whether a star's peripheral can be
recognized depends to a large extent on how much fans like this star and whether they agree with the
emotional meaning of the product. Therefore, the process of establishing the brand value of star IP
derivatives is actually to gradually transform the feelings of fans for idols into the cognition and
trust of derivatives. Therefore, the attractiveness, professionalism, and credibility of star IP itself
will not directly form brand assets, but indirectly affect brand credibility as an intermediary variable
[6].

3. The impact mechanism of social media content marketing on the brand value of star IP
3.1. Content production and IP image shaping
Whether the star IP can have a high brand value depends on whether it can attract the audience first.
On social platforms, the stars themselves and the brokerage team continue to output all kinds of
content, consciously polishing and strengthening the personality and characteristics of the stars.
Common content is roughly divided into four categories:
Daily content: share life, bring the psychological distance between stars and fans closer, and
make fans feel friendly and familiar.
Professional content: show business ability, consolidate the public's impression of his
professional strength, and create such people through marketing.
Planning content: combine node planning related content with special meaning to create the
popularity of online topics.
Interactive content: promote the distance between artists and fans through close interaction with
fans.
After long-term content output, the personal characteristics of stars will form a fixed image in the
minds of fans. Relevant theories suggest that if consumers repeatedly bind and accept a certain
character to a specific product, a strong memory connection will be formed between the two [7].
That is to say, social media content marketing with excellent quality can transfer the image
advantages accumulated by star IP to the brand of derivatives.
3.2. Social media communication and audience reach
However, after the content is made, it still needs to rely on publicity to give full play to its practical
value. Social media provides celebrity IP with a wide range of channels that can accurately reach
different groups of people, which is mainly reflected in these points:
First of all, social media breaks down the communication barriers of traditional media.
Celebrities no longer need to rely on traditional channels such as TV stations and paper magazines.
They can directly push content to a large number of fans by relying on personal accounts and
removing intermediaries for direct dissemination.
Secondly, the platform algorithm amplifies the content dissemination effect. Fans' likes,
comments, and retweets bring interactive data, and high-quality content will be pushed to more non-
fan users by algorithms, which will have the opportunity to break the circle outside the circle, so that
more people will be impressed by the stars themselves and their related products.
Finally, multi-platform differentiated operation covers audiences with different preferences.
Targeted promotion through users with different preferences and natures. Star IP releases different
content according to the characteristics of the platform, which can reach more potential consumers.
3.3. Fan interaction and emotional connection
Content marketing is not only to complete information output, but more importantly, it is to mobilize
users to participate in interaction and slowly build emotional connections. Social media platforms
mainly deepen the emotional bond between fans and star IP through multiple channels, and enhance
the stickiness of fans.
The first is the Parasocial Interaction mechanism. Fans browsing all kinds of dynamics released
by celebrities on social accounts for a long time will produce a psychological feeling as if they are

attachment [8]. When this kind of virtual interactive experience accumulates to a certain extent, fans
will project their good impression and trust in the idol himself onto all kinds of derivatives bound

The second is the formation of a sense of belonging to the community. A large number of fans
rely on the online platform to gather around the same star and slowly form an exclusive circle.
Unique communication skills will slowly form within the circle, as well as collective behaviors in
which everyone participates, such as supporting the list and showing off the surrounding area [9].

this sense of belonging from the collective will further motivate fans to spend money on derivative
products.
Finally, it is the cycle brought about by the satisfaction of demand. Relevant research mentioned
that if the online brand community can meet the demands of users to obtain information, leisure
entertainment, social interaction, and certain material incentives, then the quality of the relationship
between users and brands will be significantly improved, which will drive the desire to buy, the
willingness of the community to retain, and the active sharing of word of mouth. Behavior [10]. In
the same way, the content of all kinds of star IP derivatives produced by social media is precisely the
main way to meet the multiple needs of fans.
3.4. From fan participation to consumer and business value transformation
After fans generate emotional support and engage in community interaction, these psychological
feelings must eventually be implemented into real consumption behaviors in order to realize the
corresponding commercial value. There are four different channels in this transformation process.
The first one is to buy directly. In the process of fans browsing the content of the social platform,
the page comes with a product jump link and a mall entrance. When you see the derivatives you are
interested in, you can directly complete the order, and the content browsing and consumption
behavior are seamlessly connected.
The second is the spread of word of mouth. Fans who have purchased the product will share the
unboxing, usage experience, or return pictures within the circle or externally. Relying on this online
word-of-mouth communication, it can subtly influence the consumption choices of other potential
enthusiasts.
The third is the sense of identity. Many fans buy and display peripheral products, which is also a
kind of self-expression in essence. By using derivatives to show their fan identity and personal
preferences, this mentality of wanting to show identity will promote repurchase and collection
behavior.
The fourth type is to participate in the creation together. Some companies will test the preferences
of fans by developing various styles of peripherals, and then decide how to plan later peripherals
according to everyone's preferences. At this time, they are no longer just ordinary buyers, but also
participate in value creation, and their loyalty and willingness to consume IP derivative products will
be further improved.
Based on the above analysis, social media content marketing affects the brand value of star IP
derivatives, which can be summarized into a complete set of logical steps: first, polish and shape the
image of IP characters through the output platform content, then build fan interaction and emotional
bonds with the help of extensive dissemination of social channels, and finally complete the
consumption transformation. Now the brand value has been improved. Content marketing is not
only the beginning of the whole process, but also a core element that runs through all links.

4. Case analysis of the company
4.1. The star IP marketing model of the peak of the times
Times Peak is currently one of the most representative idol development entertainment companies in
mainland China. With the "trainee system" as the core of its operation, the company has successfully
launched well-known idol groups such as TFBOYS. Its star IP marketing model is characterized by
the local fan market, and each operational dimension revolves around the main line of "cultivation".
The core strategy of the times is "accompanying growth". The company continues to produce
trainees' daily training, rehearsal clips, and life content, so that fans can witness the whole process of
idols from the trainee stage to debut to gradual maturity. This content logic positions fans as
"witnesses" and "supporters" of idol growth, rather than mere consumers, thus establishing a deep
emotional bond between fans and stars that goes beyond the general worship relationship. These
contents focus on bringing the psychological distance between idols and fans closer, and are highly
suitable for the aesthetic preferences and language habits of young Chinese audiences.
Times Peak highly relies on Weibo as the core communication platform. The company sets up an
official account for each artist group, regularly publishes pictures, texts, and videos, and facilitates
the gathering of fan communities with the help of the Weibo super talk function. At the same time,
the company also actively expands TikTok, Bilibili, Xiaohongshu, and other platforms to achieve
differentiated content distribution and audience reach.
Times Peak has built an organizational structure with the official support association as the core.
The company maintains normal communication with the fan group through the support association,
and regularly releases welfare content to maintain the activity and loyalty of fans. At the same time,
fans win more exposure resources for idols by buying self-made peripherals from the support club.
This kind of fan labor has formed a highly organized and clearly divided operational system. It is
worth noting that the fan group at the peak of the times is mainly "sister fans" and "mother fans",
and such fans have a high degree of emotional investment. They regard the growth of artists as their
own responsibility, so they show high continuity and mobilization ability in terms of data support
and consumption expenditure. The company is also good at using this psychological feature to
stimulate fans' competitive awareness and enthusiasm for participation by publishing list rankings
and sales data.
The product system of Times Peak is mainly based on traditional peripherals, including music
albums, photo albums, official support materials, and anniversary gift boxes. The sales channel is
mainly based on official independent application software. At the same time, limited peripherals and
paid live broadcasts are set up in offline activities such as concerts and fan meetings. In terms of
strategy, the company focuses on the "commemorability" and "collectability" of products, and
derivatives are often bound to specific time nodes (such as debut anniversaries and members'
birthdays) to create a sense of scarcity and consumption urgency. However, compared with HYBE's
diversified layout in terms of derivatives and forms, the product types of the times are relatively
traditional, and there is still a lot of room for expansion in the development of digital products,
experiential consumption, and cross-border co-branding.
The participation behavior of fans on social media can be roughly divided into multiple levels,
such as daily interaction, data labor, secondary creation, support organization, and purchase and
consumption. Among them, "data labor" is particularly prominent - fan groups will carry out
organized behaviors such as "brushing topics" and "control comments" to improve the commercial
value and industry status of idols by creating hot social media data. Although this participation
model effectively supports the market performance of IP in the short term, it also implies problems

4.2. HYBE's Weverse platform IP marketing ecology
HYBE Corporation, formerly known as Big Hit Entertainment, is one of the most influential
entertainment companies in South Korea and even the world. Represented by the global success of
BTS, the company has established a comprehensive entertainment ecology integrating music
production, artist agency, platform technology, and derivative product development. Unlike Times
Peak, which partially relies on third-party social platforms, HYBE launched its own fan interaction
platform, Weverse, in 2019. This platform strategy makes HYBE's IP marketing model show
distinctive ecological and global characteristics.
HYBE has a diversified combination of artists to attract fans with different preferences by
forming different styles of boy groups and girl groups. The common characteristics of HYBE's artist
IP are high-quality musical works, distinctive group worldview, and strong members' personal
identity. Each group has an independent narrative system and style. This differentiated IP strategy
enables different groups to attract fans with different preferences, spread risks, and effectively
reduce internal competition between groups under the same company.
Weverse provides fans with content forms, including graphics and video content released by
artists at any time, exclusive behind-the-scenes content open only to members, real-time live
interactive functions, and member-exclusive content such as birthday specials and handwritten
letters. The common characteristics of this content are "exclusiveness" and "immediacy" - the
content obtained by fans on the platform cannot be obtained on other platforms. This scarcity
effectively improves the willingness of fans to pay and the stickiness of the platform. Weverse
represents a fan-exclusive platform to give rise to the platform fan model. HYBE uses platform
technology to manage the fan community and transform the emotional connection of fans into
commercial income [11].
Weverse reshapes the quasi-social interaction model on traditional social media, making the
interaction more bidirectional. Artists post updates on the platform as personal accounts, regularly
read and reply to fans' messages, and have real-time conversations with fans through live broadcasts.
This interaction mode greatly enhances the emotional connection between fans and artists, and
transforms the relationship between fans and idols from one-way worship to two-way emotional
communication, thus significantly deepening the loyalty of fans. In other words, what Weverse
creates is not only a scene of content consumption, but also a continuous and close-up emotional
relationship maintenance mechanism.
Overall, there is a significant difference between the star IP marketing model of Times Peak and
HYBE. Times Peak cultivates the local market and maintains fan relations with an emotional
"cultivating narrative". Its advantages lie in the high emotional loyalty of fans and strong
community mobilization ability, but it is limited by the dependence on third-party platforms and the
traditional product form; HYBE takes its own platform, Weverse, as the core and builds a global The
advantage of the IP operation ecology of digitalization and closed-loop is cross-market large-scale
operation and data-driven accurate management. The two models have their own characteristics, but
they both point to a core proposition: social media content marketing is the key driving force for the
brand value construction of star IP derivatives, and the efficient integration of content, community,
and business determines the growth potential and sustainability of brand value.

4.3. Comparative analysis
From the two perspectives of content marketing and fan participation, Times Peak focuses on the
operational idea of "accompanying growth". Content distribution and fan information gathering
mainly rely on external third-party platforms such as Weibo. The company released daily training
clips of trainees and various stage materials to slowly cultivate fans' emotional support for artists.
Most of the activities of the fan group are led by organizations such as the support association,
which will produce high-intensity data support behavior. In this mode, fans have a strong ability to
execute, but long-term task-based support can also easily cause psychological and emotional
consumption for fans. On the other hand, HYBE builds its own complete content environment. The
platform released exclusive behind-the-scenes clips, real-time live broadcasts of artists, and other
customized content, which changed the state of fans who used to simply do data labor, so that fans
and artists could form two-way emotional communication, effectively improve user retention, and
enhance fan stickiness.
Let's look at the platform ecology and IP derivatives. Although Times Peak has also launched its
own official APP for commodity sales, there is a problem with the overall ecology: fans' social
communication is still in third-party social software, and its own APP only undertakes the function
of selling goods online. Most of the derivatives are traditional physical peripherals and physical
albums. Merchants stimulate everyone's desire to collect with the help of limited sales and special
versions of anniversaries, but the development of digital derivative products is still relatively
limited.
Weverse is different. The platform has opened up a whole closed-loop system. Social
communication, consumption, and purchase are all concentrated in the same software, and various
derivative products such as physical peripherals, digital cards, and member-exclusive benefits are
integrated. It not only develops a variety of digital derivative products, but also completes product
distribution for users in different regions of the world.
5. Existing problems and optimization suggestions
First of all, the content is seriously homogenized, and the operation relies too much on the core fan
group. In order to maintain short-term popularity on the Internet, brokerage companies and
platforms constantly consume the emotional energy of core fans. In the long run, loyal fans need to
bear heavy money and time costs and repeatedly receive routine material content, which is easy to
cause aesthetic fatigue and physical and mental fatigue.
Secondly, there is a phenomenon of rushing to make quick profits in commercial development,
and the quality of derivative products cannot keep up with the pace of development. Many official
peripherals are priced at a high price, but the design is perfunctory and lacks new ideas, which
slowly wears out the fans' willingness to collect and buy. In addition, domestic entertainment
enterprises will also face obstacles to cross-cultural communication when entering overseas markets.
At the same time, the active interaction of the fan community cannot always be smoothly
transformed into actual business income. There is a gap between the popularity of interaction and
consumption conversion, which makes it difficult to support the long-term stable development of the
brand.
In response to the above-mentioned industry pain points, the marketing team should polish the
quality of content, break out of the uniform material routine, and produce more diverse original
content. At the same time, establish healthier community interaction rules, reduce meaningless data

6. Conclusion
The peak of the times follows the narrative route of "idols accompany growth", builds fan emotional
ties based on the local environment, and the revenue mainly depends on physical products.
However, the whole operational system is dispersed, and social communication and commodity
transactions are scattered across different third-party software, which does not form a closed loop.
HYBE's Weverse has created a complete digital ecology that integrates content, fan community, and
e-commerce. Relying on virtual derivative products and a global layout, it has obtained considerable
returns. However, the underlying logic of the two models has something in common, and both of
them take the emotional recognition of fans as the foundation of development. Whether it is
domestic companion star chasing or diverse online interaction on overseas platforms, the essence is
to build a quasi-social relationship, and then transform the attention of fans into actual purchasing
power. That is to say, content marketing based on social media can deepen fans' sense of identity and
emotional attachment, and then improve the brand value of star IP derivatives.
Overall, in the context of digital transformation, the entertainment industry cannot continue to
follow the old marketing ideas. Only by breaking through the barriers between different platforms
and building a global ecology that balances the efficiency of emotional maintenance and digital
operation can the brand value of star IP derivatives be maintained and amplified for a long time.
