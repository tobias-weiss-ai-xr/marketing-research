Semiotics examines signs and symbols, exploring meaning formation and their
functions within social contexts. Many consumers do not realize the deeper
meanings contained in advertisements, as these messages are often conveyed
implicitly through visual elements. The significance of this study is to help
audiences understand how modes of sign, such as iconic, indexi cal, and
symbolic used in digital advertising, especially in social media marketing.  The
research aims to explore how these modes of signs construct meaning in Dior
Beauty Instagram advertisements and how they support the brand’s image.
Using a qualitative method based on Mackiewicz, selected posts featuring Jisoo
(09 September 2025) and Jenna Ortega (02 January 2026) were observed and
documented. Data were analyzed using Chandler’s theory to explain how visual
signs convey meaning. The findings show that each advertisement combines
two iconic signs, one indexical sign, and one symbolic sign. Iconic signs appear
in the models and products, visually resembling reality and being recognizable
to viewers. Indexical signs are shown through visible product effects, such as
smooth skin in the setting powder advertisement and bold red lips in the lipstick
advertisement, demonstrating a cause -and-effect relationship. Symbolic signs,
such as the Dior logo and Instagram username, represent brand identity through
shared cultural understanding. Together, these signs create implic it meanings
that link Dior Beauty products with elegance, flawless appearance, and ideal
beauty. This research implies that understanding semiotic and visual strategies
in digital advertisements can increase audience awareness of how brands
communicate identity, value, and perception through subtle visual messages.

1. Introduction
Semiotics is the study of signs and symbols, focusing on how meaning is formed and how
these elements operate within social contexts  (Al-Khawaldeh & Al -Rabadi, 2023; Chandler,
2001; McBurney, 2006; Sebeok, 2001) . According to Chandler (2007), the types of signs most
easily brought to mind are those commonly encountered in daily life, such as traffic signs, pub
signs, and zodiac signs. Signs are found everywhere in daily life, including in advertising,
where a sign can be anything that represents a product, brand, or idea, whether it is an image,
slogan, sound, or gesture that conveys a particular message to the audience. In the digital era,
Instagram has become a powerful platform for brands to communicate their identity and
connect with consumers through both visual and verbal content. Many brands take advantage
of Instagram’s power to promote their products; one of them is Dior Beauty. Dior Beauty , as
a luxury cosmetics brand, consistently uses  Instagram to project its elegant and exclusive
image. However, many consumers do not realize the deeper meanings contained in Dior
Beauty Instagram advertisements, which are often conveyed through signs that form implicit
meanings within them (Sari et al., 2021).
Several studies regarding signs in advertisements have been conducted many times,
especially in digital advertisements. The advertisements that were studied were digital in
Starbucks Advertisements, where the research is about signs, and the study found an  equal
use of implicit and explicit meanings, with both verbal and nonverbal signs working together
to attract consumer attention  (Casmita et al., 2024) . Similarly, another researcher also
examined signs in the For Women by Women: IDOLE Lancôme Advertisement and found
that the advertisement includes verbal signs that occur both orally and in written form, and
also contains non-verbal elements in the form of images, graphics, and color (Dewi et al., 2022).
In addition, the study on Maybelline advertisements found that both verbal and nonverbal
signs play an important role in advertising. These advertisements often use short phrases and
connotative meanings to attract attention and create curiosity (Dewi et al., 2021).
There was also research on signs, particularly in Instagram advertisements, which
focused on how visual and written elements are used in promoting tourism. The study
analyzed three poster advertisements from a travel company based in Bali to understand how
messages were delivered through different types of signs. It found that all three posters
contained both verbal and non -verbal signs, showing that the company used multiple
methods to attract attention. The verbal signs included various phrases and comple te
sentences that were used to clearly deliver the advertiser’s message to the viewers.
Meanwhile, the non-verbal signs were made up of images, models, and scenic backgrounds,
which helped to visually support and enhance the message being shared  (Jaya & Skolastika,
2024).
Moreover, there was a study about signs in Facebook advertisements, especially those
related to tourism in Gianyar . The study focused on how signs were used in eight tourism
advertisements taken from Facebook. It found that the eight advertisements contained a total
of 24 verbal signs and 24 non-verbal signs. Verbal signs most often appeared as sentences that
carried denotative meanings, which means they gave direct and clear information to the
audience. On the other hand, non -verbal signs, usually in the form of images, tended to
convey connotative meanings, which gave additional or symbolic messages related to the
tourism content (Janarki, 2023).
Furthermore, there were studies about signs, especially in social media and web -based
advertisements. This study examines signs in Rexona Clinical Protection advertising,
identifying six verbal signs and six non -verbal signs, eight denotative meanings, and  four

study analyzed the movie as data and identified various types of signs, such as icons, indexes,
and symbols (Antari et al., 2023) . A similar study also discusses the structure of signs in the
British film Awaydays (2009), using Ferdinand de Saussure’s semiotic theory. The study
found that the film uses semiotic structures to explore themes of masculinity, belonging, and
youth rebellion. It shows how signs function not only to build narrative coherence but also to
reinforce or question dominant ideologies (Pratama & Budi, 2025). Another study is about the
types and functions of signs found in the film Spider -Man: Far from Home. It found that the
movie contains 3 icons, 5 indexes, and 8 symbols. The icons include 3 ideational

symbols consist of 6 idotional 4 interpersonal, and 4 textua l metafunctions (Loi et al., 2022) .

Bodyguard also highlights the use of various signs. It found that signs can be identified
throughout the film and this showing a diverse application of semiotic elements (Kurniawan
et al., 2022). Lastly, a study about Signs Found in the Movie ‘Uncharted’ found that the movie
contains 3 icons, 5 indexes, and 3 symbols. The icons include 2 ideational and 1 interpersonal
metafunction; the indexes include 3 ideational and 2 textual metafunctions; and t he symbols
consist of 2 ideational and 1 interpersonal metafunction (Hatin, 2024).
Thus, the previous literature demonstrates that Instagram has become a powerful
platform for major brands, particularly for promoting their products. However, in practice,
many consumers still encounter difficulties in interpreting the meanings conveyed th rough
product-related communication. Therefore, this study aims to analyze the implicit meanings
embedded in brand images and product representations through the perspective of modes of
signs.

2. Literature Review
Semiotics continues to develop its foundational theories, particularly those proposed by
Charles Sanders Peirce and Ferdinand de Saussure (Bilhaj, 2025). Peirce’s triadic model of the
sign (sign, object, interpretant) remains central, with applications across various disciplines
(Lomas, 2019; Nöth, 2014) . The interdisciplinary nature of semiotics has been emphasized,
particularly through its integration into fields such as linguistics, philosophy, and cultural
studies. This transdisciplinary approach is considered essential for addressing complex
research issues (Deely & Semetsky, 2017).
Semiotics has been applied to digital networks and user interface design. Studies have
shown that intuitive interface cues can improve website usability and the user experience.
These studies also emphasize the importance of semiotic principles in human -computer
interaction (Islam & Tétard, 2014; Jensen, 2015) . Semiotics has been used to analyze
communication processes in the marketplace by drawing analogies between language
systems and the exchange of commodities (Nöth, 2006).
Semiotics has played a significant role in analyzing cultural artifacts, such as Chinese
ceramics, to uncover cross-cultural communication and symbolic meaning (Wu, 2025). In the
arts, semiotics has been applied to theater and film to examine how signs convey ideological
perspectives and narrative structures (Khafaga, 2022).

3. Method
This research used a qualitative method, following the approach by Mackiewicz (2018).
According to Mackiewicz (2018), qualitative methods relied on text and image data, involved
specific steps in data analysis, and applied various research designs. In this research, the focus
was on how modes of signs were used in digital advertising, particularly on social media
platforms such as Instagram. This method was chosen because it aligned with the research
being conducted, which included documentation and observation to analyze the data.
This research used Dior Beauty Instagram advertisements as the main data source. The
data for this research were taken from Dior Beauty’s official Instagram account. The data
consisted of 1 image of Jisoo Blackpink, posted on 09 September 2025, and 1 image  of Jena
Ortega, posted on 2 January 2026, from the advertisements. These posts were chosen based on
their relevance to the research topic. The data were expected to contain various modes of sign,
such as iconic, indexical, and symbolic, which were used to construct meaning in the
advertisements.
This research used the observation method to collect the data. First, the data were
searched in Dior Beauty’s Instagram posts. Then, the researcher carefully observed the
advertisements that were likely to contain various modes of signs. The observations focused
on visual elements within the posts, and only relevant posts were selected. After that, the
selected data were documented through screenshots for further analysis. Finally, after data
collection, the data were organized by categorizing the visual elements and texts according to
their types. This organization helped to systematically analyze the different modes of signs
present in the advertisements.
The data were analyzed using  Chandler’s semiotic theory to identify and interpret
various modes of signs such as icons, indexes, and symbols within the advertisements
(Chandler, 2007). The analysis focused on how different modes of sign worked together to
create meaning. This combined approach aimed to provide a comprehensive understanding
of how Dior Beauty communicated through its Instagram advertisements. The results were
presented descriptively with examples from the data. To maintain reliability, the analysis was

4. Results and Discussion
This section discusses the findings of the study based on the analysis of signs found in
Dior Beauty’s Instagram advertisement. The analysis identifies three modes of signs according
to Chandler, namely iconic, indexical, and symbolic modes. Each of these modes contributes
to the construction of meaning and plays an important role in shaping Dior Beauty’s luxurious
brand identity on social media.
In the iconic mode, the signs work through visual similarity with real objects. The model
(Jisoo) and the Dior Beauty setting powder are presented in a realistic and detailed way, so
they directly resemble their real forms. The audience can clearly recognize the model’s face,
makeup, and the actual product’s shape. Because these elements look like the real things they
represent, they function as iconic signs. This mode helps the advertisement present the
product clearly and makes the promotion easy to understand. As a result, viewers can
immediately identify what product is being shown and who is representing the brand.
In the indexical mode, the signs show a direct connection between the sign and its
meaning, usually through a cause -and-effect relationship. The smooth, flawless skin shown
on Jisoo’s face is presented as the visible result of using Dior Beauty setting powder. The skin
condition functions as evidence of the product’s performance, linking the product to the
outcome. Because the facial appearance indicates the effect of the product, it b elongs to the
indexical mode. This use of indexical signs strengthens the advertisement’s message by
showing the real impact of the product, which makes the claim more convincing to the
audience.
In the symbolic mode, the signs depend on shared social understanding rather than visual
similarity. The Dior brand logo “CD” is an example of a symbolic sign in the advertisement.
This element does not physically resemble the brand itself, but people recognize it through
cultural knowledge and agreement. Their presence provides brand identity, authenticity, and
prestige. By using symbolic signs, the advertisement communicates ideas of luxury, elegance,
and high status associated with Dior Beauty. This strengthens the brand image and positions
the product as part of a premium beauty brand.

communicate product characteristics and visual messages directly to the audience. In Dior
Beauty advertisements, these signs can be observed thr ough images of beauty products,
models, and other visual elements that resemble their real -world counterparts. The data of
these iconic elements are explained in the following section.

signs show a direct relationship between the sign and the reality they represent, which makes

3. Symbolic mode
Symbolic mode refers to signs whose meanings are established through social
conventions, cultural values, and shared understanding rather than visual resemblance or
direct connections with the objects they represent. These signs require people to learn and
recognize their meanings through social interaction and cultural experience. In advertising,
symbolic signs communicate abstract ideas and values associated with a brand. In Dior Beauty
advertisements, these signs represent concepts such as luxury, elegance, exclusivity, and high
social status. The Dior brand logo, for example, symbolizes the brand's identity and the values
associated with its products. Therefore, analyzing symbolic signs helps explain how Dior
Beauty adve rtisements communicate brand identity and construct meaning beyond the
products' functional purposes.  The meanings of these symbols are reinforced through
repeated exposure to the brand across different advertising platforms. As a result, symbolic
elements can influence how consumers perceive the brand and associate its products with
particular lifestyles and social values. The following section presents the analysis of symbolic
elements identified in the advertisement.

A primary limitation of this research is the restricted sample size because this research
only focuses on two specific advertisements featuring Jisoo and Jenna Ortega. This finding
may not fully represent the semiotics strategies employed across Dior Beauty’s entire digital
marketing portfolio. Thus, generalization regarding how the brand deliver its value cannot be
drawn due to this limitation. To address the limitation, it is recommended to employ mix -
method study with a bigger sample size.

5. Conclusion
Based on the analysis of Dior Beauty Instagram advertisements, the findings show that
each image contains three modes of signs that work together to deliver the promotional
message. In terms of Chandler’s semiotic theory, two iconic signs are found in each  image,
namely the model’s face and the cosmetic product, because both look like their real forms and
can be recognized directly by the audience, and their meaning is to show the product and
beauty realistically and believably . One indexical sign appears through the visible beauty
effect, such as smooth matte skin in the setting powder advertisement and bold red lips in the
lipstick advertisement, which show a cause-and-effect relationship between the product and
the result, meaning that the product is presented as the reason for the beauty outcome. One
symbolic sign is also present, seen in the Dior logo, brand name, and Instagram username,
which represent brand identity through shared social understanding, and their meaning is to
signal authenticity and strengthen brand recognition. Overall, the analysis shows that each
Dior Beauty advertisement image consistently combines iconic (2), indexical (1), and symbolic
(1) signs to communicate that Dior Beauty products are strongly as sociated with elegant,
flawless, and ideal beauty.
