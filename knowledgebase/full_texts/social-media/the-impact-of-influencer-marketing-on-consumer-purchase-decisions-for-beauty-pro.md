Abstract: Purpose: This study compares the effect of influencer
marketing on consumers' purchase decisions for beauty products
between the current generation and the millennial generation .
Influencer marketing, in which people with a sizable online
following work with brands to promote items through relatable ,
genuine content, is one of the most potent recent developments in
marketing, especially in the beauty product industry . The study
aims to determine whether influencer marketing through social
media such as YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook  affects the
purchase decisions of Millennial and Gen Z consumers.  Design:
The study uses primary data collected from Millennial and Gen Z
consumers through a structured questionnaire. The data were
analysed using statistical techniques to examine the effect of
influencer marketing on their purchase decision.   Findings: The
findings indicate how influencer marketing influences  beauty-
product purchase decisions and reveal differences between the two
consumer groups. Research limitations/implications:  The study is
limited to selected consumers, beauty products and social media
platforms. Future studies may consider other product categories
and consumer groups.  Practical implications: The findings
provide implications for beauty brands in developing influencer
marketing strategies for different consumer groups.  Social
implications: The findings may help consumers make informed
purchase decisions and help influencers and beauty brands
develop appropriate influencer marketing strategies for different
consumer gro ups. Originality/value: The study contributes
comparative evidence on influencer marketing and beauty-product
purchase decisions among Millennial and Gen Z consumers.
Keywords: Gen Z, Millennial, Influencer Marketing , Beauty
Products, Social Media, Purchase Decisions.
I. INTRODUCTION
Marketing has changed dramatically in recent years
because of digital technology and social media networking
sites. Consumers now prefer buying online, and before they
decide to buy, they are influenced by reviews and
recommendations on websites . These networking websites
make them aware of brands and products and shape their
attitudes toward them. Once digitalised, companies use both
brick-and-mortar and online platforms to sell their products
to specific groups. This approach can be effective for
advertising, building credibility, and personalisation.

  Influencer marketing has grown as a result of this shift,
with people with large social media followings collaborating
with companies to promote their products and services.
Influencer marketing uses individual voices, music, stories,
and personal connections to reach small and large audiences.
They also share their experiences to promote the brands they
work with.
One marketing sales promotion activity is influencer
marketing, which differs from celebrity advertising because
influencers have long -term, direct contact with their
followers, making it possible to build  trust and affinity
between the influencer and the audience. If followers or
consumers see the influencer as a credible source or genuine
person rather than a paid promoter, it can undermine the
credibility of the influencer's opinion. This trust, credibility,
and interaction through likes, comments, and produ ct videos
enable influencers to shape consumer buying behaviour in the
beauty products industry.
The beauty product industry focuses on hair care, skin care,
and grooming products that receive significant consumer
attention through influencer marketing. These products often
rely on sensory cues in online influencer recommendations to
influence buying decisions . Beauty product companies
collaborate with influencers to demonstrate products and their
benefits. This is because, with the speed of digital exposure
and changing lifestyles, the global beauty industry is  valued
at 600 billion US dollars. Typica lly, when consumers are
influenced by advertising or people they know, they buy a
brand's product, but now reviews and real -time product
demonstrations by influencers influence consumers' purchase
intentions and decisions . Some products, such as facial hair
removal cream, have consumers preferring to buy after seeing
someone demonstrate and share their experiences.
Millennial and Gen Z consumers are influenced differently
in their purchase decisions . Gen Y (Millennials) grew up at
the beginning of the Internet, are 28 -44 years old, and value
brand loyalty, product value and quality, and familiarity with
social media platforms. Gen Z, by contrast, were born and
grew up in a highly digital environment, are always on social
media, and are described as having more impulsive buying
behaviour than  Gen Y or Millennials. This group is often
easily influenced by influencers who are also Gen Z and share
their experiences using the product and its benefits.

engagement, and content relevancy. These studies show that
customers are more receptive to influencers who promote
products aligned with their personality, speciality, and online
presence. High-quality influencer content drives purchase
decisions.
C. Emotional Connection and Consumer Purchase
Behaviour
Zhou et al. [8] examined fashion and beauty influencers on
Instagram and highlighted the role of parasocial interactions
and parasocial relationships in strengthening followers’
engagement. According to Lin [6], Gen Z consumers form
stronger emotional connections with influencers than Gen Y
(Millennials). Overall, Gen Z is more likely to feel connected
to influencers through social media . Past research suggests
that Gen Z consumers tend to make impulsive purchases and
that influencers influence their decisions. In contrast,
Millennial consumers evaluate the benefits they would gain
from using the brand before making a purchase and are not
influenced by social media.   One such psychological
mechanism that helps influencers gain attention and create a
positive opinion about them in the buyer's mind, which
eventually affects buying decisions, is emotional connection.

to evaluate product quality.
D. Research Gap
The literature reviews show that factors like credibility,
authenticity, influencer -product fit, content quality, and
emotional connections significantly affect the effectiveness
of influencer marketing and, in turn, consumer purchase
decisions. Most studies to date have examined these factors
separately and across different consumer groups or industry
segments. Still, only a handful have investigated the impact
of influencer marketing on beauty product purchase
decisions.  In addition, few studies have assessed  the impact
of influencer marketing on Gen Z and Millennials, which is
one of the most prevalent trends in generational marketing.
This empirical study focuses on the target audience  for
influencer marketing: consumers.

IV. METHODOLOGY
This study uses a descriptive research design to examine the
effect of influencer use on beauty product purchase decisions
among Gen Z and Millennial consumers. The researchers
used purposive sampling, a non-probability method based on
predetermined age criteria suitable for the study's objectives .
Because the study focuses on generational differences, the
researchers considered the demographic features of
consumers exposed to influencer marketing for beauty
products when selecting respondents.  170 respond ents
participated in the study. The researchers collected primary
data through a structured questionnaire via Google Forms and
secondary data from journals, research articles, books,  and
other relevant sources . The study used a five -point Likert
scale ranging from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree)
to measure the study variables. The demographic profile of
the respondents was analysed using statistical tools such as
descriptive statistics; reliability analysis was used to examine
the internal consi stency of the instrument ; correlation
analysis was applied to examine the relationship between the
factors of influencer marketing and purchase decisions for
beauty products ; multiple regression analysis was used to
determine the influence of influencer marketing on purchase
decisions; and independent-samples t-test and ANOV A were
applied to examine the differences between Gen Z and
millennial consumers.
V. SCOPE OF THE STUDY
The study aims to explore the effect of influencer marketing
on purchase decisions for beauty products such as skin care ,
hair care & grooming products among consumers. It focuses
on the impact of social media influencers on Instagram,
YouTube, and Facebook. The study also examines Gen Z and
Millennial consumers' perceptions and buying habits
regarding influencer credibility, authenticity, content quality,
emotional connection, and influencer-product fit.
VI. LIMITATIONS OF THE STUDY
The study only includes beauty products - skincare, haircare
and grooming products – so findings may not be generalisable
to other product categories, like electronics, apparel or
services. The findings may not represent the general
population because the sample size (170) was based on
purposive sampling. Additionally, social media trends and
how influencers market products or services may shift,
influencing consumer perceptions and purchase decisions.

Authenticity did not statistically significantly affect
purchase decisions (β = 0.030, p > 0.05). This indicates that
while authenticity alone may not directly affect purchases, it
could be combined with other attributes, such as credibility
and content quality, to influence purchase decisions .
Interestingly, influencer–product fit is negatively and
significantly related to purchase decisions ( β = -0.170, p <
0.05), suggesting that consumers may consider beauty
products based on their individual requirement s and
preferences, not just the match between the influencer and the
product.
Overall, the findings suggest that influencer marketing
factors significantly affect consumers' buying decisions in the
beauty product industry . Hence, the null hypothesis is
rejected.

The descriptive statistics indicate that  mean purchasing
decisions differ across  the various social media websites.
Facebook has the lowest mean (Mean = 2.7500), while other
platforms have the highest  (Mean = 3.5000),  followed by
Instagram (Mean = 2.9722). This implies that, compared with
more established platforms like Facebook, consumers are
more influenced by newer  platforms. We analysed these
changes using one -way ANOV A to determine statistical
significance. The results indicate a significant difference in
purchases across platforms (p < 0.05), suggesting that
platform selection can strongly influence customer
purchases. Further analysis using the Games-Howell post hoc
test indicates large differences between all platform pairs.
Participants in the 'Other' social media platform category had
significantly higher purchase decision scores than those in the
Facebook and Instagram categories, suggesting that emerging
social media channels may influence consumer purchase
decisions more strongly  than Facebook and Instagram.
Moreover, Instagram and Facebook differ significantly (p =
0.002), meaning Instagram has a greater effect on purchasing
decisions than Facebook. Overall, the findings indicate that
influencer marketing ef ficiency varies by platform, with
Instagram and other platforms proving more effective at
influencing consumers' purchasing decisions than Facebook.
VII. FINDINGS
The results show that influencer marketing strongly
influences consumer purchase decisions in the beauty product
industry. The correlation analysis showed positive
relationships between credibility, authenticity, fit, quality,
emotional connection, and actual purchasing decisions.
Further, the regression analysis indicates that content quality,
credibility and emotional connection significantly affect
consumers' buying decisions, with content quality being the
most important factor. However, authenticity did not have a
significant direct influence on purchases.
The study also finds generational differences in how
effective influencer marketing is. Gen Z consumers were
more responsive to influencer marketing than Millennials in
terms of purchase decisions, authenticity, influencer -product
fit, and content quality . The ANOV A results also show
significant differences in purchase decisions by  social media
platform, underscoring the need to consider the platform
when planning campaigns. Overall, the results support the
idea that influencer marketing can effectively inf luence
consumers' purchase decisions for beauty products,
especially among younger generations.
VIII. SUGGESTIONS
Based on these results, beauty companies should work with
influencers who are reliable, believable, and can create high -
quality content . Because content quality proved to be the
most significant influencer in purchase decisions, influencers
should deliver valuable, informative, and engaging content
that helps consumers make informed buying decisions.
The study also emphasises the impact of emotional
connection on customer purchasing decisions. Thus,
influencers should share real experiences and product demos
to help consumers feel connected and trust them more. Gen Z
consumers were more open to influencer marketing than their
Millennial counterparts, so marketers need to create Gen Z-
focused campaigns and use the right social media channels.
Businesses should also target platforms that encourage
interaction and engagement to make their influencer
marketing efforts as effective as possible.
IX. CONCLUSION
The study concludes that influencer marketing strongly
influences consumers' buying
decisions regarding beauty
products. The results indicate
