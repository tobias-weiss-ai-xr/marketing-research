  ABSTRACT
This s tudy aims to analyze the effect of Content Marketing, Social Media
Interaction, and Consumer Trust on Purchase Decisions on the Tokopedia
platform, with a case study of students in Banda Aceh City. This research uses a
quantitative approach with a survey m ethod involving 96 respondents selected
through purposive sampling , and the data were analyzed using multiple linear
regression. The results show that partially and simultaneously, Content Marketing,
Social Media Interaction, and Consumer Trust have a positive and significant effect
on Purchase Decisions, with Consumer T rust being the most dominant variable.
This indicates that the better the quality of the content presented, the higher the
level of social media interaction, and the stronger the consumer trust, the higher the
purchase decisions on the e -commerce platform. Therefore, improving the quality
of content marketing, increasing active interaction, and strengthening consumer
trust are important factors in encouraging higher purchase decisions..

2. Literature Review
2.1 Purchase Decision
According to Kotler and Keller (2016), a pur-
chase decision refers to  the consumer's decision
regarding the brand to be purchased af ter going
through several stages, including need recogni-
tion, information search, evaluation of alterna-
tives, purchase decision, and post -purchase be-
havior. Purchase decisions represent the fin al
stage of the consumer decision -making process
after evaluating various aspects, such as product
brand, purchasing channel, purchase quantity,
purchase timing, and payment method.
Marpaung et al. (2024) explain that purchase
decisions are one manifestati on of consumer
behavior in using or consuming products, in
which consumers analyze various information
before determining their purchase decisions. Fur-
thermore, Kotler et al. (2021) state that purchase
decisions are influenced not only by rational fac-
tors but also by emotional and social aspects.
Therefore, purchase decisions can be understood
as the final decision made by consumers to pur-
chase a product after recognizing their needs,
searching for information, evaluating alternatives,
and selecting the pro duct considered most ap-
propriate.
In this study, purchase decis ions are meas-
ured using indicators adopted from Fatya et al.
(2024), namely product choice, brand choice,
choice of seller or distribution channel, purchase
timing, purchase quantity, and payme nt method.
These indicators were selected because they are
considered relevant to measuring purchase deci-
sions in the digital marketplace environment.
2.2 Content Marketing
According to the Content Marketing Institute
(2023), content marketing is a marketi ng strategy
that focuses on creating and distributing valuable,
relevant, and consistent content to attract, retain,
and encourage profitable consumer actions.
Content marketing involves the creation,
publication, and distribution of relevant and
engaging content to attract target audiences,
build relationships with potential consumers,
increase brand awareness, and influence
consumer behavior. Pebriany (2020) emphasizes
that content marketing plays an important role in
attracting visitors through relevant and engaging
content, which can generate initial interest and
potentially convert visitors into active customers.
Apriani and Bhaihaki (2024) explain that
content marketing aims to retain and influence
consumer behavior by providing valuable,
relevant, and  consistent information. Unlike
conventional promotional activ ities that focus
directly on sales, content marketing emphasizes
useful, educational, and entertaining information
to establish long -term relationships with
consumers. Karimah, Rinonce, and Syah roni
(2025) further explain that content marketing
strategies on social media aim to increase
consumer interaction and engagement, thereby
strengthening relationships and potentially
increasing consumer loyalty. Thus, content
marketing is not merely the pr oduction of
promotional content but a strategic approach to
creating value for consumers, which can
ultimately increase trust and purchase decisions.
The indicators of content marketing used in
this study are adopted from Chen et al. (2021) in
Christanti a nd Kembau (2024), consisting of
functional information, entert ainment information,
social and brand interaction, and self -concept.
These indicators were selected because they are
considered relevant to the context of the
research.
2.3 Social Media Interaction
Social media interaction refers to a two -way
communication process that occurs among users
or between companies and consumers through
digital platforms. Such interaction can take the
form of comments, likes, information sharing,
feedback, and responsiv eness to consumer
questions or complaints. Samudra (2024)
explains that social media facilitates
communication, collaboration, and rapid
information exchange, thereby enabling closer
relationships between companies and
consumers. Yusuf et al. (2023) furthe r state that
social media not only functions as a medium for
disseminating information but also facilitates
reciprocal communication that encourages user
engagement.
Based on Engagement Theory, active user
participation through commenting, liking content,
sharing information, and engaging in discussions
can strengthen relationships between consumers
and brands. Meanwhile, Social Interaction Theory
explains that continuous social interaction can

2.4 Consumer Trust
Consumer trust refers to the consumer's be-
lief that a seller or marketplace platform is capa-
ble of prov iding secure transactions, honest in-
formation, and products that meet expectations.
In a marketplace context, trust is particularly im-
portant because consumers cannot directly see
or inspect products before making a purchase.
Sobandi and Somantri (2020) de fine consumer
trust as the belief that a seller possesses in tegrity
and competence and is capable of fulfilling its
obligations according to consumer expectations.
Meanwhile, Novendra (2024) explains that con-
sumer trust is formed through experiences relat-
ed to product quality, seller reputation, and the
seller's ability to meet consumer needs.
Marpaung (2024) states that consumer trust is
related to beliefs regarding the attributes and
benefits attached to a product. Such beliefs can
shape consumer attitude s, including purchase
decisions and interest in a product. I n mar ket-
place platforms such as Tokopedia, consumer
trust is directed not only toward sellers but also
toward the platform itself. Trust is reflected in
consumers' perceptions of payment security,
seller credibility, product conformity with descrip-
tions, delivery reliability, and the platform's ability
to protect consumers when transaction problems
occur. The higher the level of consumer trust in
sellers and the platform, the greater the tendency
of consumers to make purchase decisions.
The indicators of co nsumer trust used in this
study are based on Nurmanah and Nugroho
(2021), consisting of ability, benevolence, and
integrity. These indicators were selected because
they are considered relevant to research con-
ducted in the context of digital commerce plat-
forms.

3.1 Research Design
This study employed an associative
quantitative research approach using an
explanatory research des ign. Explanatory
research aims to explain causal relationships
among research variables through hypothesis
testing. This study examines the effect of content
marketing (X1), social media interaction (X2), and
consumer trust (X3) on purchase decisions (Y)
among university students using Tokopedia in
Banda Aceh. According to Sugiyono (2022),
quantitative research is based on the positivist
paradigm and is used to examine a selected
population or samp le through statistical analysis
to test predetermined hypotheses.
The research was conducted in Banda Aceh,
with the research object consisting of content
marketing, social media interaction, consumer
trust, and purchase decisions. The unit of
analysis was  university students studying in
Banda Aceh who had used the  Tokopedia
marketplace platform.
3.2 Research Location and Time
This research was conducted in Banda Aceh,
Aceh Province, Indonesia. The research location
was selected because Banda Aceh is an edu ca-
tional center with university students from various
regions who  actively use digital platforms and
online marketplaces, including Tokopedia. There-
fore, Banda Aceh was considered relevant as the
research setting for examining the influence of
content mark eting, social media interaction, and
consumer trust on purc hase d ecisions among
university students using Tokopedia.
The research was conducted from October
2025 to August 2026. The research activities
included the preparation of the research pro-
posal, lit erature review, research instrument de-
velopment, proposal s eminar, questionnaire dis-
tribution, data collection, data analysis, prepara-
tion of the research report, and final examination.
3.3 Population and Sample
The population in this study consisted of ac-
tive university students in Banda Aceh who use
the Tokoped ia mark etplace. The population was
classified as an unknown population because the
exact number of university students who use To-
kopedia in Banda Aceh was not available.
The sample was determined us ing the Rao
Purba formula with a 95% confidence level (Z =
1.96) and a maximum margin of error of 10%.
Based on the calculation, the required sample
size was 96.04 respondents, which was rounded
to 96 respondents. The sampling technique used
was non -probability sampling with purposive
sampling. This technique was  selected because
the study required respondents who met specific
criteria relevant to the research objectives.
The criteria for selecting respondents were:
(1) active university students studying a t higher
education institutions in Banda Aceh; (2) having
made purchases on Tokopedia at least twice; (3)
actively using social media platforms such as
Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, or X; and (4) will-
ing to participate by completing the research
questionnaire.
3.4 Research Instrument
The research instrument used in this study
was a structured questionnaire developed based
on the indicators of each research variable. The
questionnaire was designed to obtain primary
data from university students who use Tokope dia
in Banda Aceh. The instrument consisted of
statements related to content marketing (X1),
social media interaction (X2), consumer trust

The questionnaire employed a Likert scale to
measure respondents' responses to each state-
ment. The scale was used to assess respond-
ents' perceptions and opinions regarding the var-
iables examined in the study. Each statement
was assigned a numerical score according to the
respondent's level of agreement.
The content marketing variable (X1)  was
measured through four indicators, namely func-

The social media interaction variable (X2) was
measured through the ease of sharing infor-
mation with other part ies, the ability to exchange
opinions among users, and th e ease o f express-
ing opinions through social media. The consumer
trust variable (X3) was measured through ability,
benevolence, and integrity.
The purchase decision variable (Y) was
measured based on the indicators specified in the
research instrument. All  indicators were translat-
ed into questionnaire statements that were rele-
vant to the context of Tokopedia and the purchas-
ing behavior of university students.
Before being used for data analysis, the ques-
tionnaire was tested to determine the quality of
the r esearch instrument. The instrument testing
consisted of validity and reliability tests. The va-
lidity test was conducted to determine whether

was conducted t o determ ine the consistency of
the instrument in measuring the research varia-
bles. The results of these tests were then used to
determine whether the questionnaire was appro-
priate for further statistical analysis

lidity, particular attention is given to the appropri-
ateness of the ins trument's content and its use-
fulness.
A validity test is  a process used to determine
how accurately a measurement instrument
measures the intended construct. The purpose of
this test is to ensure that the instrument used,
such as a questionnaire, is actual ly capable of
measuring the variables under investigatio n. The
validity test also aims to determine whether each
question or statement used in the research is
valid and appropriate for measuring the intended
variable.
The criteria for the validity test are as follows:

3. Classical Assumption Test
Classical assumption tests are conducted to
assess the feasibility of the regression model,
with the aim of ensurin g that the regression
results obtained can be appropriately used and
interpreted. In this study, the classical
assumption tests consist of the following:
Normality Test
The normality test is an important initial statis-
tical procedure used to determine whet her the
data for each research variable, namely Content
Marketing, Social Media Interaction, Consumer
Trust, and Purchase Decision, are normally dis-
tributed. Normality is an important requirement,
particularly when the subsequent data analysis
uses parametric statistical methods, such as Mul-
tiple Linear Regression, which assumes that the
data follow a normal distribution.
Based on the Kolmogorov –Smirnov test, the
decision criteria for determining normality are as
follows:
1. If the significance value (Sig.) > 0.05,
the data are considered normally dis-
tributed.
2. If the significance value (Sig.) < 0.05,
the data are considered not normally
distributed.
Multicollinearity Test
The multicollinearity test is conducted to de-
termine whether the independent variables in the
multiple linear regression model have a very
strong or perfect relationship with one another.
The presence of multicollinearity can be detected
by examining the Tolerance and Variance Infla-
tion Factor (VIF) values using SPSS software.
Tolerance indicates the proportion of variabil-
ity in an independent variable that is not ex-
plained by the other independent variables in the
regression model. In general, if the Tolerance
value > 0.10 or the VIF value < 10, it can be con-
cluded that there is no multicolline arity in the re-
gression model (Siswanto & Aryanto, 2024).
Heteroscedasticity Test
According to Syafrida (2021), the heterosce-
dasticity test aims to identify whether there is an
inequality of residual variances from one obser-
vation to another. In this study, the heteroscedas-
ticity test was conducted using the Spearman
correlation test, which aims to detect the pres-
ence of heteroscedasticity problems in the re-
gression model.
The decision-making criteria are based on the
probability value, with the following conditions:

2. If the significance value or probability ≤ 0.05,
