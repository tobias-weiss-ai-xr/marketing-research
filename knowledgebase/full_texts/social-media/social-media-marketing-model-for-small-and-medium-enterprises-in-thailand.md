Abstract
This study has examined how small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in Thailand utilise digital marketing tools
and technology to manage business risks and drive innovations . Th e study employs three key steps to
understand how Thai SME entrepreneurs perceive these factors and their impact on digital marketing. The
research develops a unique social media marketing approach tailored for Thai SMEs. Using survey data from
400 entrepreneurs, the study employs quantitative analysis with statistical techniques, including CFA and SEM,
to examine the interconnections between various elements . Findings revealed that technology, risk control,
innovation processes, and digital promotion are crucial for the success of SMEs . CFA tests confirm that these
factors are essential for effective digital marketing .  Moreover, experts  suggest that  technology and risk
influence digital marketing performance better when combined with the active use of new technology. Specific
government actions are needed to support SMEs in building stronger digital marketing capabilities.

1. Introduction
Information technology, as well as advances in wireless and wired communication,  are developing rapidly
(Rohmatin et al., 2021) . As a result, the way of life of human beings has changed, with information and
communication technology (ICT), especially the use of the Internet, playing an increasingly important role for
modern consumers who seek convenience and speed (Struck et al., 2018). As a result, consumers are limited to
their homes, causing them to turn to online shopping for  products. As a result, an increasing number of online
businesses can effectively meet the needs of consumers during this period (Sulaksono 2020) . This enables
companies to reconnect with marketing on the Internet by utilising digital marketing to offer products and
services, as well as various promotions,  to customers through social media marketing channels, which are
convenient, fast, and low-cost (Limna et al., 2022). The COVID-19 pandemic has significantly impacted people,
regardless of their occupation or type of business. In Thailand, SMEs have been particularly affected by the
COVID-19 pandemic and the government's lockdown measures, resulting in disrupted business operations .
Small and medium-sized SMEs need to have the potential to adapt to information and communication
technology, as well as management and marketing. Changes in all matters, especially information and
communication technology, come suddenly (Khalid and Naumova 2021).
The e-commerce business in Thailand has experienced significant growth (Muangmee 2021). A key factor is that
companies are now viewing e -commerce as the primary sales channel for reaching  consumers. Therefore, if
Businesses do not adapt to the times, it will affect their competitiveness, or new companies will take over the
business role  (Johan et al., 2019) . So is reaching out to consumers. Starting from the use of various
communication devices, such as mobile phones, iPads, and tablets, to facilitate consumers by presenting
products in video  format or through online transactions  (Narang and Arora 2016) . Communication with
consumers and customer touchpoints are important because new online channels, such as Facebook, Line,
Twitter, Instagram, and various applications, enable fast service (Hallikainen et al., 2019).
Currently, global society is transitioning into the digital era, where many actions require online technology as a
means of operation. Digital marketing and social media marketing are important parts of communication that
can create and convey content creatively  (Khalid and Naumova 2021) . Communication that has the power to
reach the audience and has the knowledge and understanding of marketing for the ability to pursue a career or
become an entrepreneur to be able to integrate news content, online technology, and marketing to communicate
as effectively and meet needs as possible (Tiago and Verí ssimo 2014). Reaching out to consumers, businesses,
and especially entrepreneurs is important. SMEs in Thailand started by developing consumer analysis
capabilities. In addition to segmenting customers by area, age, education level, and income, focus on technology
vision, risk awareness and management, technology innovation, and digital marketing (Corral de Zubielqui and
Jones 2023) to determine the guidelines or strategies for marketing through social media. Creating incentives

2. Literature review
2.1Vision in Technology
Organizations must have A clear technological vision to guide their technological development (Shields 2012).
Currently, the technological status of many agencies is being evaluated (Akçay Kasapoglu 2018). A well-defined
technological vision can enhance an organization’s understanding, visibility, and value (Klein, . It can also
facilitate aligning technological goals with policies, projects, and plans (Amelda et al., 2021) . To assess the
effectiveness of a technological vision, three key elements can be considered: creation, dissemination, and
implementation (Amelda et al., 2021).
2.2Risk Management
Entrepreneurs must consider risk management when addressing consumer concerns about online ordering.
Despite advancements in tools, equipment, and technology, consumers remain vigilant about potential risks
(Soleimani et al., 2016). Key areas of concern include financial risk (Johansson et al., 2019), privacy risk, product
risk, and time risk (Cao et al., 2018) . As Smith and Merritt (2020)  emphasised, effective risk management can
significantly impact consumer confidence and trust in online platforms.
2.3Technological Innovation
Technological innovation is the creation of new ideas or processes that enhance existing products or services
(Johansson et al., 2019) . Amabile (1988) defines innovation as generating novel and useful ideas. It requires
applying natural knowledge, science, and creativity to solve problems and develop innovative solutions (Akbar
et al., 2020) . Value creation is a key outcome of technological innovation, benefiting organisations and
customers. It entails adding value from the customer's perspective, such as improving product features or
reducing costs. Additionally, technological innovation can bring economic and social benefits to organisations
and communities (Singh and Hanafi 2020) . There are two primary types of technological innovation. First,
Product Innovation is the creation of new or significantly improved products or services. Second, Process
Innovation is the implementation of new or significantly improved production or delivery methods. By focusing
on these two areas, organisations can drive innovation and achieve competitive advantages .
2.4Digital Marketing
Digital marketing is a modern approach that utilises digital channels to communicate and build relationships
with target audiences (Sayyad 2020). This includes social media platforms (Facebook, Twitter, LINE, Instagram,
YouTube, etc.) and websites. Effective digital marketing can be assessed based on three key variables: appeal,
interaction, and confidence (Struck et al., 2018). The following Hypotheses can be deducted from the literature
review.
Hypothesis (H1): The technological vision has a direct positive influence on Digital Marketing
Hypothesis (H2): Risk management has a direct positive influence on Digital Marketing
Hypothesis (H3): Innovation and technology have a direct positive influence on Digital Marketing
Hypothesis (H4): Innovation technology can be a transmitter of the relationship between technology vision and
digital marketing.
Hypothesis (H5): Innovation Technology can be a transmitter of the relationship between risk management and
digital marketing.

3. Methodology
This study investigated how technological vision affects social media marketing models with Thailand -based
small and medium -sized businesses while examining technological innovation and risk management
effectiveness. The research investigation took place through different steps to validate its results.
3.1 Research Design
This study  uses a quantitative method to examine how technological vision, risk control, and technological
innovation impact social media marketing success. The study employs a formal survey questionnaire as its main
data-collecting instrument. Structural Equation mo delling (SEM) helps  analyse multiple connected variables
(Beier and Wagner 2016).
3.2 Target Population and Sample Size
The study only studied SMEs in Thailand that were marketing using social media platforms.  A sample size of
400 SMEs joined the study through random sampling to get businesses from across all fields. Our research
included firms currently running digital marketing operations involving  at least one social media platform ,
including Facebook, Instagram, LINE, and Twitter.
3.3 Research Instrument
The research team designed a well -organized survey instrument based on a thorough analysis of relevant
scientific literature and statistical data produced in the field of business research. The survey tool was carefully
developed to fit the conceptual model of the study, making a comprehensive assessment of four areas. The
technological vision, the way we handle the risks, the development of the technologies, and the scale of influence
that social media marketing has. The instrument leans towards both qualitative and quantitative aspects , thus
a neutral approach to examining the different aspects of digital marketing among SMEs. In considering the

3.4 Validity and Reliability Testing
The research instrument was found to be reliable and valid based on intensive testing process. To confirm that
the survey captured the dimensions of interest for the study, specialists in digital marketing and technology
management were engaged and through  them, the question items were affirmed to be direct measures of the
required study areas. Furthermore, the researchers performed a robust test of survey questions to ensure that
the survey questions indeed accurately measure the underlying theoretical con structs of digital marketing and
technology management. Cronbach’s alpha (α) test was used to examine the internal consistency of the survey
items. Strong reliability, confirmed the survey instrument was dependable, and in order to collect valid data the
recommended Cronbach’s alpha results of over 0.7 was observed from Cortina (1993). This strong validation
process meant that the instrument would be able to measure the determinants affecting the digital marketing
outcomes for SMEs in the case of Thailand.
3.5 Data Collection Procedure
We distributed our survey online and offline to get responses from a wide range of participants. The researchers
explained step -by-step how to fill out the questionnaire to get information from participants. Our research
teams gathered business responses  over two months to obtain enough participants from multiple small
enterprise sectors.
3.6 Data Analysis Technique
The research team used SEM statistical modelling to analyse the multiple variable connections within the
dataset, according to Fan et al., (2016) . Confirmatory Factor Analysis examines if our measurement tools
accurately capture the studied subjects.  Our study employs SEM to verify how technological vision, risk
management, and technological innovation influence social media marketing results directly and indirectly.
3.7 Ethical Considerations
The study met ethical research standards by protecting participants' right to privacy and getting their
permission. Everyone could participate in the study without being identified to keep business information safe.

5. Discussion
A study about Thai SMEs' social media marketing strategies has revealed significant findings that improve
knowledge of digital marketing success determinants. Many Thai SMS decision-makers are educated female
professionals serving as department heads and managers. The majority of total revenue at these companies
comes from their domestic market operations rather than export sales , which total less than 10%. Thai SMSEs
target their home market because consumers show strong buying power , and social media lets them promote
their products effectively (Muangmee et al., 2023) . Exporting to foreign markets becomes difficult because of
high shipping expenses combined with official rules and constrained production space, which makes operating
within the home market more practical. The high usage rates of social media platforms , including Instagram,
Facebook, Line, and Shopee, by respondents, confirm digital channels' importance in current business strategies
for marketing and customer engagement (Cheung et al., 2020).
Several essential elements that influence digital marketing adoption emerged through the statistical analysis of
the study. Findings showed that  technological vision stands as the fundamental factor because businesses
allocate their highest value points to vision implementation and then distribution and generation processes.
The SMEs implement their technology methods efficiently as their first step before advancing to the
communication and innovation growth stages (Klein 2020) . Risk management proved important due to
financial risk-taking precedence over product risk, privacy risk, and time risk. Businesses demonstrate financial
risk management alongside data protection and product security concerns, which drives their selection for this
ranking (Baima et al., 2022) . Among the key factors was technological innovation , which placed product
innovation above process innovation. Research demonstrates that SMEs use their capital to develop new
products and optimize manufacturing processes to maintain  their business position (Ismanu et al., 2021). The
implementation of digital marketing platforms produced outstanding results regarding customer engagement ,
along with attraction and impression creation, according to Beier and Wagner (2016).
The structural equation model analysis demonstrated that the mentioned factors substantially influence what
drives SMSEs to adopt digital marketing strategies. The research established that technological vision , risk
management, and technological innovation  act as major drivers that develop digital marketing strategies. The
business world places a priority on innovative strategy formation within technological vision, where vision

(Cao et al., 2018) . Digital marketing efficiency depends first on customer interaction and second on attention
attraction, according to analysis,  which demonstrates the need for engagement -based marketing strategies
(Hitchen et al., 2017).
The findings revealed that technological innovation exhibited the strongest direct effect on digital marketing
performance. Rohmatin et al., (2021)  demonstrate that digital marketing success depends mostly on
technological innovation, which surpasses both technological vision and risk management as key factors.  The
hypothesis testing confirmed the discovered relationships in these results. The data confirmed Hypothesis H1
through statistical validation that technological vision creates a positive relationship with digital marketing. A
clear technological vision , as identified in prior studies,  enables digital strategies to develop structure while
making marketing more innovative (Klein 2020) . The study indicated a strong support for H2 about how
effective risk management enables businesses to use digital platforms for sustained customer engagement
(Baima et al., 2022) . The results endorse H3 , demonstrating that technological innovation directly impacts
digital marketing because it drives customer engagement  (Cheung et al., 2020) . Furthermore, technological
innovation speeds up the relationship between technological vision and digital marketing (H4). The study
findings proved this theory because firms that unite visionary leadership with transformative strategic
approaches achieve better digital marketplace performance (Hitchen et al., 2017) . The findings of H5
demonstrate how technological innovation strengthens the risk management and digital marketing relationship
because it helps businesses mitigate risks while achieving seamless digital transformation (Rohmatin et al.,

6. Conclusion
6.1 Findings of the study
Factors affecting the effectiveness of digital marketing in SMEs of Thailand were extensively studied in this
study. Through investigation of the central elements like the technological vision, adoption, risk management ,
and technological innovation, the study tried to find out how these factors influence the effectiveness of digital
marketing strategies. The study tested various relations among the variables using a structural equation model
(SEM) and approved the resear ch hypotheses. The results indicate that a large proportion of SME leaders in
Thailand are female professionals, and the most popular marketing channels chosen are: Instagram, Facebook,
Line, and Shopee. Such platforms were selected because of their easy availability and popularity in the region ,
which makes SMEs reach a bigger customer set more conveniently. From the findings, it seems that SMEs need
to have a clear vision in digital marketing for them to succeed , and this turns out to be the most impactful one
for positive marketing results. The study also highlights the need to control financial and product-related risk,
which was found to be the key areas contributing to the success of digital marketing initiatives. SMEs that
adopted technological innovations and developed new products could get better marketing results , thus
reiterating the need to incorporate technological adoption into business growth.  As depicted in the study,
customer interaction makes the greatest impact on the success of digital marketing. This finding heightens the
necessity for effective customer engagement amongst SMEs to establish strong mutually trusting relationships
that will contribute to loyalty and increased brand visibility. In addition, the study indicates that poor SMEs in
Thailand face challenges that will become more difficult to address if they are left to sho w only leadership
support for digital transformation and build on technological innovation. This entails a well-rounded safety
strategy that caters to financial and data security concerns that will be critical in winning the customer’s trust
and security of business transactions. The study also advocates for a higher support level, on the part of public
authorities and business organizations , to assist SMEs in developing  their digitized marketing competencies.
Governments & business groups have to educate SMEs about digital marketing strategies, with the investment
in educating them and giving financial help to allow them to implement the new technologies. With these
needed resources, training, and funding , these entities can be able to contribute significantly to helping SMEs
thrive in Thailand and become competitive in the digital massive market. To remain competitive, SME’s need to
optimize customer engagement, break through new technologies, as well as establish solid safety measures to
cover their operations and customers' data. Cooperation between SMEs, public authorities, and business

6.2 Limitations of the study
Although this study offers useful findings on the elements that have an impact on the effectiveness of social
media marketing of SMEs in Thailand. Some limitations are also associated with this study. First, the research
depends on self -reported data from a survey of 400 Thai SMEs. This might bring biases into the responses,
because the participants may give desirable answers, or the data may represent the views on more digitally
active businesses rather than a proper sample of all SMEs. Also, the research examines the SMEs only that use
social media platforms to market themselves, dropping those that may depend on traditional means of
marketing. This limitation reduces the generalizability of the findings to a far wider range of SMEs. Additionally,
the cross-sectional nature of the study does not allow for the detection of time variance, restricting the ability
of the study to explore the long-term impact of technological vision, risk management, and innovation on digital
marketing outcomes.
6.3 Future Study Recommendations
Future research should investigate a longitudinal design to determine how the variables that define digital
marketing effectiveness change over time, especially regarding emerging technologies and market dynamics.
Moreover, a diverse sample consisting of S MEs across different industries and at different levels of digital
engagement can present a better picture of the digital marketing landscape in Thailand. It is also suggested to
discuss the place of digital marketing strategies in SMEs’ internationalizati on activities, especially in the issue
of export to foreign markets. Further research could also investigate the effect of certain social media platforms
on the effectiveness of digital marketing and customer engagement. In addition, research into the fusi on of
artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning in the SME marketing strategies would offer a wealth of useful
information about future technological innovations that can be useful in the improvement of the marketing
outcomes. Finally, the results from the analysis of the effects of government policies and external supports on
SME implementation of digital marketing tools might provide directions for policymakers seeking sustainable
expansion in the SME industry.
