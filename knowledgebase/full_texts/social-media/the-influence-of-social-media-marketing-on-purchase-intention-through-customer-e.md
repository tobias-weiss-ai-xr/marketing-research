This study examines the influence of social media
marketing on purchase intention among
Indonesian marketplace users, with customer
experience serving as a mediating variable. A
quantitative approach was employed, using a
survey of 150 active marketplace u sers collected
in 2025, and the data were analysed using PLS -
SEM. The results indicate that social media
marketing has a significant and positive effect on
both customer experience and purchase
intention. Furthermore, customer experience has
a significant and positive effect on purchase
intention and partially mediates the relationship
between social media marketing and purchase
intention. These findings underscore the
importance of delivering positive customer
experiences to enhance the effectiveness of
marketing efforts.

INTRODUCTION
The advancement of digital technology and the rise in social media usage
have transformed consumption patterns and the way consumers conduct
transactions, leading marketplaces to emerge as one of the primary channels in
digital commerce activities. In this context, social media no longer functions not
just as a communication medium but also as a strategic marketing strategy that
influences consumers' purchasing intentions, perceptions, attitudes, and brand
awareness. Social media serves as the starting poin t of the customer journey,
where consumers seek information and evaluate products before making
purchasing decisions on marketplaces. Therefore, consumer activities on social
media, both directly and indirectly, influence the success of transactions on
marketplace platforms (Anugrah et al., 2023; Handayani, 2023) . The function of
social media has evolved beyond merely facilitating interpersonal
communication; it has become a strategic marketing tool used by companies and
business actors to influence consumers’ decision-making processes, ranging from
the formation of brand awareness and the creation of interest to the emergence
of purchase intentions. This goal is achieved through various SMM activities,
such as the delivery of informative and interactive content, the dissemination of
e-WOM, the use of influencer marketing, and live streaming practices that enable
real-time interaction between sellers and consumers  (Anggraheni & Haryanto,
2023; Hidayati et al., 2024) . International open -access research indicates that
SMM activities, which encompass the delivery of informative, interactive, and
persuasive content as well as the use of eWOM, influencer marketing, and live
streaming features, have a major impact on how customer attitudes, perceptions,
and behaviors are formed, especially when it comes to social commerce and
digital marketplaces, where social media serves as the initial point of interaction
between consumers and brands prior to making purchase decisions (Dincer &
Dincer, 2023; Ji & Ko, 2022; Mensah et al., 2023) . However, the effectiveness of
Social Media Marketing is not determined solely by the intensity of promotion
but also by the quality of the customer experience during interactions with digital
content and brand communication, as well as online platform sy stems and
services, all of which collectively shape consumers’ perceptions of ease of use,
clarity of information, security, convenience, and value, commonly referred to as
Customer Experience (Felix & Rembulan, 2023; Zikry et al., 2024) . “A positive
Customer Experience, reflected in the ease of platform use, the clarity and
completeness of product information, the perceived level of transaction security,
as well as consumer comfort during interactions with digital systems and the
services provided, has been demonstrated to be vital in influencing customers'
attitudes, perceptions, and trust in marketplace platforms and brands, greatly
boosting Purchase Intention in the context of online transactions and e -
commerce” (Li et al., 2022; Peng et al., 2023) . In the context of marketplaces,
customer experience is not only shaped by the quality of the platform’s systems
and features but is also influenced by cross-platform experiences that begin with
consumers’ initial interactions with marketing content on social media, followed
by the transition to product pages, the evaluation of reviews and
recommendations from other users, and extend to the ease of transactions,

THEORETICAL REVIEW
The Relationship between Social Media Marketing and Customer Experience.
The term “social media marketing” (SMM) describes marketing initiatives
that leverage social media platforms to produce, share, and provide customers
with value through educational, interactive, and persuasive content. Through
Social Media Marketing, compa nies not only focus on product promotion but
also build interactions and long -term relationships with consumers, which can
shape customer perceptions and experiences from the pre -purchase stage
(Widasmara et al., 2024) . In the digital and marketplace context, social media
often serves as the initial point of interaction between consumers and brands;
therefore, customers' first impression is greatly influenced by the caliber of social
media marketing initiatives.
Commonly used indicators of SMM include informativeness, interactivity,
entertainment, and credibility. The degree to which social media material offers
clear, comprehensive, and relevant product information, thereby helping
consumers understand the produc t. Interactivity reflects the level of two -way
communication between the brand and consumers through comments, replies,
and other interactive features. Credibility denotes the degree of customer
confidence in the information and the message's source, where as entertainment
refers to the capacity of material to provide enjoyment and emotional appeal.
SMM activities that are implemented in an informative, interactive, and credible
manner have been shown to create a positive experience for consumers before
they engage in transactions on marketplaces (Mohamed Wagih Abd El Aziz,
2024; Ranti, 2025).
H1: “Social Media Marketing has a positive and significant effect on
Customer Experience.”

The Relationship between Customer Experience and Purchase Intention
The term “customer experience” describes the general opinions and
reactions that customers develop during their pre -buy, purchase, and post -
purchase contacts with a business or platform, both directly and indirectly. This

experience encompasses cognitive, emotional, sensory, and behavioural aspects
that simultaneously influence consumers’ evaluations of a brand or digital
platform (Ahmad et al., 2022). In the e-commerce and marketplace environment,
Customer Experience becomes a key factor that determines how consumers
evaluate the value they obtain from a transaction.
Commonly used indicators of Customer Experience include ease of use,
information quality, security and trust, and enjoyment. Ease of use refers to the
convenience experienced by consumers in using the platform, including
navigation and the transaction process. Information quality relates to the clarity
and accuracy of product information, while security and trust are associated with
consumers’ perceptions of safety and the protection of personal data. Enjoyment
reflects the level of comfort and emotional pl easure experienced during
interactions with the platform. A positive Customer Experience, reflected
through these indicators, has been shown to enhance consumer trust and
satisfaction, which in turn encourages purchase intention and repurchase
intention in online transactions (Ningsih & Sutedjo, 2024; Threstia et al., 2023).
H2: “Customer Experience has a positive and significant effect on Purchase
Intention.”

The Relationship between Social Media Marketing and Purchase Intention
Purchase Intention refers to consumers’ psychological propensity to buy a
good or service later on and is often used as a primary predictor of actual
purchasing behaviour in digital marketing (Ina Oktaviana Aktusin, 2024) .
Purchase intention is formed through the process of information evaluation,
perceived benefits, and consumers’ attitudes towards the brand or product
offered.
The desire to buy a product, the inclination to suggest it, and the thought of
making it a top option are all indicators of purchase intention. The desire to
purchase reflects consumers’ motivation to make a purchase; the willingness to
recommend indicates  a tendency to engage in positive word of mouth, while
considering a product as a primary choice reflects consumer preference over
alternative options. Engaging and informative Social Media Marketing activities
are able to increase brand awareness, shape p ositive attitudes, and strengthen
consumers’ purchase intention, thereby significantly influencing Purchase
Intention among marketplace and e -commerce users (Chrisniyanti & Fah, 2022;
Kusumo et al., 2022).
H3: “Social Media Marketing has a positive and significant effect on
Purchase Intention.”

The Relationship between Social Media Marketing and Purchase Intention
through Customer Experience
Customer experience is regarded as a key mechanism in the creation of
digital marketing research that shows how SMM affects Purchase Intention.
Effective social media marketing activities, such as delivering relevant,
responsive, and credible content, first create a positive experience for consumers
during their interactions with brands and digital platforms. This experience

subsequently influences consumers’ perceived value, trust, and satisfaction,
which ultimately drives purchase intention.
Customer Experience acts as a mediating factor between SMM and
Purchase Intention, especially in the context of e -commerce and marketplaces.
This implies that social media marketing not only produces a positive and
dependable customer experience but also d irectly affects purchasing intention.
Consequently, the better a company's SMM efforts are, the more positive the
Customer Experience perceived by consumers, and consequently, the greater the
Purchase Intention that is formed (Habib, 2020; Kumar & Mishra, 2024).
H4: “Customer Experience positively and significantly mediates the effect
of Social Media Marketing on Purchase Intention.”

 Taken together, the discussion above shows that Social Media Marketing,
Customer Experience, and Purchase Intention are not independent constructs but
form an interconnected sequence within the consumer decision-making process.
Social Media Marketing acts  as the initial stimulus that shapes how consumers
perceive and engage with a brand on digital platforms; this engagement then
translates into a particular quality of Customer Experience, encompassing ease
of use, information quality, security and trust, a nd enjoyment; and this
experience, in turn, determines the strength of consumers’ Purchase Intention.
Viewed individually, each of these relationships (H1, H2, and H3) has received
empirical support in prior studies, yet most of these studies examine the
constructs in isolation or focus only on the direct path from Social Media
Marketing to Purchase Intention. By integrating these three constructs into a
single mediation model (H4), this study provides a more comprehensive
explanation of the mechanism through which Social Media Marketing influences
Purchase Intention, namely by first cultivating a positive Customer Experience.
This integrated perspective contributes to the digital marketing and consumer
behaviour literature by clarifying not only whether Soc ial Media Marketing
affects Purchase Intention, but also the experiential pathway through which this
effect occurs, thereby offering a more practical basis for marketplace managers
to design Social Media Marketing strategies that are deliberately oriented
towards strengthening Customer Experience.

METHODOLOGY
This study employs a quantitative research technique employing a non -
experimental and associative approach to examine the relationships and effects
among predetermined variables, namely Social Media Marketing (X), Customer
Experience (M), and Purchase Inte ntion (Y). A quantitative approach was used
since the study focuses on hypothesis testing using numerical and statistical data
to determine the extent to which independent factors affect the dependent
variable, both directly and indirectly through a mediat ing variable. Data from
respondents who are active marketplace users in Indonesia were gathered at a
specific point in time for this cross-sectional study. A closed-ended questionnaire
with a 1–5 Likert scale is used to gather primary data. The instrument was created
using accepted ideas and previous research, and it has completed validity and
reliability testing. All customers who have made purchases on marketplace sites
like Shopee, TikTok Shop, Tokopedia, and Lazada make up the study's
demographic. The s ampling method combines probability sampling with a
purposive sample strategy, where respondent criteria are established based on
social media usage and marketplace purchase experience, ensuring that the
selected respondents are relevant to the characteristics of the phenomenon under
study (Malhotra, 2020).
Using the most recent version of SmartPLS software, SEM was used for data
processing and analysis in order to evaluate the conceptual model and the
interactions between variables, including examining the role of customer
experience as a mediator in the rel ationship between purchase intention and
social media marketing. PLS-SEM was selected because it is suitable for research
models with complicated latent variables, data that might not have a normal
distribution, and relatively moderate sample sizes. As par t of the analytic
methods, the measuring model's construct reliability, discriminant validity, and
convergent validity are assessed. Additionally, the structural model is assessed
using bootstrapping with 5,000 samples to ascertain the relevance and streng th
of the correlations between variables. Additionally, a bootstrapping procedure is
used to test the mediating effect of Customer Experience in order to ascertain
whether the mediation path is statistically significant. Additionally, the model's
goodness of fit and the strength of relationships among constructs are evaluated
by interpreting R2, f2, Q2, and other effect size measures (Hair et al., 2022).

DISCUSSION
This study concludes that social media marketing significantly and
favorably affects customer experience based on the findings of Hypothesis 1
testing. This finding indicates that marketing activities on social media, when
delivered in an informative, interactive, and credible manner, are able to create a
positive experience for consumers from the pre-purchase stage. Logically, social
media serves as the initial point of interaction between consumers and brands as
well as marketplaces; therefore, the qualit y of content and communication
delivered will influence consumers’ perceptions of ease, convenience, and trust
in transactions. The study's conclusions are in line with those of other
investigations, which state that SMM significantly contributes to the
development of a positive Customer Experience on digital and e -commerce
platforms (Mohamed Wagih Abd El Aziz, 2024; Widasmara et al., 2024).
Furthermore, based on the testing of Hypothesis 2, Customer Experience
has been shown to significantly and favorably impact purchase intention. This
indicates that a favourable customer experience, such as ease of platform use,
clarity of product information, transaction security, and comfort throughout the
shopping process, can enhance trust and encourage consumers’ purchase

CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
According to the study's findings, social media marketing significantly and
favourably affects marketplace users' buy intentions and customer experiences.
Additionally, customer experience has a substantial favourable impact on
purchase intention and parti ally mediates the association between social media
marketing and buy intention, demonstrating both direct and indirect impacts.
These results emphasise the significance of delivering meaningful and engaging
customer experiences to enhance marketing effectiveness. Therefore, marketplace
managers and business practitioners are encouraged to prioritize clear, credible,
and interactive content, supported by responsive communication and seamless
platform integration, to strengthen consumer trust and purchase intention.
