Abstract
This study investigates the communication arts female students' perception and purchasing
behavior of human hair extensions. The study adopted a survey method with the questionnaire as
the instrument of data gathering.   The population of the study was the entire Female
Communication Arts Students which was tentatively put at 689 approximately. (This is however
subject to verification.)  This study is considered as a scoping study and therefore a sample size
of 80 female students were purposively drawn from two levels (third and final year in the regular
programme in the department on the basis of availability and willingness to participate in the
study. However, data was analyzed from 72 students who participated using WhatsApp’s
platform. The study was anchored on two theories namely: consumer behaviour theory and the
perception theory.  The study found that social media recommendations and online reviews are
the primary drivers of purchasing decisions, with 70% of participants relying on influencer
endorsements and 60% trusting online reviews. Quality, durability, and affordability were
identified as the key factors considered when purchasing hair extensions. The study also
revealed that female students prioritize hair extensions that match their natural hair texture and
color. It is recommended that hair manufacturers and marketers must develop strong and
aggressive social media policy to effectively engage with the female student demographic. The
results highlight the importance of social media in shaping perception and purchasing behavior,
emphasizing the need for brands to prioritize social media marketing strategies to remain
competitive in the market.

own hair for her hair to look and feel natural. There are different types of these human hair
extensions namely Brazilian, Indian and Peruvian hair; there are now other types such as
Venezuelan and Mongolian hair being sold in the market. The price of such hair extensions are
positively correlated to the length of hair (in inches) a woman wishes to buy; therefore the longer
the length of the hair extensions, the more expensive it will be. A pack of human hair extensions
contains either one or two 2 feet bundles of different lengths ranging between 8 to 30 inches.
Women all over the world must buy two or more packs of human hair depending on the look
they are going for as well as the length and volume they want. Thompson, (2018).

demographic and to pinpoint the motivation behind their rapid consumption in this market so as
to meticulously and exhaustively examine their consumer behavior. The answers to this question

Statement of the Problem
The demand for human hair extensions has increased significantly among young adults,
particularly female students. An increasing number of female students are turning to long and
silky human hair extensions to define their beauty as opposed to the other hairstyles such as
braids, permed hair, or leaving their hair in its natural state. However, there is a lack of
understanding of the perceptions and purchasing behavior among this demographic. What
reasons account for this preference of this hairstyle? How does the length of one’s hair define a
woman’s perception of what makes her look beautiful? This paper explores these questions on
female students who wear human hair extensions. It focuses on why these women opt to wear
such hair extensions as opposed to other the other hairstyles already mentioned. It will further
investigate the effects of this particular hairstyle has an effect on females’ students purchasing
decisions.

Hair Extensions Industry
A potentially $500billion dollar industry, is a currently a multi -million-dollar business according
to reports from Mintel. The demand of good quality hair is always there and is always growing.
The biggest exporters of hair extensions are India and China. With a total export value of
approximately 1,168,485,962 US dollars. From India it is mainly sourced from various temples
as offerings that are a common religious practice of immense importance. It is the starting point
of a multi-million-dollar business. Thousands of pilgrims make pilgrimages to specific temples;
most of them have nothing of value to offer but their hair. This religious practice is known as
‘’Tonsure’’ as way of showing gratitude and humility to the gods. Most of the donors have no
idea what the end result of this practice is exactly and it is repeated in hundreds of temples across
the Southern parts of India where tonnes of hair are gathered, sold, packaged and is dispatched to
middle men.
The hair’s value immediately raises at each different stage of middlemen its reaches. Most of
hair is cleaned and designed and packaged once again in factories in China. David A Gold is
widely believed to be the founder of the hair extension industry. He is an Italian British
entrepreneur who owns the company ‘Great Lengths’ which supplies top of the range extensions
to thousands of salons in over 50 countries globally.

is believed that consumer perception influence customer level of satisfaction and so their buying
and usage decisions.

Purchasing Decisions
The ideal outcome of a consumer decision is confirmation of a consumer’s expectations, which
triggers consumer satisfaction (Hawkins and Mothersbaugh, 2020). Purchase decisions are
however seldom straightforward and differ from one person to the next and from one product to
another (Ursic and Helgeson, 2019). Literature generally distinguishes extended (complex),
limited, and habitual (routine) decision -making (Hawkins and Mothersbaugh, 2020). Complex
purchase decisions typically require more extensive deliberation during the decision process for
whatever reason such as higher prices, the intricacy of the product’s performance or its social
visibility and therefore the decision cannot be rushed or concluded without additional
information that would support the final decision and reduce the possibility of a negative post
purchase outcome. A purchase decision is eventually influenced by multiple factors that
contribute to a consumer’s risk perception, specifically the type of risk and how the risk is
eventually dealt with and resolved.
Gronhaug (2018) for example operationalizes major household decisions in terms of the fraction
of the budget available for the purchase, which severely limits their discussion of the complexity
to financial risk. Complex decision -making generally requires higher personal involvement and
implies a significant amount of risk. Choosing expensive, visually significant products and
products with complex features generally requires higher cognitive effort and when the
functional utility of a product is predominant; decisions may be more rational and deliberated
(Bettman, 2018). For example, when purchasing a car or a home, a consumer mostly has many
options to choose from, this may be overwhelming. Both these products however also require a
consideration a multitude of attributes that have considerable cost implications. This explains
why a home and a car is typically used by scholars to explain the complexity of purchase
decisions. Chandrashekaran and Jagpal, (2022). Similarly, service -related decisions would be
particularly complex due to their intangibility and difficulty to anticipate what one is actually
paying for. Compared to a home, a car, a washing machine and a hair dryer, it is much more
difficult to evaluate a service for example life insurance because it might take quite some time to
experience actual evidence of their service offering. Consumers may furthermore struggle due to
limited processing capacity, for example lower educated consumers or young and inexperienced
consumers who lack relevant product knowledge and related experience to evaluate product
alternatives rationally.

Consumer Behavior Theory
Consumer Behavior Theory explores the processes and influences that lead individuals to make
purchasing decisions. This theory encompasses a variety of psychological, social, and cultural
factors that drive consumer choices. Consumer Behavior Theory offers valuable insights into
how these young women form attitudes towards hair extensions and what motivates their buying
decisions. John Howard and Jagdish Sheth developed the Howard -Sheth Model of Buyer
Behavior in the 1960s, which laid the groundwork for understanding consumer decision
processes. Scholars agree that understanding consumer behavior is crucial for developing
effective marketing strategies. They also emphasize the dynamic nature of consumer behavior,
which can change with technological advancements and cultural shifts.
One of the key elements of Consumer Behavior Theory is the role of social influence and peer
pressure in shaping consumer decisions. Female students are particularly susceptible to the
opinions and behaviors of their peers. Hair extensions can be perceived as a symbol of social
status or beauty, making them highly desirable in social settings such as schools and universities.
Peer influence can lead to a bandwagon effect where students adopt similar purchasing behaviors
to fit in with their social groups. Social media platforms amplify this effect by showcasing
influencers and celebrities who popularize the use of hair extensions, further encouraging female
students to emulate these trends. Consumer Behavior Theory also highlights the importance of
self-concept and identity in purchasing behavior.
Female students often use products like hair extensions to express their personal identity and
enhance their self-image. Hair extensions can offer a way to experiment with different looks and
styles, allowing students to align their appearance with their desired self -image. The use of hair
extensions can boost self -esteem and confidence, making them an appealing purchase for those
looking to enhance their physical appearance. Understanding this psychological motivation helps
explain why female students are drawn to human hair extensions and how these products fit into
their broader sense of self.

Perception Theory
Perception Theory focuses on how individuals interpret sensory information to form a coherent
understanding of their environment and experiences. Perception Theory offers significant
insights into how these young women perceive, evaluate, and decide on hair extension products.
Gustav Fechner, a pioneer in psychophysics studied the relationship between physical stimuli
and sensory perceptions which led to the theory in 1884. Scholars agree that perception is a
complex process influenced by both physiological and psychological factors. They highlight that
perception is not merely passive reception of stimuli but an active construction of reality based
on prior knowledge, experiences, and expectations.
Female students are bombarded with numerous advertisements daily, so marketers must find
ways to capture and retain their attention. Eye -catching visuals, engaging content, and
endorsements by popular influencers can help direct students' attention to hair extensions.
Selective perception plays a role here, as students tend to pay more attention to products that
align with their interests and needs. For instance, students interested in fashion and beauty are
more likely to notice and engage with advertisements for hair extensions. Understanding these
dynamics helps marketers design more effective campaigns to capture the attention of their target
audience.

Methodology
The study adopted a survey method with the questionnaire as the instrument of data gathering.
The population of the study was the entire Female Communication Arts Students which was
tentatively put at 689 approximately. (This is however subject to verification.)  This study is
considered as a scoping study and therefore a sample size of 80 female students were
purposively drawn from two levels (third and final year) in the department on the basis of
availability and willingness to participate in the study. This was done using a WhatsApp group
and students were asked to indicate their interest in the study by joining the group. 72 students
indicated interest and thus were used for the study.  The use of female students in the studies is
driven by the fact that they constitute the major target audience for  the subject matter and is
justified for several reasons namely: firstly, females are the primary consumers of human hair
extensions and therefore, it is important to understand their perspectives and attitudes towards
these products.  Also, female students are a convenient and accessible population for research
purposes, as they are often available on university campuses and can be easily recruited for
studies.

Conclusion
In conclusion, it can be stated categorically that Communication arts female students' perception
of human hair extensions is significantly influenced by social media. The study revealed that
social media platforms, particularly Instagram and Facebook, play a crucial role in shaping their
attitudes and beliefs about hair extensions. The constant exposure to images and videos of
celebrities and influencers wearing hair extensions has created a sense of desire and necessity
among female students, leading to a positive perception of hair extensions as a beauty essential.
Overall, the study suggests that social media has become a critical factor in shaping female
students' perception and purchasing behavior of human hair extensions, highlighting the need for
hair extension brands to prioritize social media marketing strategies to effectively reach and
engage with this demographic.

Recommendations
The researcher puts forward the following recommendations from the findings of the study:
1. Hair extension brands should prioritize social media marketing strategies to effectively reach
and engage with female students. This includes partnering with influencers, creating
engaging content, and utilizing targeted advertising.
2. Manufacturers and Brands managers should collaborate with influencers who have a strong
following among female students to promote their products and build credibility.
3. Brands should encourage customers to leave online reviews and ratings to build trust and
credibility among potential customers.
4. Brands should create social media content that resonates with female students, such as
tutorials, styling tips, and product demonstrations.
