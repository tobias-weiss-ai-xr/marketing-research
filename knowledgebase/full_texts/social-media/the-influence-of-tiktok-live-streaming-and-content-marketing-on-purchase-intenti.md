Abstract
______________________________________________________________________________________
The rapid growth of TikTok as a social commerce platform has created new opportunities for brands to engage consumers through
interactive live streaming and content marketing. However, empirical evidence regarding how these two forms of digital marketing
are associated with purchase intention remains mixed. This study aims to examine the relationship of TikTok live streaming and
content marketing with purchase intention for Glad2Glow products among Business Education students, Class of 2022, at
Universitas Negeri Surabaya. A quantitative cross-sectional survey was conducted involving 80 respondents selected through
purposive sampling. The respondents were active Business Education students who used TikTok and had been exposed to
Glad2Glow promotional content and live streaming on TikTok. Data were analyzed using multiple linear regression. The findings
indicate that live streaming was significantly and positively associated with purchase intention (B = 0.463; p < 0.001), while
content marketing also showed a significant and positive association with purchase intention (B = 0.422; p < 0.001). The two
predictors jointly explained 61.9% of the variance in purchase intention (R² = 0.619). These findings contribute to the social
commerce literature by providing empirical evidence that interactive live streaming and content marketing are relevant factor s
associated with consumers’ purchase intention in the TikTok context . Practically, the findings highlight the importance of
integrating interactive and informative digital content in social commerce strategies for skincare brands.

Keywords: Live Streaming; Content marketing; Purchase Intention; TikTok; Glad2Glow

INTRODUCTION
The development of social commerce has changed the way consumers obtain product
information and evaluate products before purchase. Unlike conventional e-commerce, social
commerce integrates commercial activities with social interaction and user -generated content,
allowing consumers to encounter product information while simultaneously interacting with sellers,
hosts, and other users (Dolega et al., 2021). Social interaction is an important characteristic of this
environment because consumers can exchange information, observe other consumers’ experiences,
and participate in two-way communication during the decision-making process. Wang and Yu (2017)
found that social interaction, word-of-mouth communication, and observational learning were
associated with consumers’ purchase intention in social commerce. TikTok represents this
development through a platform that combines short-form video content, live streaming, interaction,
and purchasing functions (Vieira et al., 2021). In this environment, consumers are not only exposed
to promotional messages but can also observe product demonstrations, obtain information in real
time, and evaluate products through digital interactions (Masrianto et al., 2022). Consequently,
purchase intention in social commerce may be shaped by how consumers process the information and
experiences provided through the platform.
Two marketing activities are particularly relevant in this context: live streaming and content
marketing. Live streaming enables sellers to present products directly, demonstrate their use, respond
to consumer questions, and provide product information in real time. Within social commerce, live
streaming has been examined as a mechanism through which consumers obtain product information
and interact with sellers. Sun et al. (2019) found that visibility, metavoicing, and guidance-shopping
affordances were associated with purchase intention through live-streaming engagement. Similarly,
Zhang et al. (2023), using the S-O-R paradigm, reported that live-streaming affordances such as
visibility, metavoicing, trading affordance, guidance shopping, and interactivity can enhance trust,
which is related to purchase intention. Content marketing, in contrast, communicates product-related
information through planned and engaging digital content, including visual presentations, product
explanations, and persuasive messages (Deb et al., 2022; Krisen et al., 2021). Recent evidence from
TikTok users also indicates that content-marketing characteristics such as attractiveness, emotional
appeal, trustworthiness, electronic word-of-mouth, and perceived relevance are associated with online
purchase intention (Faruk et al.,2021; Wei, 2025). Although both activities provide information, they
differ in the way consumers encounter marketing stimuli: live streaming emphasizes real -time
interaction and immediacy, whereas content marketing emphasizes the presentation and accessibility
of prepared information (Borchers & Wellman, 2026).
The Stimulus–Organism–Response (S-O-R) framework provides a useful theoretical
perspective for explaining this process (Armutcu et al., 2023). The framework proposes that external
stimuli are received and processed internally by individuals before producing a behavioral response.
In social commerce, Liu et al. (2019) applied the S -O-R model by linking external information
characteristics and social presence with internal trust and purchase intention. Their findings illustrate
how S-O-R can be used to explain the relationship between social -commerce stimuli, internal
consumer states, and behavioral intention. In the present study, live streaming and content marketing
are conceptualized as external stimuli, while purchase intention represents the response. The organism
component is not treated as a separately measured variable in the empirical model; rather, it represents
the internal psychological process through which consumers interpret and evaluate the stimuli, such
as attention, understanding, interest, and trust ( Van Esch & Stewart Black, 2021) . This
conceptualization is consistent with the purpose of using S-O-R as an explanatory framework rather
than as a complete mediation model. Thus, the study does not claim to statistically test the organism
component. Instead, S-O-R is used to explain why exposure to interactive and informative marketing
stimuli may be associated with consumers’ purchase intention.
Empirical findings concerning these relationships remain context-dependent. Previous studies
have reported different results regarding the role of live streaming and content marketing in consumer
responses on TikTok and other social commerce platforms. Putra et al. (2024), for example, found
that live streaming and content marketing did not show significant partial relationships with purchase
intention in their study of TikTok Shop users in Kediri, whereas Khamidah et al. (2025) reported
significant relationships between both variables and purchase intention for Skintific products on
TikTok Shop. International evidence likewise indicates that the strength and mechanism of these
relationships can differ according to the platform and consumer context. For example, Sun et al.

(2019) examined live-streaming shopping in Chinese social commerce, while Wei (2025) examined
content marketing among active TikTok users in China. These studies provide relevant international
evidence, but they do not specifically examine the simultaneous relationship of live streaming and
content marketing with purchase intention for a skincare brand among Indonesian university students.
The existing studies therefore leave a contextual question regarding whether the same marketing
stimuli demonstrate similar relationships when examined simultaneously in the skincare category and
among a specific student consumer segment.
The skincare category provides a relevant setting for examining this issue because consumers
generally require information about product benefits, ingredients, usage, and suitability before
developing purchase intentions. Glad2Glow is selected as the research object because the brand is
represented in the existing literature on TikTok-based skincare marketing and has been examined in
relation to digital marketing activities on the platform. Previous research has examined Glad2Glow
in relation to brand image, brand ambassador, content marketing, online customer review, and
purchasing decisions (Rahma & Sudarwanto, 2024; Fitriawati & Muanas, 2025; Susilowati et al.,
2025). However, the simultaneous relationship of TikTok live streaming and content marketing with
purchase intention remains insufficiently examined in this specific context (Cao et al., 2021). The
present study therefore focuses on these two marketing stimuli rather than adding additional
predictors, allowing their combined relationship with purchase intention to be examined within a
defined social commerce setting.
The selection of Business Education students of Universitas Negeri Surabaya, Class of 2022,
is also related to the research design rather than simply to their status as TikTok users. The respondents
were selected based on explicit exposure criteria: they were active students who used TikTok, followed
or knew the Glad2Glow account, and had watched Glad2Glow promotional live streaming and
content marketing on TikTok. These criteria ensure that respondents had relevant exposure to the
stimuli examined in the study. In addition, their educational background in business provides a
context in which exposure to marketing communication can be examined among consumers who
encounter digital marketing as both consumers and students learning about business -related
phenomena. Nevertheless, the study does not assume that this group represents TikTok users in
general; the findings are limited to the characteristics and sampling criteria of the respondents.
Accordingly, the research problem addressed in this study is not simply whether live
streaming and content marketing are significant predictors of purchase intention. Rather, the study
examines whether two distinct forms of TikTok -based marketing stimuli—real-time interactive
communication and planned digital content—are significantly associated with purchase intention
when examined simultaneously within a specific skincare social commerce context. This approach
addresses the contextual inconsistency found in previous studies while clarifying the role of S-O-R as
the theoretical explanation for the relationship between marketing stimuli and consumer response.
Based on this rationale, this study aims to examine the relationship of TikTok live streaming
and content marketing with purchase intention for Glad2Glow products among Business Education
students, Class of 2022, at Universitas Negeri Surabaya. The study contributes to the social commerce
literature by examining two complementary forms of digital marketing stimuli within the same
empirical model and by applying the S-O-R perspective to explain the transition from external
marketing exposure to purchase intention without claiming to directly measure the organism
component. Practically, the findings provide context-specific evidence for skincare brands regarding
the use of interactive live communication and informative content as complementary elements of
TikTok social commerce strategies.
METHOD
Research Design and Participants
This study employed a quantitative cross-sectional survey design to examine the relationships
of TikTok live streaming and content marketing with purchase intention for Glad2Glow products.
The study was conducted among students of the Business Education Study Program, Class of 2022,
at Universitas Negeri Surabaya (UNESA). The accessible population consisted of 99 students. The
population was derived from 100 active students in the 2022 cohort after excluding the researcher.
Although the accessible population was relatively small, the study did not employ a census design.
Instead, respondents were selected using purposive samplin g because the research required
participants with direct experience of the marketing stimuli under investigation. Accordingly, the
sample consisted of 80 respondents who met the predetermined eligibility criteria.

The eligibility criteria were: (1) an active undergraduate student of the Business Education
Study Program at UNESA, Class of 2022; (2) an active TikTok user; (3) following the Glad2Glow
TikTok account; and (4) having known and watched Glad2Glow promotional content, including
TikTok videos and live streaming. These criteria were established to ensure that respondents had
actual exposure to the two marketing activities examined in the study. Because purposive sampling is
a non-probability sampling technique, the resulting sample should not be interpreted as statistically
representative of all TikTok users or all UNESA students. The findings are therefore interpreted within
the characteristics of the selected respondent group.

Data Collection Procedure
Primary data were collected through a self-administered online questionnaire distributed
using Google Forms. The questionnaire was disseminated through student communication networks
until the predetermined number of eligible responses was obtained. Before answering the substantive
measurement items, respondents were screened using questions concerning their student status,
TikTok usage, familiarity with the Glad2Glow TikTok account, and exposure to Glad2Glow live
streaming and content marketing. The questionnaire used a five-point Likert scale ranging from 1
(strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). The measurement items were developed from indicators
identified in previous studies and adapted to the context of Glad2Glow products and TikTok social
commerce. The questionnaire contained 26 substantive items distributed across three constructs: eight
items for Live Streaming, ten items for Content Marketing, and eight items for Purchase Intention.

Measurement of Variables
Live Streaming (X1) was measured using four dimensions adapted from Ramadhan et al.
(2024): attractiveness, trustworthiness, expertise, and purchase convenience. These dimensions
represent the extent to which the live-streaming experience provides an attractive presentation,
credible and transparent information, competent product communication, and convenience during
the purchasing process. Content Marketing (X2) was measured using five dimensions: reader
cognition, sharing motivation, persuasion, decision making, and life factor. Each dimension was
represented by two items. In this study, reader cognition refers to the extent to which consumers can
understand and process the information presented in the content; sharing motivation refers to the
tendency of consumers to share content they consider useful or interesting; persuasion concerns the
ability of content to strengthen consumer consideration toward the product; decision making concerns
the extent to which content assists consumers in evaluating purchase alternatives; and life factor
represents external influences and contextual considerations relevant to consumers' responses to
marketing content. Purchase Intention (Y) was measured using four dimensions: transactional,
referential, preferential, and exploratory intention. These dimensions represent consumers' tendency
to purchase the product, recommend it to others, prioritize it, and seek additional information before
purchasing.

Instrument Testing
Before conducting the main regression analysis, the measurement items were evaluated for
validity and reliability using the collected survey data. Item validity was assessed by examining the
relationship between each item and its corresponding construct score, with statistical significance used
as the basis for determining whether an item was retained. The study used a critical correlation value
of 0.361. The results indicated that all 26 measurement items met the validity criterion. Reliability was
assessed using Cronbach's alpha. The overall 26-item instrument produced a Cronbach's alpha
coefficient of 0.932, exceeding the minimum criterion of 0.60 used in this study. This result indicates
a high level of internal consistency for the overall questionnaire. However, because the original
analysis reported the reliability coefficient for the combined instrument rather than separately for each
construct, the coefficient should not be interpreted as evidence of construct-specific reliability for Live
Streaming, Content Marketing, and Purchase Intention individually.

Data Analysis
The data were analyzed using IBM SPSS Statistics 27. Descriptive analysis was first used to
describe respondent characteristics and responses to the measurement items. Multiple linear regression
was subsequently employed to examine the statistical relationships of Live Streaming and Content
Marketing with Purchase Intention. The regression model was specified as:

Y = α + β₁X₁ + β₂X₂ + ε

associations within the observed sample rather than as evidence of causal effects.
RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
Regression Model and Diagnostic Evidence
Multiple linear regression was employed to examine the statistical associations between TikTok
live streaming, content marketing, and purchase intention for Glad2Glow products. Because the study
used a cross-sectional survey and purposive sampling, the regression results are interpreted as statistical
associations and predictive relationships within the observed sample rather than as evidence of causal
effects. The estimated regression equation was:

Y = 0.286 + 0.463X₁ + 0.422X₂

where Y represents purchase intention, X₁ represents live streaming, and X₂ represents content
marketing. The positive coefficients indicate that higher scores on each predictor were associated with
higher purchase intention scores when the other predictor was statistically held constant. Specifically,
a one-unit increase in the live streaming score was associated with a 0.463-unit increase in the predicted
purchase intention score, conditional on content marketing being held constant. Similarly, a one-unit
increase in content marketing was associated with a 0.422-unit increase in predicted purchase intention,
conditional on live streaming being held constant.
The overall regression model was statistically significant, with an F -value of 62.627 and a
significance level below 0.001. The model produced an R value of 0.787 and an R² of 0.619, while the
adjusted R² was 0.609. Thus, 61.9% of the observed variance in purchase intention in the sample was
accounted for by live streaming and content marketing included in the regressio n model. After
adjustment for the number of predictors, the proportion was 60.9%. These statistics describe the
explanatory performance of the estimated model in the observed sample and should not be interpreted
as evidence that the same proportion of variation would be explained in other populations.
The diagnostic results also provide supporting evidence for the regression specification. The
tolerance value for both predictors was 0.883 and the VIF value was 1.133, indicating no indication of
problematic multicollinearity according to the criteria applied in this study. The Glejser test also
produced significance values of 0.949 for live streaming and 0.076 for content marketing, both above
0.05. These results provide no statistical indication of heteroscedasticity based on the test employed.
Nevertheless, these diagnostic results do not establish that the model is fully robust, particularly because
the data were collected through self-reported measures at a single point in time.

Association Between Live Streaming and Purchase Intention
The regression results indicate a positive and statistically significant association between live
streaming and purchase intention. Live streaming had an unstandardized coefficient of 0.463, a
standardized coefficient of 0.435, and a t-value of 5.813 with a significance level below 0.001. Therefore,
the first hypothesis is supported within the observed sample. The positive coefficient indicates that
respondents who reported higher evaluations of Glad2Glow's TikTok live streaming also tended to
report higher purchase intention scores, after statistically controlling for content marketing. This finding
is consistent with the functional characteristics of live streaming as a form of social commerce
communication. Live streaming enables consumers to receive product information in real time and

provides an interactive environment in which product-related questions and responses can occur during
the broadcast. In the present study, the live streaming construct included attractiveness, trustworthiness,
expertise, and purchase convenience. These dimensions provide a basis for understanding why
respondents who perceived the live-streaming experience more positively also tended to report stronger
purchase intention.
The finding can also be interpreted through the S-O-R framework. In this framework, live
streaming represents a stimulus because it exposes consumers to product information and interactive
promotional communication. The resulting purchase intention represents the response. However, the
organism component in the S-O-R framework was not separately measured in this study. Consequently,
psychological processes such as attention, understanding, interest, or trust should not be interpreted as
empirically demonstrated mechanisms linking live streaming to purchase intention. Instead, these
processes provide a theoretical interpretation of how the observed association may be understood. The
present findings therefore support the relevance of the stimulus-response relationship but do not test
whether a specific organism mechanism mediates that relationship.
The result is also consistent with previous research reporting a significant relationship between
interactive digital marketing activities and consumer purchase intention. However, differences across
previous studies should not be interpreted solely as contradictions. The magnitude and significance of
relationships may vary according to product category, platform characteristics, respondent
characteristics, and the nature of consumers' exposure to promotional content. The present study
contributes evidence from the specific context of Glad2Glow products and Business Education students
at Universitas Negeri Surabaya rather than establishing a universal relationship applicable to all
consumers.

Association Between Content Marketing and Purchase Intention
Content marketing also showed a positive and statistically significant association with purchase
intention. The unstandardized coefficient was 0.422, the standardized coefficient was 0.523, and the t-
value was 6.994 with a significance level below 0.001. Thus, the second hypothesis is supported within
the observed sample. The positive coefficient indicates that respondents with higher evaluations of
Glad2Glow's content marketing tended to report higher purchase intention after live streaming was
statistically controlled. The content marketing construct in this study covered reader cognition, sharing
motivation, persuasion, decision making, and life factor. These dimensions emphasize the
informational, persuasive, social, and contextual characteristics of marketing content. Content that is
perceived as understandable, persuasive, and relevant may provide consumers with information that
can be considered when evaluating a product. However, because these psychological processes were
not independently measured as mediating variables, the present findings should be interpreted as an
association between perceived content marketing and purchase intention rather than evidence of a
specific psychological pathway.
The finding also provides a contextual interpretation of the S -O-R framework. Content
marketing can be positioned as a stimulus that presents information and persuasive messages to
consumers, while purchase intention represents the response. The framework helps explain why
exposure to marketing content may be related to consumers' behavioral intentions, but it does not
demonstrate the internal psychological mechanism in the present study. Therefore, the use of S-O-R in
this research is appropriately understood as a conceptual framework for interpreting the findings rather
than as a tested mediation model.
The result is relevant to the TikTok social commerce context because content marketing can
remain accessible beyond a single live-streaming session. Whereas live streaming is characterized by
real-time interaction, marketing content can be viewed repeatedly and considered at different points in
the consumer decision process. The positive association observed in this study therefore suggests that
the informational and persuasive characteristics of Glad2Glow's TikTok content were relevant to
purchase intention among the respondents. Nevertheless, this interpretation remains specific to the

Relative Magnitude of the Predictors
The standardized regression coefficients provide additional information about the relative
association of the two predictors with purchase intention. Content marketing had a standardized
coefficient of β = 0.523, whereas live streaming had β = 0.435. Because standardized coefficients
express the estimated association in standard deviation units, the larger coefficient for content
marketing indicates a relatively stronger statistical association with purchase intention than live
streaming within the estimated regression model. This comparison should, however, be interpreted
cautiously. The difference between the two standardized coefficients does not by itself establish that
content marketing is statistically superior to live streaming because the study did not conduct a formal
statistical test of the difference between the coefficients. Therefore, the result should not be interpreted
as evidence that content marketing is universally more effective than live streaming. Rather, it indicates
that, among the two predictors included in this particular model and sample, content marketing had
the larger estimated standardized association with purchase intention.
This distinction is important for the theoretical interpretation of the findings. The results suggest
that both forms of TikTok marketing communication are relevant to purchase intention, while their
estimated associations are not identical in magnitude. The finding therefore supports an integrated
perspective on social commerce communication rather than implying that one communication format
should replace the other. Further research using larger samples and formal coeffic ient-comparison
procedures would be required to determine whether the observed difference represents a statistically
meaningful difference in the population.

Interpretation Through the S-O-R Framework
The S-O-R framework provides a useful conceptual lens for integrating the two regression
findings. In the present study, live streaming and content marketing are treated as external marketing
stimuli, while purchase intention represents the behavioral response. The framework suggests that
consumers do not respond to external stimuli independently of internal processing. However, the
present study did not operationalize the organism component as an independent construct and did not
estimate a mediation model. Accordingly, the results should not be interpreted as empirical evidence
that live streaming or content marketing produces purchase intention through attention, understanding,
interest, trust, or another specific psychological mechanism. Those processes remain theoretical
possibilities that could explain the associations observed in the regression analysis. A stronger empirical
test of the S-O-R mechanism would require future research to measure one or more organism variables
directly and test their mediating roles between marketing stimuli and purchase intention. Within this
limitation, the findings still provide a contextual application of the S-O-R perspective to TikTok social
commerce. The results indicate that two observable marketing stimuli are statistically associated with
consumers' purchase intention, while the internal processes proposed by S-O-R remain an area for
further empirical investigation. This distinction prevents the theoretical framework from being
presented as evidence for mechanisms that were not actually measured.

Practical and Theoretical Implications
The findings have a limited but relevant theoretical implication for research on social commerce.
They provide context-specific evidence that both interactive live streaming and content marketing are
statistically associated with purchase intention in the TikTok context among Business Education
students. The results do not establish a new causal mechanism or extend S-O-R through an empirically
tested organism component. Instead, their contribution lies in applying the framework to a specific
social commerce setting and demonstrating that the two forms of marketing communication can be
examined simultaneously within one regression model. From a practical perspective, the findings

suggest that social commerce communication should not be considered solely in terms of real-time
interaction or solely in terms of prepared marketing content. Live streaming and content marketing
represent different forms of consumer-facing communication. The observed positive associations

context. The relatively larger standardized coefficient for content marketing also indicates that content
marketing had the stronger estimated association within this sample, although this should not be
interpreted as a general ranking of marketing effectiveness.

Scope and Limitations of the Findings
Several limitations should be considered when interpreting the results. First, the study employed
a cross-sectional design, meaning that the variables were measured at one point in time. Therefore, the
findings cannot establish temporal precedence or demonstrate that live streaming or content marketing
caused changes in purchase intention. The use of causal terminology should consequently be avoided
when interpreting these findings. Second, the study used purposive sampling and included 80
respondents who met predetermined exposure and respondent criteria. The sample therefore does not
represent all TikTok users, all Universitas Negeri Surabaya students, or all consumers of Glad2Glow
products. The findings should be understood as evidence concerning the respondents included in this
study and should not be generalized beyond comparable populations without additional research.
Third, all variables were obtained through self-reported questionnaire responses. Because the
predictors and outcome were collected using the same measurement approach, common -method
variance may have contributed to the observed relationships. However, common-method variance was
not separately tested in this study; therefore, its magnitude cannot be determined from the available
results. Fourth, the R² value of 0.619 should not be interpreted as demonstrating that live streaming
and content marketing account for 61.9% of purchase intention in the broader consumer population.
The statistic refers to the proportion of observed variation accounted for by the model in the current
sample. Other unmeasured factors, such as individual consumer characteristics, product perceptions,
price considerations, brand familiarity, promotional incentives, or other digital marketing exposures,
may also be related to purchase intention. These potentially omitted variables were not included in the
present model.
Overall, the results provide evidence of statistically significant positive associations between live
streaming, content marketing, and purchase intention within the studied sample. The findings are
therefore best understood as context-specific empirical evidence rather than causal proof or a universal
explanation of consumer purchase intention. The distinction is particularly important when interpreting
the S-O-R framework: the study empirically examines the relationship between observable stimuli and
the response, while the internal organism processes remain theoretically proposed rather than
statistically tested.

CONSLUSION
This study found that live streaming and content marketing on TikTok were positively and
significantly associated with purchase intention for Glad2Glow among 2022 Business Education
students at Universitas Negeri Surabaya. These findings indicate that, within the study sample,
positive perceptions of both marketing activities were associated with higher purchase intention in the
social commerce context. Theoretically, this study applies the Stimulus–Organism–Response (S-O-R)
framework by positioning live streaming and content marketing as stimuli and purchase intention as
the response. However, because the organism component was not directly measured, the study does
not empirically establish the psychological mechanism underlying this relationship. Thus, S-O-R
should be regarded as a conceptual framework rather than a tested mediation mechanism. The model
explains 61.9% of the variance in purchase intention within the study sample, while the remaining
38.1% may be related to other factors. This result should not be generalized broadly because the study
used a cross-sectional design, purposive sampling, and a relatively small sample of 80 respondents.

trust, perceived value, or consumer engagement, while using larger samples and longitudinal or
