Abstract. Micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) face increasing challenges in
adapting their marketing practices to changing consumer behavior and the growing use of
social media. This community service initiative aimed to analyze consumer behavior and
develop relevant digital marketing strategies for Moresto Dimsum, a culinary MSME in
Bandar Lampung, Indonesia, through a service learning approach that connects academic
knowledge with practical community needs.  The activity employed a qualitative
descriptive approach involving direct observation, interviews with 15 consumers, and
mentoring activities with the business partner. Consumer behavior was analyzed using
the Five -Stage Consumer Purchase Process Model and the AISAS framework (Attention,
Interest, Search, Action, and Share). The findings were used to formulate digital marketing
recommendations tailored to consumer preferences and the partner's marketing
challenges. The findings indicated that strategic location, product taste, and affordable
prices were the primary factors influencing purchasing decisions, accounting for 36.36%,
33.33%, and 21.21% of the reported responses, respectively. Although consumers
demonstrated interest in the products, digital promotional exposure and electronic word -
of-mouth activities remained limited. Based on these findings, the proposed marketing
strategy emphasized consistent Instagram content, brand storytelling, short -form video
optimization, clearer product and promotional information, and conversion -oriented
communication. These recommendations provide a practical framework for aligning
digital marketing activities with consumer behavior. Integrating consumer behavior
analysis with service learning offers an opportunity to connect academic learning with the
practical needs of culinary MSMEs. The activity generated context -specific digital
marketing recommendations for Moresto Dimsum and highlighted the importance of
understanding consumer preferences when developing social media strategies.

contextual understanding of consumers and the practical ability to use marketing
information in business decision-making.
 Consumer behavior analysis provides a foundation for developing more relevant and
responsive marketing strategies. The consumer purchase process involves need
recognition, information search, evaluation of alternatives, purchase decisions, and post -
purchase behavior, while the AISAS model emphasizes Attention, Interest, Search, Action,
and Share in digitally mediated consumer journeys (Javed et al., 2022; Suhud et al., 2026) .
These frameworks help businesses identify how consumers discover products, seek
information, make purchases, and share their experiences. In the context of social media
marketing, content quality, brand -related communication, and consumer engagement are
important considerations in building meaningful relationships between businesses and
their audiences (Hollebeek & Macky, 2019) . Research by  Ibrahim & Aljarah (2024)  also
highlights the relevance of social media marketing activities to self -brand connection and
user engagement on Instagram. These insights suggest that digital marketing strategies
should not rely exclusively on promotional frequency but should also address the
information, experiential, and relational needs of consumers. For culinary MSMEs, this
approach is particularly relevant because purchase decisions may be shaped by product
taste, price, convenience, visual presentation, and recommendations from o ther
consumers.
 Translating digital marketing concepts into consistent business practices remains a
challenge for many MSMEs. The expansion of social commerce has created additional
opportunities for product discovery and consumer interaction, but businesses must still
develop the capabilities required to use these channels effectively (Rizana et al., 2025;
Teece, 2018). A similar challenge can be observed in the culinary sector, where consumers
may discover a business through its physical location or previous consumption
experiences rather than through systematic digital promotion. The initial assessment of
Moresto Dimsum, a culinary MSME in Bandar Lampung, Indonesia, illustrates this issue.
Interviews with 15 consumers identified location, product taste, and affordability as
important considerations in purchasing decisions, accounting for 36.36%, 33.33%, and
21.21% of the reported responses, respectively. The assessment also indicated that
consumers' exposure to digital promotions was relatively limited, while the business's

 Service learning offers a potential approach for connecting academic knowledge with
community needs through structured learning and service activities. Unlike classroom -

disciplinary concepts to real -world problems while contributing to community

identifying the need for stronger evidence concerning program implementation and
outcomes (Arnaboldi et al., 2021; Verreynne et al., 2025) . In the context of digital

be used to support MSMEs through workshops and mentoring in social media, e -

learning as a practical framework for integrating consumer behavior analysis, student
learning, and business empowerment.
 Although previous studies have examined social media marketing and MSME
performance, consumer engagement, and service learning as an educational approach,
these areas are not always integrated within a single community service framework.
Research on social media marketing often focuses on the relationship between marketing
practices and business outcomes, whereas service learning  studies may emphasize
educational experiences, community engagement, or general business mentoring. The
specific connection between consumer behavior insights, the AISAS framework, student
learning, and context -specific digital marketing assistance for culinary MSMEs therefore
warrants further practical investigation. In particular, an intervention should explain not
only what marketing strategies are recommended but also how consumer insights inform
those strategies, how academic knowledge is applied to t he partner's needs, and how the
resulting recommendations can be used by the business. This article addresses that
practical gap through a service learning -oriented examination of Moresto Dimsum. Its
proposed contribution lies in integrating the Five -Stage Consumer Purchase Process
Model and AISAS framework into a contextual digital marketing development process
while positioning the MSME's marketing challenges as a real -world learning and
community service opportunity. This contribution is intended as a context -specific
application of established frameworks rather than a claim that the consumer behavior
models themselves are new.

2. Methods
 This community service initiative employed a qualitative descriptive approach within
a service learning framework to connect academic learning with the practical needs of
Moresto Dimsum, a culinary micro, small, and medium enterprise (MSME) located in
Pahoman, Bandar Lampung, Indonesia. Service learning was adopted as the guiding
pedagogical approach because it integrates structured learning activities with community
engagement and practical problem -solving (Drewery & Lollar, 2024; Salam et al., 2019) .
The activity focused on applying consumer behavior concepts to identify marketing
challenges and formulate context -specific digital marketing recommendations. The Five -
Stage Consumer Purchase Process Model and the AISAS framework (Attention, Interest,
Search, Action, and Share) served as analytical frameworks for interpreting consumer
preferences and identifying opportunities to improve the partner's social media
marketing practices (Gunarto & Putri, 2025; Soto-Santos et al., 2026).
 Moresto Dimsum  was selected as the community partner because its marketing
practices presented opportunities for improvement, particularly in the consistency of
digital promotion, content development, and consumer engagement. The initial
assessment involved direct observation of the business environment and its Instagram
marketing activities, complemented by interviews with 15 consumers. The interviews
explored purchasing frequency, product preferences, considerations influencing purchase
decisions, responses to promotio nal activities, and expectations regarding products and
services. Observational findings and interview responses were used to identify the
partner's marketing needs and establish the basis for developing appropriate digital
marketing recommendations. The consumer interviews constituted the primary source of
information for understanding purchasing behavior, while observations of social media
content provided contextual information about existing promotional practices.
 The service learning process was organized into four interconnected stages:
community needs assessment, application of academic knowledge, collaborative strategy
development, and evaluation and reflection. During the needs assessment stage, the team
identified consumer preferences and existing digital marketing challenges. In the
academic application stage, consumer behavior concepts and the AISAS framework were

3. Results and Discussion
 The community service activity at Moresto Dimsum in Pahoman , Bandar Lampung,
began by examining consumer behavior as a foundation for identifying opportunities to
strengthen the business's digital marketing practices. Understanding consumer
preferences is essential for culinary micro, small, and medium -sized enterprises (MSMEs),
as purchasing decisions may be influenced by several considerations, including product
characteristics, accessibility, price, and the availability of relevant information. Through
interviews with 15 consumers and observations of the busines s's digital marketing
practices, this activity sought to identify the factors associated with consumers'
purchasing decisions and explore how these insights could be translated into more
relevant promotional communication.
 The findings were analyzed descriptively to identify recurring considerations in
consumer decision -making and their implications for the development of a consumer -
oriented digital marketing strategy. Rather than treating social media solely as a channel
for displaying products, the analysis positioned digital platforms as a means of
communicating product value, providing practical purchasing information, and facilitating
interactions with potential customers. The results are presented in three main areas,
beginning with consumer preferences related to location, taste, and price, followed by an
assessment of digital marketing practices and the formulation of context -specific
marketing strategies based on the consumer insights obtained.
3.1. Consumer Preferences Were Primarily Driven by Location, Taste, and Price
 The interviews conducted with 15 consumers of Moresto Dimsum in Pahoman ,
Bandar Lampung, revealed that purchasing decisions were associated with three main
considerations: location, taste, and price. Based on the available findings, location was the
most frequently reported factor, accounting for 36.36%, followed by taste at 33.33% and
affordable price at 21.21%. These findings suggest that consumers consider both the
accessibility of the business and the perceived value of its products when deciding

whether to purchase. For a culinary micro, small, and medium -sized enterprise (MSME),
understanding these preferences is essential because marketing communication should
reflect the factors that consumers actually consider rather than relying solely on general
promotional messages.
 Location emerged as the most frequently mentioned consideration, indicating that
accessibility may be an important element in the consumer decision -making process. For
food businesses, consumers may prefer locations that are easy to find, convenient to
reach, and suitable for purchasing food during their daily activities. This finding provides
a practical opportunity for Moresto Dimsum  to communicate its location more effectively
through digital platforms. For example, promotional content could include a Google Maps
link, clear location information, operating hours, and visual directions to the outlet. Such
information could help potential customers move from initial awareness to the
consideration of an actual visit. However, because the available data do not specify the
precise reasons behind consumers' location preferences, accessibility should be
interpreted as a possible explanation rather than a conclusively established cause.
 Taste was the second most frequently reported consideration, accounting for 33.33%
of the available responses. This finding highlights the importance of communicating the
sensory and experiential qualities of Moresto Dimsum's products. In food marketing,
product images alone may not fully communicate the experience that consumers expect
from a meal. Moresto Dimsum  could therefore develop content that presents product
appearance, texture, serving suggestions, menu variety, and customer experiences. Short -
form videos showing the preparation or presentation of dimsum, for example, may help
make the product more tangible to online audiences. Nevertheless, these are proposed
promotional approaches; the interview findings establish taste as a reported
consideration but do not independently demonstrate which type of content would be
most effective in communicating it.
 Affordable price was identified by 21.21% of respondents as another consideration in
their purchasing decisions. This suggests that communicating price clearly may help
consumers assess whether Moresto  Dimsum's products fit their preferences and
purchasing capacity. Digital promotional content could display menu prices, package
options, portion information, and discounts when these are genuinely available. Price
communication should also explain the value offered, rather than relying exclusively on
low-price messages. For example, a menu post could combine product photographs with
the price and a concise description of the available portion or package. This approach
would allow potential customers to com pare options and make more informed decisions.
Since the available findings do not measure price elasticity or willingness to pay, the
results should not be interpreted as evidence that reducing prices would necessarily
increase sales.
 Taken together, the three factors provide a practical basis for developing a more
consumer-oriented digital marketing strategy. Moresto  Dimsum's social media content
could be organized around three complementary messages: where to find the product,
what makes the product appealing, and how much it costs. These messages could be
incorporated into a consistent content plan using informative posts, short videos,
promotional announcements, and clear calls to action. The findings are also consistent
with broader research examining online marketing and purchasing interest among food
and beverage MSMEs, which identifies product characteristics, p rice, and promotional
communication as relevant marketing considerations (Ruhiyat et al., 2023).

3.2. Digital Marketing Practices Needed More Consistent and Engaging Content
 The observation of Moresto Dimsum's  digital marketing practices identified several
opportunities to improve the consistency and quality of its social media communication.
These included the frequency of content publication, the visual presentation of products,
the clarity of captions, and the use of short -form video. These aspects are important
because social media marketing involves more than simply maintaining an online
presence; it also requires businesses to communicate product information in ways that
attract attention, generate interes t, and help potential customers make purchasing
decisions. For culinary micro, small, and medium -sized enterprises (MSMEs), well -
organized digital communication can help present products more effectively and maintain
connections with existing and prospective customers. In the case of Moresto Dimsum, the
observations indicated that its digital marketing practices could be developed into a more
structured and consumer-oriented communication strategy.
 Content consistency emerged as an important area for improvement. When
promotional content is published without a clear schedule or recognizable thematic
direction, audiences may receive fragmented information about the business, its menu,
and its promotional offers. A more systematic approach could involve establishing a
weekly content calendar and organizing posts around several content pillars, such as
product introductions, menu information, preparation processes, customer experiences,
promotional offer s, and business location. This arrangement would enable Moresto
Dimsum to communicate different aspects of its products without relying exclusively on
repeated product advertisements. However, the available observations do not establish a
specific posting frequency or demonstrate that inconsistent publication directly caused
lower engagement. A content calendar is therefore a practical recommendation derived
from the identified opportunities for improvement.
 Visual presentation and caption quality also represent important elements of digital
marketing communication. Food products are highly visual, making clear photographs,
appropriate lighting, attractive composition, and informative presentation useful for
communicating product appeal. For Moresto  Dimsum, content could feature close -up
product images, menu combinations, serving portions, and short videos showing product
preparation or presentation. Captions should complement these visuals by providing
relevant information, including product names, prices, available packages, promotional
conditions, and ordering procedures. The use of a clear call to action (CTA), such as
inviting audiences to check the menu, contact the business, or visit the outlet, could make
the intended next step more explicit. These measures are proposed improvements rather
than interventions whose effectiveness has already been demonstrated by the observation
data.

available consumer data indicate that 80% of respondents expressed a preference for

 The analysis of consumer behavior provided a foundation for formulating a digital

location, taste, and price were relevant considerations in purchasing decisions, while the
visibility of promotional information and the consistency of social media content offered
opportunities for improvement. These findings were used to identify the types of
information that could be prioritized in the business's digital communication. Rather than
adopting a generic promotional approach, the proposed strategy connects  the observed
consumer considerations with practical communication activities that the business could
undertake through its social media platforms.

4. Conclusions
 This community service activity examined consumer behavior and digital marketing
practices at Moresto Dimsum, a culinary micro, small, and medium -sized enterprise
(MSME) located in Pahoman, Bandar Lampung. Based on interviews with 15 consumers
and observations of the business's digital marketing practices, the findings identified
location, taste, and affordable price as important considerations associated with
purchasing decisions. Location was the most frequently reported factor (36.36%),
followed by taste (33.33%) and price (21.21%). The assessment also identified
opportunities to improve social media content consistency, visual presentation, caption
quality, short -form video utilization, and the visibility of promotional information.
Furthermore, 80% of interviewed consumers reportedly expressed a preference for
discount offers, highlighting the relevance of communicating promotional information
clearly and effectively.
 The findings provided a basis for developing a context -specific digital marketing
strategy by integrating the Five -Stage Consumer Purchase Process and the AISAS model
(Attention, Interest, Search, Action, and Share). The proposed strategy includes organizing
content pillars, strengthening product storytelling, utilizing Instagram Reels, providing
clear menu and price information, highlighting valid promotional offers, improving
captions, and incorporating calls to action that facilitate inquiries and orders . The strategy
also encourages opportunities for customer feedback and the sharing of authentic
consumer experiences. Through the Service Learning approach, the activity connects
academic knowledge of consumer behavior and digital marketing with the practical needs
of an MSME. Its primary contribution is the formulation of actionable recommendations
tailored to the business's identified marketing challenges.
 The findings should be interpreted in light of the scope of the available data. The
proposed digital marketing strategy represents an initial output of the community service
activity, and its effectiveness in improving audience reach, engagement, customer
inquiries, and sales has not yet been established through implementation and evaluation.
Future activities should therefore focus on implementing the recommendations
