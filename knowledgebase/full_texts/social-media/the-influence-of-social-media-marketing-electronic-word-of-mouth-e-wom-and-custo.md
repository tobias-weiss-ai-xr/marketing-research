 The development of digital technology has transformed consumer
behavior in accessing information and making purchase decisions.
Social media no longer functions solely as a communication platform
but has also become an effective marketing channel for Micro , Small,
and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs). In addition, Electronic Word of
Mouth (E -WOM) and customer trust have become important factors
influencing consumer purchase decisions. The Malioboro  area, as a
tourism and trade center in Yogyakarta, provides a relevant setting for
examining consumer behavior within the context of digital marketing.
This study aimed to analyze the effects of social media marketing, E -
WOM, and customer trust on purchase decisions for MSME products in
Malioboro, Yogyakarta. This study employed a quantitative approach
using a survey method. The research sample consisted of 200
respondents who had purchased MSME products in the Malioboro area.
The sampling technique used was purposive sampling. Data were
analyzed using Structural Equation Modeling –Partial Least Squares
(SEM-PLS). The results showed that social media marketing had a
positive and significant effect on purchase decisions, E -WOM had a
positive and significant effect on purchase decisions, and customer trust
had a positive and significant effect on purchase decisions. Among the
examined variables, customer trust demonstrated the strongest influence
on purchase decisions. These findings provide practical implications for
MSME actors to strengthen digital marketing strategies and enhance
consumer trust to improve business competitiveness.
INTRODUCTION
The digital transformation that has occurred in recent years has changed the way companies
and business actors conduct marketing activities (Verhoef et al., 2021). The development of the
internet and social media enables consumers to access product information quickly without being
limited by geographical and temporal constraints (Appel et al., 2020). Before making a purchase,
consumers tend to seek various sources of information through digital platforms to reduce
perceived purchase risk and increase confidence in the products they intend to buy (Haridasan et
al., 2021; Lăzăroiu et al., 2020). This phenomenon has positioned social media as one of the most
important marketing channels supporting modern business activities (Dwivedi et al., 2021).
The growth of social media has also transformed marketing communication patterns from
one-way communication into interactive communication that enables engagement between

companies and consumers (Li et al., 2021). Social Media Marketing has become a widely adopted
strategy among business actors because it can increase consumer engagement, strengthen long -
term relationships, and expand market reach at relatively low costs (Chen & Lin, 2019; Ibrahim,
2022). Previous studies have shown that social media marketing significantly influences
purchase decisions through dimensions such as interaction, entertainment, relevance, and
informativeness (Alalwan, 2018; Hanaysha, 2022).
In addition to marketing activities through social media, consumers are increasingly
influenced by information generated by other consumers through Electronic Word of Mouth (E-
WOM). E -WOM refers to consumer -generated communication through digital platforms
containing experiences, reviews, comments, or recommendations regarding products or services.
Information provided by other consumers is often perceived as more objective and credible than
company-generated promotional messages. Therefore, E-WOM has become an important factor
influencing consumer behavior in purchase decision-making (Handoyo, 2024; Matiukaite et al.,
2024; Roy et al., 2025; Sa’ait et al., 2016; Siregar et al., 2024).
Consumer trust is also an essential factor in digital marketing. In both online and offline
transactions, consumers are more likely to make purchases when they have confidence in product
quality, seller integrity, transaction security, and a company’s abil ity to fulfill its commitments.
Trust serves as a foundation for establishing long -term relationships between consumers and
businesses. Higher levels of consumer trust increase the likelihood of purch ase decisions
(Barreda et al., 2015; Hidayat et al., 2021; Luo et al., 2025; Vashishth et al., 2024; Zhao et al.,
2019).
The Malioboro area in Yogyakarta is one of Indonesia’s major trade centers and tourism
destinations, where various MSMEs have developed. Increasing competition among MSME
actors requires effective marketing strategies to attract consumer interest. The use of social
media, the dissemination of information through Electronic Word of Mouth (E -WOM), and the
ability to build customer trust are important factors in increasing consumer purchase dec isions
regarding MSME products.
Previous studies have extensively examined the effects of Social Media Marketing, E -
WOM, and trust on purchase decisions. However, most studies have focused on e -commerce,
marketplace platforms, or specific industries such as skincare and coffee shops. Res earch
examining these three variables simultaneously within the context of MSMEs in the Malioboro
tourism area remains limited. This condition indicates a research gap that requires further
investigation.
Based on this background, this study was conducted to analyze the effects of Social Media
Marketing, Electronic Word of Mouth (E -WOM), and customer trust on purchase decisions for
MSME products in Malioboro, Yogyakarta.
Based on the research background, the problem formulation of this study was structured as
follows: (1) Does Social Media Marketing influence purchase decisions for MSME products in
Malioboro? (2) Does E -WOM (Electronic Word of Mouth) influence purchase dec isions for
MSME products in Malioboro? (3) Does customer trust influence purchase decisions for MSME
products in Malioboro? and (4) Do Social Media Marketing, E -WOM, and customer trust
simultaneously influence purchase decisions for MSME products in Malioboro?
In accordance with the research questions, this study aimed to analyze the effects of Social
Media Marketing on purchase decisions, examine the effects of E-WOM on purchase decisions,
analyze the effects of customer trust on purchase decisions, and evaluate the simultaneous effects

METHOD
Type of Research
This study employed a quantitative approach with an explanatory research design. The
quantitative approach was selected to examine the relationships among Social Media Marketing,
Electronic Word of Mouth (E -WOM), customer trust, and purchase decisions for MSME
products in Malioboro, Yogyakarta. Hypothesis testing was conducted based on the proposed
theoretical framework and previous research findings.
Research Location and Time
The research was conducted among consumers who had made purchases of MSME
products in the Malioboro area of Yogyakarta. The location was chosen based on Malioboro's
position as a trade center and tourist destination where various MSMEs have developed with
fairly intensive digital marketing activity
The research was carried out from January to March 2026.
Population and Sample
The population in this study is all consumers who have purchased MSME products in the
Malioboro area of Yogyakarta. This population includes consumers who purchase various types
of MSME products, such as culinary items, fashion, handicrafts, accessories, and Yogyakarta
specialty souvenirs. Consumers who are part of the study population are those who  have direct
experience purchasing MSME products in the Malioboro  area. This population was selected
based on the relevance of consumer experience to the variables studied, namely Social Media
Marketing, Electronic Word of Mouth (E-WOM), Customer Trust, and Purchase Decision. Thus,
consumers who have made a purchase are considered able to provide assessments based on real
experience regarding information obtained through social media, reviews or recommendations
from other consumers, their level of trust in the MSME, and the considerations made before
purchasing a product.
Because the exact size of the population is not known (infinite population), the sample size
was determined using the formula of Hair et al. (2022), namely a minimum of 5 –10 times the
number of research indicators.
Number of research indicators:
• Social Media Marketing = 5 indicators
• E-WOM = 4 indicators
• Customer Trust = 4 indicators
• Purchase Decision = 5 indicators
Total indicators = 18 indicators
So the minimum sample size:
10 × 18 = 180 respondents

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
Overview of the Research Object
The Malioboro area is a trade center and tourist destination that is one of the icons of the
City of Yogyakarta. This area has a strong appeal for tourists because it serves not only as a space
to enjoy the atmosphere of the city, but also as a venue for various commun ity economic
activities. Various MSMEs have developed in this area, offering culinary, handicraft, fashion,
accessory, and regional specialty souvenir products. The presence of these MSMEs is an
important part of supporting economic activity while also providing a distinctive experience for
tourists visiting Yogyakarta.
The character of the Malioboro area, which is crowded with tourists, causes competition
among MSME actors to be fairly high. Consumers have many product choices with relatively
diverse types and prices. This condition drives MSME actors not only to rely on business location
and direct sales, but also to make use of various marketing media that can reach consumers more
broadly. One widely used tool is social media as a medium for promotion, conveying product
information, and communicating with consumers.
The use of social media gives MSMEs the opportunity to introduce products before
consumers come directly to Malioboro. Through photos, videos, reviews, price information, and
other content, consumers can gain an overview of the products offered. In addition, interaction
through social media allows consumers to ask questions and obtain information directly from
business a ctors. Consumers can also find the experiences and recommendations of other
consumers through reviews or comments available on social media.
In the context of this study, MSMEs in the Malioboro  area are a relevant object for
examining the role of Social Media Marketing, Electronic Word of Mouth (E -WOM), and
Customer Trust in the purchase decision process. Consumers who obtain information through
social media, see the experiences of other consumers, and have trust in the product or the business
actor will have different considerations in determining a purchase decision. Therefore, the
character of MSMEs in the Malioboro  area, which deal directly with both local consumers and
tourists, makes it a suitable context for analyzing the relationship between digital marketing
activity, consumer trust, and purchase decisions.

The Influence of Social Media Marketing on Purchase Decisions
The research results show that Social Media Marketing has a positive and significant effect
on purchase decisions. This indicates that the more attractive and interactive an MSME's social
media content is, the higher consumers' tendency to make a purchase.
The Influence of E-WOM on Purchase Decisions
E-WOM is proven to have a positive effect on purchase decisions. Consumers tend to look
for reviews and recommendations from other consumers before deciding to buy a product.
The Influence of Customer Trust on Purchase Decisions
Customer Trust has the greatest influence compared to the other variables. Consumers who
have high trust in product quality and the credibility of an MSME find it easier to make a purchase
decision.

CONCLUSION
Based on the research findings regarding the effects of Social Media Marketing, Electronic
Word of Mouth (E -WOM), and customer trust on purchase decisions for MSME products in
Malioboro, it can be concluded that Social Media Marketing had a positive and significant effect
on purchase decisions. This indicates that more effective use of social media in delivering
information, attracting consumer attention, and creating interactions with c onsumers increased
the likelihood of purchasing MSME products in Malioboro.
Furthermore, Electronic Word of Mouth (E-WOM) had a positive and significant effect on
purchase decisions. Information in the form of reviews, recommendations, comments, and
consumer experiences shared through digital platforms served as important consider ations for
potential consumers before making purchases. Therefore, more positive and informative E -
WOM strengthened purchase decisions for MSME products in Malioboro.
Customer trust also had a positive and significant effect on purchase decisions. Consumer
confidence in product quality, information accuracy, seller credibility, and MSME actors’ ability
to fulfill consumer expectations increased consumers’ willingness to  make purchases. The
findings also showed that customer trust was the most dominant variable influencing purchase
decisions compared with Social Media Marketing and E-WOM.
Simultaneously, Social Media Marketing, E-WOM, and customer trust explained 74.2% of
purchase decisions, while the remaining 25.8% was influenced by other factors outside the
research model. These findings indicate that an effective digital marketing strat egy needs to be
supported by positive E -WOM management and continuous efforts to build and maintain
consumer trust.
