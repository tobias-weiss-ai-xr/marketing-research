ABSTRAK
Uncertainty in the development of digital technology has an impact on changes in horticultural
marketing strategies, especially by using social media as a tool in the marketing communication
process. This study was conducted with the aim of examining the factors that influence brand image
and social media promotion on the purchasing decisions of horticultural consumers using a
Systematic Literature Review (SLR) approach. The SLR approach was carried out with reference to
the PRISMA 2020 guidelines, covering the steps of identification, screening, eligibility, and
inclusion. Data sources were obtained based on scientific articles published during the period 2020–
2025 through the databases Google Scholar, Scopus, ScienceDirect, and Garuda Kemdikbud. As a
result of this selection process, 25 relevant articles related to the research topic were obtained. Most
reviewed studies indicate the findings indicate that brand image has a positive and significant effect
on purchase intention due to improvements in quality perception, trust, and brand reputation. In
addition, social media promotion in the form of promotion through Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, or
digital marketplaces is proven to be effective in increasing customer engagement, brand awareness,
and interest -to-buy. Supporting factors such as content creativity, influencer marketing, and
electronic word of mouth (e -WOM) further increas e the effectiveness of social media promotion.
Furthermore, the mediators that play a role in strengthening the correlation between social media
promotion and purchasing decisions are brand trust, customer engagement, brand awareness, and
influencer credibility. This research certainly brings several practical implications for horticultural
business practitioners in designing a more scientifically effective marketing strategy.

Keywords: Brand Image, Social Media Marketing, Purchase Intention, Produk Hortikultura,
Systematic, Literatur Review.

KESIMPULAN
Dari hasil penerapan Systematic Literature
Review atas 25 buah artikel ilmiah yang
diterbitkan di tahun 2020 -2025, dapat
disimpulkan bahwa variabel brand image dan
promosi media sosial memberikan dampak
positif dan signifikan terhadap purchase
intention produk hortikultura. Pengaruh yang
ditimbulkan dari variabel brand image
berdampak positif dalam hal menaikkan
percepatan quality perception, confidence,
dan belief konsumen terhadap produk
hortikultura dengan mengarahkan pada
peningkatan purchase intention.
 Selain itu, media sosial promotion
terbukti sebagai strategi pemasaran digital
yang efektif untuk menaikkan interaksi dan
engagement konsumen. Media sosial platform
seperti Instagram, Tiktok, Facebook, dan
digital marketplaces mampu meluaskan
cakupan pasar , menaikkan brand awareness,
dan membangun dialog bilateral antara pelaku
bisnis dengan konsumen. Konten creative
marketing, influencer marketing, short videos,
dan electronic word of mouth (e -WOM)
terbukti mampu menaikkan minat konsumen
pada produk hortikultura.
 Berdasarkan penelitian yang
dilakukan, terlihat ada beberapa variabel
mediator yang mempengaruhi relasi antara
brand image dan media social promotion
terhadap buying intention, seperti brand trust,
consumer engagement, brand awareness,
influencer credibility, dan electronic Word-of-
Mouth. Variabel-variabel ini memiliki peran
penting dalam pembentukan persepsi positif
konsumen terhadap produk dan mendukung
pengambilan keputusan pembelian di era
digital.  Berdasarkan hasil analisis
research gap, penelitian tentang digital
marketing dalam bidang hortikultura masih
kurang jika dibandingkan dengan industri lain
seperti fashion, cosmetic, maupun kuliner.
Terlebih lagi, penelitian tentang TikTok
marketing, green marketing, sustainable
branding, dan consumer loyalty dalam produk
hortikultura juga masih belum banyak
ditemukan.
 Secara praktis, hasil dari penelitian ini
menunjukkan bahwa peternak hortikultura
perlu memaksimalkan teknik digital
marketing yang dapat diterapkan melalui
optimasi brand image, aktif menggunakan
media sosial, dan memanfaatkan konten
kreatif serta influencer marketing untuk
menunjang persaingan p roduk hortikultura di
era digital.    Sementara
itu, secara teoretis penelitian ini berkontribusi
pada literatur tentang konsumen behavior dan
digital marketing pada sektor agribusiness
hortikultura.
