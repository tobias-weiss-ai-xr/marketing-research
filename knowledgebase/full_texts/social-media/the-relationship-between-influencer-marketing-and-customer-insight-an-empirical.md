Abstract
The study aimed to examine the nature and strength of the relationship between influencer marketing
and customer insight, at the level of the overall variable and their respective sub -dimensions, amongst
customers of beauty and skincare products on social media.  It also sought to examine the direct effect
of the dimensions of influencer marketing on the dimensions of customer insight. The study adopted a
descriptive-analytical approach, and data were collected using an online questionnaire distributed via
social media. The study sample comprised 234 individual customers of beauty and personal care
products.
The results of the statistical analysis (SPSS V.23) revealed a positive correlation between influencer
marketing and consumer insight, both at the overall leve l and at the dimensional level. The results also
revealed a statistically significant positive effect of the dimensions of influencer marketing on the
dimensions of consumer insight. They further demonstrated a partial significant effect of the
dimensions of influencer marketing on both consumer behaviour and consumer culture, These findings
emphasise the importance of employing influencer marketing strategies to develop customer insight
and enhance understanding of customer behaviour and culture, thereby c ontributing to improving the
effectiveness of marketing activities for beauty and personal care products via social media.

Keywords: Influencer marketing, customer insight

Introduction
Recent times have seen a marked rise in the role played by influencers and the extent of their
influence on the public’s perception of product value. This has contributed to the growth and
proliferation of social media platforms; influencers have transformed from mere ordinary
individuals of no significance in the world of marketing into a force to be reckoned with, on
which products rely heavily, Organizations have also focused on using influencers to
persuade customers to buy products or brands via digital communication channels such as
social media. Furthermore, organizat ions are seeking to strengthen their relationships with
their customers, as they are the primary source of the profits they can generate. It is therefore
essential to track and monitor all changes in customer knowledge and perception in order to
ensure their acquisition and retention; consequently, organizations engage in highly complex
activities to remain competitive for as long as possible by influencing customers, where
marketing managers utilize social media influencers in targeted efforts to shape cus tomer
perception, based on a series of processes designed to engage with customers and identify
their essential needs, so that these can be met in a way that steers their perception towards
repeat consumption of the organization's products, whilst resistin g the enticements offered
by competitors. In light of the above, the researcher seeks to uncover the nature of the direct
relationship between influencer marketing and consumer perception, through field research
on customers of beauty and personal care pro ducts via social media; this issue is of
increasing importance given the growing prevalence of such products, both in terms of
market size and the rise in production by manufacturers. Based on the above, the research
has been structured into four sections.  The first section covers the scientific methodology of
the research; the second summarizes the theoretical aspects of the research variables; the
third is devoted to the scientific analysis; whilst the fourth section c ontains the key
conclusions and recommendations drawn from the study.

Chapter one: The Scientific methodology of the research
First: Research problem and questions
To identify the research problem, the researcher conducted a
preliminary study by distributing an online questionnaire
designed using Google Drive to a convenience sample of 40
individuals, during the period from 1 April 2026 to 15 April
2026, targeting a group of customers of beauty and skincare
products via social media.

Through this exploratory study, the researcher
identified a set of preliminary indicators, as follows
With regard to influencer marketing, 16 respondents
(representing 40 per cent of customers) believe that
influencers possess sufficient expertise in the field of beauty
and personal care, whilst the majority of re spondents
expressed an interest in the influencer’s appearance and
their confidence in the influencer’s credibility when
presenting products. It is worth noting here that the
influence exerted by an influencer may not depend solely on
specialist expertise,  but rather on a range of personal
characteristics that contribute to shaping customers’
perceptions and attitudes. Furthermore, the exploratory
study revealed indicators directly linked to customer insight;
24 respondents (60 per cent) reported that their opinions and
suggestions regarding beauty and skincare products on
social media are not taken into account, reflecting a
disconnect between the insights and suggestions put forward
by customers and companies’ ability to utilise them in
developing their products and marketing content.
Furthermore, 28 respondents (70 per cent) believe that the
use of beauty and skincare products promoted via social
media does not align with their psychological and emotional
state, indicating a lack of awareness on the part o f
companies regarding the emotional and psychological
dimensions of customers when designing marketing
messages. Twenty respondents (50 per cent) believe that
content related to beauty products does not keep pace with
changes in their culture, suggesting t hat companies have a
limited ability to understand the cultural and social shifts
that influence customers’ preferences and behaviour.
Consequently, taken together, these indicators reflect a
problem in utilising  information and insights derived from
customers, thereby limiting companies’ ability to form an
accurate and comprehensive understanding of their needs
and expectations. Hence, there is a need to examine the role
that influencer marketing can play in enhancing customer
insight by providing a deeper  understanding of customers’
needs, sentiments and evolving values.

Based on the above, the research probl em can be
formulated as follows
1. What is the nature of the relationship between the
dimensions of influencer marketing and the dimensions
of customer insight amongst consumers of beauty and
skincare products on social media?
2. Do the dimensions of influencer marketing have an
effect on the dimensions of consumer insight amongst
customers of beauty and personal care products on
social media, and what is the nature of this effect?

Chapter two: Theoretical framework of the research
Section one: Influencer marketing
First: The concept of influencer marketing
Influencer marketing is a form of social media marketing in
which organizations identify and select a group of
influencers on these platforms, build strong relationships
with them, and then utilize them to convey their marketing
message to the  target audience and achieve certain
marketing objectives through these platforms (Al -Hariri,
2019) [4]. Influencer marketing differs from traditional
celebrity marketing, as the latter relies on well -known
figures in the arts or sports, such as singers, a ctors and
footballers, whereas influencer marketing relies on internet
celebrities who have built up a large following by sharing a
variety of content, such as lifestyle advice and other topics.
Influencer marketing is a marketing strategy based on
collaboration with influencers to promote a brand, product
or service through their accounts and channels on social
media (Elias and Majali, 2025). Consequently, influencer
marketing is a marketing approach that relies on engaging
influencers to influence custome rs and persuade them to
adopt products or brands through the use of digital
communication channels such as social media. Influencer
marketing is also regarded as a strategy whereby an
organization selects online influencers and encourages them
to engage with their followers on social media, with the aim
of utilising the unique resources and capabilities possessed
by these influencers to promote the organization's offerings
and enhance the company’s performance.

Secondly: Dimensions of influencer marketing
Following a review of previous studies, it became apparent
that some researchers treated influencer marketing as a one -
dimensional variable, as in the study by Zhang et al. (2016)
[34], whilst most researchers agreed to treat it as a
multidimensional varia ble, as reported in the studies by
Elhariry (2019); Lou & Kim (2019); Lou & Yuan, 2020;
Schouten et al., 2020; Pham, 2022; Saud, 2023) [19, 20, 25, 28, 29,
32]. These dimensions are: (expertise, likability,
trustworthiness). Accordingly, the present study will be
based on these dimensions, and each is explained below:

1. Expertise
Schouten et al (2020) [29] noted that expertise is embodied in
the knowledge and skills that influencers possess regarding
products, which followers seek; experienced influencers  are
viewed as being able to exert a greater influence on their
followers. Meanwhile, Alfarraj (2021)  [2] argues that an
influencer’s expertise plays a significant role in influencing
the brand, as expertise is considered the key attribute that
enables an influencer to succeed, gain a large following, and
be recognized by followers as a reliable source of
information.

2. Attractiveness
Lim (2017) argues that the attractiveness of social media
influencers plays an important role in increasing ad
acceptance rates, as numerous previous studies have shown
a positive relationship between the source’s attractiveness
and the customer’s attitude and purchasing behavior. Lim
also emphasized that good -looking influencers are able to
capture their followers’ attention , which has a positive
impact on customers. George (2020) added that influencers
should create engaging and eye -catching content, whilst
ensuring it is useful and memorable, so as to leave a lasting
impression on their followers.

3. Trustworthiness
Customers are often heavily influenced by reliable sources
of information in the context of influencer marketing, as an
influencer’s ability to project a trustworthy and authentic
image is considered a crucial factor in attracting a wider
audience and increasing  engagement with the brand,
enhancing the acceptance of marketing messages and
increasing the likelihood of converting interest into actual
purchase intentions (Hu et al ., 2019)  [13]. Schouten et al .
(2020) [29] argue that trustworthiness represents follow ers’
perception of the influencer’s integrity, honesty and
sincerity in presenting information, It is important to note
that this trust is negatively affected if customers feel that the
influencer is seeking personal gain from recommending the
product, which may lead to increased skepticism about their

The second theme: Customer insight
First: The concept of customer insight
Through product development, organizations seek to
maximize profitability by adopting marketing strategies
based on the analysis of customer behavior. In this context,
customer insight can be defined as an in -depth analysis of
customer behavior through the interpretation of purchasing
and interaction patterns, and the derivation of insights into
the motivations and preferences that govern their decisions.
Accordingly, customer insight represents a strategic
marketing tool that contributes to improving the value
proposition, guiding innovation, and e nhancing competitive
advantage in the market (Baehaqi, 2017)  [9]. Huang (2011)
[14] also views B customer insight as an advanced analytical
marketing approach that enables marketing managers to
generate deep insight into market needs by analyzing
customer feedback and interactions across service touch
points with sales teams, thereby allowing behavioral
patterns to be explored and insights to be derived that
support the formulation of effective marketing decisions.
Meanwhile, Price & Wrigley (2015)  [26] defined B customer
insight as a strategic understanding of customers’ current
and future needs, analyzing both stated and unstated market
needs, with the aim of identifying customer behavior and
guiding marketing decisions to achieve a better alignment
between offerings and expected value.

Second: Dimensions of customer insight
A review of previous studies reveals a notable convergence
in the identification of the dimensions comprising this
concept. It is evident that the most common and frequently
cited dimensions in previous studies on customer insight are
three in number, namely (customer attitude, customer
behavior, and customer culture),  as reported in a number of
studies: Bracewell (2015); Al -Kalabi, 2017; Hussein, 2018;
Al-Hakim, 2020; Al -Attar et al ., 2023) [1, 3 , 10 , 15 ]. Each of
these is explained below:

1. Customer attitude
Amin & Kaplan (2021)  [7] defined customer attitude as an
opinion or feeling that an individual forms about a particular
product; this feeling may be positive, negative or
ambiguous, and may change depending on their experience
with the product or service. Zeng et al (2023) [33] also view
customer attitude as an internal cognitive process formed on
the basis of prior awareness and understanding of the
product, whereby implicit perception forms the basis for the
subsequent formation of feelings and behaviors towards it,
and cannot be reduced to a simple response or a quick
consumer decision. Mustaphi (2024)  [23] defined customer
attitude as the feeling an individual harbors towards a
product, brand or service; this attitude consists of beliefs,
feelings and behavioral intentions that influence purchasing
decisions.

2. Consumer behavior
Singh et al  (2023) [27] defined consumer behavior as the
study of how individuals act when purchasi ng products or
services: what do they buy? When do they buy it? And what
are the reasons and motivations behind these decisions? The
aim is to understand the social and economic factors that
influence their purchasing choices. Šostar & Ristanović
(2023) [30] defined it as customers’ evaluation of various
consumer offerings and their selection of the most suitable
option to meet their needs and desires. Meanwhile, Mathew
& Spinelli (2025)  [22] added a definition of consumer
behavior as the framework that exa mines the mental and
behavioral processes of customers when evaluating
available alternatives and making purchasing decisions.

3. Consumer culture
Makhanya (2025) [21] views consumer culture as the way in
which culture influences customers’ interpretation  of
marketing messages, their response to products or services,
and their purchasing decisions. Nouri Dehnavi & Sioofy
Khoojine (2026)  [24] referred to consumer culture as the
pattern of consumption associated with the behavioral,
structural and social dynamics of purchasing and interacting
with products and services. Singh et al  (2026) [35] also
defined customer culture as the social framework that
governs customers’ interaction with symbolic and material
resources and their lifestyles; this interaction is  interpreted
through the lens of markets.

Thirdly: The relationship between influencer marketing
and customer insight
Kalapurackal’s research (2024) Kanaveedu & (65)  [17] a
systematic review on influencer marketing and its impact on
consumer behaviour. T he study emphasised that influencer
marketing plays a significant role in motivating and shaping
consumers’ purchasing behaviors, which forms part of
understanding consumer trends and behavior.
Similarly, the study by Ilieva et al  (2024) [16] aimed to
investigate and identify the key factors that shape

study categorized the main characteristics of digital

perceptions and attitudes.

First: Conclusions
The researcher has reached a number of important
conclusions, which can be summarized as follows
1. There is a positive and statistically significant

correlation between the dimensions of influencer
marketing and the di mensions of customer insight,
which confirms the importance of influencers in
enhancing consumers’ awareness of beauty and
personal care products.
2. There is a significant effect of the combined dimensions
of influencer marketing on the dimensions of custome r
insight, with the dimensions of influencer marketing
explaining 37.1% of the variance in the dimensions of
customer insight.
3. The dimension influencer trustworthiness is the most
influential dimension on consumer attitude, reflecting
the importance of cre dibility and trust in the success of
influencer marketing.
4. The dimension influencer expertise is the most
influential factor on consumer behavior, as it
contributes directly to shaping consumers’ purchasing
decisions and behaviors.
5. Customer culture is most  strongly influenced by the
factors of appeal and trust, whilst the influencer’s
expertise did not appear to have a significant direct
impact on this dimension.
6. The results confirm that the effectiveness of influencer
marketing does not depend solely on th e influencer’s
personal appeal, but requires the presence of trust and
expertise to achieve  a greater impact on consumers’
perceptions, attitudes and behavior.

Secondly: Recommendations
1. Companies operating in the beauty and personal care
sector should foc us on selecting influencers who
possess a high degree of credibility and trust, given the
significant role this plays in shaping customers’
attitudes towards products.
2. Priorities collaboration with influencers who possess
genuine expertise and knowledge of  beauty and
personal care products, as an influencer’s expertise is
the most influential factor in shaping customer behavior
and purchasing decisions.
3. Work to develop the marketing content presented by
influencers in a way that combines professional
expertise, personal appeal and trust, to achieve a greater
impact on customer perception.
4. Encourage influencers to provide accurate information
and realistic, objective experiences regarding the
products, rather than limiting themselves to
promotional content, t hereby helping to enhance
customers’ awareness and understanding of the
products.
5. Take into account the cultural and social aspects of
customers when designing marketing content via
influencers, given that both appeal and trust play a role
in shaping customers’ attitudes towards products.
6. Fostering ongoing interaction between influencers and
customers via social media by answering queries and
providing educational content, thereby helping to build
stronger relationships with consumers.
7. Encourage companies t o adopt clear criteria when
selecting influencers, so that the selection is not limited
to the number of followers, but also takes into account
the level of expertise, credibility and specialization
relevant to the nature of the product.
