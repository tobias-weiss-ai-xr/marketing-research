 2. Theoretical Background and Concept Deﬁ  nition
2.1 Electronic Word-of-Mouth and Multi-dimensional Online Reviews
Electronic word-of-mouth (eWOM) is the digital evolution of traditional oﬄ   ine interpersonal word-of-mouth communication
in the internet era, serving as high-value user-generated content with strong reference signiﬁ  cance in e-commerce scenarios.
Specifically, it refers to all subjective evaluation information voluntarily released by consumers after completing online
transactions, covering personal consumption experience perceptions, product advantage and defect analysis, and practical
usage feedback
[1]
. Compared with oﬄ   ine word-of-mouth communication limited by time and space, online eWOM breaks
geographical and temporal restrictions, featuring permanent information retention, cross-region dissemination, diverse
expression forms and two-way user interaction. Such open and shareable user evaluation content can be accessed and
referenced by massive unfamiliar consumers, exerting direct and profound guiding effects on public online shopping
decision-making behaviors
[12]
.
Online reviews are the most intuitive and core carrier of eWOM information. Combined with Pinduoduo’s unique operational
rules and real review ecological environment, this study deﬁ  nes the multi-dimensional online review system investigated in
the research as ﬁ  ve interrelated core components. The ﬁ  rst component is user emotional tendency, which refers to positive,
negative or neutral subjective attitudes formed by consumers after complete product experience, representing the most basic
emotional orientation of eWOM information. The second component is textual narration, including overall generalized
evaluation content and detailed experiential descriptions, which objectively reflect users’ intuitive perceptions of product
materials, functional performance, application scenarios and quality defects
[4]
. The third component is audiovisual materials,
including real photos and usage short videos taken by buyers in real consumption scenarios. Such intuitive visual content can
restore the real state of commodities, eliminate cognitive deviation caused by merchant image beautiﬁ  cation, and make up for
the insuﬃ   cient intuitiveness of pure text descriptions
[5]
. The fourth component is long-term supplementary reviews. Diﬀ  erent
from instant comments submitted immediately after purchase, delayed supplementary evaluations can truly reﬂ  ect long-term
product usage eﬀ  ects, durability performance and after-sales service quality, possessing higher long-term decision-making
reference value
[6][7]
. The fifth component is social feedback information, including user likes, helpful tags and algorithm-
generated review rankings, which reflect public recognition of individual evaluation content and serve as effective group
consumption reference signals for potential buyers
[8]
.
The ﬁ  ve above-mentioned review dimensions complement and interact with each other, jointly building a complete eWOM
information service system suitable for social commerce scenarios. Single-dimensional evaluation content has obvious
inherent limitations, as it cannot fully and objectively reﬂ  ect the comprehensive quality of commodities. Only the coordinated
operation and complementary superposition of multiple evaluation dimensions can provide consumers with multi-angle,

comprehensive and reliable decision-making references. This systematic and three-dimensional evaluation mechanism
constitutes the core competitive advantage of Pinduoduo’s review system, which is obviously superior to the simplistic single
scoring evaluation mode adopted by traditional e-commerce platforms.
 2.2 Core Theoretical Foundations
Information Diagnosticity Theory provides solid theoretical support for analyzing the information value of multi-dimensional
reviews in this research. The core viewpoint of the theory is that the practical value of information lies in its ability to help
users distinguish quality differences, eliminate cognitive ambiguity and support rational decision-making
[11]
. In online
shopping scenarios, detailed textual descriptions, original audiovisual materials and long-term supplementary reviews
can signiﬁ  cantly improve the information diagnosticity of eWOM content, enabling consumers to accurately identify real
commodity quality and avoid potential shopping risks. In contrast, empty and templated five-star praise comments lack
eﬀ  ective information discrimination capability, cannot provide substantive decision-making support for consumers, and thus
have extremely limited practical reference value
[4]
.
Social Proof Theory reasonably explains the functional mechanism of interactive feedback elements in online review systems.
Online shopping is inherently accompanied by information uncertainty, making it difficult for consumers to fully grasp
commodity parameters and practical usage eﬀ  ects before purchase. Faced with insuﬃ   cient eﬀ  ective information and decision-
making hesitation, most consumers will refer to other users’ real shopping experiences and evaluation attitudes, taking group
public opinions as important basis for their own purchase decisions
[9]
. The like and helpful functions in Pinduoduo’s review
area convert scattered individual evaluation content into visualized group recognition data, intuitively reﬂ  ecting the credibility
and practical reference value of single reviews, and profoundly affecting users’ information screening and final decision-
making behaviors. This group reference mechanism is particularly prominent in social commerce scenarios that emphasize
social interaction and word-of-mouth ﬁ  ssion dissemination
[8]
.
Hofstede’s Cultural Dimensions Theory provides a localized analytical perspective for interpreting Chinese consumers’
acceptance, screening and utilization of eWOM information. Influenced by traditional cultural accumulation, Chinese
consumers present typical collectivist consumption traits, tending to attach importance to peer consumption experiences
and group evaluation trends when making purchase decisions. Meanwhile, local users generally have strong uncertainty
avoidance awareness. When facing unknown online shopping risks and information gaps, they will actively collect diversiﬁ  ed
and comprehensive reference information to minimize decision-making risks
[10]
. The superposition of these two localized
cultural psychologies shapes Chinese consumers’ unique dependence on online word-of-mouth information, and further
regulates their behavioral habits in screening and adopting review content in social commerce scenarios.

Simple star ratings can only provide generalized product evaluations without detailed supporting information, resulting in
limited information diagnosticity that fails to help consumers pinpoint speciﬁ  c quality problems and experiential deﬁ  ciencies.
Each dimension of multi-dimensional reviews carries unique information supplementary functions, jointly forming a
complete information support system for consumer decision-making. Detailed review texts elaborate product workmanship,
material characteristics, practical application scenarios, and real usage feelings to enrich product information dimensions
[4]

 3.1.2 Psychological Value: Adapting to Local Consumption Psychology and Regulating Decision-Making Risk
Perception
From the perspective of localized consumption culture, the collectivist cultural background endows Chinese consumers with
obvious conformity tendencies in online shopping decisions, making them inclined to refer to other users’ real consumption
experiences and group evaluation trends
[10]
. Pinduoduo’s core user groups are mainly distributed in sinking markets, with
relatively insufficient online shopping experience, higher dependence on platform eWOM information, and stronger
susceptibility to group consumption public opinion. As intuitive embodiments of group consumption attitudes, highly
recognized reviews are more likely to be adopted by potential consumers and serve as core foundations for their purchase
decisions
[9]
.
Meanwhile, local consumers generally exhibit prominent uncertainty avoidance psychology. The virtuality of online shopping
triggers persistent concerns about product-promotion inconsistency, inferior quality and inadequate after-sales services,
driving consumers to actively collect diversiﬁ  ed eﬀ  ective information to reduce decision-making uncertainty
[10]
. Simple star
rating systems cannot meet consumers’ reﬁ  ned information demands, while the multi-dimensional review system provides
multi-angle, comprehensive reference clues. Detailed textual content supplements product attribute details, real-shot
audiovisual materials provide intuitive sensory evidence, supplementary reviews enrich long-term usage information, and
positive and negative evaluations respectively reﬂ  ect product advantages and potential consumption risks. The superposition
of multi-dimensional information alleviates consumers’ decision-making anxiety, assists rational risk assessment, and
gradually builds online consumption trust
[7]
.
In terms of psychological mechanisms, diﬀ  erent review dimensions correspond to diﬀ  erentiated cognitive links in consumers’
decision-making processes and aﬀ  ect purchase judgments step by step. Text information helps users establish basic product
cognition, real-shot visual materials strengthen perceptions of product authenticity, long-term supplementary reviews make up
for temporal deﬁ  ciencies in experiential information, and group interactive feedback consolidates users’ cognitive judgments.
Ultimately, these multi-dimensional elements jointly form a complete consumption psychological chain covering information
browsing, risk judgment and trust establishment.

 3.1.4 Industrial Value: Promoting Coordinated Development of Consumers, Merchants and Platforms
From the perspective of long-term industrial development, a standardized and complete multi-dimensional eWOM ecosystem
can realize tripartite win-win development for consumers, merchants and platforms, and promote the high-quality and
sustainable progress of the social commerce industry. For consumers, multi-dimensional eWOM content serves as a free
and eﬃ   cient decision-making tool, eﬀ  ectively reducing information search costs, avoiding blind consumption and economic
losses, and optimizing overall online shopping experience
[3]

can enhance commodity credibility and boost transaction conversion rates

deﬁ  ciencies in product workmanship, packaging design and after-sales services based on user negative feedback and long-
term supplementary reviews, obtaining practical optimization directions for product iteration and service upgrading
[6]

For the Pinduoduo platform, a high-quality eWOM ecosystem improves overall platform credibility and enhances user
stickiness and retention capacity. Massive authentic UGC review data also provides effective supervision clues for the
platform to identify inferior merchants, standardize store operational behaviors and improve industrial governance systems
[2]
.
Nevertheless, in practical operation, some merchants adopt short-term speculative behaviors such as comment brushing,
fake audiovisual material production and interactive data manipulation to disrupt the benign review ecology. Such behaviors
undermine the authenticity and credibility of eWOM information, increase platform governance diﬃ   culties, and restrict the
long-term healthy development of the social commerce industry.
 3.2 Practical Dilemmas of Multi-dimensional Review Ecology

reﬂ  ect true product quality and usage experience. Meanwhile, interactive indicators including likes and helpful tags can be

flooded content and difficult for consumers to access. The artificially fabricated group eWOM signals seriously mislead
sinking market users with collectivist consumption tendencies and easily trigger irrational purchase behaviors
[10]
. In
addition, the platform suffers from unbalanced review dimension distribution. Most commodity pages are dominated by
instant five-star comments, while in-depth detailed textual evaluations, authentic original audiovisual materials and long-

complete systematic framework and veriﬁ  es that diﬀ  erent eWOM elements do not function independently, but coordinate
and complement each other to jointly aﬀ  ect consumers’ cognitive judgment and ﬁ  nal purchase decisions, supplementing the
holistic analytical perspective in eWOM research. Meanwhile, this research conﬁ  rms that mature conclusions summarized
from Western e-commerce scenarios cannot be directly applied to localized Chinese social commerce scenarios

 4.2 Theoretical Contributions
This study’s theoretical contributions are mainly reﬂ  ected in two key aspects. First, this research constructs a targeted multi-
dimensional eWOM analysis framework adapted to Chinese localized social commerce scenarios. Breaking the limitations
of previous fragmented single-indicator research, it integrates five core review elements (emotional tendency, textual
narration, audiovisual materials, supplementary reviews and social feedback) into a complete research system, systematically
elaborating the collaborative functional value and internal operation logic of diﬀ  erent elements and enriching speculative
research achievements in the ﬁ  eld of localized social commerce eWOM.
Second, this study realizes the adaptive integration of classical cultural theories and social commerce eWOM scenarios.
It closely combines Hofstede’s Cultural Dimensions Theory with typical social commerce eWOM characteristics such
as supplementary reviews, interactive likes and real-shot evaluations, elaborating how localized collectivist values and
uncertainty avoidance psychology subtly regulate consumers’ acceptance, screening and utilization of different types of
eWOM information. This finding refines the internal correlation mechanism between localized consumption culture and
online word-of-mouth behavior, providing new theoretical support for interpreting Chinese consumers’ unique eWOM
dependence psychology.
 4.3 Practical Implications
In terms of platform governance, Pinduoduo needs to upgrade its anti-cheating technical system to accurately identify and
severely punish illegal behaviors such as fake comment brushing, interactive data manipulation and forged audiovisual
evaluations, reduce the algorithm traﬃ   c weight of ﬂ  ooded false reviews, and comprehensively purify the platform’s eWOM
ecological environment
[2]
. Meanwhile, the platform should optimize its review sorting algorithm, abandoning the single
interaction-volume-oriented sorting mechanism. Multiple quality-based indicators, including textual detail richness, original
shooting authentication and supplementary review duration, should be incorporated into the algorithm system to prioritize
the display of authentic, high-quality and long-term user evaluations
[8]
. In addition, it is necessary to optimize the user-end
information screening function to help consumers quickly identify high-value eWOM content. The platform should also
adjust its incentive orientation, weaken the single reward mechanism for full ﬁ  ve-star reviews, tolerate objective neutral and
negative evaluations, and encourage users to produce detailed, authentic and comprehensive consumption feedback, so as to
alleviate users’ blind conformity decision-making bias.
For platform merchants, it is necessary to abandon short-term proﬁ  t-oriented speculative behaviors of brushing fake eWOM
data. Merchants should focus on improving product quality and after-sales service levels to optimize user consumption
experience, guide real buyers to publish objective and detailed evaluations through compliant ways, and encourage users to
release long-term supplementary reviews to accumulate high-quality sustainable eWOM resources
[7]
. Additionally, merchants
should rationally view neutral and negative evaluations. Objective two-sided evaluations with both advantages and defects
are more credible in consumers’ cognition. Merchants can accurately locate operational deﬁ  ciencies based on negative user
feedback and carry out targeted product iteration and service optimization to realize long-term stable store operation
[6]
.
For individual consumers, it is essential to improve online consumption media literacy. Users should abandon the one-sided
evaluation habit of judging commodity quality merely through star ratings and interaction volumes, and comprehensively
refer to multi-dimensional information including detailed textual descriptions, original audiovisual materials and long-term
supplementary reviews. Rational screening of false eWOM content can eﬀ  ectively avoid blind conformity consumption errors
and improve the scientiﬁ  city and accuracy of online shopping decisions
[3]
.
 4.4 Research Limitations
This study adopts a pure conceptual speculative research method, conducting theoretical deduction based on literature sorting
and platform scenario observation, without using empirical research methods such as questionnaire surveys and text data
mining. Accordingly, the research conclusions and theoretical inferences need to be further veriﬁ  ed and supplemented by
subsequent quantitative empirical studies. Secondly, this research only takes Pinduoduo, a single social commerce platform
with typical sinking market characteristics, as the research object, resulting in limited conclusion applicability. Whether the

commerce platforms requires further multi-scenario and multi-platform comparative analysis.
Furthermore, this study mainly analyzes the inﬂ  uencing eﬀ  ects of two macro cultural variables (collectivism and uncertainty
avoidance), without incorporating micro individual factors such as product categories, consumer personal shopping
experience and product involvement degree, failing to fully cover all inﬂ  uencing factors of user eWOM adoption behavior.
Subsequent research can expand research dimensions by introducing multiple mediating and moderating variables, adopt
empirical methods to quantify the functional weight of diﬀ  erent review dimensions, and carry out comparative studies across
different product categories and platforms, so as to further improve the theoretical system of localized social commerce
eWOM research.
 5. Conclusion
Local social commerce represented by Pinduoduo has constructed a unique multi-dimensional eWOM system that diﬀ  ers
from traditional e-commerce platforms, whose functional value far exceeds basic product scoring services. The five core
review elements coordinate and complement each other, which can not only alleviate the inherent information asymmetry
in online transactions and improve eWOM information diagnosticity, but also build an open interactive ﬁ  eld for consumers
to exchange consumption experiences and transmit authentic product attitudes. Meanwhile, the functional logic of multi-
dimensional eWOM is highly compatible with China’s localized collectivist consumption culture and high uncertainty
avoidance psychology, accurately matching the online shopping decision-making demands of sinking market users.
Nevertheless, affected by commercial profit intervention, algorithmic traffic bias and unreasonable platform incentive
mechanisms, prevalent problems including fake eWOM proliferation, unbalanced review dimension distribution, insuﬃ   cient
exposure of high-quality evaluations and user information overload continue to damage the platform’s benign eWOM ecology
and weaken the core functional value of multi-dimensional reviews. Giving full play to the positive empowerment eﬀ  ect of
multi-dimensional eWOM in social commerce requires joint eﬀ  orts from platforms, merchants and consumers to standardize
eWOM dissemination order and purify the online consumption ecology. This study completes theoretical sorting, mechanism
analysis and dilemma discussion of localized social commerce multi-dimensional eWOM through conceptual speculation,
and relevant research viewpoints need further empirical veriﬁ  cation and optimization in future in-depth studies.
