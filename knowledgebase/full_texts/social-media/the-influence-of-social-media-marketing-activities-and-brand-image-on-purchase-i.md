Abstract: Fierce business competi tion demands the optimization of digital promotions and
brand image to build consumer trust and purchase intent. This study aims to analyze the
influence of Social Media Marketing Activities (SMMA) and Brand Image on Purchase
Intention, both directly and indirectly through Brand Trust as a mediating variable, for Amanda
Brownies on social media platform X. This study is explanatory research using a quantitative
approach. The population consists of all users of social media platform X, with a sample
selected using purposive sampling; based on the Lemeshow formula, the sample size was
determined to be 311 respondents. Data analysis was conducted using Structural Equation
Modeling-Partial Least Squares (SEM -PLS) with SmartPLS 4.0. The results indicate that
SMMA and brand image influence brand trust and purchase intention. Brand trust also
influences purchase intention. The indirect effect test confirms that brand trust partially
mediates the effects of SMMA and brand image on purchase intention.

Keywords: Amanda Brownies, Brand Image, Brand Trust, Purchase Intention, Social Media
Marketing Activities.

INTRODUCTION
The dynamics of marketing communication today have changed drastically into
dynamic two-way interactions, requiring the strategic use of social media  (Butow et al., 2026).
Amanda Brownies is one of Indonesia’s local brands that actively engages with consumers and
markets its products through the social media platform X. Through a casual and responsive
communication style, Amanda Brownies’ social media accounts have been able t o generate
high levels of engagement. However, high audience engagement on social media is not always
accompanied by a tangible increase in purchasing activity. Massive interaction on digital
platforms does not automatically guarantee a high level of consu mer willingness to make a
purchase (Ismail et al., 2025). This suggests that the effectiveness of social media marketing in
triggering purchase intent heavily relies on other mediating factors, one of which is brand trust
(Ramadhani & Prasasti, 2023). Furt hermore, brand image plays a central role, as a positive
visual and functional reputation has been shown to effectively boost trust while stimulating
purchase intent (Khairunnisa & Gumay, 2025).

Referring to the Theory of Planned Behavior, external marketing stimuli such as social
media activities and brand image serve as initial information that influences consumer beliefs,
which then gradually build trust and strongly stimulate purchase intent (Ajzen, 1991). This
study aims to analyze the influence of soc ial media marketing activities and brand image on
purchase intention both directly and indirectly through brand trust as a mediating variable for
Amanda Brownies products on social media X. The findings from this study are expected to
elucidate how digital activities and the brand image embedded in the audience’s minds bridge
the relationship leading to the final purchase decision.

METHOD
This study is a quantitative study of the explanatory research type, which aims to
explain the causal or cause -and-effect relationship between variables. The population in this
study includes all users of social media X. The sampling technique used purposive sampling,
with the following criteria for respondents: users who follow the official X account of Amanda
Brownies, have viewed and interacted with its content, and are at least seventeen years old.
Based on the Lemeshow calculation, the final sample consisted of 311 respondents.
The data collection instrument centered on an online questionnaire measured using a
one-to-five Likert scale. Data analysis employed Structural Equation Modeling -Partial Least
Squares (SEM-PLS) using SmartPLS 4.0 software. The analysis stages included an evaluation
of the measurement model to test convergent validity, discriminant validity, and reliability, as
well as an evaluation of the structural model to test the coefficient of determination and test
hypotheses through the bootstrapping process (Ghozali & Kusumadewi, 2023).

The results of the hypothesis testing directly show that social media marketing activities
influence brand trust with a probability value of 0.000. Brand image also influences brand trust.
Furthermore, brand trust, social media marketing activities, and brand image were found to
have a partial and direct influence on increasing consumer purchase intent. The indirect
mediation effect test also confirms that brand trust strongly mediates the influence of social
media marketing activities on purchase intent, as well as the influence of brand image on
purchase intent.
A discussion of these findings confirms that interactive, transparent, and consistent social
media marketing activities particularly those involving rapid updates on promotions can
minimize information asymmetry and drastically boost consumer trust (Abdullah et al., 2025).
A strong brand image also serves as a quality signal that eliminates purchase risks, thereby
fostering a solid sense of trust. Brand trust ultimately becomes a crucial catalyst that converts
positive perceptions into actual purchase intent. The effectiveness of transforming digital
promotions into purchase intent behavior depends heavily on the level of trust a brand
successfully instills in its target market.

CONCLUSION
This study demonstrates that social media marketing activities and brand image play an
essential role in building brand trust and driving purchase intent for Amanda Brownies products
within the X social media ecosystem. A digital marketing strategy that is managed in an
interactive and timely manner, combined with a brand identity that maintains a positive
reputation, has proven highly effective in strengthening consumer trust. This deeply ingrained
trust ultimately serves as the primary driving force that bridges and solidifies consumers’
commitment to allocate their spending toward purchasing the product.
A visually appealing brand not only captures initial attention but, when validated by high
integrity and trust, successfully converts all digital interactions into genuine purchase intent.
As a practical implication and contribution to marketing management in the culinary industry,
business owners a re strongly advised to focus on creating interactive marketing content that
organically encourages online participation, such as hosting quizzes, discussion threads, or
campaigns based on consumer stories. Highlighting the unique value of products must always
be optimized in every social media campaign launch to alleviate buyer hesitation, ensuring that
the massive attention and popularity achieved in the digital space are directly proportional to
an increase in corporate transaction volume.
