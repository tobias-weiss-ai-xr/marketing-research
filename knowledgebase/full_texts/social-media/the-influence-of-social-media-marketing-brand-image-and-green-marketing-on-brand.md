This empirical investigation analyzes how Social
Media Marketing , Brand Image , and Green
Marketing impact Brand Love  and Consumer
Loyalty, focusing on a sample of 110 buyers at
Kopi Tuku Simprug, South Jakarta. Applying a
quantitative causal framework, information was
gathered via purposive sampling and evaluated
through PLS -SEM ut ilizing SmartPLS 4. The
empirical outcomes indicate that Social Media
Marketing, Brand Image, and Green Marketing exert
positive and statistically meaningful influences
on Brand Love . Furthermore, Social Media
Marketing, Brand Image , and Brand Love
demonstrate positive and significant effects on
Consumer Loyalty, whereas Green Marketing yields
a positive yet statistically insignificant outcome.
These insights emphasize the critical need to
intensify digital networking strategies, preserve
a favorable reputa tion, and cultivate emotional
bonds to elevate customer retention.

INTRODUCTION
A significant surge in the Nusantara coffee commodity sector over the past
decade indicates progressive expansion reflected through the proliferation of
domestic brands capturing the attention of urban society, one of which is Kopi
Tuku. This brand represents a business entity integrating local wisdom,
commodity innovation, digital transformation, and operational approaches
directly aligned with the public 's daily life. This underscores that the escalation
of brand existence is no longer solely based on product quality superiority but is
determined by the capacity to construct identity, formulate consumer
experiences, and foster emotional affection, primari ly targeting the young
productive age group with high sensitivity to technology penetration, societal
value orientation, and environmental sustainability discourse.
The simultaneous escalation of internet penetration and social media
platforms further unde rscores the urgency of implementing digital marketing
strategies. Research by We Are Social & Meltwater (2025) documented that the
number of internet users in Indonesia has reached 212 million, representing
74.6% of the total population, with 143 million a ctive social media participants,
equivalent to 50.2% of the population. This positions digital channels as tactical
corporate instruments to foster interaction, enhance brand awareness, solidify
brand image, and stimulate emotional closeness with customers. This aligns with
the findings of Nurhadi et al. (2025), which confirm that social media marketing
activities have a significant impact on brand affection. The use of Instagram and
TikTok by Kopi Tuku also serves as a means to disseminate local cultural
narratives and reinforce brand identity.
In addition to digital marketing strategies, brand image dimensions play a
crucial role in constructing consumer perception and loyalty, where Mansoor et
al. (2025) state that a constructive brand image supported by proactive service
and solid digital presence can accelerate customer engagement levels. This is in
line with empirical findings by Cynthia and Arifiansyah (2023) at Kopi Tuku
Grand Galaxy Bekasi, which indicate that brand image significantly affects
customer satisfaction to reinforce consumer loyalty. On the other hand, the
escalation of ecological awareness makes the implementation of green marketing
approaches increasingly urgent, as Jave -Chire et al. (2025) identify that the
application of green products , green promotions, and green distribution has
proven to boost brand value and corporate credibility. This is further supported
by Snapcart data (2024), which reports that the use of environmentally friendly
packaging ranks highest as the most sought-after sustainable commodity among
consumers in Indonesia, reaching 50%.
The development of consumer loyalty also demands an escalation of
affective dimensions through essential brand love instruments. Nurhadi et al.
(2025) explain that brand awareness and brand image serve as mediating
variables for the influence of social media marketing on brand love and customer
loyalty. This argument is supported by Weiger et al. (2025), who emphasize that
engagement in the emotional and cognitive domains with a brand plays an
essential role in accelerating the formation of long-term loyalty. Nevertheless, the
empirical reality at Kopi Tuku represents a discrepancy between the

LITERATURE REVIEW
The Influence of Social Media Marketing on Brand Love
 Referring to the theoretical perspective of Tran et al. (2021), the dynamics of
social media -based marketing activities play an essential role in weaving
affective ties with customers through mechanisms of reciprocal communication,
high levels of interactivity, and the presentation of contextual content. Empirical
confirmation from Nguyen et al. (2024) and Nurhadi et al. (2025) further
strengthens the argument that social media marketing can have a direct impact
on brand love through personalization approaches and stimulation of emotional
engagement. Based on that conceptual framework, the first hypothesis
proposition is formulated as follows.
H1: Social Media Marketing has a positive and significant effect on Brand
Love.
The Influence of Brand Image on Brand Love
 The constructive construction of brand image has the potential to evoke
pride and deep affective connections from customers toward a brand (Keller &
Swaminathan, 2020). Empirical findings from Hafidz & Astuti (2023) and
Mansoor et al. (2025) confi rm that the strength of brand image has a tangible
impact on the escalation of brand love, due to the brand's capacity to represent
symbolic and emotional relevance for its users.
H2: Brand Image has a positive and significant effect on Brand Love.

The Influence of Brand Image on Consumer Loyalty
 The constructive construction of brand image gives rise to perceptions of
value, trust, and a unique sense of pride for cus tomers, which then becomes the
main foundation in the formation of long -term loyalty (Cynthia & Arifiansyah,
2023). Empirical studies by Tran et al. (2021) and Mansoor et al. (2025) further
affirm that brand image holds a crucial position as a predictor of  consumer
loyalty.
H5: Brand Image has a positive and significant effect on Consumer Loyalty.
The Influence of Green Marketing on Consumer Loyalty
 The implementation of green marketing has the capacity to accelerate
customer loyalty, as consumers naturally associate a brand with principles of
sustainability and social responsibility (Ardiyanti & Nasir, 2024). The level of
corporate transparency and ecological sensitivity demonstrated has proven
capable of fostering deep respect and apprec iation from consumers toward the
brand.
H6: Green Marketing has a positive and significant impact on Consumer
Loyalty.
The Influence of Brand Love on Consumer Loyalty
 Brand love represents a form of intense emotional attachment that
motivates customers to maintain their loyalty, even when faced with various
alternative brand choices in the market (Batra et al., 2020; Eklund & Helmefalk,
2018). Furthermore, research cond ucted by Burhanudin & Febryanti (2023)
reinforces the finding that brand love is a strong determinant that projects
loyalty.
H7: Brand Love has a positive and significant effect on Consumer Loyalty.

METHODOLOGY
This study applies a quantitative paradigm through a causal design to
investigate the patterns of association and determination among the variables
being studied. The implementation of the research is scheduled for the period
from April to June 2025, focus ing on the demographic of young productive age
consumers of Kopi Tuku Simprug in the South Jakarta area. The classification of
the variables studied includes Social Media Marketing (X1), Brand Image (X2),
and Green Marketing (X3) as exogenous constructs, B rand Love (Z) as the
mediating variable, and Consumer Loyalty (Y) as the endogenous variable. The
target population includes all individuals who have made a purchase transaction
at Kopi Tuku Simprug, while the sample determination applies the purposive
sampling method with the criteria that participants are aged 18 –30 years and
have a transaction history at that location. Referring to a total of 21 instrument
indicators and sample size calculation parameters, a minimum of 105
respondents was determined, where the accumulated empirical data successfully
gathered reached 110 respondents, all of whom were deemed suitable for the
analysis process.
The process of obtaining primary data was carried out through a
structured questionnaire instrument distributed to respondents according to the
sample criteria. This data collection instrument is designed to measure the five
research variables based on the previously established operational indicators.
The assessment of subject responses uses a five -point Likert scale, ranging from
a score of 1 representing the category "Strongly Disagree" to a score of 5 for the
category "Strongly Agree." Before proceeding to the main hypothesis testing, the
primary data first undergoes a descriptive analysis stage to map the
sociodemographic profile of the respondents along with the distribution of
response tendencies for each indicator. The evaluation of the quality of the testing
instrument involves validity and reliability tests, where the internal reliability
coefficient of the ques tionnaire is measured using the Cronbach’s Alpha
parameter.
Data processing activities and research hypothesis testing utilize variance-
based structural equation modeling, namely Structural Equation Modeling –
Partial Least Square (SEM-PLS) with the help of the SmartPLS 4.0 computational
application. The series of analysis stages is divided into the evaluation of the

DISCUSSION
The Influence of Social Media Marketing on Brand Love among Tuku Simprug
Coffee Consumers in South Jakarta
Based on the hypothesis testing estimation, the Social Media Marketing
variable produced a t -statistics value of 5.562, a significance level p -value of
0.000, and a path coefficient of 0.434. The figure confirms that Social Media
Marketing has a positive and significant impact on  Brand Love. Therefore, the
first hypothesis (H1) in this research is statistically accepted.
These empirical findings reflect that the more optimal the execution of
digital marketing campaigns through social media by Kopi Tuku, the higher the
degree of cu stomer love for the brand will increase. The presentation of
captivating product content, continuous execution of digital promotions,
strategic partnerships with influencers, and interactive communication with
consumers play a key role in fostering interes t, emotional attachment, and a
strong sense of ownership toward the Kopi Tuku brand.
The results of this investigation reinforce the theoretical perspective
proposed by Tran et al. (2021), where social media marketing activities play an
essential role in weaving affective relationships with customers through two-way
communication patterns, high levels of interactivity, and the distribution of
information content that aligns with market preferences. In addition, this
empirical confirmation is also in line wi th previous research conducted by
Nguyen et al. (2024), Nurhadi et al. (2025), and Sikandar & Ahmed (2019), which
affirm that Social Media Marketing consistently has a positive and significant
impact on strengthening Brand Love among consumers.
The Influen ce of Brand Image on Brand Love among Tuku Simprug Coffee
Consumers in South Jakarta
Based on the hypothesis testing calculations, the Brand Image variable
recorded a t-statistics value of 2.124, a significance level of p-values at 0.034, and
a path coefficient of 0.140. The figure proves that Brand Image has a positive and
significant impact on Brand Love. Thus, the formulation of the second hypothesis
(H2) in this research is confirmed to be accepted.
The findings reflect that the improving brand image quality of Kopi Tuku
will linearly boost customer loyalty. The brand's capacity to respond to customer
needs, the credibility of information dissemination, and the differentiation of
characteristics from competing coffee shops act as the main drivers i n fostering
interest, affective attachment, and consumer ownership toward Kopi Tuku.
Referring to the descriptive analysis exposure, the indicator "I am
confident in the information I know about Kopi Tuku" recorded the highest
average or mean score within the scope of the Brand Image variable. This
achievement indicates that consumer confidence in the reputation and clarity of
information about Kopi Tuku plays a crucial role in weaving a harmonious
emotional relationship between buyers and the brand.
The results of this investigation strengthen the theoretical foundation that
states that a positive brand image significantly contributes to shaping
