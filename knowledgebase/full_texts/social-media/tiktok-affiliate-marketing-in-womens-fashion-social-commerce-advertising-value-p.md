 The rapid growth of social commerce has transformed TikTok from an
entertainment platform into a major digital marketing channel, particularly
through affiliate video content. In Indonesia, women’s fashion products are
among the best -selling categories in online shopping activities, while
TikTok affiliate videos increasingly influence consumers’ product discovery
and purchase decisions. Drawing on the Extended Advertising Value Model
(EAVM), this study examines how entertainment, informativeness,
credibility, personalization, interactivity, and irritation influence purchase
intention through perceived value in the context of TikTok affiliate videos.
Using a quantitative explanatory design, this study surveys 260 female
TikTok users in Indonesia aged 18 years and above who have watched,
interacted with, or purchased products from women’s fashion affiliate
content on TikTok. Data are collected through an online questionnaire using
purposive sampling techniques and analyzed using Structural Equation
Modelling-Partial Least Squares (SEM -PLS). The results show that five of
the six dimensions: personalization, entertainment, interactivity,
informativeness, and irritation significantly influenced perceived value,
whereas credibility did not. Perceived value strongly predicted purchase
intention and functioned as a central mediating mechanism: it  fully
mediated the effects of entertainment, informativeness, irritation, and
interactivity, partially mediated the effect of personalization, and showed
no mediation for credibility. Personalization exerted the strongest influence
on both perceived value and purchase intention. These findings extend the
advertising value model to the affiliate -marketing context and position
perceived value as the key psychological route through which advertising
attributes are translated into purchase intention.

consumption patterns in Indonesia, particularly in online shopping and social media
interaction. The number of social media users in Indonesia has reached 180 million,
equivalent to 62.9% of the total population, with an average daily usage of more than
three hours (We Are Social & Meltwater, 2025). Within this ecosystem, TikTok records
the highest level of engagement with usage duration reaching approximately 1 hour
and 53 minutes per day and has shifted information-seeking behavior from text-based
formats toward video content that is more visual, c oncise, and interactive (Soonmin,
2024). These conditions have driven the growth of social commerce, in which

entertainment, and irritation. As digital media evolved toward greater interactivity

LITERATURE REVIEW
Extended Advertising Value Model (EAVM)
The Advertising Value Model (AVM), introduced by Ducoffe (1995, 1996) ,
explains advertising value as consumers’ subjective evaluation of an advertisement
based on three dimensions: informativeness, entertainment, and irritation.
Informativeness refers to the extent to which advertising provides relevant and useful
information, entertainment reflects the enjoyment generated by advertising, while
irritation describes the annoyance or discomfort caused by advertising. These
dimensions shape consumers’ attitudes toward advertising and may influence
purchase intention (Ducoffe, 1995, 1996) . In digital platforms such as TikTok,
advertising value remains relevant because users evaluate content not only through
product information, but also through entertainment and viewing comfort (Logan et
al., 2012) . The classic AVM has been extended to better explain advertising in
interactive and participatory digital media. The Extended Advertising Value Model
(EAVM) adds dimensions such as credibility, personalization, and interactivity.
Credibility reflects consumers’ trust in the message, source, and advertising medium
(Brackett & Carr Jr, 2001; Lütjens et al., 2022; Ye et al., 2025) . Personalization refers to
the platform’s ability to tailor advertising content to users’ preferences and needs,
thereby increasing message relevance (Arora, 2022; Arora & Agarwal, 2019) .
Interactivity describes two -way communication between users, creators, and
advertising content through comments, likes, shares, and other engagement features
(Chingning Wang, 2005; Ping Zhang, 2011) . Based on this framework, this study
employs six EAVM dimensions: entertainment, informativeness, irritation, credibility,
personalization, and interactivity.

through the creation and exchange of user -generated content. (Kaplan & Haenlein,
2010) define it as a group of internet -based applications built on the ideological
foundations of Web 2.0 that support user collaboration and participation. In marketing
communication, social media functions not merely as a channel for message

Uses and Gratifications Theory (UGT)
Theoretically, this study is rooted in the communication science perspective,
particularly Uses and Gratifications Theory (UGT). The theory views audiences as
active individuals who consciously select media to fulfill particular needs, such as
information, entertainment, social interaction, and personal experience  (Katz et al.,
1973). Ruggiero, (2000)  asserts that new media introduce the characteristics of
interactivity, demassification, and asynchronous communication, which strengthen
users’ active role, making the UGT approach increasingly relevant for explaining the
behavior of internet and social me dia users (Azam, 2015; Roy, 2009) . From the UGT
perspective, the dimensions of the EAVM can be understood as forms of media
gratification that users seek when accessing TikTok affiliate video content. When
content succeeds in fulfilling these needs, consumers form a positive perceived value,
which in turn drives purchase intention toward the products offered.

EAVM Dimensions and Purchase Intention

interactivity can shape responses to purchase intention. Various studies have shown
that the advertising value deri ved from these dimensions influences consumer
purchasing decisions in the context of digital advertising, including on platforms like
TikTok and e-commerce (Awang Jual et al., 2025; Dwinanda et al., 2022). Advertising

personalization and interactive experiences in digital advertising also strengthen
advertising value, resulting in increased purchase intention (Nguyen et al., 2025) . In
the context of TikTok affiliate videos, the six EAVM dimensions create a
communication experience that can increase consumer interest in purchasing fashion
products. The hypotheses to be tested are:

EAVM Dimensions and Perceived Value
The EAVM dimensions can influence perceived value. Entertainment,
informativeness, irritation, credibility, personalization, and interactivity shape how
consumers assess the value of an advertisement. The literature shows that perceived
value is formed from consumers' cognitive and affective evaluations of consumption
experiences, including in the context of digital advertising (Zhang and Wang, 2005;
Sánchez-Fernández & Iniesta-Bonillo, 2006). In the context of digital media and short
videos, informative, engaging, and relevant advertising dimensions have been shown
to increase perceived value because consumers perceive they are receiving
commensurate benefits from the content they consume (Ping Zhang, 2011; Xiao et al.,
2019).  Furthermore, elements such as personalization and interactivity in mobile
advertising can also strengthen perceived value by enhancing user engagement and
experience (Hewei, 2022; Zhao & Wang, 2020). Martins et al. (2019) also confirmed that
advertising value derived from the quality of smartphone ads contributes significantly
to increasing consumer perceived value. Thus, in the context of TikTok affiliate videos,
the EAVM dimension plays a role in shaping consumers' perceived value perceptions
of advertising content, which can then influence their evaluation of the products
offered. The hypotheses to be tested are:
H2a: Entertainment positively influences perceived value in TikTok affiliate videos for
women’s fashion products.
H2b: Informativeness positively influences perceived value in TikTok affiliate videos
for women’s fashion products.
H2c: Irritation negatively influences perceived value in TikTok affiliate videos for
women’s fashion products.
H2d: Credibility positively influences perceived value in TikTok affiliate videos for
women’s fashion products.
H2e: Personalization positively influences perceived value in TikTok affiliate videos
for women’s fashion products.
H2f: Interactivity positively influences perceived value in TikTok affiliate videos for
women’s fashion products.

Perceived Value and Purchase Intention
Perceived value explains how consumers evaluate the benefits of a product,
service, or advertising content relative to the sacrifices involved. It is a
multidimensional, subjective, and contextual construct that includes functional,
emotional, social, epis temic, and conditional aspects (Boksberger & Melsen, 2011;
Sánchez-Fernández & Iniesta -Bonillo, 2006) . In the context of women’s fashion on
TikTok, perceived value is not limited to functional benefits, but also includes
aesthetics, style, emotional appeal, and self-representation. Perceived value influences
purchase intention. Perceived value reflects con sumers' evaluation of the benefits
gained compared to the sacrifices made, making it a crucial factor in the purchasing
decision-making process. Literature shows that when consumers perceive high value
from an advertisement or product, their propensity to purchase also increases (Zhang
and Wang, 2005; Ping Zhang, 2011) . In the context of digital media, perceived value
has been shown to have a significant influence on purchase intention because it can
shape positive attitudes toward the product offered (Martins et al., 2019b) . In short
video media, including TikTok, content that provides information, entertainment, and
relevance will increase perceived value, which then drives consumer purchase
intention (Xiao et al., 2019) . Therefore, the higher the perceived value consumers
perceive from affiliate video content, the greater the likelihood of purchase intention
for the product offered. The hypotheses to be tested are:
H3: Perceived value positively influences purchase intention toward women’s fashion
products in TikTok affiliate videos.

EAVM Dimensions, Perceived Value and Purchase Intention
Perceived value mediates the influence of EAVM dimensions on purchase
intention. From a consumer value theory perspective, consumers' perceived value
serves as a psychological mechanism that bridges advertising stimuli with behavioral
responses, including purchase intention (Chingning Wang, 2005; Sánchez-Fernández
& Iniesta-Bonillo, 2006). EAVM dimensions such as entertainment, informativeness,
irritation, credibility, personalization, and interactivity not only have a direct
influence but also shape value perceptions, which then determine the extent to which
consumers are willing to make a purchase. The literature shows that perceived value
plays a significant mediator in the relationship between digital advertising
characteristics and purchase intention (Hewei, 2022; Ping Zhang, 2011). In the context
of short video media and digital advertising, increasing perceived value has been
shown to be a key pathway explaining how advertising content can more effectively

METHODS
This study employed a quantitative approach with an explanatory research
design to examine the causal relationships among the Extended Advertising Value
Model (EAVM) dimensions, perceived value, and purchase intention in the context of
TikTok affiliate vid eos for women’s fashion products. The population of this study
comprised female TikTok users in Indonesia aged 18 years and above who had
watched, interacted with, or purchased women’s fashion products promoted through
TikTok affiliate video content. Given  that the exact population size is unknown, a
purposive sampling technique was applied with the following criteria: (1) female,
aged 18 years or older; (2) an active TikTok user; and (3) having watched or interacted
with TikTok affiliate videos promoting women’s fashion products. A total of 260 valid
responses were collected through an online questionnaire distributed via social media
platforms, a sample size that satisfies the recommended minimum for Structural
Equation Modelling –Partial Least Squares (SEM -PLS), which requires at least ten
times the largest number of structural paths directed at a construct in the model (Hair
et al., 2019). The research instrument was a structured questionnaire measured on a
five-point Likert scale ranging from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). The
measurement items were adapted from previously validated scales: entertainment,
informativeness, and irritation were adapted from (Ducoffe, 1996); credibility from
Brackett & Carr (2001) and  Tsang et. al (2004); personalization from Arora & Agarwal
(2019); interactivity from Zhang and Wang  (2005) ; perceived value from (Sánchez-

Discussion
As an interdisciplinary study positioned at the intersection of communication
science and marketing, the findings of this research can be read on two
complementary levels: how message and media characteristics shape audience
responses (a communication perspective), and how those responses are converted into
consumer value judgments and behavioral intention (a marketing perspective). The
finding that personalization is the strongest predictor of both perceived value and
purchase intention illustrates this in tersection most clearly. From a communication
standpoint, personalization on TikTok is not merely a message attribute but a property
of the medium itself: the interest -graph algorithm functions as a gatekeeper that
curates which persuasive messages reach which audiences, so that message relevance
is co-produced by the platform’s algorithmic logic rather than designed solely by the
communicator. From a marketing standpoint, this algorithmically produced relevance
is what consumers convert into value and, ultimately, purchase intention. This result
is consistent with Arora (2022)  and Dwinanda et al., (2022) , who identified
personalization as a key driver of advertising effectiveness on social media, and
extends those findings to the affiliate -marketing context, where recommendation
algorithms rather than deliberate media buying determine content exposure.
The full mediation observed for entertainment, informativeness, interactivity,
and irritation carries an important communication -theoretical implication:
advertising message attributes do not operate through a direct stimulus –response
mechanism, but throug h an audience -centered evaluative process. This is precisely
what the Uses and Gratifications perspective predicts: audiences are active
participants who assess whether content fulfills their needs for enjoyment,
information, and social interaction, and it  is this gratification -based evaluation,
crystallized as perceived value, that precedes behavioral intention (Katz et al., 1973;
Ruggiero, 2000) . The finding thus refines the mixed evidence in earlier studies:
whereas Hasbullah et al., (2023) and Awang Jual et al., (2025) reported direct effects of
entertainment on purchase intention, and Nguyen-Viet et al., (2022) emphasized the
direct role of informativeness, the present results indicate that in TikTok affiliate
videos these attributes work indirectly. Enjoyable, informative, and interactive content

The non -significant effect of credibility is a notable deviation from both the

literature, which consistently position credibility as a core determinant of persuasion
outcomes (Brackett & Carr Jr, 2001; Lütjens et al., 2022; Ye et al., 2025) . Read through
a communication lens, this finding suggests a shift in how trust operates in
algorithmic media environments. First, affiliate content on TikTok is produced
predominantly by ordinary creators whose communication style is peer -like rather
than institutional; audiences may therefore apply a uniform, relatively
undifferentiated baseline of trust across creators, weakening credibility’s
discriminating power as a message cue. Second, the visual affordances of the medium
allow consumers to evaluate fashion products directly through try-on demonstrations
the evidence is in the message itself, reducing reliance on the perceived
trustworthiness of the messenger. Third, trust appears to have migrated from the
individual communicator to the platform ecosystem, as institutional safeguards such
as buyer protection, reviews, and easy returns on TikTok Shop absorb the risk that
source credibility traditionally mitigated. This interpretation echoes Ye et al. (2025)
meta-analytic conclusion that the effects of advertising value dimensions are highly
contingent on platform context and advertising type.
Taken together, the results position perceived value as the key psychological
route through which persuasive communication in social commerce is translated into
consumer behavior. The theoretical contribution of this study is therefore twofold and
inherently interdisciplinary. For communication science, it demonstrates that the
effects of message attributes in algorithmic short-video media are audience-mediated
and platform -contingent, extending the EAVM and UGT to the TikTok affiliate -
marketing context. Fo r marketing, it establishes perceived value rather than
advertising value or attitude toward advertising used in prior models (Hussain et al.,
2023; Ye et al., 2025) as the mediating construct that best explains how content
characteristics become purchase intention. Practically, the findings suggest that
affiliate creators should design communication strategies that maximize perceived
relevance and value: leveraging algorithm -friendly personalization signals,
entertaining and informative formats such as try -on and styling videos, and

CONCLUSIONS
This study examined how the six dimensions of the Extended Advertising
Value Model (EAVM) entertainment, informativeness, irritation, credibility,
personalization, and interactivity shape purchase intention toward women’s fashion
products in TikTok affilia te videos, both directly and through the mediating role of
perceived value. The findings show that entertainment, informativeness, irritation,
personalization, and interactivity significantly influence perceived value, whereas
credibility does not. Persona lization emerges as the most influential dimension,
exerting the strongest effect on both perceived value and purchase intention, and is
also the only EAVM dimension with a significant direct effect on purchase intention.
Perceived value, in turn, is confirmed as a strong and significant predictor of purchase
intention and operates as the central mediating mechanism of the model: it fully
mediates the effects of entertainment, informativeness, irritation, and interactivity,
partially mediates the effect of personalization, and shows no mediating effect for
credibility.  These results indicate that the effectiveness of TikTok affiliate videos in
driving purchase intention for women’s fashion products depends less on the direct
persuasive force of individual c ontent attributes and more on the value that
consumers construct from their overall viewing experience. Theoretically, this study
extends the EAVM beyond conventional and general digital-advertising contexts into
TikTok’s algorithm -driven affiliate -marketing ecosystem, and positions perceived
value rather than advertising value or attitude toward advertising used in prior
models as a robust psychological bridge between content characteristics and purchase
behavior. Practically, the findings suggest that affi liate creators and brands should
prioritize personalized, entertaining, informative, and interactive content while
minimizing intrusive elements, since these strategies raise the perceived value that
ultimately drives purchase intention; investment in alg orithmic personalization and
content experience is likely to yield greater returns than efforts focused solely on
strengthening source credibility.  This study is subject to several limitations. First, the
sample was limited to female TikTok users in Indon esia and to the women’s fashion
product category, which may constrain the generalizability of the findings to other
demographic groups, product categories, or cultural settings. Second, the cross -
sectional design captures consumer perceptions at a single p oint in time and cannot
