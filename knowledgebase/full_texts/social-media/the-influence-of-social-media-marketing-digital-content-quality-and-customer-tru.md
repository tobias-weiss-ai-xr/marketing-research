Abstract. The rapid growth of internet and social media penetration in Indonesia has transformed
marketing activities from conventional approaches into interactive digital marketing, particularly
among Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs). Just Hebite, an MS ME producing modified
cassava flour (mocaf)-based food products in Banjarnegara, Central Java, faces challenges in building
brand awareness due to the public's limited familiarity with mocaf-based products compared to wheat
flour, alongside the still -developing use of social media marketing. This study aims to examine and
analyze the influence of social media marketing, digital content quality, and customer trust on brand
awareness of the Just Hebite MSME. A quantitative explanatory research design was empl oyed using
Partial Least Square-Structural Equation Modeling (PLS -SEM) with SmartPLS 4 software. Data were
collected through a structured questionnaire using a five-point Likert scale distributed to 104 respond-
ents, determined through purposive sampling fr om consumers who follow Just Hebite's Instagram
account, have been exposed to its social media content, and/or have purchased its products. [Results
of hypothesis testing (H1 -H3) — pending completion of the outer and inner model analysis in
SmartPLS 4.] The findings are expected to provide theoretical contributions to digital marketing liter-
ature, particularly regarding brand awareness formation among local, education -based MSME prod-
ucts, and practical contributions for Just Hebite in strengthening its digital marketing strategy.

Keywords: Brand Awareness; Customer Trust; Digital Content Quality; MSME; PLS-SEM; Social

1. Introduction
Digital technology development in Indonesia continues to show an increasing trend,
marked by the growing number of internet users. According to We Are Social and DataRe-
portal, internet users in Indonesia reached approximately 212 million in 2025, or around
74.6% of the total population (Kemp, 2025b). This growth has been accompanied by a shift
in marketing activities from conventional (offline) approaches toward digital marketing, with
social media marketing on platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, Facebook,  and WhatsApp
becoming a primary tool not only for communication but also for business promotion. Social
media has changed the way companies interact with consumers, making the relationship more
participatory and engagement-based (Appel et al., 2020).
Social media marketing enables Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) to
introduce their products through uploaded content, provide clear information, and build in-
teractive relationships with customers. Content quality plays a central role in this proces s:
informative, attractive, relevant, and easy-to-understand content helps build a positive brand
image and shapes consumer perceptions of brand awareness (Appel et al., 2020; Tafesse,
2020). In addition, content quality indirectly affects customer trust, which becomes a key
basis for consumer decisions and perceptions since, in social media -based marketing, con-
sumers cannot directly see or try the product. Higher levels of consumer trust increase the
likelihood that consumers will recognize, remember, and choose a particular brand over its
competitors (Anastasiei et al., 2024).

Brand awareness itself is a key factor influencing consumer decisions, reflecting the
ability of consumers to recognize and recall a brand when considering a purchase among
many alternatives. Social media marketing activities have been shown to significant ly influ-
ence brand awareness, brand image, and brand equity, thereby strengthening consumer per-
ceptions of a brand (Faisal & Ekawanto, 2022). Social media marketing is also used by the
Just Hebite MSME, a producer of mocaf (modified cassava flour) -based food products lo-
cated in Banjarnegara.
Just Hebite was established by Arin Listya Apriyani in 2019 and began digital marketing
in 2022 through Instagram (@justhebiteofficial) and TikTok (@JustHebite_Official). In prac-
tice, Just Hebite faces considerable challenges in increasing sales and brand awareness. Data
from Statistics Indonesia (BPS) through the National Socioeconomic Survey (SUSENAS)
show that wheat flour consumption in Indonesia during 2021-2025 remained at around 2.7-
2.9 kg per capita per year (Badan Pusat Statistik, 2025), while mocaf flour has not been spe-
cifically recorded in national consumption data, indicating that public awareness and utiliza-
tion of mocaf-based products remain relatively low. This condition, combined with the still
sub-optimal use of social media marketing, poses a major obstacle for Just Hebite in attracting
consumer attention and audience engagement.
A number of previous studies indicate that social media marketing and content quality
influence brand awareness and sales growth; however, findings remain inconsistent, particu-
larly regarding whether the effect is direct or mediated by brand awareness. Furthermore,
most previous studies focus on general products or large companies, leaving education-based
local products such as mocaf flour, particularly in the MSME context, underexplored. Based
on this research gap, the present study aims to examine the influence of social media market-
ing, digital content quality, and customer trust on brand awareness at the Just Hebite MSME
in Banjarnegara Regency.
Based on the background above, the research problems are formulated as follows: (1)
How does social media marketing affect brand awareness at the Just Hebite MSME? (2) How
does digital content quality affect brand awareness at the Just H ebite MSME? (3) How does
customer trust affect brand awareness at the Just Hebite MSME? Accordingly, this study aims
to empirically analyze the influence of social media marketing, digital content quality, and
customer trust on brand awareness at the Just Hebite MSME in Banjarnegara Regency. This
study is expected to contribute theoretically to the development of digital marketing literature,
particularly regarding the role of brand awareness among local, education-based MSME prod-
ucts, and practically to su pport Just Hebite and other MSME actors in developing digital -
based marketing strategies.
2. Literature Review
Social Media Marketing
Social media marketing refers to marketing activities carried out through social media
platforms that enable companies to build more intensive interaction with consumers, thereby
creating closer and more sustainable relationships in the digital era (Rahman et al., 2024).
Social media marketing is a powerful tool for companies to reach and interact with target
audiences, promote products and services, and understand consumer behavior and prefer-
ences (Appel et al., 2020). This study adopts the entertainment, informativeness, and interac-
tivity dimensions developed by Piramita et al. (2021) to measure social media marketing.
Digital Content Quality
Content quality is an important element in digital marketing that plays a role in attract-
ing attention, retaining interest, and building relationships with the audience through content
that is relevant and valuable (Karimah & Rinonce, 20 25). Content quality can be assessed
through three dimensions: intrinsic quality (completeness, originality, objectivity, timeliness,
and accuracy of information), representational quality (how well information is presented to
facilitate understanding), an d contextual quality (relevance of information to users' needs)
(Adeli & Widyaningrum, 2024).
Customer Trust
Customer trust is one of the important factors in building long -term relationships be-
tween consumers and companies. Trust arises when consumers believe that a brand or com-
pany is able to fulfill its promises, deliver value, and act honestly and consistently (Adhari,
2021).

Referring to Mayer et al. (1995), customer trust consists of three main dimensions: abil-
ity, benevolence, and integrity, which respectively reflect a seller's competence, genuine con-
cern for consumers, and consistency in upholding honesty and ethical values.
Brand Awareness
Brand awareness is the ability of prospective consumers to recognize and recall a brand
as part of a particul ar product category (Aaker, 1991). A high level of brand awareness in-
creases the likelihood that a brand will be considered and chosen by consumers. This study
adopts the recall, recognition, purchase, and consumption dimensions developed by Fadilla
and Othman (2024) to measure brand awareness.
Conceptual Framework and Hypotheses Development
The conceptual framework of this study is built upon the utilization of social media
marketing, digital content quality, and customer trust as key factors in building brand aware-
ness at the Just Hebite MSME. Social media marketing expands the reach of information and
increases brand exposure to consumers; digital content quality conveys attractive, informative,
and relevant messages that create a positive impression in c onsumers' minds; and customer
trust strengthens consumer confidence in the credibility and reliability of the brand. These
three variables are hypothesized to simultaneously influence brand awareness as follows:
H1: Social media marketing has a significant effect on brand awareness.
H2: Digital content quality has a significant effect on brand awareness.
H3: Customer trust has a significant effect on brand awareness.
3. Research Method
Research Design
This study employs a quantitative approach with an explanatory research design, which
aims to explain the causal relationships among the variables under investigation. This design
was chosen because the study not only describes the phenomenon but also tests the influence
of social media marketing, digital content quality, and customer trust on brand awareness.
Data were analyzed using Partial Least Squares -Structural Equation Modeling (PLS -SEM)
with the aid of SmartPLS 4 software, which was selected for its  tolerance toward non-nor-
mally distributed data and suitability for relatively small sample sizes compared to covariance-
based SEM (Hair et al., 2021).
Population and Sample
The population of this study consists of consumers of the Just Hebite MSME in Ban-
jarnegara Regency, Central Java, totaling 343 individuals based on data obtained from the
MSME's records. The sampling technique used is purposive sampling, in which respondents
were required to meet the following criteria: (1) being a follower of the Just Hebite Instagram
account, (2) having purchased a Just Hebite product, (3) having seen or been exposed to
marketing content on social media, and (4) having sufficient understanding to assess content
quality, customer trust, and brand awareness. Referring to  Hair et al. (2010), the minimum
sample size for multivariate analysis is determined by multiplying the number of indicators by
5 to 10. As this study uses 13 indicators, an eight-fold multiplier was applied (13 × 8 = 104),
resulting in a final sample of 104 respondents.
Data Collection
Primary data were collected through a structured questionnaire distributed to respond-
ents using a five -point Likert scale (1 = strongly disagree to 5 = strongly agree), measuring
perceptions of social media marketing, digita l content quality, customer trust, and brand
awareness. Secondary data were obtained from reference books, scientific journals, prior re-
search articles, and observation of Just Hebite's social media activity, and were used to
strengthen the theoretical foundation and support the primary data analysis.

Measurement Model (Outer Model) Evaluation
The outer model evaluation was conducted using a reflective measurement model to
assess the validity and reliability of the research instrument. Convergent validity was assessed
through outer loading values (ideally ≥ 0.70, with 0.50-0.60 considered acceptable for explor-
atory research) and Average Variance Extracted (AVE ≥ 0.50) (Hair et al., 2021). Discrimi-
nant validity was a ssessed using the Fornell -Larcker criterion and cross -loading values
(Henseler et al., 2015). Reliability was assessed through Cronba ch's Alpha and Composite
Reliability, with a construct considered reliable if both values exceed 0.70 (Ghozali & Latan,
2020).
Structural Model (Inner Model) Evaluation
The inner model evaluation was used to examine the relationships among latent varia-
bles, consisting of the coefficient of determination (R²), effect size (f²), and path coefficients.
R² values of 0.75, 0.50, an d 0.25 indicate strong, moderate, and weak models, respectively,
while f² values of 0.02, 0.15, and 0.35 indicate small, medium, and large effects (Hair et al.,
2021). Path coefficients were tested through a bootstrapping procedure, with relationships
considered significant if the t -statistic exceeds 1.96 or the p -value is below 0.05 (Ghozali &
Latan, 2020).

4. Results and Discussion
Overview of the Research Object
Just Hebite is a local, food-processing MSME focusing on products made from mocaf
(Modified Cassava Flour), operating under UKM Karunia, which was established in 2016 in
Banjarnegara, Central Java. Since 2021, the enterprise has used the brand name “JustHebite,”
with the tagline “Just Healthy in Every Bite,” reflecting its commitment to providing healthy,
locally sourced food products such as mocaf cookies, mocaf noodles, mocaf cereal, and other
snacks. Just Hebite utilizes several digital platforms, namely Instagram, TikTok, Google Busi-
ness Profile, Facebook, and WhatsApp Business, to promote its products and interact with
consumers.
Respondent Characteristics
Of the 104 respondents, the majority were female, comprising 91 respondents (87.5%),
while male respondents accounted for 13 respondents (12.5%), indicating that Just Hebite's
products are more widely used by women. In terms of purchasing behavior, 100% of re-
spondents had been exposed to Just Hebite's social media content, and 90 respondents
(86.5%) had purchase experience with Just Hebite products, while the remaining 14 respond-
ents (13.5%) had been exposed to the content but had not yet made a purchase, representing
a potential audience for pure brand awareness assessment.

Regency. Based on the PLS-SEM analysis using SmartPLS 4 involving 104 respondents, [find-
