ABSTRACT :
Influencer marketing has become an important part of social media marketing, especially among Generation Z consumers. This study examines the influence of
influencermarketing onpurchasedecisionsamongGenerationZconsumersin Kannur.The study isbasedonprimarydata collectedfrom 70respondentsusinga
structured questionnaire. Percentage analysis, weighted average, Mann-Whitney U test, Kruskal-Wallis test, and Spearman’s rank correlation were used for
analysis. The findings show that respondents had a moderately positive opinion towards influencer marketing (WA= 2.312). However, the overall purchase
behaviour score was relatively low (WA=1.629). Purchase behaviour showed a significant difference across age groups and time spent on social media, and no
significant difference was found based on gender. A moderate positive relationship was found between opinion towards influencer marketing and purchase
behaviour(ρ=0.550,p<.001).Therefore,influencermarketingplays asignificantroleinshapingthepurchasebehaviourofGenerationZconsumers.
Keywords:InfluencerMarketing,GenerationZ,PurchaseBehaviour,ConsumerBehaviour,SocialMediaInfluencers.
Introduction
Social media is a major part of Generation Z’s daily life. They use platforms like Instagram, YouTube, and Snapchat to find information and explore
products. Influencers share product reviews and recommendations with their followers. It may affect consumers’ preferences for products and brands.
Therefore,thisstudyexaminestheinfluenceofinfluencermarketingonpurchasedecisionsamongGenerationZconsumersinKannur.
Literature review
Abidin (2016) found that influencers build strong connections with followers through personal and relatable content. These connections can increase
trustintheirrecommendations.
Lou and Yuan (2019) found that useful content and influencer credibility increase consumer trust. Trust can also improve brand awareness and
purchaseintention.
Sokolova and Kefi (2020) found that influencer credibility can affect purchase intention. The relationship between influencers and followers also
influencesconsumerdecisions.
Tanjung and Keni (2023) found that influencer credibility and attractiveness affect consumer attitudes. These factors can also influence purchase
intention.
Vrontis et al. (2021) found that credibility, authenticity, engagement, and content quality are important in influencer marketing. These factors help
influencersreachyoungerconsumers.
Leungetal.(2022)foundthatusefulandattractivecontentincreasesconsumerengagement.Goodcontentqualityhelpsinfluencersmaintainfollowers’
interest.
