Abstract : This study focused on digital marketing tools on the quality of service delivery in the
hospitality and tourism industry in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria. To achieve the purpose of the
study, two research questions were raised and translated into two null hypotheses to direct the study.
Survey research design was adopted as the most suitable design for this study. The population of this
study involved all key players in the hospitality, tourism and tourism related businesses in Uyo, Akwa
Ibom State; including hoteliers, resort operators, Eateries, Cinema centres and registered transporters.
A simple random sampling technique was used to select 3 each of hotels, resorts and transports
companies and also draw 536 respondents for this study. Data obtained for this study was possible
with the use of structured questionnaire entitled: Digital Marketing Tools and Quality Service Delivery
Questionnaire (DMTQSDQ). These instruments were designed by the researchers and validated by two
experts in Hospitality and Tourism Management, and Measurement and Evaluation. The reliability of
the instrument was ascertained using Cronbach alpha reliability test with 50 questionnaires. The
reliability result showed that the instrument is good enough to measure what it was originally designed
to measure. Data obtained from respondents in the field was tested using independent t -test analysis.
The result of hypotheses testing revealed that the use of social media and use of affiliate marketing tool
significantly influence the quality service delivery in the hospitality and tourism industry in Uyo, Akwa
Ibom State, Nigeria. Based on this result, it was recommended that Managers of Hospitality and
tourism industry in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State should as a matter of necessity maintain their strong social
media presence while exploring other social media platforms to engage their target audience. This can
involve creating compelling content, interacting with followers, and levera ging social media
advertising to reach a wider customer base. Similarly, affiliate marketing strategies should be explored
to increase customer patronage of the hospitality and tourism businesses.

METHODOLOGY
The study area is Uyo, Akwa
Ibom State, Nigeria. The design
adopted for the study is survey
research design. The population of the
study comprised of 798 registered
tourism and hospitality outfits in Uyo
with7, 056 workers (Ministry of Youth
and Sports, Akwa Ibom State, 2025). A
proportional simple random sampling
technique was adopted to select 5% of
the tourism and tourism related
industries while 30% respondents each
was selected from the tourism and
tourism related industries used for this
study. Based on the selection, a sample
of 536 respondents was selected for the
study. Questionnaire tagged: Digital
Marketing Tools and Quality Service
Delivery Questionnaire (DMTQSDQ).
The questionnaire was design ed with
20 items to measure the variables used
in the study. The questionnaire was
face validated by three Test,
Measurement and Evaluation experts
while the Cronbach Alpha reliability
technique using 30 workers that are not
part of the study sample was used. The
reliability coefficient of 0.75 to 0.84 was
obtained. This implies that the
instrument is reliable enough to obtain
was it was designed for. Independent t-
test analysis was used to test and
analyze the two hypotheses adopted
for this study.
