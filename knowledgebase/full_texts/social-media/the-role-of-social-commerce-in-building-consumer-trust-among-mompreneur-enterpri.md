 This study aims to examine the role of social commerce
utilization in building consumer trust and enhancing business
sustainability among mompreneur enterprises in Makassar City,
Indonesia. The rapid growth of digital entrepreneurship and
social commerce platforms has created significant opportunities
for women -led microenterprises; however, maintaining
consumer trust remains a critical challenge in online business
environments characteriz ed by uncertainty and intense
competition. This study employed a quantitative research
approach using Partial Least  Squares Structural Equation
Modeling (PLS-SEM) to analyze the relationships among Social
Commerce Utilization, Consumer Trust, and Business
Sustainability. Data were collected through an online
questionnaire distributed to 400 mompreneurs actively
operating businesses through social commerce platforms such as
TikTok Shop, Instagram, WhatsApp Business, Facebook
Marketplace, and Shopee Live. The findings revealed that Social
Commerce Utilization significantly and positively affected
Consumer Trust and Busine ss Sustainability. Furthermore,
Consumer Trust was found to significantly influence Business
Sustainability and partially mediate the relatio nship between
Social Commerce Utilization and Business Sustainability. The
measurement model assessment demonstrate d satisfactory
reliability and validity, while the structural model showed strong
predictive capability and excellent model fit. These findin gs
indicate that social commerce platforms play an important
strategic role in strengthening customer trust and sup porting
sustainable business performance among women -led online
microenterprises. This study contributes to the development of
social commerc e and digital entrepreneurship literature by
providing empirical evidence from the context of mompreneur
enterprises in emerging digital business ecosystems.

INTRODUCTION
For many mothers engaged in entrepreneurial activities, digital platforms have become
more than communication tools; they serve as essential business infrastructures that enable
income generation, customer engagement, and market expansion while allowing entrepreneurs
to balance business responsibilities with family obligations. The growing accessibility of social
media and online commerce platforms has encouraged the emergence of mompreneur
enterprises, a form of entreprene urship in which women simultaneously manage household
responsibilities and business activities. Within this context, social commerce platforms such as
TikTok Shop, Instagram, WhatsApp Business, Facebook Marketplace, and Shopee Live have
become increasingly  important because they facilitate product promotion, customer
interaction, and commercial transac tions through a single digital environment. As a result,
social commerce has become a strategic mechanism that supports entrepreneurial participation
among women and contributes to the expansion of digitally driven microenterprises (Cheng et
al., 2024; Triswanti et al., 2025).
The increasing involvement of women in entrepreneurship reflects broader changes in
contemporary business ecosystems, where digital tech nologies reduce barriers traditionally
associated with business ownership. Through social commerce platforms, entrepreneurs are
able to interact directly with consumers, share product information, obtain customer feedback,
and build online communities arou nd their businesses. Unlike conventional electronic
commerce, social commerce integrates commercial activities with social interaction, allowing
consumers to participate actively in content creation, information exchange, and product
evaluation. Previous studies suggest that these interactive mechanisms play an important role
in shaping consume r perceptions, purchasing behavior, and online engagement (Hajli, 2015;
Liang et al., 2011; Kim & Park, 2013). Furthermore, entrepreneurial participation among
women has been shown to increase when supported by favorable entrepreneurial ecosystems,
educational opportunities, and social encouragement that strengthen confidence in
entrepreneurial activities (Oktaviana & Utami, 2025).
Although social commerce offers subst antial opportunities for business development,
maintaining consumer trust remains a persis tent challenge. Online transactions frequently
involve uncertainty because buyers and sellers are separated by physical distance, product
evaluation is often limited to digital information, and concerns regarding privacy, transaction
security, and information credibility remain prevalent. Under such conditions, trust becomes a
critical factor that influences consumers’ willingness to engage in online transactions and
sustain long-term relationships with digital sellers. Existing literature consistently high lights
the importance of social interaction, relationship quality, social presence, platform
interactivity, and perceived credibility in strengthening trust within so cial commerce
environments (Farivar et al., 2017; Lu et al., 2015; Tuncer, 2021). Similarly, social support and
interactive communication mechanisms have been found to reduce uncertainty and strengthen
consumer confidence in digital marketplaces (Liang et al., 2011; Tajvidi et al., 2021).
The strategic importance of trust becomes even more evid ent among mompreneur
enterprises because many women-led microbusinesses depend heavily on customer retention,
repeat purchases, and positive electronic word -of-mouth. Unlike large organizations that
benefit from established reputations and institutional le gitimacy, smaller online businesses
often rely on personalized communication and relationship -based interactions to cultivate
customer loyalty. Consequently, social commerce platforms function not only as transactional
channels but also as spaces where tru st is continuously developed and reinforced. Previous
studies have demonstrated that trust -oriented social commerce environments contribute
positively to customer satisfaction, loyalty behavior, and online business performance (Lin et
al., 2019; Molinillo et al., 2021). Moreover, interactions facilitated through social media
platforms enable firms and consumers to engage in value co -creation processes that support
organizational learning and business value development (Zhang et al., 2019).
The effectiveness of social commerce in supporting business performance is influenced
by multiple technological and behavioral factors. Privacy protection, communication quality,

platform usability, relationship development, and interactive user experiences have all been
identified as important determinants of consumer engagement and trust formation (Wang &
Herrando, 2018). In addition, habitual platform usage, perceived fluency, and o mnichannel
integration quality strengthen consumers’ experiences and encourage continued p articipation
in digital commerce activities (Farivar et al., 2017; Shen et al., 2018). Website quality,

encompasses technological, social, and psychologica l dimensions that jointly influence
consumer trust and business outcomes.
Beyond supporting transactional activities, social commerce also contributes to long -
term business sustainability. In increasingly competitive digital markets, business
sustainability depends on an enterprise’s ability to adapt to technological change, maintain
customer relationships, and continuously create value. Sustainable business models emphasize
innovation, adaptability, and long -term stakeholder engagement as foundations of bu siness
continuity (Schaltegger et al., 2015). Within social commerce environments, trust functions as
a valuable strategic resource that strengthens customer engagement and supports sustainable
business performance. At the same time, digitalization has bee n recognized as an important
driver of competitiveness among micro and small enterprises because it expands market access,
improves operational efficiency, and enhances organizational responsiveness to market
changes (Smith et al., 2025).
Despite extensive scholarly attention devoted to social commerce and online consumer
behavior, important ga ps remain in the existing literature. Much of the current evidence
originates from studies involving large e-commerce platforms, general consumer populations,
or technologically advanced economies. Comparatively less attention has been directed toward
women-led microenterprises operating within localized digital business ecosystems in
emerging economies. In addition, previous studies have primarily concentrated on cons umer-
centered outcomes such as purchase intention, platform participation, and online engagement,
while relatively limited empirical evidence explains how trust contributes to sustainable
business performance among social -commerce-based microenterprises. Research specifically
examining the interrelationships among social commerce utilization, c onsumer trust, and
business sustainability within the context of mompreneur enterprises in Indonesia remains
limited. Addressing these gaps is important because women -led microbusinesses represent a
rapidly growing segment of the digital economy and face u nique challenges in maintaining
customer trust and long-term business viability.
Based on these considerations, this study investigates the role of social commerce
utilization in building consumer trust and enhancing business sustainability among
mompreneur enterprises in Makassar City, Indonesia. Specifically, the study examines the
effect of social commerce utilization on consumer trust, the effect of social commerce
utilization on business sustainability, and the mediating role of consumer trust in the
relationship between social commerce utilization and business sustainability. To evaluate these
relationships, the study employs Partial Least Squares Structural Equat ion Modeling (PLS -
SEM), a methodological approach widely applied in entrepreneurship and m anagement
research for analyzing complex relationships among latent constructs (Hair et al., 2021;
Sarstedt et al., 2019; Kline, 2023). The findings are expected to e nrich the literature on social
commerce and digital entrepreneurship while providing pract ical insights for entrepreneurs,
platform providers, and policymakers seeking to strengthen sustainable digital business
ecosystems.
LITERATURE REVIEW
Social Commerce Utilization in Mompreneur Enterprises
The increasing integration of social media and digital business activities has created
new opportunities for entrepreneurs to establish closer relationships with consumers. Within

this environment, social commerce has emerged as a business model that combines commercial
transactions with social int eraction, allowing users to communicate, exchange information,
provide feedback, and participate in purchasing decisions through digital platforms. Unlike
traditional online shopping environments that primarily focus on transactions, social commerce
facilitates continuous interaction between businesses and consumers, creating a more
collaborative and relationship-oriented marketplace (Hajli, 2015; Liang et al., 2011).
For wome n entrepreneurs, particularly mompreneurs, social commerce provides
strategic advantages because it enables business activities to be conducted with relatively low
operational costs while maintaining flexibility in managing household responsibilities.
Through platforms such as TikTok Shop, Instagram, WhatsApp Business, Facebook
Marketplace, and Shopee Live, entrepreneurs can promote products, communicate directly
with customers, and strengthen market visibility. Digital platforms have therefore become
important instruments supporting entrepreneurial participation and business development
among women -led enterprises (Cheng et al., 2024; Triswanti et al., 2025). Furthermore,
entrepreneurial engagement among women tends to increase when supported by favorable
entrepreneurial ecosystems and access to business opportunities, indicating the gro wing
relevance of digital entrepreneurship within contemporary economic environments (Oktaviana
& Utami, 2025).
Previous studies have demonstrated that platform interactivity, communication quality,
and user participation significantly influence the effect iveness of social commerce activities.
Consumers actively rely on recommendations, reviews, and social interaction when evaluating
products and making purchasing decisions (K im & Park, 2013; Tajvidi et al., 2021).
Consequently, social commerce utilization is expected to create conditions that facilitate
stronger consumer relationships and improve business performance.
Consumer Trust in Social Commerce Environments
Trust repres ents a fundamental element of digital transactions because consumers
frequently face uncertainty when purchasing products through online platforms. In the absence
of direct physical interaction, consumers must rely on available information, communication
quality, seller credibility, and platform reliability when evaluating purchasing de cisions. As a
result, trust becomes a critical mechanism that reduces perceived risk and encourages
consumers to engage in online commercial activities.
Existing literature c onsistently identifies trust as an outcome of positive interaction
experiences wit hin social commerce environments. Social presence, relationship quality,
platform interactivity, and information transparency contribute significantly to the
development of consumer confidence toward online sellers (Lu et al., 2015). Similarly, Farivar
et al. (2017) argued that trust plays a central role in mitigating transaction uncertainty, while
Wang and Herrando (2018) emphasized the importance of privacy assurance and inf ormation
security in strengthening consumer confidence. Through continuous interac tion and
relationship-building activities, social commerce platforms create opportunities for businesses
to cultivate stronger trust among consumers.
Because social commerce encourages direct communication, customer engagement,
and social interaction, higher levels of platform utilization are expected to contribute positively
to trust development among consumers.
H1: Social Commerce Utilization positively affects Consumer Trust.
Business Sustainability in Digital Entrepreneurship
Business sustainability ref ers to the ability of an enterprise to maintain operational
continuity, adapt to changing environments, and continuously generate value over time. Within
digital business ecosystems, sustainability extends beyond financial performance and includes
customer retention, adaptability, innovation capability, and long -term competitiveness
(Schaltegger et al., 2015).
For micro and small enterprises, digital technologies have become i ncreasingly
important in supporting sustainable growth. The adoption of digital pl atforms enables
businesses to improve communication efficiency, expand market reach, and strengthen

consumers, creating opportunities for stronger customer relationships and business continuity.
Previous studies have also shown that value co -creation, customer participation, and
digital engagement contribute positively to organizational learning and business value creation

Consumer Trust and Sustainable Business Performance
The sustainability of online businesses depends heavily on the ability to establish and
maintain customer trust. Trust encourages consumers to remain engaged with businesses,
increases t he likelihood of repeat purchases, and promotes positive recommendati ons that
support long -term growth. For mompreneur enterprises, where personalized interaction
frequently serves as a competitive advantage, trust becomes an essential strategic resource.
Prior studies have reported that trust contributes significantly to customer satisfaction,
loyalty behavior, and online purchasing activities. Lin et al. (2019) demonstrated that trust
strengthens customer relationships and improves business performance wi thin social
commerce environments. Likewise, Wang et al. (2022) concl uded that trust positively
influences consumers’ willingness to conduct transactions through social commerce platforms.
These findings suggest that businesses capable of developing stronger consumer trust are more
likely to achieve sustainable performance and long-term viability.
H3: Consumer Trust positively affects Business Sustainability.

Consumer Trust is positioned as an intervening mechanism through which Social Commerce

RESEARCH METHODS
This study employed a quantitative explanatory rese arch design to investigate the
relationships among Social Commerce Utilization, Consumer Trust, and Business
Sustainability in the context of mompreneur enterp rises. The study was designed to examine
how the utilization of social commerce platforms contrib utes to trust development and
sustainable business performance among women -led online businesses. A quantitative
approach was considered appropriate because th e study aimed to test theoretically derived
relationships and provide empirical evidence regarding the proposed research framework.
The research was conducted in Makassar City, Indonesia, where the adoption of digital
platforms among micro and small enterprises has grown substantially in recent years. The
target population consisted of mompreneurs ope rating online businesses through social
commerce platforms. In this study, mompreneurs were defined as women entrepreneurs with
children who actively man age and operate business activities using social -commerce-based
digital platforms. To ensure alignment with the research objectives, respondents were required
to meet the following criteria: (1) actively operate an online business, (2) utilize social
commerce platforms as a primary business channel, (3) have business experience of no more
than five years, a nd (4) be between 20 and 45 years old. These criteria were established to
capture digitally active entrepreneurs who regularly engage in online commercial activities.
Data collection was undertaken using a structured online questionnaire distributed
through Google Forms. The survey link was shared through entrepreneurship communities,
women entrepreneur networks, and social-media-based business groups in Makassar. Because
the study required respondents possessing specific characteristics relevant to the inv estigated
phenomenon, purposive sampling was employed. This sampling technique is frequently
applied in entrepreneurship and technology-adoption studies because it enables researchers to
identify participants with direct experience related to the research context. Following the
screening process, 400 valid responses were obtained and retained for analysis. The final
sample exceeded the minimum recommendations commonly suggested for Structural Equation
Modeling studies and was therefore considered sufficient for evaluating complex relationships
among latent constructs.
The research instrument consisted of two sections. The first section collected
demographic information, including age, business type, business duration, preferred social
commerce platform, and daily social media usage intensity. The second section measured the
latent variables examined in the study. Social Commerce Utilization (SCU) was
operationalized through indicators reflecting platform interaction, communication activities,
customer engagem ent, digital transaction practices, and promotional activities conducted
through social commerce platforms. Consumer Trust (CT) was represented through indicators
associated with credibility, reliability, confidence in sellers, information transparency, and trust
in online interactions. Business Sustainability (BS) was measured through indicators related to
business continuity, customer retention, adaptabi lity, sales stability, and long -term business
development.
All measurement items were assessed using a five-point Likert scale ranging from 1
(strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). Likert -type scales are widely utilized in
entrepreneurship, consumer be havior, and digital business research because they effectively
capture respondents’ perceptions, attitu des, and behavioral tendencies. Prior to large -scale
distribution, the questionnaire was reviewed to ensure clarity, contextual relevance, and
consistency of interpretation among respondents.
The measurement indicators were adapted from established studies  within the social
commerce and digital entrepreneurship literature. Indicators for Social Commerce Utilization
were adapted from Hajli (2015), Liang et al. (2011), Tajvidi et al. (2021), and Zhang et al.
(2015), emphasizing social interaction, participati on, engagement, and relationship -building

activities within social commerce environments. Consumer Trust indicators were adapted from
Kim and Park (2013), Lu et al. (2015), Farivar et al. (2017), and Wang and Herrando (2018),
focusing on credibility, trans action reliability, privacy assurance, and confidence in online
sellers. Business Sustainability indicators were adapted from Schaltegger et al. (2015), Lin et
al. (2019), Molinillo et al. (2021), and Smith et al. (2025), emphasizing business continuity,
customer retention, adaptability, competitiveness, and sustainable growth.
Data analysis was performed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling
(PLS-SEM) through SmartPLS 4 software. This analytical approach was selected because the
study sought to evaluate predictive relationships among multiple latent variables and examine
the mediating role of Consumer Trust within the proposed framework. PLS-SEM is particularly
suitable for prediction-oriented research and has been extensively applied in entrepreneurship,
marketing, and digital business studies involving complex relationships among latent
constructs (Hair et al., 2021; Sarstedt et al., 20 19). In addition, SEM -based approaches have
been widely recognized as effective tools for examining beh avioral and technology -related
phenomena characterized by multiple interrelated variables (Liébana-Cabanillas et al., 2017).
The analytical procedure consisted of several stages. Initially, descriptive statistical
analysis was conducted to summarize respon dent characteristics and construct distributions.
Correlation analysis was subsequently performed to examine preliminary relationships among
variables. The measurement model was then evaluated through indicator reliability, internal
consistency reliability, convergent validity, and discriminant validity assessments. Indicator
reliability was assessed using outer loading values, while Cronbach’ s Alpha and Composite
Reliability were employed to evaluate internal consistency. Convergent validity was assessed
using Average Variance Extracted (AVE), whereas discriminant validity was examined using
the Heterotrait–Monotrait Ratio (HTMT) criterion.
Following the assessment of the measurement model, the structural model was
evaluated using path coefficient analysis,  hypothesis testing, and coefficient of determination
(R²) assessment. The significance of the proposed hypotheses was determined through
bootstrapping procedures based on t-values and p-values. Furthermore, indirect effect analysis
was conducted to evalua te the mediating role of Consumer Trust in the relationship between
Social Commerce Utilization and Business Sustainability. Through these procedures, the study
provides empirical evidence regarding the contribution of social commerce utilization to trust
development and sustainable business performance among mompreneur enterprises operating
within digital business ecosystems.

CONCLUSION AND SUGGESTION

This study examined the role of social commerce utilization in building consumer trust
and enhancing business sustainability among mompreneur enterprises in Makassar City,
Indonesia. The findings demonstrate that social commerce utilization plays a signific ant role
in strengthening consumer trust and supporting sustainable business performance within digital
entrepreneurial environments. The results revealed that Social Commerce Utilization
significantly and positively affected Consumer Trust, indicating tha t interactive
communication, online engagement, and digital relationship -building mechanisms embedded
within social commerce platforms contribute substantially to the development of consumer
confidence among online customers. These findings confirm that so cial commerce platforms
function not only as transactional channels but also as strategic instrument s for strengthening
trust-oriented customer relationships in digital business environments.
The findings further demonstrate that Social Commerce Utilizatio n significantly and
positively affected Business Sustainability. This result indicates that the util ization of social
commerce platforms contributes directly to sustainable business performance through
improved market accessibility, customer interaction, a nd digital adaptability. In addition,
Consumer Trust was found to significantly influence Business S ustainability, suggesting that
customer confidence represents an important strategic factor supporting customer loyalty,
repeated transactions, and long-term business continuity among mompreneur enterprises. The
indirect effect analysis also confirmed that Consumer Trust partially mediated the relationship
between Social Commerce Utilization and Business Sustainability. These findings indicate that
social com merce platforms strengthen sustainable business performance both directly and
indirectly through trust-building mechanisms.
Theoretically, this study contributes to the development of social commerce and digital
entrepreneurship literature by providing empirical evidence regarding the relationship between
social commerce utilization, consumer trust, and business sustainability within the context of
women-led microenterprises. While previous studies predominantly focused on general online
consumers or large e-commerce platforms, this study specifically highlights the strategic role
of social commerce among  mompreneur enterprises operating in emerging digital business
ecosystems. Furthermore, the findings reinforce the importance of trust -oriented interaction
within social -commerce-based entrepreneurial environments and extend previous studies
concerning social support, relationship quality, and online consumer behavior.

Practically, the findings provide important implications for mompreneur entrepreneurs,
policymakers, and digital platform providers. Mompreneurs are encouraged to strengthen
interactive communication, maintain information transparency, improve responsiveness, and
optimize customer engagement strategies through social commerce platforms to enhanc e
consumer trust and long -term business sustainability. Digital platform providers should also
continue improving platform interactivity, transaction security, and communication features to
support stronger trust-building mechanisms among online business a ctors and consumers. In
addition, policymakers should provide training programs and digital entrepreneurship support
initiatives that strengthen women entrepreneurs’ technological capabilities and social
commerce competencies within digital business environments.
Despite its contributions, this study has several limitations. First, the study focused only
on mompreneur enterprises in Makassar City, which may limit the generalizability of the
findings to other geographical contexts. Second, this study employed a cross-sectional design,
which limits the ability to observe changes in consumer trust and busine ss sustainability over
time. Third, the study examined only three primary constructs, while other factors such as
customer satisfaction, digital literacy, i nnovation capability, and electronic word -of-mouth
may also influence sustainable business performance in social commerce environments.
Therefore, future studies are recommended to expand the research scope by involving
broader geographical coverage and more diverse entrepreneurial sectors. Future research may
also incorporate additional variables relate d to digital consumer behavior, technological
innovation, and online relationship quality to provide a more comprehensive understanding of
sustainable business development within social -commerce-based entrepreneurial ecosystems.
Longitudinal approaches are  also recommended to examine the long -term dynamics of
consumer trust and digital business sustainability among women -led enterprises operati ng in
rapidly evolving online business environments.
