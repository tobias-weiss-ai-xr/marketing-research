Abstract
Introduction: In recent years, social media marketing has emerged as an essential instrument in the
promotion of the food industry. In Andhra Pradesh, a state celebrated for its unique culinary heritage and
diverse flavours, social media platforms have become an influe ntial medium through which food
experiences are shared. Influencers, including local food bloggers and passionate foodies with extensive
travel experiences, play a vital role in shaping public perceptions of regional cuisines, producing striking
photographs, compelling video narratives and genuine reviews.

study employed a structured questionnaire distributed online. Participants were selected through
purposive sampling to ensure they were actively engaged with food content on YouTube. The
questionnaire covered demographic details, frequency of engagement, content preferences, and the

Null Hypothesis (H₀):  Influencer marketing has no significant impact on the consumer
behaviour of food enthusiasts in Andhra Pradesh.
Alternative Hypothesis  (H₁): Influencer marketing significantly influences consumer
behaviour and enhances engagement with the local food industry in Andhra Pradesh.
7. Research Methodology
A survey was conducted among 100 customers who follow YouTube food influencers.
The stud y employed a structured questionnaire distributed online. Participants were
selected through purposive sampling to ensure they were actively engaged with food
content on YouTube. The questionnaire covered demographic details, frequency of
engagement, conte nt preferences, and the influence on dining choices. Data were
analysed using descriptive statistics and cross -tabulation to determine the relationship
between influencer content and consumer behaviour.
