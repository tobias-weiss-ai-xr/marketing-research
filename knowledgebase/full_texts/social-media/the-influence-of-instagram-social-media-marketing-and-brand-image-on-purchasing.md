 Abstract: Digital marketing through Instagram is an
important strategy for automotive service businesses, while
brand image can influence consumer purchasing decisions.
This study aims to analyze the influence of Instagram social
media marketing and brand image partially or
simultaneously on purchasing decisions at the Pro Lc
Racing Team Workshop in South Tangerang. The study
used a quantitative meth od with an associative approach.
The study population was 732 consumers for the period
July–December 2025, with 88 respondents selected using
purposive sampling based on the Slovin formula. Data
were collected through a five -point Likert scale
questionnaire and analyzed using SPSS 26 through
validity, reliability, classical assumptions, regression,
correlation, coefficient of determination, t -test, and F-test
tests. The results showed that social media marketing did
not have a significant partial effect on purchasing
decisions (t = -0.305; p = 0.761), while brand image had a
positive and significant effect (t = 14.944; p = 0.000).
Simultaneously, both variables had a significant effect with
F = 256.854 and p = 0.000 and explained 85.8% of the
variation in pu rchasing decisions. Thus, brand image
became a more dominant factor in purchasing decisions.

Introduction
The development of digital technology has transformed marketing activities from conventional
patterns to digital marketing, which is more practical, cost -effective, and able to reach consumers
widely. Social media marketing has become a widely used strategy because it functions not only as a
means of communication but also as a medium for promotio n, interaction, and building relationships
with consumers. In Indonesia, social media is increasingly used, especially by the younger generation,
to obtain information, view promotions, and seek references before making a purchase. Furthermore,
brand image also plays a role in shaping consumer perceptions of a brand and influencing preferences
and purchasing decisions (Nurussofiah & Karimah, 2022:127; Anhar et al., 2025).

Instagram, as a visual -based social media platform, is widely used for marketing activ ities
because it can showcase products, services, and business activities through photos, videos, and
interactive features. This condition is also implemented by the Pro Lc Racing Team Workshop in South
Tangerang through the Instagram account @pro.lc to promote service, bore up, tune up, mapping, and
dyno services. Based on upload data from October 2025 to April 2026, there was an increase in
interaction, especially on video content. However, the number of consumers from July to December
2025 still experienced fluctuations, namely from 139 consumers in July to 97 consumers in December.
This condition indicates the need for an appropriate marketing strategy to maintain a stable number of
consumers.
The research problem is evident in the suboptimal and inconsi stent social media marketing
activities. A pre-survey of 30 respondents showed that most respondents gave positive assessments of
content, ease of communication, and engagement via Instagram. However, some respondents still did
not frequently view or receive Bengkel Pro Lc Racing Team content. This situation indicates that content
consistency and reach still need to be improved so that social media marketing can reach a wider
audience.
The issue also relates to brand image. Pre -survey results showed that 29 respondents rated the
workshop as easily recognizable and distinctive, 29 respondents rated its reputation as good, and 28
respondents rated the service as high quality and tailored to their needs. While the brand image is
considered positive, it still ne eds to be maintained through quality service, consistent communication,
engaging visuals, and active engagement with consumers via Instagram.
Regarding purchasing decisions, the pre -survey showed that 27 respondents used the service
because it suited their vehicle needs, 25 respondents stated that social media information helped them
make purchasing decisions, 27 respondents were willing to recommend the repair shop, and 28
respondents intended to use the service again. Although the results tended to b e positive, some
respondents still did not provide a positive assessment. Therefore, research is needed to determine the
extent to which Instagram social media marketing and brand image influence consumer purchasing
decisions (Kotler & Keller, 2022:184).
This study aims to determine the influence of Instagram social media marketing and brand
image on consumer purchasing decisions, both partially and simultaneously, at the Pro Lc Racing Team
Workshop in South Tangerang. This research is important to conduct because there are fluctuations in
the number of consumers amidst the use of Instagram as a marketing medium. The novelty of the study
lies in the simultaneous study of Instagram social media marketing and brand image on purchasing
decisions at an automotiv e service object, namely the Pro Lc Racing Team Workshop in South
Tangerang. The results of the study are expected to provide academic contributions in digital marketing
and become evaluation material for workshops in improving marketing strategies and str engthening
brand image.

Method
This study uses a quantitative method with an associative approach. The quantitative method is
used to test hypotheses through statistical data collection and analysis (Sugiyono, 2022:7), while the
associative approach is used to determine the influence of Instagram social media marketing (X1) and
brand image (X2) on purchasing decisions (Y) of consumers at the Pro Lc Racing Team Workshop in
South Tangerang (Sugiyono, 2019 in Tamrin, 2023). The study was conducted on consumers who had
used workshop services and knew or had seen the Instagram activities of the Pro Lc Racing Team
Workshop.
The research instrument used a questionnaire with a five-point Likert scale, ranging from 1 for
strongly disagree to 5 for strongly agree (Sugiyono, 2022:142). Social media marketing was measured
through content creation, content sharing, building connections, and community building (Gunelius in
Sarah et al., 2021:416). Brand image was measured through brand identity, brand personality, brand
associations, and attitudes and behaviors toward the brand (Wardhana, 2024:195). Purchasing decisions
were measured through product maturity, product purchasing habits, providing recommendations to
others, and repeat purchases (Winasis et al., 2022:399). The instrument was tested through validity and
reliability tests using predetermined criteria (Sugiyono, 2022:121; Sahir, 2021:31).
The study population consisted of all 732 consumers of the Pro Lc Racing Team Workshop
from July to December 2025. The sample was determined using non -probability sampling with a

purposive sampling technique, with the criteria being consumers who had used the workshop's services
and knew or had seen the Pro Lc Racing Team Workshop's Instagram. Based on the Slovin formula with
a 10% error rate, a sample of 88 respondents was obtained (Sugiyono, 2020:215; Sugiyono, 2022:217;
Sena Wahyu et al., 2020:10).
Data collection was conducted through library research and fieldwork. Field data were obtained
through direct observation and questionnaire distribution to consumers, as well as online using Google
Forms shared via Instagram and WhatsApp. The collected data were analyzed using SPSS version 26
through descriptive analysis, validity, reliability, normality, multicollinearity, heteroscedasticity, simple
linear regression, multiple linear regression, correlation, coefficient of determination, and t- and F-tests
(Sahir, 2021:51-53; Putri and Yuni, 2022:52).
The research procedure included proposal preparation, prop osal submission and seminars,
guidance and revision, data collection, data processing and analysis, thesis preparation, and manuscript
refinement. Questionnaire data was tabulated and tested for validity and reliability, then analyzed using
classical assumption tests, regression, correlation, coefficient of determination, and hypothesis testing.
The t-test was used to determine the partial influence of Instagram social media marketing and brand
image, while the F -test was used to determine the simultaneous influence of both on purchasing
decisions (Sahir, 2021:53).

Discussion
1. The Influence of Social Media Marketing Variables (X1) on Purchasing Decisions (Y)
Based on the results of simple linear analysis, the following regression equation was
obtained:
Y = 19.684 + 0.690 X1
The equation shows that the constant value is 19.684. This means that if the Social Media
Marketing variable is considered unchanged or has a constant value, then the Purchase Decision
value is 19.684. The regression coefficient of the Social Media Marketing variable is 0.690, which
is positive, meaning that every one unit increase in Social Media Market ing will increase the
Purchase Decision by 0.690 units.
Furthermore, the correlation coefficient test yielded a value of 0.696. This value indicates a
positive relationship between social media marketing and consumer purchasing decisions. This
means that the better the social media marketing activity, the higher the consumer purchasing
decisions.
Furthermore, the coefficient of determination test results obtained an R -square value of
0.484 or 48.4%. This indicates that 48.4% of the variation in purchasing decisions can be explained
by the Social Media Marketing variable, while the remaining 51.6% is influenced by other factors
outside the study.
However, based on the results of the hypothesis test (t-test) in multiple regression analysis,
the calculate d t -value was -0.305 with a significance value of 0.761. This significance value is
greater than 0.05 (0.761 > 0.05) so that H0 is accepted and H1 is rejected. Thus, it can be concluded
that Social Media Marketing does not have a significant effect on purc hasing decisions at the Pro
Lc Racing Team Workshop.
In line with Zaenati, Hidayati, and Kurnia (2022:75), who stated that social media marketing
has no partial effect on purchasing decisions, while brand image has a significant influence. This
study explains that consumers do not always use social media activity as the primary basis for
making purchasing decisions.
The results of this study indicate that although social media marketing has a strong positive
relationship with purchasing decisions, when anal yzed together with brand image variables, the
effect of social media marketing becomes insignificant. This indicates that consumer purchasing
decisions are more influenced by other factors, particularly brand image. In other words, marketing
activities through social media alone are not enough to encourage consumers to make purchases if
they are not supported by a strong brand image.

Conclusion
 Based on the research results, it can be concluded that Instagram Social Media Marketing
partially has no significant effect on Purchasing Decisions at the Pro Lc Racing Team Workshop in
South Tangerang, with a calculated t value of -0.305 and a significan ce of 0.761. On the other hand,
Brand Image has a positive and significant effect on Purchasing Decisions, with a calculated t value of
14.944 and a significance of 0.000. Simultaneously, Social Media Marketing and Brand Image have a
significant effect on Purchasing Decisions, indicated by a calculated F value of 256.854 with a
significance of 0.000. The R Square value of 0.858 indicates that the two variables are able to explain
85.8% of the variation in Purchasing Decisions, while the remaining 14.2% is e xplained by factors
outside the research model. This finding indicates that Brand Image is a more dominant factor in driving
Purchasing Decisions than marketing activities through Instagram. Practically, workshops need to
maintain and strengthen their brand image through service quality, professionalism, consistency of work
results, and positive consumer experiences. Instagram activities also need to be developed more
strategically so that they are not only oriented towards conveying information, but are ab le to build
consumer trust and engagement.
 This study has limitations in the number of respondents (88 people), the use of purposive
sampling technique, and the research object is limited to one workshop in South Tangerang, so the
generalization of the research results must be done carefully. In addition, the study only used Social
Media Marketing and Brand Image as independent variables, so there is still 14.2% of the variation in
Purchasing Decisions that is not explained. Future research is recommended to involve a larger number
of respondents, a wider scope of objects, and consider other variables such as service quality, price,
consumer trust, customer experience, electronic word of mouth, and promotion. Future research can also
use a longitudinal approach or compare several social media platforms to obtain a more comprehensive
picture of the effectiveness of digital marketing.
