ABSTRACT :
Online shopping has emerged as an important component of modern retailing, offering consumers convenience, a wider range of product choices, competitive
prices, and doorstep delivery. Meesho is one of the prominent social-commerce platforms in India, connecting consumers, resellers, and suppliers through an
online marketplace. The present study examines customer satisfaction with Meesho online shopping in Gobichettipalayam Town, Erode District. The study
focuses on four major aspects: factors influencing purchasing behaviour, awareness level, satisfaction level, and problems faced by customers.
Primary data were collected from 100 respondents through a structured questionnaire during August–October 2025 using convenience sampling. Percentage
analysis, the Chi-square test, and Garrett’s ranking technique were employed for data analysis. The study found that reasonable price was the most important
factor influencing customers’ purchasing behaviour, while 58% of respondents had a low level of awareness and 79% had a low level of satisfaction.
Significant relationships were observed between awareness and marital status, educational qualification, and annual income. Satisfaction was significantly
associated with educational qualification. The delivery of poor-quality products was identified as the major problem faced by customers.
The study suggests improvements in product quality, delivery services, customer awareness, return procedures, and product variety to enhance customer
satisfaction.
Keywords: Meesho, Online Shopping, Customer Satisfaction, Consumer Behaviour, Awareness, E-Commerce, Garrett’s Ranking.
1. Introduction
The rapid development of information technology and internet connectivity has transformed the traditional shopping process. Online shopping enables
consumers to search for products, compare prices, place orders, and make payments without physically visiting a retail outlet. The growth of
smartphones, digital payments, and internet access has further accelerated the adoption of e-commerce in India.
Meesho is an Indian social-commerce platform founded in 2015 by Vidit Aatrey and Sanjeev Barnwal. The platform initially focused on enabling local
businesses and resellers to reach customers through digital channels. Over time, Meesho developed into a large online marketplace offering products
across several categories. Its business model connects suppliers with consumers and resellers and provides customers with access to a wide variety of
products at competitive prices.
The major advantages associated with online shopping include convenience, time savings, price comparison, product availability, discounts, and the
ability to shop from home. However, customers may also experience problems such as delayed delivery, poor product quality, difficulty in returning
products, misleading advertisements, and concerns about online transactions.
Customer satisfaction is particularly important in the highly competitive e-commerce environment. Satisfied customers are more likely to repurchase
products, recommend the platform to others, and remain loyal to the service provider. Therefore, understanding customer expectations and the problems
experienced during online purchases is essential for improving the performance of an e-commerce platform.
Against this background, the present study examines customer satisfaction with Meesho online shopping among consumers in Gobichettipalayam Town,
Erode District.
2. Review of Literature
Previous studies have examined consumer behaviour and satisfaction in online shopping.
Banerjee, Dutta, and Dasgupta (2010) found that a high proportion of online shoppers were satisfied and that satisfaction was associated with future
online purchasing intentions. Jayasubramanian, Sivasakthi, and Ananthi Priya (2015) reported that time savings and convenience were major reasons

for using online shopping services.
Jayaprakash and Pavithra (2016), in their study of customers in Pollachi Taluk, found that younger consumers constituted a significant proportion of
online shoppers and that customers were generally satisfied with online shopping. Jadhav and Khanna (2016) highlighted internet penetration,
improved security, convenience, and the availability of several retailers as important factors influencing online buying behaviour.
Muruganantham et al. (2017) observed that offers, reasonable prices, product quality, and ease of shopping influenced customer satisfaction. Santhi
(2017) found that online shopping was preferred because it saved time and provided convenience, while discounts and attractive deals were important
expectations of consumers. Lakshmanan and Karthik (2018) examined online shopping behaviour in Tirupur District and identified age and other
demographic characteristics as relevant aspects of consumer behaviour.
Nivethitha and Vanaja (2020), in a study of women’s awareness of the Meesho application in Coimbatore, found that low risk was an important factor
influencing the use of Meesho. Santhiya and Rosline (2021) reported that variety, fast service, and low prices encouraged consumers to adopt online
shopping.
The review indicates that price, convenience, time savings, offers, product quality, and service quality are important determinants of online shopping
behaviour. However, there is a need for location-specific studies focusing particularly on Meesho customers. The present study attempts to address this
gap by examining consumers in Gobichettipalayam Town.
3. Objectives of the Study
The study has the following objectives:
1. To identify the factors influencing customers’ purchasing behaviour towards Meesho online shopping.
2. To examine the awareness level of customers about Meesho online shopping.
3. To analyse the satisfaction level of customers with Meesho online shopping.
4. To identify the problems faced by customers while using Meesho online shopping.
4. Research Methodology
The study was conducted in Gobichettipalayam Town, Erode District. The area was selected purposively because of the increasing use of online
shopping among consumers. The study is based mainly on primary data collected through a structured and pre-tested questionnaire.
A sample of 100 Meesho customers was selected using convenience sampling. The primary data were collected during the period from August 2025 to
October 2025. Secondary information was obtained from journals, articles, and websites.
A pilot study involving 10 respondents was conducted to pre-test and refine the questionnaire. The questionnaire included questions relating to
demographic characteristics, purchasing behaviour, awareness, satisfaction, and problems associated with Meesho online shopping.
Percentage analysis was used to describe the characteristics of the respondents. The Chi-square test was applied to examine the relationship between
socio-economic characteristics and awareness and satisfaction levels at the 5% level of significance. Garrett’s ranking technique was used to rank the
factors influencing purchasing behaviour and the problems faced by customers.
The study tested the following null hypotheses:
H01: There is no significant relationship between the socio-economic characteristics of respondents and their awareness level of Meesho online
shopping.
H02: There is no significant relationship between the socio-economic characteristics of respondents and their satisfaction level with Meesho online
shopping.
5. Results and Discussion
5.1 Factors Influencing Purchasing Behaviour
Ten factors were considered in measuring the purchasing behaviour of Meesho customers: reasonable price, time savings, availability of offers,
availability of discounts, availability of good-quality products, saving money, payment modes, convenience, replacement, and quick delivery.
Garrett’s ranking technique showed that reasonable price was the most important factor, with a mean score of 56.31. It was followed by time savings
(55.16), availability of more offers (53.90), saving money (51.16), modes of payment (48.93), availability of more discounts (48.54), convenience
(46.84), availability of good-quality products (46.02), replacement (44.71), and quick delivery (43.43).
The findings indicate that consumers are strongly attracted to Meesho because of price-related benefits. Competitive prices and attractive offers
encourage consumers to consider Meesho an economical shopping platform. Convenience and time savings are also important, demonstrating the value
consumers place on online purchasing.
5.2 Awareness Level of Customers
The awareness level was measured using a five-point Likert scale consisting of 12 statements. Respondents scoring below 36 were classified as having
low awareness, while those scoring 36 or above were classified as having high awareness.

The results showed that 58% of respondents had low awareness, while 42% had high awareness of Meesho online shopping. Thus, despite the
popularity of online shopping, a substantial proportion of respondents demonstrated limited awareness.
The Chi-square analysis showed that age, gender, occupational status, number of family members, nature of family, and number of earning members
did not have a statistically significant relationship with awareness. However, significant relationships were found between awareness and marital status,
educational qualification, and annual income.
The calculated Chi-square values for marital status (5.100), educational qualification (9.474), and annual income (16.167) exceeded their respective
critical values at the 5% level of significance. Therefore, the null hypothesis was rejected for these variables.
5.3 Satisfaction Level of Customers
Customer satisfaction was measured using 15 statements on a five-point Likert scale. Respondents with scores below 45 were classified as having low
satisfaction, while those scoring 45 or above were classified as having high satisfaction.
The findings revealed that 79% of the respondents had low satisfaction, while only 21% had high satisfaction. This is an important finding because it
suggests that, although consumers are attracted to Meesho primarily because of price and convenience, their overall experience may not fully meet their
expectations.
Chi-square analysis showed that age, gender, marital status, occupational status, number of family members, nature of family, number of earning
members, and annual income were not significantly associated with satisfaction. However, educational qualification showed a significant relationship
with satisfaction.
The calculated Chi-square value for educational qualification was 9.304, which was greater than the critical value of 5.991 at the 5% level of
significance. Hence, the null hypothesis was rejected for educational qualification and accepted for the remaining variables.
5.4 Problems Faced by Customers
Ten major problems were ranked using Garrett’s ranking technique: formalities in returning products, delivery of poor-quality products, delivery of
damaged products, high price, misleading advertisements, delayed delivery, lack of product guarantee, discounts, warranty period, and limited
availability of brands.
The analysis showed that the delivery of poor-quality products was the most important problem, with a mean score of 55.30. Misleading advertisements
ranked second with 53.62, followed by formalities in returning products (52.11), limited availability of brands (52.11), high price (48.60), delayed
delivery (48.50), lack of product guarantee (47.33), short warranty period (46.03), fewer discounts (45.98), and delivery of damaged products (45.19).
The findings demonstrate that product quality and the accuracy of product information are major concerns among Meesho customers. Problems relating
to returns and delivery can also negatively affect consumer confidence and repeat purchases.
6. Major Findings
The major findings of the study are as follows:
 Reasonable price was the most influential factor in customers’ purchasing behaviour, followed by time savings and the availability of more
offers.
 A majority of respondents (58%) had a low level of awareness about Meesho online shopping.
 A large majority of respondents (79%) reported a low level of satisfaction.
 Awareness was significantly related to marital status, educational qualification, and annual income.
 Awareness was not significantly related to age, gender, occupational status, number of family members, nature of family, or number of
earning members.
 Satisfaction was significantly related to educational qualification.
 Satisfaction was not significantly related to age, gender, marital status, occupational status, family size, nature of family, number of earning
members, or annual income.
 The delivery of poor-quality products was ranked as the major problem faced by customers.
 Misleading advertisements and difficulties in returning products were also important concerns.
7. Suggestions
Based on the findings, the following suggestions are offered:
1. Improve product quality: Meesho should strengthen supplier screening and quality-control mechanisms to reduce complaints about poor-
quality products.
2. Improve product descriptions: Accurate descriptions, photographs, specifications, and customer reviews should be provided to reduce the
gap between customer expectations and actual products.
3. Simplify the return process: Return and replacement procedures should be made easier and faster, with clear instructions and minimum
formalities.
4. Improve delivery services: Timely delivery and better logistics management can improve customer satisfaction.
5. Strengthen awareness campaigns: Meesho can increase awareness through digital advertising, social media, television, newspapers, and
other communication channels.
6. Control misleading advertisements: Promotional claims should accurately represent product features, prices, and discounts.
7. Expand product variety: The availability of reliable brands and quality products should be increased according to consumer demand.
8. Improve customer support: Quick and effective grievance-handling mechanisms should be provided to resolve complaints related to
products, payments, delivery, and returns.

10. Encourage customer feedback: Regular feedback and satisfaction surveys can help identify emerging consumer expectations and service

8. Conclusion
Online shopping has significantly changed consumer purchasing behaviour by providing convenience, a wider range of product choices, and
competitive prices. Meesho has attracted customers through affordable products, offers, convenient purchasing, and its extensive online marketplace.
However, the present study indicates that attracting customers through low prices alone is not sufficient to ensure long-term satisfaction.
The study found that reasonable price was the leading factor influencing purchasing behaviour, whereas the delivery of poor-quality products was the
major problem faced by consumers. The fact that 58% of respondents had low awareness and 79% had low satisfaction indicates the need for
substantial improvements in customer communication and service quality.
The findings further reveal that demographic characteristics do not uniformly influence awareness and satisfaction. Awareness was significantly
associated with marital status, educational qualification, and annual income, while satisfaction was significantly associated with educational
qualification. These results suggest that customer groups may have different expectations and levels of understanding of online shopping services.
Meesho can improve customer satisfaction by strengthening product quality control, ensuring accurate product information, simplifying return and
replacement procedures, improving delivery performance, and providing effective customer support. Continuous assessment of consumer needs is
essential in the highly competitive e-commerce environment.
Overall, Meesho has considerable potential for growth, particularly because consumers value affordable prices, convenience, and time savings.
However, sustainable growth depends on converting these initial attractions into positive and reliable shopping experiences. If the major problems
identified in this study are effectively addressed, customer confidence, satisfaction, and repeat purchasing behaviour can be improved.
