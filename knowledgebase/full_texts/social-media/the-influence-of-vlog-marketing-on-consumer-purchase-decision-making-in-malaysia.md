ABSTRACT:
This conceptual paper examines how vlog marketing may influence consumer purchase decision-
making in Malaysia through consumer engagement. Vlog marketing is conceptualised as a
multidimensional construct comprising informativeness, entertainment, credibili ty, authenticity,
expertise, and product demonstration. Consumer engagement is treated as a multidimensional
mediating state involving cognitive, emotional, and behavioural involvement. Drawing on Source
Credibility Theory, Parasocial Interaction Theory, C onsumer Engagement Theory, and the
Stimulus–Organism–Response (S-O-R) framework, the paper proposes that vlog characteristics
stimulate engagement, which in turn shapes consumer decision processes. The paper moves
beyond purchase intention alone and considers need recognition, information search, evaluation
of alternatives, purchase confidence, and product selection. Existing literature indicates that
informative and credible vlogs can support information processing and trust, while entertaining
and authentic content can sustain attention and relational involvement. Expertise and product
demonstrations may further reduce uncertainty by making product attributes more observable.
The proposed framework therefore positions consumer engagement as the bridge betw een vlog
characteristics and broader purchase decision -making. The Malaysian context is particularly
relevant because of its highly connected digital environment. Recent DataReportal evidence
indicates 98.0% internet penetration and 30.7 million social -media user identities in late 2025.
The framework contributes theoretically by integrating informational, experiential, relational, and
demonstrative vlog characteristics and by positioning engagement as a mediating mechanism.
Practical implications are offer ed for brands, vloggers, and marketing managers, while future
research directions focus on empirical testing, product -category differences, influencer types,
longitudinal effects, and cross-cultural comparisons.
Keywords: Vlog marketing; Consumer engagement; Consumer purchase decision -making;
Influencer marketing; Social media marketing; Malaysia.
1. INTRODUCTION
Social media has transformed marketing communication from predominantly one -way, firm -
controlled communication into an interactive environment in which brands, creators, influencers,

and consumers continuously exchange information. Consumers can access, compare, evaluate, and
circulate product information from multiple sources before making marketplace decisions, while
firms can communicate with specialised and networked audiences (Kap lan & Haenlein, 2010;
Kietzmann et al., 2011; Mangold & Faulds, 2009), a dynamic seen increasingly across Malaysian
interactive digital and service environments (MOUSUMI & Sohail, 2026).  Influencer marketing
has consequently become an important form of digital persuasion. Influencers cultivate identifiable
audiences and can shape perceptions, attitudes, and behaviours through repeated and socially
embedded communication (Freberg et al., 20 11; De Veirman et al., 2017; Lou & Yuan, 2019).
Because commercial messages are embedded in creator –audience relationships, influencer
communication may be perceived as more personal, relatable, and authentic than conventional
advertising (Djafarova & Rush worth, 2017; Sokolova & Kefi, 2020).  Vlogging is a particularly
information-rich form of creator-generated content because video can combine personal narratives,
reviews, demonstrations, comparisons, tutorials, and recommendations. This format allows
consumers to encounter product information  in contextualised situations rather than through
isolated promotional claims. Demonstrations and first -hand experiences can also reduce
uncertainty by showing how products function in practical contexts. Prior studies have linked
message value, influencer  credibility, parasocial interaction, and influencer characteristics with
consumer responses (De Veirman et al., 2017; Lou & Yuan, 2019; Sokolova & Kefi, 2020).  The
Malaysian context provides an important setting for extending this literature. Existing Malaysian
research has examined YouTube influencer characteristics, credibility, information adoption, and
purchase intention, but the broader mechanism through whi ch multidimensional vlog
characteristics may translate into consumer purchase decision -making remains less developed.
Accordingly, this paper examines the conceptual relationship among vlog marketing, consumer
engagement, and consumer purchase decision -making. Rather than equating consumer response
with purchase intention, the paper adopts a process -oriented view encompassing information
search, alternative evaluation, confidence formation, and product selection.
2. Vlog Marketing and Its Dimensions
Vlog marketing refers to the strategic use of video blogs and vloggers to communicate information
about products, brands, services, experiences, or destinations to digitally connected audiences ,
driven by evolving digital content production capabilities (Salhab, Sohail, & Ali, 2025, April). In
this paper, vlog marketing comprises six dimensions: informativeness, entertainment, credibility,
authenticity, expertise, and product demonstration. These dimensions capture informational,
experiential, relational, a nd presentational qualities rather than treating vlog effectiveness as a
single attribute.  Informativeness is important when consumers face uncertainty about product
quality, price, benefits, risks, or suitability. Vlogs can combine explanation, visual demonstration,
comparison, and personal experience. Lou and Yuan (2019) found that informative  influencer
content is associated with trust in branded posts and purchase intention, while Cheung et al. (2009)
demonstrated the importance of information quality and credibility for information adoption.
Informative vlogs may therefore encourage viewers to scrutinise product attributes, compare
alternatives, and seek additional information.  Entertainment adds an experiential dimension.
Consumers use digital platforms for enjoyment, relaxation, novelty, social interaction, and
emotional stimulation (Whiting & Williams, 2013). Storytelling, humour, lifestyle presentation,
and visually engaging formats can hold attention and make commercial communication less
intrusive. Enjoyable content may increase continued viewing, reactions, comments, sharing,
following, and related information seeking (De Veirman et al., 2017; Casaló et al., 2020).

Credibility concerns whether viewers perceive a vlogger as knowledgeable, honest, reliable, and
sufficiently independent. Source Credibility Theory identifies expertise and trustworthiness as
central determinants of persuasive effectiveness (Hovland & Weis s, 1951; Ohanian, 1990). In
social media, credibility may develop through demonstrated competence, consistent
communication, relevant experience, transparency, and perceived honesty. Malaysian evidence
also indicates that YouTube influencer credibility and  information adoption are relevant to
cosmetic-product purchase responses (Mohamed & Gadiman, 2024; Mohamed & Jaafar, 2026).
Authenticity concerns the perceived genuineness and sincerity of creator communication. Personal
experiences, routines, preferences, and opinions can make vloggers appear relatable, but
commercialisation can threaten this perception. Audrezet et al. (2020)  highlight the tension
between commercial objectives and maintaining authentic self -presentation. Transparent,
experience-based communication may strengthen trust, whereas excessive or incongruent
promotion may create scepticism.  Expertise refers to perceived knowledge and competence
regarding a product or category. It is especially relevant for technically complex, unfamiliar,
costly, or high -risk products. A knowledgeable vlogger can explain characteristics, compare
alternatives, demonstrate functionality, and contextualise performance. Product demonstration
complements expertise by allowing viewers to observe functionality rather than relying only on
verbal claims. These characteristics can reduce information asymmetry and suppor t comparative
evaluation. The six dimensions are conceptually related but not interchangeable. Informativeness
concerns the usefulness and relevance of information; entertainment concerns enjoyment and
attention; credibility concerns acceptance of the communicator and message; authenticity concerns
perceived genuineness; expertise concerns competence; and product demonstration concerns
observable evidence of functionality. Treating these dimensions separately allows future empirical
research to determine which aspects are most influ ential for Malaysian consumers and whether
some dimensions reinforce one another.  The multidimensional approach also reflects the hybrid
nature of vlogs. A single video may educate viewers through technical explanation, entertain
through storytelling, establish trust through personal experience, and demonstrate a product in use.
Consequently, the effectiveness of vlog marketing may depend on the combined quality of content
rather than on one isolated attribute.
3. Consumer Engagement
Consumer engagement is the principal mediating construct in the proposed framework. It is
conceptualised as cognitive, emotional, and behavioural involvement with vlog content rather than
as a simple count of likes or views. Brodie et al. (2011) describe e ngagement as involving
interactive experiences and value creation, while Vivek et al. (2012) emphasise participation and
relational connection. Hollebeek et al. (2014) distinguish cognitive processing, affection, and
activation, and Dessart et al. (2015) d emonstrate the multidimensional nature of engagement in
online brand communities.  Cognitive engagement refers to sustained attention, elaboration, and
information processing. Viewers may compare claims with prior knowledge, evaluate product
attributes, search for corroborating information, and consider whether a product meets their
requirements. Informative, relevant, and credible vlogs can therefore encourage active evaluation
rather than passive exposure (Cheung et al., 2009).  Emotional engagement concerns affective
responses and perceived connection with a creator, product, or brand. Storytelling, humour,
personal disclosure, and relatability can create emotional resonance and interpersonal proximity.
Parasocial Interaction Theory explains how audiences may develop perceived relationships with
media personalities (Horton & Wohl, 1956). Social media can strengthen these perceptions
because creators communicate directly, respond to comments, and share recurring aspects of their

lives. Credibility and parasocial interaction have been associated with purchase-related responses
in influencer contexts (Sokolova & Kefi, 2020).  Behavioural engagement includes liking,
commenting, sharing, following, saving content, clicking product links, visiting brand pages,
discussing products, and searching for additional information. Such actions indicate active
participation and may expose c onsumers to further reviews, comments, recommendations, and
social information. Engagement should therefore be treated as a mechanism that extends the
information environment surrounding the original vlog. This distinction is important because high
visible activity does not necessarily mean high psychological engagement. A video can receive
many views while viewers devote little attention to its claims. Conversely, a smaller audience may
engage deeply by com paring information, asking questions, discussing the product, and seeking
independent evidence. The proposed model therefore treats engagement as a psychological and
behavioural process that can connect communication characteristics with decision outcomes. The
three engagement dimensions can also operate sequentially or simultaneously. Cognitive attention
may make information more accessible for evaluation, emotional involvement may increase the
personal relevance of the message, and behavioural participatio n may provide additional social
information. This integrated perspective is consistent with engagement research and is particularly
appropriate for interactive video environments.
4. Consumer Purchase Decision-Making
Consumer purchase decision -making is broader than purchase intention because it includes the
sequence of activities through which consumers recognise a need, search for information, evaluate
alternatives, develop confidence, and select or purchase a produc t (Engel et al., 1995; Kotler &
Keller, 2016). Digital environments have expanded the resources available at each stage through
creator reviews, demonstrations, comments, comparison videos, and electronic word -of-mouth.
Vlogs can influence several stages of this process. They may create awareness, provide
information, demonstrate functionality, shape perceptions, reduce uncertainty, and influence
consideration sets. A broader decision -making construct is therefore appropr iate because an
intention statement does not fully capture preference formation, comparative evaluation,
confidence development, or eventual selection. The framework consequently examines purchase
decision-making as a process rather than as a single behavioural intention.
5. Theoretical Foundation and Conceptual Framework
Source Credibility Theory provides the basis for explaining why consumers respond differently to
similar messages depending on the communicator. Hovland and Weiss (1951) established the
importance of source characteristics in persuasion, while Ohanian (199 0) operationalised
credibility through expertise, trustworthiness, and attractiveness. In vlog marketing, expertise
signals relevant knowledge and trustworthiness affect whether recommendations are perceived as
sincere and reliable. This supports the inclu sion of credibility and expertise within the vlog -
marketing construct.  Parasocial Interaction Theory, introduced by Horton and Wohl (1956),
explains perceived interpersonal relationships between audiences and media personalities. Social
media creates additional opportunities for familiarity because creators communicate directly with
followers and disclose aspects of everyday life. Such perceived closeness may increase the
persuasive relevance of recommendations (Labrecque, 2014). The Stimulus–Organism–Response
(S-O-R) framework provides the overarching causal logic.  Vlog marketing functions as the
external stimulus, consumer engagement represents the internal cognitive and affective organismic
state, and consumer purchase decision-making represents the behavioural response. The proposed
pathway is therefore Vlog Marketing → Consumer Engagement → Consumer Purchase Decision-

Making. Vlog content supplies informational, experiential, and relational stimuli; consumers
process these through cognitive, emotional, and behavioural engagement; and these processes may
subsequently shape purchase-related decisions (Rezaldi et al., 2026), echoing broader innovation
and technology adoption processes in consumer markets (Sohail, Haque, Aziz, & Maruf, 2023).
The conceptual framework integrates six vlog -marketing dimensions —informativeness,
entertainment, credibility, authenticity, expertise, and product demonstration —with three
engagement dimensions —cognitive, emotional, and behavioural —and a broader purchase
decision-making process comprising need recognition, information search, alternative evaluation,
purchase confidence, and product selection. It allows both direct and indirect pathways to be
examined. The theories are complementary rather than competing. Source Credibility Theory
explains why the perceived qualities of the communicator affect message acceptance. Parasocial
Interaction Theory explains how repeated creator –audience interaction can create relational
closeness. Consumer Engagement Theory explains the multidimensional involvement that follows
interaction with content. S -O-R then integrates these elements into a broader process in which
environmental stimuli are interpreted internally before p roducing behavioural responses.  This
theoretical integration also clarifies why a direct exposure-to-purchase model may be incomplete.
Consumers do not simply receive a message and immediately buy a product. They interpret the
information, assess the source, form affective reactions, interact with others, compare alternatives,
and develop confidence. Consumer engagement captures these intermediate processes and
therefore provides a theoretically meaningful mechanism for explaining how vlog marketing can
affect decision-making.
6. Hypotheses Development
Vlog marketing is expected to influence engagement because video can communicate information,
provide entertainment, facilitate interpersonal connection, and demonstrate products. Useful and
credible content can increase cognitive attention, while entertai ning and emotionally resonant
content can encourage sustained involvement. Authentic and knowledgeable creators may
strengthen relational involvement. Accordingly:
H1: Vlog marketing positively influences consumer engagement.
Engaged consumers are more likely to attend to information, evaluate alternatives, experience
affective responses, and interact with additional sources. Cognitive engagement can strengthen
elaboration and comparison; emotional engagement can influence atta chment and favourable
evaluation; and behavioural engagement can expose consumers to further reviews and
recommendations. Accordingly:
H2: Consumer engagement positively influences consumer purchase decision-making.
Vlog marketing may also exert a direct effect on decision-making when reviews, demonstrations,
or recommendations provide sufficiently diagnostic information to reduce uncertainty. Credibility,
expertise, authenticity, and product demonstration may increase the usefulness of information for
evaluation (Djafarova & Rushworth, 2017; De Veirman et al., 2017; Lou & Yuan, 2019).
H3: Vlog marketing positively influences consumer purchase decision-making.
The central proposition is that engagement mediates the relationship between vlog marketing and
purchase decision -making. Effective content may first attract attention and interest, stimulate

7. Malaysian Context and Updated Digital Evidence
Malaysia is a relevant context because consumers increasingly encounter social -media content as
a source of information, entertainment, communication, and shopping guidance. Malaysian studies
have examined YouTube influencer credibility, expertise, attract iveness, trustworthiness,
information adoption, and purchase intention, supporting the relevance of influencer -mediated
information processing in this market (Mohamed & Gadiman, 2024; Mohamed & Jaafar, 2026).
Cultural and market characteristics may also sh ape perceptions of authenticity, credibility,
expertise, and interpersonal relationships, making context-sensitive research important (Sardar &
Vijay, 2025). The latest consolidated national indicators reported in DataReportal's Digital 2026:
Malaysia provide additional support for the digital context. Most underlying observations refer to
October or late 2025. Malaysia had an estimated 35.4 million internet us ers, representing 98.0%
of the population, and 30.7 million social -media user identities, equivalent to 85.0% of the
population. These figures indicate a highly connected environment in which creator -generated
video can operate as a substantial source of c onsumer information and interaction (DataReportal,
2026). Platform-level evidence further illustrates the scale of the environment in which vlog
communication operates. DataReportal reported 23.6 million YouTube users in Malaysia in late
2025, alongside 23.0 million Facebook and 16.1 million Instagram advertising -reach estimates,
30.7 million TikTok users aged 18 and above, and 10.0 million LinkedIn members. These
measures use different definitions and should not be treated as directly equivalent active -user
statistics, but collectively they demonstrate the breadth of social and video platforms available to
Malaysian consumers (DataReportal, 2026).  The digital evidence strengthens the rationale for
examining vlog marketing while also highlighting an important methodological issue. Reach and
audience-size indicators demonstrate potential exposure, but they do not establish that viewers
process, trust,  or act on content. This distinction supports the present model's emphasis on
multidimensional engagement and purchase decision-making rather than exposure metrics alone.
8. Theoretical Contributions
The paper makes four principal theoretical contributions. First, it integrates six vlog -marketing
characteristics into one multidimensional construct, combining informational, experiential,
relational, and demonstrative qualities. Second, it positions cons umer engagement as the central
explanatory mechanism, extending multidimensional engagement theory to vlog-based marketing
communication. Third, it extends Source Credibility Theory into a contemporary video-influencer
setting by considering credibility an d expertise alongside informativeness, entertainment,
authenticity, and product demonstration. Fourth, the S -O-R framework provides an integrative
explanation in which vlog marketing is the stimulus, consumer engagement is the organismic
process, and purchase decision-making is the response.
9. Practical Implications
Brands should not evaluate vlog campaigns only through views or follower counts because

exposure does not necessarily indicate meaningful involvement. Campaigns should prioritise
useful information, credible creators, authenticity, effective demonstrations, entertainment, and
opportunities for interaction. Measures of engagement and purchase -related outcomes should be
considered alongside reach.  Vloggers should balance commercial objectives with authenticity.
Excessive promotion of unrelated products may weaken trust and relational capital. Creators
should select products consistent with their expertise and audience expectations, disclose
commercial relationships appropriately, and provide transparent, useful, experience -based
information (Audrezet et al., 2020).  Marketing managers should treat influencer selection as a
strategic decision rather than simply maximising follower counts. Creator credibility, expertise,
authenticity, audience relevance, engagement quality, content quality, and product –creator fit
should be evaluated. A smaller but highly relevant and credible creator may offer greater strategic
value than a high -reach creator whose audience is poorly aligned with the product category (De
Veirman et al., 2017; Casaló et al., 2020).
10. Discussion and Research Gap
The literature indicates that vlog marketing is a complex form of digital persuasion whose
effectiveness arises from the interaction of informational, relational, experiential, and
presentational characteristics. Information helps consumers reduce uncertai nty and develop
product knowledge; credibility affects whether information is accepted; authenticity contributes to
sincerity and relatability; and engagement transforms exposure into cognitive, emotional, and
behavioural involvement. These processes can s upport need recognition, information search,
comparison, confidence formation, and product selection.  Several gaps justify the proposed
framework. First, much prior research focuses on purchase intention rather than the broader
decision-making process. Second, studies often isolate individual influencer attributes such as
credibility, attractiveness, or ex pertise instead of integrating them with content dimensions such
as informativeness, entertainment, authenticity, and product demonstration. Third, consumer
engagement is frequently treated as an outcome rather than a mechanism linking marketing
communication with consumer decisions. Fourth, contextual and cross -cultural variation remains
insufficiently addressed. Finally, although Malaysian studies have examined YouTube influencer
credibility and purchase intention, greater attention is needed to the pathway from vlog marketing
through consumer engagement to broader purchase decision -making. The proposed framework
therefore addresses a conceptual problem in the existing literature: the tendency to separate content
characteristics, influencer characteristics, engagement, and purchase outcomes into disconnected
relationships. Bringing these elements together allows the model to explain not only whether vlog
marketing matters but also how it may matter. The framework predicts that the quality of vlog
stimuli affects the depth of engagement and that engagement subsequently contributes to consumer
decision processes. At the same time, the framework remains conceptual. The reviewed evidence
supports the plausibility of the proposed relationships, but it does not by itself establish causal
effects among Malaysian consumers. Empirical research should therefore test the hypotheses using
validated measurement scales, appropriate sampling procedures, and multivariate analysis. This
limitation is important because platform use, product category, influencer type, and consumer
characteristics may alter the strength of the relationships.
11. Future Research Directions
Future studies should empirically test the proposed relationships using validated measures and
structural equation modeling techniques suited for complex mediating structures (Lip, Abd Wahab,
Yusoff, Sohail, Muthaya, Abdulsamad, & Al-refaei, 2025). Researchers should examine whether

the relative importance of vlog dimensions differs across beauty, fashion, tourism, food,
technology, education, healthcare, and financial services, where consumer involvement and
perceived risk vary. Comparisons among nano -, micro-, macro-, and celebrity influencers could
establish whether audience size, perceived intimacy, expertise, and engagement quality produce
different effects. Longitudinal studies could assess whether repeated exposure generates sustained
changes in brand preference and purchasing b ehaviour. Cross-cultural research could compare
Malaysia with Indonesia, Pakistan, Singapore, Thailand, India, and other markets to determine
whether cultural characteristics alter perceptions of authenticity, credibility, and parasocial
relationships. Future models could also include trust, parasocial interaction, perceived value, brand
attitude, product involvement, influencer –brand fit, consumer scepticism, and advertising
disclosure as additional explanatory variables (Rezaldi et al., 2026; Sardar & Vijay,
2025).Researchers should also examine possible boundary conditions. Product involvement and
perceived risk may strengthen the value of expertise and demonstration for some categories,
whereas entertainment and authenticity may be more influential for lifestyle products. Influencer–
brand fit and advertising disclosure may affect whether commercial messages are accepted as
credible. Consumer scepticism, prior brand knowledge, and trust could similarly determine
whether engagement translates into decision confidence . Testing these factors would provide a
more nuanced account of vlog marketing effectiveness.
12. Conclusion
Vlog marketing has become an important component of contemporary digital marketing because
it combines information, entertainment, personal experience, product demonstration, and
interpersonal communication. The literature reviewed in this paper suggests that informativeness,
entertainment, credibility, authenticity, expertise, and product demonstration can shape consumer
responses, but their influence should not be understood only as a direct movement from exposure
to purchase. The proposed framework positions consumer engagement as the bridge between vlog
characteristics and broader purchase decision -making. Cognitive engagement can intensify
information processing, emotional engagement can strengthen psychological and relationa l
involvement, and behavioural engagement can encourage interaction and additional information
seeking. These processes may influence need recognition, information search, alternative
evaluation, purchase confidence, and product selection. The resulting pathway is Vlog Marketing
→ Consumer Engagement → Consumer Purchase Decision -Making. By integrating Source
Credibility Theory, Parasocial Interaction Theory, Consumer Engagement Theory, and the S-O-R
framework, the paper provides a coherent conceptual explanation of how creator-generated video
content may influence consumers in Malaysia. F or brands, vloggers, and marketing managers,
effective vlog marketing should prioritise diagnostically useful information, credibility,
authenticity, meaningful engagement, and support for consumers throughout the decision process.
Empirical research is re quired to test the proposed relationships across product categories,
influencer types, and cultural contexts.
