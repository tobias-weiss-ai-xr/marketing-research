Petfluencers
represent a distinctive and rapidly growing segment within
influencer marketing, as animals gain large online audiences through carefully
constructed digital personas managed by their owners. These dogs, cats, and other
animals frequently collaborate with brands and appear in sponsored content
across various social media platforms, particularly Facebook, Instagram, and
TikTok. Their growing popularity raises important questions regarding the
effectiveness of animal influencers compared to traditional human influencers in
shaping audience engagement and supporting marketing communication goals.
This study aims to examine how petfluencers differ from traditional human
influencers in terms of content characteristics, brand presence, and audience
engagement. The research employs manual content analysis to investigate
Instagram posts published in 2023 by Tika the Iggy, a well-known Italian
Greyhound petfluencer, and Marta Sierra, a Spanish human fashion influencer
recognized for lifestyle and fashion-related content. The analysis focuses on
several dimensions, including posting frequency, dominant content formats,
brand appearances, and engagement indicators such as likes and interactions.The
findings reveal that video content emerged as the dominant format for both
influencers, reflecting broader trends in social media communication and
audience preferences. Nevertheless, notable differences were identified between
the two cases. Marta Sierra, the human influencer, published a higher volume of
sponsored and brand-related posts, yet these often generated relatively lower
engagement rates. In contrast, Tika the Iggy, the dog influencer, posted less
frequently but achieved substantially higher audience interaction and stronger 1

engagement
overall. These results suggest that petfluencers occupy a unique
position within influencer marketing and may serve as highly effective and
emotionally engaging actors in digital campaigns.

IntroductionNowadays,
content creation is accessible to virtually anyone, while the online
environment, particularly social media platforms, provides a space where users can
interact with each other in real time. As a result, ordinary individuals can gain
visibility on the internet and exert an increasing influence on their followers [ 1].
Animal-related content has always been popular online, as illustrated by the fact
that the very first Instagram post featured a photograph of a dog. According to a 2016
American survey, a significant proportion of pet owners post about their pets more
frequently than about themselves, and many even create separate profiles
specifically for their animals [ 2].
Within this digital environment, animal influencers, commonly referred to as
petfluencers, have emerged as distinctive actors in digital marketing. Their growing
popularity raises important questions regarding their role, effectiveness, and
strategic value within influencer marketing compared to traditional human
influencers. While existing research increasingly highlights the emotional appeal and
engagement potential of animal-based content, fewer studies directly compare
petfluencers and human influencers using empirical social media data.
To address this gap, the present study examines petfluencers in comparison with
human influencers from an influencer marketing perspective. Two thematically
similar influencers with nearly identical follower bases were selected: Tika the Iggy,
a canine influencer, and Marta Sierra, a human fashion influencer. Their Instagram
posts published in 2023 were analyzed to explore differences in content strategies,
brand collaborations, and audience engagement.2.
Literature reviewOne
of the most dynamically developing tools of digital marketing today is
influencer marketing, in which opinion leaders are becoming increasingly important
actors in shaping the relationship between consumers and brands. On social media
platforms, influencers play a prominent intermediary role, as their
recommendations are perceived as more direct and credible than traditional
advertisements [3]. In his article on influencer marketing, Török highlights that
while consumers are becoming increasingly skeptical toward conventional
advertising, the strength of influencer marketing lies precisely in the ability of
influencers to build a bridge of trust between brands and audiences through their
personalities. He also emphasizes that a well-designed influencer campaign can
strengthen brand loyalty in the long term [ 4].
Influencer marketing focuses on building lasting and authentic relationships,
which makes the selection of the influencer a crucial strategic decision [ 5].
Cooperation is most effective when the chosen influencer aligns with the brand’s Marketing

values
and communication strategy [3]. The term “influencer” derives from the
word “influence,” referring to the ability to affect others. According to the definition
of the International Advertising Bureau, an influencer is an individual who is able to
directly impact their followers through their own digital platforms and indirectly
reach a broader audience as well [ 6].
Alongside the expansion of influencer marketing, several specialized subtypes
have emerged, such as artificial intelligence (AI)-generated influencers (AI
influencers), child influencers (kidfluencers), and pet influencers (petfluencers).
The latter refers to animals, particularly dogs and cats, that appear on social media
with deliberately constructed online personalities and gain substantial follower
bases. While the content is produced by humans, typically the owners,
communication takes place in the name of the animal, and the digital identity is built
around the pet itself.
Companies and marketing agencies increasingly view petfluencers as potential
partners. Their application is especially promising because they combine the
persuasive power of influencer marketing with the emotional appeal of animals,
which can evoke positive feelings and symbolic meanings among consumers.
Scientific research confirms that petfluencers often outperform human
influencers, particularly in terms of emotional engagement, perceived
authenticity, and attention generation. An experimental study demonstrated that
posts featuring animals generated stronger emotional attachment and more
favorable attitudes. Anthropomorphism plays a key role in these collaborations,
alongside the psychological mechanism through which animals’ perceived cuteness
and innocence create a climate of trust. The narrator is crucial, and messages
conveyed by pets are frequently perceived as less manipulative than those
delivered by humans [ 7].
Recent studies further indicate that dog influencers are particularly effective.
Through an Instagram content analysis, it was found that dog profiles generate
stronger emotional bonds and higher engagement rates compared to other types of
petfluencers [8]. These findings are supported by research arguing that dogs possess
exceptional attention-attracting power as visual stimuli, which enhances advertising
effectiveness [9]. It has also been demonstrated that advertisements conveyed by
dog influencers have a particularly positive impact on purchase intention, especially
when brands build on lifestyle and identity-related elements connected to
animals [10].
One of the most recent empirical contributions in this field was published in the
Journal of Advertising Research [ 11]. A comprehensive analysis of the underlying
mechanisms of petfluencer campaigns revealed that followers’ emotional responses
and attachments play a decisive role in shaping brand attitudes. Another relevant
study showed that animals, particularly dogs, not only increase attention but also
strengthen the perception of positive brand-related emotions [ 12]. The ease with
which dogs can be anthropomorphized due to their perceived friendliness and
closeness to humans significantly enhances advertising effectiveness. Similar
conclusions were reached in further research, which found that consumers spend
more time viewing dog influencer content and are more likely to engage with it
compared to content created by human influencers [ 13].
Overall, these findings suggest that the role of petfluencers extends far beyond
mere entertainment or cuteness. Their presence can function as a strategic element
that enhances the effectiveness of brand marketing communications.Petfluencer

MethodologyFor
the comparative social media analysis, the selected petfluencer was Tika the
Iggy, an Italian Greyhound whose owners created a dedicated Instagram account in
2016, which had gained 1.6 million followers by 2024. The dog’s online persona is
primarily built around fashion and clothing, but the profile also frequently features
humorous or emotionally toned posts presenting the owner-pet relationship.
On the human side, the selected influencer was Marta Sierra, a Spanish fashion
influencer producing similar content, who registered on Instagram in April 2012 and
had 1.4 million followers on the platform in 2024. Her posts are characterized by
vivid colors, striking visual effects, and rapid transitions, while emphasizing
creativity and humor.
The analysis focused exclusively on Instagram posts published between
1 January 2023 and 31 December 2023 by the two selected influencers. During this
period, a total of 422 posts were recorded. The number of likes and comments
associated with each post was collected manually, while follower count data was
obtained from the Instrack platform.4.
ResultsThe
first analytical dimension examined was posting frequency. The monthly
distribution of posts published by the two influencers in 2023 reveals notable
differences in activity levels. Tika the Iggy shared a total of 136 posts on Instagram
during the year, which corresponds to an average of approximately 11 posts per
month and roughly 3 posts per week. When analyzing the distribution, higher-than-
average activity can be observed in the first quarter of the year as well as in October.
The increased activity in early spring can likely be explained by Paris Fashion
Week, held in March 2023, which resulted in six separate related posts. In contrast,
no clear contextual factor can be identified in October that would account for the
unusually high volume of content during that month.
During the same period, Marta Sierra published a total of 286 posts, representing
an average of approximately 24 posts per month and around 5 posts per week.
A noticeable increase can be observed in May, which may be attributed to the Cannes
International Film Festival, where Marta appeared on the red carpet and shared
multiple posts related to the event. Toward the end of the year, a slight decline in
posting activity can be observed.
Overall, both influencers can be considered highly active on Instagram. Their
posts primarily consist of original content; however, in rare cases, “recycled”
elements also appear, referring to the reuploading of images or videos that had
previously been shared.
To categorize the analyzed posts, three clearly distinguishable content types were
defined: image posts, video posts, and swipeable posts, also known as carousel posts.
From a digital marketing strategy perspective, these formats offer different
advantages.
Initially, Instagram allowed users to share only images, but since 2013, it has also
enabled the uploading of videos of varying lengths. In recent years, short video
content of approximately 30 seconds has become dominant on the platform, to such
an extent that it now represents one of the main focal areas of Instagram’s ongoing Marketing

development
[14]. One major advantage of the video format is that the same content
can appear simultaneously in multiple placements, including the main feed, Reels,
and recommendation sections, thereby contributing to increased reach [ 15].
Carousel posts have also become increasingly popular on the platform. This
content type allows multiple images or videos, up to a maximum of 20, to be shared
within a single post. According to the Interword Web and Social Marketing
Agency, these posts typically generate the highest engagement rates, and this metric
tends to increase as the number of slides grows. An additional advantage of carousel
posts is that the algorithm displays them multiple times to users who have not
swiped through all slides, whereas single-element posts are not redistributed in this
manner. The repeated reappearance of carousel posts in users’ feeds can, therefore,
contribute to enhanced visibility [ 16].
The distribution of posts across the three content types shows that single-image
posts appeared only in very limited numbers or were entirely absent, while carousel
posts were already present to a greater extent. It can be clearly observed that
video-based content dominates the profiles of both influencers. In Marta’s case,
74 percent of the content consisted of videos, while for Tika the Iggy, this proportion
reached 85 percent.
Video content tends to be more attention-grabbing and is also favored by Meta’s
algorithm, which increases the likelihood of appearing in recommendation sections.
This supports the broader trend on social media platforms toward prioritizing
dynamic visual content over static images [ 17].

The overall composition of Tika the Iggy’s posts indicates that organic brand
appearances represent the largest share, accounting for 68 percent of all content.
This suggests a strong tendency to share personal recommendations with followers.
These organic mentions primarily involve small businesses producing custom-made

In contrast, the proportion of paid collaborations was relatively low, representing
only 8 percent of all posts. Nevertheless, several prominent brands appeared within

The analysis of Marta Sierra’s posts shows that 69 percent of the content was not
created for advertising purposes and therefore fell into the “own content” category.
In addition, paid appearances accounted for 20 percent, while organic appearances
represented 11 percent of the total content. Out of the 286 posts, 58 included
identifiable paid brand collaborations, meaning that, on average, every fifth post
qualified as an advertisement. These collaborations predominantly featured
clothing, footwear, and beauty brands, in line with the thematic focus of the
profile.
During 2023, the brand most frequently appearing in paid partnerships was
Calzedonia, a swimwear and hosiery brand, which was featured on nine occasions.
Repeated brand appearances indicate long-term and stable partnership relationships
and also enhance the credibility of the communicated message, as they create the
impression that the influencer genuinely likes and uses the promoted brand and its
products.

First, the relevant interaction types must be identified and aggregated. In the

The engagement values presented were calculated using the following formula:
((number of likes + number of comments) / number of posts) / number of
followers × 100.
Posts were subsequently divided and compared across the three previously
defined categories, namely paid appearances, organic appearances, and own content,
in order to identify which content types proved to be the most effective.
Among Tika the Iggy’s posts, paid appearances achieved the highest average
engagement rate at 27.63 percent. This exceptionally high value is partly explained
by the fact that one of the influencer’s most popular posts belongs to this category.
The advertisement created for the brand Riikyu generated 3,374,815 likes and 17,100
comments.
This performance is remarkable, as the number of likes alone was nearly three
times higher than Tika’s total follower count. In the case of their own content, the
average engagement rate reached 10.88 percent, while organic appearances achieved
an average of 6.51 percent. The lowest-performing post was a piece of content
created for Google, which received 7,610 likes and 67 comments, resulting in an
engagement rate of 0.64 percent ( Table 1).
The data related to Marta Sierra’s posts indicate that organic brand appearances
achieved the highest average engagement rate, reaching 4.47 percent. This suggests
that this content type generated the strongest audience response. This outcome
was also influenced by the fact that the post producing the highest number of
interactions was an organic brand appearance, which received 875,643 likes and
2,353 comments.
Paid appearances performed the weakest on average, with an engagement rate of
0.97 percent. The post showing the lowest level of activity was created for the

ConclusionsThe
aim of this research was to present the phenomenon of petfluencers in
comparison with traditional human influencers. For this purpose, a concise
comparative analysis was conducted, focusing on the Instagram posts of the dog
influencer Tika the Iggy and the human influencer Marta Sierra. Four main
dimensions were examined: posting frequency, content types, brand collaborations,
and follower activity, measured through the calculation of engagement rates.
The findings indicate that both influencers place strong emphasis on video-based
content, which represents the dominant format on their profiles. Instagram itself
actively supports this format through its algorithmic prioritization, making video
content an effective tool for increasing reach.Petfluencer

In

number of posts; however, these posts generated lower overall audience engagement
compared to those of the dog influencer Tika the Iggy, who posted less frequently
but achieved substantially stronger performance.
When analyzing the brands appearing on the profiles, it can be observed that Tika
the Iggy’s collaborations were thematically diverse, and only a single brand was
directly related to pet products. In contrast, although Marta Sierra engaged in
a higher number of collaborations, most of these belonged to the same categories,
primarily within the fashion and beauty industries, in line with the thematic focus of
her profile.
A more in depth examination of Tika the Iggy’s Instagram presence highlights
that petfluencers represent a distinctive and unique segment within influencer
marketing. The content shared by petfluencers is not limited to brands associated
with animals but can be effectively applied across a wide range of digital marketing
campaigns.
A limitation of this study is that, despite its detailed nature, it examined only
one petfluencer and one human influencer. Nevertheless, the findings provide
a valuable starting point for future research in this area. Unfortunately, further
analysis of Tika the Iggy will no longer be possible, as the dog passed away in
November 2025. Interestingly, news of this event was not only reported by the
media, but several prominent dog influencers also shared joint photos and videos in
remembrance, further illustrating the relevance and social impact of the topic
examined.Acknowledgements
