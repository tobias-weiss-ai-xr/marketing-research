ABSTRACT
Instagram social media has become one of the digital marketing strategies widely used in the hospitality
industry to attract purchase intention, including at Bali Tropic Resort & Spa in the Tanjung Benoa area, Bali.
Despite having the highest engagement rate among competitors in the area, the hotel recorded the lowest
occupancy rate, raising questions about the effectiveness of Instagram in influencing purchase intention. This
study aims to determine the influence of Instagram social media on purchase intention at Bali Tropic Resort &
Spa. This study employed a quantitative associative approach using purposive sampling on 100 respondents
who were active Instagram users and had seen, visited, or followed the hotel Instagram account. Data were
collected through questionnaires and analyzed using simple linear regression with SPSS version 26. The results
show that both variables fall into the high category, with mean scores of 3.70 and 3.63. The regression equation
Y = 7.234 + 0.590X, with a t -value of 12.753 greater than the t-table value of 1.661 and significance of 0.000 (<
0.05), led to the rejection of H0 and acceptance of H1. The R2 value of 0.624 indicates Instagram social media
contributes 62.4% to purchase intention (strong category), while the remaining 37.6% is explained by other
factors. It can be concluded that Instagram social media has a positive and significant influence on purchase
intention at Bali Tropic Resort & Spa.

1. INTRODUCTION
The rapid development of digital technology has transformed marketing strategies in the
hospitality industry, particularly through the use of Instagram as a medium for promotion,
communication, and brand awareness. Instagram enables hotels to showcase their facilities, guest
activities, promotions, and accommodation experiences through visual content, thereby attracting
potential cust omers and building positive perceptions of the hotel. Brand awareness refers to
consumers’ ability to recognize and recall a brand, which is an important aspect in developing
consumer interest in a product (Rosmayanti, 2023). One hotel that actively utilizes Instagram as part
of its marketing activities is Bali Tropic Resort & Spa, a four-star hotel located in the Tanjung Benoa
area of Bali.
Based on data from the official Instagram account @balitropicresort, Bali Tropic Resort &
Spa had approximately 10,856 followers and 1,749 posts as of 2026. During the 2023–2025 period, the
number of followers continued to increase, while profile visits and website visits fluctuated. Profile
and website visits decreased from 16,287 visits in 2023 to 14,635 visits in 2024 . This condition
indicates a gap between the growth in the number of followers and the actual actions of audiences
in visiting the hotel’s profile and accessing further information. Therefore, an increase in followers
does not necessarily indicate an increase in consumers’ interest in the hotel’s products and services.
The effectiveness of Instagram marketing activities can also be assessed through the engagement
rate, which is an indicator of the level of audience involvement and interaction with published
content (Nicky & Lestari, 2025). Based on an analysis conducted through Social Blade, the Instagram
account of Bali Tropic Resort & Spa has an engagement rate of 0.91%. This figure is lower than the

intention at Bali Tropic Resort & Spa, thereby determining the extent to which Instagram marketing
activities can encourage consumers’ interest in choosing and purchasing the hotel’s products and
services.

2. LITERATURE REVIEW
2.1 Marketing
Marketing is an important activity within a company because it serves as one of the
main drivers of sales and profitability through the products or services offered by the
company (Alimin et al., 2022). The development of marketing concepts is no longer
limited to tangible products but also includes service marketing, which has different
characteristics from physical products. Service marketing requires a more strategic
approach because services are intangible and perishable (Wolak et al., 1998). Service
marketing can be defined as a series of activities involving planning, pricing,
promoting, and distributing services to consumers with the aim of creating customer
satisfaction (Fatihudin, 2019).
In the hotel industry, service marketing plays an important role because hotels do
not merely offer rooms but also sell experiences, comfort, hospitality, facilit ies,
atmosphere, and overall guest satisfaction. Therefore, hotel marketing strategies must
be able to build positive perceptions, increase consumer trust, and establish a favorable
image in consumers’ minds. These efforts can be achieved through improving  service
quality, implementing effective marketing communication, and utilizing digital media
to reach consumers more broadly (Rachmadizal et al., 2022). Thus, service marketing is
an important factor in creating competitive advantage and increasing hotel
attractiveness amid intense competition in the hospitality industry.

2.2 Promotion Mix
The promotion mix refers to a combination of marketing communication methods
used by companies to inform, persuade, and remind consumers about the products or
services offered (Putri, 2014). The promotion mix functions as a marketing
communication tool to build brand awareness, create a positive image, and encourage
consumers to make purchasing decisions (Amelia & Iswadi, 2023). According to Kotler
and Keller (2012), as cited in Uluwiyah (2022), the promotion mix consists of five main
elements: Advertising, which refers to a form of non-personal communication used to
introduce and promote products or services through various media.  Sales Promotion,
which involves short -term incentives designed to encourage consumers to try or
purchase products or services. Personal Selling, which refers to direct communication
between sales personnel and potential consumers to provide information, answer
questions, and obtain orders. Direct Marketing, which involves direct communication
with consumers or potential consumers through letters, telephone, e-mail, the internet,
and other media to obtain an immediate response.  Public Relations, which consists of
various programs designed to build, maintain, or protect the company’s image and its
products in the eyes of the public.
With the development of digital marketing, social media has become an important
marketing communication channel because it can reach consumers broadly, quickly,
and interactively. Instagram is one of the platforms widely used by companies,
including hotels, to provide information, promote products, build brand awareness,
and create engagement with audiences.
2.3 Social Media
Social media refers to online platforms that enable users to participate, share
information, create content, and interact with other users (Rafiq, 2020). Social media
allows users to create and distribute content in various forms, including text, images,
videos, and audio, in an interactive manner (Qadir & Ramli, 2024). The emergence of
social media has transformed communication patterns from  one-way communication
into more open and participatory two-way communication.
Social media marketing is not merely a promotional tool but has developed into an
integral component of an integrated marketing strategy (Binrany Aisyah et al., 2024).
For the h ospitality industry, social media can be used to showcase hotel rooms,
restaurants, hotel environments, tourism activities, and guest experiences. Consistent
social media use can also help hotels establish a positive image and increase brand
visibility (Mustika & Maulidah, 2021; Imayan & Farid, 2026).
According to Heuer, as cited in Solis (2010), social media effectiveness can be
assessed through four dimensions: context, communication, collaboration, and
connection. Context refers to the way messages are d elivered so that they are easy to
understand and attractive to audiences. Communication concerns the ability of a
company to deliver information while also developing responses and dialogue with
audiences. Collaboration refers to cooperation between compan ies and audiences or
other parties to increase interaction and promotional reach. Meanwhile, connection
refers to the ability of a company to build and maintain relationships with audiences,
thereby creating closeness and loyalty.  Social media is also char acterized by

Ramanda, 2015). These characteristics allow consumers to act not only as information
recipients but also as content creators and information distributors. Consequently ,

consumers’ perceptions of a particular brand.
2.4 Instagram as a Marketing Medium
Instagram is a photo - and video-sharing platform that allows users to create, edit,
and shar e visual content with other users (Bate’e, 2019). Its development has
transformed Instagram from a platform primarily used for sharing personal activities
into an effective marketing communication medium for various types of businesses,
including the hospitality industry. Features such as feed, stories, reels, comments, likes,
shares, and direct messages enable companies to communicate information creatively
while establishing two-way communication with consumers (Antasari & Pratiwi, 2022).
The primary strength of Instagram lies in its visual content, which allows hotels to
present room designs, facilities, restaurants, hotel surroundings, guest activities,
promotions, and accommodation experiences in an attractive manner. Consistent,
informative, and high-quality visual content can help establish positive perceptions and
increase hotel attractiveness among potential consumers (Kusuma & Sugandi, 2018).
Therefore, effective Instagram management can become an important component of
hotel marketing strategies.
According to Irawan and Hadisumarto (2020), social media marketing can be
measured through five dimensions: entertainment, interaction, trendiness,
customization, and electronic word of mouth (e -WOM). Entertainment refers to the
ability of content to provide enjoyment and pleasure to audiences. Interaction concerns
the ability of social media to facilitate two -way communication and engagement
between hotels and their followers. Trendiness refers to the ability to provide current
and relevant information. Cus tomization concerns the ability to provide information
that matches consumers’ needs and preferences. Meanwhile, e -WOM refers to the
ability of social media to encourage audiences to share their experiences and
recommend a brand to others.
These five dime nsions are relevant to research on the Instagram account of Bali
Tropic Resort & Spa because content quality, interaction, up -to-date information,
information relevance, and user recommendations can determine the extent to which
Instagram attracts potential consumers’ attention and develops their interest in hotel
products.
2.5 Purchase Intention
Purchase intention refers to a condition in which an individual develops a desire or
willingness to obtain a product after going through a process of searching for and
evaluating information (Az -Zahra et al., 2022). Purchase intention can also be
understood as a consumer’s tendency or willingness to purchase a product after
receiving certain information or stimuli. It represents one of the stages preceding a
consumer’s purchase decision and generally emerges after awareness and perceptions
of a particular product have been established (Indika & Jovita, 2017). In the hospitality
industry, purchase intention can be reflected in potential guests’ interest in seeking

further information, visiting a hotel’s profile or website, considering its facilities and
prices, and developing an intention to make a reservation. According to Widjaja and
Firdausy Mulya (2023), purchase intention can be influenced by several factors,
including social media/Instagram promotion, brand image, e -WOM, perceived value,
trust, price, product or service quality, as well as influencers and user -generated
content. These factors indicate that purchase intention is not determined solely by
promotional act ivities but also by consumers’ perceptions of the benefits, quality,
reputation, price, and credibility of the information received.
In this study, Instagram is viewed as a marketing stimulus that can provide
information while shaping consumers’ perception s of Bali Tropic Resort & Spa.
Attractive content (entertainment), active interaction (interaction), up -to-date

and user experiences (e -WOM) can increase consumers’ attenti on and interest.
Therefore, the more effectively Instagram is managed as a marketing medium, the
greater the likelihood that consumers will develop an intention to seek further
information and purchase hotel products or services.

3. METHODS
The objects of this study consist of Instagram social media as the independent variable (X)
and purchase intention as the dependent variable (Y). Instagram social media in this study refers to
the use of the digital platform by Bali Tropic Resort & Spa as a ma rketing communication medium
for delivering information and promotions and establishing interactions with consumers through
visual content, including photos, videos, and various interactive features. Meanwhile, purchase
intention as the dependent variable focuses on consumers’ interest and willingness to make a
reservation or use the hotel’s services after receiving information and being exposed to promotional
content through Instagram. These two variables were selected because of their potential relationship
in shaping consumers’ perceptions, interest, and tendency toward hotel services in the digital era.
A population refers to the entire set of research objects that possess specific characteristics
and serve as a source of data for a study (Purwanza et al., 2022). The population in this study consists
of Instagram users who are aware of, have viewed, visited, or followed the official Instagram account
of Bali Tropic Resort & Spa. This population was selected because the respondents have had
exposure to the hotel’s marketing activities through Instagram and are therefore able to provide
relevant assessments regarding the influence of Instagram social media on purchase intention. Based
on the Bali Tropic Resort & Spa Instagram account data, the number of followers in December 2025
reached approximately 10,800 followers, which was used as the basis for determining the study
population.
A sample is a subset of the population selected to represent the characteristics of the
population in a study (H ermina & Huda, 2024). This study employs a non -probability sampling
technique using purposive sampling, which is a sampling technique based on specific considerations
and criteria that are aligned with the objectives of the study. This technique was selected because not
all Instagram users have sufficient experience or knowledge of the Bali Tropic Resort & Spa account
and its products. Therefore, respondents were selected based on the following criteria: Respondents
are active users of Instagram. Respondents have viewed, visited, or followed the Bali Tropic Resort
& Spa Instagram account. Respondents are aware of or familiar with Bali Tropic Resort & Spa, either
through Instagram or other sources of information. Respondents are at least 22 years old at the time
of the study.
Based on these criteria and Slovin’ technique, the study obtained 100 respondents who met
the requirements as research participants. All respondents were subsequently given a questionnaire

to collect data regarding their perceptions of In stagram marketing activities and their intention to

Based on the validity test results, all statement items for the Instagram social media variable
(X) and purchase intention variable (Y) are valid. Of the 18 statement items tested, the lowest
correlation coefficient (R<sub>count</sub>) was found for item Y1.7 at 0.791, while the highest value
was recorded for item X1.8 at 0.909. All values exceeded the R<sub>table</sub> value of 0.1654,

demonstrate that each statement in the questionnaire is capable of measuring the intended aspects
and can therefore be used as a research instrument. In addition to the validity test, a reliability test

The results showed that the Instagram social media variable (X) had a Cronbach’s Alpha value of
0.958, while the purchase intention variable (Y) had a Cronbach’s Alpha value of 0.946. Both values
were substantially higher than the minim um threshold of 0.60, indicating that the research
instruments were reliable. Thus, the statement items demonstrated a very good level of internal
consistency and were suitable for further analysis.
Overall, the validity and reliability test results indicate that the research instrument met the
requirements for use as a measurement tool. The Instagram social media and purchase intention
variables can be measured consistently through the developed statement items. Therefore, the data
obtained from the 100 re spondents can be used for descriptive analysis and hypothesis testing
concerning the influence of Instagram social media on purchase intention at Bali Tropic Resort &
Spa.

4. RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
Descriptive analysis was conducted to provide an overview of  the respondents’
characteristics and their perceptions of the variables examined in this study, namely Instagram social
media (X) and purchase intention (Y). The research data were obtained from 100 respondents who
met the sample criteria through an onlin e questionnaire distributed using Google Forms.
Respondent characteristics were analyzed based on age, gender, highest educational attainment,
occupation, and daily Instagram usage intensity. The analysis also examined the scores of each
statement item to identify respondents’ perceptions of the two research variables.
Based on age, the majority of respondents were between 26 and 30 years old (45%), followed
by respondents aged 22 –25 years at 30%. This composition indicates that most respondents were
young adults who are relatively active users of social media, particularly Instagram. This age group
also represents a potential segment for digital marketing because young adults tend to use social
media to obtain information, compare alternatives, and seek ref erences before using a particular
product or service. Based on gender, respondents were predominantly female, accounting for 72%,
while male respondents accounted for 28%. The dominance of female respondents indicates that
Instagram users who were aware of or interacted with the Bali Tropic Resort & Spa account in this
study were predominantly women. This condition may also suggest that visual content related to
hotel facilities, atmosphere, accommodation experiences, and promotional offers has considerable
appeal among female users.
Based on highest educational attainment, the majority of respondents held a Bachelor’s
degree (S1), accounting for 47%. This finding indicates that most respondents had a relatively strong
educational background and were assumed to have sufficient ability to understand information and
evaluate hotel marketing content presented through Instagram. Educational background may also
support respondents’ ability to evaluate information before developing an interest in the products
or services offered. Based on occupation, the majority of respondents were private-sector employees
(44%). This finding indicates that most respondents belonged to a group with employment -related

4.1 Description of the Instagram Social Media Variable (X)
The descriptive analysis showed that the Instagram social media variable (X) obtained a
mean score of 3.70, which falls within the high category. Of the 10 statement items, seven items were
classified as high, while three items, namely X1.2, X1.3, and X1.4, were classified as moderate. These
findings indicate that, overall, respondents had positive pe rceptions of Bali Tropic Resort & Spa’s
marketing activities through Instagram. The highest mean score was recorded for item X1.5 at 4.00,
which relates to the hotel’s ability to present content that follows the latest trends. This result
indicates that re spondents considered the Instagram content of Bali Tropic Resort & Spa to be
relatively relevant to current social media trends. Trend-responsive content can help hotels remain
relevant amid changing user preferences and increasingly competitive social med ia content.
Meanwhile, the lowest mean score was found for item X1.3 at 3.25, which relates to the hotel’s
interaction with its followers. Although this score remains within the moderate category, it indicates
that two-way communication between the hotel and its audience has not been perceived as optimal
compared with other aspects. Therefore, the hotel needs to improve its responses to comments, direct
messages, and other forms of interaction so that audiences are not merely recipients of information
but can also actively participate in communication with the hotel.

4.2 Description of the Purchase Intention Variable (Y)
The purchase intention variable (Y) obtained a mean score of 3.63, which falls within the
high category. Of the eight statement items used, six were classified as high, while two items, namely
Y1.7 and Y1.8, were classified as moderate. These results indicate that, overall, respondents had a
relatively high level of interest in Bali Tropic Resort & Spa after being exposed to information
through Instagram. The highest mean score was recorded for item Y1.5 at 3.89, which relates to
respondents’ preference for choosing Bali Tropic Resort & Spa over competing hotels. This result
indicates that the hotel’s presence and content on Instagram have been a ble to generate positive
interest and preferences among most respondents. In other words, Instagram functions not only as
an information-sharing medium but also has the potential to help establish consumer preferences
toward the hotel. Conversely, the lowest mean score was recorded for item Y1.7 at 3.20, which relates
to respondents’ willingness to seek additional information about the hotel. Although this score
remains within the moderate category, it indicates that interest in the hotel is not always foll owed
by active efforts to seek additional information, such as reading reviews, viewing additional photos,
comparing facilities, or searching for room prices and availability.
Overall, the descriptive analysis indicates that both Instagram social media (X) and purchase
intention (Y) fall within the high category, with mean scores of 3.70 and 3.63, respectively. However,
several aspects still require attention, particularly hotel interaction with followers and consumers’
motivation to seek additional informa tion. These findings provide an important basis for further
testing whether positive perceptions of Instagram social media actually have a significant influence
on consumers’ purchase intention at Bali Tropic Resort & Spa.

4.3.1 Instagram Social Media Variable
The instagram social media variable (X) obtained an overall mean score of 3.70, which falls
into the high category. This result indicates that, in general, respondents gave positive assessments
of Instagram as a promotional medium for Bali Tropic Resort & Spa. Of the ten statement items
representing five dimensions—entertainment, interaction, trendiness, customization, and electronic
word of mouth (e-WOM)—seven items were categorized as high, while three items were categorized
as moderate.  The high mean score initially appears inconsistent with the hotel’s Instagram
engagement rate of only 0.91%, which is lower than the average rate of approximately 2 .43% for
similar accounts with 5,000 –20,000 followers. However, this condition does not indicate a
contradiction because the two types of data measure different aspects. Engagement rate represents
actual behavioral data, reflecting real audience interactio ns such as likes, comments, and shares,
whereas the questionnaire results measure respondents’ perceptions or attitudinal assessments of
the quality, attractiveness, and usefulness of Instagram content. The difference between attitudinal
and behavioral engagement may create an engagement gap, which occurs when consumers provide
positive evaluations of content but do not necessarily demonstrate active behavioral engagement
(Barger et al., 2016).
This finding is also reflected in the Instagram data of Bali Tr opic Resort & Spa, where
impressions are higher than account reach. This condition indicates that the hotel’s content has

The trendiness dimension received the highest asses sment, particularly item X1.5

CONCLUSION
Based on the results, instagram social media at Bali Tropic Resort & Spa obtained an overall
mean score of 3.70, which falls into the high category. This indicates that respondents generally had
positive perceptions of the hotel’s Instagram accou nt as a promotional medium. The trendiness
dimension received the highest assessment, while the interaction dimension received the lowest.
Thus, although the Instagram content is considered attractive, relevant, and up to date, two -way
interaction between the hotel and its followers still needs to be improved. Consumer purchase
intention obtained an overall mean score of 3.63, which is categorized as high. This finding indicates
that respondents have a relatively strong interest and tendency to choose Bali Tropic Resort & Spa.
The preferential purchase intention dimension received the highest assessment, while the
exploratory purchase intention dimension received the lowest. This suggests that consumers already
have positive preferences toward the hotel, but their motivation to seek additional information, such
as reviews, photographs, facilities, and booking information, still needs to be strengthened.
Instagram social media has a positive and significant effect on consumer purchase intention.
The regression analysis produced the equation Y = 7.234 + 0.590X, with a calculated t-value of 12.753,
which is greater than the t-table value of 1.661, and a significance value of 0.000 < 0.05. Therefore, H₀

associated with higher consumer purchase intention toward Bali Tropic Resort & Spa. The R-Square

improving audience interaction and facilitating easier access to information and booking channels
