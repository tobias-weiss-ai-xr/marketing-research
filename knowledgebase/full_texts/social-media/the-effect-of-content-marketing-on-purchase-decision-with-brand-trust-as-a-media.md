# The Effect of Content Marketing on Purchase Decision with Brand Trust as a Mediating Variable

INTRODUCTION
The advancement of information and communication technology has reshaped how Indonesian
consumers seek information, evaluate products, and interact with brands across increasingly digitalized
marketplaces. The Digital 2025: Indonesia report shows that Indonesia has around 285 million people,
an internet penetration rate of 59.5%, and 356 million active mobile connections, equivalent to 125%
of the population, indicating that digital devices have become deeply embedded in everyday consumer
activities (We Are Social & Meltwater, 2025). This growth in connectivity has driven the rise of social
media as an alternative marketing channel that companies can use to reach and engage customers more
broadly, while simultaneously changing how consumers encounter and eva luate commercial
information. Among social media platforms, TikTok has developed into a particularly influential
environment for product discovery because its short -video format enables brands and creators to
combine entertainment, information, interaction , and promotional communication within a single
consumer journey (Statista, 2025). The platform allows consumers to encounter product information
through educational content, product demonstrations, testimonials, reviews, and promotional videos,
making soc ial media increasingly relevant to the formation of consumer perceptions. Such
characteristics have strengthened the strategic relevance of content marketing, which focuses on
creating and distributing valuable, relevant, and consistent content to attract and retain a defined
audience (Forbes Magazine, 2023). For businesses operating in highly competitive consumer markets,
particularly skincare, the ability to transform digital content into credible and persuasive consumer
experiences has consequently become an important managerial and marketing issue.
Within this changing digital environment, content marketing has evolved from a supplementary
promotional activity into a strategic mechanism for establishing customer engagement and influencing
consumer responses throughout the purchasing process. Content marketing enables firms to

Abstract
The rapid growth of internet and social media use in Indonesia has encouraged skincare brands to
rely on content marketing to engage consumers. Daviena Skincare, a local brand promoted through
TikTok, faced a public trust challenge after a skincare influencer questioned the safety of products,
sparking discussion. This study examines the effect of content marketing on purchase decision, both
directly and indirectly through brand trust as a mediating variable, among Daviena Skincare
consumers on TikTok. A quantitative approach was applied using Partial Least Squares Structural
Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) with SmartPLS 4. A total of 131 responses were obtained through
an online questionnaire and purposive sampling. The measurement model demonstrated convergent
validity, discriminant validity, and reliability. The structural analysis revealed that content
marketing significantly affects purchase decision and brand trust. Brand trust also positively affects
purchase decision in the model. Furthermore, content mar keting has a positive and significant
indirect effect on purchase decision through brand trust, which functions as a complementary
mediator. Content marketing and brand trust jointly explain 55.4% of the variance in purchase
decision. The findings highligh t the importance of relevant, accurate, informative, and trust -
building TikTok content for strengthening consumer purchase decisions and sustaining
competitiveness among local skincare brands.

to develop familiarity with products while evaluating their potential benefits (Ghulam, 2023). Empirical

audiences perceive the information as relevant to their needs and interests (Chandra & Sari, 2021). This

content toward product evaluation and purchase -related consideration within the same digital
environment. The effectiveness of content, however, cannot be understood solely from its capacity to
attract attention because consumers also assess whether the informat ion is useful, accurate, credible,

behavior because consumers must evaluate available alternatives and determine whether a product is
sufficiently valuable to justify the allocation of money, time, and effort (Schiffman & Kanuk, 2021).
The growing role of social media therefore raises a critical question concerning whether persuasive
content is sufficient to generate purchase decisions or whether its influence depends on an intervening
psychological mechanism such as brand trust.
This issue becomes particularly significant in the skincare industry, where consumers face
substantial uncertainty regarding product quality, safety, suitability, and expected benefits before
completing a purchase. Brand trust reflects consumers' confidence in a brand's reliability and credibility
and can develop through direct experiences as well as indirect information obtained from other sources
(Delgado-Ballester, 2016). In digital environments, trust formation becomes more complex because
consumers can  simultaneously encounter official brand content, influencer recommendations, user -
generated reviews, electronic word-of-mouth, and negative information from other users. Research on
skincare products has shown that social media marketing, brand image, and influencer endorsement can
influence purchase decisions, with trust functioning as an important mechanism connecting marketing
activities with consumer responses (Levina et al., 2023). Similar evidence indicates that brand trust and
brand awareness are associated with consumers' decisions to purchase beauty products, suggesting that
confidence in a brand can become an important consideration when consumers evaluate alternatives
(Harifin & Paramita, 2024). The role of trust becomes even more consequential w hen consumers
encounter contradictory claims because promotional information must compete with other narratives
that may challenge perceptions of product quality or brand credibility. This condition is particularly
relevant to Daviena Skincare, a local ski ncare brand that utilizes TikTok content marketing through
product information, skincare instructions, user testimonials, and promotional videos to attract
consumers and maintain relationships with its audience.
The relevance of this issue increased when Daviena Skincare received public attention
following a skincare influencer's review of several skincare products in 2024, including Daviena
Skincare, in which concerns were raised regarding potentially harmful ingredients and cosmetic safety
standards. The circulation of such content on TikTok illustrates how rapidly product-related information
can move beyond the control of a brand and become part of consumers' evaluation processes. Recent
research specifically examining Daviena Skincare indicates that online customer reviews, brand trust,
and perceived value are associated with purchase decisions among consumers exposed to the brand
through TikTok (Arifin & Aminah, 2026). Another study similarly identifies TikTok,  word of mouth,
and brand trust as relevant factors influencing purchase decisions for Daviena Skincare products,
reinforcing the importance of trust within this particular brand context (Alfiatussa’adah et al., 2026).
Evidence concerning overclaiming and online reviews further suggests that social -media discourse
surrounding Daviena products can influence consumer behavior and purchase-related evaluations when
consumers encounter information that challenges their expectations (Nabilah et al., 2025). These
circumstances make Daviena Skincare a meaningful empirical setting for examining how brand -
controlled content operates when consumers are simultaneously exposed to information that may
strengthen or weaken confidence in the brand. The issue is not simply whether consumers are exposed
to content but whether the content can contribute to a purchase decision when brand trust has become
an important component of the consumer evaluation process.
Although previous studies have established relationships between digital marketing activities,
brand trust, and consumer responses, the existing literature remains fragmented in explaining the
specific pathway through which content marketing translates into purchase decisions in a TikTok-based
skincare context. Research focusing on Daviena Skincare has predominantly examined online customer
reviews, influencer marketing, brand image, word of mouth, brand awareness, perceived value, and

practically relevant for local s kincare businesses that must simultaneously maintain promotional
effectiveness, information quality, and consumer confidence within highly interactive TikTok
environments.
Positioned within this research gap, the present study examines content marketing as the
independent variable, brand trust as the mediating variable, and purchase decision as the dependent
variable among Daviena Skincare consumers on TikTok. The study spec ifically investigates whether
relevant, accurate, and informative content can influence consumers' purchase decisions while
strengthening the level of trust required to transform digital exposure into favorable purchasing
behavior. The proposed model disti nguishes the direct relationship between content marketing and
purchase decision from the indirect relationship operating through brand trust. This approach enables
the study to move beyond the assumption that effective content marketing automatically prod uces
purchasing behavior by examining whether consumer confidence represents a mechanism through
which content becomes behaviorally consequential. The study aims to determine the effect of content
marketing on purchase decision and to examine whether brand  trust mediates this relationship among
Daviena Skincare consumers on TikTok. Theoretically, the study contributes to the digital marketing
and consumer-behavior literature by integrating content marketing, brand trust, and purchase decision
within a specific TikTok skincare context characterized by intensive information exposure and potential
uncertainty regarding brand credibility. Methodologically, the study contributes empirical evidence
through a quantitative mediation model that distinguishes the dire ct effect of content marketing from
its indirect effect through brand trust, providing a more precise explanation of how social-media content
may translate into consumer purchasing behavior.

RESEARCH METHODS
This empirical study employed a quantitative research design grounded in a positivist paradigm
to objectively measure and test the relationships among content marketing, brand trust, and purchase
decision. The population consisted of followers of the offic ial Daviena Skincare TikTok account,
totaling 228,500 followers as of 1 June 2026, while purposive non-probability sampling was applied by
requiring respondents to have been aware of the negative review of Daviena Skincare circulated by the
skincare influe ncer and to have previously purchased a Daviena Skincare product. The minimum
sample size was determined using Slovin's formula with a 10% margin of error, resulting in a minimum
requirement of approximately 100 respondents, while the online questionnaire distributed through
Google Forms yielded 131 valid responses that satisfied the specified criteria. The questionnaire
comprised 30 items measured using a four -point Likert scale ranging from 1 (strongly disagree) to 4
(strongly agree), with 10 items for content marketing covering relevance, accuracy, and informational
value, 10 items for brand trust covering brand liking, brand competence, brand reputation, and brand
quality stability, and 10 items for purchase decision covering product fit, perceived benef it, purchase
accuracy, and repurchase intention, following established measurement approaches (Dinanti & Kasih,
2022). The operationalization of content marketing emphasized the extent to which digital content
provides relevant, accurate, and informative v alue to consumers, while brand trust represented
consumers' confidence in brand reliability and credibility (Delgado-Ballester, 2016). Purchase decision

was operationalized as the consumer's evaluation and final behavioral choice concerning the suitability,
benefits, value, and continued purchasing of Daviena Skincare products (Kotler & Keller, 2016).
The collected data were analyzed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling
(PLS-SEM) through SmartPLS 4.1.1.8, which was selected to examine the direct and mediated
relationships among the latent constructs in the proposed research model (Hair et al., 2022). Before the
main analysis, a pilot test involving 30 respondents was conducted to evaluate the preliminary quality
of the measurement instrument, followed by assessment of the measurement model through outer
loading, Cronbach's alpha, comp osite reliability, and average variance extracted (AVE). Convergent
validity was assessed using outer loading values of at least 0.70 and AVE values above 0.50, while
internal consistency was evaluated using Cronbach's alpha and composite reliability value s of at least
0.70, with indicators having lower loadings considered for retention only when the overall construct
validity and reliability remained acceptable. Discriminant validity was assessed using the Heterotrait -
Monotrait (HTMT) ratio, while the stru ctural model was evaluated through R², f², inner VIF, and the
Standardized Root Mean Square Residual (SRMR) to assess explanatory power, effect magnitude,
multicollinearity, and model adequacy (Sarstedt et al., 2022). The mediation relationship was examined
through bootstrapping, allowing the indirect effect of content marketing on purchase decision through
brand trust to be evaluated without relying on the assumption of normally distributed sampling
distributions (Nitzl et al., 2016). Hypothesis testing us ed a 5% significance level, with relationships
considered statistically significant when the bootstrapped t -statistic exceeded 1.96 and the
corresponding p-value was below 0.05.

Mediation Analysis and Discussion
The mediation analysis was conducted to determine whether Brand Trust transmits part of the
influence of Content Marketing on Purchase Decision. The indirect effect of Content Marketing through
Brand Trust produced a path coefficient of β = 0.266, with a t -statistic of 3.533 and p < 0.001. The
positive coefficient indicates that higher-quality perceptions of Daviena Skincare's TikTok content are
associated with stronger brand trust, which subsequently contributes to a higher purchase decision. The
statistical significance of this indirect effect demonstrates that the mediating pathway is empirically
supported. The magnitude of the indirect coefficient also indicates that a meaningful portion of the
relationship between content marketing and purchase decision operates through consumers' trust in the
brand. This finding establishes Brand Trust as an important mechanism within the proposed model.
The mediation mechanism can be understood through the sequence in which consumers process
marketing information before reaching a purchase decision. Relevant, informative, and consumer -
oriented TikTok content can provide information that reduces uncertainty and helps consumers evaluate
whether Daviena Skincare can meet their expectations. When consumers perceive the content as
credible and appropriate to their needs, these perceptions can contribute to greater confidence in the
brand. Brand trust subsequent ly functions as an evaluative mechanism through which consumers
translate positive perceptions of the brand into greater willingness to make a purchase. This mechanism
is consistent with the view that trust represents an important psychological basis for consumer decision-
making when direct product evaluation is limited in digital environments (Delgado -Ballester, 2016).
The significant indirect effect therefore suggests that content marketing does not operate solely as a
promotional stimulus but also contributes to purchase decision by strengthening consumers' confidence
in the brand.

CONCLUSION
This study examined the effect of Content Marketing on Purchase Decision, both directly and
indirectly through Brand Trust among Daviena Skincare consumers on TikTok. The measurement
model demonstrated adequate convergent validity, internal consistency rel iability, and discriminant
validity for all three constructs. The structural model showed moderate explanatory power, with Content
Marketing explaining 54.5% of the variance in Brand Trust and the model explaining 55.4% of the
variance in Purchase Decision. The results confirmed that Content Marketing has a positive and
significant direct effect on Purchase Decision, with β = 0.437 and p < 0.001. Content Marketing also
has a positive and significant effect on Brand Trust, while Brand Trust positively and significantly
influences Purchase Decision. The indirect effect of Content Marketing through Brand Trust was
significant at β = 0.266, with p < 0.001, confirming the mediating role of brand trust. Since both the
direct and indire ct effects were significant, Brand Trust functions as a complementary mediator,
resulting in a significant total effect of β = 0.704 and supporting both H1 and H2.
Based on these findings, Daviena Skincare should maintain and improve the relevance,
accuracy, and informational value of its TikTok content, particularly content addressing specific skin
types and consumer concerns. Content strategies should also emphasiz e credible product information
and consistent communication so that promotional messages can strengthen consumer confidence rather
than merely encourage immediate purchases. The company should strengthen transparency and provide
responsive, professional communication when addressing consumer complaints, product concerns, or
public controversies because trust represents an important mechanism connecting content marketing
with purchase decisions. Other skincare businesses can adopt a similar strategy by combi ning
informative and need -oriented content with systematic trust -building efforts to strengthen consumer
responses in competitive TikTok environments. Future research can incorporate additional variables
such as brand image, electronic word of mouth, price  perception, product quality, and customer
satisfaction to provide a broader explanation of skincare purchase decisions. Further studies can also
compare Daviena Skincare with other local or international skincare brands to examine whether the
mediation pa ttern remains consistent across different brand contexts. Expanding respondent
characteristics, sample coverage, and research settings would provide greater generalizability and a
more comprehensive understanding of how content marketing and brand trust shape consumer purchase
decisions in social commerce.
