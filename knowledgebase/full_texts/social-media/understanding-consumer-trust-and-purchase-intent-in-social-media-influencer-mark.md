ABSTRACT
The rapid expansion of social media platforms has significantly transformed
contemporary marketing practices, leading to the emergence of influencer marketing as
a dominant promotional strategy. Social media influencers play an important role in
shaping consumer perceptions, building trust, and influencing purchase decisions. The
present study investigates the relationship between influencer credibility, consumer
trust, and purchase intention within the context of social media influencer marketing.
The study proposes a mediation framework in which influencer credibility acts as
the independent variable, consumer trust functions as the mediating variable, and
purchase intention serves as the dependent variable. A quantitative research design was
adopted, and primary data were collected from 180 social media users aged between
18 and 40 years using a structured questionnaire based on a 5-point Likert scale.
The findings reveal that influencer credibility significantly influences consumer
trust (β = 0.68, p < 0.001). Consumer trust strongly predicts purchase intention (β =
0.74, p < 0.001).

developing market context. The findings highlight the strategic importance of

1. INTRODUCTION
1.1. Background of the Study
The rapid expansion of digital technologies has significantly transformed marketing
communication practices across the globe. Traditional advertising channels such as television,
print media, and radio have gradually been supplemented —and in many cases rep laced—by
social media platforms. Platforms such as Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, and TikTok have
enabled brands to interact directly with consumers in more personalized, interactive, and
engaging ways.
One of the most prominent developments in this digital transformation is the emergence of
social media influencer marketing. Social media influencers are individuals who have
established credibility, authority, and a loyal audience base on digital platform s. Unlike
traditional celebrities, influencers are often perceived as relatable, authentic, and accessible, as
they frequently share personal experiences, product reviews, and lifestyle-related content.
Consumers increasingly rely on influencer recommendations before making purchase
decisions (Lou & Yuan, 2019; Ki et al., 2020). Research suggests that influencer -generated
content is often perceived as more trustworthy than traditional advertising because it appears
less commercial and more experience-based (Djafarova & Rushworth, 2017).

Trust plays a critical role in online environments where consumers cannot physically

1.2. Conceptual Framework
The present study is structured around three primary constructs:
• Influencer Credibility (Expertise, Trustworthiness, Attractiveness)
• Consumer Trust
• Purchase Intention
The theoretical foundation of the study is supported by:
Source Credibility Theory (Ohanian, 1990)
This theory posits that the effectiveness of persuasive communication depends on the perceived
expertise, trustworthiness, and attractiveness of the source. In influencer marketing, influencers
act as communication sources whose credibility determines persuasive impact.
Social Proof Theory (Cialdini, 2007)
According to this theory, individuals rely on the actions and opinions of others when making
decisions. Influencers function as social proof by endorsing products and shaping consumer
perceptions.
Parasocial Interaction Theory
This theory explains the one -sided psychological relationships that audiences develop with
media personalities. Followers often feel emotionally connected to influencers, which enhances
persuasion.

1.3. Problem Statement

• Whether consumers genuinely trust influencer endorsements.

limited studies have comprehensively analyzed the mediating role of consumer trust within a

Therefore, this study aims to empirically examine the structural relationship between
influencer credibility, consumer trust, and purchase intention.

2. REVIEW OF LITERATURE
2.1. Introduction
This chapter critically reviews the existing literature related to social media influencer
marketing, influencer credibility, consumer trust, and purchase intention. The objective of this
review is to establish a theoretical and empirical foundation for th e proposed research

2.2. Social Media Influencer Marketing
Social media influencer marketing has emerged as a powerful digital marketing strategy over
the past decade. Influencers are individuals who build large audiences on social media platforms
and shape followers’ attitudes and purchasing decisions through content creation.
Lou and Yuan (2019) examined how influencer credibility and content value affect consumer
trust and purchase intention. Their findings indicated that informativeness and credibility
significantly enhance trust, which in turn drives purchase intention.
Lim et al. (2017) investigated the impact of social media influencers on purchase intention and
found that credibility and perceived authenticity positively influence consumer attitudes toward
promoted brands.
Ki, Cuevas, Chong, and Lim (2020) emphasized authenticity as a key driver of influencer
effectiveness. Their research demonstrated that perceived authenticity enhances trust and
consumer engagement.
Sokolova and Kefi (2020) studied the impact of influencer marketing on Instagram and
YouTube and found that parasocial interaction strengthens trust, which ultimately influences
purchase intention.

2.3. Influencer Credibility
Influencer credibility refers to the extent to which followers perceive an influencer as
knowledgeable, trustworthy, and attractive. In modern digital contexts, credibility has become
a critical determinant of persuasion effectiveness.
Djafarova and Rushworth (2017) explored the credibility of Instagram influencers and found
that followers perceive influencers as more relatable and trustworthy compared to traditional
celebrities.
Erdogan (2016) highlighted that credibility influences consumer attitudes and behavioral
intentions in endorsement contexts.
De Veirman, Cauberghe, and Hudders (2017) found that influencers with higher perceived
expertise and relevance generate more positive brand attitudes.
Lou and Yuan (2019) further confirmed that credibility enhances consumer trust and reduces
perceived risk in online purchase environments.
Overall, contemporary research suggests that influencer credibility functions as a key
antecedent of trust and behavioral intention.
2.4. Consumer Trust in Digital Environments
Trust is a fundamental factor influencing online consumer behavior. In social media marketing
contexts, trust refers to the belief that influencers provide honest and reliable information.
Kim and Peterson (2017) found that online trust significantly influences purchase decisions in
digital commerce environments.
Sokolova and Kefi (2020) demonstrated that trust mediates the relationship between influencer
characteristics and purchase intention.
AlFarraj et al. (2021) examined influencer credibility and concluded that trust plays a mediating
role between influencer endorsement and consumer buying intention.
Trust reduces uncertainty and enhances confidence in product recommendations, making it a
central mechanism in influencer marketing effectiveness.

2.5. Purchase Intention
Purchase intention represents the likelihood that a consumer will buy a product after exposure
to marketing communication.
Lim et al. (2017) found that influencer credibility significantly increases purchase intention.
Shan, Chen, and Lin (2020) reported that influencer attractiveness and trustworthiness
positively affect consumers’ purchase intentions in social commerce settings.
Jin, Muqaddam, and Ryu (2019) demonstrated that influencer transparency and authenticity
positively influence brand trust and purchase intention.
Recent research consistently indicates that purchase intention is influenced not only by
exposure to influencer content but also by psychological constructs such as trust and perceived
credibility.
2.6. Mediating Role of Consumer Trust
Several contemporary studies emphasize the mediating role of trust in influencer marketing.
Lou and Yuan (2019) found that trust mediates the relationship between influencer credibility
and purchase intention.
Sokolova and Kefi (2020) confirmed that trust acts as a psychological mechanism translating
influencer engagement into behavioral intention.
AlFarraj et al. (2021) also validated the mediating role of trust in the influencer –purchase
relationship.
However, while prior research has examined these relationships, limited studies have integrated
credibility, trust, and purchase intention into a unified framework within a developing market
context. This gap forms the basis of the present study.

Contemporary influencer marketing research integrates modern interpretations of credibility
and consumer psychology.

5.6. Directions for Future Research
Future studies may consider:
1. Conducting longitudinal research to examine long-term trust development.
2. Comparing micro-influencers and macro-influencers.
3. Exploring moderating variables such as gender, product type, or cultural factors.
4. Expanding research to different countries for cross-cultural comparison.
5. Using advanced statistical techniques such as Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) for
deeper analysis.
These directions can further strengthen understanding of influencer marketing dynamics.
5.7. Conclusion
The present study confirms that influencer credibility significantly influences consumer trust,
which in turn strongly predicts purchase intention. Although credibility directly affects
purchase intention, its impact becomes more powerful when mediated through trust.
