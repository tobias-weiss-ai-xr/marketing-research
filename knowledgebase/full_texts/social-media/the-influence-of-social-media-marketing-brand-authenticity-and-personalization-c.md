Abstract: This resea rch aims to  examine the impact of Social Media Marketing, Brand
Authenticity and Personalization Capability on Digital Brand Loyalty of e -commerce
customers. The research was quantitative in nature employing survey technique to the
consumers of e -commerce platforms a s respondents. The sampling technique used is
purposive sampling. The data were collected by disseminating the online questionnaire
through Google Forms. Multiple linear regression was used for data analysis after  checking
the validity and relia bility of a ll study tools. The results of the study indicate that Social
Media Marketing, Brand Authenticity and Personalization Capability have a positive and
significant effect on Digital Brand Loyalty both partially and simultaneously. The value of
the coefficient of determination indicates that the three independent variables can explain
Digital Brand Loyalty. Of the three factors, Personalization Capability is the most significant
factor affecting digital brand loyalty. The study findings show that the  company’s ability to
build communication through social media, create an authentic brand, and provide a
personalized shopping experience are important strategies in increasing customer loyalty on
e-commerce platforms.

Keywords: Social Media Marketing, B rand Authenticity, Personalization Capability, Digital
Brand Loyalty

INTRODUCTION
Digital technology has changed the way consumers engage with different e -commerce
platforms. The use of the internet, mobile devices and t he easy access to a great number  of
markets has led to increased competition among digital service providers. In this context, the
companies not only focus on acquiring new customers, but also on retaining existing
customers, building long -term relationships. Digital Brand Loyalty is a s ign of succ essful
digital marketing strategy  (Utomo et al., 2024) . It is the customer’s commitment to continue

choosing, using and promoting a brand through different digital channels. Digital Brand
Loyalty constitutes a strategic asset, as loya l consumers  are inclin ed to repeat purchases,
provide positive evaluations and constitute a promotion channel through electronic word of
mouth. High digital loyalty is also useful for firms to lower the cost of client acquisition,
increase the customer lifetime value  and reinfo rce the competitive position in the digital
market (Banjarnahor et al., 2021).
Social Media Marketing is a digital marketing strategy that intends to influence the
Digital Brand Loyalty of e-commerce users (Setiadi et al., 2024). Social media marketing is a
marketing practice that  involves use of different social media platforms to enhance
communication, promote engagement and build stronger connections between organizations
and customers  (Judijanto et al., 2024) . Companies can use a wide rang e of intere sting and
relevant content to share product information on social media, build customer communities,
offer more responsive service and boost consumer engagement  (Hendra et al., 2025;
Sudarmaji et al. , 2024). Moreover, Brand Authenticity is corre lated with the Digital Brand
Loyalty of e -commerce users  (Hendra, 2017) . Brand authenticity is the consumer’s
perception of how authentic, integrated, transparent and consistent the brand is in what it says
it stands for and how it actually behaves  (Simanjuntak et al., 2023). In a digital world where
information and marketing surround us, customers are more discerning in choosing
businesses that are seen as honest, trustworthy and having a distinct identity. Authentic firms
are more successful in establishi ng emotiona l connections with the customers because
consumers believe that the firm understands them, and offers value that meets expectations
(Judijanto et al., 2024) . Another aspect affecting the Digital Brand Loyalty of e -commerce
customers is the Perso nalization Capability (Hendratni, 2024). Personalization capacity is a
company’s ability to use consumer data to provide a more relevant, customized and
personalized buying experience  (Suswanda et al., 2023) . The personalization is a key factor
for e -commerce organiz ations to be able to propose products more accurately, to provide
discounts more matched to consumer demands and to deliver a more pleasant user experience
from product search to purchase  (Rustiyana et al., 2025; Kartika et al., 2020) . The compa ny
provides customized experiences that leave customers feeling appreciated and valued for
their unique needs and preferences (Widuri et al., 2020; Astuti et al., 2015).
The purpose of this study is to analyze the influence of Social Media Marketing, Brand
Authenticity and Personalization Capability on Digital Brand Loyalty of e -commerce
customers. The research is meant in theory to contribute to the growth of literature within the
disciplines of digital marketing, consumer behavior and brand management esp ecially in
respect of aspects which affect brand loya lty in the digital environment. Furthermore, this
study is expected to provide a better understanding of how the long -term relationships are
built between companies and customers through social media mar keting stra tegies, brand
authenticity, and personalization capabilities. Practically, the results of this research will
probably be a consideration for e -commerce firms in developing more successful digital
marketing strategies through improving social med ia activiti es, creating an authentic brand
image and maximizing customization technologies based on consumer data.

METHOD
The research was conducted with a quantitative approach, using the survey technique.
The population of this study were customers who use e-commerce platforms and have made
purchases online. The sampling technique used is purposive sampling with the following
criteria, respondents aged at least 17 years, respondents who have used e -commerce
platforms for at least 1 year, respondents who have made purchase transactions through e -
commerce p latforms in the last 6 months, respondents who follow or have seen brand
marketing activities through social media, and respondents who have received product
recommendations or promotions according to user preferences. The number of respondents in

the study was 200. Data collection was carried out by distributing online questionnaires
through Google Forms to e-commerce users who met the criteria of the research. The features
used to quantify Social Media M arketing w ere entertainment, engagement, trendiness,
personalization and e -word-of-mouth. The variable Brand Authenticity was measured by the
characteristics of continuity, credibility, integrity and symbolism . The Personalization
Capability variable was operationalized through the dimensions of individualized product
suggestions, customized promotions, personalized communication and adaptable user
experience . Meanwhile the Digital Brand Loyalty variable was measured by the dimensions
of repurchase intent ion, preference for the brand, unwillingness to swit ch, desire to suggest
and commitment to continue using. All study indicators were scored on a five -point Likert
scale with 1 representing ‘strongly disagree’ and 5 representing ‘strongly agree’. Before
hypothesis testing, the research instrument was tested for validity and reliability so that each
statement item can assess the research construct properly and consistently. The validity test
was carried out by comparing the significance value of each indicat or with th e significance
level of 0.05 . The instrum ent is reliable with the minimum limit of 0.70 using Cronbach
Alpha. The hypotheses were tested using the multiple linear regression analysis. The partial
and simultaneous effect of Social Media Marketing,  Brand Authenticity and Personalization
Capability on the Digital Brand Loyalty of e -commerce users was determined with the
significance level of 5%.

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
Based on the results of data processing on respondents who are users of various e -
commerce platforms, the results obtained indicate t hat all research instruments meet the
standards of validity and reliability, thus they are eligible to be used in the next stage of
analysis. The results of the validity test show that all indicators on th e variabl e of Social
Media Marketing, Brand Authenticity, Personalization Capability and Digital Brand Loyalty
have a significance value of less than 0.05 so that all statement items are declared valid. The
results of the reliability tests also showed that  all vari ables had good level of internal
consistency. The result of multiple linear regression study showed that Social Media
Marketing, Brand Authenticity, and Personalization Capability simultaneously had a positive
and significant effect on Digital Bra nd Loyalty. The coefficient of determination was .542
which means that 54.2% of the variance in Digital Brand Loyalty can be explained by the
three independent variables in this research. Partially all independent variables have positive
and significant ef fect on D igital Brand Loyalty so that all research hypotheses are declared
accepted.
The results of the research show that social media marketing has a positive and
significant effect on digital brand loyalty of ecommerce users. The results of this study
indicate t hat marketing activities on social media that create interaction, offer interesting
content, follow trends and establish intensive communication with customers can increase
loyalty to a brand. Social media is not only a promotional tool but also a  two-way
communication channel that helps firms to create an emotional connection with their
customers. The quality of marketing activities implemented through social media has a higher
opportunity to trust the brand, make repeat purchases and recommend th e brand t o others.
Beyond boosting client participation, consistent social media engagement can help foster
excellent digital experiences that may result in lasting brand loyalty. Besides increasing
participation, social media marketing also increases the speed of dissemination of information
through elect ronic word of mouth based on the experience of other consumers. Brand
credibility can also be built in a much more general manner than one -way communication
from the firm via positive reviews and testimoni als, user -generated content and
recommendations from influencers. Customers may often be exposed to positive information,
and if they have responsive interactions with the firm, their impression of brand quality may

increase. These conditions push customer s to use the same platform, repeat their purchases
and even recommend brands to others on their own accord. In the highly competitive e -
commerce industry with relatively low switching costs, effective social media marketing
activities are an important tool  in maint aining customer loyalty. Therefore, e -commerce
enterprises should establish social media strategies based on value creation, two -way
communication, content consistency and enhancing emotional ties to enhance consumer
loyalty in a sustainable manner.
The results of this study showed that Brand Auth enticity has a positive and significant
effect on Digital Brand Loyalty of e -commerce consumers. The findings indicate that
customers’ perceptions of a brand’s authenticity, credibility, integrity and cons istency in the
digital environment are important determinants of customer loyalty. Consumers are likely to
remain loyal to brands that appear to be honest, transparent and able to deliver on the
promises they make via their digital communications channels.  The perc eption of brand
authenticity generates tru st which increases the confidence of the customers for future
purchases, and decreases the chance of switching to competing companies. Moreover,
authentic brands can create more powerful emotional connecti ons that make consumers stay
loyal not only to the product but also to the values represented by the brand. Therefore,
enhancing brand authenticity is an important way to increase customer loyalty on e -
commerce websites. Brand authenticity also creates an intellectual trust and emotional bond
between the business and consumers. Consumers tend to choose companies that they believe
reflect their own personality, lifestyle and personal beliefs. That emotional connection
translates into more customer loyalty, b ecause the buying decisions are no longer based on
price or promotions, but on the belief that the brand does have a trustworthy character. This
condition is highly relevant in e -commerce as buyers are not able to physically inspect the
goods prior to the transaction.
The results of the study show that the  most dominant variable that has an influence on
the Digital Brand Loyalty of e -commerce users is the Personalization Capability. This is
indicated by the highest regression coefficient and t -value than th e other i ndependent
variables. The results of this study suggest that the ability of a company to generate a
personalized experience is important in building consumer loyalty. Product
recommendations, personalized promotions based on user preferences, pers onalized
communication, and adaptive shopping exper iences can increase customer satisfaction and
comfort while interacting with e -commerce platforms. Customers feel more valued when
companies know individual needs and preferences. This state creates emotio nal bonds with
the company, increases the desire to make purchases repeatedly and induces consumers to
recommend the platform to other users. Therefore, e-commerce companies need to constantly
develop their data and artificial intelligence technology -based skills of customization to be
able to grow Digital  Brand Loyalty continuously . The dominant role of the customization
ability also shows that the quality of the experience obtained in the interaction process with
the platform is increasingly affecting co nsumer loyalty on digital platforms. People who get
the right product recommendations, relevant promotions, adaptive displays, and tailored
communications will like the firm more than those who get broad services. This private
experience adds value that co mpetitors can hardly replicate, increasing psycholo gical
switching costs even if clients can technically switch to a different platform.

CONCLUSIONS
The results of this study show that Social Media Marketing, Brand Authenticity and
Personalization Capability have a positive and significant impact on Digital Brand Loyalty of
e-commerce consumers, partially and simultaneously. The results also indicate that
Personalization Capability is the most dominant variable in influencing Digital Brand
Loyalty. The com pany can increase customer happ iness, emotional connection and repeat -

promotions that align with consumer preferences and adaptive shopping experiences. The
present stud y is limited by its cross -sectional approach, data relying on respondents’

improve social media marketing strategies, build up the authentic brand image, and optimize
the data -based personalization capabilities, which can increase sustainable customer loyalty
and enhance the company’s competitiveness.
