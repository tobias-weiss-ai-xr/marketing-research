Abstract
Perfume product purchases made by members of Generation Z and Millennials are investigated in this
study, along with the elements that influence these generations opinions of E -WOM, beauty vloggers,
social media marketing, and endorser congruence. We used a quantitative method by surveying 200
people from Generation Z and Millennials who had bought scent goods. . According to the findings,
there is a favorable relationship between purchase decisions and E -WOM, social media marketing,
beauty vloggers, and endorser congruence. Findings from this research stress the significance of
influencer marketing and digital marketing in molding customer views and motivating purchases. The
results give light on how perfume companies may enhance the efficacy of their digit al marketing
campaigns, especially in the realms of social media and influencer partnerships, to sway customer
decision-making and boost sales.
Keywords: E-Wom, Social Media Marketing, Beauty Vlogger, Endorse Congruence, Purchase
Decision

1. Introduction
Customers habits in making purchases have changed drastically due to the rise of digital
information and communication technologies. These days, most internet marketing campaigns
revolve around social networking (Saputra et al., 2023) , has made companies no longer fully
dependent on traditional promotional methods. Instead, companies utilize social media to
interact, promote products, and build relationships with customers (Pratama et al., 2025). This
shift indicates that digital marketing is no longer merely a complementary tool but has become
a crucial element in today’s business competition. In line with this, the increasing internet
penetration in Indonesia further strengthens the growth of t he digital ecosystem. There were
221.56 million internet users in 2024 and an estimated 229.43 million in 2025, according to
data from the Indonesian Internet Service Providers Association (APJII)  (Utari, 2025). These
internet users are predominantly from Generation Z  and Generation Y (Millennials), who are
known for their rapid adaptation to digital technology (Febriani et al., 2026) . These two
generations not only use the internet for entertainment and communication but also rely on it

as their primary source of information before making purchases  (Novita & Lina, 2024) . This
condition is particularly relevant for perfume products, which are sensory in nature and difficult

From identifying a need to gathering relevant information to weighing available options, the
purchasing choice is the culmination of a decision- making process that begins with issue
detection in the context of consumer behaviour  (Devi & Fadli, 2023) . In the digital era, the
process of information search and evaluation of alternatives has become increasingly complex
as consumers are exposed to multiple sources of information simultaneously. Social media
enables consumers to access reviews, promotional  materials, and recommendations from
public figures in a relatively short time (Saputra et al., 2023). Electronic word of mouth, or e-
WOM, is a significant channel for this kind of communication since it is more objective and
reliable than traditional forms of advertising (Abadi & Hawa, 2024). In addition, social media
marketing activities presented through visual content, storytelling, and interactive campaigns
contribute to shaping consumer perceptions and brand image of perfume products  (Rafania et
al., 2025). The influence of beauty vloggers is also increasingly significant , as they provide
detailed reviews of fragrance longevity and scent characteristics, thereby substituting for the
direct sensory experience consumers cannot obtain when shopping online. Furthermore,
endorser congruence, or the compatibility between the endorser and the perfume product, the
efficacy of advertising campaigns and customers faith in the brand are both impacted
(Grunwald et al., 2025).
Although previous studies have examined e -WOM, social media marketing, beauty vloggers,
and endorser congruence separately, research that integrates these four variables
simultaneously in relation to purchase decisions of perfume products among Generation Z and
Millennials remains limited. In addition, prior research findings show inconsistencies.
According to some research, the influence of celebrity endorsers on consumers purchasing
decisions is substantial and favorable  (Yusiara, 2022) . But other research shows that
endorsement congruence is not a big deal when it comes to consumers  final buying decisions
(Fauziridwan, 2024) , as seen in research on owner product endorsement and endorser
congruence in the Bittersweet By Najla brand. These variations in findings suggest that the
effectiveness of endorser-product congruence is situational and cannot be generalized across
all types of products (Lestari, 2025), particularly those that are subjective and involve sensory
perception such as perfume. Moreover, most previous studies have focused on general products
such as food or e -commerce, whereas perfume has unique characteristics due to its strong
dependence on sensory perception and personal experience  (Barbosa et al., 2021). Therefore,
the research gap lies in the inconsistency of findings, the limited integration of digital
marketing communication variables into a comprehensive model, and the scarcity of studies
involving both Generation Z and Millennials simultaneously as research subjects. This gap
forms the fundamental basis for conducting this study.
2. Literature Review
2.1 E-WOM
Digital media facilitate what is known as “ Electronic Word of Mouth”  (e-WOM), in which
users share their thoughts, feelings, and experiences regarding a product with one another
(Abadi & Hawa, 2024) . Because it  is so easy to get evaluations, suggestions, and
recommendations from other users through digital platforms, E-WOM also helps consumers a
lot while they are making a purchase choice (Saputra et al., 2023). Perfume items, in particular,

source to lessen ambiguity and perceived risk. Consistent with this, customers are more likely
to have a positive impression and buy perfume items if they have read positive evaluations and
suggestions (Rachmiani et al., 2024).
2.2 Social Media Marketing
In order to foster interaction, raise brand recognition, and forge stronger bonds between
businesses and their target audiences, a growing number of corporations are engaging in what
is known as social media marketing  (Pratama et al., 2025) . Businesses may reach a wider
audience and encourage customer participation through social media marketing. Research has
demonstrated that advertisements for perfumes and other personal care items posted on social
media and other websites influence consumers to make a purchase (Kusumah & Aprillia, 2023).
Furthermore, for Generation Z and Millennials, continuous exposure to promotional content
can influence their perceptions and encourage purchase decisions  (Fernandes et al., 2025) ,
including perfume products that require visual explanation and narrative descriptions of
fragrance concepts.
2.3 Beauty Vlogger
A beauty vlogger is a video content creator, either an individual or a group, who shares reviews,
experiences, and recommendations regarding beauty and personal care products they use
(Kanya & Adilla, 2022) . In the context of perfume marketing, beauty vloggers play an
important role as they often provide in-depth explanations about fragrance characteristics, scent
longevity, and the suitability of perfume usage in various situations. In addition, beauty
vloggers continuously build communication with their followers, creating a sense of closeness
that makes them more approachable and relatable compared to traditional celebrity endorsers
(Garg & Bakshi, 2024) . Since perfumes cannot be directly tested when shopping online,
reviews from beauty vloggers often serve as a substitute for consumers’ real experiences. New
research shows that consumers are swayed by favorable and statistically significant product
evaluations and the trustworthiness of beauty vloggers when making purchases  (Palilingan et
al., 2021), indicating that both theoretically and empirically, beauty vloggers have the potential
to positively influence perfume purchase decisions.
2.4 Endorse Congruence
Endorser congruence refers to the degree of compatibility between the characteristics of an
endorser and the product being promoted, including aspects such as image, personality, and
lifestyle (Apriyana & Yuliana, 2023 ). Theoretically, a high level of congruence between the
endorser and the product can enhance the effectiveness of promotional messages, as consumers
perceive the endorser as relevant and authentic in representing the product  (Maisyaroh, Umi,
2024). This effectiveness becomes more optimal when there is a strong alignment between the
attributes of the endorser and those of the product being promoted. In the context of perfume,
the compatibility between the endorser’s character and the fragrance concept or brand image
can strengthen consumers’ positive perceptions. To back up this claim, new research has
demonstrated that consumers' purchasing decisions and brand perceptions are positively and
significantly impacted by the degree to which celebrity endorsers' personalities align with those
of the items they promote (Yusiara, 2022). Therefore, in the context of perfume, the alignment
between the endorser’s character and the fragrance concept or brand image can enhance
positive consumer perceptions and ultimately encourage purchase decisions.

3.1 Research Method
A quantitative technique is utilized in this investigation. In order to gather data, researchers
used a structured online questionnaire that was based on indications and components taken
from well-established theoretical frameworks. The variables assessed include how E -WOM
(X1), Social Media Marketing (X2), Beauty Vlogger (X3), a lso Endorse Congruence (X4)
influence consumer purchasing decisions. To help in quantifying client impressions and
actions, each question is assessed using a five -point Likert scale. This research made use of a
non-probability sampling strategy based on a purposive sampling method. Through this
approach, the researcher determines specific criteria that must be met by the selected
respondents, namely individuals who belong to Generation Z and Millennials, actively use
social media, and have purchased perfume products online. Since t he population size is not
known with certainty, the sample size determination refers to the method proposed (Hair et al.,
2019). According to this technique, the number of indicators should be multiplied by five to
ten before selecting a sample, with the sample size being modified to match the number of
indicators in the questionnaire (Alawiah, 2023). According to this estimate, for this survey, a
minimum of 180 respondents, made up of Millennials and Generation Z, who have bought
perfume online, are needed. However, this study employs a total of 200 respondents, which is
regarded appropriate for SEM -PLS analysis and improves the analysis's robustness and
reliability. Primary facts, culled straight from the source, provide the backbone of our
investigation. An online survey was created and disseminated using Google Forms to gather
data. The measurement instrument uses a Likert scale, which is commonly applied to measure
respondents’ attitudes, opinions, and perceptions (Kurniati et al.  2023). There is a five- point
scale from "strongly agree" to "strongly disagree" for each statement. T his study employs
Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) based on Partial Least Squares (PLS) for data analysis.

This study's results show that there is no statistically significant relationship between the
variables of endorsement congruence, social media marketing, beauty vloggers, E-WOM. This
indicates that, according to this research, digital marketing communicat ion variables do not
have a direct impact on customers' decisions to buy scent items. A more significant influence
on purchase decisions can come from other sources, such individual tastes, sensory perception,
or past experiences.
5. Conclusion
The SEM-PLS investigation found that among Millennials and Generation Z, the purchase
decisions of perfume items are unaffected by E -WOM, social media marketing, beauty
vloggers, and endorser congruence. None of the factors had a statistically significant impact on

consumers are more likely to rely on personal preferences, sensory perception, and prior
