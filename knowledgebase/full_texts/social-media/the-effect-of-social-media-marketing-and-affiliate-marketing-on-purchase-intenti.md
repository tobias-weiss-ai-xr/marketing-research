Abstract
The growing interest in healthy lifestyles and fitness a ctivities has contributed to the
expansion of the fitness supplement industry in Indonesia. Despite its strong market position,
Evolene experienced a decline in its Top Brand Index ranking in 2026, highlighting the need
to evaluate the effectiveness of its  digital marketing strategies. This study examines the
effects of Social Media Marketing and Affiliate Marketing on consumers' Purchase Intention
toward Evolene products on TikTok Shop in Surabaya. A quantitative approach was
employed using PLS -SEM with da ta collected from 120 respondents through purposive
sampling. The findings reveal that both Social Media Marketing and Affiliate Marketing
positively and significantly influence Purchase Intention, with Affiliate Marketing
demonstrating a stronger effect. Relevance of Content emerged as the strongest indicator of
Affiliate Marketing, while Trendiness was the strongest indicator of Social Media Marketing.
These findings can be interpreted through the complementary perspectives of TPB and SCT,
providing a bro ader theoretical understanding of Purchase Intention in the context of social
commerce. From a practical perspective, the findings offer insights for optimizing social
media content and affiliate marketing strategies to enhance consumers' Purchase Intention.

Keywords: Social Media Marketing, Affiliate Marketing, Purchase Intention, Evolene,
TikTok Shop.

LITERATURE REVIEW
Theory of Planned Behavior
According to Ajzen (2020) the
Theory of Planned Behavior (TPB) pro -
poses that an individual's behavior is
primarily determined by behavioral inten -
tion, which is determined by three key
constructs: attitude, subjective norm, and
perceived behavioral control. A stronger
behavioral intention increases the
probability that the behavior will be
performed. Othman et al. (2025) suggested
that digital marketing activities, including
interactive content, social recommenda -
tions, and user -friendly platform features,
may influence consumers' attitudes,
subjective norms, and perceived behavioral
control, which subsequentl y shape their
behavioral intention.  Together, these
factors strengthen consumers' purchase
intention and ultimately influence
purchasing behavior.

Social Cognitive Theory
According to Bandura (1999), Social
Cognitive Theory (SCT) explains that
human thoughts, emotions, and behaviors
are shaped by various psychosocial deter -
minants that interact through symbolic
communication. The theory emphasizes
observational learning, in which indi vi-
duals acquire knowledge and behavioral
patterns by observing and imitating others.
This learning process is supported by
cognitive mechanisms such as attention,
retention, and motivation. SCT further
proposes the concept of triadic reciprocal
causation, which states that behavior,
personal cognitive factors, and environ -
mental influences continuously interact
and influence one another. In addition,
self-regulation, involving self -monitoring,
self-evaluation, and affective reactions,
serves as a key mecha nism through which
individuals regulate their behavior.
Communication influences behavior both
directly by providing information and
motivation, and indirectly by connecting
individuals with social networks that offer
support and reinforcement for behavior al

Purchase Intention
Kertiriasih (2023) explained that pur -

tendency to purchase a particular brand or

by prior experience, product information,
consumer reviews, and a company's
marketing strategies. According to Ong &
Firdausy (2023) purchase intention is a
stage in the consumer decision -making

recognize a need for a product or service.
According to Widhiasih et al. ( 2025)
purchase intention represents consumers'
tendency to buy products or services,
which is shaped by their expectations and
perceptions and serves as an important
indicator of future purchasing behavior.
Mutmainnah et al. (2025)  identified four
indicators of purchase intention: transac -
tional intention, referential intention,
preferential intention, and exploratory
intention.

Data Analysis
The collected data were analyzed using
Partial Least Squares –Structural Equation
Modeling (PLS -SEM) with SmartPLS 4,
which is suitable for analyzing complex
relationships among latent constructs and
can be applied with relatively small sample
sizes. The variables in this study were
modeled as reflective latent constructs
measured through multiple indicators. The
PLS-SEM analysis consisted of two main
components: the outer model a nd the inner
model. The outer model was evaluated
using outer loadings, Average Variance
Extracted (AVE), and Composite Reliabi -
lity to assess the validity and reliability of
the measurement instruments. The inner
model was assessed based on path coeffi -
cients, the coefficient of determination
(R²), and hypothesis testing using t -
statistics and p-values (Hair et al., 2019).

Discussion
The Effect of Social Media Marketing
on Purchase Intention
Based on the hypothesis testing resul ts,
Social Media Marketing has a positive and
significant effect on consumers' Purchase
Intention toward Evolene products on
TikTok Shop in Surabaya, thereby support-
ing the first hypothesis. This finding indi -
cates that the more effectively Evolene
delivers engaging, interactive, trend -
oriented, and consumer -oriented content
through its official TikTok account, the
stronger consumers' Purchase Intention
becomes. Among the four indicators,
Trendiness demonstrated the greatest
contribution, indicating that con sumers
place considerable importance on content
that reflects current trends and remains
relevant to their interests. This finding sug-
gests that continuously adapting marketing

videos, relevant and up -to-date content is

These findings can be interpreted

According to Bandura (1999), consumers

study, this perspective provides a broader
conceptual explanation of how digital
marketing may encourage consumers'
Purchase Intention.  The dominance of the

relevant and up -to-date content attracts
greater consumer attention, facilitates
observational learning, and strengthens
Purchase Intention.

influential Affiliate Marketing indicator,
suggesting that consumers  are more likely
to develop Purchase Intention when
affiliate content is closely aligned with

Conclusion
This study examined the effects of
Social Media Marketing and Affiliate
Marketing on consumers' Purch ase Inten -
tion toward Evolene products on TikTok
Shop in Surabaya. The findings demon -
strate that both Social Media Marketing
and Affiliate Marketing positively and
significantly influence consumers' Pur -
chase Intention, with Affiliate Marketing
exerting a st ronger effect. Among the
Social Media Marketing aspects,
Trendiness emerged as the strongest
contributor, indicating that relevant and
up-to-date content plays an important role
in attracting consumers and strengthening
their Purchase Intention. Meanwhile,
Relevance of Content was identified as the
strongest aspect of Affiliate Marketing,
suggesting that consumers place greater
value on affiliate content that aligns with
their needs, interests, and fitness goals.
From a theoretical perspective, the
findings can be interpreted through the
complementary perspectives of the Theory
of Planned Behavior (TPB) and Social
Cognitive Theory (SCT). Social Media
Marketing and Affiliate Marketing faci -
litate consumers' observational learning
and cognitive evaluations, whi ch may
conceptually influence favorable attitudes,
subjective norms, perceived behavioral
control, and ultimately stronger Purchase
Intention toward fitness supplement
products.
The respondent profile indicates that
Evolene consumers are predominantly
young adult males with an interest in
fitness and healthy lifestyles. In addition,
the predominance of respondents from
West Surabaya suggests that accessibility,
lifestyle-oriented urban development, and
the availability of fitness -supportive envi -
ronments may  contribute to greater
consumer interest in fitness supplement
products.

Limitations
This study has several limitations that
should be considered when interpreting the
findings. First, the respondents were
predominantly male and aged 18 –25 years,
which may limit the generalizability of the
results to female consumers and other age
groups. Second, although respondents were
recruited from all administrative regions of
Surabaya, the sample was dominated by
residents of West Surabaya. Therefore, the
findings may primarily reflect the
characteristics and purchase intentions of
consumers living in areas with strong
lifestyle and fitness development. Finally,
this study focused exclusively on the
Evolene brand and the TikTok Shop
platform. Consequently, the fi ndings may
not be directly applicable to other fitness
supplement brands, digital marketing
platforms, or geographical areas.

Recommendations
Based on the findings of this study,
Evolene is recommended to further
optimize its digital marketing strategy on
TikTok Shop by consistently creating
engaging, informative, and trend -oriented
content through its official TikTok
account. As Trendiness was identified as
the strongest Social Media Marketing
indicator, the company should conti -
nuously adapt its content t o current trends
while maintaining relevance to consumers'
interests. In addition, Evolene should
strengthen its affiliate marketing strategy
by collaborating with credible affiliates
who can produce authentic, relevant, and
easy-to-understand content, as Relevance
of Content was found to be the most
influential Affiliate Marketing indicator in
increasing consumers' purchase intention.
