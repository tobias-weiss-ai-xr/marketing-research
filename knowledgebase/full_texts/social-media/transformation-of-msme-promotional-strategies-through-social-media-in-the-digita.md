ABSTRACT
The rapid growth of digital technology has changed the way micro, small, and medium
enterprises (MSMEs) promote their products and communicate with customers. Social media,
which was initially used mainly for communication, has now become an important marketing
platform that allows MSMEs to build brand awareness, interact with customers, reach wider
markets, and support business growth. This study aims to examine how promotional
strategies among MSMEs have changed through the use of social media and to iden tify the
factors that support successful digital marketing, particularly among MSMEs in Bekasi,
Indonesia. This study uses a qualitative descriptive approach based on a structured review of
national and international journal articles indexed in SINTA, Scopus, and Web of Science.
The analysis shows that the transformation of MSME promotion generally develops
gradually, beginning with conventional promotion, followed by a basic digital presence,
content-based promotion, interactive social media marketing, and  more integrated digital
promotion. The success of this transformation is influenced by digital literacy, technological
readiness, organizational capability, innovation, customer engagement, and market
competition. Social media also gives MSMEs the opportunity to build closer relationships
with customers and encourage electronic word of mouth. For MSMEs in Bekasi, social media
provides a practical opportunity to expand their market and strengthen their competitive
position. However, meaningful digital trans formation requires more than simply having
social media accounts. MSMEs need consistent, relevant, interactive, and measurable
promotional strategies that are closely connected to customer needs and business objectives.
Keywords: digital transformation, social media marketing, MSMEs, promotional strategy,
digital marketing, Bekasi

INTRODUCTION
Micro, small, and medium enterprises have long played an important role in supporting
economic activity, creating employment, and encouraging entrepreneurship. In many local
communities, MSMEs are also closely connected to everyday consumer needs because they
operate in areas such as food and beverages, fashion, services, handicrafts, and retail.

However, the business environment in which MSMEs operate has changed significantly as
digital technology has become more widely used.
In the past, many MSMEs relied heavily on conventional promotional methods such as
brochures, banners, exhibitions, direct selling, and word of mouth. These methods remain
useful in certain situations, but consumer behavior has changed. People now spend more time
searching for information, comparing products, reading reviews, and communicating with
sellers through digital platforms. As a result, businesses are increasingly expected to maintain
an online presence and communicate with customers in ways that are faster, more interactive,
and more accessible.
Social media has become one of the most practical digital marketing tools for MSMEs.
Platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and WhatsApp Business make it possible for
businesses to introduce products, share promotions, respond to customer questions, receive
feedback, and support transactions without requiring a large promotional budget. For small
businesses that may not have access to expensive advertising channels, this creates a
significant opportunity.
Even so, digital transformation should not be understood simply as moving promotional
activities from offline media to online platforms. It involves broader changes in the way
businesses communicate, manage customer relationships, present their brands, and respond to
market information. Priyono et al. (2020) explain that SMEs experience digital
transformation in different ways depending on their level of digital maturity, available
resources, and organizational readiness. Some businesses are able to adopt digital tools across
several functions, while others begin by focusing only on marketing and sales.
This difference is important because having a social media account does not automatically
mean that a business has successfully transformed its promotional strategy. Some MSMEs
use social media only as an online catalog. They upload product pictures, prices, and contact
details but do not actively interact with customers or evaluate whether their content produces
any meaningful business results. In such cases, the business may already be present online,
but its promotional approach is still relatively traditional.
Social media becomes more valuable when it is used strategically. Unlike conventional
promotion, which is generally one -directional, social media allows customers to respond
directly to businesses. They can leave comments, ask questions, share posts, write reviews,
and recommend products to other people. This interaction changes the role of consumers.
They are no longer simply the target of promotional messages but can also participate in
creating and distributing information about a business.
Several studies have shown that this interactive environment can support MSME
performance. Chatterjee and Kar (2020) found that social media marketing can strengthen
customer relationships and contribute to SME performance. Ainin et al. (2015) also showed
that the use of social media can provide both financial and non -financial benefits. These

Organization–Environment framework explains that digital adoption is influenced by
technological factors, organizational capability, and external market conditions. Qalati et al.
(2021) demonstrate that these factors influence social media adoption and SME performance.

but also on managerial capability, human resources, customer expectations, and competitive
pressure.
Bekasi offers an interesting context for examining these issues. As part of a large
metropolitan area, MSMEs in Bekasi operate in a highly competitive environment and are
closely connected to markets in Jakarta and surrounding regions. Through social media, local
businesses can reach customers outside their immediate neighborhoods, but at the same time
they also face competition from businesses located in other cities.
For this reason, MSMEs in Bekasi need to think about social media as more than a place to
promote products. They need to use it as a tool for building relationships, strengthening brand
identity, understanding customers, and supporting sales. Therefore, this study examines how
MSME promotional strategies are transforming through social media in the digital era, with
particular attention to the stages of digital transformation, factors influencing social media
adoption, customer engagement, and the strategic implications for MSMEs in Bekasi.

1. Digital Transformation in MSMEs

Digital transformation refers to changes in business activities that occur when digital
technology becomes part of the way an organization operates. For MSMEs, these changes
can affect marketing, communication, customer service, sales, payment systems, and even the
overall business model.
Priyono et al. (2020) explain that digital transformation does not take place in the same way
for every SME. Some businesses are able to adopt digital technology more quickly because
they have stronger resources and better digital readiness. Others may only begin by using
technology for certain activities, particularly marketing and sales.
This gradual process is especially relevant for MSMEs because small businesses often face
limitations in financial resources, human resources, and technical knowledge. For this reason,
digital transformation should be seen as an ongoing process rather than a single decision to
use a particular technology.
Su et al. (2023) also argue that digital marketing adoption develops over time as businesses
respond to market pressure and improve their organizational readiness. In practice, this means
that MSMEs may begin with simple digital activities and gradually adopt more sophisticated
approaches as they gain experience.
2. Social Media Adoption
Social media adoption refers to the process through which businesses begin to use social
platforms as part of their regular activities. This process is often explained through the
Technology–Organization–Environment framework.
From a technological perspective, MSMEs are more likely to adopt social media when they
believe the platform is useful, easy to operate, and compatible with their business needs.
From an organizational perspective, adoption depends on managerial support, employee skills,
available resources, and the willingness to learn new technologies. Environmental factors
include customer demand, competitive pressure, and broader market trends.
Qalati et al. (2021) found that these three dimensions are closely related to social media
adoption and SME performance. Similar findings can be found in studies by Lia et al. (2025)
and Yuliani and Utama (2024), which focus on Indonesian SMEs.
These findings suggest that simply providing MSMEs with access to digital tools is not
enough. Businesses also need the confidence, skills, and organizational support required to
use those tools effectively.
3. Social Media Marketing
Social media marketing can be understood as the planned use of social media to support
promotion, branding, customer communication, and relationship building. Unlike
conventional advertising, social media allows businesses to communicate with customers in
real time and receive immediate responses.

advertising, branding, customer relations, and information searching. Tajvidi and Karami

For MSMEs, social media marketing can take many forms. Businesses may use short videos,
product photos, live selling, customer testimonials, educational content, influencer
collaboration, or direct links to marketplaces and payment channels.
The choice of content should depend on the platform and target audience. Content that works
well on TikTok may not necessarily be effective on WhatsApp Business or Facebook.
Therefore, MSMEs need to understand how each platform is used and what type of
communication customers expect.
4. Customer Engagement and MSME Performance
Customer engagement refers to the level of involvement that consumers have with a business
or brand. On social media, this involvement can be seen through comments, likes, shares,
messages, reviews, recommendations, and repeated interactions.
Negara and Nugroho (2025) found that social media marketing activities can increase
consumer engagement and repurchase intention among Indonesian SMEs. This is important
because customer engagement can strengthen the relationship between a business and its
customers.
Several studies also show that social media can support SME performance. Ainin et al. (2015),
Odoom et al. (2017), Chatterjee and Kar (2020), and Tatik and Setiawan (2025) all indicate
that effective social media use can contribute to stronger customer relationships, better
marketing communication, and improved business performance.
Taken together, these studies suggest that digital readiness can encourage social media
adoption, which then supports more effective social media marketing. In turn, stronger
marketing activities can increase customer engagement and contribute to better MSME
performance.
RESEARCH METHOD
This study uses a qualitative descriptive approach based on a structured literature review. The
analysis draws on national and international journal articles that discuss digital
transformation, social media marketing, MSME digital adoption, customer engagement,
innovation capability, and business performance. Twenty core references indexed in SINTA
1–4, Scopus, and Web of Science were selected as the main academic sources.
The selected literature was organized around five major themes, namely digital
transformation, social media adoption, promotional strategy, customer engagement, and
MSME performance. The analysis focused on identifying common patterns, differences in
previous findings, and relationships between digital adoption and marketing outcomes. These

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
1. Transformation of MSME Promotional Strategies
The literature shows that promotional transformation among MSMEs tends to occur gradually.
Many businesses begin with conventional methods such as banners, brochures, exhibitions,
direct selling, and personal recommendations. These methods usually depend on local
customers and relatively limited market reach.
The next stage begins when MSMEs create a basic online presence. At this point, businesses
use social media mainly to upload product pictures, prices, business locations, and contact
information. Social media works more like an online catalog than a full marketing platform.
As businesses become more familiar with digital media, their promotional strategy often
becomes more content-oriented. They start using short videos, customer testimonials, product
demonstrations, behind -the-scenes content, educational posts, and promotional campaigns.
This stage reflects a growing understanding that consumers are more likely to respond to
content that offers information, entertainment, or practical value.
The following stage involves more active interaction. Businesses begin responding to
comments, answering direct messages, holding live selling sessions, and using interactive
features. At this point, social media is no longer only a place to publish information but also a
space where customer relationships are developed.
A more advanced stage occurs when social media is connected with other digital systems.
Businesses may integrate their social media accounts with marketplaces, digital payment
systems, customer databases, online advertisements, and analytics tools. At this stage,
promotion becomes more organized and measurable.
This pattern is consistent with Priyono et al. (2020), who explain that SMEs experience
different levels of digital transformation depending on organizational maturity. Su et al. (2023)
also describe digital marketing adoption as an evolving process. For MSMEs in Bekasi, this
suggests that digital transformation should be viewed as a journey rather than a single step.
2. Social Media as a Strategic Promotional Platform
Social media has become much more than a place to display products. It now allows MSMEs
to combine advertising, branding, customer service, market communication, and sales
promotion on the same platform.

This is particularly useful for smaller businesses because they often have limited marketing
budgets. Instead of paying for several different promotional channels, an MSME can use one
or two social media platforms to reach customers, respond to questions, publish promotions,
and direct consumers toward purchase channels.
Tatik and Setiawan (2025) show that social media marketing is related to MSME

These findings indicate that social media can become a valuable business resource when it is
managed strategically. For MSMEs in Bekasi, this is especially important because
competition increasingly takes place online. A local business is no longer competing only
with nearby stores but also with sellers from Jakarta, Bandung, and other regions.
At the same time, this broader competition creates new opportunities. A small business in
Bekasi can reach customers outside its physical location as long as it is able to communicate
effectively and provide a convenient purchasing process.
3. The Importance of Content Quality
One of the biggest challenges in digital marketing is attracting attention. Consumers are
exposed to large amounts of content every day, which means that businesses need to
communicate in a way that feels relevant and interesting.
Negara and Nugroho (2025) emphasize several important dimensions of social media
marketing, including entertainment, customization, interaction, and trendiness. These
elements can influence consumer engagement and repurchase intention.
For MSMEs, this means that simply uploading product photos may no longer be enough. A
culinary business, for example, can show how the food is prepared, share customer reactions,
introduce the people behind the business, or create short videos about product
recommendations. A fashion business can use styling ideas, live shopping, or customer -
generated photos. A service business can provide educational content, practical tips, or
examples of customer experiences.
The main point is that good content should not always feel like direct advertising. It should
also provide value to the audience. When consumers find content useful, entertaining, or
trustworthy, they are more likely to interact with the business and remember the brand.
4. Customer Engagement
Social media has changed the relationship between businesses and consumers because
communication can happen directly and quickly. Customers can ask questions, leave
comments, provide feedback, and communicate with sellers without visiting a physical store.
This interaction is important because a large number of followers does not necessarily mean
that a business has a strong customer relationship. An MSME may have thousands of
followers but still receive very little meaningful engagement.

For MSMEs in Bekasi, simple actions such as replying to comments, answering messages

connected to the business.
5. Electronic Word of Mouth
Social media has also increased the importance of electronic word of mouth. Customers can
now share their experiences with a much wider audience through reviews, posts, stories,
comments, and recommendations.
For small businesses, this can be extremely valuable. Positive customer experiences can
generate promotion without requiring additional advertising expenditure. A satisfied customer
who shares a product review can influence friends, family members, and other social media
users.
Setiawan et al. (2022) show that digital promotion can encourage customer advocacy in
Indonesian businesses. This means that consumers can become part of the promotional
process when they are satisfied and willing to share their experiences.
For MSMEs in Bekasi, encouraging customers to provide reviews and share user -generated
content can strengthen digital credibility. Reposting positive customer experiences can also
help potential buyers feel more confident about trying a product.
6. Organizational Readiness
Even though social media is widely accessible, not every MSME is equally prepared to use it
effectively. Successful digital marketing requires more than a smartphone and an internet
connection.
Qalati et al. (2021) show that technological, organizational, and environmental factors
influence social media adoption. Similar conclusions can be found in the studies of Lia et al.
(2025) and Yuliani and Utama (2024).
For many MSMEs, one of the main challenges is limited human resources. A business owner
may have to manage production, finance, customer service, sales, and social media at the
same time. As a result, social media activities may become inconsistent or receive less
attention.
Digital transformation therefore requires better organizational capability. MSMEs need to
improve digital skills, divide responsibilities where possible, and develop simple promotional
plans that can realistically be maintained.

Training programs should not only teach MSMEs how to open social media accounts but also

7. Innovation and Digital Leadership
The digital environment changes quickly. Social media trends, algorithms, advertising tools,
and consumer preferences continue to evolve. For this reason, MSMEs need to be willing to
experiment and adapt.
Borah et al. (2022) found that innovation capability and digital leadership can strengthen the
relationship between social media use and SME performance. Fan et al. (2021) also
emphasize the importance of innovation capability.
For small businesses, digital leadership does not necessarily mean having advanced technical
expertise. It can simply mean that the business owner is willing to learn, try new approaches,
evaluate what works, and involve people with stronger digital skills when needed.
Innovation may also be reflected in the way businesses create content. MSMEs can
experiment with short videos, live selling, storytelling, promotional bundles, customer
testimonials, or collaborations with local influencers. By evaluating customer responses,
businesses can gradually identify which approaches are most effective.
8. Impact on MSME Performance
The literature generally shows that social media can contribute to MSME performance, but
the process is not always immediate. Social media can improve market reach, brand
awareness, customer relationships, customer engagement, and repurchase intention.
Tatik and Setiawan (2025) found that social media marketing contributes to MSME
performance in Indonesia. Tajvidi and Karami (2021) also show that marketing capability
strengthens the relationship between social media use and business performance.
In practice, social media does not automatically lead to higher sales. A consumer may first
see a post, become interested, interact with the business, develop trust, and only later make a
purchase. This means that businesses should evaluate not only final sales results but also the
earlier stages of customer response.
For example, MSMEs can observe whether their content generates more profile visits,
messages, reviews, marketplace clicks, or repeated interactions. These indicators can help
businesses understand whether their promotional activities are moving consumers closer to a
purchase decision.
Over time, business outcomes can also be evaluated through transaction growth, repeat
purchases, customer retention, and conversion rates. This allows MSMEs to make better
decisions about which social media activities should be continued or improved.

9. Strategic Implications for MSMEs in Bekasi
For MSMEs in Bekasi, social media marketing should be managed in a practical and focused
way. Businesses do not need to be active on every platform. It is more important to choose
platforms that match the behavior of their target customers.
Each social media activity should also have a clear purpose. Some content may be designed
to increase brand awareness, while other content may aim to encourage interaction or
generate direct sales. When the objective is clear, the business can evaluate whether the
content is effective.
Another important issue is the purchasing process. Social media may attract customer interest,
but the transaction must also be simple and convenient. MSMEs should connect promotional
content with accessible purchasing channels such as WhatsApp Business, marketplaces,
websites, or digital payment systems.
Consistency is equally important. Businesses that post very irregularly may find it difficult to
maintain customer attention. However, consistency does not mean posting every day. It
means maintaining a realistic schedule that fits the business's available time and resources.
Finally, MSME owners need to continue improving their digital skills. Social media
marketing changes quickly, so businesses must be willing to learn about content creation,
digital advertising, analytics, customer communication, and online sales.
10. Novelty of the Study
The novelty of this study lies in the way it brings together digital transformation, social media
adoption, promotional strategy, customer engagement, and MSME performance in one
integrated discussion focused on MSMEs in Bekasi.
Previous studies often examine these issues separately. Some focus on technology adoption,
while others emphasize customer engagement or business performance. This study views
promotional transformation as a gradual process that develops from conventional promotion
to basic digital presence, content -based promotion, interactive social media marketing, and
finally integrated digital promotion.
This perspective is useful because it distinguishes between simply using social media and
actually transforming promotional strategy. An MSME may have several active social media
accounts but may still be at an early stage of digital transformation if its content is
inconsistent, interaction is limited, and the business does not evaluate promotional
performance.
Therefore, the study emphasizes that meaningful digital transformation depends not only on
technology but also on organizational readiness, innovation, digital leadership, content quality,
and customer engagement.

Digital transformation has changed the way MSMEs promote their products and build
relationships with customers. Social media has become an important platform for branding,
communication, customer engagement, product promotion, electronic word of mouth, and
sales support.
The literature suggests that MSME promotional transformation generally develops gradually.
Businesses often move from conventional promotion to a basic digital presence, then to
content-based promotion, interactive communication, and more integrated digital marketing.
The success of this process depends on several factors, including digital literacy,
technological readiness, managerial capability, organizational resources, innovation, and
customer engagement. Therefore, digital transformation should not be measured simply by
whether an MSME has a social media account.
For MSMEs in Bekasi, social media offers a valuable opportunity to reach wider markets and
strengthen competitiveness. However, businesses need to use social media in a more
structured and strategic way. Relevant content, consistent communication, simple purchasing
processes, strong customer relationships, and regular evaluation are all important.
In the long term, MSMEs that are willing to learn and adapt are more likely to benefit from
digital transformation. Technology itself does not guarantee business success. What matters is
how effectively the business uses technology to understand customers, communicate value,
and support business growth.
RECOMMENDATIONS
MSMEs in Bekasi should develop social media strategies that match their target customers,
available resources, and business goals. Rather than trying to use every platform, businesses
should focus on the channels that are most relevant to their customers and maintain consistent
communication through those platforms.
Business owners should also pay greater attention to content quality and customer interaction.
Promotional content should not only focus on selling products but also on building trust,
providing useful information, and encouraging participation. Social media should also be
connected with simple and reliable transaction channels to reduce barriers during the
purchasing process.
Government institutions, universities, and business associations can support MSMEs by
providing practical and continuous digital marketing training. Training should include content
creation, branding, digital advertising, customer communication, analytics, marketplace
integration, and customer relationship management.
Future studies should collect primary data from MSME owners and managers in Bekasi to
test the relationships identified in this study. Quantitative methods such as Structural
Equation Modeling or Partial Least Squares could be used to examine how digital readiness,
social media marketing, customer engagement, and business performance are connected in
the local MSME context.
