Abstract
The rapid growth of social media has transformed marketing strategies
in the restaurant industry. Despite this, limited research has examined
the integration of social media marketing activities (SMMA), brand
experience, and purchase intention within the I ndonesian restaurant
context. This study aims to investigate the influence of SMMA on brand
equity and purchase intention, with brand experience serving as a
mediating variable. A quantitative survey was conducted among 310
Instagram followers of Medja Res taurant. Data were analyzed using
Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) with AMOS 22 to test the
relationships among SMMA, brand experience, brand equity, and
purchase intention. Findings indicate that SMMA positively impacts
both brand experience and brand e quity. Brand equity significantly
predicts purchase intention, while brand experience partially mediates
the relationship between SMMA and brand equity. The results suggest
that restaurant managers should develop interactive, personalized, and
emotionally engaging social media content to enhance customer
experience, strengthen brand equity, and increase purchase intentions.
This study is the first to integrate SMMA, brand experience, and
purchase intention into a single SEM framework in the Indonesian
restaurant industry, providing empirical evidence for the mediating role
of brand experience.

Keywords: Brand Equity, Brand Experience, Purchase Intention,
Restaurant Industry, Social Media Marketing Activities

INTRODUCTION
The rapid evolution of digital technology has significantly transformed communication and
marketing practices within businesses. Social media websites have enabled organizations to interact
directly with customers, increase customer engagement, and positio n themselves more effectively in
competitive environments. Social media websites have become crucial tools for marketing campaigns, as
they facilitate rapid information dissemination, personalized communication, and the development of
customer relationships. Kim & Ko (2012) argue that social media marketing campaigns improve customer
equity and customer attitudes towards products. Likewise, Godey et al. (2016) have concluded that social
media marketing campaigns considerably enhance brand awareness, brand image, and customer behavior.

One of the nations with many social media users in the world is Indonesia. The proliferation of
Internet connectivity and smartphone use has made Indonesian users adopt social media more and more.
According to Data Indonesia (2023), there are more than 190 million active social media users in
Indonesia, with Instagram among the most commonly used platforms for marketing communication. Such
a development has led many food and beverage organizations to use social media  extensively for
marketing. More and more restaurants use Instagram to promote their products and interact with their
customers. The restaurant business has become highly competitive, especially in urban regions like Bogor
City, where the number of restaurants continues to grow year after year. For this reason, restaurant firms
need to adopt new marketing approaches to enhance customer loyalty and purchase intentions.
Marketing activities performed on social media channels (SMMA) refer to marketing campaigns
conducted on social media to enhance interaction, information exchange, customization, fashion, and
electronic word of mouth among consumers (Yadav & Rahman, 2017). Research has shown that SMMA
contribute significantly to improving customer engagement, brand equity, and loyalty. Social media
marketing activities significantly impact brand equity and customer behavior in the airline sector,
according to Seo and Park  (2018). Furthermore, Koay et al. , (2020) noted that social media marketing
activities positively influence consumer -based brand equity. Additionally, according to Ismail (2017),
social media marketing activities play an important role in brand loyalty by enhancing consumer
perceptions and awareness of value.
Apart from social media marketing actions, another factor that can influence consumer behavior
is brand experience. According to Brakus et al. (2009), brand experience is defined as the sensory,
affective, behavioral, and intellectual reactions that consumers have through interactions with a particular
brand. When consumers have positive brand experiences, they develop emotional attachments to brands
and form strong connections. Brand experience significantly impacts customer satisfaction, trust, and
loyalty. Moreover, positive brand experiences correlate with higher purchase intentions, as consumers
often choose to buy again from brands that deliver memorable experiences.  While many papers have
examined social media marketing practices and brand equity, some research issues remain insufficiently
explored. Specifically, most of the existing literature has focused on the fashion, retail, tourism, and e -
commerce industries. Me anwhile, very few empirical studies have looked at the restaurant sector. In
addition, in most cases, prior literature did not examine the mediating role of brand experience in
assessing the impact of social media marketing on brand equity. Finally, little  attention has been paid to
how social media practices affect consumer behavior regarding local restaurants in Indonesia. Future
studies should cover this gap.
The Medja Restaurant in Bogor was chosen as the subject of this study because it uses social
media, such as Instagram, as its primary advertising channel and has thus established a strong online
presence among its consumers. Medja Restaurant effectively us es a variety of promotions and social
media content to capture consumers' attention and sustain engagement. Even amid increased competition
from other restaurants in Bogor, Medja Restaurant has gained popularity among consumers; thus, its
social media mark eting practices may provide an advantage in building brand equity and influencing
purchase intentions. The objective of the research, based on these assumptions, is to examine the impact
of social media marketing activities on brand equity and purchase intention through brand experience
among consumers of Medja Restaurant in Bogor. The research contributes to existing knowledge of
digital marketing and consumer behavior by integrating social media marketing activities, brand
experience, brand equity, and purchase intention in the context of the restaurant industry. In practice, the
research results may help restaurant management devise a successful social media marketing strategy for
their business.
In consideration of the above gaps, the following research questions will guide this study: (1) Do
social media marketing efforts significantly impact the brand experience of restaurant customers? (2) Do
social media marketing efforts and brand experience significantly impact brand equity? (3) Does brand
equity significantly impact purchase intention? Finally, (4) d oes brand experience mediate the
relationship between social media marketing efforts and brand equity?. The uniqueness of this study lies
in its ability to link social media marketing efforts, brand experience, brand equity, and purchase intention
within the Indonesian restaurant industry, especially among Instagram users of Medja Restaurant Bogor.
This study differs from others that have focused on the retail, tourism, and e -commerce industries by
focusing on digital and experiential branding in the food industry.
RESEARCH METHOD

The present research employed a quantitative methodology, with a survey as the research
technique. The quantitative research methodology was chosen because the present study sought to
investigate the cause -and-effect relationships among social media market ing, brand experience, brand
equity, and purchase intention using statistical methods. A cross -sectional research design was used to
collect data at a single point in time from the target population that met the research requirements.
The study was conducted at Medja Restaurant in Bogor, Indonesia. The data -gathering process
took place from June to July 2025. Medja Restaurant was chosen because it heavily uses Instagram for
marketing, has a large following, and enjoys strong customer engagement  on the platform. The study's
target population comprised consumers who followed Medja Restaurant's official Instagram page.
Purposive sampling was used to select participants, ensuring they had prior experience with Medja
Restaurant's social media posts and services. The i nclusion criteria for selection of research participants
were as follows: (1) following the official Instagram page of Medja Restaurant, (2) viewing content on
the Instagram page, and (3) visiting/ making purchases from Medja Restaurant.
The total number of respondents for this study was 310. The sample size determination was done
according to the recommendations of Hair et al. (2021). They recommend that, to carry out Structural
Equation Modeling (SEM), one needs a minimum of 5 to 10 time s as many indicators as are used in the
study. Since this study used 25 indicators, the minimum required sample size was 310 respondents. Thus,
the sample size chosen for this study is statistically adequate, as it provides sufficient power and stability
in the SEM -based estimates. This sample size was also adequate for SEM analysis, as it exceeded the
minimum recommended sample size of 200 for covariance-based SEM models.
The research procedure was conducted in several stages. First, the researcher identified research
problems related to social media marketing activities and consumer behavior in the restaurant industry.
Second, a literature review was conducted to develop the conceptual framework and research hypotheses.
Third, research instruments were prepared by adapting measurement indicators from previous validated
studies. Fourth, a pilot test was conducted to ensure the clarity and reliability of the questionnaire ite ms.
Fifth, the questionnaires were distributed online via Google Forms on Instagram and WhatsApp. Finally,
the collected data were analyzed using Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) with AMOS 22 software.
The research instrument used in this study was a structured questionnaire administered on a five-
point Likert scale, ranging from 1 = strongly disagree to 5 = strongly agree. The questionnaire consisted
of four main variables: social media marketing activi ties, brand experience, brand equity, and purchase
intention. The measurement indicators were adapted from previous studies to ensure validity and
reliability. Social media marketing activities were measured using dimensions developed by Yadav and
Rahman (2018), including interactivity, informativeness, personalization, trendiness, and word of mouth.
Brand experience was measured using indicators adapted from Brakus et al. (2009), including sensory,
affective, behavioral, and intellectual dimensions. Brand equity indicators were adapted from Aaker
(1996), including brand awareness, perceived quality, and brand loyalty. Purchase intention was measured
using indicators developed by Ferdinand (2014), including transactional, preferential, and exploratory
intentions. Primary data were collected directly from respondents through online questionnaires.
Secondary data were obtained from journal articles, books, statistical reports, and prior studies on digital
marketing and consumer behavior.
Validity testing was conducted using Confirmatory Factor Analysis (CFA). Indicators with factor
loading values greater than 0.50 were considered valid and retained for further analysis. Reliability testing
was conducted using the Construct Reliability (CR)  and Average Variance Extracted (AVE) indices. A
construct was considered reliable if the CR value exceeded 0.70 and the AVE value exceeded 0.50 (Hair
et al., 2021).  Data analysis was conducted using Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) with AMOS 22
software. SEM was selected because it enables simultaneous analysis of relationships among latent
variables and mediating effects within a comprehensive research model. The d ata analysis procedure
consisted of several stages, including descriptive statistical analysis, normality testing, validity and
reliability testing, goodness -of-fit evaluation, and hypothesis testing. The goodness -of-fit model was
evaluated using several f it indices, including Chi -square/df (CMIN/DF), Goodness of Fit Index (GFI),
Adjusted Goodness of Fit Index (AGFI), Comparative Fit Index (CFI), Tucker Lewis Index (TLI), and
Root Mean Square Error of Approximation (RMSEA). A model was considered acceptable  if it met the
recommended threshold values proposed in SEM literature. Hypothesis testing was conducted by
examining the critical ratio (CR) and probability (p -value) of each structural relationship. A hypothesis
was accepted if the CR value exceeded 1.96  and the p-value was less than 0.05. Mediation analysis was

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
Respondent Characteristics
Respondents in this study were 310 Medja Restaurant customers in Bogor who met the
predetermined criteria. Based on Table 1, the majority of respondents were aged 25 –29 years (64.2%),
followed by those aged 30–34 years (23.9%), indicating that most customers belonged to the young adult
and productive age group. By gender, female respondents slightly outnumbered male respondents in the
sample (51.9% vs. 48.1%).  Most respondents were unmarried (67.7%) and held a bachelor's degree
(71%), indicating they were generally well educated. Regarding employment status, government
employees (42.6%) and private employees (41.9%) represented the largest occupational groups.
Furthermore, most respondents were domiciled in Bogor (65.5%), followed by Jakarta (23.9%),
suggesting that Medja Restaurant primarily attracts consumers from urban and surrounding metropolitan
areas. In terms of economic characteristics, the majority of respondents reported monthly incomes ranging
from IDR 10,000,001 to IDR 20,000,000 (71%), while monthly expenditures were also predominantly in
this range (69.7%). These findings indicate that most Medja Restaurant customers belong to the middle -
to upper-income segment.
Regarding restaurant spending behavior, most respondents spent between IDR 100,001 and IDR
250,000 per individual visit (91.3%). For visits with two people, expenditures were primarily within the
IDR 250,001–500,000 range (86.8%). Meanwhile, family visits consisting of four to five people were
dominated by expenditures between IDR 750,001 and IDR 1,000,000 (52.3%). In group visits, the
majority of respondents reported spending between IDR 2,000,001 and IDR 3,000,000 (44.2%). Overall,
these findings indicate that Medja Restaurant is positioned as a mid -to-premium restaurant destination,
frequently visited for social gatherings, family dining, and group occasions. The respondent profile also
shows that Instagram users in the pro ductive, digitally active age group constitute the largest customer
segment for Medja Restaurant. This finding highlights the importance of social media marketing as a
strategic communication tool for attracting and engaging modern restaurant consumers.

The Effect of Social Media Marketing Activities on Brand Experience
The findings indicate that Social Media Marketing Activities (SMMA) significantly influence
Brand Experience among Medja Restaurant customers. This result suggests that consumers increasingly
perceive social media platforms not only as communication channels but also as experiential spaces that
shape emotional and sensory interactions with brands. Interactive Instagram content, visually appealing
food presentations, and engaging digital communication help create memorable customer experiences
before consumers physically visit the restaurant.
These findings support Schmitt's (1999) experiential marketing theory, which emphasizes that
customer experiences are created through sensory, affective, cognitive, and behavioral stimuli. In the
context of Medja Restaurant, Instagram content serves as a d igital experiential tool that stimulates
consumers' curiosity, emotional attachment, and engagement with the brand. The results are also
consistent with previous studies by Kaur and Singh (2023) and Chang et al. (2020), which found that
interactive, visual ly appealing social media marketing activities positively influence customer
experiences and engagement in the hospitality industry.
Furthermore, the findings indicate that restaurant consumers in the digital era seek immersive and
emotionally engaging experiences rather than merely functional product information. Consumers are
increasingly attracted to restaurants that successfully create aesthetic, entertaining, and socially shareable
experiences through social media platforms.

The Effect of Social Media Marketing Activities on Brand Equity
The results also demonstrate that Social Media Marketing Activities significantly influence Brand
Equity. This finding confirms that effective digital marketing strategies play an important role in
strengthening consumers’ perceptions regarding brand aware ness, perceived quality, and brand loyalty.
The restaurant industry has become increasingly competitive, particularly within urban culinary markets
where consumers are continuously exposed to large amounts of digital content. Consequently, social
media branding has become a critical strategic asset for restaurants seeking to differentiate themselves
from competitors. Medja Restaurant's consistent visual identity, attractive Instagram aesthetics, and
interactive communication strategies significantly strengthen brand memorability and customer trust.
This finding supports the consumer -based brand equity theory proposed by Aaker (1996) and
Keller (1993), which holds that strong brand equity is built on positive customer perceptions and
experiences. The results also align with studies by Koay et al. (202 0) and Laradi et al. (2024), which
found that social media marketing activities positively influence consumer -based brand equity by
strengthening emotional engagement and brand interaction.
The findings further suggest that consumers tend to perceive brands with strong social media
presence as more credible, modern, and trustworthy. Therefore, restaurant businesses that actively
manage their digital branding strategies are more likely to stre ngthen long-term customer relationships
and competitive advantage.

The Effect of Brand Experience on Brand Equity
This study further reveals that Brand Experience significantly influences Brand Equity. Positive
customer experiences strengthen emotional attachment, foster favorable perceptions, and drive long-term
consumer loyalty toward the restaurant brand. Consumers who experience enjoyable dining atmospheres,
visually appealing food presentation, and emotionally engaging interactions tend to develop stronger
perceptions regarding restaurant quality and credibility. In addition, memorable experiences encourage
consumers to associate the restaurant with positive emotions, which ultimately strengthens brand value.
The findings support previous studies by Brakus et al. (2009) and Alloza (2008), which
emphasize that brand experience serves as an important determinant of customer loyalty and brand
perception. In the restaurant context, experiential value becomes increa singly important, as consumers
seek not only food quality but also emotional and symbolic experiences that can be shared socially
through digital platforms. Furthermore, the findings indicate that customer experiences generated through
social media interac tions and physical dining experiences are interconnected. Consumers who form
positive impressions through Instagram content are more likely to perceive the restaurant favorably during
actual visits, thereby strengthening brand equity.

Mediation Effect of Brand Experience
The mediation analysis further demonstrates that Brand Experience plays an important mediating
role in the relationship between Social Media Marketing Activities and Brand Equity. This finding
suggests that consumers do not develop strong brand perceptions  solely from promotional exposure on
social media. Instead, social media activities first create positive customer experiences, which
subsequently strengthen brand equity.
This study demonstrates the significance of using experiential digital marketing approaches in
the restaurant industry. Customers appreciate immersive, emotionally charged experiences that let them
virtually experience the restaurant's atmosphere before ma king any purchases. Using Instagram content
focused on the restaurant atmosphere, food, customer interactions, and storytelling can foster stronger
sensory and emotional engagement, thereby enhancing brand value.
Furthermore, this research shows that the effectiveness of social media depends not only on
promotion but also on the quality of the experiences offered through digital marketing. Hence, restaurants
should focus more on developing marketing content that creates such experiences, rather than relying on
traditional marketing methods. This study proves that the combination of experiential marketing, social
media marketing, and engagement is key to successful digital marketing in the restaurant industry.
This research makes several significant contributions to the extant literature on digital marketing
and consumer behavior theories. First, this paper's results contribute to experiential marketing theory by
demonstrating that social media marketing actions  can help develop meaningful sensory, affective,
behavioral, and cognitive experiences for consumers in the restaurant industry. The study shows that
interactions on digital platforms like Instagram are not only promotional tools but also experiential ones
that influence consumers' perceptions and behavioral tendencies.  Second, this study contributes to
consumer-based brand equity theory by empirically demonstrating that brand experience is a key driver
of brand equity in the restaurant industry. It has been shown that consumers tend to have greater brand
awareness, quality perceptions, and loyalty when their experience with the brand is positive. Thirdly, this
study is significant for the development of digital engagement theory, in which interactive, personalized,
and visually compelling social media activities are an essential factor influencing consumer engagement
and purchase intention.
Additionally, this paper makes a valuable contribution to the literature on social media branding
by integrating Social Media Marketing Activities (SMMA), Brand Experience, Brand Equity, and
Purchase Intention into a single Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) framework. Different from existing
papers, which largely examine retail, tourism, or e -commerce contexts, this study presents practical
evidence from the Indonesian restaurant sector, specifically the Instagram followers of Medja Restaurant
Bogor. Finally, the current paper finds that restaurants' social media marketing efforts will be effective
only if they deliver an engaging customer experience  and build sustainable brand value through digital
engagement tactics.
The results of this study have several strategic implications for restaurant managers regarding
social media marketing activities that help build brand experience and purchase intentions. As Social
Media Marketing Activities (SMMA) play an important role i n shaping consumers' perceptions and
intentions, managers need to go beyond traditional promotional materials and adopt more innovative
digital marketing approaches.

should also consistently refine the interactive digital campaign by incorpor ating quizzes, polls, live

frequency with the brand. In general, the results show that for restaurants to have a successful marketing
strategy in the digital age, there must be informative, immersive, and emotional consumer experiences on
social media.

CONCLUSION
This study demonstrates that social media marketing activities positively influence brand
experience and brand equity, which in turn significantly affect purchase intention among restaurant
customers. Brand experience also partially mediates the relationsh ip between SMMA and brand equity,
highlighting the importance of creating engaging, interactive, and personalized social media content to
enhance customer perceptions and behavioral intentions. The findings contribute to digital marketing and
consumer behavior literature by confirming the mediating role of brand experience in strengthening the
effect of social media marketing on brand equity and purchase intention. Practically, restaurant managers
can apply these insights to design more effective social med ia strategies that improve customer
engagement, build brand value, and increase repeat purchase intentions. Future research should expand
the scope to include other social media platforms, diverse restaurant types, and additional variables such
as trust, customer satisfaction, and electronic word-of-mouth to further validate and extend the proposed
model.
