The rapid growth of music festivals in Indonesia has transformed live events into
experience-based industries where visitor loyalty becomes essential for long -term
sustainability. Prambanan Jazz Festival, as a cultural music festival held at the
Prambanan Temple complex, faces the challenge of maintaining visitor loyalty
beyond temporary attraction from performers and social media trends. Experiential
marketing, event quality, and social media engagement are considered important
factors that shape visitor s atisfaction and loyalty.  This study aims to examine the
effects of experiential marketing, event quality, and social media engagement on
visitor loyalty at Prambanan Jazz Festival, with visitor satisfaction as a mediating
variable. This research employed a quantitative approach with an explanatory
design. Data were collected through online questionnaires from 180 visitors who
had attended Prambanan Jazz Festival at least twice during 2024 –2025. The data
were analyzed using Partial Le ast Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS -
SEM) with SmartPLS 4.  The findings indicate that experiential marketing, event
quality, and social media engagement significantly and positively affect visitor
loyalty and satisfaction. Visitor satisfaction also significantly influences loyalty
and partially mediates the relati onships between the three independent variables
and visitor loyalty. Social media engagement showed the strongest direct effect on
loyalty, while event quality had the strongest effect on satisfaction.  Visitor loyalty
at Prambanan Jazz Festival is shaped by experiential, operational, and digital
factors. Strengthening event quality, creating memorable experiences, and
enhancing social media interaction are important strategies for sustaining visitor
satisfaction and loyalty.

INTRODUCTION
Indonesia's tourism and creative-economy sectors have grown rapidly in recent years, with
music festivals emerging as a significant part of the experience industry that delivers economic,
social, and cultural value rather than serving merely as entertainme nt. As live-experience events
multiply, competition among organizers has intensified, requiring them to deliver memorable

American Journal of Economic and Management Business                       1555
experiences and consistent quality to remain the audience's preferred choice  (D. A. Baker &
Crompton, 2000; J. Baker et al., 1994).
Prambanan Jazz Festival, held at the Prambanan Temple complex in Yogyakarta, has
become one of Indonesia's leading music festivals by combining music with cultural heritage.
Attendance rose sharply from around 49,000 visitors in 2024 to about 76,000 in 2025, yet growing
attendance does not necessarily reflect genuine loyalty, since many visitors may attend because of
celebrity line-ups, social-media trends, or viral marketing rather than sustained attachment to the
event. Visitor loyalty, reflected in repeat attendance and positive recommendation, is therefore a
critical asset for ensuring an event's long-term sustainability.
Prior research points to experiential marketing, event quality, and social media engagement
as key factors shaping visitor satisfaction and loyalty. Experiential marketing (Schmitt, 1999)
emphasizes creating experiences across sensory, emotional, cognitive , behavioral, and social
dimensions, and studies such as (Carmo et al., 2022) , (Bramantoko & Maridjo, 2024) , and
(Genoveva & Fauziah, 2025) have found that positive experiences strengthen satisfaction and, in
turn, loyalty. Event quality, covering program quality, performer quality, facilities, comfort, safety,
accessibility, and service, has similarly been linked to visitor perception and re visit intention
(Armbrecht, 2021; Çiki et al., 2024; Jeong & Kim, 2020; Nguyen et al., 2025) . Social media
engagement, reflecting visitor interaction with event-related content and information, has also been
associated with tourist satisfaction and destination attachment (Ambarawati et al., 2024;
Ramsoondar et al., 2025) , while satisfaction itself, understood as the evaluation of experience
against prior expectations (Oliver, 1980), has been shown to translate positive experience and
quality perceptions into loyal behavior (Genoveva & Fauziah, 2025).
However, the relationships among these variables are not always direct, since visitors first
evaluate their experience, the quality of the event, and their social media interactions before
satisfaction is formed and subsequently shapes loyalty, positioning  satisfaction as a potential
mediating variable. Prior findings also remain inconsistent: several studies report that experiential
marketing has no significant direct effect on loyalty or satisfaction, with its influence occurring
instead through brand image or satisfaction as a mediator (Chen & Wu, 2022; Adha et al., 2023;
Hasan et al., 2024; Rahmania & Wahyono, 2022), while findings on event quality and social media
engagement also vary depending on the outcome examined, with some studies even reporting
negative effects on certain outcomes (Akkaya et al., 2024; Zhou & Yu, 2022). In addition, most
existing research on experiential marketing and event quality has focused on retail, hospitality,
sports events, and general tourism destinations, leaving the spe cific context of music festivals
comparatively underexplored.
Building on this empirical and contextual gap, this study offers novelty by testing the effects
of experiential marketing and event quality on visitor loyalty with satisfaction as a mediating
variable, while integrating experiential marketing, event qualit y, and social media engagement
within a single comprehensive model set in the context of Prambanan Jazz Festival, a leading
Indonesian music festival combining entertainment, culture, and tourism. Accordingly, this study
is titled "The Effect of Experienti al Marketing, Event Quality, and Social Media Engagement on

American Journal of Economic and Management Business                       1556
Visitor Loyalty at Prambanan Jazz Festival through Satisfaction as a Mediating Variable," and is
expected to contribute both theoretically to marketing and event management scholarship and
practically to strategies for enhancing sustained visitor satisfaction and loyalty.
Based on the background above, this study examines whether experiential marketing, event
quality, and social media engagement each affect visitor loyalty at Prambanan Jazz Festival,
whether each of these three factors affects visitor satisfaction, whether satisfaction affects visitor
loyalty, and whether satisfaction mediates the effect of experiential marketing, event quality, and
social media engagement on visitor loyalty.
In line with the problem statement, this study aims to analyze the effects of experiential
marketing, event quality, and social media engagement on visitor loyalty at Prambanan Jazz
Festival; to analyze the effects of these same three factors on visitor sa tisfaction; to analyze the
effect of satisfaction on visitor loyalty; and to analyze the mediating role of satisfaction in the
relationships between experiential marketing, event quality, and social media engagement,
respectively, and visitor loyalty.
Theoretically, this study is expected to contribute to the development of marketing
scholarship, particularly regarding experiential marketing, event quality, social media
engagement, satisfaction, and visitor loyalty within the context of music festivals, and to serve as
a reference for future research examining the relationships among marketing factors, satisfaction,
and visitor loyalty. Practically, this study is expected to provide input for Prambanan Jazz Festival
organizers in evaluating and enhancing  visitor experience, event quality, and social media
engagement, thereby helping to increase visitor satisfaction and, in turn, encourage loyalty through
repeat-visit intention and willingness to recommend the festival to others.

METHOD
Research Design
This study employs a quantitative approach with an explanatory research design, aimed at
objectively and measurably testing the relationships among experiential marketing, event quality,
social media engagement, satisfaction, and visitor loyalty at Prambanan Jazz Festival, in line with
(Creswell, 2014) view that quantitative research tests theory by measuring variable relationships
through research instruments and statistical analysis. Data were collected through a survey method,
distributing questionnaires to visitors of Prambanan Jazz Festival to captu re their perceptions of
the experience obtained (experiential marketing), event quality, social media engagement,
satisfaction, and loyalty toward the event, in accordance with Malhotra's (2010) view of surveys
as an efficient technique for gathering data on consumer perceptions and attitudes from large
numbers of respondents.
The unit of analysis is the individual visitor who has attended Prambanan Jazz Festival,
chosen because the research variables are subjective and personal in nature, allowing the study to
capture the diversity of visitor experiences and perceptions that sh ape the relationships among
variables. Data were analyzed using Partial Least Squares -Structural Equation Modeling (PLS -
SEM), selected for its ability to analyze complex models with multiple latent variables without

American Journal of Economic and Management Business                       1557
requiring strict normal -distribution assumptions, and for its suitability for predictive, theory -
development research involving simultaneous testing of independent, mediating, and dependent
variables (J. Hair, 2019; J. F. J. Hair, 2022).

Research Object and Timeframe
The object of this study is Prambanan Jazz Festival, an annual music event held at the
Prambanan Temple complex in Yogyakarta known for its cultural appeal and distinctive
experience. Rather than being conducted on -site, the research focuses on individuals  who have
previously attended the festival, enabling data collection through an online questionnaire based on
respondents' actual past experience of experiential marketing, event quality, social media
engagement, satisfaction, and loyalty. The research was  planned to take place from April to June
2026, covering questionnaire preparation and pretesting, online distribution, and subsequent data
collection, screening, and verification.

Population
Following Sekaran and Bougie (2020), the population in this study consists of all visitors
who have attended Prambanan Jazz Festival, chosen because they directly experience the variables
under study. As the festival attracts tens of thousands of visitors annually with an unknown exact
total, the population is categorized as an infinite population.

Sampling Technique and Sample
This study uses purposive sampling, a non -probability sampling technique, since
respondents must possess the specific characteristic of having attended Prambanan Jazz Festival
and be able to provide accurate assessments based on real experience (Sugiyono, 2019). This
technique is further supported by the infinite nature of the population and the online mode of data
collection. Sample criteria required respondents to have attended Prambanan Jazz Festival at least
twice between 2024 and 2025 and to be at least 17 years old or otherwise capable of providing an
objective assessment. Following Hair et al.'s (2017) guideline of 5 -10 times the number of
indicators for PLS-SEM, and with 18 indicators in this study, the recommended sample size ranged
from 90 to 180 r espondents; this study used the upper bound of 180 respondents to enhance the
stability, validity, and reliability of the analysis.

Type of Data and Data Collection Procedure
This study uses primary, quantitative data obtained directly from respondents through an
online questionnaire distributed via Google Forms and social media to individuals meeting the
sampling criteria. The questionnaire contained statements measuring experiential marketing, event
quality, social media engagement, satisfaction, and visitor loyalty using a Likert scale; responses
were self -administered based on respondents' actual experience, then collected, screened, and
verified for completeness and eligibility until the target sample size was reached.

Classification of Research Variables
Variables in this study are classified into independent, dependent, and mediating variables.
The independent variables are Experiential Marketing (X1), Event Quality (X2), and Social Media

revisit the event and recommend it to others; and the mediating variable is Visitor Satisfaction (Z),
which bridges the effects of experiential marketing and event quality on visitor loyalty.

Variable Measurement Scale
All variables were measured using a 5-point Likert scale, ranging from 1 (strongly disagree)
to 5 (strongly agree), chosen for its ease of understanding among respondents and its ability to

accurately measure levels of agreement across experiential marketing, event quality, social media
engagement, satisfaction, and visitor loyalty; the resulting data were analyzed using PLS -SEM to
test the relationships among variables.

Data Analysis Technique
Descriptive analysis was used to systematically present respondent characteristics and
response distributions across variables through tables, percentages, means, standard deviations,
and minimum and maximum values, without testing relationships among variables (Subhaktiyasa
et al., 2025). Quantitative analysis was conducted using Structural Equation Modeling based on
Partial Least Squares (SEM -PLS), beginning with instrument testing to ensure the questionnaire
produced accurate, consistent, and reliable data.

a stricter criterion). Reliability testing used Cronbach's Alpha (≥ 0.70, with 0.60 -0.70 acceptable
for exploratory research) and Composite Reliability, the latter being more recommended in PLS -
SEM (≥ 0.70 reliable, ≥ 0.80 highly reliable), as summarized in Table 2.

Experiential Marketing and Visitor Loyalty
Experiential Marketing positively and significantly affected Visitor Loyalty (β = 0.149; p =
0.012). Sensory, emotional, cognitive, behavioral, and social experiences can create memorable
impressions that encourage visitors to revisit and maintain commitment to the festival. This finding
is consistent with Hasan et al. (2024), (Aryandha et al., 2025; Asyhari & Dermawan, 2024) ,
Setiawati (2025), Riyanto et al. (2025), and Genoveva and Fauziah (2025), which emphasize
experiential value and emotional engagement as contributors to loyalty.

Event Quality and Visitor Loyalty
Event Quality positively and significantly affected Visitor Loyalty (β = 0.255; p < 0.001).
Program organization, service responsiveness, facilities, and information clarity provide a reliable
basis for visitor confidence and continued preference. The find ing is consistent with Liu et al.

(2024), Yoon et al. (2010), and Molina -Gómez et al. (2021), which associate event quality with
perceived value, satisfaction, and repeat behavior.

Social Media Engagement and Visitor Loyalty
Social Media Engagement had the strongest direct effect on Visitor Loyalty (β = 0.289; p <
0.001). Visitors’ activities in consuming, contributing to, and creating festival-related content can
maintain their connection with the event beyond the festival period. The dominance of Instagram
and the young respondent profile support the relevance of digital interaction. The result is
consistent with Ramli et al. (2023), Zhou and Yu (2022), and Ambarawati et al. (2024).

Experiential Marketing and Visitor Satisfaction
Experiential Marketing positively and significantly affected Visitor Satisfaction (β = 0.349;
p < 0.001). The multisensory and emotional experience provided by the festival contributes to
positive visitor evaluations. This finding is consistent with Carmo et al. (2022) and (Arief et al.,
2022), which identify experiential dimensions as important determinants of satisfaction.

Event Quality and Visitor Satisfaction
Event Quality had the strongest direct effect on Visitor Satisfaction (β = 0.352; p < 0.001).
Program quality, services, facilities, and information are fundamental components of the festival
experience. This result is consistent with Wijono et al. (2025),  Nasution and Siregar (2024), (M.
I. Harahap & Pasaribu, 2024)   and Sitanggang and Lubis (2024), which report positive
relationships between event or service quality and satisfaction.

Social Media Engagement and Visitor Satisfaction
Social Media Engagement positively and significantly affected Visitor Satisfaction (β =
0.240; p < 0.001). Digital interaction can shape expectations before attendance and influence post-
event evaluation when visitors compare online information with their actual experience. The
finding is consistent with Wilopo and Nuralam (2025), Ambarawati et al. (2024), Ramsoondar et
al. (2025), and Zhou and Yu (2022).

Visitor Satisfaction and Visitor Loyalty
Visitor Satisfaction positively and significantly affected Visitor Loyalty (β = 0.259; p <
0.001). Satisfaction related to decision quality, expectation fulfillment, and positive feelings can
strengthen confidence, revisit intention, and willingness to pay  more. This finding is consistent
with (Y. Harahap et al., 2025), (Khairunnisa et al., 2023), and (Agustina & Maulany, 2025).

Ramsoondar et al. (2025), and Zhou and Yu (2022).

Overall Implications
The findings show that Visitor Loyalty at the Prambanan Jazz Festival is shaped by
experiential, operational, and digital factors. Event Quality is the strongest direct predictor of
Visitor Satisfaction, whereas Social Media Engagement is the strongest direct predictor of Visitor
Loyalty. Visitor Satisfaction partially mediates all three relationships with loyalty, with the
strongest indirect pathway occurring between Event Quality and Visitor Loyalty.
The results support the combined relevance of Expectancy Disconfirmation Theory and
Stimulus-Organism-Response Theory. Event Quality, Experiential Marketing, and Social Media
Engagement function as stimuli that are evaluated by visitors and contribute to satisfaction before
producing loyal behavioral responses. Practically, organizers should maintain consistent program
quality, responsive services, adequate facilities, and clear information while continuously
developing memorable sensory and emotional experiences. Social media should also be managed
through interactive and authentic content to strengthen engagement and sustain visitor loyalty.

CONCLUSION
This study was conducted to analyze the effects of Experiential Marketing, Event Quality,
and Social Media Engagement on Visitor Loyalty at the Prambanan Jazz Festival, with Visitor
Satisfaction serving as a mediating variable. Based on the results of data analysis using the Partial
Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS -SEM) method, Experiential Marketing, Event
Quality, and Social Media Engagement were found to have positive effects on Visitor Loyalty.
The findings indicate that the sensory, emot ional, cognitive, behavioral, and social experiences
provided by the festival are able to create memorable impressions that encourage visitors to

American Journal of Economic and Management Business                       1565
maintain their commitment and intention to revisit the festival. In addition, the actual performance
of event organization, including event programs, committee services, facilities, and information
clarity, also contributes to visitor loyalty by establishi ng confidence in the overall quality of the
festival.
Social Media Engagement was identified as the strongest direct predictor of Visitor Loyalty
in the research model. This finding demonstrates that visitors’ involvement in consuming,
contributing to, and creating social media content can strengthen their emotional connection with
the festival beyond the event period. Digital engagement allows visitors to maintain interactions
with the festival through social media before, during, and after the event, thereby supporting the
development of a lasting relationship with the festival. These findings highlight the importance of
social media as not only a communication platform but also a medium through which visitor
engagement and loyalty can be continuously maintained.
The results further demonstrate that Experiential Marketing, Event Quality, and Social
Media Engagement have positive effects on Visitor Satisfaction. Event Quality was found to be
the most dominant predictor of Visitor Satisfaction, indicating that the op erational and technical
performance of the festival plays a fundamental role in determining whether visitors’ expectations
are fulfilled. The quality of event programs, services provided by the committee, facilities, and the
clarity of information contribu te to visitors’ overall evaluation of their festival experience.
Meanwhile, Experiential Marketing contributes to satisfaction by providing comprehensive
experiences that can meet or exceed visitors’ expectations. Social Media Engagement also
contributes to satisfaction by shaping visitors’ expectations and evaluations before, during, and
after their participation in the festival.
Visitor Satisfaction was also found to have a positive effect on Visitor Loyalty. This finding
indicates that visitors who experience a high level of satisfaction are more likely to develop
stronger confidence, revisit the festival, and demonstrate a willingness to pay more to attend future
events. Furthermore, Visitor Satisfaction partially mediates the effects of Experiential Marketing,
Event Quality, and Social Media Engagement on Visitor Loyalty. The partial mediation results
indicate that these three variables influence Visitor Loyalty not only through direct effects but also
through the satisfaction formed from visitors’ evaluations of their festival experiences. Among the
three indirect relationships, the mediating contribution of Visitor Satisfaction  to the relationship
