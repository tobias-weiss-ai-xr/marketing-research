Abstract
This Study is a Survey of 300 users of social media in Bangalore to understand how the influence marketing by them
affects the corresponding purchase intention of consumers. Five independent variables tested using consumer attitude
as a mediator include the credibility, professionalism, trust, content authenticity and engagement. The results showed
that a combination of these factors had a significant explanation to purchase intention (about 31 percent). Alarmingly,
engaging turned out to be the strongest driving force and more so than the credibility that came second. Direct strongest
effect was on content authenticity but the purchase intention was also affected indirectly through consumer attitude.
This measurement model was shown to be statistically consistent and robust and the following analysis revealed that
consumer attitude mediates a level bet ween the influencer factors and the purchase intention. The only significant
demographic differences in purchase intention were age, the other demographic influencing purchase intention
differences were gender, occupation, platform and type of influencer (not significant).

Keywords: Influencer Marketing, Consumer Purchase Intention, Social Media, Consumer Attitude, Source
Credibility, Instagram, YouTube, Digital Marketing, India

1. Introduction
The role of influencer marketing in the consumer purchase intention in the contemporary digital era whereby Instagram
and YouTube have become one and the same thing it is worth mentioning that they are now a significant part of the
brand-to-consumer relations, will be analyzed in this paper. Compared to traditional adverts, influencer content appears
more personal and informative and has the power to influence what consumers think and buy. Also, this effect is felt
more in a fast developing digital market like India. However, most studies treat factors of influence separately. This
paper brings them together and goes a step further to analyse the effect of consumer attitude by giving a clear sight of
how influencer marketing actually affects buying behaviour.

Hypothesis
To determine the impact of each of the dimensions of influencer marketing to purchase intention, the dimension is
further divided to four hypotheses, the first (H1) one is: The credibility of influencers has a strong positive influence
on the consumer purchase intention.
H2: There is very great influence of influencer professionalism on consumer buy intention. H3: Consumer purchase
intention has a positive high correlation with trust in influencers.
H4: The connection between the content authenticity and quality and the consumer purchase intent is great and
positive.
H0 (Null): The extent to which the influencer marketing affects the consumer buying intentions cannot be followed
Each of the hypotheses also has a null (H01-H04) that the relationship is not significant.

Research Methodology

2.1 Research Design
A cross-sectional descriptive study is also quantitative and involves 1 analytically structured questionnaire of 300
Bangalore residents contemplating about influencers and buying to give a single picture of this section.
2.2 Sources of Data
The primary data was collected through the use of a close-ended questionnaire that was spread through Google Forms
with a limited sample of paper-based surveys that were sent out offline to the participants in colleges in hostels and
workplaces. Conference proceedings and research journals of Google Scholar and Scopus were used as secondary data.

Convenience sampling was the only possible strategy because there is no list of individuals in Bangalore who follow

2.4 Statistical Tools Used
A percentage analysis of the demographics should be studied in five ways; the mean and SD of the SD give a summary
of the responses; a Cronbachs Alpha is used to check the scale reliability; a Pearson correlation is used to determine
bivariant relationship and a multiple linear regression compares all five of the IVs to check mediation by consumer
attitude. Chi-square test examines the relationships among demographics.

3. Findings Suggestions and Conclusion

a.  Findings & Discussion

Three consequences are noteworthy because they elicit the common perceptions.
Engagement beats credibility. First preference was credibility- both Mabkhot et al. (2022) and Ao et al. (2023) have
mentioned it to be the biggest predictor. Here, engagement edged ahead: β= 0.236 versus 0.224. The two -way
relationship is converted to purchase willingness more reliably than passive credibility as the influencers respond to
the comments, a poll or question and answer session takes place. Credibility will earn you a following; commitment
will earn you goodwill in making a purchase.
The degree of trust is decreased. The trust beta was a very low 0.136. Q11 (I believe those who advertise products
online are genuine users of such products) had the lowest rate of 3.31. Consumers do know sponsored posts; they are
advertisements. But scepticism does not hinder their purchase.
They are now desensitized to commercialisation of content: they no longer believe it, but still perceives some value in
it.

One can expand on this research in many directions, utilizing research to gain a more accurate insight into consumer

b. Conclusion
The findings of 300 respondents in Bangalore are a clear indication that influencer marketing does have an influence
on purchase intention but not precisely in the manner that we will normally anticipate All the proposed factors showed
a relative influence and combine together they explain approximately 31 of the willingness of people to purchase
Consumer attitude is also important factor and attributes the factors in relation to purchase decisions.
On key insight is the most influential as the engagement counts more than credibility Yet trust had the lowest effect
content authenticity was found to significantly influence the consumer feeling and make the purchase.
In sum, the important lesson is that it is not about refined material but the development of trustful and authentic
relationships between influencers and their audience that works.
