The purpose of this study is to investigate the
impact of Perceived Value, Store Atmosphere, and
Social Media Marketing on Consumer Loyalty
through Consumer Satisfaction a s a mediating
variable at Mie Gacoan MERR Surabaya. As
competition within the culinary sector continues to
intensify, understanding the determinants of
customer loyalty has become essential for business
sustainability. This study utilizes a quantitative
explanatory research approach. Primary data were
obtained from 100 respondents selected through
purposive sampling and analyzed using Path
Analysis conducted with SPSS 27. The findings are
expected to provide insights into the relationships
among the variabl es and their contribution to
enhancing consumer loyalty.  The results indicate
that Perceived Value, Store Atmosphere, and Social
Media Marketing have a positive and significant
influence on Consumer Satisfaction both partially
and simultaneously. Furthermore, the three
independent variables also exhibit a positive and
significant influence on Consumer Loyalty.
Mediation test results reveal that Consumer
Satisfaction significantly mediates the relationship
between the independent var iables and the
dependent variable. These findings confirm that
Perceived Value is the most dominant factor in
driving loyalty; however, direct experiential
satisfaction remains an absolute prerequisite for
consumers to remain loyal to the Mie Gacoan brand
amidst numerous competing alternatives.

INTRODUCTION
One of the rapidly growing culinary brands in Indonesia is Mie Gacoan.
Mie Gacoan has successfully captured public attention through a concept of
affordable pricing, unique menu variants, spacious and modern venues, and
active promotion via social media. T he Mie Gacoan MERR branch in Surabaya
has become one of the most crowded outlets due to its strategic location in an
area surrounded by educational institutions, residential complexes, and centers
of public activity(Rasyidi et al., 2025) . This high volume of visitors indicates that
Mie Gacoan possesses a strong market appeal. However, a high number of visits
does not necessarily reflect full consumer loyalty, as consumers can easily switch
to other brands if their expectations are not met (Alesha et al., 2023). Therefore,
the company needs to understand the factors capable of maintaining sustainable
customer loyalty.
The phenomenon occurring at Mie Gacoan MERR Surabaya can be
observed through the long customer queues, the buzz of reviews on social media,
and the increasing business competition in the spicy noodle and fast -food
restaurant sector in Surabaya. On one hand, this condition shows that the brand
has a strong attraction. On the other hand, several challenges have emerged, such
as long waiting times, limited seating during peak hours, complaints regarding
slow service, and competitors offering similar co ncepts. If these conditions are
not managed effectively, consumer loyalty may decline. Consequently, the
company needs to determine the extent to which Perceived Value, Store
Atmosphere, and Social Media Marketing can create consumer satisfaction and
loyalty.
Within the scope of contemporary marketing, consumer loyalty is shaped
by various key determinants, including perceived value, store atmosphere, and
social media marketing. Perceived value refers to consumers’ overall evaluation
of the benefits obtained from a product or service relative to the costs, effort, and
experiences involved in acquiring it (Hatta, 2024). Consumers who perceive
greater value from their purchases are more likely to experience satisfaction,
which subsequently encourages repeat purchasing behavior.
Previous studies have provided evidence of the relationship between
perceived value, satisfaction, and loyalty. Research conducted by Mukarromah
and Istanto (2023) on customers of Mie Gacoan in Surabaya revealed that
perceived value posi tively affects repurchase intention through customer
satisfaction. Similarly, a study by Yolanda et al. (2021) in Yogyakarta
demonstrated that perceived value contributes positively to consumer loyalty
when mediated by satisfaction. Nevertheless, inconsist ent findings have also
been reported. Mukarromah and Istanto (2023), in a different study involving
customers of Mie Gacoan Tebet Jakarta, found that perceived value did not
significantly influence customer satisfaction. These contrasting results indicate
the existence of a research gap, highlighting the need for further investigation to
clarify the relationship among these variables in different research settings.
In addition to perceived value, another crucial factor is Store Atmosphere.
The atmosphere of a store or restaurant encompasses interior design, cleanliness,

lighting, music, layout, and overall comfort (Saputra et al., 2024). In the culinary
business, store atmosphere can be the reason consumers choose a restaurant,
especially for the younger generation who utilize dining establishments as social
spaces. Empirical studies have produced varying findings regarding the
influence of store atmosphere on customer responses. Hartanto et al. (2025)
reported that store atmosphere exerts a positive and sign ificant impact on

found that store atmosphere does not significantly affect either customer
satisfaction or customer loyalty. These inconsistent findings demonstrate that the
influence of store atmosphere still needs to be re -tested in different research
locations, specifically at the MERR Surabaya branch.
In the digital era, social media marketing has become an important factor
in influencing consumer behavior. Platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, and X
enable businesses to enhance brand awareness, customer engagement, and
emotional connections with consumers (Syamsu et al., 2024). Through
promotional content, customer interactions, and online reviews, social media can
shape purchasing decisions and consumer perceptions. Although Mie Gacoan
actively utilizes social media to stren gthen its brand image and promote its
products (Yolanda et al., 2021), limited research has examined its impact on
consumer loyalty through consumer satisfaction, particularly at Mie Gacoan
MERR Surabaya.
Consumer Satisfaction in this study is positioned a s an intervening
variable, as satisfaction often serves as a bridge between consumer experience
and loyalty. Consumers who obtain high value, experience a comfortable
restaurant atmosphere, and are exposed to engaging digital marketing
communication tend t o feel satisfied  (Kaffi & Mujanah, 2023) . This satisfaction
then drives the desire to repurchase, recommend the brand to others, and remain
loyal to the same brand. Research by Adam et al. (2025) shows that consu mer
satisfaction has a significant effect on Mie Gacoan customer loyalty, whereas
research by Alesha et al. (2023) indicates an insignificant result.
This study is motivated by the limited number of studies that
simultaneously examine the effects of percei ved value, store atmosphere, and
social media marketing on consumer loyalty through consumer satisfaction,
particularly in the context of Mie Gacoan MERR Surabaya. Previous research has
generally focused on only one or two of these variables, leaving a gap  in
understanding the factors that collectively shape customer loyalty.
The study is important given the intense competition within Surabaya’s
culinary industry and the growing number of consumer choices. Customer
loyalty plays a crucial role in sustaining business performance, as retaining
existing customers is more cost-effective than attracting new ones. Therefore, the
findings are expected to assist Mie Gacoan MERR Surabaya in developing
effective marketing strategies while also contributing to the literature on
consumer behavior in Indonesia’s culinary sector.

LITERATURE REVIEW
Perceived Value
Perceived value refers to consumers’ evaluation of the benefits gained
from a product or service in relation to the costs, effort, or sacrifices required to
obtain it. Kotler and Keller (2016:151) describe perceived value as the difference
between the total benefits and total costs perceived by customers when compared
with available alternatives. Similarly, Zeithaml (1988) defines it as an overall
assessment of a product’s usefulness based on what consumers receive versus
what they give. According to Sweeney and Soutar (2001), perceived value can be
measured through several key indicators, namely:
1. Emotional value
2. Social value
3. Quality/performance value
4. Price/value for money
Store Atmosphere
Store atmosphere re fers to the physical environment and ambiance of a
store designed to create comfort and a positive experience for consumers.
Meanwhile, Berman and Evans (2018:462) state that store atmosphere consists of
the physical characteristics of a store used to buil d an image and attract
customers. According to Berman and Evans (2018:463), the indicators of store
atmosphere include:
1. Exterior (the outer part of the store)
2. General interior (the inner part of the store)
3. Store layout (the spatial arrangement of the store)
4. Interior display (decorations and internal displays)
Social Media Marketing
Social media marketing refers to promotional activities that use social
media platforms to engage consumers, strengthen brand awareness, and
encourage purchasing behavior. Tuten and Solomon (2017:15) define social
media marketing as the application of social media tools, channels, and
technologies to create, communicate, and deliver value to stakeholders.
According to As’ad and Alhadid (2014), social media marketing can be assesse d
through several key indicators, including:
1. Online communities
2. Interaction
3. Sharing of content
4. Accessibility
5. Credibility
Consumer Loyalty
Consumer loyalty refers to a customer’s willingness to continue
purchasing and supporting a particular product or service over time, even when
faced with competing alternatives. Kotler and Keller (2016:153) define loyalty as
a strong commitment to repurchase a preferred product or service in the future.
Similarly, Griffin (2015:31) explains that loyal customers are characterized by
repeat purchases, buying various products from the same company,
recommending the brand to others, and resisting competitors’ o ffers. According

Consumer Satisfaction
Consumer satisfaction reflects the degree to which customers feel pleased
or dissatisfied after evaluating a product’s performance against their
expectations. Kotler and Keller (2016:153) describe satisfaction as a feeling that
emerges from comparing perceive d performance with prior expectations.
Likewise, Tjiptono (2019:378) explains that customer satisfaction is a post -
purchase evaluation in which the selected product or service meets or exceeds
consumer expectations. Higher satisfaction is achieved when per formance
surpasses what customers anticipate. According to Tjiptono (2019:379), consumer
satisfaction can be measured through the following indicators:
1. Conformity to expectations
2. Interest in revisiting
3. Willingness to recommend
4. Overall satisfaction

METHODOLOGY
This research adopts a quantitative methodology using an explanatory
approach. The population in this study consists of all consumers who have
purchased from Mie Gacoan MERR Surabaya. The population size is categorized
as infinite, as it cannot be precisely determined. This is due to the fluctuating
number of daily customers and tr ansactions, and the absence of absolute data
regarding the total number of consumers who have ever visited the outlet.
To obtain representative data, a Purposive Sampling technique was
applied during the initial screening phase based on the following criteria:
1. Consumers residing in the Surabaya area.
2. Have purchased from Mie Gacoan MERR Surabaya at least once within
the last three months.
3. Have personally utilized the dine -in facilities at the MERR Surabaya
outlet.
4. Have viewed Mie Gacoan’s promotions or information via social media.
5. Willing to complete the research questionnaire in its entirety.

DISCUSSION
The Influence of Perceived Value on Consumer Satisfaction
Based on the hypothesis testing, Perceived Value has a significant positive
influence on Consumer Satisfaction at Mie Gacoan MERR Surabaya. This indicates
that the higher the value perceived by consumers in terms of the balance between
the price paid and the quality received the higher their satisfaction level. At Mie
Gacoan MERR, this is reflected in the extremely affordable pricing for large
portions and a distinctive spicy taste. Theoretically, this aligns with Kotler & Keller
(2016), who state that satisfaction arises when the perceived value meets or exceeds
expectations. This finding is supported by Yolanda et al. (2021), confirming that in
the budget-culinary sector, "value for money" is the strongest driver of satisfaction.
The Influence of Store Atmosphere on Consumer Satisfaction
The results show that Store Atmosphere has a significant positive influence
on Consumer Satisfaction. The modern, industrial design, spacious seating, and
lively music at the MERR branch create a "hangout" ecosystem that ap peals to
Surabaya’s youth. This supports Berman & Evans’ (2018) theory that physical
characteristics build a brand image that attracts and satisfies customers. This result

is consistent with Alesha et al. (2023), who found that a comfortable dining
environment reduces the negative perception of long queues, thereby maintaining
high satisfaction.
The Influence of Social Media Marketing on Consumer Satisfaction
Social Media Marketing has a significant positive influence on Consumer
Satisfaction. Mie Gacoan’s active engagement on TikTok and Instagram creates
clear expectations regarding new menus and promos. When the reality at the
MERR outlet matches the digital content, satisfaction is achieved. This follows
Tuten & Solomon (2017), stating that social media b uilds engagement that
enhances the consumption experience. This aligns with Benata (2021), who proved
that effective digital communication minimizes the information gap for
consumers.
The Influence of Perceived Value on Consumer Loyalty
Perceived Value is proven to have a significant positive influence on
Consumer Loyalty. Consumers return to Mie Gacoan MERR because they feel they
cannot find a similar "benefit-to-cost" ratio elsewhere. According to Griffin (2015),
loyalty is built when a  product provides consistent utility. This finding mirrors
Mukarromah & Istanto (2023), showing that economic value is a rational
foundation for long-term repurchasing behavior in the noodle industry.
The Influence of Store Atmosphere on Consumer Loyalty
Store Atmosphere significantly influences Consumer Loyalty. While food
taste is important, the "vibe" of the MERR outlet makes it a preferred recurring
meeting point for students and workers. Theoretically, Kotler (1973) argued that
atmosphere is a primary tool for differentiation. This study reinforces Saputra et
al. (2024), suggesting that a unique restaurant atmosphere creates an emotional
bond that prevents customers from switching to competitors.
The Influence of Social Media Marketing on Consumer Loyalty
Social Media Marketing has a significant positive influence on Consumer
Loyalty. By staying in the followers' feeds, Mie Gacoan maintains "Top of Mind"
awareness. This constant digital presence encourages repeat visits and builds a
digital community. As noted by As’ad & Alhadid (2014), credibility in social media
leads to long -term commitment. This is consistent with Yolanda et al. (2021),
highlighting that viral marketing is essential for sustaining brand relevance in a
saturated market.
The Influence of Consumer Satisfaction on Consumer Loyalty
Consumer Satisfaction shows a strong significant positive influence on
Consumer Loyalty. Satisfaction is the "psychological gate" to loyalty; a satisfied
customer at Mie Gacoan MERR is likely to return and provid e word-of-mouth
recommendations. This adheres to Tjiptono (2019), where post -purchase
evaluation determines future behavior. This result is supported by Adam et al.
(2025), confirming that satisfaction is the most reliable predictor of loyalty.

Perceived Value on Consumer Loyalty through Consumer Satisfaction
Consumer Satisfaction significantly mediates the relationship between
Perceived Value and Loyalty. This means Perceived Value alone might not lead to
loyalty if it does not first result in a sati sfying experience. Satisfaction acts as a
bridge that transforms the "low price" perception into a "loyal commitment." This
finding supports the mediation theory by Kurniawan (2023), proving that value
drives loyalty indirectly through a positive emotional state (satisfaction).
Store Atmosphere on Consumer Loyalty through Consumer Satisfaction
Consumer Satisfaction significantly mediates the influence of Store
Atmosphere on Loyalty. A good atmosphere creates an immediate feeling of
pleasure (satisfaction), which then motivates the consumer to revisit the MERR
outlet. Without satisfaction, a good layout is just a visual gimmick. This is in line
with Alesha et al. (2023), suggesting that the "joy of dining" is the necessary link
between a restaurant's physical design and the customer's decision to return.
Social Media Marketing on Consumer Loyalty through Consumer Satisfaction
Consumer Satisfaction significantly mediates the relationship between
Social Media Marketing and Loyalty. Digital promos attract custome rs, but the
satisfaction felt during the actual transaction is what secures their loyalty. This
underscores Rachman et al. (2025)'s view that marketing tools are only effective at
building loyalty if they successfully deliver a satisfying real-world experience.

CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATIONS
Based on the results of the analysis, this study concludes that consumer
loyalty at Mie Gacoan MERR Surabaya is significantly determined by Perceived
Value and Store Atmosphere as the primary driving factors. The perceived
benefit value particularly through the balance between highly affordable prices
and consistent product quality  proves to be the most crucial foundation in
building sustainable loyalty. As the most dominant variable, Perceived Value
functions as t he rational grounds for consumers to engage in repeat purchases
amidst numerous competing alternatives. Meanwhile, Store Atmosphere plays a
vital role in creating a comfortable emotional experience for consumers, and
Social Media Marketing effectively main tains digital engagement with
customers. Simultaneously, the integration of these three variables, reinforced by
the mediating role of Consumer Satisfaction, forms a resilient marketing
ecosystem. This indicates that Mie Gacoan MERR's success in maintainin g its
market share does not depend solely on the physical product, but rather on
management's ability to synergize economic value, spatial comfort, and targeted
digital communication.
To maintain and increase customer loyalty in the future, the management
of Mie Gacoan MERR Surabaya is recommended to adopt a more holistic
customer experience -based marketing strategy by maintaining product value
consistency through quality control of taste and service speed to minimize
dissatisfaction caused by long queues. Furthermore, the company needs to
continuously update elements of the Store Atmosphere with "Instagrammable"
decorative touches and improvements in physical facility comfort to remain
relevant to the youth target market, as well as optimize Social Media Ma rketing
