ABSTRACT

Social media marketing has become a critical digital marketing strategy for influencing consumer purchase decisions; howeve r,
existing evidence remains fragmented regarding the relative effectiveness of different social media marketing strategies and the
roles of consumer trust and loyalty . Previous reviews have largely focused on isolated dimensions, such as influencer marketing,
customer engagement, or trust in social commerce, providing limited integrated evidence on the broader influence of social media
marketing. This study systematically reviewed empirical evidence published between 2020 and 2025 to identify the most effective
social media marketing strategies and examine how consume r trust and loyalty influence consumer purchase decisions . The
review was guided by the Stimulus -Organism-Response (SOR) Theory and followed the PRISMA 2020 guidelines . A total of
19,948 records were identified from Scopus, Google Scholar, Emerald Insight,  and SpringerLink, of which 32 empirical studies
met the inclusion criteria after systematic screening, eligibility assessment, and methodological quality appraisal using the  Mixed
Methods Appraisal Tool (MMA T) (2018). Thematic synthesis identified two overarching themes. First, social media marketing
activities, electronic word-of-mouth (eWOM), influencer marketing, consumer engagement and personalization, and brand image
were the most effective strategies influencing consumer purchase decisions. Second, b rand trust, brand loyalty , consumer trust,
and information credibility emerged as the principal mechanisms linking social media marketing to purchasing behaviour, wit h
brand trust representing the most consistent pathway . This review provides an updated sy nthesis of recent empirical evidence,
extends existing literature by integrating multiple social media marketing strategies and trust -related mechanisms within a single
review, and offers evidence -based guidance for researchers and practitioners seeking to  enhance consumer purchase decisions
through social media marketing. The study concludes that integrated, informative, interactive, credible, and trust -oriented social
media marketing strategies can strengthen consumer purchase decisions, while organizations should tailor these strategies to their
target audiences, products, and market contexts. Future research should employ longitudinal and mixed -method designs and
examine emerging social media technologies and contextual factors to strengthen understanding of consumer behaviour.

I. INTRODUCTION

Social media has changed the world of marketing as it has moved business –consumer interaction from a
broadcast model to an interactive, relationship -based one. Social media marketing is a strategic element of digital
marketing today, enabling companies to distribute information, engage with customers, foster user -generated content,
and develop lasting relationships via platforms like Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and X (Twitter)
(Hanaysha, 2022; Manzoor et al., 2020). The growing global adoption of the internet and the use of smart phones have
made social media a key source for organisations to sway consumers' buying behaviour and enhance their competitiv e
edge.
Due to the increasing significance of social media marketing, a large body of literature exists examining the
impact of social media marketing on consumer behavior. Social Media Marketing (SMM) is generally found to have a
positive impact on purchase intention as well as through interactive marketing activities, electronic word -of-mouth
(eWOM) marketing, influencer marketing, consumer engagement, and brand image (Leong et al., 2021; Zhao et al.,
2020; Hasan & Sohail, 2021; Alnaser et al., 2024). There is conflicting evidence in the Literature on the relative
effectiveness of these strategies, however. Some research points to the informative and interactive nature of content as
the most significant factors impacting the intention to purchase (Hanaysha, 2022; Thomas, 2024), while others
highlight the importance of the credibility of influencers, e WOM, or personalized engagement (Özbölük & Akdoğan,
2022; Babu et al., 2024; Deepika & Massand, 2025). Likewise, scholars also have varying views on the contribution
of the brand constructs: one study found that brand image plays a more consistent and sign ificant role than brand
awareness in purchasing (Ali & Naushad, 2023) while another study indicated the opposite (Ardiansyah & Sarwoko,
2020).

In addition to marketing strategies, recent studies have identified that consumer trust and loyalty are important
mechanisms explaining how social media marketing is related to purchase decisions. It has been found that social
media marketing enhances the trust in brands, consumer trust, credibility of information and brand loyalty, which
further boosts consumers' co nfidence and purchase intention (Azhar et al., 2023; Karunasingha & Abeysekera, 2022;
Walean et al., 2025). However, the relative significance of these pathways is yet to be confirmed, with some research
suggesting that brand trust is the stronger link to consider, while others indicate that information credibility or
consumer loyalty may be more important factors in purchase behaviour (Leong et al., 2021; Ngo et al., 2024; Shian  et
al., 2025). Such inconsistencies highlight the need for a synthesis of the recent empirical evidence.
While there have been a number of evidence syntheses that have focused on parts of this topic, they are
limited. Previous studies have been conducted on influencer marketing (Kanaveedu & Kalapurackal, 2022), credibility
of social media influencers (Sesar et al., 2022), influencers' role in customer engagement and purchase intention (Ao
et al., 2023), or trust in social commerce (Wang et al., 2022; Lăzăroiu et al., 2020). As a result, there has bee n little
focus on integrating the overall impact of social media marketing tactics on consumers' buying decisions, while also
analyzing the importance of consumer trust and loyalty. This gap is significant especially in the context of the social
media platforms and digital consumer behaviour which is developing at an increasing pace between 2019 and 2025.
This systematic review aims to fill these gaps by pulling together evidence from 32 empirical studies
published from 2020 to 2025 following Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews  and Meta -Analyses
(PRISMA). The study concentrates on empirical research analyzing the impact of social media marketing on purchase
decision with a special consideration of social media marketing strategies that have the most significant effect on
purchase decision and the role of consumer trust and consumer loyalty in social media marketing. Through the
incorporation of evidence from a broad range of industries and geographic locations, the review offers an up -to-date
view of recent advances in social med ia marketing, expands on existing knowledge beyond the scope of single -topic
reviews, and offers evidence -based guidance for researchers and practitioners who want to create more successful
digital marketing strategies.

1.1 Statement of the Problem
Despite the widespread adoption of social media marketing by businesses, organizations continue to face
challenges in identifying the strategies that most effectively influence consumer purchase decisions and build
consumer trust and loyalty (Hanaysha, 2022; Has an & Sohail, 2021). Although empirical studies consistently report
positive effects of social media marketing, the evidence is dispersed across different industries, platforms, and
geographical contexts, with studies emphasizing different marketing strategies and behavioural mechanisms (Leong et
al., 2021; Zhao et al., 2020; Azhar et al., 2023). Consequently, marketers lack a consolidated evidence base to guide
strategic decision-making, which may result in ineffective allocation of marketing resources and reduced returns on
social media marketing investments.
Previous evidence syntheses have examined specific aspects of social media marketing, including influencer
marketing (Kanaveedu & Kalapurackal, 2022), influencer credibility (Sesar et al., 2022), customer engagement (Ao et
al., 2023), and trust in social commerce (Wang et al., 2022; Lăzăroiu et al., 2020). However, these reviews prima rily
focused on individual dimensions of social media marketing rather than synthesizing the broader empirical evidenc e
on which social media marketing strategies most effectively influence consumer purchase decisions while
simultaneously examining the roles of consumer trust and loyalty.  Therefore, this systematic review addresses these
gaps by synthesizing empirical evidence published between 2020 and 2025 to identify the most effective social media
marketing strategies influencing consumer purchase decisions and to examine how consumer trust and loyalty shape
this relationship. By addressing these gaps, this review provide s an updated and comprehensive understanding of
consumer behaviour in social media marketing. The findings contribute to digital marketing literature and provide
evidence-based insights for practitioners seeking to design more effective social media marketing strategies.

1.2 Research Objectives
i. To identify the social media marketing strategies that most effectively influence consumer purchase decisions.
ii. To examine how consumer trust and loyalty influence the relationship between social media marketing an d
consumer purchase decisions.

II. LITERATURE REVIEW

2.1 Theoretical Review
This study is based on the Stimulus –Organism–Response (SOR) Theory originally developed by Mehrabian
and Russell (1974). The theory is that it is the external environment stimuli (Stimulus) that affect the cognition and
emotions (Organism) of the individua l, which in turn determine the behavioral response (Response) (Mehrabian &
Russell, 1974). The SOR theory is applied in marketing to understand how stimuli in the environment affect consumer

perception, attitude and emotion, which in turn affect their buyi ng behavior (Riaz et al., 2021; John et al., 2025). In
this research, these marketing strategies in social media (social media marketing activities, eWOM, influencer
marketing, consumer engagement, personalisation, and brand image) are considered as stimul i. The organism is
consumer trust and brand loyalty, which is consumers' internal psychological assessment and emotional reaction to
such marketing stimuli. This creates a reaction, which shows in consumer buying.
Thus, it is reasonable to conclude that t he theory could explain why, and how, social media marketing can
have an impact on consumers' purchasing behavior through trust and loyalty factors (Azhar et al., 2023; Riaz et al.,
2021). The SOR theory was chosen as it provides a complete understanding o f consumer behaviour by incorporating
external marketing stimuli, internal psychological processes and behavioural consequences. This is especially
pertinent to the domains of digital marketing and social media platforms, where consumer behaviour, perceptions, and
trust are key determinants in their purchasing decisions (John et al., 2025; Walean et al., 2025). However, there are
some drawbacks to the SOR theory. While the theory assumes that consumer behaviour goes from stimulus to
response in a fairly lin ear manner, consumer decision -making could be modified by other factors like cultural
differences, individual characteristics, technological changes, etc. (Mehrabian & Russell, 1974). Moreover, the theory
doesn't take into consideration the dynamic and interactive nature of social media platforms, where the consumers can
also be information receivers and content creators. However, SOR theory can be still used as a theoretical lens in this
study since it is suitable to understand the relationship between SMM, consumer trust and consumer loyalty and how it
can influence consumer purchasing decisions.

2.2 Empirical Literature Review
2.2.1 Social Media Marketing Strategies Influencing Consumer Purchase Decisions
The various studies consistently show that SMM ha s a positive impact on consumer buying behavior, though
there are variations in the levels of effectiveness among the marketing strategies. Empirically, it has been found that in
different countries and industries, social media marketing activities, electr onic word-of-mouth (eWOM), influencer
marketing, consumers' engagement, personalization and brand related factors are important determinants of consume r
purchasing behaviour.
The majority studies conclude that SMM that is informative, interactive and perso nalized has a huge positive
impact on consumer purchasing decisions. Similarly, in a quantitative survey conducted in Malaysia, Hasan and Sohail
(2021) discovered that social media marketing had a positive impact on consumers' buying decision of both local  and
overseas brands. Likewise, Hanaysha (2022) found that informative and interactive social media marketing features
play a significant role in improving purchase decisions with the aid of brand trust among fast -food consumers in
Malaysia using Structural Equation Modelling (SEM). Alnaser et al. (2024) and Ali and Naushad (2023) found similar
results in Palestine and India respectively, suggesting that social media marketing enhances consumers' intention to
buy through brand perceptions.
The relevance of electronic word -of-mouth (eWOM) is also found to be quite similar across studies.
Consistent with Leong et al. (2021) in Malaysia, Ngo et al. (2024) in Vietnam, and Zhao et al. (2020) in China,
information quality, credibility, and information adoption are  found to significantly impact purchase intention.
Similarly, Winarno and Indrawati (2022) and Jasin (2022) found that eWOM positively influences purchase intention
both directly and indirectly via brand image.  Evidence about the influencer marketing is al so predominantly positive;
both Özbölük and Akdoğan (2022) in Türkiye and Babu et al. (2024) in India reported that the credibility of
influencers significantly affects purchase intent, especially among the Generation Z. This is consistent with Nofela
(2022) who found that influencer endorsers have a significant effect on purchase intention on TikTok Shop.
However, there is some controversy over the relative effect of the various marketing strategies. Though
Hanaysha (2022) found there is no significant dif ference between entertainment content and purchase decisions,
Deepika and Massand (2025) found entertainment as part of the purchase intention of the Gen Z customers. Likewise ,
Ardiansyah and Sarwoko (2020) stated that brand awareness alone was not enough to understand their purchasing
decisions, while Ali and Naushad (2023) and Sanny et al. (2020) indicated that brand image had the greatest influ ence
on purchasing behaviour.
The methodologically sound empirical evidence was seen in that the majority of the  studies used quantitative
survey designs with large samples and advanced analytical techniques, like SEM and PLS -SEM, which strengthened
the reliability of the relationships reported. But the evidence also has a number of limitations. The majority studies  on
this topic were conducted using a cross -sectional design and non -probability sampling, which prevented the
development of causal conclusions and made the results difficult to generalize. In addition, a number of studies were
specific to a product, an industry or a country, limiting the generalization of the results to other consumer markets. The
empirical findings suggest that social media marketing affects consumers' buying decisions in a positive manner b y
using various strategies, which can complemen t each other. The present review, however, addresses these differences
in the relative effectiveness of specific strategies and their context applicability by synthesizing recent empirical
evidence into one comprehensive review.

2.2.2 Consumer Trust and Loyalty in Social Media Marketing
Consumer confidence and consumer loyalty are consistently singled out as a key pathway by which social
media marketing impacts on purchases. The literature reviewed shows that trust in the literature is generally
considered to have a positive impact on the consumer's confidence in the brand and online shopping environment,
resulting in greater intentions to purchase and longer -term consumer relationships. Brand trust was consistently found
to be the main mediating process in several studies. In Pakistan, Manzoor et al. (2020), in Malaysia Hanaysha (2022),
and in Bangladesh Azhar et al. (2023) reported that social media marketing helps to build brand trust and consequently
to increase purchase intention. Karunasingha and Abeysekera (2022) found similar results in Sri Lanka and Chan et al.
(2020) showed that consumer trust has a significant positive impact on consumer online buying. Research has also
shown that brand loyalty will enhance the long -term effectiveness of social me dia marketing. Walean et al. (2025)
conducted a study in Indonesia and revealed that social media marketing can help create greater brand loyalty, wh ich
in turn helps support purchasing decisions and repeat buying behavior. Kurniawan and Tanujaya (2024) an d Shian et
al. (2025) also discovered similar findings.
Trustability of information was also identified as a key factor in credibility. Credible and useful online
information positively contributes to consumer trust and purchase intention, as shown in the studies of Leong et al.
(2021), Ngo et al. (2024), and Zhao et al. (2020). In the same vein, Özbölük and Akdoğan  (2022) discovered that the
credibility of influencers significantly enhances the confidence of consumers in their recommendations.  However,
there is disagreement about which is the prevailing trust mechanism. Some studies cited brand trust as the most
important determinant of purchase intention, others mentioned consumer trust, while information credibility or br and
loyalty were the most importan t determinants per study context and product category. The distinctions indicate that
trust mechanisms can vary between industries and electronic environments, and not be the same in each consumer
context.
The empirical study has a number of methodological  strengths, such as the use of validated measurement
scales, large sample sizes and sophisticated analysis techniques. But most used cross -sectional survey designs which
precluded causal inferences, and most used convenience samples, which also affect exte rnal validity. In addition, few
studies examined long-term consumer behaviour and new social media.  The empirical literature shows that trust and
loyalty play a key role in the relationship between social media marketing and consumers' purchasing decisions . The
present systematic review aims to fill this gap, though, as evidenced by the lack of studies focusing on particular
constructs and their relative impacts, as well as the predominance of context -specific studies, a comprehensive
synthesis of recent empirical evidence across industries and geographical settings is needed.

III. METHODOLOGY

This study adopted a systematic literature review design guided by the Preferred Reporting Items for
Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses (PRISMA) 2020 framework to synthesize empirical evidence on the influence
of social media marketing on consumer purchase decisions. The four PRISMA stages, identification, screening,
eligibility, and inclusion, were followed as recommended by Page et al. (2021) to ensure a transparent, systematic, and
reproducible review process while minimizing selection bias.  The identification stage involved a structured search of
four electronic databases: Scopus, Google Scholar, Emerald Insight, and SpringerLink. The search focused on
empirical studies published between 2020 and 2025 to capture recent developments in social media marketing and
consumer behaviour. Database -specific search strings were developed using Boolean operators (AND, OR) and
adapted to the indexing requirements of each database. The primary search string used was: ("social media marketing"
OR "social media" OR "digital marketing") AND ("purchase decision" OR "purchase intention" OR "consumer
behavior" OR "buying behavior") AND ("consumer trust" OR "brand trust" OR "brand loy alty" OR "customer
loyalty"). Minor modifications were applied where necessary to improve retrieval across individual databases.
The screening was done by the researcher based on the inclusion and exclusion criteria. Screening was done in
two phases. Titles and abstracts were first screened to determine potentially relevant studies. Second, the full texts of
articles were assessed to decide whether they met the criteria for inclusion. The studies included had to be (i) written
in English, (ii) empirical journal articles, (iii) investigate the effect of SMM on consumer purchase decisions, purchase
intention, consumer trust,  brand loyalty, and (iv) published between 2020 and 2025. Review papers, conceptual
papers, conference papers, book chapters, editorials, dissertations, duplicate publications, non-English research papers
and papers that provided insufficient methodologica l information were excluded. The predefined screening criteria
were applied in a consistent manner during the review process.
The methodological quality of included studies was appraised by using the Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool
(MMA T) (2018). The research designs of all studies included were quantitative, so the quantitative MMA T criteria
were used. First, each study underwent a review with the two MMA T screening questions to ensure that the study was
appropriate for appraisal. Eligible studies were then a ppraised using five methodological quality criteria (S1 -S5) that
evaluate the appropriateness of the sampling strategy, representativeness of the sample, measurements adequacy, risk

"No" and results were recorded in a standardized quality appraisal table highlighting the study design, screening
questions, each criterion, overall quality assessment and remarks. The quality appraisal was done to assess the
methodological rigor of the included studies and to inform in the interpretation of the synthesized results, and not to
exclude studies from the review.
To achieve uniformity in data collection, a standardized data extraction form was cr eated. Details collected
from each study comprised author(s), year, country, context of the study, study design, sample characteristics,
theoretical framework, variables studied, analytical techniques, and findings and conclusions. Thematic synthesis
proposed by Thomas and Harden (2008) was used to synthesize the data extracted. Study findings were first coded
then thematic development was done both descriptively and analytically according to the study objectives. The
synthesized findings were then grouped into two main themes: (i) strategies that affect consumer purchase decisions in
relation to social media marketing and (ii) the role of consumer trust and loyalty in social media marketing.
Additionally, a comparative analysis was used to highlight the agr eements and disagreements, strengths and
weaknesses in methodology of the included studies.  The included studies showed significant variations in study
settings, research designs, measurement instruments, methods of analyses, and outcome variables, and it was not
possible to perform a formal meta -analysis. Therefore, a qualitative thematic synthesis was seen as the best approach
to synthesising and interpreting the empirical evidences.
The study selection process has been described with the PRISMA 2020 flow  diagram showing the number of
records identified, screened, excluded and included at each stage of the study selection procedure. A similar approach
was used by (Jonas, 2025). Ethical issues were considered, with all sources accurately cited and full credit given to
original auth ors and use of academic honesty principles followed. Only published secondary data was used in the
review, so no ethical approval/ informed consent was necessary.  This review has a number of drawbacks. Single
reviewer bias could occur in the screening, study selection, data extraction and quality appraisal despite the use of pre-
defined protocols and standard procedures. Second, language bias and exclusion of other than English publications
might have led to the omission of relevant studies published in oth er languages. Third, as only empirical studies
published from 2020 to 2025 were included in the review, this may restrict historical comparisons. Finally, the
included studies were heterogeneous, and it was not possible to calculate pooled effect sizes acr oss studies. The
reviewers used the PRISMA framework and quality assessment using the MMA T to perform the review
systematically, which improves the transparency, reliability, and credibility of the review findings.

4.1.2 Quality Appraisal of Included Studies
Methodological quality was assessed using the Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool (MMA T, 2018). Most included
studies demonstrated moderate to high methodological quality. Common strengths included clearly defined research
objectives, appropriate quantitative survey designs, and robust statistical analyses such as Structural Equation

4.1.3 Thematic Synthesis
Social Media Marketing Strategies that Influence Consumer Purchase Decisions: Thematic synthesis revealed
five interrelated strategies of social media marketing which affect the purchase decision of consumers in the studies
included. These strate gies include social media marketing activities, electronic word -of-mouth (eWOM), influencer
marketing, consumer engagement and personalization, and brand image and brand awareness. While the strategies
were explored in various industries, consumer groups, and geographical contexts, the evidence consistently
demonstrates that the strategies have a positive effect on consumers' purchase intentions and purchase decisions in
various but complementary ways. The combination of these themes highlights that the value of social media marketing
goes beyond promotional communication as it enables the exchange of information, reinforces brand perceptions, an d
builds consumers' confidence during the purchase decision.
Social Media Marketing Activities: The most consistent activity that crossed the studies included in this report
affecting consumer purchases was social media marketing. The evidence suggests that marketing activities through
social media, such as providing information, facilitating interactive communication s, creating relevant ads,
customization and entertainment, overall have a positive effect on consumers purchase intention and purchase
decisions. It was noticed that this relationship exists across many different industries, such as cosmetics, skincare,
tourism, retail, food services, shopping malls, social commerce, and e -commerce and indicates that social media
marketing activities are effective in different market contexts (Alnaser et al., 2024; Harvina et al., 2022; Dai et al. ,
2024; Sanny et al., 2020; Hanaysha, 2022; Raniya et al., 2023; Jasin, 2022; Manzoor et al., 2020).
The most constant dimension of social media marketing in all the synthesized evidence is informative conte nt
that is relevant. Interactive communication and customized marketing mes sages were also found to be closely related
to a higher purchase intention, suggesting that consumers react more positively to marketing activities they can
actively participate in and that are providing useful information. Although entertainment was also found in a few
studies as a part of social media marketing, it is less consistently connected with purchase decisions than informational
and interactive (Hanaysha, 2022; Deepika & Massand, 2025; Thomas, 2024; Walean et al., 2025).
The results also show th at there are direct and indirect effects of the consumer purchase decision towards
social media marketing activities. Several studies found that social media marketing activities had a direct and
significant association with purchase intention, while other  studies showed that the activities of SMM had an indirect
impact by strengthening consumers' evaluations of brands and purchasing environments before impacting purchase
intentions (Ali & Naushad, 2023; Azhar et al., 2023; Harvina et al., 2022; Shian et al ., 2025). As a whole, the
synthesized evidence reveals that the main strategic factor underpinning purchase decisions of consumers is socia l
media marketing activities, which is the platform for other factors synthesized in this review to be played.  Electronic
Word-of-Mouth (eWOM) : Electronic word of mouth (eWOM) became an important tool for consumer buying
decision. The studies included here show that users' generated content, online reviews, recommendations, and shar ing
of their experiences serve as a major basis for their assessment of products or services when using social media. In all
the evidence, eWOM had a positive impact on purchase intention, by making product -related information more
available, credible, and useful (Leong et al., 2021; Zhao et al ., 2020; Ngo et al., 2024; Winarno & Indrawati, 2022;
Jasin, 2022).
The synthesis also reveals that the quality and credibility of information being shared, is a significant
determinant of the effectiveness of eWOM. The importance of information quality, credibility, relevance and
usefulness continued to come out as the most prominent information properties that affect consumers' intention to
purchase online information. Additionally, several studies found that the information adoption is another key
behavioural consequence that mediated the relationship between high -quality eWOM and purchase intention. It
indicated that the consumers not only accessed the information but also evaluated it and used it to inform their
purchasing decisions (Leong et al., 2021; Ngo et al., 2024; Zhao et al., 2020).
Thus, the results suggest that eWOM is one of the key social media marketing strategies since it provides
consumers with credible peer-generated information to support their informed decision-making. Influencer Marketing:
Another key strategy that impacted consumer buying decisions is the rise of influencer marketing, especially in digital
or social commerce. The results of synthesized evidence suggest that the credibility of influencers is the main
determinant o f effectiveness of an influencer marketing. When influencers are seen as credible, authentic, and
knowledgeable, consumers are more likely to make a purchase, underscoring the significance of source credibility in
social media marketing (Özbölük & Akdoğan, 2022; Babu et al., 2024).
The results also indicate that the relationship set up by the influencers and consumers enhances the influencer
marketing. Research on influencer characteristics found that consumers' purchase intentions were positively affected
by their psychological proximity to the influencers, inspiration, and credibility, but that some influencer characteristics
did not have the same effect on all consumer groups. The evidence suggests that overall, influencer marketing does aid
in the buyin g process of consumers when the influencer is viewed as credible and reliable in terms of providing
information about a product.  Engagement and Personalization : Consumer engagement and personalization were

designed to their interests, preferences and information needs, as opposed to the marketing messages sent to everyone.
Consequently, engaging consumers by communicating with them, involving them in online communities and engaging
in B2C relationship also always increased consumers' willingness to buy products via social media. So  the research
shows that personalization and engagement are a powerful way to enhance the effectiveness of social media marketing
by fostering more meaningful interactions with the consumer and the brand.  Brand Image and Brand Awareness :
Important brand outcomes related to social media marketing strategies were brand image and brand awareness. In all
the studies included, social media marketing improved the brand image of the consumers, which in turn led to an
increased purchase intention and purchase decis ion. These results indicate that social media marketing is not just a
tool for conveying product -related information but also influences positive consumer attitudes towards brands in

trustworthy relationship with consumers. All the studies i ncluded in the review consistently found that these

4.2 Discussion
4.2.1 Social Media Marketing Strategies Influencing Consumer Purchase Decisions
The review shows that social media marketing strategies are always found to affect the purchase decision o f
the consumers; informative and interactive marketing activities, electronic word of mouth (eWOM), influencer
marketing, consumer engagement and personalization, and positive brand image are found to be the most effective
strategies in the studies included. These findings  taken together suggest that consumers are more positive towards
marketing strategies that offer credible information, allow interaction and evoke meaningful brand experiences as
opposed to purely promotional messages.  In all of the evidence reviewed, info rmation-, interaction- and relationship-
building strategies were found to be more strongly associated with purchase intention than passive, entertainment
marketing activities. This means social media is no longer seen as just an advertising platform, but c onsumers have
come to see it as an information and engagement platform.
The results mostly agree with other studies not covered by this review. For instance, Kanaveedu and
Kalapurackal ( 2022) mention in their systematic review that influencer marketing and  consumer engagement are
important factors affecting consumers' behaviour and Lăzăroiu et al. (2020) point to online trust, perceived risk , and
social commerce interactions as important determi nants of purchasing behaviour. While generally, social media
marketing has a positive effect, a differential effect was found on the effectiveness of certain strategies. Informative,
interactive, and personalized content consistently evidenced stronger influence on purchase intention (Hanaysha,
2022; Thomas, 2024; Deepika & Massand, 2025), while entertaining content in some contexts significantly influenced
purchase intention or had no effect at all (Hanaysha, 2022). Likewise, while social media marketing generally
improved brand awareness, it's impact on buying decisi ons was not always as consistent as brand image (Ali &
Naushad, 2023; Ardiansyah & Sarwoko, 2020; Sanny et al., 2020), suggesting that the efficacy of certain social media
marketing tactics might vary depending on the type of product and the market conditions.
The results may be explained by the Stimulus -Organism-Response (SOR) theory which suggests that an
external stimulus in the environment affects a person's internal appraisal, which, in turn, affects the person's respon se.
In this review, social media marketing strategies are considered as the stimuli, which affect consumers' cognitive and
emotional evaluations through information quality, engagement, brand image and e -word of mouth. These internal
assessments, in turn, lead to the behavioural outcome i n terms of purchase intention and purchase decisions. The
similarities found across industries and geographic areas makes the SOR framework appropriate to explain consumer
behaviours in SMM settings.  This review adds to the literature by synthesizing the f indings of the recent empirical
studies that suggest that several social media marketing strategies act in a complementary, not in an independent way,
in order to affect consumers' purchase decisions. The synthesis does not reveal a single dominant strateg y, but rather
the impact each has on the others, and how combined, informative content, credible peer communication, interactiv e
engagement, personalisation and positive brand perceptions generate effective purchasing outcomes.
From a practical point of view, the findings indicate that integrated social media marketing strategies based on
the premise of high -quality information and consumer interaction, enabling creation of authentic user -generated
content and enhancing the brand image are to be used for th e benefit of the organizations. Complementary strategies
are more likely to positively influence purchase intention and increase the effectiveness of digital marketing under
various market conditions for businesses that focus on these strategies.

4.2.2 The Role of Consumer Trust and Loyalty in Social Media Marketing
The results show that social media marketing exerts an impact on consumers' purchase decisions, and brand
trust, brand loyalty, consumer trust and information credibility are found to be import ant mechanisms in this
relationship. Of these, brand trust was the most consistent pathway; meaning that successful social media marketing
has a positive impact on consumers' level of trust in brands, which in turn increases their intention to purchase and
leads to long-term loyalty. This means that social media marketing is best when it's not just about brand awareness, it's
about building trust -based relationships. The results agree with prior research which highlighted the importance of
trust and loyalty  in the social media marketing. For instance, Sohail et al. (2020) reveal that there is a significant

V . CONCLUSION & RECOMMENDATIONS

5.1 Conclusion
This systematic review aimed to analyse the impact of social media marketing on consumer purchasing
decision by synthesising evidence from 32 empirical studies published between 2019 and 2025. The results show that
all social media marketing elements are important in determining consumers' purchasing behaviour, and that
informative and interactive social media activities, electronic word -of-mouth (eWOM) social media, influencer
marketing, consumer engagement and personalization, and positive brand image are the most important ones that
affect consumers' purchase intention and purchase decisions. Together, these strategies improve the consumer's ability
to get information, interact with brands, and assess products – and purchase them.
The review has also revealed that brand trust, brand loyalty, consumer trust and information credibility are key
mechanisms that affect consumer behaviour in the context of social media marketing. Of these, brand trust proved to
be the most stable relationship between SMM and purchase intention, and brand loyalty strengthened the relationsh ip
between consumers and brands. Altogether, these insights emphasize that successful social media marketing is not just
about promotion; it's about establishing trust, credibility, and authentic connections with consumers.  This review
article makes a valuable and important contribution to the literature that synthesizes recen t empirical findings from a
variety of sectors and locations. The results provide feedback for researchers looking to further develop research on
digital consumer behaviour and for practitioners looking to create better social media marketing strategies.
Organizations that focus on integrated marketing activities that foster trust, engagement, and positive brand
perceptions can reinforce consumer purchase decisions and cultivate long -term, sustainable relationships with
customers.

5.2 Recommendations
Based on the findings of this systematic review, organizations should adopt integrated social media marketing
strategies that combine informative and interactive content, electronic word-of-mouth (eWOM), influencer marketing,
personalization, and consumer engag ement to strengthen consumer purchase decisions. Marketers should prioritize
transparent communication, credible information, authentic interactions, and positive user -generated content to build
brand trust and loyalty, which emerged as important mechanism s linking social media marketing with consumer
purchasing behaviour. Organizations should also monitor online reviews and consumer feedback and tailor their social
media strategies to the characteristics of their target audiences, product categories, and m arket contexts, given that the
effectiveness of specific strategies varies across consumer segments and settings.
