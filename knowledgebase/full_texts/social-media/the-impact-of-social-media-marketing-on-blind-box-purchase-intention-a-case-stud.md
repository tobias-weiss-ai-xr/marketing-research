Abstract. The swift evolution of social media marketing has established platforms like
Xiaohongshu and Douyin as pivotal conduits for disseminating product details and engaging
with consumers. Concurrently, the inherent characteristics of blind box consumption—
namely its unpredictability, entertainment appeal, collectibility, and emotional resonance—
have garnered significant attention from young consumers in recent years. The convergence
of the blind box economy with social media marketing strategies not only facilitates the
transmission of fundamental product information but also ignites consumer curiosity.
Factors such as heightened emotional engagement, elevated perceived value, and amplified
social influence directly shape purchasing intentions. While existing literature
predominantly focuses on individual determinants of blind box consumption or the broader
landscape of social media marketing, there remains a notable scarcity of research addressing
this topic. This study investigates the variations in how distinct categories of social media
platforms impact consumer behavior regarding blind boxes, with a specific focus on
comparing Xiaohongshu and Douyin. Grounded in the Stimulus-Organism-Response
framework, the research employs a mixed-method approach comprising literature reviews,
case studies, and textual analysis to examine blind box-related content on these two
platforms. By synthesizing the observed disparities, this paper aims to deepen the
understanding of the psychological mechanisms driving blind box purchases within the
context of social media marketing. Furthermore, the findings are intended to offer strategic
guidance for blind box brands, enabling them to develop differentiated content marketing
strategies that align with the unique characteristics of each social media platform.
Keywords: Blind Box Economy, Social Media Marketing, Purchase Intention, S-O-R Model

1. Introduction
1.1. Research background
1.1.1. Development of the blind box market
With the continuous development of the global economy, people's desire for consumption and their
demand for products are also increasing day by day. Under such market demands, some new
marketing models have gradually emerged, and the blind box economy is one of the newly emerging
marketing models. Within the Chinese market, blind boxes have gained significant traction among
the younger demographic, largely due to their inherent element of unpredictability [1]. At the same
time, from a global perspective, the blind box economy continues to grow [2].
1.1.2. Characteristics of blind box consumption
However, at the same time, blind box consumption also has its own characteristics. The blind box, in
addition to surprise, also has the elements of uncertainty, experience, and scarcity [3]. First is the
element of randomness: the consumer can not have a clear understanding of a specific style or type
of product before he is going to purchase. Therefore, there is a sense of surprise and uncertainty in
the process of consumption; consumers are often stimulated by the hope of an unknown result and
the feeling of surprise, which is also the entertainment value. Next is the emotional value: during the
purchasing process, the consumer not only gets the product, but also the emotion, such as surprise,
happiness, excitement, and the desire to collect. Finally, there is the social aspect: the random nature
of blind boxes has led to a community, in which consumers interact with others and build
connections with each other by such ways of sharing photos of the boxes, trading, talking, and
collecting. Finally, there are the factors of collectibility and scarcity, and also impulse buying and
repeat purchase. The collectible and scarce nature of blind boxes, for example, series-based design,
hidden edition, and limited edition, directly leads to impulse buying and repeat purchase of the same
product.
1.1.3. Development of social media marketing
The past decade has witnessed the development of complex, multifarious, and intensified
interactions between firms and by using the social media to get their customers [4]. This can be split
into two lines of development. The first is the evolution from the media to social media. In the early
days, the internet mainly served the function of information acquisition and spreading; in the case of
consumers, that is to say, they could only passively get the information released by the company.
However, with the continuous development and popular use of new media, the Internet market has
gradually evolved from one-way communication to a mass market with the characteristics of
interactivity, sociality, and mass. The second is the evolution from text-based social media to
images, then to short video and live streaming, which means that, in the Internet market, consumers
are no longer only "binge-watching ads," but they directly participate in the spreading of consumer
information in the form of interaction, recommendation, review, unboxing video, likes, and
comments.

1.1.4. The role of social media in blind box purchase intention
Given the characteristics of blind box consumption and the development of the social media market,
the two are very compatible. If people focus on purchase intention, social media can magnify all
kinds of characteristics of blind box consumption and have an impact on consumers' purchase
intention. First, the unboxing videos that show the "unknown result" have magnified the
characteristic of blind boxes that is random; it can make consumers' curiosity and increase the
willingness to buy. Second, the emergence of online communities, where people comment, share,
show off their collection, and even trade blind boxes on social media, strengthens the social
acceptance and peer influence of consumer groups, which can also boost the willingness to buy.
Next, the entertainment value of blind box purchases has been magnified by short unboxing videos
and live-streamed unboxing on various social media, which can greatly enhance the entertainment
experience for blind box consumers. Finally, people can also show the completeness of product
series and hidden editions on new media, which can boost purchase intent by stimulating consumers'
desire to collect.
1.2. Research problem and gap
At this stage, social media marketing has been deeply integrated with the blind box economy.
Different social media platforms are different in terms of communication environment, audience,
and the dissemination model. As social media marketing and the blind box economy come together,
different social media platforms may influence the decision of the consumer in different ways. But
so far, the relevant studies mainly study the relationship between social media marketing and general
consumers. There is limited research on the relationship with blind box purchase intention. There is
also limited comparative research on how different content dissemination patterns on Xiaohongshu
and Douyin influence blind box consumers.
1.3. Research questions and objectives
1.3.1. Research questions
• How do different types of social media content influence consumers' blind box purchase intention?
• What differences exist between Xiaohongshu and Douyin in influencing blind box consumer
behavior?
• How do consumer psychological factors influence the relationship between social media content
and blind box purchase intention?
1.3.2. Objectives
• To examine the influence of different types of social media content on blind box purchase
intention.
• To compare the effects of Xiaohongshu and Douyin on blind box purchase intention.
• To examine the role of consumer psychological factors in the relationship between social media
content and blind box purchase intention.

2. Literature review
2.1. Social media marketing and blind box purchase intention
The research shows that social media is a medium of interaction, not only as it is used in customer
relationship management and sales execution [5]. Social media marketing is the term that this
research is using for the method in which organizations use online media to interact with target
consumers and customers, to market their products or services, to relate with others, to build
relationships, and to build the community. Here, it is not one-way like in other media.
On the contrary, this domain is characterized by a large degree of engagement and interactivity.
Consumers are not only passive receivers of corporate promotional materials, but also participate in
spreading the information by doing actions, such as commenting, posting, liking, and producing, etc.
The main elements of social media marketing are user-generated content (UGC), electronic word-of-
mouth (eWOM), product reviews and recommendations, strategies with Key Opinion Leaders
(KOLs) and Key Opinion Consumers (KOCs), and the initiative with short-form video format and
live broadcasting [6-10].
At the fundamental level, the practice of using social media for marketing includes a scope that is
larger than simple promos;
This is a good combination of creating content, interaction with the audience, and spreading of
information to a wide range of people. There are some studies in academic work that show how
these marketing tricks have a significant impact on purchasing behavior. In this respect, electronic
word-of-mouth (eWOM) is especially a key element. Indeed, eWOM is seen as an important source
of information that influences human behavior. It is crucial in how a consumer makes buying
decisions [7]. In other words, before buying, prospective customers often use social media in order
to check peer evaluations, personal experience, and suggestions. These have a great influence on
their final decisions and intent to buy. From the consumer's point of view, this type of user-created
content is considered more reliable and convincing than regular corporate advertising.
The relevance of this information distribution model is particularly clear in the blind box sector.
By watching unboxing videos, by looking at product reviews, and by talking about collection
highlights, the consumer can make its perception of these products and makes the willingness to
buy.
2.2. KOL, KOC and influencer
Apart from digital word of mouth, the KOLs and KOCs are another important part of social media
marketing. They are able to create trust in the consumer [9]. Usually, they shape the attitude and
views of the consumers concerning the product by giving recommendations, talking about their own
experiences of using the product, and keeping interaction with the audiences. Therefore, the effect of
social media marketing on purchasing intent is not only due to the contents of the promotion but also
due to the trust, identification, and emotion of the consumer with the contents.

2.4. S-O-R model (stimulus–organism–response)
The S-O-R framework was introduced by Mehrabian and Russell [14], who used it to explain the
way the external environmental signals may affect the internal psychological condition of an
individual and determine the behavioral results. The idea is based on three elements: stimulus,
organism, and response. In this triad, the word stimulus mainly refers to those informational inputs
and activities coming from the environment, while the word organism means the cognitive and
emotional-psychological changes that an individual experiences with such an external trigger.
The term "response" means the actions and inclinations that people finally show in this process.
In the study of consumer behavior, the S-O-R is an strong mechanism to explain the relations
between the outside marketing stimuli and the mental conditions as well as the conduct of buyers.
Thus, in the present investigation, the marketing materials and the campaigns on the social platform
for the blind boxes are taken as outside stimuli. The internal psychological states that consumers
have experienced after their exposure to such content (such as curiosity, collecting motives,
emotional reaction, perceptual appraisal, and social pressure) are finally converted into the ultimate
behavioral result, that is, one buys the blind boxes or his/her intention to buy the blind boxes.
Obviously, the S-O-R model not only provides the theoretical basis for this kind of research but also
helps to examine the logical relations and distinctions among the different variables under study.
2.5. Literature summary and research gap
Although the link between social media marketing campaigns and consumer behavior has been
firmly established by previous investigations, the literature that is currently prevailing has mostly
focused on standard commodities and services. Thus, there is a lack of scholarly attention to items
that are characterized by their own intrinsic unobservability and appeal to emotional purchase
motives. In addition, the current studies mostly consider social media as a unitary construct and do
not investigate the different variations across different platforms in terms of the types of content, the
way the content is shown, the way the users are involved, and the kind of users. Thus, using the
general framework of "social media marketing" only is not enough to capture the heterogeneity and
the different effects exerted by the specific digital channel.
In the particular context of blind box merchandise, the buying decisions are actually based on a
mixture of elements that are not only on the physical aspect of these things. In particular, in the
psychological drivers of the purchase, people can consider inquisitiveness, the need to have the
complete collection, the like, the emotional bond, and peer pressure. Therefore, there is a need to
investigate more in-depth the working of the content of the social networking sites in any way it

2.6. Methodology: literature review, case study, text analysis
In order to explore the relationship between social media marketing strategies and the behavior of
consumers of blind boxes, as well as the differences between various marketing formats, this
research takes a qualitative approach. The methodological design is a literature review, case studies,
and text analysis. This research choose Xiaohongshu and Douyin as the main case platforms. With
the systematic collection, analysis, and interpretation of both the literature and the marketing
material of blind box purchases on these two sites, the study examines the differences in marketing
content in this consumption domain.
3. Comparative analysis of Xiaohongshu and Douyin
3.1. Case background
3.1.1. Xiaohongshu
Xiaohongshu is also a social media platform, which is mainly characterized by sharing content, the
discussion on lifestyle, the exchange of experiences, and the acquisition of consumer information.
The users mainly share parts of their lives, product recommendations, and consumer experiences in
text-and-image posts and videos. In addition, they like, comment, save, and share with other users.
In Xiaohongshu, consumers can use it as an important channel to discover products, search for
information, and make purchase decisions.
For brands, Xiaohongshu is not only a platform suitable for displaying products, but also a
channel for brands to reach consumers and do content marketing to consumers. For the purchase of
blind boxes, with the help of Xiaohongshu, the information about the product is combined with the
personal experience of the consumer; the consumer is able to learn about and be interested in the
products when browsing.
3.1.2. Douyin
Douyin is a social media platform that focuses on short-form video content and real-time interaction.
It has kept short videos as its content and slowly expanded to many other features such as live
streaming, creator marketing, and social interaction. The content is suggested by the content
recommendation algorithm based on users' interests. Users can like, comment, share, and follow the
content and help to spread the content.
For brands, Douyin is a place for brands to promote products and do marketing via short videos
and other forms, and reach users with the same interests quickly. For content like blind box
unboxing, which has a high degree of randomness and the look is more beautiful in itself, it also fits
Douyin well and can also attract the attention and emotions of users with the same interests.
3.2. Comparison of Xiaohongshu and Douyin
Today, both Xiaohongshu and Douyin are major social media platforms. They are not only similar in
the sense that both of them can be good tool to do social media marketing, but also in different terms

3.4. Effects of social media content
Through the comparison of the content on social media and the influence it creates when research

At first, the core content on the Xiaohongshu platform is product shares, reviews, experiences,

use of other consumers' experiences, they can help the consumer to make a rational and objective
decision on whether to buy the product or not.
The content in Douyin is mainly presented in the form of short videos, live streams, and visual
stimuli. The content complements the highly visual and random nature of blind boxes very well. For
users and consumers on Douyin, they do not have to know a great deal about brands and products in
advance. While they are browsing short videos, they would be drawn in by the element of surprise,
visual stimuli, and emotional appeal.

4. Discussion
4.1. Why different types of social media content influence blind box purchase intention
Given that the inherent attributes of the blind boxes are randomness, entertainment value, and
collectibility, consumers' purchasing intentions are not only influenced by the product information
itself, but also by curiosity, emotional experiences, emotional value, and the intentions of others.
Different types of social media content would influence consumers' willingness to purchase blind
boxes, because different content provides different external stimuli and gives different psychological
response of the consumers.
On the one hand, specific types of content, which mainly talk about or provide information about
the product itself, i.e., product reviews, unboxing videos, purchasing experience, etc., can also
stimulate the consumers' perceived value of blind boxes directly, because they can provide
information about the product, product quality, price, product lines, collectible value, etc., and they
can guide the consumers' purchasing intention. On the other hand, specific forms of entertainment
content, such as short-time unboxing videos, live streams, etc., can create entertainment and
suspense by stimulating the visual, the immediate outcome of the things, the collective resonance,
and the emotional value, which can make consumers curious and emotionally engaged, and can also
influence the consumers' purchasing intention.
Besides, some types of marketing content (e.g., posts by KOLs and KOCs) can also show that the
product is popular and accepted by most of the people on social media, which can use social
influence to lead and stimulate consumers' purchase intention.
4.2. Why Xiaohongshu and Douyin may have different effects on blind box purchase intention
Because the mainstream content formats on Xiaohongshu and Douyin are different, and since they
stimulate consumers in different ways, of course the psychological response is also different, and of
course the willingness to purchase of the consumers will be affected.
On the Xiaohongshu platform, the content is more about product information; it stimulates
consumers by emphasizing the perceived value and make the consumer tends to make more rational
purchases. On the contrary, Douyin depends more on entertaining photography, emotion, and
immediate stimulation to stimulate the consumer to have the curiosity and emotion of the visuals,
and make the consumer make more impulsive purchasing decisions.
4.3 How consumer psychological factors explain the relationship between social media content
and purchase intention
Social media marketing triggers consumer awareness through external information. So when
consumers are faced with different kinds of blind box contents, they will have different
psychological reactions, and these psychological reactions will affect the ultimate purchase intent of

5. Implications
The significance of this study lies primarily in its practical implications, which can be explained
from three perspectives.
First, this study can also be used as a reference for the social media marketing strategy of the
blind box brand. For other social media platforms on the market, such as Xiaohongshu and Douyin,
they will have some differences in the characteristics of content dissemination; the brand should not
use exactly the same content strategy on all social media. For instance, on Xiaohongshu and Douyin,
the strategies for marketing should be different for the fact that the emphasis on product information
and entertainment value is different in these two.
Second, from the perspective of consumer psychology, this study can also help the marketer to
know whether social media marketing is effective or not. Meanwhile, if one is going to do social
media marketing, the brand should not only pay attention to the number of views, likes, and reach of
the content itself, but also whether the content can make consumers feel whether the content can
stimulate consumers' curiosity, feelings, perceived value, and social influence. For instance,
Marketers can give out "mystery things" and show the unboxing result to stimulate consumers'
curiosity, and marketers can mark the IP story and character present to evoke consumers' feelings.
Finally, this study can also help with content distribution and allocation of marketing resources
for the different social media platforms. Brands can have differentiated strategies according to the
characteristics of the users and the content dissemination of each platform, and deploy the content
formats that are suitable for each platform according to the marketing strategies and the goals of the
brands. For instance, when the brand wants to make consumers interested in a new product line, it
can make consumers curious by creating suspense or showing a random result.
6. Conclusion
Focusing on the blind box economy, this research investigates social media marketing practices
specifically within the contexts of Xiaohongshu and Douyin. The study delves into the variations in
promotional tactics employed across diverse social media environments, while also scrutinizing how
distinct stimuli evoke unique psychological reactions that subsequently influence final purchasing
decisions. Grounded in the S-O-R framework, the analysis utilizes a combination of literature
reviews, case studies, and textual analysis to compare the nature of blind box content and its
dissemination patterns on these two platforms.
Research outcomes indicate that diverse categories of social media channels can provoke distinct
psychological reactions in buyers by means of varied content stimuli, yet these pathways ultimately
converge to generate identical purchasing intentions. Within this context, the two platforms
examined in this investigation, namely Xiaohongshu and Douyin, demonstrate notable divergences.
Specifically, Xiaohongshu enhances users' perceived value by providing informational and

In summary, the research indicates that no universal content format exists that is superior across
all digital platforms. Instead, the effectiveness of content in driving purchase intention depends on
how well its specific attributes resonate with consumers' psychological states. The influence of
social media materials on the willingness to buy blind boxes is not a straightforward phenomenon.
Rather, the primary function of social media marketing is to trigger diverse psychological reactions
by employing varied content styles. This insight clarifies why distinct content strategies on different
platforms can alter consumer purchasing desires for blind boxes, offering valuable direction for
brands aiming to craft tailored marketing approaches for each specific channel.
