Challenges of ChatGPT in AI

Although ChatGPT provides significant opportunities for improving marketing practices, its adoption also involves
several challenges that organizations need to carefully address. One of the major challenges is

AI

Another important challenge is the

-

Organizations need to develop ethical guidelines to ensure responsible and fair use of AI technologies.
