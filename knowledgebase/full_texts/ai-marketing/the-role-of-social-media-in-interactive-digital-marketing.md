Unlike traditional marketing methods, social media platforms enable brands to
communicate with their target audiences more interactively and dynamically, while also
allowing consumers to engage directly and instantaneously with brands. As an integral
part of contemporary marketing, the impact of social media on interactive marketing is
profound. This highlights the need to explore the strong synergy between social media
and interactive marketing, which has become a vital component of digital marketing. This
research seeks to answer the pivotal question: “What is the role of social media in enhancing
interactive digital marketing strategies?” A systematic literature review using the PRISMA
framework was conducted to provide a comprehensive analysis of relevant research in the
field. This study aims to examine the evolving role of social media in interactive marketing
and to highlight how this synergy contributes to shaping effective digital marketing
strategies. The findings show that social media platforms have revolutionized marketing
strategies by facilitating direct, real-time interaction with consumers. In addition, the study
underlines the critical role of continuous innovation in maintaining competitive advantage,
particularly through artificial intelligence (AI) and personalized interaction. Marketers can
optimize social media strategies by focusing on real-time, interactive engagement and
utilizing AI technologies, such as chatbots and virtual influencers, to strengthen consumer
relationships and create personalized brand interactions. This study contributes to the
literature by demonstrating that the integration of social media and interactive marketing
can significantly enhance brand loyalty and business performance through more effective
and personalized consumer engagement.

1. Introduction
The evolving landscape of social media offers marketers innovative
avenues to engage consumers through tailored content and targeted
strategies, particularly in digital marketing (Appel et al., 2020). These
platforms have become essential tools, reshaping consumer engagement
and brand promotion while significantly influencing customer
relationships and enhancing marketing effectiveness (Hennig-Thurau et
al., 2010). Increasingly, individuals perceive social media applications
as integral to their daily lives, actively engaging with platforms such
as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Twitter (Alalwan et al., 2017).
These platforms empower companies to influence consumer behavior by
enhancing brand awareness, providing product information, impacting
purchasing decisions, facilitating direct interactions, and enabling post-
purchase evaluations (Mangold & Faulds, 2009). According to Kotler
and Keller (2014), modern marketing prioritizes understanding and
meeting customer needs through value-driven strategies, with social
media at the heart of this process. Effective marketing necessitates a
strategic approach to value creation and delivery, as emphasized by
Kotler (1994).
The profound impact of social media on digital marketing strategies
is evident through Web 2.0 tools, which present valuable opportunities
for analyzing social networks and creating targeted engagement
strategies, deepening our understanding of consumer behavior (Cooke
&Buckley, 2008). Companies increasingly integrate social media into
their business plans to establish a robust online presence (Chatterjee
&Kar, 2020), while technological advancements have broadened
digital marketing methods to include mobile devices and other digital
channels (Desai &Vidyapeeth, 2019). These platforms facilitate real-
time consumer engagement, allowing brands to gain visibility within
dynamic communities (Roberts &Kraynak, 2008), and social media
marketing leverages diverse channels to promote brands and products,
complementing traditional online marketing strategies such as email
campaigns and web advertising (Barefoot &Szabo, 2009).
Given the rapid development of technology, the relationship between
social media and marketing requires continuous exploration. Kaplan
and Haenlein (2010) examine social media user behaviors and highlight
how businesses can leverage these platforms to develop effective
marketing strategies. Similarly, Mangold & Faulds (2009) explore the
hybrid nature of social media in the marketing communication mix,
emphasizing the integration of traditional methods with social media
strategies. Tuten et al. (2015) provide an overview of social media
marketing, identifying key strategies for maximizing online presence.
In contrast, He et al. (2013) delve into the competitive advantages
derived from social media analysis and text mining, demonstrating how
companies can extract actionable insights from user-generated content.
Computational techniques now play a pivotal role in automating
influencer selection, optimizing campaign targeting, and enhancing
interactive consumer engagement (Gui et al., 2025).
Qualman (2012) discusses the broader societal impacts of social
media, offering insights for businesses navigating this complex
landscape. Collectively, these studies underscore the necessity of
ongoing exploration at the intersection of social media and marketing.
The emergence of new platforms like TikTok and Snapchat highlights
the importance of continually reassessing their interactions, particularly
within digital marketing strategies. In this context, Chen, Harncharnchai,
and Saeheaw (2021) conducted a systematic literature review of 16 peer-
reviewed articles published between 2016 and 2020, focusing on SMEs’
use of social media marketing. Their study identified current research
trends, methods, theoretical approaches, and platforms, while also
highlighting the potential for future research in areas such as customer
knowledge management and TikTok marketing. Similarly, Bryła et al.
(2022) conducted a systematic literature review of 70 empirical studies
published between 2014 and 2022, examining the impact of social
media marketing on consumer engagement in sustainable consumption,
and found that influencer marketing, creatively designed content, and
strong brand relationships are key drivers—particularly among socially
driven and entrepreneurial youth. AI-generated influencers can evoke
similar levels of perceived personalization and consumer engagement
as human influencers, albeit with slightly lower trust (Sands et al.,
2022). This study aims to explore how social media contributes to the
advancement of interactive digital marketing by enabling dynamic
communication and fostering real-time consumer engagement in the
development of strategic marketing practices. The findings provide
valuable insights into the integration of social media and interactive
marketing, highlighting how real-time consumer engagement and
personalized communication contribute to enhancing brand loyalty and
business performance.
The subsequent sections are structured to provide a comprehensive
analysis of the role of social media in digital marketing strategies.
We begin with the methodology, detailing our systematic literature
review approach guided by the PRISMA (Preferred Reporting Items
for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses) framework. This is
followed by a discussion of social media’s strategic role and impact
in enhancing brand visibility and customer engagement. We then
explore the opportunities and challenges presented by social media in
marketing, highlighting both benefits and potential risks. The results
section presents our findings on the effectiveness of social media
strategies, while the discussion interprets these results in the context of
existing literature. Finally, we conclude by summarizing key insights
and offering recommendations for future research and practice in this
dynamic field.
2. Materials and Methods
This study investigates the evolving relationship between social media
and digital marketing, focusing specifically on their interaction within
interactive marketing strategies. As digital platforms continuously
reshape consumer behavior and marketing practices, a systematic and
transparent review of the existing literature is crucial to uncover how
social media enhances the effectiveness of digital marketing initiatives.
The methodology is introduced by detailing a systematic literature
review approach guided by the PRISMA (Preferred Reporting Items
for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses) framework (Page, 2021).
A PRISMA framework was employed to systematically assess
publication trends, authorship patterns, and collaboration dynamics
among the selected studies. To ensure academic quality and relevance,
the search was limited to peer-reviewed journal articles published
in English between 1995 and 2025. Key indicators summarizing the
dataset—such as the number of publications, average document age,
authorship statistics, and collaboration metrics—are presented in Table
1. This table provides a comprehensive overview of the publication
and authorship characteristics of studies conducted over a 30-year
period. The average age of the documents is 9 years, indicating that the
analyzed research is relatively recent, while still incorporating some
older studies. In terms of authorship, a total of 150 individual authors
contributed to the literature. Of these, 20 were responsible for single-
authored publications. Approximately 28% of the analyzed documents
were written by a single author, suggesting that although individual
contributions are notable, collaborative research dominates the field. On
average, there were 2.3 co-authors per document, further emphasizing
the prevalence of co-authorship and collaboration within the dataset.
Through a comprehensive literature search, strict eligibility criteria,
and systematic screening, the study synthesizes prevailing themes,
challenges, and research gaps. This foundation enables an informed
discussion on the contributions of social media to interactive digital
marketing strategies.

2.1. Research Questions
This study aims to address the central research question: “What
is the role of social media in enhancing interactive digital marketing
strategies?” This clearly defined research question establishes the focus
and scope of the systematic review.
2.2. Eligibility Criteria
To ensure the inclusion of relevant and academically rigorous
studies, the following eligibility criteria were applied: only peer-
reviewed journal articles published in English and indexed in reputable
academic databases such as Scopus and Web of Science were included.
Special attention was given to studies that directly address topics such
as the effectiveness of social media marketing, consumer engagement
on social media platforms, and the impact of social media on brand
perception. To maintain methodological consistency and academic
quality, non-academic sources, conference proceedings, book chapters,
and grey literature were excluded from the review.
2.3. Studies Synthesis
The selected articles were subjected to a detailed analysis to identify
major trends, authorship characteristics, collaboration patterns, and
thematic focuses within the field. These insights provided the foundation
for synthesizing key findings, identifying research gaps, and proposing
future directions.
2.4. Literature Search and Selection
A systematic search was conducted in Scopus, Web of Science,
and Google Scholar databases, targeting the TITLE, ABSTRACT,
and KEYWORD fields. The keywords used were “Social Media
Marketing”, “Digital Marketing” and “Interactive Marketing”. Filters
were applied to select only peer-reviewed journal articles published
in English. The keywords were primarily searched within the titles,
abstracts, and/or keyword sections, resulting in an initial retrieval of
7,652 articles. After applying the inclusion and exclusion criteria—such
as journal relevance and limiting the timespan from 1995 to 2025—a
total of 4450 articles were selected, as illustrated in Figure 1. A total
of 62 articles were identified across these databases. Throughout the
process, studies unrelated to the research topic were excluded, and
duplicate records were removed. To ensure the reliability and quality of
the review, only peer-reviewed articles were included.
3. Results
3.1.Transformation of marketing strategies
through social media
Technological advancements have significantly transformed
marketing processes, making them more dynamic, data-driven, and
personalized. Social media is a crucial component of digital marketing,
influencing various promotional activities. It enables brands to
communicate quickly and directly with customers, thus personalizing
and accelerating interactions. Unlike traditional marketing, social media
marketing promotes products and services while reaching a broader and
more diverse audience (Weinberg, 2009). Through its Social Customer
Relationship Management (CRM) capabilities, social media enhances
marketing performance with features such as real-time feedback and
customer engagement (Luo et al., 2024). These platforms facilitate
interactive, customer-focused marketing approaches, contributing
to more effective strategies. The viral nature of social media makes
it an appealing tool for businesses seeking to market their offerings
effectively (Xiang and Gretzel, 2010). Mangold and Faulds (2009)
argue that social media serves as a hybrid element in the promotion mix,
bridging traditional and digital marketing strategies to enhance brand
communication and customer engagement.
Social media encompasses various sub-disciplines within marketing,
including promotions, marketing intelligence, sentiment analysis,
public relations, marketing communications, and product management
(Tanuri, 2010). This transformative power fundamentally changes how
brands engage with consumers, fostering personalized and meaningful
connections. Receiving real-time feedback allows brands to swiftly
adapt their strategies, while extensive reach facilitates engagement
with diverse demographic groups. Ultimately, social media serves as
a critical bridge between traditional and digital marketing, enabling
brands to create integrated campaigns that build deeper relationships
with consumers. The strategic use of social media enhances brand
visibility and cultivates long-term loyalty. This multifaceted impact
underscores the necessity for brands to continually refresh their social
media strategies to ensure they remain competitive and relevant in an
ever-evolving marketplace.
This theme includes 15 studies that specifically focus on the
transformation of marketing strategies through social media. The
studies were selected based on their contribution to understanding how
personalization, interactivity, and real-time responsiveness—though
long-standing marketing concepts—have been radically redefined
in the age of digital platforms. For instance, Cooke and Buckley
(2008) highlight how Web 2.0 tools allow marketers to analyze
social networks and extract behavioral insights, a development far
beyond the reach of traditional methods. Recent literature introduces
new approaches and deeper transformations. Studies such as Koay
et al. (2021) and Omeish et al. (2024) explore how AI-enhanced
targeting, influencer marketing, and platform algorithms have elevated
personalization to a hyper-contextual level. Meanwhile, Chatterjee
and Kar (2020) provide insights into how SMEs strategically utilize
social media for cost-effective marketing scalability. Luo et al. (2024)
emphasize that social media’s real-time responsiveness is no longer
a bonus but a requirement in competitive digital spaces. Appel et al.
(2020) further note that the future of social media marketing lies in
adapting to platform-native behaviors and the algorithmic logic of
content visibility. Moreover, Bryła et al. (2022) demonstrate how social
media marketing now supports sustainable consumption behaviors,
particularly among entrepreneurial youth motivated by social influence
and brand affiliation. Their systematic review shows that influencer
marketing, creative content strategies, and strong brand relationships
are now essential to achieving both marketing goals and sustainability
engagement. The transformation of marketing strategies through social
media is not merely a shift in tools but a paradigm change in strategy,
structure, and interaction. Social platforms have evolved from passive
communication channels into strategic marketing ecosystems, requiring
brands to be continuously adaptive, responsive, and aligned with both
technological trends and consumer expectations. This multifaceted
impact underscores the necessity for brands to continually refresh their
strategies to remain relevant in a rapidly evolving landscape

3.2. Role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in
enhancing brand-consumer engagement
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become a pivotal element in the
evolution of social media marketing, deeply impacting how brands
engage with their audiences. The literature within this theme,
comprising 12 key studies, highlights several specific AI technologies
that play critical roles in transforming brand-consumer interactions,
such as virtual influencers, programmatic advertising, chatbots, and
augmented reality (AR).
Virtual influencers—computer-generated personas managed by AI—
have emerged as innovative brand ambassadors, allowing companies
to craft consistent, controllable, and engaging narratives without the
unpredictability linked to human influencers (Omeish et al., 2024).
These virtual figures foster consumer engagement by delivering
personalized and relatable content, bridging the gap between consumers
and brands in unique, immersive ways. Programmatic advertising,
driven by AI algorithms, automates the buying of digital advertising
space, enabling brands to target specific audiences with high precision
and optimize their campaigns in real-time. This technology enhances
marketing efficiency by reducing costs and maximizing relevance,
which improves user experience and engagement (Chen & Lin, 2019).
Chatbots serve as frontline customer service agents, providing instant,
round-the-clock responses to consumer inquiries. Their capacity to
deliver personalized, context-aware communication fosters positive
user experiences, increases customer satisfaction, and enhances
engagement by maintaining continuous dialogue between the brand
and its consumers (Omeish et al., 2024). Augmented reality (AR)
technologies allow brands to create immersive experiences that blend
digital content with the real world, enabling consumers to interact with
products virtually before purchase. This experiential marketing strategy
increases emotional connection, improves product understanding, and
boosts consumer confidence, leading to stronger brand loyalty (Chen
& Lin, 2019).
Beyond these technologies, AI’s advanced data analytics capabilities
enable brands to capture and interpret vast quantities of consumer
behavior data, allowing for the development of highly tailored
marketing strategies. This deep understanding of user preferences
facilitates personalized messaging and campaign optimization, which
in turn strengthens brand-consumer relationships (Luo et al., 2024).
Together, these AI-driven technologies collectively enhance brand-
consumer engagement by creating highly personalized, interactive, and
immersive experiences that transcend traditional marketing approaches.
They help brands respond swiftly to consumer needs, foster trust through
continuous communication, and build emotional connections that drive
loyalty in increasingly competitive digital markets. Therefore, while
traditional marketing emphasized mass communication, AI facilitates
hyper-personalization and immediacy, transforming consumer
expectations and brand strategies alike. Brands that effectively integrate
these AI tools within their social media marketing efforts are better
positioned to cultivate lasting engagement and sustained competitive
advantage.
3.3. Social media’s influence on marketing
strategies
The evolution of marketing definitions demonstrates a clear shift
from a simple business activity focused on the flow of goods to a
comprehensive organizational process centered on creating, delivering,
and managing customer value and relationships. This progression
underscores the increasing strategic and relational dimensions of
marketing, which have been significantly amplified by the rise of social
media. Table 2 presents a timeline of marketing definitions spanning
from 1935 to 2008 (Rownd and Heath, 2008), reflecting the expanding
focus from transactional exchanges to value creation among diverse
stakeholders. However, given the rapid expansion of social media’s
role in marketing strategies since 2020, this timeline would benefit
from incorporating more recent definitions to capture contemporary
developments. For instance, Appel et al. (2020) conceptualize marketing
as the process of creating personalized and data-driven customer
experiences through digitalization and social media tools, highlighting
the transformation brought about by social media marketing. Likewise,
Luo et al. (2024) describe social media marketing as a dynamic process
involving real-time customer interaction and social CRM capabilities,
emphasizing its interactive and relational nature.
Companies are increasingly refining their social media strategies by
leveraging platforms such as Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube
(Leonardi et al., 2013). These platforms are integral to contemporary
marketing, facilitating enhanced engagement and strategic outreach.
Facebook, as one of the most utilized social media platforms, enables
direct user connections and is vital for brand marketing, allowing for
seamless information exchange and interaction between brands and
consumers (Brodie et al., 2013; Simon & Tossan, 2018). Its substantial
monthly visitor count provides brands with invaluable insights into
consumer behavior and trends (Casteleyn, 2019). Twitter, launched
in 2006, permits users to share brief messages, fostering a vital
communication channel for businesses to engage with consumers in
real-time (Kaplan & Haenlein, 2010). Instagram, established in 2010,
emphasizes visual content, where influencers significantly shape
followers’, impulse buying behaviors (Koay et al., 2021). Additionally,
YouTube, especially after its acquisition by Google, has emerged as
a premier platform for video content, facilitating interactions between
consumers and brands (Agnew & Sindhav, 2009).
In the realm of digital marketing, these platforms collectively offer
diverse opportunities for brands to connect with audiences. Facebook’s
detailed targeting capabilities allow for tailored campaigns, while
Twitter (now “X”) facilitates real-time conversations. Instagram’s
visual storytelling and YouTube’s long-form content enable deeper
brand connections. However, challenges such as adapting to algorithm
changes and maintaining content quality remain significant. Successfully
navigating these dynamics is crucial for marketers aiming to harness
social media effectively. Kaplan and Haenlein (2010) assert that social
media transforms consumer interactions by enhancing communication
and interaction, thus creating a dynamic environment where brands
can engage instantly with their audiences. This user-generated content
fosters meaningful relationships, allowing brands to adapt their
strategies based on immediate consumer feedback, thereby increasing
brand awareness. Further, Mangold and Faulds (2009) highlight the
hybrid role of social media in the marketing communication mix,
emphasizing its capacity to elevate brand visibility and strengthen
consumer interactions.
Social media’s facilitation of content creation and sharing enables
brands to cultivate deeper relationships with consumers, enhancing
brand loyalty through positive behavioral reinforcement. In terms of
strategic decision-making, He et al. (2013) illustrate how systematic
analysis of social media data can yield actionable insights. Brands that
analyze social media data are better positioned to understand consumer
behavior, identify trends, and develop effective marketing strategies.

emergence of new technologies and trends necessitates ongoing
adaptation of strategies, complicating efforts to maintain effective
engagement and manage information flow. This dynamic environment
enables marketers to deliver nuanced messages and leverage detailed
customer data for precise targeting, thereby enhancing social interaction
and customer service. However, challenges such as managing user-

Understanding the key dimensions and drivers of engagement in
online brand communities is essential for maximizing the effectiveness
of social media and digital marketing strategies. According to Dessart
et al. (2015), engagement can be broken down into critical dimensions,
including cognition (mental processes involved in content interaction),
affect (emotional responses to brand interactions), and behaviors
(actions resulting from engagement). Each of these dimensions
comprises specific sub-dimensions influencing how consumers connect
with brands online. Recognizing and analyzing these dimensions
helps businesses tailor their social media strategies to foster deeper,
more meaningful interactions. For instance, understanding cognitive
engagement allows brands to create relevant and compelling content that
captures consumers’ attention. Emotional engagement can be enhanced
by developing content that resonates with consumers’ feelings, fostering
stronger brand loyalty. Additionally, donation intention is influenced by
emotional attachment to content creators and functional dependency on
social media (Wan, 2017). This underscores the significance of social
factors like identity and engagement, alongside technical factors such
as sociability and personalization, in shaping consumer behavior within
digital marketing strategies.
Social media has markedly enhanced the effectiveness of marketing
strategies by increasing brand visibility and facilitating direct
engagement with target audiences. Real-time interactions provided
by these platforms allow for more dynamic and responsive marketing
efforts. This capability supports improved customer relationship
management and enables personalized marketing approaches, crucial
for achieving higher levels of engagement and loyalty. Understanding
the factors that drive online content to become viral significantly
impacts how social media can be leveraged for marketing success.
Strategies for creating viral content or utilizing content effectively are
essential for enhancing the success of social media campaigns (Berger
& Milkman, 2012).
Social media marketing presents numerous benefits and opportunities.
Social media platforms offer advanced tools for targeted advertising,
enabling precise audience segmentation. Social media analytics provide
valuable insights into consumer behavior and preferences, facilitating
data-driven decision-making and more effective marketing strategies.
These advantages contribute to greater brand recognition and enhanced
marketing effectiveness. Despite the benefits, integrating social media
into digital marketing strategies involves challenges. Continuous
content creation is necessary to maintain audience engagement, and
businesses must adapt to frequent changes in social media algorithms,
which can affect content visibility and effectiveness. Additionally,
maintaining a consistent brand message across various platforms and
managing user interactions efficiently requires strategic planning and
adaptability. Furthermore, structural equation modeling analysis has
shown that social stressors and technical stressors associated with social
media use can lead to employee exhaustion and anxiety, negatively
impacting work performance (Cao et al., 2024). This highlights the need
for organizations to consider employee well-being when developing
social media strategies, as a healthy workforce is crucial for sustaining
a competitive edge in the digital marketplace.

the digital marketplace. Brodie et al. (2013) emphasize that consumer
engagement within virtual brand communities is essential for enhancing
brand loyalty and driving marketing effectiveness, highlighting the

engagement not only strengthens the relationships between brands
and consumers but also provides valuable insights for refining digital
marketing strategies and enhancing the overall customer experience.

influences brand loyalty by fostering deeper emotional connections and
engagement with consumers, thereby enhancing the effectiveness of

social media as a tool for cultivating brand loyalty in the digital age. By
creating interactive environments that resonate with audiences, brands
can strengthen their relationships with consumers, leading to increased
loyalty and more favorable marketing outcomes. Understanding these
dynamics of social media interactions is crucial for marketers aiming to
refine their strategies and maximize their impact in today’s competitive
landscape.

understanding of both its opportunities and challenges. As the digital
environment continually evolves, marketers must adapt their strategies
to effectively engage with audiences while managing the complexities
of user-generated content, feedback, and privacy concerns. By
focusing on the dimensions of engagement—cognitive, affective, and

equally important to address the mental and emotional well-being of
employees involved in social media management. A balanced approach
that prioritizes both consumer engagement and employee wellness is
essential for maintaining a competitive edge in this dynamic landscape.

The findings of this study reveal the profound and multifaceted

the very foundations of marketing—from promotional activities
to customer engagement, and from content creation to data-driven
decision-making. First, the shift from traditional to digital and now

simultaneously supporting and challenging conventional marketing
logic. The immediacy and personalization facilitated by platforms such
as Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube exemplify how brands can now
foster real-time, tailored interactions. However, while this interactivity
enhances consumer engagement and brand visibility, it also demands a

saturated and fast-paced digital environment.

customize content at scale introduces new strategic possibilities

5. Conclusions
The study highlights the transformative role of social media and AI
in shaping contemporary marketing strategies. Throughout the thematic
analysis, key dimensions of how these technologies influence brand-
consumer interactions were explored, revealing the multifaceted nature
of their impact. This study addresses a current and significant topic in
digital marketing and social media strategies, aiming to contribute to
the field through a systematic literature review. The findings emphasize
several critical themes. First, UGC emerges as a catalyst for deeper
brand engagement, allowing consumers to contribute authentic content
that provides brands with real-time feedback and opportunities for
continuous strategy refinement. Second, social media platforms foster a
shift from traditional transactional marketing to relational marketing by
encouraging authentic brand-consumer interactions that build trust and
long-term loyalty. Third, the integration of AI tools—including chatbots,
virtual influencers, and augmented reality—enhances personalization
and immersive experiences, thereby enriching engagement and enabling
more precise targeting based on sophisticated data analysis. Fourth, the
vast amount of data generated through social media empowers brands
to tailor campaigns more effectively, increasing return on investment
and visibility while enhancing customer responsiveness and loyalty.
However, these advantages are accompanied by notable challenges.
Managing user-generated content, addressing negative feedback,
and ensuring data privacy and security require robust frameworks.
Furthermore, the operational demands and risks related to intellectual
property, privacy compliance, and employee well-being necessitate
careful resource allocation and risk mitigation strategies. It is important
to clarify that the thematic findings detailed earlier in the study form
the foundation of these conclusions, creating a coherent narrative that
links theoretical insights to practical implications. To strengthen the
academic contribution, this study acknowledges certain methodological
limitations, including the number of articles analyzed and the evolving
criteria for theme development. Explicit articulation of these aspects
would benefit future research.
Future studies could expand the scope by including more recent
and diverse publications, while clearly defining inclusion criteria and
thematic frameworks. In addition, further research is needed to enhance
data privacy and security measures within social media marketing,
particularly as reliance on personalized data and UGC grows.
Investigations into advanced encryption methods, compliance with
shifting privacy regulations, and cyber threat mitigation will be crucial.
By addressing these areas, future research can provide clearer guidelines
for practitioners and contribute to a more holistic understanding of the
evolving intersection between social media, AI, and marketing.
