ABSTRACT
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become an important part of modern digital marketing, especially through the use of AI-
based chatbots and recommendation systems. These technologies help businesses interact with customers more
efficiently by providing instant responses, personalized product suggestions, and continuous customer support. The main
objective of this study is to analyse consumer perception toward AI -based marketing chatbots and their influence on
online shopping behaviour. The research also examines the level of consumer awareness, satisfaction, trust, and
understanding of AI marketing tools. Primary data was collected from 100 respondents using a structured questionnaire,
while secondary data was obtained from books, journals, and online sources. The collected data was analysed using
percentage analysis and simple charts. The findings of the study indicate that most consumers are aware of AI marketing
tools and have a positive perception of chatbot services due to their convenience, quick responses, and personalized
recommendations. However, some consumers still have concerns about privacy, lack of human interaction, and technical
errors. The study concludes tha t AI chatbots play a significant role in improving customer experience and digital
marketing efficiency, but businesses must focus on improving accuracy, trust, and user experience to increase consumer
acceptance.

INTRODUCTION
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has significantly reshaped modern marketing by enabling businesses to communicate with
consumers in fast er and more intelligent ways. In today ’s digital world, customers expect immediate responses and
personalized experiences. To meet these expectations, companies are increasingly integrating AI -driven technologies
into their marketing strategies. Among these innovations, AI-based chatbots have gained considerable attention for their
ability to provide automated yet interactive customer support.AI -based marketing chatbots are software applications
designed to simulate human conversation through text or voice.
These systems can understand customer queries, process information, and deliver relevant responses in real time. The
early development of chatbot technology can be traced to programs such as ELIZA, which demonstrated that machines
could imitate basic hum an dialogue. Over time, advancements in artificial intelligence led to the creation of more
sophisticated systems like Siri and Alexa, which further expanded the possibilities of AI-driven communication.

STATEMENT OF THE PROBLEM
In the digital era, Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become an integral part of marketing activities, especially through the
use of AI-based chatbots and recommendation systems. These technologies are designed to improve customer interaction,
provide personalized services, and enhance overall marketing efficiency. However, despite their widespread adoption,
there is limited clarity on how consumers perceive these AI -based marketing tools. Many consumers still face issues
such as lack of trust, concerns abou t privacy and data security, absence of human touch, and doubts regarding the
accuracy and transparency of AI recommendations. At the same time, companies are increasingly relying on AI without
fully understanding consumer expectations and acceptance levels. This creates a gap between technological advancement
and consumer satisfaction.
 OBJECTIVES OF THE STUDY
 To study the level of consumer awareness towards AI-based marketing tools
 To analyse consumer perceptions regarding the and effectiveness of AI-based marketing tools in influencing purchase
decisions
 To examine consumer satisfaction with AI-driven chatbots
 To evaluate the impact of AI-based recommendation systems on consumer buying behaviour
SCOPE OF THE STUDY
  This study examines the level of consumer awareness regarding AI-based marketing chatbots and how well consumers
understand the features and services provided by these chatbots on online platforms. It also explores the usage pattern of
consumers by analysing how frequently they interact with marketing chatbots and the purposes for which they use them,
such as online shopping assistance and customer service support. In addition, the study evaluates the level of consumer
satisfaction with chatbot performance by considering factors like response speed, accuracy of information, and
convenience.

 LIMITATIONS OF THE STUDY
This study has certain limitations that should be considered while interpreting the results. The research is conducted with
a limited number of respondents, which may not fully represent the opinions of the entire consumer population. In
addition, the study may be confined to a particular region or area, and consumer perception towards AI-based marketing
chatbots may vary across different locations and cultural backgrounds. The research is also carried out within a short
period of time, whereas consumer att itudes and perceptions towards AI technology may change in the future as
technology continues to develop.
REVIEW OF LITERATURE
PHILIPKOTLER (2016) Philip Kotler explained that technological advancements are transforming traditional marketing
practices. Artificial Intelligence and automation are becoming important tools in modern marketing strategies. AI
chatbots allow companies to communicate with customers instantly and efficiently. These systems help businesses
understand consumer needs and preferences. The study also emphasized that customer satisfaction increases when
companies provide fast and reliable information. It concluded that AI technologies will play a major role in future
marketing activities.
SHERYLSANDBERG (2016) Sheryl Sandberg explained that digital communication platforms have changed the way
businesses connect with consumers. Many companies started using automated messaging systems to respond to customer
queries quickly. Chatbots became an effective tool for improving customer engagement on online platforms. The study
highlighted that consumers prefer brands that provide quick and convenient communication. AI chatbots help businesses
maintain continuous interaction with customers. These systems also help companies collect feedback from consumers.
The research concluded that chatbot technology improves customer communication and strengthens brand relationships.

CONCLUSION:
The study concludes that most consumers have a positive perception towards AI-based marketing chatbots. The majority
of respondents are aware of AI marketing tools and many of them frequently use chatbots while shopping online. The
findings show that chatbots help consumers by providing quick responses, useful information, and saving time during
the purchasing process. However, some respondents still face issues such as wrong suggestions and slow responses, and
many consumers are concerned about data privacy and security. Overall, AI-based marketing chatbots play an important
role in influencing consumer purchase decisions and improving the online shopping experience. With further
improvements in accuracy, transparency, and privacy protection, AI chatbots can become more effective and widely
accepted by consumers.
