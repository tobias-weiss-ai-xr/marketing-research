Purpose: This research explores the transformative role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and 8
Machine Learning (ML) in brand management. It aims to understand how AI technologies can 9
optimize brand awareness, enhance personalized communication, and improve brand equity 10
measurement, thereby redefining brand management strategies in the digital era. 11
Design/methodology/approach: A systematic literature review was conducted, analyzing 12
research studies published between 2014 and 2024. The methodology focused on articles that 13
delve into the integration of AI and ML within brand management contexts. This approach was 14
chosen to consolidate and synthesize a broad spectrum of findings on AI’s impact on brand 15
management practices. 16
Findings: The review identified significant enhancements in brand management facilitated by 17
AI, particularly in consumer engagement and customization of consumer interactions through 18
data-driven insights. AI's capability to analyze big data has enabled more precise consumer 19
segmentation and targeting, thereby influencing brand loy alty and overall brand equity 20
positively. 21
Research limitations/implications: The primary limitation of this study is its reliance on data 22
from articles indexed only in Scopus, potentially omitting relevant studies published in other 23
languages or databases. Future research should expand the scope to include these sources and 24
explore empirical validations of the proposed theoretical impacts of AI on brand management. 25
Practical implications: This research highlights the practical applications of AI in improvin g 26
brand management strategies, offering insights into effective AI integration into marketing 27
practices. Businesses are encouraged to adopt AI -driven tools for better market segmentation, 28
consumer behavior predictions, and enhanced customer relationship management. 29
Social implications: The findings could influence public attitudes towards brand interaction 30
by fostering greater acceptance of AI in consumer relations. The research supports enhanced 31
corporate social responsibility through AI's ability to provi de more targeted and meaningful 32
consumer interactions. 33
Originality/value: This paper contributes to the academic and practical understanding of AI’s 34
role in brand management by synthesizing existing research and identifying future research 35
directions. It is valuable to academicians, marketing professionals, and policymakers interested 36
in the implications of AI technology in brand strategies. 37

1. Introduction  4
In an era dominated by digital transformation, brand management is experiencing  5
a profound shift fueled by the integration of advanced technologies such as Artificial 6
Intelligence (AI). Within this broad domain, Machine Learning (ML) emerges as a pivotal tool, 7
significantly influencing strategic marketing and brand equity development. As brands strive 8
for attention in an increasingly crowded market, the adept utilization of AI, particularly through 9
ML applications, in crafting personalized, engaging consumer experiences has transitioned 10
from a competitive advantage to a fundamental necessity. 11
This paper seeks to systematically explore and synthesize the expanding landscape of AI, 12
with a special focus o n ML, in brand management. By harnessing various AI applications,  13
from predictive analytics to consumer sentiment analysis through ML, companies are reshaping 14
how brands communicate, understand, and connect with their customers. This review delves 15
into how these technologies are integrated into brand strategies to enhance brand equity and 16
manage brand identity in a dynamic market environment. 17
This research aims to provide a comprehensive overview of how AI, particularly its ML 18
subset, is being employed to redefine the paradigms of brand management. Through  19
a systematic review of literature published between 201 4 and 2024, this paper identifies key 20
trends, assesses the effectiveness of different AI-driven strategies, and evaluates the theoretical 21
and practical implications of AI in brand management.  22
2. Background study  23
According to the American Marketing Association in 2010, a brand is defined as any name, 24
symbol, design, or other characteristic that distinguishes a company's goods or services from 25
those of others. Brand management is the process of developing, maintaining, and enhancing  26
a brand to ensure it remains relevant, competitive, and valuable in the marketplace.  27
The purpose of brand management is to build brands that create market value (Chernev, 28
2020). It involves strategies for positioning the brand, targeting the right audience, managing 29
the company’s reputation, and aligning all marketing communications to emphasize the brand's 30
identity and values. Effective brand management aims to build customer commitment to a brand 31
by creating positive brand perceptions (Sugiarto, Suryanadi, 2019), establishing brand equity, 32

Brand management is a multifaceted discipline that orchestrates a company's product 7

This multifaceted view of brand management makes the integration of diverse data sources 10

efforts were largely guided by market research, consumer feedback, and competitive analysis, 12
often relying on slow, manual processes. This required comprehensive quantitative and 13

Wierenga, 1992). 15
However, the rapid advancement of digital technologies, particularly Artificial Intelligence 16
(AI) and Machine Learning (ML), has transformed the landscape of br and management.  17
With big data technologies, AI allows for aggregating and analyzing vast amounts of data from 18
social media, customer transactions, and market trends ( Jelonek, 2017; Liu, Gao, 2021 ; 19
Pawełoszek, Korczak, 2023).  20
A holistic view of customer interaction and multimodal marketing opens new perspectives 21
on data gathering and processing. The complexity and volume of data necessitate machine 22
learning techniques to process and interpret this information efficiently. This enables brands to 23
anticipate market dynamics and effectively tailor their marketing efforts to meet evolving 24
consumer preferences. For example, ML algorithms can analyze vast amounts of consumer data 25
to identify patterns and predict consumer behavior with high accuracy (Badea, 2024), 26
facilitating more targeted marketing strategies. Thus, machine learning is an indispensable tool 27

and regional coverage of scienti fic journals, conference proceedings, and books. The study 4
focuses on the research publications that describe artificial intelligence applications in brand 5
management. The inclusion criteria for the present systematic review include: 6
 Scholarly articles and reviews. 7
 Quantitative and qualitative research studies. 8
 Research associated with AI and machine learning applications in brand management. 9

psychology, sociology, economics, and computer science, making it a rich area for academic 11

considerable interest from both academia and industry, signaling a shift towards more  3

brand management span several critical areas, including legal and ethical issues, neuroscience, 5
HR, and recruitment, and notably, social media, which serves as a vibrant platform for research 6
