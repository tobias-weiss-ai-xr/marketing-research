# The impact of artificial intelligence on consumer behavior towards brands: a systematic review

Electronic Commerce Research (2026) 26:4483–4524
https://doi.org/10.1007/s10660-025-10063-7
Abstract
Artificial Intelligence is increasingly integrated into consumers’ daily lives, mak -
ing it essential to understand the future impact of this relationship. This systematic
literature review aims to understand the influence of AI on consumer behavior,
identifying future trends for brands. Using Web of Science research, following the
PRISMA method, and employing VOSviewer software for cluster and keyword
analysis, the results demonstrate that AI impacts consumer behavior and brands’,
presenting five clusters that highlight: consumer experience, relationship, online
challenges, functionality, and autonomous purchasing. The study contributes to cur -
rent knowledge by verifying that the AI-consumer relationship is complex, focusing
on five key aspects: convenience, effectiveness, trust, security, and personalization,
which collectively influence purchase decisions and loyalty. This article also dem -
onstrates that AI can facilitate strategic marketing decisions through segmentation
and prediction capabilities, enabling personalization in an increasingly digital and
e-commerce world. Marketers must consider AI as a consumer and expand their
capabilities.

1 Introduction
The world of business and marketing is undergoing a transformation with the recent
emergence of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies, which are significantly impact-
ing the process [1]. Although AI is a trending concept, it has been a subject of study
for several years. Claude Shannon, John McCarthy, Marvin Minsky, and Nathaniel
Rochester were already studying the concept in the 50 s when the Dartmouth confer-
ence became known as the birthplace of AI [2].
AI is a system’s ability to understand data, compare possible approaches to achieve
the established objective, and repeat and learn from the process [ 3]. AI streamlines
consumer data collection, analysis, and presentation for marketing professionals to
inform their decisions [ 4]. In the computer world, the interaction between humans
and technology requires attention [ 5]. Studying the evolution of consumer behavior
is essential for companies [6].
AI development and humanization [ 1] impact numerous industries and research
fields [7–11]. In traditional marketing, the influence of IA opens a new role of change-
less [1] beyond robotics [5]. Now, AI humanization can influence the consumer expe-
rience and acceptance of AI [ 12] and impact purchase decisions through emotional
connections [ 13]. In fact, consumer satisfaction and brand trust increase when AI
tools, such as chatbots, exhibit more anthropomorphic characteristics, which can
lead to more informed purchasing decisions, especially among more involved and
engaged customers [14]. Interactions in the digital world, through, for example, chat-
bots, are transforming the way marketers work [15].
The impact of technological evolution on marketing is growing [16]. Studies indi-
cate the possible use of AI’s predictive factor to understand future purchasing inten-
tions [1]. Understanding consumer behavior towards AI is crucial [5], as well as their
level of engagement and the impact of AI on enhancing the consumer experience
[17]. There are studies on the anthropomorphism of AI, specifically humanized AI
[18], and the impact of this phenomenon on consumer trust [ 19]. Research by Noh
and B. K. Lee [20] suggests that abstract-level messages are more effective in gain -
ing trust from individuals who tend to require more anthropomorphism in AI than
those that are objective. However, anthropomorphism can also cause distrust [ 21].
Understanding the humanization of technology, including which levels of anthropo -
morphism are most effective in different areas, cultures, and types of technology, is
crucial [14]. Further research is necessary to inform the future design of AI tools [21].
It is still necessary to study consumer behavior experiences with AI [9] to understand
how similarities between AI and humans can influence behavior [ 22] and the trust
required for AI to be accepted by consumers [23].
The adoption of AI in daily tasks, aimed at seeking efficiency [10], further under-
scores the need to comprehend consumption and the impact of humanizing technol -
ogy [18]. More studies are needed to analyze the emotional impact on consumers of
the humanized AI-consumer relationship [24].
Creating technologies capable of imitating human interaction to make the experi -
ence less intimidating for the user [ 12] also highlights the importance of analyz -
ing ethical and privacy considerations [ 16]. Privacy is still a cause for distrust and
concern [4]. Moral behavior is also a concern, and it benefits the humanization of

2  Methodology
This paper presents a systematic literature review that provides an overview of
consumer behavior in the context of AI. The Prisma method guides this systematic
review [44]. Prisma has three phases — identification, screening, and inclusion —
that make replicability explicit [45]. This method was chosen to improve the quality
of systematic reviews, as it provides a framework for the review process that guides
reliability and replicability. The visual flow further helps illustrate the process [46].
In summary, adhering to PRISMA guidelines enhances the quality, transparency,
and replicability of systematic reviews, thereby increasing their reliability [45].
The search for articles was conducted in August 2025 on the Web of Science
(WOS), a significant platform for searching academic publications [ 47]. The search
included two general themes: “Artificial Intelligence” and “Consumer Behaviour”.

In other words, the search was done by Topic (searches title, abstract, keyword plus,
and author keywords): “Artificial Intelligence” and “Consumer Behaviour,” which in
itself resulted in 1082 articles. The search process then filtered the results using the
following parameters: the articles are of the “Article” type in the “Business Econ -
omy + Communication” area and are written in the “English” language. The topics
“Artificial Intelligence” and “Consumer Behavior” were chosen because a need for
more studies addressing AI in consumer behavior was identified, which falls within
the scope of marketing studies. Therefore, the research area selected, “Business
Economy + Communication,” at WOS aimed to cover articles that could contribute
to this more market-oriented approach to brands, not just from the psychological per-
spective of behavior or AI computing. The filter by “article” and studies in English
only was a way to filter more comprehensive and viable articles. For the research
and filtering process, 373 articles were identified. These articles are subsequently
analyzed using version 1.6.20 of VOSviewer software. This software identified the
bibliometric maps through a bibliographic coupling of documents, resulting from
the citation numbers shared between them. For that coupling, 20 was defined as the
minimum citation number. The VOSviewer software presents results with 5 clusters,
representing 132 papers. The research protocol, including identification, screening,
and inclusion criteria for articles, is presented in Fig.1.
The VOSviewer software is used to analyze bibliometric data and many scientific
studies. This software utilizes data from search engines, such as Web of Science, to
map bibliometric data networks [ 48]. Bibliometric studies cover scientific literature
based on citations to map research trends [ 49]. In this case, the study utilizes the
bibliographic coupling of documents option, which analyzes the connection between
documents based on the number of bibliographic references they share. Bibliographic
coupling enables researchers to identify relevant articles with precision, facilitating
the replication of research and covering most articles, making it crucial in bibliomet-
ric studies [ 49]. This coupling is a widespread form [ 50]. The idea is to be able to
group articles that deal with similar topics, understanding the search networks that
emerge in a refined way. The minimum number of citations was set to 20 to create a
more effective filter, a sieve that helps eliminate articles with less influence and that
will not contribute to explaining the defined clusters. In short, VOSviewer helps to
identify significant themes or topics within the leading research in an organized and
visual way.
Following the VOSviewer analysis, which yielded five clusters, the review pro -
ceeded by examining the 132 articles within these clusters, utilizing the AI software
Sciscape as an aid. This program helped the study to more quickly understand the
authors’ points of view and the article’s contributions to the topic. Sciscape helps
organize and manage articles, analyzes content through its AI assistant, compares
articles, and considers the possibility of grouping articles into folders and tables with
filters. Exporting to Excel is also a good option. This tool helped simplify the article
review process, making it more objective and faster [51].
Next, the study summarized the data, presenting it in the form of resume tables
and explaining text, with each cluster presenting and relating the ideas of the authors
present in it. Using this analysis by clusters is an advanced approach over conven -
tional reviews and can help identify trends and lines of future research [52].

loyal to the brand and the usual quantity [27].

5  Conclusion
AI is transforming business, consumption, and society. Technological advances drive
innovation across industries, expanding opportunities while raising concerns about
ethics, privacy, and security. In marketing, AI reshapes brand–consumer relation -
ships, promoting personalization based on data. AI grants power and speed in this
data and information economy by rapidly presenting data extracted from massive
databases, accelerating decisions. AI can expand opportunities. However, humaniza-
tion remains key to trust and authenticity, as acceptance depends on consumer traits
and context. A balance between efficiency, control, and fairness can strengthen loy -
alty and openness toward AI. A fair interaction, focused on a good experience, can
help consumers become more open to AI.
Many doubts remain on the path to the so-called Third Industrial Revolution.
Publications on AI and consumer behavior have grown significantly, showing the
topic’s growing relevance. Nevertheless, as AI advances rapidly, society must define
its limits and regulations to ensure ethical progress. The challenge lies in accepting
automation while preserving human values. Properly used, AI can optimize areas
such as health and education, and may even allow humans to reclaim time to think
and be more human.

5.1  Theoretical implications
These articles, organized into five clusters, provide an expanded view that contex -
tualizes the existing literature on consumer behavior towards AI in the marketing
context, thereby fulfilling the proposed objective. They cover the consumer expe -
rience with AI and its potential, the relationship between consumers, online chal -

purchases/consumption. In this way, it is possible to understand the humanized inter-
action between consumers and AI, AI as a service in the market, and the relationship
between marketing and consumers with AI. Further, it awakened interest in knowing
the future of brands using the influence of AI in the purchasing decision process.
The review reinforces the importance of trust, security, consumption experience, per-
sonalization, engagement, and satisfaction in the decision process. Knowing that, as the
authors show, the usefulness and effectiveness of AI must be the mandatory starting point.
AI presents enormous potential in marketing, as highlighted in this systematic litera-
ture review. Its predictive potential will be capable of changing the future of marketing
work, which will increasingly involve using data and history to design more personal-
ized and adapted strategies. Both services, products, and communication will continue
to move online, influencing consumers’ use of AI in their purchasing decisions. In this
way, AI presents a challenge and an opportunity. On the one hand, AI will be a new
consumer that brands must serve, while on the other hand, the same AI will present pos-
sible paths based on history, which could accelerate brands’ decision-making processes.
Theoretically, this study aligns with the ideas reflected in the clusters presented,
which suggest that the relationship between consumers and AI is complex and var -
ies depending on the individual’s context, as discussed. The most important pillars
of this relationship are convenience, efficiency, trust, security, and personalization.
The context, perceived risks, and the marketing’s ability to utilize AI to its advantage
influence this relationship. When AI can guarantee the essential pillars to the con -
sumer based on their needs, the process can result in a relationship of engagement,
satisfaction, purchase decision, and even brand loyalty. This loyalty seems more
threatened by the many solutions presented, especially in the online world, where
e-commerce generates enormous pressure to have the best product or service appear
first. One thing seems inevitable: consumption is heading toward a different future,
and marketers must understand the role brands will play in this new landscape.
5.2  Practical implications
AI has a significant impact on the market and industry today, and this impact is likely
to increase. The potential of AI for marketing and the future of brands is enormous.
In addition to being able to access immense data more easily and with greater filter -

ers. Processes that would previously take much longer are now quick with AI. That
acceleration provides an excellent opportunity for those who utilize AI and a disad -
vantage for those who resist it. Based on facts and up-to-date information, AI can
aid in informed decision-making processes. The interaction between marketing and
technology is beneficial in making strategic decisions.

6  Study’s limitations and future work proposals
6.1  Study’s limitations
This literature review has some limitations, including the inability to generalize find-
ings due to the limitations of the included studies. The study also concluded that the
conclusions could differ if the selection criteria and software used differed. However,
the chosen paths are within the intended scope.
With constant updates, this topic also suffers from biases due to the changes fol -
lowing the creation and adoption of new types of AI tools. The studies included are
primarily in the management area, with a majority of publications originating from the
USA, followed by China. Many less developed countries, whose opportunities differ,
are excluded, so this does not provide a comprehensive view of the world. Finally,
there is undoubtedly a limitation associated with the fact that reviews often incor -
porate studies from multiple authors with varying methodologies and perspectives.
6.2  Future work proposals
This review presents some authors’ suggestions, as these studies and suggestions
stood out from the research and analysis carried out. Considering the objective of this
systematic literature review, the five main paths of future studies are listed below in
order of priority.
1 Understand consumers’ emotional attachment to humanized AI assistants and
their impact on behavior (cluster 3 article).
 Emotional connection is crucial, and a stronger bond can lead to increased loy -
alty and more positive interactions with the brand, which is why this study path
is relevant.
2 Explore the influence of the personal assistant’s personality on engagement with
the brand (cluster 1 article).
 This suggestion is important because assistants’ personalities can shape brand
interactions. An increase in engagement influences the perception of brand value.
3 Investigate loyalty to AI and the limits of convenience addiction (cluster 5
article).
 Convenience is a crucial factor in the regular adoption of AI, but loyalty may
be more closely tied to the tool itself rather than the brand. Therefore, a future
study can help clarify how brands can leverage this relationship between AI and
consumers.
4 Examine the impact of diverse AI communication styles on low-involvement
products and evaluate how these styles influence consumer behavior (cluster
1 article). This suggestion is important, as different communication styles can
