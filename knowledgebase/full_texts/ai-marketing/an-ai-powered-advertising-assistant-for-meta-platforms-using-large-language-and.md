Abstract
In today’ s digital landscape, managing advertising campaigns on platforms like Meta (Facebook
and Instagram) has become increasingly complex. Advertisers face challenges such as creating
engaging ad copy, targeting the right audience, optimizing budgets, a nd analyzing performance
data in real time. This project introduces an AI-powered advertising assistant that leverages large
language models (LLMs) and generative AI to automate ad creation, suggest audience targeting
strategies, and provide performance in sights. The main objective of this system is to enhance
efficiency, reduce human workload, and improve campaign effectiveness by generating creative ad
content and actionable recommendations. The methodology involves designing a cloud -based
system integrated with Meta’ s marketing APIs, using LLMs to generate ad copy and generative AI
for multimedia content suggestions. Initial evaluation focuses on the assistant’ s ability to produce
relevant and high-performing ad drafts. The findings indicate the potential of AI-driven assistants
in streamlining digital marketing workflows.

Keywords: AI advertising assistant, Meta platforms, large language models, generative AI, digital
marketing optimization.

Literature Review
The literature review examines the theoretical and practical foundations of AI-powered advertising

2.1 Meta Platforms and Digital Advertising
Meta platforms, including Facebook and Instagram, dominate the social media advertising
ecosystem due to their extensive user base and sophisticated targeting capabilities. According to
Smith and Anderson (2022), these platforms allow advertisers to segmen t audiences based on
demographics, interests, online behavior, and interactions, which enhances the effectiveness of
campaigns. The advertising ecosystem on Meta includes tools such as Ads Manager, Meta Pixel,
and Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO), enabli ng precise control over ad delivery and
performance measurement (Kapoor et al., 2021).
Despite these advanced tools, managing campaigns remains challenging due to the dynamic nature
of social media trends, frequent algorithm updates, and the sheer volume of ad content required to
maintain engagement. Manual campaign management is time -intensive, often leading to
suboptimal performance and missed revenue opportunities (Chen et al., 2020). This situation has
created a need for intelligent systems that can automate content generation, audience targeting,
and performance analysis.

2.2 Large Language Models (LLMs) in Advertising
Large language models, such as GPT -4 and BERT, have revolutionized natural language
processing by enabling machines to understand context, generate coherent text, and perform
language-based tasks with human -like accuracy (Brown et al., 2020). In advertisin g, LLMs can
generate persuasive ad copy tailored to different audience segments, reducing the need for human
writers while maintaining high levels of relevance and engagement (Radford et al., 2019).
Research by Zhang and Wang (2021) demonstrates that LLMs can optimize the messaging of
digital advertisements by analyzing historical performance data and audience interaction patterns.
This enables AI systems to suggest variations in ad tone, style, and c all-to-action phrasing,
ultimately increasing click -through rates and conversion performance. LLMs also allow for
multilingual advertising, enabling businesses to reach global audiences without the cost of human
translation or localization.

2.3 Generative AI in Digital Marketing
Generative AI refers to algorithms that can create new content, including text, images, and videos,
based on learned patterns from training data (Goodfellow et al., 2014). In the context of
advertising, generative AI can produce multimedia assets that align with brand identity and target
audience preferences. For instance, AI -generated images or short promotional videos can
accompany LLM-generated ad copy to enhance visual appeal and engagement (Shen et al., 2022).
A study by Johnson et al. (2021) found that combining generative AI with LLMs for campaign
design resulted in higher engagement metrics compared to campaigns created solely by human
marketers. Generative AI also facilitates rapid content iteration, allowing multiple ad variations to
be tested simultaneously, which is critical for optimizing performance in dynamic digital
environments.

2.4 AI-Powered Advertising Assistants
The concept of AI -powered advertising assistants integrates LLMs, generative AI, and analytics
into a single system capable of automating various aspects of digital marketing. Existing systems,
such as Phrasee and Persado, utilize AI to generate email and social media marketing content,
demonstrating significant improvements in conversion rates (Kaplan & Haenlein, 2020). However,
most prior solutions are limited in integrating with large-scale platforms like Meta or in producing
multimedia content alongside text.
Recent research emphasizes the potential of fully integrated AI assistants that can:
1. Generate context-aware ad copy using LLMs.
2. Produce supporting visual or video content using generative AI.
3. Analyze historical performance data to recommend targeting strategies.
4. Automate A/B testing and campaign optimization (Li et al., 2022).
These AI assistants address the core challenges of digital advertising—content creation, audience
targeting, and campaign optimization—while reducing human workload and increasing scalability.

3.2 System Architecture
The AI-powered advertising assistant is structured around three core modules:
1. Ad Copy Generation Module: Utilizes a large language model (LLM), such as GPT-4, to
create contextually relevant and persuasive text for ads. The module analyzes campaign objectives,
audience demographics, and historical performance data to generate multiple ad variations for A/B
testing.
2. Multimedia Content Generation Module: Employs generative AI models (e.g., DALL·E 3
or Stable Diffusion) to create visual content, including images, short videos, and graphics that align
with brand identity and campaign goals. This module ensures that multimedia content
complements the LLM-generated ad copy.
3. Analytics and Optimization Module: Integrates with Meta Ads Manager API to retrieve
real-time campaign data. Using this data, the module provides actionable recommendations for
audience targeting, budget allocation, scheduling, and content performance. It also tracks
engagement metrics, enabling dynamic campaign optimization.

Conclusion
This study explored the development of an AI -powered advertising assistant for Meta platforms
using large language models (LLMs) and generative AI. The system was designed to automate the
creation of ad copy, generate multimedia content, optimize audience targeting, and provide data-
driven recommendations for campaign management.

The findings indicate that the assistant:
• Reduces human workload: Automating repetitive tasks such as content creation and audience
targeting saves time and resources.
• Enhances creativity and relevance:  LLMs and generative AI produce high -quality,
contextually relevant ad copy and multimedia assets that align with campaign objectives.
• Improves campaign performance:  Analytics-driven recommendations for targeting and
budget allocation can increase predicted engagement and conversion rates.
• Supports data-driven decision-making: Real-time insights allow marketers to optimize
campaigns dynamically, enhancing overall effectiveness.
While promising, the system’s performance depends on high -quality data, ethical use, and
compliance with Meta’s advertising policies. Despite these limitations, the project demonstrates
the practical potential of AI-powered tools in transforming digital advertising workflows, making
campaigns more efficient, scalable, and impactful.

Recommendations
Based on the findings and discussion, the following recommendations are proposed:
1. Implementation in Real Campaigns:  Businesses should adopt AI -powered assistants for
Meta campaigns to reduce manual workload and improve efficiency.
2. Continuous Model Fine -Tuning: LLMs and generative AI models should be periodically
updated and fine-tuned with new campaign data to maintain content relevance and quality.
3. Ethical and Policy Compliance: Advertisers should ensure that AI-generated content adheres
to Meta’s guidelines and respects user privacy and ethical standards.
4. Multiplatform Expansion: Future development should extend the assistant’s capabilities to
other social media platforms, allowing for cross-platform campaign management.
5. Performance Monitoring and Feedback: Regular evaluation of AI-generated ads should be
conducted to refine targeting, optimize budgets, and enhance engagement metrics.
6. Integration with Human Oversight:  While AI automates many tasks, human oversight is
essential to maintain brand consistency, creativity, and ethical standards.
