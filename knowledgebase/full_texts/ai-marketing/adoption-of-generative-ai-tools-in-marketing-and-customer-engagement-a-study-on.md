ABSTRACT
The rapid advancement of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) has significantly transformed
marketing and customer engagement practices across businesses worldwide. In the Indian context,
increasing digitalization, expanding e-commerce, and supportive government initiatives have accelerated
the adoption of AI -driven technologies. This study examines the extent and patterns of adoption of
Generative AI tools among Indian businesses, with specific focus on marketing and customer engagement
activities. U sing a descriptive and analytical research design, primary data were collected from 150
respondents representing businesses in Coimbatore District through a structured questionnaire based on a
five-point Likert scale. The study analyzes key determinants in fluencing adoption, the types of GenAI
tools utilized, and the perceived impact of these tools on marketing performance and customer experience.
Descriptive statistics, multiple dichotomy analysis, and correlation analysis were employed to interpret
the data. The findings reveal high levels of awareness, digital readiness, and training initiatives, indicating
strong organizational preparedness for GenAI adoption. Businesses predominantly use GenAI for
customer data analysis and content creation, while advanced applications such as personalized marketing
and product recommendations remain at an early stage. The correlation analysis confirms a strong and
positive relationship between adoption patterns, tool usage, and the impact of GenAI. The study concludes
that while Generative AI offers substantial potential to enhance customer engagement and marketing
effectiveness, broader adoption requires supportive policies, skill development, ethical governance and
strategic integration.

Keywords: Gen AI, business, customer engagement.

1.1 INTRODUCTION
The swift progress of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has brought about a revolutionary period for
contemporary companies, especially in the areas of marketing and customer interaction. Among the
various branches of AI, Generative Artificial Intelligence (Gen AI)—encompassing technologies such as
large language models (LLMs), chatbots, image and content generators, and automated personalization
engines—has emerged as a major catalyst for innovation. These tools are reshaping how firms understand,
interact with, and deliver value to customers by enabling highly personalized, real -time, and scalable
marketing solutions. Organizations all across the world are using GenAI more and more to improve service
responsiveness, optimize customer journeys, automate content creation, and extract deeper insights from
consumer data.
Due to increased internet penetration, e -commerce expansion, and government measures encouraging
digital transformation, the adoption of digital technology has accelerated in India. This change has made
it easier for Indian companies to test and use genera tive AI technologies to boost their competitive edge.
Sectors such as retail, banking, hospitality, healthcare, and telecommunications are increasingly
integrating generative AI into their marketing ecosystems to improve personalization, reduce operational
costs, and elevate customer experience.
Despite the growing interest, adoption of Generative AI in India is varied and influenced by multiple
factors including technological readiness, organizational capabilities, perceived benefits, cost
considerations, and concerns related to data privacy and ethical use of AI. Even if early adopters are seeing
notable increases in consumer interaction, many firms are still wary because of talent shortfalls,
implementation difficulties, and doubts about the long-term effects of these technologies. In light of this,
it is crucial to examine how Indian companies use generative AI technologies for marketing and consumer
interaction in order to comprehend the present level of digital transformation, spot innovative potential,
and resolve any obstacles to broader ado ption. Policymakers, managers, marketers, tech developers, and
academic researchers looking to use generative AI for company expansion can benefit greatly from the
results of such studies.  Therefore, the purpose of this study is to examine the degree of ac ceptance, the
effect on consumer engagement, and the factors that influence the adoption of generative AI in Indian
marketing strategies. Through this investigation, the study advances knowledge of how new AI
technologies are changing consumer interactions and marketing tactics in the Indian business environment.

1.3 STATEMENT OF THE PROBLEM
The rapid proliferation of digital technologies has significantly changed the marketing landscape, and
Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) has emerged as one of the most disruptive innovations in this
domain. GenAI tools —such as chatbots, automated content generators, recommendation engines, and
personalized communication platforms —offer businesses powerful mechanisms to enhance customer
engagement, streamline marketing operations, and deliver tailored experiences at scale. While global
organizations have begun to integrate these technologies widely, the extent, effectiveness, and challenges
of GenAI adoption among Indian businesses remain insufficiently understood.
Digital transformation is accelerating in India, but companies must contend with differing degrees of
infrastructure capacity, technology maturity, and knowledge of new AI applications. Although many
businesses recognize that generative AI has the potentia l to enhance marketing performance, adoption is
still unequal across industries. Concerns related to high implementation costs, lack of skilled personnel,
data privacy risks, ethical issues, and uncertain return on investment continue to limit widespread
integration. Moreover, there is limited empirical evidence on how the use of GenAI actually influences
customer engagement, brand perception, and marketing outcomes within the Indian context.

1.6 LIMITATIONS OF THE STUDY
The present study is subject to certain limitations that should be considered while interpreting the findings.
The research is based on a limited sample of Indian businesses, which may not fully represent the diversity
of industries, organizational sizes, and regional contexts across India. The study relies on self -reported
data collected through structured questionnaires, which may be influenced by respondents’ perceptions,
biases, or limited awareness of Generative AI tools. Since the research follows a cross-sectional design, it
captures adoption patterns and impacts at a single point in time and does not account for changes over
time. The rapid evolution of Generative AI technologies may affect the relevance of the findings in the
future. The study primarily examines perceived benefits rather than objective performance indicators such
as revenue growth or return on investment. Sector -specific variations in AI adoption may not be fully
captured due to unequal representation. Geographical constraints may li mit the generalizability of the
results. Regulatory and ethical concerns related to AI are evolving and may not be comprehensively
reflected. Finally, organizational confidentiality and reluctance to disclose strategic information may
affect the accuracy and completeness of responses.

FINDINGS OF THE STUDY
The findings related to AI for Customer Data Analysis reveal that this tool is the most widely adopted
among Indian businesses, accounting for 32 percent of total usage. Organizations primarily utilize
Generative AI to process large volumes of customer dat a, identify behavioral patterns, and generate
actionable insights that support data -driven marketing decisions. The high adoption level indicates that
businesses perceive customer analytics as a critical area where Generative AI delivers immediate value,
particularly in enhancing targeting accuracy, understanding customer preferences, and improving overall
marketing effectiveness.
The study further finds that AI for Content Creation is the second most adopted tool, with 21.3 percent of
respondents reporting its usage. Businesses increasingly rely on Generative AI to create promotional
content, social media posts, product description s, and advertising copy. This reflects a strong inclination
toward improving efficiency, creativity, and consistency in marketing communication. The adoption of
AI-driven content creation suggests that firms aim to reduce time and cost while maintaining high-quality
and engaging marketing outputs.
With respect to AI Chatbots for Customer Interaction, 16.6 percent of the respondents indicated their use
of chatbots to manage customer queries and provide real-time assistance. The findings show that chatbots
are primarily adopted to enhance customer eng agement, improve response time, and ensure continuous
customer support. Although adoption is moderate, it demonstrates that businesses recognize the potential
of conversational AI in improving customer experience and strengthening brand–customer relationships.

cross-selling and upselling, factors such as high implementation costs, data quality issues, and technical
expertise requirements may be restricting wider adoption. Overall, the findings indicate that Indian
businesses currently prioritize Generative AI tools that offer immediate analytical and operational benefits,
such as customer data analysis and content creation, while more complex applications like automated
personalization and product recommendation systems are gradually gaining traction.

POLICY FOR IMPLICATIONS
Based on the findings of the study, several important policy implications emerge for promoting the
effective adoption of Generative AI tools among Indian businesses. Policymakers should prioritize the
development of a clear and comprehensive regulatory framework that encourages responsible and ethical
use of Generative AI in marketing and customer engagement. Given the high adoption of AI for customer
data analysis, strong data protection and privacy policies must be enforced to ensure secure handling of
customer information and to build consumer trust. Government initiatives should support capacity -
building programs that enhance digital skills and AI literacy, particularly to address gaps in advanced
applications such as personalization and product recommendation systems. Incentives such as tax benefits,
subsidies, or grants can be introduced to encourage small and medium enterprises to invest in Generative
AI technologies. Policies should also promote standardization and interoperability to facilitate seaml ess
integration of AI tools into existing business workflows. Industry –academia collaborations can be
strengthened to foster innovation, research, and skill development in AI -driven marketing solutions.
Furthermore, guidelines for transparency and explainability of AI-generated content should be established
to prevent misuse and misinformation. Support for pilot projects and AI sandboxes can help businesses
experiment with advanced AI applications in a controlled environment. Finally, continuous monitoring
and evaluation mechanisms should be implemented to assess the impact of Generative AI adoption and to
refine policies in line with technological advancements and business needs.

CONCLUSION
In conclusion, the study highlights the growing significance of Generative AI tools in transforming
marketing and customer engagement practices among Indian businesses. The findings reveal a high level
of awareness, digital readiness, and training initiati ves, indicating that organizations are increasingly
prepared to adopt AI -driven solutions. Businesses primarily utilize Generative AI for customer data
analysis and content creation, reflecting a focus on efficiency and insight -driven decision -making.
However, the adoption of advanced applications such as personalized recommendations and automated
engagement remains limited, suggesting scope for further technological maturity. The strong and
significant correlations among adoption patterns, tool adoption, and the impact of Generative AI confirm
that structured and strategic implementation enhances business outcomes. Effective integration,
management support, and skill availability play a crucial role in maximizing AI benefits. Despite positive
trends, challe nges related to cost, expertise, and integration persist across sectors. Overall, the study
concludes that Generative AI holds substantial potential to improve customer experience and marketing
effectiveness in India, provided supportive policies, skill development, and responsible adoption practices
are strengthened.
