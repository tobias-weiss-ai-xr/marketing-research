Abstract: The emergence of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has greatly changed the manner in which companies comp rehend and
engage with consumers. AI -based personalization helps businesses to analyze consumer data, forecast preferences and deliver
personalized offers, products, services, recommendations, promotions and many more. This paper seeks to investigate consu mer
perception of AI based personalization and examines socio-economic factors that affect consumers' perception and evaluation of
personalized offerings. 120 sample online consumers from Ernakulam District of Kerala were chosen by applying convenience
sampling. Primary data was gathered through a structured survey among consumers and appropriate statistical tool i.e. multiple
regression analysis has been used for analysis of the gathered data. The findings help to gain an understanding of consumers'
perceptions and response to AI personalization being used increasingly in marketing activities. In marketing, AI -driven
personalisation supports a variety of acti vities, including customised email communication, targeted digital advertising, product
recommendations, and personalised promotional content. By analysing consumer characteristics and beh avioural information,
marketers can develop communications that are more closely aligned with specific consumer interests and preferences.
Consequently, AI -driven personalisation has become an important component of contemporary digital marketing, while
consumer trust, privacy, transparency, and perceived relevance remain critical factors in determining how consumers respond to
personalised interactions.
Keywords: Artificial intelligence, Consumer, Forecast, Promotions, Personalisation.

I. INTRODUCTION
AI-driven personalization is an application of artificial intelligence technologies to personalize mess ages, recommendations,
services and digital experiences for customers, based on their personal traits and preferences. With  the help of analyzing co nsumer
data and behavioral patterns, personalized experiences can be achieved by AI systems. This kind of p ersonalization allows
organizations to build a better experience for customers and increase interaction with them. The con stant development of AI
technologies, especially the generation of AI, opened new possibilities for marketing. Current AI systems ar e able to generate and
provide customized content and recommendations with almost no time delay, allowing companies to quickly react to the behavior of
customers. All of this contributes to the rise of omnichannel hyperpersonalization, where consumers rece ive the same personalized
experience across multiple channels depending on their interactions and preferences. The constantly increasing capabilities of AI-
driven personalization affect the expectations of consumers as well. People increasingly expect rele vant recommendations and
personalized experience according to their personal needs. According to industry research, consumers  become more interested i n
AI-assisted shopping and personalized interactions. It has been reported that consumers also tend to expect business organisations to
offer personalised content, which may lead to dissatisfaction from the consumer’s side when such int eractions do not meet the ir
particular demands. Thus, personalisation became an essential aspect of business strategy for improv ing relations between a
business and its consumers, engagement and performance in general. The usage of artificial intellige nce for personalisation i s now
widespread in various industries in order to offer relevant recommendations and context -based experience to consumers. It can be
applied not only to individual consumers who browse websites but also to corporate customers, employ ees and other users who
interact with business organisations through different digital channels. By using different types of d ata, AI systems allow to make
tailored content and services available and to control personalisation in relation to various segmen ts of consumers. In e -commerce,
AI-powered personalisation is used in order to analyze browsing habits, purchase history, search and in terests of a consumer. Thus,
AI systems can offer particular products which can be interesting for a particular consumer.

A. Significance of the study
There are AI powered tools that could interact with customers during different stages of the custome r journey process. The us e of
such tools could start from personalizing interactions when consumers are searching for products and  services online through
personalized communication after the purchase process. In such ways, personalization through AI help s organizations deliver
consistent and relevant experience to consumers at multiple points in the journey. Proper use of AI personalization has been found
to help firms perform much better and generate revenues. According to a survey carried out by the IBM Institute for Business Value
among top business leaders, companies that focused more on customer experience had the potential to grow revenues much faster
compared to their peers. The survey also found that most of the leaders surveyed believed that perso nalization was an integral part
of their customer experience strategy.

B. Problem Identification
However, along with implementing AI -based personalization, companies should pay more attention to transparency, data privacy,
and responsible use of consumer data. As AI algorithms depend on the huge amount of consumer data fo r personalized
recommendations and offers, customers can be worried about ho w the process of collection, analysis, storage, and sharing of their
personal and behavioral data takes place. Insufficient transparency may provoke questions related to  the nature and extent of  data
usage and decrease consumer trust.  Moreover, consumers c an be worried about the possible distortion of personalized
recommendations, promotions or AI -based information. Sometimes, it is difficult for consumers to comprehend the way the AI -
based system makes recommendations or offers. Also, the use of AI in pricing may provoke concerns about price discrimination and
price rises because consumers can be treated differently and offered various prices or offers depend ing on their individual features,
browsing habits, purchase history or other factors.

C. Aim of the Study
The study aimed to know the consumer perception of AI-driven personalisation in digital marketing.

II. LITERATURE REVIEW
Hoang et al. (2023) examined the personalisation –privacy paradox and found that consumers balance the benefits of personalised
services against privacy risks, with trust and perceived control influencing their willingness to sh are personal information. Canhoto
et al. (2024) studied AI -enabled personalisation in retail and found that consumers valued relevant and customised experie nces,
while privacy, surveillance, and loss of control remained important concerns. Athaide et al. (2024) examined personalised smart-
device experiences and reported that convenience and perceived value encouraged adoption, whereas pr ivacy concerns and reduced
consumer control could negatively affect acceptance. Jain et al. (2024) reviewed AI and consumer behaviour research and identified
trust, perceived usefulness, engagement, and technology acceptance as important factors shaping consumer responses to AI-enabled
applications.

III. METHODOLOGY
The study was carried out among the 120 sample online consumers residing in Ernakulam District of Kerala. Since the study
revolved around consumers having previous experience in online shopping and digital platforms, con sumers who could be
approached and were ready to participate in the study were considered the respondents. Convenience sampling technique, which is a
non-probability sampling technique, was used to select the respondents in this study. Primary data were gathered by directly
approaching the online consumers through the use of a questionnaire. The questionnaire was distribut ed to the respondents onl ine,
facilitating the process of gathering data from consumers having experience in digital platforms and  online purchases. Respondents
were aware of the reason for conducting the study, and the data obtained was only for academic and research purpose.

V. RECOMMENDATIONS AND CONCLUSION
Personalization using AI has emerged as a crucial aspect of online consumer interaction as it allows  organizations to give
personalized offers, recommendations, content, and shopping experience to customers. The current stu dy analyzed the consumer
perception of AI personalization in terms of online consumers from Ernakulam district in Kerala stat e. The results from multi ple
regression analysis reveal that the independent variables taken into consideration have a significan t re lationship with consumer
perception, which explains 27.9% variation in perception, and adjusted explanatory ability of 21.2%.  The age, gender, and inc ome
level are found to be significant at the 5% level, while occupation, form of personalization used, family size, type of online shopping
website, education level, frequency of online shopping, and product type are not statistically signi ficant at 5% level. It is  further
observed that the residual histogram shows that the regression residuals are normally distributed. Therefore, the study indicated that
consumer perception of AI-driven personalisation is associated with selected consumer characteristics, while the effectiveness  and
acceptance of personalisation also depend on how consumers perceive its releva nce, transparency, privacy and fairness. So,
organisations should adopt a consumer -centric and responsible approach to AI -driven personalisation, balancing customised
experiences with privacy protection, transparency, consumer control and fair practices.
