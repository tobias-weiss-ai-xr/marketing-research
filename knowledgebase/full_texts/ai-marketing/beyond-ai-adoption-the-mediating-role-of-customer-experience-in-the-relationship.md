ABSTRACT

The rapid expansion of e-commerce in Indonesia has accelerated the adoption of artificial intelligence (AI)-
based digital marketing tools, such as product recommendation systems and chatbots. However, their ability
to directly influence consumer purchase intentions remains questionable. Grounded in the Theory of Planned
Behavior (TPB), this study examines the effect of AI product recommendation systems and chatbot quality
on online purchase intention, with customer experience as a mediating variable. Data were collected from a
minimum target of 385 respondents via online questionnaires. Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) with
SmartPLS 4 was employed to conduct descriptive analysis, outer model validation (using A VE, Cronbach’s
Alpha, and Composite Reliability), and inner model evaluation (R², Q², and path coefficients), followed by
hypothesis testing using p -values and t -statistics. The findings reveal that AI product recommendation
systems and chatbot quality had no significant direct effect on online purcha se intention. In contrast,
customer experience exerted a significant direct influence and fully mediated the relationship between AI
recommendation systems, chatbot quality, and purchase intention. Theoretically, these results highlight that
mismatched recommendations and low -quality chatbot interactions generate negative consumer attitudes,
reduce perceived control, and increase the dominance of social norms in shaping purchase decisions. This
suggests the need to expand TPB by integrating a technological trust dimension in the digital commerce
context. Practically, e -commerce companies should focus on developing more personalized, relevant
recommendation systems and improving chatbot responsiveness, clarity, and interaction quality to build
trust. Enhancing customer education about AI functionality and benefits is also essential for strengthening
positive user experiences and encouraging online purchase intentions.

Keyword: Artificial intelligence, customer experience, online purchase intention, product recommendation
system, Theory of Planned Behavior.

INTRODUCTION

E-commerce has become the backbone of Indonesia's digital economy with its rapid and significant
growth. Data from the Ministry of Trade shows that the value of e-commerce transactions in Indonesia
will reach Rp534 trillion by 2023, with expected growth on  a yearly basis of 20% until 2025
(Kementerian Perdagangan RI, 2024). Indonesia ranks as the fourth largest country in terms of internet
population, with over 170 million active users and an internet penetration rate of 63% in terms of its
total population (APJII, 2023). The high growth rate of e -commerce indicates a fundamental shift in
people's consumption patterns from conventional shopping to digital platforms. Digital commerce
platforms, including Tokopedia, Shopee, Lazada, and Bukalapak in Indonesia, have become dominant
players in this landscape, bringing millions of products and services to Indonesian consumers (Rahman
& Saputra, 2023). This growth is also being driven by the COVID-19 pandemic, which has forced many
businesses to shift to digital models, as well as the increasing digital literacy of the public. It is important
to understand Indonesia's e-commerce dynamics as the sector plays a pivotal role in driving economic
engagement, opening up new jobs, facilitating MSMEs to enter a wider market, and catalyzing broader
digitization in society (Rimaldi et al., 2024).

The implementation of artificial intelligence (AI) -based product recommendation systems in e -
commerce platforms has revolutionized the way products are offered to consumers. These platforms
rely on advanced algorithms to analyze how users behave, their purchase history, and user preferences
to provide appropriate product suggestions. This increases the relevance of the products offered, which
can increase consumer buying interest, and the strategy to present personalized product
recommendations with more precise recommendations, so as to make customers feel more valued and
cared for. This better shopping experience contributes to a rise in online purchase intention  (Evina et
al., 2024). Although this technology has been shown to increase sales by up to 35% and conversion
rates by 15% on several leading platforms (Erwin et al., 2023), several negative impacts should be
investigated as well. These include the following issues: the "filter  bubble" effect, which repeatedly
displays similar products (Orimus, 2023), thereby reducing the relevance and quality of
recommendations for consumers (Abdollahpouri et al., 2019); disappointing experiences when
recommendations do not match consumers' nee ds or preferences (Muslik et al., 2025); and concerns
regarding consumer data privacy (Fazlioglu, 2024). These challenges have encouraged e -commerce
platforms to seek more effective alternatives, such as AI chatbots, to enhance customer interaction and
provide a better shopping experience.

Quality AI chatbots in e-commerce customer service have become a fundamental component in digital
marketing strategies. Modern AI chatbots are capable of simulating human conversations and provide
a fast and accurate response that can improve the overall user experience. When customers feel
supported and get the information they need easily, they tend to be more confident in making the
decision to make a purchase. Additionally, positive interactions with chatbots can increase customer
satisfaction, thus driving their intention to shop online. However, the incorporation of AI chatbots into
e-commerce platforms also presents several notable negative effects, namely limitations in contextual
understanding, over-reliance on chatbots, having rigid and limited conversation flows, and collecting
sensitive data but not being equipped with adequate security protocols  (Moriuchi et al., 2021). These
drawbacks can seriously affect the overall consumer experience.

Customer experience is a determining and crucial component in establishing loyalty and encouraging
online purchase intent. A comprehensive customer experience encompasses all consumer interactions
with e -commerce platforms, from product searches, site navigation, payment processing, to post -

LITERATURE REVIEW AND HYPOTHESIS DEVELOPMENT

This research is based on the theory introduced by Ajzen (1991) , which is the Theory of Planned
Behavior. TPB serves as a conceptual model for analyzing and forecasting human actions. According
to this theory, the intention to act plays a determining role in shaping an individual’s behavior, which is
determined by the following three primary components: thei r attitude toward the behavior; subjective
norms (the perceived social pressure); and perceived behavioral control. This control refers to an
individual's assessment of the simplicity or complexity involved in carrying out the behavior, based on
prior experiences and potential challenges. This theory is a development of the Theory of Reasoned
Action (TRA), which was previously developed by Fishbein & Ajzen (1975).

In TPB theory, especially the premise of perceived behavioral control (PBC), TPB can be used as a tool
to interpret how consumers form their perceptions of ease and control when using recommendation
systems and Chatbots. Factors such as ease of access, tru st in AI, and prior experiences, can improve
their perception of control (Venkatesh et al., 2012). If customers feel capable and confident in using the
technology, they tend to have higher intentions to make purchases through platforms with AI systems.
AI systems capable of improving customer experience through personalization, quick response, and
smooth interaction will improve consumers' perception of the shopping experience, which reinforces
positive attitudes (Huang & Rust, 2021). With the increase in positive attitudes, supportive social norms,
and a perception of high control over the use of AI, customer intent to buy products through AI
recommendation systems and conversation quality will increase and vice versa. If consumers f eel
uncomfortable with the answers provided by Chatbots, it can affect consumers' purchase intention (Luo
et al., 2019).

The Influence of an AI Product Recommendation System on Online Purchase Intention

Algorithmic product recommendation systems are systems that use algorithms to provide suggestions
or recommendations to users based on the data and preferences that have been collected (Benlian et al.,
2022). Research has found that there is a direct relationship between AI recommendation systems and
purchase intention based on experience and information obtained through the marketplace (Juliyando
& Saputra, 2023) . Appropriate recommendations can make decision -making easier and improve the
convenience of online shopping (Permana et al., 2024) . This technology shortens the process of
consumers having to search for the products they need (Tang et al., 2024) . The application of AI in

consumer behavior analysis will improve the personalization of the shopping experience. The AI
recommendation system has a positive effect on purchase intent. Marketplaces can understand
consumer behavior patterns and customize their shopping experience, potentially increasing consumer
loyalty and purchase frequency (Dang et al., 2021).

The characteristics of proximity, relevance, and accuracy of information by e-commerce platforms have
a substantial positive impact on customer satisfaction, indirectly and positively influence consumer
purchase decisions by improving the flow experience, satisfaction, and customer trust (Addison & Fitri,

and expression quality, which ultimately influences consumers ' desire to buy products recommended
by the platform (Shen, 2024). Thus, the first hypothesis H1 proposed for the study is:

H1: The AI product recommendation system has a positive and significant effect on online purchase
intention for e-commerce users in Indonesia.

The Influence of the Quality of AI Chatbots on Online Purchase Intention

A chatbot represents a system designed to act as a conversational agent or virtual assistant that can
communicate with humans through the internet. This system serves as a communication channel
between companies and customers, especially in the field of ma rketing and service (Chung & Han,
2022). AI conversations are relevant because they affect consumers' purchase intent for recommended
products. Researchers concluded that to improve the user experience, developers should prioritize the
improvement of the quality and relevance of AI chatbot responses rather than it just looking or behaving
like a human (Chung & Han, 2022) . Good communication quality increases customer satisfaction,
which will then have a greater effect on the intention to reuse the chatbot, while strengthening online
purchase intent (Akdemir & Bulut, 2024). The use of AI can improve purchase intention through various
aspects, such as the type of message used, including a warm communication style that makes customers
trust AI as a source of information and a competent communication style that makes customers perceive
the recommended content as high quality (Schwede et al., 2023) . The use of chatbot features in the
marketplace affects consumers' buying interest and intention to use the marketplace. The greater the
degree of interaction, communication convenience, responsiveness, and user impression, the higher the
level of consumer satisfaction. Consumer buying interest is often an early trigger in the online shopping
experience, which can in turn affect the chatbot features used in e-commerce (Saputra & Sutarso, 2024).
Thus, the second hypothesis H2 proposed is:

H2: A quality AI chatbot has a positive and significant effect on the online purchase intention of e -
commerce users in Indonesia

The Influence of Customer Experience on Online Purchase Intention

Customer experience is defined as the overall interaction and experience experienced by consumers
when engaged in transactions with a brand or company, especially within the scope of e -commerce
activities or marketplaces (Threstia et al., 2022) . Positive consumer interactions can increase
satisfaction and build loyalty, which ultimately drives purchase intent online (Grace & Pardede, 2023).
Elements of consumer experience, such as the quality of online services, ease of website navigation,
transaction security, and the responsiveness of customer support, are recognized as significant indicators
of online purchase intention. Additionally, th ere is a linear relationship between the quality of

information and the level of trust in reviews, marketplace platforms, and rating systems (Peña-García
et al., 2024). Consumer experience improves relationship quality, which in turn influences consumer
purchase intent (Hakim & Dhafi Iskandar, 2025). Increased consumer value perception drives purchase
intent, while consumer trust and attitudes act as positive regulators in the correlation between how value
is perceived and the intent to buy (Wang et al., 2023) . A good experience has a positive impact on

in Indonesia.

Customer Experience as a Mediator: The Effect of AI Recommendation Systems  on Online
Purchase Intention

Customer experience serves as a mediating factor between the application of Artificial Intelligence and
gamification, and the intention to make online purchases among marketplace users in Indonesia. This
highlights the crucial role of customer experience in enhancing the impact of technological innovations
on consumer buying behavior (Santy & Iffan, 2023) . Customer experience is a key factor that can
mediate the relationship between AI technology and consumer purchase intent. It can strengthen the
influence of service quality and AI recommendation systems on consumer purchase intent. A good
customer experi ence increases satisfaction and loyalty, which leads to increased online purchase
intention (Lang et al., 2024) . Influential use of AI on online purchase intention, with customer
experience as an intermediary, strengthens the recommendation technology system and consumer
purchasing behavior (Chhikara & Tanner, 2026) . The function of management information systems
(MIS) in facilitating AI-driven recommendation systems and their influence on consumer behavior and
purchase intent shows that AI recommendation systems supported by quality data and good
personalization can improve purchase intent and customer satisfaction (Ajit Kumar Singh, 2025). AI-
based personalized product recommendations influence consumer emotions, which in turn impact
purchase intent, emphasizing the importance of customer experience in the online purchasing decision-
making process (Nguyen et al., 2025) . The AI recommendation system does not merely affect online
purchase intent directly, but also plays a role in improving customer experience as a mediator that
strengthens the relationship. Thus, the fourth hypothesis H4 proposed is:

H4: Customer experience mediates the impact of an AI recommendation system on online purchase
intention among e-commerce users in Indonesia.

Customer Experience as a Mediator: The Effect of AI Chatbot Quality on Online Purchase
Intention

A good experience with the use of chatbots can enhance the probability of customers making an online
purchase. In addition, a positive customer experience is influenced by dimensions such as entertainment
and media appeal, which indirectly support increased purchase intent through an increase in the overall
customer experience (Martínez Puertas et al., 2024) . Online customer experience functions as a
mediator in the link between digital marketing communication and sustainable purchase intention. This
underscores the significance of online customer experience as a key intermediary, emphasizing its
pivotal role within the landscape of contemporary digital marketing (Thakur et al., 2025).

METHODOLOGY

Population

The population in this study was the e -commerce users in Indonesia, especially from the Yogyakarta
area, who were at least 18 years old, with an unknown (infinite) population size.

Methods, Techniques, Measurement, and Sample Data Collection

The sampling approach employed is non -probability sampling using a purposive sampling technique.
This method was selected as it permits researchers to identify participants based on particular criteria
aligned with the goals of the study. These criteria were as follows: Indonesian citizens aged 18-50 years
who had made online purchases in e-commerce at least three times in the last six months. Data collection
was carried out using an online questionnaire (Google Form), which was distributed to e -commerce
users in the Yogyakarta area. Sample size calculation was carried out based on the Cochran formula.
The Cochran formula was used to determine the minimum required sample size, whereas a non -
probability sampling technique was used to identify individuals who met the eligibility criteria  for

Data Analysis Techniques

This study uses two main approaches in data analysis, namely descriptive analysis and inferential
analysis applying Partial Least Squares - Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) with support from
SmartPLS software. Descriptive analysis aims to describe th e characteristics of respondent data, such
as mean, standard deviation, and distributions of frequencies. Descriptive statistics are used to
understand the demographic profile and level of perception of each research variable before further
analysis (Asrulla et al., 2023) . PLS-SEM analysis is employed due to its capability to examine the
relationships among latent variables within complex models, and can still be used even though the
sample size is relatively small and not normally distributed. This analysis comprises two stages,
specifically the outer model (measurement model) and the inner model (structural model).

In the outer model, there are several tests, namely the validity convergence, which is evaluated based
on the loading factor values of each indicator and the average variance extracted (A VE). The A VE value
is declared good or valid if the A VE value of each variable is ≥ 0.50 (Henseler et al., 2015) .
Discriminatory validity was tested with cross-loading values between indicators and the test reliability
rated using Composite Reliability and Cronbach's Alpha (value ≥0.7 declared reliable). In the Inner
Model, there are several tests, namely the Coeffi cient of Determination (R²), which measures the
strength of the influence of an independent variable on the dependent variable (Henseler et al., 2015).
Predictive Relevance (Q²) to see the model's predictive capabilities (Q² > 0 indicates predictive
relevance), and Path Coefficient indicates the correlation’s orientation and degree between positive or
negative variables (Ghozali, 2011) . With this approach, the model is thoroughly tested from the
measurement aspect to the causal relationship between the constructs.

DISCUSSION

AI Product Recommendation System and Online Purchase Intention

The first findings of this study were that the AI-based recommended e-commerce products had not yet
met the needs of respondents in online purchasing decisions. This follows the variable indicator X1.7
as the lowest mean, namely "Recommendations for AI-based e-commerce products that suit my current
needs," indicates that the recommendation system had not met user expectations in the context of
personalization. When the recommendation system is irrelevant, users will feel that the platform does
not understand their preferences accurately (Ao Ting, 2025) , so they tend to ignore the
recommendations. This situation undermines the effectiveness of AI features in accelerating purchasing
decision-making and increasing engagement (Chau et al., 2025) . Psychologically, inappropriate
recommendations create perceived misfit between the product and the customer's needs, which has a
direct impact on the purchase intention (V o et al., 2022). Mismatches can also result in negative digital
experiences and reduce trust in AI technology in e-commerce (Choung et al., 2023).

Recommendation systems are a central part of AI-driven marketing strategies, and low performance can
hinder the achievement of digital business goals. To address this, the recommendation system must be
improved through more adaptive approaches, such as uti lizing context -aware recommendations
(Venkatachalam & Ray, 2022) and deep learning personalization (Anggoro & Saputra, 2023)  which
considers the time, location, and emotional behavior of the user. The integration of user explicit
feedback also needs to be strengthened to increase the relevance of recommendations. Thus, the system
can provide truly contextual recommendations and support purchase decisions.

AI Chatbot Quality and Online Purchase Intention

The findings of this study indicated that using the information from an AI chatbot only in e-commerce
was not reliable or felt less convincing for respondents in purchasing products, which was indicated by
the variable indicator X2.7 as the lowest mean, namely "I can rely on the information provided by the
AI chatbot in e-commerce." This indicates that, in the context of this study, how good or bad Quality
AI chatbots were used did not seem to directly affect consumers' interest in making online purchases.
This was mainly caused by skepticism over the reliability of the chatbot’s information. Distrust of
chatbots can cause consumers to switch to competitors who provide human interaction or seek
information from other sources, thus lowering customer retention rates (Kumar & Metta, 2025). Good
communication quality increases customer satisfaction, which has a greater effect on the intention to
reuse the chatbot, while strengthening online purchase intent (Akdemir & Bulut, 2024). The use of AI
can improve purchase intention through various aspects of assessment, such as the type of message
used. A warm communication style can make customers trust AI as a source of information, while a
competent communication style can make customers perceive the recommended content as high quality
(Schwede et al., 2023).

The use of chatbot features in the marketplace affects consumers' buying interest and their intent to use
the marketplace; the higher the level of engagement, clarity of communication, promptness in
responses, and user perception during usage, the higher the level of consumer satisfaction (Misischia et
al., 2022). Consumer buying interest is often an early trigger in the online shopping experience, which
can affect chatbot features in e -commerce (Saputra & Sutarso, 2024) . Even though Chatbot AI is a
valuable tool for customer service and operational efficiency, its current quality does not directly
correlate with increased interest in buying online . Marketers may need to reevaluate the roles of their
chatbots. In their strategy, they should focus on improving the overall customer experience which can
then indirectly affect purchase intent.

Customer Experience and Online Purchase Intention

The findings of this study were that a visually pleasing e-commerce browser experience for respondents
ultimately attracted their online shopping interest, which was in accordance with the M5 variable
indicator as the highest mean, namely "Browsing experience in e-commerce is visually pleasing." This
indicates that pleasing visual aspects, responsive browsing experience, and overall satisfaction from the
shopping experience online are very dominant in driving buying interest (Chen & Yang, 2021). In the
context of e-commerce, a positive customer experience encompasses various aspects, stemming from
how easily users can navigate the website, to speed in loading, quality product information, a smooth
checkout process, and responsive after-sales service. When consumers experience a fun, efficient, and
personalized experience, they tend to feel more confident and motivated to complete transactions.

Consumer experience improves relationship quality, which in turn affects purchase intention or
consumer purchase intent (Hakim & Dhafi Iskandar, 2025). Increased consumer value perception drives
purchase intent, while consumer trust and attitudes act as regulators in the connection between perceived
value and the intention to make a purchase (Wang et al., 2023) . A positive experience influences
purchase intention, with customer satisfaction serving as a mediating factor. (Grace & Pardede, 2023).
Customer experience has an impact on customer satisfaction, which then impacts other online purchase
intentions (Risma et al., 2024). A good experience is one of the factors in encouraging online purchase
intent. Positive consumer interactions can increase satisfaction and build loyalty, which ultimately
drives purchase intent online (Grace & Pardede, 2023).

These findings are in stark contrast to prior results related to an AI product recommendation system and
AI chatbot quality. This indicates that while AI technologies (such as recommendations or chatbots)
may not directly drive buying interest, their positive impact is most likely manifested through improved
customer experience. Put differently, AI has the potential to be an effective instrument in creating an
exceptional customer experience, and it is this superior experience that ultimately fuels buying interest.
Overall, this study has emphasized that in AI-based digital marketing, the strategy must be centred on
creating a positive customer experience as the main key to increasing interest in buying online . This
means focusing not only on the implementation of the AI technology itself, but on how it is integrated
to offer an uninterrupted, personalized, and satisfying experience for consumers.

Mediation Effect of Customer Experience between Product Recommendation System and Online
Purchase Intention

The fourth finding of this study was that through an AI -based product recommendation system,
respondents found interesting new products in e-commerce and pleasant visual experiences, so that they
felt satisfied. The X1.4 indicator was "I find interesting new products in e -commerce through an AI -
based Product Recommendation System." Meanwhile, for the customer experience (M)  variable, the
indicators with the highest scores were M5 "Browsing experience in e-commerce is visually pleasing"
and M4 "I feel satisfied with the shopping experience in e-commerce." This reveals that aspects of new
product discovery through AI recommendations, as well as visual experience and overall s atisfaction,
serve a crucial function in mediating the relationship between AI recommendation systems and online
buying interest.

The AI product recommendation system does not directly affect buying interest online, but rather
through an escalation intermediary, the customer experience has become positive. These mediation
findings are crucial because they explain why the AI product recommendation system initially did not
show a direct influence on buying interest online, as discussed in the first finding. The main function of
the AI product recommendation system is to improve the quality of customer experience, which in turn
will drive buying interest. A good recommendation system can make the shopping process more
personalized, efficient, and enjoyable, and it is this positive experience that motivates consumers to
make a purchase. The effect of AI use on online purchase intention with customer experience as an
intermediary, strengthens the recommendation technology system and consumer purchase behavior
(Chhikara & Tanner, 2026) . Customer experience is a key factor that can mediate the relationship
between AI technology and consumer purchase intent, it can strengthen the influence of service quality
and AI recommendation systems on consumer purchase intention. A good customer exp erience
increases satisfaction and loyalty, which leads to increased online purchase intention (Lang et al., 2024).

The function of management information systems (MIS) in facilitating AI -driven recommendation
systems and their influence on consumer behavior and purchase intent shows that AI recommendation
systems supported by quality data and good personalization can improve purchase intent and customer
satisfaction (Ajit Kumar Singh, 2025) . AI-based personalized product recommendations influence
consumer emotions, which in turn impact purchase intent, emphasizing the importance of customer
experience in the online purchasing decision formulation process (Patnaik et al., 2025) . Thus, even
though the AI product recommendation system does not exert a significant direct influence on online
purchase intention, its role as a critical driver of customer experience, must be acknowledged as such.
This suggests that investments in AI for digital marketing should be focused on how AI can improve
consumer interaction, make the shopping process more intuitive and enjoyable, and ultimately build
experiences that motivate purchases. An effective strategy is to use AI as a tool to create a superior
customer journey, which will indirectly increase sales conversions.

Mediation Effect of AI Chatbot Quality on Customer Experience and Online Purchase Intention

The study's fifth finding concerns the user -friendliness and response speed of AI chatbots in e -
commerce. The issue is whether these features will increase the convenience of browsing visually, and
ultimately contributes to satisfaction. The highest scoring indicators for the AI chatbot quality (X2)
variable were X2.1 "AI chatbot in e -commerce is easy to use" and X2.2 "Chatbot AI e -commerce
responded quickly to my questions." Meanwhile, for the customer experience (M) variable, the highest
scoring indicators were M5 "Browsing experience in e-commerce is visually pleasing" and M4 "I feel
satisfied with the shopping experience in e -commerce" (both 4.11). This underscores the fact that the
efficiency and rapid replies provided by AI chatbots, when combined with a pleasing visual experience,
indirectly increase online buying interest. Based on the combination of these results, it is clear that the
quality of AI chatbot did not directly affect online buying interest, but rather improved the customer
experience. These findings provide clarity on the role of AI chatbots in driving purchase s. Why does
quality AI chatbot not directly affect online buying interest (as discussed in the previous analysis), but
has a mediating effect through customer experience? This shows that AI chatbots, when designed and
implemented well, serve as a customer experience enhancer , and it is this positive experience that
ultimately fuels buying interest.

Online customer experience mediating the relationship between digital marketing communication and
sustainable purchase intention reveals that online customer experience is an important mediating
element in the context of digital marketing communication and  continuous purchase intent,
demonstrating the central role of customer experience as part of modern digital marketing (Thakur et
al., 2025) . The good experience that consumers get through interaction with chatbots increases
satisfaction and trust, which in turn strengthens consumer purchase intent (Lang et al., 2024). The user’s
perception of usability of AI acts as a key mediator that reinforces the influence of psychosocial factors
on purchase intentions indirectly (Lopes et al., 2024) . Thus, customer experience also plays an
important role as a bridge in the influence of AI chatbots on consumer purchasing behavior. These
results emphasize that the quality of the AI Chatbot is not just an additional feature, but rather an
important feature of strategic investments that affect buying interest indirectly, namely by increasing
customer experience. Marketers should focus on developing a Chatbot that is not only functional, but
also provides a seamless, informative, and helpful experience, because customer experience is the
bridge to buying interest online.

CONCLUSION

From the analysis and discussion of the data, it may be inferred that the AI product recommendation
system and the quality of AI chatbots did not have a direct effect on online purchase intention in e -
commerce users in Indonesia. However, customer experien ce had a significant direct influence and
acted as a mediator in the relationship between the variables of the AI product recommendation system
and the quality of AI chatbots, with online purchase intentions.

Theoretical Implications

The implications of TPB (Theory of Planned Behavior) are reflected in respondents’ perceptions that
AI-based e-commerce product recommendations do not adequately meet their needs in online purchase
decisions, and that information provided solely by AI chatbots in e -commerce is not perceived as
sufficiently reliable or convincing when purchasing products.

Attitude: The mismatch of product recommendations and low trust in AI chatbots can result in a negative
attitude towards online purchases. This negative attitude arises because AI systems do not provide
tangible benefits felt by consumers, which should be the basis for the formation of positive attitudes in
the SDGs.

Subjective Norms: When AI technology in e -commerce is not convincing, consumers may be more
influenced by opinions or recommendations from friends, family, or communities rather than by AI.
This shows that trust in social systems is more dominant than technology in influe ncing purchase
intentions.

Perceived Behavioral Control: If AI is considered unhelpful or even hindering in the purchase process,
then consumers' perception of control over online shopping decisions becomes low. This reduces the
likelihood that they have the intention to make a purchase online because they feel they do not have
complete control.

Practical Implications

The practical implications are based on respondents’ perceptions that AI -based e-commerce product
recommendations have not yet matched their needs in online purchasing decisions and that AI chatbots
are perceived as less convincing when making product purchases.

Development of a More Personal and Relevant Recommendation System: E-commerce companies must
optimize AI recommendation algorithms to be more accurate in understanding user preferences and
needs, for example, by analyzing shopping behavior or search history.

Increasing Trust in AI Chatbots:  The design of the chatbot needs to be improved in terms of speed,
relevance of answers, and the language used to make it feel more professional, informative, and
convincing. It can also be done by adding hybrid features, chatbots + human support (human age nt
fallback).

Consumer Education on AI Features. E-commerce needs to educate and socialize to increase consumers'
understanding of the benefits and how to use AI features so that their perception is more positive and
their behavior control increases.

Focus on User Experience (UX): The integration of AI into e-commerce is supposed to strengthen the
user experience, not confuse or undermine user trust. Therefore, it is important to conduct tests.

Recommendations for Future Research

Future research is encouraged to examine additional factors that may better explain consumers' online
purchase intentions, such as trust in AI, perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, perceived risk, AI
transparency, and privacy concerns, as mediating  or moderating variables. Researchers are also
encouraged to extend the theoretical perspective beyond the Theory of Planned Behavior (TPB) by
incorporating theories such as the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), Unified Theory of
Acceptance and Use of Tec hnology (UTAUT2), Expectation Confirmation Theory (ECT), or Trust
Theory. Furthermore, future studies should investigate different e -commerce platforms, product
categories, and demographic or cross-country contexts to improve the generalizability of the fi ndings.
