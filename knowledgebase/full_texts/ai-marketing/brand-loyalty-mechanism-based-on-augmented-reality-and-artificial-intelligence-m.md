Abstract. As digital technology evolves towards intelligence, the collaborative application of
artificial intelligence (AI) and augmented reality (AR) has become a key factor in brand
repositioning consumer relationships and building core competitiveness. This article takes
the global sports brand giant Nike as the core research object, exploring how Nike uses
cutting-edge technologies to enhance brand loyalty. Through case analysis, this article
systematically examines the AR interaction functions and AI personalized algorithms in
Nike Fit and SNKRS and conducts an in-depth analysis based on the logical path of
"technology perception - perceived value - consumer participation - brand loyalty". The
study finds that the key to Nike's success lies in deeply integrating the spatial computing
visual perception of AR with the precise prediction logic of AI: AR technology enhances
consumers' functional and hedonic perceived value through functions such as virtual try-on;
while AI achieves personalized customization of the experience by mining sports data,
significantly strengthening consumers' emotional participation. The research conclusion
indicates that the collaboration of AI and AR not only effectively solves practical pain points
such as size matching but also strengthens brand stickiness by building an immersive
interaction loop, providing an empirical strategy path for brands in the digital
transformation.
Keywords: Augmented Reality, Perceived Value, Consumer Engagement, Brand Loyalty,
Nike.
1. Introduction
This study focuses on the continuous promotion of digital transformation, and the brand
communication mode is gradually shifting from the traditional large-scale information delivery to
the data-driven personalized interaction mode. In an environment where information is highly
fragmented, consumers are more likely to be attracted by novel and interactive content. By
superimposing virtual information on real situations, augmented reality (AR) technology enables
consumers to experience products in a close to the real environment, thus effectively improving the
effect of information transmission [1]. At the same time, the development of generative AI enables
enterprises to provide more accurate personalized services based on user data, thus further
optimizing user experience [2]. This research is very meaningful for brands in digital transformation
and enterprises seeking to use cutting-edge technology to build long-term user stickiness. It can not

2. Core theoretical framework
2.1. Consumer perception and information processing mechanism
In the digital marketing environment, consumers' processing of information primarily relies on the
perceptual process. When individuals face a large amount of information, they usually use selective
attention and perception filtering mechanisms to screen external stimuli and enhance visual
expression and real-time interaction, making brand information more prominent and increasing the
possibility of prioritization. In addition, the dynamic display and spatial interaction experience
provided by AR helps to enhance consumers' understanding of product attributes and make the
virtual experience closer to the real situation [3].
2.2. The mediating role of perceived value
In existing research, perceived value is considered to be an important intermediary factor linking
technical experience with consumer behavior. First of all, at the practical level, AR technology can
reduce information asymmetry. For example, virtual try-on or measurement functions can reduce
consumers' uncertainty in the purchasing process, thus reducing the risk of decision-making [4].
Secondly, at the level of enjoyment, the interactive experience provided by AR is very interesting
and immersive, which helps to enhance the emotional experience of consumers [3]. Third, in the AR
marketing system, artificial intelligence (AI) is mainly used as an auxiliary technology. Relevant
studies show that artificial intelligence can predict consumer behavior through data analysis and
algorithm models, so as to achieve personalized information transmission [5]. Specifically, artificial
intelligence technology analyzes users' historical behavior and preference data to provide content
support for augmented reality experiences, so that the displayed content is more in line with
personal needs. This kind of personalized service not only improves the degree of information
matching but also enhances consumers' sense of identity with the brand [2]. In addition, artificial
intelligence can improve users' participation in AR experience through intelligent recommendations

2.3. Consumer participation mechanism
In digital marketing systems, augmented reality (AR) technology enhances interactivity,
transforming consumers from passive information recipients to active participants. According to the
research of Peddamukkula and others, immersive customer participation does have a great impact on
brand loyalty [1]. Such an interactive experience can significantly improve the participation of
consumers. Moreover, this kind of participation is not only reflected in behavior, such as what to
buy and where to order, but also reflected in emotional investment, so that the emotional connection
between consumers and brands will become closer [3, 6].
In addition, artificial intelligence (AI) also plays a strengthening role in this process. Brands can
predict consumer behavior through AI algorithm models, so as to achieve personalized information
push and make users more satisfied during the interaction process. Some studies have also
mentioned that generative AI will have a long-term impact on consumers' experience and subsequent
behavior. Because AI can better optimize the interaction process, the information conveyed by the
brand can naturally be transformed into a deeper participation at the stage when users first perceive
it. With this AI-driven content optimization, consumers can get a continuous and personalized
interactive experience on different channels, and the relationship between brands and users will
become deeper.
2.4. Mechanism of brand loyalty formation
The formation of brand loyalty is the ultimate result of the joint role of perceived value and
participation mechanism. Under the action path of AR marketing, consumers are more likely to have
a positive attitude towards the brand, which further translates into the willingness to repeat
purchases and long-term brand loyalty. Taub and others pointed out that AR can effectively enhance
purchasing intentions and strengthen brand relationships by shaping the perceived value of digital
products. When the practical value and enjoyment value provided by technology are highly unified,
consumer satisfaction will be greatly improved [4].
In the process of establishing long-term relationships, the combination of artificial intelligence
technology and immersive experience is considered to be the technological innovation direction of
digital brand management. Through the continuous mining and optimization of AR interactive data
by artificial intelligence technology, brands can establish competitive barriers on the basis of
technical empowerment, causing consumers to depend on the path. This mechanism not only helps
to reduce the risk of consumers' purchasing decisions but also leaps from "technical awareness" to
"emotional attachment" through digital precision services and finally achieves stable brand loyalty.
Therefore, AR marketing systematically influences the stability of brand long-term relationships
through the complete path of "perception - value - participation - loyalty".

2.5. Research status
In the field of research on the impact of AI and AR marketing on brand loyalty, the academic
community has accumulated certain theoretical foundations, but there is still room for further
exploration. Firstly, from the content and contributions of the existing studies, explored the primary
impact of AR immersive technology on consumer behavior, particularly in how the sensory
experience of the technology can be transformed into brand loyalty [1]. At the same time, Taub et al.
in their research on "enhanced impression", detailedly analyzed how AR functions can enhance
purchase intentions by shaping the value of digital products, which provided key support for this
study in constructing the "perceived value" as an intermediary variable [4]. In the aspect of
personalized application of artificial intelligence, Bidram and Salehi proved the positive effect of
AI-driven personalized services on enhancing brand identity [2], while Rauschnabel et al. defined
the multi-dimensional value integration of augmented reality marketing, which laid the theoretical
foundation for this study [2, 7].
Secondly, although the aforementioned scholars have conducted in-depth studies on the
application of individual technologies, there are still significant research gaps in the area of
technological synergy. Most of the existing literature discusses AR or AI as independent variables,
and rarely systematically dissects how the AI decision-making logic and AR visual perception work
collaboratively in the same marketing ecosystem. Moreover, the related research is also insufficient
in the in-depth analysis of specific cases. Most of the literature focuses on the construction of
quantitative models or the analysis of macro trends, lacking detailed dissection of the technical links
in complex digital scenarios for mature brands such as Nike [6, 8].
Finally, based on the current research status mentioned above, this paper will take Nike's digital
marketing practice as the starting point. Through in-depth research on the technological synergy
mechanism of AI and AR, we can supplement the gap in the existing research on the "dual
technology-driven" path. At the same time, the case analysis of this article is based on the theoretical
cornerstone of "perception- value - participation - loyalty" recognized by the academic community,
thus ensuring the ristrictness and logic of the research.
3. Nike AR marketing case analysis
3.1. Application of AR technology and consumer perception
Nike uses augmented reality (AR) technology in their official applications (Nike App and SNKRS)
to give customers a more user-friendly product experience. Among these is the "Nike Fit" feature,
which matches shoe sizes by scanning the user's feet using the camera on the phone. This AR-based
assessment technique improves the perception of product information by giving customers a close-
to-physical try-on experience prior to purchase.
Additionally, the SNKRS app's AR unlocking feature needs users to scan particular photos to
receive product details or purchase qualifications. In this manner, information presentation becomes
more interactive, which facilitates the recognition of brand information during the perception stage.
3.2. The manifestation of perceived value
In Nike's AR marketing, the perceived value that consumers can feel is actually multifaceted. Let's
talk about practicality first. Nike's "fit" function has indeed helped users reduce a lot of trouble. For
example, the worry about the size when buying shoes has been effectively reduced, and the whole

mechanism in Nike AR marketing.
3.3. Consumer participation mechanism
With AR technology, Nike has turned the previous passive consumption mode into a highly
interactive and participatory experience. Specifically, when they promote new products, they will
design some complicated participation tasks, such as using AR scanning and other interactive forms.
For example, in the SNKRS App, consumers have to find a specific image to scan by themselves to
qualify for purchase. On the one hand, such a mechanism can increase the depth of user
participation, so that users can no longer passively receive information, but become people who
actively complete tasks; on the other hand, it can also lengthen the time of contact between users and
brands through technical means. Some studies have also mentioned that immersive interactive
experience can significantly improve the emotional participation of consumers and make users be
more emotionally and psychologically closer to the brand.
In addition, Nike will also use artificial intelligence technology to optimize the data-driven AR
interaction process, so as to ensure that the user's participation is continuous and personalized.
Through AI to accurately analyze users' browsing records and purchase preferences, Nike App can
push AR display content that is more suitable for personal needs, making every interaction quite
targeted. This transformation from "being perceived by technology" to "active participation" not
only improves the interaction satisfaction of consumers, but also sets up a complete set of
participation ecology through the consistency of online and offline multi-channel experiences.
Relying on this multi-dimensional participation mechanism, Nike can effectively deal with the
problems caused by too fragmented information, and continuously strengthen the real-time
connection between brands and users in the process of dynamic interaction.
3.4. Brand loyalty and long-term relationships
The formation of brand loyalty is the final result of the evolution of Nike's technology marketing
path. Firstly, through the functional and hedonic values brought by AR visual perception, consumers
have developed a high level of satisfaction and trust towards the brand. This trust not only reduces
the decision-making risks and trial-and-error costs for consumers, but also gradually nurtures users'

4. Discussion
In the deconstruction of Nike's technology marketing practice, it can be found that the collaboration
between AI and AR is not a simple functional superposition, but a deep logical coupling. First of all,
from the perspective of perceived value, Nike's success confirms the discussion of Rauschnabel et al
[7, 9]. on the complexity of augmented reality marketing. AR technology provides high practical
value through functions such as Nike Fit, and this accurate visual perception directly reduces the
risk of consumers' decision-making [4, 10]. However, the realization of this perceived value cannot
be separated from the underlying support of AI. AI's rapid processing and prediction algorithms for
user data give the AR interface the professional attribute of "decision-making assistance", so that
consumers can establish initial trust in the brand in the perception stage.
Secondly, to discuss how brands maintain long-term consumer participation, we must pay
attention to the AI-driven personalized logic. According to Peddamukkula, immersive customer
participation is the key to driving brand loyalty [1]. Nike has proved through applications such as
SNKRS that when AI algorithms can push customized AR interactive content based on users'
historical behavior data, consumers' enthusiasm for participation will be significantly increased. This
transformation from "popular marketing" to "individual response" not only improves the pleasure
value of interaction, but also strengthens the emotional connection between brands and users
through the continuous learning function of AI.
Finally, based on the above discussion, this article provides important management inspiration for
brands in digital transformation. When carrying out technical layout, enterprises should break the
single misunderstanding of pursuing "visual wonders" and build a technical path with solving users'
pain points as the core. Brands should follow Nike's logic, take AR as the foreground of interaction,
and AI as the backstage of intelligent decision-making, and build competitive barriers by improving
the practical perception of technology. At the same time, managers should pay attention to the
cultivation of "path dependence". Through the deep combination of AI and immersive experience,
short-term technical curiosity can be transformed into long-term emotional dependence, which is of
great strategic significance for the precipitation of brand assets in the fragmented market.

technology to build their competitive advantages, this study also provides a more actionable

infrastructure like Nike, the research conclusion may be discounted in universality when promoting
to enterprises of other sizes or industries. Therefore, it is better for future research to pay more

AI and AR under different resource conditions. At the same time, it can also combine quantitative
research methods and use larger samples for empirical testing, so as to continuously improve the
theoretical framework of digital brand loyalty.
