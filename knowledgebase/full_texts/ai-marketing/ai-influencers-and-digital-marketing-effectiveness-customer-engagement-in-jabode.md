ABSTRACT

This study aims to analyze the influence of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and influencer marketing on the
effectiveness of digital marketing campaigns  with customer engagement as a mediating variable in creative
agency startups in Greater Jakarta. This study uses a quantitative approach with a survey method of 156
creative agency startup employees  involved in the implementation of digital marketing campaigns. The
data was analyzed using Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) based on Partial Least Squares (PLS -SEM)
with the help of SmartPLS 4. The results of the study show that Artificial Intelligence and influencer
marketing have a positive and significant effect on Customer Engagement and the effectiveness of Digital
Marketing campaigns. Customer engagement has also been proven to have a positive and significant
influence on the effectiveness of digital marketing campaigns  with a path coefficient value of 0.363 (P <
0.001), and acts as a mediating variable in the relationship between AI and influencer marketing on the
effectiveness of digital marketing campaigns. These findings show that the use of AI technology through
personalization and data analysis, as well as influencer marketing strategies  through credibility and
proximity to the audience, is able to  increase customer engagement which has an impact on the
effectiveness of digital campaigns.  This research contributes by integrating Artificial Intelligence and
influencer marketing in one research model through the role  of customer engagement as a connecting
mechanism, as well as providing practical implications for creative agency startups in designing  more
adaptive and technology-based digital marketing strategies.

Keywords: Artificial Intelligence; creative agency; Customer Engagement;  influencer marketing; Digital
Marketing campaign success

1. INTRODUCTION
The fast -paced digital era has brought major changes in the business landscape, creating new
opportunities and challenges for companies, especially for startups (startups) that are looking to thrive and
survive in an increasingly competitive market. The ro le of digital startups is becoming increasingly
important due to the rapid digital transformation that occurs from time to time. Moreover, the digital
creative industry in Indonesia in recent years has experienced significant growth every year. This can be
seen from the number of startups in Indonesia, from a total of 992 startups in 2018 (MIKTI, 2018) to
1,190 startups in 2021 ( MIKTI, 2021 ). Likewise, the average number of employees grew from 56
employees in 2017 to 84 employees in 2019 for medium -scale businesses, and grew from 846 employees
in 2017 to 1025 employees in 2019 for large-scale businesses (MIKTI, 2021).
This growth requires startups to have high flexibility and innovation. Startups must be able to
adapt quickly to technological developments and changes in consumer behavior. In this context, startups
often rely on digital strategies to reach a wider audience, increasing brand awareness, and creating a more
personal relationship with consumers (Chaffey & Ellis-Chadwick, 2016).
One of the essential elements of a successful digital strategy is the existence of a creative agency
who are able to develop innovative and solutive ideas to market startup products or services. Agustin et
al. (2023) explained that creative agencies focus on marketing design and product design. Creative agencies
have a strategic role in designing and running engaging and effective marketing campaigns, utilizing various
digital platforms to achieve client goals. In the  face of challenges and opportunities in the digital world,
creative agencies must be able to leverage the latest technology and marketing methodologies to ensure
that the campaigns they run can compete in a market full of diverse content and information.
Chaffey and Ellis -Chadwick (2016) explained that every day, there are millions of new content
published in the digital world. The more time consumers spend reading and interacting with this amount
of content online, the challenge of engaging them is even greater, so every company must dev elop a
strategy customer engagement as a key part of a digital marketing strategy. Strategy customer engagement
It reviews various approaches to creating content and experiences that appeal to customers. Some of the
strategies that are increasingly popular used by creative agencies are the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)
and influencer marketing.
AI  is a technology modeled to simulate human intelligence (Santoso, 2023). AI has revolutionized
digital marketing with its increasingly sophisticated content analytics and personalization capabilities. This
technology allows brands to conduct more in -depth data analysis, automatically optimize ads, and create
more relevant user experiences. Over time, AI has demonstra ted its ability to influence consumer
decisions, by providing more precise recommendations, tailored content, and higher conversion rates
(Kusnadi et al., 2025).
Meanwhile, influencer marketing remains an equally important strategy, especially in the context
of social media. An influencer is someone who has a large following on social media, with a loyal audience
and credibility in a particular field, has the power  to influence consumer behavior and build brand
awareness through the information or opinions they share (Syukur & Salsabila, 2022). Research by Adryan
et al. (2024) shows that influencer marketing is effective in increasing sales conversions and building trust
in the digital market. Through the power of social media, influencers help brands connect more deeply
with their audiences. This can increase consumer engagement and loyalty.
Each approach has its own advantages, but it also faces different challenges and limitations.
Although there are many studies on these two approaches separately, there is still little research that
discusses the comparative effectiveness of the two, especi ally in Greater Jakarta (Jakarta, Bogor, Depok,
Tangerang, and Bekasi), a highly dynamic and diverse market region, amid rapid technological
developments and changing consumer behavior , companies and creative agencies face a major challenge
to remain relevant and competitive. Therefore, it is important to compare these two strategies in a single
study that integrates the two, focusing on customer engagement as a mediating variable that connects
marketing strategies and desired outcomes, in order to provide much-needed insights for creative agencies
in designing successful marketing strategies.

2. LITERATURE REVIEW
2.1. Theoretical Framework
This study integrates the Elaboration Likelihood Model (ELM) and the Stimulus -Organism-
Response (S-O-R) theory to explain the relationships among AI, Influencer, customer engagement, and
digital marketing campaign effectiveness. ELM explains that consumers  process marketing messages
through central and peripheral routes, where AI operates through data-driven information personalization,
while influencers operate through message credibility and attractiveness ( Petty & Cacioppo, 1986 ).
Meanwhile, S-O-R explains that AI and influencers as external stimuli can influence customer engagement
as an internal consumer process, which subsequently generates responses in the form of digital campaign
effectiveness (Forde et al., 2023). Based on these theories, this study positions customer engagement as a
mediating variable that explains the mechanism through which AI and influencers influence digital
marketing campaign effectiveness.
2.2. Artificial Intelligence in Digital Marketing
AI in digital marketing refers to technology used to analyze consumer data, personalize content,
and optimize marketing strategies automatically ( Kaplan & Haenlein, 2019 ). In this study, AI includes
data-driven personalization, marketing automation, and predictive analytics, which enable companies to
deliver more relevant messages to consumers (Belinda & Nofitasari, 2025). From the S-O-R perspective,
AI acts as a stimulus that can enhance customer engagement and digital campaign effectiveness. Lv et al.
(2022) demonstrated that AI capabilities influence customer engagement, while Guha et al. (2021) proved
that AI improves campaign performance through marketing personalization.

2.3. Influencer Marketing
Influencer marketing is a marketing strategy that utilizes influential individuals on social media to
deliver brand messages to audiences ( Freberg et al., 2011). The effectiveness of influencers is influenced
by credibility, authenticity, and their ability to establish relationships with followers ( Vrontis et al., 2021).
Based on Source Credibility Theory, influencers with high expertise, trustworthiness, and attractiveness
can increase message acceptance and customer engagement ( Winata & Alvin, 2022). Casaló et al. (2020)
showed that influencer credibility improves consumer behavior, while Tafesse and Wood (2021)  found
that influencer content quality influences engagement rates and digital campaign effectiveness.

2.4. Customer Engagement as a Mediating Variable
Customer engagement refers to customer involvement at cognitive, emotional, and behavioral
levels toward brands or marketing activities ( Brodie et al., 2011 ). In this study, customer engagement
functions as a mediating variable within the S -O-R framework, explaining the consumer process after
receiving stimuli from AI and influencers. Customer engagement can increase interaction, participation,
and positive re sponses toward digital campaigns ( Nurwijayanto & Dharmawan, 2023). Harrigan et al.
(2017) demonstrated that customer engagement serves as a link between digital marketing activities and
marketing outcomes, while Naumann et al. (2020)  found that customer engagement is associated with
improved consumer behavior.

2.5. Digital Marketing Campaign Effectiveness
Digital marketing campaign effectiveness refers to the level of success of digital marketing activities
in achieving organizational communication and business objectives. Campaign effectiveness is not only
measured through reach and impressions but also in cludes engagement rate, conversion rate, and return
on investment (Chaffey & Ellis-Chadwick, 2016). In this study, digital marketing campaign effectiveness
is positioned as the response within the S -O-R framework, influenced by AI, influencers, and customer
engagement. The integration of AI technology and influencer strategies is expected to improve campaign
effectiveness by delivering more relevant and attractive messages to audiences (Yusian & Aulia, 2021).

2.6. Hypothesis Development
2.6.1. The Effect of Artificial Intelligence on Customer Engagement
Based on the Stimulus-Organism-Response (S-O-R) framework, AI acts as a stimulus that creates
more relevant digital experiences through personalization, automation, and consumer behavior analysis.
The ability of AI to provide information according to customer needs can increase attention, interaction,
and customer engagement toward brands. Mustak et al. (2021)  and Lv et al. (2022)  found that AI
capabilities positively influence customer engagement. Therefore, the first hypothesis is formulated as
follows:
H1: Artificial Intelligence has a positive and significant effect on Customer Engagement.

2.6.2. The Effect of Influencer on Customer Engagement
Based on Source Credibility Theory, influencers with credibility, expertise, and attractiveness can
increase audience trust toward marketing messages. Emotional relationships between influencers and
followers also encourage interaction and customer engagem ent with digital content. Vrontis et al. (2021)
and Tafesse and Wood (2021)  showed that influencer characteristics and content quality influence
customer engagement. Therefore, the second hypothesis is formulated as follows:
H2: Influencer has a positive and significant effect on Customer Engagement.

2.6.3. The Effect of Artificial Intelligence on Digital Marketing Campaign Effectiveness
The Resource-Based View perspective explains that AI capabilities represent strategic resources
that can improve marketing advantages through data analysis, consumer behavior  prediction, and
campaign optimization. The implementation of AI enables companies to develop more effective and
efficient marketing strategies. Guha et al. (2021)  and Puntoni et al. (2021)  demonstrated that AI
implementation improves digital campaign performance. Therefore:
H3: Artificial Intelligence has a positive and significant effect on Digital Marketing Campaign
Effectiveness.

2.6.4. The Effect of Influencer on Digital Marketing Campaign Effectiveness
Based on the ELM, influencers act as peripheral cues in consumer persuasion processes through
personal credibility and attractiveness. Influencers who successfully build trust and emotional closeness
with audiences can improve digital campaign success. Casaló et al. (2020)  and Trivedi and Sama (2020)
showed that influencer marketing influences consumer responses and marketing effectiveness. Therefore:
H4: Influencer has a positive and significant effect on Digital Marketing Campaign Effectiveness.

2.6.5. The Effect of Customer Engagement on Digital Marketing Campaign Effectiveness
Customer engagement describes consumer involvement at cognitive, emotional, and behavioral
levels toward marketing activities. Consumers with high engagement levels tend to interact more actively,
share content, and perform actions that support campaign success. Studies by Brodie et al. (2011), Harrigan
et al. (2017) , and Naumann et al. (2020)  indicate that customer engagement plays an important role in
improving digital marketing outcomes. Therefore:
H5: Customer Engagement has a positive and significant effect on Digital Marketing Campaign
Effectiveness.

2.6.6. The Mediating Role of Customer Engagement in the Relationship Between Artificial
Intelligence and Digital Marketing Campaign Effectiveness
From the S-O-R perspective, AI acts as a stimulus that influences consumers’ internal conditions
through customer engagement, which subsequently generates responses in the form of digital campaign
effectiveness. Thus, AI not only has a direct effect but also influences campaign effectiveness through

engagement functions as a mechanism connecting digital marketing activities and marketing outcomes.
Therefore:
H6: Customer Engagement mediates the effect of Artificial Intelligence on Digital Marketing Campaign
Effectiveness.

2.6.7. The Mediating Role of Customer Engagement in the Relationship Between Influencer and

Based on Source Credibility Theory and S -O-R, influencers can build customer engagement

3. METHODOLOGY
This study employs a quantitative approach with an explanatory causal research design to examine
the relationships between AI, influencer marketing, Customer Engagement, and the effectiveness of
Digital Marketing campaigns. The quantitative approach is bas ed on the positivist paradigm, which
emphasizes empirical testing of relationships among variables through statistical analysis. The research
model assumes that AI and influencer marketing influence campaign effectiveness both directly and
indirectly throu gh Customer Engagement as a mediating variable. Therefore, seven hypotheses are
formulated to test the causal relationships among variables in creative agency startups in Greater Jakarta
(Sekaran & Bougie, 2016).
The population of this study consists of employees of creative agency startups in the Greater
Jakarta area who are involved in planning, implementing, or evaluating digital marketing campaigns using
AI technology and influencer marketing strategies. Sampli ng was conducted using purposive sampling
based on predetermined criteria, including respondents who have experience managing digital marketing
campaigns, utilizing AI-based marketing tools, collaborating with influencers, and having at least one year
of experience in the digital marketing industry. The final sample consisted of 156 respondents. Purposive
sampling was selected because respondents were determined based on specific considerations related to
the research objectives (Sugiyono, 2016).
Data were collected using a structured online questionnaire distributed through Google Forms.
The research instrument used a five -point Likert scale ranging from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly
agree) to measure AI, influencer marketing, customer engagement , and digital marketing campaign
effectiveness. Primary data were obtained from questionnaires, while secondary data were collected from
scientific literature and previous studies relevant to the research topic ( Noor, 2016). Instrument testing
was conducted through validity and reliability evaluations to ensure that the indicators accurately
represented the constructs and produced consistent measurements (Suharyat et al., 2023; Yusup, 2018).
Data analysis was conducted using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM)
with SmartPLS 4 software. The measurement model (outer model) was evaluated through outer loading,
Average Variance Extracted (AVE), Composite Reliability, Cronbach’s Alpha, and discriminant validity.
The structural model (inner model) was assessed through path coeffic ients, R-square values, effect size,
and indirect effects to examine direct and mediating relationships among variables. Hypothesis testing was
performed using bootstrapping analysis with criteria of t -statistic > 1.96 and p -value < 0.05 at a 5%
significance level (Hair et al., 2010).

5. DISCUSSION

5.1. The Influence of Artificial Intelligence on Customer engagement
The test results showed that Artificial Intelligence had a positive and significant effect on Customer
engagement, which was shown by the path coefficient value of 0.265, T -Statistics of 2.423, and P-Values
of 0.015. These findings indicate that the use of AI is able to increase customer engagement through
content personalization, recommendations that match user preferences, and faster and more responsive
interactions. The more optimal the implementation of AI technology in digital marketing activities, the
higher the customer participation in providing comments, likes, shares, and other forms of interaction so
that the relationship between the company and customers becomes stronger.
The results of this study are in line with the research Su (2025) which states that the application of
AI, particularly through AI -driven virtual influencers, able to improve consumer engagement because it
provides a more interactive and personalized experience to users. In addition, the research Fattouh et al.
(2026) also proves that AI personalization, trustworthiness, and responsiveness has a significant effect on
customer engagement in a digital marketing environment. Thus, the results of this study strengthen the
empirical evidence that AI is an important factor in increasing customer engagement in the digital age.

5.2. The Influence of Artificial Intelligence on the Effectiveness of Digital Marketing Campaigns
The results of the study show that Artificial Intelligence has a positive and significant effect on the
effectiveness of digital marketing campaigns  with  a path coefficient value of 0.289, T-Statistics of 4.432,
and P-Values of 0.000. These results show that the use of AI is able to increase the success of campaigns
through more accurate analysis of customer data, personalization of marketing messages, au tomation of
marketing processes, and the ability to identify appropriate target audiences. This condition makes
campaigns more efficient and able to generate better conversion rates.
These findings are supported by Abdullah (2025) which states that AI technology is becoming
game changer in digital marketing because it is able to increase the effectiveness of marketing strategies
through service personalization, data -driven decision -making, and improved customer interaction. In
addition, the research Fattouh et al. (2026) shows that AI personalization contributes significantly to the
improvement of customer engagement which ultimately has an impact on the success of digital marketing
activities. These findings strengthen the results of this study that the implementation of AI is able to
increase the effectiveness of digital marketing campaigns directly and through incr easing customer
interaction.

5.3. The Influence of Influencers on Customer Engagement
The test results showed that Influencers had a positive and significant effect on customer
engagement with  a path coefficient value of 0.307, t-statistics of 2.498, and p-values of 0.013. these results
show that the existence of influencers who have credibility, appeal, and proximity to the audience is able
to increase customer engagement in digital campaigns. Audiences tend to trust the information conveyed
by influencers so they are more motivated to respond, discuss, or share published content.
The results of this study are in line with the research Aldlimi et al. (2025) which concludes that
influencer credibility, content quality, and parasocial relationships with followers are the main factors
driving the increase customer engagement in social media. Research Kumar et al. (2025) also found that
influencer marketing is more effective in increasing customer engagement  compared to direct brand
communication because it is able to build a stronger emotional connection with consumers. Therefore,
the results of this study further strengthen that the use of influencers is an effective strategy in increasing
customer engagement in digital marketing campaigns.

5.4. The Influence of Influencers on the Effectiveness of Digital Marketing Campaigns
The results of the hypothesis test show that Influencers have a positive and significant effect on
the effectiveness of digital marketing campaigns  with  a path coefficient value of 0.240, T -Statistics of
2.285, and P-Values of 0.022. These results show that the higher the credibility, appeal, and communication
skills of influencers, the more effective the digital marketing campaign run. Influencers are able to increase
campaign reach, build consumer trust, and strengthen brand image, encouraging audiences to take action
according to campaign goals.
The results of this study are in line with the research of Kumar et al. (2025)  which found that
influencer-directed social media marketing has a positive effect on consumer behavior and the
effectiveness of digital marketing, especially in improving purchase behavior and achieving campaign goals.
In addition, Fattouh et al. (2026)  also explained that influencer characteristics, especially expertise and
communication quality, contribute to increasing the effectiveness of digital marketing activities through
increasing consumer interaction. Thus, the results of this study strengthen the empirical evidence that
influencers are still one of the effective strategies in increasing the success of digital marketing campaigns.

5.5. The Influence of Customer engagement on the Effectiveness of Digital Marketing
Campaigns
The results of the study show that customer engagement has a positive and significant effect on
the effectiveness of digital marketing campaigns  with  a path coefficient value of 0.363, T -Statistics of
4.261, and P-Values of 0.000. These values are the biggest direct influence on the research model. These
findings show that the higher the customer engagement in responding to a campaign, the higher the

The test results showed that Customer engagement was able to mediate the influence of Artificial
Intelligence on the effectiveness of digital marketing campaigns  with  a path coefficient value of 0.096, T-
Statistics of 1.987, and P-Values of 0.047. These results show that the application of Artificial Intelligence
not only increases the effectiveness of campaigns directly, but also through increased customer
engagement. In other words, AI that is able to generate personalized content, quick responses, and a better
user experience will increase customer engagement, so that the effectiveness of digital campaigns will also
increase.
These findings are in line with research by Fattouh et al. (2026) which shows that personalization,
responsiveness, and trustworthiness in AI can increase customer engagement , which in turn has an impact
on consumer behavior and digital marketing success. Su's (2025) research also explains that AI technology
that is able to  create interactive digital experiences will increase consumer engagement so that it has a
positive impact on the effectiveness of digital marketing activities. Thus, Customer engagement has proven
to be a mediating variable that strengthens the influence of AI on the effectiveness of digital marketing
campaigns.

5.7. The Role of Customer Engagement in Mediating the Influence of Influencers on the
Effectiveness of Digital Marketing Campaigns
The results of the study showed that customer engagement significantly  mediated the influence
of Influencers on the effectiveness of digital marketing campaigns  with  a path coefficient value of 0.112,
T-Statistics of 2.090, and P -Values by 0.037. These findings show that influencers' success in increasing
campaign effectiveness comes not only from direct influence, but also through their ability to bui ld close
relationships with audiences thereby increasing customer engagement during the campaign.
The results of this study are supported by Kumar et al. (2025) who state that marketing through
influencers is able to increase customer engagement, which further contributes to improving purchasing
behavior and the effectiveness of digital marketing. In addition, Fattouh et al. (2026) found that customer
engagement is an important mechanism that connects influencer characteristics with consumer behavior
in the digital environment, so that the higher the customer involvement, the higher the success of the
digital marketing strategy. These results reinforce the findings of this study that Customer engagement
plays a role as a mediating variable that is able to increase the influence of influencers on the effectiveness
of digital marketing campaigns.

6. CONCLUSION

Based on the results of the research, it can be concluded that Artificial Intelligence and Influencers
have a positive and significant effect on customer engagement and the effectiveness of digital marketing
campaigns. In addition, customer engagement has also been proven to have a positive and significant
effect on the effectiveness of digital marketing campaigns  and is able to mediate the influence of AI and
Influencers on the effectiveness of digital marketing campaigns. These findings show that the application
of AI that is able to generate content personalization, accurate data analysis, and marketing automation,
as well as the use of influencers who have credibility and proximity to the audience, can increase customer
