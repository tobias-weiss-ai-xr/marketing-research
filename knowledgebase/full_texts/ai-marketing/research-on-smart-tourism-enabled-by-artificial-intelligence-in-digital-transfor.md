Abstract
In	the	era	of	the	digital	economy,	digital	transformation	has	become	the	core	path	for
the	 tourism	 industry	 to	 improve	 quality,	 increase	 efficiency	 and	 achieve	 high‐quality
development.	 As	 a	 core	 branch	 of	 digital	 technologies,	 artificial	 intelligence	 (AI)
provides	crucial	technical	support	for	the	construction	of	smart	tourism	and	industrial
upgrading.	Against	the	backdrop	of	the	digital	transformation	of	the	tourism	industry,
this	paper	defines	the	core	concepts	of	artificial	intelligence,	smart	tourism	and	digital
transformation.	 Based	 on	 industrial	 integration	 theory,	 experience	 economy	 theory
and	 other	 relevant	 theoretical	 foundations,	 this	 paper	 analyzes	 the	 application	 status
of	 artificial	 intelligence	 in	 four	 major	 scenarios	 of	 smart	 tourism,	 including	 tourism
services,	 operation	 management,	 marketing	 and	 cultural	 inheritance.	 It	 identifies
prominent	 problems	 in	 the	 current	 integrated	 development,	 such	 as	 data	 silos,
superficial	 technology	 application,	 shortage	 of	 interdisciplinary	 talents,	 high
transformation	 costs	 and	 imperfect	 supervision	 systems.	 Targeted	 optimization
countermeasures	are	further	proposed	to	promote	the	in‐depth	integration	of	artificial
intelligence	 and	 the	 tourism	 industry,	 facilitate	 the	 steady	 development	 of	 smart
tourism,	and	realize	the	digital	and	intelligent	transformation	of	the	tourism	industry.
Keywords

2. Relevant	Concepts	and	Theoretical	Foundations
2.1. Definition	of	Core	Concepts
2.1.1. Artificial	Intelligence
Artificial intelligence is a technical science that simulates h uman intelligent activities. Relying
on algorithms, data and computer technologies, it realizes func tions including data processing,
autonomous learning, logical judgment and interactive decision- making. Covering machine
learning, speech recognition, machine vision, natural language processing, intelligent robots,
generative AI and other technica l categories, artificial intell igence features automation,
intelligence, personalization and high efficiency.[3] Widely ap plicable to the entire industrial
chain of tourism, it serves as a core technical driving force for the digital transformation of the
tourism industry.
2.1.2. Digital	Transformation
The digital transformation of the tourism industry refers to a process that takes data as the
core production factor and digital technologies as the core mea ns to comprehensively
reconstruct the business processes, operation modes, management  s y s t e m s  a n d  v a l u e

tourism, realizes optimal resource allocation, accurate service supply and refined and efficient
management, and promotes the transformation of the tourism indu stry from resource-driven
development to digital innovation-driven development.
2.1.3. Smart	Tourism
Smart tourism is a new tourism model relying on new-generation information technologies
such as artificial intelligence, big data and IoT. Centering on  t o u r i s t  e x p e r i e n c e ,  i t  c o v e r s  a l l

smart tourism aims to improve tourist satisfaction, industrial operation efficiency and the
utilization value of cultural and tourism resources.

The industrial integration theory holds that different industri e s  c a n  b r e a k  i n d u s t r i a l

2.2.2. Experience	Economy	Theory
The experience economy theory takes consumer demand as the core  and focuses on creating

2.2.3. Data	Factor	Theory
The data factor theory points out that data is a key production  factor in the digital economy
era. The collection, analysis an d utilization of data can reali ze optimal resource allocation and
scientific decision-making. Throug h mining and analyzing touris m data, artificial intelligence
enables precise services, intelligent management and efficient marketing, highlighting the
core value of data factors in the development of smart tourism.
