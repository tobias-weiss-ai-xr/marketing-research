Abstract:
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has been increasingly used in personalized marketing to provide appropriate
recommendations and information to consumers. However, there is growing  concern that an AI
hallucination can affect the consumer responses and the experiences. Therefore, this study focuses on the
effect of AI hallucination on trust and purchase intention, post -purchase behavior, and the differences in
consumer perceptions of an AI hallucinations based on age. This study has adopted a quantitative research
design, and the data were collected from 230 participants using a five-point Likert scale questionnaire. In
this study, Pearson Correlation, Regression Analysis and One -Way ANOV A were performed. The study
revealed that there was a strong correlation between AI hallucinations and consumer trust and purchase
intention. Moreover, AI-generated recommendations that mislead consumers and AI-generated inaccurate
information significantly affected consumer post-purchase behaviors. Besides that, there was a significant
difference in consumer perceptions of AI hallucinations based on age. This study provides important
insights into the effects of personalized marketing driven by AI hall ucinations and highlights the
significance of being transparent and accurate with consumers.

Keywords: AI Hallucinations, Personalized Marketing, Consumer Trust, Purchase Intention, Post -
Purchase Behavior.

INTRODUCTION
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has transformed personalized marketing by enabling organizations to analyse
consumer preferences and deliver tailored recommendations, advertisements and product information. AI-
driven personalization can improve the relevance and convenience of marketing commun ication and
influence consumer decision-making.
However, the increasing use of AI also creates the risk of AI hallucinations, where AI systems generate
inaccurate, misleading or fabricated information that may appear credible. In personalized marketing, such
inaccuracies can occur through misleading product recommendations, incorrect product information or

1. Davenport et al. (2020) examined that how an impact of an artificial intelligence on the modern
marketing by improving an interaction of consumers, personalisation and the decision making
processes. They discovered that by using of an artificial intelligence, an organisations can analyse the
large amount of the consumer data and thus it generate the personalised recommendations and also the
marketing communication. By using of an artificial intelligence, the effectiveness, the prediction and
the consumer engagement in the marketing will becomes easier.

of view. It was discovered that the influence of an AI on the experiences of consumers is related to

consumers perceive the trustworthiness, intelligence, emotional aspect and personalisation of the
technology.

considered while determining the consumer willingness to interact with an AI-powered chatbot.

RESEARCH GAP
The previous stud ies related to an AI -assisted personalised marketing have mainly concentrated on the
positive impacts of an artificial intelligence. These include personalisation, customer engagement, trust
and the purchase intention. Recent research on AI hallucinations,  misinformation and an ethical issues
with generative AI technology have focused on these issues from a technological perspective or how such
issues may affect the trust. Very little is known empirically about how an AI hallucinations in personalised
marketing affect consumer trust, purchase intention and the post purchase behavior at once. Therefore,
this study is to fill the gap by examining the effects of AI hallucinations in personalized marketing on the
consumer responses. Further, limited attention ha s been given to whether consumer perceptions of AI
hallucinations differ across age groups. Therefore, the present study addresses these gaps by examining
the relationship between AI hallucinations, consumer trust and purchase intention, their effect on po st-
purchase behavior, and differences in perceptions across age groups.

RESEARCH METHODOLOGY
Research Design: Quantitative
Nature of Data: Primary
Sample and Data Collection: The data were collected 230 respondents and selected through convenience
sampling from BTM Layout, South Bengaluru. And Primary data were collected through using a
structured questionnaire based on 5-point Likert Scale
Data Analysis: Frequency and percentage were used to present the demographic profile of respondents.
Pearson Correlation was used to examine the relationship between AI hallucinations, consumer trust and
purchase intention. Regression Analysis was used to analyse the effect of AI -generated misleadi ng
recommendations and inaccurate AI-generated content on post-purchase behavior. One-Way ANOV A was
used to examine differences in consumer perceptions of AI hallucinations across selected demographic
variables.

CONCLUSION
This study concludes that the influence of an AI hallucinations on personalized marketing has a moderate
positive correlation with consumer trust and purchase intention and also has a significant influence of
Post-Purchase Behavior. The results has shown that the perception of an AI hallucinations will differ
significantly across age groups. This study highli ghts and suggests that there should be need for
prioritizing accuracy, transparency and reliability in the utilizing of AI for personalized Marketing.  This
study also  highlights of  the need to ensure the accuracy and the relevance  of all an AI -generated
recommendations and the content to the consumers. By reducing the possibility of an AI hallucination and
an increasing the level of the trust in the accuracy of an information provided by an AI and the companies
will be able to avoid the consumer dissatisfa ction and enhance the quality of their personalization.
This way, it will be possible for an organizations to deliver more  value to their  customers and also offer
a higher level of personalized marketing solutions based on artificial intelligence.

SUGGESTIONS
 Implement AI verification and validation mechanisms to ensure that information that is disseminated
through personalization is not deceptive, inaccurate or fabricated.
 Ensure that there is a transparency in personalization by providing disclosure of the use of an AI in
marketing and also ensuring that the information provided is reliable and not misleading.
 Ensure that there are audits on AI recommendations and  contents including correction of
any inaccuracies, recommendations and inconsistencies.
 Develop responsible AI guidelines for marketing to ensure that recommendations
and contents are reliable, ethical and of relevance to the consumers.
 Conduct consumer feedback on the recommendations and marke ting contents to determine the
effectiveness and the reliability based on consumer response and experience.
 Provide the measures  for consumers to report  any AI-generated information  that is incorrect or
misleading. This will enable the organisation to dete ct the hallucination and address  the issue
promptly to enhance the consumer experience.
 Constantly update and refine the systems based on the information which is provided
by consumers and other relevant data. This can help in minimising the occurrence of the
hallucinations and also ensure that recommendations are appropriate and are dependable.
