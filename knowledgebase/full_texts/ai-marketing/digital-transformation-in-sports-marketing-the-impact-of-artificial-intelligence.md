Purpose: In today’s digital age, customers are inundated with many sports
marketing messages, prompting businesses to adopt data -driven marketing
strategies to minimize dissatisfaction and engage customers on a deeper level.
At the core of these strategies lies personalization, a powerful technique that
enables sports ma rketers to tailor their messages and offers according to
individual preferences. This study investigates the influence of AI on enhancing
personalization and improving customer engagement in this domain.
Methodology: Data was gathered using a questionnaire  created by the
researcher. This was done through simple random sampling from 210 people
who are interested in digital sports and digital sports marketing. The research
employed a quantitative approach and structural equation modeling (SEM). The
model was developed using Smart PLS TM3 software and evaluated in two
sections: measurement and structural model.
Findings: The results indicated a good fit for the model, with a reported GoF
index of 0.44, demonstrating substantial validation power. Findings from 2 10
respondents revealed that AI technologies can enhance personalization and
customer interaction in digital sports marketing. However, challenges such as
privacy concerns and dissatisfaction with non -human interactions need to be
managed in this process.
Originality/value: By emphasizing the integration of AI in digital sports
marketing, this research contributes to the literature and provides a robust
framework for future studies. As the sports industry increasingly embraces
digital transformation, underst anding the role of AI in shaping consumer
experiences becomes crucial. This study addresses the current research gaps and
highlights AI's potential  to redefine marketing strategies in a competitive
landscape.

1. Introduction
With the emergence and expansion of advanced technologies stemming from the Fourth
Industrial Revolution (IR4) and the development of information and communication technologies
(ICT), the sports industry has undergone substantial transformations [1]. These changes have turned the
sports industry into one of the most dynamic and competitive markets, highlighting the need for
innovative and effective strategies to retain and attract new customers [2-4]. In this context, artificial
intelligence (AI) has played a crucial role in transforming and improving sports marketing processes
[5]. Additionally, researchers expect an increasing trend in the transformation of the labor market
because of the development and widespread use of AI [3, 6].
By making it possible to collect, analyze, and interpret large amounts of data from different
sources, AI has helped sports organizations manage their marketing strategies more efficien tly [7].
However, most past research has focused on traditional marketing methods and general digital
techniques [8], with less attention given to the application of AI in improved personalization (IP) [9-
11] and interaction with customers (IC) [12]. The use of AI in sports marketing provides the opportunity
to offer unique and targeted marketing experiences through customer data analysis and the creation of
personalized profiles, leading to improved business performance in attracting and retaining customers
[13].
Personalization is one of the key aspects of digital marketing that directly impacts customer
experience [14]. AI customizes marketing messages and exper iences based on specific needs and
preferences by analyzing consumer behavior data and profiles and adapting targeted advertisements and
product recommendations to specific consumer segments [15-17]. This enhances customer experience
and increases their satisfaction and loyalty [14].
Customer interaction, as one of the fundamental principles of successful marketing [18], plays a
vital role in boosting customer motivati on and engagement in sports activities and increasing
companies' sales and revenues [19, 20]. AI can create targeted and tailored advertising campaigns that
meet customer needs by analyzing data related to customer purchase behavior [21]. For instance, sports
clubs have been able to offer customized suggestions to their fans using AI, resulting in in creased
engagement and revenue from ticket and merchandise sales [22]. Thus, using AI in digital sports
marketing enhances customer experience and increases their satisfaction and loyalty [17].
AI can contribute to increased sales, export development, and gr eater profitability for
organizations and companies [23]. Recent studies have shown that sports teams leveraging AI in their
marketing strategies have substantially increased their return on investment (ROI) [22, 24-26]. In sports
marketing, AI develops more accurate strategies by ana lyzing fan behavior and preferences, which
improves fan experience, enhances their engagement with brands and sports teams, and leads to
increased marketing revenues [27]. For example, sports clubs create more targeted advertising
campaigns and improve fan experience by analyzing ticket purchase data and fans' online activities
[28].
Research shows that AI can identify behavioral patterns and offer personalized suggestions that
increase customer engagement and satisfaction by analyzing user data [29]. With its advanced
capabilities, this technology predicts that sports marketing will undergo substan tial transformations
[17]. The results indicate that concepts such as customer, social, industry, tourism, and brand are
emphasized in digital marketing, while concepts like club, social, and competitive are highlighted in
articles related to sports and digital marketing [6]. The use of AI in digital sports marketing plays an
essential role in enhancing service personalization and increasing customer engagement [30]. By
utilizing big data an alysis and advanced algorithms, AI can identify customer behavior patterns and
provide suggestions tailored to their needs and preferences [31]. Moreover, studies show that using
intelligent chatbots on sports platforms has led to increased user engagement and improved post -sales

Recently, there has been a significant surge in AI  and cloud computing adoption across various
sectors, including the sports sector [39]. Technologies such as AI algorithms, machine learning,
computer vision, cloud computing, and natural language processing are being employed to derive
meaningful insights from sports -related data [40]. As AI systems become increasingly essential in
various applications, it is crucial to ensure their dependability [41]. In a time when technological
progress is reshaping the essence of humanity, AI emerges as a significant transformative force among
various technologies [42].
The swift development of generative AI tools (i.e., ChatGPT), particularly since the end of 2022,
has attracted attention to the effects of this technology on the economy, society, and especially on higher
education (HE) [43]. AI initially emerged in the form of computers and related technologies and
gradually transitioned to intelligent web -based and online learning systems. This trend also includes
using humanoid robots and web -based chatbots to perform educational activities [44]. Although AI
systems are designed for sound decision -making, most Americans distrust AI's decisions  [45].

2-2. The Application of AI in Marketing
Today, machine learning and AI adoption are driven by numerous advantages, including ease of
use, high speed, and exceptional accuracy in predicting various processes. Additionally, these
technologies do not require compl ex equipment and tools  and are supported by various applications
across different scientific fields  [50]. In today's fast -changing digital environment, propelled by
technological innovations like AI, organizations, and businesses must quickly initiate digital
transformation efforts to secure their survival and growth. Artificial Intelligence Marketing (AIM) is a
strategy to maximize technology and market data to enhance customer experiences and understand their
needs. This type of marketing bridges the gap between data science and marketing by analyzing large
volumes of data. In today’s world, intelligent marketing has significantly transformed the market
environment, and it is expected to revolutionize it completely in the near future, being regarded as the
core of business [51].
For developing countries such as Iran, digital transformation offers a chance to improve the
efficiency and effectiveness of businesses within their economic framework, especially in the face of
economic challenges [52]. AI can revolutionize various facets of business operations . [53-56]. As

for growth [59]. Moreover, incorporating advanced analytics techniques and AI algorithms allows
organizations to derive valuable insights from large datasets, enhance decision -making processes, and
secure a competitive advantage in today’s data-driven economy [60].
2-3. The potential of AI in Improved Personalization (IP)
Personalization is a comprehensive concept that appears in various marketing research studies,
including those related to human-computer interaction, data mining, and machine learning [61]. In the
realm of marketing, personalization typically refers to a customer-centric marketing strategy designed
to convey the appropriate message to the right customer at the right moment [62]. In other words, the
messages are directed toward specific customers or customer segments based on their established
characteristics [63]. Moreover, personalization is one of AI's most intriguing applications in digital
marketing. AI algorithms analyze user behavior and preferences to create personalized experiences [4].
The use of AI in sports marketing and tele -sales provides novel opportunities for improving
performance and results in this area. Data analysis, sales automation, and the ability to predict and
enhance strategies are all part of the benefits of utilizing AI in this field [42].
The growing interest in personalization among researchers can be attributed to the rising use of
the Internet [64] and the features of e-commerce services [62]. However, the concept of personalization
actually predates the Internet [64]. As noted by Vesanen [65], personalization may be as ancient as trade
itself. For example, in traditional retail settings, sales representatives often tailor their approach to
customers by adapting their behavior, such as addressing them by name [66]. Despite this historical
context, the internet has significantly enhanced the methods and opportunit ies for personalization.
Today, customers can receive customized search results that reflect their individual preferences and

focus on the context, describing personalization as a tailored web experience [68]. Montgomery and
Smith [64] define it in a technological context, stating that personalization involves adapting products

AI significantly tra nsforms customer relationship management (CRM) and how  businesses

The adoption of AI is rapidly increasing, and there are compelling reasons for this trend [11]. It

relationships within the business marketing process [51]. AI-based CRM is a hybri d approach to
enhance long-term customer relationships by providing personalized and tailored services. Hence, the
second hypothesis states:
H2. AI positively influences IC.
3. Methodology
The primary objective of this research is to examine the impact of artificial intelligence (AI) on
improved personalization (IP) and interaction with customers (IC) in digital sports marketing.
Methodologically, this study is descriptive -correlational and based on structural equation modeling
(SEM), and its purpose is app lied. A quantitative research method was selected to test the specified
hypotheses, enabling the researchers to understand the relationships between variables better using
statistical evidence. The statistical population of this study in 2024 consisted of individuals interested
in digital sports and digital sports marketing. This population included users of digital sports platforms
and websites, mobile and web applications, customers of online sports product stores, and those
interested in technology and AI in sports. The demographic characteristics of the statistical population
included gender, age, education level, and familiarity with digital technologies.
In this study, 210 participants were selected using simple random sampling according to the
Morgan table. This method was chosen to ensure the statistical population's representativeness and
reduce bias in the results. Individual contact information (such as email, phone number, and social

47

media accounts) was used to select the samples. The authors assured respondents that their data would
be confidential and used solely for academic research purposes.
Data for this study were collected online through a res earcher-designed questionnaire. The
questionnaire was developed based on a comprehensive literature review and consultation with digital
sports marketing and AI experts . The questionnaire included various sections addressing specific
aspects of AI's impact on personalization and customer interaction. The questions were arranged on a
five-point Likert scale, ranging from "strongly disagree" to "strongly agree." The questionnaire included
three sections: the use of AI (5 questions), the level of personalizati on of customer experience (5
questions), and the degree of customer interaction (7 questions).
A pilot test was done with a small group from the statistical population to ensure the questionnaire
was valid and reliable. This helped find and fix any problems with the questions. Construct validity was
assessed using confirmatory factor analysis (CFA), and reliability was measured using Cronbach's alpha
(α) coefficient. The results indicated that all validity and reliability indicators were satisfactory,
confirming that the data collection tool was appropriate for this research.
After data collection, statistical analyses were performed using SPSS® TM26 and Smart PLS TM3
software. PLS is justified for this research due to its predictive focus, ability to handle smaller sample
sizes and non-normal data, flexibility in modeling complex relationships, and ease of interpretation. In
addition, the mode used in the software is B (Significance Mode). Initially, the data were prepared for
analysis, including data cleaning, handling missing data, and ensuring no outliers were present. Partial
least squares (PLS) modeling was then used to analyze the relationships between variables. The
estimated model was evaluated using various criteria. The model comprised two sections:  the
measurement model and the structural model. To assess the validity and reliability of the measurement
tools, confirmatory factor analysis (CFA) was utilized. The structural part of the model was evaluated
using path coefficients (β), predictive relevance (Q2), and the goodness-of-fit index (GoF), along with
the standardized root mean square residual (SRMR) to simultaneously assess the fit of both the
structural and measurement models.

6. Discussion
The use of AI in digital sports marketing has caused significant changes in the industry. This
technology allows sports companies to improve their marketing strategies and enhance customer
experiences by analyzing data accurately and creating detailed customer profiles. This discussion
explores the positive effects of AI on improved personalization (IP) and interaction with customers (IC)
and compares the results with various studies. The r esults of the tests confirm the robustness of the
Smart PLS model through significant R² values and path coefficients (β), validating the research
hypotheses.
The current study demonstrated that the use of AI has a positive and significant impact on
improved personalization (IP) [11] in digital sports marketing. The beta coefficient (β) for this
relationship was above 0.60, and the t-value was greater than 2.58, confirming Hypothesis 1 (H1). These
findings are consistent with previous research [17, 63, 76, 77]. For instance, a study by Rane (2023)
also showed that AI could provide more personalized experiences, increasing customer satisfaction and
loyalty [29]. These results suggest that AI notably enhances  personalization in the digital sports
marketing space [6]. AI can be utilized to personalize each user's shopping experience, including
product recommendations based on customer interests and targeted discounts. Validating this
hypothesis provides crucial information for organizations and decision -makers in the industry, as it
highlights the importance of how  modern technologies like AI can enhance and deepen customer
relationships and experiences. On the other hand, there are also incongruent studies that highlight
challenges and concerns related to privacy [9, 38, 78]. This discrepancy in results indicates that careful
and balanced management is required to fully leverage the benefits of AI in improving personalization.
The research results also revealed that AI positively and significantly impacts  interaction with
customers (IC) in digital sports marketing. The beta coefficient (β) for this relationship was above 0.60,
and the t -value was greater than 2.58, confirming Hy pothesis 2 (H 2). This finding is valuable for
decision-makers and researchers in the digital sports marketing industry, as it suggests that leveraging
AI can enhance and develop customer interactions, thus improving business performance [12]. This
result aligns with studies by Lee et al. (2021) and Pashaie and Golmohammadi (2024) that confirmed
the positive impact of modern technologies on sports marketing [70, 79]. Conversely, there are also
incongruent studies. For instance, Nam and Kannan (2020) suggest that digital technologies and media
are changing the environments in which businesses engage with customers [80]. Additionally, Twenge,

Spitzberg, and Ca mpbell (2019) indicate that increased digital interactions might deprive some
customers of human interactions and create dissatisfaction  [81]. These results underscore the need for
strategies to balance digital and human interactions to enhance customer satisfaction.
The research findings demonstrate AI's positive and significant impact on IP and IC in digital
sports marketing. The important role of AI in this field is highlighted by confirming both research
hypotheses and comparing the results with congruent and incongruent studies. This research contributes
to the existing knowledge and provides solutions for improving digital sports marketing.

7. Conclusion
The use of artificial intelligence (AI) in sports marketing is rapidly increasing, fueled by key
trends and possible future developments. The findings show that AI, as a powerful tool , can analyze
customer data and create personalized profiles, which results in unique and targeted marketing
experiences. These experiences include messages, targeted advertisements, and product
recommendations tailored to customer behaviors and needs. Additionally, personalizing the customer
experience is a crucial factor in creating effective interactions and communication with customers. AI
and customer relationship management (CRM) systems enable  precise data analysis, allowing for
broader customer experience personalization and improved interaction and communication. However,
there are challenges, including privacy concerns and some customers’ dissatisfaction with impersonal
interactions, which must be considered when using this technology [39]. Using AI in the sports industry
brings some ethical challenges, such as the need for fairness, transparency, and accountability in
decision-making. This might require creating new roles and responsibilities to ensure ethical oversight
and governance of AI systems [3].
AI in sports marketing can revolutionize several areas of the industry. While it presents
opportunities for improved personalization (IP), interaction with customers (IC), and operational
efficiency, it also brings ethical challenges. Organizations and individuals must embrace these changes
and build a continuous learning and adaptability culture to succeed in the AI-driven sports industry. By
doing this, they can take advantage of AI’s benefits, address potential concerns, and create a sustainable
and inclusive future for the sports industry. Practical recommendations for managers and organizations
include developing and utilizing AI algorithms for precise customer data analysis and personalized
experiences, employing intelligent chatbots to address customer queries and provide relevant
recommendations, and creating targeted marketing campaigns bas ed on customer behavior data to
enhance engagement and loyalty.
Although the discussion on AI in sports marketing provides useful information, it is important to
recognize the existing limitations. The data and research available on the impact of AI on the  sports
industry and sports marketing are not extensive enough. Further research is needed to explore the extent
of AI's displacement of traditional methods in IP and IC.
Future research could build on existing studies and address their limitations. One ar ea of focus
could be reviewing recent advancements in AI technologies related to sports marketing. To expand the
scope of research, future studies might compare different sports sectors and geographic regions to
examine how AI adoption and its impact on the job market vary. These comparisons could help identify
specific challenges and opportunities related to using AI in sports marketing.
