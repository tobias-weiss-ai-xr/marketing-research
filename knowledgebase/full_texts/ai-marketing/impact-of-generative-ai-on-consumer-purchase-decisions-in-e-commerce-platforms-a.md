Abstract
Generative Artificial Intelligence (Generative AI) has emerged as a transformative technology
in the e-commerce industry by revolutionizing the way businesses interact with consumers and
deliver personalized shopping experiences. Modern e-commerce platforms increasingly utilize
Generative AI to generate personalized product recommendations, intelligent virtual shopping
assistants, AI-powered chatbots, automated product descriptions, visual search, personalized
advertisements, and content generation to impro ve customer engagement and purchasing
decisions. In India, leading fashion e -commerce platforms such as Myntra and AJIO have
adopted advanced artificial intelligence technologies to enhance customer experience, improve
recommendation accuracy, and increase  online sales. These AI -driven innovations help
consumers discover relevant products more efficiently while enabling businesses to understand
customer preferences through predictive analytics and behavioral data analysis. The primary
objective of this stud y is to examine the impact of Generative AI on consumer purchase
decisions in Myntra and AJIO by evaluating factors such as personalization, trust in AI
recommendations, customer satisfaction, shopping convenience, purchase intention, and
overall shopping experience. The study also analyzes how AI -powered marketing influences
consumer behaviour and compares the effectiveness of Generative AI applications between the
two platforms. A descriptive research design was adopted for the study. Primary information
was obtained through structured responses from consumers, while secondary information was
collected from peer -reviewed journals, books, company reports, industry publications, and
authentic online databases. Percentage analysis and graphical techniques were used to present
and interpret the findings. The results indicate that AI -powered recommendations, virtual
assistants, personalized promotions, and intelligent product suggestions positively influence
consumer purchase decisions by improving shopping conv enience and reducing decision -
making time. However, concerns regarding data privacy, algorithm transparency, excessive
personalization, and trust in AI-generated recommendations remain important challenges. The
study concludes that effective implementation  of Generative AI significantly enhances
customer satisfaction, strengthens brand loyalty, improves purchase intention, and provides a
sustainable competitive advantage for e-commerce platforms such as Myntra and AJIO.
Keywords: Generative Artificial Intelligence, Consumer Purchase Decisions, E -Commerce,
Myntra, AJIO, Personalized Recommendations, AI Chatbots.

IV. FINDINGS
The study reveals that Generative Artificial
Intelligence (Generative AI) has a significant
positive impact on consumer purchase
decisions in the e -commerce platforms
Myntra and AJIO. The majority of
consumers reported that AI -powered
personalized product recommendations help
them discover relevant products quickly and
simplify the shopping process. AI chatbots
and virtual shopping assistants were found to
enhance customer engagement by providing
instant support, answering product -related
queries, and offer ing personalized fashion
suggestions. The findings also indicate that
consumers generally trust AI -generated
recommendations, although concerns
regarding data privacy, algorithm
transparency, and excessive personalization
still exist. AI -powered search fea tures,
intelligent product descriptions, and
customized promotional messages positively
influence purchase intention and improve the
overall online shopping experience.
Furthermore, consumers expressed high
satisfaction with AI-enabled features because
they reduce product search time, improve
decision-making, and increase shopping
convenience. The comparative analysis
suggests that both Myntra and AJIO
effectively utilize Generative AI to
strengthen customer relationships and
improve digital shopping experi ences, with
Myntra demonstrating slightly higher
consumer preference due to its advanced
personalization capabilities and AI -driven
fashion recommendations. Overall, the study
concludes that Generative AI significantly
enhances customer satisfaction, purch ase
intention, brand loyalty, and consumer
confidence, making it a key driver of
competitive advantage in the modern e -
commerce industry.

V. CONCLUSION
The study concludes that Generative
Artificial Intelligence has become a
transformative technology in the e -
commerce industry by significantly
influencing consumer purchase decisions on
platforms such as Myntra and AJIO. AI -
powered features including perso nalized
product recommendations, intelligent
chatbots, virtual shopping assistants, AI -
generated product descriptions, and
predictive analytics have enhanced customer
engagement, simplified product discovery,
and improved overall shopping experiences.
The findings indicate that consumers are
increasingly willing to rely on AI -generated
recommendations because they save time,
provide relevant product suggestions, and
improve purchasing confidence. Among the
two platforms, Myntra demonstrates a
slightly stronger impact due to its advanced
AI-driven personalization and fashion
recommendation capabilities. However,
issues related to data privacy, transparency,
algorithmic bias, and consumer trust remain
important challenges that require continuous
attention. Therefore, both Myntra and AJIO
should continue investing in responsible AI
technologies, ethical data practices, and
customer-centric innovations to strengthen
consumer trust, improve customer
satisfaction, increase purchase intention, and
maintain long -term competitiveness in the
rapidly evolving e-commerce industry.
