Summary. Interest in artificial intelligence is rapidly growing in the modern world. It is believed that
soon artificial intelligence will become a catalyst for the development of humanity. Through using
AI, it is possible to expand the economic boundaries between countries and businesses. We can
safely say that there is no future without artificial intelligence. Accordingly, the use of a rtificial
intelligence use is one of the promising directions for economic well -being in our country. Artificial
intelligence is already among us, and in our professional and daily activities it participates in one
form or another, mostly unintentionally. Artificial intelligence (AI) has transformed many industries
in recent years, and digital marketing is no exception. In particular, artificial intelligence can analyze
large amounts of data, make predictions, and provide personalized experiences. Because o f this,
artificial intelligence has become a powerful tool for marketers. It is changing the way companies
interact with customers and helping to increase the number of sales.
The article examines the features of using artificial intelligence in marketing and defines the
possibilities for its improvement.
Keywords: digital marketing, artificial intelligence, sustainable economic growth.

Introduction. Modern information technologies, the Internet and the new
digital reality have led to the creation of e-business and e-commerce systems, which
differ from traditional activities by adding virtual capabilities that dramatically
increase its effectiveness. Over time, the global information space has occupied a
large part of our daily life, shaped the world's eco nomic, political and social
environment, changed the relationship between people, and covered almost all
areas of activity, such as education, health care, economy, etc. That is a unique
system, the development of which contributes to the economic growth o f different
countries in the world. A relatively strong branch of the Internet market, such as e -
commerce, allows entrepreneurs to create an open market anywhere in the world
and achieve a competitive advantage by improving customer service. One of the
directions of electronic commerce, such as online sales, which involves the sale of
goods and services through Internet technology, is developing at an unreal speed in
the world [1].

Thus, the aim of the paper is to identify the problems in the development of digital

The research pur pose and objectives . The study's purpose is to reveal the
problems in the development of digital marketing in Georgia, to study the features

Research methodology. In the research process, relevant scientific literature

Marketing is certainly found in traditional forms with any commercial establishments,
television, magazines and e -mail. In recent years, marketers have developed a
number of new approaches, from creative websites and onlin e social networks to
mobile phones. Contact is personal and direct. Today's marketers want to become

Today, marketing should be understood not in the old way - "telling and selling"
but in a new way - to meet the customers' needs. No professional marketer misses
an opportunity to build new relationships with buyers, marketing partners, and the
world. Modern marketing companies prefer to know and interact with their buyers.
They aim to  attract and retain profitable buyers with a long -term perspective [4].
Thus, modern companies inte ract through new ways to obtain superior value and
build relationships with buyers. Marketing is closely related to human existence, life,
past, present and future. Therefore, what marketing will be like considerably
depends on the world and humanity's future in general. Some people think that only
big firms should be interested in marketing services. However, marketing determines
the success of all firms, whe ther commercial or non -commercial, national or

Modern economic conditions in the world and Georgia, globalization and
acceleration of development processes of markets, technologies, socio -political

business information require continuous im provement of digital marketing
management forms and methods. Marketing coordinates the company's forces

business partners. In connection with the development of modern methods of

The use of artificia l intelligence in digital marketing is appropriate in various
directions, among which the most relevant are the following:
• Data analyzing and customer behavior prediction. In this case, with the help
of artificial intelligence, digital marketing companies collect, analyze and process a
large amount of data regarding customers to determine their activities and interests,
through which their behavior will be determined in advance. For example, a company
can use artificial intelligence to analyze the behavior of its users on social networks
to determine which products or services are most popular among them. AI can also
be used to predict demand for certain products or services, a llowing companies to
plan marketing strategies more effectively.
• Personalization o f content and offers. In this case, artificial intelligence
allows companies to create more personalized content and offers for their
customers based on their views and interests. For example, artificial intelligence can
be used to analyze a customer's pur chase history and search queries in order to
offer them a personalized product or service. It is also possible to create
personalized emails and advertisements with the help of artificial intelligence to
attract users.
• Marketing campaigns and advertising m anagement automation. At this
time, artificial intelligence allows companies to automatically run and manage
advertising campaigns, thereby optimizing advertising costs and i ncreasing
advertising effectiveness. For example, artificial intelligence can be u sed to
determine the optimal time and channel for the distribution of an advertising
message, as well as to analyze the effectiveness of advertising campaigns in order to
optimize the advertising budget.
• SEO optimization. In this case, artificial intellige nce helps companies
optimize their websites for search engines and improve their position in search
results. The use of AI for SEO optimization can include keyword analysis, the use of
machine learning (to determine the best optimization methods), competitor analysis,
and creating content based on user interests and requests.
• Content generation. Here, content such as headlines, articles, descriptions
of products and services, as well as social media content can be created with the
help of artificial intelligence. AI can create content tailored to the interests and needs
of a specific audience by analyzing large volumes of data and processing information.

data more quickly and ac curately, which helps them make better decisions and
reduce the cost of marketing activities; The use of artificial intelligence allows

When using artificial intelligence in digital marketing, along with the positives,
its negatives should be considered, namely, the dependence on technology, the risk
of errors and failure s. Artificial intelligence use in digital marketing requires the
presence of relevant technologies and experts in this field, which can be difficult and
expensive for some co mpanies, especially in our country. Finally, although artificial
intelligence allows companies to improve their marketing strategies, there is a risk of
errors and omissions that can negatively affect the business [11].
Despite everything, artificial intel ligence use in digital marketing has no
alternative. It helps companies improve their marketing strategies and increase their
effectiveness. As for the risks, most can be overcome through the appropriate safety
measures adoption, effective quality control systems, and personnel training.
The ability of artificial intelligence to help co mpanies increase profits and
competitiveness requires a balance between artificial and human components. In
order to create unique digital marketing campaigns and meet custom er needs, it is
necessary to use artificial intelligence together with creativity and intuition [12].
Conclusion. Hence, it is significant to implement artificial intelligence in digital
marketing. That may involve integrating AI solutions into existing marketing systems
and processes or creating new ones. However, when implementing art ificial
intelligence in the marketing strategy, it is essential to consider the ethical and legal
implications (privacy and security) of using artificial intelligence technol ogy. It is
imperative that AI solutions comply with all relevant regulations and g uidelines. In
addition, it is necessary to consider the potential impact of artificial intelligence on
employees and customers and take appropriate steps to mitigate any nega tive
impact.
In the future, the use of artificial intelligence in marketing is exp ected to be a
game-changer for businesses as artificial intelligence continues to evolve and
become more and more sophisticated. With the development of artificial intelligence
technology, companies will be able to obtain more valuable and in-depth information
regarding consumer behavior and preferences and create more targeted and
effective marketing campaigns.
Overall, artificial intelligence use in digital marketing is expe cted to bring
significant changes and advancements in the field. Companies will be able to create
a more personalized and attractive service for customers, which will inevitably lead
to increased sales and customer loyalty. In addition, AI can help compani es make
better use of their data and make more informed decisions  about marketing
strategies, which will improve their return on investment and make them more
competitive in the market.
