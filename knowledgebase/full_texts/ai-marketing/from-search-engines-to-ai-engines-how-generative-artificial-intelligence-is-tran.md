Abstract
The rapid evolution of generative artificial intelligence (AI)
significantly transformed consumer discovery, decision-
making behaviour, and digital marketing ecosystems across
digital platforms. This study examined the influence of AI-
powered technologies on online consumer interactions and
marketing transformation in contemporary digital
environments. The research investigated the role of
conversational AI, recommendation systems, predictive
analytics, and automated content generation in improving
personalized consumer experiences and marketing efficiency.
A quantitative research design was adopted, and primary data
were collected from 420 respondents consisting of digital
consumers and marketing professionals through a structured
questionnaire. Statistical analysis revealed high respondent
agreement regarding the impact of generative AI on consumer
discovery processes (M = 4.24, SD = 0.61), consumer
decision-making behaviour (M = 4.18, SD = 0.64), and digital
marketing ecosystem transformation (M = 4.36, SD = 0.56).
The findings indicated that AI-powered systems improved
information accessibility, personalized engagement, customer
interaction, and operational efficiency within digital
marketing platforms. The study also identified ethical concerns
related to data privacy, transparency, misinformation, and
algorithmic bias that influenced consumer trust and
technology acceptance. The results confirmed that generative
artificial intelligence fundamentally reshaped digital
consumer behaviour and modern marketing communication
systems. The study contributed valuable insights for marketers,
organizations, and policymakers seeking to implement
responsible and effective AI-driven marketing strategies in
increasingly automated digital ecosystems.

2. Literature Review
Generative Artificial Intelligence and Consumer Discovery
GAI has revolutionized online consumer discovery processes, by integrating conversational interfaces,
predictive recommendations, and personalized information retrieval systems. While traditional search engines
had keyword matching and ranking mechanisms, AI-powered search engines could produce context-specific
responses, enhancing both user-engagement and access to information. Researchers noted that consumers'
ability to discover products and services was improved by generative AI systems by enabling adaptive search
experiences and intelligent-lifting recommendations (Dwivedi et al., 2023; Huang & Rust, 2024). As the use
of AI tools with the purchasing process continued to rise, consumers were increasingly drawn to those products
that offered them an interface that was easier to search and more intuitive, as these suggested more relevant
products in line with personal tastes and habits. As a result, the role of conversational AI in digital platforms
has revolutionized the way businesses can now find their customers. As such, the introduction of
conversational AI into digital environments played a major role in changing the way businesses can now
discover their customers in today's online world.
AI personalisation is reaching new heights in the digital ecosystems. Use of machine learning
algorithms and generative artificial intelligence (AI) tools on e-commerce websites had been increasingly
deployed to analyse the behaviours of shoppers, demographics, and browsing patterns to suggest specific
products for each user. Customised suggestions had a positive impact on customer satisfaction, positive
attitude towards the purchase and engagement in online marketplaces (Chylinski et al., 2024; Puntoni et al.,
2021). The way AI systems were used to help businesses identify their target audience and understand
consumer behaviour more precisely was applicable to digital marketing. There is a use of AI systems to
precisely target business to better understand consumer behaviour, which is applicable to digital marketing.
Many of these advancements have demonstrated generative AI's ability to make information more accessible,
but also enhance consumer-brand interactions in the digital commerce landscape.
Generative AI's impact on boosting consumer confidence and upholding information credibility in this
online discovery phase was also highlighted. Conversational systems and chatbots fuelled by AI answered
promptly, provided context and in-depth explanations, and enabled an interactive experience, leading to a
positive impact on customers' experience with reliability and convenience. Studies have been found
documenting the anthropomorphic AI interactions which resulted in emotional engagement and user trust
towards digital platforms (Longoni et al., 2022; Adam et al., 2021). There were some concerns regarding
misinformation, biased recommendations and excessive reliance on automated systems, though. As more
information was created by AI, transparency and ethical responsibility came into the discussion of digital
consumers.
Consumer Decision Making with Generative AI
All along the Customer Journey within the purchasing process, they were supported intelligently by
Generative AI, assisted by predictive analytics and customised ideas that had a significant impact on their
buying patterns. The AI system could offer real-time comparisons, ease consumer interface and personalize
product recommendations, enabling consumers to make more efficient comparisons and evaluations. The
research results indicated that using an AI decision-making system in online shopping make the customers
more confident in the shopping process, and also make it easier for the human brain. The finding ultimately
showed that AI Decision Making transformed the online shopping experience making it more confident, and
not demanding the brain of the users (Ameen et al., 2022; Mariani et al., 2023). AI ability to instantly crunch
numbers on product, pricing and fit was gaining traction as a tool for consumers evaluating products prior to
purchase. These developments highlighted the evolution of AI technologies and their influence on consumer
behavior in digital transactions, particularly in the online shopping landscape.

Generative AI and Digital Marketing Ecosystems
GUIs ushered in a new era of digital marketing by streamlining content production and production,
making more effective use of digital ads, and optimizing customer interactions on digital platforms. A growing
number of companies implemented AI systems to create and design promotional image materials, social media
contents, email messages, and graphics for advertising campaigns faster and more efficiently. Companies
increasingly embraced AI deployment to produce various forms of promotional visual content, including
social media posts, email communications, and advertisements, at faster and more efficient rates. The results
of the studies showed that AI-driven marketing content boosts personalization, audience targeting, and the
effectiveness of marketing campaigns in digital channels (Kaput, 2024; Kshetri, 2023). AI tools also helped
companies process vast amounts of consumer information instantaneously, helping marketers craft adaptive
and data-driven communication campaigns. These innovations have introduced generative AI as a competitive
advantage and tool to improve marketing performance.
With the advent of AI-powered analytics and predictive modelling, organizational marketing practices
and customer relationship management (CRM) systems were further transformed. Digital marketers have
increasingly turned to machine learning algorithms to predict customer behaviour, divide consumers into
various groups and target ads to maximize ad spend effectiveness across platforms. Research showed that the
use of predictive AI can lead to more efficient marketing efforts by predicting customer trends and providing
personalized marketing experiences (Verma et al., 2021; Davenport et al., 2020). Within digital ecosystems,
AI-driven automation facilitated dynamic pricing and methods such as sentiment analysis and influencer
marketing. The developments showed how generative AI enhanced today's marketing systems and made them
more adaptable in the competitive digital environment.
The ethical, social and legal aspects of utilizing AI within the marketing ecosystem were explored.
The growing generation of AI-generated content raised debates on the credibility of AI, misinformation,
copyright issues, and the responsibility for the algorithm in communication media. Floridi & Chiriatti (2020)
and Feuerriegel et al. (2024) emphasized the importance of consumer trust and consumer welfare in the
framework of “AI-driven marketing systems” and fairness and transparency. Questions began to emerge
regarding ethical standards and rules for the utilization of AI in marketing activities. Companies started to
form questions about the ethics and guidelines for using AI in marketing. As the literature suggests, the success
of an AI-powered marketing ecosystem on the long run required innovation amidst responsibility and
customer-centricity.

3. Research Methodology
Research Design
In this study, a quantitative method was chosen to analyse the influence of Generative Artificial
Intelligence (genAI) on three aspects of consumer discovery and consumer decision making as well as the
digital marketing surroundings. To obtain numerical data used the quantitative approach and statistical data
analysis to study the relationship of the object and the subjects that varied in the study. This research design
helped to investigate consumer perceptions, consumption responses and marketing responses to the
transformation in marketing whereby the application of AI-based technologies was investigated in a digital
environment.
Population of the Study
The study has targeted Digital Consumers and Online shoppers, Social Media Users and Social
Marketers that uses Digital platforms for a frequent period of time. Customers in the population were well
aware of conversational AI, recommendation systems, automated customer service, and the creation of digital
content via AI. This population was selected due to their practical exposure with the effects of Generative
Artificial Intelligence on customer on-line behaviour and interactions in real life scenario for a digital
marketing.
Samples and Sampling Technique
A sample size of 420 was used for reliability in statistical analysis and representation of the target
population in the study. The sample was made up of consumers and marketing professionals from a variety of
backgrounds and ages, including different levels of education, gender and how much experience they have
around digital platforms. The sample was intentionally selected with the aid of purposive sampling method
because the samples are those who have knowledge and experience in the use of generative AI technologies
and digital marketing platforms. This type of sampling enabled the researcher to access individuals that were
committed and engaged in digital environments with AI.
Data Collection Method
Structured questionnaire was used for primary data collection, based on the study objectives and
variables. The questionnaire included closed ended questions scored according to the 5-point Likert scale
which were strongly-disagree – strongly-agree. The survey document had three modules: Generative Artificial
Intelligence, Consumer oriented discovery process, Consumer behavioural decision making and Digital
marketing ecosystem transformation and ethical questions relevant to the AI systems. The questionnaire was
sent to respondents using online survey companies or e-mailing. Data collection tools online helped to
participation, ease and responsiveness of those using the Internet.
Measurement of Variables
Generative artificial intelligence, which is broken down into conversational AI, recommendation
systems, predictive analytics, and automated content creation, were all featured on the independent variable
of the study. The processes consumer discovery, consumer decision making and the transformation to digital
marketing ecosystem were the dependent variables. However, the ethical implications and future prospects of
AI systems have also been taken into account as a factor that makes customer engagement and trust. All items
were crafted on the basis of earlier studies and the artificial intelligence, consumer behaviour and literature
regarding digital marketing were consulted to guarantee the conceptual overlap and consistency of all the
measurement items.
Data Analysis Techniques
The SPSS was used to analyse the collected data. The demographic variables and distributions were
summarized using descriptive statistics such as a frequency, percentage, mean and standard deviation value.
To test the scale's internal consistencies, Cronbach's alpha was used. The study herein conducted correlation
analysis to analyse the relations between the study variables, and regression analysis was conducted to test the
proposed hypothesis to measure the influence of generative Artificial Intelligence on consumer discovery,
decision-making and digital marketing ecosystem. The statistical results provided empirical evidence that
there were significance and strength of the relationships among the study constructs and it is an indicator of
the support for each construct.

5. Discussion
The study results revealed that generative AI had a profound impact on the “how consumers find
content in digital spaces” landscape. The respondents were very confident that AI-based solutions like search
systems have improved their personal search experience, facilitated the retrieval of information, and made
products and services online more accessible. The results also corroborated the latest literature, which
discussed that conversational AI and intelligent recommendation systems affect consumer search behaviours,
offering context sensitive, adaptive interactions (Agarwal et al., 2025; Huang et al., 2025). The surge in user
numbers seemed to indicate that users preferred to engage with more dynamic, interactive, and in-depth
information and systems as opposed to systems that rely on keywords.
The research also revealed that G. AI played a pivotal role on digital markets in their operation of
buying and selling decision-making. They expressed feeling more confident about products and purchasing
them due to AI-powered recommendations, conversational support and predictive analytics. The findings are
consistent with the previously mentioned research which reveals how artificial intelligence-generated
personalized, relevant suggestions can positively impact purchase intentions (Siau & Wang, 2024; Barta et
al., 2023). The user becomes more reliant on AI-generated data, in determining product differences, judging
its quality, and verifying services' reliability. The results revealed the use of generative AI technologies and
also velocity with which they are contributing to the prevailing era of digital commerce as a decision-
supportive tool.
It also revealed huge differences in the digital ecosystems when employing technologies of Generative
Artificial Intelligence (GAI) in digital marketing. Participants felt that AI technology will enhance marketing
automation, customer marketing, customer engagement and effectiveness of communication. AI-powered
systems are being adopted for content generation, predictive analytics, and conversational marketing, with the
aim of optimizing customer engagement and tactics.AI is being used across various aspects of business, from
content creation and predictive analytics to conversational marketing, to maximize customer engagement and
tactics. These findings support other studies that suggested that AI tools could lead to more efficient marketing
and make it possible to offer highly personalized experiences on digital channels such as websites and
newsletters for customers. (Kumar et al., 2025; Chintalapati & Pandey, 2023).
The findings also indicated that personalization with AI and interactions on digital platforms garnered
positive feedback from consumers. Digital solutions powered by AI-driven systems were recognized for their
ability to deliver quicker responses, tailored information and tips, and enhanced interaction experiences,
leading to increased customer satisfaction and confidence in these solutions. The results were similar to
previous studies on how AI affects the digital customer journey that identified an increase in the emotional
connection and integration with the brand after introducing AI features into the customer journey (Jarek &

7. Recommendations
Especially in the realm of digital marketing, businesses have to prioritise responsible and consumer-
centric use of AI tools to improve their marketing strategies and customer engagement. Consumers need to
have confidence in AI systems and trust them, so enterprises should develop transparent and user-friendly AI
designs that explain the recommendation processes they use for making business decisions, as well as how
information is leveraged to make recommendations. Ethical principles need to be embedded in strategies for
using AI in marketing to mitigate privacy concerns, misinformation and algorithmic bias on digital platforms.
Ethical measures should also be embraced as part of the deployment of AI in marketing efforts to help
minimize privacy concerns, misinformation and algorithms in digital platforms.
8. Future Directions
Research needs to explore how generative artificial intelligence can impact consumer attitudes, buying
habits, and brand loyalty in various online settings. Further research will need to consider how successful an
AI system will be when adopted in the global digital market, with a focus on how culture, social factors and
demographics will influence this. However, the possible future (colony) contributions of the comparative
economy (standard) and the comparison economy (developing/developed) in terms of AI marketing techno’s
may have greater weight outside this focus.
Additional research should be conducted on the emerging technologies that are relevant to digital
commerce ecosystem like GenAI, AR, and VR technologies. For more details, other ways of doing ethical
governance in terms of consumer protection of their information and implications on how communication in
the promotion of transparency and trust becomes more effective in digital marketing offers can be found.
While the following are essential to the sea change of CX and consumer marketing strategies, ongoing research
is necessary to evaluate the effect that any new advancements of AI have on CX as a technology change over
time and as consumer marketing approaches evolve over time.
