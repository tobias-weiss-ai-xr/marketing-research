Abstract. The algorithm of the marketing environment analysis process as an
important element of strategic management of the enterprise is studied. The main
stages of the analysis of the internal and external marketing environment are
considered, and the role of modern digital technologies in increasing the effectiveness
of marketing research is also determined. The feasibility of using artificial intelligence,
big data analytics, generative AI, augmented and virtual reality technologies,
robotization of business processes, and modern cybersecurity tools for collecting,
processing, and analyzing marketing information is substantiated. It has been proven
that the implementation of digital tools contributes to improving the quality of
management decisions, adapting the enterprise to changes in the market environment,
and strengthening its competitive advantages.
Keywords: marketing environment, process algorithm, marketing analysis, digital
technologies, artificial intelligence, Big Data, generative AI, marketing research,
competitiveness.
Introduction. In the modern conditions of the development of the digital economy,
enterprises operate in a transformational environment that is constantly evolving in
conditions of radical changes. The ability to timely detect changes in the environment,
analyze the impact and quickly adapt marketing activities is one of the key conditions
for ensuring the competitiveness and sustainable development of the enterprise. That
is why there is a need to form an effective algorithm (Figure 1). The algorithm of the
marketing environment analysis process is a sequence of interrelated stages, which
includes the collection of marketing information, its systematization, analysis,
assessment of influencing factors, forecasting possible changes and making
management decisions. The effectiveness of the marketing strategy, the ability of the
enterprise to respond to market challenges in a timely manner and use new
opportunities for development depend on the quality of each stage.

As can be seen from Figure 1, the algorithm is a sequence of interconnected
stages, which includes problem definition, research plan development, research plan
implementation, data processing and analysis, and report and recommendations
preparation. The effectiveness of the marketing strategy, the ability of the enterprise to
respond to market challenges in a timely manner and use new opportunities for
development depend on the quality of each stage.
The effectiveness of the implementation of the specified stages depends on the
use of modern digital technologies that allow you to automate marketing processes,
increase the accuracy of analysis, and ensure prompt management decision-making.
That is why it is advisable to consider innovative digital technologies that help increase
the efficiency of marketing environment analysis.

As can be seen from Table 1, examples of modern digital technologies indicate

Their use ensures rapid processing of significant amounts of information, increases the

management decisions. The comprehensive implementation of these technologies
allows enterprises to respond more quickly to changes in the external environment,
more effectively meet consumer needs, and strengthen their competitive positions.
Conclusion. Therefore, the algorithm for the marketing environment analysis
process is an important component of effective enterprise management, as it provides
a systematic approach to collecting, processing, analyzing, and evaluating information
about internal and external environmental factors. Using such an algorithm allows you
to timely detect changes in market conditions, predict consumer behavior, evaluate
competitors' activities, and identify potential risks and opportunities for enterprise
development.
The study showed that the use of modern digital technologies, including artificial
intelligence (AI), Big Data Analytics, virtual (VR) and augmented (AR) reality
technologies, generative AI and content automation, cybersecurity and Zero Trust
architecture, and robotic business process automation (RPA), significantly increases
the effectiveness of marketing environment analysis.  These tools automate marketing
processes, accelerate the processing of large amounts of information, increase
forecasting accuracy, and facilitate informed management decisions.
Thus, i t is the integration of innovative digital technologies into the algorithm of
the marketing environment analysis process that is an important direction for
increasing the competitiveness of enterprises. Their use allows you to quickly adapt
marketing activities to changes in the market environment, more effectively meet
consumer needs, and ensure sustainable development of the enterprise in the context
of digital transformation of the economy.

Abstract: With the in-depth advancement of digital transformation in the cultural
tourism industry, urban old streets, as unique carriers of urban context and folk culture,
have outstanding cultural value and tourism development potential. However, most
traditional old streets are plagued by disorderly business layouts, undefined target
audiences, single communication channels, low traffic conversion rates,  and
fragmented operational mechanisms, which fail to adapt to evolving cultural tourism
consumption demands and new media communication trends. To address the core
dilemmas of homogeneous competition, closed com munication circles, and
unsustainable popularity, this study constructs a closed- loop new media marketing
system featuring “research and positioning, content marketing, traffic conversion, and
private domain operation” based on practical cultural tourism projects of urban ol d
streets. By implementing industrial investigation, precise customer positioning, visual
content dissemination, audio- visual traffic empowerment, merchant alliance
collaboration, and data-driven refined operations, this paper explores f easible digital
marketing and sustainable development strategies for traditional old street blocks. The
results indicate that systematic, scenario -oriented, and refined new media marketing
can effectively activate the commercial vitality of old streets, reshape regional cultural
tourism brands, and convert public domain traffic into sustainable private domain
assets. This research provides practical references and theoretical support for the digital
transformation and high- quality development of similar old street cultural tourism
projects.
