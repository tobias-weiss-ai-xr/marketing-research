Abstract: Trust in AI -based marketing systems held by consumers is different from trust created using regular marketing
processes. Whereas conventional trust building process relies on brand familiarity, consistency in t he provision of
products/services, and interaction reliability, trust building in AI-based marketing considers psychological and behavioral issues.
These include consumers' understanding of automation in the marketing processes, independence of AI system in d ecision-
making, credibility of results generated by AI, reliability of recommendations made to customers, an d the degree to which
consumers perceive themselves to be in control. It is, therefore, important for organizations to understand the unique features of
consumer trust in order to successfully conduct AI -based marketing activities. The present study aimed to know the association
between demographic forces and, consumer awareness and trust on AI -generated marketing messages In Kozhikode district of
Kerala. 100 respondents in Kozhikode District were chosen by convenience sampling method and interview schedule was applied
in the study to collect primary data. Through Artificial Intelligence (AI), organisations have been able to transform their
marketing environment through the creation of personalized, automatic, and real -time marketing messages. The use of AI -
generated marketing messages is common in advertise ments, product descriptions, marketing emails, social media posts,
chatbots, and personalized marketing recommendations. Although these tools have many advantages like efficiency, consistency,
personalization, and fast content creation, there are also some  disadvantages such as the validity of the messages, transparency,
accuracy, privacy, and whether or not the messages were created by humans. These issues can affect t he awareness and trust of
consumers towards marketing messages.
Keywords: Artificial Intelligence, Marketing Messages, Social Media, Privacy.

I. INTRODUCTION
Rapid developments in the field of Artificial Intelligence (AI) have revolutionized marketing due to  their effect on the proc esses
associated with the creation and distribution of personal ized marketing communications. Technologies based on AI have started to
be used in marketing campaigns involving advertising, product description, promotions, email marketi ng, social media posts,
customer interactions, product recommendation, and chatbots.  Marketing communications produced by artificial intelligence
systems are capable of analysing huge amounts of consumer data and generating content tailored to the needs of specific consumers.
The above qualities allow organizations to optimize processes o f communications production and enhance efficiency,
personalization, and consistency of their communications while saving time and effort. Thus, AI has become an integral part of the
current technological environment in marketing. AI -generated marketing me ssages mean promotional or informational messages
developed with the help of artificial intelligence systems utilizing algorithms, machine learning, n atural language processin g,
generative AI, and other technologies. Unlike conventional marketing communication, in which content is predominantly developed
by human marketers, AI -generated messages may be produced, adapted, and distributed automatically based on consumer
information and predefined or learned patterns. The increasing use of generative AI has further expanded the ability of organizations
to create marketing content rapidly and at scale.
The increase in the use of AI in marketing highlights the importance of consumer awareness. The cons umers can get into contac t
with marketing communication produced through AI without being able to differentiate between what was created by human beings
or through AI technology or even both. Consumer awareness can be defined as the understanding of the  use of AI in marketing
communication, awareness of AI -generated messages, personalization and automation of decision -making, and awareness of the
strengths and weaknesses of AI-based marketing communication. Increased consumer awareness can help them make more rational
judgments about the marketing messages.

In additi on to consumer awareness, consumer trust can also be considered as a major factor for the effectiven ess of AI -based
marketing communication. Trust can be defined as the perception of reliability, accuracy, credibilit y, and dependability of A I-
generated marketing communication by the consumers. Consumers may develop trust when AI -generated content provides relevant
information, consistent recommendations, clear explanations, and dependable interactions. However, c oncerns regarding inaccurate
information, excessive personalization, lack of transparency, privacy, data usage, manipulation, and uncertainty abo ut whether a
message has been generated by AI may influence consumers’ willingness to trust such communication.  Therefore, the adoption of
AI in marketing does not automatically guarantee consumer acceptance; rather, organizations need to establish condit ions that
support confidence and credibility.  The present study aimed to know the association between demographic forces and, consumer
awareness and trust on AI-generated marketing messages In Kozhikode district of Kerala.

II. PROBLEM OF THE STUDY
Rapid development of the implementation of artificial intelligence in marketing has dramatically changed the way of how marketing
messages are produced and delivered through advertising, social media, product recommendations, emai ls, chatbots, and other
digital tools.
While there are many advantages of AI generated marketing messages, including customization, fastnes s, consistency, and
efficiency, some issues related to the precision, truthfulness, transparency, confidentiality, and t rustworthiness of such  messages
also exist. There is a possibility that customers do not know that marketing messages they get are p roduced and/or modified using
AI technologies. Moreover, while trust towards traditional marketing messages and trust towards AI marketing messages may differ
because of reliability of the system, the use of private data, control, and disclosure of the fact t hat marketing messages ar e created
using AI technologies.
Thus, there is a necessity to research consumer awareness, perception, and trust tow ards AI -generated marketing messages and
factors influencing consumer behavior towards such innovations in marketing.

III. MATERIALS AND METHODS
The growing application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in marketing has generated increasing academ ic interest in understanding
how consumers perceive, evaluate, and respond to AI-generated marketing communication. In this background, Lefkeli et al. (2024)
examined the effect of interacting with AI compared with humans on brand trust and found that consum ers could exhibit lower
levels of trust when sharing information with AI, particularly when they perceived greater possibilities of information dissemination
and exploitation.
The study highlighted the need and importance of privacy perceptions in shaping consumer trust and established that consumers’
responses to AI are influenced not only by technological functionality but also by their perc eptions of how their information is
handled. The findings of Wang et al. (2025) indicate d that transparency is an important dimension of AI -based marketing
communication; however, subsequent research has shown that the effect of disclosure is not necessari ly uniform across consumers
and contexts. Le et al. (2026) examined AI role -disclosure transparency in advertising and found that consumers’ perceptions of
how clearly and informatively AI was involved in the advertising creation process influenced the cre dibility of the advertising
process.
Higher perceived transparency enhanced perceived credibility, which subsequently resulted in more fa vourable advertising
evaluations. The study also found that the effectiveness of disclosure could vary depending on the way the disclosure was presented
and whether regulatory compliance signals were present. This indicates that consumer awareness shoul d not be viewed merely as
knowledge that AI was used; rather, consumers may also need meaningful information about the ro le played by AI in creating the
marketing message.
The study has made use of both primary data as well as secondary data. The technique used for collec ting primary data in the study
is that of field survey. In this case, 100 samples have been collected from the Kozhikode district through an interview schedule. The
type of research design chosen for this particular research is descriptive research. Descriptive res earch has been selected a s it
includes the description of the current state of affairs. In case the population from which a sample is taken is not homogeneous, the
technique of convenience sampling is employed to get a representative sample. Multiple Regression an alysis was used for furth er
analysis.

V. RECOMMENDATIONS AND CONCLUSION
AI generated messages are currently utilized for making product recommendations, personalized advert isements, promotional
communication, product descriptions, and customer interaction online. However, although the use of s uch techno logies can
contribute to more efficient, consistent, and accurate marketing communication, the awareness of con sumers about such
technologies is a critical issue.  Businesses should disclose whether the marketing content is AI generated or assisted to some extent.
This disclosure will enable consumers to know the origin of the message and be able to make an infor med choice. Despite the
efficiency of AI in creating marketing messages, human intervention should be considered to detect m istakes, irrelevant con tent,
inflated claims and communications that might harm consumer confidence.  AI-generated marketing messages should be well-vetted
prior to publication. Businesses should confirm that the product descriptions, price, offers, specif ications among other co ntent
provided are truthful and do not contain misleading information.
Marketers should carry out awareness campaigns on how AI is being used in marketing communications t hrough social media,
websites, advertisements and consumer education initiatives. Organizations utilizing AI for personalized marketing communications
should provide a detailed explanation of how consumers' data are collected, analyzed and utilized. P rivacy and data protectio n
measures should be considered to instill consumer confidence. The messages from AI should be relevant recommendations without
getting overly personalized. Consumers should have control over their personalized marketing communi cations. Therefore, i t is
essential that organisations concentrate their attention on using A I for marketing communications in an open, precise, accountable
and consumer-centred way. Human supervision, accurate information, protection of privacy, and proper disclosure o f AI-created
material may help in creating a credible AI-assisted marketing environment.
