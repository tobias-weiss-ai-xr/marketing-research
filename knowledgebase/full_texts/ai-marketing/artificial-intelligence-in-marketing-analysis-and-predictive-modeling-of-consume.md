ABSTRACT

(AI)

has

by

to

consumer

This

The

XGBoost

Among

Overall,

to

marketing

Machine-learning

The

In

These

including

that

may

not

be

adequately

represented

through

simpler

(Wedel;

2016).

However,

Predictive

and
