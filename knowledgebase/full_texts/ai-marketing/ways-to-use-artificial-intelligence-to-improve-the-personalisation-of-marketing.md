1. Introduction

Globalisation and the dynamic development of technologies have led to qualitative changes in all spheres of life,
which scientists describe as the fourth industrial revolution, which consists of the interaction of machines and their abilit y to
exchange information; therefore, a critical component is the development of artificial intelligence (Manesh et al., 2020 ;
Verma et al., 2021). AI is essential in the economy as a universal means of information, communication and marketing, which
minimises costs and resources. However, there is a question of whether modern entrepreneurs successfully use technology
and how consumers feel about AI. After all, recently, AI technologies have constantly changed and improved, and the
number of industries that take advantage of digitalisation and adapt the operation of AI to the requirements of their field i s
growing (Vlacic et al., 2021) . Today, there is no doubt that it is only possible to succeed in business by using innovative
technologies, so companies invest in AI technologies. However, a large selection of digital marketing offers makes
understanding the feasibility of various functions of intelligent technologies difficult. This leads to unjustified costs and
reduces the profitability of business activities (Potwora et al., 2023).
Due to its advantages, artificial intelligence was rapidly implemented and continues to be implemented in various
fields, namely medicine, economy, education, and transport, which caused various types of functionality per the set goal
(Borges et al., 2021). The improvement of software to the needs of a specific industry became the reason for contradictions
in the definition of artificial intelligence among authors. For example, Nikitas et al. (2020) defined artificial intelligenc e as a
powerful concept for positive changes in the direction of a resource -saving paradigm of life from the point of view of building
an intelligent city according to the needs of citizens through the automation of transport. Helm et al. (2020) described
artificial intelligence technology as a driving force capable of transforming the practice of medicine through its ability to
process large databases. Based on artificial intelligence, holistic virtual systems are being developed, such as a metauniver se
Abstract Thanks to its capabilities, artificial intelligence (AI) contributes to business development by attracting
customers and investors. However, the wide spread of various AI technology software complicates understanding its
concept and reduces its implementation in local businesses. Our research was devoted to the development of an
algorithm for building marketing strategies with the use of AI based on the definition of the concept, functions,
principles of action and assessment of the perception of digitalisation by consumers for the application of a
personalised approach to interaction with the client and improving communication. The results confirmed the low
awareness of the population, including entrepreneurs, about the possibilities of AI. We identified the main functions,
concepts, and types of AI and provided examples of their application. We explored ways to personalise marketing and
improve communication based on the achievement of trust criteria, customer satisfaction, ease of purchase,
fulfilment of seller obligations, quality support service, security of personal data, and creation of recommended offers
based on the analysis of data obtained during communication with the consumer. To achieve these criteria,
recommendations are given for using different types of AI: chatbots, automated mailings, intelligent assistants,
installation of filters and sorting programs, automatic recommendations and multifactor identification. The AI
algorithm created in this way to ensure the personalisation of marketing and quality communication will increase the
implementation of digitalisation in the country's entrepreneurship and improve the economy.

marketing surpasses traditional marketing in speed, scale, flexibility to change and personalised approach  (Chintalapati &
Pandey, 2022). However, when choosing a digital marketing strategy, an entrepreneur is faced with the problem of both the
universality of artificial intelligence and, on the other hand, the large volume of digital offers offered by marketers and
software specialists, which do not always fit the specifics of the product or service. Therefore, it is crucial to evaluate w ays of

manufacturer and the client.
The study aimed to create an algorithm for building a marketing strategy based on the study of the functions,
principles of action, and types of AI, as well as to determine ways to use AI for personalisation and effective communication
with the client.

2. Materials and Methods

An analysis of literature and foreign modern reviews related to AI and marketing was carried out to achieve the goal.
A survey was conducted among 1265 randomly selected respondents via phone calls, messengers, and popular social
networks. The survey complies with  ethical standarts. The survey included personalised questions about age, gender, and
type of activity, as well as the following: “Do you know what artificial intelligence is? ” “Do you use AI? ” “Do you trust AI? ”,
“Do you often make purchases on the Internet? ”, “Are you satisfied with the support service? ” “Is the support service
represented by artificial intelligence technology? ” “Have you made a purchase recommended by the site? ” to determine the
effective implementation of AI in the country's entrepreneurial activity.  The survey also included clarifying open questions,
namely, if respondent answered positively to the question  “ Do you know what AI is?”, the clarifying question s were: “What
types of AI do you know ?”, “How can they be used? “. If respondent answered positively to the question “ Do you use AI?",
the clarifying question was "What types of AI do you use?", and if respondent answered positively to the question about
service, he had to indicate what types of service was useful for him? Based on systematisation and data analysis, diagrams of
the structure of AI, the principle of operation and methods of business -consumer interaction using AI technology were
created. The results were compared with data from the literature, and the results were summarised in the conclusions.

The search for a type of AI should begin with defining the expected functionality. The function of absorbing databases,
their analysis, and structuring are the main elements of the technology since they are based on information that makes
further activities possible. This function is competitive with the usual marketing technologies because processing large
databases at high speed is beyond the power of a human being. In companies such as Google and Amazon, you can assess
the scope of AI capabilities because a person cannot cope with such a large amount of diverse information.
Automatic updating of databases allows the entrepreneur to be flexible and modern and adapt to changes in
customer preferences. In order to update the data, personnel must perform extensive work, including statistical processing,
searching for new information, and repeating the same type of actions that are considered boring for humans. However, AI
copes with these tasks quickly and efficiently. Even working with small databases requires significant resources to update,
structure and analyse the data, so it is a prospect for small businesses to expand.
Automated customer communication is the most frequent form of using AI as a support service, providing
instructions, personalised advertising, and loyalty programs, which has shown its high effectiveness. After all, thanks to th e
speed of chatbots, it is possible to achieve colossal results that confirm customers' satisfaction. In our survey, customers
chose to communicate with the operator in only 12% of cases and described this option as poorly organised. Instead, most
respondents were satisfied with the robotic support service.
Forecasting and predicting an event is an essential component that is technologically complex and requires the
availability of many different databases to make offers of goods and services that may potentially appeal to the client based
on their analysis. Prediction is a step into the future of personalised marketing. Our survey revealed high purchases based o n
predicted advertising offers, 52.0% among all respondents.
Understanding the concept of AI allows you to understand the complexity of programming this technology. Thus,
machine learning, deep learning, and neural networks are distinguished among AI concepts. Machine learning is the simplest
form of programming, characterised by a repetitive task according to the code. This learning requires a constant return to th e
initial action, which slows down the system. Deep learning involves a more complex concept of code, which includes making
an independent decision without returning to the initial action after analysing a large amount of information. For example,
the image recognition analysis, for which the program first analyses millions of different images and acquires knowledge of
face and pattern recognition, no longer requires a return to the original code. Deep learning is built on the principle of ne ural
networks, which are similar to the principle of the human brain. Neural networks are the most complex form of AI coding,
involving input, hidden, and output layers that contain thousands of nodes  (De Bruyn et al., 2020). Thus, on the principle of a

4. Discussion

According to the results of the survey, citizens' awareness of the definition and practical application of AI was revealed
to be low. At the same time, based on the analysis of the literature related to AI, a problem was identified in the formulation
of a single concept of AI, which creates risks for its effective use. In addition to the problem of understanding the concept  of
AI, many studies point to the lack of a standardized classification of types of AI. For example, Khatniuk et al. (2023) defin e the
concept of AI as Weak AI, Strong AI and Artificial Superintelligence, although the principle of complexity remains similar.
Pantano and Scarpi (2022) describe five types of AI: logic -mathematical social, visual-spatial, verbal-linguistic and processing-
speed intelligence. Chung et al. (2022) distinguish four types of AI: reactive machines, limited memory, theory of mind, and
self-awareness, which differ in mechanism and principle of action, functions, similarity to the imitation of human intelligence,
and complexity of tasks. The authors also note the lack of a single classification of AI, which would improve digitalisation and
help regulate requirements for AI systems. Thus, the literature confirms our observations about the lack of structured
information on the basics of AI.
Our research found low trust in AI technologies among consumers (less than 37%), which is why conservative citizens
refuse to shop online. Low trust is caused primarily by concerns about the security of personal data and transactions and fea r
of fraudulent schemes. Our results have been confirmed in the world literature, as many scientists have studied the issues of
trust in AI and identified trust as a key factor in the effective use of AI in business (Glikson & Woolley, 2020 ; Khan et al.,
2021). In addition, societal demands to protect users' rights from the possible negative effects of AI have prompted the
European Commission to create requirements for AI, including transparency, technical reliability, security, data privacy and
fairness. (European Commission, 2019; Lins et al., 2021; Saienko et al., 2020).
The results of our survey showed the benefits of using artificial intelligence for marketing, namely the effectiveness of
predictive advertising offers that led to online purchases by 52% of respondents, automated communication with customer
service, with which 64.5% of customers are satisfied, and customer involvement ( more than 80% of respondents shop
online). The effectiveness of AI has also been confirmed in numerous scientific works, which describe the role of AI in the
effectiveness of marketing with the involvement of customers, fair pricing through the analysis of changes in demand, price
adjustment according to the competitor's price, convenient warehousing with the appropriate use of warehouse space,
acceleration of the search for goods in the warehouse , help with logistics, order tracking and replenishment of goods (Huang
& Rust, 2021; Peyravi et al., 2020; Potwora et al., 2023). The authors also emphasized the need to popularize AI among
entrepreneurs. Customer satisfaction with AI tools has also been highlighted in research, namely aspects such as 24/7
support, content analytics that ensure selection and sorting of products at optimal prices, and emotional AI algorithms that
determine customer likes and dislikes (Jarek & Mazurek, 2019).
Thus, the results of our research determined the importance of AI for both entrepreneurs and consumers, which was
confirmed in the works of other researchers. Therefore, an algorithm is proposed to select the type of AI based on requested
criteria such as customer trust, convenience, memory, company responsibilities, security, loyalty program, customer
satisfaction, personalized offers and new customer acquisition. This algorithm was created to help in the selection of an AI
method to optimize the marketing strategy with an emphasis on a personalized approach and quality communication that
meets the modern requirements of marketing (Zhao & Zhu, 2023).

5. Conclusion

Our research showed low awareness (30.6%) of the population, including entrepreneurs, about the possibilities of
artificial intelligence, which was caused by the lack of a single definition, classification of AI types in the literature. H owever,
despite the low level of knowledge about the features of AI, consumers use AI on a daily basis and (64.5%) of respondents
express satisfaction with chatbots and automatic support service advisors. The results of the survey also indicated a positiv e
impact of predictive advertising offers that led to online purchases by 52% of respondents. Thus, the positive impact of AI on
marketing and consumer satisfaction, which contributes to attracting customers and business success, was noted, therefore it
is advisable to popularize information about AI among entrepreneurs.  Since the use of AI increases the effectiveness of
marketing, it is advisable to develop a marketing strategy using the latest AI technologies, for this it is necessary to
understand the principle of operation and functional capabilities of AI. That is why, in our research, we turned to the
development of an AI type selection algorithm based on the expected functionality, by implementing criteria, among which
customer trust, convenience, memory, company responsibilities, security, loyalty program, customer satisfaction,
personalized offers and new customer acquisition. Our algorithm corresponds to modern marketing principles, which are
based on a personalized approach and quality communication.  However, we found a low level of trust of the population
(63.1% of respondents distrusted AI tools), which requires the improvement of personal data protection and legal regulation
of possible violations.
