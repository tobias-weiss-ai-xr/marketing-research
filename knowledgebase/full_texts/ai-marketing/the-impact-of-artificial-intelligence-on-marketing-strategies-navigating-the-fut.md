Abstract:
  This scholarly article explores the profound impact of artificial intelligence
(AI) on marketing strategies in the contemporary business landscape. Drawing on a synthesis
of academic literature, case studies, and empirical evidence, the article analyzes the
transformative effects of AI on various facets of marketing, including customer targe ting,
personalization, data analysis, and automation. By examining the dynamic interplay between
AI and marketing strategies, this article aims to provide insights into how businesses can
harness the power of AI for strategic advantage in an increasingly d ata-driven and
technologically advanced environment.
Keywords: Artificial Intelligence, Marketing Strategies, AI in Marketing, Personalization,
Automation, Data Analysis, Customer Targeting.
Introduction:
The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into marketing practices is reshaping the
landscape of business strategies. This article explores the multifaceted impact of AI on
marketing, from enhancing data analytics to enabling personalized customer experiences. By
delving into the evolving relation ship between AI and marketing strategies, this scholarly
work aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of how businesses can leverage AI for
strategic success in the digital age.
Evolution of Artificial Intelligence in Marketing:
This section provides  a historical overview of the evolution of AI in marketing, tracing its
roots from rule -based systems to the contemporary era of machine learning and neural
networks. The article explores how advancements in AI technologies, such as natural
language proces sing (NLP) and computer vision, have unlocked new possibilities for
marketers to analyze data, automate tasks, and personalize interactions.
The evolution of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in marketing has been a transformative journey
that has reshaped the landscape of the industry. From its initial applications in automating
routine tasks to its current state of powering advanced predictive analytics, AI has become an
indispensable tool for marketers worldwide. This evolution can be traced back to the early
2000s when basic AI algorithms began to find their way into marketing automation
platforms.
In the early stages, AI was primarily used for data analysis, enabling marketers to gather
insights and make data -driven decisions. As technology advanced, machine  learning
