ABSTRACT
The study explores how generative AI affects small and medium enterprise
marketing results as well as expansion in the business sector of Aba within Abia State
Nigeria. This research evaluates how broadly AI features are used alongside their effect
on mark eting performance together with the problems facing SMEs that try to
implement artificial intelligence marketing systems. This study adopted quantitative
research with a structured questionnaire distributed to 450 SME owners and marketing
experts from reta il production and service domains which the researchers selected
through stratified random sampling protocols. The researchers used multiple
regression techniques which included ANOVA, model summary, and correlation tests
to analyze the data collected from AI adoption and marketing effectiveness and business
growth analysis. Aba-based SMEs show awareness of generative AI opportunities, but
their adoption remains minimal because of monetary restrictions combined with
technical expert shortages alongside prot ection issues for their data availability. AI -
enabled marketing methods improve both brand recognition and consumer
relationships together with business revenue streams. SMEs need formal AI courses
along with financial assistance and industrial relations t o reach their maximum AI -
based transformation capability. Specific solutions that eliminate these barriers will

1. Introduction
Aba serves Abia State as its major commercial center because industry and
entrepreneurial activity operate within the innovative urban background. Aba has maintained
its position as a thriving commercial center with its active small and medium enterprises
(SMEs) dealing with manufacturing along with textile production and retail and services
sectors. The famous Ariaria International Market which people call the "China of Africa"
displays Aba's business owners as they endure and invent new solutions [1]. The se SMEs
sustain local and regional economies despite working under difficult conditions which include
weak infrastructure and limited financial resources as well as old-fashioned business procedures
[2]. The business strategies currently used by small orga nizations fall short in their power to
compete effectively as we move further into the digital era. Evolution in global markets
advances quickly so businesses must adopt new technologies to survive or they will become
obsolete [3]. The transformative techn ology of artificial intelligence (AI) specifically through
its generative form has massive potential to upgrade marketing techniques for enhancing the
expansion of SMEs in Aba.
Generative AI systems use deep-learning algorithms trained with extensive datasets to
generate human -simulated content including text images videos and audios. Businesses
applying generative AI technology can produce automated content and specific personalization
for customer experiences while making their ads more effective and using AI to make data -
based strategic choices in their marketing programs. Organizations worldwide deploy AI -
powered marketing tools with AI-powered chatbots to provide immediate customer support and
AI-generated descriptions to boost e-commerce platforms while utilizing AI-driven ad targeting
to accurately reach audiences [4].

must maintain their tight budget constraints [1]. A significant portion of business owners avoid
utilizing free or cheap AI solutions mainly because they cannot effectively work with them nor
do they know these solutions exist.

electricity networks and poor internet services together with restricted cloud service access
place further barriers in front of SMEs who want to adopt AI marketing solutions [3]. Through
AI-powered chatbots, Kenyan SMEs automate customer service operations which reduces their

adoption. The research highlights the importance for SMEs in Aba to use AI market ing tools

small businesses need purposeful interventions to adopt AI successfully.

3 Methodology
The study implemented a quantitative research design to assess how generative AI
affects SME marketing performance within the region of Aba state in Abia, Nigeria. The data
analysis utilized multiple regression techniques to measure the connections among AI adoption
rates and their market effects and various difficulties along with their impact on Small and
Medium-sized Enterprise (SME) expansion. A structured survey was distributed to 450 SME
owners and marketing professionals via stratified random samplin g to get a representative
sample from retail manufacturing and service sectors.
The survey tool consisted of both multiple -choice options and Likert -type scales to
determine AI understanding levels and adoption frequencies alongside performance
measurements for marketing activities and difficulties with AI system integration. The research
employed SPSS 25 software for statistical testing to evaluate how generative AI affects small
and medium enterprise marketing development and competition strength.
The survey’s validity and reliability were established through a pilot test involving 40
SME owners who checked its clarity and Cronbach’s alpha measured the survey’s internal
consistency [13]. A systematic approach facilitated the complete exploration of AI marketing
patterns and their changes within SME business operations.
The study followed strict ethical measures that protected participant rights and kept their
data confidential. Every participant received informed consent for the study before joining
which clarified all study purposes alongside risks and withdrawal rights aligning with [14]. All
data were kept anonymous for strict confidentiality purposes to block unauthorized access and
protect against misuse. Businesses received no legal consequences for declining to take part

4.3 Discussion of Findings
H01: Small businesses operating in Aba are adopting generative AI marketing tools at
rising levels which depend on their understanding of AI technology alongside their industry
and individual perception of solution advantages.
The study revealed that AI adoption as a variable resulted in a 0.312 coefficient which
produced a 0.000 p -value thus demonstrating its positive influence on SME marketing
performance. The educational levels of SMEs in Aba about AI along with their percept ions of
its value for their sector positively influence their decision to implement generative AI
marketing tools. The visual evidence of R² equal to 0.274 shows that AI adoption generates
substantial influences on the marketing results of SMEs. The study results support the
Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) by confirming that perceived ease of use and perceived
usefulness create adoption behavior. The research by [8] declares that companies that
understand AI better tend to implement it across their marketing operations through automated
processes and analytical methods alongside connective customer solutions. The present trend
demonstrates SMEs in Aba require specialized learning programs and sector -specific training
about AI technology because adoption rates must improve.
H02: The implementation of generative AI marketing strategies leads SMEs in Aba to
build better customer relationships while achieving higher brand visibility which results in
increased sales numbers.
The study demonstrated that AI -driven marketing strategies created a positive
significant impact which was accredited by a regression coefficient value of 0.274 at a p-value
of 0.000 on SME marketing performance. SMEs that adopt generative AI marketing strategies
discover better customer interaction while becoming more visible to markets and generating
increased sales revenue. The evaluated data shows that AI marketing strategies have a 0.523
moderate relationship with growing business metrics. The research reflects Kaplan and Kotha's
(2024) claim about how AI marketing generates personalized experiences and predictive
capabilities that strengthen customer bonds. [15] established that AI technology enables the
creation of content through automation while also delivering chatbots and accurate advertising
strategies which boost brand visibility and convert more customer leads into sales. SMEs in
Aba can gain a strategic advantage through AI exploitation of automated content production
and sentiment evaluation t ogether with predictive campaign techniques because market
competition remains intense.

H03: Several barriers such as high costs, expert technical requirements, and regulatory

The results show that a negative and significant impact of AI-related challenges (0.195)

barriers to the widespread adoption of AI technology by SMEs in Aba. The analysis shows that

The study results match those of [7] who determined fi nancial restrictions together with
insufficient AI professional access to be significant barriers for small companies adopting new
technology platforms. The AI market report from [16] documents regulatory ambiguities
together with ethical doubts as roadblo cks to AI's general acceptance. The limited financial
resources of SMEs operating in Aba act as barriers to widespread AI integration because upfront
investments in solutions require funding and hiring specialized personnel. SMEs can overcome
their barriers to adopting emerging technologies by joining forces with government agencies
that provide incentives while creating accessible AI solutions and industry-specific AI capacity-
building programs for SMEs [17].
5 Conclusion
The research evaluated how generative AI helps Aba SMEs in Abia State Nigeria
improve their marketing efficiency and competitiveness. This research shows that AI adoption
creates substantial effects on SME marketing performance because organizations that
understand AI technology along with its benefits tend to use it for their marketing approaches.
Generative AI implementation brings better customer connections improved brand recognition
and increased sales outcomes which demonstrates its transformative str ength in SME
development. AI realizes its maximum benefits for organizations but difficulties with price
levels technical skills shortages and regulatory uncertainties limit its planet -wide acceptance.
The study shows why SMEs in Aba require goal -oriented assistance such as AI education
financial backing and regulatory transparency to reach their maximum AI potential. SMEs
should overcome existing obstacles so they can use AI marketing innovations to boost their
market position in a progressively digital environment.
