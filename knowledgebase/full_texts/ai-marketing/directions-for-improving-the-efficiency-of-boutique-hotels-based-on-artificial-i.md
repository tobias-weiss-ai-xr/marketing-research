HOTELS BASED ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE MODELING

Abstract. The rapid development of artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming
the management and operational processes of the hospitality industry. Boutique
hotels, characterized by relatively small room capacity, personalized services,
distinctive design, and strong dependence on guest experience, require flexible and
data-driven approaches to improving operational and economic efficiency. This
article examines the directions for improving the efficiency of boutique hotels
through artificial intelligence -based mod eling. Particular attention is given to AI
applications in demand forecasting, dynamic pricing, occupancy optimization, guest
personalization, workforce planning, energy management, digital marketing, and
operational decision-making. The study proposes an integrated AI-based efficiency
model that combines historical booking data, room prices, occupancy rates,
customer reviews, seasonal factors, events, weather conditions, and online market
information. The article also considers machine learning, neural net works, time -
series forecasting, ensemble models, and explainable artificial intelligence as tools
for supporting managerial decisions. Recent research demonstrates that AI -based
forecasting can improve the accuracy of hotel demand prediction, while AI -driven
personalization can support revenue stability and guest engagement. However,
effective implementation requires high -quality data, appropriate technological
infrastructure, employee competencies, data protection, and human supervision. The
study concludes that AI-based modeling should be considered not as a replacement
for hotel managers but as a decision -support mechanism that can improve resource
allocation, pricing decisions, service personalization, and overall economic
efficiency.
Keywords: artificial intelligence, boutique hotels, machine learning, hotel
efficiency, demand forecasting, dynamic pricing, revenue management,
personalization, predictive modeling, hospitality.
The hospitality industry is experiencing significant technological
transformation as artificial intelligence, machine learning, big data analytics, and
digital platforms become increasingly integrated into hotel management[6].
Artificial intelligence can s upport decision -making in areas such as revenue
management, demand forecasting, customer relationship management, personalized
services, workforce planning, and operational optimization.  Boutique hotels

70

represent a particularly interesting area for AI applications[5]. Unlike large hotel
chains, boutique hotels generally operate with a limited number of rooms and rely
heavily on personalized service, distinctive design, local identity, and individual
guest experiences.
Consequently, their economic efficiency depends not only on room occupancy
but also on the ability to optimize limited human, financial, technological, and
physical resources.  Therefore, the application of AI -based modeling can create
opportunities for boutique hotels to improve the utilization of limited resources and
increase economic efficiency.
Artificial intelligence refers to computational systems capable of performing
tasks that traditionally require human cognitive abilities, including pattern
recognition, prediction, classification, optimization, and decision support [1].
Demand forecasting is one of the most important areas where AI can improve hotel
efficiency[7]. Traditional forecasting approaches frequently depend on historical
averages and managerial experience[3]. However, hotel demand is affected by
multiple variables and may change ra pidly due to seasonality, holidays, events,
weather, economic conditions, competitor pricing, and online booking behavior. AI-
based models can identify complex relationships between these variables.

Recent systematic research on machine intelligence in hospitality identifies
CRM and personalization, revenue management and pricing, operations, and
governance as major application areas[4]. It also emphasizes barriers such as data
governance, privacy, fragmented systems, and the need for human–AI collaboration.
A simplified forecasting model can be represented as:
Future Demand = f(Seasonality + Historical Bookings + Prices + Events +
Market Conditions + Customer Behavior)
Machine learning models can process large datasets and identify nonlinear
relationships that may not be captured by traditional statistical methods.
The methodology used in this study is conceptual and analytical. The
methodology involves synthesizing relevant academic literature on topics such as
artificial intelligence, hospitality management, predictive analytics, hotel revenue
management, and opera tional efficiency. An AI -based conceptual model of
efficiency for boutique hotels is proposed.
The analysis indicates that artificial intelligence can influence boutique hotel
efficiency through several interconnected mechanisms. First, AI -based forecasting

71

can improve the accuracy of demand estimates. More accurate forecasts can support
better room inventory and pricing decisions. Research on hotel demand forecasting
has demonstrated the growing use of machine learning and deep learning approaches
for this purpose. Second, AI can improve pricing decisions by identifying patterns
in historical and forward-looking market prices.
Recent research shows that historical and advance booking prices can provide
useful predictive information for hotel price forecasting. Third, AI -based
personalization can connect operational performance with guest behavior. This is
particularly relevant to boutique hotels because personalized experiences are central
to their positioning.
Fourth, AI can improve resource utilization. Workforce scheduling, energy
management, and predictive maintenance can reduce unnecessary resource
consumption. Fifth, AI can support evidence-based management. Instead of relying
exclusively on intuition, hotel managers can use continuously updated forecasts and
performance indicators. However, AI should not be regarded as an automatic
solution to all hotel -management problems. Its effectiveness depends on data
quality, model selection, organizational readiness , employee competencies, and
managerial supervision.
Artificial intelligence -based modeling creates new opportunities for
improving the operational and economic efficiency of boutique hotels. Due to their
limited room capacity and strong emphasis on personalized service, boutique hotels
can particularly bene fit from technologies that improve forecasting, resource
allocation, pricing, and guest experience.
The most important directions for AI -based efficiency improvement include
demand forecasting, dynamic pricing, occupancy optimization, guest
personalization, workforce planning, energy management, predictive maintenance,
and customer-review analysis.
An integrated AI -based model should combine internal hotel data with
external market information and transform these data into forecasts and managerial
recommendations. The effectiveness of such systems should be evaluated using both
traditional hotel indicators and AI-specific metrics.
Occupancy Rate, ADR, RevPAR, and GOPPAR remain important measures
of hotel performance, while MAPE and RMSE can be used to evaluate forecasting
accuracy. The main conclusion is that AI should be implemented as a decision -
support system rather than as a com plete substitute for human management. The
most effective approach is based on human –AI collaboration, where artificial
intelligence provides data -driven forecasts and recommendations while managers
interpret these results within the specific context of the hotel.
For boutique hotels, the long -term objective should therefore be the creation
of an integrated digital management environment in which AI supports revenue
management, customer experience, labor allocation, energy efficiency, and
operational decision -making. Such an approach can contribute to more efficient
resource utilization, improved service quality, stronger financial performance, and
sustainable development of boutique hotel businesses.
