Abstract
:
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has emerged as a transformative force in

modern marketing,
significantly influencing consumer behavior and enabling highly personalized customer experiences.
With the increasing adoption of AI technologies, companies such as Amazon, Netflix, and Google are
leveraging advanced algorithms, machine

learning, and big data analytics to understand consumer
preferences, predict purchasing behavior, and deliver tailored recommendations in real time. This shift
from traditional mass marketing to data
-
driven personalization has fundamentally altered how
co
nsumers interact with brands and make purchase decisions.

This research paper aims to examine the impact of AI on consumer behavior, focusing on how
personalized marketing strategies influence customer engagement, satisfaction, and loyalty. The study
adopt
s a descriptive and analytical research design, utilizing both primary data collected through
surveys and secondary data from academic journals, industry reports, and case studies. Key areas of
investigation include recommendation systems, targeted adverti
sing, chatbots, and predictive analytics.

The findings suggest that AI
-
driven personalization enhances the overall consumer experience by
reducing information overload, simplifying decision
-
making, and increasing perceived relevance of
products and service
s. Consumers are more likely to engage with brands that provide customized content
and recommendations aligned with their preferences. However, the study also identifies critical
challenges, including concerns over data privacy, lack of transparency in AI
systems, and the potential
for algorithmic bias. Many consumers express discomfort when personalization becomes overly
intrusive, leading to reduced trust in brands.

In conclusion, while AI offers significant opportunities for improving marketing effective
ness and
customer satisfaction, it also raises important ethical considerations. Businesses must strike a balance
between personalization and privacy by ensuring transparency, data security, and responsible use of AI
technologies. The study highlights the
need for marketers to adopt ethical AI practices to build long
-
term
consumer trust and sustain competitive advantage in the evolving digital landscape
.

Despite its numerous advantages, Artificial Intelligence (AI) also has several negative impacts on consumer behavior,

Another significant issue is the “filter bubble” effect, where consumers are repea

dissatisfaction in

Overall, while AI enhances convenience and personalization, its negative impacts
