Abstract. In the article, the essence of the marketing strategy of the trading
enterprise within digitalization is examined. The impact of digitalization on the
marketing strategy of the trading enterprise is analyzed. Three areas are identified,
namely: search, study and processing of information (collection of information about
consumers (online analytics, monitoring of competitors and the market, processing of
large volumes of data, personalization of offers), creation of new communication
channels with both potential buyers and various counterparties (instant communication
through digital channels, personalized communication, chatbots and AI consultants,
interactivity and feedback, omnichannel communications), use of digital technologies
in trade operations (effe ctive management of inventories and supply chains, use of
mobile payments, cooperation of stores with banks and service companies, automation
of business processes, expansion of sales geography, integration of virtual and
augmented reality). A conceptual s cheme of the influence of digitalization on the
marketing strategy of the trading enterprise is proposed. The mechanism of making
decision by a potential buyer to purchase a product is presented. Features of developing
the marketing strategy for the trading enterprise within digitalization are outlined. It is
substantiated that that effective marketing strategy cannot exist without proper
management and some management actions depend on it, namely: resource allocation,
formation of strategic vision, coordin ation of all departments, control and adjustment
of the marketing strategy. It is proved that the marketing strategy of the trading
enterprise should simultaneously contribute to profitability in the long term and meet

Problem statement. Success of trade enterprises in the modern economy,
economic growth and positive results in entrepreneurial activity, as a rule, are in some
way connected with the marketing development. Often in trade, when building a
business model, there is practice of t ransferring marketing practices of successful
enterprises, especially from countries with developed economies, to activities of trade
enterprises that are at initial stages of the life cycle, especially in countries with the so-
called catch-up economy. Blind copying of advanced and successful variants of market
behavior aimed at the consumer within the framework of trade marketing, are forced
to deviate from reference models due to inconsistency of local economic realities with
reference economies. Differences lie in different resource availability, consumer
requirements, and business conditions. At the same time, it is external environmental
factors that have the greatest impact on the marketing strategy, as proven by the
example of Ukraine with the beginning of a full-scale invasion.
Analysis of recent research and publications.  Features of developing
marketing strategies for development of commercial enterprises within digitalization
are covered in publications by domestic and foreign scientists, including: P. Doyle,
P . Kotler, M. Bagarka, N. Ivanova, A. Lyaluk, A. Oliynyk, N. V asyutkina, O. Vdovichena,
M. Zozulya, O. Kubetska, A. Strenkovska, D. Terekhov, S. Povna, Yu. Olyfirenko, etc.
However, without downplaying importance and relevance of existing research, the
issue of developing the marketing strategy for development of the commercial
enterprise within digitalization requires further analysis and specification.
The purpose of the article is to determine features of developing the marketing
strategy for development of the trading enterprise within digitalization.
Presentation of the main material.  The marketing strategy of the commercial
enterprise within digitalization is a comprehensive action plan aimed at promoting
goods and services using digital technologies, online tools and data analysis. It takes
into account changing consumer behavior, emergence of new sales channels, and the
need for perso nalized interaction with customers. It  is a flexible, analytically based
system that allows the enterprise to effectively interact with modern consumers and
increase competitiveness. In the marketing bestseller by P. Doyle “Marketing
Management and Strateg y” it is emphasized that understanding the consumer is the
central idea of marketing, and success in the market depends on the ability to satisfy
his needs better than competitors [1].
Digitalization is one of factors of the external environment of the ent erprise,
which simultaneously influences both the the trading enterprise, and everyone with

– collecting information about consumers (online analytics). Popular tools
include Google Analytics, Facebook Insights, CRM systems that provide information
about customer behavior on the site, popular products, traffic sources, shopping time,
geolocation, age, interests, etc. For example, a retailer has discovered increased
demand in the city supermarket for certain food products every Friday evening; it is
recommended to launch new promotions for these products at this time;
– competitor and market monito ring. The most common tools are SimilarWeb,

pricing policy, and social media presence, which gives a trading company information
about the need to quickly respond to changes and adjust its own marketing strategy;
– processing of large amounts of data (Big Data). Most commonly used Power
applications BI, Tableau, Python, which allow you to identify patterns, segment
customers, build sales forecasts. For example, you ca n use these technologies to
quickly analyze thousands of reviews, checks, and behavioral patterns ;
– personalization of offers. Thanks to digital technologies, companies ca nnot

Examples of service, especially after -sales, can be digital warranty cards and

Digital tools today also play a key role in the risk management system of  a

The second direction of digitalization in the marketing of the trading enterprise
is creation of new communication channels with both potential buyers and various
counterparties. Digital technologies have significantly transformed communications of
trading enterprises with buyers, making them faster, more personalized and two -way.
The main results of these communications are as follows:
– instant communication through digital channels. Retailers actively use
messengers (Viber, Telegram, WhatsApp), social networks (Instagram, Facebook) and
email newsletters to inform about discounts, new products, promotions, changes in
work schedules, and order status;
– personalized communication. Analytics allows you to segment the audience
and send personalized offers, such as recommended products, birthday discounts,
individual selections based on previous purchases;
– chatbots and AI consultants. Virtual assistants provide 24/7 communication,
answer typical questions, and take orders;
– interactivity and feedback. Social networks and online surveys allow buyers to
leave feedback, influence the assortment, and share impressions, which strengthens
loyalty and trust in the seller.
– omnichannel communications. Combining online and offline : for example, a
buyer can reserve a product in the app and pick it up in a physical store.
Communications with various partners and contractors also indirectly affect the
marketing strategy:
– automation of information exchange. Modern ERP systems (e.g. SAP,
Microsoft Dynamics) allow you to automatically send orders to suppliers, update
shipment status, and monitor inventory balances, which reduces errors and the human
factor, and speeds up data processing;
– real-time logistics monitoring. Using GPS trackers, I oT devices, and logistics
platforms, a trading company can track cargo in transit, assess delays, and predict
delivery times;

The third direction of digitalization of marketing activities of the trading
enterprise concerns the use of digital technologies in trading operations. The  main
components are:
– effective inventory and supply chain management. Digital technologies have
significantly improved the inventory and supply management process in retail
enterprises, making it more accurate, fast and cost -effective. The marketing str ategy
should involve cooperation only with reliable contractors, avoiding the sale of low -
quality goods. Modern retail enterprises use ERP and WMS systems (e.g. SAP, Oracle
NetSuite) for monitoring balances in real time, automatically generating orders to the
supplier when goods become less than a critical level, analyzing the turnover of goods:
which items are selling faster, which are selling slower;
– use of mobile payments. The latter simplify, speed up and make the purchase
process more convenient for both the buyer and the trading enterprise. The mobile
payment process takes a few seconds, mobile payments are automatically synchronized
with POS systems and CRM, which allows you to keep accurate records of sales,
generate reports, and track customer pur chasing habits. In the case of online stores,
mobile payments allow you to complete the purchase without entering long details,
safely;
– cooperation of stores with banks and service companies is a very important
factor for successful modern trade. It allows to improve financial services, increase the
quality of service and strengthen the trust of buyers. Financial services for customers
(bank partners) are important in providing installments, consumer loans, payment by
installments directly in the store, t he ability to pay with payment cards, Google Pay,
Apple Pay thanks to the acquiring bank, joint loyalty programs (the bank and the store
launch bonus programs, cashback, promotions for a common audience). Effective
after-sales and service, warranty period are also quite important components of the
purchase decision;
– automation of business processes is a key factor in successful transformation
of modern retail enterprises. It allows you to reduce costs, speed up operations and
improve the quality of servic e. Automated systems perform routine tasks. As a result,

allocated for advertising, promotion, new products, and impleme ntation of marketing
technologies depends on financial management;
– formation of strategic vision. The management mechanism defines goals of the
marketing strategy (increasing sales, expanding the market, strengthening the brand),
determines competitive advantages (what distinguishes the company from others);
– coordination of all departments. Sales, logistics, marketing, and finance

Therefore, the marketing strategy of the trading enterprise should simultaneously
contribute to profitability in the long term and meet needs of a potential buyer. A more
important requirement for the marketing strategy is its compliance with external
conditions. At the global level, the mode rn external environment is characterized
primarily by high rates of digitalization of both business and consumers. It is also an
indisputable fact that the struggle for the consumer is increasingly moving into the
digital space. Accordingly, the enterprise that masters digital technologies faster and
better and uses them better will gain competitive advantages.
Conclusions. Economic changes significantly affect marketing strategies by
affecting consumer purchasing power and demand patterns, and can take the  form of
inflation, recession, economic growth, changes in exchange rates, or changes in
government policies. Martial law in Ukraine is a major factor in economic changes.
These changes can seriously affect companies ’ marketing strategies. For example,
during periods of economic growth, consumers typically have higher disposable
income. This increase in purchasing power can lead to increased demand for goods and
services. In response, companies may choose to invest more in marketing, launch new
products, or target new market segments.
On the other hand, during the recession, consumers are looking to cut back on
their spending. This reduced demand can force businesses to change their marketing
strategies. They may need to focus more on promoting the value and  affordability of
their products, offer discounts or promotions, or target a different demographic that
may still have purchasing power. Therefore, economic changes can significantly
impact marketing strategies. Businesses should monitor economic environment and be
prepared to adapt their marketing strategies in response to these changes.
