ABSTRACT :
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is being integrated into social media marketing through personalizations, recommendation systems, automated content creation,
chatbotsanddata-drivencontentdistribution.ThisresearchinvestigatestheefficacyofAI-poweredsocialmediamarketingonbrandengagementofGenerationZ
consumers in Vijayawada. A descriptive cross-sectional study design was followed, and a structured online questionnaire was administered to 86 participants.
Thisstudyanalyzedtherespondents’characteristics,social-mediausage,awarenessofAImarketing,perceptionaboutAI-poweredmarketing,brandengagement,
purchase intention and concerns regarding AI marketing. Frequency and percentage distributions, mean scores, Cronbach’s alpha and Pearson correlation were
performed. Perception about AI-powered social media marketing had a mean score of 3.94 out of 5, whereas brand-engagement had a mean score of 3.97.
Pearson correlation was r = 0.744 (p < 0.001), signifying a strong, positive association between respondents’ perception about AI-marketing and brand
engagement. Personal data privacy and excessive personalized advertising were the prominent concerns. Interpreting these results should be done with caution
duetothenon-probabilitysamplingtechniqueandshouldnotbegeneralizedtotheentirepopulationofGenerationZconsumersinVijayawada.

1. Introduction
Digital marketing has transformed traditional modes of brand-consumer engagement. Social-media platforms enable businesses to publish content,
promote products, address queries and establish relationships with customers. Artificial Intelligence has enhanced the scope and depth of such
engagement through recommendation systems, personalized advertising, automated content creation, chatbots, customer-data analysis and algorithmic
contentplacement.
GenerationZrepresentsavitalsegmentofdigitalconsumersassocialmediaisdeeplyintegratedintotheircommunication,entertainmentandproduct-
searchingactivities. Brandengagementencompasses followingabrand,likingorsharingcontent,commenting, seekinginformation,establishingtrust,
feeling connected to a brand and exhibiting loyalty. AI-powered marketing can facilitate these activities by delivering relevant, timely and valuable
contenttoconsumers.
However, concerns regarding personal data privacy, complex recommendation algorithms and excessive personalization could impact consumer
receptivity. The present study explores GenerationZ consumers inVijayawada and analyzes theirawareness andperceptions aboutAI-powered social
mediamarketing,brandengagementandconcernsregardingAImarketinguse.

3. Research Gap
Existing research has investigated AI-enabled personalization,social-media engagement, consumertrust, privacy and AI-generated marketing content.
However, limited empirical studies have examined the association between perceptions of AI-powered social-media marketing and brandengagement
among Generation Z consumers in the local context. Several studies focus on alternative geographical locations or narrower outcomes, such as ad-
clickingbehaviours andpurchaseintention.Thisstudy, therefore, aims toinvestigatethe relationshipbetween perceptionsofAI-powered social-media
marketingandbrandengagementinthelocalsample.

8. Research Methodology
8.1 Research Design
Thestudyfollows adescriptiveandanalyticalquantitativeresearchdesign.Thedescriptiveaspectprovidesanoverviewofrespondents’characteristics,
social-media usage, awareness, perceptions, engagement and concerns. The analytical aspect investigates the association between AI-powered social-
mediamarketingperceptionandbrandengagement.
8.2 Study Area and Population
The study area comprises Generation Z consumers in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, who use social-media platforms and could be exposed to AI-
poweredmarketingcontent.
8.3. Sample and Sampling Technique
The study analyzes 86 valid online questionnaireresponses. A non-probabilityonline samplingtechnique was used. As the samplingmethod does not
ensure that every member of the target population has an equal known chance of selection, the findings should be interpreted as applicable to the
surveyedrespondentsandnotasrepresentativeofallGenerationZconsumersinVijayawada.
8.4. Data Collection
Theprimarydatawere collectedusingastructuredonlinequestionnaire.It compriseddemographicquestions,social-mediausagequestions,awareness
questions,five-pointLikert-scalestatements,anoverall-opinionquestionandaconcernquestionaboutAI-poweredmarketing.
8.5. Questionnaire Preparation
The AI-powered social mediamarketing perception index was formed usingQ13–Q20,and the brand-engagementindex was formed using Q21–Q30.
The Likert scale was coded as 1 = Strongly Disagree, 2 = Disagree, 3 = Neutral, 4 = Agree and 5 = Strongly Agree. The reported Cronbach’s alpha
valueswere0.955and0.964fortheperceptionandengagementscales,respectively,indicatinghighinternalconsistencyinthisdataset.
9. Data Analysis
Frequency and percentage distributions were used for categorical variables, mean scores for Likert-scale statements, Cronbach’s alpha for internal
consistencyandPearsoncorrelationtoinvestigatetheassociationbetweenAI-poweredsocial-mediamarketingperceptionandbrandengagement.
10.Analysis
TheanalysisshowsthatrespondentshavepositiveperceptionsaboutAI-poweredsocialmediamarketing(mean=3.94)andbrandengagement(mean=
3.97). Both scales exhibit high reliability with Cronbach’s alpha values of 0.955 and 0.964, respectively. Pearson correlation (r = 0.744, p < 0.001)
indicates a strong, significant positive relationship, supporting the alternative hypothesis. However, privacy concerns and excessive personalized
advertisingareprominentissues.

11. Conclusion
The study indicates that AI-powered social-media marketing is positively associated with brand engagement among the surveyed Generation Z
consumersinVijayawada.ThemeanscoresshowgenerallyfavourableperceptionsofAI-poweredmarketingandpositivebrandengagement,whilethe
Pearson correlation (r = 0.744, p < 0.001) demonstrates a strongand statistically significant association between the two constructs. However, privacy
and excessive personalization remainimportant concerns. Effective AI-powered social-mediamarketing should therefore combine personalization and
engagementwithtransparency,responsibledatause,authenticityandhumanoversight.Becausethestudyuses86non-probabilityonlineresponses,the
findings should be interpreted as evidence from the selected sample rather than as representative estimates for all Generation Z consumers in
Vijayawada.
