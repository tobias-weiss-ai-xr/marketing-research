Abstract
The article examines the improvement of marketing management under the conditions of artificial
intelligence and digital personalization. Modern marketing increasingly requires individualized and context-
relevant customer interactions, effective use of data, automation of marketing communications, and near-
real-time evaluation of campaign performance. The study proposes viewing marketing not merely as
advertising and sales activity, but as an integrated system connecting data, technologies, customer
experience and managerial decision -making. A systems approach, comparative analysis, scientific
abstraction, grouping and synthesis were applied. Based on recent international marketing research and
academic literature, the effects of artificial intelligence on personalization, customer experience,
marketing analytics, content creation and managerial responsiveness are examined. As a result, a
functional mechanism of marketing management is proposed, consisting of data integration, customer
segmentation and personalization, AI analytics, content and communication management, customer
experience management, and control and KPI evaluation. The approach can contribute to optimizing
marketing expenditures, strengthening customer relationships and improving the evidence base of
marketing decisions.
Keywords: marketing, marketing management, artificial intelligence, digital marketing, personalization,
customer experience, marketing analytics, data-driven marketing, KPI, digital transformation.
1. Introduction
The development of the digital economy is
significantly changing the content of marketing
activity and its management mechanisms.
Consumers use several digital channels at the
same time to search for information, compare
products, make purchases and communicate with
brands. As a result, for enterprises, quickly
identifying consumer needs, creating a suitable
offer and managing the customer experience
continuously have become among the central tasks
of marketing strategy.
In 2026, one of the most important trends in
marketing is the strengthening of personalization
with the help of artificial intelligence. Recent
marketing studies note that AI is widely used in
processes ranging from content creation,
advertising optimization, strategic planning and
forecasting to the formation of individual offers for
customers [8; 9]. At the same time, implementing
the technology does not in itself guarantee
marketing effectiveness: the result depends on
data quality, the reorganization of processes,
employee competence and human oversight.
The role of artificial intelligence in marketing is
twofold. On the one hand, AI rapidly analyzes large
volumes of customer data and automates
segmentation, recommendations, advertising
optimization and content creation. On the other
hand, it gives rise to risks such as excessive
automation, misuse of personal data, algorithmic
errors and loss of consumer trust. For this reason,
marketing management needs to abandon the
approach of “the more technology, the better the
result” and to assess the value that technology
adds to customer and business outcomes.

enabled personalization as one of the main

2. Literature review
The scientific literature on the impact of artificial
intelligence on marketing is developing in several
main directions. Review studies published in 2025 –
2026 identify AI -driven personalization, customer
engagement, big data analytics, predictive
modeling, generative AI, ethical issues and data
privacy as the main scientific directions [6]. These
directions require the technological and managerial
nature of marketing to be studied simultaneously.
One of the studies published in 2026 empirically
examined the impact of AI -driven personalization
on customer experience, purchase intention,
consumer trust and the organization’s
responsiveness. The study used the PLS -SEM
method on data from 402 respondents and showed
that AI personalization can improve customer
experience, and that customer experience can in
turn strengthen purchase intention [5]. It also notes
that privacy risk can weaken the positive link
between AI personalization and trust.
In modern research on the digital transformation
of marketing, the concept of first -party data is also
of particular importance. If an organization can
collect high -quality data from direct relationships
with its customers and link it to segmentation and
personalization, the accuracy of marketing
decisions may increase. However, if data quality is
low, the results of AI algorithms are also likely to be
unreliable.
Gartner’s analysis of 2026 marketing trends
identifies agentic AI, one -to-one customer
interactions, AI -driven search and maintaining
brand trust as important future directions of
marketing management [10]. A BCG study notes
that although many marketing leaders consider AI
transformation important, only a portion of
organizations have carried out a deep operational
transformation [11]. This shows that there is a gap
between technological experience and genuine
organizational transformation.
Google’s analyses emphasize that in 2026
consumers’ AI -based search and content
experience is changing, and that brands must
create a useful, trustworthy and visually rich
content ecosystem in the generative search
environment [12]. Thus, a marketing manager is no
longer only someone who manages an advertising
campaign, but a specialist who shapes the brand’s
digital information environment.
Canva’s 2026 report on marketing and AI also
notes that AI -assisted content production has
accelerated, but that human creative direction,
brand meaning, trust and human oversight remain
important [13]. This conclusion shows the need for
a balance between automation and human
creativity in marketing management.
The literature review shows that the separate
effects of AI marketing tools have been widely
studied in existing research. However, integrating
AI, personalization, customer experience, data
management and marketing decisions into a single
organizational and economic mechanism,
especially in the context of enterprises in
Uzbekistan, requires additional scientific research.

The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Marketing
Management. Artificial intelligence is penetrating
all stages of marketing management. Data
segmentation, forecasting customer behavior,
selecting advertising audiences, creating content
variants, communicating through chatbots and
optimizing campaign results can all be accelerated
with the help of AI.
One of the most important capabilities of AI in
marketing management is identifying, within large
volumes of data, the patterns and relationships
needed for decision -making. For example, product
or content offers can be tailored to buyers on the
basis of their previous purchases, site visits,
searches and reactions to communications.
However, the effectiveness of AI -based
marketing depends on the quality of the data. If
customer data are duplicated, outdated or
incorrect, the result of personalization will also be
incorrect. Therefore, in marketing management,
data governance, data cleaning, building a single
customer profile and compliance with privacy
requirements should be regarded as a separate
management task.
AI is also changing creative activity in marketing.
Generative AI can prepare many text, image and
video variants in a short time. But evaluating brand
positioning, audience psychology, cultural context
and the ethical aspects of communication must
remain the responsibility of the human specialist.
Marketing studies from 2026 likewise show that the
value of AI output is determined not only by the
volume of content, but by its relevance, reliability
and fit with the brand strategy [13].

Managing Personalization and Customer
Experience. Personalization is becoming one of the
main competitive tools of modern marketing. Its
essence lies not in sending the same advertisement
or offer to every customer, but in shaping
communication that fits the customer’s needs,
purchase history, behavior and context.
In simple personalization, a customer may be
addressed by name or sent an offer suited to a
general segment. AI -enabled hyper -
personalization, on the other hand, makes it
possible to adapt product recommendations,
advertising content, offers and the timing of
communication on the basis of the customer’s
near-real-time behavior. Marketing studies from

Personalization is directly related to customer

desired product, is shown relevant content and
receives a quick response to an inquiry, a positive
experience with the brand can be formed. At the
same time, excessive personalization may give the

The economic effect of personalization should
not be measured by sales alone. Customer
retention, repeat purchases, customer lifetime
value, reduction in the number of complaints,
brand loyalty and customer experience indicators
should also be assessed.
Functional Mechanism of Marketing
Management. Within the framework of the study, it
is proposed to organize marketing management
based on artificial intelligence and personalization
through six functional blocks. Viewing marketing as
a system broader than advertising activity, this
mechanism forms a continuous management cycle
from data to economic results.
The first block is data integration. Combining
data coming from CRM, web analytics, sales
systems, social networks and other sources into a
single system creates the information base for
marketing decisions.
The second block is segmentation and
personalization. With the help of AI and machine
learning algorithms, segments are formed
according to customers’ needs, behavior and
purchase probability. A separate offer and
communication strategy is developed for each
segment.
The third block is marketing analytics and
forecasting. This block covers evaluating campaign
results, forecasting demand, identifying the
probability of customer churn and optimizing the
marketing budget.
The fourth block is content and communication
management. Text, visual and video content
variants can be created with the help of generative
AI, but their final brand and ethical oversight
belongs to marketing specialists.
The fifth block is customer experience and
relationship management. All of the customer’s
interactions with the brand are coordinated through
CRM, chatbots, omnichannel communication and
service analytics.
The sixth block is control, KPIs and feedback.
Indicators such as campaign ROI, conversion,
engagement, retention and customer lifetime value
are monitored. Depending on the results, the
marketing strategy is re-optimized.

Balancing AI and the Human Factor in
Marketing. One of the important issues of digital
transformation in marketing is the balance between
automation and the human factor. AI can perform
large volumes of repetitive operations quickly and
efficiently, but strategic decisions, brand
positioning, creative direction and ethical
responsibility require the participation of the
human manager.
Studies from 2026 show that there is a
significant gap between carrying out AI
transformation and using AI tools. According to

The competencies of marketing employees are

Therefore, in marketing management, an “AI +
human” model, rather than “human instead of AI,”
may be more effective. While AI provides speed,
scale and analytical capabilities, the human
manager provides strategic purpose, creative
direction, context and ethical oversight.
Data Privacy and Management Risks in
Marketing. Because personalization requires the
use of customer data, privacy is one of the
important risks of marketing management. In the
processes of collecting, storing and processing
data, the organization must take into account legal,
consent and security requirements.
In AI -based marketing there are also risks of
algorithmic bias, incorrect recommendations, fake
or unverified content, deepfakes, damage to brand
reputation and cybersecurity. To manage such
risks, it is appropriate to introduce an AI policy,
human approval of content, control of data quality
and audit mechanisms.
For a marketing manager, risk management
means not restricting technology, but creating rules
for its responsible use. In particular, the factual
accuracy of AI -generated content, the
substantiation of advertising promises and claims,
and the transparency of the use of customer data
must be continuously monitored.
An Improved System for Evaluating Marketing
Performance. Under the digital transformation of
marketing, the KPI system should not be limited to
sales volume or the number of ad views. Marketing
activity must be evaluated across the entire
lifecycle, from acquiring a customer to retaining
that customer.
In the proposed approach, marketing
effectiveness is evaluated at three levels. The first
level is communication indicators: reach, CTR,
engagement and content response. The second
level is customer indicators: conversion, retention,
CSAT, NPS and CLV. The third level is economic
indicators: CAC, ROAS, marketing ROI, sales
growth and profit.
In this way, the activity of the marketing
department is evaluated not with the question
“How many people were shown the
advertisement?” but with the question “How much
value was created?” This strengthens the strategic
status of marketing management.

Practical Proposals for Enterprises in
Uzbekistan. In developing AI -based marketing in
enterprises in Uzbekistan, it is first of all necessary
to build a high -quality customer database.
Implementing a CRM system alone is not enough;
customer data must be maintained on a single
standard, duplicate records must be reduced, and
integration between the marketing and sales
departments must be ensured.
The second direction is developing the digital
competencies of marketing employees. Employees
should undergo regular training in using AI tools,
analyzing data, interpreting advertising results,
verifying content and complying with privacy
requirements.
The third direction is developing personalization
step by step. At first, simple segmentation and
recommendation systems can be introduced, and
later behavior-based personalization and predictive
analytics. This reduces investment risk for small
and medium-sized enterprises.

4. Conclusion and Recommendations
The research conducted shows that artificial
intelligence and personalization are significantly
changing the content of marketing management.
Marketing is now emerging not merely as a function
of promoting advertising and sales, but as a system
that integrates data, technologies, customer
experience and strategic management.
As a result of the research, a functional
mechanism of marketing management was
proposed, consisting of data integration,
segmentation and personalization, marketing
analytics, content and communications, customer
experience, and control and KPIs.
Although AI makes it possible to increase
marketing effectiveness, its result depends on data
quality, employee competence, the reorganization
of workflows and human oversight. Therefore,
before implementing a technology, the marketing
problem and the business objective should be
clearly defined, and only then should the
appropriate AI tool be chosen.
Personalization creates great opportunities in
marketing, but it is necessary to maintain a balance
with customer trust and data privacy. In AI -based
marketing, human oversight, data governance,
monitoring of algorithmic risks and verification of
the factual accuracy of content should be regarded
as mandatory management elements.
To develop AI marketing in enterprises in
Uzbekistan, it is appropriate first to create a high -
quality first -party data base, integrate CRM and
analytical systems, raise the digital competencies
of marketing employees, introduce personalization
step by step, and link marketing KPIs to business
results.
In future research, it is appropriate to test the
proposed marketing effectiveness mechanism
empirically using the example of enterprises in
Uzbekistan, and to evaluate the impact of AI
personalization on sales, customer loyalty and
marketing ROI using econometric methods.
