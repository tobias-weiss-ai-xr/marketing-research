ABSTRACT: Digital advertising is a vital element of online marketing, and businesses now have the means to create personalized,
measurable advertising strategies with a data -driven approach. The digital advertising world has been reshaped by Artificial
Intelligence (AI) with enhanced audience targeting, optimization of campaigns, real -time decision-making, content creation, and
recommendation systems. This survey is a thorough overview of how digital advertising is used in the modern -day Internet and
what strategies and methods are used in the context of AI. It also covers audience segmentation, personaliz ation, programmatic
advertising, metrics for measurement, and key advertising strategies, consumer perception, AI optimization techniques and
generative and predictive AI. The survey also analyzes the current literature to uncover research trends, challeng es and emerging
topics, such as data privacy, algorithmic bias, explainability, ad fraud and scalability. It also identifies current research opportunities
and explores the significance of ethical and trustworthy use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in digital advertising. AI has the potential
to revolutionize advertising efficiency, personalization, campaign performance, customer interaction and ROI, and a robust et hical
and privacy-protective framework is needed to construct intelligent advertising systems of the future.
KEYWORDS: Digital Advertising, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Campaign Optimization, Consumer Behavior,
Personalized Advertising, ROI, Ethical Challenges.
I. INTRODUCTION
Digital platforms have grown rapidl y and are now an
important part of digital advertising. The benefits of online
advertising have spurred a move across all verticals towards
online advertising as a means to target specific audiences,
measure performance metrics and optimize campaigns as th ey
go. With digital advertising on the rise, businesses are putting
significant efforts into optimizing their ad placement to ensure
ads are delivered to the right people at the right time, maximizing
user engagement and ROI. Conventional ad placement
approaches are mainly based on demographic targeting, heuristic
rule-based models, and manual segmentation, in which affiliate
marketers set certain rules to show ads according to their
browsing history, location, gender, and age [1]. Advertising on
Internet is a type of online advertising, and a source of income
for a growing number of websites and companies. In modern
times, regardless of whether a small business or a large multi -
national company, online advertising is a necessary part of any
business marketing plan [2]. Online ads are basically there at
least two companies, the ad agency, the buyer or sponsor and the
ad network, the publisher. Because technical innovation and
online advertising go hand -in-hand, nume rous companies
specialize in both fields. The complexity of analytics data and
cross-platform conversion attribution means that many
organizations still require assistance when trying to measure the
Return on Investment (ROI) of their digital advertising
initiatives. These are some of the barriers that must be overcome
and solutions that can be adopted in the future to make digital

advertising more effective and efficient. It is critical to
comprehend and resolve these theoretical and practical concerns
to guarantee the efficacy of digital advertising campaigns in light
of the evolving technology and shifting consumer attitude [3].
What is called  "intelligence" in the real world is really the
capacity of computers and other electronic devices to mimic
human intellect in areas like learning, problem-solving, and
decision-making. AI is quickly becoming a revolutionary
force in several industries, including digital advertising [4][5].
The advent and growth of AI have laid the groundwork for
intelligent operations in advertising across several domains
[6][7][8]. Computational advertising (targeting, personalization,
content creation, and ad optimization): these four areas of
artificial intelligence in advertising have received a lot of
attention and research from academics [9].

II. DIGITAL ADVERTISING STRATEGIES IN THE
MODERN INTERNET ERA
Digital advertising strategies are now a fundamental element
of Internet-based marketing, allowing companies to connect
with the target audience via a variety of online channels. The
integration of AI, data analytics, and automation has
significantly improved audience targeting, campaign
optimization, personalization, a nd overall advertising
effectiveness. Fig. 1. presents the classification of major digital
advertising strategies, including web display advertising,
Amazon advertising, influencer marketing, PPC advertising, and
geofencing advertising used in modern digital marketing [10].
• Web Design Advertising: A website is like a house
online; a lot of people who might buy something go there
to learn more about it. A company's website is the initial
point of contact between the company and potential
customers. Websites should be engaging enough to meet
the expectations of users so that they stay longer and
continue to be more immersed in the company's
offerings, as the purpose of online advertising campaigns
is to drive traffic to the website.

• Amazon: Amazon is now among the most effective
internet advertising platforms, ranking third in
popularity, just behind Facebook and Google. The
company may advertise its wares and name in many
Amazon places, including search results, product pages,
and more, through the use of Amazon Advertising.
• Influencer: Another kind of advertising that works is
influencer marketing. Brand recognition, user trust [11],
and revenue generation are all aided by influencer
marketing, which is employed by businesses of all sizes.
One way to use influencers in advertising is to form a
partnership with them and have them promote brands,
products, or services on their exclusive channels.
• Pay-per-click (PPC): Pay-per-click (PPC) advertising
is a method of promoting o nline products and services
by having ads show up in response to user queries. Paid
search allows to buy placement for chosen keywords,
whereas search engine optimization focuses on winning
ranks through optimization and content. With PPC,
marketers compet e with one another to have their
adverts displayed in search results for certain keywords
by using a bidding mechanism.
• Geofencing Advertising:  One of the most effective
forms of internet advertising for businesses seeking to
reach rivals, top talent, expensive leads, and nearby
consumers [12]. Using geofencing, a company may
construct an electronic perimeter around an identifiable
place, such as a university campus, a competitor's office,
or a conference center. A retargeting audience is added
once someone penetrates this barrier. After that, the firm
may remarket to those users,  which can increase their
familiarity with the brand, direct their next shopping
excursion, or even affect which vendors their company
chooses for quarterly quotations and proposals [13].

B. Traditional Advertising vs Digital Advertising
The development of advertising has changed the marketing
approach from traditional mass communication to a very focused
and data -informed form. Digital advertising is significantly
different from its analog predecessor due to a number of
important disti nctions. Real-time analytics, data-driven
decision-making, personalized engagement provide the
underpinning for effective and measurable marketing through
digital advertising.
• Traditional advertising: Traditional advertising is one
of these forms of media advertising, which is the one
that reaches a large audience, segmented in terms of
their demographic and geographical characteristics [15].
Highly costly to produce and cannot be modified once it
has been released, but is much more credible and
recognized for the brand in question. The measurement
of ROI is also difficult because the most prevalent
method used to measure performance is circulation
count, television ratings or audience estimation.
• Digital advertising: Digital advertising has its own set
of tools including cutting -edge technology, artificial
intelligence and data analysis that enable it to serve ads
that fit people's preferences and behavior [16].
Businesses have the option to microtarget consumers on
social media platforms, such as Instagram or Facebook,
to boost engagement and conversion rates. Google
Analytics and other analytics tools enable data -driven
optimization and real-time performance monitoring, and
PPC and CPA pricing models e nable cost -effective
campaign management [17]. Table I presents a

III. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TECHNIQUES IN
DIGITAL ADVERTISING
ML enables advertisers to enhance the efficacy of their
advertising efforts by doing thorough analyses of customer
behavior and preferences. AI technology does this optimization
in four primary methods [18][19].
• Real-Time Bidding (RTB):  New platforms that use
RTB are called ad exchanges. Auctions take place in real
time whenever a user creates  an ad impression. Using
data on ad success and real-time customer behavior, AI
systems automatically optimize advertising budget
through bidding. The right audience helps to maximize
ROI, by ensuring ads are shown to the right audience.

• A/B Testing: AI can speed up the A/B testing process,

A. Types of AI in Digital Advertising
AI-driven digital advertising basically comes in two forms:
generative and predictive. The two are instances of narrow AI,
which is designed to carry out targeted activities with the goal of
enhancing ad effectiveness, customization, and decision -
making.
• Generative AI: It uses advanced DL models to generate
personalized content like text, photos, videos, and audio.
It has wide applications in digital advertising when it
comes to making advertisements, promotional graphics,
customized ads, and interaction with customers through
chatbots [22].
• Predictive AI: Predictive AI uses past and current data
to anticipate what users do, what they like and the results
of campaigns. Utilized in digital advertising campaigns
including audience segmentation, individualized
recommendations, click-through rate prediction and
campaign optimization. Netflix and Amazon are great
illustrations of the power of predictive AI to provide
personalized experiences. Furthermore, predictive
insight can be utilized for data-driven decision-making,
recommending the most effective approaches ,
budgeting and audience targeting to optimize the
effectiveness of the advertising campaign.

IV. AI-DRIVEN ADVERTISING APPLICATIONS AND
PERFORMANCE EVALUATION
AI has revolutionized digital advertising, allowing for
intelligent audience targeting, personalized content delivery,
automated campaign management, and data-driven performance
analysis. The section focuses on key AI advertising use cases and
metrics to analyze ad campaigns.
A. AI-Based Audience Targeting and Customer Segmentation
AI can segment the audience using demographic, behavioral,
and transactional data, and make user preference predictions
[23]. The use of ML algorithms can also enable precise targeting
of the customer, allowing for e ffective advertisements from a
targeted group of people and significantly increasing the number
of interactions, conversions, and in general the efficiency of the
marketing process [24].
B. Personalized Advertising and Recommendation Systems:
AI recommendation systems can interpret customer data —
such as their interactions, browsing history, and purchase
patterns—to provide tailore d advertising and product
suggestions [25]. They work through learning from user
feedback, making the recommendations more accurate,
improving user  satisfaction, and the effectiveness of the
campaigns on digital platforms.
C. AI-Driven Advertising Content Generation
Generative AI and NLP generate advertising texts, images
and videos automatically [26]. Furthermore, these tools allow for
the optimization of contextually relevant ads towards targeted
and contextually relevant ones, as well as optimization of
contextually relevant ads towards dynamic ones, maximizing
advertising effectiveness and user engagement [27].
D. Programmatic Advertising and Real-Time Optimization
AI can help with ad placement, targeting viewers, and
optimizing bidding in programmatic advertising [28]. Real-time
data analysis and Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO) give
advertisers the opportunity to optimize performance in real-time,
allocate budgets more efficiently and maximize the impact of
advertising [29].
E. Advertising Performance Evaluation
The campaign performance data that can be tracked include
metrics like the Click -Through Rate (CTR), Convers ion Rate
(CVR), Cost Per Click (CPC), Return on Investment (ROI), and
Return on Ad Spend (ROAS). These insights can be applied for
continuous improvement and data-driven decisions to optimize
ad performance [30].
1) Challenges of AI in Digital Advertising
AI is revolutionizing the effectiveness of digital ads, but
there are technical and ethical issues regarding AI's application
in digital advertising. However, there are some technical, ethical,
and operational challenges associated with the widespread use
of AI in digital advertising. These challenges must be resolved
to ensure the availability of reliable, transparent, secure, and
responsible AI-powered advertising systems.
F. Data Privacy and Security
A lot of data is used in AI-driven ads, which is essential for
targeted and personalized ads. The threats of unauthorized
access and privacy concerns are growing, and maintaining

G. Algorithmic Bias And Fairness
AI models can be trained on biased or imbalanced data,
which can result in unfair advertising decisions, such as targeting
users inappropriately or discriminating against user segments.
Equity and impartial AI algorithms are fundamental to
responsible digital advertising.
H. Transparency and Explainability
AI models can be difficult to understand and are often
referred to as “black boxes.” The lack of transparency is
challenging for advertisers to trust the site, challenging to
analyze campaigns, challenging to ensure campaign
accountability and challenging to ensure regulatory compliance.
I. Ad Fraud and Invalid Traffic
As digital advertising platforms emerged, click fraud, bots,
fake impressions, and fake interactions have become a big
challenge. AI is essential to power fraud detection technology
that can help uncover fraud and improve ad reliability.
J. Scalability and Real-Time Processing
The modern digital advertising environment creates a ton of
real-time data from many platforms. This is a huge technical
challenge to process the information efficiently with low latency,
high accuracy, and scalable AI models.

V. LITERATURE REVIEW
This section provides an overview of the recent research on
how digital advertising strategies use AI methodolo gies, main
approaches, main findings, advantages, disadvantages, and gaps.
A. K. R. Seella (2026) proposes a model that leverages AI to
predict a marketing campaign's success and estimate the ROI
using data from the Marketing Campaign Performance Dataset
(Kaggle). Boosting -based regression models (LightGBM and
CatBoost) were evaluated by regression metrics and k-fold cross
validation with the models. The results of the models were
interpreted using SHAP, and here the top performer was
CatBoost Regressor with 98.17% of the models and the lowest
prediction error [32].
S. K. Aggarwal and J. Singh (2025) exami ne the impact of
ad blockers on digital advertising and user concerns regarding
privacy. It discusses adaptive strategies like ethical advertising
approaches and alternate revenue models, which help reconcile
user desires with advertising effectiveness and  encourage a
sustainable digital advertising ecosystem [33].
S. O. Kediya et al. (2024)  examine the ethical implications
of AI in advertising and marketing, its current trends,
regulations, and provide best practices. It provides practical
insights from case studies and empirical research, highlighting
the need for ethical AI usage, responsible implementation, and
well-balanced AI regulations that enable innovation while
ensuring consumer protection [34].
A. Leonardo P. C. S, and A. Ramadhan (2024)  investigate
the impact of digital advertising and trust on consumer purchase
intention using data from 100 respondents with PLS-SEM. This
study's findings —a positive and statistically significant
correlation between digital advertising trust and purchase
intention—point to the importance of trustworthy digital
marketplaces and advertising tactics in influencing consumers'
online purchasing behavior [35].
A. Samad et al. (2023) look at the practices of advertising in
virtual worlds, gaps in the research, and the differences between
traditional and metaverse advertising. It offers best practices in
the creation of successful advertising campaigns and offers
information to help make informed marketing decisions in
metaverse environments [36].
Surjandy and C. Cassandra (2022) aim to analyze the impact
of risk factors, trust, and information quality of advertisements
on purchase intention and purchasing decisions by using SEM -
PLS with Smart PLS modeling dat a, which was obtained using
a snowball sampling technique with 200 samples. The results
demonstrate that decisions and intentions to buy are affected by
financial risk (19.8%) and performance risk (23.4%), whereas
factors related to information quality (na mely, accuracy (−23.4
%), integrity (50.5 %), timeliness (21.6 %), and validity
(20.7%)) considerably impact these intentions, enabling the
development of digital advertising campaigns to be more
effective [37].The selected literature is summarized in Table. II,

Research Gap : Although considerable research has been
conducted on digital advertising and AI-driven marketing, most
studies focus on specific areas such as personalization, consumer
behavior, trust, campaign optimization, or ROI prediction. The
research is still in its  early stages and does not yet provide a
comprehensive picture of AI techniques, their applications, their
performance assessment, or a common approach to
implementation issues. Additionally, new concepts like
Generative AI, explainable AI, data privacy, a lgorithmic bias,
and ethical aspects of digital advertising have not been fully
discussed or developed. Hence, there is a need to have a
comprehensive survey that incorporates all these factors and
highlights the potential avenues for future research on AI -
powered digital advertising.
VI. CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORK
Digital advertising is a key element of Internet -based
marketing and is essential to providing organizations with
personalized, data -driven and measurable advertising
campaigns. This survey covered t he latest trends in digital
advertising strategies and the increasing influence of artificial
intelligence on the shifting landscape of advertising, including
its role in audience targeting, recommendation algorithms,
programmatic advertising, real -time bi dding, content creation
and campaign optimization. Security of personal information,
combating algorithmic bias, explainability, advertising fraud,
and scalability were among the important issues discussed, as
were the measures used to evaluate AI effectiv eness. Also, the
study has highlighted the expanding importance of trustworthy,
ethical, and transparent AI in intelligent advertising, as well as
the most recent advances in the field and any known research
gaps. Ad efficacy, consumer engagement, decision-making, and
ROI are all positively affected by AI, according to the results. AI
also provides a good foundation for further study and the
development of intelligent digital advertising systems for the
future.
Implementing Generative AI, Explainable AI, la rge
language models and multimodal learning into digital
advertising platforms would benefit future studies. The creation
of privacy-respecting, fair, transparent, and energy-efficient AI
models, combined with effective fraud detection and cross -
platform optimization strategies, will contribute to more reliable,
ethical, and intelligent advertising environments.
