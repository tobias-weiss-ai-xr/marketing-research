ABSTRACT :
The rapid proliferation of social media platforms has redefined how businesses identify, engage, and convert prospective customers. As of early 2026, there are
5.66 billion social media user identities worldwide — representing approximately 69% of the global population — making social media the single most powerful
customer acquisition infrastructure available to businesses of any size. Global social media advertising expenditure reached USD 275.98 billion in 2025 and is
projected to grow at a 13.4% CAGR through 2030, reflecting the accelerating commercial importance of these platforms. This study examines the strategies, tools,
and consumer perceptions surrounding customer acquisition through social media, with particular focus on the 2025–2026 landscape characterised by AI-
powered personalisation, social commerce, short-form video dominance, community-led growth, and the emergence of AI agents as autonomous acquisition tools.
A survey of 72 respondents comprising MBA students, marketing professionals, and entrepreneurs was conducted to assess platform preference and perceived
effectiveness of social media for customer acquisition. Statistical analyses including percentage analysis, chi-square tests, and regression analysis are employed.
The findings reveal Instagram as the most preferred acquisition platform (39%), followed by Facebook (25%) and YouTube (17%). The study further underscores
the growing role of hyper-personalisation, first-party data strategies, and AI-driven content automation in defining best-in-class customer acquisition outcomes in
2025–2026.
Keywords: Customer Acquisition, Social Media Marketing, AI-Powered Advertising, Social Commerce, Influencer Marketing, Short-Form Video,

INTRODUCTION
The year 2025 marks a decisive inflection point in the history of digital marketing. For the first time, total global advertising expenditure crossed USD
1 trillion, with digital channels accounting for 68.7% of that spend. Social media alone contributed USD 275.98 billion — a 13.6% year-on-year
increase — and now accounts for approximately 32.1% of all digital advertising globally. These figures are not merely statistical milestones; they
represent a structural transformation in how businesses acquire customers.
Social media platforms have evolved from communication networks into full-stack customer acquisition ecosystems. The customer journey — from
first exposure to final purchase — is increasingly completed entirely within a single platform. Instagram Shops, TikTok Shop, Facebook Marketplace,
and YouTube Shopping have collapsed the distance between content consumption and commerce. According to We Are Social's Digital 2026 Global
Overview Report, global social commerce sales are projected to reach USD 2.6 trillion in 2026, a figure that underscores the extraordinary commercial
value embedded in social media engagement.
The 2025–2026 period is also defined by the mainstreaming of artificial intelligence in marketing operations. According to HubSpot's 2025 State of
Marketing Report, 94% of marketers plan to use AI in their content creation workflows in 2026, and Meta has announced its objective to fully automate
ad creation and targeting through AI by the end of 2026. Simultaneously, 52% of social users express concern about AI-generated content being posted
without disclosure, creating a critical tension between automation efficiency and consumer trust that marketers must navigate with care.
Against this backdrop, understanding the most effective strategies, platforms, and consumer preferences for social media-driven customer acquisition is
of paramount importance for MBA students, marketing practitioners, and business leaders. This study provides a structured, data-backed analysis of the
customer acquisition landscape on social media platforms as it stands in 2025–2026.

SOCIAL MEDIA PLATFORMS AS CUSTOMER ACQUISITION CHANNELS: 2025–2026 OVERVIEW
The social media landscape in 2025–2026 is characterised by platform maturity, intensified competition for user attention, and the integration of
commerce and AI capabilities. Each major platform offers distinct acquisition pathways:
Instagram (2.4 billion MAU as of 2025) remains the dominant platform for B2C discovery and purchase, particularly among millennials and Gen Z. Its
Reels format generates 3.2 times more interactions on mobile than desktop ads, and Instagram Shopping enables frictionless in-app purchases.
Facebook (3.22 billion MAU) continues to lead in precise demographic targeting through Meta's Advantage+ AI system, which delivers 17% lower
cost-per-acquisition compared to manual campaign management. YouTube (2.54 billion advertising reach) has emerged as the primary long-form video
acquisition channel, with YouTube ad revenue reaching USD 36 billion in 2025. TikTok is the fastest-growing platform, adding users at 17.6% year-
on-year — more than three times Facebook's growth rate — with TikTok ad revenue forecast to reach USD 44 billion by 2026. LinkedIn remains the
premier B2B acquisition platform, with ad revenue exceeding USD 4.7 billion per quarter in North America alone.
KEY STRATEGIES FOR CUSTOMER ACQUISITION ON SOCIAL MEDIA IN 2025–2026
1. AI-Powered Paid Social Advertising
Paid advertising has become increasingly automated and intelligent in 2025–2026. Meta's Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns use machine learning to
automatically optimise creative, audience, and placement decisions, achieving 17% lower cost-per-acquisition than manually managed campaigns.
TikTok's Smart Performance Campaigns and Google's Performance Max similarly deploy AI to maximise acquisition ROI. Mobile now accounts for
93.2% of all social media ad clicks globally, making mobile-first creative a non-negotiable standard. The global CPM benchmark has risen to USD 9.32,
reflecting increased competition for premium placements, while AI-driven creative assistants enable real-time, performance-based content optimisation.
2. Short-Form and Long-Form Video Content Strategy
Video content has become the universal acquisition currency across all platforms. Instagram Reels, TikTok videos, and YouTube Shorts dominate the
discovery phase of the customer journey, while long-form YouTube content serves as a trust-building and conversion tool for consumers conducting
deep product research. Video ads improve brand recall by 80% compared to static ads, and 90% of marketers report that video ads increase conversions.
According to National University's 2026 Social Media Trends analysis, brands that deploy a tiered video strategy — short-form for awareness, mid-
form for consideration, and long-form for conversion — achieve significantly higher full-funnel acquisition efficiency. Pinterest has also expanded its
AI-powered shopping features, boosting conversion rates by 30%.
3. Micro-Influencer and Creator Economy Partnerships
Influencer marketing has institutionalised as a core acquisition channel, with the global market projected to be worth USD 24 billion in 2025. The
creator economy has matured, with brands increasingly preferring micro-influencers (10,000–100,000 followers) and nano-influencers for their higher
engagement rates and authentic audience relationships. Sprout Social's Content Benchmark Report (2025) found that 48% of consumers want to see
front-line employees in brand content, and 42% prefer real customers — signalling a broader shift toward authenticity-first acquisition content. Brands
are also building long-term creator ambassador programmes rather than one-off sponsored posts, improving brand consistency and audience trust.
4. Social Commerce and Frictionless In-App Shopping
Social commerce has transitioned from an emerging trend to a mainstream acquisition mechanism. TikTok Shop's Shopify integration enables in-app
checkout for merchants, while Meta's WhatsApp Business now supports shoppable status ads in the Updates tab. As of 2025, 59% of consumers prefer
shopping online versus in-store, and the in-app social commerce experience is progressively eliminating the need to visit external websites. Global
social commerce revenue is forecast to reach USD 2.6 trillion in 2026. Frictionless checkout, personalised product feeds, and AI-powered product
recommendations are the primary conversion drivers within social commerce ecosystems.
5. AI Agents and Automated Community Management
The most significant emerging trend in customer acquisition for 2026 is the deployment of AI agents — autonomous tools capable of executing entire
social media workflows including content publishing, audience engagement, DM-based lead qualification, and customer handoff. Sprinklr's 2026 Social
Media Trends Report highlights that hybrid human-AI workflows across Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, TikTok, and Messenger allow brands to respond
instantly, personalise recommendations, and escalate complex issues without increasing headcount. Approximately 65% of consumers are comfortable
with brands using AI for faster customer service on social media. Community management — including broadcast channels, exclusive member groups,
and surprise-and-delight engagement — is identified by Sprout Social's Q4 2025 Pulse Survey as a top priority for Gen Z engagement in 2026.
6. Social SEO and Platform-Native Search
A paradigm shift in search behaviour is reshaping customer acquisition strategy. As of 2025, 41% of Gen Z consumers go to social media first when
they need information — bypassing traditional search engines entirely. Google's AI Overviews have correlated with a 58% drop in click-through rates
for top organic search results, accelerating the migration of discovery to social platforms. Brands that optimise their social profiles, hashtags, captions,
and content for platform-native search on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube are gaining significant organic acquisition advantages. Social SEO is now a
distinct strategic competency within digital marketing teams.

REVIEW OF LITERATURE
✓ Chaffey (2022) argues that the convergence of first-party data, artificial intelligence, and social media has ushered in a precision marketing
era where customer acquisition strategies are continuously optimised based on real-time signals. He emphasises the growing urgency of
first-party data strategies as third-party cookie deprecation reshapes the digital advertising landscape across all major platforms.
✓ Dwivedi et al. (2023) conducted a comprehensive global meta-analysis on digital marketing effectiveness and identified social media
platforms as responsible for 34% of all new customer acquisitions across industries in 2022, a figure that has grown substantially through
2024–2025. Their research identifies content consistency, engagement responsiveness, and platform-specific customisation as the three most
critical factors driving acquisition success.
✓ Sprout Social's 2025 Index Report highlights that two of the most differentiated brand behaviours on social media are engagement quality
and response speed. Approximately 75% of social users expect a brand to reply within 24 hours, and users who receive no response are
highly likely to switch to a competitor — making community management a direct acquisition retention tool in 2025–2026.
✓ Hootsuite's Social Media Trends 2026 Report identifies deep audience intelligence as the defining competitive advantage in social media
marketing. The report argues that brands which invest in social listening, trend prediction, and audience analysis tools will consistently
outperform competitors in customer acquisition efficiency, particularly as algorithmic content rewards cultural relevance and speed of
response.
✓ We Are Social and Meltwater's Digital 2026 Global Overview Report documents that global social media advertising spend reached USD
277 billion in 2025 — a 13.6% year-on-year increase — and that 76% of all consumers globally say that social media content influenced a
recent purchase decision, confirming social media's central role in the modern customer acquisition funnel.
OBJECTIVES OF THE STUDY
1. To examine the evolving role of social media platforms in customer acquisition, with specific reference to the 2025–2026 trends of AI
automation, social commerce, and short-form video.
2. To identify the most preferred and effective social media platforms for customer acquisition from the consumer's perspective in the current
digital environment.
3. To analyse the key factors influencing consumer engagement, trust, and conversion behaviour on social media platforms in 2025–2026.
4. To explore the challenges — including AI transparency concerns, data privacy, rising CAC, and platform algorithm dependence — faced by
businesses in acquiring customers through social media.
NEEDS OF THE STUDY
✓ With 5.66 billion social media user identities globally and consumers spending an average of 18 hours and 36 minutes per week on social
platforms, customer acquisition through social media has become a strategic imperative for all categories of business.
✓ The rapid advancement of AI tools, social commerce, and creator economy dynamics in 2025–2026 demands an updated analytical
framework to help businesses, students, and practitioners understand and deploy the most current and effective acquisition strategies.
✓ Despite exponential growth in social media ad spend, many organisations continue to face challenges in demonstrating measurable ROI
from social media acquisition activities, necessitating structured research to bridge the gap between strategy and measurable outcomes.
SCOPE OF THE STUDY
This study encompasses the analysis of the major social media platforms — Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn, and TikTok — as customer
acquisition channels in the 2025–2026 context. The scope includes both organic and paid acquisition strategies, with particular attention to the
emerging forces of AI-driven advertising, social commerce, short-form video content, micro-influencer partnerships, community management, and
social SEO. The study draws on primary survey data from 72 respondents as well as current secondary data sourced from industry reports published by
Sprout Social, Hootsuite, We Are Social, HubSpot, Meta, Statista, and DataReportal. The findings are directly relevant to MBA students, digital
marketing professionals, entrepreneurs, and business strategists seeking to build or optimise their social media customer acquisition capabilities in the
current landscape.

CONCLUSION
The 2025–2026 period represents a transformative era for customer acquisition on social media. With 5.66 billion global users, USD 275.98 billion in
advertising spend in 2025, and social commerce projected to reach USD 2.6 trillion in 2026, social media platforms have become the most
consequential and measurable customer acquisition infrastructure in the history of marketing.
This study demonstrates that Instagram remains the most preferred platform for customer acquisition (39%), with Facebook and YouTube following
closely, while TikTok represents the highest-growth opportunity for the near-term future. The statistical analyses confirm that platform preference is
aligned with perceived acquisition effectiveness (chi-square test) and that higher social media usage frequency is a strong positive predictor of social
media acquisition preference (regression analysis).
The defining trends of 2025–2026 — AI-powered advertising automation, short-form video dominance, social commerce integration, micro-influencer
partnerships, community-led growth, and the emergence of social SEO — collectively demand that businesses adopt a more sophisticated, data-driven,
and platform-native approach to customer acquisition. Businesses that embrace transparency, authenticity, and AI-augmented marketing strategies will
be best positioned to acquire customers cost-effectively and build lasting competitive advantage in the digital economy of 2026 and beyond.
