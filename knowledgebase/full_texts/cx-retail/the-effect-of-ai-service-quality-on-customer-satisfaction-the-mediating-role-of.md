ABSTRACT: The rapid adoption of artificial intelligence (AI) in Indonesia's e-commerce sector has made AI service quality a critical
determinant of customer experience, yet the mechanisms through which it influences customer satisfaction remain
underexplored. This s tudy examines the effect of AI Service Quality (AISQ) on Customer Satisfaction (CS) and investigates the
mediating role of Perceived Responsiveness (PR). Grounded in the SERVQUAL framework and Expectation -Confirmation Theory,
a mediation model was tested u sing survey data from 211 active e -commerce users and analyzed through Structural Equation
Modeling (SEM) with 5,000 bootstrap replications. The findings reveal that AISQ exerts a significant positive effect on both PR and
CS, while PR also positively infl uences CS. However, the indirect effect of AISQ on CS through PR is not statistically significant,
indicating the absence of a mediating effect. The direct effect of AISQ on CS remains dominant, suggesting that customer
satisfaction is shaped primarily by the overall quality of AI -enabled services rather than by responsiveness as an intermediary
mechanism. These findings contribute to the emerging literature on AI-driven service quality by demonstrating that AISQ functions
principally as a direct driver of customer satisfaction and provide practical guidance for e-commerce platforms seeking to enhance
customer outcomes through investments in comprehensive AI service capabilities.
KEYWORDS: AI Service Quality; Customer Satisfaction; Perceived Responsiveness; E -commerce; Indonesia

I. INTRODUCTION
A. Research Background
Indonesia is one of Southeast Asia's fastest-growing digital markets. The rapid expansion of internet access, smartphone adoption,
and shifting consumer behavior has driven significant growth in the e -commerce sector, with major platforms such as Shopee,
Tokopedia, Blibli and Lazada competing intensely for market share (Nuralam et al., 2024). In this environment, delivering best
service quality is no longer optional but, it has become a core or main competitive strategy.
To meet rising service demands, e-commerce companies have increasingly integrated artificial intelligence (AI) into their customer
service operations. AI -powered tools such as chatbots enable platforms to handle large volumes of customer interactions
efficiently, delivering real-time, automated responses at scale. Studies by Singh and Singh in 2024 confirm that AI service quality
has a significant and positive effect on customer satisfaction, establishing it as a key determinant of positive customer out comes
(Singh & Singh, 2024).
However, the direct relationship between AI service quality and customer satisfaction does not fully explain how customers fo rm
their satisfaction judgments. Customers do not simply evaluate AI based on its technical capability. Now, they assess how wel l it
responds to their needs. Perceived responsiveness, defined as a customer's perception of how promptly and adequately their
concerns are addressed, plays an important role in shaping this evaluation. Prior research on digital services confirms that
responsiveness-related service quality is a strong predictor of customer satisfaction (Yum & Yoo, 2023), and that chatbot response
quality directly influences how customers engage with and assess a platform (Meier et al., 2024).
Despite this, the mediating role of perceived responsiveness in the relationship between AI service quality and customer
satisfaction still remains underexplored, particularly within Indonesia's e -commerce sector. This study addresses that gap by
examining whether perceived responsiveness serves as a mechanism through which AI service quality influences customer
satisfaction.

B. Research Questions
Based on the research background above, the research questions in this study are as follows:
1. Does AI Service Quality significantly affect Perceived Responsiveness in Indonesia's e -commerce sector?
2. Does Perceived Responsiveness and AI Service Quality individually significantly affect Customer Satisfaction in Indonesia' s e-
commerce sector?
3. Does Perceived Responsiveness mediate the relationship between AI Service Quality and Customer Satisfaction in Indonesia's
e-commerce sector?
C. Research Objectives
This study aims to examine the effect of AI Service Quality on Customer Satisfaction in Indonesia's e -commerce sector and to
analyze the mediating role of Perceived Responsiveness in the relationship between AI Service Quality and Customer Satisfaction.

II. LITERATURE REVIEW
A. Theoretical Background
a. AI Service Quality
Service quality has long been grounded in the SERVQUAL framework (Parasuraman et al., 1988), which defines it through five
dimensions: tangibles, reliability, responsiveness, assurance, and empathy. With the rise of digital service delivery, this framework
has been extended to AI-based contexts, giving rise to the concept of AI Service Quality (AISQ).
AISQ refers to the customer's overall evaluation of the performance of AI -driven service systems, including accuracy, reliability,
personalization, and interaction efficiency (Shahzad et al., 2024; Megdadi et al., 2025). In e -commerce, AISQ is most commonl y
experienced through AI chatbots and virtual assistants that handle inquiries, recommend products, and resolve issues in real time
(Chen et al., 2022; Chen et al., 2025).
Empirical research consistently shows that high -quality AI services shape how customers perceive a platform's attentiveness.
When AI systems respond accurately and consistently, customers perceive the platform as more responsive to their needs
(Megdadi et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2022). Therefore:
H1: AI Service Quality has a significant positive effect on Perceived Responsiveness in Indonesia's e -commerce sector.
AISQ has also been shown to directly enhance customer satisfaction. Customers who experience reliable, personalized, and
efficient AI interactions report greater overall satisfaction with the platform (Shahzad et al., 2024; Singh & Singh, 2024; C hen et
al., 2025). Thus:
H3: AI Service Quality has a significant positive direct effect on Customer Satisfaction in Indonesia's e -commerce sector.
b. Perceived Responsiveness
Perceived responsiveness refers to the customer's subjective evaluation of how promptly, accurately, and adequately a service
provider addresses their needs (Parasuraman et al., 1988). It is one of the five core SERVQUAL dimensions and has been
consistently identified as a critical determinant of customer experience.
In AI-driven e-commerce, perceived responsiveness now encompasses the chatbot's ability to deliver real -time, context-aware,
and emotionally appropriate responses (Chau et al., 2025). Advanced AI technologies powered by natural language processing
and sentiment analysis enable systems to detect user emotions and adapt accordingly, reinforcing perceptions of attentive and
personalized support (Megdadi et al., 2025).
Empirical evidence shows that perceived responsiveness has a strong positive effect on customer satisfaction. When customers
feel that their concerns are addressed quickly and competently, they form more favorable evaluations of the overall service
(Shahzad et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2024). This is particularly important in e -commerce, where customers expect immediate
support during purchase, payment, and post -purchase concerns. Studies further confirm that responsive AI interactions reduce
perceived wait times and increase overall service efficiency, both of which translate into higher satisfaction (Zhu et al., 2023;
Megdadi et al., 2025). Based on these findings, the second hypothesis is proposed:
H2: Perceived Responsiveness has a significant positive effect on Customer Satisfaction in Indonesia's e -commerce sector.
c. Customer Satisfaction
Customer satisfaction is the customer's overall evaluation of a product or service based on the comparison between prior
expectations and actual perceived performance (Oliver, 1980). This is grounded in Expectation -Confirmation Theory (ECT), which
remains the dominant framework for explaining how customers form satisfaction judgments in digital service contexts (Chau et
al., 2025).

In e-commerce, customer satisfaction is shaped by platform usability, service responsiveness, and the quality of customer support
(Akdemir & Bulut, 2024). With AI now central to service delivery, satisfaction increasingly depends on how well AI systems me et
expectations of accuracy, speed, and personalization (Shahzad et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2023).
Beyond the direct effects, prior research suggests that AISQ influences customer satisfaction not only directly but also indi rectly
through perceived responsiveness (Singh & Singh, 2024). When customers perceive an AI system as responsive, this perception
serves as the mechanism that translates technical quality into a favorable satisfaction outcome. Based on this, the fourth
hypothesis is proposed:
H4: Perceived Responsiveness mediates the relationship between AI Service Quality and Customer Satisfaction in Indonesia's
e-commerce sector.

III. METHODOLOGY
A. Research Design
This study adopts a quantitative research design with a causal -explanatory approach to examine the direct and indirect effects
between AI Service Quality, Perceived Responsiveness, and Customer Satisfaction in Indonesia's e -commerce sector. A cross -
sectional survey method is used to collect primary data from active e-commerce users who have interacted with AI-based services
such as chatbots or virtual assistants. The data is analyzed using a mediation regression model to test the four proposed
hypotheses (H1–H4).
B. Data Collection Method
a. Questionnaire
Data collection was conducted using an online self -administered questionnaire distributed via Google Forms. The questionnaire
was provided in both Bahasa Indonesia and English to ensure comprehension and reduce language barriers. Participation was
voluntary and anonymous, with informed consent obtained at the beginning of the survey.
A total of 211 respondents were collected. All variables (AISQ, PR, CS) were measured using a 5-point Likert scale, which is widely
applied in consumer behavior research and provides adequate differentiation between response categories while remaining
simple for respondents. This scale supports parametric statistical analysis. The 5 -point Likert Scale, follow:

C. Mediation Regression Model
This study tests the hypothesized relationships using a mediation model in which AI Service Quality (AISQ) may affect Custome r
Satisfaction (CS) both directly and indirectly through Perceived Responsiveness (PR). Model is specified through two regressi on
equations:
PR = β0 + β1AISQ + ε          (1)
CS = β0 + β2PR + β3AISQ + ε          (2)
Where β1 captures the effect of AISQ on PR, β 2 the effect of PR on CS, and β 3 the direct effect of AISQ on CS while controlling for
PR; ε denotes the error term and β 0 the intercept. The two equations are estimated simultaneously within a structural equation
model using maximum likelihood estimation with 5,000 bootstrap replications. The indirect effect is given by the product β 1β2,
with significance assessed through bootstrapped confidence intervals. Mediation is supported when the bootstrap confidence
interval for the indirect effect does not include zero.

CONCLUSION
This study examined how AI Service Quality affects Customer Satisfaction in Indonesia's e -commerce sector, and whether
Perceived Responsiveness mediates this relationship. Drawing on survey data from 211 respondents analyzed through a
bootstrapped structur al equation model, the findings show that AI Service Quality is a strong and direct driver of Customer
Satisfaction, that it significantly improves Perceived Responsiveness, and that Perceived Responsiveness in turn has a signif icant
positive effect on Cus tomer Satisfaction. However, the indirect path from AI Service Quality to Customer Satisfaction through
Perceived Responsiveness was not significant, indicating that Perceived Responsiveness does not mediate the relationship. The
effect of AI Service Quality on Customer Satisfaction is therefore essentially direct. Although each individual link in the chain holds,
customers in this market appear to respond to the overall quality of AI service as a whole, rather than to responsiveness as a
distinct mediating mechanism.
These findings suggest that e-commerce platforms should treat AI service quality as an integrated capability rather than optimizing
responsiveness in isolation, since overall service quality, not perceived responsiveness, is what directly shapes satisfacti on. For
future research, the non-significant mediation points to the value of testing alternative mechanisms such as trust, perceived value,
or perceived ease of use. The findings should be read in light of two limitations: the purposive sample of 211 resp ondents
constrains generalizability, and the modest reliability of the AI Service Quality and Customer Satisfaction scales may have
attenuated the observed effects. A larger, more representative sample with refined measures would strengthen and extend these
results.
