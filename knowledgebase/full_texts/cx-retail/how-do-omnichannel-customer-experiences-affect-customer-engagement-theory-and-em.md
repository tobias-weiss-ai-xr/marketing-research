ABSTRACT
To clarify the effect of the omnichannel customer experience (OCX) on customer engagement, this article
empirically analyzes how OCX affects direct and indirect customer engagement intentions. Using data from two
surveys (Study 1n = 312; Study 2n = 822) of U.S. omnichannel retail customers, the empirical findings
demonstrate that the positive relationship between OCX and a measure of direct customer engagement (i.e.,
repurchase intentions) depends on the stage of the customer–retailer relationship. Customers’ novelty-seeking
and time-consciousness traits strengthen the positive relationship of OCX with indirect measures of customer
engagement (i.e., influence, feedback, and referral intentions). The findings caution against oversimplistic as-
sumptions about how customer experiences create value in omnichannel retailing. To boost customers’
repurchase intentions, omnichannel retailers should employ different OCX strategies in different relationship
stages.Inaddition,theycanbenefitfromtargetingcustomerswhoexhibithighinherentnoveltyseekingandtime
consciousness.

2.1. Main Relationships: Hypotheses
2.1.1. OCX and customers’ repurchase intentions
As established in both single- and omnichannel retail and services
literature(Lemon &Verhoef,2016),customers’intentionstocontinuea
transactional relationship with a retailer depend on their previous ex-
periences. High-quality retail experiences, which encompass excellent
value, personalized services, and consistent interactions across chan-
nels,stronglyshapecustomers’repurchaseintentions(Kumar &Pansari,
2016). Various studies emphasize that improvements across different
stages of the omnichannel journey, such as fast delivery, accurate
product assortments, and prompt customer support, enhance customer
satisfaction too (Gauri et al., 2021). Positive experiences across di-
mensions imply an appealing OCX, which corresponds with customer
loyaltyandrepurchaseintentionoutcomes(Rahman et al.,2022b).That
is, customers who experience high levels of OCX likely display strong
repurchase intentions. Formally:
H1: Elevated OCX levels are associated with greater customer
repurchase intentions.
2.1.2. OCX and customers’ influence intentions
Single-channel retail studies establish links between customers’ ex-
perienceswitha retailerandtheir intentionstorecommendtheretailer
to others (Lemon & Verhoef, 2016). Such recommendations often take
placethroughsocialinteractionsonpublicplatforms(e.g.,socialmedia,
blogs) or retailer-specific channels (e.g., retailer’s website). When the
retailer encourages honest reviews and interactions among its cus-
tomers, thosecustomersgainvaluableinformationthatinfluencestheir
purchasedecisions(Broniarczyk &Griffin,2014).Forexample,positive
reviews can enhance their purchase confidence, and negative reviews
help customers avoid unsatisfactory purchases. Due to reciprocity ef-
fects, customers who benefit from an open and transparent retailer
platform may feel compelled to share their own positive experiences
through these same channels (Animesh et al., 2011). Although trans-
parencydoesnotguaranteepositiveinfluenceintentions,weexpectthat
a favorable OCX is associated with increased influence intentions.
Formally:
H2: Elevated OCX levels are associated with greater customer in-
fluence intentions.
2.1.3. OCX and customers’ feedback intentions
Feedback from customers provides retailers with valuable insights
into areas for improvement and innovation (Kumar et al., 2010). Posi-
tive experiences across omnichannel environments, such as seamless
shopping, personalized service, and convenient returns, contribute to
thelikelihoodthatcustomersofferhelpfulfeedbacktoretailers(Karpen
etal.,2015).Whencustomersperceivethattheretailerisactingintheir
best interest and providing superior experiences, they also are more
likely to share constructive opinions (Bolton et al., 2018; Lemon &
Verhoef,2016).Inomnichannelcontexts,apositiveOCXstemsfromthe
overallbenefitscustomersgainfrominteractingacrosschannels,which
they might reciprocate by providing feedback (Animesh et al., 2011).
Formally:
H3: Elevated OCX levels are associated with greater customer feed-
back intentions.

2.2.1. Relationship stage and repurchase intentions

continue purchasing from and gathering more information about the
retailer (Palmatier et al., 2013). A mature relationship no longer in-

informationonwhichtobasetheirattitudestowardtheretailer,sothey
likely continue to engage regularly with it, as long as the retailer con-
