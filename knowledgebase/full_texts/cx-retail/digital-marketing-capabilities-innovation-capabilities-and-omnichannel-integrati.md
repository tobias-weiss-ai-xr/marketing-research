Abstract
Digital transformation has fundamentally changed the marketing paradigm, moving
organizations beyond simply delivering information toward technology-driven ecosystems that
emphasize data, innovation, channel integration, and customer participation as impor tant
sources of value. This transformation requires organizations to develop digital capabilities that
effectively connect technological resources with evolving customer needs. This study aims to
synthesize existing literature on the relationships among Di gital Marketing Capability,
Innovation Capability, Omnichannel Integration Quality, Customer Value Co -Creation
Behavior, and Digital Marketing Performance, while proposing a conceptual framework that
explains how these constructs are interconnected. A Syst ematic Literature Review was
conducted through several stages, including literature identification, selection, quality
assessment, data extraction, and synthesis of findings from previous studies. Relevant literature
was obtained from academic databases an d search engines using combinations of keywords
associated with Digital Marketing Capability, Innovation Capability, omnichannel integration,
Customer Value Co -Creation, and marketing performance. The review primarily considered
scholarly journal articles published from 2020 to 2026, complemented by seminal studies to
establish construct definitions and theoretical foundations. The findings indicate that Digital
Marketing Capability enables organizations to effectively combine technology, data, and
marketing activities, while Innovation Capability supports the continuous development of
services and processes. Meanwhile, Omnichannel Integration Quality contributes to a
consistent and seamless customer experience. Together, these capabilities can encourage
Customer Value Co -Creation Behavior, which functions as a key mechanism for improving
Digital Marketing Performance. Grounded in Dynamic Capability Theory, the study proposes
an integrative conceptual model and highlights future research opportunities, partic ularly in
digital public services and utility platforms such as New PLN Mobile.

INTRODUCTION
Digital transformation has reshaped how organizations create, deliver, and capture
value. Digital technology has now become a strategic capability for gaining customer insights,
fostering innovation, integrating service channels, and building stronger inte ractions. In this
context, Digital Marketing Capability (DMC) is understood as the ability to integrate
technology, data, analytics, media, and digital platforms.
Homburg and Wielgos (2022) demonstrate that digital transformation drives companies
to develop new digital marketing capabilities. Based on cross -industry research, DMC
contributes significantly to profitability beyond traditional marketing capabilities.
Apasrawirote et al. (2022), through a review of 143 articles from 57 journals, confirm that the
DMC concept still requires further development, particularly regarding its dimensions and
organizational outcomes.
Another relevant capability is innovation capability —an organization's ability to
generate and implement innovations across products, services, processes, technologies, and
customer experiences. Hiong et al. (2020) found that "Techno -Resonance Innovation
Capability" plays a crucial role in enhancing the marketing performance of MSMEs in West
Kalimantan. These findings underscore the necessity of communicating innovations so that
their value can be perceived by the market.
Digitalization drives customers to interact via apps, websites, social media, contact
centers, and physical channels. Consequently, organizations require Omnichannel Integration
Quality (OCIQ) to ensure consistency across information, transactions, processes, and customer
experiences. Gao and Huang (2021) demonstrate that OCIQ positively influences customer
engagement and receptiveness to relationship programs, which in turn fosters customer loyalty.
The shift in marketing paradigms positions the customer as an actor in value creation.
The concept of Customer Value Co-Creation Behavior (CVCB) was developed by Yi and Gong
(2013) through two dimensions: customer participation behavior and customer citize nship
behavior. These behaviors encompass information seeking, information sharing, responsible
behavior, personal interaction, feedback, advocacy, helping, and tolerance, all of which can be
manifested through digital interactions.
An academic issue arises because DMC, Innovation Capability, OCIQ, and CVCB are
frequently examined in isolation, even though organizational capabilities do not necessarily
translate directly into Digital Marketing Performance. These capabilities can be co nverted
through value co -creation with customers. Therefore, this study employs a Systematic
Literature Review to construct a model: Organizational Digital Capabilities → Customer Value
Co-Creation → Digital Marketing Performance within the context of New PLN Mobile.
Dynamic Capability Theory serves as the primary theoretical lens for this study. This
theory explains an organization's ability to integrate, build, and reconfigure internal and
external resources to adapt to a changing environment.
In the digital context, technological and customer behavior changes occur rapidly.
Therefore, organizational excellence is determined not merely by the technological resources
possessed, but by the organization's ability to transform those resources into r elevant
capabilities.
In this research model, DMC, Innovation Capability, and OCIQ are positioned as
capabilities that enable organizations to engage in sensing, seizing, and
transforming/reconfiguring. These three capabilities create conditions that allow customers to
participate in the value co -creation process. The research's theoretical framework can be
formulated as follows: Dynamic Capability → Digital Organizational Capabilities → Customer
Value Co-Creation → Digital Marketing Performance.

RESEARCH METHODS
The study employs the Systematic Literature Review (SLR) method. SLR was selected
because the research does not aim to collect primary data but rather to identify patterns,
relationships, gaps, and theoretical developments in existing research. The review process
comprises five stages: 1) literature identification, 2) screening, 3) eligibility evaluation, 4)
quality assessment, and 5) thematic synthesis.

RESULT AND DISCUSSION
A. Evolution of Digital Marketing Capability
A synthesis of the literature indicates that the concept of Digital Marketing Capability
(DMC) has shifted from the mere use of marketing technologies toward a strategic capability.
Homburg and Wielgos found that DMC contributes to firm profitability even after accounting
for traditional marketing capabilities. This finding carries significant theoretical implications:
DMC is not merely an operational capability but can function as a strategic organizational
capability. In the context of this study, DMC is understood through four specific capabilities:
1) technology utilization, 2) customer data utilization, 3) digital interaction capability, 4) digital
marketing integration. Therefore: Proposition 1: The higher the Digital Marketing Capability,
the greater the organization's ability to generate digital marketing performance.

B. Innovation Capability and Marketing Performance
Hiong et al. demonstrated that "techno-resonance" innovation capability plays a crucial
role in enhancing marketing performance. However, the study also indicated that innovation
alone is insufficient if the value of the innovation fails to be accepted by and resonate with the
market. Heng et al. further showed that service innovation capability can serve as a strategic
bridge between customer orientation and marketing performance. These findings offer a vital
insight:
Innovation → Value → Performance , rather than merely: Innovation → Performance.
In other words, innovation must generate value that is understood and utilized by customers.
Proposition 2: Innovation capability enhances digital marketing performance when the
innovation is capable of creating and communicating value to customers.

C. Omnichannel Integration Quality and Customer Engagement
Gao and Huang found that OCIQ positively influences customer engagement and
receptiveness to relationship programs, subsequently contributing to customer loyalty. Gao et
al. also demonstrated that channel integration can enhance the customer experience. Th us,
OCIQ is not merely a matter of back-end technology.
OCIQ creates a progression of: Consistency → Convenience → Seamlessness →
Engagement. In the context of the New PLN Mobile, this integration ensures that customers do
not lose information when transitioning between the app, website, contact center, social media,
and physical service points. Proposition 3: Omnichannel Integration Quality enhances customer
value co-creation behavior by creating a seamless and consistent customer experience.

D. Customer Value Co-Creation Behavior as a Mechanism
CVCB is the most important element in the conceptual model. Yi and Gong
demonstrated that CVCB comprises eight forms of behavior categorized into two main

E. CVCB and Digital Marketing Performance
Value co-creation yields benefits that extend beyond the customer. Active customers:
1) provide information; 2) offer feedback; 3) make recommendations; 4) assist other customers;
and 5) interact with the organization, thereby generating customer -generated marketing
resources. Consequently, customers have the potential to act as users, evaluators, innovators,
advocates, and information providers. Thus, Proposition 5 states: Customer Value Co-Creation
Behavior has a positive effect on digital marketing performance.

F. Integrative Relationships Among the Five Constructs
The synthesis reveals that the relationships among the five constructs should not be
viewed merely as a collection of independent, linear connections. Instead, they form a
capability–engagement–performance mechanism:

Stage 1 is Capability, representing organizational development. It consists of: 1) Digital
Marketing Capability, 2) Innovation Capability, and 3) Omnichannel Integration Quality. Stage
2 is Customer Activation, where in these capabilities create the conditions for: 1) engagement,
2) interaction, 3) feedback, 4) participation, 5) advocacy, and 6) helping. Stage 3 is Value
Creation, characterized by Customer Value Co-Creation; and Stage 4 is Performance, resulting
in: 1) engagement, 2) satisfaction, 3) retent ion, 4) acquisition, 5) conversion, and 6) digital
marketing effectiveness.

H. Research Gap
Based on a synthesis of the literature, four research gaps have been identified. Gap 1 —
Conceptual fragmentation  DMC, Innovation Capability, OCIQ, and CVCB have evolved
within distinct streams of research. Gap 2 - Outcome fragmentation DMC is frequently linked
to firm performance, whereas OCIQ is often associated with engagement and loyalty. Homburg
and Wielgos support the DMC–performance relationship, while Gao and Huang support the
OCIQ–engagement–loyalty relationship.  Gap 3 - Missing mechanism , There is limited
synthesis explicitly positioning CVCB as the mechanism that converts capabilities into
performance. Gap 4 - Contextual gap, Most evidence regarding omnichannel approaches stems
from the retail sector, whereas the application of integrative models to digital public services
and utilities remains relatively limited.  This is where New PLN Mobile offers an interesting
research context.

I. Research Novelty
The novelty of this article extends beyond the mere combination of five variables. Its
primary novelty lies in the reconceptualization of Customer Value Co -Creation Behavior as a
transformational mechanism that converts organizational digital capabilities into digital
marketing performance. Consequently, this study offers a) a capability perspective, b) a
customer perspective, c) a value co-creation perspective, and d) a performance perspective, all
integrated within a single model.

J. Theoretical Implications
First, this study extends Dynamic Capability Theory by incorporating customer
behavior as a crucial element in the process of converting organizational capabilities into
performance. Second, the study bridges the dynamic capabilities perspective with custo mer
value co-creation. Third, the study shifts the understanding of performance from the sequence
of "technology → performance" to "capability → customer activation → value co -creation →
performance." Fourth, the study expands the scope of omnichannel rese arch from the retail
context to potential applications in digital public services.

K. Managerial Implications
1. Strengthening Digital Marketing Capability: PT PLN needs to strengthen:
a. customer analytics;
b. digital CRM;
c. personalization;
d. digital communication;
e. real-time customer insight.
2. Strengthening Innovation Capability
Innovation for the New PLN Mobile app should be based on the following cycle:
customer feedback → innovation → testing → implementation → evaluation.
3. Strengthening Omnichannel Capabilities
Integration must encompass: New PLN Mobile ↔ Website ↔ Social Media ↔ Contact
Center ↔ Physical Service Points.
4. Customers as Co-Creators
Customers should not be viewed merely as users; they should be positioned as: sources
of information → sources of innovation → evaluators → promoters.
5. Measuring Digital Marketing Performance
Performance assessment should not rely solely on transaction volume. A measurement
model comprising Reach → Acquisition → Engagement → Satisfaction → Retention
→ Conversion → Overall Effectiveness better reflects the entire customer journey.

CONCLUSIONS
This systematic literature review demonstrates that digital transformation shifts
marketing from a mere information -dissemination activity into a value -creation process
involving the organization, technology, and customers. In this context, Digital Marketi ng
Capability, Innovation Capability, and Omnichannel Integration Quality can be viewed as
strategic capabilities that enable organizations to respond to changes in the digital environment.
Digital Marketing Capability (DMC) enables organizations to integrate technology, data,
customer insights, and marketing activities.
Innovation Capability allows organizations to innovate products, services, processes, and
experiences. Meanwhile, Omnichannel Integration Quality (OCIQ) enables organizations to
provide consistent and seamless experiences across various channels.  However, these three
capabilities should not be viewed as factors that automatically yield performance outcomes. A
synthesis of the literature reveals that Customer Value Co -Creation Behavior plays a strategic
role as a mechanism linking organizational capabilities to value creation and digital marketing
performance.
Consequently, the model proposed in this study posits the following pathway: Digital
Marketing Capability + Innovation Capability + Omnichannel Integration Quality → Customer
Value Co -Creation Behavior → Digital Marketing Performance.  This model makes a
theoretical contribution by integrating Dynamic Capability Theory with the customer value co-
creation perspective. In the context of New PLN Mobile, the implication is that the success of
digital transformation cannot be measured solely by the existence of an application or the
volume of digital transactions; rather, it depends on the organization's ability to integrate digital
capabilities, innovation, service channels, and customer participation to achieve sustainable
digital marketing performance.
