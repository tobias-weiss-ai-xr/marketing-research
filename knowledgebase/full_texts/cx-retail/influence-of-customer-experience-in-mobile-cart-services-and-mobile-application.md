ABSTRACT

This study aims to analyze the effect of customer experience on mobile cart services and
application integration on customer loyalty of Kopi Jago in Jakarta. Using  a quantitative
causal approach, data were gathered via digital questionnaires distributed to consumers.
Out of 120 respondents collected, a purposive screening identified 53 clean respondents
who actively use both the mobile cart and the application. Data analysis was conducted
using Variance -Based Structural Equation Modeling (PLS -SEM) via SmartPLS. The
descriptive results show high satisfaction  for the physical mobile cart experience, while
application utilization is highly rated but faces an adoption gap among manual buyers.
Furthermore, price sensitivity remains a critical constraint on long -term loyalty, as scores
drastically dropped regarding potential price increases. This study provides managerially
critical insights into balancing physical retail mobility with digital application ecosystem
optimization within an omnichannel strategy in the modern coffee cart industry.

Keywords: Customer Experience, Mobile  Cart, Application Integration, Customer
Loyalty, Omnichannel

INTRODUCTION

The coffee shop industry is currently facing increasingly intense competition,
requiring businesses to differentiate themselves through innovative business models.
Research by Clara and Angelita (2025) highlights that offering high -quality coffee and a
standard value proposition is no longer sufficient to maintain customer interest. Instead,
businesses need to stand out by adopting innovative business models, strengthening digital
capabilities, and enhancing Customer Experience. One of the  modern mobile retail
innovations in Indonesia is the adoption of an omnichannel service model, as implemented
by Kopi Jago through  the integration of physical Mobile Cart Services and a  mobile
application. This omnichannel approach is essential for meeting the needs of today's
consumers, who increasingly rely on digital technology. Hidayati and Rahmawati (2023)
explain that Customer Experience plays a crucial role in shaping omnichannel consumer
behavior by creating an integrated channel management system that delivers a seamless
and consistent experience across all customer touchpoints. Despite providing greater
convenience, Kopi Jago still faces several challenges, including the technology adoption
gap among customers who are accustomed to purchasing directly from mobile carts and
the high level of price sensitivity. Therefore, this study aims to examine the causal effects

THEORETICAL STUDIES

Customer Experience (X1)
Customer Experience refers to consumers' direct perceptions of service quality and
the physical condition of the mobile cart, which are considered key factors for success in
the highly competitive coffee shop industry. In the context  of mobile coffee retail,
Customer Experience is shaped through direct interactions, including the comfort of the
Mobile Cart Services and the friendliness of baristas during service delivery. Previous
studies in similar industries have shown that superior product quality and excellent
physical service make a significant contribution to building positive customer perceptions.

Application Integration (X2)
In the digital era, physical service channels need to be supported by reliable
technology. Mobile Application Integration in this study refers to the ease of use, system
accuracy, and smooth ordering process provided through a digital application that is
directly connected to field operations. The reliability of this system integration has become
an important factor for modern consumers in evaluating service efficiency and the practical
value offered by a mobile retail brand.

Customer Loyalty (Y)
Customer Loyalty reflects customers' long -term commitment to repeatedly
purchasing a brand's products and recommending them to others. In the context of digitally
supported services, genuine loyalty cannot be achieved solely through short -term
promotional incentives, such as low prices. Instead, strong customer loyalty is built on trust
and long-term relationships  between customers  and service providers. Therefore, the
effective integration of high -quality Customer Experience (X1) and reliable Mobile
Application Integration (X2) provides the theoretical foundation for examining their
influence on Customer Loyalty among Kopi Jago customers.

RESEARCH METHODS

Research Design
This study employed a quantitative causal -associative approach to examine the
effects of Customer Experience (X1) and Mobile Application Integration (X2) on
Customer Loyalty (Y) among Kopi Jago customers in Jakarta. Customer Experience (X1)
measures customers' perceptions of the comfort of the Mobile Cart Services and the quality
of barista service. Mobile Application Integration (X2) assesses the reliability and
functionality of the digital application, while Customer Loyalty (Y) measures customers'
commitment to repeat purchases. All research indicators (X1.1 –X1.5, X2.1–X2.5, and Y1–
Y5) were measured using a five-point Likert scale.

Population, Sample, and Data Collection
The population of this study consisted of all Kopi Jago customers in  Jakarta, whose
total number could not be accurately identified ( unidentified population ). Following the
10-Times Rule, the minimum required sample size was 50 respondents. The study
employed a non -probability sampling technique using a purposive sampling approach.
Data were collected through a digital questionnaire distributed via Google Forms. To
ensure the suitability of the respondents, a screening process was conducted, requiring
participants to have experience using both the Mobile Cart Services and the Kopi Jago
mobile application. Of the 120 responses collected, 67 respondents (55.8%) were excluded
because they did not use the mobile application, resulting in a final sample of 53 valid
respondents.

Tdata Analysis
The data were analyzed using the Variance -Based Structural Equation Modeling
(PLS-SEM) approach with SmartPLS 4 software. The analysis consisted of two stages:
1. Outer Model Evaluation, which assessed convergent validity through  Outer Loadings
(> 0.70) and Average Variance Extracted (AVE) (> 0.50), discriminant validity using
the Fornell–Larcker Criterion, and construct reliability based on Cronbach's Alpha and
Composite Reliability values greater than 0.70.
2. Inner Model Evaluation, which examined the model's explanatory power using the
Coefficient of Determination (R²) and tested the significance of the structural
relationships through Path Coefficients using the bootstrapping procedure. The hypotheses were
considered statistically significant  when the t-statistics exceeded 1.96 and the p-value was
below 0.05 at the 5% significance level.

The Influence of Customer Experience on Customer Loyalty
The rejection of the first hypothesis (H1) indicates that the positive physical
experiences provided through Kopi Jago's Mobile Cart Services have not been sufficient to
build long -term customer loyalty. Although customers appreciate the friendliness of the
baristas and the attractive appearance of the mobile carts, these experiences appear to be
perceived mainly as part of the daily transaction process rather than as factors that
encourage long-term emotional commitment or consistent repeat purchases.

The Influence of Application Integration on Customer Loyalty
Similarly, the rejection of the second hypothesis (H2) suggests that the reliability  of
the mobile application, including its  connectivity, user -friendly menu features, and
integrated digital payment system, is primarily perceived as a functional convenience
rather than a source of customer attachment. While these technological features improve
the purchasing experience, they have not yet created the psychological connection
necessary to strengthen long-term customer loyalty.

Analisis Komprehensif Loyalitas dan Faktor Pasar
From a broader perspective, the inability of both variables to significantly influence
Customer Loyalty appears to be closely related to customers' high price sensitivity. The
descriptive analysis revealed particularly low scores for the  loyalty indicators measuring
customers' willingness to pay higher prices. This finding suggests that customer loyalty is
driven mainly by the affordability of the products rather than by service experience or
digital features. When customers perceive a price increase, neither the quality of the
Mobile Cart Services nor the reliability  of the mobile application is sufficient to prevent
them from switching to competing brands. Furthermore, the  technology adoption gap
identified during the screening  process, in which 55.8%  of respondents were  excluded
because they did not use the mobile application, indicates that a large proportion of Kopi
Jago's target market still prefers direct, in -person purchases from mobile carts. This
presents a significant challenge for Kopi Jago in implementing its omnichannel retail
strategy.

CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

This study concludes that the combination of Mobile Cart Services and Mobile
Application Integration does not have a significant effect on Customer Loyalty among
Kopi Jago customers in Jakarta. The R² value of only 4.6% indicates that customer loyalty
is largely influenced by factors outside the proposed research model. The physical
appearance of the mobile carts and the features provided by the mobile application
function mainly as tools that facilitate daily transactions rather than as drivers of long -term
emotional attachment. These findings are closely associated with two major challenges:
customers' high price sensitivity  and the technology adoption gap, as most  customers still
prefer making direct purchases from mobile carts instead of using the digital application.

Based on these findings, Kopi Jago is encouraged to reconsider its strategic priorities
by focusing more on improving the operational efficiency of its Mobile Cart Services to
maintain competitive pricing, rather than investing heavily in increasingly complex
application features. The mobile application could be optimized  as a platform for location-
based promotional campaigns (localized flash promotions ) to encourage impulse
purchases, supported by promotional incentives delivered by baristas ( Jagoan) to reduce
the technology adoption gap among customers who prefer conventional purchasing
methods. Future studies are recommended to incorporate additional variables that may
better explain  Customer Loyalty , such as price perception, brand image, and product
quality, while also employing larger sample sizes to improve the generalizability of
findings within the omnichannel mobile retail industry.
