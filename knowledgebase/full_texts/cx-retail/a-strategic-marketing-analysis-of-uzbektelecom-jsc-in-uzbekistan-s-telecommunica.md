Abstract
The article examined Uzbektelecom JSC’s marketing strategy in Uzbekistan’s
telecommunications market and opportunities to improve customer experience. A
qualitative case study used secondary sources, customer journey mapping, SWOT
analysis, competitor comp arison, marketing mix analysis, and qualitative content
analysis. Infrastructure, service diversity, and brand recognition were described as
competitive advantages. Selection, purchase, and service usage stages were compared
for customers with different le vels of awareness. Opportunities were identified to
improve information provision, technical support, and digital communication.
Recommendations addressed youth promotion, loyalty programmes, and integrated
marketing communications. These recommendations a imed to strengthen customer
relationships, improve service processes, and enhance the company’s competitive
position.
Keywords: strategic marketing, Uzbektelecom, telecommunications market,
customer experience, customer journey mapping, SWOT analysis, marketing mix,
customer loyalty.

INTRODUCTION
The telecommunications sector plays an important role in the digital
transformation of Uzbekistan. The increasing use of the Internet, mobile
communication, cloud technologies, and other digital services has intensified
competition among telecommunications  operators. In line with the country s digital
development strategy, telecommunications companies are expected to improve service
quality and expand digital infrastructure1.
Uzbektelecom JSC was selected for this study because it is one of the largest
telecommunications operators in Uzbekistan. The company provides a wide range of
services, including fixed and mobile communication, broadband Internet, IPTV , cloud
services, and  cybersecurity, while serving millions of customers across the country
through its extensive fiber-optic network.
The aim of this study is to analyze Uzbektelecom s current marketing strategy by
evaluating its customer value proposition, customer journey, competitive environment,
SWOT factors, market positioning, and marketing mix. Based on the findings, several
recommendations are proposed to improve customer satisfaction and strengthen the
company s competitive position in the telecommunications market.
LITERATURE REVIEW
Customer Journey  CJ  has become one of the most important concepts in modern
marketing because customers interact with companies through multiple online and
offline touchpoints before, during, and after purchasing a product or service. According
to Lemon and Verhoef  2016 , customer experience is created across the entire
customer journey rather than at a single point of interaction. The authors emphasize
that organizations should manage every touchpoint in order to improve customer
satisfaction and long -term loyalty. This concept is particularly relevant in the

Customer Journey Mapping  CJM  is a strategic management tool used to
visualize customers  experiences across different stages of interaction with a company.
Rosenbaum, Otalora, and Ramírez  2017  argue that many organizations incorrectly
assume that all t ouchpoints have equal importance. Their study demonstrates that
companies should identify the most influential touchpoints and redesign customer
experiences based on customer priorities. The authors conclude that customer journey
mapping helps firms improve service innovation and strategic decision-making.2
Service quality is another important determinant of customer satisfaction and
loyalty. Parasuraman,  eithaml, and Berry  1988  introduced the SERVQUAL model,
which measures service quality through five dimensions  reliability, responsiveness,
assurance, em pathy, and tangibles. Among these dimensions, reliability and
responsiveness are especially significant in telecommunications because customers
expect stable network performance and quick technical support. Companies that
consistently deliver high -quality services are more likely to retain customers and
strengthen brand loyalty.3
Integrated customer experience management has become a competitive advantage
in digital markets. Richardson  2010  explains that customer journey maps enable
organizations to identify pain points, eliminate service failures, and redesign customer
interactions based on customer expectations. Rather than focusing only on products or
prices, companies should understand customers  emotions and behaviors throughout
the entire buying process. This approach supports continuous service improvement and
stronger customer relationships.4
Recent research further suggests that customer journeys should not always be
effortless or identical for every consumer.  opaldas and Siebert  2022  argue that
organizations should design meaningful customer journeys that create engagement and
memorable experiences rather than simply reducing customer effort. Their findings
indicate that emotional engagement throughout the customer journey contributes
significantly to customer retention and brand preference.5

SWOT Analysis
Strengths
• Strong brand recognition: Uztelecom is a well -known, the largest, and reliable
Internet Communications company in Uzbekistan.
• Diverse products  It offers a variety of services like SIM Cards, Internet, Home
Internet, Satellite communication services, cybersecurity, cloud services, and data
centers.
• Robust Infrastructure  well-developed network infrastructure that covers both
urban and rural areas.
Weaknesses
• Limited global coverage  Uztelecom is well -known in Uzbekistan but not yet
popular internationally.
• High operating costs  Providing infrastructure throughout the country demands
high operational costs.
• Service deficiencies  Inconsistent customer services impact negatively on
customer satisfaction.

CONCLUSION AND SUGGESTIONS
This article analyzed the strategic marketing activities of Uzbektelecom by
examining its customer journey, competitive environment, SWOT factors, market
segmentation, positioning strategy, and marketing mix. The results indicate that
Uzbektelecom maintain s a strong competitive position in Uzbekistan s
telecommunications market due to its extensive network infrastructure, affordable
pricing, broad service portfolio, and high level of brand recognition. These strengths
enable the company to offer services millions of customers across both urban and rural
areas.
However, the analysis also indicated several challenges that may affect the
company s future competitiveness. Increasing competition from private
telecommunications operators, growing customer expectations regarding service
quality, limited digital promoti onal activities, and weaknesses in customer support
require greater strategic attention. To solve these issues, Uzbektelecom should
strengthen its integrated marketing communications, expand its presence on digital and
social media platforms, improve custo mer experience throughout the entire customer
journey, and introduce more innovative loyalty programs targeting young and digitally
active consumers.
Overall, although Uztelecom’s competitors are offering high discounts, big sales,
and using More advertising in social media, Uztelecom stays more reliable, affordable,
cheaper, and the largest Internet operator In Uzbekistan.
