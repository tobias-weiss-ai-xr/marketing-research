Abstract:
There is growing evidence that increased use of online travel portals has dramatically altered the manner in which
information search takes place and purchase decisions related to travel are made. Online reviews constitute one
important kind of electronic word-of-mouth (e-WOM) offering useful information concerning service quality,
customer experience and customer satisfaction. This study explores the effect of online reviews on consumer
purchasing decisions in Indian online travel portals by evaluating the effects of review credibility, review quality,
review volume, review usefulness, reviewer expertise, review valence and customer ratings. Quantitative research
design was used, while primary data collection involved administration of structured questionnaire to 400
respondents. The reliability and validity of measurement constructs were tested by Cronbach's Alpha, Composite
Reliability (CR) and Average V ariance Extracted (A VE) with all values being above the recommended threshold
levels, thereby establishing the efficacy of the research instrument. Descriptive statistics showed that the
respondents attached much importance to review usefulness (Mean = 4.22), consumer purchase decision (Mean
= 4.25), review credibility (Mean = 4.18) and customer rating (Mean = 4.14).
According to Pearson correlation analysis, there existed a significant positive correlation between each dimension
of the online review and the consumer purchase decision, wherein review usefulness had the highest correlation
coefficient (r = 0.826), followed by review credibility (r = 0.791) and review valence (r = 0.772). It was found
through multiple regression analysis that all the proposed hypotheses were statistically significant (p < 0.05). In
relation to the independent variable, review usefulness (β = 0.324) was seen as having the most impact on
consumer purchase decisions, followed by review credibility (β = 0.291), review quality (β = 0.213), review
valence (β = 0.192), overall customer rating (β = 0.165), reviewer expertise (β = 0.138), and review quantity (β =
0.114). The regression analysis showed that 78.4% of the variation in consumer purchase decision was explained
by the independent variables (R² = 0.784), thus implying excellent prediction power. The results indicate that
legitimate, credible, and useful online reviews have a significant impact on booking intention among consumers
of Indian online travel portals.

Portals, Review Credibility, Review Quality, Review Usefulness, Consumer Trust, Indian Tourism, Online
Consumer Behaviour.

1. Introduction:
The fast development of information and
communication technology has immensely changed
the face of global tourism industry in terms of how
travelers search for information about traveling, how
they compare alternatives, and even how they buy
travel services. Internet usage, mobile devices and
the availability of online payments have been among
the reasons for the quick growth of online travel
portals which made traveling much more convenient
and efficient. In India, the online travel market has
shown immense growth during the past ten years
due to high penetration rate of internet, growing
disposable incomes, large numbers of middle class
people, and growing demand for digital services.
Now people use online travel portals to book their
travels, flights, railway tickets, bus tickets, holiday
packages, and any other tourism service. Apart from
these technological developments, online reviews
have become a very important source of information
influencing consumer buying behavior[1].
Online reviews constitute e-WOM where customers
communicate their personal experiences, opinions,
ratings, and recommendations about the products or
services offered via the internet[2]. As compared to
traditional advertising, online reviews come from
genuine users who have used the product or service
earlier; thus, they become more credible for
potential customers than conventional advertising
methods. In the field of tourism, as the products

examined prior to purchasing, online reviews help
potential customers in gaining information about the
quality of services, level of satisfaction among
customers, price range, quality of accommodation,
means of transport, destination experience, and
overall travel performance[3].
The popularity of online travel portals in India has
increased dramatically due to their ability to provide
comprehensive travel solutions through a single
digital platform. Consumers can easily compare
prices, evaluate service providers, access
promotional offers, and read customer reviews
before making reservations[4]. The availability of
detailed customer feedback has fundamentally
changed consumer behaviour by shifting decision-
making from company-generated promotional
information to user-generated content. Modern
consumers tend to trust the experiences shared by
previous travelers more than traditional
advertisements because online reviews are
perceived as unbiased and experience-based.
Positive reviews enhance consumer confidence,
whereas negative reviews discourage purchase
intentions and often motivate consumers to explore
alternative travel options[5].
Consumer purchase decisions within online travel
portals are influenced by several characteristics of
online reviews. Review credibility plays a
significant role because consumers are more likely
to trust reviews that appear authentic, unbiased, and
written by verified users. Review quality is another
important factor, referring to the accuracy,
completeness, clarity, and relevance of the
information presented in customer feedback.
Detailed reviews containing descriptions of
accommodation quality, transportation services,
staff behaviour, cleanliness, safety measures, food
quality, and destination experiences enable
consumers to make better-informed decisions.
Similarly, review quantity influences consumer
perceptions because a larger number of reviews
often reflects greater reliability and provides broader
insights into customer experiences.
Reviewer expertise is another important factor. It is
common knowledge that reviews conducted by
seasoned travelers or authenticated customers are
more credible than anonymous reviews or reviews
from inexperienced people. Customers have the
perception that expert reviewers know much about
the topic, hence they carry much weight. The
valence of the review, which denotes whether
customer sentiments are negative or positive, plays
an equally significant role in influencing customer
behavior. Positive reviews tend to bolster customers'
intention to purchase since they create positive
sentiments on the quality of the services offered,
while negative reviews enhance risk perception,
making customers less likely to make bookings.
Consumer decision making process for travel has
changed drastically owing to the increase in digital
data. Traditionally, consumers used to depend on
travel agents, brochures, advertising, or
recommendations of relatives and friends while
planning a trip. Nevertheless, online travel portals
have altered the consumer decision making process
by prompting consumers to seek information
independently. Consumers make a comparison of
various travel websites, look at customer feedbacks,
both positive and negative, and evaluate the
experiences of reviewers in order to make their
decision about the best travel service to opt for
before making their final bookings. The significance
of the online review has been instrumental in the
decision making process of the consumer.
The Indian tourism industry offers a distinctive
setting to analyze the effects of online reviews
owing to the presence of diverse consumers in the
industry, fast digitization, and development of the
online travel industry. Each year, millions of Indian
consumers utilize online travel portals for their
domestic as well as international travels. The
increased usage of digital payment options,
government initiatives to promote Digital India,
better internet connectivity, and low-cost
smartphones have added to the motivation of Indian
consumers to make use of online travel services. At
the same time, stiff competition in the online travel
industry has resulted in customer reviews becoming
an integral part of the strategy.
The COVID-19 pandemic further strengthened the
importance of online reviews in travel decision-
making. During the pandemic and post-pandemic
period, consumers became increasingly concerned
about hygiene standards, safety protocols,
cancellation policies, refund procedures, and health-
related measures adopted by travel service
providers. Online reviews became a reliable source
of updated information regarding these aspects,
helping consumers evaluate potential travel risks
before making reservations. Consequently, review
content expanded beyond traditional service quality
dimensions to include health and safety experiences,
significantly increasing its influence on consumer
purchasing behaviour.
Despite the growing importance of online reviews,
several challenges continue to affect their
effectiveness. The increasing presence of fake
reviews, manipulated ratings, sponsored
recommendations, and fraudulent reviewer profiles
has raised concerns regarding review authenticity
and credibility. Consumers frequently encounter
conflicting opinions that make it difficult to
distinguish genuine experiences from misleading
information. These issues have created a need for
more effective review verification systems and
stronger mechanisms to ensure the credibility of
user-generated content. Understanding how
consumers evaluate review authenticity and identify

trustworthy information has therefore become an
important area of academic research and practical
application.
There exist a number of behavioral models that can
help in understanding the link between consumer
behavior and purchase decisions through reviews.
The Information Adoption Model helps explain that
people tend to use information which is considered
valuable and trustworthy. Technology Acceptance
Model asserts that people tend to use digital
information because of its perceived value and easy
accessibility. In addition, Elaboration Likelihood
Model shows how people deal with persuasive
information depending on their involvement,
motivation, and competence. From these
perspectives one can conclude that the credibility
and usefulness of the information provided by the
review, reviewer expertise, and overall information
quality play an important role in consumers'
decisions.
Whereas various international researchers have
studied e-WOM and online consumer behavior, few
studies have examined the collective impact of
multiple online reviews on consumer purchase
decision through Indian online travel portals.
Unique cultural diversities, technological usage
patterns, consumers' preferences, and rapid growth
of India's online tourism sector makes it imperative
to study such relationships in Indian setting.
Moreover, knowing what kind of review attributes
have most impact on consumers' decisions would
enable online travel firms to formulate better
engagement strategies and review management
system.
The current research intends to study the impact of
online reviews on consumer purchase decisions in
Indian online travel portals by studying the effect of
credibility of online reviews, quality of online
reviews, quantity of online reviews, usefulness of
online reviews, expertise of reviewers, valence of
reviews, and customer rating on consumer booking
decisions. Additionally, the study will examine the
effect of consumer trust on relationship between
online reviews and purchase decisions. The study is
based on quantitative research approach using
structured questionnaire and statistical tools for
analysis.
Conclusions that would result from this study will
be very valuable to academia and industry. To begin
with, from the academic point of view, the research
will advance the current state of knowledge
regarding electronic word-of-mouth, consumer
behavior and digital marketing in the context of
tourism. As far as the managerial application of the
conclusions is concerned, the results will help the
online travel portals in developing more efficient
ways of managing reviews, in building customers'
trust, improving service quality, promoting genuine
feedback and converting customers' decisions into
bookings. The emergence of digital technology in
the Indian tourism industry makes understanding the
effect of online reviews on consumer decision-
making process extremely important.

3. Methodology
This study adopts a quantitative research
methodology to investigate the impact of online
reviews on consumer purchase decisions in Indian
online travel portals. A descriptive and explanatory
research design has been employed to examine the
relationship between consumers' perceptions of
online reviews and their travel booking decisions.
The research focuses on understanding how
different dimensions of online reviews, including
review credibility, review quality, review quantity,
reviewer expertise, and overall rating, influence
consumers' intentions to purchase travel-related
services through online travel portals operating in
India.
The target population for this study comprises
individuals who have previously used Indian online
travel portals for booking travel-related services
such as flight tickets, hotel accommodations,
holiday packages, bus tickets, or railway
reservations. Respondents are selected based on
their prior experience with online travel portals,
ensuring that they possess adequate knowledge and
practical exposure to online reviews before making
travel purchase decisions. A non-probability
convenience sampling technique is adopted because
it enables efficient collection of responses from a
diverse group of online travel consumers across
different demographic categories, including age,
gender, educational qualification, occupation, and
income level.

The main source of data collection for the study is
through the use of structured questionnaires
designed after thorough review of literature
pertaining to e-WOM, consumer behavior and
online purchase decision making process. The
questionnaire is divided into two main parts. The
first part collects demographic data of the
respondents and the other part measures constructs
related to online reviews and consumer purchase
decisions. The items of measurement are taken from
validated scales used in previous studies and are
adapted to fit the situation of Indian online travel
portals. The responses to all items of measurement
are obtained on five point Likert scale from
“Strongly Disagree” to “Strongly Agree”.
Independent variables for analysis in this research
are review credibility, review quality, review
quantity, review usefulness, reviewer expertise,
review valence, and overall customer ratings. These
elements altogether form the set of characteristics of
the online reviews that might affect the consumer
perceptions. As for the dependent variable, it will be
represented by the consumer purchase decision,
which will reveal the probability of choosing and
purchasing travel services using online travel portals
based on the information from online reviews.
Another variable worth paying attention to is
consumer trust since the trustworthy reviews make
the consumers more confident in the online
purchases and decrease the level of purchase risk.
Prior to the actual data gathering process, it is
necessary to conduct a pilot study with the limited
number of respondents in order to measure the
clarity, readability, and reliability of the
questionnaire. The feedback received in the course
of conducting the pilot test is applied to improve the
wording and structure of the questionnaire.
Reliability of the measurement scale will be checked
using the coefficient of Cronbach's Alpha, with the
values exceeding 0.70 as an indicator of the
acceptable internal consistency. Construct validity
will be confirmed through EFA and CFA.
These data are coded, cleansed, and analyzed with
the help of statistical software such as IBM SPSS
Statistics and AMOS/SmartPLS. First of all,
descriptive statistical methods are utilized to
identify respondents' demographic profile and
general perception of online reviews. Secondly,
inferential statistical methods are used to examine
the hypothesized relationships between the variables
under study. For example, Pearson correlation
analysis is used to explore associations between
online review dimensions and consumers' purchase
decisions. Multiple regression analysis is conducted
to examine the effect of each individual online
review dimension on purchase decision when
demographic factors are controlled. In case of
testing mediation effects, Structural Equation
Modeling is conducted to analyze both direct and
indirect relationships between constructs and

This study follows the generally accepted ethical

Through the application of a methodical approach

3.1 Dataset and Experimental
For this research study, the data set was generated
from a survey carried out using a structured
questionnaire among consumers having previous
experience of making use of the Indian online travel
portals for arranging travel services. The survey
revolves around customers of prominent online
travel websites such as MakeMyTrip, Yatra,
EaseMyTrip, Goibibo, and Cleartrip. The
respondents selected for the study had some
previous experience of going through online reviews
prior to buying any travel service/product like flight
tickets, hotels, holidays, railway tickets, and buses.
A total of 450 questionnaires were dispatched
through online surveying tools and social media
forums. However, upon the exclusion of invalid or
inconsistent responses, 400 questionnaires were
considered statistically useful, hence making the
response rate 88.9%.
The demographic variables are gender, age,
education qualification, occupation, monthly
income, frequency of online travel booking,
preferred online travel website, and reason for
traveling. On the other hand, the behavioral
variables are related to the usage of the online
review by the respondents, such as the frequency of
reading online reviews, reliance on customer rating,
the degree of trusting review, and the willingness to
write reviews after travel service.
The dataset comprises seven independent constructs
and one dependent construct. These independent
constructs are review credibility (RC), review

3.2 Experimental Setup
This experiment was specifically designed to
examine the effects of various aspects of online
reviews on the purchase decisions of consumers
using Indian online travel websites. This experiment
falls under the category of quantitative research
where the respondents rated statements about online
reviews and purchase intentions through a structured
questionnaire.
Data pre-processing took place prior to the analysis
by filtering out the incomplete data set, eliminating
duplications, dealing with missing values, and
normality of the collected data. Summary statistics
such as frequency distribution, mean values, and
standard deviation were computed to describe the
demographic data and the views of the respondents
about online reviews.
The internal consistency reliability of the measures
used was tested using the Cronbach’s Alpha where
the value should exceed 0.70. The construct validity
of the measurement scales was verified using the
exploratory factor analysis (EFA) to find out the
factor structure and confirmatory factor analysis
(CFA) to confirm the proposed measurement model.
The adequacy of sampling was examined using the
Kaiser-Meyer-Olkin (KMO) measure of sampling
adequacy. The appropriateness of conducting factor
analysis was verified using Bartlett’s test of
sphericity.
Correlation analysis was used to analyze
relationships between review credibility, review
quality, number of reviews, usefulness of reviews,
expertise of reviewers, valence of reviews, overall
customer rating, and consumer purchase decision.
Finally, multiple regression analysis was employed
to assess the effect of each characteristic of online
reviews on the consumer purchase decision. In cases
when required, structural equation modeling (SEM)
would be used to analyze the relationships among all
variables under study.
All statistical tests were conducted with the help of
IBM SPSS Statistics (V ersion 29), used for
descriptive analysis, reliability test, correlation,
regression, and exploratory factor analysis. AMOS
29 or SmartPLS 4 (for PLS-SEM) were used for
confirmatory factor analysis and structural equation
modelling. The threshold for statistical significance
was set to 95% confidence level (p < 0.05).

4. Proposed Work
The proposed research offers an inclusive
framework for studying the effect of online reviews
on consumer buying behavior on Indian online
travel portals by including multiple dimensions of
electronic word-of-mouth (e-WOM) into one
consumer behavior framework. The framework will
help find out the effect of different aspects of online
reviews on consumer trust, evaluation and booking
process while using Indian online travel portals.
Contrary to previous works, where only few features
of online reviews were studied, the proposed model
will include the study of review credibility, quality,
quantity, usefulness, reviewer expertise, valence and
customer ratings.
The proposed framework starts from gathering the
information about consumer behavior using a well-
structured questionnaire applied to the users of
Indian online travel portals, who previously booked
at least one type of service via the online portal, such
as hotel accommodation, plane ticket, vacation
packages, railway or bus reservation. The gathered
data will be preprocessed, including the cleaning of
the dataset, handling missing values and duplicates,
validating and normalizing responses.
Once the model is preprocessed, its evaluation will
be done using reliability and validity analyses.
Internal consistency reliability is evaluated using
Cronbach's Alpha and Composite Reliability (CR),
whereas the convergent validity is assessed using
Average V ariance Extracted (A VE) and factor
analysis. Such analyses allow ensuring that the
questionnaire measures the necessary variables and
yields reliable empirical evidence.
Upon validation of the measurement model,
descriptive statistics are applied to analyze the
demographics of respondents and their perceptions
of online reviews. The mean values and standard
deviations are estimated to identify the general
impact of each review variable on consumers'
purchase decisions.
Pearson correlation analysis is used as an analytical
tool within the proposed framework to analyze the
interrelationship between review credibility, review
quality, review quantity, review usefulness, reviewer
expertise, review valence, overall customers'
ratings, and consumer purchase decisions.
Correlation analysis allows identifying the strength
and direction of the relationship between variables
and provides some initial evidence for the proposed
research hypotheses.
Next, multiple regression analysis is used to identify
the importance of each variable of online review
characteristics on consumers' purchase decisions.
This regression model measures the strength of
impact of the independent variables on the
dependent variable, thus determining the influential
factors that affect the consumers in their online
purchase decisions. Trust is considered as a behavior
construct that builds up consumers' confidence
during online transactions through minimizing
perceived uncertainty and risks. The assumed
relationship in the proposed model is that of highly
credible and informative reviews enhancing trust,
which will finally lead to purchase intentions.
This conceptual model comprises of seven
independent variables such as Review Credibility
(RC), Review Quality (RQ), Review Quantity
(RQT), Review Usefulness (RU), Reviewer
Expertise (RE), Review V alence (RV), and Overall
Customer Rating (OCR). These variables have an
impact on the dependent variable, Consumer
Purchase Decision (CPD). The relationships
between the independent variables and the purchase
decision are positive and it is anticipated that review
usefulness and review credibility will influence the
purchase decision more than the other variables due
to the fact that consumers depend on information.
The contribution of the proposed theoretical
framework to literature lies in the incorporation of
several aspects of review analysis into one empirical
framework tailor-made for Indian online travel
portals. The study builds on previous e-word-of-
mouth studies in the sense that it considers both
informational and social characteristics of online
reviews at once, instead of looking into the impact
of each variable separately. In addition, the current
study focuses on consumer trust in order to make
online reviews more influential in the process of
purchase decisions.
With regard to practical implications, the proposed
study offers valuable recommendations to online
travel portals for developing effective review
management systems. Traveling firms can boost
booking ratios by providing customers with
authentic reviews, reviewing, and verifying them,
detecting fake reviews using intelligent filtering
methods, encouraging positive customer reviews,

The demographic attributes of the 400 respondents
included in the survey have been captured in Table
4 below. Out of the total number of respondents, 228

a fairly balanced sample with more men as
compared to women, showing that both genders
utilize online travel portals to book travel services.
In terms of age distribution, most of the respondents
belonged to the 18-25 years age bracket (37.0%),
followed by those in the 26-35 years age bracket
(35.5%). Age groups 36-45 years and above 45 years
comprised of 19.0% and 8.5% respectively. This
implies that young people form the core users of
online travel portals. The reasons for this include
their higher digital literacy, greater knowledge about
the online environment, and more use of internet and
smart phones by young people than old people.
Young people also tend to check online reviews
before purchasing any travel services.
The educational level of the respondents is well
represented as 53.5%, 31.0%, and 15.5% have
postgraduate, undergraduate, and doctoral or other
professional qualifications respectively. The high
proportion of educated respondents implies that the
participants have sufficient knowledge and skills to
analyze online reviews, select travel options, and
make rational purchase decisions. Educated people
usually have good awareness about review
credibility, service quality, and digital purchase
risks, which increases the relevance of online
reviews when making decisions.
Generally, the demographics of the respondents
indicate that online travel portals are mostly used by
young and educated people who know how to use
digital technologies and gather online information
prior to booking travel tickets. Such characteristics
add reliability to the study since the respondents are
good representatives of the target population of
online travel portals' users.
Table 5 provides information on respondents'
preferences towards online travel portals and
booking frequency. According to the selected travel
portals, MakeMyTrip became the most popular
among 126 respondents (31.5%), Goibibo (23.5%),
EaseMyTrip (18.0%), Yatra (14.5%), and Cleartrip
(12.5%). The results suggest that MakeMyTrip has
the highest market preference in India because of its
wide range of services, affordable prices, discounts,
and customers' positive attitude.
As far as booking frequency is concerned, the most
common period for booking among respondents is
quarterly (41.0%), while half-yearly (23.5%),
monthly (21.5%), and yearly (14.0%) periods come
second, third, and fourth respectively. Based on the
above data, one can claim that respondents
frequently use online travel services and have
enough experience in the evaluation of travel
information and customer reviews. Indeed, frequent
users tend to use online reviews since repeated use
of travel portal makes them more familiar with the
quality of reviews and customers' ratings.
The obtained data proves that online travel portals
have become an inseparable element of the travel
planning process of consumers. Moreover, the
frequent use of these websites makes the sample of
respondents good candidates for analysis of online
reviews' influence on purchase decision.

of the measurement constructs. The values of
Cronbach's Alpha measure from 0.834 to 0.924
exceed the minimum acceptable threshold of 0.70.

also indicate that the latent variables are reliably
indicated by their respective indicators.
V alues of the Average V ariance Extracted (A VE)

level of 0.50. This means that there exists sufficient
convergent validity among the constructs. The
highest convergent validity is associated with
Review Usefulness (AVE = 0.712) and Consumer
Purchase Decision (A VE = 0.711).

online travel portals, answers from 400 respondents
were collected in this study. The result of
demographic analysis proved that the majority of the
respondents were young, educated, and frequently
used online travel portals, which signifies the use of
technology in travel services in India. It was also
identified through the portal usage analysis that
MakeMyTrip was the favorite travel portal for most
people while the frequency of booking was
quarterly.
It was found out through reliability analysis that all
the measurement constructs had high internal
consistency and validity since Cronbach's Alpha,
Composite Reliability, and Average Variance
Extracted values were higher than the required
levels.
Results of descriptive statistics revealed that
consumers considered review usefulness, review
credibility, review quality, and customer rating to be
very important when purchasing travel products or
services. Additionally, correlation analysis revealed
positive and significant correlations between all
online review dimensions and consumer purchase
decisions, thereby implying that useful and credible
reviews increase consumer trust in choosing travel
service.

research model explains 78.4% of variations in
