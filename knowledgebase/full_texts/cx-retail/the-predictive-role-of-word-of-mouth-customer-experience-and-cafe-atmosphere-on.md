Abstract
The coffee shop industry has remained a rapidly growing sector due to its profound
social and economic influence on consumer lifestyles and urban business development. While
several studies have addressed basic functional products as the main driver of consumer loyalty,
studies simultaneously addressing experiential and environmental human factors seem sketchy.
Therefore, this study examines the predictive role of word of mouth, customer experience, and
cafe atmosphere on revisit intention among consumers a t Holly Coffee Shop in Madiun,
Indonesia. A causal associative survey design was adopted while accidental sampling technique
was used to select the study population. A validated questionnaire was used to collect data from
100 participants and analyzed using multiple linear regression to test hypotheses which were
accepted at p less than 0.001 level of significance. The result demonstrated that the predictive
model significantly explained the variation in revisit intention among study participants,
Adjusted R Square = 0.873, F (3, 96) = 228.800, p less than 0.001. Furthermore, cafe atmosphere
(Beta = 0.631, p less than 0.001), customer experience (Beta = 0.228, p less than 0.001), and
word of mouth (Beta = 0.170, p less than 0.001) significantly predicted con sumer revisit
intention. The study concludes that cafe atmosphere, customer experience, and word of mouth
significantly contribute to shaping revisit intentions among study participants. The study
recommends cafe management and marketing stakeholders should take proactive measures to
optimize cafe aesthetics, service delivery, and organic positive reviews to systematically
enhance customer retention.

INTRODUCTION
The Indonesian coffee industry has undergone a significant transformation over the
past decade, evolving from routine caffeine consumption into a complex lifestyle
phenomenon, particularly among younger generations. Pangaribuan et al. (2020) noted that
this trend has driven a global surge in coffee consumption, with Indonesia experiencing
steady domestic growth as the middle class expands and modern coffee shops proliferate in
both metropolitan and secondary cities such as Madiun. Modern coffee shops now se rve as
essential third places, providing social and functional spaces for individuals to work,
network, and relax. This shift demands that businesses move beyond product quality to
prioritize aesthetic cafe atmospheres and curated customer experiences. In a highly
saturated market, the capacity to foster revisit intention has become the critical determinant
of business sustainability and long-term viability.
Several drivers influence consumer revisit intention, with word of mouth serving as
a primary mechanism. Pangaribuan et al. (2020) defined word of mouth as interpersonal
communication encompassing product evaluations and recommendations, which
consumers of ten find more credible than formal corporate marketing. Furthermore,
customer experience is a fundamental determinant of loyalty, defined by Nabila (2025) as a
subjective response to direct or indirect interactions with a firm. Research by Hanafi et al.
(2025) suggests that sensory, social, and natural elements are vital in elevating these
experiences. Complementing these factors, cafe atmosphere, which is the holistic
combination of design, lighting, music, and layout, shapes consumer emotions. Dewi and
Wirsa (2026) emphasized that a well orchestrated atmosphere creates a positive spatial
experience that increases customer dwell time and strengthens the inclination to return.
Holly Coffee in Madiun operates within this competitive landscape, offering a modern,
visually oriented environment to attract youth demographics. To maintain its market
position, the management must determine whether revisit decisions are primarily driven by
digital peer recommendations, the quality of direct customer experiences, or the comfort of
the cafe atmosphere. Despite the importance of these factors, research gaps persist,
particularly regarding their simultaneous influence within the specific con text of medium
sized cities. Previous studies by Pangaribuan et al. (2020), Porajou et al. (2024), and Nabila
(2025) have explored these variables in isolation or different geographical settings, leaving
a need for a unified model focused on Holly Coffee. Consequently, this study addresses the
question whether word of mouth, customer experience, and cafe atmosphere predict revisit
intention among consumers at Holly Coffee in Madiun. The findings will provide theoretical
insights into consumer behavior and o ffer management actionable marketing strategies to
enhance customer retention and sustainable business growth.

THEORETICAL REVIEW
Management
Management is no longer viewed merely as a technical process but as a complex
entity that responds to contemporary needs. Ratten (2025) offered a contemporary
definition by viewing management as a process of assessment, identification, monitoring,
and resolution of business problems facilitated by human interaction through both direct
and indirect communication. This definition emphasizes the active role of managers in
creating a competitive advantage through the coordination of organizational activities.
Meanwhile, Toshov (2025) argued that in a rapidly changing world, companies can no longer
rely on traditional systems, leading to the development of modern management principles.
These principles encompass a balance of centralization and decentralization, individual and
collegial leadership, scientific foundations, planning, autonomy, feedback hierarchies,
integrated responsibilities, motivation, democratization, and accountability. According to
Toshov (2025), modern management functions include planning, or ganizing, motivating,
controlling, and coordinating, establishing it as a strategic approach where the human factor
serves as the core element.
In a prior study, Habsy et al. (2024) asserted the close relationship between
organizations and management, positioning management as a crucial element in
organizational success and stating that understanding basic concepts is a prerequisite for
effective organizational life. Complementing this perspective, Bowden (2020) expanded the
definition of management beyond classical functions. Bowden (2020) argued that proper
management must value labor, maximize competitive advantage through specialization,
direct outputs toward competitive mass markets, and operate within a legal framework that
secures contracts and protects individual rights. Collectively, these definitions demonstrate
that management is a multifaceted discipline characterized by relational compl exity,
environmental responsiveness, and accountability for organizational outcomes.

implementation, and control, a method for identifying target markets to implement pricing,
promotion, and distribution policies, or a dynamic system reflecting modern development
trends. This study confirms that marketing management manifests in managing corporate

Word of Mouth
Word of mouth is a powerful marketing communication form that influences
consumer decisions through interpersonal processes where individuals share evaluations
and recommendations. Pangaribuan et al. (2020) defined word of mouth as oral
interpersonal communication containing product evaluations and recommendations shared
with others. This information dissemination can occur through direct communication or via
communication media such as social networks and internet forums. Kwun et al. (2013) in
Pangaribuan et al. (2020) emphasized that word of mouth plays an essential role in helping
businesses introduce and market their products because it is perceived as more credible
than firm generated promotions. Additionally, Delgadillo and Escalas (2004) in Pangaribuan
et al. (2020) stated that word of mouth shapes behavioral intentions by allowing individuals
to share positive or negative experiential evaluations, thereby influencing others.
The occurrence of word of mouth is driven by specific factors. According to Indriyati
et al. (2023) and Pangaribuan et al. (2020), these factors include customer satisfaction,
product and service quality, unique emotional appeal, referral incentives, and s ocial media
engagement. Consequently, word of mouth offers strategic benefits for corporations.
Pangaribuan et al. (2020) and Indriyati et al. (2023) noted that its advantages include
enhancing credibility, lowering marketing costs, expanding market reach organically,
increasing customer retention, and building a positive brand image. To measure this

Customer Experience
Customer experience represents the comprehensive perception and emotional
response formed through a customer interactions with a brand. Nabila (2025) defined
customer experience as an internal and subjective response that customers have toward any
direct or indirect contact with a company. Direct contact typically occurs during purchase,
usage, and service, while indirect contact involves unplanned encounters with product
representations, services, or brands through word of mouth, advertisements, or online
reviews. Pangaribuan et al. (2020) highlighted its importance in the coffee shop industry,
finding that cafe atmosphere and service quality significantly affect customer experience,
which in turn impacts satisfaction and revisit intention. Porajou et al. ( 2024) also asserted
that customer experience encompasses the entire sequence of customer interactions and
perceptions, where positive experiences drive customers to return.
Environmental and interactive elements heavily shape these perceptions. Mahendra
(2025) explained that customer experience results from physical and emotional interactions
that leave an impression in the minds of consumers and influence their corporate
evaluations, noting that physical environment factors like atmosphere generate positive
customer experiences. Highlighting a broader perspective, Hanafi et al. (2025) introduced
the concept of experiencescape, which describes the interaction between customers  and
their environment that forms individual experiences across five components: sensory,
functional, social, natural, and cultural. According to Nabila (2025), Mahendra (2025), and
Hanafi et al. (2024), customer experience is shaped by sensory, emotional,  cognitive,
relational, environmental, and personal factors. Managing these elements effectively offers
critical advantages, such as increasing satisfaction, fostering loyalty, driving positive
recommendations, and establishing a sustainable competitive advantage. To operationalize
this variable, synthesis from previous research establishes product quality, service quality,
and sensory emotional experiences as the primary indicators for customer experience.

Revisit Intention
Revisit intention serves as a critical post purchase behavioral outcome that indicates
long term customer loyalty. Nabila (2025) defined revisit intention as an individual intention
to repeat a visit to a destination, which is influenced by positive experiences during previous
visits and reflects satisfaction and perceived value. Nabila (2025) further cited perspectives
equating revisit intention with purchase intention, describing it as a strong desire to buy
back that arises after transactions and acts as  a direct response of post purchase behavior
within a certain period. Similarly, Pangaribuan et al. (2020) defined revisit intention as a
customer willingness to return to the same place or their probability to repurchase products
from the same establishment, serving as a customer initiative to continue a relationship with
a store or brand.

such as store atmosphere and electronic word of mouth exert a significant influence on i t,

METHOD
Research Design
This study adopts a quantitative research design with a causal associative approach
to examine the predictive power of structural marketing factors on customer retention. A
causal associative design is deployed as it allows a systematic statistical verific ation of the
cause and effect relationships among multiple variables based on pre established
hypotheses. Specifically, this framework evaluates how word of mouth, customer experience,
and cafe atmosphere function as independent predictors that drive the variance in customer
revisit intention, both partially and simultaneously. The analytical model operationalizes
numeric data derived from structured participant responses, which are systematically
processed using the Jeffreys Amazing Statistics Program software to ensure statistical rigor
and to minimize errors in empirical interpretation.

Setting and Study Population
The environmental setting for this empirical investigation is Holly Coffee Shop, a
modern hospitality establishment located in Madiun, Indonesia. The target population
comprises an infinite parameters framework encompassing all contemporary consumers
who have visited the establishment at least once. This specific population is chosen because
individuals who have personally engaged with the cafe possess direct, substantive exposure
to its physical environment, sensory stimuli, and service quality, making the ir subjective
assessments highly relevant to the core research objectives.

Given the infinite and unquantified nature of the consumer population, a non

approach selects participants based on their immediate availability and contextual presence
at the location, provided they fulfill predefined eligibility parameters. The established
inclusion criteria require that respondents have visited Holly Coffee Shop at least once, are
at least seventeen years of age to ensure autonomous purchasing capacity , and voluntarily
agree to complete the data collection instrument. To establish an statistically viable sample
size for an infinite population, the Lemeshow mathematical formula is introduced with a
ninety five percent confidence interval, a maximum variance estimate of zero point five, and
a ten percent margin of error. This calculation yields a baseline of ninety six participants,
which is converted to a final balanced sample of one hundred respondents to represent the
population characteristics effectively.

Instruments
Data collection is executed using a structured, closed ended questionnaire designed
around a five point Likert scale ranging from a score of one for strongly disagree to five for
strongly select agreement. The measurement scales are derived from comprehens ive
theoretical indicators where word of mouth is evaluated through positive communication,
personal recommendations, and customer advocacy.
Customer experience  represents the overall perception and evaluation of a
customer's interactions with a company throughout the entire consumption cycle, from the
pre-purchase to post-purchase stages. Nabila (2025) and Herdiana et al. (2022) break down
the indicators of this  variable into intrinsic and aesthetic product quality, the quality and
speed of staff service interactions, and sensory and emotional experiences that encompass
the five senses, feelings of satisfaction, mental stimulation, lifestyle, and social interactions.
Cafe atmosphere is defined as the physical and non-physical conditions designed by
service providers to create comfort and shape the customers' emotional experiences while
on-site. A synthesis of research by Nabila (2025), Porajou et al. (2024), and Dewi and Wirsa
(2026) establishes cafe atmosphere indicators consisting of lighting and color, type of music,
signature aromas or fragrances, appropriate room temperature, level of cleanliness, as well
as spatial layout and interior design.
According to Sugiyono (2023), a dependent variable is influenced by independent
variables, which in this study is determined as revisit intention . Nabila (2025) defines
revisit intention as an individual's tendency to return or repurchase a product based on
previously experienced positive events, with measurement indicators in the form of

Data Collection
The data collection procedure is conducted through the direct distribution of physical
questionnaires to eligible patrons within the premises of Holly Coffee Shop. Researchers
approach potential participants during operational hours, present an introductor y brief
detailing the study objectives, and secure informed consent regarding data confidentiality
and voluntary participation. Respondents complete the self reported instruments on site,
ensuring immediate retrieval and a maximum recovery rate for the pri mary data. This field
derived primary information is further supplemented by secondary data, including
academic literature, organizational archives, and contextual notes on the cafe operations, to
establish a robust foundation for the subsequent regression modeling.

Data Analysis
JASP 0.96.0.0 was used to analyze data collected. Both descriptive and inferential
statistics were collected. The hypothesis was tested using simple linear regression analysis
and accepted at p <.001 level of significance.

DISCUSSION
The empirical findings demonstrate that word of mouth, customer experience, and
cafe atmosphere partially and simultaneously serve as robust predictors of customer revisit
intention at Holly Coffee Shop in Madiun. Notably, cafe atmosphere emerges as the mo st
dominant determinant of repeat visitation. This major outcome aligns with previous
literature by Porajou et al. (2024), which stated that a well -designed cafe environment
significantly increases customers' willingness to return. It also corroborates the  findings of
Nabila (2025) and Dewi and Wirsa (2026), proving that sensory-rich physical elements such
as comfortable seating, pleasant lighting, clean spaces, and curated music deeply influence
consumers' emotional states, making them feel at ease and encouraging them to return.
Furthermore, direct customer experience and positive word of mouth are confirmed
as highly significant predictors of customer retention. The direct subjective interactions that
consumers have with the employees and products build a memorable connection. Th is is
consistent with Hanafi et al. (2025), who found that memorable experiences act as a strong
mediator between environmental characteristics and revisit intention. Additionally, when
customers encounter outstanding service and products, they naturally e ngage in organic
word of mouth promotion. As supported by Pangaribuan et al. (2020) and Kurniawan and
Fitriyah (2025), positive reviews and peer recommendations create a credible brand image,
which effectively motivates both existing and new clients to ret urn, thereby establishing a
sustainable customer loyalty loop.

CONCLUSION
The study establishes that word of mouth, customer experience, and cafe atmosphere
act as positive and statistically significant predictors of customer revisit intention at Holly
Coffee Shop in Madiun, both individually and simultaneously. Higher frequencies of positive
recommendations and peer evaluations systematically increase repeat patronage, while
positive experiential encounters during customer interactions reinforce long term
consumer commitment. Furthermore, the aesthetic and functional elements of  the cafe
atmosphere exert the most dominant predictive impact on customer retention, and when
combined in a unified framework, these three marketing drivers collectively shape overall
consumer revisit intentions.

Recommendations
Based on the research findings, several proactive measures are put forth for
corporate management and stakeholders. First, the management of Holly Coffee Shop must
prioritize substantial investment in maintaining and refining the physical environment,
focusing on cleanliness, layout efficiency, ambient comfort, and sensory infrastructure, since
cafe atmosphere functions as the primary determinant of customer retention. Additionally,
operational managers should implement continuous staff retraining programs to secure
