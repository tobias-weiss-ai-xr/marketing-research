ABSTRACT
This research aims to investigate the relationship among Marketing Gamification (Avatars), Customer Experience, and
Customer Purchase intentions. Wishful Identification/ Imitate Intentions, Perceived Similarity, and Embodied Presence
create the Marketing Gamification of the Digital Human the main focus of this research and its relationship with Customer
Purchase intentions. Customer Engagement, Entertainment, Interaction, Novelty, and Closeness/Relevance that shape the
Customer Experience act as a mediator between Marketing Gamification and Customer Purchase Intention. The S -O-R
framework is used in which marketing gamification acts as a stimulus, Customer experience as an Organism, and Customer
Purchase intention as a Response. This research is quantitative, survey questionaries were used to collect the primary data,
and a sample of 385 individuals was collected and analyzed by the software Smart PLS and SPSS. The results show that
Marketing Gamification has a significant positive impact on Customer Purchase  intentions and Customer Experience does
not mediate the relation between Marketing Gamification and Customer Purchase intentions. This research is conducted
in Pakistan and its implications can be found in academia, theory, and practice for marketers to m ake better-informed-
decision.

SOR Framework
Stimulus, Organism, and Response framework proposed by Mehrabian and Russell in 1974 and later used by other
researchers to explain the interaction of humans with an environment and evaluation of specific responses
(Mehrabian and Russell, 1974). This framework consists of three stages that a human undergoes first is Stimulus-
which is a change in environment noticed by humans, Organism- a phenomenon in which consumers' emotional
and cognitive states are established by perception and last Response- the conscious and unconscious response to
the observed phenomenon (Irimia-Diéguez, et al, 2023). For our specific research, we can apply this framework
to our conceptual model and try to explain it as the stimulus is marketing gamification (Avatars), the organism is
customer experience, and the Purchase intentions of consumers act as Response. Marketing gamification is a
stimulus change in the environment noticed by the consumer, later customer experience organizes the cognition
and feelings and derives perception, and at the end, the purchase intention of the consumer derived by the
marketing gamification and customer experience creates a conscious and unconscious response of intension of
purchasing of the products by consumers.

CONCLUSIONS
This research round about the trendiest topic Marketing Gamification -The Digital Human and its impact on
Customer Purchase intentions within virtual world. Along with that, a major Construct of Customer Experience
mediates the relationship between Marketing Gamification and Customer Purchase Intention. The s-O-R Model is
used to explain the conceptual model, Marketing Gamification functions as the Stimulus, Customer Experience
acts as the organism, and Customer Purchase intentions act as the Response. This research is conducted among
the fashion and social media users of Pakistan to suggest brand whether they should implement marketing
gamification technology for their brands in 2024 or not. A sample of 385 individuals was collected and analyzed
by using the software Smart PLS and SPSS. The finding suggested that a strong positive relationship exists between
marketing gamification and customer purchase intentions, but the mediation of customer experience between
marketing gamification and customer purchase intention came to be insignificant. The results suggested that for
Pakistani customers Marketing gamification will be very influential in increasing the customer purchase intention
for brands, other than customer experience within the virtual world.
Limitations and Future Directions
The limitations of this research are that it only ran a sample of 385 individuals and collected the data from only
two cities in Pakistan i.e., Islamabad and Attock. This result cannot be normalized in the whole of Pakistan. Other
than this research is for  Pakistani Customers who are fashion and Social media users, people who do not have
these qualities might not show these intentions. The data collection method is also a limitation using online
questionnaires leads to many discrepancies and data loss.
For future research, researchers can use different variables like Brand Equity as a mediator and age as a
moderator to find whether age has an impact on the relationship, Customer Satisfaction and Customer Retention
can also be used as dependent variables. This research does not describe the purchase intention virtually or in the
real world, other researchers can specify the virtual purchase intentions or real -world purchase Intention. The
population and sample of research can be increased. Other than Pakistan this research can be used to understand
the pattern of customer purchase intention influenced by marketing gamification. Customer experience can be
used with other variables to check whether it impacts the user's intent, other theories can be checked b y this
research. Other software can be used to analyze the data for more accuracy and better understanding.
