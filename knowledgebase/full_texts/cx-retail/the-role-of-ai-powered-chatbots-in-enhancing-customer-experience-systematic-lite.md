Abstract. The engagement of chatbots in customer experience management is increasing as a disruptive innovation in
the digital ecosystem. The goal of the research conducted by the authors was to explore the application of AI chatbots
to be used in e-commerce to improve customer satisfaction levels. The systematic literature review and data-analysis
presented in the paper involve the peer-reviewed literature published in the last three years. This study includes peer-
reviewed research articles on AI-powered chatbots and customer experience published between 2021 and 2024. After
searching Scopus and PubMed and applying exclusion criteria, five relevant full-text articles from the last three years
were selected. Scopus and PubMed databases were used for record searching; finally, twenty-one study-relevant articles
were chosen for the research. After applying some exclusion criteria, which were duplicates, other than the English
language, non-AI-powered chatbots, and not accessed for full-text articles, five peer-reviewed articles were finally used
for analysis. The PRISMA approach was utilized to synthesize the findings. Cross-study compatibility, validity, and reli-
ability of sources were validated and assessed to derive more accurate data-analysis results. Consequent themes include
the impact of anthropomorphism on perceived personalization qualities, behavioral trust and intention, and the part
played by chatbots in reducing perceived risks in electronic commerce. The results of the research support chatbots as
stewards of business and customer service operations to achieve global sustainability and citizenship objectives, includ-
ing inclusive interactions for all users.
Keywords: e-commerce, customer experience management, personalization, AI chatbots.

1. Introduction
AI chatbots have emerged as significant instruments for
delivering and improving customer service across indus-
tries by providing instant, customized solutions while
reducing costs. Their incorporation into e-commerce,
customer service, and conversational commerce is a rev-
olutionary change in the business-consumer relationship
(Wang, 2022; Hsu & Lin, 2023).
Using NLP and machine learning, chatbots offer an
efficient and easily scalable means to meet customer re-
quirements and respond to changing market conditions.
This technological advancement is equally compatible
with the current shift towards sustainability transition
and creating digital access as a fundamental aspect of
citizenship in the digital society (Vassilakopoulou, 2023;
AlAfnan, 2024).

2. Literature review
AI-powered chatbots have revolutionized customer ser -
vice by enabling automated, real-time interactions. Chat-
bots use Natural Language Processing (NLP) and ma-
chine learning algorithms to understand and respond to
customer inquiries (Allouch et al., 2021). These Chatbots
offer benefits such as 24/7 availability, quick response
times, and cost savings for businesses (Resolve Tech
Solutions, 2024). Research highlights that companies
integrating chatbots experience increased efficiency and
enhanced user experience (Gentsch, 2019).
Several studies confirm the significant impact of AI-
driven chatbots on customer experience. AI chatbots
personalize responses based on user history and prefer -
ences, fostering improved customer satisfaction (Przega-
linska et al., 2019). According to Luo et al. (2019), cus-
tomer satisfaction increases when AI chatbots provide
consistent and contextually relevant responses.
The evolution of chatbot technology has led to hu-
man-like interactions through advanced sentiment anal-
ysis, deep learning, and contextual awareness (Huang &
Rust, 2020). Conversational agents simulate human con-
versation, reducing customer frustration and increasing
engagement (Adamopoulou & Moussiades, 2020).
Some studies highlighted customers often per -
ceive chatbots as impersonal, leading to dissatisfaction
(McLean & Osei-Frimpong, 2019). Additionally, AI
bias and ethical concerns regarding data privacy remain
major challenges in chatbot deployment (Martin et al.,
2016).
By Wu et al. (2025) demonstrates the application of
proactive AI in complex scenarios, showcasing chatbot’s
autonomy in conducting diagnostic conversations by an-
ticipating user needs and providing proactive solutions.
3. Methodology
A systematic literature review and data-analysis to as-
sess the contribution of AI chatbots to improving cus-
tomer experience was conducted based on the PRISMA
checklist. Therefore, the methodology applied in the
study highlights the criteria for study selection, informa-
tion retrieval, assessment, and data analysis to guaran-
tee valid results (Page et al., 2021). This implies that the
method used was transparent and replicable.
The review involved four key phases: identification,
screening, eligibility, and inclusion. A bibliographic
search was conducted in the Scopus, Web of Science,
and PubMed databases to obtain the studies needed for
analysis based on a predetermined criterion for inclu-
sion. The inclusion and exclusion criteria ensured the
relevance and quality of the selected studies (Table 1)
(de Bell et al., 2023).

5. Discussion
The themes extracted from the selected studies present
insights about the potential opportunities for improve -
ment of the customer experience, customer needs sat-
isfaction and value co-creation by using AI-powered
chatbots.
These themes are quite relevant to the research ques-
tions and help to provide the answers for some essential
aspects of how AI chatbots work in various businesses.
In the following discussion, the identified themes will be
discussed concerning the research questions to establish
how the study addresses the research questions as well
as a detailed analysis of the use of AI chatbots in en-
hancing customer satisfaction and engagement and the
limitations/challenges faced.
Below, the authors present the answers to the research
questions formulated in the Introduction.
 – Research Question 1: How do AI-powered chatbots
enhance customer experience in various business con-
texts?
Chatbots developed with the help of artificial intel-
ligence positively affect customer relations as they offer
quick, individualized, and standardized services that can
apply to various types of customers and businesses. As
for the typology of issues studied, trust in AI chatbots is
recognized as one of the critical antecedents that posi-
tively influence the customer experience. It is necessary
to emphasize that trust plays a vital role in adopting chat-
bots, as both Prakash et al. (2023) and Silva et al. (2022)
have pointed out.
To interact with a chatbots, a customer must be as-
sured of receiving relevant, relevant and secure informa-
tion in return. This trust can only be developed through
messages conveying integrity and timely and accurate
replies to customer inquiries. Emotional connection is
defined as the level of customer trust in the chatbots,
which is reflected in the overall satisfaction rating of the
interaction.
Moreover, through the use of a text interface and
with the aim of mimicking users’ everyday lives, chatbots
make the users feel more at ease and understood.
According to the concept of anthropomorphisation
in chatbots (Sidlauskiene et al., 2023), the use of hu -
man-like template characteristics, for example, friendly
and warm language, as well as the employment of emo-
tionally positive responses, makes the user feel more
connected to the chatbots. This emotional relationship
can have a profound impact on the customer experi-
ence significantly for companies in the e-commerce
business, where customers are bound to the businesses
by the interactions they have with them. As the bots
imitate human-like behaviours, the information pro-
cessing and exchange become more appealing while
satisfying the customer’s emotional and cognitive de -
mands.

 – Research Question 2: What factors influence the ef-
fectiveness of AI-powered chatbots in improving cus-
tomer satisfaction and engagement?
The enhancement of customer satisfaction and en-
gagement by AI-powered chatbots depends on some fac-
tors revealed in the research. First, trust is a core value
that goes straight to the satisfaction and engagement of
the customers. Drawing from the work of Prakash et al.
(2023) trust depends on the chatbots likelihood of being
transparent, responsive, and helpful in offering solutions
to problems the customers might encounter. Belief in
the competence of the chatbots not only results in sat-
isfaction but also optimizes the frequency of use. Silva
et al. (2022) emphasize the role of trust, which mitigates
perceived risks even in financial and retail areas where
people exchange personal information.
Other factors include conversational cues and per -
ceived attributes, enhancing chatbots efficiency. Prakash
et  al. (2023) found that users prefer chatbots with hu-
man-like conversational signals such as friendliness.
These cues mainly encompass the tone of voice, the lan-
guage used, and the perceived character of the chatbots
to make the experience feel more realistic. When a user
believes that a chatbots can recognize their feelings or
requirements, the person is likelier to trust and utilize
the tool more actively, which results in higher satisfac-
tion levels.
Chatbots have an anthropomorphic design, as noted
by Sidlauskiene et  al. (2023), and are also an essential
factor that influences engagement. Thus, when chatbots
interact with customers and mimic human features like
a friendly or empathetic tone, consumers often feel that
their grievances are heard and they get a proper solu-
tion. This design enhances communication as it covers
customers who are more inclined to emotions or those
searching for a touch of humanity in their automates.
Anthropomorphic features are also found to help
increase levels of customer satisfaction because such
characteristics stimulate positive feelings during com-
munication, which are crucial in maintaining a long-
term business relationship with customers, according to
Sidlauskiene et al. (2023).
 – Research Question 3: How do AI-powered chatbots
address customer needs compared to traditional cus-
tomer service approaches?
Chatbots based on artificial intelligence help to meet
customer requests that are sometimes difficult to solve by
traditional methods of working with customers on-site
or over the phone, mainly for time reasons. The conven-
tional customer service call center model that involves
traditional human interaction is generally characterized
by longer hold times, limited coverage and sporadic ser -
vice at best. However, chatbots can serve numerous cus-
tomers simultaneously; they answer questions instantly,
and the possible emotional variance is significantly lower.
The ability to provide on-demand services is a signifi-
cant advantage that enhances customer satisfaction be-
cause users do not have to wait long for a response or for
the complaint to be solved. According to Mariani et al.
(2023) and Bălan (2023), using AI chatbots enhances the
evaluation of the global buying experience because they
are always active and respond more quickly, which is the
trend in today’s world.
Another area that has great potential in which chat-
bots provide a higher level of efficiency compared to tra-
ditional channels of customer service is personalization
and customization. This aspect may be actual because
human agents, despite providing individualized service,
can do so in a minimal capacity. Chatbots, however, can
handle large volumes of customer information to pro-
vide detailed and customized replies to fit the customer’s
needs, such as product suggestions, business sale promo-
tions, and solutions to the client’s grievances. It also helps
improve customer satisfaction since the business is get-
ting more personalized according to customer wants and
needs. It also helps to create a better bond between the
business and the customer.
Sidlauskiene et  al. (2023) also noted that anthro-
pomorphic content designs add to the development of
a customized approach that creates a feeling of caring
mainly expected from human interaction.
 – Research Question 4: What are the limitations and
challenges of using AI-powered chatbots to enhance
customer experience?
Nevertheless, several drawbacks and difficulties re-
garding the prospects of AI-driven chatbots need to be
outlined. Among the issues pointed out in the studies,
one of the most pressing ones is the difficulty of estab-
lishing trust with AI chatbots. Although engagement is
critical in this interaction, establishing and maintaining
trust in conversation with models can be challenging,
especially if the chatbots are unhelpful and unresponsive
with generic and often wrong messages. Prakash et  al.
(2023) and Silva et al. (2022) also note that it takes little
for customers to lose their trust and become dissatisfied
with a chatbots if they make many mistakes. Further, the
constant requirement of updating and training the mod-
els to achieve better responses presents another problem
for businesses.
There are also some drawbacks: first, a chatbots can-
not solve complex or emotional cases effectively. One
weakness of chatbots is their inability to solve complex
customer problems since they do not incorporate human

6. Conclusions
The results of the study have revealed that the AI chat-
bots application in e-commerce may redesign how busi-
nesses interact with their customers, while presenting
new possibilities to build better customer relations, cus-

This paper, therefore, responds to specific research
questions concerning the efficiency of AI chatbots in
different business domains. It can be inferred from the
study that trust, anthropomorphism, and personalized
interaction are vital ingredients that affect customers’
satisfaction and their lengths of engagement as more and
more consumers demand efficient, effective, and custom-
ized services.
On the other hand, the application of AI chatbots
has some shortcomings. Lack of solid trust relationships
between company and customer, inability to handle de-
tailed or emotionally charged customer problems, and
privacy issues are all significant barriers to chatbots ef-
fectiveness. Nevertheless, the present issues give rise to
the idea that companies focusing on further developing
chatbots’ functions and overcoming these constraints can
quickly generate substantial value – both from the view-
point of customers’ engagement and the impact on the
business image.
As the prospects of AI chatbots in providing custom-
er service look bright. The future of the chatbots in the
verticals is promising. In the subsequent developments,
the maturity of the applications is expected to increase
with better interaction management, individualism, and
security enhancement. For AI-supported chatbots to
reach maximum effectiveness, businesses need to revise
their chatbots approaches further while considering the
challenges outlined in this paper. Finally, AI chatbots sig-
nify a revolutionary tool to advance customer relations,
on the one hand, and overall organizational improve-
ment, on the other.
This study investigates the prediction of customer
experience enhancement through AI-powered chatbots
in an e-commerce arena, offering instant assistance,
smoother communication and better service data. We
know that the benefits are clear, yet key insights high-
light a more nuanced reality: chatbot adoption needs to
be strategically aligned with both customer expectations
and also business objectives. On the downside, an over -
reliance on this kind of automation causes depersonaliza-
tion and, ultimately, may damage customer satisfaction.
On the other hand, ethical issues surrounding data pri-
vacy, bias, and ambiguity are often left untreated.
While the dataset was limited to five studies, the
findings reached thematic saturation and were sufficient
to draw meaningful conclusions. Future research could
broaden the dataset to validate these findings across
more contexts.
These findings suggest that chatbot implementation
is not merely a technological decision but a strategic one
that influences brand perception and customer trust. Fu-
ture research should focus on longitudinal studies that
examine the sustained impact of chatbot use on custom-
er loyalty and brand engagement. Additionally, there is
a need for comparative studies across industries and ge-
ographies to understand the contextual effectiveness of
chatbot features. Exploring the integration of sentiment
analysis and adaptive learning into chatbot frameworks
also presents promising avenues for innovation and per-
sonalization.
