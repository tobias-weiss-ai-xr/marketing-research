ABSTRACT :
Artificial Intelligence (AI) plays an important role in modern social media marketing. This study examines the
awareness and use of AI t ools among retail stores in Tirupur. It focuses on applications like chatbots, predictive
analytics, personalized advertising, and automated content to improve customer engagement and sales. The research
also identifies challenges such as lack of technical  knowledge, cost issues, and data privacy concerns while
evaluating the impact of AI on business growth.

Keywords: Artificial Intelligence, Social Media Marketing, Retail Stores Tirupur, Digital Marketing, Marketing
Performance.

INTRODUCTION :
In the modern business environment, technology plays an important role in marketing activities. One of the latest
technologies used in marketing is Artificial Intelligence (AI). AI helps businesses analyse customer behaviour, create
personalized advertisements, a nd automate communication with customers.  Social media platforms such as
Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp have become powerful marketing tools for businesses. Retail stores use these
platforms to promote their products, interact with customers, and increase sales. AI tools such as chatbots, automated
advertisements, and content generators help businesses manage their social media marketing more efficiently.
Tiruppur is well known for its textile and garment industry and has a large number of retail stores.  Many retailers
use social media platforms for marketing their products. Therefore, it is important to understand how Artificial
Intelligence is used in social media marketing among retail stores in Tiruppur.

BACKGROUND OF THE STUDY :
In recent years, digital transformation has significantly changed the way businesses communicate with customers.
Social media platforms have become powerful marketing channels that allow businesses to reach a large audience at
a relatively low cost. With the introduction of ar tificial intelligence, social media marketing has become more
advanced and efficient.
AI technologies help businesses analyse customer data, identify consumer preferences, and deliver personalized
advertisements. Retail stores can use AI to automate respon ses, schedule posts, analyse engagement, and provide
better customer service. These capabilities enable businesses to improve marketing efficiency and strengthen
customer relationships. In Tiruppur, adopting AI-driven marketing strategies helps retail stores remain competitive
and attract more customers. Understanding how AI tools are currently used by retailers is important for improving
marketing strategies and encouraging wider adoption of advanced technologies in the retail sector.

OBJECTIVES OF THE STUDY :
• To study the influence of artificial intelligence in social media marketing.
• To study the role of AI in improving customer engagement on social media platforms.
• To identify the challenges faced by retail stores in adopting AI-based marketing tools.

RESEARCH METHODOLOGY :
This study is descriptive in nature and focuses on understanding the use of artificial intelligence in social media
marketing among retail stores in Tiruppur. Primary data was collected through a structured questionnaire from 100
retail store owners and managers. Secondary data was collected from journals, articles, research papers, and online
sources related to artificial intelligence and digital marketing.
Percentage analysis was used as the statistical tool to analyse the collected data. The results were interpreted using
tables and charts to provide a clear understanding of the findings.

FINDINGS:
 The majority of respondents (36%) belong to the 25–35 age group, indicating that young business owners
dominate the retail sector.
 Most respondents (66%) are male, showing higher male participation in retail business ownership.
 The majority of respondents (46%) are engaged in the textiles/garments business, highlighting its dominance
among retail stores in Tiruppur.
 Most respondents (84%) use social media for business promotion, demonstrating its importance in marketing
strategies.
 The majority of respondents (37%) use WhatsApp Business for promotion, making it the most preferred social
media platform.
 Most respondents (42%) post business content weekly, reflecting consistent social media activity.
 The majority of respondents (52%) use AI tools in social media marketing, showing moderate adoption of AI
technologies.
 Most respondents (50%) use AI design tools, indicating they are the most commonly used AI applications.
 The majority of respondents (40%) agree that AI saves time in marketing activities, showing perceived efficiency
benefits.
 Most respondents (38%) agree that AI improves the quality of marketing content, enhancing promotional
effectiveness.
 The majority of respondents (36%) agree that AI increases advertisement effectiveness and helps understand
customer preferences.
 Most respondents (48%) are willing to invest more in AI tools in the future, reflecting a positive outlook on AI
adoption.

SUGGESTIONS :
 Retail store owners should be provided with training programs and workshops to improve their knowledge about
artificial intelligence tools.
 Government and business organizations should support small retailers by providing affordable AI technologies.
 Retail businesses should invest in AI -based marketing tools to improve customer engagement and marketing
efficiency.
 Retailers should regularly analyse customer data to develop more personalized marketing strategies.
 Businesses should adopt modern digital marketing practices to remain competitive in the growing online market.

CONCLUSION:
The study concludes that Artificial Intelligence plays an important role in improving social media marketing among
retail stores in Tiruppur. AI tools help retailers improve communication with customers, create attractive marketing
content, and analyse customer preferences. Although some retailers are still facing challenges in adopting AI
technologies, the use of AI in marketing is gradually increasing. With proper awareness and training, retail businesses
can effectively use Artificial Intelligence to improve their marketing performance and achieve better business
growth.
