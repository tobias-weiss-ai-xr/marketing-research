Annotatsiya
Maqolada tijorat banklarida yashil moliyaviy mahsulotlarni ilgari surishda
raqamli marketing strategiyalaridan foydalanishning nazariy va uslubiy asoslari
oʻrganilgan. Yashil bank faoliyati, yashil ishonch, mijoz sayohati va texnologiyani
qabul qilish kons epsiyalarini umumlashtirib, yashil kredit, omonat, karta va chakana
obligatsiyalarni targʻib qilishda axborot assimetriyasini kamaytirish markaziy vazifa
asoslangan. Mijoz sayohati bosqichlari boʻyicha integral samaradorlik indeksi va
konversiya zanjiri formulalari taklif etildi. Oʻzbekistonda muomaladagi bank kartalari
soni 63,6 mln donaga, masofaviy bank xizmatlari foydalanuvchilari 62,1 mln kishiga
yetgani, xalqaro moliya institutlarining yashil kredit liniyalari va suveren yashil
obligatsiyalar raqamli targʻibot uchun keng baza yaratgani koʻrsatilgan.
Segmentatsiya, shaxsiylashtirish, taʼlimiy kontent va grinvoshing xavfini nazorat
qilishni birlashtiruvchi konseptual model ishlab chiqildi hamda banklar uchun amaliy
takliflar asoslandi. Tadqiqot natijalaridan tijorat banklari yashil mahsulotlar boʻyicha
marketing siyosatini ishlab chiqishda va raqamli kanallar samaradorligini baholashda
foydalanishi mumkin.
Kalit soʻzlar: yashil bank faoliyati, yashil moliyaviy mahsulotlar, raqamli
marketing, mijoz sayohati, yashil ishonch, grinvoshing, mobil banking,
shaxsiylashtirish.
Аннотация
В статье исследованы теоретические и методические основы использования
стратегий цифрового маркетинга при продвижении зеленых финансовых
продуктов в коммерческих банках. Автор обобщил концепции зеленого
банкинга, зеленого доверия, клиентского пути и принят ия технологий и
обосновал, что центральной задачей продвижения зеленых кредитов, депозитов,
карт и розничных облигаций является снижение информационной асимметрии.
Предложены формулы интегрального индекса эффективности по этапам
клиентского пути и цепочки конверсии. Показано, что число банковских карт в

линии международных финансовых институтов и суверенные зеленые
облигации сформировали широкую базу для цифрового продвижения.

Abstract
The article examined the theoretical and methodological foundations of using
digital marketing strategies to promote green financial products in commercial banks.
Synthesising green banking, green trust, customer journey and technology acceptance
concepts, the author substantiated that reducing information asymmetry is central to
promoting green loans, deposits, cards and retail bonds. Formulas for an integral
effectiveness index across customer journey stages and for a conversion chain were
proposed. It wa s shown that bank cards in circulation in Uzbekistan reached 63.6
million, remote banking users 62.1 million, and green credit lines of international
financial institutions together with sovereign green bonds created a broad base for
digital promotion. A c onceptual model combining segmentation, personalisation,
educational content and greenwashing risk control was developed, and
recommendations for banks were substantiated. The results can be used by commercial
banks in developing marketing policy for green  products and in assessing the
effectiveness of digital channels.
Keywords: green banking, green financial products, digital marketing, customer
journey, green trust, greenwashing, mobile banking, personalisation.
