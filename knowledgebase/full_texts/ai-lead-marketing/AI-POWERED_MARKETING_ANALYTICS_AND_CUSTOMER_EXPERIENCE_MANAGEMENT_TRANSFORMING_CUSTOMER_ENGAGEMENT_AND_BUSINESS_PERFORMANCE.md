Earlier marketing practices depended heavily on sales records, surveys, demographic profiles, and managerial experience. These sources are still useful, but they do not always provide a sufficiently detailed or timely picture of customers whose behaviour changes across digital and physical channels. AI-based marketing analytics helps address this gap by combining large volumes of customer information with machine learning, predictive models, natural language processing, recommendation techniques, and automation. These tools can help marketers recognise patterns, estimate future behaviour, tailor communications, and respond more quickly to customer needs. This chapter examines how AI-supported marketing analytics can strengthen customer experience and business performance. It considers applications such as behavioural segmentation, personalisation, recommend-dation systems, sentiment analysis, predictive analytics, conversational tools, churn prediction, lead scoring, dynamic pricing, and marketing automation. The discussion also considers the connection between customer relevance, responsiveness, engagement, satisfaction, loyalty, retention, and long-term value. A conceptual framework is presented in which customer data is converted into actionable insight, followed by personalised and predictive interactions that can improve customer experience and, in turn, organisational&nbsp;outcomes. At the same time, the chapter recognises that AI brings concerns relating to privacy, bias, transparency, cybersecurity, cost, and excessive automation. The central argument is that AI creates the greatest value when technological capability is combined with human judgement, responsible data practices, and a clear customer focus.
