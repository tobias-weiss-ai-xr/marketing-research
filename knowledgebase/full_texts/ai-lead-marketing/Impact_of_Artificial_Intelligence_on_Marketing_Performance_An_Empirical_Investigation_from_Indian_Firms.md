Published June 11, 2026 | Version v1 Model Open Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Marketing Performance: An Empirical Investigation from Indian Firms Authors/Creators Dr.SRINIVASA.M (Researcher) Description This study empirically examines the impact of artificial intelligence (AI) on marketing performance among Indian firms. Drawing on the Resource-Based View (RBV) and Dynamic Capabilities theory, the study develops and tests a conceptual model encompassing four AI-enabled marketing dimensions&mdash;AI-Driven Customer Analytics (AICA), Marketing Automation (MA), Personalisation Capability (PC), and Predictive Lead Scoring (PLS)&mdash;as antecedents of marketing performance, with Customer Engagement (CE) as a mediator and Firm Innovativeness (FI) as a moderator. Data were collected from 390 marketing professionals across six industry sectors using a structured questionnaire. Structural Equation Modelling (SEM) with AMOS 24 was employed for hypothesis testing, supplemented by bootstrapped mediation analysis (5,000 resamples). All four AI dimensions demonstrated significant positive effects on marketing performance. Customer engagement mediated the AI&ndash;performance relationship, while firm innovativeness positively moderated the AICA&ndash;performance link. The findings provide actionable insights for marketing managers and contribute to the nascent empirical literature on AI-driven marketing in emerging market contexts.
