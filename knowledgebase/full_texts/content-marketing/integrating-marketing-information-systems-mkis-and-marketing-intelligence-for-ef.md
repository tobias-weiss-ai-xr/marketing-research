Digital Marketing
The rapid digitalization of markets has heightened the importance of integrating
marketing information systems and marketing intelligence for effective decision -making,
particularly in e-commerce environments. This study investigates the impact of Marketing
Information Systems (MkIS), AI -based marketing tactics, email marketing, and content
marketing on marketing decision -making, while examining the moderating role of
marketing data analytics. Using a quantitative design, data were collected from 379
marketing and decision -making professionals working in e -commerce firms in Saudi
Arabia. The proposed model was tested using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation
Modelling (PLS -SEM). The findings reveal that MkIS, AI -based marketing tactics, email
marketing, and marketing data analytics have a significant positive impact on marketing
decision-making, whereas content marketing does not exert a direct effect. Moreover,
marketing data analytics does not moderate the relationships between marketing tools
and decision-making, indicating its role as an independent decision -enabling capability.
The study contributes to marketing intelligence literature by offering an integrated, data-
driven framework and provides actionable insights for e -commerce managers seeking to
enhance decision quality in emerging digital markets.

Introduction
High-paced digitalization of the global markets has radically changed the way organisations gather,
process, comprehend and use information in strategic and operational decision-making (Zulkifli, 2024).
Marketing decisions made in modern business settings are not based on intuition and small market
observations as much as they are based on systematic data collection, real -time intelligence and high
analytical tools (Rahman & Hye, 2022). This has increased the strategic significance of the incorporation
of t he Marketing Information Systems (MkIS), marketing intelligence, and data -oriented marketing
practices to facilitate suitable and timely decision-making (Shuvo et al., 2025). There is no better place
where this transformation can be felt than in e-commerce where companies exist in very competitive,
technology-intensive, and customer-oriented markets (Vesterback, 2024).
The last twenty years have seen marketing shift its position in terms of being a more promotional and
sales support activity to be more of a strategic discipline whose main concern lies in the customer,
value co -creation and long -term management of relatio nships (Muñiz -Martínez, 2026). The
development has been largely linked with improvement in information technology, artificial
intelligence (AI), and online communication platforms (Olan et al., 2022). MIS have proved to be an
important organizational infrastructure that allows companies to collect internal records, market

Saudi Arabia is one of the most dynamic e -commerce markets in the Middle East and the companies
are keen to use the digital marketing technologies in order to gain competitive edge. The skill to
combine formalized Marketing Information Systems with real -time marketing smarts obtained using
digital channels is crucial in such an environment to make marketing choices (Jabbar et al., 2020).
Traditionally, Marketing Information Systems have been in the format of formalised systems that assist
in the decision -making process of managers through provision of timely, accurate and relevant
information (Harmon, 2002). At first, MkIS was oriented on internal reporting systems including sales
records, inventory data and financial reports. As digital markets grow, and with the proliferation of
large external data sources (Önden, 2026), MkIS has now become a dynamic technology -enabled
system that is now able to incorporate customer behavior data, competitive intelligence and market
trends. On the setting of e -commerce, MkIS can be considered a platform that plays a central role in
ensuring customer interaction in various touchpoints, campaign optimization , and supporting
marketing decisions on evidence (Prajapat, 2024). The success of these systems is not just in the
collection of the data but the synthesis of information to knowledge that can then be used in the choice
of strategy, pricing, products to offer, promotion, and more so the way to interact with customers.
Along with the development of MkIS, artificial intelligence has become a disruptive technology in the
field of marketing (Akinsola et al., 2022). Application of AI-based marketing strategies, such as machine
learning algorithms, predictive analytics, recom mender systems, and chatbots, have transform the
engagement between companies and their clients and the manner in which they make marketing
decisions (Moqaddem, 2025). The technologies also allow organizations to process large amounts of
structured and uns tructured data and make predictions and forecasts on the behavior of the
consumers and automate decisions making. AI-based technologies are becoming useful in e-commerce
to make individualized product recommendations, dynamic pricing, targeted advertisements, and real-
time customer service and improve the accuracy and responsiveness of decisions (Vashishth et al.,
2024). AI-based marketing strategies provide great potential to optimize marketing investments and
enhance the customer experience to companies w orking in competitive markets, as long as they are
successfully combined with the current marketing information systems (Hasan, 2025).
Although greater use of advanced technologies is increasingly adopted, the older digital tools in
marketing like email marketing as well as content marketing still remain relevant in informing the
decision making in marketing (Paliwal & Singh, 2026). Email marketing is also one of the cheapest and
most measurable digital communication tools, which allow companies to send personal messages,
strengthen customer relations, and impact buying behaviors (Thomas et al., 2022). The email
campaigns in e -commerce giv e effective information on customer preferences, engagement and
conversion behavior, which may be injected into MkIS to make informed decisions (Alkadrie, 2024). On
the same note, content marketing has also come up as a strategic tool aimed at developing and sharing
useful, topical, and continuous content to appeal and sustain target audiences. Content marketing

The combination of email marketing and content marketing data with Marketing Information Systems

Marketing data analytics is a key intermediary since it transforms massive amounts of digital
information into actionable insights using descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive methods (LaValle et

out of MkIS and digital marketing tools and enhance the quality of decisions. Although there is an
increasing literature, minimal empirical studies have investigated their interaction especially in the
changing e-commerce market in Saudi Arabia, so integrated research is necessary (Algharabat et al.,
2022; Dwivedi et al., 2021).
This study to e -commerce companies in Saudi Arabia can make both theoretical and practical
contributions. Theoretically, it builds on the marketing information systems and marketing decision -
making literature by taking an integrative, data-driven approach that is in line with the current realities
of the digital marketing. Practically, it provides practical information to e -commerce managers trying
to maximize marketing decisions based on efficient system integration and development of analytical
capability. The study highlights the strategic significance of using marketing intelligence and analytics
to enhance the sustainability of growth and competitive advantage in the environment of e-commerce
as part of the wider context of the digital transformation agenda in Saudi Arabia.

Literature Review
Introduction and conceptualization
The modern marketing decision -making process is highly data -oriented: companies are gathering
significant amounts of customer, transaction, and online interaction data, and turn that data into the
operational intelligence with the help of information syste ms and sophisticated analytics (Nordin and
Ravald, 2023). Previously confined to the internal sales and inventory reporting features, Marketing
Information systems (MkIS) currently represent hybrid socio -technical sites capable of integrating
internal reco rds, market research, and social/digital signals and automated analysis (Rosario, 2021;
Kurniawanto, 2023). Similar advances in artificial intelligence (AI), email and content marketing
strategies, and marketing analytics have provided new points of contro l that marketers can use to
shape consumer behaviour, real-time measuring outcomes, and refining decisions quickly (Haleem et
al., 2022; Cruz and Rosario, 2025). This information-to-intelligence pipeline is particularly significant in
the case of e -commerce companies in Saudi Arabia with the rapid digital adoption as part of Vision
2030 and significantly high rates of smartphone usage in urban centres (BCG, 2021; Monshaat, 2024).
In the literature review below, each of the core aspects of your study-MkIS, AI-based marketing tactics,
email marketing, content marketing, and marketing decision -making is treated, and then the
interrelations between these aspects and decision outcomes are moderated by marketing data
analytics.

Marketing Information Systems (MkIS)
MkIS can also be defined as subsystems within organizations that store, capture, distribute, and
process marketing-relevant data to aid in managerial decisions (Rosario, 2021). The recent scholarly
work has highlighted the transformation of MkIS as a reporting system to dynamic, integrative systems,
which integrate internal transactional systems, CRM, web analytics, social listening, and decision -
support modules (Kurniawanto, 2023; Rosario, 2021). Recent studies (since 2020) point to three trends,
including (1) decentralization of MkIS and integration with cloud services and APIs to provide real-time
feeds (Aggarwal, 2025; Kurniawanto, 2023);
(2) more focus on knowledge accumulation and the iterative learning cycles in which MkIS can not only
report but also prescribe actions (Rosario, 2021); (3) the necessity of governance, data quality
standards, and human-system alignment to make the output
Positive relationships are observed inmaturity of MkIS and decision quality, marketing, and
responsiveness (Etim et al., 2023; Nugroho, 2023). In cases where MkIS also use external social and
behavioural data, companies show high level of better segmentati on, campaign targeting and quicker
corrective action (Rosario, 2021). Boundary conditions are, however, also reported in extant work:
MkIS only boost the quality of decisions when organizations have complementary capabilities data
literacy, managerial acce ptance, and processes to transform insights into action (Rosario, 2021;
Kurnieawanto, 2023).
Hypothesis 1 (H1). In e-commerce companies, the impact of marketing Information Systems (MkIS) on
marketing decisions is positive.

AI-based marketing tactics
As of 2020, the literature on AI -in-marketing has also moved at a faster pace, describing real -world
uses (predictive targeting, recommendation engines, dynamic pricing, automated creative
optimization, chatbots) and theories of the business value of AI (Haleem et al., 2022; Chen et al., 2025).
According to reviews and bibliometric analyses, AI has two aspects (a) it enhances information
processing (pattern recognition in large, unstructured data) and (b) it can be used to take automated
and near-real-time decision-based actions (e.g., bid adjustments, on -site personalization) (Haleem et
al., 2022; Cruz and Rosario, 2025).
Artificial intelligence enhances the prediction of the marketer and reduces the response time between
the action and the outcome to the marketer (Haleem et al., 2022). According to the recent
experimental and field research, the machine learning models hav e the potential to enhance the ROI
of the campaign through enhanced audience targeting and personalization (Chen et al., 2025; ACM
study, 2025). However, researchers warn against traps as well: the problems of algorithmic bias,
explainability (interpretability), and governance failure may undermine trust in managers, making AI
useless in decisions (Cardona -Acevedo et al., 2025; Haleem et al., 2022). Notably, AI is prone to the
quality of input data and connection with MkIS isolated AI models are less valuable than AI integrated
in a coherent information architecture (Cruz & Rosario, 2025).
In Saudi e -commerce companies, AI has been slow but increasing; according to market reports and
vendor case studies, it is being adopted in the recommendation systems, customer service chatbots,
and programmatic advertising, particularly by large marketpla ce players based in Saudi Arabia
(Monshaat, 2024; BCG, 2021).
Hypothesis 2 (H2). Implementation and application of AI -based marketing strategies have positive
effects on the marketing decision-making process of e-commerce business.

Content marketing
Social media content marketing, video and blogging has now become a strategic strength that
correlates with brand equity, search engine optimization, community building and conversion funnel
(Zhang & Zhang, 2024; Tafesse and Dayan, 2023). Recent research analyzes the influence of the content
features (authenticity, emotional appeal, information quality, co-creation modes) on the engagement
and conversions, particularly with short-form video content and channels that rely on influential figures
(Zhang and Zhang, 2024; Bu et al., 2022). Depending on the type of creator and platform economics,
the game-theoretic and empirical studies reveal that content co -creation and influencer choice are
important to monetization and conversion (Zhang and Zhang, 2024).
The results of content marketing are the creation of qualitative and quantitative intelligence, namely
sentiment and eWOM cues, UGC performance indicators, conversion attribution, and customer
journey mapping. Such indicators are used to make decisions reg arding the product positioning,
promotional schedule, and influencer partnerships (Zhang and Zhang, 2024). Nevertheless, content
analytics is noisy due to the complexity of attributions and competition between the attention drivers;
companies that have a m ore efficient content measurement system (A/B testing, incremental lift
measurement, multi-touch attribution) will retrieve more decision value (Tafesse and Wood, 2023).
Hypothesis 4 (H4). The impact of content marketing on marketing decision making in an e -commerce
environment is positive.

Marketing decision-making
Marketing decision -making is the process in which managers identify opportunities/problems,
measure the alternatives and take actions (Gao et al., 2026). According to Nordin and Ravald (2023),
there are three fundamental behavioural attributes of contempor ary marketing decision -making
agility, inventiveness, and reflexiveness (the AIR model), which the authors observe is a complex, data-
sensitive, and dynamic environment. Digital marketing technologies have facilitated more agile and
reflexive decisions yet  brought the necessity of organizations to operate under cognitive load,
ambiguity, and conflicting data streams (Nordin and Ravald, 2023). Other works point to the fact that
the quality of decisions is not only the availability of data and models but also  sense-making by
managers, the interpretability of results, as well as processes of transformation within the
organization, turning insights into actions (Wierenga, 2011; Nordin and Ravald, 2023).
The decision problems of e -commerce are commonly high frequency and operational (campaign
adjustments, pricing, inventory allocation) and strategic (customer lifetime value management,
marketplace strategy) problems. Due to it, MkIS, AI tools, email and co ntent metrics are especially
useful when they reduce the response time to signal (Cruz & Rosario, 2025; Monshaat, 2024).

Marketing data analytics
Marketing data analytics is composed of descriptive, predictive, prescriptive analytics and human and
technical abilities to apply them. An emerging literature also describes analytics as an aptitude that
constrains the extent to which technology and tacti cs can be converted into the quality of decisions
(Cruz & Rosario, 2025; Petrescu and Krishen, 2024). In other words: MkIS, AI, email and content
marketing generate data and signals; marketing analytics identify the extent to which organizations
can derive signal out of noise, trust outputs, and put recommendations into practice.
Bibliometric and systematic reviews demonstrate that companies with higher analytics achieve a
higher performance improvement of AI and MkIS investments (Cruz & Rosario, 2025). Research articles
about e-commerce and retail surveys confirm that analytics competency enhances the effectiveness of
personalized email and programmatic AI campaigns in terms of conversion and ROI, whereas the
misinterpretation and strategy of suboptimal decisions occurs in case of poor analytics governance
(Grandhi et al., 2021; Cruz and Rosario, 2025; Haleem et al., 2022).

Research Gap
In all the reviewed literature, one common theme is that information is not enough, but value arises
where information systems (MkIS), analytic capabilities, and digital marketing tactics are combined in
the process of organizational decision making. AI should be regarded as a tool that boosts information
processing; email and content marketing can be considered valuable sources of data that feed MkIS,
marketing analytics is the competency that transforms data into action taken by management
(Haleem et al., 2022; Rosario, 2021; Nobile and Cantoni, 2023; Zhang and Zhang, 2024; Cruz and
Rosario, 2025).
Although it has been well-researched, there are three gaps that are evident and inspire your research:
(1)integration gap — most of the literature focuses on MkIS, AI, email or content marketing in isolation;
(2)moderation gap - even though a theoretical argument suggests analytics is an enabler,
(3)contextual gap - literature on such integrations is limited in high -growth emerging markets ( e -
commerce ecosystems in Saudi Arabia) despite significant local investment in digital technologies (BCG,
2021).
These gaps are directly focused on by your research that amalgamates MkIS, AI strategies, email,
content marketing, and the moderating effect of analytics in the Saudi e-commerce environment.

Research Methodology
In the current research, the chosen study design is quantitative and cross-sectional in order to explore
the impact of the Marketing Information Systems (MkIS), AI -based marketing strategies, email
marketing, and content marketing on the decision-making in marketing and test the moderating effect
of the marketing data analytics. The quantitative methodology was used to test hypothesis based on
theories and test the relationships between various latent constructs. Due to the predictive and
complex nature of the proposed research framework, the PLS-SEM was used with the help of SmartPLS
4 that are used in exploratory and theory-extension research in marketing and information systems.

Research Context and Sampling
The empirical setting of the research is the Saudi Arabian e -commerce industry, with key trading and
digital platforms, being the main subject of the research due to the fast expansion of e -commerce,
high-quality digital infrastructure, and Vision 2030 pro jects. The target group included marketing and
decision-making professionals such as marketing managers, digital marketing executives, CRM and
analytics experts, e -commerce platform managers, and top executives who are in charge of the
marketing strategy f ormulation and implementation. The purposive sampling method was also
employed so that the respondents would have a required knowledge in the field of MkIS, digital
marketing tools, and data-oriented decision-making.
The collected and analyzed 379 valid responses met the requirements of PLS -SEM sample size based
on the ten -times rule and consideration of the statistical power. The sample structure is consistent
with the previous marketing analytics and e-commerce research studies using Smart PLS.

Data Collection and Measurement
A structured and self-administered survey  questionnaire were  used to collect data through electronic
means, specifically, professional networks, industry associations, LinkedIn groups, and contacts
organizations. The anonymity and confidentiality of the respondents were also established to ensure
that the respondents provided unbiased and honest answers. The pilot test of the questionnaire was
conducted on the e-commerce marketing professionals, which led to minor changes in the wording but
not the structure of the construct.
The measurement items were based on the previous literature that validated the scales: MkIS
(Gounaris et al., 2007), email marketing (Ligaraba et al., 2023), AI-based marketing and marketing data
analytics (Cao et al., 2022), and marketing decision -making (Berthon et al., 2001). Measures of all
constructs were on a five point Likert scale and procedural and statistical remedies were used to deal
with common method bias.

Data Analysis
The analysis of the data was conducted in two steps of PLS -SEM. Measurement model was initially
evaluated on reliability and validity based on the Cronbach alpha, composite reliability, average
variance extracted (AVE), cross -loadings, and Fornell -Larcker, which were evaluated on the
measurement model. The structural model was then tested based on the path coefficients, coefficient
of determination (R 2 ) and bootstrapping (5,000 resamples) in order to test the direct and moderating
effects. This has a meth odological rigor and improves the credibility of the results in the dynamic
environment of e-commerce in Saudi Arabia.

Conclusion of the Study
The aim of the current research was to investigate the role of the marketing intelligence tools
incorporation of Marketing Information Systems (MkIS) and the impact of these tools on marketing
decisions in the e -commerce industry with specific focus on com panies in Saudi Arabia. Based on a
strong data sample of 379 e -commerce marketing practitioners and using PLS -SEM, the study offers

empirical conclusion of the straightforward and mediating outcomes of major digital marketing
constructs on managerial choice-making.
Solving the first objective, findings prove that Marketing Information Systems play a great and positive
role in marketing decision-making. This reiterates the age old applicability of structured, trusted, and
integrated marketing information systems that would continue to provide evidence -based decisions,
despite an extremely digitalized environment. The outcome correlates with the previous studies that
indicate that MkIS can increase the quality of decisions making them more accessible, accurate, and
more confident by managers (Gounarios et al., 2007; Rosario, 2021).
Regarding the second goal, the use of AI in marketing strategies was discovered to have a substantial
impact on marketing decisions. This is an indication of the increasing role of artificial intelligence,
automation and predictive analytics in making quic ker and more precise decisions in e -commerce
scenarios. The result is consistent with the current studies focusing on the importance of AI in
processing big data of marketing and creating valuable insights (Miklosik and Evans, 2020; Haleem et
al., 2022).
The third goal was the role of email marketing and the findings reveal a positive impact of high
significance on marketing decisions. This is an indication that when used as a promotional tool, opt -in
and personalized email campaigns in addition to that ar e also important sources of customer
intelligence useful in informing tactical and strategic decisions. This observation is in line with the
recent evidence of the analytical and behavioural relevance of email marketing (Ligaraba et al., 2023).
In its turn, the fourth objective, content marketing, did not portray a meaningful direct effect on the
marketing decision -making process. Although it is well -known that content marketing is associated
with brand building and customer engagement, the outcomes indicate that the impact of the practice
on managerial decision-making can be indirect or long-term and depends on highly developed analytics
and attribution systems (Koob, 2021). The research is especially applicable in the context of Saudi e -
commerce, where the effectiveness of the content can rely on cultural alignment and interpretive
analytics significantly.
Lastly, the fifth objective investigated the moderating effect of marketing data analytics. In spite of the
marketing data analytics showing a large direct influence on marketing decision -making, all of the
interaction effects were not statistically signif icant. This implies that as of now analytics is an
independent decision-enabling feature and not conditional enhancer of a certain marketing tool. The
same has been observed in recent research, which states that the maturity of analytics and the level
of integration will dictate the moderating or extending supportive nature of analytics (Cao et al., 2022;
Cruz and Rosario, 2025).
All in all, the research has enhanced the marketing and decision -making literature, since it empirically
justifies a combined model of marketing systems and digital tactics in a new e -commerce market.
Regarding management, the results highlight the fact th at advanced technologies, including AI and
analytics, are essential but cannot be used strategically without being integrated systematically with
MkIS but not implemented independently. In the case of Saudi e -commerce companies, this
strengthens the necess ity to advance towards greater analytical maturity and closer dependence of
technology, intelligence and decision processes.

empirically confirms an integrated model in which Marketing Information Systems (MkIS), AI -based
marketing strategies, email marketing, content marketin g, and marketing data analytics are
interconnected to marketing decision-making. These constructs have been primarily studied separately
in previous research; this study reveals the influence of conventional marketing apparatus and the
modern digital systems on the overall managerial decision -making process. The enormous impacts of
MkIS and AI-driven marketing strategies reconfirm both the system and technology-enabled decisions
theories, and it is important to note that the quality of decision making is enhanced when structured
information systems are supplemented with intelligent, forecasting technologies (Gounaris et al., 2007;
Miklosik and Evans, 2020).
Notably, the study adds to the literature on analytics by demonstrating that marketing data analytics
has a direct impact on but not moderating effect on marketing decision-making. This conclusion makes
assumptions of analytics being a universal enhancer o f the effectiveness of marketing tools incorrect
and implies that theoretical use of analytics can be conditioned by the level of analytics maturity,
integration, and prescriptive power. The direct effect of content marketing is also not significant,
furthering the current theories since it points to the possibility of content -based strategies having a
direct impact on decisions at longer time frames or indirect, instead of instant managerial results.
Managerial Implications
The findings are effective strategic advice to managers and practitioners working in the Saudi Arabian
e-commerce industry. To begin with, the high pressure by Marketing Information Systems is a reminder
of the importance of investing in an integrated, rel iable and real -time marketing information
infrastructure. System integration is what needs to be prioritized among the managers in terms of CRM,
digital platforms, and analytics dashboards that can be used to facilitate faster and more confident
decision-making.
Second, the considerable importance of AI -based marketing strategies implies that the electronic
commerce companies must not stop at the experimental usage of AI but implement AI -based insights
into the daily marketing activities, i.e., targeting, pricing, and optimization of the campaigns. Likewise,
the effectiveness of email marketing with its positive aspect emphasizes the worth of the person -
centered, opt-in communication as a source of revenue and sources of intelligence.
Its findings are also indicative of a warning sign in relation to content marketing. Although the content
is still important in the engagement and brand equity, managers must not believe that it is directly
informing the decision-making, as it should be backed by strong measurement and attribution models.

Lastly, marketing data analytics cannot be considered a moderating influence, which means that

On a larger scale, the results have a policy implication on policymakers and ecosystem facilitators of
the digital transformation agenda of Saudi Arabia in terms of Vision 2030. The findings highlight the
necessity to implement capability -building programs  not only related to the implementation of

marketing decisions in e -commerce as opposed to an individual adoption of digital tools. Through
