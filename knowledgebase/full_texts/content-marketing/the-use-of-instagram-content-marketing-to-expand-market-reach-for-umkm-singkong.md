Abstract: This study aims to analyze the effectiveness of content marketing strategies through
Instagram social media in increasing competitiveness and market reach in local food MSMEs. The case
study was conducted at Argotelo Cheese Cassava MSME in Salatiga. The re search method employed
is descriptive qualitative with a case study approach, which involved observing digital marketing
activities, conducting in-depth interviews with key stakeholders, reviewing digital documentation, and
analyzing Instagram insight data. The results indicate that attractive and consistent visual content plays
a significant role in boosting engagement and strengthening brand identity. By leveraging Instagram’s
features, Argotelo Cheese Cassava MSME has been able to maintain audience inter est and develop a
strong online presence. The study also highlights the importance of content storytelling, audience
response, and visual consistency as key elements in fostering long -term relationships with followers
and customers. These factors contribute not only to increased customer loyalty but also to expanding
the MSME’s market reach. Furthermore, the research reveals that data -driven decision -making in
content creation, based on Instagram insights, allows the MSME to refine its strategies and engage  its
target market more effectively. The consistent use of high -quality images, informative captions, and
timely interactions with the audience has proved beneficial in improving the MSME's visibility. In
conclusion, a creative, consistent, and data -driven content marketing strategy on Instagram can
significantly enhance the digital presence, visibility, and competitiveness of MSMEs, helping them to
stand out in the competitive food industry market. This study contributes to understanding how local
MSMEs can leverage social media platforms like Instagram to effectively compete and expand their
market reach.

1. Introduction
In today's digital era, social media has become one of the most effective and efficient
marketing tools for businesses, including Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs).
The utilization of social media such as Instagram allows businesses to reach a wider audience
without geographical limits and at a relatively low cost (Sihura, 2025). Therefore, the presence
of social media is not only a means of communication, but also an important marketing
strategy in strengthening brand image and increasing sales. Businesses can introduce their
products or services to  potential audiences by building profiles on social media platforms
(Permadi, 2022). In addition, MSMEs can tailor their marketing strategies to the
demographics, interests, and behaviors of their audience through social media (Dhani and
Widya, 2024).
The importance of MSMEs' presence in the digital world lies in their need to increase
product visibility among an increasingly online -connected audience. MSMEs that are active
in the online world and actively engage on various social media platforms and are ab le to

are still a number of MSMEs that are lagging behind in adopting information technology.

Ledok Village, Argomulyo Subdistrict, which is known as one of the cassava production
centers in Salatiga City, has significant potential in the development of cassava -based
processed industries. The abundance of high -quality raw materials has encouraged the
emergence of various MSMEs that focus on cassava processing, including the production of
ready-to-eat cassava with various toppings which has become one of the region's leading
products. This potential needs to be supported by an effective marketing strategy to maximize
the economic value generated.
Singkong Keju Argotelo is one of the MSME players in Salatiga City that has focused
on the production of processed frozen cassava since 2016. By using high -quality local a nd
non-local cassava raw materials and a well -maintained production process, this MSME has
succeeded in producing approximately 25  frozen cassava products  with distinctive flavors
that are in demand by the audience. Argotelo Cheese Cassava MSME has start ed the
digitalization step by utilizing social media for marketing. The use of social media is a positive
effort to adapt to the times and market needs (Amali et al., 2025) . However, in practice, the
digital marketing strategy implemented has not shown ma ximum performance. Uploaded
content is still inconsistent in attracting the attention of a wide audience, marketing messages
do not fully reflect the advantages of the product, and interaction with the audience is still
low. As a result, Argotelo Cheese Cassava's market reach has not developed optimally, despite
its presence on digital platforms. This study aims to analyze the content marketing strategy
implemented on Instagram and evaluate its effectiveness on audience visibility and
engagement.

2. Proposed Method
This research uses a qualitative approach to deeply understand the application of content
marketing through Instagram social media in expanding the market reach of Argotelo Cheese
Cassava MSMEs in Salatiga. This approach was chosen to explore the  social phenomena,
behaviors, and strategies of MSME players in utilizing digital media as a marketing tool.
Qualitative research allows researchers to gain a contextual understanding of the experiences,
meanings, and interpretations of research subjects i n real situations (Creswell, 2014). This
research does not aim to measure or test hypotheses quantitatively, but rather describes the
reality and dynamics of using social media as a promotional medium.
This research is descriptive qualitative, so this research will explain narratively and
analytically the content marketing strategy applied, the type of content used, and the

audience's response to the content uploaded through the Instagram account. The research
also looks at the impact of these strategie s on increasing market reach, both in terms of the
number of followers, audience engagement, and general product demand. Social media such
as Instagram has become an important tool in the digital marketing strategy of MSMEs due
to its ability to reach a wide and fast audience (Kotler & Keller, 2016).
Data collection was carried out through in-depth interviews with the owner or manager
of Argotelo Cheese Cassava MSMEs, direct observation of uploads on Instagram accounts,
and documentation of digital promotio n activities. Interviews are used to explore
understanding, motivation, and marketing communication strategies used. Observations were
made to see the frequency of uploads, content variations such as photos, videos, or captions,
the use of hashtags, and in teractions between business accounts and users. Documentation
served as a complement to strengthen the data obtained from interviews and observations.
Data analysis used an interactive model from Miles and Huberman (2014) consisting of
three stages, namely  data reduction, data presentation, and conclusion drawing. Data
reduction was carried out by sorting out relevant information from the results of interviews
and observations and then grouping them according to themes. Data presentation was
organized in the form of narratives, tables, or pictures to facilitate understanding. Conclusions
were drawn by interpreting the data that had been collected to answer the research focus,
namely how content marketing on Instagram can expand the market reach of MSMEs.
Through this qualitative approach, the research is expected to provide a complete picture
of the effectiveness of content marketing on social media and provide practical benefits for
other MSME players in utilizing digital platforms (Kaplan and Haenlein, 2010).

4. Results and Discussion
4.1. Results
4.1.1 Content Marketing Strategy Implemented
Based on interviews with MSME owners, it is known that the main strategies applied in
content marketing include the use of attractive visuals, product storytelling, and c onsistency
in building brand image. The content uploaded is not only in the form of product photos, but
also includes videos of the production process, customer testimonials, and light interactions
such as quizzes or giveaways.
The social media account use d for marketing, Instagram @argotelo_singkongkeju,
adopts an educational and emotional content approach. Educative in the sense of providing
information about product excellence, natural raw materials, and unique flavors, while
emotional is related to the story of the origin of the business, the struggle of the owner, and
the local values that want to be highlighted. This approach is in line with Kotler and Keller's
(2016) view that consumers today do not only buy products, but also experiences and values
offered by brands.

4.1.2 Content Type and Audience Engagement
From the results of two months of observation, it was found that the most effective type
of content in increasing audience engagement is short video content such as Instagram Reels
that show behind-the-scenes of the production process or customer expressions when tasting
products. This kind of content gets a high response in the form of likes, comments, and re -
shares by followers of accounts. On the other hand, static posts such as product pho tos
without strong narratives or captions tend to attract less attention.
This finding supports previous research by Kaplan and Haenlein (2010) who emphasized
the importance of originality and visual appeal in social media content. Interactivity is key, as
accounts that reply to comments, address audiences through Instagram Stories, or respond
actively to DMs will build stronger emotional connections with customers.
4.1.3 Frequency and Consistency of Posts
From the content documentation, it was noted that the frequency of uploads ranged
from 3-5 times per week, with a consistent upload time in the afternoon, between 16.00-18.00
WIB. This time selection is recognized by MSME players as a strategy based on the habits of
audiences who actively use Instagram after work or school hours. Consistency in uploading
content signals to the Instagram algorithm that the account is active, thus increasing the
chances of appearing in the feed and explore other users.
This frequency and consistency is an important factor in exp anding market reach
because Instagram will more often recommend active content to other users, both through
the exploration feature and similar account recommendations. This is in accordance with the
views of Hootsuite (2022) which states that the Instagram algorithm prioritizes accounts that
are regular and relevant in presenting their content.
4.2. Discussion
The findings of this study indicate that the content marketing strategy implemented by
Argotelo Cheese Cassava MSMEs through the Instagram platform is able to make a positive
contribution in expanding market reach. In the context of MSMEs that have limitations in
terms of budget promotion and market access, social media such as Instagram is an effective
alternative because of its low cost, wide reach,  and allows for two-way communication with
the audience. Social media is not only used as an information medium, but also as a tool for
interaction, promotion, and even transactions. This is in line with Mangold and Faulds' (2009)
view that social media is  an important part of the modern integrated marketing
communications system.
One of the main strengths of Argotelo Cheese Cassava's content marketing
implementation is the consistency and creativity in managing the Instagram account. The
uploaded content not only serves to introduce the product visually, but also strengthens brand
storytelling. Stories about the production process, quality of ingredients, business history, and
local values are able to create an emotional bond between the brand and the audie nce. This

strategy emphasizes that content marketing is not only about "selling" products, but also
building trust, brand identity, and audience engagement (Pulizzi, 2014).
In practice, Argotelo Cheese Cassava MSMEs utilize various types of content such as
product photos, short videos, customer testimonials, to quizzes and giveaways to attract
audience attention. However, this study found that video-based content, especially those that
highlight the emotional side or uniqueness of the production process, ha s a higher level of
engagement than ordinary visual content. This is in line with Kaplan and Haenlein's (2010)
study, which states that audiovisual content that is authentic and contains narrative elements
tends to be more effective in building relationships with digital audiences.
In terms of frequency and consistency, Argotelo Cheese Cassava MSMEs show an
understanding that activeness on social media plays an important role in maintaining account
visibility in front of the Instagram algorithm. The algorithm tends to prioritize content from
accounts that are active, interactive and consistent in sharing relevant content. In this context,
maintaining an upload pattern of 3-5 times per week is an appropriate strategy, while showing
an effort to adapt to the way social media works.
Another finding of this study revealed that the expansion of market reach is not only
measured by the number of Instagram account followers, but also by the increase in demand
for products from outside the Salatiga area. This proves that social media has transformed
into a medium for information distribution and promotion that transcends geographical
boundaries. The interaction of customers from surrounding big cities facilitated by the
presence of engaging content shows that social m edia can be a catalyst for the growth of
locally-based small businesses. This finding strengthens the argument that content marketing
through social media plays a role in the market expansion process, especially for MSMEs that
have limitations in reaching new markets through conventional methods (Tiago and
Veríssimo, 2014).
However, this research also found that there are challenges faced in the practice of
content marketing. One of them is limited resources, both in terms of time, energy, and
expertise in creating creative and relevant content. In addition, MSME owners do not fully
understand the use of Instagram's analytics feature to evaluate content performance and
adjust future strategies. This means that although social media has been utilized quit e
optimally, there is still room for strengthening digital capacity, especially in terms of media
literacy and digital data management. This is in line with the results of research by Chatterjee
& Kar (2020), which shows that the ability to analyze and uti lize digital insights is key in
increasing the effectiveness of content marketing campaigns.
This research also shows that in the context of small businesses, marketing is not limited
to one -way communication. Instead, interactivity and emotional engagemen t with the
audience are added values that can differentiate one brand from another. Active engagement
such as replying to comments, creating polls, or resharing customer content is a new way of
creating long-term customer relationships. In general, this research confirms that the success

5. Conclusions
This research shows that a targeted,  consistent, and adaptive digital content approach
has an important role in supporting the marketing success of MSMEs in the digital era.
Strategies designed with audience preferences, visual trends and narrative strengths in mind
are proven to increase content reach and build stronger engagement with audiences. Through
content management that includes data-driven planning, production and evaluation, Argotelo
Cheese Cassava MSME has significantly improved the performance of its Instagram account.
The most effective types of content are found in the format of reels and storytelling, which
are able to connect with audiences emotionally and strengthen the brand image as an authentic
local product. In addition, a consistent visual identity also strengthens brand  recall and
differentiation amidst market competition. The results of this study confirm that achieving a
wider market reach by MSMEs depends not only on the presence of social media, but also
on the right content strategy and responsive digital management. Thus, a social media-based
content marketing strategy can be an effective and sustainable solution for MSMEs in facing
modern marketing challenges and increasing their competitiveness digitally.
