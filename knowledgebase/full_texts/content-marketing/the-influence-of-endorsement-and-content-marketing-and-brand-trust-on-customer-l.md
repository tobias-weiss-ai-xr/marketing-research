 Rapid progress in the field of e-commerce in Indonesia has made
competition between platforms increasingly fierce, so consumer
loyalty is important for business sustainability. Shopee, as a well-
known e-commerce platform in Indonesia, has problems in
maintaining buyer loyalty when there are so many marketplace
choices. This research was created to examine the effects of
endorsements, content marketing, and brand trust on consumer
loyalty on the Shopee platform. The research method used is
quantitative with a survey method. Data was obtained from the
distribution of questionnaires to 135 respondents, namely active
Shopee users in Cirebon City, who were selected using the purposive
sampling technique. Data analysis was carried out using the Partial
Least Squares–Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) technique
using the SmartPLS application. The results of the measurement
model test show that all constructs have met the requirements for
validity and reliability. The results of the structural model analysis
indicate that endorsements have a good and important impact on
buyer loyalty. Content marketing also shows a good and important
impact on buyer loyalty, with the greatest impact compared to other
variables. In addition, brand trust has been proven to have a good and
important impact on Shopee user loyalty. A determination coefficient
(R²) value of 0.575 indicates that endorsement, content marketing,
and brand trust may explain shopper loyalty in the moderate group.
The findings of this study show that e-commerce user loyalty is
influenced by effective digital marketing communication strategies,
supported by relevant content and consumer trust levels in brands.

INTRODUCTION
The development of Indonesia's digital economy has increased rapidly, especially due to
the development of the e-commerce sector. Indonesia now has more than 220 million active
internet users, and more than 85% of them have made online purchases. Shopee is the most
popular platform in Indonesia, with a market share of 37.9%. However, this rapid growth also
brings new challenges for industry players, especially in maintaining customer loyalty amid
increasingly fierce competition. (Social & Meltwater, 2024)
During the month of July from the 10th to the 26th, Populix conducted a survey of 1,005
people aged 18 to 44. Those who respond are active internet users and e-commerce customers.
Based on the survey, the e-commerce that is most remembered by the public is as follows:
Tokopedia 44%, Shopee 40%, and Lazada 11% show a high conversion rate in terms of
retention or conversion of usage over the past month. This shows that Tokopedia has the most
loyal users compared to Shopee and Lazada. Bukalapak, Blibli, and Tokopedia are also choices
for consumers regarding loyalty. Nonetheless, when it comes to attracting customers to try the
platform, Shopee is the best (Katadata.co.id).

Digitalization has changed consumer shopping patterns, which requires companies to be
adaptive in changing and developing their marketing strategies. In Indonesia, one of the
companies that has succeeded in strengthening its position in the market by implementing a
digital marketing strategy is Shopee, including influencer endorsements and content marketing.
According to Kotler and Keller, in more personalized marketing interactions and experiences,
there is more focus on marketing to build an emotional connection with consumers. In the
implementation of digital marketing, consumer loyalty is not only based on functional
satisfaction, but also on emotional attachment and a positive image of the company. Therefore,
formulating a marketing strategy that is not only feasible, but also able and effective to build
marketing relationships in the long term is a challenge for companies. (Kotler & Keller, 2022).
One of the most prominent strategies is Endorsement, where public figures or influencers
are involved to strengthen the image and appeal of the brand. Erdogan (2021) said that the
effectiveness of endorsements largely depends on how credible, attractive, and relevant the
endorsement is to the product being promoted. In the context of Shopee, the use of international
celebrities such as Jackie Chan and BLACKPINK has been shown to increase brand awareness
and customer engagement.
Content Marketing is part of Shopee's digital communication strategy, in addition to
support. Content Marketing is a technique related to the creation and distribution of content
that is useful and relevant for business interests and corporate purposes, to acquire and retain
customers, according to Pulizzi (2020). Shopee tries to use various formats, such as educational
videos, reviews, and MSME Success Stories, to gain customer emotions and trust. However,
from existing literature studies, the influence of content marketing on customer loyalty tends
to vary greatly. Found that while engaging content can increase engagement, without brand
trust, engaging content doesn't necessarily invite loyalty. (Ardiansyah & Nugroho, 2021)
Brand Trust is an important part of building customer loyalty. According to Fitriani et al.
(2023), Brand Trust is the belief that a brand can keep its promises and provide the value that
customers expect. In the context of e-commerce, this trust includes how secure the transaction
is, how genuine the product is, and how quickly complaints are handled. People are more likely
to buy from the platform again and tell others about it if they feel safe and trust it. However, a
report from Katadata (2024) states that Shopee still receives many complaints about products
that don't match descriptions, late deliveries, and fake stores that steal money. This situation
can make customers less trusting and make it difficult to build long-term loyalty. (Fitriani et
al., 2023)
Academic studies on customer loyalty in endorsements, content marketing, and brand
trust have largely disagreed. In the context of e-commerce in Indonesia, (Hapsari & Yuliani,
2022) found three factors that contribute positively and significantly to custom er loyalty.
However, research conducted by Syafitri, Wulandari, and Hartono (2023), Endorsement and
Content Marketing can only have a significant effect if mediated by Brand Trust. This means
that if both methods don't have an element of brand trust, they can't build customer loyalty. The
research's explanations contradict and point to many research shortcomings in the fast-growing
e-commerce industry such as Shopee. (Syafitri et al., 2023)
In addition, most previous research has focused on purchase intent as a dependent
variable, rather than on customer loyalty, which is characterized by long-term commitment (Ali
et al., 2022). On the other hand, research focusing on the interaction of the three variables of

support, content marketing, and brand trust in the context of e-commerce in Indonesia is still
relatively limited. In fact, customer loyalty is a sign of digital business success that is more
strategic than just increasing short-term sales. The integrity and capability of service providers
are two important things to build long-term relationships with customers. These findings
emphasize the importance of innovation in digital services and strengthening customer trust as
a key strategy to maintain loyalty in an increasingly competitive e-commerce environment.
This research is very important to overcome the identified gaps (Rizky Ramadhan, 2025).
The novelty of this research lies in its holistic and multidimensional perspective. Unlike
previous studies, this research investigates the integrative relationship between endorsement,
content marketing, and brand trust, positioning them as three distinct  but complementary
constructs that jointly influence customer loyalty. It looks at endorsement not just as a
promotional tool, but as a complete experience where the visual and informational stimulus of
the brand ambassador is inextricably linked with the participatory and responsive quality of the
content. This approach advances the application of consumer behavior theory by testing it in
the complex, multi-stimulus environment of e-commerce, proposing that both the marketing
communication and its interactive elements act as external stimuli.
This study seeks to contribute to the literature on digital consumer behavior theory by
examining the relationship between endorsement, content marketing, brand trust, and customer
loyalty. From this method, we aim to gain insight into customer loyalty in the internet age. In
addition, we are optimistic that this study will help Shopee management and e -commerce
practitioners to increase customer trust through proper communication and relevant content.
So, this research departs from the existing phenomenon, that e-commerce users in Indonesia
are increasing, but there are problems in building trust and maintaining customer loyalty. More
studies are still needed to be able to uncover the relationship between support, content
marketing, brand trust, and customer loyalty on Shopee. The results of previous studies show
that this approach can help build a trust-oriented digital marketing strategy and long-term
relationships between brands and consumers (Firmansyah et al., 2023).

METHOD
Research Approach
This research used a descriptive-verifiable quantitative approach. The goal is to examine
the relationships and influences between variables. In addition, it can result in a broader
generalization of the population because it tests hypotheses that have been made that are based
on previous theories and research (Sugiyono, 2019).
Population and Sample
Active Shopee users who live in Cirebon City are the subjects of this study. This study
uses the purposive sampling method to select a non -probability sample. This means that
researchers do not provide every person or member of the population with the same opportunity
to be a sample (Sugiyono, 2023).
According to Hair et al. (2017), the exact sample size is 5 –10 times the number of
indicators. The required sample number is 135 respondents, or 5 times 27 indicators. (Hair et
al., 2017)

Data Collection Techniques
The main data of the study was obtained through the distribution of online questionnaires
to respondents who met the set criteria. This questionnaire is sent through social media such as
WhatsApp and Instagram. In addition, as a supporting source, this study also uses secondary
data from scientific journals, reference books, media news, and other relevant sources.
(Indriantoro & Supomo, 2018)

Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) approach, which was assisted by SmartPLS software. This
method has the ability to analyze both measurement models (inner model) and stru ctural
models (outer model). In addition, PLS-SEM is considered appropriate for studies that have a

statistically significant. Endorsements have a positive and significant impact on Customer
Loyalty (T = 2,338; P = 0.019). Content Marketing also provides significant positive impact

declared accepted.
The Effect of Endorsements on Customer Loyalty
The results of the hypothesis test show that endorsements have a positive and significant
impact on customer loyalty of Shopee platform users in Cirebon City. So, the formulation of
the H1 problem is accepted. The results of the Endorsement hypothesis test  on Customer
Loyalty stated that this variable had a major influence on customer loyalty, with a p-value of
less than 0.05 (0.019) and a T-statistic of 2.338.This shows that the use of an endorser that is
credible, attractive, reputable and in accordance with Shopee's brand image can create a

customer brand awareness and improve customer brand attitudes, resulting in repeat purchases
and long-term commitments.
The findings of this study are in line with Lim et al. (2023) and Raissa et al. (2025) who
found that Endorsements (influencers) have a positive and significant impact on customer

that endorsements are an effective digital marketing strategy to build customer loyalty for

The Influence of Content Marketing on Customer Loyalty

This research is in line with the research of Pulizzi (2022), Al Qardhawi & Ridanasti

The Influence of Brand Trust on Customer Loyalty
Hypothetical research shows that Brand Trust positively and significantly affects
Customer Loyalty of Shopee users in Cirebon City. Thus, H3 is acceptable. This is evidenced
by p-value = 0.002 (<0.05) and T-statistic = 3.103 which shows that brands have a significant
influence on consumer loyalty. Consumer loyalty Showing that Shopee has brand trust

This finding is also in line with Hosna and Insiyah (2023) and Nguyen et al. (2022) who

This shows that the higher the brand trust that customers have towards Shopee, the higher the
loyalty consumers have to Shopee.

CONCLUSION
The results of the study show that endorsements, content marketing, and brand trust
partially and significantly affect customer loyalty on the Shopee platform. These findings
confirm that credible digital marketing strategies and customer perceptions of trust in brands
affect customer loyalty in e-commerce. Endorsements have been proven to contribute
significantly to increased customer loyalty. Brand credibility, appeal, and trust are also
influenced by Compared to other factors; content marketing has the greatest influence on
customer loyalty. This shows that relevant, informative, consistent, and interactive content has
a strategic role in increasing customer engagement and building long-term relationships with
brands. These results show that content marketing is the main factor that determines customer

1805
loyalty in a competitive and digital-based e-commerce environment. In addition, it is proven
that brand trust has a positive and significant impact on customer loyalty. Brand trust helps
lower risk perception and increase customer commitment on e-commerce platforms. This is
due to customer confidence in transaction security, system reliability, and service consistency.
According to the R-square value of 0.575, endorsements, content marketing, and brand trust
can explain customer loyalty with a moderate level of predictive ability. This suggests that,
while all three factors have a significant influence, there are other factors that can affect
customer loyalty. Therefore, to improve the empirical model of e-commerce customer loyalty,
further research should include additional variables such as e-service quality, customer
satisfaction, or user experience
