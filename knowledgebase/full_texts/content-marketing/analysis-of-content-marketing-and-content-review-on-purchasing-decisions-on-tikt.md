ABSTRACT
Advancements in digital technology have transformed how consumers engage with brands and make purchasing
decisions. TikTok, especially popular among Generation Z, has emerged as a leading platform for implementing
content marketing and content review strategies. This study investigates the influence of content marketing and
content review on purchasing decisions, mediated by Electronic Word of Mouth (E-WOM). Using a quantitative
approach, data were collected through surveys involving 100 active TikTok users from Generation Z in Bekasi
Regency. The analysis was conducted using Partial Least Squares (PLS) with the SmartPLS software. The
findings indicate that both content marketing and content review have a significant impact on E-WOM and
purchasing decisions. Furthermore, E-WOM effectively mediates the relationship between these variables and
purchasing decisions. These results underscore the strategic importance of creating relevant and engaging
content to foster consumer interaction and influence purchasing decisions. The study contributes to the
academic literature on digital marketing, particularly in understanding the behavior of Generation Z on social
media platforms. Overall, combining content-based strategies with the power of E-WOM can enhance
marketing effectiveness and drive consumer purchase decisions more efficiently.
Keywords: Content Marketing, Content Review, Electronic Word of Mouth (E-WOM), Purchase Decision,
TikTok

2. LITERATURE REVIEW
Purchasing Decisions
Purchasing decisions refer to the process by which consumers evaluate and select
from a set of alternatives to fulfill their needs and desires. This process typically involves
several stages: need recognition, information search, evaluation of alternatives,
decision-making, and post-purchase evaluation. Each stage represents a cognitive and
behavioral response to marketing stimuli and personal preferences.(PERILAKU
KONSUMEN, n.d.) According to Sumarwan, purchasing decisions reflect consumers'
direct involvement in obtaining and utilizing goods or services offered in the market
(Sumarwan, 2003). These decisions are rarely made spontaneously. Instead, individuals
tend to consider multiple internal and external factors, such as product features, price,
brand reputation, and peer opinions.
Kotler and Armstrong explain that purchasing decisions are part of a broader problem-solving

process, where individuals identify their needs and evaluate available alternatives to choose
the most appropriate product or service (Kotler & Armstrong, 2012). The process is also

and attitude. In the current digital environment, this decision-making journey is becoming
increasingly influenced by online content, product reviews, and digital word-of-mouth, which
provide consumers with more diverse and comprehensive information sources than ever

Previous studies have supported the importance of these stages. Sri et al. found that

evaluate options before making a purchase (Br Marbun et al., 2022). This suggests that
purchasing decisions today are increasingly influenced by online media and social
interaction.
Electronic Word Of Mouth
 Electronic Word of Mouth (e-WOM) refers to the process by which consumers share
information, opinions, and personal experiences regarding products or services through
digital channels such as social media, online reviews, blogs, and discussion forums. Unlike
traditional word of mouth, e-WOM has the potential to reach a much larger audience in a
shorter period. According to Litvin, Goldsmith, and Pan, e-WOM is any informal
communication directed at consumers through internet-based technology regarding the use or
characteristics of particular goods and services (Litvin et al., 2008). This form of
communication has gained popularity due to its perceived credibility and the increasing trust
consumers place in peer recommendations over brand advertisements.
 Erkan and Evans emphasize that e-WOM plays a key role in shaping consumer behavior,
particularly in the stages of information evaluation and purchase intention (Erkan & Evans,
2016). Consumers tend to perceive online reviews, user comments, and influencer
testimonials as more authentic and credible than paid promotional content. As a result,
e-WOM influences consumer awareness, attitudes, preferences, and even loyalty, making it a
powerful component in digital marketing strategies, especially for platforms like TikTok,
where user-generated content can quickly go viral and influence trends.
 Supporting this, Wardhana et al. (2019) revealed that e-WOM significantly influences
consumer awareness, perceived trust, and purchase intention, especially on social media
platforms such as TikTok and Instagram. Wijaya et al. showed that digital reviews and
informal consumer conversations can shape brand image and purchasing confidence (Wijaya
et al., 2022). These findings suggest that companies must actively manage online word of
mouth, as it can significantly impact a product's market success.

Content Review
Content Review is a product review that presents information about the product from a
consumer perspective and can serve as a recommendation from previous consumers (L. et al.,
2020). According to Marbun (2020), product reviews serve as a medium for consumers to
select and search for information that supports their purchasing decisions. (Sugiharto et al.,
2022) Content Review strongly refers to reviews or product evaluations made by users or
consumers who often share their experiences about a product or service. This content is
important because it influences consumer purchasing decisions. Authentic and honest reviews
on TikTok can provide social proof that strengthens a product's image in the eyes of potential
buyers.
 L. et al. emphasize that content reviews function as a recommendation system from past
users and can directly affect product popularity and sales (L. et al., 2020). Sugiharto et al.
support this by demonstrating that product evaluations on TikTok have a significant influence

before making purchases (Sugiharto et al., 2022). These findings suggest that user reviews
not only validate product quality but also create a sense of community, enhancing the
emotional connection between consumers and brands.

3. METHODS
This study employed a descriptive quantitative approach to examine the causal
relationships among the variables of content marketing, content review, Electronic Word of
Mouth (E-WOM), and purchase decisions. A survey method was used for data collection,
utilizing a structured questionnaire as the primary research instrument. Data analysis was
conducted using Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) based on Partial Least Squares (PLS)
with the assistance of SmartPLS software version 3.2.9.
The population of this study consisted of active TikTok users who belong to
Generation Z (individuals born between 1997 and 2012) and reside in Bekasi Regency. The
sample comprised 100 respondents, selected using purposive sampling with specific inclusion
criteria: aged between 18 and 26 years, actively using the TikTok application for at least one
hour per day over the past three months, having purchased a product promoted through
TikTok, and residing in Bekasi Regency.
The data collection technique involved distributing an online questionnaire via
Google Forms to respondents who met the inclusion criteria. The questionnaire consisted of
two sections: demographic information and statements related to the research variables,
measured using a 4-point Likert scale (1 = strongly disagree to 4 = strongly agree). To ensure
the quality of the instrument, validity and reliability tests were performed. Validity was
assessed using Average Variance Extracted (AVE), where an AVE value above 0.5 indicates
good convergent validity. Reliability was measured using Cronbach's Alpha and Composite
Reliability (CR), with a threshold value above 0.7 indicating that the constructs are reliable.
The data analysis was conducted in two stages: outer model testing and inner model
testing. The outer model assessed how well the indicators reflected their corresponding latent
constructs. In contrast, the inner model evaluated the relationships between latent constructs
by analyzing R-squared values and path coefficients. Hypothesis testing was performed using
the bootstrapping method to obtain t-statistics and p-values. The criteria for significance were
a t-statistic > 1.96 and a p-value < 0.05, indicating that the hypothesis has a statistically
significant effect.

on personal experience are considered more credible by consumers. The review's authenticity
fosters greater trust in the product, thereby encouraging other consumers to share their
experiences through digital interactions, such as comments, re-uploads, or reaction videos.
