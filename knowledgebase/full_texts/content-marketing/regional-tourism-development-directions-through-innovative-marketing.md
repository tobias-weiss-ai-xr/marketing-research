Abstract. This article reviews the role and practical effectiveness of innovative marketing
technologies in the development of the regional tourism sector of Uzbekistan. In recent years, as
global tourism steadily recovers from the pandemic, digital technologies, AR /VR, IoT
infrastructures, big data analytics and digital platforms are becoming the main driving forces of
tourism services. The existing tourism potential, current problems and weaknesses in marketing
are systematically analyzed in the regions of the coun try, in particular in the Fergana Valley,
Samarkand, Bukhara, Khiva, Tashkent city and mountainous regions. The results of the research
show that the incomplete use of innovative marketing tools limits the competitiveness of regions
in the international ma rket. Also, introduction of AR/VR -based virtual tours, IoT infrastructure,
digital branding, mobile applications and content marketing to the regions can increase the flow
of tourists, improve service quality and increase investment attractiveness. In the article,
proposals for modernization of tourism infrastructure, creation of regional brands, strengthening
of digital marketing and expansion of smart services have been developed through the
implementation of innovative technologies.
Keywords: regional tourism, innovative marketing, AR/VR technologies, IoT
infrastructure, tourism branding, digital marketing, smart tourism, big data analytics, virtual
tours, regional competitiveness, tourism development in Uzbekistan.

Introduction.
In recent years, the tourism industry around the world has consistently recovered from
the consequences of the pandemic and is showing a steady growth rate. According to UN
Tourism (formerly UNWTO), international travel is expected to reach more than 1.4 b illion in
2024, nearly 99% of pre -pandemic 2019 levels. Compared to 2023, the flow of global travel
increased by 11%. About 790 million international tourists visited in January-July 2024, which
is 96% of the pre-pandemic figures (en.unwto-ap.org, 2025).
The reason for the recovery of tourism is the increase in international flights, the
expansion of visa-free regimes, the opening of new destinations, and the increased demand for
travel after the pandemic. Also, innovations such as digital technologies, smart services, AR/VR,
big data and IoT are becoming an important factor in the development of global tourism
services.
These global trends are also observed in Uzbekistan. According to the National Statistics

Today, Uzbekistan is one of the countries with huge tourist potential. There are about
8,400 objects of cultural heritage in the country, 209 of which are located in the territory of

addition, our nation's rich historical heritage, national values, customs and traditions, diverse
climatic conditions and unique natural landscapes serve as an attractive factor for foreign
tourists.
At the same time, insufficient development of marketing strategies and digital
promotion tools in the regions of the country hinders the rapid development of regional
tourism. In many areas, branding is weak, innovative technologies are underutilized, and online
platforms are not used effectively. This situation limits the competitiveness of regional tourism
in the global market.
Therefore, the effective use of innovative marketing technologies - IoT, AR/VR, mobile
applications, digital platforms and big data analysis - in the development of regional tourism is
an urgent issue.

Literature review.
In recent years, scientific research on the use of innovative technologies and digital
marketing tools in the tourism industry has been developing rapidly. World scientists point out
that AR/VR, IoT, Big Data, mobile applications and digital branding are i mportant factors in
increasing the competitiveness of tourism.
Tussyadiah (2020) noted in his research that virtual reality (VR) technologies have a
strong influence on the decision -making process of tourists, and 360℃ video and immersive
experiences significantly increase interest in a destination. Mariani, Perez, an d Wirtz (2022)
also support these views, stating that AR/VR technologies serve as an “emotional engagement
mechanism” in tourism marketing.
Astanakulov et.al.’s (2024) research on the impact of IoT technologies on tourism clearly
shows that smart infrastructure - automated ticketing system, QR -code guides, GPS -audio
guides and touch panels - can significantly improve the experience of tourists and increase the
efficiency of tourism flow management. IoT is also said to reduce maintenance costs by 15-25%.
In the field of digital marketing, research by Hannah, Kevin and Park (2021) shows that
the formation of digital branding through social networks fundamentally changes perceptions
of a destination. In their opinion, the visual content distributed on platforms such as Instagram,
YouTube, and TikTok directly affects the choice of destination by tourists and increases the
competitiveness of the region.
Research conducted on Uzbekistan also pays special attention to the role of innovative
marketing in regional tourism. Sokhibova (2024) studied the international experience of using
digital technologies in tourism and noted that AR/VR, mobile applications, and digital platforms
are not sufficiently implemented in the regions of Uzbekistan. Shukurov and Karimov (2022)
noted the importance of innovative approaches in the development of regional tourism and
emphasized the need to create a brand in the regions, expand information resources in English
and strengthen digital marketing.
Berdiyorov (2025) states that the main problems of regional tourism - transport and
logistics limitations, lack of personnel, weak branding and outdated infrastructure - can be
overcome through digital marketing tools. His research shows that digital platforms and media
branding also play an important role in attracting investment.
As well as, Li and Wang (2020) analyzed the economic impact of digital transformation
and scientifically proved that digital marketing is an important factor in increasing tourist

Summarizing the above scientific sources, innovative marketing is crucial in the

Research methodology.
This study used several scientific and analytical approaches to examine the role of
innovative marketing technologies in the development of regional tourism in Uzbekistan. First
of all, using the descriptive statistical method, key indicators such as the n umber of foreign
tourists, their geographical distribution, and the general dynamics of tourism development
were analyzed. This helped to identify the current situation and digital readiness of regional
tourism.
In addition, through regional descriptive analysis, the tourism conditions of major
regions such as Samarkand, Bukhara, Khiva, the Fergana Valley, Tashkent, and mountainous
areas were studied. This made it possible to determine the level of digital marketing, branding,
AR/VR usage, and smart service development in each region.
The study also applied a tabular analytical method, using the information summarized
in Table 1 and Table 2. These tables systematize regional tourism potential, existing marketing
problems, and possible applications of innovative technologies such as AR/V R, IoT, mobile
applications, and digital platforms.
Furthermore, through content analysis, scientific articles, national tourism programs,
digitalization initiatives, and reports of international organizations were reviewed. This made
it possible to assess global trends in innovative marketing and their applicability to Uzbekistan.
As a result, the integration of these methods allowed the study to identify the current
challenges of regional tourism, evaluate the potential of innovative marketing tools, and
develop scientifically grounded recommendations for enhancing the competitiven ess and
digital transformation of Uzbekistan’s regional tourism.

Analysis and discussion of results.
To develop regional tourism, the main attention should be paid to innovative marketing
research. In order to increase the quality of services by creating more jobs in the service sector
for the population of the region and subsequently attract more tourist s, it is necessary to
develop an effective marketing strategy.
Taking into account the Fergana valley is a densely populated region of Uzbekistan, rich
in tourism resources, distinguished by handicrafts, traditional art centers, natural parks and
ecotourism destinations. Nevertheless, the tourism potential of the vall ey has not been
adequately promoted internationally, brand building processes have not been systematically
implemented, and digital content is very limited. Therefore, it remains a difficult issue to fully
demonstrate the potential of existing tourist resources.
Samarkand, Bukhara and Khiva are considered the main tourist centers of Uzbekistan,
and these cities are in the leading position in terms of infrastructure, hotel system, museums,
preservation of historical monuments and quality of service. Despite high in ternational traffic
to these cities, lack of innovative marketing tools such as AR/VR technologies, smart guides,

prevents the tourism brand in cities from being fully competitive in the global market.
The city of Tashkent is the main transport, business and cultural center of the country,

However, there is no single branding strategy for urban tourism, smart serv ices and digital

In addition, the introduction of IoT technologies allows the creation of smart

Territorial branding is also one of the important areas of innovative marketing. It is
difficult for each region to stand out in the global market without a logo, motto and unified
visual identity. Therefore, it is necessary to create brand content consist ing of professional
photo and video materials, content emphasizing the unique aspects of the region and promote
it in the international information space. A strong brand creates a stable image in the mind of a

As well as, the improvement of mobile applications and digital platforms will bring the
level of service in the regions to a new level. A single reservation system covering several
regions, a route planning function, an event calendar, smart maps and onlin e guide services
make the travel process easier for tourists. Such systems allow providing customized offers to
tourists, online access to services, and integration of regional tourism services into a single
system.
Digital marketing is the most effective tool for promoting tourism globally. On social
networks such as Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, it is possible to quickly and accurately reach
the target audience through the creation of attractive content, cooperatio n with influencers,
SEO and contextual advertising. With the help of digital marketing, regions strengthen their
tourism brand, increase their visibility in the international market and significantly increase
the tourist flow.
The impact of innovative marketing technologies on regional tourism is manifested not
only by improving the quality of services, but also by significantly increasing economic
efficiency. Modern digital tools - AR/VR technologies, mobile applications, perso nalized
advertising, IoT systems and smart services - have a direct impact on increasing tourists'
spending on tourism. Virtual tours, interactive services and mobile platforms make the tourist's
travel process more interesting and convenient, and increase the possibility of using additional
services (guide, transport, souvenirs, excursions). This leads to an increase in the total income
of the regional economy.
Another important economic benefit of innovative marketing is the expansion of
opportunities to attract investments to the region. Regions with a strong brand, actively
promoted on digital platforms, and well-developed AR/VR content seem attractive to investors.
In such areas, private investment in infrastructure objects such as hotels, restaurants, cafes,
camping, entertainment centers and transport services will accelerate. Practical experience
shows that investment volume increases significantly in areas  with strong digital marketing
and branding.
At the same time, the introduction of IoT technologies into the tourism infrastructure
allows for cost optimization. Smart control systems can reduce energy consumption, water
consumption, lighting and maintenance costs by 15 -25%. This reduces the operatin g costs of
tourism facilities and increases the profitability of income. Because some of the staff's work is
automated, the service process is accelerated, resources are saved, and overall efficiency is
increased.
Innovative marketing also plays an important role in strengthening the competitiveness
of regions. Active advertising on digital platforms, promotion on social networks, content

Conclusion and suggestions.
A number of practical suggestions on the wide application of innovative marketing in
the development of regional tourism of Uzbekistan are put forward. First of all, it is desirable
to create a single digital platform at the state level, which will serve a s a single system that
combines tourist information, reservation systems, virtual tours, smart maps and online
services for all regions. Also, it is necessary to strengthen the support mechanisms of the state
in the form of marketing programs, media projects and exhibitions for the formation of regional
brands and their promotion at the level of international markets. Allocation of state grants for
startups related to innovative marketing, AR/VR projects, smart tourism services and mobile
application develo pers will accelerate digital transformation in the regions. In addition,
modernization of tourist infrastructure - improvement of roads, navigation systems, smart
information panels and transport services - serves as an important factor in increasing the
competitiveness of regions.
At the regional level, a number of tasks for the implementation of innovative
technologies should be solved. Creating virtual tours based on AR/VR technologies for the main
attractions of each region, organizing professional content centers, regularly prep aring photo
and video materials of the region, serves to strengthen tourism branding. Also, the gradual
introduction of IoT-based smart services - digital queue systems, touch information panels, GPS
audio guides - will create a comfortable environment for  tourists. The training of qualified
personnel in digital marketing and the organization of training courses in the regions will help
tourist organizations to adapt to the requirements of the digital market.
There are also a number of effective directions for the private sector. Creating mobile
applications for tourist services, developing interactive products in cooperation with AR/VR
services, introducing premium services in the hotel and service sector will  offer tourists more
quality and convenience. Also, organizing professional SMM campaigns on social networks,
establishing cooperation with influencers, and active use of digital advertising formats play an
important role in promoting regions on a global s cale. The gradual implementation of these
proposals will increase the competitiveness of regional tourism and significantly increase
economic efficiency.
The tourism potential of Uzbekistan is increasing year by year, the recovery of the global
tourism market after the pandemic and the rapid development of digital technologies make the
introduction of innovative marketing approaches in regional tourism a particularly urgent task.
The results of the research showed that innovative marketing serves not only to improve
tourism services, but also to significantly increase their economic efficiency. The use of AR/VR
technologies, IoT systems, big data analysis, m obile applications and digital advertising tools
can increase the flow of tourists, increase the recognition of regional brands and bring the
quality of services to a new level.
In order to increase the competitiveness of regional tourism in the coming years, it is
necessary to expand the digital infrastructure, support innovative startups, create a unique
tourist brand of each region, and accelerate the implementation of smart se rvices. Strategic
approaches based on innovative marketing serve as an important factor for strengthening the
