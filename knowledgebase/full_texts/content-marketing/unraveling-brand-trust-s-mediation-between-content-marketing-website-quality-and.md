Abstract
The shift in societal lifestyles toward internet -based information consumption must contend with the proliferation of hoax
content. Consequently, digital media companies must implement strategies to cultivate public trust, thereby enhancing brand
loyalty. This study analyzes the impact of content marketing and website quality on brand loyalty, with brand trust as a
mediator, using data from Indonesia’s leading digital news platform, Detikcom.  The analysis employs structural equation
modelling, partial least squares (SEM -PLS), mediation testing, and hypothesis testing. The findings reveals that while content
marketing does not directly affect brand loyalty, it significantly boosts brand trust, indirectly fostering loyalty. Brand tr ust has
the strongest influence (f² = 0.436) on loyalty. Website quality also directly enhances loyalty and trust, with an indirect effect
mediated by trust. Mediation tests show brand trust partially mediates the relationship (74.1% for content marketing; 60.1%
for website quality). Descriptive statistics (73.9%) rate the variables as ‘good,’ highlighting t he need for Detikcom to prioritize
trust-building through content and website improvements.

1.0 INTRODUCTION

The shift in society’s desire for information is closely tied to the growing digitalization. People use the internet to
search for information through trusted websites. Technological advancements have driven media convergence,
enabling broader access to di gital content through digital television, websites, and mobile applications. In
Indonesia, the number of internet users increased by 97 million between 2023 and now, totalling 5.35 billion users
(We Are Social, 2022). As many as 83.1% of internet users use the internet to find information. As many as 61.1%
of internet users aim to stay up-to-date with the latest news and events, and there is no doubt that an increasing
number of people are searching for information on line. Following the needs of users to search for information
online, Indonesia has several digital media platforms that the public can access .
The public increasingly relies on the internet to seek information through credible websites, thereby
driving the growth of digital competition aimed at fostering consumer engagement and trust (Hollebeek & Macky,
2019). Users who use the internet to search for information must face the spread of hoax news. According to
Nihayah & Adila (2020), a hoax is the dissemination of false information to deceive the public into not trusting an
individual, organization, or service provided by that individual or organization. This news can be disseminated
online or offline through various types of media.
Identified by the AIS Team of the Directorate General of Informatics Applications, Ministry of
Communication and Informatics, total hoax or false content is 11,642 for August 2018 - May 2023. The MASTEL
survey (2019) reported that the most common media for spreading hoaxes are chat applications, such as
WhatsApp, Line, and Telegram, at 67%, followed by websites at 28.20%. According to the Digital News Reports
2023 report, based on the type of media, 84% of survey resp ondents chose online media as their primary news
source. This data shows that the spread of hoax news has led to increasingly tight competition in digital media to

meet users’ needs for information or news from trusted sources. Therefore, digital media must increase user trust
to prevent them from migrating to other media.
Trust alludes to the reliability of a product or service provider to behave in a manner that safeguards the
long-term interests of customers (Khairunnisa & Rachmawati, 2018). According to Shin et al. (2019), this concept
of trust works as the cornerstone for building connections between consumers and brands. When consumers
consistently have positive experiences with a brand, they gradually develop a strong sense of confidence in the
brand’s ability to fulfil  their expectations. As a long -term relationship between the brand and its customers
develops, what initially starts as a general sense of trust may gradually evolve into strong brand trust (Shin et al.,
2019). Trust is one of the factors influencing brand loyalty (Ulfah, 2020). The way digital media is used in society
affects its perceived trustworthiness (Strömbäck et al., 2020) . To establish such relationships with the public,
companies must earn user trust (Supertini et al., 2020). When users have trust in digital media, user loyalty can be
established. This trust pertains to brand trust, which is the propensity of customers to depend on a brand despite
the associated risks, based on their positive expectations of the brand (Ulfah, 2020). Theoretically, brand trust
entails customer having faith in the brand’s security, honesty, and ability to consistently meet their needs. This
confidence encourages consumers to continue using and recommending the brand. According to research,
consumer loyalty towards a brand is significantly shaped by brand trust (Mai & Nguyen, 2021; Supertini et al.,
2020). Thus, brand loyalty emerges when consumers trust a brand (Adogo et al., 2024).
With the critical role that brand loyalty plays, it is necessary to determine the factors that affect it. One
factor that can influence brand loyalty is content marketing. The spread of hoax news can damage user trust in
the brand, so companies must presen t trusted content marketing to maintain credibility. Companies can attract
and retain loyal customers with the right digital marketing strategy, including personalized content and a user -
friendly website (Gahlot & Rani, 2023; Kanodia, 2020) . Content marketing provides valuable and quality content,
building a brand as a trusted authority (Chen & Xu, 2022). Content that meets user needs can increase trust and
loyalty (Julaeha, 2024). The user also serves as a watchdog, actively scrutinizing and challenging content that is
invalid, irrelevant, or inaccurate. Thus, user trust becomes an important bridge to creating loyalty, and companies
can use various content marketing strategies to encourage users to identify and be loyal to them (Chen & Xu,
2022). Several studies have demonstrated the impact of content marketing on brand loyalty (Baydaş et al., 2023;
Chen & Xu, 2022; Jafarova & Tolon, 2022) . Additionally, content marketing, which encompasses sub -dimensions
of interaction and information, has a positive influence on user loyalty to the brand (Baydaş et al., 2023). Mediated
by brand trust, content marketing affects brand loyalty (Ashrafa et al., 2022) . Digital media content marketing is
typically displayed on websites and can influence user loyalty (Yusniar et al., 2022).
For online businesses and digital media, websites today pay attention to detailed visitor information by
utilizing interactive features to implement one -to-one marketing (Effendi & Prasetio, 2020) . If a website is
frequently down or a page is unavailable, customers may lose trust in the company and seek alternative options
(Hidayat & Nasution, 2024) . Digital media should utilize website quality (webqual) to assess the quality of a
website, thereby providing benefits to the company by monitoring and evaluating the business operations
(Susanti, 2020). Webqual 4.0 is a measurement that provides accurate information and assesses the feasibility of
a website (Sinuraya, 2019) . This website quality consists of information quality, service interaction quality, and
usability quality (Purwandani & Syamsiah, 2021) . Research suggests that web quality has a significant impact on
brand trust (Hai & Nguyen, 2022) dan terhadap loyalitas merek (Mai & Nguyen, 2021). Therefore, it is essential to
manage website quality to provide high trust to consumers. However, if trust is used as a mediator, trust does not
increase the influence of website quality on other variables (Ramialis & Besra, 2021).
In this study, the researcher wants to use Detikcom as the object of research. Detik.com is a digital media
platform that features online news and articles in Indonesia. Detikcom was officially acquired on August 3, 2011,
by Transmedia, part of CT Corporat ion. Detikcom is a media that has an online edition and relies on advertising
revenue. As a digital media platform, the Detikcom domain has been active since May 29, 1998, and began online
with news content on July 9, 1998. The emergence of Detikcom coincided with the wave of digital transformation
in Indonesia, positioning it as a pioneering force in the realm of online news and solidifying its status as one of the
largest digital media platforms in the country today.
In this research, Detikcom serves as the focal point of investigation. In addition to its popularity, Detikcom
was chosen because it is a platform that actively produces content and is also vulnerable to hoax issues, making it
suitable for exploring how website quality and content marketing strategies can build (or damage) th e trust of
Detikcom’s audience. Most previous studies on Detikcom have only analyzed website quality using the
importance-performance analysis (IPA) method (Christianto et al., 2020; Liani et al., 2020; Sholeh et al., 2021) ,
without integrating the role of other variables. Research by Welyani & Melano (2025)  is one of the studies that
analyzes Detikcom content using qualitative methods and implements Brand Expression theory. This study
demonstrates the role of Detikcom content as a branding strategy that can contribute to increasing credibility.

This study also suggests a relationship between content marketing and Detikcom’s branding, as well as credibility
or trust. However, there are still very few studies that integrate content marketing, website quality, and brand
trust in the context of digital news media in Indonesia to understand the relationship and strengths between them.
As a digital media platform that provides the latest and most reliable information, the issue of hoaxes poses a
challenge for Detikcom in determining the influence of content marketing and website quality on branding.
The interest in research about trust and loyalty within digital media has not been adequately matched by
studies that thoroughly integrate content marketing as a key determinant of brand trust and brand loyalty in the
digital media landscape (du Plessis, 2022). Consequently, this study also necessitates the inclusion of brand trust
as a mediating variable, which has been established as significant in either reinforcing or diminishing the
influencing factors. Thus, this research can serve as a reference and consideration for digital media to increase
brand loyalty through the analyzed factors.

constructs. First, the grand theory of marketing management proposed by Kotler et al. (2018) , which explicates
marketing as the art and science of choosing target markets and gaining, keeping, and expanding a customer base
by producing, delivering, and sharing greater customer value. The second is the grand theory of social psychology,
which serves as the basis for Fishbein and Ajzen’s Theory of Reasoned Action (TRA) (Ajzen, 2020). Figure 1 depicts
the formation of the research’s theoretical framework.

The results of this study can support the two integrated theories, namely that brand loyalty can be formed
not only by functional quality but also by credible content strategies, in line with the concept of customer -based
brand equity according to Keller (Kohli & Haenlein, 2021) . This is because the concept of brand equity involves

The application of social psychology theory can also enhance the theories of source credibility and the elaboration

This section reviews previous research that discusses the impact of content marketing and website
quality on brand loyalty, the relationship between content marketing and website quality on brand trust, the role
of brand trust in influencing brand loyalty, and the potential mediating role of brand trust.

Content Marketing and Website Quality on Brand Loyalty
Research by Baydaş et al. (2023) indicates that the R-value of 0.40 and R² of 0.16 suggest that approximately 16%
of the variance in brand loyalty can be explained by the information variable from content marketing. According
to research by Chen & Xu (2022) , culture, entertainment, emotion, and interaction are the four aspects that
determine customer loyalty. On the other hand, Mai & Nguyen (2021)  in their research on website quality and
brand loyalty, using the SEM approach, showed that perceived quality also positively affects brand loyalty, with a
standard coefficient of 0.441 and a significance level of p = 0.000. These results show that the hi gher the quality
perceived by customers, the more likely they are to be loyal to the brand.

Content Marketing and Website Quality on Brand Trust
Research on content marketing towards brand trust by Parahita et al. (2023) involved 150 samples focusing on the
food and beverage industry. Using the PLS -SEM (Partial Least Squares -Structural Equation Modelling) technique,

the study’s findings demonstrated that the content marketing variable had no discernible effect on the customer
loyalty variable. Research by Qalati et al. (2021) , involving 600 respondents in China and utilizing the SEM -PLS
analysis technique, found that service quality, website quality, and reputation have a significant impact on trust.
The study’s results showed that web stores have paid more attention to attrac tiveness, colour, and real -time
services, providing all necessary information to eliminate ambiguity.

Brand Trust on Brand Loyalty
Essentially, trust encompasses the knowledge that individuals possess and the conclusions they draw regarding an
object, its attributes, and its benefits (Desfitriady et al., 2023). Trust between companies and customers is vital to
establish and maintain in order to establish a mutually beneficial and lasting relationship (Islam et al., 2021). This
relationship creates conditions for customers to rely on a brand to fulfil its brand functions, which is known as
brand trust. Briliana & Sari (2020)  study shows a connection between consumer trust and brand loyalty. With a
value of 10.94 and a coefficient value of 0.63 in PLS analysis results, Go -Ride consumers' brand loyalty and
customer trust are significantly influenced by each other. With loyalty in users who have a slightly different
concept from consumers, this study can illustrate that loyalty influences brand loyalty.

Brand Trust as a Mediator
Research by Lova & Raharjo (2023) , which utilized 120 respondents and applied Structural Equation Modelling
(SEM) using AMOS, found that brand trust is a crucial mediating factor in the link between social media marketing
and purchasing decisions. The study found that social media marketing indirectly influences consumers' decisions
by fostering brand trust (0.151), which was higher than its direct effect (0.107), emphasizing the significant role of
trust in mediating this relationship. Although social media marketing alone showed a positi ve but insignificant
direct impact on purchase decisions, its indirect influence via brand trust highlights the potential of trust as a
mediator in enhancing the effectiveness of marketing strategies. This finding highlights the importance of further
investigating brand trust’s mediating capabilities in diverse contexts and with various variables.

3.0 METHODOLOGY

This study employs a quantitative approach, utilizing survey methods for data collection. According to Tojiri et al.
(2023), surveys are a data collection method that involves pre -designed questions. Through the survey data
collection method, researchers can measure respondents’ preferences regarding a research variable. As a result,
this study collected data through questionn aires distributed to a selected sample using the purposive sampling
method, ensuring that the sample aligned with the desired objectives and characteristics. Given the unknown
population size, sample size determination was performed using the formula by Hair et al. (2022), which involves
multiplying the number of indicators by 5 –10. This multiplier represents a range that ensures the validity of
analytical results and guarantees accurate estimation for each indicator. The study comprises 53 indicators, which
are represented by 51 questionnaire items. By applying the minimum multiplier as per Hair et al.’s formula, the
required sample size was calculated based on the number of indicators, resulting in a baseline threshold of 265
respondents, who are Detikcom website users wh o tend to search for news through the Detikcom website. To
ensure robust statistical power and account for potential data exclusions, the final analyzed sample consisted of
270 valid responses, exceeding the minimum requirement and aligning with best pract ices for model stability in
PLS-SEM. The survey was conducted using the semantic differential method with a measurement scale ranging
from 1 to 7 intervals. This method is used to measure respondents’ attitudes and perceptions (Simamora, 2022).
The research used a multivariate analytic method that is distribution -free and does not depend on
distributional assumptions: Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS -SEM). PLS -SEM analysis
involves two primary stages: the outer model and in ner model. The outer model assesses validity and reliability.
The Fornell -Larcker Criterion decision rule compares the Average Variance Extracted (AVE) with squared
correlations, where AVE must exceed 0.5. Cross-loadings results were deemed acceptable if no errors were
detected. Construct reliability was evaluated using Cronbach’s Alpha and composite reliability, with acceptable
thresholds set at >0.7, while values >0.9 were considered excellent.
 For validity testing, the study applied the Fornell-Larcker Criterion and cross-loadings analysis. The inner
model evaluation examined path coefficients, R -squared values, and hypothesis testing. Path coefficients were
deemed significant if their absolute values exceeded 1.96 (derived from a 5% significance level). R-squared values
were categorized into three strength levels: ≥0.75 (strong), 0.5 –0.75 (moderate), and <0.5 (weak). Hypothesis
testing relied on p -values, where results ≤0.05 led to the rejection of the null hypothesis (H0) in favor of the
alternative (Ha). Following PLS-SEM analysis, mediation effects were assessed using Variance Accounted For (VAF)
calculations.
To facilitate data interpretation, descriptive statistical analysis was performed based on predefined
measurement scale criteria. The scoring interpretation framework was structured as follows on Table 1.

The calculations reveal the following mediation effects:
Content Marketing → Brand Trust → Brand Loyalty: A VAF of 0.74 14 (74,14%) implies that this link is

5.0 CONCLUSION

Conclusion
The study reveals that 80.6% of brand loyalty variation and 76.9% of brand trust variation among Detikcom users
can be attributed to content marketing and website quality. However, content marketing demonstrates no
significant direct influence on brand loy alty. Content marketing builds brand trust, and through this trust, it
indirectly increases Detikcom’s brand loyalty. Website quality exhibits statistically significant effects on both
constructs. About brand loyalty, brand trust exerts a more substantial indirect effect on website quality compared
to its direct influence. Brand trust itself functions as a partial mediator, accounting for 74.14% of content
marketing’s indirect effect and 60.05% of website quality’s impact, based on the VAF results, undersco ring
customer trust as a pivotal factor in fostering brand loyalty. User evaluations indicate strong performance across
all measured variables, with content marketing, website quality, brand loyalty, and brand trust of Detikcom each
scoring an average of 7 3.9%, categorizing them as “Good.” These results reflect general user satisfaction with
platform services, suggesting that enhancing content marketing alone may not directly improve the loyalty
metric. However, increasing trust can still indirectly affect Detikcom’s brand loyalty. Conversely, superior website
quality—particularly in terms of usability, information quality, and service interaction —proves effective in
strengthening both brand trust and loyalty.

Implication
The research findings suggest that Detikcom can employ strategies to foster audience trust and enhance its loyalty.
A quality website with superior competitiveness and user experience is Detikcom’s primary model for continuous
innovation in UI/UX design, l oading speed, and interactive features, which are key competitive advantages. The
analysis also revealed that Detikcom could implement a more engaging loyalty program or enhance the quality of
exclusive content to encourage word-of-mouth.

Limitation Research

Therefore, there are inherent limitations and complex assumptions behind all the figures. Furthermore, there are
limitations to the type of cont ent marketing; this study used content from the website only and did not include
content from social media or other platforms. Furthermore, this study is limited to analyzing the influence of
variables. Further analysis of other aspects is needed to unders tand how content marketing quality builds
audience trust.
