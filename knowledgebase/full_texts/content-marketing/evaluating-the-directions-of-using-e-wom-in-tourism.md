Abstract

Electronic word of mouth (eWOM) has evolved dramatically in the past 20 years, and is
substantially shaping modern consumer behaviors  and altering marketing management dynamics
across both consumer and industry markets. Furthermore, eWOM influences customer purchasing
behavior. A positive eWOM influences customers and may persuade them to buy because it is an
honest unpaid opinion from a previous customer. Therefore, it is critical to evaluate the relationship
between content marketing, E-Promotion, eWOM, and customers’ intentions to book hotel rooms.
The objective of this article is understanding the impact of  eWOM in tourism, the benefits for tourists
and management of hotels, including for researchers area, by making a structured literature review
starting with the purpose of eWOM  from the customer point of view, increasing value of service in
tourism by hotel management and finally to foster innovation and to create greater customer value.
Because of the dominance of eWOM and social media-related research, digital marketing
managers should prioritize strategies that enhance online reputation management.

Key words: tourism, eWOM, marketing, tourists, reputation management

1. Introduction

has been reinvigorated in the Internet age (Dellarocas, 2003). Two decades ago, in their article,
Hennig-Thurau et al. (2004) explored how the Internet age had a llowed individual consumers to
engage in electronic word of mouth (eWOM) via consumer-opinion platforms. Despite these early
efforts and the ongoing attention devoted to the examination of  eWOM by both academics and
practitioners, there have been renewed calls for a more nuanced understanding of eWOM in order to
maximize its business value (Babic Rosario et al., 2020). These  calls have been legitimized in light
of ever emerging and - evolving phenomena in the market – technological advancements, consumers

3. Research methodology

This study employs a structured literature review methodology t o investigate the general
framework of eWOM in tourism a nd which are the modalities and b enefits for tourists and hotels,
even for researchers. The review focuses on identifying the aim  of eWOM, reasons for applying
eWOM in tourism and how it is used by consumers of tourism services and by management of hotels
with a particular attention to the benefits to follow the resul ts of reviews for the benefit of their
business by the management of hotels from the last five years.
The primary data source used for this review is the WoS and Sci enceDirect, due to its
comprehensive indexing of high-quality, peer-reviewed journals across disciplines relevant to
tourism, hospitality and service management.
A systematic search was conducted using a combination of the fo llowing keywords: “Ewom,
Tourism” and then “. Boolean operators (AND, OR) were used to r efine results, and filters were
applied to limit the search to articles published between 2021 and 2025, written in English, and
indexed as Articles or Reviews in the fields of Hospitality, Tourism, Business, and Management.
The selection process followed included four stages:
 Identification – A total number of articles were retrieved based on the initial keyword search.
 Screening – Duplicates and irrelevant titles were removed based on abstract and title relevance.
 Eligibility – Full texts of the remaining studies were examined  for alignment with the inclusion
criteria (focus on service quality in hotel settings).
 Inclusion – Final studies included in the review were those offering conceptual models, empirical
analyses, or validated measurement scales relevant to hotel service quality.

4. Findings

One of the most significant findings is the growing importance of eWOM in online reputation
management (ORM). Hotel managers actively monitor online reviews and ratings to assess customer
perceptions and maintain a favorable public image. The reviewed  studies show that effective
management of online reviews con tributes to stronger customer trust, improved brand reputation,
and increased competitiveness in t he tourism marketplace. The f indings demonstrate that eWOM
serves as a continuous feedback mechanism, enabling organizations to improve operational
performance, enhance service quality, and increase customer satisfaction.

5. Conclusions

From a managerial perspective, eWOM has emerged as a valuable t ool for reputation
management, service quality impr ovement, customer relationship management, and strategic
decision-making. Tourism operators and hospitality managers inc reasingly exploit eWOM data
through review monitoring, sentiment analysis, customer feedback systems, and online engagement
strategies to enhance competitiv eness and improve customer expe riences. The growing application
of artificial intelligence and text-mining techniques has furth er strengthened the capacity of
organizations to transform eWOM into actionable market intelligence.
