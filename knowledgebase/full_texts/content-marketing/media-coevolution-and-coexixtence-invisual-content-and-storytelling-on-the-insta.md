Abstract
This study examines the coevolution and coexistence principle within Roger Fidler's (1997)
mediamorphosis theory as manifested in the digital marketing communication transformation of PT
Bank Central Asia Tbk's Instagram account, @goodlifebca. Using a qualitative netnographic approach,
data were collected through nine weeks of non-participant observation of the account's content, in-
depth online interviews with three purposively selected external informants who are active followers,
and digital documentation, then analyzed thematically. The findings show that coevolution and
coexistence operate at three interrelated levels. First, at the channel level, Instagram content
consistently redirects audiences back to BCA's conventional service channels, including branches, the
Halo BCA call center, and ATMs, rather than replacing them. Second, at the brand -awareness level,
long-standing customers' awareness of BCA is co -produced by conventional transactional experience
and digital content, whereas non -transactional followers' awareness is built almost entirely through
digital content, showing that digital and conventional communication complement rather than
substitute one another. Third, at the content-format level, the historically static feed format continues to
coexist with the newer, more dynamic Reels format at a near -equal share, each serving distinct
communicative functions, reinforced by campaigns that deliberately bridge physical lifestyle events
with Instagram promotion. Consistent visual identity and storytelling -based, conversational language
emerge as connective devices that sustain this coexistence, humanizing the brand while preserving
institutional credibility. The study concludes that @goodlifebca's digital transformation does not erase
conventional marketing communication but reorganizes visual and narrative elements within a hybrid,
mutually reinforcing communication ecosystem.

1. Introduction
Digital transformation has fundamentally reshaped the landscape of banking marketing
communication. The Digital 2023 report published by We Are Social and Hootsuite (2023) recorded
more than 4.76 billion active social media users globally, while the Indonesian Internet Service
Providers Association (APJII, 2023) reported that Indonesia's internet penetration rate had reached
78.19 percent. Instagram, as one of the most widely used visual social media platforms, has driven
various industry sectors, including banking, to build a strong digital presence. PT Bank Central Asia
Tbk (BCA) responded to this trend through its Instagram account @goodlifebca, which was designed
with a lifestyle -branding approach to build closeness with customers and prospective customers from
the younger generation (PT Bank Central Asia Tbk, 2026).
This kind of digital marketing communication transformation can be understood more fully
through the mediamorphosis theory developed by Roger Fidler (1997), who argues that new media
never emerge suddenly and completely replace old media, but instead coevolve and coexist within a
single, interdependent media ecosystem. The principle of coevolution and coexistence is relevant for
explaining why banking institutions such as BCA have not entirely abandoned conventional
communication channels when adopting Instagram as a digital marketing channel, and how visual and
narrative elements function to bridge the two ecosystems.

channels, transactional experience and digital content, and old and new visual content formats coexist
with one another, with particular emphasis on the role of visual identity and storytelling as connecting
elements. Theoretically, this study is expected to enrich the application of mediamorphosis theory
within the context of digital banking marketing communication in Indonesia; practically, the findings
are expected to serve as input for the managers of the @goodlifebca account in designing content
strategies that balance digital innovation with the strength of established conventional channels and
narratives.

2. Literature Review
The mediamorphosis theory introduced by Roger Fidler (1997) in Mediamorphosis:
Understanding New Media explains the evolution of communication media through six fundamental
principles: coevolution and coexistence, metamorphosis, propagation, survival, opportunity and need,
and delayed adoption. This study focuses on the first principle, coevolution and coexistence, which
holds that all forms of communication coevolve and coexist within an interdependent family of media
ecology; when a new form of media emerges, older media do not simply disappear but instead adapt
and coexist within an increasingly complex media ecosystem. Fidler (1997) also introduced the concept
of the mediasphere as the ecological arena in which various  forms of communication interact, compete,
and evolve together, encompassing the oral mediasphere, the print mediasphere, and the digital
mediasphere, with slow transitional periods between them that are full of hybrid forms.
In the context of digital marketing communication, Kotler, Kartajaya, and Setiawan (2021), in
Marketing 5.0, argue that contemporary marketing needs to combine the power of digital technology
with an authentic human touch, an idea that is relevant  to the role of storytelling and personal language
in humanizing the brand of a financial institution. Meanwhile, Keller and Swaminathan (2020) assert
that consistency in visual elements, such as color, logo, and language style, is a key determinant of
brand recognition, namely the audience's ability to quickly recognize a brand amid the diversity of
content formats. Both perspectives form the conceptual  basis for understanding how visual identity and
storytelling narratives function as elements that bridge the coexistence of new digital content with the
legacy of conventional marketing communication in the case of @goodlifebca.

3. Research Method
3.1 Research Object, Time, and Location
The object of this study is the Instagram account @goodlifebca, a digital lifestyle -branding
marketing communication channel belonging to PT Bank Central Asia Tbk. The study uses a
qualitative approach with a netnography design as formulated by Kozinets (2020), chosen for its
suitability to a research object that takes place entirely in digital space. The research was conducted
entirely online, with observation of the account's content carried out over nine weeks.
3.2 Data Collection Technique
Data were collected through three complementary techniques. First, non -participant
netnographic observation of the types, formats, and interaction patterns of @goodlifebca's feed, Stories,
and Reels content. Second, online in -depth interviews with three external informants, namely active
followers of the @goodlifebca account selected through purposive sampling (Moleong, 2021) based on
the criteria of being 20 -35 years old, having actively interacted with the content for at least the past six
months, and representing a variety of customer statuses (active customers, non-transactional customers,
and new followers). Third, digital documentation (Sugiyono, 2022) in the form of archived public
content and PT Bank Central Asia Tbk's annual report (2026) relevant to the company's digital
transformation strategy.

3.3 Data Analysis Technique
Data from the three sources were integrated and analyzed thematically following Kozinets's
(2020) netnographic analysis stages, comprising data familiarization, initial coding, theme searching,
theme reviewing, and theme defining, which were mapped onto Fidler's (1997) mediamorphosis
principle of coevolution and coexistence. Data validity was examined through source triangulation, by
comparing and cross -confirming the perspectives of the three informants, as well as method
triangulation, by juxtaposing the interview results with the observation data and digital documentation.

4.2 The Role of Visual and Storytelling as a Connector of Coexistence
The research data show that the consistency of visual identity, BCA's characteristic blue color
and logo, functions as a marker of brand recognition that remains identifiable to audiences even as
content formats and styles continue to change over time, in line with Keller and Swaminathan's (2020)
proposition that consistent visual elements strengthen brand  recognition across touchpoints. This visual
element is one of the main mechanisms that allows the coexistence of old and new formats to take
place without blurring BCA's institutional identity. This consistency is also evident in posts that bridge
the digital ecosystem with physical moments, such as collaborations with public figures at international
sporting events, which still maintain BCA's color identity and campaign tagline.

In addition to visuals, storytelling strategy also plays an important role in bridging the
coexistence between formal institutional communication and casual digital language style.
@goodlifebca's content consistently packages financial education through personal narratives and
everyday language, rather than complex banking terminology, so that the account is perceived by
audiences as more “humane” compared with conventional financial institution communication. This
approach reflects Kotler, Kartajaya, and Setiawan's (2021) proposition that effective digital marketing
combines technology with an authentic human touch. In other words, storytelling functions as a
narrative bridge that allows institutionally valuable messages, such as product information and terms
and conditions inherited from conventional marketing communication, to still be conveyed in a more
relaxed digital package that is closer to young audiences.

4.3 Discussion
These findings confirm and extend Fidler's (1997) proposition that new media never emerge in
isolation but instead coevolve within an interconnected mediamorphic network. In the case of
@goodlifebca, coexistence occurs not only at the level of communication channels (digital and
conventional), but also at the level of content form (feed and Reels) and at the psychological level of
the audience (brand awareness shaped by both digital and transactional experience). These findings
align with Budianto and Suryawati (2023), who likewise found that the transformation of BCA's
marketing communication on Instagram takes place gradually and does not entirely abandon
conventional communication elements. This study enriches those findings by showing more
specifically that visual identity and storytelling are two concrete mechanisms that allow this
coexistence to take place coherently, rather than as a mere unintegrated overlap of formats.
These findings also enrich Rusfian and Rizkiyan's (2021) perspective on the three dimensions of
digital banking marketing communication transformation, the dimensions of content, interaction, and
transformation, by showing that the content dimension in particular is bridged by a combination of
consistent visuals and adaptive storytelling narratives. In addition, in line with Hamid and Budianto
(2020), this study affirms the importance of careful Instagram content management for banking
corporations, particularly in maintaining a balance between digital format innovation and the
preservation of visual elements and institutional messages that have built audience trust over time.
4.4 Relevance to the Research Objective
The results of this study answer the research objective, namely to describe and analyze the
application of Roger Fidler's mediamorphosis principle of coevolution and coexistence to visual and
storytelling strategies on the Instagram account @goodlifebca. The findings show that @goodlifebca's
digital marketing communication transformation is not a total shift from conventional to digital
communication, but rather a layered process that integrates the two through consistent visual and
narrative elements, so that older channels, experiences, and formats remain relevant alongside
continuously evolving digital innovation.

5. Conclusion
This study concludes that the principle of coevolution and coexistence within Roger Fidler's
(1997) mediamorphosis theory is clearly manifested in @goodlifebca's digital marketing
communication transformation through three interrelated levels: the coexistence of conventional and
digital communication channels, the coexistence of transactional experience and digital content in their
contribution to brand awareness, and the coexistence of old and new visual content formats. Consistent
visual identity and a humanizing storytelling strategy prove to be the two main connecting elements
that allow this coexistence to take place coherently without blurring BCA's institutional credibility.
Thus, @goodlifebca's digital transformation is fundamentally not a process of substitution, but rather a
gradual process of integration that reorganizes the visual and narrative elements of conventional
marketing communication into a broader digital ecosystem. Future research is recommended to further
examine how visual and storytelling elements can be optimized in a more measurable way to
strengthen coexistence among the increasingly diverse channels, formats, and audience segments of
digital banking.
