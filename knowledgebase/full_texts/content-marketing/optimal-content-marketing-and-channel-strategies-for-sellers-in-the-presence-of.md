Abstract5
Online consumer purchasing is shaped by seller-provided product information and elec-
tronic word-of-mouth. As social commerce platforms evolve, sellers strategically use these
channels to optimize proﬁtability, while consumer-generated reviews inﬂuence peer decisions
and provide market intelligence. We develop a two-period game-theoretic model that inte-
grates three dimensions of consumer behavior (namely satisfaction levels, review preferences,10
and repurchase behaviors) along with the spillover eﬀect of online reviews to investigate op-
timal strategies for content marketing, pricing, and sales strategies. The ﬁndings reveal that:
proﬁt maximization requires optimal content marketing quality; high-brand-eﬀect sellers ben-
eﬁt more from reviews; low-brand-eﬀect sellers may ignore reviews unless spillover is high;
considering spillover beneﬁts sellers with more satisﬁed buyers; and hybrid channel mod-15
els are preferred when ignoring spillover, while high spillover favors traditional e-commerce
channels for greater proﬁtability.
Keywords: social commerce platforms, content marketing, strategy selection, online review,
hybrid e-commerce channel

1 Introduction20
The proliferation of e-commerce has fundamentally transformed consumer purchasing patterns,
with digital platforms becoming the predominant retail channel. According to China Business
Industry Research Institute reports, China’s online shopping user base attained 974 million by
December 2024, reﬂecting year-on-year growth of 59.67 million users and penetration rate of
87.9% among internet users. This digital platform facilitates bidirectional information exchange,25
whereby sellers disseminate product speciﬁcations through content marketing activities native to
the platform, while consumers generate user-generated content via posting reviews, thereby en-
hancing product visibility and facilitating informed decision-making. Consumers predominantly
rely on two information archetypes when evaluating online purchases: the sellers’ descriptions
and products’ online reviews. invespro.com indicates that 93% of purchasers attribute purchase30
decisions to review inﬂuence, with 90% systematically consulting reviews before purchasing and
88% considering peer evaluations as credible as personal recommendations. Online reviews have
become crucial factors in e-commerce. They help reduce uncertainty about the product and
enhance understanding of product-related information( Chen and Xie , 2008; Godes and Mayzlin ,
2004).35
As social commerce platforms mature, consumers’ lifestyles are notably changing. More individ-
uals depend on platforms like Instagram and Xiaohongshu to share opinions and look for product
information. The role of social commerce platforms in providing online reviews is indispensable.
The Social Sciences Academic Press of China has created a tailored evaluation system for gaug-
ing communication eﬀectiveness. Classifying platforms for posting online reviews into various40
categories reveals that online reviews on social networking platforms exhibit the highest commu-
nication eﬀectiveness( Zhao, Shuguang , 2021). Consumers can acquire product details via social
commerce platforms when shopping online. Moreover, these platforms serve as places for users
to look around without purchase intentions, stimulating their product interest and transforming
2

them into consumers. Therefore, content marketing and online reviews on these platforms play45
a decisive role in consumers’ purchasing decisions( Zhang and Zhang , 2024; Chen and Xie , 2008),
and are thus crucial factors that sellers must consider seriously.
From the perspective of sellers’ content marketing, many sellers actively promote the content-
oriented development of e-commerce and focus on the social commerce platform’s community
traﬃc. For instance, Taobao sellers have opened oﬃcial accounts on Xiaohongshu, leveraging50
content marketing to attract users and convert them into consumers. From the perspective
of electronic word-of-mouth (eWOM) on the social commerce platform, consumers often share
their product usage experiences, quality appraisals, and other attributes after purchasing. These
online reviews stimulate the buying desires of users and attract them to become consumers. For
example, well-known cosmetics brands such as Shu Uemura and MAC actively engage in content55
marketing on Xiaohongshu. Simultaneously, online reviews of their products on Xiaohongshu
signiﬁcantly inﬂuence consumers’ purchase decisions.
Technological advancements have progressively boosted the shopping functions of the social com-
merce platform, making it more prominent in the global e-commerce market. This progression
pressures traditional e-commerce platforms competitively and generates new opportunities for60
transformation and growth. Many sellers have diversiﬁed their sales channels by integrating
the social commerce platform, forming a hybrid e-commerce channel model. For instance, the
fashion brand URBAN REVIVO , in addition to operating a Taobao e-store, has established an
oﬃcial purchasing channel on Xiaohongshu. Consequently, whether to maintain a traditional
e-commerce channel sales model or a hybrid e-commerce approach has become a critical deci-65
sion for sellers. By optimizing platform operation, sellers can enhance sales eﬃciency and user
engagement, gaining a competitive advantage in the market.
Based on the above background, we raise the following questions: (1) Does the seller in the
traditional e-commerce platform take the spillover eﬀect of online reviews on the social commerce
3

platform into account when pricing and designing content marketing strategies? (2) What are70
the optimal pricing and content marketing strategies for the seller in the traditional e-commerce
platform based on the spillover eﬀect of online reviews? (3) What is the optimal sales model for
the seller, between traditional and hybrid e-commerce channels?
Therefore, we develop a two-stage model focusing on consumer behavior. In the ﬁrst stage of
this model, a monopolistic seller sells products on the traditional e-commerce platform. Con-75
sumers make purchase decisions based on the brand eﬀect and product price. After receiving the
products, buyers post reviews on the social commerce platform. In the second stage, the seller
does content marketing for the product on the social commerce platform using images, text,
and videos. Based on online reviews on the social commerce platform, the seller determines the
quality of content marketing and pricing. New consumers (who haven’t bought the product) can80
learn about the product’s price, quality, and reviews. And then they decide to purchase from
the traditional e-commerce platform. Furthermore, we discuss the seller’s pricing and content
marketing strategies in a hybrid e-commerce channel and explore the optimal sales model.
Our research reveals the following ﬁndings. Firstly, the product pricing and the quality of content
marketing on the social commerce platform are subject to the satisfaction level of buyers when85
the spillover eﬀect is considered. Secondly, considering the spillover eﬀect of online reviews on
the social commerce platform, the seller’s decision is inﬂuenced by the level of spillover eﬀect and
factors such as the brand eﬀect and the buyer’s experience gap. Finally, regardless of whether the
seller considers the spillover eﬀect of online reviews on the social commerce platform, diﬀerent
channel models can yield distinct beneﬁts.90
The contributions of our research are as follows. Firstly, we introduce the spillover eﬀect of online
reviews into the research on the quality of content marketing decisions on the social commerce
platform, addressing the limitations in existing literature regarding the inﬂuence of online reviews
on content marketing strategies. Additionally, by incorporating satisﬁed customers’ repurchase
4

behavior and the brand eﬀect of sellers into consumer behavior, a dynamic model integrating95
consumer uncertainty, channel selection, and pricing strategies is constructed, providing new
insights for sellers to optimize channel design and pricing strategies. Lastly, based on platform
characteristics, the research systematically compares the marketing diﬀerences between tradi-
tional and hybrid e-commerce channels. It oﬀers a decision-making basis for sellers to select
operational models under diﬀerent market conditions.100
Remaining sections are organized as follows. In section 2, we review the relevant literature. In
section 3, we formate the game model. In section 4, the equilibrium results are derived and
analyzed. In section 5, a comparative analysis of diﬀerent models is conducted. In section 6, the
model is extended for further discussion. In section 7, we conclude the ﬁndings.
2 Literature review105
Our literature review includes online reviews, content marketing and retailing on the social

2.1 Online reviews
Online reviews, key forms of product word-of-mouth, signiﬁcantly shape consumers’ and com-
panies’ decisions. For consumers, online reviews are crucial sources of information that greatly110
impact consumers’ purchase decisions and brand engagements( Fernandes et al. , 2022). Online
reviews not only supplement information about products, services, and shopping experiences
but also are more likely to gain the trust of potential consumers compared to seller-provided
information(Gu et al. , 2012; Ismagilova et al. , 2020). Most consumers collect many online re-
views before making purchase decisions( Filieri et al. , 2018). This allows consumers to obtain115
relevant product information and make informed decisions( Kwark et al. , 2014). For companies,
5

online reviews inﬂuence decisions on product pricing, quality, and quantity( Zhang and Tian ,
2024). Considering online reviews in supply chain decisions can increase total supply chain and
platform proﬁt( Shi and Liu , 2023). When online reviews are present, companies adjust pric-
ing strategies according to market types. For instance, penetration pricing works best in high120
willingness-to-pay markets(Feng et al. , 2023).
The attitudes of consumers and companies toward online reviews vary signiﬁcantly. Diﬀerent
consumers have varying willingness to post reviews( Pan et al. , 2018). Consumers only post re-
views when they’re extremely satisﬁed or dissatisﬁed with products( Anderson, 1998). Companies
use various strategies to improve the quality and quantity of online reviews, such as improving125
product quality(Feng et al., 2023), responding to reviews( Liu et al. , 2021b), adjusting prices( Sun
et al. , 2021; Yang et al. , 2021) and oﬀering rebates( Yang and Dong , 2018).
Online reviews can be positive or negative. Their nature greatly aﬀects consumer purchasing
decisions, invespro.com shows that 72% of consumers trust positive reviews and are likely to
buy based on them, while 86% of consumers hesitate to buy after encountering negative reviews.130
Moreover, online reviews can be found on traditional or social commerce platforms. The for-
mer reviews are user-generated and accessible on platforms such as Taobao and Amazon. They
are relatively passive information sources that shoppers can easily access. The latter reviews
like Xiaohongshu and Instagram require consumers to actively seek information( Chen and Xie ,
2005). The cross-platform dissemination of online reviews is increasingly aﬀecting consumer be-135
havior. As information dissemination channels become more diverse, consumers are active across
diﬀerent platforms. This brings diﬀerent views and enriches cross-platform information ﬂow( Ji
et al. , 2024). Consumers exchange and interact about products across multiple platforms, cre-
ating cross-platform reviews( Gao et al. , 2021). These cross-platform reviews inﬂuence purchase
intentions and sales in various ways( Gu et al. , 2012; Song et al. , 2019).140

2.2 Content marketing and retailing in social commerce platform
As crucial channels for information dissemination, social media platforms have garnered growing
research attention for their marketing potential( Rosário and Dias , 2023). From the companies’
perspective, marketing on social platforms deepens consumers’ understanding of products, en-
hancing user conversion rates and expanding market share( Agnihotri et al. , 2022; Dolan et al. ,145
2019). Content marketing on the social platform promotes products and enhances consumer
experience(Bowden and Mirzaei , 2021; Ki et al. , 2020). It has become an essential approach for
companies to provide brand experience and deliver value to consumers( Gümüş, 2017; Mallipeddi
et al. , 2022). Song et al. (2024) analyze the impact of content marketing eﬀorts in virtual
communities on social platforms, especially when traditional and social commerce platforms co-150
operate. These eﬀorts can optimize the user shopping experience, strengthen trust in brands and
platforms, and ultimately boost sales.
From the consumers’ point of view, compared to traditional marketing strategies, social networks,
as emerging communication technologies, have a profound impact on consumer behavior( Arango-
Botero et al. , 2021; Li et al. , 2023). Consumers widely accept eWOM generated through social155
networks, perceiving it as impartial, dependable, and trustworthy( Bélanger et al. , 2014). So-
cial networks serve as a platform enabling consumers to voice their evaluations of purchased
products, thus facilitating eWOM dissemination. eWOM encompasses users’ shared personal ex-
periences, evaluations, discussions, and recommendations( Zhao et al. , 2020). Consumers beneﬁt
from social media marketing by gaining product insights and engaging in real-time interactions160
with companies(Wang et al., 2019). Information sharing on the social platform increases trust in
platform companies and signiﬁcantly reduces perceived privacy risks( Bugshan and Attar , 2020).
Furthermore, embedding commercial attributes on social media positively inﬂuences consumers’
purchase intentions and loyalties, leveraging their high engagement with the social platform( Liu
et al. , 2021a; Rao and Ko , 2021).165

The rapid expansion of social commerce retail has increasingly impacted platforms, companies,
and consumers. Cai et al. (2024) ﬁnd that when the social commerce platform charges sellers
lower commissions than the traditional e-commerce platform, sellers, consumers, and social wel-
fare gain more from the social commerce retail model. Song et al. (2024) show that social and
traditional e-commerce platforms’ strategies (cooperative or competitive) in the seller’s diﬀerent170
market share.
Existing research on online reviews and content marketing within social commerce platforms
reveals several patterns. Studies on online reviews primarily examine their inﬂuence on ﬁrm and
consumer decisions, managerial responses to reviews, characteristics and origins. Fewer works
explore the spillover eﬀect of online reviews from third-party platforms on consumer behavior.175
Similarly, research on content marketing in social commerce often focuses on how branded con-
tent shapes consumer and ﬁrm behavior, with limited attention to its spillover eﬀect. Method-
ologically, while empirical approaches dominate this literature, game-theoretic models remain
relatively scarce. To address these gaps, we employ a game model to analyze how the spillover
eﬀect of online reviews on social commerce platforms inﬂuences consumer purchase decisions.180
By emphasizing spillover eﬀects from the seller’s perspective, this study oﬀers actionable insights
for seller strategy and contributes to theoretical advancement.
3 Modeling formulation in traditional e-commerce channel
We consider a monopolistic market involving a social commerce platform (e.g., Xiaohongshu,
Instagram), a traditional e-commerce platform (e.g., Taobao, Amazon), a seller, and consumers.185
The traditional e-commerce platform is located at one end of a Hotelling unit line( Hotbllino,
1929). Without loss of generality, we assume the traditional e-commerce platform is positioned
at 0. Consumers are uniformly distributed along the Hotelling unit line, and their heterogeneous
preferences for platforms are measured by their distance from each platform. Speciﬁcally, the
8

distance between a consumer and the traditional e-commerce platform is denoted as x. The unit190
travel cost is denoted as k, representing the intensity of consumer preferences( Bae et al. , 2022;
Song et al. , 2024). A consumer located at x incurs a disutility of kx when purchasing a product
from the traditional e-commerce platform. Each consumer can purchase at most one product in
each stage. For simplicity, we assume that the unit production cost of the product is 0. The
parameters are provided in Table 1.195

7 Conclusion
We investigate how the spillover eﬀect of online reviews in the social commerce platform aﬀects
the seller’s pricing and marketing strategies. Moreover, we perform the seller’s optimal decision595
strategy and sales models. The ﬁndings are as follows:
Firstly, when considering the spillover eﬀect of online reviews on the social commerce platform,
the pricing and quality of content marketing for the seller depends on the buyer’s satisfaction. If
satisﬁed consumers outnumber dissatisﬁed ones, taking the spillover eﬀect leads to higher product
pricing, the quality of content marketing, and demand than when ignoring it. Conversely, if600
dissatisﬁed consumers are in the majority, these metrics are lower when the spillover eﬀect is
neglected.
Secondly, the seller’s consideration of the spillover eﬀect of online reviews in decision-making is
inﬂuenced by the spillover level and factors such as the brand eﬀect and buyer’s experience gap.
The seller with a high brand eﬀect beneﬁts more from considering online reviews, while that605
31

with a low brand eﬀect can better protect proﬁts by ignoring the eﬀect unless the spillover eﬀect
is extremely high and brand awareness is very low, in which case considering it can yield more
beneﬁts. The more satisﬁed buyers there are, the more beneﬁcial it is for sellers to consider the
spillover eﬀect. With a lower number, The seller generally ignores the spillover eﬀect. However,
The seller may still deem it necessary to consider if the eﬀect is not excessively low. As the610
traditional e-commerce platform and seller share proﬁts, the platform also beneﬁts when the
seller considers the spillover eﬀect of online reviews.
Finally, no matter whether the seller considers the spillover of the online review, various channel
models can bring beneﬁts. The seller should choose the hybrid e-commerce channel model
ignoring the spillover eﬀect. The traditional or hybrid e-commerce channel can be the best615
option considering the spillover eﬀect.
Furthermore, managerial insights into pricing, marketing decisions, and operation strategies
for the seller regarding the consideration of the spillover eﬀect of online reviews on the social
commerce platform can be concluded. In terms of product pricing, our research reveals that
price is not invariably higher when the seller considers the spillover eﬀect of online reviews.620
In cases where buyer satisfaction is low, product prices might be lower than those set without
factoring in the spillover eﬀect. Therefore, the seller should base the pricing decision on factors
such as the magnitude of the spillover eﬀect and brand eﬀect to maximize proﬁts. From the
standpoint of product marketing, as consumers increasingly turn to social commerce platforms
for their shopping needs, positive eWOM and well-calibrated marketing eﬀorts can signiﬁcantly625
inﬂuence consumers’ purchasing decisions. The seller’s consideration of the spillover eﬀect of
online reviews hinges on various factors, including the magnitude of the spillover eﬀect, brand
eﬀect, and buyer’s satisfaction. From the platform operation perspective, the seller can achieve
maximum proﬁts by selecting the optimal sales model-either a traditional or hybrid e-commerce
channel-depending on whether to consider the spillover eﬀect of online reviews. The traditional630
