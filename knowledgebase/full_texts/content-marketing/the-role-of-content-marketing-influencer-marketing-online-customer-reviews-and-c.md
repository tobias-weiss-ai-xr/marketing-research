ABSTRACT
This study examines the effects of content marketing, influencer marketing, online customer
reviews, and celebrity endorsements on purchase decisions within the TikTok social commerce
ecosystem. As TikTok increasingly integrates entertainment with commerce , these marketing
elements play a critical role, particularly for Generation Z consumers who value authenticity,
emotional relevance, and trustworthy digital interactions. Using a quantitative explanatory design,
data were collected from 145 Generation Z respondents in Indonesia who actively use TikTok and
have made at least one purchase via TikTok Shop. The data were analyzed using Partial Least
Squares–Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) to test the direct effects of the four marketing
constructs on pu rchase decisions. The results indicate that content marketing, influencer
marketing, online customer reviews, and celebrity endorsements each have a positive and
significant influence on purchase decisions. These findings suggest that both cognitive factor s
(such as perceived usefulness and information quality) and affective factors (such as trust and
emotional engagement) are central in shaping consumer behavior in social commerce. This study
contributes to digital marketing literature by offering an integrated model of key marketing drivers
in TikTok -based social commerce and provides practical insights for marketers seeking to
optimize engagement and conversion strategies on social media platforms.

INTRODUCTION
The rapid advancement of digital technology has significantly transformed consumer behavior and
modern marketing practices. In Indonesia, these changes are particularly evident through the
growth of social commerce, which integrates online shopping functio nalities into social media
platforms. Social commerce enables users to search for information, evaluate products, and
complete purchases directly within the platforms they use daily. With more than 194 million active
social media users and over 221 million  internet users, Indonesia represents one of the most
dynamic digital markets in Southeast Asia.
Among social media platforms, TikTok has emerged as a dominant force. Initially designed for
entertainment and short-form video content, TikTok has evolved into a powerful marketing and
commerce platform. Through features such as TikTok Shop, live shopping , and creator -based
promotions, TikTok allows brands to combine storytelling, community interaction, and seamless
purchasing experiences. Indonesia is projected to become one of TikTok’s largest global markets,
making it an important context for studying social commerce behavior.
Generation Z consumers play a central role in this ecosystem. As digital natives, Gen Z users are
highly engaged with social media and tend to prioritize authenticity, personalization, emotional
relevance, and peer validation in marketing messages. Unlike previous generations, they rely
heavily on content creators, influencers, reviews, and celebrities when evaluating products. As a
result, marketing strategies that succeed on TikTok often depend on a combination of engaging
content, credible influencers, t rustworthy customer feedback, and aspirational celebrity
endorsements.
Four marketing elements are particularly influential in shaping purchase decisions in TikTok -
based social commerce: content marketing, influencer marketing, online customer reviews, and
celebrity endorsements. Content marketing focuses on creating informat ive, entertaining, and
value-driven content that resonates emotionally with users. Influencer marketing leverages trusted
content creators who maintain close relationships with their followers. Online customer reviews
function as electronic word -of-mouth t hat reduces uncertainty and perceived risk. Celebrity
endorsements, meanwhile, provide aspirational appeal and symbolic value when the endorser –
brand fit is perceived as authentic.
Although previous studies have examined these factors individually, empirical findings remain
inconsistent, and limited research has integrated all four variables into a single explanatory model
within the TikTok social commerce context. Therefore, this st udy aims to address this gap by
analyzing the combined effects of content marketing, influencer marketing, online customer
reviews, and celebrity endorsements on purchase decisions among Generation Z consumers in
Indonesia. By adopting a quantitative expla natory approach, this study seeks to enrich the
understanding of social commerce behavior and provide actionable insights for digital marketers.

LITERATURE REVIEW

The Effect of Content Marketing on Purchase Decision
Content marketing emphasizes the creation and distribution of valuable, relevant, and engaging
content to attract and retain consumers. In social commerce, content marketing moves beyond
direct selling by addressing consumer needs, building trust, and fost ering emotional connections.
This approach aligns with the Elaboration Likelihood Model (ELM), which explains persuasion
through cognitive (central route) and affective (peripheral route) processes.
On TikTok, visually appealing and authentic short -form videos are particularly effective in
capturing attention and shaping perceptions. Informative content enhances perceived usefulness,
while entertaining and relatable storytelling stimulates emotional e ngagement. Empirical studies
indicate that high-quality content positively influences brand attitudes and purchase decisions by
strengthening trust and involvement.
H1: Content Marketing has a positive and significant effect on Purchase Decision.

The Effect of Influencer Marketing on Purchase Decision
Influencer marketing involves collaboration with individuals who possess credibility, expertise,
and strong social influence over their followers. According to Source Credibility Theory, messages
delivered by trusted and likable sources are more persuasive . On TikTok, influencers often
integrate products into their daily routines, making promotional messages appear authentic and
relatable.
Research shows that influencer credibility, authenticity, and parasocial interaction significantly
increase purchase intention. Micro -influencers, in particular, tend to generate higher trust due to
their perceived closeness with followers. In social comme rce, influencers reduce uncertainty,
enhance brand trust, and act as social proof for potential buyers.
H2: Influencer Marketing has a positive and significant effect on Purchase Decision.

The Effect of Online Customer Reviews (OCR) on Purchase Decision
Online customer reviews (OCR) represent electronic word -of-mouth that provides experiential
information from other  consumers. Based on the Stimulus –Organism–Response (S –O–R)
framework, reviews function as external stimuli that influence internal evaluations and subsequent
behavioral responses.
In TikTok social commerce, reviews appear in comment sections, short videos, and live -stream
interactions, offering real -time feedback. Positive and credible reviews enhance trust, reduce
perceived risk, and strengthen confidence in purchasing decisions. C onversely, negative reviews
can discourage purchases, highlighting the importance of review valence and credibility.
H3: Online Customer Reviews have a positive and significant effect on Purchase Decision.

The Effect of Celebrity Endorsement on Purchase Decision
Celebrity endorsements have become a strong way for companies to market their products. They
affect how people decide to buy things by using the fame, trustworthiness, and likability of
celebrities. The Source Attractiveness Model and Source Credibility Theory show how celebrities
can change how people think about products. These theories explain that when a celebrity is linked
to a product, it makes the product seem more credible and easier to connect with, which makes
people more likely to like it (Hasyim & Rofiah, 2024). Studies show that celebrity endorsements
can greatly increase the chance that young people, especially those from Generation Z, will want
to buy a product, especially if they believe the product is high quality and worth the money (Sun
et al., 2022).
One important result shows that celebrities create more aspirational value than micro-influencers,
especially when their endorsements are supported by positive online reviews (Jun, 2024). People
often link celebrities with qualities like success, confidence, and beauty, which they usually want
to copy, making the products they promote more appealing and valuable in their eyes (Jun, 2024).
The success of using celebrities to promote a brand depends on how well the celebrity matches the
brand. If there's a poor fit, people might doubt the message and the campaign might not work as
well. But when the celebrity and the brand are a good match, i t can make the brand look better,
build trust, and get more people interested in the product ( Hasyim & Rofiah, 2024 ; Jun, 2024).
Choosing the right celebrity is very important to make the endorsement work well and keep
customers confident.
On social media platforms like TikTok, celebrity endorsements are made even more powerful
through live shopping events and short video promotions. These interactive formats help create
excitement and emotional connections, which can lead to sudden buying d ecisions. When
celebrities take part in live events, they not only spread their influence further but also use real -
time interactions to build trust in a brand and influence consumer behavior through emotional and
symbolic messages (Kalam et al., 2023; Chiu & Ho, 2023).
H4: Celebrity Endorsement (CE) has a positive and significant effect on Purchase Decision (PD).

By fostering trust, emotional engagement, and perceived legitimacy, content marketing, influencer
marketing, online customer reviews, and celebrity endorsements work together to influence

METHODS

Research Design
This study adopts a quantitative explanatory design using a cross sectional survey to test causal
relationships in which Celebrity Endorsement, Content Marketing, Influencer Marketing, and
Online Customer Reviews act as predictors of Purchase Decision. Dat a are collected through a
structured self  administered questionnaire. Analysis uses variance based structural equation
modeling in the partial least squares tradition to estimate direct effects and interaction effects under
realistic distributional conditions. All focal constructs are modeled reflectively.

Population and Sample
The target population of this study consists of individuals who actively use TikTok as a platform
for making purchases. The target group is Generation Z (Gen Z) consumers in Indonesia who are
active TikTok users. Gen Z was selected because they are highly engaged with digital platforms
and tend to form strong emotional and experiential connections with brands.

The sample size determination in this study refers to the PLS -SEM approach, which emphasizes
the importance of sample adequacy to ensure the validity of the analysis results. Hair et al., (2014)
recommend a minimum sample size of five to ten times the number of indicators or structural paths
in the model. With a total of 29 indicators in this study, the ideal sample size is in the range of 90
to 180 respondents. Therefore, the researcher determin ed a minimum sample size of 145
respondents, which is considered sufficient for the purposes of PLS -SEM-based structural model
analysis. However, in its implementation, the researcher used 145 respondents.
Based on the results of the sample size calculation above, it can be determined that the number of
respondents who will be used as samples in this study is 145 respondents.  The sampling method
applied in this study is purposive sampling, with specific inclusion criteria to ensure the
respondents’ relevance to the research objectives. The criteria are as follows: (1) respondents must
belong to the Generation Z cohort (born bet ween 1997–2012); (2) Active users of the TikTok
platform who access the application  at least 1 times per week; (3) Have been exposed to TikTok
marketing content, specifically promotional videos or influencer endorsements within the last 3
months; (4) have made at least one online purchase in the last month. The detailed criteria are
presented in Table 1. By applying these criteria, the selected sample is expected to accurately
represent Generation Z consumers who are actively engaged with TikTok content and influenced
by online promotional activities. This sampling approach allows the study to obtain deeper insights
into how TikTok content exposure and influencer marketing shape consumer perceptions and
ultimately influence purchase decisions.

For H1, the findings indicate that content marketing (CM) has a positive and significant effect on
purchase decision (PD) (β = 0.223, t = 2.007, p = 0.045). This result suggests that when brand
content on TikTok is perceived as informative, entertaining, r elevant, consistent, and credible,
consumers are more likely to proceed to a purchase decision on TikTok Shop.

Regarding H2, influencer marketing (IM) also demonstrates a positive and statistically significant
relationship with purchase decision (β = 0.158, t = 2.088, p = 0.037). Although this coefficient is
the smallest among all predictors, it still indicates tha t competent, trustworthy, and relatable
influencers can meaningfully encourage consumers to decide to buy through TikTok Shop.

For H3, online customer reviews (OCR) exert a positive and highly significant influence on
purchase decision (β = 0.272, t = 2.828, p = 0.005). This implies that reviews on TikTok Shop
which are informative, authentic, numerous, up to date, and enriched wi th photos or videos
strongly enhance consumers’ willingness to purchase.

Finally, H4 is also supported, as celebrity endorsement (CE) shows a positive and significant effect
on purchase decision (β = 0.287, t = 2.594, p = 0.010). In other words, credible, attractive
celebrities whose image fits the advertised product increase c onsumers’ trust and subsequently
their decision to purchase via TikTok Shop. Overall, these results confirm that content marketing,
influencer marketing, online customer reviews, and celebrity endorsement all play important roles
in shaping purchase decisions on the TikTok social commerce platform.

Discussions
The current study explores the influence of four pivotal forms of digital communication on
consumers’ purchase decisions within the context of TikTok Shop: content marketing, influencer
marketing, online customer reviews, and celebrity endorsement. Each of  these variables not only
has a distinct impact on consumer behavior but also plays a synergistic role in shaping purchase
intentions. The findings confirm that all four forms exert a positive and statistically significant
influence on purchasing decisions , thus reinforcing existing theoretical frameworks in social
commerce and marketing communications.

Effect of Content Marketing on Purchase Decision
First, content marketing was found to significantly enhance purchase decisions on TikTok Shop,
demonstrating a path coefficient of β = 0.223 (p = 0.045). This aligns with previous literature that
underscores the importance of quality content in digital spa ces, as engaging and informative
content tends to enhance consumer trust and reduce uncertainty (Sun & Choo, 2023). When users
encounter brand content that is not only entertaining but also relevant and credible, they are more
likely to move from mere exposure to intent to purchase (Othman & Rahim, 2019). This finding
emphasizes that successful content marketing must prioritize authenticity and consistency in
messaging to build trust and encourage transaction behaviors (Zahrah et al., 2024).

Moreover, the role of perceived credibility is critical. In an era saturated with promotional
messages, consumers have become adept at discerning authentic communications. Brands that
deliver transparent and honest content tend to foster positive brand attitudes, reinforcing the notion
that credibility significantly enhances the effectiveness of content (Firmanza & Artanti, 2022) .
Therefore, effective content marketing should not only aim to capture attention but also strive to
establish a credible and trustworthy communication foundation.

Effect of Influencer Marketing on Purchase Decision
The second hypothesis regarding the role of influencer marketing found a positive impact on
purchase decisions, albeit with a smaller path coefficient of β = 0.158 (p = 0.037). This relatively
modest effect suggests that while influencer marketing remains relevant, its effectiveness is
contingent upon several factors, including the perceived authenticity of influencers and the nature
of the relationship that consumers feel they share with them (Chung & Cho, 2017). Users are more
likely to respond positively when they perceive influencers as relatable and trustworthy figures
who authentically engage with the products they endorse.

Such findings align with previous research indicating that influencer credibility can enhance brand
attitudes and ultimately influence purchase intentions ( Weismueller et al., 2020 ; Torres et al.,
2019). However, in an environment flooded with influencer promotions, consumers may become
discerning, requiring stronger connections and genuine engagement from the endorsers before they
choose to convert (Wang, 2014). Therefore, while influencer marketing is beneficial, its success
may hinge on carefully selected partnerships and authentic communication strategies.

Effect of Online Customer Reviews on Purchase Decision
The study robustly confirmed that online customer reviews serve as a strong predictor of purchase
decisions, achieving the highest path coefficient of β = 0.272 (p = 0.005). This substantial influence
is commensurate with emerging literature highlighting the pivotal role of electronic word of mouth
(eWOM) in shaping consumer behaviors in digital marketplaces ( Sun & Choo, 2023; Othman &
Rahim, 2019). Consumers often rely on peer -generated content as it provides a nuanced view of
product performance—enhancing their confidence and mitigating perceived risks associated with
online shopping.

The effectiveness of customer reviews is further amplified within the TikTok Shop framework,
where reviews are readily accessible, and the interaction between consumers can be real-time and
dynamic (Zahrah et al., 2024) . The presence of multimedia elements within reviews, such as
images or videos from previous buyers, enhances perceived authenticity, making the reviews more
persuasive (Firmanza & Artanti, 2022). This reinforces the necessity for brands to actively manage
and encourage user-generated content, as it plays a critical role in informing potential buyers.

Effect of Celebrity Endorsement on Purchase Decision
Lastly, the study found celebrity endorsement to be a significant predictor of purchase decisions,
with a path coefficient of β = 0.287 (p = 0.010). This finding aligns with theories regarding the
impact of source credibility and attractiveness on consumer  behavior (Wang et al., 2012) .
Consumers are drawn to endorsements from celebrities they consider trustworthy and likable, who
can seamlessly integrate products into their individual narratives (Vidyanata et al., 2018 ). The
personal presentation of celebrities in short video format allows for a connection that can feel more
intimate and relatable compared to traditional advertising methods.

However, it is essential to recognize that celebrity endorsement is not universally effective;
alignment with brand identity and authenticity in the presentation of endorsements play
determinant roles in their effectiveness (Andryani & Salim, 2024). The implications suggest that
while leveraging celebrity power can yield significant results, marketers must ensure that
endorsements resonate with their target audience and reflect genuine connections with the
endorsed products.

Integrated Interpretation and Implications
In summarizing the findings, it is clear that purchase decisions on TikTok Shop are influenced by
