ABSTRACT
Penetration of short-form video platforms like TikTok and Instagram Reels demands culinary MSMEs in Jayapura
to adopt authentic, culturally relevant digital marketing. This study analyzes the effectiveness of combining Visual
Storytelling and local wisdom-based Copywriting in enhancing brand engagement and physical store visits for
Magic Coffee Jayapura. Using a Netnographic qualitative approach with a 7-day post-launch observation window
(September 8–15, 2026), quantitative metrics were extracted from TikTok Studio and Instagram Reel Insights,
integrated with video narrative transcripts and in-depth owner interviews. Results show that a 3-second Dual-Hook
Strategy (Sensory Visual Hook of cooking fried rice and Geo-Targeting Audio Hook 'DI SENTANI') effectively
prevented scrolling, yielding average watch times of 15.7s on TikTok and 13.0s on Reels. Inclusive local dialect
('tong makan yuk') and interactive Call-to-Action ('tag kam pu teman di kolom komen') achieved an Engagement
Rate (ER) of 8.56% on TikTok and 5.37% on Reels, reaching over 86% non-followers across both platforms.
Qualitative evidence confirms this digital virality directly triggered a surge in physical visits, overloading store
capacity. Culturally grounded video marketing paired with local media collaboration proves highly effective for
MSME competitiveness.
Keywords: Digital Marketing; Visual Storytelling; Copywriting; Local Wisdom; Engagement Rate; Magic Coffee
Jayapura.

INTRODUCTION
Digital economic transformation in Indonesia requires Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises
(MSMEs) to adopt information technology to maintain business competitiveness (Sugiyono, 2022). In
Papua Province, particularly across Jayapura City and Regency, the coffee shop industry has experienced
rapid growth driven by changing lifestyle preferences. Coffee consumption is no longer merely about
beverage intake, but has evolved into a social interaction vehicle and lifestyle symbol for millennials and
Gen-Z consumers (Rumadas & Purnomo, 2023). However, this rapid market growth creates intense
commercial competition, where local MSMEs frequently face major obstacles in building brand awareness

The proliferation of short-form video platforms such as TikTok and Instagram Reels has gradually
replaced conventional search engines in recommending culinary destinations (Widyaningtyas, 2022).
Social media users in Jayapura are heavily dominated by youth segments, representing the primary target
market for coffee shops. Nevertheless, most culinary MSMEs in Jayapura upload promotional content
without structured narrative concepts, thereby failing to generate optimal brand engagement (Fadilah et al.,
2024). To address this challenge, a digital marketing formulation that integrates Visual Storytelling and
local wisdom-based Copywriting is urgently required.
Visual Storytelling compiles engaging visual narratives through combinations of video, text
overlays, and audio to establish emotional connection with audiences (Sari & Widowati, 2023). Meanwhile,
local wisdom-based copywriting—such as employing Papuan dialect and everyday cultural references—
breaks down formal advertising barriers and creates strong psychological proximity with local consumers
(Syahputra & Aziza, 2022). The urgency of this research lies in modeling an independent, cost-effective
content strategy for MSMEs without relying on expensive external agency services (Putri et al., 2025).
The primary focus of this study is to analyze the effectiveness of combining Visual Storytelling
and local wisdom-based Copywriting on short-form video content for Magic Coffee MSME Jayapura.
Specific research objectives include: (1) identifying the effectiveness of the Dual-Hook Strategy within the
first 3 seconds; (2) dissecting linguistic patterns of Papuan dialect copywriting in triggering audience
interaction; (3) measuring Engagement Rate (ER) comparatively between TikTok and Instagram Reels; and
(4) evaluating the physical conversion impact of digital attention into actual store visits.

METODE PENELITIAN
This study adopts a descriptive qualitative approach based on digital observation or Netnography
(Kozinets, 2020). Netnography focuses on analyzing social interactions and digital footprints on social
media platforms to capture natural consumer behavior without disturbing the subjects' social context
(Pratama & Mutiaz, 2023). The primary observation object is the promotional video content of Magic
Coffee MSME posted simultaneously on TikTok Studio and Instagram Reels on September 8, 2026.
Quantitative metric data collection utilized a 7-day post-launch observation window (September
8–15, 2026). Data were extracted directly from official analytics dashboards in TikTok Studio and
Instagram Reel Insights. Recorded metrics include total views, account reach, average watch time, likes,
comments, shares, saves, reposts, and audience demographics (age, gender, follower status). Audience
engagement was evaluated using the standard Engagement Rate (ER) formula based on Total Views
(Budiarto & Santoso, 2023):
Engagement Rate (ER) = ((Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves) / Total Views) x 100%
In addition to quantitative metrics analysis, qualitative investigation was conducted through video
narrative structure analysis (audio transcripts and text overlays) and in-depth interviews with the owner of
Magic Coffee (Melinus Jikwa) to verify the relationship between digital performance and physical store
visitor surges.

RESULT AND DISCUSSION
Result and Discussion 1: Visual Storytelling Analysis and Dual-Hook Strategy
Netnographic analysis demonstrates that early audience attention capture is determined by
implementing a Dual-Hook Strategy within the first 3 seconds (golden seconds). A simultaneous synergy
between two hook elements was identified:
• Sensory Visual Hook: A close-up action shot displaying the cooking process of 'Nasi Goreng Magic'
on a hot pan, stimulating culinary appetite appeal and sensory perception.
• Geo-Targeting Audio Hook: Explicit verbal and text overlay opening 'DI SENTANI' ('IN
SENTANI') on the very first second of the video.
Combining appetizing food visuals with localized geographic audio hooks served as a pattern
interrupt that successfully prevented audience scrolling. This strategy achieved an average watch time of
15.7 seconds on TikTok and 13.0 seconds on Instagram Reels, representing high retention for a 96.31-
second video.

Result and Discussion 2: Local Wisdom-Based Copywriting Analysis
Linguistic analysis of audio transcripts and caption text identified three primary manifestations of
local wisdom copywriting:
• Inclusive Verbal Tagline ('Tong makan yuk'): Using 'tong' (short for Papuan dialect 'kitorang',
meaning 'we/us') establishes an inclusive, friendly invitation that breaks conventional advertising
barriers.
• Interactive Call-to-Action ('Tag kam pu teman di kolom komen'): Combining digital social
terminology ('tag') with Papuan dialect ('kam pu' = your) effectively triggers active audience
participation to share and comment.
• Community Persuasive Message ('Ayo tong terus dukung wirausaha asli Papua'): This message
imparts social emotional value, motivating store visits as an active form of support for indigenous
Papuan youth entrepreneurship.

Audience demographics align closely with the target coffee shop market. Audiences across both platforms
were heavily dominated by young productive groups aged 18–34 years (77% on TikTok and 73.4% on

Result and Discussion 4: Qualitative Impact on Partner's Physical Store Visits
This study identified strong causal evidence linking digital metric achievements with actual physical
business conversion. High save counts (115 on TikTok and 151 on IG) and shares (162 on TikTok and
423 on IG) prove the video served as a location bookmark. Based on interview feedback and chat logs
from Magic Coffee's owner (Melinus Jikwa):
"...this review helped so much, my team and I were overwhelmed receiving so many new
guests that we had never served before..." (Melinus Jikwa, Owner of Magic Coffee).
This finding confirms that local wisdom-based Visual Storytelling and Copywriting move beyond
vanity metrics, effectively driving real consumer purchasing decisions and store visits in Sentani.

CONCLUSION
Based on research findings, several key conclusions are drawn:
Implementing a 3-second Dual-Hook Strategy (Sensory Visual Hook of cooking fried rice and Geo-
Targeting Audio Hook 'DI SENTANI') significantly prevented scrolling and maintained audience watch
time retention.
Local wisdom copywriting formulations ('tong makan yuk', 'tag kam pu teman', 'Papua pasti bisa')
built strong psychological proximity, generating high Engagement Rates (8.56% on TikTok and 5.37% on
Reels) while reaching >86% non-followers.
Collaborative Marketing with local aggregator media (@info_sentani) multiplied content views to
53,991 and successfully converted digital attention into overwhelming physical store visits.
Practical recommendations for culinary MSMEs include consistently pairing sensory visuals with
local dialect in short-form videos. Future research should evaluate longitudinal financial sales conversions
quantitatively.
