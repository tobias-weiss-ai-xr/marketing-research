Abstract
Objective: The aim of this study is to examine how content marketing and customer experience, both
separately and together, influence revisit intention.
Design/Methods/Approach: The method used in this research is a quantitative approach. The research
population consists of tourists who access the Instagram content of Sumber Biru tourist destination and
those who have visited the site. A total of 100 respondents were selected through purposive sampling. The
data was then analyzed using methods suitable for the study's  objectives, this study uses Multiple Linear
Regression Analysis.
Findings: Content marketing has a strong and meaningful impact on revisit intention. Similiarly, customer
experience has been proven to which is proven to have a positive and significant impact on the desire of
tourists to return to visit the destination.
Originality/Value: This research explores the ralationship between content marketing and customer
experience on visitors' intention to return to Sumber Biru Tourism Park. In this study, the authors analyze
the various factors that influence customer experience and how content marketing can increase visitors'
desire to return. This research is useful for marketing professionals, destination managers, and researchers
interested in the effect of digital marketing strategies on visitor behavior. The authors employed a
quantitative research approach with a quantitative approach to measure the relationship and provide new
insights into the influence of content marketing and customer experience in the tourism industry.
Practical/Policy implication : This study focuses on visitors to Sumber Biru Tourism Park, offering
insights for managers to optim ize content marketing and enhance customer experiences to boost return
visits. Future research could explore other destinations and include variables like visitor demographics or
promotion types to deepen understanding of factors influencing revisit intention.

Introduction
Tourism is a labor -intensive industry because it has a multiplier effect by creating various beneficial
benefits. Through the tourism industry developed in an area, it will create jobs for the surrounding
community because running it requires management an d services for visitors. Tourism is one of the most
important sectors because is one of the sources of foreign exchange for the country and is able to make a
significant contribution to significant contribution to nation building (Priyanto, 2016).
A tourism village is a combination of attractions, accommodations, and facilities that blend into the
local community's lifestyle, customs, and traditions (Allyreza & Rahmi Winangsih, 2023). Villages have the
potential to become community -based tourist destination s rooted in local cultural wisdom, while also
serving as a driver for economic growth through mutual cooperation and sustainability.
Sumber Biru is a natural tourist attraction in Wonomerto Village, Wonosalam District, Jombang
Regency, East Java. Sumber Biru nature tourism is located in the Anjasmoro mountain area. It was named
Sumber Biru because in the past there was a water source under a large banyan tree. The water that comes
out seems to be blue in color because of the lush shade of the tree. Wana Wisata Sumber Biru also provides
benches that are neatly arranged on the river flow so that tourists really enjoy a very cool place with green
natural nuances.
Instagram was originally created by Kevin Systrom and Mike Krieger. In official website, Instagram
defines itself as a free photo and video sharing application for smartphone users, and video sharing
application for smartphone users. Instagram users can share photos and videos with friends and followers
while also interacting by viewing, liking, and commenting on posts (Watajdid et al., 2021). Instagram is
used as a promotional media by the manager of Wana Wisata Sumber Biru with the aim of increasing the
attractiveness and interest of visitors and tourists, so tha t they are encouraged to come and enjoy the
beauty and facilities offered by the destination. The uploaded content feels very authentic and relevant,
giving visitors the satisfaction of enjoying Sumber Biru tourism and encouraging them to return.
Content marketing is a relatively new yet highly dynamic field, with various definitions and views
among academics and practitioners on its necessity and application. It is often referred to by terms such as
branded content marketing, digital content marketing, or content marketing through social media. Overall,
previous research provides a deeper understanding of content marketing as a strategy in digital marketing
that emphasizes a strategic approach to increase engagement and build consumer trust. Marketers seek to
increase brand engagement and trust through valuable digital content on digital platforms, which
ultimately provides long -term benefits for brands, such as strengthening relationships and building
consumer loyalty to brands (du Plessis, 2022). Content marketing contained in Wana Wisata Sumber Biru
displays the sensation of eating on a clean river stream and is still very well preserved its naturalness is
unique because visitors can enjoy a variety of culinary menus while playing water, a va riety of culinary
menus that visitors can enjoy are available at the cashier on the banks of the river so it is easy to place an
order with a very friendly price in the visitors' pockets. After seeing Conten on relevant social media, it will
create a good Customer Experience for a village.
Customer experience is an experience that is memorable and not easily forgotten, including positive
experiences and holistic experiences that are felt through all five senses (Anjani et al., 2021). According to
Kavitha and Haritha (2018) in (Makudza, 2021), customer experience is how customers perceive an
organization's treatment of them. According to Buttle (2009), it results from cognitive and emotional
responses triggered by interactions with employee s, processes, technology, products, services, and other
business aspects (Makudza, 2021). Customer experience encompasses how customers react to their
interactions with a company across different channels and timeframes, whether before, during, or after
making a purchase or using a service. It has become a key factor in maintaining a competitive advantage
(Holmlund et al., 2020).
This research aims to identify whether each visitor who came before had seen the marketing content
uploaded on the social media of Sumber Biru tourist destination. In addition, this research also wanted to
understand whether visitors had a positive or negative experience during their visit, which could influence
interest in returning. Previous research conducted by (Evelyna, 2022) shows that customer experience has
a positive and significant influence on revisit intention. This finding indicates that customer experience
plays an important role in revisit intention. Therefore, the authors feel int erested in conducting research
with the title Revisit intention Wana Wisata Sumber Biru: A Review of Content Marketing and Customer
Experience.

Literature Review and Hypotheses Development
Digital Marketing
Digital marketing involves leveraging multiple online strategies and platforms to engage with customers in
the digital spaces they frequent most. From websites to digital branding elements such as online
advertising, email marketing, digital brochures and more, there are a variety of approaches that fall under
the category of “digital marketing”.  Digital marketing refers to efforts to market products or services by
utilizing digital technology, primarily through the internet, but also includes the use of mobile phones,
display advertising, and various other digital media (Desai, 2019).
Content Marketing
Content marketing is the management of strategies that aim to ensure accurate identification, supported
by in-depth analysis, in order to attract the attention of potential customers through content distribution
on digital media (Pandrianto & Sukendro, 201 8). According to Clesson and Jonsson (2017) in (Sya’idah &
Jauhari, 2022), content marketing is a managerial process in which companies seek to identify, evaluate,
and meet the needs of their customers through the use of content that is distributed electro nically via the
internet.

Customer Experience
Based on this general understanding, customer experience can specifically be defined as the result of
consumer interactions with products, companies, or other elements in the organization, which then
generate certain reactions (Hasniati et al., 2021).  According to Shaw and Ivens (2002:6), customer
experience is a combination of two elements, namely physical and emotional, implemented by the
company. These two elements play a role in influencing consumer percept ions that are formed at every
interaction between customers and companies (Mokalu et al., 2019).

Revisit Intention
Interests are individual decisions that are closely related to attitudes, where they form the main basis for
forming prejudices when making decisions. According to Arhisondha (2012) in, visiting interest is a
consumer response that reflects the desire to visit an object. This revisit intention is adapted from the
concept of purchase intention, which ultimately forms perceptions (Susanto & Astu tik, 2020). Interest in
returning to visit is consumer behavior that  is shown through a positive response to the quality of  service
or products provided by a company, which in turn encourages the desire to visit the company again (Umur
et al., 2022).

Method
This research uses a quantitative approach. The data sources in this study consist of primary data and
secondary data. Primary data was obtained through distributing questionnaires to respondents who fit the
criteria set based on the research variables. Wh ile secondary data is obtained through literature studies,
namely by collecting articles, journals, or other written sources relevant to the variables studied. The
population in this study includes tourists who access Instagram content about Sumber Biru to urist
destination and those who have visited the destination. The sample was taken using purposive sampling
technique, with a total of 100 respondents. The sample criteria include tourists who have seen the Sumber
Biru tourist destination content via Instagram and / or tourists who have visited the destination. To analyze
the data collected, this study uses the Multiple Linear Regression Analysis method to determine the effect
of content marketing variables and customer experience on revisit intention.

Conclusion and future direction

Based on the research results, it can be concluded that content marketing and customer experience have a
significant influence on the revisit intention Sumber Biru Tourism Village. Partially, each variable -both
content marketing and customer experience -contributes significantly to increasing visitors' interest in
returning. In addition, simultaneously, the two variables also have a strong influence on visitors' decisions
to visit this tourist destination again.
As a recommendation, the manager of Sumber Biru Tourism Village is expected to create more
attractive marketing content to increa se tourist attractiveness. In addition, improving service quality,
including venue renovation and increasing comfort, needs to be considered so that the visitor experience
is getting better. Establishing active communication with visitors can also help increase word of mouth. For
future researchers, the results of this study can be used as a reference and can be developed by examining
other variables that have the potential to influence revisit intention.

Implication

The implication of this research is that the manager of Sumber Biru Tourism Village needs to pay
more attention to content marketing strategies and improving customer experience to attract and maintain
visitor interest. By utilizing interesting and relevant content marketing on social media, tourist villages can
increase exposure and attract more potential visitors. In addition, a positive customer experience, bo th in
terms of atmosphere, service, and facilities, will create a good impression and encourage them to return
and recommend this tourist spot to others.
From an academic perspective, this research contributes to enriching the literature related to the
influence of content marketing and customer experience on revisit intention. These findings can also be the
basis for further research to explore other factors that influence visitor loyalty. In addition, for tourism
industry players, the results of this study can be a guide in designing marketing strategies and managing
tourist destinations more effectively.
