ABSTRACT
The increasing use of TikTok Shop and the growing demand for
skincare products among Generation Z have significantly
influenced consumer purchasing behavior. For Muslim
consumers, purchase decisions are shaped not only by digital
marketing factors but also by awareness of products' halalness,
making it important to examine the role of halal awareness in the
digital marketplace. This study aims to analyze the influence of
content marketing and online customer reviews on skincare
purchase decisions through TikTok Shop among Generation Z
consumers in Surabaya and to examine the moderating role of
halal awareness. A quantitative approach was employed using a
survey method. Data were collected through questionnaires
distributed to 115 respondents selected through Purposive
sampling and analyzed using Partial Least Squares -Structural
Equation Modeling (PLS -SEM). The findings indicate that
content marketing and online customer reviews have a positive
and significant effect on purchase decisions, with content
marketing emerging as the most influential factor. Halal
awareness was found to weaken the influence of both variables
on purchase decisions, indicating that Muslim Generation Z
consumers carefully consider the halal aspect before making
purchases. Therefore, skincare companies should provide
informative content, encourage authentic customer reviews, and
clearly communicate halal certification and product ingredients.

Introduction
The digital transformation is now bringing about crucial changes in consumer preferences,
particularly in the context of e-commerce transactions. The digital era is characterised by the rapid growth
in internet usage, which has driven changes in consumer b ehaviour regarding the use of products and
services (Fatun, 2024). The primary focus of companies in digital marketing is to build consumer awareness
of products and maximise interaction with consumers via social media, thereby increasing their interest in
seeking further information through to the point of purchase decisions (Haqiqotus Sa’adah, 2023).
The rapid pace of digital transformation has significantly altered consumers' behaviour and
intensified competition across various business sectors. In this environment, digital marketing is no longer
viewed as a complementary strategy but rather as a crucial element for sustaining business growth and
maintaining a competitive advantage. Digital marketing can be defined as the utilization of internet-based
technologies through various digital platforms, such as social media, e-commerce, and websites, to promote
products or services, establish relationships with consumers, and facilitate online transactions. Through
these platforms, businesses are able to expand market reach, strengthen communication with consumers,
and simplify purchasing activities without being limited by distance and time . (Karundeng & Suyanto,
2025)
In line with these developments, electronic commerce (e -commerce) is a system for buying and
selling goods and services online, utilising electronic media and the internet as intermediaries. E-commerce
facilitates the exchange of products and services via internet-based platforms and can be applied to various
business activities, including business-to-business transactions, which are commercial activities conducted
between one business entity and another via digital systems (Arisandy et al., 2022).
E-commerce also offers various benefits as a digital business strategy, such as time and cost
efficiency due to fast transaction processes that do not require direct interaction, as well as the ability to
expand market reach beyond geographical boundaries.  Furthermore, e -commerce facilitates access to
product information, enabling consumers to compare products and supporting more effective digital
marketing activities. However, e-commerce also has several limitations, such as data security and privacy
risks, the potential for fraud in online transactions, reliance on an internet connection, and the inability of
consumers to inspect the product in person before purchasing, which can lead to a mismatch between
expectations and the quality of the product received (Lia et al., 2025)
TikTok is one of the e-commerce platforms experiencing rapid growth. It offers users a great deal
of freedom, such as the ability to interact and be creative through a wide range of content. It was developed
by Zhang Yiming, a software engineering graduate  from Nankai University, through the Chinese
technology company ByteDance in September 2016. In addition to providing entertainment content, the

The development of platforms such as TikTok reflects the rapid advancement of digital

internet connectivity, and data-driven systems that significantly influence communication patterns and
information dissemination in society. Social media has become an important medium in digital
communication, enabling information to spread quickly and interactively to a wide audience. The
emergence of digital pl atforms also illustrates how technological innovation has gradually transformed
communication practices in the digital era  (Dimas Ahmad Rifandi Irwansyah, 2021).

Literature Review
Theory of Planned Behaviour
The Theory of Reasoned Action (TRA) was developed in 1967 by Icek Ajzen and Martin Fishbein.
Subsequently, in 1988, the Theory of Reasoned Action (TRA) evolved into the Theory of Planned Behaviour
(TPB). This was due to the limitations of the previous model in explaining behaviour that was not entirely
within an individual’s control. This theory aims to examine how attitudes influence human behaviour,
including decision making, a person’s reasons for waking, and various other forms of behaviour. According
to this theory can be used to analyse behaviour that is driven by an individual’s motivation or intention.
Such intentions are formed through three main factors: (attitude towards the behaviour), (subjective norm),
and (perceived behavioural control).
Content Marketing
( Jeo Pulizzi, 2013)  defines content marketing as a marketing and business process that involves
creating and distributing valuable and compelling content to attract, acquire, and retain a clearly defined
target audience, ultimately encouraging profitable customer actions. (Chaffey & Ellis -Chadwick, 2019)
explain that one of the methods used in marketing is content marketing, which focuses on developing
content that is valuable, consistent, engaging , and entertaining. This can take the form of blog articles,
videos, infographics, or other digital formats, with the aim of disseminating information about a product.
According to (Karr, 2016) Content marketing can be measured using five indicators:
1. Reader Cognition: This indicator measures the extent to which audiences are able to understand
the information presented in the content. Content that is clear, informative, and easy to

understand enables consumers to obtain product information effectively and reduces
misunderstandings regarding the product being promoted.
2. Sharing motivation: This indicator reflects the ability of content to encourage audiences to
share information with others.  Content that is perceived as useful, relevant, and interesting
tends to be shared more frequently, thereby increasing the reach and visibility of the product
among potential consumers.
3. Persuasion: The indicator measures the extent to which content can influence consumers'
attitudes, interests, and preferences toward a product. Persuasive content can strengthen
consumer confidence and encourage a positive perception that supports purchasing decisions.
4. Decision making: The indicator assesses the role of content in helping consumers make
purchasing decisions. Content that provides complete, accurate, and relevant information can
reduce consumer uncertainty and facilitate the evaluation of product alternatives before making
a purchase.
5. Life factors: This indicator measures the relevance of content to consumers' daily lives, needs,
and lifestyles. Content that aligns with consumers' personal experiences and interests is more
likely to attract attention and influence purchasing behaviour.
The indicators serve as benchmarks for evaluating the effectiveness of content marketing and
delivering information, building engagement, influencing consumer perceptions, and encouraging
purchasing decisions.
Referring to previous findings (Nabila Ayu Kristina, n.d. 2023) , (Eka Yuni Astuti et al., 2025) ,
(Ansir Launtu & Chaerunnisa Rumianti, 2025), (Listiyani et al., 2023) consistently demonstrate that content
marketing positively and significantly influences purchase decisions. In addition, existing research
highlights that well-designed content can attract consumer attention and enhance interest in products.
Content that is clear, up -to-date, and aligned with audience preferences has been shown to improve
engagement, strengthen brand awareness, and increase the likelihood of purchase (Nuril Qomariah et al.,
2024). Based on these theoretical considerations and empirical findings, the following hypothesis is
proposed:
H1: Content Marketing has a positive influence on purchasing decisions.

Purchase Decision
(Philip Kotler dan Gary Armstrong, 2012)  explain that consumers generally tend to purchase the
brand they prefer most. However, the actual purchase decisions may be influenced by two main factors,
namely the attitudes or opinions of others and unexpected situational conditions that arise during the
purchasing process. (Kotler, Philip & Keller, 2006)  also emphasise in their book that consumer buyer
behaviour relates to the buying behaviour of individual final consumers and households that buy goods and
services for personal consumption. Based on this definition, a purchase decision can be understood as the
consumer’s act of selecting a product, namely the stage at which the consumer makes a final choice
regarding the product or brand to be purchased. According to (Philip Kotler dan Gary Armstrong, 2012) ,
indicators of a purchase decision include:
1. Identification of Problem or Need: Before making a purchase, consumers first identify a
problem or a need when they realise the discrepancy between the current situation and their
desired outcome.
2. Information Search: consumers do not always active out information about what they need.

3. Evaluation of Alternatives: Manufacturers need to understand how consumers process
information about products, brands, and purchasing decisions.
4. Purchase Decision: Manufacturers need to understand that consumers have their own ways of
processing the information they obtain through various channels, which ultimately leads them
to decide to purchase the product they deem suitable.
5. Post Purchase Behaviour: In the post-purchase stage, dissatisfaction with the purchased product
will lead to a negative attitude towards a brand, potentially resulting in it being removed from
the list of future choices, or conversely, the opposite may occur.
With the five indicators used by purchase decision theory to explain consumer behaviour, the

platforms have become the primary sources of reference for consumers in making purchasing decisions
(Haqiqotus Sa'adah, 2026). Furthermore, purchasing decisions are also influenced by customer satisfaction
with product quality and responsive service, as well as consumers' perception of price, trust, and brand
image. These factors encourage consumers to remain loyal to a product, which is ref lected in consistent
repeat purchasing behaviour. Therefore, companies are required not only to provide attractive digital

increasingly competitive market conditions. Referring to previous findings (Listiyani et al., 2023) ,
(Salsabilah et al., 2024) , (Amalia, 2023), content marketing and online customer reviews have a positive

Halal Awareness
Before being adopted into the Indonesian language, the word halal originated from the Arabic
“halla-yahillu-halalan”. The term halal refers to something that is permitted, allowed, or in accordance with
the provisions of Islamic law. Halal in accordance wi th Islamic law. Islam emphasises the importance of
considering the halal status of everything that is to be consumed (Awwaliah, 2022).
  (Aziz & Chok, 2013)  define halal awareness as the extent to which consumers understand halal
products. In this context, halal awareness is a process aimed at conveying information whilst enhancing
consumers ‘understanding of products that are permissible for consumption to be used  by Muslims.
Furthermore, according to (Golnaz, R., et al 2010) halal awareness is closely linked to consumers’ attitudes
and knowledge regarding halal principles, as reflected in the selection of ingredients, handling, packaging,
and hygiene of products in accordance with Islamic law. Consumers with a high level of halal awareness
tend to view halal products as those whose halal status has been certified by the relevant authorities.
Previous studies indicate that halal awareness plays an important role in influencing purchasing
decisions among Muslim Generation Z consumers, particularly in the context of cosmetic and skincare
products. Consumers with higher levels of halal awareness tend to be more selective in evaluating products
and prioritize halal-certified products as a form of compliance with Islamic values (Septianda & Priantina,
2024). Furthermore, digital marketing exposure through social media has been shown to significantly
influence consumers' purchase intentions toward halal cosmetic products. However, the influence of digital

content, recommendations, and social media exposure is till evaluated based on religious considerations,
suggesting that halal awareness may strengthen or weaken the effect of marketing stimuli on consumer
purchasing decisions (Sokhiful Jannah & Indra, 2024).
According to (Rachmawati E, 2022) , a person’s level of halal awareness is measured based on four
indicators, including:
1. Checking the ingredients before use: consumers check the ingredients or consumption of the
product before using it.
2. Do not purchase products without a halal logo: consumers will not purchase a product if it does
not bear a halal logo, as it is considered to lack a guarantee of halal status.
3. Choosing halal products from well-known brands: products that are certified halal and familiar
are more likely to be chosen by consumers.
4. Choosing halal products with a good reputation: consumers are more likely to choose halal
products with a good brand reputation, as they are seen as consistent in terms of quality and
compliance with halal standards.
Referring to previous findings (Romi & Sukma, 2021), (Agus Arwani, 2021), (Amin & Fikriyah,
2023), (U. P. Lestari, 2021), which indicates that halal awareness has a positive and significant influence
on purchasing decisions. Based on the theory and previous research findings, the following hypotheses are
formulated:
H3: Halal Awareness moderates the effect of content marketing on purchasing decisions.

Discussion
The Influence of Content Marketing on Purchase Decision
The analysis results show that content marketing positively and significantly influences purchase
decision, with a path coefficient of 0.373, a t-statistic of 5.202, and a p-value of 0.000. These results suggest
that an improvement in the quality of content presented on TikTok Shop significantly increases skincare
purchase decisions among Generation Z consumers in Surabaya. Therefore, H1 is accepted.
The theory, these findings support the view ( Jeo Pulizzi, 2013) that content marketing is a strategic
process involving the creation and distribution of valuable content to attract audiences and encourage
actions that benefit the company. In this study, the content formats used were educational videos, visual
testimonials, and after examples, which were effective in fostering a positive perception of the product.
From the perspective of the Theory of Planned Behaviour (Ajzen, 1991), this influence reflects the
formation of an attitude toward the behaviour , namely a positive consumer attitude towards the act of
purchasing following the receipt of information stimuli deemed relevant and suited to their skincare needs.
For Generation Z, who are deeply immersed in social media, brief, visual, and easily understandable content
serves as a means of forming beliefs prior to making a purchasing decision. This finding is consistent with
the research by (Salsabilah et al., 2024)  and (Damayanti & Rahayu, 2025) , which confirms that content
marketing on TikTok is highly effective in driving skincare purchasing decisions. However, the empirical
findings in this study indicate that the effectiveness of content marketing is particularly pronounced among
Generation Z in Surabaya, who have high levels of skincare consumption and are accustomed to using
TikTok as their primary source of information.

The Influence of Online Customer Reviews on Purchase Decision
The analysis results show that online customer reviews positively and significantly influence decisions,
with a path coefficient of 0.262, a t -statistic of 3.139, and a p -value of 0.002. These results suggest that
higher quality and more credible customer reviews increase the likelihood of Generation Z consumers in
Surabaya purchasing skincare products through TikTok Shop. Therefore, H2 is accepted.
In theory, the findings reinforce the view (Hennig-Thurau et al., 2004) that online customer reviews
form a form of electronic word of mouth (e-WOM), which explains that consumer opinions on the internet
serve as a source of information capable of influencing the purchasing decisions of other consumers. For
Generation Z in Surabaya, who actively use TikTok as a reference source for beauty products, reviews are
considered important because they relate directly to the results of use, skin compatibility, and potential risks
of use, making the experiences of other users the primary source of validation. By utilising reviews in the
form of positive comments, ratings, and videos showing the results of product use, consumer uncertainty
regarding product quality can be reduced.
From the perspective of the Theory of Planned Behaviour (Ajzen, 1991), this influence reflects the
formation of a subjective norm, that is, an individual’s tendency to be influenced by the opinions and
experiences of others within a digital environment. In the context of the TikTok Shop platform, customer
reviews in the form of comments, ratings , and user experiences shape a digital social norm that fosters
positive consumer perceptions of products, thereby increasing the likelihood of making a purchase.
The findings are consistent with the research by (Arridho et al., 2023) , (Dwi Puspitaningrum et al.,
2024), and (Erdiansyah & Wijaya, 2023), which confirms that online customer reviews have a significant
influence on skincare purchasing decisions. However, this study expands upon previous empirical evidence
by demonstrating that, within the context of the TikTok platform, video reviews exert a stronger influence
on shaping purchase confidence compared to text-based reviews on other e-commerce platforms.
The Role of Halal Awareness in Moderating the Effect of Content Marketing on Purchase
Decisions
  The analysis shows that the moderating role of halal awareness in the relationship between content
marketing and purchasing decisions produced a path coefficient of -0217, a t -statistic of 2.659, and a p-
value of 0.008. This negative and significant coefficient suggests that halal awareness weakens the effect
of content marketing on purchasing decisions. Therefore, H4 is accepted.
The theory, these findings reinforce the view (Aziz & Chok, 2013) that a high level of halal awareness
influences how consumers respond to marketing information. Among Muslim Generation Z skincare users
in Surabaya, consumers with a high level of halal awareness are not only attracted to visually appealing
content but a lso conduct further assessments of the product’s halal aspects, such as halal certification,
ingredient safety, and brand reputation.
From the perspective of the  Theory of Planned Behaviour (Ajzen, 1991),  these findings reflect the
formation of perceived behavioural control, namely the consumer’s ability to control decisions based on
their internal values. In other words, although content marketing is capable of fostering a positive attitude
towards a product, Generation Z in Surabaya still exercises control over their purch asing decisions by
considering halal aspects. It is this situation that causes the influence of content marketing on purchasing
decisions to be weak, as consumers tend to be more cautious before making a purchase.

The Role of Halal Awareness in Moderating the Influence of Online Customer Reviews on
Purchase Decisions
The analysis results show that the moderating role of halal awareness on the relationship between
online customer reviews and purchasing decisions produced a path coefficient of -0.185, a t -statistic of
2.334, and a p -value of 0.002. This negative and significant coefficient suggests that halal awareness
weakens the effect of online customer reviews on purchasing decisions. Therefore, H4 is accepted.
These findings confirm that positive reviews from other users do not always directly drive purchasing
decisions, particularly when Generation Z possesses a high level of halal awareness. From the perspective
of the  Theory of Planned Behaviour (Ajzen, 1991)it is evident that the influence of subjective norms formed
through online customer reviews is limited when consumers possess perceived behavioral control based on
religious values. In other words, although online reviews create social pressure or a normative push to buy,
the final decision of Generation Z in Surabaya will still be influenced by their ability to control their
behaviour in accordance with th eir religious beliefs. Thus, the digital social pressure arising from the
dominance of positive reviews does not necessarily dominate purchasing decisions when consumers hold
strong halal principles.
In addition to content marketing, online customer reviews, and halal awareness, purchasing decisions
among Generation Z may also be influenced by several other factors identified in previous studies. Price is
considered one of the main determinants because Generation Z consumers tend to evaluate product
affordability in the balance between cost and perceived benefits before making a purchase (Jurnal & Mea,
2025). Product quality is another important consideration, particularly regarding product effectiveness,
ingredient safety, and suitability for individual skin conditions (Devi et al., 2025) . Furthermore, brand
image can strengthen consumers' trust and increase the likelihood of purchase decisions. (Ayu et al., 2023).
The influence of influences or key opinion leaders (KOLs) also plays an important role, especially on social
media platform such as TikTok, where recommendations from public figures can shape consumer
perceptions and enhance a product's credibility (Mandala et al., 2025) . In addition, lifestyle factors and
social media trends may encourage Generation Z consumers to follow popular products that align with their
preferences and self-image. Therefore, purchasing decisions are complex and may be influenced by various
factors beyond the variables examined in this study (Safitri et al., 2025).
These findings are consistent with research (Rachmawati E, 2022), which suggests that halal awareness
can act as a moderating mechanism in purchasing decisions. However, this study provides new empirical
evidence showing that for Muslim Generation Z users of skincare products in Surabaya, the moderating
role tends to be weakened in the context of purchases made via TikTok Shop. These results contribute to
enriching the study of young Muslim consumers’ behaviour within the digital social commerce ecosystem.
Conclusion
The research examines how content marketing and online customer reviews shape skincare
purchase decisions through TikTok Shop among Generation Z consumers in Surabaya, while positioning
halal awareness as a moderating factor. The empirical results indicate  that Bolt variables positively
contribute to purchase decisions, whereas halal awareness reduces the strength of these associations. These
outcomes further reinforce the applicability of the Theory of Planner Behaviour of Muslim Generation Z
skincare consumers.

The findings suggest that positive content and customer reviews can encourage purchasing
decisions. However, consumers with higher levels of halal awareness tend to be more selective and consider
religious values before making a purchase. This indicates th at purchasing decisions among Muslim
Generation Z consumers are influenced not only by digital marketing factors but also by personal beliefs
and values. In addition, previous studies have shown that purchasing decisions may also be affected by
other facto rs, such as price, product quality, brand image, influencer recommendations, lifestyle , and
promotional activities.
Practically, these findings highlight the importance for skincare companies to develop informative
content, encourage authentic customer reviews, and provide clear halal product information to build
consumers’ trust. Nevertheless, this study has several limitations, including its focus on Generation Z
consumers in Surabaya who purchase skincare products through TikTok Shop and the limited number of
variables examined. Therefore, future research is recommended to involve broader respondent groups,
different geographical areas, and additional variables in order to develop a more comprehensive
understanding of skincare purchasing decisions in the digital marketplace.
