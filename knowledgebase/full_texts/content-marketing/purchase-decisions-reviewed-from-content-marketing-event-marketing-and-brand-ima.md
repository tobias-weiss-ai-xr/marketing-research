Abstract
The purpose of this study is to determine the influence of content marketing, event marketing,
and brand image on consumer purchasing decisions at the Deliwafa Store in Sidoarjo. This
study uses a quantitative approach with a population of Sidoarjo residen ts aged 17 years and
over who have made purchases at the store. The sampling technique was carried out using a
non-probability sampling method through a purposive sampling approach with a total of 100
respondents. Data were collected through a questionnaire using a Likert scale as a measuring
tool. Data analysis was carried out using the PLS-SEM method using SmartPLS 4.0 software.
The results of the study prove that content marketing, event marketing, and brand image
influence consumer purchasing decisions.
Keywords— Content Marketing; Event Marketing; Brand Image; Purchase Decision

INTRODUCTION
The existence of businesses and enterprises today requires them to be capable of innovating,
utilizing available opportunities, and creating strategies that align with technological developments to
reach consumers more effectively and efficiently, as well as to attract them to purchase the distributed
products (Kenneth & Traver, 2021). To increase sales and grow brand loyalty, companies use promotion
as a communication technique to introduce their brand, products, and services to consumers. The
purpose of promotion itself is to influence consumers in making purchasing decisions (Abdul et al.,
2021).
The economy continues to develop and is moving toward increasingly competitive business rivalry,
particularly among companies that produce similar goods and services, especially in the retail sector.
Retail is a type of business that sells goods directly t o consumers and has a broad reach in meeting
consumer needs (Mumtaz et al., 2024 ). The presence of information and communication technology
has made a significant contribution to the growth of the retail industry today, particularly in terms of
marketing. In the past, marketing could only be done directly or through word of mouth, but no w the
opposite is true. Social media has evolved into an efficient marketing communication model for
building business communication  (Hamid, 2022). In Indonesia, social media usage is dominated by
several major platforms. The following is an overview of social media usage in Indonesia:

A purchase decision is the initial step in a consumer's decision-making process to buy a product
(Liana & Oktafani, 2020 ). Purchasing decisions are closely related to consumer behaviour, which is
based on their experiences and insights regarding a product (Mariani & Sari, 2023 ). Consumers will
seek what can fulfil their needs and wants. After identifying a suitable option, they will evaluate and
consider various information related to the timing and amount of money required for the purchase
(Firmansyah, 2018). Therefore, consumers’ purchasing decisions when buying goods and services vary
greatly. These decisions are also influenced by the marketing strategies implemented by the product -
creating companies  (Hidayati et al., 2024) . Through content marketing, event marketing, and brand
image, consumers can determine how the chosen product will affect their final purchasing decision.
Content marketing is one of the factors that can influence purchasing decisions (Mukarromah
et al., 2022). It plays an important role in driving consumer engagement and building brand trust, which

Previous studies on the content marketing variable have shown differing results. Some earlier

LITERATURE REVIEW
Purchase decision is a decision-making process that involves selecting one option from two or
more alternatives based on certain considerations  (Indrasari, 2019). According to another opinion, a
purchase decision is the consumer’s direct involvement in the process of selecting and purchasing a
product offered by the seller (Rahayu, Sri, 2021). Based on these definitions, it can be concluded that a
purchase decision is a process involving individuals in choosing and deciding to buy a product from
several available alternatives based on certain considerations. Therefore, purchase decisions not onl y
involve the evaluation process in selecting the appropriate product but also include the consumer’s
direct involvement in accepting and using the product after purchase . According to Indrasari (2019),
some indicators of purchasing decisions include the following:
1. Product choice refers to the consumer’s decision to use their money to acquire a certain product or
to use it for other needs.
2. Brand choice refers to the consumer’s decision about which brand to buy.
3. Channel choice refers to the consumer’s decision regarding which distribution channel to visit.
4. Purchase timing relates to when the consumer makes the purchase, which may vary depending on
their needs.
5. Purchase amount refers to the consumer’s decision on the quantity of the product to be bought at
a certain time, which may include more than one product.
Content marketing is a marketing strategy that involves the planning, creation, and targeted
distribution of content to reach consumers in a relevant way and support them to become customers
(Hamidah et al., 2021). Content marketing is a promotional effort focused on creating and publishing
valuable, relevant, and consistent content to stimulate interest and engage the audience, with the primary
goal of generating business profit (Sari et al., 2021). Based on these two definitions, it can be concluded
that content marketing is a marketing method involving the process of planning, creating, and
distributing valuable and relevant content to attract and engage the right audience, to increase business
profits. According to Hamidah et al. (2021), some indicators influencing the content marketing aspect
include the following:
1. Reader Cognition: The shared content is easy to understand, facilitating consumers in receiving
the information conveyed.
2. Sharing Motivation: The information in the shared content provides insights to consumers.
3. Persuasion: Effective content can attract consumer interest to further explore a particular product.
4. Decision Making: The shared content can assist consumers in the decision-making process related
to product purchase.
5. Life Factors: The shared content has benefits and positive values for consumers.
Content marketing utilizes videos containing engaging information to support promotions and
build a positive brand image (Ayesha et al., 2022 ). Additionally, content marketing is regarded as a
valuable asset that helps companies build trust, educate consumers, increase engagement, and influence
purchasing decisions (Erwin et al., 2023). This statement is supported by previous research identifying
a relationship between content marketing and purchasing decisions (Putri et al., 2024). Further support
indicates that content marketing partially has a positive effect on purchasing decisions  (Hidayati,
Fitriana Nurul, 2024) . Moreover, other studies show a significant relationship between content
marketing and purchasing decisions  (Mahardini et al., 2022) . This is also corroborated by earlier
research demonstrating that the content marketing variable has a significant impact on purchasing

Event marketing is a promotional strategy that connects a brand with cultural, social, or other
types of activities that convey public relevance to provide experiences to consumers  (Candra, 2023).
Additionally, event marketing serves as a promotional tool aimed at capturing attention and engaging
consumers to interact directly during the event (Firmansyah, 2020). From these definitions, it can be
concluded that event marketing is not only a means of promotion but also provides consumers with
experiences and opportunities to interact directly with the brand.  According to Candra (2023), some
indicators used to measure the effectiveness of event marketing include the following:
1. Innovative (Enterprise): Refers to companies that organize innovative events, attracting consumers
to participate.
2. Entertaining (Entertainment): Refers to events that provide benefits by delivering enjoyable and
entertaining experiences, allowing consumers to feel the excitement firsthand.
3. Impressive (Excitement): Refers to how the event can create memorable experiences, increasing
consumer engagement and satisfaction.
In addition to content marketing, event marketing also influences purchasing decisions. These
two marketing strategies are interconnected, where event marketing can be used to deliver content
directly to consumers. By providing interactive and engaging exp eriences, event marketing can build
relationships with consumers that influence their purchasing decisions  (Khoerunnisa, 2020) . This
statement aligns with previous research findings that event marketing has a significant effect on
purchasing decisions (Ardiansyah, Putra, 2024). Further support from other studies indicates that event
marketing positively affects purchasing decisions  (Tri et al., 2021) . Additionally, other findings
demonstrate that event marketing has a positive impact on purchasing decisions (Meitasari & Setiawati,
2020). Moreover, some studies state that event marketing influences purchasing decisions both partially
and simultaneously (Puspitasari & Yunani, 2022). This is consistent with earlier research showing that
event marketing drives the occurrence of purchasing decisions (Normaladewi, 2024).
Brand image is the perception formed toward a brand, originating from consumers’ or
customers’ prior information and experiences with that brand (Sitorus et al., 2022). Brand image refers
to the perception in consumers’ minds that shapes their trust in a brand (Sitorus et al., 2022). Based on
these definitions, it can be concluded that brand image refers to the perception that arises in consumers’
minds when recalling a particular brand, influenced by their experiences with that brand. According to
Putri et al. (2021), several indicators are included in the concept of brand image, as follows:
1. Favourable: This refers to the condition where a brand receives positive attitudes from consumers
because its benefits meet their needs and desires.
2. Strength: Refers to how information about a brand is stored in the consumer's memory and how
that information endures as part of the brand's image.
3. Uniqueness: Represents the unique and attractive characteristics of a brand that form positive
associations in the consumer's mind.
Brand image is formed through marketing communications such as advertising, promotions,
personal selling, word of mouth, and various other advertising media, where information enters the
consumer’s mind and increases the popularity of a particular brand (Sitorus et al., 2022 ). A positive
brand image not only attracts consumer interest but also shapes their perception of product quality,
which ultimately drives purchasing decisions  (Putri et al., 2021 ). This finding is consistent with
previous research results showing that brand image has a positive effect on purchasing decisions
(Syahputri & Sari, 2024 ). Other findings also indicate that the brand image variable positively

RESEARCH METHOD
This study uses a quantitative approach with a descriptive method. A quantitative approach
means that the data obtained in this study are presented in numerical form, and the analysis is conducted
using statistical methods (Machali, 2021). Meanwhile, the descriptive method describes a phenomenon
using accurate data that is systematically examined (Sahir, 2021). There are two types of variables used
in this study: independent variables, which influence or cause the formation of the dependent variable,
and dependent variables. The independent variables in this study include content marketing (X1), event
marketing ( X2), and brand image (X3). Conversely, the dependent variable is influenced by the
independent variables, namely purchasing decisions (Y) (Machali, 2021).
A population is a complete set of elements, generally consisting of individuals, objects,
transactions, or events that are intended to be studied or used as the research subject  (Meilida, 2022).
The population focused on in this study is the consumers of Deliwafa Store Sidoarjo, with the exact
population size unknown.

The population size for this study is undetermined (infinite population) because the researcher

The sample size is considered appropriate according to Roscoe's theory, which states that an ideal

of questionnaires to consumers of Deliwafa Store Sidoarjo. The questionnaire responses are assessed
using a Likert scale with five levels, ranging from scale 1 (strongly disagree) to scale 5 (strongly agree),
evaluated based on the indicators of each variable  (Sahir, 2021). In addition, this study also utilizes
secondary data obtained from books, news articles, and published journals relevant to the research topic
to strengthen the arguments.
Data analysis in this study was conducted using the Partial Least Squares – Structural Equation
Modelling (PLS-SEM) method with the assistance of SmartPLS 4.0 software. The PLS -SEM method
was chosen because it is suitable for testing theoretical frameworks with complex structural models,
involving numerous constructs and indicators, and can be applied with sm all sample sizes despite the
presence of many constructs and items  (Asari et al., 2023 ). In this study, two stages of testing were
conducted, including measurement model testing (outer model) and structural model testing (inner
model) (Singh et al., 2024).
The measurement model (outer model) test aims to assess the relationship between latent
variables, both endogenous and exogenous, and their respective indicators. Convergent validity is
evaluated based on the loading factor value, where a value greater tha n 0.7 is considered acceptable.
However, for newly developed models, a minimum loading factor of 0.5 may still be tolerated. In
addition, an Average Variance Extracted (AVE) value of at least 0.5 indicates that the construct
adequately explains the varianc e of its indicators. Construct reliability is measured using composite
reliability and Cronbach’s alpha, both of which are recommended to have a minimum value of 0.7,
although a value of 0.6 is still acceptable for exploratory research.  Next, the structural model (inner
model) test is used to examine causal relationships between latent variables according to the research
hypotheses. The R-square value of the endogenous variable indicates the extent to which the exogenous
variables explain data variability, which is categorized as high (0.67), moderate (0.33), and low (0.19).
The path coefficient represents the magnitude of influence between constructs and is tested for
significance using the bootstrapping method to obtain statistically valid and stable estimates.
Significance testing is conducted using a two -tailed test, with critical t -values of 1.65 at a 10%
significance level, 1.96 at 5%, and 2.58 at 1%.

According to the results of data analysis, content marketing influences consumer purchasing
decisions. This indicates that the information conveyed by Deliwafa through its content effectively
assists consumers in making purchasing decisions. The presentation of information in a simple and
easy-to-understand manner enables con sumers to quickly grasp the intended message. In addition,
content that provides insights related to the product helps consumers understand the benefits and
functions of the offered products. The positive value perceived by consumers from this information
shows that Deliwafa's content is relevant to their needs. Furthermore, content that is packaged
engagingly also stimulates consumer curiosity to explore more of the available products.  The findings
of this study are consistent with the theory that content marketing involves the creation and distribution
of valuable and relevant content, whether informative or persuasive, that appeals to consumers. Such
content must align with consumers’ needs, problems, or interests and provide useful information or
solutions. Furthermore, content marketing is considered a valuable asset that helps companies build
trust, educate consumers, increase engagement, and influence purchasing decisions (Erwin et al., 2023).
Through the content marketing shared by Deliwafa  Store, the company can provide opportunities to
interact and build personal relationships with consumers.

CONCLUSION
Based on the results of data analysis and the discussion regarding the influence of content
marketing, event marketing, and brand image on consumer purchase decisions at Deliwafa Store
Sidoarjo, it can be concluded that all three variables have a positive and significant impact on purchase
decisions. Content marketing has been proven effective in influencing consumer decisions. The content
delivered by Deliwafa Store successfully provides clear, relevant, and easy-to-understand information,
helping consumers to understand the benefits and uses of the products. The engaging presentation of
content also stimulates curiosity and encourages consumers to further explore the products offered.
Furthermore, event marketing in the form of the “Deliwafa Festival” musi c concert significantly
influences purchase decisions. This event not only provides entertainment but also creates direct
experiences that build emotional connections between consumers and the brand. Positive experiences
during the event lead to increased consumer enthusiasm, stronger interactions, and ultimately encourage
purchases. In addition, Deliwafa Store’s strong and unique brand image plays an important role in
influencing purchase decisions. Consumers show a positive perception of Deliwafa’s brand image, as
the products offered are considered to meet their needs and expectations. The brand’s ability to be
recognized, remembered, and to build trust becomes a key factor in strengthening consumer loyalty and
confidence in product quality. These findings present several important implications. First, informative
and engaging content marketing significantly shapes consumer purchase decisions. Second, experience-
based event marketing, such as music concerts, enhances emotional engagement and strengthens th e
relationship between the brand and consumers. Third, a strong brand image reinforces consumer trust
and directly influences their purchasing behaviour.
Moreover, this study highlights the importance of integrating the three variables —content
marketing, event marketing, and brand image—into a comprehensive marketing strategy. The synergy
among these elements not only enhances the effectiveness of communica tion with consumers but also
creates a holistic brand experience that can strengthen loyalty and drive purchasing decisions
sustainably. Therefore, business actors, especially at Deliwafa Store, are encouraged to continuously
develop and optimize these thr ee aspects as part of efforts to maintain competitiveness and expand
market share. However, this study has several limitations. The population studied was limited to the
Sidoarjo area, so the results may not be generalizable to other regions. Additionally, the variables used
in this study were limited to three main aspects. For future research, it is recommended to explore other
and more diverse variables, as well as to expand the scope of the study area to obtain more
comprehensive and representative results.
