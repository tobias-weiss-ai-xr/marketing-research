Abstract
The rapid development of digital technology has significantly transformed how institutions communicate with
their audiences, shifting from traditional to digital methods. In this case, digital content marketing plays a strategic
role in establishing commun ication with audiences, creating value, and ultimately influencing their perception.
For this reason, this study aims to determine the effectiveness of DCM, in both its informative and entertaining
dimensions, as used by the Center for Aging Wellness (CAW)  to build brand awareness and brand image. This
study is a quantitative research with 115 respondents from all civitas academia, lecturers, students, and
administrative staffs, who have seen the content of CAW Instagram. The data is then analysed using SEM
(Structural Equation Modeling). The results show that DCM, with its informational and entertainment dimensions,
positively influences brand awareness and brand image. This confirms that DCM is an effective marketing
communication tool for building long -term interaction with the audience, helping them remember the brand's
existence (brand awareness) and significantly strengthening the brand image.
Keywords: social media marketing, digital content marketing, brand awareness, brand image
1. Introduction
The development in digital technology has significantly transformed the way organizations
communicate and build relationships with their audiences. Social media, particularly Instagram,
has become one of the most dominant marketing channels because it enab les the delivery of
visual content that is engaging, informative, interactive, and easy to share. In this context,
Digital Content Marketing (DCM) plays a crucial role. DCM serves as a strategy to create value,
influence audience perception, and build long -term relationships between a brand and its
consumers. Furthermore, within an organization, DCM involves creating, distributing, and
exchanging relevant, engaging, and timely content to engage audiences/customers during the
decision-making process, thereby encouraging them to indirectly help the organization achieve
beneficial outcomes (Galdón-Salvador et al., 2024). In the digital context, DCM takes various
forms, such as blog articles, social media posts, podcasts, videos, and other content formats,
designed to reach and captivate the target audience more effectively (AlFraihat et al., 2025).
This approach clearly differs from traditional marketing strategies, which tend to be more
limited in media variety. Furthermore, changes in consumer behavior, in which consumers now

2. Literature Review
2.1 Digital Content Marketing
Digital Content Marketing (DCM) is a modern marketing strategy focused on the process of
creating, managing, and distributing digital content that is relevant, valuable, and engaging to
audiences. AlFraihat et al. (2025) explain that DCM is the process of producing and distributing
content at the right time, content that is valuable and capable of capturing consumers’ attention,
thereby driving engagement and positive audience responses toward the brand. Here, we see
that DCM serves not only as a promotional tool but also as a mechanism for building long-term
relationships with audiences through intensive interaction. DCM differs from traditional
marketing in that it is focused on delivering meaningful digital experiences. For example,
through videos, infographics, or social media posts that enable two -way communication

Mulyani and Hermina (2023) state that digital marketing, including content marketing, involves

influence brand awareness and enhance brand image among consum ers. Consistent and
valuable content strengthens consumers’ familiarity, knowledge, and positive associations with
a brand. Ultimately, through intensive communication via digital content, companies can instill
brand messages more deeply in consumers.
Furthermore, Barkah et al. (2019) view content marketing as a process of developing valuable,

fosters trust. For example, content marketing shapes a positive brand image among consumers.

consumers and a brand or organization, which in turn, fosters consumer loyalty. This condition

Overall, DCM is a key element in marketing communication strategies that can boost brand
awareness and strengthen brand image through consistent digital engagement. Through
informative, educational, and engaging digital content, organizations can build emo tional and
cognitive connections with their audience and create a more personalized and memorable brand
experience.
2.2 Brand Awareness
Brand awareness refers to the extent to which audiences recognize and recall a brand in various
situations. Bilgin (2018) explains that brand awareness refers to a consumer’s ability to
recognize a brand and recall it when faced with a specific product or service category. Brand
awareness is a fundamental element in a consumer’s decision -making process because a more
familiar brand is more easily considered and chosen than an unfamiliar one. Keller (2020)

explains that brand awareness consists of two main components, brand recognition (the ability
to recognize a brand when it is presented) and brand recall (the ability to recall a brand without

consumer’s memory, which in turn influences the consumer’s tendency to pay attention to or

In digital marketing, brand awareness is heavily influenced by the intensity and quality of brand
communication through digital content. Mulyani and Hermina (2023) state that digital
marketing activities, such as social media engagement, visual branding, a nd the dissemination
of digital information, play a significant role in enhancing brand awareness. This is because
consistent content strengthens consumers’ recognition of a brand and increases the frequency
with which a brand appears in consumers’ memorie s. The role of social media platforms like
Instagram provides organizations with opportunities to expand the reach of their information,
introduce their brand identity, and show institutional engagement in a visual and interactive
manner, thereby helping the audience gain a deeper understanding of a brand’s presence.
Brand awareness is also influenced by the relevance and value of the brand's content. Bilgin
(2018) asserts that content that is informative, trendy, and shareable tends to strengthen the
audience’s familiarity with a brand because repeated exposure create s stronger memory
associations. This aligns with the findings of Schmidt & Eisend (2015) , who explain Aaker’s
theory, which emphasizes that brand awareness can increase through continuous exposure to
information, consistent communication, and the brand’s p resence across  contexts, thereby
increasing the likelihood of a brand occupying the top-of-mind position.
In this study, brand awareness refers to the extent to which the audience recognizes and
remembers the Ubaya  Center for Aging Wellness (CAW) as an institution that focuses on
education, research, and supporting healthy, happy, and prosperous aging programs. The digital
content presented on the Center for Aging Wellness’s Instagram account, such as education on
senior health, workshop activities, community services, scientific publications etc, is expected
to strengthen public recognition of CAW’s identity,  mission, and activities. The more
frequently the audience is exposed to relevant, consistent, and engaging content, the greater the
likelihood that they will remember the brand and associate it with healthy, happy, and
prosperous aging. Thus, brand awaren ess becomes a key variable in assessing the success of
the digital content marketing strategy in introducing the Ubaya Center for Aging Wellness to
the general public.
2.3 Brand Image
Brand image refers to the perceptions, beliefs, and associations that form in consumers’ minds
about a brand. Bilgin (2018) explains that brand image is the mental representation consumers
hold of a brand. This representation encompasses perceptions of a b rand’s quality, credibility,
character, and reputation, which are shaped by consumer experiences and digital marketing
communications. Brand image does not emerge suddenly. It is formed through a process of
learning and consumer interaction with the brand's messages. Brand image itself encompasses
both functional and symbolic attributes that consumers associate with a brand. These

According to Barkah et al (2019), valuable, informative, and engaging  content can enhance

thereby strengthening the brand’s credibility in the public eye. Consequently, digital content
serves as a tool for brand image building, enablin g a brand to showcase its performance, the
value of its services, and its commitment to organizational values. When the audience receives
consistent, high-quality messages, it creates positive associations with the brand, strengthens
its reputation, and builds long-term consumer trust.
Mulyani and Hermina (2023) add that digital marketing activities via social media play a crucial
role in shaping brand image, as visual communication, educational content, and digital
interactions provide the audience with insights into an institution’s professionalism and identity.
Repeated exposure to high -quality content makes it easier for the audience to associate the
brand with certain qualities, such as academic competence, reliable service, or the institution’s
concern for specific social issues. This demonstrates that social media serves not only as a tool
for conveying information but also for brand building that shapes an institution’s reputation.
In this study, brand image refers to the extent to which the audience views the Ubaya Center
for Aging Wellness as a credible, professional institution with a strong reputation in education,
research, and services related to healthy, happy, and prosperous aging. It is hoped that this
perception will be formed through the audience’s interaction with content on the CAW
Instagram account, education on elderly health, documentation of community activities,
scientific publications, and testimonials or success st ories of programs implemented. This
content reflects CAW’s academic capacity, research expertise, and commitment to providing
public education. This is expected to help the audiences build a perception of CAW’s quality
and integrity. The clearer, more consistent, and more professional the content, the stronger the
reputation formed in the audience’s minds. Thus, brand image becomes a crucial component
within the research’s conceptual framework, as it reflects how CAW credibility and reputation
are perceived through digital content marketing activities.
2.4 Relationships Among Variables
The Impact of DCM on Brand Awareness . Digital Content Marketing (DCM) plays a crucial
role in enhancing brand awareness because engaging, consistent, and relevant digital content
can expand a brand’s reach and exposure to its audience, helping it remember the brand’s
presence. Furthermore, Bilgin (2018) explains that marketing activities through social media,
as well as the presentation of informative, entertaining, and interactive content, have a strong
influence on increasing brand awareness because such content can help consumers recognize
and remember the brand more effectively. Mulyani and Hermina (2023) also emphasize that
digital marketing via social media, with its wide reach and real -time nature, facilitates the
process of conveying information to the audience, thereby strengthening bra nd recognition.
Thus, the more effective the DCM strategy employed, the higher the level of brand awareness
that is generated.

3. Research Method
This study is a quantitative study. Data collection was conducted using research instruments.
These instruments covered all variables used in this study, namely (1) Digital Content
Marketing, (2) Brand Image, and (3) Brand Awareness. All of these instruments were developed
(Haviananda and Widodo, 2025) and modified by the researcher to suit this study. The sampling
technique used in this study was non-probability sampling with a purposive sampling method,
which is a sampling technique that requires responde nts to meet specific criteria. Purposive
sampling in this study involved selecting respondents who had previously viewed or been
exposed to content from the Instagram account of the Ubaya  Center for Aging Wellness, and
who had been exposed to such content for six months or less. The sample size for this study
was 115 respondents. Sample analysis indicates that for studies using path analysis, the
minimum appropriate sample size is 100 respondents. Based on these requirements, the sample
size used in this st udy is 115 respondents to ensure both representativeness and analytical
feasibility. This study also employs SEM (Structural Equation Modeling) to conduct a construct
validity test of  the exogenous variables. The construct validity test for these exogenous
variables will utilize factor loading values. According to Dachlan (2014), construct validity
testing is used to ensure that the measurement items (indicators) actually represent the
hypothesized construct (latent variable) they are designed to measure. A s a reference, the
minimum loading value is 0.45. If a loading value of < 0.45 is found, the indicator of the latent
variable must be excluded from the research data.

medium capable of building a psychological connection between an institution and its audience.
