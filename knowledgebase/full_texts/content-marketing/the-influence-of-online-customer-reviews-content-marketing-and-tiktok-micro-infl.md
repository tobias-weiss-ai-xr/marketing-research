According

(Herdioko

in

before

(Septyadi

that

can

83

predict

Therefore,

is

a

behavioral

tendency

to

make

a

purchase

that

arises

after

considering

the

benefits,

quality,

and

information

about

a

product.

Online

(OCR)

(e-WOM)

Therefore,

can

as

that

trust,

and

interest

in

,

The

The

.

The

indicators

for

online

reviews

,

content

marketing

,

TikTok

micro-influencers

,

and

consumer

All

1

(strongly

(strongly

In

The

The

Furthermore,

Hypothesis

(

-test

),

(

F

-test

),

and

(R²)

to

of

online

,

,

in
