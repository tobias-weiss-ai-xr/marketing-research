2.2 Self Disclosure
Self-disclosure is a complex concept that has been studied extensively in fields such as psychology,
communication, and sociology. This review brings together the latest evidence on what it means to build
relationships. The way it w orks online and the psychological impact of sharing personal information is why
Self-disclosure is an important part of building and maintaining personal relationships. Research shows that
sharing personal thoughts and experiences can create intimacy and increase relationship satisfaction.
For example, Lai (2016) shows that sharing personal information on platforms such as Facebook has a
significant impact on the development and maintenance of friendships. Because it helps users reveal parts of
their inner life and experiences. Create a sense of intimacy and connection. Likewise, Sprecher et al. (2012)
provides evidence that people feel more loved and connected when they share information with each other.
This supports the idea that sharing information with  each other will strengthen relationships. This is in
accordance with social penetration theory which states that the depth of self -disclosure increases as the
relationship increases (Yokoyama et al., 2024).
As digital technology advances, the way persona l information is shared has also changed. Metzger
(2007) explains how people often hide personal details in the early stages of a relationship. This shows that a
careful attitude can lead to clear thinking later. In the context of online interactions, Bara k & Gluck -Ofri
(2007) shows that the way people exchange personal information in online forums is consistent with existing
concepts such as social exchange theory. equity theory states that digital spaces can increase intimacy as well
as face -to-face inter actions. Bak et al. (2014) also emphasizes that sharing information on platforms like
Twitter strengthens interpersonal relationships.
However, the results of self -disclosure are not always beneficial. Research shows that it can have
negative effects. Thi s is especially true for vulnerable groups. For example, Blau (2011) found a link

The psychological aspect of self -disclosure must also be considered. The role of self -disclosure in
counseling is also discussed in Muallifah & Hannani (2023). Sharing personal experiences is associated with
better self-acceptance and more positive communication. However, further analysis revealed that individuals

This relationship shows that individual differences, such as personality and certain circumstances, have a
