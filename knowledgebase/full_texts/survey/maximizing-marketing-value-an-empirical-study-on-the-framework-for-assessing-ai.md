Abstract - To address this issue, we conduct an empirical
investigation into the application of AI and ML in marketing
management using a rich framework that aims to optimize
marketing value as much as possible. The framework is
structured in four pillars: data gathe ring and processing,
customer insights & segmentation, personalized marketing
strategies and performance improvement. The utility and
