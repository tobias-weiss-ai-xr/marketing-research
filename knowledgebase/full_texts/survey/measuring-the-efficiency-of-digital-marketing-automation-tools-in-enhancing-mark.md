Abstract

Objective: The main aim of this study is to assess the efficiency of Digital Marketing Automation Usage (DU)
in enhancing marketing performance among businesses in Bangladesh and to investigate the mediating role of
social influence (SI) in the adoption process.
Methodology: This study employed a quantitative methodology utilizing a structured questionnaire to gather
primary data from 331 professionals, managers, and business proprietors across various industry sectors in
Bangladesh. The research utilized the UTAUT as its the oretical framework, integrating constructs including
Performance Expectancy (PE), Effort Expectancy (EE), Facilitating Conditions (FC), Behavioral Intention (BI),
Perceived Risk (PR), and Social Influence (SI). Data were examined utilizing PLS -SEM in SmartPLS version
4.1.1.7 to evaluate both direct and indirect effects.
Results: Findings indicate that EE, PR, FC, and SI substantially influence DU, but PE and BI exhibited minimal
or adverse direct effects. The mediating function of social influence enhances the effect of entrepreneurial
engagement, perceived risk, and BI on digita l utilization, signifying that peer, managerial, and industry factors
are crucial in converting intention into real adoption.
Methodological Contributions: The study extends UTAUT by incorporating perceived risk and empirically
validating the mediating role of social influence in an organizational technology adoption context.
Methodologically, it demonstrates the applicability of PLS -SEM for evaluating compl ex technology adoption
models in emerging markets.
Social Contributions: Industry standards, training programs, and peer networks can foster collective adoption
and strengthen technology-driven marketing practices in emerging markets.
Relevance/originality: This study extends UTAUT by incorporating PR and examining the nuanced mediating
role of SI, offering both theoretical and managerial insights for digital marketing adoption.

2.2 Marketing Performance Metrics

Assessing the return on investment (ROI), optimizing methods, and properly allocating
resources are crucial for firms to measure the efficacy of their digital marketing activities. Lead
generation, conversion rates, customer engagement, and return on inves tment (ROI) are the
primary marketing performance measures used to assess the effectiveness of digital marketing
efforts (Silva et al., 2020) . Lead generation indicators, such as lead volume and lead acquisition
cost, provide valuable insights into the efficacy of marketing initiatives in attracting new
consumers and generating interest in goods or services (Dewnarain et al., 2019). Conversion rates,
which quantify the proportion of potential customers who take desired activities like making a
purchase or signing up, are crucial metrics for evaluating the efficacy of a campaign and
determining client intent (Roy & Sharma, 2021) . Customer engagement indicators, including as
social media interactions, email open rates, and website traffic, are used to measure the degree of
involvement and interest from the intended audience (Bozkurt et al., 2021). ROI measures measure
the financial return derived from marketing activities, enabling firms to evaluate the profitability
and effectiveness of their marketing endeavors (Nakash & Bouhnik, 2022).

2.3 Bangladesh Perspectives

In Bangladesh, the use of digital marketing has been consistently expanding due to reasons
such as the increasing availability of internet, usage of smartphones, and the growing impact of

in comparison to developed nations. Businesses have difficulties linked to awareness,

2021). There is a small amount of research on the efficacy of digital marketing in Bangladesh, but
it is starting to emerge. In a research conducted by Babu et al., (2020), the increasing significance
of social media marketing in Bangladesh was emphasized. Businesses are using platforms such as
Facebook and Instagram to effectively connect with and captivate customers. Nevertheless, the
survey also emphasized the need for i ncreased knowledge and comprehension of optimal digital
marketing strategies among firms in Bangladesh. Ahmed et al., (2021) conducted research that
analyzed the difficulties and advantages of implementing digital marketing in SMEs in
Bangladesh. The results revealed that SMEs acknowledge the potential advantages of digital
marketing. However, they face obstacles such as insufficient finances, technical limitations, and a
lack of proficiency in digital skills, which impede their ability to adopt and use digital marketing
strategies.

2.4 Theoretical Background and Hypothesis Development
2.4.1 Theoretical Background

The implementation and application of DMA technologies can be elucidated through the
Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology (UTAUT), proposed by Venkatesh et al.
(2003). UTAUT integrates constructs from eight significant models of technology adoption, such
as the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), Theory of Planned Behavior (TPB), and Innovation
Diffusion Theory (IDT), offering a comprehensive framework to elucidate the decision-making
processes of individuals and organizations regarding the adoption of new technologies (Twum &
Yalley, 2025). This theory is especially pertinent to the realm of DMA in Bangladesh, since it
encompasses various dimensions-performance, effort, social, and organizational factors-that affect
adoption decisions in both individual and corporate contexts (Ahmad & Rasheed, 2025; Bhatnagr
& Rajesh, 2024) .

adoption. The findings clarify the complex fun ction of social impact, showing that it can both
promote and hinder adoption effects, providing more understanding of mediation mechanisms in
technology adoption models (J. Hair & Alamer, 2022; Myamba et al., 2024; Venkatesh, 2022).
