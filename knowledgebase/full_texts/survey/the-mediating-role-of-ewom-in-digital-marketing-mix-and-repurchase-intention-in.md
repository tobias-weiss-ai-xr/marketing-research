Abstract: The rapid growth of e-commerce has intensified the importance of digital
marketing strategies in shaping consumers’ post -purchase behavior, particularly
repurchase intention. However, the mechanism through which digital marketing
activities are translated into consumers’ will ingness to repurchase remains
fragmented in the existing literature. This study examines the conceptual mediating
role of electronic word -of-mouth (eWOM) in the relationship between the digital
marketing mix and repurchase intention in Indonesian e -commerce. A qualitative
library research approach was employed by synthesizing 37 scholarly publications
published between 2017 and 2025, covering eWOM, digital and social media
marketing, online customer reviews, trust, customer experience, perceived value,
convenience, e-satisfaction, perceived risk, and repurchase intention. The findings
indicate that digital marketing activities can stimulate consumer engagement and
facilitate the generation and circulation of eWOM. In turn, the credibility, usefulness,
relevance, and trustworthiness of eWOM can influence consumers’ information
evaluation and behavioral intentions. The synthesis further indicates that eWOM can
conceptually function as an intermediary mechanism connecting digital marketing
activities with repurc hase intention, particularly when supported by trust,
perceived value, customer experience, convenience, and e -satisfaction. The study
contributes a literature -based conceptual framework that integrates previously
fragmented relationships between digital m arketing, eWOM, and repurchase
intention within the Indonesian e -commerce context. Rather than statistically
testing mediation, the study establishes a theoretically grounded pathway for future
empirical investigation.

INTRODUCTION
The rapid expansion of digital technologies has transformed how firms interact with
consumers, particularly in e -commerce. Digital platforms have shifted marketing communication
from firm -controlled, one -way communication toward more interactive and consumer -driven
exchanges. Consumers can search for information, compare alternatives, evaluate sellers, share
experiences, and communicate recommendations through digital channels. Consequently, digital
marketing effectiveness increasingly depends not only on promotional delivery but also on
consumer engagement, information exchange, and interpersonal communication. One important
manifestation is electronic word -of-mouth (eWOM), through which consumers share opinions,
experiences, evaluations, and recommendations about products, brands, selle rs, and platforms .
Ismagilova et al. (2020) identify eWOM as an important form of digital marketing communication,
while Ahmad and Laroche (2016) emphasize its role in social commerce. Unlike traditional word -
of-mouth, eWOM can reac h large and geographically dispersed audiences, making it particularly
relevant in e -commerce, where consumers often face information asymmetry and uncertainty.
Thus, understanding how digital marketing stimulates eWOM and how eWOM influences
continued purchasing is important for contemporary digital marketing research.
The strategic importance of eWOM lies in its function as an information source that
complements firm-generated communication. Consumers encounter reviews, ratings, comments,
recommendations, social media discussions, influencer content, and other user -generated
information that may shape their perceptions . Dewi et al. (2018), demonstrate the relevance of
online customer reviews to repeat purchase intention, whil e Ismagilova et al. (2020) identify a
relationship between eWOM and purchase intention . Qahri-Saremi & Montazemi (2019) further
show that eWOM adoption depends on how consumers evaluate in formation, while. Rahaman et
al. (2022), emphasize information evaluation and adoption in shaping purchase intention. These
findings suggest that eWOM influences behavior not merely through its existence or volume but
through perceived credibility, usefulness, relevance, and trustworthiness.
Digital and social media marketing also provide opportunities for eWOM generation. Firms
can communicate information, engage consumers, provide promotions, collaborate with
influencers, and create di gital experiences that encourage consumer -generated content. Castillo
and Sánchez -Fernández (2019) demonstrate the relevance of digital influencers to engagement
and brand recommendation, while Zhou et al. (2020) explain how influencer narrative strategies
can stimulate eWOM. Indonesian studies provide similar evidence. Winarno and Indrawati (2022)
identify relationships among social media marketing, eWOM, and purchase intention, while  Putri
et al. (2022) and Iksyanti & Hidayat (2022) demonstrate the relevance of social media marketi ng
and eWOM in purchasing processes, including the TikTok-Shopee context. These findings indicate
that digital marketing may influence consumers not only directly but also through consumer -to-
consumer communication.
The importance of this mechanism becomes  clearer when the outcome shifts from initial
purchase to repurchase intention. Repurchase intention reflects consumers' willingness to conduct
subsequent transactions and is an important indicator of customer retention. Indonesian studies
show that repurc hase intention is associated with electronic service quality, eWOM, satisfaction,
trust, customer experience, and ease of use (Murdifin et al., 2020; Prahiawan et al., 2021; Rachbini
et al., 2021; Tirta Wiyata et al., 2020) . Hamdan et al. (2025) further demonstrate the relevance of
website quality, e -promotion, and e -trust to e -repurchase intention, while  Lufiati & Suparna
(2023) show that digital marketing can influence repurchase intention through trust . Wu et al.
(2023) also indicate that online repurch ase behavior is shaped by consumers' motivations and

Despite extensive research on eWOM, digital marketing, and online consumer behavior, th e
literature remains fragmented. One stream focuses on eWOM concepts and antecedents
(Ismagilova et al., 2017); Bhaiswar et al., 2021), another examines eWOM adoption and behavioral
consequences (Qahri-Saremi & Montazemi, 2019; Ismagilova et al., 2020; Rahaman et al., 2022) ,
while another examines digital and social media marketing as antecedents of eWOM (Castillo &
Sánchez-Fernández, 2019; Winarno & Indrawati, 2022; Zhou et al., 2020) . A further stream
examines repurchase intention through eWOM, trust, satisfaction, customer experience,
convenience, and perceived value  (Rachbini et al., 2021;  Prahiawan & Purba, 2021; Zeqiri et al.,
2023). Although these relationships are individually established, they have not been sufficiently
integrated into a single literature -based explanation of how digital marketing may influence
repurchase intention through eWOM in Indonesian e-commerce.
The need for synthesis is reinforced by differences in platforms, product categories, and
consumer contexts. Research has examined Lazada, Shopee, Tokopedia, Traveloka, online grocery
retailing, mobile applications, and social networking sites. Amalia et al. (2023),  Lestari &
Nurtjahjadi (2024), and  Setiani et al. (2024) provide evidence from Tokopedia, Traveloka, and
Shopee, respectively. Memarista & Wijaya (2024) indicate that the relationship between eWOM
and repurchase-related behavior may involve additional factors. Handoyo (2024), highlights trust,
risk, security, and eWOM, while Ngo et al. (2024) emphasize information credibility and Anastasiei
et al. (2024) examine antecedents of eWOM.  Ngo et al. (2025) connect service quality with
repurchase intention and eWOM, while Pramudana et al. (2025) examine digital marketing, eWOM,
trust in store, and repurchase intention among marketplace consumers. These findings support
the need to synthesize the relationships into a coherent conceptual mechanism.
Accordingly, this study aims to qualitatively synthesize the literature concerning the
conceptual mediating role of eWOM in the relationship between digital marketing mix and
repurchase intention in Indonesian e -commerce. The study integrates three research domains:
digital marketing as a source of engagement and communication, eWOM as a consumer -to-
consumer information mechanism, and repurchase intention as a behavioral outcome. The
proposed mechanism suggests th at digital marketing stimulates engagement and eWOM, while
eWOM provides information that influences consumer evaluation and repurchase intention. Trust,
perceived value, customer experience, convenience, e-satisfaction, perceived risk, and information
credibility may strengthen or condition this mechanism. Because this study uses qualitative library

METHOD
Research Design
This study employed a qualitative library research approach to synthesize evidence
concerning digital marketing, eWOM, and repurchase intention in e-commerce. The approach was
selected because the study aimed to identify, compare, and integrate findings rather than
statistically estimate relationships. Par ticular attention was given to eWOM generation and
adoption, digital and social media marketing, online reviews, trust, customer experience, perceived
value, convenience, and repurchase intention. eWOM was conceptualized as consumer -to-
consumer communicati on in digital environments  (Ahmad & Laroche, 2016; Ismagilova et al.,
2017), while its behavioral consequences were examined through studies of purchase and
repurchase intention (Ismagilova et al., 2020; Dewi et al., 2018; Rachbini et al., 2021).

Literature Selection
The study analyzed 37 scholarly publications published between 2017 and 2025.
Publications were purposively selected based on their relevance to the research objectives and
conceptual framework. The literature included intern ational and Indonesian studies involving
Shopee, Tokopedia, Lazada, Traveloka, mobile applications, and other online marketplaces.
Relevant studies addressed digital marketing, social media marketing, eWOM, online reviews,
information credibility or adopti on, trust, customer experience, perceived value, convenience, e -
satisfaction, purchase intention, and repurchase intention. Conceptual studies, empirical research,
systematic reviews, bibliometric studies, and meta -analyses were included to provide both
theoretical and empirical perspectives.

Data Collection and Extraction
Data were collected through structured reading and extraction of information concerning
research context, focal constructs, relationships examined, principal findings, and implications for
the conceptual pathway. Particular attention was given to whether eWOM functioned as an
antecedent, outcome, mediator, moderator, or information mechanism. Studies on digital
marketing and eWOM generation included Castillo and Sánchez -Fernández (2019), Zhou et al.
(2020), and Winarno and Indrawati (2022), while studies on eWOM adoption included Qahri -
Saremi and Montazemi (2019), Ismagilova et al. (2020), Rahaman et al. (2022), and Ngo et al.
(2024). Studies concerning repurchase intention were examined t hrough eWOM, trust,
satisfaction, customer experience, perceived value, convenience, and perceived risk (Rachbini et
al., 2021; Prahiawan & Purba, 2021; Zeqiri et al., 2023).

The extracted information was analyzed through qualitative themati c synthesis. First,
publications were categorized into digital marketing and eWOM generation, eWOM information
processing, eWOM and purchase intention, and eWOM and repurchase intention. Second, recurring
constructs and relationships were compared to ident ify convergence and divergence. Third, the

Analytical Framework
The analytical framework is:
Digital Marketing Mix → eWOM → Repurchase Intention
Digital marketing represents activities that stimulate engagement and consumer -generated
communication. eWOM functions as an information mechanism through which consumers
evaluate products, services, sellers, and pla tforms. Trust, perceived value, customer experience,
convenience, e -satisfaction, perceived risk, information credibility, and security are treated as
contextual factors rather than primary variables. The framework integrates evidence concerning
digital marketing and eWOM generation (Castillo & Sánchez -Fernández, 2019; Zhou et al., 2020),
eWOM adoption (Qahri -Saremi & Montazemi, 2019; Rahaman et al., 2022), and eWOM -related
repurchase intention (Rachbini et al., 2021; Prahiawan & Purba, 2021; Zeqiri et al., 2023).

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
Overview of the Literature Synthesis
The synthesis of 37 publications identified four interconnected research streams: eWOM
conceptualization, eWOM information adoption, digital marketing and eWOM generation, and
eWOM-related purchase or repurchase intention. The literature shows that eWOM has developed
into an important information mechanism in digital commerce. Ismagilova et al. (2017), Ahmad
and Laroche (2016), and Bhaiswar et al. (2021) establish its conceptual  and research relevance,
while Qahri-Saremi and Montazemi (2019), Ismagilova et al. (2020), and Rahaman et al. (2022)
demonstrate its importance in information adoption and purchase-related behavior.
The Indonesian literature further demonstrates the relev ance of eWOM to customer
retention. Rachbini et al. (2021), Prahiawan and Purba (2021), Lita and Auliya (2019), and Tirta
Wiyata et al. (2020) identify relationships involving eWOM, trust, customer experience, and
repurchase intention. More recent studies extend these relationships across Tokopedia, Traveloka,
Shopee, and broader marketplace environments (Amalia et al., 2023; Lestari & Nurtjahjadi, 2024;
Setiani et al., 2024; Pramudana et al., 2025).

The literature indicates that digital marketing activities can stimulate consumer engagement
and eWOM generation. Rather than merely transmitting promotional messages, digital marketing
allows consumers to respond, comment, share, evaluate, and redistribute information. Castillo and

eWOM and Repurchase Intention
The third finding is the recurring relationship between eWOM and repurchase i ntention.
Dewi et al. (2018) demonstrate the relevance of online reviews to repeat purchase intention, while
Rachbini et al. (2021) and Prahiawan and Purba (2021) identify eWOM as relevant to repurchase

Evidence from spe cific platforms further supports this relationship. Amalia et al. (2023)
examine eWOM and perceived value on Tokopedia, Setiani et al. (2024) integrate eWOM,

repurchase behavior.

eWOM and repurchase-related behavior may involve additional factors, including trust, perceived
value, customer experience, convenience, satisfaction, and perceived risk.

DISCUSSION
Integrating Digital Marketing, eWOM, and Repurchase Intention
The central contribution of this study is the integration of digital marketing, eWOM, and
repurchase inten tion. Digital marketing can stimulate engagement, eWOM can transform
engagement into consumer -generated information, and repurchase intention represents a
subsequent behavioral outcome. Zhou et al. (2020), Castillo and Sánchez -Fernández (2019),
Winarno and Indrawati (2022), Putri et al. (2022), and Iksyanti and Hidayat (2022) support the
relationship between digital marketing activities and eWOM.
Hamdan et al. (2023) and Lufiati and Suparna (2023) further support the digital marketing –
repurchase pathway by demonstrating the relevance of e -promotion and digital marketing to
repurchase intention, with trust emerging as an important mechanism. Therefore, digital
marketing creates not only promotional exposure but also a communication environment in which
consumers interact, evaluate, share, and generate information.

Why eWOM Can Function as a Mediating Mechanism
eWOM can function as a conceptual mediator because it transforms firm -generated
communication into socially transmitted information. Qahri -Saremi and Montazemi (2019) show
that consumers evaluate factors affecting eWOM adoption, while Rahaman et al. (2022) emphasize
information adoption and Ngo et al. (2024) highlight credibility.
The process can be summarized in three stages. First, digital marketing generates exposure
and engagement. Second, engagement facilitates the production, circulation, or encounter of
eWOM. Third, consumers evaluate eWOM alongside their experience, trust, p erceived value, and
risk before developing behavioral intentions. This sequence provides a plausible explanation of
how marketing activities can influence behavior through social information.

From Purchase Intention to Repurchase Intention
An important distinction is between purchase and repurchase intention. Much foundational
eWOM research focuses on initial purchase intention (Ismagilova et al., 2020; Rahaman et al.,
2022), whereas repurchase involves previous experience with a product, seller, or platfo rm. This
makes customer experience and satisfaction particularly important. Tirta Wiyata et al. (2020),
Mehta et al. (2022), and Setiani et al. (2024) demonstrate the relevance of experience -related
factors in repurchase behavior.
Consequently, eWOM can pr ovide new information while simultaneously confirming or
challenging consumers' previous experiences, making it particularly relevant to customer
retention.

The Role of Trust in the Mechanism
Trust is a key condition in the proposed pathway because online transactions involve
uncertainty regarding products, sellers, payment, delivery, privacy, and security. Consumers
therefore rely on reviews, ratings, recommendations, platform reputation, and previous
experiences.
Lita and Auliya (2019), Amarullah e t al. (2022), Prahiawan and Purba (2021), and nisa et al.
(2022) demonstrate the relevance of trust to eWOM and repurchase -related behavior. Hamdan et
al. (2023) further link website quality, e -promotion, e -trust, and e -repurchase intention, while
Lufiati and Suparna (2023) show that trust mediates the relationship between digital marketing
and repurchase intention. Trust therefore helps explain why consumers may accept some eWOM
information and translate it into continued purchasing behavior.

Customer Experience and Perceived Value
eWOM should also be interpreted alongside customer experience and perceived value. Digital
marketing may attract attention, but continued purchasing depends partly on whether actual
experience meets expectations. Mehta et al. (2022) and Tirta Wiyata et al. (2020) demonstrate the
relevance of customer experience, while Amalia et al. (2023) and Zeqiri et al. (2023) highlight
perceived value.
Thus, eWOM can reinforce, validate, or challenge consumers' perceptions of value and
experience. Digital marketing may initiate the process, eWOM may transmit social information, and
experience and perceived value may influence whether that information translates into continued
purchasing.

Implications for Indonesian E-Commerce
The Indonesian context demonstrates that eWOM, social media marketing, digital marketing,
trust, and repurchase intention are interconnected across Shopee, Tokopedia, Lazada, Traveloka,
mobile applications, and broader marketplaces. Rachbini et al. (2021), Amalia et al. (2023), Lestari
and Nurtjahjadi (2024), Setiani et al. (2024), and Pramudana et al. (2025) provide evidence across
different platforms.
The practical implication is that digital marketing should not focus only on promotional reach.
Firms should also create conditions for credible consumer -generated communication through
product quality, service experience, responsiveness, transaction security, and transparency. Digital
marketing therefore needs to be integrated with customer relationship and experience
management.

Theoretical Contribution and Research Gap
The theoretical contribution lies in positioning eWOM as an intermediate communication and
information mechanism rather than solely as an antecedent or outcome. Existing studies have
established relationships between digital marketing and eWOM, eWOM and purchase intention,
and eWOM and repurchase intention. This study connects these relationships into one conceptual
pathway:
Digital Marketing Mix → Consumer Engagement → eWOM → Information Evaluation →
Trust/Perceived Value/Experience → Repurchase Intention
The framework does not represent a statistically tested sequential mediation model. Instead,
it reflects recurring mechanisms identified in the literature. The research gap therefore lies in the
absence of an integrated literature-based explanation of how digital marketing may contribute to
repurchase intention through eWOM in Indonesian e-commerce.

Limitations and Future Research
This study has several limitations. First, it relies on secondary literature and therefore cannot
establish statistical causality or mediation. Second, the reviewed studies differ in platforms,
products, samples, methodologies, and construct definitions. Third, digital marketing mix is
operationalized differently across studies, including social media marketing, digital promotion,
influencer marketing, and website -related activities. Finally, the literature combines Indonesian
and international contexts, so findings cannot always be generalized directly to Indonesian
consumers.
Future research should empirically test the proposed framework using primary data.
Quantitative studies could examine whether eWOM mediates the relationship between specific
digital marketing dimensions and repurchase intention, while qualitative research co uld explore
how consumers interpret, trust, and use eWOM. Comparative studies across platforms, products,
and consumer segments could further clarify the conditions under which eWOM contributes to
customer retention.

CONCLUSION
This study examined the conceptual mediating role of eWOM in the relationship between
digital marketing mix and repurchase intention in Indonesian e -commerce through qualitative
library research. The synthesis of 37 publications indicates that digital marketing incre asingly
operates beyond direct firm -to-consumer communication. Social media marketing, digital
promotion, influencer communication, and other digital activities can stimulate engagement and
consumer-generated information, positioning eWOM as an important m echanism linking firm -
initiated marketing with consumer-to-consumer communication.
The findings indicate that eWOM functions not merely as promotional information but as an
information and evaluation mechanism through which consumers assess products, selle rs,
services, and platforms. Its behavioral consequences depend on perceived credibility, usefulness,
relevance, and trustworthiness. This is particularly important for repurchase because consumers
combine new eWOM information with their previous experiences and evaluations.

repurchase intention into a coherent conceptual pathway. Digital marketing can stimulate

important contextual factors. Evidence from Hamdan et al. (2023), Lufiati and Suparna (2023), and
Wu et al. (2023) further supports the importance of digital marketing, trust, and consumer
evaluation in understanding repurchase behavior.
The study also contributes to the Indonesian e -commerce context by integrating findings
from Shopee, Tokopedia, Lazada, Traveloka, mobile applications, and other marketplace
environments. However, eWOM is conceptualized here as a mediating mechanism derived from
recurring relationships in the literature rather than as a statistically established mediation effect.
Future research should empirically test the proposed framework using primary data from
Indonesian e-commerce consumers. Quantitative research can examine the statistical mediation of
eWOM between digital marketing and repurchase intention, while qualitative research can explore
how consumers evaluate and use eWOM in repurchase decisions. Comparative research across
platforms, product categories, and consumer segments may further clarify the  conditions under
which eWOM becomes an effective mechanism for translating digital marketing activities into
customer retention.
