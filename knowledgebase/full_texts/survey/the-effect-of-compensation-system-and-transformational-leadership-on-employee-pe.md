Abstract

 This study aims to analyze the effect of the compensation system and
transformational leadership on employee performance, with employee
engagement as a mediating variable in the Marketing Division of PT Power
Asetindo Selaras. This research is motivated by the decline in total auction
results despite an increase in auction activities and units sold, indicating
suboptimal employee effectiveness and productivity. A quantitative method
with an explanatory approach was employed. The population co nsisted of
all 51 employees in the Marketing Division, using a census or saturated
sampling method. Data were collected through an online Likert -scale
questionnaire. Data analysis was conducted using Structural Equation
Modeling (SEM) with the Partial Leas t Squares (PLS) approach through
SmartPLS version 4.1.1.6. The analysis included the measurement model
(outer model), structural model (inner model), and hypothesis testing. The
results show that compensation system has no significant effect on employee
performance, while transformational leadership has a positive and
significant effect. Both compensation system and transformational leadership
positively and significantly affect employee engagement. Employee
engagement also positively and significantly affe cts employee performance.
Furthermore, employee engagement fully mediates compensation system
and partially mediates transformational leadership effects on performance.
Keywords  Compensation System, Transformational Leadership, Employee
Performance, Employee Engagement

INTRODUCTION
Human resources (HR) represent one of the most important assets of an organization
because they play a direct role in carrying out operational activities and achieving
organizational objectives. Organizational success is determined not only by the availabi lity
of resources but also by the quality of employee performance in carrying out assigned
responsibilities and achieving established targets. Employee performance reflects individual
productivity, effectiveness, and contribution to organizational objectives. Therefore, effective
human resource management is essential for organizations to respond to increasingly

failure to achieve targets, and low employee involvement ma y hinder an organization’s
ability to maintain and improve its performance.
These performance issues are particularly relevant in the asset auction service
industry, where companies are required to optimize both operational activities and sales
outcomes. P T Power Asetindo Selaras is a National Private Auction House established in
2009 that provides professional auction services for various types of assets, including
property, automotive units, furniture, office equipment, and machinery. The company
operates eight pools (warehouses) located in Jakarta, Serang, Surabaya, Lampung, Baturaja,
Bengkulu, Palembang, and Makassar. To support its business activities, the company utilizes
an application -based auction system through PASBid -Automotive Auction and the
Lelang.go.id portal for mortgage execution auctions. Within this business context, the
Marketing Division plays a strategic role in increasing the number of auction activities, units
sold, and total auction proceeds.
However, internal data from PT Power Aseti ndo Selaras for the 2024 –2025 period
reveal a phenomenon that requires further attention. In 2024, the company conducted 998
auction activities, with 123 units sold and total auction proceeds amounting to IDR 316.23
billion. In 2025, the number of auction activities increased to 1,300, representing an increase
of approximately 30%, while the number of units sold increased to 154 units, or
approximately 25%. Despite these increases, total auction proceeds decreased to IDR 283.79
billion, representing a decline of approximately 10.3% compared with the previous year. This
phenomenon indicates that an increase in operational activities and the number of units sold
has not necessarily been accompanied by greater effectiveness in generating sales value.
Consequently, the success of the Marketing Division cannot be assessed solely based on the
number of auction activities conducted or units sold, but also on employees’ ability to
optimize the sales value generated from each auction activity. The decline in total auc tion
proceeds therefore indicates potential room for improvement in employee performance,
particularly in maximizing the commercial value of auction activities.
One factor that may influence employee performance is the compensation system.
Compensation represents the rewards provided by an organization to employees in return
for their contributions, including both financial and non -financial forms. An appropriate
compensation system is expected to encourage employees to improve their performance.
Bonifasia (2024) found that compensation can improve employee performance. However,
Febriani (2023) reported that compensation did not have a significant effect on employee
performance. Similarly, Sulastri (2020) found that compensation did not have a direct effect
on performance without the presence of another mediating variable. These inconsistent
findings indicate that the relationship between compensation and employee performance
remains inconclusive and requires further investigation.
Transformational leadership  is another factor that may influence employee
performance. Transformational leadership emphasizes a leader’s ability to inspire, motivate,

METHODS
This study employed a quantitative research method with an explanatory approach
to examine the causal relationships among the variables. The research used a causal survey
design, with data collected through a structured questionnaire distributed to employe es of
the Marketing Division of PT Power Asetindo Selaras. The study population consisted of 51
employees of the Marketing Division. Because the population was relatively small and
accessible in its entirety, a census or saturated sampling technique was em ployed, whereby
all 51 employees were included as respondents. The research focused on the Marketing
Division because its employees are directly involved in target -oriented marketing activities
and face dynamic workloads and performance demands. The study was conducted at the
company’s head office located at Rukan Botanic Junction, Mega Kebon Jeruk, West Jakarta.
Data were collected using a structured questionnaire administered online through
Google Forms and distributed via WhatsApp to the respondents. The  questionnaire
measured four research constructs: compensation system, transformational leadership,
employee engagement, and employee performance. All items were measured using a five -
point Likert scale ranging from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agr ee). The compensation
system was measured through salary, incentives/bonuses, allowances, and employee welfare
guarantees; transformational leadership through idealized influence, inspirational
motivation, intellectual stimulation, and individualized consi deration; employee
engagement through vigor, dedication, and absorption; and employee performance through
work quality, work quantity, responsibility, and task completion. The measurement
instrument was evaluated for validity and reliability. Convergent va lidity was assessed
using factor loadings and Average Variance Extracted (AVE), while discriminant validity
was evaluated using the Fornell –Larcker criterion. Construct reliability was assessed using
Cronbach’s alpha and composite reliability, with values above 0.70 generally considered
acceptable.
The collected data were analyzed using Structural Equation Modeling with the
Partial Least Squares approach (PLS -SEM) using SmartPLS version 4.1.1.6. PLS -SEM was
selected because it is suitable for explanatory an d predictive models, can accommodate
relatively small samples, and does not require normally distributed data. The analysis
consisted of model specification, evaluation of the measurement model (outer model),
evaluation of the structural model (inner model ), and hypothesis testing. The outer model
was assessed through indicator loadings, convergent validity, discriminant validity, AVE,
Cronbach’s alpha, and composite reliability. The inner model was evaluated using the
coefficient of determination (R²), pre dictive relevance (Q²), and path coefficients. The
hypotheses were tested using a 5% significance level, with a hypothesis considered

The Effect of the Compensation System on Employee Performance
The results of this analysis show that the compensation system does not have a
significant influence on employee performance. This means that the amount of compensation
received by employees does not necessarily directly impr ove employee performance in the
Marketing Division of PT Power Acetindo Selaras. However, this result does not mean that
the compensation system is not important for employees. Based on the results of the
descriptive analysis, the salary indicator obtained  the highest average score, which is 4.24
which is included in the very high category. This shows that most employees feel that the
salary they receive is in accordance with the workload and paid on time. However,
satisfaction with the compensation system has not been able to have a direct influence on
performance improvement.  This shows that providing good compensation alone is not
enough to improve employee performance. Employees may need other factors that can
encourage them to work more optimally, such as employee engagement, motivation from
within, and a supportive work environment. Therefore, companies not only need to provide
fair compensation, but also create working conditions that can increase employee morale and
engagement.
The results of this study are in line with Febriani (2023) who stated that compensation
does not have a significant effect on employee performance. Sulastri (2020) also found that
compensation does not have a direct influence on performance without other variables that
act as me diators. However, the results of this study are different from Bonifasia (2024) who
said that compensation has a positive and significant effect on employee performance. The
difference in results may be due to differences in respondent characteristics, ind ustry types,
and organizational conditions that are the object of the research. Based on these results, it can
be concluded that the compensation system in the Marketing Division of PT Power Acetindo
Selaras is running well and needs to be maintained. Howe ver, companies also need to pay
attention to other factors that are more influential in improving employee performance. In
this study, employee engagement is proven to be a more dominant factor in improving
performance. Therefore, in addition to maintainin g the existing compensation system,
companies are also advised to increase employee engagement through good communication,
providing opportunities to develop, and creating a comfortable and supportive work
environment.
