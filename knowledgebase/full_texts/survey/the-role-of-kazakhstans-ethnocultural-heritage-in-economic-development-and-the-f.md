THE ROLE OF KAZAKHSTAN’S ETHNOCULTURAL HERITAGE IN ECONOMIC
DEVELOPMENT AND THE FORMATION OF TOURISM BRAND CAPITAL
Abstract
This	article	examines	Kazakhstan’s	ethnocultural	heritage	in	relation	to	economic	development	and	the	formation
of	tourism	brand	capital.	The	preservation	of	cultural	and	historical	values	is	directly	linked	to	their	appropriate	use	in
tourism.	National	traditions,	historical	sites,	crafts,	and	local	cultural	features	reflect	the	country’s	distinctive	identity
and	can	increase	tourist	interest.	They	can	also	support	the	development	of	regional	service	industries	and	create
opportunities	for	new	tourism	products.	The	study	aims	to	assess	the	relationship	between	ethnocultural	resources,
the	tourism	economy,	investment	attractiveness,	and	tourism	brand	capital	using	empirical	data.	The	study	involved
40	respondents.	The	survey	data	were	analysed	using	descriptive	statistics,	the	Shapiro–Wilk	test,	factor	analysis,
Cronbach’s	Alpha	coefficient,	and	multiple	regression	analysis.	The	responses	showed	that	participants	placed	a	high
value	on	ethnocultural	heritage,	authenticity,	and	the	attractiveness	of	the	tourism	brand.	The	factor	analysis	identified
three	dimensions	relating	to	cultural	value,	marketing	support,	and	investment	potential.	The	regression	analysis
revealed	positive	relationships	between	the	attractiveness	of	tourism	brand	capital	and	ethnocultural	value	(β=0.42;
p=0.001),	authenticity	(β=0.31;	p=0.008),	and	marketing	support	(β=0.27;	p=0.015).	The	model	explained	61%	of
the	variation	in	the	indicator	under	consideration	(R²=0.61).	The	findings	indicate	that	ethnocultural	heritage	has
economic	potential	in	addition	to	its	cultural	significance.	Its	appropriate	use	in	tourism	development	may	contribute
to	regional	economic	diversification,	support	entrepreneurial	and	investment	activity,	and	strengthen	the	competitive
advantages	of	tourism	destinations.	Measures	intended	to	preserve	cultural	heritage	should	therefore	be	coordinated
with	tourism	branding	strategies	and	regional	economic	development	plans.
Keywords:	 ethnocultural	 heritage,	 tourism	 brand	 capital,	 authenticity,	 tourism	 economics,	 investment
attractiveness,	regional	development,	cultural	tourism,	territorial	branding.
