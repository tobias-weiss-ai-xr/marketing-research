Abstract—The development of digital technology has driven shifts in tourist destination marketing strategies through the
use of online advertising, while the provision of adequate tourist faci lities remains a critical factor in creating a satisfying
experience for visitors. Sunyaragi Cave Tourist Attraction in Cirebon, as one of the leading cultural tourism destinations in
West Java Province, needs to optimize both aspects to enhance tourist sa tisfaction. This study aims to analyze the influence
of online advertising and tourist facilities on tourist satisfaction, both partially and simultaneously. A quantitative appro ach
with a survey method was employed. Data were collected by distributing que stionnaires to 100 tourists who had visited
Sunyaragi Cave Tourist Attraction Cirebon. A purposive sampling technique was used, while data analysis was conducted
using multiple linear regression via SPSS version 25. The results showed that online advertisi ng had a positive and
significant effect on tourist satisfaction with a tcount of 5.164 > ttable of 1.984 and a coefficient of determination of 20. 6%.
Tourist facilities also had a positive and significant effect on tourist satisfaction with a tcount of 6. 426 > ttable of 1.984 and a
coefficient of determination of 28.9%. Simultaneously, online advertising and tourist facilities significantly influenced tou rist
satisfaction with an Fcount of 33.670 > Ftable of 3.09 and a significance value of 0.000 < 0.05. B oth variables accounted for
39.8% of the variance in tourist satisfaction, while the remaining 60.2% was influenced by other factors outside the research
model. These findings indicate that enhancing digital promotional effectiveness supported by high -quality tourist facilities
is a crucial strategy for increasing tourist satisfaction and strengthening the competitiveness of cultural tourism destinati ons
in Cirebon.
Keywords— online advertising; tourist facilities; tourist satisfaction; Sunyaragi Cave; tourism
I. INTRODUCTION
West Java Province is one of Indonesia's premier tourism destinations, featuring diverse natural, cultural, historical,
religious, and creative economy attractions. Among these, Sunyaragi Cave Tourist Attraction in Cirebon stands out as  a
cultural tourism destination with rich historical significance, originating from the Cirebon Sultanate and serving as a
cultural icon in West Java. Its unique architectural design —dominated by coral rock formations —strong historical value,
and regular c ultural events make Sunyaragi Cave an attractive destination for both domestic and international tourists.
However, intensifying competition among tourism destinations requires managers to continuously enhance digital
promotional strategies and service qua lity to maintain a competitive edge. The rapid growth of the internet has shifted
tourist information search behavior from traditional channels to digital platforms. Prior to traveling, visitors tend to gath er
information via search engines, official websi tes, social media, promotional videos, and user reviews on digital channels.
Consequently, online advertising has become an essential marketing instrument for building destination image, establishing
prospective visitor trust, and influencing travel decisi ons. Effective online advertising is characterized by accurate
information, engaging content, ease of access, and interactive communication, which together form positive expectations
toward the destination.
On the other hand, successful digital promotion m ust be supported by adequate physical tourist facilities. Tourist
facilities represent service components directly experienced by visitors upon arrival. The availability of parking areas, cle an
restrooms, worship facilities, information centers, accessibil ity features, culinary options, rest areas, security, and
environmental cleanliness directly impacts visitor comfort and overall experience quality. When on -site facilities align with
information presented in digital media, tourists are highly likely to fe el satisfied. Conversely, discrepancies between
promotional content and actual field conditions can significantly diminish tourist satisfaction. Tourist satisfaction serves as a
primary indicator of successful destination management. Satisfaction occurs wh en the actual experience meets or exceeds
expectations established prior to the trip. Satisfied tourists are more inclined to revisit (revisit intention), spread posit ive
word-of-mouth recommendations, and share positive experiences on social media, ultima te strengthening destination image
and competitiveness.
Previous studies indicate that both online advertising and tourist facilities share positive relationships with tourist
satisfaction. Research by Chang and Wang (2019) demonstrated that digital inform ation and online advertising shape tourist
expectations and influence post -visit satisfaction levels. Other studies have highlighted facility quality and the physical
environment as critical determinants of visitor experience and satisfaction. However, mos t prior research examined these
variables separately or focused primarily on natural attractions, hotels, or restaurants. Studies integrating online advertis ing

Conceptual Framework
This research utilizes two independent variables, Online Advertising (X ₁) and Tourist Facilities (X ₂), and one dependent
variable, Tourist Satisfaction (Y). Online advertising is conceptualized as a marketing communication strategy that builds
expectations and influences travel decisions through engaging, accessible, and credible information. Tourist facilities
represent physical service quality that directly affects visitor comfort and on -site experience. Both variables are
hypothesized to contribute positively to tourist satisfaction, both individually and combined.
Research Hypotheses
Based on theoretical foundations and prior empirical literature, the following research hypotheses were formulated:
H1: Online Advertising has a positive and significant effect on Tourist Satisfaction at Sunyaragi Cave Tourist Attraction
Cirebon.
H2: Tourist Facilities have a positiv e and significant effect on Tourist Satisfaction at Sunyaragi Cave Tourist Attraction
Cirebon.
H3: Online Advertising and Tourist Facilities simultaneously have a positive and significant effect on Tourist Satisfaction a t
Sunyaragi Cave Tourist Attraction Cirebon.
II. METHOD
This study adopted a quantitative approach using a survey method. A quantitative approach was selected to test causal
relationships among Online Advertising (X ₁), Tourist Facilities (X ₂), and Tourist Satisfaction (Y) via stat istical analysis.
Survey questionnaires were distributed directly to visitors who had completed their visit to Sunyaragi Cave Tourist
Attraction in Cirebon City, West Java Province.
The study population comprised all tourists visiting Sunyaragi Cave during the research period. Purposive sampling
was used to select 100 respondents based on specific criteria: (1) aged 17 years or older; (2) had visited Sunyaragi Cave
Tourist Attraction; (3) had previously accessed digital information regarding Sunyaragi  Cave (via websites, Instagram,
Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Google, or other digital outlets); and (4) were willing to complete the questionnaire.
Data collection included primary data (questionnaires administered on -site and observational field insights) a nd
secondary data (academic journals, industry reports, and official documentation). Variable operationalization was defined
as follows:
• Online Advertising (X ₁): Promotional activities via digital media aiming to inform and attract visitors. Indicators i nclude
information discoverability, visual/content attractiveness, completeness and accuracy, promotional frequency,
information credibility, and ease of digital access.
• Tourist Facilities (X ₂): Supporting physical infrastructure and amenities provided b y destination managers. Indicators
include facility cleanliness, parking space availability, clean restrooms, worship areas, information center, rest areas,
security, and accessibility.
• Tourist Satisfaction (Y): Overall psychological state comparing pre -visit expectations with actual experiences. Indicators
include overall satisfaction, expectation alignment, revisit intention, and willingness to recommend to others.
All items were measured using a 5 -point Likert Scale ranging from 1 (Strongly Disagree) t o 5 (Strongly Agree). Data
were analyzed using IBM SPSS Statistics Version 25, comprising descriptive statistics, Pearson product -moment validity
tests, Cronbach's Alpha reliability tests (≥ 0.70 threshold), classical assumption tests (normality, multicoll inearity,
heteroscedasticity), multiple linear regression, t-tests, F-tests, and coefficient of determination analysis (R²).
Multiple Linear Regression Model:  Y = α + β₁X₁ + β₂X₂ + ε
Y = Tourist Satisfaction; α = Constant; β₁, β₂ = Regression Coefficients; X₁ = Online Advertising; X₂ = Tourist Facilities; ε = Error term.

IV. CONCLUSIONS
Based on the results of the simultaneous hypothesis test, advertising and tourism facilities were found to have a
significant effect on tourist satisfaction, as indicated by a significance value (Sig.) of 0.000, which is lower than 0.05 (0.000 <
0.05), and an F -value greater than the F -table value (33.670 > 3.09). These findings indicate that advertising and tourism
facilities simultaneously have a significant influence on tourist satisfaction. This result suggests that the more intensive the
advertising efforts carried out by Goa Sunyaragi Tourism, the higher the level of tourist satisfaction. Likewise, the more
complete and adequate the supporting tourism facilities provided by Goa Sunyaragi, the greater the satisfaction experienced
by visitors. High levels of tourist satisfaction are expected to encourage visitor loyalty toward the destination.Furthermore ,
the Adjusted R Square (R²) value of 0.39 8 indicates that the advertising and tourism facilities variables collectively explain
39.8% of the variation in tourist satisfaction at Goa Sunyaragi Tourism, Cirebon. The remaining 60.2% is explained by other
factors that were not included in the research model.
Practical Implications
Advertising for Sunyaragi Cave tourism in Cirebon is already fairly good, but it would be better to expand the reach of
the information through various existing media rather than focusing only on the Cirebon area and its sur roundings. Judging
from the results of the advertising statements, the highest average score is found in print media advertising, such as poster s
and brochures. However, statements with relatively low scores —which fall below the average —should be improved,  such
as electronic media that has not been utilized optimally. Therefore, it is recommended that the management of Sunyaragi
Cave tourism in Cirebon expand and make more optimal use of electronic media, so that tourists feel more satisfied with
receiving accurate information and having positive expectations toward Sunyaragi Cave tourism.
Tourism facilities at Sunyaragi Cave in Cirebon are also quite good, similar to the advertising aspect, but it would be
advisable to enhance them further. Based on the res ults of the tourism facilities statements, the highest average scores that
should be maintained relate to parking spaces, clean water installations, nearby hospitals or health centers, and service
quality. However, statements with relatively low scores tha t fall below the average must be improved, such as the lack of
security, travel agents, and dedicated tourist transportation at Sunyaragi Cave. Therefore, it is recommended that the
management of Sunyaragi Cave tourism in Cirebon provide a stronger sense o f security, travel agency services, and special
transportation for tourists so that visitors feel more satisfied when using the tourism facilities at Sunyaragi Cave.
The level of tourist satisfaction perceived by visitors to Sunyaragi Cave in Cirebon is fa irly good, but it would be
beneficial to improve it even further. Judging from the results of the tourist satisfaction statements, the highest average
scores that deserve to be maintained pertain to high -quality products, excellent service levels, building  relationships with
tourists, creating loyalty, prioritizing tourists as the most valuable asset, showing care toward tourists, well -functioning
goods and services at Sunyaragi Cave, service quality assurance, customer trust, and organized resource support . However,
statements with relatively low scores that fall below the average must be addressed, such as the lack of discounts and
vouchers, as well as the difficulty in contacting the tourism management. Therefore, it is recommended that the
management get  closer to customers by offering discounts or vouchers and making it easier for tourists to reach the
management, so that visitors feel more satisfied and even become loyal to Sunyaragi Cave tourism.
