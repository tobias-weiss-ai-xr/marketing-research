ABSTRACT

With

issue

However,

overall

onboard

by

(meals,

etc.)

contribute

to

airlines’

environmental

impacts

and

have

only

recently

been

targeted

sustainability

This

study

investigates

the

effects

of

sustainable

supply

chains

for

onboard

products

on

customers’

perceptions

of

U.S.

airlines’

brand

image.

It

uses

a

mixed-methods

approach,

including

a

literature

review,

a

case

study,

and

a

survey

of

customer

perceptions,

to

identify

customer

attitudes

towards

U.S.

airlines’

sustainability

initiatives

related

to

onboard

products.

Findings

show

that

there

are

some,

albeit

insignificant,

impacts

of

the

quality

and

marketing

of

sustainable

onboard

products

on

customer

perception,

although

customers

who

are

aware

of

sustainable

onboard

products

generate

positive

responses

towards

airline

brand

image

through

a

higher

perception

of

efficiency.
