Abstract - In today's highly competitive world of businesses,
there is a need for efficient management of marketing
investments in order to ensure good financial performance and
sustained growth. Nevertheless, very little is known about the
impact of properly allocat ed marketing expenses on corporate
financial performance. This research seeks to evaluate the effect
of marketing expenditure efficiency on corporate financial
performance by developing a conceptual framework that treats
marketing investment as a corporate  resource used to create
value. The uniqueness of this study is the combination of
marketing expenditure efficiency with corporate financial
performance analysis by determining the influence of efficient
use of marketing resources on profitability and fina ncial
growth. The methodology applied will be quantitative in nature
and will employ financial data gathered second -hand from the
annual reports of PT Indosat Tbk and PT Telkom Indonesia
Tbk for the period 2021 –2026. The analysis of data was done
through the use of Microsoft Excel, SPSS, and AMOS, where
descriptive analysis, Shapiro-Wilk normality test, ANOVA, and
Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) were done. It is evident
from the results that there were significant v ariations in
financial performance indicators during the research period,
while SEM analysis shows a positive correlation between
efficiency in marketing expenditure and corporate financial
performance. The model proposed in this paper obtained good
fit values for PT Indosat Tbk (χ² = 6.214, CFI = 0.984, RMSEA
= 0.059), and PT Telkom Indonesia Tbk (χ² = 5.842, CFI = 0.982,
RMSEA = 0.062).

I. INTRODUCTION
The current business setting has seen firms concentrating
increasingly on the proper exploitation of strategic resources
so as to gain competitive advantage, profitability, and
economic sustainability. Corporate marketing expense is one
major corporate inv estment that needs to be used efficiently
to create value for the firm through improved market
performance and increased revenues. Efficiency in the use of
corporate resources has been found to be an important factor
in corporate financial performance since good corporate
governance, strategic decision -making, and investment
activities make the firm perform better (Kamola et al., 2026).
It has been observed from previous literature that efficient
corporate strategy and management practices greatly impact
the financial performance of the firm by increasing
profitability and economic value (Matuszewska-Pierzynka,
2021).
In this regard, the efficiency of marketing expenditure is of
considerable importance in determining whether there is any
financial benefit from marketing investments or whether such
expenses become a burden on the company's operations . It
has been proven that the efficient allocation of marketing
investments impacts corporate investment efficiency and
improves firms' financial performance through efficient
resource utilization (Wang et al., 2025 ). Moreover, efficient
financial management and corporate governance practices
lead to effective resource allocation by helping align the
organization's expenditures  with its objectives and
performance improvement efforts  (Fan, 2025). The research

II. LITERATURE REVIEW
From a financial perspective, corporate performance depends
on how well organizations manage their strategic resources
and translate  operations into economic performance.
Efficient resource management, operational competitiveness,
and strategic investment decision-making are perceived as
key factors contributing to organizational financial
performance. Several studies have proven that efficient use
of resources and operational management positively affect
financial performance, as it allows optimizing costs,
improving efficiency, and creating competitive advantages
for firms  (Amarasuriya et al., 2024 ). Likewise, corporate
transparency and information management have been
recognized as critical factors contributing to the efficiency of
the decision -making process and to improving financial
performance by creating  stakeholder trust  (Hariri, 2022 ).
Corporate governance and managerial effectiveness can
contribute to the organization's efficient allocation of
resources. Various studies from different business settings
prove that corporate governance contributes to financial
performance through responsible resource management and
strategic investment decision-making (Sodnomdorj et al.,
2024).
Besides, there is evidence suggesting that proper expenditure
management has been a vital element in determining
financial performance since the effective management and
use of expenditure in the organization help firms realize
enhanced profitability and e fficiency (Osoro et al., 2025 ).
The approaches underscore the importance of understanding
that marketing expenditure efficiency should be evaluated in
relation to the broader issue of financial resources
management, as the optimal marketing expenditures will help
companies generate mor e revenues and financial
performance. Effective strategies within corporations, which
ensure alignment of operations with the corporate objectives,
help in achieving better financial performance and creating
sustained value (Dhanasekar et al., 2023 ). Strategic
marketing management approaches integrated with
organizational capacities have a significant impact on
corporate financial performance by improving market
positions and resource usage efficiency  (Rajindra, 2024 ).
Financial architecture, corporate ownership structure, and
organizational design are other elements that affect the
efficiency of converting investments into corporate financial
performance. Effective financial architectures help
organizations optimize t heir resource allocations, make
investment decisions, and create corporate value (Widnyana
et al., 2021).
The efficiency of marketing spending is related to the need
for proper financial planning and strategic alignment, which
will help achieve sustainable returns on investment . In
addition, the use of digital platforms and relationship
marketing is believed to improve corporate performance
through the means of increased customer engagement,

Problem Statement
In the dynamic world of today, where competition continues
to increase, businesses use a considerable amount of money
for marketing initiatives with the goal of improving the level
of customer engagement, presence in the market, and income
generation. However, one of the issues faced by companies is
related to the assessment of whether the marketing spending
of the company is spent effectively and whether the company
receives a positive effect from investing in marketing.
Improper distribution of marketing resources leads to higher
costs and ineffective investments in contributing to the
organization's development . Even though marketing
expenditure is perceived as an important strategic investment,
the link between expenditure efficiency and financial
performance is not well understood, particularly regarding
how effective spending affects  financial performance. Thus,
the aim of this research is to analyze the link between
marketing expenditure efficiency and corporate financial
performance.
Research Objective
1. To examine the role of marketing expenditure efficiency
in enhancing corporate financial performance.
2. To investigate the influence of effective marketing
expenditure allocation on organizational growth and
profitability.
3. To develop an understanding of how strategic marketing
investments contribute to sustainable corporate
financial outcomes.
Research Hypothesis
• H1: Marketing expenditure efficiency has a significant
positive influence on corporate financial performance.
• H2: Effective allocation of marketing expenditure
contributes significantly to improved corporate
profitability and business growth.
• H3: Strategic utilization of marketing investments
enhances the long -term financial sustainability and
competitive performance of corporations.
Research Questions
RQ1: How does the efficiency of marketing expenditure
influence corporate financial performance?
RQ2: What is the role of effective allocation of marketing
expenditure in improving corporate profitability and
business growth?
RQ3: How can strategic marketing investments contribute
to achieving sustainable financial performance and
long-term corporate success?

better financial performance gives organizations the
opportunity to improve future marketing investments and
creates a cycle of efficient marketing investments and the
development of corporations.

3.2 Research Design
In this study, the researchers employ a research design where
there is a quantification of the relationship between the
efficiency of marketing expenditure and the financial
performance of the companies involved. This is done through
the use of the financial data of the two companies. The
research will make use of panel data obtained from the
audited annual reports of PT Indosat Tbk and PT Telkom
Indonesia Tbk  covering the years 2021 to 2026. In the
collected data will be marketing expenditure data indicators
and corporate financial performance indicators for the
examination of the efficiency of marketing expenditure in
contributing to organizational outcomes. For data
pre-processing and statistical analysis, Microsoft Excel
software will be used for organizing and preparing the data.
For descriptive statistics and normality test through
Shapiro–Wilk test and the One -Way ANOVA test, SPSS
software will be used in evaluating the data characteristics
and performance variation per year. Finally, AMOS software
will be used for structural equation modeling (SEM).
3.3 Samples and Data Collection Methods
In this study, the use of structured data panels of companies
obtained from secondary sources is applied in order to obtain
valid conclusions. The factors to consider include companies
from different industries having information regarding
marketing costs and financial performance indicators. The
samples will be applied in the telecommunications company
operating in Indonesia like PT Indosat Tbk and PT Telkom
Indonesia Tbk . Data will be obtained from the financial
statements of www.idx.co.id from 2021 to 2026
(www.idx.co.id). Factors to consider when selecting firms
include those firms that provide information regarding their
marketing costs (digital marketing, brand marketing, sales
promotions, and innovation costs) and financial performance
indicators (ROA, ROE, growth in revenue, profit margin, and
market value). The process of data gathering will mainly rely
on audited annual reports and financial databases of
companies where data validity is high.  Furthermore, data is
also to be gathered from industry reports and analyses, which
may assist in filling up any possible data gap that may occur.
Data will be collected in panel data format to make use of
variation between sections (firms) and periods to p redict
financial performance of the firms through machine learning
algorithms. Some of the common methods of data
pre-processing will be adopted.

4.6 Discussion and Key Findings
The conclusions indicate that marketing expenditure
efficiency positively impacts  corporate financial
performance. From the financial analysis, it can be concluded
that PT Indosat Tbk registered consistent growth in revenue
from 7,345,302 in 2021 to 15,220,661 in 2026, whereas PT
Telkom Indonesia Tbk sustained stable revenue performance
throughout the research period. The ANOV A results reveal
that there are significant differences in yearly performance in
relation to sales revenue, net income, net profit, and
investment expenditure among other key fin ancial
parameters. SEM analysis using AMOS confirms the
relationship through fit model values in both companies,
where CFI for PT Indosat Tbk is 0.984 and RMSEA is 0.059,
while CFI for PT Telkom Indonesia Tbk is 0.982 and RMSEA
is 0.062.
4.7 Practical and Theoretical Implications
This empirical finding is useful for corporate managers and
decision makers who must recognize the necessity of
perceiving marketing expenditure as an investment that is
strategic in nature. Improved financial performance can be
achieved through proper all ocation of marketing resources
according to the firm's goals, efficient monitoring of
investments, and optimal selection of marketing strategies
based on  performance results. Thus, the study helps
companies to develop more effective systems of expenditure
management to increase profitability and competitiveness.
The current research will make a contribution to the literature
since it broadens the understanding of the association
between marketing expenditure efficiency and the financial
performance of the firms. The majority of previous studies
were oriented to resource management, governance and
efficiency of investments in general; meanwhile, this paper
shows that marketing expenditure efficiency is an important
aspect of the company's strategy.
4.8 Recommendations
Organizations should develop systematic methods for
measuring the effectiveness of marketing expenses and
ensuring marketing costs align with the organization's
strategic goals. It is recommended for companies to enhance
decision-making based on data, allocate resources efficiently,
and assess the financial value of their marketing efforts.
Future research can further explore this area by using more
data samples, covering several industries, and adding more
organizational variables.

between marketing expenditure efficiency and firms'

investment issue, not just an operational cost. The research
