Effective corporate social responsibility tools in sustainable digital marketing.
The aim of the article . The study aims to systematise and comparatively evaluate the
effectiveness of corporate social responsibility (CSR) instruments wit hin the strategic framework of
sustainable digital marketing. It seeks to conceptualise sustainable digita l marketing by integrating
environmental, social, and governance (ESG) criteria into digital communicat ion channels, while
empirically verifying the mediating roles of consumer trust and emotional engag ement in generating
brand loyalty and purchasing intentions.
Analysis results. Methodologically, the research implements a mixed design, combining an
online consumer survey (n = 160) with semi-structured qualitative interview s, structural equation
modelling (SEM), and K-means cluster analysis. The empirical results demonstra te that personalized
digital CSR content significantly outperforms traditional advertising ch annels. Personalized digital
communications achieved the highest mean evaluation metrics for consumer trust (4.4  out of 5) and
integration of emotional engagement (4.6 out of 5). Furthermore, a strong positive Pearson correlation
coefficient was established between trust in digital CSR tools and purchase intentions (r = 0.73). In
contrast, traditional formats like television and print CSR advertising exhibited lower trust values (2.5–
2.8) and a weak behavioral association (r = 0.27 –0.33). Structural equation modelling confirmed that
consumer trust and emotional engagement act as critical full mediators linking digital CSR exposure to
behavioral loyalty. Market segmentation identified three distinct consumer clusters, led by a dominant
segment of "digital enthusiasts" (primarily aged 18 –35) who display maximum responsiveness to
interactive online CSR initiatives. Age-related cohorts reveal a n inverse relationship between digital
channel efficiency and respondent age, while gender analysis indicates a height ened emotional
responsiveness among female consumers toward tailored digital data.

corporate marketing must transition toward integrated communication models where personalized
