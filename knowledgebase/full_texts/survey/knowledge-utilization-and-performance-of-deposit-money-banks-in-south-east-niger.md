Abstract
The study investigates the effect of knowledge utilization and organizational performance of
deposit money banks in South-East, Nigeria. The study aims to examine the determine the effect of
knowledge generation on marketing performance of deposit money banks; knowledge creation on
adoptive performance of deposit money banks and knowledge  acquisition on organizational
effectiveness of deposit money; in South -East, Nigeria. Relevant conceptual, theoretical and
empirical literature were reviewed. The study was anchored on Resource Based View (RBV)
theory. Descriptive research survey was adopted in this study. The study was carried out in South-
East. Primary and secondary sources of data were employed. The population of the study
comprises comprised 1288 employees of Deposit Money Banks in South-East while Sample Size of
251 was obtain using statistical formula devised by Borg and Gall. Face and content validity
method was used to ensure validity of the instrument. The reliability of the instrument was achieved
through test re-rest method. Simple percentage was employed to answer the research questions.
Linear Regression Analysis was used in testing hypotheses  at 0.05 level of significance. It was
discovered that knowledge generation had a positive significant effect on marketing performance
of deposit money banks (B=.473, t=8.003, p=0.00). knowledge creation had a positive significant
effect on adoptive performance of deposit money banks (B=.743, t=22.221, p=0.000 ) and
knowledge acquisition had a positive significant effect on organizational effectiveness of deposit
money banks i (B=.367, t=23.490, p=0.000). The study concluded that concluded that knowledge
utilization had a significant positive effect on performance using of deposit money banks in South-
East, Nigeria. The study recommended that d eposit money banks should encourage knowledge
generation within the organization so as to gain a better-quality service and better performance.
Knowledge creation should be made availability for formal and informal networks between
organizations employees and external experts; memberships of professional bodies; constant
experimentation with new ideas; and employee efficiency use in deposit money banks Management
of deposit money banks should prioritize internal Knowledge acquisition strategies that will enable
harnessing of internal knowledge and capitalizing on such knowledge to enhance employee
performance. The s tudy empirically established that knowledge utilization are positively and
significantly related to organizational performance of deposit money banks.

Knowledge Utilization
There are as many definitions for knowledge utilization as there are people who are working on
this subject. Given below, are some of the most commonly used definitions: Knowledge utilization
is to understand, focus on, and manage systematic, explicit, and  deliberate knowledge building,
renewal, and application that is, manage effective knowledge processes. Knowledge utilization
refers to the process in which companies create and use their institutional and collective
knowledge. knowledge utilization deals with organising and controlling the operational processes
in the knowledge value chain in the most efficient way. Knowledge utilization focuses on
facilitating and managing knowledge related activities. knowledge utilization is systematic,
explicit and deliberate building, renewal and application of knowledge to maximise an enterprise’s
knowledge-related effectiveness and returns from its knowledge assets (Kemp et al, 2022).
Knowledge sharing takes place at the beginning of the knowledge creation process, where tacit
knowledge is shared among individuals through socialization. Typically this happens in a team
with members from various backgrounds, and tacit knowledge can only be shared through shared
experience. Apprenticeship is a common way of sharing (tacit) knowledge (Nonaka & Takeuchi
2022). Knowledge is “naturally” shared through any social activity, within numerous communities
of practice. Ouitas et al (2019) Knowledge u tilization is to discover, develop, utilize, deliver and
absorb knowledge inside and outside the organization through an appropriate management process
to meet current and future needs Knowledge utilization is knowledge creation followed by
interpretation, knowledge dissemination and use, and knowledge retention and refinement.
Knowledge utilization is the process of critically managing knowledge to meet existing needs, to
identify and exploit existing and acquired knowledge assets and to develop new opportunities.
Alan (2022) defined Knowledge utilization as a process of managing firm’s knowledge assets so
as to create value and meet tactical and strategic requirements through the creation, sharing, and
storage of knowledge. It has to do with the systematic process by which the firm gets value from
its knowledge-based assets. Thus, it can be seen as the management of behaviour which can raise
the efficient use of knowledge and thereby the performance ability of the organization. Further,
Uriarte (2018) expressed that Knowledge utilization tools are hi -tech infrastructure consisting of
computers, database, network applications which helps the organization to facilitate effective
Knowledge utilization. The tools for Knowledge utilization are focused on assimilation,
comprehension, and learning of the information by indivi duals who will then transform data and

Knowledge utilization tools are used in the creation, organization and sharing knowledge in the

Theoretical Framework
Several theories exist that explains the association between strate gic knowledge utilization
practices and organizational performance. These theories include Resource Based View (RBV)
theory. The theoretical foundation of RBV dates back to the year 1950 when Penrose‘s viewed
organization as a pool of resources and articulation of the same by Penrose. The RBV consider the
resources of a firm as being a fundamental predictor of a firm‘s competitive advantage and
performance. Whereas resources can be categoriz ed in different ways, for instance tangible and
intangible, tangible resources facilitate execution of business process while the intangible
resources are the ones that might result in competitive advantage by allowing organizations to
incorporate unique a nd valuable practices (Barney, 1991). As noted by Barney (1991), RBV is
based on two assumptions of resources being heterogeneously distributed across organizations and
the non-transferability of productive resources from one organization to another without incurring
cost.
Thus, given the two assumptions, RBV holds that only an intangible resource that is valuable, rare,
hard to imitate and without strategically equivalent substitutes is critical in sustaining a firm‘s
performance (Barney, 1991). Within projects, RBV is critical in that project management practices
are based on tangible and intangible resources ( Fernie, Green, Weller & Newcombe 2003). For
instance, resources that are tangible include the use of codified methodologies, templates, tools
and techniques that are readily available across the discipline (Crawford, Pollack, & England,
2018) and (Jugdev & Mathur, 2018 ). On the other hand, intangible resources include leadership,
teamwork, knowledge etc. that might contribute towards competitive advantage (Killen, Jugdev,
Drouin, & Petit 2012). Thus, given leadership, knowledge and teamwork are valuable, rare, and
imperfectly imitable resources, these resources are expected to have an effect on organizational
innovation performance. In terms of applicability, RBV is criticized due to lack of c onsensus in
the uses of various definitional terms such as capabilities, assets, resources and competences. In
addition, RBV is criticized on the basis of whether it can be tested due to lack of methodology to
measure intangible resources (Barney, et al., 1991).
Resource based view theory is relevant for the current research on influence of strategic knowledge
utilization practices on organizational performance since knowledge can be a unique resource that
must be managed efficiently and effectively to contribute to organizational performance. The
knowledge resources identified by the Resource Based View Theory should be organized and
managed efficiently and effectively to ensure organizational competitive in deposit money bank.

5.1 Summary of Findings
This work evaluated the effect of Knowledge utilization on organizational performance using
deposit money banks in South-East, Nigeria. The data generated were analyzed and the following
were established.
1. Knowledge generation had a significant positive effect on marketing performance of deposit
money banks in South-East, Nigeria with (B=.473, t=8.003, p=0.00).
2. Knowledge creation had a significant positive effect on adoptive performance of deposit
money banks in South-East, Nigeria with (B=.743, t=22.221, p=0.000
3. Knowledge acquisition had a significant positive effect on organizational effectiveness of
deposit money banks in South-East, Nigeria (with B=.367, t=23.490, p=0.000).

Conclusion
This work examined the effect of knowledge utilization and performance of deposit money banks
in South -East, Nigeria.  Data were obtained from of deposit money banks in South - East and
subjected to various statistical tests. The study found that It was discovered that k nowledge
generation had a positive significant effect on marketing performance of deposit money banks;
Knowledge creation had a positive significant effect on adoptive performance of deposit money
banks and Knowledge acquisition had a positive significant effect on organizational effectiveness
of deposit money banks in South- East, Nigeria. Based on the result, the study concluded that
knowledge utilization had a significant positive effect on performance using of deposit money
banks in South-East, Nigeria.

Recommendations
Based on the findings of the study, it is recommended that:
1. Deposit money banks should encourage knowledge generation within the organization so as to
gain a better-quality service and better performance.
2. Knowledge creation should be made availability for formal and informal networks between
organizations employees and external experts; memberships of professional bodies; constant
experimentation with new ideas; and employee efficiency use in deposit money banks
3. Management of deposit money banks should prioritize internal Knowledge acquisition
strategies that will enable harnessing of internal knowledge and capitalizing on such
knowledge to enhance employee performance.
