A B S T R A C T

The purpose of the research – this article develops a conceptual framework explaining
how AI -personalized learning can build relationship -critical e -commerce competencies and
thereby strengthen digital business relations. It addresses the gap in the literature, where AI -
enabled adap tive learning systems, e -commerce competency taxonomies, and relationship
digital trust constructs are usually examined separately rather than within one integrated causal
model.
The methodology of the research  – the study is based on a conceptual and theo retical
synthesis of adaptive learning theory, capability -based reasoning, and relationship marketing.
On this basis, three core constructs are defined as AI -personalized learning, relational e -
commerce skills, and digital business relationship quality. In  addition to that the mechanisms
connecting them are specified. The model also incorporates contextual factors such as digital
literacy, experience, and interaction context, and proposes an operationalization roadmap for
later empirical validation through objective competency tests, scenario -based performance
rubrics, and learning analytics.
The practical importance of the research  – the framework provides value for
researchers, educators, and e-commerce practitioners by offering a basis for designing adaptive
learning interventions that explicitly develop trust -building, communication quality, and

The results of the research – the study proposes that AI-personalized learning improves

I N T R O D U C T I O N

Electronic commerce has become a structural element of the global digital economy. Over
the past two decades, digital platforms, marketplaces, and online services have fundamentally
transformed interactions among companies, suppliers, and consumers, eliminating geo -
graphical barriers and reshaping traditional value chains. At the same time, the expanding scale
of digital trade has exposed a deep -seated contradiction. “The pace of technological platform
development significantly outstrips the rate at which professionals develop the competencies
required for effective participation in digital business interactions.”
Literature review. Relationship marketing theory demonstrates that the sustainability of
business interactions is determined not so much by the technological parameters of transactions
as by the quality of interpersonal and interorganizational connections. It has been argued that
trust and commitment serve as central mediators between the perceived benefits of relationships
and their long-term behavioral outcomes [9, s.20-38]. A meta-analysis of 38 studies confirmed
that the relational competencies of interaction participants are more reliable predictors of
partner satisfaction than the technical characteristics of a product or service [12, s.136-153].
In the digital environment, the importance of relational factors increases. In online
interactions, the absence of direct personal contact makes trust a critical condition for successful
transactions [7, s.51 -90]. A study  based on a sample of 321 companies established that the
quality of AI-powered services on e -commerce platforms significantly affects partner loyalty
and engagement, with trust acting as a moderator of this relationship [13, s.1483-1522].
Despite growing recognition of the role of relational factors, educational programs in e -
commerce continue to focus predominantly on technical competencies. As a result, profes -
sionals frequently possess strong technical knowledge yet experience persistent difficulties in
building trustworthy and long -term partnerships online. This gap is particularly acute in light
of World Economic Forum data indicating that six in 10 workers will require training before
2027 [22].
One promising avenue for modernizing professional educati onal programs is the appli -
cation of artificial intelligence to personalize learning. A systematic review of 78 articles do -
cumented a rapid transition to sophisticated models integrating machine learning, natural

Problem statement. A systematic analysis of the literature across three relevant research
streams like AI-personalized learning, e-commerce competencies, and digital business relations
reveals a structural theoretical gap. “Despite the active and independent development of each
stream, the existing academic literature contains virtually no conceptual models integrating all
three constructs within a unified causal framework”.
In support of this claim we operated a targeted search of academic databases using the
keywords "AI -personalized learning" AND "e -commerce skills" AND "digital business re -
lations", and did not identify studies that simultaneously operationalize all three constructs
within a single unified theoretical model, within the defined search scope.
This gap has both theoretical and practical dimensions. Theoreti cally, the mechanism
through which educational interventions translate into the quality of business relations remains
unconceptualized. Practically, it deprives organizations of a theoretical foundation for de -
signing AI-supported professional development programs explicitly oriented toward relational
outcomes.
Adjacent studies approach this gap without filling it. AI algorithms can identify individual
competency gaps, yet relational skills are not treated as a distinct category [6, s.199 -229]. A
study of 367 companies links organizational AI competencies to business relationship outcomes
but does not examine the mechanism of educational interventions [2, s.39 -47]. Another study
examines AI applications in customer relationship management but remains confined to CRM
discourse [11, s.195 -210]. Accordingly, the present study occupies a distinct position at the
intersection of these three streams.
Theoretical foundations.  To provide a theoretical foundation for the problem under
investigation, this study integra tes three established research domains through a capability -
based lens that treats professional skills as strategic, relationship-enabling capabilities.
The capability-based view argues that firms achieve sustained advantage by developing
valuable, rare, inimitable, and well-organized resources and capabilities [1, s.99-120]. In digital
commerce, many critical resources are intangible and skill -based. The ability to communicate
credibly online, manage interdependence with partners, and recover service failures becomes a
relationship capability that supports long-term cooperation. Dynamic capability theory further
emphasizes that such capabilities must be continuously renewed to match fast-changing digital
platforms and customer expectations [17, s.509 -533; 5 , s.1105 -1121]. In our framework,
relational e-commerce skills represent the microfoundations of relationship capability. These
observable behaviors can be developed and strengthened through targeted, adaptive training.
Adaptive learning theory and AI in e ducation provide the conceptual toolkit for
explaining how educational pathways can be personalized in a scalable manner. AI -
personalized learning relies on personalization inputs like diagnostics, learner profiles, and
behavioral traces, and personalization outputs like content sequencing, adaptive task difficulty,
and feedback tailored to an individual’s learning gaps and professional context [4, s.3 -48; 18,
s.118-130]. For e-commerce skills, scenario-based tasks and adaptive feedback are especially
relevant because they support transfer from classroom knowledge to relationship -critical
behavior in real digital interactions [10, s.146-154].

E-commerce competence theory provides the classificatory basis for systematizing pro -
fessional skills in digital comm erce. Beyond instrumental platform skills, relationship -critical
competencies include digital communication, trust-building, negotiation, and service recovery
in online environments where uncertainty and information asymmetry are high [15, s.726-743].
For baseline digital competence, we align with DigComp 2.2 [19], while extending it by
emphasizing how competencies translate into relationship capability through repeated, context-
specific practice and feedback.
Relationship marketing theory provides construc ts for explaining why digital business
relations endure. Trust and commitment are central determinants of relationship quality and
long-term cooperation [9, s.20 -38; 12, s.136 -153]. However, digital relationships are also
shaped by communication quality an d how partners handle service failures and complaints.
Service recovery and perceived fairness after failures can either restore or damage trust, making
recovery capability a key relational competence in e-commerce [16, s.60-76; 14, s.356-372].

Research questions.  The aim of the present study is to develop and theoretically
substantiate a conceptual model describing the mechanisms through which AI -personalized
learning influences the development of relational e -commerce skills and the subsequent
improvement of digital business relationship quality.

questions are formulated below:

Construct I: AI -Personalized Learning. AI-personalized learning is an educational
system that employs machine learning and data analytics algorithms to dynamically adapt
educational trajectories, content, complexity, and feedback mechanisms to the individual
characteristics, needs, and professional context of each learner, with the aim of maximizing
competency outcomes.
This construct is operationalized through four interrelated dimensions. The first one is so
called “adaptivity of educational trajectories” and draws on the concept of the Zone of Proximal
Development [20, s.84 -91; 4, s.3 -48]. The second is so called “quality of AI -generated
feedback” [18, s.118-130]. The third is so called “learning gap analytics”, enabling proactive
identification of competency def icits [6, s.199 -229]. The fourth is so called “contextual
relevance of content”, ensuring correspondence to the learner's professional context [10, s.146-
154].
Construct II: Relational E-Commerce Skills. Relational e-commerce skills are the set
of professional competencies enabling an e-commerce specialist to effectively initiate, develop,
and sustain trustworthy, mutually beneficial, and long-term business relations with partners and
clients in the digital environment in the absence of direct personal contact.
This construct performs the function of mediator and constitutes a new theoretical
contribution of the present study. Its composition was determined on the basis of a synthesis of
the e-commerce competence classification [15, s.726 -743], research on d igital trust [7, s.51 -
90], empirical work on B2B interactions [13, s.1483-1522], and AI in CRM research [11, s.195-
210]. Five skill groups are identified:
- digital communication;
- online trust-building;
- relationship management;
- interaction personalization;
- digital negotiation.
Construct III: Digital Business Relationship Quality. Digital business relationship
quality is a multidimensional construct reflecting the perceived value, sustainability, and mutual
orientation toward long -term collab oration among participants in interactions conducted
predominantly in the digital environment, operationalized through four dimensions:
- perceived trust;
- commitment;
- interaction satisfaction;
- long-term cooperation intention.
This definition synthesizes classical relationship marketing theory [9, s.20-38; 12, s.136-
153] with the findings of contemporary empirical research in the B2B e-commerce context [13,
s.1483-1522]. Perceived trust reflects a participant's conviction regarding the competence and
goodwill of the partner under conditions of digital uncertainty [7, s.51-90].

Alternative Explanations. To make the model more understandable, we propose alter -
native explanations below.
Alternative 1. Is it sufficient to develop only technical skills? Technical competencies do
not directly produce trust and commitment between business partners [12, s.136 -153]. The
present model focuses on the relational dimensi on, which is systematically undervalued in
existing educational programs.
Alternative 2. Why AI personalization rather than standard training? Empirical research
with 300 students demonstrates that AI -personalized platforms significantly outperform tra -
ditional training formats on academic performance, engagement, and satisfaction. Nevertheless,
poorly designed AI learning may stimulate cognitive offloading [8, s.67-78].
Alternative 3. Are organizational factors not more important than individual compe -
tencies? The present model does not compete with organizational -level analysis but comple -
ments it by focusing on the individual level. The specific specialist's competence is treated as
the mechanism through which organizational potential is realized [2, 2.167-175].
Theoretical Contributions. First, the model introduces a new construct as “relational e-
commerce skills” - a distinct category of professional competencies. This construct bridges the
conceptual gap between digital competence research [15, s.726-743] and relationship marketing
theory [9, s.20-38].
Second, the model establishes a mediation mechanism linking educational technologies
to business relationship outcomes through the development of professional competencies,
thereby extending adaptive learning theory [18, s.118-130; 4, s.3-48].
Third, the model integrates relationship marketing theory with research in educational
technology, opening a new direction for empirical investigation and providing a theoretical
foundation for the development of AI -supported educational programs expl icitly oriented
toward relational outcomes.

C O N C L U S I O N

This article developed an integrated conceptual framework explaining how AI -perso-
nalized learning can strengthen digital business relations by building relationship -critical e-
commerce competencies. Drawing on the capability -based view and dynamic capabilities, we
position relational e-commerce skills as microfoundations of relationship capability in digital
markets, where information asymmetry and limited face-to-face contact make trust formation,
communication quality, negotiation behavior, and service recovery decisive for relationship
continuity. We further specify how AI -personalized learning through adaptive learning tra -
jectories, targeted feedback, and learning -gap analytics can accelerate the acquisition of these
competencies at scale.
The model makes three main contributions. First, it synthesizes adaptive learning theory,

Tədqiqatın metodologiyası – tədqiqat adaptiv öyrənmə nəzəriyyəsi, kompetensiyalara
əsaslanan yanaşma və münasibətlər marketinqinin konseptual və nəzəri sintezinə əsaslanır. Bu
əsasda üç əsas konstrukt – süni intellektə əsaslanan fərdiləşdirilmiş öyrənmə, münasibətlərin
qurulmasına yönəlmiş elektron ticarət bacarıqları və rəqəmsal biznes münasibətlərinin keyfiy -
yəti müəyyən edilmişdir. Bundan əlavə, bu konstruktlar arasında əlaqəni təmin edən mexanizm-
lər konkretləşdirilmişdir. Modeldə, həmçinin rəqəmsal savadlılıq, təcrübə və qarşılıqlı əlaqə
konteksti kimi kontekstual amillər nəzərə alınmış, gələcək empirik yoxlama üçün obyektiv
kompetensiya testləri, ssenari əsaslı fəaliyyət qiymətləndirmə meyarları və öyrənmə analitikası
vasitəsilə əməliyyatlaşdırma yol xəritəsi təklif edilmişdir.
Tədqiqatın tətbiqi əhəmiyyəti – təklif olunan konseptual çərçivə tədqiqatçılar, təhsil
işçiləri və elektron ticarət sahəsi ndə fəaliyyət göstərən praktiklər üçün əhəmiyyət kəsb edir.
Çərçivə rəqəmsal ticarət mühitində etimadın formalaşdırılması, kommunikasiya keyfiyyətinin
yüksəldilməsi və xidmətin bərpası üzrə bacarıqların məqsədyönlü şəkildə inkişaf etdirilməsini
təmin edən adaptiv öyrənmə müdaxilələrinin hazırlanması üçün əsas yaradır. Bu yanaşma,
xüsusilə Azərbaycan və Özbəkistan kimi müxtəlif məkanlarda gələcəkdə çoxsaylı tədqiqat
məntəqələrini əhatə edən empirik yoxlama və tətbiq baxımından aktualdır.
Tədqiqatın nəticələr i – tədqiqatda irəli sürülür ki, süni intellektə əsaslanan fərdiləş -
dirilmiş öyrənmə münasibətlərin qurulmasına yönəlmiş elektron ticarət bacarıqlarının inkişafı
vasitəsilə rəqəmsal biznes münasibətlərinin keyfiyyətini dolayı yolla yüksəldir. Təklif olunan
çərçivədə münasibətlərin qurulmasına yönəlmiş elektron ticarət bacarıqları tam vasitəçi rolunu
oynayır, kontekstual amillər isə bu əlaqələrin gücünə təsir göstərir. Gələcək tədqiqat larda em-
pirik yoxlama üçün dörd müddəa irəli sürülmüşdür.
Tədqiqatın ori jinallığı və elmi yeniliyi  – məqalə adaptiv öyrənmə, elektron ticarət
kompetensiyalarının inkişafı və münasibətlər marketinqini vahid konseptual modeldə inteqra -
siya etməklə orijinal elmi töhfə verir. Tədqiqat münasibətlərin qurulmasına yönəlmiş elektron

və konseptual çərçivənin müxtəlif milli kontekstlərdə empirik yoxlanılması üçün strukturlaş -
dırılmış yol xəritəsi təqdim edir.
