ABSTRACT
The fast development of e -commerce in India has transformed consumer markets, presenting both
opportunities and challenges for advancing sustainability in online consumption. While environmental
awareness among Indian consume rs is growing, sustainable purchasing behaviour in electronic
marketplaces remains inconsistent and not fully understood. This conceptual paper, titled “Green is the
New Click: Exploring How Environmental Awareness and Perceived Value Influence Sustainable
Consumer Behaviour in Indian E -Commerce,” proposes a theoretical framework to examine how
environmental awareness and perceived value influence consumers’ sustainable buying intentions and
behaviours in the online shopping platform. Illustrate on the Theory of Planned Behaviour (Ajzen, 1991)
and the Value-Belief-Norm Theory (Stern, 2000), the framework join key constructs such as concern for
the environment, perceived eco -product value, trust in eco -labels, attitude toward green products,
perceived behavioural control, and green purchase intention.
The paper argues that strengthening environmental awareness, enhancing the perceived value of eco -
friendly products, and upgrade trust in eco -labels are imporatant strategies for encouraging sustainable
consumer behaviour in Indian e -commerce. Furthermore,  targeted electronic marketing strategies and
environmental education initiatives can foster eco -awareness decision -making among e -shoppers. By
combine psychological, technological, and environmental dimensions, this study contributes to a deeper
understanding of green consumerism in the digital era. It provides valuable knowledge into how
sustainable values and perceptions shape consumer choices, supporting India’s broader transition toward
a low-carbon and environmentally responsible economy.

Keywords:  Sustainable Consumer Behaviour, Environmental Awareness, Perceived Value, E -

2. THEORITICAL BACKGROUND
2.1 Theory of Planned Behaviour
The Theory of Planned Behavior (Ajzen, 1991) indicates that every human action is determined through
three intellectual determinants: attitude towards the behaviour, subjective norm, and perceived behavioural
control (PBC). These determinants together form the basis for behavioural intent, which further leads to
actual behaviour forecast. Attitude is the term for the extent to which an individual has a positive or
negative judgment of the behaviour; subjective norm denotes the social pressure trusted to be there to
carry out the behaviour; and PBC indicates the perceived ease or difficulty of doing it (Ajzen, 1991).
In the case of sustainable e -commerce, TPB asserts that a purchaser’s aim f or online green purchases is
influenced by their attitudes (which depend on environmental awareness and perceived value), the current
social norms regarding eco -friendly consumption, and their perceived control over the capacity to
purchase green products over the internet. Previous research (e.g., Kumar, 2012; Paul, Modi, & Patel,
2016; Yadav & Pathak, 2017) has confirmed TPB as a powerful tool in forecasting green purchase
intention and behaviour of Indian consumers. Therefore, in the current research, environmental awareness
and perceived value are seen as the main factors influencing consumers' attitudes towards online
sustainable purchasing, which finally determine the consumers' intentions and actions.
2.2 Value–Belief–Norm (VBN) Theory
The VBN theory (Stern, 2000) not only explicates the concept of pro -environmental behaviour but also
provides through its argument a value -moral perspective. It specifies a channel of causation from values
to beliefs to personal norms to behaviour. People with biospheric and altruistic values end up being more
approachable to the pro-environmental hopes convinced that the ''environment does not suffer by use'' and
''killing the land means killing our source of life''. Moreover, those and similar thoughts activate the
personal norms i.e. they declare the individual's inner obligation to behave sustainably.
Integrating VBN into the TPB framework enhances the understanding of environmental awareness as not
only logical knowledge, but also as an absorbed moral and value -driven belief system that motivates
sustainable behaviour (Alzubaidi, Slade, & Dwivedi, 2021; Hein, 2022). In the Indian e -commerce
context, heightened environmental awareness —shaped by cultural, social, and educational factors —can
strengthen consumers’ personal  norms, thereby influencing their intention to purchase eco -friendly
products online. The VBN framework thus highlights the ethical and normative dimensions of sustainable
consumer behaviour, bridging the “value–action” gap often found in sustainability research.

3.1 Environmental Awareness
Environmental awareness of Indian consumers has increased drastically according to a survey conducted
by PWC India in 2024 where 60% of Indian consumers declared that they have modified their b ehavior
and are picking sustainable products like those with eco -friendly packaging, waste reduction and so on
one of the main reasons for this shift is the growing concern for the environment rising pollution levels
plastic waste and climate change have d riven consumers to make more responsible purchasing decisions
another key factor is health and safety the case of organic goods etc. research in the Indian eCommerce
context confirms that consumers having higher environmental awareness are more likely to give a positive
response to sustainable business practices like packaging carbon footprints reduction and other green
initiatives what this means for online shopping platforms is that environmental factors are no longer edge
they have now become key to the value propositions.
3.2 Sustainable Consumer Behaviour
The Indian market has a very strong outcome when it comes to value which does not refer to the lowest
price only but it is a whole concept enclose the quality, durability, ethical, eco impact and how the purchase
related  with the personal values one report indicates that a lot of Indian consumers are showing sustainable

3.3 E-Commerce
The e-commerce expansion in India speeds up the sustainability process as the growth of the Indian digital
economy is forecast to reach one trillion dollars by 2040 . E-commerce platforms are adapted to green
strategies such as packaging that reduces waste, optimization of logistics, and usage of electric vehicles
for deliveries. These developments imply that whenever a consumer selects the purchase button, all the
activities from design to packaging to delivery can express environmentally-friendly practices at the same
time. This not only increases the product's value but also extends the proceeds for value of the purchase
beyond the product itself.
3.4 Green Marketing
Green marketing refers to the process of promoting and marketing the sale of products or services by
emphasizing their environment -friendly attributes. The traditional marketing practices used to result in
environmental degradation in terms of pollution caused during manufacturing, packaging, and disposal of
products. In light of these issues, green marketing has been initiated with the targets of reducing pollution
and protecting the environment. The main goal of green marketing is to create products t hat are eco -
friendly, refillable, or reusable thus, making it possible to decrease the waste created and the consumption
of resources. Environmentally friendly packaging is of greatest importance in green marketing because it
prevents pollution and other h armful environmental impacts of materials. Overall, green marketing not
only benefits the planet but also provides an opportunity for producers to maximize their profits by
winning over the green consumers and elevating their brand image.

4. CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATIONS
The study shows that the consumer intentions for sustainable purchasing in India’s rapid e -commerce
market are highly influenced by the consumers’ environmental consciousness and the perceived value of
eco-friendly in products. The interest in green produc ts is increasing, but the major concern about the
trustworthiness of the eco-labels and the unpredictable nature of the purchasing habits are the signals that
the companies have to work on communication and education first. If marketers underline the functional,
health, and social advantages of the sustainable products and build confidence through transparency and
the trustworthiness of the certification, they will be able to induce consumers towards eco-friendly buying
choices frequently. The application o f these strategies in internet shops will not only make it easier for
consumers to accept green products but will also help in India’s expeditious overall transformation to a
low-carbon and eco-friendly economy.
