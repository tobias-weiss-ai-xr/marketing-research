Abstract
Introduction: Green innovation plays a pivotal role in addressing environmental challenges while
fostering economic growth. As global concerns over climate change and resource depletion rise,
sustainable technologies have become integral to the future of industries and  society. Ecopreneurs,
individuals who merge entrepreneurial spirit with environmental responsibility, are at the forefront of this
transformative movement. Their ability to introduce innovative solutions that minimize environmental
impact and promote sustainable practices is reshaping industries across the globe.
Methodology: This study employs a qualitative research approach to explore the role of ecopreneurs in
the development of sustainable technologies, using a multiple case study design to analyze the ir
strategies, challenges and contributions. Data are gathered through semi -structured interviews with
ecopreneurs, industry experts and policymakers; a structured questionnaire distributed among
ecopreneurs; document analysis of publicly available reports , business case studies and academic
literature; and direct or virtual observation of ecopreneurial enterprises. A purposive sampling technique

1. Introduction
“Green innovation,” which refers to the d evelopment of new technologies and practices
aimed at minimizing environmental impact, is significantly driven by “ecopreneurs” —
entrepreneurs who actively pursue sustainable business models, playing a pivotal role in
creating and implementing environment ally friendly solutions by identifying market
opportunities for eco -conscious products and services, thus accelerating the transition
towards a more sustainable future.
Ecopreneurs play a key role in transforming industries by championing renewable
energy, circular economy principles, and green manufacturing processes. Their
commitment to sustainability not only helps reduce carbon footprints but also
encourages the adoption of responsible consumption and production patterns. As
governments, businesses, and  consumers increasingly recognize the urgency of
environmental stewardship, ecopreneurs are paving the way for a more sustainable
future.
1.1 Purpose
The purpose of the article “Green Innovation: The Key Role of Ecopreneurs in the
Development of Sustainable Technologies” is to explore and highlight how ecopreneurs
are at the forefront of creating and implementing innovative technologies that
contribute to environmental sustainability. Introducing the concept of green innovation
and how it involves the devel opment of new technologies, processes, or products that
reduce negative environmental impacts, increase resource efficiency, and promote
sustainability.

3. Design / Methodology / Approach
Ecopreneurs, driven by entrepreneurial skill s and innovation, play a crucial role in
developing sustainable technologies by identifying environmental and social issues and
creating solutions, fostering economic growth, and promoting sustainability.
3.1 Research Design
This study employs a qualitative research approach to explore the role of ecopreneurs in
the development of sustainable technologies. A multiple case study design is used to
analyze the strategies, challenges, and contributions of ecopreneurs in fostering
technological sustainability.
3.2 Data Collection Methods
The research gathers data through the following methods:
•  Interviews: Semi-structured interviews are conducted with ecopreneurs, industry
experts, and policymakers to gain insights into their experiences, motivations, and
contributions to sustainable technology.
•  Surveys: A structured questionnaire is distributed among ecopreneurs to collect
quantitative data on their sustainability practices, business models, and
technological innovations.
•  Document Analysis:  Publicly available reports, business case studies, and academic
literature on sustainable entrepreneurship are analyzed to contextualize findings and
support data triangulation.
