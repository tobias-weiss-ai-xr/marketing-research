ABSTRACT
This study aims  to analyze the influence of digital marketing on the growth of MSMEs in the
post-pandemic era. The method used is a literature review by examining various scientific
journals, research articles, and relevant library sources.  The data obtained were  analyzed
descriptively to identify the impact of digital marketing implementation on MSME
development. The results of the study indicate that digital marketing has a positive impact on
the growth of MSMEs, particularly in expanding market reach, increasing sales,
strengthening customer relationships, and enhancing business competitiveness. Various
studies reviewed also show that the use of social media, marketplaces, websites, and other
digital platforms helps MSMEs conduct promotions more effectively and efficiently. Thus,
digital marketing can be an effective strategy in supporting the growth and sustainability of
MSMEs in the post -pandemic era and accelerating MSME adaptation to developments in the
digital economy.

Keywords: Digital Marketing, MSMEs, Literature Review, Post-Pandemic Era

PENDAHULUAN
Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) are a sector that plays a crucial role
in supporting national economic growth. In addition to contributing to  increased incomes,
MSMEs are also  capable of creating jobs and promoting economic equality across various
regions. However, the COVID -19 pandemic has had a significant impact on business
activities, particularly on product marketing and sales. Many MSMEs have experienced a
decline in revenue due to limited direct interaction with consumers and changes in consumer
transaction patterns. Hadi & Zakiah (2021) explain that these conditions have pushed MSMEs
to seek more effective marketing strategies to ensure their businesses can survive and thrive
amidst an increasingly dynamic business environment.
Entering the post -pandemic era, developments in information and communication
technology have transformed consumer behavior in obtaining information and making
purchases. People now increasingly utilize  the internet and various digital platforms to search
for products, compare  prices, and even make purchases online. This behavioral shift presents
both an opportunity and a challenge for MSMEs adapting to digital technology -based
marketing systems. Al -azhar (2025) stated that utilizing digital technology in marketing
activities is one step that can help MSMEs expand their market reach and increase their
competitiveness.
Digital marketing is a marketing strategy that utilizes internet-based media to introduce
products or services to consumers. Various platforms such as social media, marketplaces,
websites, and digital communication applications can be used as effective promotional tools
at a relatively affordable cost. In addition to facilitating information dissemination, digital
marketing also enables businesses to establish more intensive communication with
consumers. According to  Laksamana et al., (2022) the use of digital media in marketing
activities can increase the effectiveness of marketing communications, allowing businesses to
reach a wider consumer base without being limited by geographic location.
The implementation of digital marketing has become a contributing factor to the
development of MSMEs. Various studies have shown that the use of digital media can
increase product visibility, expand market share, and drive increased sales. Karoma & Mire,
(2023) suggest that digital marketing has a positive impact on increasing MSME  sales,
especially when supported by product innovation and good product quality. This
demonstrates that marketing strategies utilizing digital technology can be an effective means
of strengthening the position of MSMEs amidst increasingly fierce business competition.
In addition to increasing sales, digital marketing also plays a role in building closer
relationships between businesses and consumers. Interactions through social media and other
digital platforms allow businesses to obtain direct feedback  from customers. Andriani, R., &
Aminah, (2024) explain that digital marketing strategies function not only as a promotional
medium but also as a means to increase customer loyalty through faster, more responsive, and
more interactive communication.
Therefore, digital marketing is a crucial instrument in supporting business
sustainability in the digital era. Despite offering various benefits, implementing digital
marketing in MSMEs still faces several obstacles. Some business owners still face limitations
in technological mastery, access to digital  information, and the ability to optimally manage
marketing strategies. Yuan Valentino Elim et al., (2024) revealed that low digital literacy and
limited resources are factors that influence the success of digital marketing implementation in
MSMEs. This situation demonstrates that digital transformation requires not only technology
but also the readiness of human resources to utilize it effectively.
Optimizing digital marketing is becoming increasingly important for MSMEs in the
post-pandemic era. Farasi, (2025) explains that an appropriate digital marketing strategy can
help MSMEs increase competitiveness, expand marketing networks, and create greater

METODE
This research employed a literature review with a qualitative descriptive approach.
This method was used to identify, review, and analyze  various research findings related to
digital marketing and the  growth of Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in the
post-pandemic era. A literature review is a research method that collects, evaluates, and
synthesizes information from various relevant scientific  sources to provide a comprehensive
overview of a research topic.  The data used in this study were secondary data obtained from
various scientific articles, national journals, and international journals discussing digital
marketing and the development of MSMEs. Literature sources were obtained through
searches of scientific databases such as Google Scholar, Garuda, and various openly
accessible e-journal portals.
The criteria for literature used included articles published between 2020 and 2025,
related to the topic of digital marketing and MSMEs, and available in  full-text, accessible
scientific articles  for analysis.  The research process was conducted in stages, starting with
identifying the research topic, conducting  a literature search, selecting articles based on
inclusion criteria, collecting data, analyzing content,  and drawing conclusions. In the initial
stage, researchers identified keywords such as digital marketing, digital marketing, MSMEs,
micro, small, and medium enterprises, and the post-pandemic era. Next,  the obtained articles
were selected  based on the  relevance of the  title, abstract, research objectives, research
methods, and research findings that align with the study's focus.
The collected data was then analyzed using content analysis techniques. This
technique involves identifying, categorizing, comparing, and interpreting the various findings
contained in the articles reviewed. The results of the analysis were then synthesized to obtain
an overview of the influence  of digital marketing on the growth of MSMEs, as well as the
various benefits and challenges faced in its implementation. Data analysis in library research
is conducted through a process of interpreting information obtained from various sources,
resulting in a deeper understanding of the phenomenon being studied.

HASIL DAN PEMBAHASAN
Development of Digital Marketing for MSMEs in the Post-Pandemic Era
Advances in information and communication technology have brought significant
changes to the  marketing patterns implemented by Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises
(MSMEs). In the post-pandemic era, digitalization  has become a driving factor in  the
transformation of business activities, particularly in the marketing of products and services.
Changing consumer behavior, which is increasingly  reliant on the internet, requires businesses
to adapt to various digital platforms to remain competitive in the market. This situation has led
to an increase in the use of social media, marketplaces, websites, and various digital
applications as promotional and communication tools with  consumers. Research by Al-azhar
(2025) shows that the use of digital marketing has a positive impact on increasing MSME
marketing activities because it allows them to reach a wider range of consumers without
being limited by geographic location. These findings indicate that digital marketing  has
become a crucial requirement  for MSMEs in facing increasingly competitive business
conditions.
The digital transformation that occurred after the pandemic has not only changed the

The Influence of Digital Marketing on the Growth of MSMEs
The result of a review of various studies show that digital marketing has a significant
impact on improving  MSME performance in the post-pandemic era. The use of digital media
allows businesses to reach a wider consumer base than conventional marketing methods.
Through various digital platforms, product  information can be delivered quickly to
consumers, increasing  the opportunity for transactions. Al -azhar (2025) explain that the
implementation of digital marketing can increase MSME sales volume because the products
marketed have a higher level of affordability compared to before using digital media. This
demonstrates that digital marketing plays an effective role in increasing MSME economic
activity.
In addition to increasing sales, digital marketing also contributes to increased business
competitiveness. In an increasingly competitive business environment, MSMEs are required to
adapt to technological developments to remain relevant in the market. (Lestari et al., 2024)
states that digital marketing provides MSMEs with the opportunity to compete with larger
businesses through the  use of relatively  accessible technology. By utilizing social media,
marketplaces, and other digital platforms, businesses can introduce their products to a wider
audience without incurring significant promotional costs. Digital marketing also offers the
benefit of increasing the efficiency of marketing activities.

Unlike conventional methods, which require printing, distribution, and direct

strengthen their competitiveness.

Impact on Sales and Market Expansions
One of the most frequently identified impacts in various studies is increased MSME
sales after implementing digital marketing strategies. The use of social media, marketplaces,
and websites  allows products to be promoted to a wider audience, thereby increasing the
opportunity for transactions. Al -azhar (2025) explain that digital marketing contributes to
increased sales volume because products can be accessed by consumers from various
regions without geographical restrictions. This provides significant benefits for MSMEs that
previously relied solely on local marketing.
In addition to increasing sales, digital marketing also plays a role in expanding
market reach. Marketplaces such as Shopee, Tokopedia, and other e -commerce platforms
provide opportunities for MSMEs to market their products nationally. (Andriani, R., &
Aminah, 2024) stated that the use of digital platforms helps businesses introduce products to
consumers previously difficult to reach through conventional marketing. Thus, digital
marketing opens up greater  opportunities for MSMEs to scale their businesses and expand
their marketing networks.
Hamid & Yusuf (2025) explain that engaging content marketing also plays a role in
increasing the effectiveness of digital marketing. The use of high -quality product photos,
promotional videos, and educational content can increase consumer attention to the products
offered. The more engaging the content, the more likely consumers are to engage with and
purchase. Therefore, content quality is a crucial factor in determining the success of digital
marketing. Overall, various studies show that digital marketing significantly contributes to
increased sales and market expansion for MSMEs. The ability of digital technology to reach
consumers broadly makes this strategy a factor supporting business growth in the post -
pandemic era.

Challenges and Strategies for Optimizing Digital Marketing
Despite its numerous benefits, digital marketing implementation still faces a number
of challenges. One major obstacle is the low level of digital literacy among MSMEs. Not all
business owners have adequate skills in managing social media, creating marketing content,
or understanding effective digital marketing strategies. Yuan Valentino Elim et al. (2024)
explain that limited digital knowledge and skills are factors that prevent some MSMEs from
utilizing digital marketing optimally. In addition to human resources, limited infrastructure

These conditions demonstrate that digital transformation requires not only the

increasing sales, expanding markets, and strengthening the competitiveness of MSMEs.
While various challenges remain in its implementation, the benefits far outweigh the
obstacles encountered. Therefore, continuous optimization of digital marketing is necessary
to enable MSMEs to develop sustainably and adapt to changes in the increasingly
technology-driven business environment.

SIMPULAN
Based on the results of the literature review, it can be concluded that digital
marketing has a significant impact on the growth of MSMEs in the post -pandemic era.
Utilizing various digital platforms such as social media, marketplaces, and websites has
been proven to increase sales, expand market reach, strengthen relationships with
consumers, and enhance business competitiveness. Furthermore, digital marketing also
makes it easier for MSMEs to conduct promotions more effectively and efficiently
compared to conventional marketing methods. These findings indicate that digital
marketing has become a crucial strategy supporting the growth and sustainability of
MSMEs in the face of increasingly technology -driven business changes. However, the
implementation of digital marketing still faces various challenges, such as low digital
literacy, limited human resources, unequal access to technology, and a lack of
understanding of effective digital marketing strategies. Therefore, ongoing support through
training, mentoring, and capacity building is needed for MSMEs to enable them to
optimally utilize digital technology. Collaboration between the government, educational
institutions, the private sector, and businesses is also necessary to accelerate digital
transformation in the MSME sector. Future research is expected to examine  the impact of
digital marketing on MSMEs using an empirical approach through surveys or field studies
in various business sectors. Furthermore, future research can explore the effectiveness of
each digital platform in improving MSME performance, thereby providing more specific
and applicable recommendations for businesses developing sustainable digital marketing
strategies.
