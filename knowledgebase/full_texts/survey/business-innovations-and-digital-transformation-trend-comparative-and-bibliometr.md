Abstract: The rapid development of digital technologies, digital transformation, business innovation, and the emergence of
such technologies as artificial intelligence, blockchain, the Internet of Things  provides significant potential for transforming
business processes and creating new innovative models. The trend analysis conducted in this article (using the Google Trends
tool) around the world for 2009 -2023 showed that public interest in “business innovation” was almost constant until 2021
(according to the GT Scale, it ranged from 0.25 to 0.5). After 2021, it increased (according to the GT Scale, it was more than
0.5). At the same time, public interest in “digital transformation” has significantly incre ased since 2014, reaching almost
maximum values in 2020-2023 (0.75 - 1.00 on the GT Scale). This confirms a growing need in society to acquire new knowledge
related to the ability of businesses to adapt to digital transformation, including through creating innovative developments.
Unlike trend analysis, bibliometric and comparative analyses identify trends in developing interest in this topic not among
ordinary citizens - users of the Google search platform ‒ but among scientists who publish the results of their research either
in monographs or in authoritative peer -reviewed scientific journals indexed by leading scientometric databases. The
comparative analysis conducted in the article using the Google Books Ngram Viewer (Google) tool, which displays the
frequency of references to relevant terms in English -language books for 2009 -2019 worldwide, showed similar trends: the
persistence of scholars' attention to business innovation, while their interest in digital transformation is growing: in 2009-2013
- a slight increase, in 2013-2019 ‒ a significant increase. In the bibliometric analysis, a five-level mechanism for limiting the
research sample was applied: only English-language and most recent (for 2018-2022) scientific articles indexed by the Scopus
database in the subject area of Business, Management and Accounting were subject to analysis. Based on these limitations, the
research sample for the keyword “digital transformation” was reduced from 12131 to 1700 papers, and for “business
innovation” from 865 to 70. The analysis of the published articles, their views and citations, carried out using the SciVal tool,
fully confirmed the conclusions drawn from the trend and comparative analysis results. In terms of digital transformation, the
leaders in terms of the number of papers and the number of views are Germany, Italy, the UK and the US; in terms of citations
- the UK, Germany, the US, and in terms of individual scientists - Brazil and Germany. As for the topic of business innovation,
the United States is the undisputed leader in all these parameters, followed by China and the United Kingdom. Some scientists
from Germany and Spain also attract the attention of the academic community (in terms of views and citations). Changes in
trends and research priorities in each of the studied topics were identified and described using SciVal (analysis of the reference
dynamics to the top 50 keywords in articles for 2018-2023). For example, in 2023, compared to 2022, scientific interest in issues
related to “value creation” (+61.5%), "incumbents" (+66.7%), “service economy” (+83.3%), “digital marketing” (+75.0%),
and “design thinking” (+150.0%) increased the most. VOSviewer software made it possible to cluster the scientific work by
thematic areas. The largest cluster on digital transformation relates this concept to small and medium -sized businesses and
technologies, and the largest cluster on business innovation relates it to the development of small and medium-sized enterprises
and the protection of property rights.

Introduction
Business innovation and digital transformation have become critical success factors in the organisational
environment. They give companies a competitive advantage by providing new opportunities to improve
processes, interact with customers and create new b usiness models. Given the constant changes in the
economic environment and growing competition, business innovation is becoming a critical success factor for
organisations in all economic sectors. In today's world, where technological progress is rapidly e volving,
digital transformation is becoming an integral part of the successful functioning of a business. The scientific
significance lies in that bibliometric research allows us to study scientific productivity, trends, and
development of certain scientific fields. Researchers can determine which digital transformation and business
innovation are relevant by analysing publications. Such research helps to identify important topics and
directions and develop strategies for further study. In addition, bibliom etric analysis can help identify key
authors who are actively working in the field of digital transformation and business innovation. This provides
an opportunity for collaboration, knowledge sharing, and the development of research networks. In addition,
such studies can reveal the geographical distribution and scientific focus of research in these areas.
This paper aims to systematically review the literature and identify the key thematic areas that dominate
research in these areas. Using bibliometric methods will allow getting an objective picture of the study and
identify the main areas of development in  these areas. The following questions are investigated using an
integrated approach that combines bibliometric analysis, trend analysis, and visualisation.
Who are the most influential and frequently cited authors in business innovation and digital transformation?
What are the key topic areas covered in research on business innovation and digital transformation?
Which countries are leaders in business innovation and digital transformation research?
How has the trend in research in these areas changed over the past ten years?
Literature Review
Business innovation is when an organisation introduces new procedures, services, or products, affecting
positive business change (Morkunas, Paschen, & Boon, 2019). The concept of “business innovation” is a part
of the broader meaning of the concept of “inn ovation”. The term “innovation” has different interpretations
used in scientific research. However, there are three main approaches to defining and understanding
innovation. The first approach, evolutionary, proposed by J. Schumpeter, considers innovation as a change or
update in a product, technology, system or method. According to this approach, innovation implies the
existence of innovations that are introduced to the market and lead to the improvement or change of existing
solutions (Schumpeter, 1934).
The second approach, known as process or dynamic , considers innovation as a process of implementing,
developing and using new solutions. In this context, innovation changes and improves a product or method in
the economic sphere (Tidd, 2001; Pavitt, 2003; Santos et al., 2004).  The third approach, known as the object
or static approach, proposed by Hippel et al., defines innovation as the result of an innovation process in the
form of a new technique (product), technology or new method introduced to the market. According to this
approach, innovation is viewed as a specific object resulting from the innovation process and can be presented
in the form of new products, technologies or methods (Hippel, 2006; Hippel, 2016).
The introduction of computerisation in the early 1990s opened the way for significant investigations in digital
technologies. One of the main challenges companies face is understanding the terms related to digital
transformation. This issue often creates confusion and misunderstanding of concepts, including digitisation,
digitalisation and digital transf ormation. For further research, it is necessary to differentiate the terms and
propose precise definitions conceptually. Discussions about understanding the se terms usually arise because
of their similarities and differences in various aspects (Yagub & Alsabban, 2023). The first term, digitisation,
describes the technical process of converting analogue data into digital. It is the first stage that creates the basis
for digitalisation. Digitalisation is used to describe all changes and transformations in an organisation's
business models reflected in the introduction and integration of digital technologies. Digitalisation aims to
increase business productivity and scalability (Ilcus, 2018). Digitalisation opens opportunities for creating
radically new products, services and innovations by combining various technological trends, such as cloud
computing, artificial intelligence, big data and others (Rachenger et a l., 2018). Tilson describes digitalisation
as the sociotechnical process of applying digitisation techniques to broader social and institutional contexts
that make digital technologies infrastructural, changing traditional business models (Tilson, 2010).

Digital transformation is not synonymous with digitalisation. Digital transformation requires a combination of
digitisation and digitalisation to improve or create new business models (Kathan et al., 2016; Kraus et al.,
2022). Saarikko defines digital transformation as follows: “Digital transformation is the socio-cultural process
of firms adapting to new organisational forms and skill sets required to remain viable and relevant in the digital
space” (Saarikko et al., 2020; Appio et al., 2021; Rego et al., 2021). Digital technologies facilitate the
digitalisation of business in all industries. Accordingly, improving business with the help of digital
technologies to enhance customer experience and engagement allows for business optimisation. It helps to
create entirely new ways of working. Conversely, a company may face fierce competition and be unable to
survive in today's market (Agustian et al., 2023; Ginting et al., 2023). Digitalisation has a significant impact
on the modern market and transforms it deeply, leading to the speed of change as one of its key characteristics.
This process is accompanied by a growing level of interconnectedness and constant change in physical, social
and digital (Urbach and Röglinger, 2019; Stich et al., 2020).
Modern companies need to be flexible and open -minded to adapt to these rapid changes (Hendriarto, 2021).
Research confirms that successful digital transformation requires companies to take a strategic approach and
focus on important aspects such as leaders hip, openness, collaboration and flexibility (Jacobi et al., 2018).
Companies that successfully implement digital technologies and work to create new business models can gain
a competitive advantage and ensure their sustainability in a rapidly changing digital environment.
Methodology, Theory and Conceptual Framework
Bibliometrics, a statistical method commonly employed in academic literature reviews, quantitatively assesses
scientific output (e.g., Baier -Fuentes et al., 2019; Goksu, 2021; Shi et al, 2022). In this study, we adopt a
bibliometric analysis to fulfil our research objectives, a methodology that has gained popularity in the field of
business and management studies (Zupic & Čater, 2015; Block and Fisch, 2020; Donthu et al., 2021; Mas-Tur
et al., 2020). To address our research question regarding digital transf ormation, we employ co -countries
analysis, citation analysis, and cluster analysis.
The research approach of this paper entails three steps:
➢ Conducting a comprehensive search in scientific digital libraries
➢ Extracting relevant data
➢ Preparing and analyzing the collected data
A comparative analysis was conducted using the Google Books Ngram  Viewer (Google) to study the
popularity of the concepts “business innovation ” and “digital transformation ” among authors. This tool
displays the frequency of the relevant terms in English-language books for the applicable period, expressed as
a ratio to the maximum number of mentions for the specified period. The analysis results of the popularity of
the conce pts “business innovation ” and “digital transformation ” published in English -language books
worldwide from 2009 to 2019 indicate their widespread use in the literature. Using Google Books Ngram
Viewer made it possible to assess the frequency of references to these concepts and their relative popularity
during the study period.

references during the study period. This growth was twofold: in the first phase, from 2009 to 2012, there was

should note that in 2013, the term  “digital transformation ” overtook “business innovation ” in terms of
frequency of mention and continued to lead until the end of 2019. This indicates that digital transformation is
of much greater interest to readers than business innovation. Given that demand drives supply, we can conclude
that digital transformation is a topical and exciting issue for readers compared to business innovation.
One can use the Google Trends analytical tool to analyse the popularity of terms among users of the Google
search network. This tool provides the relative popularity of a search query over a specified period, converting
the results into a 100-point scale. Thus, it allows you to compare two search queries regardless of relative data
and assess their popularity.

The rapid growth in popularity of the term “digital transformation” in recent years can be explained by several
factors. First, there has been rapid progress and increased availability of digital technologies, such as artificial
intelligence, cloud services, big data analytics and others. These technologies open up new bu siness
opportunities to optimise processes and improve products and services. Another possible factor contributing
to the growing popularity of digital transformation is the need to adapt to a rapidly changing business
environment. Companies face large-scale technological and social changes and must adapt to them to remain
competitive.
Digital transformation allows businesses to transform their business processes, management models and
communication strategies to meet the requirements of the new environment and maintain their competitive
advantage. In addition, successful digital transfo rmation implementations have been the subject of extensive

The importance of researching business innovations is primarily driven by the following factors:
➢ Changed consumer preferences. Modern consumers increasingly value innovative products and services.
They look for uniqueness, personalisation and high quality. This encourages businesses to introduce
innovative solutions to meet consumers' changing needs and expectations.
➢ Increased competition. The business environment becomes increasingly competitive, and businesses realise
that they must constantly innovate to maintain their market position. Competitive advantages, such as new
products, processes or business models, can be crucial for success in a fiercely competitive environment.
➢ Technological progress. The rapid development of technologies, such as artificial intelligence, augmented
reality, quantum computing, etc., is opening up new opportunities for innovation. Businesses use advanced
technologies to improve their processes, ensure efficiency and find new ways to grow.
➢ Changes in the regulatory environment. Government policies and regulatory changes can stimulate business
innovation. For example, government support for start -ups, research and development, and simplification
of procedures for introducing new technologies can create favourable conditions for innovative
development of enterprises.
Growth in data volumes. The large volumes of data that are accumulated in the modern world provide
businesses with the opportunity to analyse and gain valuable insights. Data analytics and machine learning
allow companies to identify new trends, understand  consumer needs, and make informed decisions based on
facts (Bin & Hui, 2021; Ta & Lin, 2023).
The obtained results emphasise the relevance and necessity of conducting a bibliometric analysis of “business
innovation” and “digital transformation”. Bibliometrics is a methodological tool that identifies and studies
quantitative patterns and trends in the scientific literature by analysing publication data and citations (Donthu
et al., 2021; Lim et al., 2022). Bibliometric analysis of “business innovation” and “digital transformation” is
essential in understanding their spread, development and impact on academic and practical fields. By analysing
scientific publications, the bibliometric approach allows us to identify the frequency of references to these
terms, their distribution over time, authors, and scientific journals (de Oliveira, 2019; Carrera -Rivera et al.,
2022).
This analysis has several purposes. First, it helps to determine the extent of interest in the topic among the
scientific community and shows trends in its development. This allows us to identify important research areas
and key researchers in business inn ovation and digital transformation. Secondly, bibliometric analysis helps
identify significant publications and papers that are highly cited and impact the development of the discipline
(Guendouzi et al., 2023). This allows us to identify key sources of knowledge and information that researchers
and practitioners can use for in -depth study of these terms. In addition, bibliometric analysis can serve as a
basis for developing strategies and recommendations for further research, implementation and development of
business innovation and digital transformation in organisations. It provides objective data and indicators that
help to understand which aspects of these concepts are most important and relevant for further research and
development.
The first step was to search for data in Scopus, which has the largest amount of information compared to other
databases. In addition, Scopus provides more detailed information on publications, including full titles of
articles, exact journal names, keywords, and a complete list of researchers' names (from the first to the eighth
author). Thus, th is database provides more detailed information on digital transformation trends than if the
sample were based on data from other sources.

Conclusions
When analysing the terms “digital transformation” and “business innovation” using the Google Ngram Viewer
and Google Trends tools, it was found that the term “business innovation ” has been growing steadily and
indicates a constant interest in business innovation among users of the Google search engine and book authors.
The term “digital transformation” has significantly increased in popularity recently. This indicates a growing
interest and understanding of the importance of digital transformation in the modern business environment.
Compared to “business innovation ”, “digital transformation ” has become more popular, indicating that
companies become more aware of the need to adapt to digital technologies and new business models.  The
bibliometric analysis of two samples of articles using the terms “digital transformation ” and “business
innovation” revealed a constant interest in digital transformation and confirmed the need for further research.
Business innovation has the potential for further development and expansion of knowledge in the field.  By
country, Germany, Italy, and the United Kingdom are the leaders in the number of scientific publications on
digital transformation. In turn, the most scientific publications related to the topic of business innovation was
found in the United States, China and the United Kingdom, which indicates a broad interest in this topic in the
scientific community. The study examined the authors of scientific papers with the highest number of views.
Frank Alejandro, Ayala Néstor, Lichtenthaler Ulrich, Hess Thomas, Kraus Sascha, and Ghezzi Antonio are
the authors whose works include digital transformation. Brazil, Germany and Italy stand out as countries with
high scientific productivity and impact. Among the authors of papers that contain digital transformation are
Leipold Sina, Petit -Boix Anna, Aragon Richard, Calcagno Anna Maria, and Onken James. The keyword
clustering maps from both samples were generated and analysed using VOSwiever.
Four main clusters were identified in the articles containing the keyword “digital transformation”. The first
cluster describes the general concepts of digital transformation and the objects affected by digital
transformation. The keywords collected in the second cluster consider digital transformation in terms of
Industry 4.0, its methodology and its impact on business processes. The third cluster focuses on the

clusters. This indicates closer connections in terms of other clusters' works rather than with the central term,

demand for artificial intelligence topics that have emerged in the last year.
Three main clusters were identified in the articles containing the keyword “business innovation”. The first
cluster describes the general concepts of innovation in terms of entrepreneurship and the functions it affects.
The keywords of the second cluster deal directly with business innovation. The third cluster focuses on the
role of government regul ation and its impact. In general, it is worth noting that keywords overlap with the
sample with the keyword “digital transformation ”, for example, innovation, technological innovation,
sustainable development, etc. Undoubtedly, this is because digital tran sformation can also be considered
business innovation as part of the field of innovation research in general. Despite this, the sample of articles
with the keyword “business innovation” includes a whole cluster of state regulation, so it can be assumed that
future research papers will investigate the impact of state policy and regulation on digital transformation processes.

innovation” appeared in scientific papers only in 2020 and is the newest keyword in the context of this sample
of articles. In general, the results confirm the growing dynamics of research.
