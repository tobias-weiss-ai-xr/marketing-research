 The study examines the planning of a digital data m anagement system named

analysis, including SWOT and competitor evaluation.  The proposed system

identity system for improved access and interactive  features. Major ﬁndings

marketing into a participatory "phygital" experience that boosts brand recall and
loyalty. The system enables organizers to analyze a udience interaction data in
real-time, providing actionable insights to reﬁne marketing messages for future
events. By bridging the gap between physical and di gital engagement, this
planning serves as a practical, scalable solution f or event organizers navigating
the digital transformation.

II.  Literature Review and Hypothesis Development

2.1.  Previous Studies

The integration of digital technology into the musi c and event industry has been widely studied,
highlighting a shift in how brands engage with audi ences. Dewatara and Agustin (2019) emphasize how
digital transformation has altered music distributi on and marketing, providing a foundation for using digital
touchpoints to reach broader audiences eﬃciently. W hile their study focuses on streaming platforms,
ALTAEXPERIENCE.ID applies this digital approach directly to the live event experience to streamline organizer-
attendee communication. Furthermore, Munawaroh and Nurjanah (2023) stress the importance of
understanding attendee preferences to build eﬀectiv e marketing communication strategies in music event s.
Sharma (2024) adds that digital marketing strategie s must continuously adapt to evolving consumer
behaviors, particularly the shift toward personaliz ed digital interactions. Amor et al. (2024) highlig ht the
strategic value of uniﬁed data collection in digita l industries, noting that organized data management  is key
to understanding consumer behavior and fostering lo yalty—a principle directly applied in the developme nt
of ALTAEXPERIENCE.ID.

2.3.  Marketing Communication

Marketing communication is a strategic process that  aims to convey messages from a brand to its
target audience to create strong relationships and support marketing goals. According to Arora (2024),
marketing communication is a means to inform, convi nce, and remind consumers regarding products or
services, either directly or indirectly. Modern mar keting communication theory emphasizes that
communication today is no longer just linear but in volves two-way interaction (Abaev, Sharkov, &
Aleshnikova, 2021).

2.4.  Marketing 6.0

Marketing 6.0 is an evolution of marketing strategies designed to answer the challenges of the digital
era and the phygital (physical-digital) generation (Kotler, Kartajaya, & Setiawan, 2023). It focuses o n creating
immersive consumer experiences through advanced tec hnology such as AR, VR, and metaverse. Marketing
6.0 involves ﬁve key elements: seamless transaction s, multisensory experiences, active consumer
participation, two-way interactions, and strong nar ratives.

2.5.  Integrated Marketing Communication (IMC)

Integrated Marketing Communication (IMC) is a proce ss of coordinating various marketing
communication tools to convey a consistent message to the target audience (Silviani & Darus, 2021). IMC aims
to create a uniﬁed and coherent consumer experience  across various communication channels. Modern IMC
emphasizes the importance of creating a holistic co nsumer experience and involves two-way interaction
between brands and consumers (Blakeman, 2023).

2.6.  Big Data

Integrated data collection oﬀers comprehensive insights by gathering various data points, increasing
the understanding of subjects like brand perception  and loyalty (Amor et al., 2024). Digitalization ha s
transformed event management, enabling organizers t o manage data eﬃciently through online registration ,
QR codes, and data analytics (Bladen et al., 2017).

2.7.  WhatsApp

WhatsApp oﬀers various features that can be integra ted with the ALTAEXPERIENCE.ID system to
support more eﬃcient communication. The WhatsApp Bu siness API allows for the automation of messages
such as ticket conﬁrmation, event reminders, and fe edback surveys. Statistics show that 95% of WhatsAp p
messages are opened and read within 5 minutes after  being received. This platform provides ﬂexibility for
users to interact with the system anytime and anywhere, supporting a better user experience during the event.
Furthermore, it ensures end-to-end encryption for c onsumer data security (Johns, Matamoros-Fernández, &
Baulch, 2023).

creating unique and meaningful experiences for part icipants (Raj, 2017). Technology has allowed organi zers

III.  Research Method

3.1.  Situational Analysis

Before implementing the ALTAEXPERIENCE.ID system, a  comprehensive situational analysis was
conducted to understand technical, logistical, and marketing environments. A primary challenge in musi c
events is managing crowd ﬂow and real-time interact ions, as manual registrations often cause long queu es
and data entry errors. ALTAEXPERIENCE.ID provides a  digital solution that ensures eﬃciency, utilizing QR
codes to minimize queues and gamiﬁcation to encoura ge engagement. To evaluate the system's viability, a
SWOT analysis was formulated:

a.  Strengths: Facilitates fast access without queues, enhances digital interaction (e.g., registrations,
polling, gamiﬁcation), and streamlines real-time da ta collection for preference analysis.
b.  Weaknesses: Highly dependent on strong internet inf rastructure, particularly in outdoor venues,
and requires signiﬁcant development and maintenance  costs.
c.  Opportunities: The digital event trend increases vi sitor loyalty through personalized experiences
and opens doors for strategic partnerships with lar ge brands.
d.  Threats: Potential technical disruptions during the  event and competition from events utilizing
similar technologies.

3.2.  Objectives

To ensure a successful implementation, the project' s goals were structured using the SMART
framework:

a.  Speciﬁc: Implement ALTAEXPERIENCE.ID to manage acce ss, enhance visitor interaction, and collect
real-time data at music events.
b.  Measurable: Achieve at least 50% visitor engagement  in digital activities (gamiﬁcation/booth visits)
and reduce venue entry/queue times by 80% using QR codes.

IV.  Result and Discussion

4.1.  Implementation Framework

The implementation of the ALTAEXPERIENCE.ID system is not merely a technological solution, but a
strategic manifestation of Integrated Marketing Com munication (IMC). The system operates on three core
pillars: interactive experience, digital gamiﬁcatio n, and data-driven marketing. By shifting communica tion
from a passive, one-way broadcast to an active, par ticipatory experience, attendees form a stronger
emotional connection with the brand. Furthermore, d igital gamiﬁcation serves as an interactive promoti onal
strategy that strengthens brand recall, aligning wi th Marketing 6.0 principles which combine technolog y
with human-centric experiences. All interactions ac t as data touchpoints, allowing organizers to under stand
audience behavior and personalize future marketing eﬀorts.

V.  Conclusion

From an academic perspective, the planning of the A LTAEXPERIENCE.ID digital system contributes
signiﬁcantly to the development of marketing commun ication studies, particularly regarding two-way
communication, digital experiences, and the utilization of data-driven technology. This project demonstrates
how experience-based approaches and real-time interactions can be eﬀectively applied within the context of
music events. It reinforces the importance of integrating technologies like the WhatsApp Business API and QR
Codes as tools to establish closer, more personaliz ed relationships between brands and their audiences .
Furthermore, this approach strongly aligns with the principles of Integrated Marketing Communication (IMC),
which emphasizes the necessity of consistent messag ing across all interaction touchpoints.  Practicall y,
ALTAEXPERIENCE.ID provides concrete solutions to co mmon challenges faced by event organizers, such as
slow registration processes, unstructured participant data, and a lack of two-way interaction. By implementing
QR Code and WhatsApp systems, the entry process bec omes faster and more convenient, while participant
data is collected automatically. The availability o f a real-time dashboard enables the organizing team  to
monitor the event seamlessly and make swift, data-backed decisions. From a brand perspective, personalized
