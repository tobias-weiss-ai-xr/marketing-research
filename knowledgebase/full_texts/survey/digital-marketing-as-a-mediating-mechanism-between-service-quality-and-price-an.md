2. METHOD
This study employed a quantitative explanatory research design to examine the relationships
among service quality, price perception, digital marketing, and patient satisfaction at RS Mitra Delima,
Malang Regency. The target population consisted of all patients who received healthcare services at the
hospital, including both inpatient and outpatient patients. A proportional random sampling technique
was used to ensure that each patient group had an equal opportunity to be included in the study. The
sample size was determined using the Slovin formula with a five percent margin of error , resulting in a

3. 6 Discussion
The findings of this study demonstrate that patient satisfaction in healthcare services is shaped by
an interplay of service quality, pricing perceptions, and digital marketing effectiveness. The significant
influence of service quality confirms that patie nt satisfaction remains fundamentally grounded in how
healthcare services are delivered. Dimensions such as reliability, responsiveness, assurance, empathy,
and tangible aspects of care collectively shape patients’ evaluations of healthcare experiences. Wh en
services are delivered consistently, staff respond promptly, and patients feel respected and secure,
satisfaction levels increase. This finding is consistent with prior studies in healthcare service
management, which identify service quality as a primar y determinant of patient satisfaction (Aiken et
al., 2018; Ampaw et al., 2020; Attar et al., 2020; Endeshaw, 2026) . The present results also align with
the SERVQUAL-based evidence reported by Indartono, (2024) who emphasize that perceived service
excellence fosters trust and positive patient attitudes.
In addition to service quality, price perceptions were found to significantly influence patient
satisfaction. This result indicates that patients evaluate healthcare services through a value -oriented
lens, balancing perceived benefits against financial sac rifice. When prices are perceived as fair ,
transparent, and proportional to service quality, patients tend to report higher satisfaction. This finding
supports earlier research suggesting that perceived price fairness enhances satisfaction in service
contexts, including healthcare (Jannah & Rachmat, 2025; Zhong & Moon, 2020). Furthermore, Dhaifina
& Santoso, (2025) argue that price fairness strengthens satisfaction particularly when it is aligned with
consistent service quality, reinforcing the value-for-money framework that underpins patient decision-
making.
Among all predictors examined, digital marketing emerged as the most dominant factor influencing
patient satisfaction. This finding highlights the increasingly strategic role of digital platforms in shaping
patient experiences. Digital marketing channels s uch as websites, social media, and online
communication tools enable patients to access information efficiently, interact with healthcare
providers, and develop trust before and after service encounters. Similar conclusions have been
reported by Cook et al., (2021)  and Cheung et al., (2021) , who note that digital engagement enhances
transparency and patient empowerment. Studies by Ulfitriana et al., (2025) and Arizka & Wijaya, (2025)
further confirm that effective digital marketing contributes significantly to patient satisfaction by
improving accessibility, interactivity, and perceived credibility of healthcare services.

The mediating role of digital marketing provides deeper insight into the mechanisms through which
service quality and price influence patient satisfaction. The results indicate that high -quality services
and fair pricing yield stronger satisfaction outcome s when these attributes are effectively
communicated through digital channels. This finding aligns with Gajewska et al., (2019), who argue that
digital communication enhances customers’ perceptions of service value by translating operational
performance into accessible and meaningful information. Insani et al., (2017) and Sumanti & Mandagi,
(2022) similarly report that digital platforms amplify the impact of service quality by reinforcing
positive perceptions and facilitating customer engagement.
Likewise, the indirect effect of price on patient satisfaction through digital marketing underscores
the importance of transparent and informative digital communication. Digital platforms allow
healthcare providers to explain pricing structures, highlight service benefits, and reduce uncertainty
related to healthcare costs. This finding is consistent with the work of Pradani et al., (2018), who found
that clear digital price information improves patient satisfaction, as well as Sembhodo, (2022)  and
Ulfitriana et al., (2025), who emphasize that digital transparency strengthens perceived price fairness.
Dhaifina & Santoso, (2025)  further suggest that digital promotion and value communication play a
critical role in aligning price perceptions with patient expectations.
Overall, the findings suggest that patient satisfaction in healthcare is a multidimensional outcome
shaped by both traditional service factors and modern digital interactions. Service quality and price
remain essential foundations of satisfaction; however, digital marketing acts as a strategic enabler that
translates these foundations into positive patient perceptions. This study contributes to healthcare
management literature by demonstrating that digital marketing is not merely a promotional instrument
but a mediating mechanism that integrates service performance and value communication. By aligning
service excellence, fair pricing, and effective digital engagement, healthcare institutions can foster
stronger patient satisfaction, trust, and long-term loyalty.
4. CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
This study examines the relationships between service quality, price perception, digital marketing,
and patient satisfaction at RS Mitra Delima, Malang Regency. The results indicate a significant positive
relationship between service quality and patient sa tisfaction. This suggests that dependable services,
the responsiveness of healthcare personnel, empathy, and sufficient facilities are crucial in determining
patients' evaluations of healthcare service quality.
Furthermore, the data demonstrate that price perception substantially affects patient satisfaction.
When patients believe that the costs of healthcare services are equitable and align with the value they
receive, their satisfaction levels rise. This unders cores the significance of transparent and just pricing
strategies within healthcare services.
In addition, digital marketing significantly affects patient satisfaction. Digital platforms offer
hospitals a way to share information more readily, streamline communication, and foster greater patient
involvement. Consequently, strategic digital marketing efforts can positively influence how patients view
the quality of care they receive and the clarity of pricing.
Furthermore, digital marketing plays a mediating role in the relationship between service quality,
price perception, and patient satisfaction. This result indicates that digital marketing strengthens the
influence of service quality and price perception on  patient satisfaction. These results indicate that
hospitals should incorporate service quality enhancement, transparent pricing models, and strategic
digital marketing initiatives to improve patient satisfaction and sustain a competitive edge within the
healthcare industry.
