1. Introduction
The rapid advancement of digital technology has fundamentally transformed consumer
purchasing behaviour and the global business landscape. One of the most significant outcomes of
this transformation is the rapid expansion of e -commerce, which enables consumers to purchase
products and services regardless of geographical and temporal limitations. In Indonesi a, this
transformation has been accelerated by the increasing number of internet users, reaching
approximately 229 million people, alongside substantial growth in online transactions and mobile
commerce (Haryanto, 2025). Consequently, businesses across various industries, particularly the
food and beverage (F&B) sector, have increasingly adopted digital marketing strategies to remain
competitive within the evolving digital ecosystem (Faza & Deslia, 2024; Irawanda, 2025).

 The rapid growth of e-commerce has intensified competition among
food and beverage brands, encouraging marketers to combine viral
marketing strategies with celebrity endorsements to influence
consumer purchasing decisions. However, limited studies have
simultaneously examined the relative influence of these two
marketing approaches in the context of local F&B brands operating
through e -commerce platforms. Therefore, this study investigates
the influence of viral marketing and celebrity endorsers on purchase
decisions for Bittersweet by Najla products. A quantitative survey
was co nducted with 479 respondents, and the data were analyzed
using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM).
The findings reveal that viral content information, celebrity
attractiveness, trustworthiness, and expertise significantly influence
purchase decisions, whereas viral content credibility and usefulness
show no significant effects. These findings demonstrate that
consumers' purchase decisions are driven more by the persuasive
and emotional characteristics of celebrity endorsers than by  the
perceived credibility or usefulness of viral content. This study
contributes to digital marketing communication literature by
providing empirical evidence that the effectiveness of integrated
marketing strategies in e -commerce depends more on emotiona l
engagement and endorser quality than on informational aspects of
viral content, offering practical implications for developing more
effective promotional strategies for digital-based local brands.

The expansion of e-commerce has also altered the way consumers make purchasing decisions.
Rather than relying solely on traditional advertising, consumers increasingly seek product
information through social media, electronic word -of-mouth (e -WOM), online reviews, and
recommendations from public figures before making purch ase decisions (Loupiac & Le Nagard,
2024; Putra & Darma, 2021 ). This behavioural shift has strengthened the importance of digital
marketing communication strategies that are capable of generating engagement, trust, and
persuasive influence in online environments. Among these strategies, viral marketing and celebrity
endorsement have become two of the most widely adopted approaches for influencing consumer
purchase decisions (Bukit et al., 2023; Kholiq & Sari, 2021; Nggilu et al., 2019).
Viral marketing refers to a digital marketing strategy that encourages consumers to voluntarily
disseminate marketing messages through social networks and online platforms, creating a rapid
electronic word -of-mouth effect (Zhang & Huang, 2022 ). The effectiveness of viral marketing
depends not only on the dissemination of information but also on the informativeness, credibility,
and usefulness of the shared content. Previous studies ha ve suggested that viral marketing
positively influences consumer purchase decisions because it increases product awareness and
facilitates information exchange among consumers (Maulida et al., 2022). However, contradictory
findings have also been reported. Parmariza (2019), for instance, found that viral marketing did not
significantly influence purchase decisions because the products examined were already familiar to
consumers and lacked viral characteristics. Similarly, Angelica & Tj  (2025) emphasized that
although social media marketi ng facilitates promotional activities, its effectiveness depends on
consumers' perceptions of message relevance and engagement. These findings indicate that the
influence of viral marketing remains inconclusive and may vary across different industrial contexts.
Another communication strategy that has attracted considerable attention is celebrity
endorsement. Celebrity endorsers utilize their popularity, credibility, attractiveness, and expertise
to strengthen persuasive communication and influence consumer  perceptions toward a brand
(Parmariza, 2019). According to the Source Credibility Theory, consumers are more likely to accept
persuasive messages delivered by communicators perceived as trustworthy and knowledgeable.
Previous studies generally reported that celebrity endorsement positively affects p urchase
decisions because consumers tend to develop stronger trust in products promoted by well-known
public figures (Alifyanti et al., 2022). Nevertheless, inconsistent findings remain evident. Parmariza
(2019) demonstrated that celebrity endorsement did not significantly influence purchasing
decisions because consumers focused more on product quality than on the celebrity promoting it.
These inconsistencies suggest that the effectiveness of celebrity endorsers m ay depend on
contextual factors, including product characteristics, industry type, and consumer behaviour.
Although viral marketing and celebrity endorsement have been extensively examined in
previous studies, the empirical findings remain inconsistent.  Kristanto et al. (2017);Kholiq & Sari
(2021) found that both variables significantly influenced purchase decisions. In contrast, Karisma
& Hanifah (2023) reported that viral marketing had no significant effect, whereas celebrity
endorsement remained significant. Meanwhile, Katiandagho & Syarif Hidayatullah  (2023)
concluded that neither viral marketing nor celebrity endorsement significantly influenced purchase
decisions when examined individually. These contradictory findings reveal an important research
gap, suggesting that the effectiveness of digital marketing communication strategies may differ
depending on the industrial contex t, product characteristics, and consumer behaviour. Therefore,
further empirical investigation is necessary to clarify these inconsistencies, particularly within
rapidly growing digital industries (Azhar et al., 2023).

The Indonesian culinary industry provides an appropriate context for addressing this research
gap. In recent years, the dessert box segment has experienced remarkable growth, largely driven
by social media exposure, influencer marketing, and changing consumer lifestyles. One of the most
prominent brands within this industry is Bittersweet by Najla, which has successfully developed its
market through intensive digital marketing communication, electronic word -of-mouth, and
collaborations with celebrity endors ers across multiple social media platforms. The substantial
number of online product reviews and millions of views generated by celebrity content indicate
that both viral marketing and celebrity endorsement play central roles in building consumer
awareness and stimulating purchasing intentions. Consequently, Bittersweet by Najla represents a
relevant empirical setting for examining the effectiveness of contemporary digital marketing
communication strategies.
This study offers several contributions to the digital marketing communication literature. First,
it addresses the inconsistent findings reported in previous studies by simultaneously examining the
influence of viral marketing and celebrity endorsement on purchase decisions within a single
research mode l. Second, unlike earlier studies that primarily focused on conventional retail
products or other industries, this research investigates the rapidly growing digital culinary industry
represented by Bittersweet by Najla, thereby extending the empirical cont ext of digital marketing
communication research. Third, this study employs contemporary measurement dimensions of
viral marketing, including Viral Content Informativeness, Viral Content Credibility, and Viral
Content Usefulness Singh et al. (2025), together with the dimensions of Attractiveness,
Trustworthiness, and Expertise for celebrity endorsers  (Andita et al., 2021 ). This integrated
approach provides a more comprehensive understanding of how informational and persuasive
communication jointly influence consumer purchase decisions in e-commerce environments.
Finally, the findings are expected to provide practical insights for digital marketers regarding the
relative effectiveness of viral marketing content and celebrity endorsement strategies in enhancing
consumer purchasing decisions.  Based on these considerations, this study aims to examine the
influence of viral marketing and celebrity endorsers on consumers' purchase decisions regarding
Bittersweet by Najla products through e-commerce platforms.

2. Method
Research Approach
This study employed a quantitative research approach with an explanatory research design to
examine the causal relationships between viral marketing, celebrity endorsement, and consumers'
purchase decisions. An explanatory design was selected because the study aims to test hypotheses
regarding the influence of independent variables on the dependent variable. The relationships
among variables were analyzed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS -
SEM), which is appropriate for simultaneously estimating complex relationships between latent
constructs and their indicators while accommodating non -normal data distributions and
prediction-oriented research objectives.
Type and Source of Data
Respondents are given questionnaires to complete in order  to collect quantitative data for this
study in numerical form. The original data used in this study came directly from individuals who fit
the predetermined research criteria. Data were collected through an online questionnaire
employing a five-point Likert scale, in which a score of 5 corresponds to “strongly agree” and a score
of 1 corresponds to “strongly disagree.” This scale is applied to systematically evaluate
respondents’ attitudes, perceptions, and assessments of each variable indicator.

Population and Sample
The population consisted of consumers who had purchased Bittersweet by Najla products
through e-commerce platforms. Since the exact population size could not be determined, purposive
sampling was employed. Respondents were selected based on the following criteria:
1. Have purchased Bittersweet by Najla products through Shopee, Tokopedia, or Lazada.
2. Have been exposed to Bittersweet by Najla's viral marketing content.
3. Have viewed celebrity endorsement content promoting Bittersweet by Najla.
4. Are between 21 and 25 years old.
5. Are active users of at least one e-commerce platform.
According to the minimum sample size recommendations for PLS -SEM, the required sample
should be at least five to ten times the largest number of structural paths or indic ators. A total of
479 valid questionnaires were successfully collected and analysed, exceeding the recommended
minimum sample size and ensuring sufficient statistical power.
Research Variables
This study includes one dependent variable, purchase decision, and two independent variables,
viral marketing and celebrity endorsement. The viral marketing variable is broken down into three
dimensions: Viral Content Information, Viral Content Credibility, and Viral Content Usefulness. The
celebrity endorse ment variable is characterized by three core dimensions: attractiveness,
trustworthiness, and expertise. The dependent variable, the purchase decision variable, measures
how customers choose and acquire Bittersweet by Najla items.  Each variable is transla ted into a
set of measurable indicators formulated on the basis of established theoretical frameworks and
relevant prior research.
Data Collection Technique
Primary data were collected through an online questionnaire distributed to respondents who
fulfilled the predetermined sampling criteria. The questionnaire employed a five-point Likert scale
ranging from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree) to measure respondents' perceptions of
each research variable. Prior to the main survey, the research instrument was evaluated through a
pilot test involving forty respondents to assess item validity and internal consistency. All
measurement items satisfied the validity requirements, while Cronbach's Alpha values exceeded
the recommended threshold of 0.70, indicating acceptable reliability.
Data Analysis Technique
Data analysis was conducted using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS -
SEM) with SmartPLS software. PLS-SEM was selected because it is suitable for predictive research
models involvi ng multiple latent variables and does not require multivariate normality
assumptions. The analysis consisted of two stages:
Measurement Model (Outer Model)
The outer model evaluation assessed the reliability and validity of the measurement model
through: I ndicator reliability using outer loading values (>0.70); Internal consistency reliability
using Cronbach's Alpha and Composite Reliability (>0.70); Convergent validity using Average
Variance Extracted (AVE) (>0.50); Discriminant validity using the Fornell –Larcker Criterion and
Heterotrait-Monotrait Ratio (HTMT).

After confirming that the measurement model met the required criteria, the structural model

The results demonstrate that viral content information positively and significantly influences
Purchase Decision (β = 0.263, t = 3.841, p < 0.001) in Table 7. This finding indicates that consumers
are more likely to purchase Bittersweet by Najla products when viral content provides clear,
relevant, and attractive information. In the context of e -commerce, informative content reduces
information asymmetry and helps consumers evaluate produc ts before making purchasing
decisions. This finding is consistent with the concept of electronic word-of-mouth (e-WOM), which
explains that online information shared among consumers facilitates purchasing decisions by
reducing uncertainty (Romadhoni et al., 2023). It also supports previous studies by Setiawan et al.
(2023); Tungki & Balqiah (2023), who found that informative e -WOM significantly enhances
consumers' purchase decisions. These findings suggest that informative viral content functions as
an effective digital marketing communication strategy by strengthening consumers' confidence and
encouraging purchasing decisions.
The results reveal that Viral Content Credibility does not significantly influence Purchase
Decision (β = −0.051, t = 0.937, p = 0.349). Although consumers perceive viral content as credible,
credibility alone is insufficient to directly encourage purchasing decisions. From a digital marketing
communication perspective, consumers are simultaneously exposed to multiple information
sources, including online reviews, influencer recommendations, and user -generated content.
Consequently, purchasing decisions are inf luenced by a combination of informational and
emotional factors rather than by credibility alone. This finding is consistent with Chen et al. (2021),
who reported that e -WOM credibility mainly shapes consumer attitudes rather than directly
influencing purchasing decisions. Likewise, Kadang & Berlianto (2022);Amarullah et al.  (2022)
found that credibility primarily influences consumer trust and perceived usefulness, which
subsequently affect purchase intention. Therefore, credibility should be viewed as a
complementary communication attribute rather than the primary determinant of purchasing
decisions.
The findings indicate that Viral Content Usefulness does not significantly influence Purchase
Decision (β = 0.087, t = 1.536, p = 0.125). Although respondents considered the viral content useful,
usefulness alone was insufficient to stimulate actual purchasing decisions. One possible explanation
is that consumers of food products are more strongly influenced by visual appeal, emotional
engagement, and viral trends than by functional info rmation. This finding differs from Astuti and
Pratama & Rahmawati (2023), who reported that perceived usefulness positively affects purchase
decisions in e -commerce. However, it is consistent with Romadhoni et al. (2023), who suggested
that useful information tends to influence purchase intention indirec tly rather than directly
affecting purchase decisions.

These findings imply that, in digital marketing communication, useful information should be
integrated with persuasive and emotionally engaging content to maximize its effectiveness.  The
results demonstrate that Attractiveness positively and significantly influences Purchase Decision
(β = 0.156, t = 2.615, p = 0.009). This finding supports the Source Attractiveness Model, which
explains that attractive communicators are more effective in capturing cons umers' attention and
creating positive attitudes toward promoted products. The finding is consistent with Wiedmann &
von Mettenheim (2021);Sokolova & Kefi (2020);Lou & Yuan (2019), who reported that attractive
influencers and celebrity endorsers positively influence consumer responses and purchasing
behaviour. For Bittersweet by Najla, visually attractive celebrity endorsers suc cessfully enhance
product appeal and strengthen the effectiveness of digital marketing communication.
The findings reveal that Trustworthiness positively and significantly influences Purchase
Decision (β = 0.138, t = 2.197, p = 0.028). This result is consi stent with Source Credibility Theory,
which argues that trustworthy communicators increase audience acceptance of persuasive
messages. Consumers are more likely to trust recommendations from endorsers who are perceived
as honest, authentic, and reliable. T his finding is supported by Sokolova & Kefi (2020);Kim & Kim
(2021);Djafarova & Rushworth  (2017), who found that trustworthiness strengthens consumer
confidence and positively affects purchasing behaviour. Therefore, trustworthy celebrity endorsers
play an essential role in improving the effectiveness of digital marketing communication.
The results indicate that Expertise positively and significantly influences Purchase Decision (β =
0.247, t = 3.828, p < 0.001). This finding supports Source Credibility Theory, which emphasizes that
consumers perceive knowledgeable and experienced endorsers as more persuasive and credible.
Consumers are more likely to trust product recommendations delivered by endorsers who
demonstrate relevant expertise. This finding is consistent with Sokolova & Kefi (2020);Lou & Yuan,
(2019);Wiedmann & von Mettenheim (2021), who concluded that endorser expertise significantly
enhances consumer evaluations and purchasing decisions. In the context of Bittersweet by Najla,
expertise enables celebrity endorsers to communicate product quality more convincingly, thereby
increasing consumers' confidence in purchasing the product.
Taken together, these findings indicate that consumers’ purchase decisions are influenced more
strongly by informative viral content and the characteristics of celebrity endorsers than by the
credibility or usefulness of viral content alone. Viral Content Information, Attractiveness,
Trustworthiness, and Expertise signifi cantly influence purchase decisions, while Viral Content
Credibility and Viral Content Usefulness do not show a significant effect. This suggests that effective
digital marketing communication for Bittersweet by Najla requires more than simply providing
credible or useful information. The content needs to deliver relevant information while also
creating an appealing and convincing communication experience through the characteristics of the
endorser. In this context, celebrity endorsers who are attractive, t rustworthy, and knowledgeable
can make the information presented in viral content more persuasive and encourage consumers to
make purchasing decisions. These findings highlight that communication effectiveness in
Indonesia’s digital culinary industry depen ds on the combination of informative content and
persuasive endorsement, rather than on a single communication attribute.

4. Conclusion
This study examined the influence of viral marketing and celebrity endorsement on consumers'
purchase decisions for Bittersweet by Najla products through e-commerce platforms. The findings
indicate that the dimensions of viral marketing do not contribute e qually to consumer purchasing
behaviour. Among the viral marketing dimensions, only Viral Content Information positively and

significantly influences purchase decisions, suggesting that informative content helps consumers
understand product value and reduc es uncertainty during online purchasing. In contrast, Viral
Content Credibility and Viral Content Usefulness do not significantly affect purchase decisions,
indicating that consumers are not solely influenced by the credibility or usefulness of viral content.
Furthermore, all three dimensions of celebrity endorsement, Attractiveness, Trustworthiness, and
Expertise, positively and significantly influence purchase decisions, demonstrating that consumers
are more responsive to persuasive communication delivered by credible and appealing endorsers.
From a theoretical perspective, this study contributes to the digital marketing communication
literature by demonstrating that emotional and persuasive communication through celebrity
endorsers exerts a stronger influ ence on purchase decisions than rational evaluations of viral
content. These findings extend previous research by showing that the effectiveness of digital
marketing communication depends not only on the quality of information but also on the
communicator's ability to establish trust, attractiveness, and expertise in highly competitive e -
commerce environments.
From a managerial perspective, the findings suggest that digital businesses, particularly those
operating in the food and beverage industry, should prioritize the development of informative viral
content while simultaneously collaborating with celebrity endorsers who possess strong
attractiveness, trustworthiness, and expertise. Rather than focusing solely on increasing the
credibility of promotional content, companies should design integrated communication strategies
capable of creating emotional engagement and strengthening consumer confidence toward their
products. This study is subject to several limitations. First, the research focused exclusively on
consumers of Bittersweet by Najla, which may limit the generalizability of the findings to other
industries or product categories. Second, the study examined only viral marketing and celebrity
endorsement without considering other factors that may influ ence purchase decisions, such as
brand image, perceived value, consumer trust, or purchase intention. Therefore, future research is
encouraged to investigate additional variables, employ different industrial contexts, and examine
potential mediating or mod erating variables to provide a more comprehensive understanding of
consumer purchasing behaviour in digital marketing communication.
