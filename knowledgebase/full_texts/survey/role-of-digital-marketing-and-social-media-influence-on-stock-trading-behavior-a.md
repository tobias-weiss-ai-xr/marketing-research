ABSTRACT
The current research paper studies the effects of digital marketing as well as social media on stock trading behavior
amongst young investors (aged 18–35). In this case, convenience sampling method was used alongside the use of
a survey to obtain data from 200 participants. Inferential and descriptive statistics were used to analyze the data.
According to the research findings, 75% of respondents stated that they spend 2–4 hours per day using social
media sites, while 50% claimed that they view digital marketing advertisement posts at least once per day. The
most popular websites that were identified as effective sources for getting financial news include Instagram (85
users) as well as YouTube (75 users). The correlation analysis has proved that there is a positive relationship (r ≈
0.62) between social media usage and stock trading frequency, implying that with an increase in social media
usage, the frequency of transactions increases. Also, 60% of the participants confirmed that social media is highly
influential regarding their investment decisions.

1. Introduction
The quick advancement of technological innovations
has greatly changed how financial markets function.
It has especially changed how young people trade
stocks within the market [1]. The advancements in
technology have increased the number of
smartphones, fast internet connections, and easy-to-
use trading platforms, increasing participation in the
stock markets by people [2]. Digital marketing is one
of the essential ways through which the changes have
been brought [3]. By providing investors with
targeted information about financial products and
services, advertisements, and financial information,
young people get exposure to stock trading much
earlier [4].
Social media sites have become powerful tools that
change how young people invest their money [5].
Social media sites, such as Twitter, Instagram, and
YouTube, are used to disseminate information on
financial products and services [6]. They are used to
share information on what is trending in the stock
markets and other financial products. Information on
the stock market, expert opinion, and personal
accounts of how people trade their money is shared
on social media sites [7].
The use of digital marketing tactics in terms of
personalized advertisement, algorithms, and real-
time notifications is another way of increasing
investor participation through capturing users'
attention [8]. In addition, there are gamification
features that include bonuses, scores for investors,
and easy interfaces [9]. While all these help increase
participation, they could possibly motivate novice
investors to make irrational decisions in their trading
operations [10].
In addition, some emerging trends within the online
community, especially those associated with social
media, have shown how powerful the internet can be
when it comes to influencing markets [11]. FOMO,
the fear of missing out, is one such trend whereby
many young traders do not conduct any kind of
analysis based on fundamentals or technicality but
simply follow what is viral.
It is due to such changing trends that there arises a
need to investigate the effects of digital marketing
and social media on stock trading by young investors.

2. Literature review
Some of the recent literatures related to this study are
discussed as follows,
Awad et al. (2025) investigated the influence of
social media on the behaviors of the investors in the
Egyptian stock market. In their study which used
survey from 300 investors, they identified that social
media increases trading activities, herding effect, and
investor overconfidence. From their regression
model, it was established that investors usually
respond reactively after reading information in social
media. The study concludes that social media has a
great impact on investment decisions especially in
emerging markets, thus the need for investor
education.
The researchers Junaidi & Nurhidayah (2023)
researched Indonesian young investors to determine
the influence of social media on trading activities by
considering capital market literacy as the mediating
variable. According to their findings based on the
sample size of 314 respondents, social media has a
positive impact on trading activities. Nevertheless,
the presence of financial literacy enhances the
process of making rational decisions when investing.
The study by Zaki and Tubastuvi (2025) examined
the mediation role played by capital market literacy
regarding social media and financial motivation on
young investors in Indonesia. Based on a sample size
of 218 participants and an SEM-PLS technique, it
was revealed that social media and financial
motivation significantly affected literacy levels.
Capital market literacy had the highest impact on
investment behavior. Moreover, it was found that
capital market literacy mediated the effect of social
media on investment behavior.
In their study, Jokhu & Ilmu (2023) examined the
effect of digital influencers on Generation Z
investment intentions by applying the Theory of
Planned Behavior. It was observed that digital
influencers have the greatest effect on shaping the
attitudes, subjective norms, and perception of
behavioral control among the generation. An
individual's exposure to social media enhances
his/her intention to make investments in stocks.
Effect of social media on investment decisions was
also studied by Atoom et al. (2021). The impact of
social media on the investment decisions of
individuals was assessed using a sample of 150
investors in the Amman Financial Market. 60% of
the participants were found to depend upon social
media as a source of information about financial
matters. Facebook was used the most among all
digital media. Analysis revealed that there is a
significant correlation between the use of social
media and the investment de cisions made by
investors.
In this study, Antczak (2024) analyzed the effect of
digital marketing and social media marketing
practices on customer buying behavior. According to
the findings, online advertisements, influencer
marketing, and customer reviews have a great deal of
influence on customer purchases. Through their use,
consumers are able to become more aware of the
brand and engage themselves with products. This
research shows that digital marketing techniques are
quite influential in shaping the behavior of users.
Amran et al. (2025) analyzed the effect of digital
literacy, financial literacy, and social media use on
cryptocurrency investment behavior. According to
their results based on SEM, higher levels of digital
literacy have a positive impact on comprehension of
financial information by investors. Social media
usage is very important in determining investment
trends, especially for millennials and Gen Z. Higher
levels of financial literacy help reduce risks and
make better decisions.
In their study of the impact of motivation and
technology on the behavior of young adult internet
financial traders, Sharif and Naghavi (2021) used an
integrated model based on TPB, TAM, and Flow
Theory. Intrinsic and extrinsic motivators had a
considerable impact on investment intentions.

2.1 Challenges
The first challenge of this research would be the
increasing level of complexity in comprehending the
behavioral pattern of investors due to the advent of
technology and social media platforms. Social media
influences younger generations' decisions to invest
significantly, and they can get affected quickly by
new trends on platforms like Instagram, YouTube,
and Twitter. Thus, separating rational behavior from
emotional and psychological factors becomes
difficult when dealing with investors' data. Also,
there could be an increase in the prevalence of
misinformation and unreliable pieces of information
circulating on social media, which could further
affect the reliability of data collection. Besides, one
of the critical challenges of the research would be
measuring the influence of social media platforms
and other subjective variables such as
overconfidence and herding behavior. There might
be some issues regarding sampling biases since the
researcher will use convenient sampling methods and
online questionnaires. Respondents' responses may
be biased since they try to give socially desirable
answers rather than their true responses. It would also
become difficult for researchers to find patterns of
influence from digital marketing techniques and
algorithms. However, it is possible to address the
problem using a carefully designed questionnaire and
validated statistical models.
3. Materials and methods
Research design serves as the general structure that
helps to conduct scientific investigation in an
organized way. A descriptive and analytical research
design will be utilized to explore the impact of digital
marketing and social media on the behavior of
younger stock traders. In particular, description can
provide information regarding the frequency of using
different social media platforms as well as exposure
to certain content of digital marketing. Moreover,
investors' demographic features, their age, education,
and investment experience are also included into the
scope of the description. Analyzing, in turn, allows
studying relations between variables and assessing
how strongly a factor influences certain decision-
making processes.
Some hypotheses should be formed in accordance
with the objectives of the research. One of them
might be the following: "Social media has a
significant impact on stock trading behavior".
Moreover, there could be other hypotheses about the
influence of digi tal advertisement or
recommendations of influencers, and so on. In
general, such research design combines the
advantages of qualitative and quantitative
approaches and provides more reliable data and
results of the study.
3.1 Questionnaire Design
Questionnaire serves as an important instrument for
gathering primary data. Structured questionnaire
aims to capture measurable information from
participants. It makes use of Likert Scale in
measuring attitudes and perceptions about digital
marketing and social media influence on stock
trading. Responses can easily be quantified and
statistically analyzed using this method.
The questionnaire is subdivided into distinct parts.
Section one comprises questions on demographics
that include age, sex, educational attainment, and
income level of participants. The second part is
concerned with social media use behavior such as
frequency of visits, purpose, and platform. In the
third part, digital marketing influences are
considered by determining the degree of their
exposure. Section four consists of questions related
to stock trading behavior.
Questions are carefully constructed in an objective
manner to ensure that there is no confusion regarding
the answers. In some instances, it can even be
subjected to a pilot test to verify its accuracy and
effectiveness. A good questionnaire leads to
improved data and relevant information for the
purposes of the research.

A key stage in making sure that the findings from the
research are valid and accurate is sampling. For this
research, either convenience sampling or purposive
sampling will be employed to identify those people
who are actively involved in the business of stock
trading and regularly use digital platforms. Such
techniques will work perfectly for the research since
it seeks to find the views of those people who have
been exposed and experienced with stock trading.
This research will focus on the young generation
aged between 18 and 35 years as they tend to interact
a lot with online marketing tools such as social media
sites. Such individuals are also actively involved in
online trading and therefore form the best t arget
sample for the research. Respondents will be reached
through various online mediums, including social
media groups and trading sites.
The size of the sample will be within the range of 100
and 300 respondents. This will not only be realistic
but also offer enough information for analysis.
While there might be issues with the possibility of
bias when conducting convenience sampling, it
works well for the purpose of an exploratory and
behavioral study such as the one described here. With
the careful selection of individuals to participate, the
study will have data that is pertinent and relevant for
analysis.
3.3 Data Collection
The collection of information is done through both
primary and secondary data in order to make an
effective analysis. Primary data will be obtained via
conducting online surveys; for example, the use of
tools such as Google Forms makes the whole process
convenient, time-saving, and cost-effective. This
data collection technique is best suited to the study
because it reaches out to many young investors who
have access to the internet.
The online survey link will be distributed to the target
group through various online and offline channels
such as social media networks, email, and trading
forums. Instructions will be provided on how to
respond to the questionnaires in order to get reliable
information from the respondents.
Besides obtaining primary data, secondary data will
be collected from various sources such as books,
journals, research articles, and other publications.
This type of data is necessary for providing a good
basis for theoretical knowledge and analyzing results
obtained during the research.
The use of both primary and secondary data adds to
the validity of the study through cross-checking of
information. This process makes the study robust,
credible, and backed by both practical and theoretical
aspects of knowledge.
3.4 Data Processing
The first thing to do after collecting the data is to
clean it to prepare it for analysis. In data cleaning, the
data is organized to eliminate inconsistencies,
duplicates, and incomplete information. As a result,
only consistent and reliable information will be used
in the analysis.
Coding follows data cleaning, where responses from
the respondents are encoded systematically. For
instance, responses from the Likert scale, such as
strongly agree, may be coded as 5, while strongly
disagree would be coded as 1. The process makes it
easier to conduct the analysis.
After encoding the responses, the data should be
keyed into analysis software like Excel and SPSS.
Data analysis tools allow researchers to arrange data
in tabular form to analyze the information. It is
essential to label all the variables and categories
appropriately.
Data processing is an important stage that prepares
the data for analysis. Therefore, the researcher should
ensure that it is done well to facilitate successful
analysis of the information.
4. Analysis
The results section entails the statistical data analysis
for information gathered from young investors
concerning the impact of digital marketing and social
media on their stock trading habits. The data analysis
involves the use of descriptive statistics, correlations,
and graphs to analyze the investor's behavioral
trends. Some of the critical factors considered
include social media activity, exposure to digital
marketing, preferred social platforms, and the level
of trading frequency. There is clear evidence that the
young investors have high levels of involvement
with online activities and platforms including
Instagram and YouTube.

5. Conclusion
According to the study, social media and digital
marketing affect the stock trading activities of
millennials to a great extent. It was found that a large
proportion of respondents (60%) were influenced by
social media posts during their investment-related
decisions while daily viewing digital advertisements
(50%) increased their participation in trading through
digital platforms. The positive relationship (r ≈ 0.62)
between the use of social media and trading reveals
that frequent interaction with the former results in
frequent interaction with the latter. Moreover,
Instagram and YouTube were identified as influential
digital platforms where a large amount of
engagement from young users takes place. At the
same time, personal research still plays a key role as
it holds the highest level of influence on decision-
making process (80%). Thus, it is important to note
that while the internet helps increase awareness
about various investments, it promotes impulsive
behavior in terms of choosing particular companies
to invest money in.
