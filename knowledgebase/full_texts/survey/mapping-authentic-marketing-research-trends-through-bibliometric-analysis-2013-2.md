Abstract
Authentic marketing is a widely debated subject in several industries, including gastronomy, tourism, and culture.
This study utilizes bibliometric analysis to identify the current state of the art in authentic marketing themes across
multiple scientific disciplines. Bibliometric research analyzes the progress of research on authentic marketing from
2013 to 2023 by examining data from the past 10 years. Bibliometric analysis is to identify and analyze research
trends across time, with the goal of generating new research ideas for future studies. Bibliometric research analyzes
the progress of research on authentic marketing from 2013 to 2023 by examining data from the past 10 years.
Based on bibliometric study, it is evident that the United States has a predo minant presence in publications about
legitimate marketing. In addition, the most commonly published type of article is the article, and the Journal of
Business Research has the highest scientific influence in terms of publication sources, as it has the largest number
of citations. Bibliometric analysis aims to identify and map the evolving trends in authentic marketing research,
such as perception, sustainability, brand authenticity, social media, influencer marketing, purchase intention, and
online social networking. This analysis can provide valuable insights and research ideas for future studies.
Keywords authentic, authentic marketing, bibliometrics, marketing, trends.
1. Introduction
The concept of authentic marketing is widely debated across numerous industries, including
gastronomy, tourism, and culture (Kadirov et al., 2014) . Authentic marketing refers to the
strategic approach used by brands to effectively connect with their target market by promoting
their fundamental values, vision, and mission. This is done in order to create a genuine and
meaningful connection with customers (Marks & Prinsloo, 2015).
When executing authentic marketing, brands must have a clear understanding of their offerings,
their values and principles, and the specific target group they are catering to. Brands must also
be aware of the aspects that customers like about the company, the things that customers
prioritize, and the platforms where customers allocate their time both online and offline. After
obtaining this information, brands may generate genuine content and campaigns that genuinely
correspond to both brand values and customer values (Koontz, 2010).
Authentic marketing refers to a marketing strategy implemented by communities,
organizations, and business sectors with the aim of establishing a culture, objectives, and values
that are rooted in the pre -existing local knowle dge and wisdom. Authentic marketing is the

2. Literature Review
Authentic marketing is a strategic method employed by brands to actively connect with
audiences, establish human relationships, and ensure that customers feel acknowledged and
comprehended (Dwivedi & McDonald, 2018). Products/services are deemed authentic due to
their capacity to encapsulate genuine cultural values, as opposed to manufactured culture or
anything that is prevalent among the general populace. Authentic products/services are
marketed in t he same manner as other offerings and must be tailored to suit the consumer's
tastes.
The primary means of validating authentic marketing are through difference and
traditionalization aspects (Koontz, 2010). Producers and consumers consider products/services
to be legitimate when they originate from sources outside the prevailing general culture, such
as foreign influences, minorities, and subcultures. These sources are referred to as
differentiating factors. Traditionalization is achieved by establishing  a strong link between
products/services and consumers' historical perspectives  (Murshed et al., 2023) . Marketers
strategically create items and services that create a perception or illusion, enabling consumers
to experience a sense of nostalgia or revisit prior memories. The assessment and recognition of
genuineness by customers are significant factors in the process of traditionalization. Both
producers and marketers are engaged in the process of verifying the authenticity of their
products/services, wh ile consumers participate in evaluating their purchases (Hermawan,
2022).
Brands utilize distinguishing elements to amplify the uniqueness of their products/services and
fulfill consumers' curiosity about the unknown. Typically, brands differentiate them selves
through two primary methods: by positioning themselves as apart from mainstream society, and
by emphasizing their representation of genuine local culture, free from broader commercial
influences (Matos & Barbosa, 2018).
Brands cultivate a sense of  affinity by saying that their products/services respect traditions or
cultural heritage (Södergren, 2021). Brands do not create a division between producers and
customers, but rather engage people in the act of consuming the product or service. Consumers
have an active role in the development of tradition and the establishment of authenticity by
evaluating their expectations against the marketing tactics employed by firms (Nunes et al.,
2021). Brands consider consumer preferences while evaluating authentication methods.
Brands and consumers establish a sense of historical continuity by employing one or a
combination of the following five strategies: (1) preserving traditional production methods, (2)
establishing a link to the historical importance of th e physical location, (3) reconstructing
historic sites, (4) evoking a nostalgic sentiment for a way of life that is perceived as increasingly
rare, and (5) replicating the original formula. Authentic marketing is equivalent to two specific

3. Research Method
Bibliometric research utilizes the Scopus database to gather 2,033 data points using the
keywords "authenticity" OR "authentic" AND "marketing". Subsequently, the data was refined
by using the criterion of exclusively selecting publication data in the English language, resulting
in a total of 1,966 data points. Subsequently, the publishing time was narrowed down to the
period between 2013 and 2023, resulting in the acquisition of 1,443 data poin ts. The Scopus
database search was conducted on May 17, 2024, at 10:44 AM (GMT+7) and saved in Comma
Separated Value (.csv) format.
Bibliographic analysis is conducted using a framework consisting of three phases: data
collection, data visualization, and data analysis. The data collection phase involves data mining
by considering the article titles, abstracts, and keywords used with the Boolean expression
criteria "authenticity" OR "authentic" AND "marketing". The Scopus database is utilized as a
repository of several document types, including Article, Book, Book Chapter, Conference
Paper, Editorial, Erratum, Letter, Note, Retracted, Review, and Short Survey, spanning the time
period from 2013 to 2023.

The data visualization phase involves exporting and analyzing the data obtained from the

Jan van Eck and Ludo Waltman, who are affiliated with Leiden University in the Netherlands.

and debating the findings from the bibliometric analysis, completing a review of relevant
literature, and outlining future prospects for legitimate marketing research.
4. Finding and Discussion
Further explorat ion of scientific advancements in the field of authentic marketing is a
captivating area of study. According to the Scopus database, the number of publications on
authentic marketing was 245 in 2023 and 191 in 2020. The Scopus database documented the
highest number of publications throughout the period from 2013 to 2023, with Articles being
the most prevalent category, with 1,022 documents. This was followed by Book Chapters, with
215 documents, and Conferences. Out of a total of 1,443 documents, there are 115 papers. This
indicates a growing fascination among scholars with authentic marketing.

5. Conclusion
The field of authentic marketing is an evolving subject, with the quantity of publications
steadily rising each year till 2023. Discussions pertaining to genuine marketing encompass a
wide range of topics, including tourism, culinary arts, economics and commerce, digital
marketing, as well as human behavior and culture. According to bibliometric study, it is evident
that the United States has a significant presence in publications relevant to authentic marketing.
In addition, the most commonly published type of article is the article, and the Journal of
Business Research has the highest scientific influence in terms of publication sources, as it has
the largest number of citations. According to bibliometric trend mapping, the most recent areas
of focus in authentic marketing are perception, sustainability, brand authenticity, social media,
influencer marketing, purchase intention, and online social networking. This subject matter
could b e explored in forthcoming publications pertaining to authentic marketing.  Authentic
marketing research is primarily based on studies conducted in Europe and the United States.
