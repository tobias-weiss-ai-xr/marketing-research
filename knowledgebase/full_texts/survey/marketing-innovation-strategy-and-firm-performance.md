Introduction
Innovation and strategy have developed into the
main focus of marketing for firms in today's markets, which
are highly competitive and customer -driven, and have
undergone digital transformation [1, 2]. Firms have
transformed their value creation, communica tion and
delivery as a result of the growth of digital platforms,
analytics powered by artificial intelligence, social media,
mobile commerce, omnichannel communication and data -
driven personalization. Product promotion, price
competition and distribution efficiency are no longer
adequate tools to tackle the "new" challenges of firms needing
to respond to the changing expectations of customers, the
technological disruption, competitive imitation and market
uncertainty [3-5]. This is defined as marketing inn ovation as
a process of developing and applying new or substantially
changed marketing methods and customer interaction,
branding strategies, digital communication systems, service
delivery models and market sensing mechanisms, which
improve the performanc e of the organization [6, 7].

Abstract
This empirical study seeks to explore the impact of marketing innovation strategy on firm performance in the emerging
digital markets. The study builds on the concept of dynamic capability theory, market orientation theory, and the
innovation capability literature to explore t he impact of digital marketing capability, customer engagement orientation,
strategic marketing agility, and innovation capability on perceived firm's performance. For methodological purposes, a
simulated Cross-Sectional Survey was created of 412 marketing managers, SME owners and Digital Business executives.
The proposed relationships were tested using the reliability analysis, correlation analysis, multiple regression and
mediation testing and structural equation modeling logic. The results reveal that Di gital marketing capability, Strategic
marketing agility and customer engagement orientation directly affect the innovation capability and innovation
capability is a significant predictor of the firm performance. The findings also indicate that there is a p artial mediation
between marketing innovation strategy and firm performance, through innovation capability. The study is a valuable
addition to the already existing work in the literature of marketing strategy because it unites the concepts of digital
capability, agility and customer engagement into a single marketing innovation concept. Managerially, the findings point
to a move beyond campaign level digital adoption, and to building systematic routines of innovation, customer insight
systems based on data  and a cross-functional marketing capability and agile strategic decision-making capacity within
firms. The study finds marketing innovation to be not so much a promotion activity as a capability of the organization,
which has a decisive influence not only  on competitiveness, but also on creating customer value and sustainable
performance of the business in the markets going through digital transformation.

Literature Review and Hypothesis Development
Marketing innovation strategy is the intentional
application of innovativ e marketing methods, technologies,
marketing channels, customer interaction systems and data
usage and value communication to enhance the competitive
position and outcomes of the organization [43 -45]. It is
different from standard marketing because it dema nds
intentional and strategic transformation instead of the
repetition of current marketing campaigns. Marketing
innovation can take the form of digital marketing innovation,
social media marketing model, personalization systems,
redesigning the customer e xperience, service innovation,
data-driven segmentation, omnichannel integration,
development of a brand community, and marketing
communication for sustainability [46, 47]. Managerial
intentionality is also a part of marketing innovation strategy.
A firm m ay not be strategically innovative without using
digital platforms. Promoting brand awareness on social
media is not a form of marketing innovation if it fails to
deliver better customer insight, engagement quality, enable
brand differentiation and/or impr ove business performance
[48-52]. When firms blend in the technology, the knowledge
of the market, the interaction with customers and the
organizational learning, one can expect to generate strategic
marketing innovation that leads to a superior value. Thi s

corresponds with the dynamic capability approach where the
firm's competitive advantage is attributed to its resource
adaptation, integration and reconfigures ability in a variable

Digital marketing capability is a firm's ability to
leverage digital technologies, platforms, analytics and
communication systems to understand customers, send
personalized messages, manage customer relationships and
meet business goals. Contains features such as social media
marketing, search engine optimi zation, digital content
strategy, online advertising, mobile communication,
customer analytics, CRM systems and marketing automation
[59, 60]. Digital marketing capability increases the
competitiveness of the firm by contributing to the extension
and enhan cement of market reach, targeting efficiency,
enabling firm interaction, developing measurement and
giving market intelligence that can contribute to innovation
[61-65]. But the impact of digital marketing capability on
performance is not guaranteed. Those  digital investments
will not lead to strategic results if the companies do not have
the right employees, data quality, integrated systems or
management to be innovative. Digital capability needs to be
transformed to innovation capability [66 -70]. For exam ple,
customer information can show that there are unmet needs,
but the company needs to implement the knowledge through
a product redesign, service redesign, process redesign, or
communication redesign. Therefore, digital marketing
competence is hypothesiz ed to positively affect the
innovation capability and company performance [71-75].
Customer engagement orientation is a company's
strategic focus on establishing interactive, relational and
value-creating relationships with customers. It is more than
just customer satisfaction, and it centers on customer
engagement, input, advocacy, co -creation, and community
involvement and emotional connection with the brand [76 -
80]. Consumers are no longer the recipients of marketing
messages in digital markets. They exp ress opinions about
products, experiences, alternatives, they influence others and
relate to brands. Customer engagement can thus help to
facilitate marketing innovation processes by offering
feedback, uncovering service issues, proposing service
improvements and enabling firms to understand their new
preferences [81 -85]. Strategic marketing agility is the firm's
capability to quickly detect changes in the market, make
marketing decisions promptly, modify marketing campaigns,
reallocate resources and respon d to customers' and
competitors' actions. Agility is crucial in volatile digital
markets, where customer preferences, platform algorithms,
competitor moves and technological tools are constantly
evolving [86 -90]. Companies that follow a hardline annual
marketing strategy may find it difficult to react to change and
uncertainty in the market. Marketing agility is conceptually
related to the processes of sensing, seizing and reconfiguring
in marketing decision making that are captured by the
dynamic capabilities [91-95]. Innovation capability is when a
firm is able to create, develop or acquire new ideas, products,
services, processes, technologies and business models. In the
marketing strategy, innovation capability is defined as the
capability to develop new  customer experiences, enhance
brand communication, redefine service delivery, customize
offerings and present new value propositions [96] and [97].
Serves as a link between marketing resources and results.
Companies with a high level of innovation capabil ities can
stand out, enhance the customer experience and maintain a
competitive edge [98-100]. In this study, firm performance is
defined as perceived business performance, which is defined
as the performance of the firm as perceived by the firm itself,
and includes the firm's sales growth, customer retention,
market position, brand competitiveness, customer
satisfaction, and overall business results [101 -105]. Even
though objective performance metrics are desirable,
perceptual measures are widely used in m anagement and
marketing research when companies do not want to release
financial data, or when companies vary in size and industry
[106-110]. Accordingly, this study adopts perceived
performance indicators which conceptually are related to
marketing outcomes [111]. Based on the literature review, the
following hypotheses are proposed in the study:

H1: Digital marketing capability positively influences
innovation capability.
H2: A digital marketing capability has a positive relationship
with firm performance.
H3: Customer engagement orientation has positive impact on
innovation capability.
H4: Customers' orientation towards engagement has a
positive relationship with the performance of the company.
H5: Strategic marketing agility has a positive impact on
innovative capability.
H6: The strategic marketing agility positively impacts firm
performance.
H7: Innovation capability positively influences the
performance of the firm.
H8: Innovation capability mediates the relationship between
digital marketing capability and firm performance.
H9: Customer engagement orientation is indirectly related to
firm performance through innovation capability.
H10: Innovation capability partially mediates the relationship
between strategic marketing agility and firm performance.

Conceptual Framework
The conceptual framework puts forward marketing
innovation strategy as a multidimensional strategic capability
which includes digital marketing capability, customer
engagement orientation and strategic marketing agility [112 -
115]. These dimensions affect t he innovation capability of
firms, and in turn the performance of the firm. The model also
incorporates the possibility of direct effects of the three
marketing innovation strategy dimensions on firm
performance [116 -118]. The framework is consistent with
dynamic capability logic; the firms need to have market
sensing, customer learning, technological capability and
adaptive decision-making to generate and exploit innovation
[119-122]. The conceptual model of the study is shown in
figure 1. The model has as sumed that marketing innovation
strategy has both direct and indirect impact on the
performance of a firm via innovation capability. The direct
relationship reflects immediate gains like better targeting,
quicker communication, better market responsiveness  and
better customer interaction. The indirect relationship reflects
deeper capability development, where customer engagement
and agility, digital marketing practices are converted to new
products, services, processes, and customer experiences [123-
127]. The conceptual model is also congruent with the SDL
perspective as it does not consider customer value as a
unidirectional output of the firm but as an interactive process
between the firm, the customers and the market actors [128 -
130]. Digital platforms am plify this interaction by making it
more visible to the customers, speeding up word -of-mouth,
and providing opportunities for co -creation. Hence, firms
must regard customers not just as transaction targets, but as
knowledge partners to create marketing inn ovation strategy
[131-135].

Methodology
The type of research design undertaken in this study
is quantitative with cross sectional and empirical style. The
aim is to explore the statistics relationships between digital
marketing capability, customer engagement orientation,
strategic marketing agi lity, innovation capability and firm
performance. As the constructs involve managerial
perceptions, strategic practices and organizational capability
assessments, which are typically measured in marketing and
management research by structured questionnaires, a survey-
based methodology was chosen [136-140]. The data set in this
manuscript is a simulated data set for ethical transparency.

executives and st rategic decision makers from emerging
digital markets responses. The study is presented as a

respondents unless real field data are collected, analyzed and
reported transparently. This disclosure is significant because
the integrity of empirical research relies on data sources being
accurately represented, sampling procedure being properly
described, and the results of the analysis [141-145].

Discussion
This study aimed to investigate the effect of
marketing innovation strategy on the performance of the firm
in the new digital markets. The results indicate that digital
marketing capability, customers’ orientation to engagement
and strategic marketing agil ity has significant effects on
innovation capability. Firm performance is a strong positive
influence of the capability of innovation. These findings
provide evidence that marketing innovation is not just about
the use of digital media, but also requires t he use of
technology, knowledge of customers, agility and the ability to
learn. The analysis shows that the level of the digital
marketing capability positively affects the innovation
capability, which further reinforces the argument that digital
platforms, systems of digital analytics and communication
offer valuable market intelligence for the firms. Companies
that are savvy in digital marketing are able to discover
customer preferences, track competitor actions, experiment
campaign reactions and derive i nsights from data. All these
insights can then be applied to enhancing products and

services, communication campaigns and customer
experience. This result is in line with the general marketing
capabilities and digital transformation literature that states
that digital capability is strategically valuable when learning
and innovation is enabled [214]. Digital marketing
capability's direct impact on firm performance should also be
considered. The findings indicate that companies that have a
higher level of digital marketing are more likely to boost their
sales, customer retention and brand visibility and market
position. This can be due to the fact that digital marketing
offers accuracy in targeting, it is cost -effective in
communication, it can be personalize d and it offers
measurable results from campaigns. However, the results of
the mediation analysis suggest that part of this effect goes
through innovation capability. However, digital marketing
capability must not be considered as merely a promotional
role but must be associated to innovation management.
Also, customer engagement orientation had
significant positive impact on innovation capability and firm
performance. This discovery corroborates the idea,
introduced in previous research, that customers ar e key
sources of knowledge in digital markets. Actively engaged
customers give feedback, share experiences, and uncover
needs and join brand communities. Firms which actively pay
attention to customers and take customer feedback into
account when making de cisions, are more likely to innovate
successfully. This implies that marketing is not only about
creating value for the customer, but also about co -creating
value with the customer as in customer -dominant and
service-dominant marketing [215]. The direct re lationship of
the orientation of the customer engagement with firm
performance suggests that engagement leads to loyalty, trust,
advocacy and re -purchasing behavior. But the impact was
less than that of innovation capability. This indicates that
customer e ngagement can be important but if companies
don't convert customer insights into better products and
services, it is not enough on its own. Feedback may be
delivered many times to a firm without it being analyzed and
acted upon - then the firm does not innovate.
Therefore, customer engagement needs to be linked
to customer internal innovation systems. Strategic marketing
agility was also found to be a positive factor affecting the
innovativeness and firm performance. This outcome can be
explained by the th eoretical approach of dynamic capability
theory [216]. Agile companies can easily pick up on changes
and adapt marketing strategies and resource allocation. In
digital markets, this is crucial as customer preferences and
platform conditions are dynamic. Co mpanies that test, learn
and adapt are more likely to find new, innovative marketing
practices and stay relevant in the market. Innovation
capability was the best predictor of performance of the firms.
The results indicate that the overall mechanism that connects
marketing innovation strategy to business outcomes is the
ability of the firm to innovate. Innovation capability serves as
the catalyst and converts the inputs of digital marketing
capability, engagement orientation and agility into new
value. Companies which are able to shape a better customer
experience, innovate their services and enhance their digital
communication are more likely to perform well. The outcome
of the mediation is an important theoretical contribution. All
three dimensions of marketing innovation strategy related to
firm performance were positively influenced, either partially
or fully, by the innovation capability. This implies that the
performance is directly and indirectly influenced by
marketing innovation strategy. The direct effects can be the
immediate benefits of improved customer reach, response
and engagement. The indirect effects refer to the deeper
changes in the organization, that is, the enhancement of the
marketing practices has a positive impact on increasing the
innovation capability of the organization and improving
organizational performance. The results of this research have
a number of implications for marketing strategy theory. They
promote a holistic perspective on marketing innovation.
First, they encourage a  "whole marketing"
perspective on marketing innovation. The study does not
view digital marketing, customer engagement and agility as
independent practices, but rather as related aspects of
marketing innovation strategy. Second, the results confirm
the sig nificance of capability based explanations of firm
performance. Differences in performance are not captured by
the mere use of technology, they are captured by the strategic
use of technology. Thirdly, the findings of this study reveal
that the role of inn ovation capability as a mediator between
marketing innovation and organizational learning and
implementation capability must be connected. The results
indicate that from a management standpoint, companies
need to refrain from adopting digital marketing in a
fragmented way. Many companies spend money on social
media pages, digital ads, CRM or analytics solutions, but
don't create a strategy for innovation routines. The solution
should be to build integrated marketing innovation systems
to tie the marketing c ustomer data, feedback on customer
engagement, agile decision making and innovation process.
This is a cross -functional problem that involves Marketing,
Sales, IT, Operations and Top management. The findings are
especially applicable to SMEs in the emerging markets. SMEs
have limited resources and budget available for marketing
innovation, but they can do so at a low cost, with the help of
customer feedback mechanisms, speedy experimentation and
focused engagement techniques. For instance, Social Media

Theoretical and Practical Implications
This study is of theoretical significance in three ways.
First, it extends marketing innovation research by
incorporating digital marketing capability, customer
engagement orientation and strategic marketing agility at the
same time in the empirical framework. This integration is
crucial because modern innovations in marketing usually
don't come from a single capability. Rather, the outcome of
innovation is when technology, customer engagement and
flexible decision -making combine. Second, the study
provides an improvement of capability theory by revealing
that innovation capability is a means by which marketing
resources translate into performance outcomes. Third, the
article contributes to a growing body of literature i n the field
of marketing strategy in emerging markets by highlighting
the importance of strategic routines in learning,
experimenting and responding to the market for the resource-
constrained firm to reap the benefits of innovation. The
management is also very important. Marketing Innovation is
a capability of the enterprise and not a department. While the
marketing department might spearhead the customer
communication, collaboration is needed with sales,
operations, IT, finance and the top management team for
innovation. For instance, complaints received by the
marketing staff can show the deficiencies in service delivery
that need to be fixed in the operations. Social media analytics
can detect the need for new aspects on the product that needs
to be developed. Campaign performance data can show price
or distribution problems that would need to be addressed.
Therefore, marketing innovation is a successful
phenomenon when insights cross organizational boundaries.
The study offers helpful insights for compan ies that want to
enhance performance in new digital markets. They must do a
marketing capability audit to discover strengths and
weaknesses in the digital channels, customer analysis tools,
content control, CRM and marketing performance
measurement. Second , they must design a system that will
enable them to capture feedback from various touchpoints of
their customers. Thirdly, they need to establish agile review
cycles that allow the marketing team to review the data from
the campaigns and take actions rapidly. Fourth, they need to
bring marketing insight to innovation projects, thus ensuring
that digital insight and customer knowledge leads to tangible
enhancements in products, services and customer experience.
The results indicate that support for digital entrepreneurship
should not only be limited to training firms, but should also
cover other aspects for policy makers and business
development institutions. While many SMEs are familiar
with the opening of digital accounts, they are not as well
equipped in terms of strategic capacity with regard to
analysis, customer segmenting, and innovation planning and
performance measurement. The marketing strategy, the
development of customer insight, the interpretation of the
data, the innovation management and the responsible digital
communication are therefore components of the training

economies where SMEs create an even larger proportion of

Recommendations
Based on the findings, here  are some
recommendations. One, businesses need to create the
capacity to develop digital marketing as a strategic tool, not a
marketing tool. Digital platforms should also be adopted not
just for product promotion but to gather customer insights,
gain mar ket insights and test messages and support
innovation. It's important that companies have a clear
direction to data monitoring and that marketing decisions are
not made just on a hunch. Secondly, customer engagement
should be institutionalized in companies . Systematically
collect and incorporate customer feedback, online reviews,
social media interaction, complaints and suggestions in
marketing and innovation decisions. Engagement should not
be just “talking at.” It must become a learning process, a
process that is structured, so that companies can discover new
needs, gaps in their services, new expectations. Third,
managers should build up strategic marketing agility.
Businesses need to use a flexible approach to planning, quick
experimentation, monitoring performance in real-time and be
able to adapt the campaign based on the results. Marketing
teams need to be given the freedom to make changes to the
strategy as soon as customer behavior or market conditions
change. Leadership support is also needed for agility as some
employees may be reluctant to try new ideas if they feel their
decision making is centralized or they are risk averse.
Fourthly, there is a need for firms to invest in their innovation
capability. This is for employee training, cross -function
teamwork, a culture of experimentation, service
improvement systems and leadership support of new ideas.
Technology investment is not the only ingredient to
innovation capability, people, processes and culture play a
role as well. Firms should have their own processes for the
generation, evaluation, implementation, and learning
processes. Fifth, SMEs in emerging markets need to learn to
use low cost digital innovation practices. They can be social
media analytics, customer surveys, mobile communication
with customers, basic CRM systems, content personalization
and community interaction and engagement with the brand.
These methods can lead to impactful innovation even for
companies that do not have a big marketing budget or
enterprise software with the lates t innovation. Finally,
researchers in the future should validate the proposed model
using real survey data from individual industry and country.
Longitudinal studies would be helpful to see if marketing
innovation strategy can result in ongoing performance in the
long term. In future, the differences between SMEs and large
companies could be compared and moderating factors like
digital literacy, market turbulence, leadership support and
organizational culture and data governance maturity could
be explored.

Limitations and Future Research
The study has a number of limitations. The first and
foremost, the data is simulated and therefore cannot be
considered as evidence data from the actual respondents. The
results would be appropriate for being used in
methodological demonstration, manuscript development
and research design planning, and cannot be used to make
generalizable claims until it is tested with empirical data. In
the future, the simulated data should be replaced with actual
field data from the firms and the respondents collected via
clear sampling process. Secondly, the study was conducted
through cross-sectional design. A true cross sectional survey
would provide perceptions at a given moment in time and
would not be able to confirm causation entirely. Longitudinal
data should be used in future studies to investigate how the
marketing innovation capability evolves and impacts
performance over time. Other designs, such as experimental
or quasi-experimental, can be helpful to assess the effect of
certain digital marketing interventions. Thirdly, the study
uses perceptual measures of firm performance. While
perceived performance is widely used in management
research, future research should incorporate some objective
measures like sales growth, customer re tention rate, market
share, return on marketing investment or profitability.
Additional perceptual data would enhance the validity and
minimize common method concerns if combined with
objective data. The model emphasizes certain aspects of
marketing innova tion strategy. Other dimensions, such as
brand orientation, entrepreneurship orientation, artificial
intelligence readiness, innovation in sustainable marketing,
and organizational culture and leadership style can also affect
innovation and performance. Th e model can be extended to
include these variables as predictors, moderators or control
variables in future research.

Conclusion
The relationship between marketing innovation
strategy and firm performance was studied through an
empirical study that is sim ulated. The results show that the
digital marketing capability is positively associated with the
innovation capability, customer engagement orientation and
strategic marketing agility. The innovation capability also
positively influences the firm performan ce and partly plays
as the mediator between marketing innovation strategy and

performance. It is found that marketing innovation strategy
is a multi -dimensional capability of an organization. It
demands a strategic approach to use digital technologies, an
interactive approach to customers, reacting quickly to market
changes and turning market knowledge into innovation.
Companies that build these capabilities not only enhance
customer value, but will also enhance their competitive
positioning and business pe rformance. The most critical
finding is that innovation capability is the main channel by
which marketing innovation strategy leads to performance
outcomes. Digital marketing tools and techniques are
valuable, but when integrated into a firm's business mod el,
strategy, and service design to create new ideas, enhance
services, and redefine service journeys, they are even more
powerful. Thus, marketing innovation efforts must be
organized strategically as a process of the organization
instead of as a series of isolated marketing activities.
