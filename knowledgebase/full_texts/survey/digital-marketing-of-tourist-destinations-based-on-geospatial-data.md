Abstract
Digital transformation has significantly changed the way touris t destinations are promoted and
managed. In the modern environmen t, geospatial data and GIS (Ge ographic Information Systems) are
becoming key tools in the digital marketing of tourism.  The aim of the paper is to analyze the role of
geospatial data in improving the digital marketing of tourist d estinations, with a special focus on
location targeting and the concept of smart tourism. The subject of the research includes the integration
of GIS technologies in the processes of promotion and positioning of destinations on digital platforms.
Work methods: Analytical, descriptive and comp arative methods were used in th e work, as well as a
review of relevant international literature. The results indica te that the applicati on of geospatial data
enables more precise market segmentation, personalized communic ation with tourists and more
efficient management of the destination brand. Conclusion: digital marketing based on geospatial
systems is a key element of the competitiveness of modern tourist destinations.
Keywords: Digital Marketing, GIS, Geospatial Data, Tourism, Geo-targeting, Smart Tourism. 
Introduction
The development of information and communication technologies h as significantly transformed the
tourism industry. The Internet, mobile applications and social networks have become the primary
channels through which tourists gather information and make travel decisions. Information technologies
represent a key factor in the modernization of tourism and the shaping of tourist behavior.1
In such an environment, geospatial data and GIS systems are gaining more and more importance. They
enable the analysis of the location, movements and preferences of tourists, thus improving the
effectiveness of digital marketing. The research problem of this paper is the examination of the role of
geospatial data in improving the digital promotion of tourist destinations.
Many authors see tourism as science, art and business, seen as attracting visitors, providing
accommodation and pleasant hospitality. Tourism as a business entity provides places and events with
the aim of occupying people while they are on vacation, tourism  is also described as a source of
relaxation. It is a fact that tourism increases the social relationship, which means that it could affect the
relationship and behavior between tourists and locals, and thus affect social changes.2
Tourism is an activity that can be used as a supplementary factor in the economic development of these
areas, which is confirmed by European experiences. It is necess ary to stimulate the development of
rural tourism, primarily by providing financial support from the state. 3

1. Digital Marketing in Tourism 
Digital marketing is a key component of promotional strategies in modern tourism. It is a marketing
approach that uses digital channels and technologies to reach target groups of travelers at the right time
and place. Digital marketing allows tourism organizations to communicate directly with consumers,
create personalized messages and monitor user behavior in real time, which significantly improves the
competitiveness of destinations in the digital environment.4
The development of digital propaganda and the use of social networks significantly change the way in
which information is disseminated in the digital environment, w hich is particularly relevant for the
promotion of tourist destinations through online channels.5
Digital marketing implies the use of digital channels for the promotion of products and services.6
In tourism, it includes:
 destination website
 social networks
 online advertising
 mobile applications
 review platforms. 7
Digital marketing enables organizations to accurately measure c ampaign results and adapt content to
target segments.
 Tourist destinations use digital channels to:
 strengthening the brand
 increasing visibility
 communication with tourists
 attraction promotion.8

3. Location Marketing and Geo-Targeting
Location marketing is a form of digital marketing that uses geo graphic data about users to show them
personalized promotional messages. Geo-targeting, which is its practical tool, allows ads and
information to be shown only to users in a specific location, region or vicinity of a destination.
Location-based marketing uses information about a user's location to deliver personalized promotional
messages.12
Geo-targeting enables:
 market segmentation according to geographic location
 personalization of the offer
 increasing the conversion rate
Today, mobile applications and Google Maps allow tourism organi zations to promote attractions to
users in real time.13
According to research, personalization of content based on location data increases user engagement and
effectiveness of campaigns.

5. Research
5.1. Digital Promotion of The Destination - Example of the Jahorina Mountain
Jahorina is one of the most famous mountain tourist destinations in Bosnia and Herzegovina, known
for its ski resorts, natural beauties and active winter tourism . In the last few years, the promotion of
Jahorina has been significantly digitized through the integrati on of GIS technologies, social networks
and location marketing, which has enabled better visibility and targeted engagement of tourists.
The analysis of the digital promotion of the mountain destinati on Jahorina shows the importance of
geospatial data in marketing.
Elements of analysis:
 Presence on Google Maps
 Digital ski resort maps
 Geo-targeted campaigns
 Online reviews and ratings
Destinations that use digital maps and location advertising rep ort higher online visibility and better
interaction with users.
5.2. Digital Marketing Strategies
The tourist organization of Jahorina and private operators appl y the following digital marketing
strategies:
1. Website and SEO optimization
o The official website of Jahorina (www.jahorina.com) contains in teractive maps, prices of ski
passes, booking options and information about accommodation.
o SEO optimization enables high ranking on Google search engines for key terms such as "ski
Jahorina" or "winter vacation BiH".
2. Social networks
o Facebook and Instagram are used to promote photo and video cont ent of ski slopes, snow
conditions and events.
o Geo-tagging allows tourists to share their experiences and incr eases the organic reach of the
campaign.
3. Mobile applications and digital maps
o Interactive GIS maps show ski slopes, lifts, restaurants and hotels.
o Applications for navigation within the ski resort help tourists better plan their stay.
4. Geo-targeted campaigns
o Paid digital campaigns target users in the region (eg Sarajevo, Belgrade, Zagreb) and show
promotional content to tourists who are nearby or planning a trip.
5.3. Integration of GIS Technologies 
GIS technology in Jahorina enables:
o Monitoring visits to various ski slopes and mountain attractions.
o Analysis of the density of visits and identification of areas of high interest.
o Linking digital marketing with spatial data (eg sending promotional messages to users located near
a specific cable car);
According to the previously mentioned model of Ljubojević and A vramović (2021), GIS solutions
provide an architecture that enables efficient processing and v isualization of spatial data, which is
directly applicable for digital marketing of destinations like Jahorina.

The data from this platform provides insight into the relative volume of searches for the term "Jahorina",

6. Discussion
The results of the analysis indicate that the integration of GI S technologies into digital marketing
enables:
 more precise targeting
 better understanding of the market
 personalization of the offer
 increasing competitiveness
 Compared to traditional marketing, digital marketing based on geospatial data provides measurable
results and greater efficiency.
For instance:
 The application of digital marketing in combination with GIS te chnologies in Jahorina shows that
modern tools can increase online visibility, engagement and effectiveness of destination promotion.
Visits by tourists throughout the year confirm the success of t he year-round promotion and the
concept of smart tourism.
 SEO strategies and social networks enable high search rankings and increased reach. The seasonal
increase in searches and engagement on social networks confirms that digital campaigns effectively
attract the attention of potential visitors.24
 Geo-targeted campaigns enable the personalization of promotion according to the location of
tourists. Mobile applications and GIS analysis help with more even distribution of visitors, optimize
the flow of tourists and contribute to the sustainable development of the destination.25
 More than 100,000 tourists in the summer of 2024 and 27,000 in the early phase of 2025 confirm
the practical application of these strategies. The integration of digital marketing and geo-targeting
enables effective destination management and improves the tourist experience.
 The combination of digital campaigns, GIS data and location mar keting increases the
competitiveness of the destination and the quality of the tourist experience. The example of Jahorina
can serve as a model for other tourist destinations in BiH and the region.
Conclusion
Digital marketing of tourist destinations based on geospatial d ata represents a modern approach to the
promotion and management of destinations. The integration of GIS technologies and location marketing
enables a better understanding of tourist behavior and a more effective marketing strategy.
It is confirmed that geospatial data has a significant role in increasing the competitiveness of tourist
destinations in the digital economy. Future research could incl ude empirical analysis of specific
campaigns and measurement of their effects.
