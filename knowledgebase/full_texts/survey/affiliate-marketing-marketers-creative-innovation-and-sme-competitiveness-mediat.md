Abstract
Aﬃliate marketing trends and their contribution to sustainable business competitiveness from the
perspective of business organizations and aﬃliates as independent marketers is an interesting study in
the current literature, but it still ignores marketers' creative innovation and customer mix. Therefore, this
study aims to explore the relationship between aﬃliate marketing, marketers' creative innovation, and
business competitiveness through the mediation of customer mix in the culinary and fashion SME
sectors in tourist destinations in the Indonesian context. Quantitative research using a survey was
developed to build a predictive relationship model. The survey successfully collected 128 selected
respondents. Statistical analysis was applied using Partial Least Squares-Structural Equation Modeling 4
(PLS-SEM4). The research ﬁndings indicate that aﬃliate marketing and marketers' creative innovation
have a signiﬁcant direct effect on competitiveness. Aﬃliate marketing and marketers' creative
innovation have a positive and signiﬁcant effect on customer mix. Customer mix has a positive and
signiﬁcant effect on competitiveness. The results also conﬁrm that customer mix has a positive partial
mediation effect on the relationship between aﬃliate marketing and competitiveness. Customer mix
also has a positive partial mediation effect on the relationship between marketers' creative innovation
and competitiveness in this sector. These ﬁndings provide valuable evidence reﬂecting the current
empirical trend that almost all customer mix parameters act as important facilitators that bridge the
creation of effective aﬃliate marketing trends and creative innovations from e-commerce platform-
based marketers that are able to inﬂuence the competitiveness of SMEs in tourist areas.
Introduction
Businesses in the era of digital disruption continue to evolve and are increasingly challenged by massive
technological advances. As a result, business ﬂexibility is increasingly under transformational pressure,
highlighting the importance of adopting digital technologies in their operations for business survival and
growth. Furthermore, the dynamic marketing landscape is reducing barriers to market entry and
increasing competition to attract increasingly savvy and educated customers (Pitri et al., 2025).
Maintaining a superior and high-performing business requires dynamic capabilities and innovation
capabilities, as outlined in the resource-based view (RBV) (Heenkenda et al., 2022), and this also applies
to more creative and innovative marketing resources and personnel (Hartika et al., 2023). Due to the
adaptive and innovative business culture, human resource (HR) practices in the development of
marketing strategies by marketers can be a more dynamic business life potion (Firmansyah et al., 2024)
that allows products to be offered above the preferences of customer personnel that meet their
expectations (Wahdiniwaty et al., 2023), so that businesses avoid the trap of fragility and destruction
(Wahdiniwaty, Syafei, Firmansyah, Masduki, et al., 2025), but instead can be a fundamental force for
creating competitive advantages in sustainable business practices (Wahdiniwaty, Soegoto, et al., 2025).
From a marketing perspective, marketing communications foster customer relationships. Marketing
strategy is the vanguard that bridges business objectives and customer needs by offering products that
are valuable to customers (Indriani & Firmansyah, 2020), and this is widely recognized as applicable to
Page 2/38

both large-scale businesses and small and medium enterprises (SMEs). An effective marketing strategy
can even be a driving force that strengthens customer value creation and the competitiveness of SMEs
(Falát & Holubčík, 2017). Interestingly, the massive emergence of digital platforms driven by
technological advances in the 21st century has further facilitated the implementation of online
marketing strategies within the digital ecosystem to promote products, increase sales, and strengthen
business competitiveness (Firmansyah, 2024). As suggested by Patrick & Hee (2021) SMEs in this era
need to consider and enter the e-commerce market to communicate their products online by involving
aﬃliates through aﬃliate marketing programs on social media channels.
Responding to this trend, SMEs are adapting to reach global markets and connect with millions of
audiences. Digital marketing practices have become an alternative and crucial tool in efforts to increase
customer engagement (Afolabi et al., 2024; Wahdiniwaty et al., 2022). Even adopting a commission-
based aﬃliate marketing model that relies on the role of competent and trusted independent marketers
allows for more effective marketing practices at a relatively low cost (Firmansyah et al., 2026).
Marketing models involving third parties through aﬃliate marketing based on e-commerce platforms are
increasingly trending due to their positive beneﬁts in increasing customer participation and engagement
(Mangiò & Di Domenico, 2022), inﬂuencing customer behavior and purchasing intentions (Jyotsna et al.,
2024; Kertiriasih, 2023). For example, aﬃliates act as bloggers or YouTubers and TikTokers who actively
promote the brand owner's goods or services, seeking to create mutually beneﬁcial relationships
between the parties involved (Xu et al., 2025). A successful framework in this online marketing model
allows for the formation of digital customer community relationships and a wider market share through
social media channels, the growth of trust and a good brand image to inﬂuence sales and business
competitiveness among SMEs as actors whose products are marketed by aﬃliates.
Aﬃliate marketing trends related to the competitiveness of SMEs in the era of digital disruption are
increasingly receiving attention among businesses, marketing managers, practitioners, and academics,
and have even become an interesting issue explored and developed in many previous modern marketing
studies. For example, Patrick & Hee (2021) identiﬁed the determinants of the driving factors of the
intention to use aﬃliate marketing and business sustainability in SMEs by considering the importance of
development culture; Hammoud et al., (2026) analyzed the impact of social media inﬂuencer-based
aﬃliate marketing on the sustainability of the tourism industry; Jyotsna et al., (2024) analyzed the
impact of aﬃliate marketing on customer behavior; Abdelhady et al., (2020) explored its relationship with
customer loyalty; and Sharaﬁ et al., (2024) identiﬁed the success factors of aﬃliate marketing.
Firmansyah et al., (2026) documented their qualitative study related to the brilliant trend of aﬃliate
marketing in the era of artiﬁcial intelligence based on human resources and marketer competencies as a
path to business competitiveness, increasing customer participation and engagement, customer
behavior and purchasing intentions towards commission-based aﬃliate income, expanding part-time job
opportunities and active economic actors, strengthening people's purchasing power and its positive
contribution to increasing gross domestic product (GDP).

Other similar studies that enrich the literature on aﬃliate marketing and SME competitiveness include
those exploring the contribution of the digital economy and e-marketing as drivers of growth, innovation,
and competitiveness for SMEs (Popescu et al., 2020); the effectiveness of user-generated and company-
generated content as a response for SMEs in strengthening competitiveness (Afolabi et al., 2024); the
inﬂuence of aﬃliate marketing on sustainability in SMEs based on technological advancements and
government support for the development of e-commerce beneﬁts (Tanuwijaya & Princes, 2025); an
exploration of the role of websites and social media platforms in online marketing practices and their
impact on improving competitiveness and business performance (Lányi et al., 2021); TikTok-based
social media aﬃliate marketing as a driver of digital transformation and competitiveness for SMEs
(Santoso, 2025); and aﬃliate marketing as a driver of increasing SME competitiveness in the digital
economy era (Andrianata et al., 2025). Interestingly, existing valuable works largely examine the trend of
aﬃliate marketing and its contribution to sustainable business competitiveness from the perspective of
business organizations and aﬃliates as independent marketers, but still neglect their creative
innovation. Furthermore, the customer perspective with all its characteristics remains neglected.
Therefore, it is undeniable that investigating creative innovation as a strategic competency of aﬃliates,
and the customer proﬁle reﬂected in the customer mix, is crucial for improving aﬃliate marketing
effectiveness.
To ﬁll the existing gap, this study proposes a framework for a business competitiveness model based on
aﬃliate marketing and customer mix. Critically, the creative aspects of aﬃliate innovation and
sensitively, the customer mix aspects are considered for exploration, which also underlie the urgency of
novelty and originality proposed in this paper. Because marketers' creative innovation is essential in
efforts to discover, implement, and develop viral, innovative, and strategic marketing strategies (Lu et al.,
2026; Hartika et al., 2023), and the customer mix is a crucial information source regarding customer
characteristics and proﬁles that underpin the development of promotional strategies, content, and brand
marketing through e-commerce platforms in online marketing practices, it has greater potential to reach
target markets in clear and precise market segmentation, encouraging increased customer reviews,
participation, and purchase intentions (e.g., Pitri et al., 2025; Suryana, 2023; and Jackson & Ahuja, 2016).
The customer mix is an important facilitator that can strengthen the effectiveness of aﬃliate marketing
to enhance business competitiveness among SMEs.
Therefore, this study aims to explore the predictive relationship model between aﬃliate marketing and
competitiveness by examining the mediating effect of customer composition on SMEs in the culinary
and fashion sectors in tourism areas in the Indonesian context. This research provides valuable insights
into modern marketing management literature, offering a practical framework for business managers
and marketers who have creative innovations on how customer mix can bridge the gap between aﬃliate
marketing trends and business competitiveness to enable businesses to perform better and sustainably
amidst increasingly dynamic and challenging online marketing competition, as well as increasingly
educated and savvy customers in the digital era business landscape.

Literature review
Aﬃliate Marketing
Aﬃliate marketing programs in the era of digital disruption are increasingly becoming a trend for
businesses, markets, and the global community. Aﬃliate marketing essentially describes the main
framework of the role of aﬃliates who connect with product owners (primary sellers or advertisers) on
online marketing platforms to increase customer engagement and sales (Wahdiniwaty et al., 2023).
Online marketing platforms expand e-commerce platform-based marketing practices across various
social media channels. To achieve proﬁtable success for all parties involved, the aﬃliate marketing
framework can be likened to a collaborative scheme in the Helix model (such as the triple helix,
quadruple helix, quintuple helix, and their extensions) where efforts to achieve common goals require a
cooperative mechanism based on interconnected contributions, involvement, and collaboration through
the involvement of key components and actors, as described in research by Wahdiniwaty, Firmansyah,
Dede, et al., (2022). Thus, the core actors involved in an aﬃliate marketing programs are "aﬃliates,"
businesses or merchants ("brands" or "retailers"), third-party e-commerce platforms, and "customers" or
audiences as the target market.
According to Firmansyah et al., (2026) aﬃliate marketing is deﬁned as a digital platform-based online
marketing practice that involves partnerships in a scheme where independent marketers promote other
companies' products without feeling ownership of those products in exchange for commissions based
on the number of products successfully sold through their marketing accounts. Aﬃliate marketing is an
online marketing partnership model based on a revenue-sharing marketing scheme in the form of sales
commissions where an aﬃliate, such as a blogger, YouTuber, or TikToker, plays an active role in
promoting a merchant's goods or services, thus enabling a mutually beneﬁcial relationship between the
parties involved (Xu et al., 2025). Perceived usefulness, social environment, observability, motivation and
purchase intention (Patrick & Hee, 2021), audience engagement, number of reviews, sales and
commission rates describe the practical parameters of aﬃliate marketing based on e-commerce
platforms (e.g., Ghosal et al., 2020; Giron et al., 2025).
Marketers’ Creative Innovation
Kim & Pierce (2013) have explained that creativity is the basis for emerging new solutions, leading to the
development of various innovative ideas. Therefore, creativity and innovation carried out by individuals,
communities, and businesses that demonstrate distinct and interesting aspects are none other than the
application of creative innovative thinking. Creative innovation is the application of new and unique ideas
that develop into well-managed knowledge and can be transformed into effective product production
processes, service processes, new system applications, and the implementation of new procedures that
can generate proﬁts for the business (Popa et al., 2020).

Hartika et al., (2023) in their research provide an overview of creative innovation. They view creative
innovation as the intelligence and thinking ability of organizational personnel to discover new things as
appropriate methods, demonstrating innovation and usefulness by offering added value that aligns with
changing needs over time. In the modern era, creative innovation in marketers supports a culture of
adaptation that aligns with needs, thus becoming key to the success of business models, preventing
extinction and fragility (Wahdiniwaty & Firmansyah, 2026). A more dynamic business enables
sustainable competitive advantage amidst the challenges of digital transformation and disruption
(Marković et al., 2020; Hillson, 2023; Wahdiniwaty, Syafei, Firmansyah, Azhari, et al., 2025). Empirical
parameters reﬂecting the existence of creative innovation can be evaluated through divergent thinking,
seeking novelty, and adequate connectivity.
Customer Mix
The 21st century digital landscape offers ever-evolving marketing techniques and challenges, including
hybrid marketing communications with customer-centric services (Batat, 2024; Firmansyah et al., 2024).
The increasingly discerning and selective purchasing behavior of today's generation of customers, as
well as the effectiveness of online marketing communications within the e-commerce ecosystem,
demonstrates the complex relationship between internal business objectives and the personal
preferences of online customers, with all their volatile characteristics (Jyotsna et al., 2024; Wahdiniwaty
et al., 2023). However, a suﬃcient understanding of customer proﬁles and characteristics, as reﬂected in
the customer mix, provides suﬃcient information to inform appropriate marketing activities in
developing online marketing strategies and mapping the distribution of target markets on platforms by
classifying them across various criteria and social class levels, with all their potential engagement and
purchasing behaviors. According to Jackson & Ahuja (2016) the customer mix is a set of important
customer elements that determine demand drivers, purchasing preferences, consumption behavior and
patterns, as well as the relationship between customers with goods or services, and their experiences.
Its main characteristics consist of people-audience, personality, perception, and participation (Pitri et al.,
2025).
Business Competitiveness
From a current perspective, the level of company competitiveness is increasingly considered important,
especially for SMEs, because it not only results in better organizational performance but also other
growth metrics (Firmansyah et al., 2024), such as the ability to build anti-fragility and maintain business
continuity. Competitiveness is a crucial dimension in accurately assessing the use of organizational
resources and capabilities, which, if utilized effectively and eﬃciently, enable companies to achieve
various competitive advantages (Bastos et al., 2023). Porter et al., (2007); Porter & Magretta (2014) state
that competitiveness is also a relative concept, relating to how competitive a company is compared to
other industries. Firmansyah et al., (2024) deﬁne competitiveness as a business's ability to perform well.
Business competitiveness can be seen from the level of productivity, market share and sales, technology

adoption, innovation capabilities, operational performance, and organizational growth (Firmansyah et al.,
2024; Bastos et al., 2023; Le & Ikram, 2022).
Hypothesis development
Aﬃliate marketing and SME competitiveness
In the modern e-commerce platform-based marketing landscape, aﬃliate involvement as independent
marketers in aﬃliate marketing programs with ethical and transparent conversion rates can bring
positive beneﬁts to participating SMEs. This is particularly true for brands and products marketed online
through social media channels, allowing SMEs to incur relatively lower marketing costs, while potentially
increasing market share, enhancing brand image, trust, and satisfaction, as well as increasing sales,
supporting operational smoothness, and strengthening their business competitiveness.
Research by Andrianata et al., (2025) revealed evidence that aﬃliate marketing is an effective tool for
expanding digital transformation practices and enhancing SME competitiveness in the digital economy
era. Interestingly, innovative, differentiated, and unique digital platform-based marketing
communications contribute to inﬂuencing SME competitiveness (Moreno-Gómez et al., 2023). Aﬃliate
marketing programs that leverage the development of e-commerce platforms resulting from advances in
digital technology signiﬁcantly beneﬁt the competitiveness and sustainability of SMEs (e.g., studies by
Hammoud et al., 2026; Tanuwijaya & Princes, 2025; Lányi et al., 2021). Thus, the aﬃliate marketing trend
brings direct beneﬁts to improving business competitiveness among SMEs.
H1. Aﬃliate marketing has a direct positive effect on SME competitiveness.
Marketers Creative Innovation and SME Competitiveness
Creativity and innovation are the lifeblood of humanity and business operations, exploiting opportunities
for survival and growth (Firmansyah et al., 2024). In business development, competitiveness can be
inﬂuenced by the creative innovation of employees and management at various levels, as a mechanism
for practical, critical, and adaptive thinking, enabling organizations to become more competitive (Ocasal
et al., 2022). In response to the impact of digital technology advancements, one effective breakthrough
is creativity and innovation, which together can be the key to success for SMEs and their marketers,
increasing competitiveness and sustainably developing their businesses.
In short, creativity and innovation are crucial instruments for strengthening the competitiveness of SMEs
currently undergoing transformation and digitalization, which require adaptive behavior and business
model adjustments (Darwish et al., 2024). Studies by Otache (2024); Hien & Tam (2025); Taneo et al.,
(2022) found that creative innovation has a positive inﬂuence on competitiveness and sustainable
business performance in SMEs. This condition indicates that divergent thinking, novelty seeking, and

connectivity, as strategic parameters of marketers' creative innovation, can drive increased
competitiveness in SMEs.
H2. Marketers' creative innovation has a positive effect on SME competitiveness.
Aﬃliate Marketing and Customer Mix
Aﬃliate marketing challenges the complexity of marketing techniques, requiring a thorough
understanding of the various characteristics of the customer mix to support successful online marketing
targeting, address preferences and needs, inﬂuence purchasing behavior, inspire customers, and
generate feedback from followers and potential buyers on social media platforms. The customer mix
provides the basis for developing engaging, adaptive, and innovative online marketing strategies within
aﬃliate marketing programs to reach target markets, while considering changing customer
characteristics and behaviors in the online marketing landscape (Pitri et al., 2025). Online marketing
practices based on e-commerce platforms, along with developed marketing strategies, are strategic
components in product communication, value creation, customer engagement, and customer experience
and satisfaction in the digital marketing landscape, by recognizing and reﬂecting customer mix trends
(e.g., Bezhovski, 2025; Sulaj & Pfoertsch, 2024; Pitri et al., 2026; Wichmann et al., 2022; Pitri et al., 2025).
Therefore, the effectiveness of aﬃliate marketing can positively inﬂuence the customer mix.
H3. Aﬃliate marketing has a positive effect on the customer mix.
Marketers’ Creative Innovation and Customer Mix
With an increasingly dynamic business environment, survival has become a crucial issue for improving
business performance, requiring adequate employee creative innovation to address this (Bamidele,
2019). In marketing practice, marketing creativity and innovation must be able to generate strategies that
meet customers' primary needs (F. Wang et al., 2018). Therefore, creative innovation is increasingly
important for understanding customer characteristics (Racela & Thoumrungroje, 2020).
Marketers' creative innovation, which is able to develop unique and differentiated marketing techniques
from competitors, contributes to the creation of added value that meets the customer mix, for example
by increasing attention and participation, engagement, and purchase intention (Al Khazraje et al., 2026).
Research ﬁndings by Liao & Tsai (2019); and X. Wang (2021) conﬁrm that creative marketing innovation
can consistently increase customer demand. This reﬂects the marketer's ability to analyze customer
behavior, generate ideas, and implement them as adaptive marketing strategies aligned with the
customer mix. Therefore, creative innovations developed in marketing activities correlate with the
customer mix.
H4. Marketers' creative innovations have a positive effect on the customer mix.

The Mediating Role of Customer Mix
In the modern business landscape of the 21st century, where digital disruption has a signiﬁcant impact
on various aspects of life, including business practices and sustainability, the marketing landscape is
increasingly challenged by technological advancements, with e-commerce-based marketplaces across
various online media channels marking a new chapter in what is known as the digital transformation of
business models. In this era, barriers to market entry are decreasing, but many businesses remain fragile
and vulnerable. To respond to this realistically and adequately, businesses need dynamic capabilities,
innovation and adaptation capabilities, the presence of e-commerce platforms and digital platforms
based on social media channels, as well as the development of knowledge management integrated with
Artiﬁcial Intelligence (AI) generated by rapid technological advances, managerial effectiveness and the

relationships and customer engagement to increase market share and sales to support the achievement

Heenkenda et al., 2022; Afolabi et al., 2024).

based on e-commerce platforms, which involve independent marketers in proﬁtable partnerships, has
been shown to have a positive impact on competitiveness and sustainable business performance.
Andrianata et al., (2025) found that the effectiveness of aﬃliate marketing in digital business
transformation practices has a positive inﬂuence on the competitiveness of SMEs. Strengthening this,

Research method
Research design
Quantitative research using a survey method was adapted to empirically test the relationship between
each latent variable constructed in a predictive model based on sophisticated and established
theoretical constructs, relying on predictive power (Wahdiniwaty, Soegoto, et al., 2025). The research
design adopted an explanatory survey method (e.g., Agranoff & Kolpakov, 2019; Strydom, 2013;
Firmansyah et al., 2024).
Sampel size, sampling techniques, and data collection
The sample size for this study was validated at the ﬁnal stage of questionnaire distribution, and data
collection was deemed complete and adequate. A survey model was applied to the primary data
collection process, successfully capturing complete responses from 128 respondents, who were
selected using a purposive sampling technique. Respondents were selected based on criteria that met
the research objectives. They were customers of SMEs in the fashion-based creative industry sector in
the tourism region of West Java, Indonesia, with the proportion of sample participation ranging from
59.38% to 40.63% of all responses collected.
Anonymous aﬃliate accounts connected to these SME products served as an initial means of reaching
the research sample through their networks. The culinary SMEs, as the research subjects, already had
online store accounts and adopted online marketing practices based on e-commerce platforms,
engaging in aﬃliate marketing programs through independent third-party marketers, and using inﬂuencer
advertising on various social media platforms. Customers, as respondents in this study, often obtain
Page 11/38

their initial information from aﬃliate marketing broadcasts. Respondents were then directed to complete
a research questionnaire developed based on the parameters of each research object. Alternative
responses were provided using a point scale from 1 to 7, with statements ranging from strongly disagree
to strongly agree. The questionnaire was distributed via Google Form, WhatsApp, and face-to-face for six
months in the second half of 2025.
Research objects and parameters
The research objects consist of aﬃliate marketing, marketer creative innovation, customer mix, and SME
competitiveness. Aﬃliate marketing (AM) consists of parameters of perceived usefulness, social
environment, observability, audience engagement, number of reviews, sales, and commission rate
operationalized with fourteen indicators (AM1-AM14), following Firmansyah et al., (2026); Wahdiniwaty
et al., (2023); Ghosal et al., (2020); Giron et al., (2025). Marketers' creative innovation is shaped by
divergent thinking, novelty seeking, and connectivity operationalized by eight indicators (MC1-MCI8),
adapted from Wahdiniwaty & Firmansyah (2026); Hartika et al., (2023); Popa et al., (2020). Customer mix
(CM) includes people (audience), personality, perception, and participation, which are developed with ten
indicators (CM1-CM10), as documented in the research of Pitri et al., (2025); Jackson & Ahuja (2016).
Competitiveness (CP) consists of productivity, sales, technology adoption, innovation capability,
operational performance, and organizational growth, which are operationalized with twelve indicators
(CP1-CP12), adapting the concept of competitiveness and empirical parameter development from Porter
et al., (2007); Porter & Magretta (2014); Firmansyah et al., (2024); Bastos et al., (2023); Le & Ikram
(2022), see Appendix.
From a customer perspective, competitiveness indicators provide a snapshot of observed or perceived
competitive outcomes and capabilities through products, services, marketing activities, technology-
enabled interactions, and changes in SMEs' market offerings. SME competitiveness is measured from a
market-oriented and customer-perceived perspective. This perspective emphasizes that customers value
observable manifestations of competitiveness over objective internal ﬁnancial or operational statistics.
Therefore, the competitiveness construct and its parameters as outlined in the questionnaire in the form
of a research instrument align with the marketing orientation of this study, which logically and practically
represents the clarity of customers' assessment of the construct.
Data analysis
To test and develop the theory of predictive relationships between the exogenous and endogenous latent
variables built into the model, the research data parameters were estimated using Partial Least Squares-
Structural Equation Modeling 4 (PLS-SEM4). Data analysis was conducted in two stages, as is common
in SmartPLS-SEM estimation models: the measurement model phase and the hypothesis testing phase
(Hair et al., 2011).
Outer phase measurement model
Page 12/38

Evaluating convergent and discriminant validity is an essential part of the measurement model phase.
Convergent validity assesses validity indicator criteria based on the standard weighting factor (λ) value;
which is λ > 0.7 (Firmansyah & Wahdiniwaty, 2023), and construct reliability (Cα), and average variance
extracted (AVE) ≥ 0.5 (Black & Babin, 2019), and Composite Reliability (C.R) ≥ 0.7 (Dijkstra & Henseler,
2015; Mâță et al., 2020. Meanwhile, divergent validity is carried out by evaluating the cross-loading value,
kriteria Fornell-Larcker (Fornell & Larcker, 1981; Joe F Hair et al., 2024), and Heterotrait-Monotrait
(HTMT) < 0.90 (Joe F Hair et al., 2024; Ringle et al., 2023; Franke & Sarstedt, 2019; and Rönkkö et al.,
2023) or less than 1 (Henseler et al., 2016; Manley et al., 2021; Becker et al., 2023). To minimize
statistical bias in prediction, full collinearity was applied among the parameters of each construct and
inter-construct collinearity at a VIF threshold of < 3.3 (Kock & Dow, 2025; Kock & Lynn, 2012), and
predictive correlations between latent variables in the constructed model were also evaluated at a
threshold of r < 0.85.
Overall model ﬁt (Goodness of Fit/GoF) testing was conducted by evaluating predictive power using the
R2 parameter criterion (Henseler et al., 2016; Hair et al., 2019; Wahdiniwaty, Soegoto, et al., 2025), and
effect size using the f2 criterion (Cohen, 1988; Hair et al., 2019), as well as model ﬁt using the
Standardized Root Mean Residual (SRMR) criteria at a threshold value of SRMR < 0.08 (Hu & Bentler,
1999), or looser at the SRMR threshold < 1.0, and Normed Fit Index (NFI) at the NFI ≥ 0.90 threshold, the
model goodness of ﬁt criteria are met (Henseler et al., 2016; J. Hair et al., 2017; Wahdiniwaty, Soegoto, et
al., 2025), Chi–square (χ2) is also evaluated with lower value criteria that are expected to be achieved
(Firmansyah, 2024).
Inner model – Hyphotesis testing
The inner model, also known as the hypothesis testing phase, was conducted using a bootstrapping
procedure. Direct and indirect effects based on the mediation effect were assessed by evaluating the t-
statistic (t-stat) at a signiﬁcance level of p = 0.05, using the t-stat value criterion and a critical number of
1.96 as the lower limit parameter to compare the decision-making criteria for supporting and accepting
the hypothesis. The strength of the mediation effect was evaluated using the VAF (Value Adjustment
Factor) criterion. The VAF assessment refers to parameters that consider the magnitude of the
coeﬃcients of the direct effect, indirect effect, and total effect. A VAF value of less than 20% (VAF < 20%)
indicates no mediation effect, a VAF value of more than 20% but less than 80% indicates a partial
mediation effect, and a VAF value of more than 80% (VAF > 80%) indicates a full mediation effect (Bagga
et al., 2023; Firmansyah & Wahdiniwaty, 2023).

Discussion
The research ﬁndings adequately conﬁrm that aﬃliate marketing has a positif and signiﬁcant direct
effect on the competitiveness of SMEs. Perceptions of e-commerce platform usability on social media
platforms, social environment, observability, purchase intention, audience engagement, number of
reviews, sales rate, and commissions, as integrated parameters in the empirical concept of aﬃliate
marketing, have been shown to positively impact the competitiveness of fashion SMEs in tourist areas.
This ﬁnding aligns with research ﬁndings from Hammoud et al., (2026); Tanuwijaya & Princes (2025);
and Lányi et al., (2021) that aﬃliate marketing programs that leverage the development of e-commerce
platforms generated by technological advances signiﬁcantly beneﬁt the competitiveness and
sustainability of SMEs. The social media platform YouTube is a familiar platform for YouTubers and
inﬂuencers to develop their advertising and content.
The latest trend, Facebook Shop, is starting to develop, but TikTok is often utilized by aﬃliates in aﬃliate
marketing programs that contribute to the expansion of digital transformation practices of business
models and strengthen the competitiveness of SMEs in the digital economy era (Andrianata et al., 2025;
and Santoso, 2025). More interestingly, the SME product content strategy developed in online marketing
communications by fashion product aﬃliates based on the uniqueness and local wisdom of tourist
areas through video broadcasts and live videos presented attractively on social media platforms can
inspire, motivate, increase follower reviews, purchase intentions, and the number of sales. The effect of
SME product owners, with increased sales volume, empirically illustrates an increase in market share
that affects productivity and proﬁtability as well as the competitive strength of their businesses.
Conversely, successful aﬃliates beneﬁt from large commission percentages and bonuses received
based on the increase in gross market value (GMV) at each level of their sales targets.
These ﬁndings also reveal evidence that marketers' creative innovation has a direct, positive, and
signiﬁcant impact on SME competitiveness. Skilled marketing strategy competency plays a crucial role
in effective marketing activities. Divergent thinking and the intensive pursuit of novelty, as new marketing
methods, develop from new ideas that continually emerge in marketers' minds. With broader marketer
connectivity, these new ideas, creativity, and innovation are transformed into superior marketing
strategies, offering new, distinct appeals, encouraging inspiration and engagement from customers and
target audiences across various marketing channels, leading to high conversion rates, product
purchases, and sales, which can beneﬁt SMEs in the tourism sector. Heilman et al., (2003), as cited by
Hartika et al., (2023), deﬁnes creative innovation as a superior value creation process. The capabilities
and skills of creative and innovative, customer-centric marketers are crucial to managing marketing
strategies, enabling effective marketing performance and success in attracting customers and
increasing sales. This is in line with the ﬁndings of studies by Darwish et al., (2024); Hien & Tam (2025);
Otache (2024); Ocasal et al., (2022); Taneo et al., (2022), which emphasize that business
competitiveness in SMEs is inseparable from the important role of creative innovation by marketers in
communicating and promoting their products. Even in e-commerce platform-based marketing practices
on various social media, the creation, management, and utilization of knowledge as market knowledge

management are increasingly integrated with the presence of artiﬁcial intelligence (AI) (traditional AI and
generative AI) which is useful as a sophisticated and real-time tool for analyzing market trends,
customer behavior, and competitors so that various creative ideas, necessary innovations, and
appropriate marketing solutions can be found that enable relevant development in implementing
marketing strategies according to current market needs trends and are able to increase customer
participation and engagement. To achieve success in this market orientation, of course, productive SME
marketers and those who are able to strengthen business competitiveness really need marketing
intelligence as well as creativity and innovation as its foundation.
In practice, aﬃliate marketing further complicates the importance of understanding the various
characteristics of the customer mix to support its success. As a reinforcement, research ﬁndings reveal
evidence that aﬃliate marketing trends have a positive and signiﬁcant effect on the customer mix. The

expectations and preferences for products marketed by aﬃliates on various social media platforms. The
ﬁndings of studies by Sulaj & Pfoertsch (2024); Pitri et al., (2026); Bezhovski (2025); Wichmann et al.,

constitutes a strategic asset within a platform, whether through social media networks, e-commerce
websites, or smartphone applications. This now inﬂuences customer value perceptions (Chaffey & Ellis-

programs to reach a target market that is inherently sensitive and constantly changing in the online
marketing landscape (Pitri et al., 2025). Therefore, offering products to SMEs in the fashion-based
creative industry that meet individual customer expectations and needs, complemented by an engaging
online marketing communication model through aﬃliate marketing on social media platforms, has been
shown to increase active participation and interaction, enhance the effectiveness of the digital marketing

contemporary era, where the circulation and interactivity of audiences on e-commerce platforms on
websites require digital marketers to understand how audience engagement leads to purchases, whether
marketing communications by aﬃliates meet customer preferences, and various product needs, as
mentioned by Charlesworth (2018) and documented in Wahdiniwaty et al., (2023: pp.57–59).
Therefore, digital marketers, aﬃliates, and inﬂuencers need to understand the speciﬁc categories of
each individual audience integrated into the customer mix. The intensity of questions and answers,
responses, and increased reviews from followers of SME products marketed through aﬃliates, for
example on the social media platform TikTok, reﬂect the appeal of the content and the effectiveness of
creative aﬃliate marketing communications in reaching the market. Actual purchase intent can be seen
as the potential success of aﬃliates, which can practically be used as a projection of future sales,
contributing to improving brand image, business reputation, and SME competitiveness.
Consequently, marketers' creative innovation enables the creation of dynamic capabilities in a customer-
centric marketing landscape, reﬂecting marketing practices that must address the primary needs of
customers, with all their unique characteristics. This involves developing successful marketing
strategies with engaging advertising and promotional programs based on customers' personal needs,
expectations, and preferences, which can be studied and analyzed, allowing marketers' creative
innovation to inﬂuence their customer mix. Furthermore, the research ﬁndings provide evidence that
marketers' creative innovation has a signiﬁcant positive impact on the customer mix of fashion-based
SMEs in tourist areas. This ﬁnding is conﬁrmed in line with the research results of Al Khazraje et al.,
(2026); X. Wang (2021); Liao & Tsai (2019).
It is noteworthy that the market segmentation of SMEs seeking fashion products in tourist areas differs
from the market segmentation of casual products in general (Firmansyah et al., 2024), as does the
culinary sector (Wahdiniwaty & Firmansyah, 2026). SME products in this sector rely more on the
uniqueness and wisdom of local culture, with an environmentally friendly business model that supports
green practices and circular economy campaigns that uphold environmental sustainability and respect
the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). However, the products are still designed to follow the latest,
contemporary, and modern trends that are marketed based on geographic segmentation (domestic,
international, and location-speciﬁc tourists), demographic segmentation (age, gender, and income), and

psychographic segmentation (environmentally conscious, self-expressive, and cultural tourists),
including behavioral segmentation (impulse buyers, user loyalty, and user functionality). Based on all of
that and with reference to the conﬁrmation of empirical ﬁndings, this emphasizes two important aspects
related to marketers' creative innovation in relation to the customer mix, both oﬄine and online
marketing communications.
First, the adequacy of creative innovation of marketers allows the speed and accuracy of product
communication in an effort to persuade and attract tourists who are traveling or are at tourist locations,
because this type of marketer has dynamic behavior, adaptive and innovative culture, and is rich in
interesting and realistic marketing ideas that can be developed so as to create good customer
relationship management (CRM) (Wahdiniwaty & Firmansyah, 2026; Wahdiniwaty et al., 2023), which has
the potential to inﬂuence the customer mix and has implications for the growth of customer attention
and purchasing interest in purchasing decisions.
Second, based on divergent thinking, the search for novelty, and the connectivity of marketers, marketers'
creative innovation allows them to communicate products earlier through digital platforms on various
social media to expand their market with more attractive and always targeted promotional techniques,
even before tourists arrive at tourist locations, they can have information and be interested in the
products offered. The possibility of this aspect is relatively undeniable because marketers with high
innovation creativity always study their customer mix before developing a marketing strategy and
combine it with an evolving marketing mix, such as studying customer proﬁles, individual behaviors of
their target customers, expectations, and personal preferences of their customers so that their
marketing communications will be more focused on potential customers who have been studied and
mapped clearly and they become proﬁcient in digital marketing (Al Khazraje et al., 2026; Pitri et al., 2025;
Hartika et al., 2023). It is not surprising that the implications are able to increase customer participation
and engagement as well as customer purchasing interest, thus supporting efforts to maintain and
improve competitive advantages in SMEs.
Furthermore, customer mix aspects are now increasingly considered in marketing practices among
SMEs. The customer mix parameters veriﬁed in this study are proven to provide a positive and signiﬁcant
contribution to the competitiveness of SMEs in the culinary and fashion-based creative industries in
tourist areas. From a modern marketing perspective, competitiveness, which is shaped by parameters
such as productivity, sales and market share, technology adoption, innovation capability, operational
performance, and organizational growth, can be inﬂuenced by participation, personality, perception, and
engagement, as empirical parameters of the customer mix. These ﬁndings validate previous research
conducted by Pitri et al., (2025); Suryana (2023); and Jackson & Ahuja (2016). Understanding the
customer mix is crucial for marketers, especially in their interactions and communications on social
media platforms, as it can provide insight into customer behavior and their ongoing tendencies related to
purchasing decisions.

customer mix can be used as strategic and dynamic information to create and market products that truly
offer value to target customers (Pitri et al., 2025) with connectivity and marketing orientation that is

Conclusion
The research ﬁndings reveal evidence that aﬃliate marketing has a signiﬁcant direct effect on the
competitiveness of SMEs in the fashion-based creative industry sector in tourist areas. Similarly,
marketers' creative innovation is conﬁrmed to have a positive and signiﬁcant direct effect on the
competitiveness of SMEs in this sector. Aﬃliate marketing and marketers' creative innovation also have
a positive and signiﬁcant effect on customer mix. At the same time, the ﬁndings conﬁrm that customer
mix has a positive and signiﬁcant effect on SME competitiveness. The research ﬁndings also conﬁrm
that customer mix has a positive partial mediation effect on the relationship between aﬃliate marketing
and SME business competitiveness in this sector. Customer mix also has a positive partial mediation
effect in the relationship model between marketers' creative innovation and SME competitiveness.
Impressively, the positive partial mediation effect of customer mix leads to a moderate mediation effect
in strengthening the relationship between aﬃliate marketing and SME competitiveness. These ﬁndings
provide suﬃcient valuable evidence to conﬁrm the current empirical gap that most of the customer mix
parameters act as important facilitators that bridge the creation of the effectiveness of e-commerce
platform-based aﬃliate marketing trends that are able to inﬂuence competitiveness in SMEs, and several
other customer mix parameters can facilitate the role of creative innovation marketers in inﬂuencing the
competitiveness of SMEs in the fashion sector of tourist areas in a more dynamic modern marketing
landscape in the era of digital disruption.
Theoretical and practical implications
This study contributes to expanding valuable insights in the modern marketing management literature.
Empirically, this study also expands empirical evidence that offers a practical framework for business
Page 28/38

managers and marketers, both professional marketers in internal business functions, especially with the
involvement of independent marketers (aﬃliates or inﬂuencers), on how the relevance of customer mix
can bridge the empirical gap to strengthen the relationship between e-commerce platform-based aﬃliate
marketing trends, marketers’ creative innovation and business competitiveness in increasing customer
participation, engagement, and purchase intentions through social media channels.
Limitations and recommendations for future research agendas
The limitation of this study is that the predictive model built only explores the relationship between
aﬃliate marketing trends and the competitiveness of fashion sector SMEs in tourist areas based on
customer mix mediation, with a limited sample size, only considering the business model of fashion
sector SMEs that have shifted to digital in online marketing practices based on digital platforms in
several social media, so that statistical bias may occur for generalization at a broader level. In addition,
methodological aspect approaches, sample size expansion, comparative models for product variations
and aﬃliate marketing effectiveness, market segmentation and customer mix, as well as considering
other relevant empirical latent variables that have the potential to increase customer participation and
engagement, market share, sales levels, and competitiveness in the business landscape in the era of
digital disruption, can all be considered for future research development agendas.
