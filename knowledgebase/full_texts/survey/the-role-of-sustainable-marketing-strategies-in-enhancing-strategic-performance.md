1. INTRODUCTION
The tourism industry plays a vital role in both local and global economic development by generating
employment opportunities, increasing foreign exchange earnings, and contributing to GDP growth [1]. In recent years,
tourism companies have increasingly adopted sustainable marketing strategies as an important approach to strengthening
competitive advantage and achieving long-term growth [2]. Service in the tourism sector refers to a set of intangible
activities provided to satisfy customer needs and expectations [3]. Therefore, improving service quality remains a primary
objective for tourism organizations to ensure customer satisfaction and loyalty.
ABSTRACT:
This study examines the role of sustainable marketing strategies—customer-oriented marketing, customer value
marketing, innovative marketing, mission-driven marketing, and social marketing —in enhancing strategic
performance through the dimensions of financial perspective, customer perspective, internal processes, and learning
and growth in tourism companies in Erbil, Kurdistan Region of Iraq. Despite the growing importance of sustainability
in tourism, limited empirical studies have explored how sustainable marketing strategies contribute to strategic
performance in tourism companies within the Kurdistan Region of Iraq, creating a clear research gap. The study
addresses this gap by providing empirical evidence from the local tourism sector and highlighting the strategic value
of sustainable marketing practices. A descriptive-analytical approach was adopted, and data were collected through
an electronically distributed questionnaire from 96 managers working in tourism companies in Erbil. Statistical
analysis was conducted using SPSS V.24 and AMOS V.24. The results revealed significant positive correlations and
a strong positive effect of sustainable marketing strategies on strategic performance. The study contributes by
demonstrating that adopting sustainable marketing strategies enhances organizational performance, improves
customer relationships, supports environmental responsibility, and strengthens long-term competitiveness in tourism
companies.
Keywords: Sustainable Marketing Strategies, Strategic Performance, Tourism Companies.

In the context of globalization and growing environmental challenges, marketing strategies are increasingly
aligned with the principles of sustainable development [4]. Sustainable marketing combines economic efficiency with
environmental protection and social responsibility, making it an essential factor for long -term organizational
competitiveness [5]. Marketing research supports the successful implementation of sustainable marketing strategies by
helping organizations identify market trends, understand changing customer preferences, and design effective business
models [6]. In addition, communication tools play an important role in promoting sustainability values among customers
and stakeholders, which strengthens corporate image and increases trust in products and services [7].
Despite the growing academic interest in sustainable marketing, most previous studies have focused on
manufacturing, retail, and hospitality sectors in developed economies, while limited attention has been given to tourism
companies in developing regions such as Iraq and the Kurdistan Region. In particular, empirical studies examining the
relationship between sustainable marketing strategies and strategic performance remain scarce. This creates a clear
research gap, as tourism organizations in Kurdistan operate in a highly competitive environment and require modern
strategic approaches to improve performance and maintain sustainability.
The Kurdistan Region of Iraq represents an important context for this study because tourism is considered one
of its most vital economic sectors, with significant potential for investment, employment, and regional development.
However, many tourism companies still rely on traditional marketing approaches and have not fully integrated sustainable
marketing strategies into their operations. This makes the region a suitable environment for investigating how sustainable
marketing can enhance strategic performance.
This study contributes to the literature by providing empirical evidence on the role of sustainable marketing
strategies in enhancing strategic performance in tourism companies in Erbil, Kurdistan Region of Iraq. It extends previous
studies by examining sustainable marketing through five dimensions: customer-oriented marketing, customer value
marketing, innovative marketing, mission-driven marketing, and social marketing, and linking them with strategic
performance dimensions based on the balanced scorecard perspective. The study also offers practical recommendations
for tourism companies to strengthen sustainability practices and improve long-term competitiveness.
2. GENERAL FRAMEWORK OF THE STUDY AND ITS METHODOLOGY
2.1. Research Problem
The research problem arises from the increasing intensity of competition, rapid market changes, and
globalization, which have forced organizations to improve their services and align them more closely with customer
needs and expectations. Tourism companies, in particular, operate in highly dynamic environments where customer
preferences continuously change, making strategic adaptation essential for survival and growth [1], [2]. In such
conditions, organizations are required to adopt modern marketing approaches that not only improve customer satisfaction
but also ensure long-term competitiveness and sustainability [5].
Recent literature emphasizes that sustainable marketing strategies have become an important tool for enhancing
organizational performance by integrating economic, environmental, and social dimensions into marketing activities [6],
[7]. These strategies help organizations create customer value, strengthen relationships with stakeholders, and improve
their strategic positioning in competitive markets [8], [24]. At the same time, strategic performance has gained significant
attention as a comprehensive measure of organizational success through financial outcomes, customer satisfaction,
internal processes, and learning and growth perspectives [34], [49].
Despite this importance, many tourism companies in Erbil, Kurdistan Region of Iraq, still rely on traditional
marketing practices and have not fully adopted sustainable marketing strategies as part of their strategic management
approach. In addition, the relative novelty of sustainable marketing strategies in the local business environment has
created ambiguity regarding their practical application and their impact on strategic performance. This gap highlights the
need for empirical investigation, especially in the tourism sector, which is considered one of the most vital sectors for
regional economic development.
Accordingly, the present study seeks to examine the role of sustainable marketing stra tegies through their
dimensions: customer-oriented marketing, customer value marketing, innovative marketing, mission-driven marketing,
and social marketing in enhancing strategic performance. Based on this issue, the research problem can be expressed
through the following main question:

2. 6 Study Methodology
Achieving the theoretical and practical objectives of the study requires a clear methodology that guides the
research process and determines its scientific direction at all stages. Accordingly, the study adopted the descriptive-
analytical approach in both its theoretical and practical aspects, as it is considered appropriate for examining the
relationship between sustainable marketing strategies and strategic performance in tourism companies. The descriptive
method was used to review the theoretical literature and clarify the concepts and dimensions of the study variables,
namely sustainable marketing strategies and strategic performance.
In the applied aspect, the analytical method was employed to analyze the data collected from the study sample
and to test the study hypotheses using appropriate statistical techniques. A structured questionnaire was specifically
designed for this purpose and distributed electronically to managers working in tourism companies in Erbil, Kurdistan
Region of Iraq.
The study population consisted of approximately 300 managers working in tourism companies in Erbil. A
convenience sampling technique was adopted due to the accessibility of respondents and the practical limitations related
to time and data collection. Based on the recommendations of sampling adequacy in social science research, obtaining
responses from at least 30% of the population is considered acceptable for statistical analysis in descriptive studies.
Accordingly, 110 questionnaires were distributed, of which 96 valid questionnaires were returned and used for analysis,
representing a response rate of 87.3% and approximately 32% of the total study population. This sample size was
considered sufficient to achieve reliable statistical results and to support the use of SPSS V.24 and AMOS V.24 for
hypothesis testing and model analysis.

3. LITERATURE REVIEW
3.1. Sustainable Marketing Strategies
3.1.1. The Definitions of Sustainable Marketing Strategies:
The concept of sustainable marketing emerged in the mid-1970s, alongside the growing interest of researchers
in the role of marketing in ongoing environmental and social changes [8]. This prompted organizations and institutions
to allocate resources toward addressing various social and environmental considerations while also satisfying the needs
and desires of their customers [9].
As sustainability assumes a central role in consumer decision -making, organizations are progressively
integrating sustainable marketing strategies into their operations [10]. Sustainable marketing involves promoting products
and services that fulfill consumer needs while simultaneously supporting environmental, social, and economic well-being
[11]. Given the increasing significance of sustainability in the contemporary business landscape, organizations of varying
sizes and ownership structures are allocating resources to incorporate environmental and social considerations into their
operations [12].

In addition, Sustainable Marketing Strategies represents a new marketing approach that is expected to replace
traditional marketing, lead to sustainable development, enable organizational communication with customers, deliver
added value, and preserve and enhance human and environmental resources across various domains [13]. In general, it is
a marketing approach that seeks to achieve sustainable development through marketing activities [14]. It is based on the
principles of environmental and social responsibility and aims to create value for the customer by encouraging changes
in consumption patterns in a sustainable manner [15].
Sustainable Marketing Strategies has also been described by [16] as marketing policies that provide genuine
support for sustainable development. According to [17] is a set of processes and activities aimed at building long-term
relationships with customers through the establishment of marketing policies that ensure the offering of a sustainable
product that supports environmental sustainability. Sustainable Marketing Strategies is an integrated approach
encompassing ethical and green marketing, corporate social responsibility (CSR), and sustainable branding  [18].
Adopting a sustainable business orientation can strengthen the company’s image, reputation, and consumer awareness,
ultimately contributing to a competitive advantage [19].
After reviewing the concepts presented in the above the researchers defines Sustainable Marketing
Strategies as the development of the organization’s vision regarding the markets it focuses on, setting and
developing objectives, and preparing marketing pro grams that achieve organizational positioning while
responding to customer requirements in the target market.
3.1.2. Significance and objectives of Sustainable Marketing Strategies:
In a business context, the integration and balance of economic, environmental, and social dimensions of
sustainability are essential [20]. Sustainable marketing extends beyond green marketing by emphasizing the development
and maintenance of enduring and profitable relationships with customers, society, and the natural environment. Outcomes
of sustainable marketing initiatives encompass economic, environmental, and social sustainability [19].
Among the main objectives that companies strive to achieve are gaining a competitive advantage and fostering
positive work-oriented initiatives by presenting a favorable image of the company [21]. This can result in higher customer
loyalty, enhanced trust in the company, and a reduction in negative perceptions of the organization [22]. Sustainable
marketing strategies contribute to financial stability by strengthening customer loyalty, improving brand reputation, and
reducing regulatory uncertainties [23]. Integrating sustainable marketing into an organization’s strategy can also lead to
numerous benefits, including increased customer engagement, enhanced brand image, and the creation of a stronger link
between the company’s image and sustainable purchase intentions [24].
Sustainable marketing strategies objective is to create value that supports societal development, protects the
environment, and fulfills customer expectations, thereby becoming an integral component of corporate strategy [25]. In
contrast to traditional marketing, which often emphasizes short-term customer satisfaction, sustainable marketing seeks
to establish long-term relationships with customers through the cultivation of loyalty and trust [9].
Consequently, the researchers believes that sustainable marketing extends beyond a mere tool for
boosting sales; it represents a critical component of corporate social and environmental responsibility, aimed at
improving the quality of life for present and future generations while ensuring economic stability and growth.
3.1.3. Dimensions of Sustainable Marketing Strategies:
The study adopts the five dimensions of sustainable marketing strategies based on the framework of Kotler and Armstrong
(2011), which includes customer-oriented marketing, customer value marketing, innovative marketing, mission-driven
marketing, and social marketing [9]. These dimensions represent the integration of customer satisfaction, innovation,
social responsibility, and long-term organizational sustainability.
1. Customer-oriented marketing: Customer-oriented marketing is a marketing approach that focuses on
understanding and satisfying customer needs and interests by placing the customer at the center of all marketing
activities [26]. It requires organizations to design and organize their mar keting efforts from the customer’s
perspective and to continuously identify and respond to current and future customer expectations [23]. This approach
helps organizations improve customer satisfaction and strengthen long-term relationships.
2. Customer value marketing: Customer value marketing emphasizes creating and enhancing value for customers
based on their changing needs and expectations [16]. Organizations must pay close attention to customer complaints

and feedback, as ignoring them may lead to customer dissatisfaction and loss of loyalty [27]. This dimension aims
to strengthen customer relationships and build a positive organizational image compared to competitors.
3. Innovative marketing: Innovative marketing refers to improving existing products and services or developing new
ones by applying new ideas, creative solutions, and modern marketing practices [12]. It includes significant changes
in product design, packaging, pricing, promotion, distribution, and market expansion [28]. This dimension enables

4. Mission-driven marketing: Mission-driven marketing refers to aligning marketing activities with the broader

change for social benefit and public welfare [31]. It is closely related to sustainable development and corporate social
responsibility, as it seeks to balance organizational objectives with the long-term interests of society [32]. This
dimension enhances the organization’s role in promoting positive social values and improving community well-
being.
3.2. Strategic Performance
3.2.1. The definitions of Strategic Performance:
Researchers have differed in their opinions regarding the definition of strategic performance. Despite this
difference, many researchers express strategic performance as the organization's ability to achieve its desired goals, as it
reflects the success or failure of organizations in achieving those goals [33]. Strategic performance has been defined as
the process by which the results achieved are compared with the results that the organization seeks to achieve through
feedback in order to take the required actions [34]. The degree to which an organization accomplishes its long-term goals
by coordinating its resources, strategies, and actions is known as organizational strategic performance [35]. Strategic
performance is the organization’s comprehensive long-term performance, encompassing the attainment of strategic goals
such as market share, profitability, and competitive strategies [36].
It is a multifaceted concept that is driven by a distinct strategic vision and includes competitive positioning,
operational effectiveness, and financial results [37].One important measure of an organization's capacity for adaptation
in a changing environment is its strategic performance [38]. Based on this, the researchers defines strategic
performance as the organization’s actual outcomes or outputs compared to the desired results over a given period.
3.2.2. The Importance of Strategic Performance
The importance of strategic performance lies in that it is central role in predicting the success or failure of
organizations in their strategic decisions and plans, due to the lack of a unified set of criteria that can explain all aspects
of organizational performance [39].
 Strategic performance contribute to building a competitive advantage by comparing an organization's performance,
including its strengths and weaknesses, with the performance and strengths and weaknesses of similar and competing
organizations [40]. It is crucial to an organization's success or failure, as it provides the information required for the
strategy formulation process and helps identify problems by monitoring and evaluating the business strategy [41]. It
contributes to minimizing costs by establishing performance standards, maintaining defect rates, and preserving quality
as well as it enhances the organization's ability to adapt its strategic options to align with ongoing changes in the
organization's internal and external environments [42].
3.2.3. Dimensions of Strategic Performance:
Based on the researchers’ review of relevant scholarly literature, strategic performance is most commonly
measured through the Balanced Scorecard framework, which includes four core dimensions: financial perspective,
customer perspective, internal processes perspective, and learning and growth perspective [34], [49]. These dimensions
provide a comprehensive evaluation of organizational performance by integrating both financial and non -financial
indicators and are widely used in strategic management research [35], [36].
The financial perspective focuses on economic outcomes such as profitability, revenue growth, and cost
efficiency, which reflect the financial success of the organization [39], [41]. The customer perspective examines customer

satisfaction, retention, and market share as key indicators of external performance [44], [47]. The internal processes
perspective emphasizes the efficiency and effectiveness of organizational operations and service delivery systems [45],
[48]. Finally, the learning and growth perspective focuses on human capital, innovation, and organizational learning as
drivers of long-term sustainability and improvement [40], [43].
Accordingly, these four dimensions were adopted in the present study due to their strong theoretical foundation,
their widespread use in previous empirical research, and their suitability for evaluating strategic performance in tourism

1. The Financial Perspective: it is closely linked to organizational strategy and its execution, and it plays a critical
role in supporting the implementation of improvement plans [43]. Moreover, financial objectives vary according to
the organization’s life cycle stages growth, survival, and maturity. During the growth stage, financial goals primarily
emphasize revenue expansion, the acquisition of new customers, the introduction of new products or services, and
the maintenance of a high level of excellence in continuous improvement processes related to systems and employee
capabilities [44].
2. The Customer perspective: focuses on the views of the beneficiaries of an organization’s services and how the
organization seeks to capture their attention. Customer satisfaction is a top priority for many organizations, especially
in today’s highly competitive business environment, and it can serve as a critical key performance indicator of
organizational success [45]. This perspective allows the organization to align internal processes, services, and
products with both current and anticipated future demands [46].
3. The Internal Process Perspective: This perspective reflects the organization’s capability to achieve its strategic
objectives for both shareholders and customers. This perspective also assists organizations in identifying and
focusing on critical and value-creating processes and activities [34]. This perspective also involves internal
operational processes to ensure the highest possible product quality, meeting market requirements in terms of both
quantity and quality. Key performance indicators include process improvements and supply chain management [47].
4. The Learning and Growth Perspective: This perspective focuses on identifying how human capital managed in a
manner that secures the organization’s future sustainability [48]. It reflects the organization’s capacity for renewal
and innovation, as progress in achieving and improving objectives requires sustained investment in employee
retraining, the evaluation and development of information systems and technologies, and the st ructuring of
organizational procedures and workflows [49]. Employee morale is essential for enhancing productivity, quality, and
customer satisfaction. Moreover, effective retention recognized as vital since it develops the organization’s
intellectual capital, representing a significant non-financial asset [50].

5. CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
5.1 Conclusions
1. The statistical results for diagnosing the study sample regarding sustainable marketing strategies and its dimensions
in the tourism companies under investigation indicate that the overall weighted mean of sustainable marketing
exceeded the hypothetical mean. This reflects the respondents’ awareness of the importance of the role played by
sustainable marketing in achieving long-term, continuous success for the tourism companies studied in Erbil City.
2. The results demonstrated that tourism companies place considerable emphasis on customer-oriented marketing,
recognizing the customer as the central element of the market and a key determinant of their success and
advancement. These companies strive to meet and satisfy customers’ current and future needs and preferences, while
enhancing their ability to gain customer trust through continuous communication and engagement.
3. The results indicated that tourism companies demonstrate both a need and a willingness to adopt innovative
marketing in order to implement new ideas and introduce continuous improvements to products and services. This
includes adopting novel approaches to marketing and distribution, as well as utilizing modern communication
methods to inform customers about the services offered, with the aim of achieving customer satisfaction and
sustaining long-term relationships through the adoption of innovative marketing and the generation of new ideas.
4. The findings indicate that tourism companies are aware of the importance of mission-driven marketing, as reflected
in their efforts to translate their mission into written statements or actionable marketing policies to achieve their
primary objectives. This approach also aligns with the evolving preferences of society, enabling them to differentiate
their activities. Furthermore, these companies take into account the need to explore and assess the impact of
environmental changes in their various dimensions, as well as conditions of instability, and seek appropriate means
to address these changes to ensure their continuity.
5. The results of the descriptive analysis revealed high levels of strategic performance implementation, indicating that
the surveyed tourism companies are capable of achieving strategic performance outcomes, developing a qualified
workforce with new knowledge, and applying it in future situations to attain high performance in the tourism sector.
6. The findings showed a high implementation levels of internal processes dimension and the customer perspective of
strategic performance, with a high degree of consistency. This reflects strong managerial attention within tourism
companies to supporting customer orientation and internal processes, as these companies utilize technologies and
internal procedures that help employees perform their tasks effectively.
7. The statistical results revealed a strong and statistically significant correlation between sustainable marketing
strategies and strategic performance across their respective dimensions. This indicates that tourism companies adopt
sustainable marketing strategies, which support all managerial levels in utilizing strategic tools and techniques within
entrepreneurial processes, thereby contributing to the enhancement of their strategic performance.
8. The statistical results showed a significant impact of sustainable marketing on enhancing the strategic performance
of the tourism companies under study. This indicates that the adoption of sustainable marketing by tourism
companies has a direct impact on improving strategic performance across its dimensions.
5.2 Recommendations
1. The application of sustainable marketing strategies in tourism companies should be strengthened, and their
dimensions adapted to their operational reality, as they represent modern marketing approaches that aim to protect
the environment, conserve natural resources for future generations, manage customer relationships, and achieve
societal well-being, which ultimately leads to organizational excellence.
2. Tourism company management should increase attention to innovative marketing by offering new services based on
information technology, applying continuous improvements in operations, and developing appropriate strategies to
guide functions and marketing activities, as well as organizing projects that reflect their superiority over competitors.
3. It is necessary to enhance the attention of tourism companies to mission-driven marketing by clarifying the mission
statement in a broad rather than narrow sense, regularly reviewing the company’s mission, and determining the extent
of its suitability to its conditions and objectives.
4. Enhancing tourism companies’ focus on customer value marketing can be achieved through continuous technological
improvements that make it possible to meet customer needs, while improving service quality in accordance with
international standards and specifications.
5. Strengthening social marketing in tourism companies can be achieved by establishing new specialized departments
concerned with consumer rights and social responsibility toward society and customers, through offering

complementary initiatives and increasing participation in charitable activities, in order to enhance their distinction
among other organizations.
6. Maintaining the high levels of the strategic performance variable is necessary by encouraging employees to generate
innovative ideas and participate in their implementation, which contributes to developing a qualified workforce with
new knowledge that can be applied in future situations and achieve high performance in the tourism sector.
7. Tourism companies should also pay attention to their human resources and develop existing staff by sending capable
and qualified employees to training courses, scientific missions, and participation in academic conferences in the
tourism field.
