 The fourth industrial revolution has created economic prospects in
marketing analytics techniques and measurements. For originality, indigenous
corporate research, the case study is on a cement company that is part of a hybrid
conglomerate, which has a business and social welfare perspective. The chosen
corporation is part of a developing economy's top ten corporate conglomerates.
The company's distinctiveness lies in its control by a combination of military
personnel and the public, with a focus on business and welf are. It has not been
extensively investigated due to the severe rules developed by the institutional
culture of the forces. Purposive sampling was used since the problem required
specialists from varied domains with relevant and remarkable expertise for
"knowledge-thickness." Interviews were conducted with the company's senior,
middle, and lower executives and officers. Response content and theme mapping
were done in NVivo. Marketing analytics is a strategy shift that requires a clear
connection between organizational transformation, matching strategies and
projects, relevant metrics, field difficulties, and remedial actions. Failures are
inevitable when change occurs in silos without a rigorous transition. The study's
significant contribution is that it shows the "How" and "What" of ecosystem
change through marketing analytics, according to Rouse (2005), by pattern
identification and thick explanation of a significant set of measurements, multi-
layered challenges, and remedies.

5 Discussion Company of the Case
Businesses in the cement industry can benefit from a digital transformation (Adjie
Eryadi & Nizar Hidayanto, 2020). The first step in transforming and linking performance
is for companies to recognize the need to do so . When these flaws are exposed, they can
no longer provide a competitive edge in the market. According to Rouse's (2005)
theoretical framework, the identification of value deficiencies is a crucial step in enterprise
transformation. This is followed by a c oncentrated effort on the necessary pr ocesses,
personnel, and technology to facilitate such transformation. Rouse (2005) is considered the
primary reference for the methodology of digital transformation.
Senior management's recognition of the digital transformation's significance sparked a
series of meetings to design the digital twin  of the current operations and the transition to
the new process, which presents significant challenges.  To that end, ACL's marketing
division was a pioneer in this field since management wanted to make decisions based on
data, supported by reports that w ere both insightful and easy to implement thanks to the
