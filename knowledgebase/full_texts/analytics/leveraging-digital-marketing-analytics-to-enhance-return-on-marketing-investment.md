ABSTRACT:
Digital marketing has become an important tool for startups to promote their products and services, reach target customers, and improve business performance.
Startups in Vijayawada are increasingly using digital marketing platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, Google Ads, websites, and email marketing to attract
customersandincrease sales. However, spendingmoney ondigitalmarketingdoesnot always guarantee the expected results.Marketing analytics therefore plays
animportantroleinmeasuringcampaignperformanceandunderstandingwhethermarketinginvestmentsaregeneratingpositive returns.
This study examines how digital marketing analytics may help startups in Vijayawada improve their Return on Marketing Investment (ROMI). An important
noteonthedata:the empiricalanalysisuses83simulatedquestionnaireresponses,notdatacollectedfromrealstartups.Thestudyshouldthereforebereadasan
illustrative, simulation-based application of the research design, and its results do not describe real startups. The responses were analysed using frequencies,
means,one-sampleWilcoxontests,Spearmancorrelation,multipleregression,andKruskal–WallisandMann–Whitneytests.
In the simulated dataset, social media marketing (79.5%), content marketing (53.0%), and email marketing (44.6%) are the most used channels. Analytics use is
moderate (mean3.20outof5),andperceived improvementinROMIismoderately positive (mean3.33; 49.4%agree).Use ofanalyticsisstronglyandpositively
related to ROI measurement (ρ =.74), marketing performance (ρ = .73), budget efficiency (ρ = .62), improved ROMI(ρ = .55), and staff analytical capability (ρ
= .50). Analytics use is the strongest predictor of ROMI improvement in regression (β = .47), and staff capability and channel insight add to the explanation of
overall outcomes. Reported challenges, firm age, firm size, role, and number of channels are not significantly related to analytics use. Intention to increase
analytics use is high (mean 4.05) and is unrelated to current use. The paper discusses what such a pattern would imply for startups if it were confirmed with real
data,andrecommendsthatthesameinstrumentbeadministeredtorealstartupsinVijayawada.

1:INTRODUCTION
1.1 Introduction
In the present business environment, digital marketing has become an essential part of business growth and development. The increasing use of the
internet,smartphones,andsocialmediahas changedthe waybusinesses communicatewithcustomers. Digitalmarketing allows businesses topromote
their products and services through online platforms such as social media, search engines, websites, email, and online advertisements. Compared with
traditional marketing methods, digital marketing can provide businesses with opportunities to reach specific customer groups, communicate quickly,
andmonitorcampaignperformance.
Startups play an important role in creating employment opportunities, introducing new products and services, and supporting economic development.
However, startups often face challenges such as limited financial resources, strong competition, low brand awareness, and difficulties in attracting
customers. For these businesses, using marketing budgets effectively is important. They need to select suitable marketing channels and understand
whichactivitiescontributetocustomeracquisition,sales,andbusinessgrowth.

Digital marketing analytics helps businesses collect, measure, and analyse data generated through their online marketing activities. It provides
information aboutcustomer behaviour, advertisement performance, website visits, social media engagement,leads, conversions, and sales. Tools such
as Google Analytics, Meta Business Suite, and advertising dashboards help businesses evaluate their marketing campaigns and identify areas that
requireimprovement.
One of the important concepts associated with marketing analytics is Return on Marketing Investment (ROMI). ROMI helps businesses evaluate the
financialbenefitsgeneratedbytheirmarketingexpenditure.Bycomparingthereturnsfrommarketingactivitieswiththeirassociatedcosts,startupscan
betterunderstandwhethertheircampaignsarecontributingtobusinessobjectives.Forexample,astartupcanuseanalyticstocomparetheperformance
ofInstagramadvertisementsandGoogleAdsanddeterminewhichchannelgeneratesmorevaluableleadsorsalesrelativetoitscost.
Despite the availability of digital marketing tools, some startups may find it difficult to interpret marketing data, choose appropriate performance
indicators,orconnectonlineengagementwithactualrevenue. Limitedtechnicalknowledge,financial constraints,andalackofskilledemployees may
alsoaffecttheeffectiveuseofanalytics.Asaresult,somestartupsmaycontinuespendingonmarketingcampaignswithoutclearlyunderstandingtheir
financialreturns.
Vijayawada is an important commercial centre in Andhra Pradesh, with businesses operating in areas such as retail, food services, education,
technology,fashion,andotherserviceindustries.Asstartupsinthecityadoptdigitalmarketingtoreachlocalandwidermarkets,understandingtherole
ofanalyticsinimprovingmarketingreturnsbecomesincreasinglyrelevant.
Therefore, the present study, titled “Leveraging Digital Marketing Analytics to Enhance Return on Marketing Investment: A Study of Startups in
Vijayawada,” aims to examine the use of digital marketing analytics amongstartups in Vijayawada and understand how analytical practices influence
marketing performance and investment returns. The study also seeks to identify the challenges faced by startups and suggest ways to improve their
marketingdecisionsthroughdata-drivenstrategies.

5.4 Conclusion
This paper has set out the design and analysis of a study of digital marketing analytics and Return on Marketing Investment among startups in
Vijayawada,andappliedittoasimulateddatasetof83responses.Inthatdataset,socialmedia,content,andemailmarketingarethemostusedchannels,
analytics use is moderate, and perceived ROMI improvement is modestly positive. Analytics use is strongly related to ROI measurement, budget
efficiency,performance,andROMI,andstaffcapabilityisrelatedtobothuseandoutcomes.Reportedchallenges,firmcharacteristics,andchannelmix
arenotrelatedtouse,andstrongintentionstouseanalyticsarenotmatchedbycurrentpractice.
Because the responses are simulated, these results illustrate the method and the kind of conclusions that the design can support; they do not establish
howreal startups in Vijayawada use analytics. The paper’s valueis in the framework, the instrument,and the analyticalprocedure, whichare ready to
be applied to real responses. The conclusions should be confirmed by repeating the analysis with real data before any claim is made about startups in
Vijayawada.
