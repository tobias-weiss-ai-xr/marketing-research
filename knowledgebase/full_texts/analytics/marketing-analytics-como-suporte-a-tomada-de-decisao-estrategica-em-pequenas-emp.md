ABSTRACT

This

study

aimed

to

analyze

how

Marketing

Analytics

can

contribute

to

strategic

decision-making

in

small

businesses,

considering

the

increasing

availability

of

data

from

sales,

customers,

and

digital

channels.

The

research

adopted

a

qualitative

approach

with

an

exploratory

and

descriptive

nature,

developed

through

a

literature

review

of

studies

related

to

Marketing

Analytics,

Business

Intelligence,

Business

Analytics,

Big

Data

Analytics,

data-driven

management,

and

the

decision-making

process.

The

analysis

revealed

that

the

structured

use

of

data

can

expand

understanding

of

consumer

behavior,

facilitate

the

identification

of

market

patterns

and

opportunities,

and

contribute

to

tracking

metrics

and

performance

indicators.

Key

relevant

indicators

for

marketing

management

include

customer

acquisition

and

retention,

conversion,

recurrence,

average

order

value,

and

channel

performance.

It

was

also

found

that

using

analytical

tools

can

reduce

reliance

on

managerial

intuition

alone,

favoring

decisions

that

are

more

well-grounded,

agile,

and

aligned

with

organizational

goals.

However,

its

adoption

in

small

businesses

still

faces

limitations

related

to

financial

resources,

technical

knowledge,

infrastructure,

and

a

data-driven

culture.

It

is

concluded

that

Marketing

Analytics

can

represent

an

important

tool

to

support

small

business

management

when

applied

in

a

manner

compatible

with

their

structure,

combined

with

selecting

relevant

indicators

and

properly

interpreting

information

for

decision-making.

Keywords

MÉTRICAS

E

INDICADORES

NO

No
