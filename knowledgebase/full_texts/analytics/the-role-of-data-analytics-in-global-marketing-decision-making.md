ABSTRACT
The increasing availability of digital data and the rapid advancement of
digital technologies have transformed marketing from intuition -based
practices into evidence-based strategic decision-making. This study aims
to analyze and synthesize the strategic role of data analytics in
supporting global marketing decision -making among export -oriented
small and medium -sized enterprises (SMEs). A qualitative library
research approach was employed by critically reviewing and integrating
contemporary literature on data analytics, marketing analytics, artificial
intelligence, digital transformation, and international marketing. The
analysis was conducted through qualitative content analysis and
thematic synthesis to identif y recurring concepts, compare theoretical
perspectives, and develop an integrated conceptual framework. The
findings indicate that data analytics has evolved beyond a technological
tool into a strategic organizational capability that enables firms to
transform diverse data resources into actionable marketing intelligence.
By integrating customer insights, predictive analytics, and managerial
judgment, organizations can improve market selection, customer
segmentation, pricing strategies, promotional planning , and resource
allocation in increasingly dynamic international markets. The study
further highlights that the effectiveness of data analytics depends not
only on technological adoption but also on complementary
organizational capabilities, including analy tical competencies, digital
culture, leadership commitment, organizational learning, and data
governance. The principal contribution of this research is the
development of an integrated conceptual framework that connects
organizational data resources, data  analytics capability, strategic
marketing intelligence, and global marketing decision -making. This
framework provides a comprehensive theoretical foundation for future
empirical studies while offering practical insights for managers and
policymakers seeking to strengthen the international competitiveness of
export-oriented SMEs through data-driven marketing strategies.

INTRODUCTION
The r apid advancement of digital technologies has fundamentally transformed the global
business landscape, particularly in the way organizations formulate and implement marketing
strategies. The increasing availability of digital data generated through online t ransactions, social
media interactions, customer relationship management systems, mobile applications, and Internet
of Things (IoT) technologies has shifted marketing from intuition-based practices toward evidence-
based decision-making. Organizations are n o longer solely dependent on managerial experience or
conventional market surveys to understand consumer behavior; instead, they increasingly utilize
data analytics to identify market opportunities, predict customer preferences, evaluate competitive
dynamics, and formulate more effective marketing strategies. This transformation has accelerated
the emergence of data -driven marketing, where data becomes a strategic organizational asset
capable of creating sustainable competitive advantage (France & Ghose, 20 19; Wedel & Kannan,
2016; Verhoef et al., 2021). Consequently, marketing decision -making in the digital era has evolved
into a multidisciplinary process integrating marketing knowledge, business intelligence, information
systems, artificial intelligence, and predictive analytics.
The growing importance of data analytics is closely associated with the unprecedented
expansion of global digital markets. Globalization has intensified market competition by reducing
geographical barriers while simultaneously inc reasing customer heterogeneity across countries.
Consumers in different regions exhibit diverse cultural values, purchasing behaviors, digital literacy,
and consumption patterns, making international marketing decisions substantially more complex
than domestic marketing decisions. Export-oriented small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), in
particular, face considerable challenges in identifying attractive international markets,
understanding rapidly changing customer preferences, optimizing digital market ing investments,
and minimizing uncertainty associated with international expansion. In this context, data analytics
provides organizations with analytical capabilities to transform raw data into actionable insights that
support strategic marketing decisio ns, including market selection, customer segmentation, pricing
strategies, promotional planning, and performance evaluation (Kannan, 2017; Davenport et al., 2020;
Kumar et al., 2021).
Recent developments in artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning, cloud computing, and
advanced business analytics have further strengthened the strategic value of data analytics in
marketing. Artificial intelligence enables firms to process massive volumes of structured and
unstructured data, recognize hidden patterns, forecast future market behavior, and automate
complex marketing decisions. Rather than replacing managerial judgment, AI complements human
decision-making by enhancing analytical accuracy, improving forecasting capability, and enabling
real-time responses to dynamic market conditions (Davenport et al., 2020; Huang & Rust, 2021). As
marketing environments become increasingly volatile, organizations require analytical capabilities
that allow continuous monitoring of customer behavior, competitor movements, and environmental
changes. Consequently, data analytics has evolved beyond a technical function into a strategic
capability influencing organizational competitiveness and long -term business performance (Rust,
2020; Verhoef et al., 2021).
The strategic relev ance of data analytics is particularly evident for export -oriented SMEs.
Compared with large multinational corporations, SMEs generally operate with limited financial
resources, constrained managerial expertise, and relatively restricted access to international market
information. Nevertheless, digital transformation has created new opportunities for SMEs to
compete globally through digital platforms, e -commerce ecosystems, and cross -border online
marketplaces. The availability of digital data enables SMEs to identify international demand patterns,

Although the importance of data analytics has been widely acknowledged, the existing
literature remains fragmented across multiple disciplines, including marketing, information systems,
operations management, strategic management, and digital transformation. Many studies primarily
investigate the relationship between big data analytics capability and organizational performance ,
competitive advantage, innovation, or operational efficiency (Wamba et al., 2017; Akter et al., 2016;
Mikalef et al., 2020). Other studies focus on artificial intelligence adoption in marketing (Davenport
et al., 2020; Huang & Rust, 2021), digital transformation (Verhoef et al., 2021; Kraus et al., 2021), or
marketing analytics methodologies (France & Ghose, 2019). However, comparatively fewer studies
synthesize these perspectives into a comprehensive conceptual understanding of how data analytics
supports global marketing decision -making specifically within export -oriented SMEs. Existing
discussions also tend to examine technological implementation separately from strategic marketing
decision processes, creating a conceptual gap regarding the integration of analytical capability,
managerial judgment, and international marketing strategy.
Another limitation observed in previous research concerns methodological orientation.
Numerous publications employ quantitative surveys, structural equation modeling, bib liometric
analyses, or systematic review approaches to evaluate the influence of analytics capabilities on
organizational outcomes. While these approaches contribute valuable empirical evidence, they often
emphasize statistical relationships rather than de veloping an integrative conceptual explanation of
the strategic role of data analytics throughout the global marketing decision -making process.
Consequently, there remains a need for qualitative library research capable of critically synthesizing
contemporary theoretical perspectives and empirical findings into a coherent framework explaining
how analytical capabilities influence international marketing decisions, particularly under
conditions of increasing digital transformation, artificial intelligence ad option, and international
market uncertainty (Davis et al., 2021; Tanjung, 2024; Tawil et al., 2023).
This study addresses these gaps by employing a qualitative library research approach to
synthesize contemporary literature on data analytics, marketing a nalytics, artificial intelligence,
digital transformation, and international marketing. Rather than conducting a systematic literature
review or bibliometric analysis, this research adopts an interpretative and thematic synthesis to
integrate findings from  authoritative publications and construct a comprehensive conceptual
understanding of the strategic role of data analytics in global marketing decision -making. Through
this approach, the study seeks to explain the relationships among analytical capability,  customer
intelligence, market knowledge, predictive decision -making, digital transformation, and export
competitiveness. The proposed synthesis also highlights the interdependence between technological
capability and managerial decision -making, emphasizin g that organizational value is created not
merely through data availability but through the effective translation of analytical insights into
strategic marketing actions (France & Ghose, 2019; Verhoef et al., 2021; Davenport et al., 2020).
The novelty of this research lies in its integrative conceptual perspective. Existing studies
generally investigate marketing analytics, artificial intelligence, business analytics, digital
transformation, export performance, or SME competitiveness as separate streams of  inquiry. This

METHOD
This study employed a qualitative research a pproach using library research as its primary
research design. Library research was selected because the objective of this study was not to test
hypotheses or measure causal relationships empirically, but rather to develop a comprehensive
conceptual understanding of the role of data analytics in global marketing decision -making for
export-oriented small and medium -sized enterprises (SMEs). Through this approach, the study
synthesized theoretical perspectives and empirical findings from scholarly publi cations to explain
how data analytics contributes to strategic marketing decisions within the context of digital
transformation and international business. Unlike Systematic Literature Review (SLR), which follows
a rigid protocol for identifying, screening , and statistically summarizing previous studies, library
research emphasizes interpretative analysis, conceptual integration, and critical evaluation of
relevant literature. Therefore, the present study focused on understanding the relationships among
concepts, identifying emerging themes, comparing scholarly arguments, and constructing an
integrated conceptual framework based on existing academic knowledge.
The data used in this study consisted entirely of secondary data  obtained from academic
literature. The principal sources included peer -reviewed journal articles, scholarly books, and
academic publications addressing marketing analytics, business analytics, artificial intelligence,
digital transformation, customer analytics, international marketing , global marketing strategy,
export-oriented SMEs, and data -driven decision -making. To maintain academic consistency, the
analysis was based exclusively on the references that had been identified and verified prior to the
preparation of this manuscript. Th ese references represent internationally recognized publications
from journals and publishers such as Journal of Marketing, Journal of Business Research, Journal of
the Academy of Marketing Science, Information & Management, International Journal of Research in
Marketing, Marketing Intelligence & Planning , MIS Quarterly , Management Decision , Routledge,
Emerald, and Pearson. Collectively, these sources provide comprehensive theoretical and empirical
foundations for understanding the strategic  role of data analytics in contemporary marketing
environments.

The literature selection was guided by several inclusion criteria to ensure relevance and
analytical consistency. First, the selected publications explicitly discussed one or more of the
following topics: data analytics, marketing analytics, business analytics, artificial intelligence in
marketing, digital transformation, customer intelligence, export marketing, global marketing,
international marketing, or SME competitiveness. Second, priorit y was given to recent publications
that reflect current developments in digital technologies and global business environments, while
several seminal references were retained because they provide foundational theoretical concepts
that continue to underpin c ontemporary research. Third, the selected publications originated from
reputable academic journals and scholarly publishers to ensure the credibility and reliability of the
conceptual evidence. Finally, every selected source demonstrated clear theoretical or practical
relevance to strategic marketing decision-making, thereby supporting the objective of developing an
integrated conceptual explanation rather than merely describing previous findings.
Data collection was conducted through an intensive document  review process. Each
publication was examined repeatedly to identify its theoretical perspectives, conceptual definitions,
research objectives, methodological characteristics, principal findings, and implications for
marketing decision-making. During this process, particular attention was given to recurring concepts
related to analytical capability, market intelligence, customer behavior analysis, predictive analytics,
artificial intelligence, digital transformation, organizational capability, export compe titiveness, and
international entrepreneurial marketing. Relevant ideas from each source were extracted, compared,
and organized into thematic categories that reflected the evolving discourse concerning data
analytics and global marketing decision -making. Rather than treating each publication
independently, the analysis emphasized relationships among studies, areas of convergence,
contrasting viewpoints, and emerging conceptual patterns.
The analytical procedure adopted qualitative content analysis  combined with thematic
synthesis. Initially, the collected literature was read comprehensively to obtain a holistic
understanding of the existing body of knowledge. Subsequently, key concepts and theoretical
arguments were coded according to their substantive meanings and grouped into broader analytical
themes. These themes were continuously refined through an iterative comparison process to identify
conceptual similarities, complementary explanations, and areas where previous studies offered
different interpretations. The thematic synthesis enabled the integration of dispersed findings into a
coherent conceptual structure explaining the strategic functions of data analytics in global marketing
decision-making. Throughout the analysis, emphasis was placed o n interpreting the significance of
analytical capabilities, rather than quantifying publication trends or measuring statistical
relationships among variables.
To enhance the trustworthiness of the findings, this study emphasized four principles of
qualitative rigor: credibility, dependability, confirmability, and transferability. Credibility was
strengthened by drawing evidence from authoritative scholarly publications representing multiple
disciplinary perspectives, including marketing, information system s, strategic management, and
digital transformation. Dependability was maintained through a transparent analytical procedure in
which the stages of literature selection, document review, thematic categorization, and conceptual
synthesis were conducted systematically and consistently. Confirmability was achieved by ensuring
that every conceptual interpretation remained grounded in the reviewed literature rather than
personal assumptions. Transferability was supported through the development of an integrative
conceptual framework that can inform future empirical investigations in different organizational and
industrial contexts, particularly among SMEs engaged in international markets.

RESULT AND DISCUSSION
Evolution of Data Analytics as a Strategic Marketing Capability
The literature synthesis indicates that data analytics has evolved from a technology-oriented
function into a strategic organizational capability that supports evidence-based marketing decision-
making. Earlier studies primarily viewed analytics as an extension of business intelligence designed
to collect, organize, and process large volumes of organizational data for operational purposes (Chen
et al., 2012; George et al., 2014). However, the rapid expansion of digital technologies, including e -
commerce, social media, cloud computing, and the Internet of Things, has significantly transformed
the role of analytics. Organizations increasingly utilize analytical capabilities to identify customer
needs, monitor market dynamics, evaluate competitors, and generate strategic insights that improve
marketing performance. This evolution reflects a shift from descriptive reporting toward predictive
and prescriptive decision support, positioning data as a strategic organizational resource rather than
merely an operational asset (Wedel & Kannan, 2016; France & Ghose, 2019).
The reviewed literature further demonstrates that the strategic value of data analytics
depends not solely on technological infrastructure but on an organization's ability to integrate
analytical capabi lities with digital transformation and managerial decision -making. Firms that
successfully combine analytical resources with organizational learning, strategic alignment, and
innovation are better positioned to respond to rapidly changing market conditions  and sustain
competitive advantage (Bharadwaj et al., 2013; Verhoef et al., 2021). Consequently, data analytics
should be understood as a multidimensional organizational capability that supports strategic
marketing intelligence, enhances organizational ada ptability, and strengthens evidence -based
decision-making within increasingly competitive global markets.

Data Analytics as Strategic Marketing Intelligence
The thematic synthesis reveals that data analytics functions as the foundation of strategic
marketing intelligence by transforming diverse data sources into actionable knowledge that supports
managerial decision-making. Through the integration of transactional data, customer interactions,
digital platforms, and market information, organizations gain deeper insights into consumer
behavior, market trends, and competitive dynamics. These analytical capabilities enable firms to
improve customer segmentation, forecas t market demand, personalize marketing strategies, and
optimize resource allocation based on objective evidence rather than managerial intuition alone
(Kannan, 2017; France & Ghose, 2019). As global markets become increasingly dynamic and data -

Data Analytics Capability in Export-Oriented SMEs
The literature consistently demonstrates that data analytics capability has become a critical
determinant of competitiveness for export -oriented SMEs operating in increasingly dynamic
international markets. Despite limited financial resources, technologica l infrastructure, and
managerial expertise, digital transformation has enabled SMEs to access global markets through e -
commerce platforms and digital ecosystems. Data analytics supports these firms by identifying
export opportunities, monitoring internatio nal customer preferences, evaluating foreign market
potential, and improving strategic resource allocation. Consequently, analytics capability should be
viewed not merely as a technological resource but as an organizational competence that integrates
analytical skills, market orientation, and strategic decision-making to strengthen export performance
(Akter et al., 2016; Wamba et al., 2017; Luu, 2024).
However, the reviewed literature also highlights that technology adoption alone is
insufficient to genera te sustainable competitive advantage. SMEs frequently encounter challenges
related to data quality, limited analytical competencies, inadequate organizational readiness, and
weak data governance, all of which constrain the effective use of analytics in str ategic marketing
decisions (Miah, 2022; Tawil et al., 2023). Therefore, the effectiveness of data analytics depends on
complementary organizational capabilities, including leadership commitment, organizational
learning, digital agility, and continuous capa bility development. Firms that successfully integrate
these elements are better positioned to enhance international competitiveness and respond
proactively to changing global market conditions (Yang et al., 2025; Purmono et al., 2026).

Integrated Discussion and Proposed Conceptual Framework
The synthesis of the reviewed literature indicates that the strategic role of data analytics can
be understood through an integrated framework linking organizational resources, analytical
capability, strategic mar keting intelligence, and global marketing decision -making. Organizational
data generated from digital platforms, customer interactions, and market activities represent the
primary input, while data analytics capability transforms these resources into strat egic intelligence
through customer analysis, market forecasting, and predictive insights. This intelligence
subsequently supports key marketing decisions, including market selection, customer segmentation,
pricing, positioning, promotional strategy, and re source allocation, ultimately strengthening export
competitiveness and organizational performance (France & Ghose, 2019; Verhoef et al., 2021).
The effectiveness of this process is influenced by several organizational enablers rather than
technology alone. Leadership commitment, analytical competencies, digital culture, organizational

Theoretical and Practical Implications
The findings contribute to marketing and strategic management literature by extending the
understanding of data analytics beyond its traditional role as a technological or operational tool. The
synthesis demonstrates that analytics functions as a strategic organizational capability that connects
digital transformation, marketing intelligence, managerial judgment, and international marketing
strategy. By integrating previously fragmented research streams, this study offers a comprehensive
conceptual framework that explains how analytical capabilities support evidence -based decision-
making in global marketing contexts, particularly among export-oriented SMEs.
From a managerial perspective, the findings suggest that investments in analytics should be
accompanied by capability development in areas such as analytical skills, organizational learning,
leadership, and data governance. Managers should priorit ize building a data -driven organizational
culture in which analytical insights complement managerial experience when formulating
international marketing strategies. Furthermore, governments and SME support institutions can
facilitate digital transformation by improving access to digital infrastructure, analytical training, and
export development programs that strengthen SMEs' analytical capabilities and global
competitiveness.
Future research should empirically examine the proposed conceptual framework acr oss
different industries, countries, and organizational contexts. Additional variables, including
organizational culture, analytics capability maturity, artificial intelligence adoption, and
environmental uncertainty, may further explain how data analytics  influences international
marketing performance. Such investigations would refine the proposed relationships and strengthen
the theoretical understanding of analytics-driven marketing decision-making in the digital economy.

in supporting global marketing decision-making among export-oriented SMEs. The findings indicate
that data analytics has evolved beyond a technological tool into a strategic organizational capability
that enables firms to transform diverse data resources into actionable marketing intelligence. By
integrating customer insights, predictive analytics, artificial intelligence, and managerial judgment,
organizations can improve market selection, customer segmentation, pricing, promotional strategies,
