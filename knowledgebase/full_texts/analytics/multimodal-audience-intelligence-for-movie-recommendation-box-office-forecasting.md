Abstract:
Multimodalaudienceintelligencebringstogetherbehavioralanalytics,contentunderstanding,
statistical forecasting and decision systems for cinema and streaming markets. This book
develops a unified framework for movie recommendation, box-office forecasting and
streaming-content strategy using only the supplied research corpus. Audience behavior is
treated as a dynamic, partially observed process shaped by exposure, preference, social
context, market structure and platform intervention. The manuscript connects time-series and
econometric modelling with causal inference, multimodal representation learning,
recommendation, sequentialpersonalization, large-scaledata engineering and trustworthy AI.
Particular attention is given to separating prediction from attribution, measuring uncertainty,
monitoringdriftandpreservingreproducibilityacrossdata,features,modelsandexperiments.
The applied chapters translate these methods into theatrical release planning, streaming
discovery and retention, marketing attribution, audience development, greenlighting,
localization and portfolio governance. Global applicability is addressed through research
designs that can accommodate differences in language, platform maturity, device access,
distributionstructureandregulationacrossSouthAsia,Europe,AfricaandtheAmericas.The
resulting architecturesupports scholarly research, media-industrydecision supportand policy
analysis while maintaining a continuous evidence chain from audience signals to strategic
action.
