Abstract. As short-video platforms like Douyin play an increasingly prominent role as
places where post-1995 and post-2000 consumers engage socially, entertain themselves, and
consume goods and services, nostalgic emotional marketing has emerged as an essential tool
for brands targeting young consumers. However, from a practical point of view, there is not
enough consensus in many organizations regarding the relationship between nostalgic
feelings and consumer behavior. In this context, it becomes difficult to implement adequate
control of the efficiency of marketing activities in connection with the use of nostalgia.
However, currently existing scientific works have traditionally focused on either nostalgic
marketing or short-video consumer behavior, but not on the relationship between nostalgic
feelings and consumer behavior. Considering these gaps in the literature, the present study
selects Douyin as the research platform and White Rabbit Creamy Candy as the research
subject – one of the national trademarks. Specifically, this study seeks to determine the
impact of different content types and algorithmic recommendations on nostalgic feelings of
post-1995 and post-2000 consumers and, based on this information, analyze how nostalgia
affects consumer behavior.
Keywords: Nostalgia marketing, Short-video platforms, Douyin, White Rabbit Creamy
Candy, Purchase intention
1. Introduction
1.1. Research background
At present, short-video platforms, like Douyin, have become important places where post-1995 and
post-2000 generations get information, relieve stress, have fun, and meet brands. With the constantly
evolving recommendation mechanism, brand communications no longer depend only on the
information about products; instead, brands gradually focus more on arousing emotions to boost
consumer attention and purchasing willingness. Among those efforts, nostalgia marketing has gained
traction in short-video content marketing using childhood memories, outdated items, and culturally

recognizable elements. For instance, with retro packaging design and childhood advertisements,
White Rabbit Creamy Candy has attracted considerable young consumer attention on Douyin
through creative unboxing. From existing research, emotional responses to some extent contribute to
user attention, involvement, and purchasing inclination, which implies the significance of the
nostalgic emotion in platform marketing.
Nevertheless, in practice, there still seems to be little knowledge regarding nostalgia emotion and
its impact on purchase intentions at the psychological level. Thus, it becomes challenging to
effectively control the effect of related marketing activities. Additionally, there is a lack of
systematic discussion about the relationship between "platform stimulus-nostalgia emotion-purchase
intention" in short videos. Based on this background, this article will investigate platform marketing
as the research context, focusing on aspects such as content format and algorithmic
recommendation, to analyze how they affect consumers' nostalgia emotion and purchase intention.
1.2. Research purpose and research questions
The short-video platform chosen for this study is Douyin, while the target consumer audience is the
young population. This research work aims to focus on the influence of emotional marketing based
on nostalgic themes towards the youth demographic. Specifically, this research will focus on how
the algorithm recommendation system, the content format, and nostalgia emotion interact with each
other. This research work will further discuss the impact that nostalgia emotions have on the
intention to buy products or services. It attempts to explore how nostalgia marketing influences the
activities of young users in the short videos platform.
In the context of marketing through the short video platform, how would the content format, the
algorithm recommendation system, and other forms of social media affect the nostalgia emotion
among youth customers? What is the relationship between nostalgia emotion and the intention to
purchase? What are the impacts associated with this kind of emotional effect on the short video
platform setting, and what are its limitations? This paper attempts to explore such issues and come
up with findings to contribute to this field.
1.3. Research significance
Theoretical perspectives have also played an important role in motivating the current research effort
as it endeavors to explore the link between the nostalgia emotion and consumer purchase behavior
within the domain of short video clips. In terms of practice, by taking Douyin as a research
background, it is helpful to understand the effect of brand on the buying behavior of youngsters due
to nostalgic videos.
1.4. Literature review
Firstly, in past studies, several scholars have studied the concept of nostalgia and its role in
marketing from diverse angles. Firstly, from the psychological angle, nostalgia is considered an
emotional experience associated with one's memories that can induce emotional responses by
reminiscing [1]. This study can serve as a theoretical base for understanding the formation of
nostalgia.
Secondly, within the scope of consumer behavior research, some researchers indicate that
nostalgic emotions can strengthen consumers' emotional attachment to brands and affect their
consumption preference to some degree [2]. Hence, it can be argued that nostalgic marketing may

1.5. Research methods
This paper mainly employs the analysis of literature and questionnaires, as well as combining data
statistical analysis to conduct the research.
On the one hand, through the analysis of existing literature related to the topic of short-video
marketing, nostalgic marketing, and consumption behavior, the research foundation and literature
basis for this study were determined. On the other hand, data about the exposure to content,
emotional reactions, and purchase intentions of young consumers on short video sites were collected
using questionnaires. Then, descriptive statistics were performed based on the results obtained from
the questionnaires. Correlation analysis may be applied when necessary, according to the data
condition.
2. Theoretical foundation and research hypotheses
2.1. Theoretical foundation
This paper mainly applies the Stimulus-Organism-Response (S-O-R) theory to analyze the research
problem [6]. According to this theory, stimulus variables (Stimulus) that occur in the surrounding
environment trigger certain changes in individuals' psychological state (Organism), which then
result in certain behavioral reactions (Response). This theory has been extensively used for studying
consumer behavior in order to identify how external information can affect their final decisions via
certain emotions and cognitive processes.
In the present case of short videos available on the Douyin platform, the algorithmic
recommendation and presentation of the content serve as stimulus variables, providing users with
the respective information. In turn, the nostalgic emotion experienced by users after exposure to the
content can be treated as one of their psychological reactions, whereas purchase intention or action
is associated with their behavioral reaction. Thus, S-O-R theory creates a solid foundation for
researching how emotional marketing works on consumers via short video content in terms of
purchases.
Therefore, considering all aspects discussed above, in this paper, the algorithmic recommendation
and content form are to be viewed as stimulus variables, while nostalgia emotion is an emotional
variable. As for the outcome variable, it is defined as purchase intention.

2.2. Research variables
According to the above theory, this paper proceeds to discuss the relevant variables. In order to
better analyze the process of nostalgia emotion marketing on Douyin, this paper specifically
analyzes from four perspectives: algorithm recommendation, content form, nostalgic emotion, and
purchase intention. Algorithm recommendation is the process of content recommendation according
to the users' browsing history, preferences, and behaviors, and so forth. The algorithm
recommendation would affect the probability of the users being exposed to nostalgic content.
Content form represents the means of presenting content through music, visuals, character, prop,
and so forth. The difference in content forms would have a certain degree of effect on users' sense of
immersion and emotion. Nostalgic emotions represent the memory and emotion resonance aroused
in consumers when they are exposed to certain nostalgic content. Purchase intention represents the
results that consumers, under the influence of the above factors, generate interest and willingness in
purchasing certain products/services.
2.3. Research hypotheses
H1: Algorithmic recommendations and content formats have a positive impact on nostalgia
emotions.
H2: Nostalgia emotions have a positive influence on purchasing decisions.
3. Research design
3.1. Research model construction
Following the Stimulus-Organism-Response model (S-O-R) and considering the special case of
nostalgia marketing on short video apps, the current study examines the connections among the
variables under investigation. In particular, algorithmic recommendations and content formats are
considered as external stimulus variables; the nostalgia emotion variable plays a crucial role within
the emotion domain, while the purchasing decision constitutes the dependent variable. The
following section outlines the relationships between the mentioned variables.
3.2. Questionnaire design
In terms of the selected brand for this research, White Rabbit Creamy Candy will be chosen.
Therefore, the questions asked in the survey should be based on nostalgic short videos of this brand
on Douyin. As for the audience of this study, the focus of the survey will lie on the younger
generation. Specifically, the survey will look into the opinion of these people concerning the four
key factors after watching nostalgic short videos on White Rabbit brand. All the questions regarding
the nostalgic content will include only short videos concerning White Rabbit products, such as
packaging, advertising, and retro unboxing of the product.
As for the design structure, the first step is to gather information about respondents' basic
characteristics and frequency of using short-video platform. These characteristics include age,
platforms of short videos used, frequency of using these platforms, and frequency of being exposed
to the nostalgic content. From the gathered data, it follows that the majority of the respondents have
been born after 1990 and up to 2000. The platform, which respondents frequently use is Douyin.
Also, respondents use the short video application several times per day. Moreover, respondents
usually or occasionally see nostalgic content. Thus, it is expected that the sample respondents belong

3.3. Data sources and collection
This article focuses on young consumers as its research subjects and collects data through online
questionnaires. These questionnaires were primarily distributed to users with experience using short-
video platforms, and the samples were primarily drawn from the youth group.
When it comes to data collection, the questionnaires in question were mainly distributed and
collected online. It allowed the study to gather data from target respondents more easily. Moreover,
after the process was completed, the data obtained were sorted and filtered to make sure that they
were generally reliable.
Taking into account all of the above-mentioned information, it may be stated that 85 valid
questionnaires have been gathered so far according to the currently existing statistical data. In
general, the sample can provide a basic data foundation for the following analysis.
4. Data analysis
4.1. Sample profile analysis
In total, there were 85 effective surveys completed within this research. Among the respondents, a
relatively large percentage of the individuals are in their youth age group, i.e., 18 to 24 years old,
which generally reflects the purpose of this research since its primary target audience is people born
after 1995 and those born after 2000. For frequency of utilization, many users reported that they use
Douyin frequently, more than several times a day, and are relatively familiar with the contents of the
platform. As for exposure to White Rabbit nostalgia short videos, most respondents reported that
they have seen such videos, particularly recommended by Douyin on its homepage. Accordingly, the
participants chosen for the study meet the basic criteria of exposure to nostalgia videos about White
Rabbit sweets, and their survey responses provide accurate data for future analysis.

4.3. Brief summary
As analyzed above, there are several major conclusions that can be derived from the data. Firstly, the
presence of nostalgic cues in Douyin short videos, including settings, background music, and classic
objects, is correlated positively with the purchasing intent of young consumers. Stronger nostalgic
feelings are associated with higher purchase intention among young consumers. Secondly, emotional
nostalgia and practicality of the product are the two major determinants of the consumers' decision-
making process. Herd behavior exerts minimal influence on consumers' decision-making process,
while plain brand nostalgia plays a negligible role compared to emotional resonance.
5. Problems and recommendations
5.1. Problems
Nevertheless, despite the fact that emotional marketing on Douyin based on nostalgia shows some
impact on young consumers' purchase intentions, a number of serious problems should be
mentioned.
The first problem concerns herd consumption, which takes place quite often. Considering, for
example, the case of nostalgic promotion of White Rabbit on Douyin, due to the algorithmic
recommendations used in this social network, the popular videos tend to be multiplied. When seeing
a lot of nostalgic videos getting a lot of attention, consumers can adopt the mentality of the herd –
that is, follow the others' choice. This kind of consumer behavior is associated with imitation rather
than rational decision-making. Thus, herd-driven consumer behavior does not have an emotional
component in its background. The orders may be placed under the pressure of popularity, but when
the popularity goes away, the incentive for buying decreases, and consumers no longer purchase
goods.
The second problem relates to the sustainability of nostalgia marketing campaigns. The attention
of Douyin users is quite fragmented and volatile; therefore, a nostalgic video can reach millions of
people in a very short period of time. However, the popularity of a video can quickly go away within
days, and a new trend will replace it. White Rabbit is experiencing such issues too – their video can
gain traction soon after release, and then its attention level decreases. Yet, the success of nostalgia
marketing is tightly linked to a particular social context. Once the context changes, sustaining the
emotional component becomes challenging. The one-shot explosive communication is not enough,
and if the emotional connection with consumers cannot be maintained constantly, transformation
into brand loyalty is hardly possible. Therefore, the effectiveness is not durable, and the repurchase
rate is low.
Lastly, the sentiment about a brand is hardly able to drive consumers. Emotional nostalgia and
practical usefulness of products serve as primary driving factors for making purchases, whereas
dependence only on the brand's history or childhood memories cannot ensure consistent buying
behavior. Even such iconic brands as White Rabbit face these challenges. In this regard, nostalgia
marketing sometimes faces the problem of "high views but low conversion". Because of the lack of

5.2. Recommendations for enterprises
Considering the difficulties stated above, the following recommendations can be proposed.
First of all, there is a necessity to enrich the content of the nostalgic experience. Instead of
staying on the elementary level of nostalgic packaging or advertising, brands should seek stories
capable of making users have more connections with the brand. The studies on the distribution of
content in the online environment demonstrate that emotionally engaging content tends to be
distributed faster [8]. Therefore, it is essential for brand manufacturers to consider the profundity of
the emotions when creating such videos. For instance, brands may create a series of short videos
showcasing nostalgia brands such as White Rabbit in Douyin. Some of the topics that may arise
during discussions are the childhood stories and experiences of buying the particular product. This
will create a more profound emotion of nostalgia rather than trend-hopping contents by the crowd.
Secondly, it is also necessary to modify the format of short videos to find the optimal
combination of an emotional factor and the practical advantage. Namely, brands could provide the
suggestions about the ways in which the mentioned nostalgic products can be used in modern
circumstances. For instance, brands could associate the classic products, such as White Rabbit
candies, with such aspects as baking and wearing clothes. Considering the role of the level of
nostalgia on consumers' preferences, it is vital to create the correlation between the elements of
nostalgia and consumption [9]. Namely, apart from evoking nostalgia in the consumer base, it is
necessary to propose the goods satisfying the consumer's requirements. Thus, in addition to the
nostalgic element of goods, the practicality of goods could serve as another motivator and prevent
possible returns associated with herd behavior.
Thirdly, it is important to introduce the algorithmic recommendations for obtaining precision of
positioning of nostalgic brands and conducting constant work with the brand. On one hand, in order
to position the brand in a nostalgic manner, brands need to produce different forms of content
associated with the audience's age and interests, while enhancing the accuracy of content
recommendation on the basis of time tests and small-scale interactions. On the other hand,
considering that the influence of the proposed positioning method on brand equity is dependent on
external and internal conditions, it is vital to focus not only on the historic value of the brand but
also combine it with ongoing activities and innovations [10]. At the same time, it is necessary to
improve production and operations system of the brand. For example, White Rabbit should
coordinate its logistics infrastructure to cope with nostalgic flow.
6. Research reflections and future prospects
6.1. Research limitations
Although this study has produced some useful findings, it still has several limitations. The first
limitation of the research is connected with the limited sample size. Indeed, only 85 participants
completed the questionnaire, while most of them were in the age group 18-24 years old. Moreover,
the research covered only a particular group of young consumers but did not extend to the whole
target audience. As a result, it can be concluded that the lack of representativeness of the sample will

The second limitation can be related to the limited scope of the research. It is worth mentioning
that the research covers only one specific short video channel and one nostalgic brand – the White
Rabbit Creamy Candy. Thus, researchers did not include other short video platforms and other
nostalgic goods, e.g., everyday products or classical literature pieces.
The third disadvantage can refer to research methods. In this paper, researchers mainly applied a
survey questionnaire and basic correlation analysis. However, no more sophisticated statistical
analyses of relationships between algorithmic recommendations, content form, nostalgia emotion,
and purchase intentions have been performed.
6.2. Future research directions
As far as the above-mentioned limitations are concerned, the research in the future may benefit from
improvements in the following directions.
Firstly, expand the sample size of the study. This can be done by increasing the channels through
which questionnaires are distributed, as well as expanding the age groups of participants and their
geographical distribution. Thus, the findings will be more representative and reliable.
Secondly, broaden the scope of research. For instance, future research can be conducted not only
on multiple platforms that offer short videos but also on other nostalgic brands and products for
comparison. Furthermore, according to the review articles on nostalgic marketing, it is advised to
pay closer attention to different contexts of consumption and types of products [11]. Thus,
researchers can explore how nostalgic marketing works in different contexts and product categories
and draw more realistic conclusions.
Thirdly, improve the methodological framework. In future research, structural equation modeling,
experiments, and follow-up surveys can be used to measure the interrelationships between the
variables. Moreover, when conducting structural equation modeling, researchers should consider the
recommendations for the presentation of results [12]. An effective tool in exploring nostalgic
marketing is conducting in-depth interviews and analysis of comments to identify triggering factors.
7. Conclusion
This paper takes Douyin as the research background and analyzes the effects of nostalgia marketing
on the purchasing behaviors of young consumers and the practical significance and drawbacks of
nostalgia marketing. By means of literature review, questionnaire investigation, and case study, this
paper selects the nostalgia marketing strategy of White Rabbit Creamy Candy as a typical case and
analyzes the communication features and consumer behavior theory behind nostalgia marketing on
Douyin. The research shows that nostalgic emotional marketing is likely to arouse young consumers'
nostalgia for childhood experiences and initially trigger their purchase intentions. Nevertheless,
nostalgia marketing also suffers from practical issues, including obvious herd consumption
behaviors, weak sustainability of marketing popularity, and difficult transformation of brand
sentiments into purchasing behaviors. Besides, excessive imitation of nostalgia elements, lack of in-
depth content creation, and insufficient innovation of products are the factors leading to the decline
of marketing conversion rates. The research contributes to the relevant studies about nostalgia
marketing on short-video apps and offers some implications for nostalgia food brands and
conventional brands conducting marketing campaigns on Douyin. Future studies may consider
