Abstract - Awareness about environmental issues is becoming a
significant determinant of consumer behavior in the consumer
goods sector, especially in the case of green cosmetics that are
made using natural components, do not involve animal testing,
and use eco -friendly packaging. In the case of Kerala, a state
known for its high literacy rate and rising interest in adopting
sustainability practices, knowledge about the influence of
environmental awareness on consumer satisfaction becomes
necessary for devising an ef fective marketing strategy. The
present paper seeks to fill this existing research gap by studying
the effect of environmental awareness on consumer satisfaction
in relation to green cosmetics. The uniqueness of this study is
that it focuses on the influen ce of environmental awareness on
consumer satisfaction and not on purchase intentions in Kerala.
Data were collected using questionnaires from 200 randomly
selected respondents on a Likert scale. The data analysis process
entailed tests of reliability (Cronbach’s alpha test), correlations,
and regression analysis using SPSS. High internal consistency is
revealed by Cronbach’s alpha value of 0.929 for environmental
awareness and 0.925 for customer satisfaction. A strong positive
correlation (r = 0.714; p < 0.01) is found, and regression analysis
proves that there is a significant influence of environmental
awareness on customer satisfaction (β = 0.714; p < 0.001),
accounting for 51% of the explained variance (R² = 0.510;
F = 206.010, p = 0.000). It can be concluded that consumer
environmental awareness can positively impact consumer
satisfaction from green cosmetics when increased by means of
proper education and labeling. Therefore, companies should
make their operations more sustainable to improve customer
satisfaction and loyalty.

I. INTRODUCTION
Issues related to the environment have gradually become
powerful factors influencing consumer preferences in the
fast-moving consumer goods industry. Awareness about
climate change, pollution, chemicals, waste disposal, and the
environmental effect of pack ing materials has made
consumers consider more than just price and performance of
products while evaluating their choices. Green cosmetic
products have emerged as popular products in this scenario
owing to the presence of natural ingredients, minimal
chemical elements, cruelty -free production methods,
recycling friendly packing, and environmentally conscious
manufacturing processes. The state of Kerala, which enjoys
higher literacy rates and rising sustainability consciousness
among the masses, could benefit significantly from the issue
of the environment for meeting customer expectations. As per
previous studies, environmental consciousness and health
consciousness have been found to play critical r oles in the
purchase intention of green products among customers in the
FMCG sector in Kerala  (Sajini et al., 2025) . Customer
perceptions of green products in South Kerala depend upon
factors like product awareness, environmental consciousness,
product quality, and claim of environmental benefits  (Raj,
2025). Moreover, green marketing activities in Kerala also
reveal the role of customer attitude during the promotion of
environmentally responsible products  (Thangavel et al.,
2024). This research would be quite pertinent to green
cosmetics because the decisions made while buying such
cosmetics will largely be based on consumers’ trust regarding
their composition, safety, packaging, brands, and their
environmental benefits. In this c ontext, satisfaction levels
will probably rise when customers find that what have
expected from green cosmetics actually corresponds to
reality. The use of environmentally friendly cosmetics is also
associated with altered lifestyles and the desire to use natural
products. A study on organic food consumption in Kerala
reveals that consumer decisions were affected by health
consciousness, product quality, awareness, and values  (Ms
Krishna & Balasubramanian, 2021).
Even though both organic products and green cosmetics
belong to distinct product lines, there are behavioural
variables that can play a role in determining customer
satisfaction when buying these products. For example, people
buying green cosmetics might b e interested in enhanced
safety, ethical manufacturing, less harm to the environment
and transparency regarding the ingredient list. In turn, studies
concerning service quality in the wellness industry in the
Kerala region state that the degree of satisfac tion with a
product is based on the degree of meeting customer

current research aims to explore the impact of environmental
awareness on customer satisfaction with green cosmetic
products in Kerala.

II. LITERATURE REVIEW
The environment has turned out to be a key component when
formulating customer expectations and satisfaction within
sustainable products and services. While different research
studies have been conducted in the areas of tourism and
hospitality, organic pro duce, and consumption in the
wellness area, these results give useful knowledge about
consumer satisfaction in the context of green cosmetics in
Kerala. Customer satisfaction depends on perception,
possibilities for realization, and consistency between
expectations and the reality that customers experience
(Joseph et al., 2022) . The same principle can be applied to
green cosmetics, as consumers may judge different aspects of
products, such as the quality of products, claims, packages,
components, prices, and brands (Mathew et al., 2024).
Another factor worth considering in connection with
environmental awareness is waste -management behavior.
Waste management strategies in relation to plastics in Kerala
depend on certain sociodemographic and individual factors
(Kannankai et al., 2024) . This information is useful in the
context of green cosmetics, as cosmetics typically have
plastic packaging. Customers who possess environmental
awareness might opt for products using reusable,
biodegradable, refillable, or minimal waste packaging. In
addition, the approach to customer experiences and their
physical, mental, spiritual, and environmental well -being
underscores the significance of environmental variables
(Kandan Parakkal et al., 2024). Given that cosmetic products
are associated with wellness, cosmetics brands with an
environmentally friendly attitude might contribute to
enhancing the satisfaction of customers, as well.
Further evidence to consider satisfaction in the green
cosmetics industry can be found in the consumer behavior
studies with regard to cosmetics products  (Rai et al., 2022) .
Factors affecting consumer behavior with regard to cosmetics
include product and personal variables. With regards to the
purchase of green cosmetics, factors such as effectiveness,
safety, affordability, availability, scent, packing, as well as
confidence in their environmentally responsible nature may

Nature-inspired well -being innovations imply that

Marketing mix continues to play an essential role in shaping

Environmental consciousness, price justice, subjective
norms, and perceived behavioural control also determine
consumer intention for buying organic products (Fairoos,

customer satisfaction in relation to green cosmetics.
Environmental sustainability is also emphasized by research
conducted in the tourism industry of Kerala. Sustainable
development of environmentally fragile areas may lead to a
positive perception and value addition  (Prasad et al., 2024) .
The use of resilient development approaches that are based
on responsible utilization of resources may also contribute
towards emphasizing the importance of sustainable practices
(Sooraj et al., 2025) . Even though these examples relate to
the tourism industry, their significance for the cosmetics
industry cannot be overlooked.
Other recent research on consumer perceptions focuses on
ethics and environmental awareness within cosmetics firms.
Consumer satisfaction regarding vegan cosmetic products is
a function of their perceived quality, ethicality, and user
marketing (Kusumah & Lee, 2020). These types of cosmetics
include cruelty -free, natural ingredients, and environmental
sustainability. The practices of environmental sustainability
need to be balanced with profitability as well  (Singh et al.,
2024). This is very important in the case of green cosmetics,
since consumers want products that are environmentally
sustainable but do not sacrifice affordability, quality, and
efficiency.
Green supply chains might also affect customer satisfaction
indirectly by boosting the credibility of green brands.
Competitive sustainability is achieved through responsible
sourcing, minimized waste, and efficient resource utilization
(Sujatha & Maheswari, 2023) . These processes will help in
supporting green cosmetic brands through sustainable
sourcing of ingredients, minimized packaging waste, energy
conservation in production, and environmentally -friendly
distribution. Young consumers often find it hard to pay for
green products, making them price sensitive  (Pathak &
Malakar, 2024) . This may negatively impact their level of
customer satisfaction if perceive green cosmetics to be too
costly compared to regular products. Based on the above
literature review, environmental awareness affects consumer
satisfaction through product quality , safety, value systems,
packaging, pricing fairness, brand credibility, availability,
and sustainability measures in business practices. However,
the relationship between environmental awareness and
customer satisfaction in the consumption of green cosmet ics
in Kerala has not been researched adequately. Most of the
related literature has focused on tourism, hospitality, waste
management, organic foods, wellness, and general green
consumption habits. This paper fills this research gap by
examining the effec ts of environmental awareness on
satisfaction with green cosmetics among consumers in
Kerala.
Problem Statement
Though the demand for green cosmetics is on the rise, little
is known about the impact of the level of environmental
awareness among consumers on their satisfaction from using
such products, especially in the context of Kerala. Though
environmental awarene ss is playing an increasingly
prominent role in consumer behavior, there are gaps in the
ability of firms to gauge whether environmental awareness
leads to greater customer satisfaction. This is important
because it presents problems for green cosmetic
manufacturers in formulating effective marketing strategies
for their products.
Research Objective
• To assess the environmental awareness levels of
consumers purchasing green cosmetics in Kerala.
• To measure customer satisfaction levels with green
cosmetics among environmentally aware consumers.
• To examine the influence of environmental awareness
on customer satisfaction towards green cosmetics in
Kerala.
Research Hypothesis
• H1: Higher environmental awareness among consumers
positively influences their satisfaction with green
cosmetics in Kerala.
• H2: Consumers with greater knowledge of
environmental issues exhibit higher satisfaction levels
with eco-friendly cosmetic products.
• H3: Environmental awareness is a significant predictor
of customer satisfaction with green cosmetics in Kerala.
Research Questions
RQ1: What is the level of environmental awareness among
consumers of green cosmetics in Kerala?
RQ2: How satisfied are consumers with green cosmetics
based on their environmental awareness?

IV. RESEARCH METHODOLOGY
4.1 Data Design & Tools and Techniques
The research methodology employed a descriptive design
and used a quantitative approach to investigate the influence
of environmental awareness on customer satisfaction with
green cosmetics in Kerala. Data collection involved the
administration of questionnaires using the Likert scale in
order to achieve quantification and standardization of
measurement of the variables. SPSS was used for analysis of
data whereby internal consistency of the variables was
checked through Cronbach’s Alpha test, relationship between
the variables was calculated via correlation analysis, and
regression analysis was used to determine the influence of
environmental awareness on customer satisfaction. The use
of statistical tools made it possible to obtain  measurable and
statistically sound results from the data analyzed.
4.2 Measurement of Variables
The research methodology followed by the study is
descriptive with the use of a quantitative design to study the
impact of environmental awareness on customer satisfaction
in the case of green cosmetics among customers of Kerala
state. The primary data was  gathered using structured
questionnaires that were scored using the Likert scale method
for consistent evaluation. Environmental Awareness served
as the independent variable and consisted of dimensions
evaluating consumers' level of awareness and understa nding
of green products, concern for the environment, and positive
attitude towards environmental protection. Customer
Satisfaction was considered the dependent variable and was
defined based on dimensions such as satisfaction in general,
satisfaction with  the product, satisfaction with brand
practices, repurchase intention, and positive recommendation
intention. In total, 200 respondents were selected through
purposive sampling, and their responses were analyzed using
SPSS. Measures of reliability (Cronbac h’s Alpha),

5.5 Discussion
The research results conclusively indicate a very positive
impact of environmental awareness on consumer satisfaction
with green cosmetics among the people of Kerala. The
reliability test showed that internal consistency was
extremely high (Cronbach’s alph a: 0.929 for environmental
awareness and 0.925 for consumer satisfaction), indicating
validity in how the two constructs were measured. The
correlation analysis also showed a strong positive
relationship (r = 0.714; p < 0.01) between the two variables.
The regression results showed that awareness accounts for
approximately 51% of the variance in customer satisfaction
(R² = 0.510). With respect to awareness being positively
correlated with customer satisfaction (β = 0.714, p < 0.001),
it can be seen that the higher the awareness amongst
customers regarding the environment, the higher their
satisfaction with green cosmetics. This is consistent with past
literature, which states that greater awareness about green
features increases consumer satisfaction.
From a management perspective, the findings suggest that
green cosmetic brands should increase consumer awareness
of environmental issues and the features of sustainable
products. Promoting sustainable practices, such as
eco-friendly ingredients, recyclable packaging, and ethically
responsible production, can contribute to greater consumer
satisfaction. Furthermore, transparency in product labelling,
together with the brand's commitment to sustainability, will
lead to high er levels of consumer loyalty. The moderating
effect of such demographic variables as age, education, and
income can be expected.
Such results help broaden our theoretical perspective on
green consumers, not only regarding their intentions but also
the impact of such awareness on their satisfaction. In fact,
many researchers focus solely on green purchase intentions,
paying little at tention to what makes customers satisfied.
Thus, it is essential to emphasize that any company
manufacturing eco -friendly products should meet its
customers' demands regarding environmental awareness,
product pricing, and other factors. Environmental aware ness
plays an important role in customer satisfaction with the use
of green cosmetics in Kerala. In this regard, it becomes
possible to speak of high levels of consumer satisfaction if
companies successfully raise consumer awareness.
5.6 Managerial Implications
From the research findings, one learns that higher consumer
awareness of the environment contributes positively to
increased satisfaction from the use of green cosmetics in the
state of Kerala. Therefore, in developing marketing strategies
for promoting th eir products, the management needs to give
priority to strategies that involve education of customers
regarding the green nature of their products. The promotion
campaigns for these products must include not only
environmental benefits but also information  about the
high-quality, affordable, and credible green nature of the
products offered. Green chain management will also prove
useful in increasing brand credibility and loyalty.
Communication campaigns and products must be tailored to
suit customers from different demographics.

VI. CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORK
Environmental awareness has been established to play an
important role in influencing customer satisfaction with
green cosmetics in Kerala. Internal reliability was high
(Environment Awareness: α = 0.929; Customer Satisfaction:
α = 0.925). Results from cor relation tests indicate a
significant positive relationship between the two variables
(r = 0.714, p < 0.01). Regression analysis showed that
environmental awareness explained 51% of the variance in
customer satisfaction (R² = 0.510; β = 0.714, t = 14.353,
p < 0.001). These results indicate that consumers who are
aware of environmental concerns and green product features
tend to be highly satisfied with their purchases. Future
research may consider using longitudinal approaches to
investigate how consumer sa tisfaction levels change as
environmental awareness evolves over time. The scope of the
study may be broadened by considering different product
categories and by using random rather than convenience
samples, as in this paper. It would be valuable to examin e
how consumer demographics, involvement with green
products, and perceptions of the value offered affect
consumer awareness and satisfaction. Furthermore, specific
marketing campaigns designed to educate consumers about
the benefits of green products could also be analyzed for their
effectiveness in generating consumer satisfaction.
