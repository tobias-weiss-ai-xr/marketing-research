Abstract
Organisations tend to position their products and services based on their generations, that is,
based on people born within the same span of years who share similar experience. Generation
Z also known as Gen Z form s the largest age cohort and their buying power is considered to
be influential. Despite their characteristics, captivating them is somewhat challenging due to
their inclination to express themselves through their shopping behavior and choices. This study
aim to examine the relationship between di gital marketing and consumer purchase intention
among Gen Z in University of Port Harcourt, Rivers State  with focus on Jumia Nigeria . The
study employed a explanatory design targeting students University of Port Harcourt, Rivers
State, with sample sizes of 339 determined using Taro Yamane formula. Data were collected
using a structured questionnaire and analyzed with regression to test hypotheses. The findings
indicated that social media marketing has a positive and statistical significant influence on
consumer purchase intention  (t = 3.323, p = 0.001), video marketing has a negative and
significant influence on consumer purchase intention  (t = 5.074, p = 0.000). Furthermore,
digital marketing has a significant influence on consumer purchase intention (t = 8.808, p =
0.000). It is therefore recommended that Jumia should c ontinue to develop high -quality,
visually appealing, and relevant content that resonates with the Gen Z.  They use of data
analytics to identify student preferences and create personalized advertisements that cater to
their specific needs.

2.1.1 The Concept of Digital Marketing
The term “Digital marketing” originated as a term in the 1990s, but the landscape was vastly
different compared to the present -day. During the early days of the internet, web 1.0 was
characterized by static content with minimal interactivity ( Kingsnorth, 2019). It was in 1993
that the first banner advertising appeared, followed by the establishment of the first web crawler
in 1994, marking the advent of search engine optimization (SEO). The digital marketing
landscape experienced rapid evolution with the in troduction of influential platforms like
Google in 1994 and YouTube, the video community, in 2005.
Digital marketing refers to the promotion of products through digital media connected to the
internet (Novitasari, Siswanto, Purwanto & Fahmi, 2021). It has made communication between

2.1.2.2 Content Marketing
The term "Content Marketing" was coined in the late 1990s but truly emerged as a standalone
concept in 2008 -2009 ( i-SCOOP, 2018). Content marketing is a well -known marketing

Given that customers seek diverse information, content marketing places emphasis on

2.1.2.4 Email Marketing
Email marketing has revolutionized the landscape of marketing and electronic commerce,
emerging as a reliable and powerful tool in the digital era. With its efficiency and convenience,
email has become the fastest method for exchanging digital messages ove r the internet. It
allows users to effortlessly manage and access their received messages within their email
inboxes at their own convenience. This digital communication channel gained significant
traction as the use of internet networks proliferated, with prominent email platforms like Yahoo
and Google Gmail playing a pivotal role (Sabbagh, 2021).
Email marketing can target a broad group of audiences or cater to individual preferences. While
social media platforms have gained popularity, they often lack the guarantee of message
visibility among all followers. In contrast, an email message remains in recipients' inboxes until
they decide to delete it, ensuring a longer lifespan and increased visibility of the marketing
content (Sabbagh, 2021).
Email marketing provides businesses with a versatile and impactful tool to promote their
products, build lasting relationships with customers, and deliver tailored messages that resonate
with their target audience. Its widespread reach, personalized approach, and extended visibility
make it an invaluable component of modern marketing strategies. As technology continues to
advance, email marketing remains a steadfast and effective means for businesses to
communicate directly with their customers, foster enga gement, and achieve their marketing
objectives. Email marketing offers numerous advantages, such as cost -effectiveness,
measurability, ease of use, automation, and efficiency, making it a meaningful and impactful
marketing tool (Zahedifard, 2012). However, there are also drawbacks, including undelivered
emails, email response decay, rendering issues, and the potential for overwhelming recipients
with excessive emails.
2.1.3 Consumer Purchase Intention
Purchasing intention has been extensively studied in marketing research ( Garcia, Saura, &
Orejuela, 2020) . It serves as a valuable tool for predicting customers' future buying trends.
Purchase intention refers to the motivations that drive customers to prefer a particular brand or
marketer (Nguyen, & Dinh, 2017). In simple terms, it represents the likelihood of a customer
purchasing a product promptly (Laksamana, 2018).
The consumer's purchase intention reflects their willingness to buy a product and directly
influences their purchase behaviour, making it a crucial factor in determining their planned
future actions (Tian & Lee, 2020). It indicates the consumer's readiness to engage in a specific
purchase in the future and can be determined by their willingness to take action on a given
matter. Furthermore, higher product prices and customer engagement increase the likelihood
of actual purchase behaviour, making purchase in tention a significant metric for measuring a
company's performance.
Typically, consumers acquire information about products based on their personal needs and
engage in an information search process, which frequently triggers purchase intentions through
the impact of advertising ( Ju & Jung, 2019) . Purchase intention refers to the degree of
consumers' willingness to make purchases within the marketing context, representing the
transformation of beliefs into actions. It demonstrates a positive correlation with purchase
behavior, as it reflects consumers' anticipated future actions. Therefore, purchase intention can
be regarded as a preliminary stage preceding purchase behavior, exerting greater influence on
consumers' decision-making than their attitude towards the product itself. This study defines
purchase intention as the wi llingness to buy goods and services influenced by the
characteristics of social networking sites (SNS) and brand trust actions (Tian & Lee, 2020).
Consumer purchase intention encompasses various constructs such as consumer attitudes,

2.1.4 Measures of Consumer Purchase Intention
2.1.4.1 Brand Reputation
Brand reputation, which refers to how others perceive a brand, is widely recognised as a
significant factor influencing the value of both the brand and its products (Hollebeek, & Macky,
2019). It plays a crucial role in the customer's purchasing process by providing awareness,
perceived quality, specific mental associations, uncertainty reduction, and fostering customer
loyalty (Keller, 2003).
However, the significance of brand reputation can be seen from various perspectives. In online
platforms, customers encounter a multitude of information, including competitive offerings and
other customers' feedback, leading to a greater reliance on these sources rather than solely on
brand reputation as an indicator of quality ( Mu, Thomas, Qi & Tan, 2018) . Brands may lose
their value in the online environment due to the convergence of product features and the
availability of detailed product quality information through reviews and opinions. As a result,
the impact of brand reputation on quality signals may diminish as customers have access to
more information.
Additionally, online platforms face limitations in providing customized product presentations,
which poses challenges for branded products to establish strong customer relationships (Eggert,
et al., 2018). Consequently, brands have primarily become recognized as trademarked goods,
with limited opportunities for meaningful customer engagement. The role of brands in evoking
consumer excitement is gradually diminishing.
Online platforms excel in facilitating product search and comparison, offering a wide range of
options with similar functionalities. This shift directs consumer attention away from well -
established brands towards a larger variety of niche products that may  have lower sales
volumes (Mu, Thomas, Qi & Tan, 2018).
Furthermore, sellers on online platforms are increasingly promoting their own private labels,
prioritising their profits over the interests of brand owners ( Mu & Zhang, 2021) . This
misalignment of interests between sellers and brands can lead to a negative impact on brand
image and brand equity (Gielens, & Steenkamp, 2019). Moreover, while brand reputation can
alleviate uncertainties and facilitate purchasing decisions, a strong brand can also create higher
and sometimes unrealistic expectations, resulting in greater post-purchase dissatisfaction due
to increased cognitive dissonance.

derive from a product and the total cost incurred, including time, en ergy, and money

2.2 Theoretical Review
It is pertinent to know that theories in a particular field of study are assumptions or speculation

theoretical foundation of this study is anchored on the followi ng: Theory of Reasoned Action
(TRA) and Technological Acceptance Model or Theory.
2.2.1 The Theory of Reasoned Action (TRA)
The Theory of Reasoned Action (TRA) is a social psychological model aimed at explaining
consciously intended behaviours ( Xiao, 2020).  According to the TRA, an individual's
behaviour is influenced by their intention to engage in that behaviour, which is determined by
their attitude towards the behaviour and subjective norms ( Fishbein, & Ajzen, 1975) . This
theory seeks to elucidate the connection between attitudes, norms, and behaviours in human
actions. It has been widely employed to predict an individual's inclination to participate in a
specific action (Hagger, 2019).
The emergence of digital technologies has opened up new avenues for shopping. The unique
nature of these technologies not only intrinsically motivates individuals but also externally
influences them to utilise these platforms for various tasks. The Theory of Reasoned Action
(TRA) serves as an appropriate theoretical framework for understanding consumers' intention
to use digital media for different purposes. This theory links behaviours related to digital media
usage with factors such as belief, attitude, i ntention, and subjective norms. It elucidates the
connection between people's beliefs and their behavioural intentions, explores how attitudes
impact intentional behaviour, and also examines how the social environment influences
behaviour.
2.2.2 Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology (UTAUT)
A prominent theoretical framework used to explain user intentions toward information systems
and the subsequent behavioural changes resulting from their usage is the Unified Theory of
Acceptance and Use of Technology (UTAUT) (Venkatesh, et al., 2003). This theory has been
extensively applied to understand the level of acceptance of information technologies.
According to the UTAUT, an individual's decision to use a specific system is influenced by
their perceived capability to handle that particular te chnology. Moreover, the theory
acknowledges that factors like gender, age, and experience can moderate the impact of
behavioural intentions (Chao, 2019). The integration of these technologies into organisational
settings has marked a significant shift in the way business processes are carried out. These
advancements have not only enabled more efficient operations but also opened up new
possibilities for d ata-driven decision -making, automation, and personalised customer
experiences. Organisations that embrace these technologies are better equipped to adapt to the
rapidly changing business landscape and gain a competitive edge in the market (Owuor, 2022).
In conclusion, incorporating TRA and TAM into the study of digital marketing and purchase
intent gives a sound theoretical foundation, predictive capacity, and practical insights that can
assist both researchers and marketers in better understanding and op timizing consumer
behavior in the digital marketing landscape. The integration of the theory of reasoned action
and the unified theory of acceptance and use of technology (TRA and TAM) in existing studies
is restricted. While both ideas have been widely used individually, it is possible that there is a

3.2 Empirical Review
2.3.1 Digital marketing and Consumer Purchase Intention
To gather empirical data, a quantitative research method was employed, and 202 online
shoppers were selected using the snowball sampling technique. Data were collected through a
self-administered questionnaire in the greater Klang Valley region of Malaysia . The
instrument's normality and reliability were assessed using IBM SPSS 22, followed by
confirmatory factor analysis and structural equation modeling using IBM SPSS AMOS 24. The
validity of the measurement model and mediation analysis were performed in a lignment with
the research objectives. The results indicated a positive and significant impact of digital
marketing on online purchase intention ( Dastane, 2020) . However, the mediating effect of
CRM was found to be insignificant. By providing a structural model of digital marketing and
purchase intention, this study made a valuable contribution to the literature and offered insights
into measuring the impact of d igital marketing on online purchase intention in the context of
Malaysian e-commerce (Dastane, 2020).
The impact of digital marketing channels (E -mail Marketing, Mobile Marketing, and
Retargeting) on the consumer buying decision process in the Egyptian market 125. The research
utilized an online questionnaire administered through simple sampling methods to collect data
from the Egyptian market. Out of 285 distributed questionnaires, 213 complete responses were
collected, resulting in a response rate of 74.7%.
The findings reveal that E -mail Marketing has a significantly positive influence on consumer
buying decisions in two phases: post -purchase and information research. However, in the
purchase decision phase, it has a negative effect on customer decisions. On  the other hand,
Mobile Marketing shows a negative impact on consumer decisions across all stages of the
consumer buying decision process in the Egyptian market (Omar & Atteya, 2020).
Regarding Retargeting, it has a significant effect on consumer decisions in the evaluation stage,
as well as during information research, need recognition, purchase decision, and post-purchase
stages. Among the digital marketing channels studied, Retargeti ng proves to be the most
influential variable in the consumer buying decision process. This significant effect in the
evaluation stage can be attributed to Retargeting's targeted approach towards consumers who
have already shown interest in the product (Omar & Atteya, 2020).
The impact of various digital marketing forms on customer attitudes towards digital marketing
and their purchase intention was explored (Nguyen & Nhu, 2022). The study involved a sample
of 210 potential customers, specifically individuals considered important in the B2B
purchasing chain at the enterprise level. Through the utilization of partial least squares
structural equation modelling techniques, the findi ngs indicated that 3 out of the 4 digital
marketing forms examined were perceived as important and suitable by the participants

Conclusion
Digital marketing is of increasing importance in many organisations as it is a synthesis for
research and practical experience, thus, this research reveals more insight into the digital
research field. The conclusions of this study are based on the results . The study was able to
show that social media marketing has a positive and significant impact on consumer purchase
intention among University of Port Harcourt students. The study also found that video
marketing has a negative and significant impact on con sumer purchase intention among
University of Port Harcourt students. The study also shows that email marketing has a positive
and non-significant impact on consumer purchase intention among University of Port Harcourt
students.
