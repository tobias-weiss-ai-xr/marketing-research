# A Review of Multidimensional Integration Mechanism of High-end Service Innovation under the Background of Pet Economy

Wannuo Jin

Abstract. With the rapid development of the pet economy, the pet industry is gradually transforming
from the basic consumption stage dominated by products to the high-end consumption stage
centered on services. In this process, service innovation has become an important driving force to
promote the upgrading of the industry, and it also profoundly affects the evolution of consumer
behavior and business models. Based on the existing literature and industry development practice,
this paper systematically combs and summarizes the development logic of high-end pet service from
four aspects: industry transformation, service innovation types, consumer behavior, and business
model. It is found that the innovation of pet service presents the characteristics of multi-dimensional
parallel development, which can be divided into four dimensions: function, experience, emotion, and
technology, and forms a "multi-dimensional integration" innovation model through collaborative
integration in practical application. On this basis, this paper further constructs an integrated analysis
framework, pointing out that service innovation promotes consumers' willingness to pay a premium
by improving perceived service quality and customer value. At the same time, consumer behavior
shows an obvious trend of high-end, and its decision-making process realizes a comprehensive
balance between functional needs, safety needs, and emotional needs. In addition, high-end pet
service enterprises have realized the continuous optimization and value integration of their business
models through the application of the "medical+hotel" mixed model, membership system, and digital
platform. From the perspective of multi-dimensional service innovation, this paper enriches the
relevant research in the field of service marketing and consumer behavior, and provides theoretical
reference and practical enlightenment for high-end pet service enterprises to achieve differentiated
development in competition. However, this paper is mainly based on literature and industry data,
lacking empirical tests, and future research can further combine quantitative or experimental
methods to further verify the relevant mechanisms.
Keywords: Pet economy; Service innovation; High-end consumption; Industry transformation.
1. Introduction
In recent years, with the improvement of residents' income level and the change of lifestyle, the
pet economy has shown an obvious trend of upgrading from "basic consumption" to "high-end
service". The traditional consumption structure based on pet food, basic foster care, and simple care
is gradually changing to a more professional, refined, and experience-oriented service form
(Euromonitor International, 2025; Grand View Research, 2025). At the same time, the role of pets in
human families is also changing, from "functional companionship" to "emotional family members".
This trend of "pet humanization" has significantly promoted consumers' spending on pet-related
products and services (Boya, Dotson, & Hyatt, 2012; Holbrook & Woodside, 2008).
In this context, a series of new service models are constantly emerging. For example, the
"medical+hotel" model, which combines professional medical care with high-end foster care
experience, reflects the development of services in the direction of specialization and integration; The
"AI+ service" model of pet health monitoring and service optimization through intelligent devices
and data analysis reflects the deep embedding of digital technology in the service industry (Rust &
Huang, 2014); In addition, "emotion-driven consumption", which takes emotional care as the core
and emphasizes the emotional connection between pets and owners, has gradually become an
important feature of high-end pet services (Belk, 1988; Holbrook & Hirschman, 1982). These
innovations not only reshape the value creation mode of the pet service industry but also promote the
development of the overall market to a higher level.

2. Theoretical Foundations
2.1 Service-Dominant Logic
Service-Dominant Logic (SDL) emphasizes that value is not unilaterally created by enterprises
and passed on to consumers, but "value co-creation" realized in the process of multi-participant
interaction (Vargo & Lusch, 2008; Vargo & Lusch, 2016). In the context of pet service, this logic is
embodied in the process of value creation in which enterprises, pet owners, and pets participate
together: enterprises provide professional services and resource support, pet owners participate in
service selection and experience feedback, and pets, as the core service objects, their health status
and behavioral response have also become an important part of value realization. Therefore, high-end
pet service is not only an upgrade of service supply, but also a multi-agent interactive and
collaborative value creation system.
2.2 Experience Economy
According to the theory of experience economy, enterprise competition has shifted from providing
products and services to creating unique consumption experiences, and finally, the "transformation"
of consumers will be realized, that is, it will have a lasting impact on the psychological, emotional,
or state level (Pine & Gilmore, 1999; Pine & Gilmore, 2011). In the field of pet service, this logic is
manifested in the transformation from basic foster care (functional layer) to immersive experience
(experience layer), such as pet vacation and rehabilitation care, and further extends to the
"transformation layer" results, such as pet health improvement and emotional stability. Research in
recent years also shows that experience-oriented service can significantly improve consumer
satisfaction and loyalty, especially in high participation service scenarios (Lemon & Verhoef, 2016).
2.3 Emotional Consumption
Emotional consumption theory emphasizes that consumers' decisions are not only based on
rational evaluation, but also driven by emotional experience and symbolic meaning (Holbrook &
Hirschman, 1982). Against the backdrop of the pet economy, "pet humanization" has become an
important trend, and pets are regarded as an important part of family emotions (Belk, 1988; Holbrook
& Woodside, 2008). This change makes consumers more inclined to pay a premium for the happiness,
health, and emotional state of pets, thus promoting the growth of demand for high-end pet services.
Recent research further points out that emotional attachment significantly enhances consumers'
willingness to pay and brand loyalty in pet-related consumption (Zhang et al., 2023).
2.4 Technology-Enabled Services
With the development of digital technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI) and Internet of
Things (IoT), the service industry is undergoing a profound technological transformation.
Technology not only improves the service efficiency but also enhances the visualization and
transparency of the service process through data collection and real-time feedback (Rust & Huang,
2014; Huang & Rust, 2021). In the field of pet service, smart wearable devices, health monitoring
systems, and mobile applications enable pet owners to know the status of their pets in real time, thus
reducing information asymmetry and improving service experience. The latest research shows that
AI-driven services can further enhance consumer satisfaction by improving perceived control and
information transparency (Wirtz et al., 2018; Mariani et al., 2023).

3. Service Transformation and Innovation in the Pet Economy
3.1 Industry Transformation
The existing research generally believes that the pet industry is undergoing a multi-dimensional
structural transformation, and its development path is mainly reflected in service-oriented, high-end,
and digital aspects (Euromonitor International, 2025; Grand View Research, 2025). Specifically, the
industry not only gradually shifts from product sales as the core to a service-oriented value creation
mode, but also shows a trend of extending from functional satisfaction to emotional and experiential
demand in terms of consumer demand and service supply. In addition, technological progress and the
intensification of market competition have also promoted the industry to evolve in the direction of
digitalization and specialization.
In order to sort out the main direction of pet industry transformation more systematically, this
paper summarizes the relevant literature and constructs a summary matrix of industry transformation
(see Table 1). Starting from the transformation dimension, the table systematically sorts out the core
viewpoints on the evolution path of the industry in different studies and their corresponding
documents, thus providing a structural basis for the follow-up analysis.

As shown in Table 1, the transformation of the pet industry is mainly reflected in seven aspects.
First of all, in the logic of value creation, the industry has changed from "product-oriented" to
"service-oriented", and service has gradually become a new growth engine. Secondly, in terms of
consumption motivation, with the deepening of the trend of "family membership" of pets,
consumption extends from functional demand to emotional demand, which promotes the
development of the high-end service market. In terms of service supply, the industry has evolved
from standardized services to customized services to meet individual needs. At the same time, the
service form has also changed from a single service to a comprehensive service system, and has
gradually integrated multiple elements such as medical care, nursing, and experience.
In addition, in the value structure, the service extends from low added value to high added value,
and the unit consumption value is improved through health management and experience improvement.
On the technical level, the application of digital technology (such as artificial intelligence and Internet
of Things) promotes the transformation of service mode to digitalization, making services more
visible and traceable. Finally, in the market structure, the industry is gradually developing from highly
decentralized to brand and specialization, and the high-end market relies more on professional ability
and service standards (Huang & Rust, 2021; Mariani et al., 2023).
Overall, Table 1 reveals the multi-dimensional characteristics of the transformation of the pet
industry, that is, from a single-dimensional structural adjustment to a systematic upgrading process
covering value creation, consumer demand, service supply, and technology application. This
transformation provides an important background for subsequent service innovation and business
model analysis.
3.2 Typology of Service Innovation
On the basis of industry transformation, pet service innovation presents the characteristics of multi-
dimensional parallel development. The existing research on service marketing and innovation shows
that the promotion of service value usually comes from the synergy of various innovative elements,
rather than the improvement of a single dimension (Vargo & Lusch, 2016; Wirtz et al., 2018). In
order to understand the relationship between different innovation forms more systematically, this
paper further constructs a two-dimensional classification framework based on the two core
dimensions of "functional value" and "emotional value", as shown in Figure 1.

Specifically, in this framework, different types of service innovation can be divided according to
their emphasis on functional and emotional dimensions. Located in the quadrant of high functional
value and low emotional value is functional innovation, which is mainly reflected in the introduction
of professional medical care and rehabilitation services, which expands pet services from basic care
to health management and recovery support, thus strengthening the professional attributes of services
(Rust & Huang, 2014). Correspondingly, in the quadrant of high emotional value and low functional
value, emotional innovation, such as enhancing the emotional connection between owners and pets
through real-time video and daily feedback, reflects the transformation of consumption from
functional orientation to emotional orientation (Holbrook & Hirschman, 1982).
Furthermore, experiential innovation is located in an area with high emotional value but relatively
low functional attributes, which emphasizes enriching service scenes and enhancing immersive
experience through pet vacations, interactive spaces, and leisure facilities (Pine & Gilmore, 1999;
Lemon & Verhoef, 2016). Technical innovation is mainly distributed in the dimension of high
functional value, which can dynamically monitor the behavior and health status of pets through
artificial intelligence and data analysis, thus improving the service efficiency and refined level
(Huang & Rust, 2021; Mariani et al., 2023).
As Figure 1 shows, these four types of innovations are not isolated from each other, but form a
dynamic integration relationship around "integrated value creation". The core feature of high-end pet
service lies in the cross-dimensional integration of different types of innovative elements, thus
achieving a balance between functionality and emotion, and ultimately promoting the transformation
of the service model from single supply to multi-dimensional value creation.
3.3 Consumer Behavior in Premium Pet Services
With the upgrading of service forms, consumer behavior has also shown systematic changes.
Existing research shows that pet consumption is gradually changing from basic expenditure to
premium, and high-income groups are more inclined to choose high-quality and high-value-added
services and show higher willingness to pay (Grand View Research, 2025; Morgan Stanley, 2022).
This trend is consistent with the overall logic of pet consumption upgrading under the background of
"family membership", that is, consumers pay more attention to quality and long-term value in pet-
related expenditures (Boya et al., 2012).
In the specific decision-making process, consumers are highly sensitive to the safety and health of
pets. Previous studies have pointed out that security and risk aversion are two of the important factors
that affect the choice in the context of high participation in services (Lemon & Verhoef, 2016). In the
field of pet service, this concern is further reflected in the emphasis on medical support ability,
especially in the context of the aging of pets and the rising demand for health management.
Professional nursing ability has become the key decision-making basis. At the same time, consumers
also attach importance to emotional satisfaction in service experience, such as maintaining emotional
connection with pets through real-time information feedback and interaction mechanism, which
reflects the important role of emotional consumption in service decision-making (Holbrook &
Hirschman, 1982; Zhang et al., 2023).
Generally speaking, the consumer behavior of high-end pet services presents the characteristics of
multi-dimensional demand superposition, that is, seeking a comprehensive balance between health-
related services, risk reduction, and emotional engagement, thus promoting the development of
service supply in the direction of multi-dimensional value integration.
3.4 Business Model Innovation
The deepening of service innovation has further promoted the reconstruction of the business model.
Existing research shows that under the background of parallel development of experience-oriented
and service-oriented enterprises, they tend to improve their overall value creation ability by
integrating various service elements (Vargo & Lusch, 2016). In the pet service industry, this trend is
embodied in the mixed mode of "medical treatment+hotel", that is, combining professional nursing

At the operational level, the membership system is widely used to enhance customer stickiness

On the whole, the business model of high-end pet service shows obvious integration characteristics,
that is, through the coordinated configuration of functional services, experience design, and technical
support, the systematic output of value is realized, and a continuous consumption relationship and
repurchase mechanism are formed in the process. This model marks the further evolution of the
service industry from single-value provision to multi-value integration.
4. Discussion
Based on the summary and analysis of industry transformation, service innovation types, consumer
behavior, and business model in Chapter 3, this paper holds that the multi-dimensional innovation of
pet service (including functional innovation, experiential innovation, and technical innovation) works
together on consumers' perception of service quality and overall value, and then affects their
willingness to pay a premium.
First of all, functional innovation can directly improve consumers' evaluation of service quality by
improving the ability of medical care and health management and enhancing the professionalism and
reliability of services (Rust & Huang, 2014). Secondly, experiential innovation can improve
consumers' perceived experience and satisfaction in the service process by constructing immersive
service scenarios (Pine & Gilmore, 1999; Lemon & Verhoef, 2016). At the same time, technological
innovation improves the transparency and controllability of the service process by means of artificial
intelligence and data analysis, and further optimizes user experience and service efficiency (Huang
& Rust, 2021). These innovative elements do not play a role in isolation, but form a synergistic effect
in the service system to jointly shape consumers' overall perception of service value.
On this basis, consumers' comprehensive perception of service quality and value is further
transformed into their willingness to pay a premium. This mechanism is consistent with the
conclusion of the relationship between experience value and willingness to pay in the existing
research, that is, high-quality and high-experience services can significantly enhance consumers'
value perception and willingness to pay (Lemon & Verhoef, 2016; Zhang et al., 2023). Therefore, the
development path of high-end pet service can be summarized as follows: Starting from service
innovation (function+experience+technology), it affects the perceived service quality and customer
value, and ultimately leads to an increase in willingness to pay a premium.
4.1 Theoretical Contributions
Based on the existing research, this paper provides a further integrated perspective on the
relationship between service innovation and consumer behavior. First of all, from the perspective of
service innovation, this paper breaks through the analytical framework that traditional research
regards innovation as a single dimension (such as technology or experience), and puts forward that
service innovation should be understood as an integrated system of multi-dimensional elements such
as function, experience, emotion, and technology (Vargo & Lusch, 2016). This perspective is helpful
to explain the value creation mechanism in the high-end service situation more comprehensively.
Secondly, this paper emphasizes the integration of experience and technology in service
innovation. Existing studies usually discuss the role of experience economy or technology
empowerment service, respectively (Pine & Gilmore, 1999; Huang & Rust, 2021), and this paper

4.2 Management Enlightenment
Based on the above analysis, this paper provides the following management enlightenment to the
practice of high-end pet service enterprises. First of all, in terms of service design, enterprises should
focus on strengthening safety and medical capabilities, and take professional nursing and health
management as core competitive elements to respond to consumers' high concern about pet safety
and health (Grand View Research, 2025). Secondly, in the process of service delivery, the
visualization level of services should be improved by means of digital technology, such as providing
real-time video and health data feedback, so as to enhance service transparency and user experience
(Huang & Rust, 2021).
In addition, enterprises should also attach importance to the construction of emotional value and
enhance consumers' sense of participation and connection in the service process through continuous
information interaction and emotional communication. This kind of emotion-oriented service design
is helpful to improve customer satisfaction and promote the establishment of long-term relationships
(Holbrook & Hirschman, 1982). Finally, at the level of business model, enterprises can build a stable
customer relationship system through the combination of a membership system and a data platform,
so as to achieve sustainable consumption and value growth (Wirtz et al., 2018).
To sum up, high-end pet service enterprises should build a multi-dimensional service value system
through the collaborative configuration of functional ability, experience design, and technical support
to meet the escalating consumer demand.
5. Conclusion
To sum up, the pet industry is experiencing a structural upgrading path from basic consumption to
high-end services. This upgrade is not only reflected in the transformation from product to service,
but also in the evolution of service from single function to comprehensive value creation, that is, by
integrating medical care, experience design, and technical support, a diversified and systematic
service system is formed (Vargo & Lusch, 2016). In this process, service innovation has become the
core driving force to promote the development of the industry, and innovative elements of different
dimensions (function, experience, and technology) can jointly improve service quality and consumer
perceived value through synergy (Rust & Huang, 2014; Huang & Rust, 2021).
At the same time, the change of consumer behavior has further strengthened this development
trend. With the deepening of "family membership" of pets, consumers' requirements for service
quality, safety, and emotional experience are constantly improving, and a stable demand for high-
value-added services is gradually formed, thus promoting the continuous expansion of the high-end
pet service market (Grand View Research, 2025; Zhang et al., 2023). Therefore, it can be considered
that the industry upgrading path, service innovation drive, and high-end consumption trend have
formed a mutually reinforcing relationship and jointly shaped the current development pattern of the
pet service industry.
Although this paper systematically examines the high-end pet service from the perspective of
service innovation and industry transformation, there are still some limitations. First of all, this paper
is mainly based on the conceptual integration of existing literature and industry reports, lacking
empirical data support, and future research can further verify the relationship through a questionnaire
survey or experimental methods. Secondly, the analysis of this paper focuses on the high-end market
