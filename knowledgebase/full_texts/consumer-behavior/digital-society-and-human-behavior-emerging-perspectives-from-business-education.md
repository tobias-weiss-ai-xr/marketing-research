Abstract
The rapid development of digital technologies has affected human behavior in business, education, communication,
finances, and in their daily lives in such a way that new opportunities arose as well as new social, ethical and
organizational problems emerged. This editorial paper will discuss nine papers that have been developed under
the common topic of Digital Society and Human Behavior: Emerging Perspectives from Business, Education,
and Social Sciences. Altogether, the papers follow the interdisciplinary approach while discussing the effects of
digitalization on consumer behavior, organizational communication, educational process, financial well-being,
and others. In particular, the papers emphasize the importance of digital marketing, social networks, and Internet
sites for consumer behavior; the role of the English language in economic growth; and the opportunities and
challenges of online learning for the youth. In addition, it highlights the importance of good financial management
in improving personal financial happiness and analyzing the concept of platform capitalism and digital power
dynamics via social theory in modern times. Last but not least, this topic also deals with persistent social issues
by explaining the theory behind intimate partner violence. Taken all together, the above contributions stress the
necessity of taking an interdisciplinary and evidence-based approach to studying human behavior in the modern
digital era and offer useful information for those dedicated to building sustainable society.

Overview of the Features Articles
The speedy development of digital technology has brought about drastic changes to how people interact, learn,
consume, use finances, and relate with others (Jian et al., 2024; Xia et al., 2024). The digital age has gone beyond
being simply an issue of technological development to be one of major influence in relation to economic development,
organizations, education, and human interaction. In our contemporary world, comprehending human behavior
needs to take an interdisciplinary approach that incorporates the study of business, communication, education,
psychology, sociology, and critical social science (Boyd & Markowitz, 2025). The need for scholarly investigation
into these interrelated fields is more relevant than ever in fostering sustainable human development. In this regard,
the articles discussed in this issue embody this interdisciplinary perspective through the analysis of how the digital
transformation impacts human decision-making, organizational performance, consumer behavior, educational
results, financial health, and broader social structures. Despite the fact that every article focuses on a specific research
problem, their overall contribution is the development of a comprehensive view of issues related to a digitalized
society.
Consumer behavior has emerged as one of the most exciting research fields within contemporary business owing to the
emergence of digital platforms and e-commerce websites. The impact of social media influences, digital marketing,
and online advertising has dramatically changed the conventional consumer decision-making process (Mrisha &
Xixiang, 2024; Stephen, 2016). Consumers tend to use digital information resources, peer reviews, and influences of
influencers while evaluating products or services (Wanigapura et al., 2025). These studies relate to the analysis of the
impact of influencer marketing on online purchase decisions, the attitudes of consumers towards newly launched
mobile phone advertisements, and factors affecting motorcycle purchase intention among undergraduate students.
In general, all these researches prove that the process of making purchase decisions is not dependent anymore on
product qualities or prices only.
The significance of communication skills in the economy of the digital age goes beyond marketing into organizations
and even the country's development. English has been identified as the lingua franca of international businesses,
international transactions, education at higher levels, and innovations (Ivanyan, 2026). Communication allows the
creation of collaborations, sharing of information and economic competition in today's global market. The article
looking at the use of the English language for Nepal's economic ambitions shows how language is more than just
a means of communication but a strategic asset that increases employability, entrepreneurial opportunities, and
international business involvement (Ahamed, 2025). The development of higher education using digital technologies
is yet another crucial topic that is covered in this journal. Learning online has increased the accessibility of education
while posing challenges in terms of the effectiveness of learning, engagement, and academic success of students
(Akpen et al., 2024; Zou et al., 2025). In addition to these benefits, excessive use of digital technology can lead to
unwanted outcomes for students as well. The studies on online learning and the effect of long-term mobile games are
good examples of the double-edged sword of digital technologies in education (Adekoya et al., 2026; Rajput, 2025).
Although digital technologies can increase the flexibility of learning and accessibility of knowledge, their excessive
use might be damaging for concentration and academic success of students (Chen & Qin, 2024; Daud, 2025).
Financial well-being has become increasingly significant within the realm of present-day professional practice as
well. Satisfaction with financial well-being encompasses more than mere salary and is directly linked to the ability of
financial planning, budgeting, saving, and prudent financial decision making (Kuutol et al., 2025; Mitra et al., 2025).
As illustrated by the study of engineers, the ability to engage in proper financial management has become an integral
aspect of the process of achieving personal satisfaction in terms of finance and quality of life in general. However,
while technological development offers many opportunities, there are also very significant social and ethical issues
that arise in this context. Digital platforms are becoming ever more prevalent in the ways that people communicate
with each other, engage in political discourse, and consume and distribute information (Asimakopoulos et al., 2025;
Chadwick et al., 2025). The article analyzing platform capitalism from a Neo-Gramscian approach contributes some
very useful theoretical insight into the way that digital platforms have an impact on power dynamics, ideology,
and cultural hegemony in modern society (Davies, 2025; Dean, 2024). Instead of acting as mere technological
infrastructure, digital platforms are becoming institutions that can have a big impact on opinion, preferences, and
norms in society (Aytac, 2024; Caruso, 2025; Dolata & Schrape, 2025). Together with these analyses, the critical
evaluation of theories related to the problem of abuse in intimate partner relationships serves as a reminder that
technical advancement alone cannot solve serious social problems. Interpersonal violence needs to be understood
using a theoretical approach from many disciplines, including psychological, sociological, feminist, ecological, and
structural theories. It is important to study these matters further for practical reasons such as prevention and policy-
making (Berktas & Eryurt, 2025; Meyer et al., 2024).

In combination, the contributions in this journal’s second issue of 2026 reflect how interdependent business,
education, communications, finance, technology, and society have become. This reflects the reality that human
behaviors cannot be studied via one narrow perspective but instead need to be looked at from an integrative point
of view that takes into account the complex nature of today’s social and organizational environment. The articles
included herein provide lessons for educators, managers, policy makers, and researchers alike on how to deal with
digitalization challenges. It gives us great pleasure to present this collection of scientific articles, which clearly
reflects the commitment of the journal in fostering high-level research. The variety of topics addressed by these
articles is not only important for the scholarly debate, but also provides insight into the continuously changing
reality of people, organizations, and societies. We hope that these papers will serve as an incentive for future
research and productive debate.
