ABSTRACT
This study explores the impact of AI-driven personalized marketing on consumer behavior in the
fashion industry, focusing on purchase decisions and intentions. Using a quantitative approach,
data from 350 respondents were analyzed to examine direct, mediated, and moderated
relationships among key variables. The results reveal that AI -driven personalized marketing
significantly influences consumer purchase decisions and intentions, highlighting its effectiveness
in aligning with consumer preferences. Message relevance and brand  loyalty are confirmed as
mediators, demonstrating their critical roles in enhancing the impact of personalization strategies.
Additionally, consumer trust in AI moderates the relationship between AI -driven marketing and
purchase decisions, amplifying its effect when trust levels are high. These findings contribute to
the theoretical understanding of personalized marketing by integrating mediating and moderating
variables, offering a nuanced perspective on consumer engagement. Practically, the study
emphasizes the importance of building trust, delivering relevant messages, and fostering loyalty
to optimize marketing strategies in the fashion industry. The research provides actionable insights
for businesses aiming to leverage AI -driven personalization to dri ve both short -term sales and
long-term consumer engagement in a competitive digital marketplace.
Keywords: AI -driven marketing, personalized marketing, consumer behavior, brand loyalty,
consumer trust
INTRODUCTION
The rapid advancements in artificial intel ligence (AI) have transformed the marketing
landscape, enabling businesses to deliver highly personalized and data -driven experiences to
consumers. AI-driven personalized marketing leverages machine learning and big data analytics
to craft tailored recommendations, targeted advertisements, and customized shopping experiences
(Feng, 2024). In the fashion industry, where trends shift rapidly and consumer preference s are
diverse, personalization has become a critical tool for engaging consumers and driving sales.
Personalized marketing not only captures attention but also fosters a sense of relevance and trust,
significantly influencing purchase behaviors (Chandra et al., 2022) (Suleman et al., 2021). Despite
its growing adoption, the effectiveness of AI-driven personalized marketing in shaping consumer
purchase decisions remains underexplored, particularly in the context of the fashion industry.

Indonesia, as one of Southeast Asia’s largest fashion markets, presents a unique opportunity
to examine the impact of AI -driven personalized marketing. The Indonesian fashion industry,
driven by its diverse cultural heritage and growing e-commerce sector, has experienced significant
growth over the past decade  (Thien Duc & Mujahida, 2024) . According to Statista, Indonesia's
online fashion market is expected to generate substantial revenue growth, fueled by the increasing
adoption of digital platforms and AI technologies. With over 75% of Indonesian consumers
actively engaging in e-commerce, personalized marketing has emerged as a key strategy for brands
to stand out in a competitive market. However, local fashion brands often f ace challenges in
leveraging AI effectively due to limited technological infrastructure and varying levels of
consumer trust in AI systems.
Existing studies on personalized marketing often focus on traditional methods or single
aspects of AI-driven strategies, such as recommendation systems or dynamic (Luo et al., 2022) .
However, the holistic impact of AI-driven personalization—combining tailored content, predictive
analytics, and real -time adaptability —on consumer purchase decisions in Indonesia remains
underexplored. Moreover, while the role of personalization in enhancing consumer satisfaction has
been established, its impact on consumer trust, loyalty, and actual purchase decisions in Indonesia's
dynamic fashion market remains ambiguous. This gap in the literature highlights the need for a
comprehensive investigation into how AI -driven personalized marketing influences consumer
purchase decisions and intentions in the Indonesian fashion industry.
Adding to this complexity, consumer trust in AI and the relevance of personalized marketing
messages are emerging as critical factors influencing the success of such strategies. While research
has examined the general role of trust in marketing, few studie s explore its moderating effect on
the relationship between AI-driven personalization and consumer behaviors (Song & Lin, 2024).
Similarly, the mediating role of message relevance and brand loyalty in driving purchase decisions
remains under-researched, particularly in fashion, where emotional connections and perceived
alignment with personal style are key determinants of consumer behavior (Andrade & Tumelero,
2022) (Suleman, Sabil, et al., n.d.; Suleman, Supiandi, et al., 2023) . Addressing these gaps, this
study aims to provide a deeper understandin g of the mechanisms through which AI -driven
personalized marketing influences consumer purchase decisions, with a particular focus on
Indonesia’s fashion industry.
The novelty of this research lies in its integrated approach, examining not only the direct
effects of AI -driven personalized marketing on consumer purchase decisions but also the
mediating roles of message relevance and brand loyalty, as well as the moderating role of consumer
trust in AI. By focusing on Indonesia’s fashion industry, the study sheds light on a context where
personalization is both highly valued and challenging to implement due to rapidly changing trends,
diverse consumer preferences, and v arying levels of trust in AI systems. The findings will
contribute to the theoretical understanding of AI applications in personalized marketing and offer

LITERATURE REVIEW & HYPOTHESIS DEVELOPMENT
AI in Marketing
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has revolutionized marketing by enabling data-driven strategies
that cater to individual consumer needs. AI applications in marketing include predictive analytics,
recommendation systems, and real-time personalization, which allow businesses to create tailored
experiences (Feng, 2024) . AI-powered tools, such as chatbots and virtual assistants, enhance
customer engagement by providing instant support and personalized recommendations (Luo et al.,
2022). In e-commerce, AI has been shown to improve marketing efficiency by predicting consumer
behavior and automating targeted campaigns (Zelda et al., 2024)(Rukiastiandari et al., 2023).
Despite these advancements, challenges remain, such as consumer mistrust of AI systems
and concerns about data privacy (Huang et al., 2023). Research highlights that while AI enhances
marketing precision, its success depends on consumer acceptance and trust. Particularly in
industries like fashion, where aesthetic preferences and emotional connections influence behavior,
understanding how AI influences consumer decisions is crucial. This study addresses the gap by
exploring the role of AI in personalized marketing and its impact on consumer purchase behavior
in the fashion industry.
Personalized Marketing
Personalized marketing refers to strategies that tailor content, recommendations, and offers
to individual consumers based on their preferences, behaviors, and demographics (Jha et al., 2022;
Mujahida et al., 2024). AI has made personalized marketing more scalable and efficient, allowing
businesses to analyze vast datasets and deliver real -time personalized experiences. Personalized
marketing increases consumer satisfaction and engagement by making interactions more relevant
and meaningful (Chandra et al., 2022)(Herawaty et al., 2022; Suleman, Zuniarti, et al., 2023).
In the fashion industry, personalized marketing plays a vital role due to the diversity of
consumer preferences and the dynamic nature of trends. For instance, AI-driven recommendation
systems can suggest clothing and accessories that match a consumer ’s style and past purchases,
enhancing the likelihood of purchase (Yaswanthraj et al., 2024). However, while the benefits of
personalization are wel l-documented, its effectiveness in influencing purchase decisions,
particularly in fashion, remains underexplored. This study examines how personalized marketing
impacts both purchase intentions and decisions, contributing to the literature on AI -driven
strategies.

Brand Loyalty
Brand loyalty is a critical outcome of effective marketing strategies, reflecting a consumer's
commitment to repeatedly purchase from a specific brand (Andrade & Tumelero, 2022; Bawazir
et al., 2024; Mujahida et al., 2024). Loyalty is influenced by trust, satisfaction, and perceived value,
and it plays a significant role in driving long -term business success. Research shows that
personalized marketing fosters brand loyalty by creating emotional connections and demonstrating
a brand’s understanding of consumer needs (Zelda et al., 2024).
In the context of AI -driven marketing, loyalty is enhanced when consumers perceive
personalization as authentic and valuabl e. Loyal customers are more likely to engage with
personalized recommendations and remain committed to the brand, even in competitive markets.
However, the mediating role of brand loyalty in the relationship between AI -driven personalized
marketing and consumer purchase behavior has received limited attention in the literature. This
study explores how personalized marketing fosters loyalty and how that, in turn, influences
purchase decisions, particularly in the fashion industry.
Consumer Trust
Consumer trust refers to the confidence consumers have in the ability of a system or brand
to deliver reliable, secure, and beneficial experiences (Ilmi et al., 2023). In the context of AI-driven
personalized marketing, trust is a critical factor influencing the effectiveness of marketing
strategies. When consumers trust the AI system , they are more likely to perceive personalized
recommendations as credible and valuable, enhancing their engagement and likelihood of purchase
(Huang et al., 2023) . Conversely, a lack of trust can lead to skepticism, reducing the impact of
personalized marketing efforts. T rust is particularly relevant in industries like fashion, where
subjective preferences and personal style play a significant role in purchase decisions (Andrade &
Tumelero, 2022).
Building consumer trust in AI requires transparency about how data is collected and used,
as well as the ability of the system to provide consistent and accurate recommendations. Studies
highlight that consumers are more like ly to trust AI systems that demonstrate ethical practices,
such as respecting data privacy and avoiding biases in personalization (Luo et al., 2022)(Suleman,
Nurhayaty, et al., n.d.). Moreover, trust moderates the relationship between AI-driven personalized
marketing and consumer behavior, amplifying the positive effects of personalization when trust is
high (Zelda et al., 2024). This study addresses the role of consumer trust in influencing the success
of AI-driven marketing strategies, exploring its moderating effect on purchase decisions and
intentions within the fashion industry.

AI-Driven Personalized Marketing and Consumer Purchase Decisions
Personalized marketing has become a cornerstone of modern marketing strategies, driven by the
increasing capabilities of artificial intelligence (AI). AI -driven personalized marketing utilize s
machine learning algorithms and big data analytics to create customized recommendations, offers,
and experiences tailored to individual consumer preferences (Feng, 2024). Such personalization
enhances the relevance of marketing efforts, making consumers feel valued and understood, which
directly impacts their purchase decisions (Chandra et al., 2022) . In the fashion industry,
personalization is particularly critical due to the diversity of consumer tastes and rapidly changing
trends. When consumers receive recommendations or offers that align with their preferences, they
are more likely to proceed with a purchase (Zelda et al., 2024)(Suleman, Suyoto, et al., n.d.).
Previous studies indicate that consumers are more likely to trust and act on recommendations when
they perceive them as personalized and relevant  (Luo et al., 2022) . By leveraging AI to deliver
such experiences, brands can influence consumer purchase decisions effectively. Ther efore, we
hypothesize:
H1: AI-driven personalized marketing has a positive and significant effect on consumer purchase
decisions in the fashion industry.
The Mediating Role of Message Relevance
The relevance of personalized marketing messages is a critical  factor influencing their
effectiveness. Message relevance refers to how well the content aligns with the consumer's
interests, preferences, and needs (Huang et al., 2023). AI-driven marketing enhances relevance by
analyzing consumer data and generating tailored content tha t resonates with individual buyers.
When messages are perceived as relevant, consumers are more likely to engage with them, leading
to higher trust and eventual purchase decisions (Jha et al., 2022).
In the fashion industry, where consumers are constantly  exposed to a plethora of options,
message relevance helps brands stand out and build stronger connections with their audience.
Relevance also reduces decision fatigue, as consumers are presented with options that match their
preferences (Yaswanthraj et al., 2024). Given its pivotal role, we hypothesize:
H2: The relevance of personalized marketing messages mediates the relationship between AI -
driven personalized marketing and consumer purchase decisions.
The Moderating Role of Consumer Trust in AI
Consumer trust in AI plays a significant role in determining the effectiveness of AI -driven
personalized marketing. Trust in AI reflects the consumer's confidence in the system's ability to
provide accurate, secure, and beneficial recommendations . When consumers trust AI, they are
more likely to perceive personalized marketing messages as credible and act on them. Conversely,

AI-Driven Personalized Marketing and Consumer Purchase Intentions
Purchase intentions represent the likelihood of a consumer buying a product in the future.
AI-driven personalized marketing influences purchase intentions by creating a seamless and
engaging shopping experience that aligns with consumer p references (Bunea et al., 2024) .
Personalized offers and recommendations enhance consumer satisfaction and increase the
likelihood of purchase (Chandra et al., 2022). In the fashion industry, where consumers seek unique

By understanding consumer preferences and delivering tailored solutions, AI -driven
personalized marketing bridges the gap between consumer expectations and brand offerings, thus
influencing purchase intentions. We hypothesize:
H4: AI-driven personalized marketing has a positive and significant effect on consumer purchase

likely to remain loyal, which subsequently increases their purchase decisions (Zelda et al., 2024).
In the fashion industry, where con sumer-brand relationships are often built on trust and
satisfaction, personalized marketing plays a pivotal role in nurturing loyalty. Loyal customers are
not only more likely to purchase but also act as advocates for the brand. Therefore, we hypothesize:
H5: Brand loyalty mediates the relationship between AI -driven personalized marketing and

RESEARCH METHOD
This study employs a quantitative approach to explore the effects of AI-driven personalized
marketing on consumer purcha se decisions and intentions in the fashion industry. Data were
collected through a structured survey distributed to 350 respondents, focusing on individuals with
experience in interacting with AI -driven marketing strategies such as personalized
recommendations, targeted advertisements, or curated shopping experiences.
The target population included active consumers of fashion products who had recently
interacted with AI-based marketing tools in Indonesia. Respondents were recruited through online
platforms and social media to ensure diversity in age, gender, and geographic location. A total of
350 valid responses were collected. Each respondent provided informed consent, ensuring the
ethical collection of data.

This study examines the impact of AI-driven personalized marketing on consumer behavior
in Indonesia’s fashion industry, focusing on purchase decisions and intentions. The findings
confirm that personalized marketing strategies significantly influence consumer decisions, with
