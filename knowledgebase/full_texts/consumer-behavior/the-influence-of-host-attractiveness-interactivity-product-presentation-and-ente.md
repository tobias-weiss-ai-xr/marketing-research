Abstract
This study aims to analyze the influence of host attractiveness, interactivity, product presentation, and
entertainment value on purchase intention mediated by trust within the context of live show shopping on
Shopee Live. The evolution of live commerce has shifted consumer behavior toward more interactive and
experience-based engagement, making trust a pivotal factor in purchasing decisions. This study employs a
quantitative approach using an online survey distributed to Shopee Live users. Data was analyzed using the
PLS-SEM method to examine the relationships between variables. The results indicate that entertainment
value and product presentation have a positive and significant impact on trust, whereas host attractiveness and
interactivity do not show a significant influence. Furthermore, trust is shown to have a very strong positive
effect on purchase intention and acts as a mediating variable in the relationship between several independent
variables and purchase intention. These findings confirm that in live commerce, entertainment and product
presentation quality play a more dominant role in building consumer trust than other factors. This study
contributes theoretically to the development of S-O-R-based consumer behavior models and offers practical
implications for businesses in designing effective live streaming strategies.

Keywords: host attractiveness; interactivity; live commerce; purchase intention; trust

INTRODUCTION

The evolution of digital technology has transformed consumer behavior in e-commerce from
passive shopping into a more interactive experience through live commerce on platforms such as
Shopee Live. According to Liu & Zhang (2024), live streaming e-commerce integrates real-time
interaction among consumers, hosts, and platforms, thereby enhancing the shopping experience and
driving purchase intention through social presence and trust. In the live commerce environment, trust
is a crucial factor because consumers cannot evaluate products firsthand. N. Chen & Yang (2023)
explain that trust helps mitigate perceived risk, thereby increasing consumer confidence in conducting
online transactions. Furthermore, Jiang et al. (2024) found that host characteristics specifically the
ability to provide entertainment and demonstrate expertise can boost both trust and consumer
purchase intention.
In addition to host characteristics, Liu & Zhang (2024) demonstrated that interactivity between
the host and the consumer enhances social presence and trust, ultimately driving purchase intention.
Research by H. Zhang et al. (2024) further indicates that l ive streaming characteristics such as
interaction and perceived consumer value contribute to increased purchase intention on live
commerce platforms. Although research on live commerce continues to evolve, most studies have
focused on specific factors and have yet to integrate host attractiveness, interactivity, product
presentation, and entertainment value with trust acting as a mediating variable particularly within the

The rise of live commerce has not only transformed how consumers access product information
but has also altered the purchase decision -making process by offering a more immersive and
interactive experience. Unlike conventional e-commerce, which relies solely on images and product
descriptions, live streaming allows consumers to watch live product demonstrations, ask hosts
questions, and receive real-time responses. Liu et al. (2022) explain that the characteristics of live
commerce specifically interactivity, authenticity, and entertainment enhance trust and the "flow
experience," thereby positively influencing purchase intention. Their research indicates that an
interactive shopping experience helps reduce consumer uncertainty during the product evaluation
process. Similar findings were reported by Qing & Jin (2022), who identified perceived trust, perceived
value, and perceived usefulness as key drivers of purchase intention on live-streaming e-commerce
platforms. Furthermore, N. Zhang (2023a) demonstrated that various technological features of live
streaming such as visibility, interactivity, guided shopping, and trading affordance boost trust, which
in turn directly impacts purchase intention, as explained by the Stimulus–Organism–Response (S-O-
R) framework.
In the context of live commerce, trust serves as a psychological mechanism bridging the
influence of various live streaming characteristics on purchase decisions. Consumer trust is built not
only through platform credibility but is also shaped by the host' s ability to convey accurate
information, respond promptly to inquiries, and maintain transparent communication throughout the
live session. Liu et al. (2022) found that interactions during live streaming enhance social presence
and consumer conformity, thereby indirectly driving an increase in purchase intention. Meanwhile,
Dong et al. (2022) demonstrated that interactions whether between the host and consumers or among
consumers themselves boost perceived value, which in turn strengthens purchase intention. These
findings indicate that trust and perceived value play pivotal roles in explaining how communication
during live streaming influences consumer purchasing behavior.
Beyond interaction quality, product presentation and entertainment value are also key
characteristics distinguishing live commerce from other forms of digital marketing. Live product
demonstrations allow consumers to realistically evaluate a product's functions, quality, size, and usage,
thereby reducing information asymmetry and perceived risk. N. Zhang (2023a) explains that richer,
interactive, and informative product presentations enhance perceived product value, which positively
influences purchase intention. Conversely, Liu & Zhang (2024) show that entertainment elements
during live streaming sessions enhance the positive consumer experience and strengthen trust in both
the host and the promoted products. Thus, a combination of host attractiveness, interactivity, product
presentation, entertainment value, and trust acts as a set of complementary factors in shaping
purchase intention. However, research integrating all these variables into a single mediation model—
particularly within the context of Shopee Live in Indonesia remains relatively limited; this study
therefore aims to address that gap using the Stimulus Organism Response (S -O-R) framework
(Makmor et al., 2024).
Despite the rapid development of live commerce, the increasing number of consumers who
watch live shopping sessions does not necessarily translate into purchase intention. This condition
raises an important research issue regarding which characteristics of  live streaming are actually
capable of building consumer trust and encouraging purchase intention. In live commerce, consumers
are exposed to various stimuli simultaneously, including the attractiveness of the host, interaction with
the host, product presentation, and entertainment value. However, these stimuli may not have the
same level of influence on consumers' psychological responses and purchasing intentions. Therefore,
identifying which factors are more effective in developing trust is important for  understanding
consumer behavior in the Shopee Live environment.
The relevance of this issue is supported by the substantial development of the Indonesian e-
commerce market. According to data reported by Katadata based on Momentum Works, the total gross
merchandise value (GMV) of e-commerce in Indonesia reached US$53.8 billion in 2023, equivalent to
approximately Rp867.2 trillion. Shopee accounted for approximately 40% of the total e-commerce
GMV, making it the largest platform among the major e-commerce platforms in Indonesia. In addition,
a report on live shopping in Indonesia showed that Shopee Live recorded the highest share of both
transaction volume and transaction value among the platforms examined, with 56% of transaction
volume and 54% of transaction value during the reported period. These figures indicate that Shopee

Based on this phenomenon, a research problem emerges concerning the relative importance of
host attractiveness, interactivity, product presentation, and entertainment value in developing
consumer trust and subsequently influencing purchase intention. Although previous studies have
examined live streaming, trust, and purchase intention, fewer studies have simultaneously
incorporated these four characteristics as antecedents of trust within a single research model,
particularly in the context of Shopee Live in Indonesia. This issue is important because understanding
the role of each characteristic can help explain why consumers may be attracted to a live shopping
session but still hesitate to purchase. Therefore, this study investigates the influence of host
attractiveness, interactivity, product presentation, and entertainment value on purchase intention
through trust, providing a more comprehensive understanding of the mechanisms underlying
consumer purchase intention in Shopee Live (Arisman & Imam, 2022).
Purchase intention refers to a consumer’s willingness or likelihood to purchase a product after
evaluating information obtained during the shopping process. In live commerce, purchase intention
can be influenced by consumers’ interactions with the host, the quality of product information, the
overall shopping experience, and their level of trust in the seller and platform. Unlike conventional e-
commerce, live commerce allows consumers to obtain information and responses in real time, which
can strengthen consumers’ confidence in making purchasing decisions. Liu & Zhang (2024) found that
interactions between consumers, hosts, and the platform significantly enhance purchase intention by
strengthening social presence and trust. FurthermoreZou & Fu (2024) demonstrated that the host’s
social image and emotional connection established during live streaming can increase consumers’
desire to purchase products.
Trust refers to consumers’ belief that the information provided, products offered, and seller
involved in a transaction are reliable and credible. Trust is particularly important in live commerce
because consumers cannot physically inspect products before purchasing them. Therefore, consumers
rely on the information presented by the host and their interaction with the seller to reduce uncertainty
and perceived risk. Marcelita Weyna et al. (2026) found that the quality of streamer interaction can
enhance consumer trust and social presence, which subsequently contributes to purchase intention.
Liu & Zhang (2024) also found that trust plays a mediating role between interactivity and purchase
intention in live streaming e-commerce. These findings indicate that trust functions as an important
psychological mechanism through which consumers evaluate the credibility of information and
develop confidence in purchasing products through live commerce.
Host attractiveness refers to the physical appearance, communication style, personality, and
overall appeal of a host that can shape consumers’ perceptions during a live shopping session. An
attractive host can create a more positive first impression and increase consumers’ attention toward
the information and products presented. In addition, attractiveness can contribute to the development
of favorable perceptions toward the host, which may subsequently increase consumers’ confidence in
the information delivered by the host. Q. Zhang et al. (2024) found that host attractiveness positively
influences consumers’ perceived value and purchase intention in live commerce. The attractiveness of
a host may also facilitate the development of trust because consumers tend to respond more positively
to hosts who are perceived as appealing and credible. When consumers perceive the host positively,
they may be more willing to accept the information presented during the live stream and perceive the
host as a reliable source of product information. Therefore, greater host attractiveness is expected to
increase consumer trust in the live commerce environment.

H1: Host attractiveness has a positive effect on trust.

Interactivity refers to the extent to which consumers can engage in real -time, two-way
communication with the host, seller, or other consumers during a live shopping session. Interactivity
enables consumers to ask questions, obtain immediate responses, and receive additional information
regarding the products being offered. This real-time communication can reduce uncertainty and
increase consumers’ perceptions that the host is responsive and willing to assist them.
Liu & Zhang (2024) demonstrated that interactions between consumers and streamers, as well
as interactions among consumers, enhance social presence, trust, and purchase intention. Similarly,
Marcelita Weyna et al. (2026) found that the quality of streamer interaction contributes positively to
trust and social presence. These findings suggest that active and responsive communication during
live commerce can strengthen consumers’ confidence in the host and the information provided.
Therefore, a higher level of interactivity is expected to increase consumer trust.

H2: Interactivity has a positive effect on trust.
Product Presentation
Product presentation refers to the way a host communicates product information, demonstrates
product usage, explains product features, and presents product benefits during a live shopping session.
Effective product presentation allows consumers to obtain more detailed and observable information
about a product, which is particularly important because consumers cannot physically examine
products through an online platform. A clear and informative product presentation can increase
consumers’ understanding of product characteristics and reduce uncertainty regarding product
quality. Amarullah et al. (2025) found that product visibility and perceived diagnosticity obtained
through live product presentations can enhance consumers’ purchase intention in live commerce.
When products are presented clearly and their features are demonstrated in detail, consumers may
perceive the information as more useful and credible. This can increase their confidence in the product
and the seller, thereby strengthening trust. Therefore, better product presentation is expected to have
a positive effect on consumer trust.

H3: Product presentation has a positive effect on trust.
Entertainment Value
Entertainment value refers to the extent to which consumers perceive a live shopping session as
enjoyable, interesting, and entertaining. In live commerce, entertainment can make the shopping
experience more engaging and encourage consumers to remain involved in the live stream. Unlike
conventional online shopping, live commerce combines product information with entertainment and
social interaction, creating an experience that can generate stronger emotional responses from
consumers. Jiang et al. (2024) found that entertainment provided by streamers enhances consumers’
trust and flow experience, which subsequently contributes positively to purchase intention. An
entertaining live stream can create a more enjoyable interaction between consumers and hosts, making
consumers more willing to engage with the host and pay attention to the information provided. As
consumers develop a more positive experience during the live stream, their perceptions of the host and
the shopping environment may become more favorable, which can strengthen trust. Therefore, higher
entertainment value is expected to positively influence consumer trust.

H4: Entertainment value has a positive effect on trust.
Trust and Purchase Intention
Trust is an important determinant of purchase intention in live commerce because purchasing
decisions are often made without consumers physically examining the products. When consumers
trust the host, seller, and information provided during a live stream, they are more likely to perceive
the purchasing process as reliable and less risky. This increased confidence can encourage consumers
to proceed with a purchase. Marcelita Weyna et al. (2026) demonstrated that streamer interaction can
strengthen trust and subsequently contribute to purchase intention. Liu & Zhang (2024) similarly
found that trust plays an important role in explaining consumers’ purchase intention in live-streaming
e-commerce. These findings suggest that consumers who have greater trust in the information and
seller are more likely to develop a stronger intention to purchase. Therefore, trust is expected to have
a positive effect on purchase intention.

H5: Trust has a positive effect on purchase intention.

Trust can also function as a mediating variable between characteristics of the live commerce
environment and consumers’ purchase intention. In this study, host attractiveness, interactivity,
product presentation, and entertainment value represent external stimuli that can shape consumers’
psychological evaluations, particularly their level of trust. Once trust is established, consumers may
become more confident in the information and products presented, which can subsequently increase
their purchase intention.
Host Attractiveness, Trust, and Purchase Intention
An attractive host can create favorable consumer perceptions and increase consumers’
willingness to engage with the host. Positive perceptions toward the host can contribute to greater
confidence in the information delivered during the live stream. Q. Zhang et al. (2024)  found that host
attractiveness positively influences perceived value and purchase intention. When attractiveness
contributes to the development of trust, that trust can subsequently encourage consumers to purchase
the promoted product. Therefore, trust is expected to mediate the relationship between host
attractiveness and purchase intention.

H6: Trust mediates the relationship between host attractiveness and purchase intention.
Interactivity, Trust, and Purchase Intention
Interactivity provides consumers with opportunities to communicate directly with the host and
obtain immediate responses to their questions. This process can reduce uncertainty and increase
consumers’ confidence in the information provided. Liu & Zhang (2024)  found that interactivity
enhances social presence and trust, while trust subsequently contributes to purchase intention.
Therefore, interactivity may influence purchase intention not only directly but also through the
development of consumer trust.

H7: Trust mediates the relationship between interactivity and purchase intention.
Product Presentation, Trust, and Purchase Intention
Product presentation provides consumers with information that helps them evaluate product
characteristics, quality, and benefits. A detailed and observable product presentation can increase the
diagnosticity of the information and reduce uncertainty. Amarullah et al. (2025) found that live
product presentation can enhance product visibility and perceived diagnosticity, which contributes to
purchase intention. When consumers perceive product information as clear and reliable, their
confidence in the product and seller may increase, leading to greater trust and, ultimately, purchase
intention. Therefore, trust is expected to mediate the relationship between product presentation and
purchase intention.

H8: Trust mediates the relationship between product presentation and purchase intention.
Entertainment Value, Trust, and Purchase Intention
Entertainment value can create an enjoyable and engaging live shopping experience, which may
encourage consumers to develop a more positive perception of the host and shopping environment.
Jiang et al. (2024) found that entertainment provided by streamers enhances trust and flow
experience, which subsequently contributes to purchase intention. This indicates that entertainment
may influence consumers’ purchasing decisions through the psychological process of developing trust.
Therefore, consumers who experience greater entertainment value during live shopping are expected
to develop higher trust, which subsequently increases their purchase intention.

H9: Trust mediates the relationship between entertainment value and purchase intention.
Conceptual Framework
This study adopts the Stimulus–Organism–Response (S-O-R) theory proposed by Makmor et
al. (2024) as the underlying theoretical framework to explain how characteristics of the live shopping
environment influence consumers’ internal psychological states and subsequently affect their
behavioral responses. The S-O-R theory proposes that external environmental stimuli (S) influence an
individual’s internal psychological state (O), which subsequently leads to a behavioral response (R). In
the context of online and live-streaming commerce, this framework is useful because live shopping
combines commercial information, real-time interaction, social presence, and entertainment in one
digital environment. Previous studies have applied the S-O-R framework to explain how live-streaming
features influence consumers’ psychological evaluations, trust, flow experience, and purchase
intention (Liu et al., 2022; Wongkitrungrueng & Assarut, 2020; N. Zhang, 2023a)

In this study, the Stimulus (S) represents the external characteristics experienced by consumers
during Shopee Live shopping. These stimuli consist of host attractiveness, interactivity, product
presentation, and entertainment value. Host attractiveness refers to the visual appeal, personality,
credibility, and overall attractiveness of the host. An attractive and persuasive host may increase
viewers’ attention and positive perceptions of the live -streaming session. Research by
Wongkitrungrueng & Assarut (2020) shows that the characteristics and credibility of live-streaming
sellers can strengthen consumer engagement and trust. Similarly, Xu et al. (n.d.) found that the
attractiveness and expertise of live-streaming hosts influence viewers’ engagement and purchase-
related responses.
Interactivity refers to the extent to which consumers can communicate with the host and receive
immediate responses during the live session. It includes real-time communication, responsiveness,
opportunities to ask questions, and interaction through commen ts or other platform features.
Interactivity is an important stimulus because it reduces the psychological distance between
consumers and sellers and creates a more social shopping experience. N. Zhang (2023a) found that
interactivity is an important factor in developing trust in live-streaming commerce. In addition,
Wongkitrungrueng & Assarut (2020) reported that real-time interaction contributes to consumer
engagement and relationship development in live-streaming commerce.
Product presentation refers to the way products are demonstrated, explained, visualized, and
evaluated during the live session. In Shopee Live, consumers may observe product demonstrations,
product details, usage instructions, prices, promotions, and responses to questions. Therefore, product
presentation can reduce information asymmetry and help consumers evaluate product quality before
purchasing. Liu et al. (2022) explain that visualization and the quality of information presented during
live streaming can influence consumers’ psychological experiences and purchase intention. Similarly,
Liu et al. (2022) found that visualization and professional presentation are important live-streaming
features that affect consumers’ responses.
Entertainment value refers to the extent to which the live shopping experience is enjoyable,
exciting, and engaging. Unlike conventional online shopping, live shopping often includes humor,
storytelling, games, demonstrations, limited-time promotions, and other entertaining activities. These
elements can increase consumers’ attention and encourage them to remain in the live session.
According to N. Zhang (2023b), entertainment is one of the important characteristics of live streaming
that can influence consumers’ internal experiences and purchase intention. Liu et al. (2022) also found
that live-streaming features can influence purchase intention through consumers’ flow experience and
trust. Based on these findings, host attractiveness, interactivity, produc t presentation, and
entertainment value are theoretically appropriate as stimulus variables in this study.
The Organism (O) in this study is represented by trust. Within the S -O-R framework, the
organism refers to the consumer’s internal cognitive or affective state resulting from exposure to
external stimuli. Trust is particularly relevant in live-streaming commerce because consumers must
evaluate the credibility of the host, the accuracy of the product information, the reliability of the seller,
and the expected quality of the product before making a purchase decision. In an online environment,
consumers cannot directly inspect the product or meet the seller face-to-face. Consequently, trust
becomes an important psychological mechanism that helps reduce perceived uncertainty and
perceived risk (Makmor et al., 2024).
Previous studies support the position of trust as an organism variable in live -streaming
commerce. Wongkitrungrueng & Assarut (2020) found that live -streaming commerce can build
consumer trust and engagement through real-time interaction and value creation. Liu et al. (2022)
also demonstrated that interactivity contributes to trust formation in live-streaming commerce. In
addition, (N. Zhang, 2023a) positioned trust as one of the psychological mechanisms through which
live-streaming features influence consumers’ purchase intention. These findings indicate that trust is
not merely an outcome of live-streaming characteristics, but also an internal psychological state that
explains how external stimuli are transformed into behavioral responses.
The Response (R) represents the behavioral outcome resulting from the consumer’s internal
evaluation, which in this study is purchase intention. Purchase intention refers to the consumer’s
willingness, likelihood, or plan to purchase a product after watching and evaluating the Shopee Live
session. In the S-O-R framework, purchase intention represents the behavioral response that occurs
after consumers process the external stimuli and develop an internal evaluation of the seller, host,
product, and shopping environment. Trust is expected to have a positive influence on purchase
intention because consumers who trust the host, seller, and product information are more likely to

perceive the purchase decision as safe and reliable. H. Zhang et al. (2024) found that trust positively

through live-streaming commerce contributes to consumer engagement and purchasing-related

Based on this theoretical logic, the conceptual framework of this study assumes that host
attractiveness, interactivity, product presentation, and entertainment value function as Stimuli (S)
that influence consumers’ trust as the Organism (O), which subsequently affects purchase intention as
the Response (R). Therefore, trust is positioned as a mediating variable between the four independent
variables and purchase intention. This framework enables the study to examine not only whether the
characteristics of Shopee Live directly contribute to consumers’ purchase intention, but also how these
external stimuli influence purchase intention through the formation of consumer trust. The proposed
relationship can be summarized as follows:
Host Attractiveness, Interactivity, Product Presentation, and Entertainment Value (Stimuli) →
Trust (Organism) → Purchase Intention (Response)

This framework is supported by previous empirical studies that have examined live-streaming
commerce using the S-O-R perspective. Liu et al. (2022) found that live-streaming features influence
purchase intention through trust and flow experience. Liu & Zha ng (2024) demonstrated that
interactivity, visualization, entertainment, and professionalization influence consumers’ purchase
intention. Wongkitrungrueng & Assarut (2020) showed that live-streaming commerce characteristics
can develop consumer trust and engagement. R. Chen et al. (2019) further confirmed that the
characteristics of live-streaming hosts influence viewers’ engagement and purchase-related behavior.
Therefore, the proposed framework is theoretically and empirically relevant for examining consumer
behavior in the context of Shopee Live.

METHOD

This study employed a quantitative research design using a survey method to examine the
influence of host attractiveness, interactivity, product presentation, and entertainment value on
purchase intention through trust in the context of Shopee Live. The target population consisted of
individuals who had experience watching and purchasing products through Shopee Live. Data were

587
collected using an online questionnaire developed through Google Forms and distributed through
social media platforms, including WhatsApp, Instagram, and LINE. A purposive sampling technique
was employed, requiring respondents to have prior experience purchasing products through Shopee
Live. According to Sekaran and Bougie (2019), questionnaires are an appropriate data collection
instrument for quantitative research when standardized information is required from a defined group
of respondents. In addition, Taherdoost (2022) noted that online questionnaires provide an efficient
and accessible approach for collecting data from geographically dispersed respondents.
The questionnaire consisted of 30 measurement items adapted from previously validated
studies, representing six constructs: Host Attractiveness, Interactivity, Product Presentation,
Entertainment Value, Trust, and Purchase Intention. Each construct was mea sured using five
indicators. All items were assessed using a five-point Likert scale ranging from 1 (strongly disagree)
to 5 (strongly agree). A five-point Likert scale is commonly used in behavioral and marketing research
because it enables respondents to express the degree of their agreement with each statement while
maintaining a relatively simple response format (Taherdoost, 2022).
Data analysis was conducted using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-
SEM) with SmartPLS 4. Following the guidelines of TomassMHultt (n.d.), the measurement model
was evaluated by examining outer loadings, Cronbach’s alpha, composite reliability (CR), average
variance extracted (AVE), and discriminant validity using the Fornell -Larcker criterion and
heterotrait-monotrait ratio (HTMT). Variance inflation factor (VIF) was also assessed to identify
potential collinearity issues. Subsequently, the structural model was evaluated through path
coefficients, coefficient of determination (R²), effect size (f²), predictive relevance (Q²), and
bootstrapping to assess the significance of the proposed relationships and test the research
hypotheses. PLS-SEM is appropriate for this study because it enables the simultaneous examination
of relationships among multiple latent constructs and is particularly useful for models involving
mediation relationships (Hair et al., 2022).

CONCLUSION

This study examined the influence of Host Attractiveness, Interactivity, Product Presentation,
and Entertainment Value on Purchase Intention through Trust in the context of Shopee Live shopping.
The findings reveal that Product Presentation and Entertainment Value have a positive and significant
effect on Trust, whereas Host Attractiveness and Interactivity do not significantly influence Trust.
Furthermore, Trust has a significant positive effect on Purchase Intention, indicating that consumer
trust plays a crucial role in encouraging purchasing decisions during live shopping sessions. The
mediation analysis further demonstrates that Trust significantly mediates the relationships between
Product Presentation and Purchase Intention, as well as between Entertainment Value and Purchase

Intention. In contrast, Trust does not mediate the effects of Host Attractiveness and Interactivity on
Purchase Intention. These findings suggest that consumers place greater emphasis on clear product
demonstrations and engaging shopping experiences than on the attractiveness of the host or the level
of interaction alone when making purchase decisions on Shopee Live.  Theoretically, this study
contributes to the application of the Stimulus Organism Response (S-O-R) Theory by confirming that
Product Presentation and Entertainment Value act as effective stimuli that strengthen consumer trust
and subsequently increase purchase intention. Practically, the findings provide implications for sellers,
brands, and live stream hosts on Shopee Live to prioritize informative product presentations and
engaging live-streaming content to enhance consumer trust and encourage purchasing behavior.
Future research is recommended to examine additional variables, such as perceived value, social
presence, influencer credibility, or perceived risk, and to investigate different live commerce platforms
or broader consumer populations to improve the generalizability of the findings.
