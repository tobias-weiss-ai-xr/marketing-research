ABSTRACT
This study explores the relationships between personalization, trust, customer
satisfaction, and online purchase intention in e -commerce. As online shopping
continues to grow, understanding the factors that drive consumer behavior
becomes increasingly important for businesses seeking to improve their digital
marketing strategies. The objective of this research is to examine how
personalization and trust influence customer satisfaction and purchase intention,
with a focus on the mediating ro le of customer satisfaction. Using Structural
Equation Modeling (SEM), the study analyzes data from 173 buyers of a
furniture retailer, CV Melindo Jember. The results reveal that both
personalization and trust positively influence customer satisfaction, wh ich, in
turn, significantly impacts online purchase intention. Personalization was found
to enhance customer satisfaction, and trust played a critical role in shaping both
satisfaction and purchase intention. The study further demonstrates that
customer sa tisfaction mediates the relationship between personalization, trust,
and purchase intention. These findings have practical implications for e -
commerce businesses, highlighting the importance of personalization strategies
and trust -building measures in impr oving customer experiences and boosting
sales. The research contributes to the understanding of consumer behavior in
online shopping, providing actionable insights for businesses to increase
purchase intention. However, further research is needed to explor e these
relationships in other industries and geographical regions, as well as to consider
other potential mediators or moderators of the consumer decision -making
process.

INTRODUCTION
Digital marketing plays a crucial role in shaping consumer behavior, particularly in the context of online
shopping. As e -commerce continues to grow, the need for businesses to connect with customers in meaningful ways
becomes increasingly important (Rosário & Raimundo, 2021) . One of the key elements that influence consumer
decisions is personalizationthe practice of tailoring the online shopping experience to individual preferences, behaviors,
and needs(Riegger, Merfeld, Klein, & Henkel, 2022) . Personalized experiences, such as product recommendati ons and
customized content, create a sense of relevance and value for customers, which can significantly enhance their purchase
intention(Lau & Ki, 2021) . Additionally, trust in the online store is essential, as consumers are more likely to purchase
from websites they perceive as reliable, secure, and transparent (Tran & Nguyen, 2022). When both personalization and
trust are effectively integrated into the customer journey, they contribute to higher levels of customer
satisfaction(Ahmed & Aziz, 2025) . This, in turn, acts as a mediator, amplifying the cons umer's intention to make a
purchase, making personalization and trust vital components for businesses to focus on in their digital marketing
strategies(Beyari & Hashem, 2025).
Purchase intention refers to the likelihood or willingness of a consumer to make a purchase after being exposed
to various stimuli, such as marketing efforts, product information, or website design (Li & Peng, 2021) . It is influenced
by a combination of personal preferences, external factors,  and the perceived value of the product or service (Nguyen,
Tran, & N guyen, 2021) . In the context of online shopping, purchase intention is shaped not only by the quality and
appeal of the products but also by the trust a consumer places in the online store (Zhong, Zhou, Zhang, & Ji, 2025) . A
higher level of trust, coupled with a personalized shopping experience, enhances the consumer’s confidence in the
purchase process, increasing their intention to follow through with a purchase (Wang, Shahzad, Ahmad, Abdullah, &
Hassan, 2022).
Customer satisfaction refers to the overall contentment of a customer with a  product, service, or brand, based on
their expectations and experiences(de Azambuja, Rodriguez Peña, & Vargas, 2023). It is a critical factor in determining
repeat business, loyalty, and positive word-of-mouth(Marcos & Coelho, 2021). High levels of customer satisfaction are
achieved when a company meets or exceeds customer expectations through product quality, service, and the overall
shopping experience(Abigail, Sari, & Saputra, 2024) . In the context of online shopping, satisfaction can be influenced

Personalization, trust in online stores, and custome r satisfaction are interconnected factors that significantly

satisfaction, which increases the consumer’s intention to purchase(Hamouda, 2021). Satisfied customers are more likely

purchases(Kumar & Ayodeji, 2021) . Collectively, these elements create a comprehensive framework that  influences
consumers’ purchase decisions, highlighting the importance of an integrated approach to digital marketing
strategies(Chen, 2024).

LITERATURE REVIEW
While extensive research has explored the individual effects of personalization, online store trust, and customer
satisfaction on purchase intention, there remains a notable gap in understanding how these factors interact within a
holistic framework, parti cularly in the context of e -commerce. While previous studies have demonstrated that
personalization enhances the shopping experience (Lau & Ki, 2021)  and that trust in an online store is a significant
predictor of purchase intention (Tran & Nguyen, 2022), fewer studies have examined how the simultaneous integration
of personalization and trust influences customer satisfaction and, in turn, their purchase intention. Most research has
focused on the direct effects of t hese factors, leaving a lack of comprehensive understanding regarding how customer
satisfaction mediates this relationship(Miao et al., 2021).
Furthermore, the existing literature often examines these variables in isolation, failing to account for the
dynamic, interactive nature of consumer decision -making in the digital space. While studies have highlighted the
importance of building trust and offering personalized experiences, little research has focused on how these elements
may work synergistically to enhance customer satisfaction and, ultimately, purchase intention (Soleimani, 2022;
V ashishth et al., 2024). The integration of these factors within a single framework, considering their combined effect on
purchase behavior, remains underexplored, especially in the context of modern e -commerce where consumer
expectations are increasingly shaped by personalized, trustwor thy, and user -centric experiences. This gap presents an
opportunity for further investigation into the intricate relationships between personalization, trust, customer satisfaction,
and purchase intention in the digital marketing landscape(Krishna et al., 2023).

METHOD
This study utilizes Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) as the primary research method to examine the
relationships between personalization, trust, customer satisfaction, and purchase intention. SEM is an advanced
statistical technique that allows for the t esting of complex relationships between multiple variables, both direct and
indirect, making it particularly suitable for understanding the interdependent nature of these factors. By employing
SEM, the study can test the hypothesized model that links perso nalization and trust to customer satisfaction, which in
turn mediates the effect on purchase intention. This methodology provides a robust framework for analyzing the direct
and mediating effects of the variables involved, allowing for a more comprehensive  understanding of how these factors
interact within the context of online shopping. Using SEM ensures that the relationships are measured simultaneously,
taking into account measurement error and providing more reliable estimates of the relationships betwe en the
constructs.
The population for this study consists of 173 buyers from CV Melindo Jember, a furniture retailer. The sample
was selected based on the growing trend of online furniture shopping, where the phenomenon of purchasing furniture
online has surpassed in-store visits in recent years. The increase in e-commerce activity, particularly within the furniture
industry, makes this population particularly relevant for investigating the impact of personalization, trust, and customer
satisfaction on purc hase intention. This population offers a unique insight into how consumers behave in an
environment where the digital shopping experience is becoming the primary method of purchasing furniture, as
opposed to traditional brick -and-mortar shopping. By focusi ng on the customers of CV Melindo Jember, this study
seeks to provide a detailed analysis of how these factors play a role in shaping purchase intention, offering valuable
insights into the evolving consumer preferences in the online shopping space.

between personalization, trust, and purchase intention.

DISCUSSION
The results of this study provide valuable insights into the relationships between  personalization, trust, customer
satisfaction, and online purchase intention. The findings support the conceptual framework and hypotheses, confirming
the significant roles of personalization and trust in shaping consumer satisfaction and purchase intenti ons. In particular,
the strong positive effect of customer satisfaction on online purchase intention (β = 0.561, p < 0.000) underscores the
pivotal role that a positive consumer experience plays in influencing future purchase behavior. This aligns with previous
studies that have emphasized the importance of satisfaction as a mediator between customer perceptions and purchasing
decisions (Khan, Salamzadeh, Iqbal, & Yang, 2022; de Azambuja, Rodriguez Peña, & V argas, 2023). The evidence that
satisfaction stron gly influences purchase intention emphasizes the need for online retailers to prioritize creating an
exceptional customer experience to drive sales. The relationship between personalization and customer satisfaction (β =
0.279, p < 0.000) highlights the cr itical role of tailoring the online shopping experience to individual preferences. This
finding is consistent with prior research that suggests personalized experiences, such as product recommendations and
tailored content, foster a stronger sense of relev ance and value for the consumer, which subsequently enhances
satisfaction and increases the likelihood of a purchase (Lau & Ki, 2021; Riegger, Merfeld, Klein, & Henkel, 2022). In

The direct effect of personalization on online purchase intention (β = 0.158, p < 0.05) was also significant ,
although smaller compared to its effect on customer satisfaction. This result suggests that while personalized
experiences do directly affect purchase decisions, their influence is largely mediated through satisfaction. This finding
aligns with previous research by Miao et al. (2021), who noted that personalization enhances customer engagement,
which in turn contributes to higher purchase intention. However, the fact that personalization has a stronger indirect
effect on purchase intention through satisfaction rather than a direct one suggests that retailers should focus on building
personalized experiences that enhance the overall customer experience rather than simply pushing personalized
products.
Trust emerged as another significant factor in shaping c ustomer satisfaction, with a strong effect size (β = 0.597,
p < 0.000). This result corroborates the findings of Tran & Nguyen (2022), who found that trust is a fundamental
predictor of consumer behavior, especially in online contexts where buyers face hei ghtened risks regarding product
authenticity, privacy, and security. In e -commerce, where consumers cannot physically interact with products, trust
plays a crucial role in reducing perceived risk and facilitating purchase decisions. The significant role of trust highlights
the need for online retailers to build and maintain a trustworthy image through transparent policies, secure payment
systems, and effective customer support mechanisms. In addition to its direct effect on satisfaction, trust also influenc es
online purchase intention (β = 0.167, p < 0.05), albeit to a lesser extent than its impact on satisfaction. This result is in
line with the work of Zhong, Zhou, Zhang, & Ji (2025), which highlighted that trust directly enhances purchase
intention by ins tilling confidence in the purchasing process. However, the influence of trust on purchase intention is
primarily mediated by customer satisfaction, as seen in the indirect path through satisfaction (β = 0.335, p < 0.000).
This suggests that trust influence s purchase behavior not only by directly motivating customers to make purchases but
also by enhancing their overall satisfaction with the online store, which subsequently drives purchase intentions.
The mediation effects of customer satisfaction are highly  significant in this study. Both personalization and trust
show strong mediation effects through customer satisfaction, with the indirect effects of personalization (β = 0.156, p <
0.000) and trust (β = 0.335, p < 0.000) on online purchase intention. These results underscore the importance of creating
a positive customer experience through personalized, trustworthy interactions that lead to increased satisfaction, which
in turn increases the likelihood of purchase. This mediation effect reinforces the notio n that in the online retail
environment, it is not just the direct factors like product appeal or price that matter, but how well an online store can
engage customers and foster a satisfying, trustworthy relationship that influences their future purchasing decisions.
The results of this study highlight the significant role that personalization and trust play in enhancing customer
satisfaction and driving online purchase intention. The findings support the notion that online retailers should prioritize
personalized experiences and build trust with their customers as essential strategies for improving satisfaction and
fostering long-term customer loyalty. The significant mediation effect of customer satisfaction further emphasizes that
these strategies should not be seen as isolated marketing tactics but as integral components of a broader customer
relationship management strategy aimed at boosting purchase intentions. Future research could explore other
moderating variables, such as cultural differences or pro duct types, to further refine the understanding of these
relationships in different e-commerce contexts.

CONCLUSION
This study examined the relationships between personalization, trust, customer satisfaction, and online purchase
intention, finding that pe rsonalization and trust significantly influence both customer satisfaction and purchase
intention. The results highlight the critical role of customer satisfaction as a mediator, emphasizing that personalized
experiences and trust -building measures are ess ential strategies for enhancing consumer engagement and increasing
purchase likelihood. By focusing on these elements, businesses can create more meaningful interactions with
customers, ultimately fostering higher satisfaction and driving sales. These find ings provide valuable insights into how
e-commerce platforms can refine their marketing strategies to align with customer expectations, thereby improving
customer retention and conversion rates.
However, there are several areas for improvement in future st udies. The sample size and the focus on a single
furniture retailer limit the generalizability of the findings to broader industries or regions. Expanding the sample to
include a more diverse range of products and geographical locations would offer a more comprehensive understanding
of these relationships in different e -commerce contexts. Furthermore, additional research could explore other potential
mediators or moderators, such as customer engagement, to deepen the understanding of consumer behavior. Desp ite
these limitations, the study’s results contribute to the ongoing discourse on digital marketing strategies, providing
actionable insights for businesses seeking to enhance customer satisfaction and boost online purchase intention.
