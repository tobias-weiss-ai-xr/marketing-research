Abstract: The 4 th industrial age trigger s a new business environment. E -commerce has significantly transformed
socioeconomic landscapes by reshaping traditional business models and consumer practices. It has expanded market access,
allowing businesses to overcome regional constraints, hence improving sales opportunities and fostering global trade. E -
commerce also has influenced employment trends by creating new job opportunities in information technology, logistics, and
digital marketing, while concurrently promoting the ex pansion of the gig economy. As the digital economy progresses,
authorities and enterprises must confront these socioeconomic effects to maximize the benefits of online commerce while
alleviating any disadvantages. Nowadays, consumers change their purchase habit s from traditional to online due to
technological advancement. So, businesses are rapidly moving to this new phenomenon of marketplace. In Bangladesh,
among other products, the electronics sector constitutes the largest sector of the online market. Therefore, this study aimed
at uncovering the key elements affecting the purchase intention of electrical devices online in Bangladesh. To achieve the
research target, this study follows the self -generated integrated model. The questionnaire consists of 24 items and was
completed by 308 respondents. Among them, 35.4% were women and 64.6% were men. To test the proposed conceptual
model, this study uses the structural equation approach. Here, SmartPLS and IBM SPSS 25 w ere utilized to analyze the
proposed relationship in the model. After the analysis, this study discovered that e-trust and e-service quality have a strong
impact on e-word of mouth and e-satisfaction, while hedonic motivation found no impact. Further, e -word of mouth and e-
satisfaction are significantly increasing the purchase intention of electronics products in Bangladesh. Such findings indicate
that ensuring proper quality, achieving trust , managing customer satisfaction , and electronic word of mouth enhance
purchases in an online setting. This study provides significant theoretical contributions for educators and researchers, while
also delivering essential insights for businessmen, policymakers, and practitioners to improve online business operations in
Bangladesh, especially in the electr onic goods sector, by tackling current challenges, clarifying key ambiguities, and
ultimately fostering the industry's overall development and sustainability.

INTRODUCTION
E-commerce has significantly impacted global socioeconomic dynamics. Social institutions are crucial for
fostering the growth of Internet companies, particularly in developing economies like Indonesia (Kusuma &
Rahman, 2018). The rise of e -commerce giants like Amazon has transformed traditional retail, presenting
difficulties for brick -and-mortar retailers and creating new employment opportunities (Yang, 2023). Social
connections are crucial to the digital economy, influencing Internet adoption, usage patterns, and business models
through network externalities and virtual word -of-mouth (Pénard & Suire, 2008). E -commerce has accelerated
economic development and expanded business opportunities while presenting challenges related to intellectual
property rights, privacy protection, and information accessibility (Sharma, 2005). As the digital economy evolves,
authorities and businesses must address these socioeconomic impacts to optimize the advantages of online trade
while mitigating any drawbacks. The Industry 4.0 age has adopted an enhanced business model through the global
utilization of innovative digital technologies, transforming customer behavior (Reinartz et al., 2019).  It further
increased during and after COVID -19 in Bangladesh (Hossain et al., 2024). Nowadays, consumers increasingly
depend on digital innovation to buy on e-commerce platforms rather than visit conventional retail locations (Vasić
et al., 2019). Consequently, firms have adjusted to this alteration in consumer buying behaviors. Engaging in and
sustaining a presence in Internet commerce is highly competitive. Service quality is essential for survival in a
highly competitive online world (Gounaris et al., 2010a). Brady and Robertson (2001) indicated that enhancing
service quality will elevate client satisfaction, fostering more positive behavioral intentions. A corporation must
provide an exceptional client experience through elevated service quality to guarantee purchase intention
(Gounaris et al., 2010a).
In the digital age, trust influences consumers' online purchasing intentions. E -trust, denoting customers'
confidence in online merchants and transactions, profoundly affects their propensity to participate in e-commerce
(Gefen et al., 2003). The intangible characteristics of online buying necessitate that elements like website security,
privacy safeguards, and favorable online evaluations are essential for fostering consumer confidence (Kim et al.,
2008). Prospective purchasers may be reluctant to finalize  agreements without robust trust due to perceived
dangers, including fraud or data breaches (Pavlou, 2003). In addition to trust, internet purchasing needs utilitarian
objectives and the satisfaction of sensory desires such as enjoyment, exhilaration, and escapism. Research
demonstrates that hedonic motives, along with information accessibility and trust, significantly influence
customers' intentions to persist in online purchases (Santo and Marques, 2022). Moreover, research has shown
that e-Word Of Mouth (eWOM) considerably influences consumer decisions by altering product credibility and
utility perceptions. Research employing the Information Acceptance Model (IACM) demonstrated that the quality
and credibility of eWOM positively influence customers' views of information utility, therefore augmenting their
purchase intentions (Cuong, 2024). Furthermore, the abundance of eWOM information, including comprehensive
text, photographs, and videos, has enhanced eWOM legitimacy and acceptance, thus f avorably affecting online
purchase intentions (Roy et al., 2024).
Besides the world market, Bangladesh has a strong online business market. In Bangladesh, the e-commerce
market is projected to experience a contin ual increase in consumers, totaling 5.2 million (+48.28 per cent) from
2024 to 2029. Following 10 straight years of growth, it is projected to attain 15.92 million users, reaching a new
height in 2029. The e -commerce market has grown consistently over the last few years (Statista Resear ch
Department, 2025).
Bangladesh is the 37th largest e-commerce market, with US$8,020.1 million in revenue by 2025, surpassing
Portugal. Revenue will reach a growth rate (CAGR 2025 -2029) of 17.7%, culminating in a market volume of
approximately US$15,407.3 million by 2029. The Bangladeshi e -commerce market will experience a 30.1%
increase in 2025, contributing to the year's global growth rate of 9.8%. Like Bangladesh, global e-commerce sales
are projected to rise in the following years. ECDB identifies seven markets within the B angladeshi e-commerce
sector. The electronics sector constitutes the largest market, representing 23.9% of Bangladesh's e -commerce
revenue. Subsequent categories include Hobby & Leisure at 20.4%, Fashion at 18.9%, Furniture & Homecare at
12.1%, Grocery at 9.0%, Care Products at 8.3%, and DIY at the last 7.5% (ECDB, 2025). The market for
electronics products is at its peak and popular among the present generation.

Thus, Hossain et al. (2018) identified critical factors, including security, personal interests, payment methods,

LITERATURE REVIEW
This study proposes a theoretical framework to comprehend the major elements that impact online
consumer behavior, such  as e-service quality, e -trust, e -WOM, hedonic motivation, and e -satisfaction. These
constructions have their roots in well-known theories such as expectancy disconfirmation theory, social influence
theory, the SERVQUAL model, trust theory, and self-determination theory.
E-service quality denotes the perceived caliber of service provided via online platforms, encompassing
elements such as website design, navigational ease, and responsiveness (Zeithaml et al., 2002). It is based on the
SERVQUAL model (Parasuraman et al., 1988a) and is essential for customer satisfaction, trust, and intention to
purchase (Kim et al., 2009). E-trust, characterized as a consumer's confidence in the dependability and honesty of
an online store, is essential in digital transactions (Gefen, 2000) and is shaped by trust theory (Mayer et al., 1995).
Increased e -trust enhances consumer confidence and purchasing intentions (Kim et al., 2009). E -WOM, or
consumer-generated online feedback, impacts purchasing decisions, with social influence theory (Cialdini, 2009)
emphasizing its significance in behavior modification (East et al., 2008). Hedonic motivation, propelled by
pleasure and happiness in buying, enhances engagement and satisfaction, elevating purchase intentions (Huang,
2023). E-satisfaction, influenced by website functionality and service delivery (Oliver, 1999), is contingent upon
the fulfillment or surpassing of expectations (Oliver, 1980) and is a crucial determinant of consumer purchase
intentions (Anderson & Srinivasan, 2003).
By synthesizing these elements, the framework elucidates the principal determinants of online consumer
behavior, yielding significant consequences for e -commerce enterprises seeking to augment trust, contentment,
and buy intentions.
E-Service Quality
According to Santos (2003), e-service quality is the total consumer assessment and evaluation of e-service
offered in the marketplace, regardless of geographic borders. Prasasti et al. (2022) state that e -service quality
encompasses the services rendered by consumers to internal and external service providers, including all types of
infrastructure, facilities, equipment, and the products and services delivered. The dimensions of customer
expectations evaluate the adequacy of service delivery in meeting customer wants (Parasuraman et al., 1988b). It
is a critical factor in marketing efforts to attract prospective clients (Teresa & Pravitasari, 2023). ESQ is a variant
of service quality designed for broader accessibility through internet platforms that link s vendors and customers
to facilitate buying activities effectively and efficiently (Chase et al., 2006). Research consistently indicates that
e-service quality favorably affects e-satisfaction across diverse online environments. Research in several domains,
such as mobile applications (Haria & Mulyandi, 2019), e-commerce (Gounaris et al., 2010b), and online banking
(Sasono et al., 2021), substantiates this correlation. A recent study by Bung (2024) demonstrates that all selected

E-Trust
Trust is a crucial element in the relationship-building process and is recognized as a fundamental precursor
to commitment to a firm (Morgan & Hunt, 1994). It establishes a notable precedent for commercial engagement,
especially in online environments; the enhanced accessibility of online participation enables retailers to operate
opportunistically (Reichheld & Schefter, 2000). Verhoef et al. (2002) assert that trust is fundamental in
establishing a relationship between buyers and vendors. Trust refers to the customer's confidence in the excellence
and reliability of the service provided by the seller (Garbarino  & Johnson, 1999). However, Sirdesh mukh et al.
(2002) discovered that Internet consumers favor conducting transactions with shops they perceive as more
trustworthy. As a result, e-trust is recognized as a vital element in online transactions, exerting a favorable impact
on e-WOM (Hsu & Wang, 2008). It has a good and substantial effect on ratings, which are associated with -WOM
(Kojongian & Ariadi, 2024).
Furthermore, e-trust positively and substantially influences e-WOM (Yaqub et al., 2024). Other research
has shown the beneficial effect of e-trust on e-satisfaction across diverse e-commerce settings. A study on Shopee
consumers in Bandung revealed that e -trust considerably impacts e -satisfaction (Roisah et al., 2022). Si milarly,
Rintasari and Farida (2020) indicated that e-trust strongly influences e-satisfaction among Shopee users in Sleman
Regency. This study proposes the following hypotheses:
H3: There is a positive relationship between e-trust and e-word of mouth.
H4: There is a positive relationship between e-trust and e-satisfaction.
Hedonic Motivation
According to Van Der Heijan (2004), a hedonic motivation system is closely connected with an information
system that facilitates adopting digital technology — the hedonic motivation system correlated with product
virtualization technology in online buying (Kim, 2007). The enjoyment or satisfaction obtained from "hedonic
motivation" is associated with "the use of technology" (Venkate sh 2012). These results are based on users being
driven by a pleasurable experience and entertainment when utilizing a technolo gy-based service. Moreover,
hedonic incentives, such as pleasure, have been identified as crucial in technology adoption, as they foster a
positive disposition among users (Rose, 2012). Furthermore, Park (2020) and Mikalef et al. (2013) ascertain
that hedonic motivation directly influences e -satisfaction and word of mouth. They stated that hedonic incentive
positively influences word -of-mouth communication. Atulkar and Singh (2021) have discovered that hedonic
motivation positively influe nces e-WOM. In Indo nesia, hedonic motivation significantly influences customer
satisfaction with e -wallet usage (Pambuko et al., 2021). Further, it is the primary factor influencing consumer
happiness in online shopping. Consequently, hedonic motivation favorably influences e -satisfaction by
improving the total buying experience for consumers (Redda, 2024). It pertains to emotional aspirations,
augmenting the buying experi ence and resulting in increased e -satisfaction when consumers sense joy and
comfort while online grocery shopping (Kolondam et al., 2023). Based on this, this study postulates the
subsequent hypotheses:

E-WOM
E-WOM denotes a favorable or unfavorable assertion that consumers disseminate about a product or service
across many websites (El -Baz et al., 2022). A study by Nam et al.  (2020) established that 91% of consumers
depend on online user-generated content, such as blogs and product reviews, before purchasing. It signifies that
electronic word -of-mouth influences consumers' purchasing decisions. A recent study indicates that e-WOM
positively affects the buying intention of Egyptian internet users within the fashion sector (Sayed & Fahmy, 2024).
In addition, Roy et al. (2024) discovered that eWOM significantly affects purchase intention (PI) for both tangible
and experiential products, with credible and persuasive eWOM augmenting perceived usefulness, particularly
influencing services more than commodities in the Indian market. Moreover, Chunyan a nd Cheng (2024)
demonstrate that e-WOM positively affects buying intention within China's manufacturing industry, underscoring
its crucial role in augmenting consumer happ iness and influencing purchasing decisions. This study posits the
following hypothesis based on the rationale above:
H7: There is a positive relationship between e-WOM and online purchase intention.
E-Satisfaction
According to Wang and Chang (2014), satisfaction is the emotion that a person experiences as a result of
evaluating the performance of a product or service in comparison to their expectations. E-satisfaction refers to the
level of contentment a client experiences when interacting with an online platform, website, or electronic service
(Muhammad et al., 2024).
The implications of consumer experiences during various purchasing stages include the need for
recognition, information collecting, appraisal of alternatives, purchase intention, and post -purchase behavior
(Chung and Al-Khaled, 2021). Furthermore, assessing customer satisfaction is crucial for online retailers since it
can significantly impact consumer purchasing decisions (Sun et al., 2016). The study of Muhammad et al. (2024)
demonstrates that satisfaction has a substantial and favorable effect on purchas e intention within the realm of e -
commerce flash sales. Consequently, recent research has investigated the influence of e -satisfaction on online
purchasing intentions. Several researches have demonstrated that e -satisfaction positively influences online
purchasing intention (Chiang, 2018; Chung & Al-Khaled, 2021). Nonetheless, a study indicated that e-satisfaction
exerted a favorable albeit minor influence on purchase intention for online retailers (Prahiawan et al., 2021). This
study posits the following hypothesis based on the rationale above:
H8: There is a positive relationship between e-satisfaction and online purchase intention.
METHODOLOGY
Research Design and Sampling Method
This study was quantitative. A descriptive research approach was selected for this study. The study
employed a descriptive methodology to gather primary data using surveys, investigating the factors influencing
online purchasing intentions in Bangladesh and focusing on descriptive data gathering. The study employ s a
convenience sampling method.
Sources of Data
The research effort used both primary and secondary data. The core data was collected using a meticulously
organized questionnaire. Previous research was utilized as a secondary data source.

Respondents submitted general information, including age categories, gender identities, educational
attainment, and occupations. The initial component of the questionnaire requests fundamental information about
the respondents. The second portion includes inquiries regarding the factors influencing destination selection
within the context of Bangladesh. A five-point Likert scale, ranging from strongly disagree to agree strongly, was
employed to assess respondents' agreement or disagreement with the survey's conclusions. Before completing the
questionnaire, a pretesting step was conducted with 30 internet customers of electronic products.
Population and Sample
The target population was students from private and public universities, professionals from public or private
organizations, business people, job seekers, and housewives who are used to shopping for electronic products
online. The selected respondents have more possibility to engage in online purchases. This study follows the
convenience sampling technique to determine the sample. To determine the sample size, th is study follows
Roscoe's suggestions that the sample size should be at least ten times the measurement items.
Data collection
The research gathered data through personal interviews using a structured questionnaire utilizing five-point
Likert scales, targeting 308 online buyers from various regions in Bangladesh from December 2024 to January 2025.
Data analysis
A two-step modeling procedure was utilized to analyze the data and investigate the hypothesis that was put
forth. The SEM method has two stages: the measurement and structural models. This survey research employed
Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) to assess the measurement and structural models of the survey data. SEM
is significantly more advantageous than other analytical methods. This study employs several methodologies,
including linear regression, which is utilized solely for evaluating the relational  pathways across components.
This study employed the Partial Least Squares (PLS) analytical method using SmartPLS 3.0 software (Ringle et
al., 2015). This version is solely utilized to evaluate the presented hypothesis. SmartPLS has predominantly

CONCLUSIONS
The objective of this research was to identify the factors influencing the online purchase intention of
electrical products in Bangladesh. To achieve the research purpose, this study created a self-generated integrated
model. This study employs the structural equation technique to evaluate the model. After the analysis, this study
ascertains that e-service quality positively influences e-WOM. The results demonstrate that e -service quality

improves e-word of mouth (e-WOM). Superior online services, characterized by reliability, responsiveness, and
user-friendly interfaces, motivate users to disseminate favorable experiences. Content consumers proactively
endorse services via digital platforms, enhancing business reputation and customer interaction. Consequently,
enhancing e-service quality cultivates more robust e-WOM impacts.
This study examines the determinants affecting the online purchasing intentions of electronic devices in
Bangladesh. The results indicate that e -service quality substantially influences e -WOM and e -satisfaction,
underscoring the significance of reliability , responsiveness, and user -friendliness in improving customer
experiences. E-trust significantly impacts e-WOM and e-satisfaction, as customers are more inclined to endorse
and persist in utilizing online platforms they regard as secure and dependable. Fur thermore, hedonic motivation
enhances e-WOM and electronic satisfaction, underscoring the significance of engaging and pleasurable
interactions between purchasers and vendors. Ultimately, e-WOM and happiness are essential factors influencing
online buying intents, as consumers evaluate product security, cost, and delivery speed, thereby enhancing online
shopping.
Furthermore, e-service quality enhances e -satisfaction. The findings validate that e -service quality has a
favorable impact on e -satisfaction. Efficient, trustworthy, and user -friendly internet services enhance client
happiness. Critical elements, including timeliness, security, and usability, improve user experiences, resulting in
elevated satisfaction levels. Therefore, enhancing e -service quality is essential for maintaining client happiness.
The findings affirm the positive link between e-trust and e-WOM, indicating that when customers trust an online
platform, they are more inclined to share favorable experiences and endorse services. Trust cultivates confidence
in transactions, security, and service dependability, prompting users to disseminate positiv e feedback, thus
enhancing company reputation and engagement. E -trust also induces e-satisfaction, aligning with prior research
findings. Customers who trust an online platform's security, reliability, and openness exhibit greater confidence
in their interactions. This trust mitigates uncertainty and improves their overall experience, resulting in elevated
satisfaction ratings. Enhancing electronic trust is essential for cultivating enduring consumer relationships and
loyalty.
Subsequently, this study discovered that hedonic motivation positively influences e-WOM and enjoyment.
It indicates that users are incentivized when establishing a robust connection between the customer and the vendor.
When individuals are content with something, they discuss it with others. E-trust positively impacts e-WOM and
e-satisfaction. Individuals typically experience satisfaction due to the perceived security of monetary transactions
with Internet sellers. The favorable or unfavorable characterization of a product or service is called e-WOM.
In contrast, e-satisfaction denotes the customer's evaluation of a website and its impact on several quality
dimensions. Upon examining these constructs, this study ascertains that e-WOM and contentment positively
impact online buy inclinations. Individuals can deliberate on transaction security, product pricing, product variety,
and expedited delivery. Consequently, people become content and endorse the purchase of products or services
online to others.
This study offers significant theoretical and practical consequences in online commerce. The integration of
Expectancy Disconfirmation Theory, Social Influence Theory, the SERVQUAL model, Trust Theory, and Self -
Determination Theory provides a thorough framework for elucidating the determinants of consumer purchase
intentions. This multifaceted approach integrates cognitive, social, psychological, and service quality
perspectives, offe ring an enhanced understanding of customer decision -making. This study fra mework
synthesizes various ideas to comprehensively understand purchasing intentions, focusing on expectation
management, service quality, trust, and psychological motivation. This method offers significant information for
enterprises to improve customer e xperiences and refine marketing tactics.  Furthermore, analyzing after -sales
online marketing and other influencing elements could enhance the comprehension of online purchasing behavior.
Incorporating gender and experience -based distinctions into the resea rch would yield significant insights into
technology acceptability and e-commerce trends among various customer demographics.
This study has some tremendous theoretical contributions to the literature but some limitations. During the
pandemic situation, direct connection with the respondent was not possible. The conceptual model is open for
further modification. This study illumi nates the elements influencing online electronics purchase intentions in
Bangladesh; therefore, the proposed research approach and results should be tested in other cultures. Researchers
could analyze the same technology in different countries to understan d how national culture affects technology
