Abstract.
Digital transformation has changed marketing patterns and consumer behavior, including among Micro,
Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in Bogor City. This study aims to analyze the marketing
transformation of MSMEs in facing changes in consumer behavior in the digital era, particularly in the
use of social media, marketplaces, WhatsApp Business, and d igital content. The approach used was
descriptive qualitative with informants from MSMEs in Bogor City. Data were obtained through in-depth
interviews, observation, and documentation, then analyzed thematically with the help of NVivo. The
results of the st udy indicate that marketing transformation occurs through a shift from conventional
marketing channels to the use of various digital platforms that enable MSMEs to expand market reach
and increase interaction with consumers. Changes in consumer behavior ar e evident in the increasing
habit of searching for product information online, comparing options, paying attention to reviews,
expecting a quick response, and being attracted to visual content. These conditions encourage MSMEs to
adjust their communication, promotion, service, and product information delivery strategies. However,
the transformation still faces obstacles such as limited digital literacy, content production capabilities,
human resources, and the use of consumer data. Therefore, strengthening digital capacity and adaptive
marketing strategies are needed so that MSMEs can respond to consumer dynamics sustainably.
Keywords: Marketing Transformation, MSMEs, Consumer Behavior, Digital Marketing and  Bogor City.

I. INTRODUCTION
Management is the process of planning, organizing, directing, and controlling various resources to
achieve goals effectively and efficiently. In the business world, management is not only related to human,
financial, and operational resource management, but also encompasses  the organization's ability to
understand changes in the business environment and consumer needs. The development of digital technology
has changed business competition patterns and the way businesses conduct marketing activities. Consumers
have increasing ly easily obtained information, compared products, viewed reviews, communicated with
sellers, and conducted transactions through various digital platforms. This situation requires MSMEs to have
adaptive managerial skills to maintain the sustainability and increase the competitiveness of their businesses.
One aspect of management that has undergone significant change is the transformation of MSME
marketing. Marketing, previously largely carried out conventionally, has shifted to the use of social media,
marketplaces, messaging apps, and various digital platforms. This transformation provides MSMEs with the
opportunity to expand their market reach at a relatively more flexible cost. Johanis Paransa (2024) explains
that digital marketing can increase MSME visib ility while supporting business growth. The use of digital
technology also enables businesses to build more intensive communication with consumers and convey
product information more quickly.
Marketing transformation for MSMEs is becoming increasingly impo rtant due to the rapid changes in
consumer behavior. Consumers no longer solely consider product quality and price, but also consider ease of
access to information, speed of service, transaction convenience, the availability of customer reviews, and
the ex perience of interacting through digital media. Natsir and Bangun (2024) explain that changes in
consumer behavior are a crucial factor driving changes in marketing strategies, as consumers increasingly
use the internet and social media to seek information and make purchasing decisions. This situation requires
MSMEs to understand the characteristics of digital consumers and adapt their marketing strategies to these
habits.
This situation is highly relevant to the development of MSMEs in Bogor City. Bogor Cit y has diverse
MSME activities, including culinary, trade, crafts, fashion, and various service businesses. However, digital
transformation has not been fully utilized optimally by all business actors. Sukartaatmadja, Hermawan, and
Silaen (2023) showed that  some MSMEs in Bogor City still conduct conventional marketing, so the use of

marketing and have not yet developed a planned digital strategy. This phenomenon dem onstrates that

MSMEs understand consumers and manage their relationships with the market.
The emerging issues include suboptimal marketing transformation, l imited digital literacy among
MSMEs, inconsistent promotional activities through digital media, limited ability to produce marketing
content, suboptimal utilization of marketplaces, rapid changes in consumer preferences, and increasingly
fierce competition in the digital market. Therefore, it is necessary to understand the strategies that MSMEs in
Bogor City can use to adapt marketing activities to changes in consumer behavior. Based on these
conditions, research on "Marketing Transformation of MSMEs in Bogor City: Strategies for Facing Changes
in Consumer Behavior in the Digital Era" is important to understand the form of marketing transformation,
the challenges faced, and the strategies that MSMEs can implement in facing consumer dynamics in the
digital era.
Previous research has shown that digital marketing transformation is crucial for improving the ability
of MSMEs to navigate market changes. Ruhiyat, Sulaeman, and Purnawati (2023) found that price,
promotion, product quality, and payment security are related to consumer purchasing interest among MSMEs
in Bogor City. Kusumawati and Saputri (2023) demonstrated that digital skills and e -readiness support
MSME digital transformation. Meanwhile, Sahrul and Nuringsih (2023) demonstrated the role of e -
commerce, social media, and digital transformation in business performance. Hutabarat, Soedargo, and
Mashadi (2023) emphasized the importance of digital marketing capabilities for MSMEs in Bogor City.
Prakoso et al. (2024) also demonstrated that implementing digita l marketing can expand the reach and
support the competitiveness of MSMEs in Bogor.
Research on digital marketing has extensively discussed the use of social media, e -commerce, digital
skills, purchasing intentions, and MSME performance. However, these stu dies tend to discuss the influence
or utilization of technology separately and have not specifically integrated marketing transformation with
changes in consumer behavior in the context of MSMEs in Bogor City. Previous research also has not
thoroughly outlined marketing adaptation strategies based on changes in digital consumer preferences and
interaction patterns. This situation indicates that there is room for further research.
The novelty of this research lies in its discussion of the marketing transform ation of MSMEs in Bogor

II. LITERARURE REVIEW
Digital Marketing Transformation
Digital marketing transformation is the process of changing marketing strategies through the use of
digital technology to expand markets, increase interactions, and adapt business activities to consumer trends.
For MSMEs, transformation involves more than just using social media or marketplaces, but also changing
the way they design communications, promotions, services, and customer relationships. Evangelista et al.
(2023) emphasize that digitalization requires MSMEs to be more adaptive to change. Masliardi et al. (2023)
add that online presence, social media, content marketing, and mobile strategies can strengthen MSME
marketing effectiveness.
Digital Consumer Behavior
Digital consu mer behavior describes changes in how consumers seek information, evaluate
alternatives, interact with sellers, and make purchasing decisions through digital technology. These changes
require MSMEs to understand consumer needs more responsively because consumers can obtain a variety of
product options quickly. Siregar, Nasution, and Lubis (2026) show that digital transformation has changed
consumer behavior and encouraged MSMEs to develop more adaptive business strategies. Fatimah,
Hurriyati, and Maulany (2026) explain that changes in purchasing behavior have also increased the need for
an integrated consumer experience across online and offline channels.
MSME Marketing Strategy
An MSME marketing strategy is a series of decisions and actions used to align pr oducts,
communications, promotions, distribution, and services with market needs. In the digital era, this strategy
needs to be directed at utilizing various digital channels in an integrated and consumer -oriented manner.
Saputra, Rif'ah, and Andrianto (20 23) demonstrated that implementing digital marketing can support
increased sales and competitiveness for MSMEs. Johanis Paransa (2024) also explained that social media can
help increase visibility, build trust, and strengthen customer interactions, making digital marketing
adaptation a crucial part of MSME growth.

III. METHOD
The research method serves as a basis for obtaining, processing, and interpreting information
regarding the marketing transformation of MSMEs in Bogor City. The approach used is quali tative because
this study focuses on an in-depth understanding of MSME actors' experiences, marketing strategies, changes
in consumer behavior, and various obstacles in utilizing digital technology. A qualitative approach is also
appropriate for describing phenomena based on the informant's perspective in a contextual manner. Nobelta
and Rahmalia (2024) used a qualitative approach to understand MSME digital marketing management, while
Indrayanti et al. (2024) used a qualitative case study approach to examine MSME digital marketing strategies
in the Bogor region.
Research Approaches and Types
This study uses a qualitative, descriptive approach. This approach was chosen to obtain an overview of
how MSMEs in Bogor City are transforming their marketing efforts t o address changing consumer behavior
in the digital era. The data reviewed included information, experiences, opinions, and marketing practices
carried out by MSMEs. Rakhman (2024) demonstrated that a descriptive qualitative approach can be used to
understand digital marketing strategies based on observation, documentation, and interviews with MSMEs.
A similar approach was also used by El Ghifari and Supendi (2024) through semi -structured interviews,
observation, and documentation to understand the implementation of MSME digital marketing strategies.

Location and Time of Research
The research was conducted on MSMEs in Bogor City, considering the diversity of business sectors
and digital marketing activities used. Bogor City was selected based on its relevance to the research focus on
marketing transformation and changes in consumer behavior. The research was conducted in February 2026.
During this period, information collection, interviews with informants, observations of digital marketing
activities, docum entation, data organization, and analysis using NVivo were conducted. This specific
timeframe was intended to ensure the data obtained reflects the marketing conditions of MSMEs during the
research period.
Research Informants
Informants were selected purposively, based on the consideration that informants have experience and
knowledge relevant to the marketing transformation of MSMEs. The main informants were MSME owners
or managers in Bogor City who have used at least one digital media or platform in their marketing activities.
Information gathered covered the use of social media, marketplaces, promotional strategies, communication
with consumers, changes in purchasing patterns, obstacles to digitalization, and strategies for dealing with
changing consumer behavior. Purposive selection of informants allowed for information to be obtained from
parties with direct understanding of MSME digital marketing activities.
Data Collection Techniques
Data collection was conducted through in -depth interviews, obser vation, and documentation.
Interviews were used to obtain information on the experiences and strategies of MSMEs in digital marketing.
Observations were conducted by observing marketing activities on social media, marketplaces, and forms of
digital communication with consumers. Documentation was used as supporting data, such as promotional
materials, social media posts, product catalogs, and other forms of marketing communication. Budiarti et al.
(2024) emphasized that digital marketing provides opportuniti es for MSMEs to increase market reach and
sales, therefore, these various digital activities are important to consider in understanding marketing
transformation.
Data Analysis Using NVivo
Data from interviews, observations, and documentation were analyzed using NVivo to assist with
organizing, coding, grouping, and mapping themes. The analysis phase began by entering interview
transcripts and relevant documents into NVivo. Next, coding was carried out based on emerging themes,
such as marketing transformati on, social media, marketplaces, consumer behavior, promotional strategies,
digital literacy, customer interactions, and barriers to digitalization. These codes were then grouped into
broader themes so that relationships between concepts could be systematic ally identified. Thematic analysis
can be conducted through data familiarization, initial coding, theme discovery, theme review, theme naming,
and compiling analysis results (Ahmed et al., 2025).
Data Validity
Data validity was ensured through source and t echnique triangulation. Source triangulation was
conducted by comparing information from several MSMEs, while technique triangulation was conducted by
comparing interview results, observations, and documentation. The coding results using NVivo were then
reviewed to ensure that the themes formed align with the information provided by the informants. This
approach ensures that the analysis does not rely solely on a single source of information but instead considers
a variety of complementary pieces of evidenc e. This strategy is also used in qualitative research on the
implementation of digital marketing for MSMEs to increase the credibility of the analysis results (El Ghifari
& Supendi, 2024).

Transformation of MSME Marketing Channels
The data grouping results show that the marketing transformation of MSMEs in Bogor City is
primarily evident in the changes in marketing channels used. MSMEs no longer rely solely on physical
stores, word -of-mouth marketing, or loyal customers, but have begun utilizing Instagram, WhatsApp,
TikTok, marketplaces, and other digital platforms. Social media is used to introduce products, display
catalogs, provide pricing information, and communicate with consumers. Marketplaces are primarily used to
facilitate transactions and expand consumer reach beyond the business environment.
These findings demonstrate that digital marketing goes beyond simply replac ing conventional
promotional media with digital ones. Changes have also occurred in communication patterns between
businesses and consumers. Consumers can submit questions, comments, reviews, and feedback directly.
MSMEs are required to respond more quickl y and provide more comprehensive product information. This
finding aligns with Sulistiyowati and Rahmawati (2024), who found that marketplaces, social media, and
digital promotional activities play a role in supporting MSME sales growth.

Strategy Changes Following Consumer Behavior
The analysis shows that changes in consumer behavior are driving MSMEs to adapt their marketing

is related to Kusuma Negara (2024), who showed that customization, interaction, and trendiness in social

Barriers to Digital Marketing Transformation
The coding results revealed several barriers to marketing transformation. These barriers include
limited digital literacy, content creation ski lls, time constraints, limited human resources, and difficulty
understanding the evolving features of digital platforms. Some businesses are able to use social media for
simple promotions, but not all are able to leverage analytics, digital advertising, consumer segmentation, and
customer behavior data.
This situation demonstrates that digital device ownership does not automatically indicate readiness for
marketing transformation. MSMEs need the ability to plan content, identify target consumers, analyze
market responses, and evaluate promotional effectiveness. This finding aligns with Iskamto, Kharisma, and
Lesmana (2024), who explained that digital marketing can be a marketing solution for MSMEs, but business
actors' ability to utilize technology varies.

Thus, the analysis shows that the marketing transformation of MSMEs in Bogor City is taking place
through four main patterns: digitalization of marketing c hannels, content customization, increased consumer
interaction, and strengthening adaptation strategies. Digitalization offers opportunities to reach a wider
consumer base, but its success is heavily influenced by businesses' ability to understand consumer  behavior.

increase business competitiveness.
Discussion
The discussion focuses on interpreting the findings regarding the marketing transformation of MSMEs
in Bogor City by linking them to theory and previous research findings. Four main aspects are discussed:
marketing channel transformation, changes in consumer behavior, digital adaptation strategies, and barriers
to transformation. Comparisons with previous research are used to assess the suitability and discrepancies of
the findings, thus gaining a more comprehensive understanding of MSME marketing strategies in the
evolving digital environment.

Transformation of MSME Marketing Channels
Marketing cha nnel transformation shows that MSMEs in Bogor City are beginning to combine
conventional and digital channels. Social media serves as a communication and promotional platform, while
marketplaces facilitate transactions. This situation demonstrates that mar keting transformation is not simply
a shift in promotional activities to the internet, but rather a shift in how MSMEs build relationships with
consumers. This finding aligns with Ruhiyat, Sulaeman, and Purnawati (2023), who demonstrated that online
marketing presents an opportunity for food and beverage MSMEs in Bogor City to increase consumer

These results are consistent with Sulistiyowati and Rahmawati (2024), who found that marketplaces,
social media, and promotional activities are essential components of MSME digital marketing. The
difference is that this study focuses not only on digital channels as a tool for increasing sales but also on
adapting to changing consumer interaction patterns. Therefore, the effectiveness of the transf ormation
depends on the ability of MSMEs to integrate various channels based on their functions and consumer

Consumer Behavior Change as a Driver of Transformation
Changing consumer behavior is one of the main factors driving MSMEs to tran sform their marketing.
Digital consumers tend to seek information before purchasing, compare various options, pay attention to
reviews, and expect a quick response. This situation requires MSMEs to transform their marketing
communications to be more inform ative, interactive, and responsive. This finding supports research by
Ruhiyat et al. (2023), which shows that price, promotion, product quality, and payment security are
important aspects of online marketing for MSMEs in Bogor City.
The results of this study align with Kusuma Negara (2024), who found that customization, interaction,
and trendiness in social media marketing influence MSME consumer purchasing decisions. This means that
consumers not only need product availability on digital platforms but also  relevant communication
experiences. Therefore, MSMEs need to understand that changes in consumer behavior require changes in
how they structure messages, choose media, provide services, and maintain relationships with customers.
Digital Marketing Adaptation Strategy
Marketing adaptation strategies are evident through the use of digital content, increased interaction,
marketplace utilization, and product information adjustments. These strategies enable MSMEs to respond
more quickly to consumers and expand m arket reach. Adaptation also needs to be carried out continuously
because digital trends, platform algorithms, and consumer preferences are constantly changing. Indrayanti et
al. (2024), through research on MSME digital marketing strategies in the Bogor re gion, demonstrated the
importance of digital strategy, business planning, and a supportive business environment for MSME
development.
These findings are supported by Prakoso et al. (2024), who demonstrated that the implementation of
digital marketing provi des opportunities for Bogor MSMEs to increase market reach and competitiveness.
Broader research also indicates that technologies such as social media, e -commerce, AI, and big data can
help MSMEs expand their markets, although levels of understanding of th ese technologies vary (Tindi &
Silaban, 2025). Therefore, adaptation strategies need to be tailored to the capabilities and needs of each
MSME.

The main barriers to marketing transformation relate to dig ital skills, consistent content management,
limited resources, and the ability to utilize consumer data. These conditions demonstrate that digital
transformation cannot be achieved simply by providing devices or creating social media accounts.
Businesses require the ability to manage content, understand consumer characteristics, utilize digital features,
and evaluate market response. Iskamto et al. (2024) also show that not all MSMEs possess the same skills
and utilize technology to support digital marketing.
This finding aligns with Tindi and Silaban (2025), who mapped that Indonesian MSMEs still face

V. CONCLUSION
Based on the results and discussion, it can be concluded that the marketing transformation of MSMEs
in Bogor City is an increasingly important adjustment process to address changing consumer behavior in the
digital era. The use of social media, marketplaces, WhatsApp Business, and digital content has changed the
way MSMEs introduce products, communicate with consumers, and conduct sales activities. This
transformation is not only related to the use of technology but also encompasses changes in communication
patterns, service delivery, information delivery, and customer relationship management.
Changing consumer behavior is a key driver of marketing transformation. Consumers ar e increasingly
accustomed to searching for product information online, comparing various options, paying attention to
reviews, seeking quick responses, and prioritizing ease of purchase. This situation requires MSMEs to
develop marketing strategies that ar e more informative, interactive, and responsive, and tailored to the
characteristics of digital consumers.
Despite offering numerous opportunities, marketing transformation still faces several obstacles,
particularly limited digital literacy, content creat ion capabilities, limited human resources, suboptimal
utilization of consumer data, and inconsistent digital media management. Therefore, strategies to address
changing consumer behavior need to be directed at increasing digital capacity, developing creati ve content,
optimizing various marketing platforms, improving service quality, and utilizing consumer information as a
basis for marketing decision-making. With an adaptive and sustainable strategy, MSMEs in Bogor City can
adapt their marketing activities to the dynamics of the digital market.
