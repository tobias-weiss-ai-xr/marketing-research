Abstract
Celebrity endorsements have become one of the most effective marketing strategies for
enhancing brand awareness, influencing consumer perceptions, and strengthening brand equity
in highly competitive markets. Companies increasingly collaborate with celebrities to establish
credibility, create emotional connections with consumers, and differentiate their brands from
competitors. This study examines the impact of celebrity endorsements on brand equity at ITC
Company, one of India's leading diversified enterprises with a strong presence in fast -moving
consumer goods (FMCG), personal care, packaged foods, stationery, hotels, and other business
segments. The research focuses on understanding how celebrity endorsements in fluence key
dimensions of brand equity, including brand awareness, perceived quality, brand associations,
customer trust, purchase intention, and brand loyalty. The study also evaluates the effectiveness
of celebrity characteristics such as credibility, at tractiveness, popularity, expertise, and
trustworthiness in shaping consumer attitudes and strengthening the overall image of ITC's
brands. Using a descriptive research approach based on secondary data collected from research
journals, company reports, books, industry publications, and reliable online sources, the study
analyzes the relationship between celebrity endorsement strategies and consumer behavior in
the Indian market. The findings indicate that well -planned celebrity endorsement campaigns
significantly enhance brand recognition, improve customer engagement, increase consumer
confidence, and positively influence purchasing decisions when the celebrity's image aligns
with the brand's values and target audience. However, the research also highlights that product
quality, customer satisfaction, and consistent brand performance remain essential for sustaining
long-term brand equity beyond celebrity influence. The study concludes that integrating
celebrity endorsements with innovative marketing strategie s, digital media engagement, and
customer-centric branding can strengthen brand equity and provide a sustainable competitive
advantage for ITC Company. The outcomes of this research offer valuable insights for
marketers, business managers, researchers, and  policymakers in designing effective
endorsement strategies that maximize brand value, improve consumer relationships, and
support long-term organizational growth in an increasingly competitive marketplace.
Keywords:  Celebrity Endorsements, Brand Equity, ITC Company, Consumer Behaviour,
Brand Awareness, Brand Loyalty, Brand Image.

II. REVIEW OF LITERATURE
Erdogan (1999) conducted an extensive
review of celebrity endorsement as a
marketing communication strategy. The
study examined why companies use
celebrities to support corporate and brand
images and discussed factors such as
celebrity attractiveness, trustworthiness,
expertise, and likeability. The author
emphasized that celebrity characteristics can
be transferred to the endorsed brand and
influence consumer perceptions. However,
the study also pointed out that an
inappropriate celebrity-brand association can
negatively affect advertising outcomes. The
research provides a strong theoretical
foundation for examining how celebrity
endorsements can contribute to brand equity.
Ohanian (1990)  examined the role of
celebrity credibility in advertising
effectiveness and developed a measurement
scale based on three major dimensions:
attractiveness, trustworthiness, and expertise.
The study suggested that consumers'
perceptions of an endorser's cre dibility can
influence their responses toward the
advertised product or brand. The findings are
particularly relevant to brand equity because
a credible celebrity can strengthen favorable
brand associations and improve consumer
attitudes toward the brand. This framework
can be applied to ITC brands that use well -
known personalities in their advertising
campaigns.
Bergkvist and Zhou (2016)  reviewed the
extensive literature on celebrity
endorsements and identified six major areas,
including campaign management, financial
effects, celebrity persuasion, meaning
transfer, and brand -to-celebrity transfer.
Their review showed that celebrity
endorsement effects depend on several
psychological and marketing mechanisms
rather than simply on celebrity popularity.
The authors also identified conflicting
findings in previous research and
emphasized the importance of celebrity -
brand fit and consumer perc eptions. Their
work provides a comprehensive framework
for understanding how celebrity
endorsements can influence brand -related
outcomes.
Sharma (2016) empirically investigated the
effect of celebrity endorsements on
dimensions of customer -based brand equity
in the Indian luxury market. Data were
collected from 202 consumers, and the study
examined four dimensions of brand equity:
brand awareness, brand associations,
perceived quality, and brand loyalty. The
findings revealed that celebrity
endorsements had a positive and significant
effect on brand awareness and brand
associations, while their influence on
perceived quality and brand loyalty was not
significant. The study demonstrates that
celebrity endorsements can be particularly
effective in increasing brand visibility and

Jaikumar (2015) examined celebrity

Kumar and Ramana (2019) conducted a
literature review examining the relationship
between celebrity endorsement and
consumer-based brand equity. The study
described celebrity endorsement as an
important brand -management strategy
through which companies attempt to add
value to their brands. The review concluded
that celebrity endorsement generally has a
positive relationship with consumer -based
brand equity, particularly through improved
brand image, awareness, and favorable brand
associations. The authors suggested that
managers should carefully evaluate celebrity
partnerships because the right celebrity can
strengthen the value consumers associate
with a brand.
Knoll and Matthes (2017) conducted a
meta-analysis of 46 studies involving more
than 10,000 participants to assess the
effectiveness of celebrity endorsements.
Their findings showed that celebrity
endorsements can produce substantial
positive effects when important moderators,
particularly celebrity -product congruence,
are taken into consideration. The study also
found that poorly matched celebrity -product
combinations could produce negative
outcomes. The research therefore
emphasizes that celebrity selection should be
based not only on popularity but also on the
celebrity's suitability for the product and
target audience.
Singh and Banerjee (2018) investigated the
factors influencing celebrity endorsement
credibility in the Indian context. Based on
responses from 432 participants, the
researchers used exploratory and
confirmatory factor analysis to identify the
dimensions contributing to celebrity
credibility. The study demonstrated that
consumers evaluate celebrities on multiple
credibility-related characteristics before
accepting their endorsement messages. The
findings suggest that celebrity credibility is
an important mechanism through which
endorsements can influence consumer
perceptions of brands. For companies such as
ITC, selecting celebrities perceived as
credible and appropriate for a particular
brand can therefore contribute to stronger
brand equity.
Halder, Pradhan, and Roy Chaudhuri
(2021) conducted a structured and
bibliometric review of 45 years of celebrity
credibility and endorsement research. The
study analyzed major authors, theories,
methodologies, journals, and research
themes within celebrity endorsement
literature. The researchers found that
celebrity credibility remains a central
element of endorsement research and
observed a growing shift toward digital and
social media environments. Their review also
developed an antecedent -consequence
framework explaining how source credibility
influences consumer outcomes. The study is
useful for understanding the broader
theoretical relationship between celebrity
credibility, brand communication, and brand
equity.
Dwivedi, Johnson, and McDonald (2015)
examined the relationship between celebrity
endorsement, self -brand connection, and
consumer-based brand equity. The study
focused on how consumers' psychological
connection with a celebrity can transfer to
their relationship with an endorsed brand.
The fi ndings suggested that celebrity
endorsement can contribute to consumer -
based brand equity when consumers develop
stronger connections between themselves,
the celebrity, and the brand. The study

IV. FINDINGS
The findings of this study reveal that
celebrity endorsements play a significant role
in strengthening the brand equity of ITC
Company by enhancing brand awareness,
improving consumer perception, and
increasing customer engagement. The
research indicates that celebrities with strong
credibility, popularity, expertise, and
trustworthiness positively influence
consumer attitudes and purchas e intentions
when their personalities align with the values
of ITC's brands. The study further shows that
celebrity endorsement campaigns
significantly improve brand recall, brand
associations, and customer trust, particularly
when supported by high produc t quality,
effective advertising, and consistent brand
communication. In addition, ITC's
integration of celebrity endorsements with
digital marketing, social media promotions,
and customer-centric branding strategies has
contributed to increased brand loya lty and
sustainable competitive advantage. However,
the findings also emphasize that long -term
brand equity depends not only on celebrity
influence but also on continuous product
innovation, customer satisfaction, ethical
branding, and maintaining consistent product
quality.

V. CONCLUSION
This study concludes that celebrity
endorsements have a substantial positive
impact on the brand equity of ITC Company
by i ncreasing brand awareness,
strengthening consumer trust, improving
brand image, and positively influencing
purchasing decisions. ITC has effectively
utilized celebrity endorsements as part of its
integrated marketing strategy to enhance the
visibility and credibility of its diverse
product portfolio. The research demonstrates
that successful endorsement campaigns
create stronger emotional connections
between consumers and brands while
contributing to higher customer loyalty and
improved market competitiveness. However,
the study also highlights that celebrity
endorsements alone cannot guarantee long -
term success unless they are supported by
superior product quality, customer
satisfaction, innovation, and effective brand
management. Overall, a balanced
combination of celebrity endorsements and
customer-focused marketing strategies
enables ITC to strengthen its brand equity
and achieve sustainable business growth in
the competitive FMCG industry.

VI. FUTURE SCOPE
Future research can expand this study by
comparing the effectiveness of celebrity
endorsements across different FMCG
companies and product categories to identify
industry-wide best practices. Researchers
may also examine the growing influence of
social media influencers, digital creators, and
virtual brand ambassadors on consumer
behavior and brand equity. In addition, future
studies can investigate the impact of artificial
intelligence, personalized advertising, and
data-driven marketing strategies on
endorsement effectiveness. Primary data
collected through customer surveys,
interviews, and focus group discussions can
provide deeper insights into consumer
preferences and attitudes toward celebrity -
endorsed brands. Furthermore, longitudinal
studies can evaluate how changing consumer
expectations, technological advancements,
and evolving marketing trends influence the
long-term relationship between celebrity
endorsements and brand equity, enabling
organizations to develop more effective and
sustainable branding strategies.
