Abstract: Unilateral This research is motivated by the high number of cybercrimes in the form
of fraud in e-commerce in Karawang Regency, with the increasing digital lifestyle of the
community. The main objective of this study is to explore this phenomenon using a victimology
perspective, where the Lifestyle Exposure theory of Hindelang and his colleagues and the
concept of functional responsibility of Stephen Schafer are the foundation. The methodology
applied in this study is empirical juridical, with primary data obtained th rough in-depth
interviews with the Tipidter Unit of the Karawang Police and individuals who have become
victims of online fraud. The findings of the study indicate that the key factors causing
victimization in Karawang are caused by less vigilant consumer behavior, including
transactions outside official platforms (85%) and being influenced by unreasonable prices
(78%), which theoretically indicates exposure to risks due to digital lifestyles. The Karawang
Police's countermeasures utilize a preventative approach, providing digital literacy education
to strengthen social resilience, and a repressive approach through law enforcement based on
digital forensics and inter-agency collaboration (Kominfo, BSSN, and OJK) to create a
deterrent effect on perpetrators. This study concludes that preventing online fraud requires
collaboration between legal enforcement and increased awareness among victims to break the
cycle of opportunity for crime.

Keyword: Online Fraud, Victimology, Lifestyle Exposure, Karawang Police, E-Commerce

INTRODUCTION
Technological developments have shifted people's economic interaction patterns from
conventional markets to virtual markets or e -commerce. However, this accelerated
digitalization is a double-edged sword: on the one hand, it offers efficiency, but on the other,
it opens up criminological opportunities for the emergence of cyber-fraud. In the jurisdiction
of Karawan Regency, an industrial area with a densely populated workforce, this phenomenon
demonstrates a worrying trend.

Trade is a business activity that utilizes electronic technology to connect companies,
customers, and the public through digital transactions. Essentially, businesses using e -
commerce are a paperless form of commerce. Buying and selling transactions conduc ted
through e-commerce generally begin with a sales offer, a purchase request, and the receipt of
the sale or purchase. Before a transaction occurs, an online offer is often made, for example,
through an internet site.
The digital economy era is a cycle of postmodern economic activity that is attempting
to overthrow (reproduce) the old paradigm of modern business. The digital economy era also
serves as a pre-paradigm for new interactions and communications in business ec onomic
activities. (Timbo Mangaranap Sirait, 2024)
Empirically, the highly mobile nature of Karawang society and its heavy reliance on
digital transactions creates specific patterns of vulnerability. Observations indicate that many
individuals who fall victim to online fraud in Karawang come from groups that are productively
active on social media but pay little attention to risk mitigation in their transactions. This
suggests a link between daily behavior and the likelihood of crime.
In this context, this study investigated the phenomenon of e -commerce fraud in
Karawang Regency, incorporating Lifestyle Exposure Theory to analyze the digital lifestyle
patterns and roles of victims in these crimes. Primary data for this study were collected directly
in the field through in-depth interviews with members of the Tipidter Unit (specific crimes) of
the Karawang Police and individuals who have fallen victim to online fraud in the region.
Furthermore, this study considered relevant legal norms, such as the Electronic Information
and Transactions Law (UU ITE) and victimology theory (lifestyle exposure theory), to assess
the effectiveness of legal protection and police response efforts related to the digital lifestyle
vulnerabilities of the Karawang community. This research adopts an empirical juridical
approach, where law is considered a social reality within Karawang society. The researcher
feels the need to conduct an in-depth study of the influence of the local community's digital
lifestyle on online fraud. This is crucial because law enforcement has so far focused on
repressive measures against perpetrators, while victim-oriented preventative measures are
often overlooked. Next, to Problem of this research, namely:
1. What are the online shopping habits of Karawang residents that make them
susceptible to fraud, as viewed from the perspective of Lifestyle Exposure Theory?
2. How are the Karawang Police's efforts to address the high number of online fraud
victims in e-commerce transactions within the jurisdiction of Karawang Regency?

METHOD
This research uses an empirical juridical method, an approach to legal studies that aims
to observe law in a real context and examine how law functions in society. In this case, the
researcher explores the phenomenon of e-commerce fraud in Karawang Regency by linking
Lifestyle Exposure Theory to analyze the digital lifestyle patterns or lifestyles of victims that
contribute to these crimes.
This methodological analysis is based on Stephen Schafer's perspective on functional
responsibility, which assumes that the likelihood of a crime occurring results from a complex
interaction between the victim's negligence and the perpetrator's intent. (Lilik Mulyadi, 2012)
The primary data for this study was collected directly from the field (primary data) through in-
depth interviews with members of the Karawang Police Criminal Investigation Unit and
individuals who have been victims of online fraud in the area. Furthermore, this study also
considered existing legal norms (secondary data), such as the Electronic Information and
Transactions Law (UU ITE) and theories of victimology, to evaluate the effectiveness of legal
protection and police response efforts related to the digital lifestyle vulnerabilities of the
Karawang community.

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
Online Shopping Habits of the Karawang Community That Trigger Risks Fraud in a
Lifestyle Exposure Theory Review
In terms of terminology, victimology comes from the words victim (victima) and logos
(science). Broadly speaking, it is a scientific discipline that analyzes all aspects related to
victims, including their rights and the process of crime (victimization). (Rena Yulia , 2010)
Victimology is a branch of science that focuses on the study of individual victims, including
the factors that cause suffering, the interaction between victims and perpetrators, and the
victim's function within the legal system and social life. (J.E. Sahetapy, 1987)
Research on victimology, Lifestyle Exposure Theory, was introduced by Michael J.
Hindelang, Michael R. Gottfredson, and James Garofalo in 1978. This theory was published in
their book "Victims of Personal Crime: An Empirical Examination of a Conceptual Model of
Victimization." (Michael J. hindelang, 1978)
Lifestyle Exposure Theory proposes that a person's opportunity to become a victim of
crime is not accidental, but rather the result of daily habits and lifestyles. (I.S. Susanto, 2011)
Essentially, this theory asserts that differences in lifestyle, including activities at work,
education, or leisure, create variations in exposure to conditions, locations, and times that
increase the likelihood of crime. (Topo Santoso, 2001) This is based on age, gender, and
socioeconomic status; for example, younger groups who frequently spend time outdoors at
night are theoretically more likely to encounter individuals with malicious intent in public
spaces. (Rena Yustiana, 2017)
From a sociological perspective, the increase in cybercrime cases in Karawang Regency
is influenced by shifts in people's digital lifestyles. In the case of e -commerce fraud, the
Lifestyle Exposure Theory suggests that people's habits of actively shopping online without
adequate digital literacy, such as transacting outside official platforms or being swayed by very
low prices, have created a high level of social vulnerability. This situation aligns with the
sociological approach to cybercrime research, which focuses on identifying the reasons behind
variations in crime rates within the social structure itself. (Zarisnov Arafat, 2018)
It demonstrates that a person's likelihood of becoming a target of crime is closely related
to their daily lifestyle patterns and routines. This theory's connection to the existing discussion
is based on the idea that crime does not occur randomly, but rather is caused by opportunities
that arise when individuals 'expose' themselves to risk through certain habits. In this case,
exposure occurs when a person's digital lifestyle leads them to interact with criminals in
unsecured cyberspace.
In developments in Karawang Regency, residents' digital lifestyles also demonstrate
insecurity when conducting transactions on official platforms like Shopee. Based on the
Lifestyle Exposure Theory, although official platforms offer protection features, victims' habits
of frequently switching communication outside the app (for example, switching to WhatsApp
due to the lure of bonuses or discounts) make them vulnerable to fraud. Empirically, many
Karawang residents have fallen prey to fake accounts that mimic official admins or the "held
order" scheme. Legally, information manipulation on these e-commerce platforms is strictly
prohibited under Article 28 paragraph (1) of Law No. 1 of 2024 (the ITE Law) concerning fake
news that harms consumers. (Undang Undang Nomor 1 Tahun 2024, 2024) Furthermore, if
fraudsters successfully hack accounts through phishing links, Law No. 1 of 2024 also applies.
Law No. 27 of 2022 concerning Personal Data Protection (UU PDP), which in 2026 provides
criminal sanctions for violations of the illegal use of identity data, has been amended. (Undang-
Undang Nomor 27 Tahun 2022, 2022) However, the reality on the ground at the Karawang
Police Department shows that despite strict regulations and the official Shopee platform, the
public's mindset of being easily persuaded by false authorities and ignoring app security
warnings remains a major factor in these frauds. (Results of interviews, 2026)

Karawang Police's Efforts to Address the High Number of Online Fraud Victims in E-
Commerce Transactions in Karawang Regency
To resolve e-commerce fraud cases in 2026, the Karawang Police prioritized inter-
agency collaboration to disrupt increasingly complex cybercrime networks. (Josua Sitompul,
2021) Technically, investigators from the Karawang Police Cyber Unit collaborated with the
Ministry of Communication and Digital and the National Cyber and Crypto Agency (BSSN)
to trace digital footprints and block accounts or websites suspected of involvement in fraud.
(Undang-Undang Nomor 1 Tahun 2024, 2024) Financially, the Karawang Police coordinated
with the Financial Services Authority (OJK) and banking institutions to expedite the tracing of
fund flows and freeze accounts held by perpetrators. (Dikdik M. Arief Mansur, 2021)
Furthermore, effective collaboration was established with platform providers such as Shopee
to collect transaction data and user identities to obtain valid evidence in accordance with the
2024 ITE Law. (Undang-Undang Nomor 27 Tahun 2022, 2022) Collaboration between these
institutions is crucial in 2026 so that law enforcement is not limited to arresting perpetrators
but also oriented towards recovering losses for victims through available legal channels.
(Results of interviews, 2026)
The Karawang Police are collaborating with the Ministry of Communication and
Information Technology (Kominfo) to address cybercrime, reflecting the interconnected
integration of law enforcement and technical regulations. The Tipidter Unit of the Karawang
Police Criminal Investigation Unit acts as the field implementer, conducting investigations,
physically searching for perpetrators, and taking legal action regarding reports of e-commerce
transaction fraud within the Karawang Regency jurisdiction. Meanwhile , the Ministry of
Communication and Information Technology (Kominfo) provides support in the form of digital
infrastructure and legal aspects by providing expert witnesses for information technology (ITE)
and managing reporting databases, including the cekrekening.id and aduankonten.id portals.
Data from the Ministry of Communication and Information Technology is then used by
investigators at the Karawang Police as initial evidence to trace the digital footprints of
perpetrators, who typically use websites or links from overseas to avoid detection.
While the lifestyle exposure theory is generally associated with Hindelang, Gottfredson,
and Garofalo, (Micheal J.Hindelang, 1978) Stephen Schafer's thinking provides a deeper
dimension through the concept of functional responsibility. (Stephen Schafer, 1968) In this
view, lifestyle is not simply a routine but a form of moral obligation, where victims are
considered to have a role in creating opportunities for crime. Through daily activities that place
a person in a risky situation such as being in a vulnerable location without victim supervision.
indirectly "attracts" the threat, so that crime is no longer viewed solely as the result of the
perpetrator's intent, but also as a consequence of the victim's disregard for precautionary
principles. (Ibid)
Schafer emphasizes the existence of a typology of victims based on the level of error or
carelessness arising from their lifestyle. (Dikdik M. Arief Mansur, 2007) A provocative or
reckless lifestyle creates physical and temporal proximity to potential perpetrators, which, from
this perspective, is seen as a victim's failure to perform self-protection. Therefore, the core of
this thinking concludes that the opportunity for crime to occur is greatly influenced by the

The Karawang Police's efforts to address the high number of e-commerce fraud cases
in the Karawang Regency jurisdiction are implemented through two main approaches: a
preventive and a repressive approach. (Undang-Undang Nomor 2 Tahun 2002, 2002) In the
preventive aspect, the Karawang Police, with the assistance of the Community Guidance Unit
(Binmas) and the Cyber Unit, implement a prevention strategy that includes increasing digital
literacy among the public to reduce security gaps arising from a lack of vigilance on social
media users. (Dikdik M. Arief Mansur, Lok. Cit) In the field, reality shows that outreach
regarding fraudulent methods and education through official digital channels serve as important
tools to minimize victim responsibility, which can open up opportunities for crime. (Vidya
Prahassacitta, 2021) The following methods can be implemented:
1. Mapping E-commerce Fraud Patterns. The high rate of fraud in Karawang makes it crucial
for the police to map the latest patterns so that outreach materials remain relevant to
conditions on the ground. (Lail Aoelia Anjani Rachmat, 2023)
2. Maximizing the Role of the Community Development Unit in the Field, to emphasize the
importance of a preventative approach through the Community Development Unit. Direct
education is crucial for reaching communities with low digital literacy. (I Putu Gede Denny
Krisnatha, 2020)
3. Innovative Content from the Cyber Unit. Innovative digital content is more effective in
raising the awareness of today's social media users.
4. Activating Official Digital Channels, explaining that official channels help reduce
opportunities for crime. These channels serve as a place for rapid complaints and
verification before people become victims.
5. Building Active Social Resilience, related to the ultimate goal of the text initiative, which
is to transform the Karawang community from a vulnerable, passive position to an active,
critical and resilient individual in the cyber world. (Mukminin dan Rhamadan, 2024)
The goal of this initiative is to build social resilience that transforms Karawang residents
from passive subjects into active participants alert to unusual electronic transactions. (Results
of interviews, 2026)
As part of its efforts to strengthen community engagement, the Karawang Police
actively conducts face-to-face outreach by participating in weekly meetings (minggon) at the
village level. This direct approach ensures that important information and community security
programs are delivered efficiently, evenly, and effectively to remote villages. Beyond relying
solely on traditional methods, the Karawang Police also actively utilizes digital platforms to
expand access to education. Through official social medi a accounts such as Instagram
@polres_karawang and @cyber.karawang, they consistently share information about security,
digital learning, and counter the spread of fake news (hoaxes) to create a safe and conducive
environment.
Repressive efforts (law enforcement and/or prosecution) carried out by the Criminal
Investigation Unit rely on digital evidence and digital forensic procedures. According to field
reports, the Karawang Police Department follows up on victims' complaints by tracking the
movement of funds and coordinating with other regions to apprehend perpetrators, who often
operate outside Karawang's jurisdiction. They also collaborate with banks if there is a
connection between funds going out and coming into a bank account. These actions extend
beyond arrests and include formal legal action, including implementing Article 28 paragraph
(1) of the Electronic Information and Transactions (ITE) Law, to create a deterrent effect. This
combination of structured prevention and firm law enforcement reflects the Karawang Police
Department's dedication to addressing the development of cybercrime, which directly impacts
the financial and psychological well-being of the community in Karawang Regency.

Based on information obtained through interviews report by the Karawang Police's
Tipidter Unit on April 9, 2026, observed a continuous upward trend in online fraud cases,
ranging from 15% to 20% annually. In 2024, an average of 75 case reports were received each
month, with a cumulative total of 900 cases throughout the year. This figure experienced a

reports per month, resulting in an annual total of 1,296 cases.
This striking increase in cases, sociologically, reflects the high vulnerability of the
Karawang Regency community to cyberspace. Analyzing this through lifestyle exposure
theory, the rise in crime rates can be traced to a lifestyle that is less vigilant a bout digital
transactions. Approximately 85% of victims tend to transact outside of official e-commerce
platforms and are easily swayed by attractive price offers. These careless shopping habits
consistently provide easy targets for criminals to exploit, posing a significant challenge for the
Karawang Police's Tipidter Unit in enhancing law enforcement efforts in the field.

CONCLUSION
Online shopping practices among residents of Karawang Regency are strongly
correlated with their vulnerability to e-commerce fraud, based on the Lifestyle Exposure
Theory. Clearly, a digital lifestyle lacking in cyber awareness, such as a tendency to transact
outside of official platforms (85%) and a lack of responsiveness to extremely low price offers,
leads to the exploitation of security gaps. This indicates a neglect of "functional responsibility"
on the part of victims, where careless online interactio n patterns indirectly open up
opportunities for perpetrators to commit crimes. Crime in this context arises not only from the
perpetrator's malicious intent but is also driven by exposure to risks inherent in the victim's
lifestyle.
The steps taken by the Karawang Police to address online fraud involve a combination
of two main strategies. From a prevention perspective, the Karawang Police prioritize digital
literacy outreach and cyber patrols to build community resilience and increas e vigilance.
Meanwhile, from a law enforcement perspective, actions are carried out through digital
forensics-based investigations with reference to Article 28 paragraph (1) of the ITE Law.
Although the Police have collaborated with various agencies such a s the Ministry of
Communication and Information and banking institutions, significant challenges remain
related to the anonymity of the perpetrators and their location, which is often outside the scope
of Karawang's legal jurisdiction. Thus, the effectiveness of handling depends heavily on the
balance between firmness in law enforcement and increased preventative behavior by the
community (victims).
