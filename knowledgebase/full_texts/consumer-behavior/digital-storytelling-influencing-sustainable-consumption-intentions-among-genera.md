ABSTRACT

The rapid growth of e -commerce has transformed consumer behavior while creating
environmental challenges through excessive consumption and fast fashion. Digital
storytelling has emerged as a communication strategy to enhance environmental awareness
and promote sustainable consumption. This study examines the effect of digital storytelling
on sustainable consumption intention through environmental awareness in purchasing
sustainable fashion and accessories on Shopee. A quantitative explanatory approach was
employed using data collected through an online questionnaire from 100 Generation Z
respondents in Palembang City selected by purposive sampling. Data were analyzed using
Structural Equation Modeling –Partial Least Squares (SEM -PLS) with SmartPLS. The
results show that digital storytelling positively and significantly affects environmental
awareness (β = 0.449, p < 0.001) and sustainable consumption intention (β = 0.393, p <
0.001). Environmental awareness also positively influences sustainable consumption
intention (β = 0.510, p < 0.001) and mediates the relationship between digital storytelling
and sustainable consumption intention. These findings support the Stimulus -Organism-
Response (SOR) theory and provide practical implications for e -commerce businesses in
designing effective sustainability-oriented marketing communication strategies.

Keywords: Digital Storytelling, Environmental Awareness, Sustainable Consumption
Intention, Sustainable Fashion, Generation Z, Shopee

INTRODUCTION

The rapid advancement of digital technology has significantly transformed consumer
purchasing behavior through the continuous growth of e-commerce. E-commerce platforms
such as Shopee provide consumers with convenient, fast, and efficient access to a wide range
of products without geographical or time constraints. Despite these advantages, the
expansion of e-commerce has also created substantial environmental challenges, including
excessive consumption, increased use of single -use packaging, and the rapid g rowth of the
fast-fashion industry, all of which contribute to environmental degradation. According to
Vu et al. (2026), the development of e -commerce has significantly reshaped consumer
purchasing patterns and has become one of the key factors influencing  environmentally
responsible consumption. Furthermore, the fashion industry is recognized as one of the
largest contributors to environmental pollution due to its intensive use of natural resources,
high carbon emissions, and increasing textile waste (Lee et al., 2020). Consequently,
sustainable consumption within digital commerce has become an increasingly important
issue in both marketing and sustainability research.

In recent years, sustainable fashion has attracted growing attention as public awareness
of environmental preservation continues to increase. Sustainable fashion refers to the
consumption of fashion products that considers environmental and social impacts
throughout a product's life cycle. Yang et al. (2024) argue that environmental values, beliefs,
and norms play a significant role in shaping sustainable fashion consumption behavior.
Likewise, Acikgoz et al. (2026) found that consumers' perceptions of product sustainability
are among the primary drivers of their intention to purchase environmentally friendly
fashion products. Therefore, strengthening sustainable consumption intention has become
an essential strategy for promoting sustainable development.
Generation Z represents an important consumer segment in the context of sustainable
consumption. As digital natives, Generation Z possesses high levels of digital literacy and
actively engages with social media and e -commerce platforms. According to Islam et al.
(2025), Generation Z relies heavily on digital media as a primary source of information
during the purchasing decision -making process. In addition, previous studies have shown
that this generation demonstrates relatively strong concern for environme ntal issues and
climate change (Madera et al., 2025). Nevertheless, an interesting behavioral paradox
remains. Despite their high level of environmental awareness, many Generation Z
consumers continue to purchase inexpensive fast -fashion products driven by  trends,
affordability, and impulsive buying behavior. Zimand-Sheiner and Lissitsa (2024) reported
that environmental information can positively influence Generation Z's purchase intentions;
however, price considerations, social trends, and personal prefer ences often remain the
dominant determinants of purchasing decisions. This phenomenon illustrates the existence
of an intention-behavior gap in the context of sustainable consumption.
Within Indonesia's e -commerce ecosystem, Shopee has become one of the most
widely used online marketplaces, particularly among Generation Z consumers. Its user -
friendly interface, interactive features, extensive promotional campaigns, and integration
with social media have positioned Shopee as a primary platform for searching information
and purchasing fashion products. Beyond serving as a transactional platform, Shopee also
functions as a digital marketing communication channel that enables brands to deliv er
product information through various digital formats, including short videos, live streaming,
customer reviews, and visually engaging content. These characteristics make Shopee an
appropriate context for investigating how digital communication strategies  can influence
consumer behavior, particularly in encouraging more sustainable consumption practices.
To reduce the gap between environmental awareness and actual consumption
behavior, companies need to implement communication strategies that extend beyond
providing rational information about the benefits of sustainable products. Effective
communication sh ould also establish emotional connections with consumers. One
increasingly adopted approach is digital storytelling. Digital storytelling is a marketing
communication strategy that utilizes narratives, visual content, videos, and consumer
experiences to deliver messages in a more engaging and meaningful manner. According to
Duong (2026), sustainability messages presented through compelling stories can strengthen
the emotional bond between consumers and brands. Furthermore, Luo et al. (2026)
demonstrated tha t persuasive and interactive digital communication enhances consumer
engagement with environmental issues. Similarly, Elkashif et al. (2026) found that
sustainability messages delivered through digital influencers and relevant narratives
generate more positive consumer responses toward sustainability initiatives.
From the perspective of the Stimulus -Organism-Response (SOR) theory, digital
storytelling can be conceptualized as the stimulus (S) delivered through digital platforms.
This stimulus influences consumers' internal psychological state, represented by

THEORETICAL STUDIES

Digital Storytelling
Digital storytelling is a communication strategy that utilizes narratives, visual content,
audio, videos, and various digital media elements to deliver messages in an engaging and
meaningful manner. In the marketing context, digital storytelling is employe d to establish
emotional connections between brands and consumers by presenting authentic, relevant, and
easily understandable stories. According to Duong (2026), sustainability messages conveyed
through storytelling strengthen consumers' emotional attachm ent to a brand. Furthermore,
Luo et al. (2026) reported that interactive digital communication enhances consumer
engagement with social and environmental issues. Similarly, Elkashif et al. (2026) found
that sustainability messages communicated through relevant narratives positively influence
consumers' responses toward sustainable products and sustainability-oriented campaigns.
Within the e -commerce environment, digital storytelling has become increasingly
important because it enables companies to communicate product values and benefits more
persuasively than merely presenting functional product information. Through engaging
stories, consumers not only understand product characteristics but also appreciate the
meanings and values embedded in sustainable products. Therefore, digital storytelling is
considered an effective marketing strategy for shaping consumers' perceptions and
encouraging sustainable consumption behavior.

Environmental Awareness
Environmental awareness refers to an individual's level of knowledge, concern, and
sensitivity toward environmental issues and the impacts of human activities on
environmental sustainability. Such awareness encourages individuals to consider
sustainability aspects when making consumption decisions. According to Yang et al. (2024),
environmental values, beliefs, and norms play a significant role in shaping sustainable
consumption behavior. Individuals with higher levels of environmental awareness are more
likely to consider the environmental impacts of the products they purchase and use.
Madera et al. (2025) explained that individuals' connectedness with nature enhances
environmental concern and encourages more responsible behavior. Moreover, Zimand -
Sheiner and Lissitsa (2024) found that information regarding a product's environmental
impact can significantly influence consumers' attitudes and purchase intentions, particularly
among Generation Z. Therefore, environmental awareness represents an essential
psychological factor in explaining sustainable consumption behavior.

Sustainable Consumption Intention
Sustainable consumption intention refers to an individual's intention to engage in
consumption that considers environmental, social, and economic aspects in order to support
long-term sustainability. This concept reflects consumers' willingness to select, purchase,
and use products that generate lower environmental impacts than conventional alternatives.
According to Acikgoz et al. (2026), consumers' perceptions of product sustainability
are among the primary determinants of sustainable fashion purchase intention. Likewise,
Yang et al. (2024) argued that environmental values and beliefs positively contribu te to
sustainable fashion consumption behavior. In addition, Rese and Baier (2025) emphasized
that increasing concern for sustainability encourages consumers to choose products that
support more responsible consumption practices. Therefore, sustainable con sumption
intention has become a key indicator for understanding consumer behavior toward
sustainable fashion and accessories.

Stimulus–Organism–Response (SOR) Theory
This study adopts the Stimulus –Organism–Response (SOR) theory as its primary
theoretical foundation. The SOR theory explains that external stimuli influence individuals'
internal psychological states before generating behavioral responses. In this study, D igital
Storytelling is conceptualized as the stimulus (S) delivered through digital platforms. The
stimulus subsequently influences Environmental Awareness as the organism (O),
representing consumers' internal psychological condition in terms of their leve l of
environmental consciousness. This internal condition ultimately leads to the behavioral
response (R), namely Sustainable Consumption Intention.
From the SOR perspective, this study argues that effective digital storytelling
enhances consumers' environmental awareness, which subsequently encourages stronger
intentions to engage in sustainable consumption. Accordingly, Environmental Awareness
functions as the psychological mechanism that mediates the relationship between Digital
Storytelling and Sustainable Consumption Intention.

Hypothesis Development
Digital storytelling enables companies to communicate sustainability messages in a
more engaging and emotionally appealing manner, allowing consumers to better understand
environmental issues associated with products. According to Duong (2026), sustainabil ity
narratives delivered effectively strengthen consumers' emotional attachment and improve
their understanding of sustainability values. Likewise, Luo et al. (2026) demonstrated that
interactive digital communication increases individuals' engagement with  environmental
issues. Therefore, digital storytelling is expected to enhance consumers' environmental
awareness.
H1: Digital Storytelling has a positive effect on Environmental Awareness.

Environmental awareness reflects individuals' concern for the environmental
consequences of their consumption behavior. The higher an individual's environmental
awareness, the greater the likelihood of choosing products that support sustainability. Yang
et al. (2024) found that environmental values and beliefs positively contribute to sustainable
consumption behavior. These findings indicate that environmental awareness has the
potential to increase sustainable consumption intention.
H2: Environmental Awareness has a positive effect on Sustainable Consumption Intention.

From the perspective of the SOR theory, digital storytelling, as an external stimulus,
influences consumers' behavioral responses by altering their internal psychological
conditions. Effective digital narratives enhance consumers' understanding of and conc ern
for environmental issues, thereby encouraging stronger intentions to purchase sustainable
products. Consequently, environmental awareness is expected to mediate the relationship
between digital storytelling and sustainable consumption intention.
H3: Environmental Awareness mediates the relationship between Digital Storytelling and
Sustainable Consumption Intention.

Figure 1 illustrates the conceptual framework of this study, which is developed based

Awareness (H1) by increasing consumers' understanding and emotional enga gement with

Sustainable Consumption Intention (H2), indicating that consumers with higher
environmental awareness are more likely to purchase sustainable fashion and  accessories.
Finally, Environmental Awareness is proposed to mediate the relationship between Digital
Storytelling and Sustainable Consumption Intention (H3), suggesting that digital storytelling
indirectly enhances consumers' sustainable consumption inte ntion by strengthening their
environmental awareness.

RESEARCH METHODS

This study employed a quantitative approach with an explanatory research design to
examine the relationships among Digital Storytelling, Environmental Awareness, and
Sustainable Consumption Intention. The target population consisted of Generation Z
consumers in Palembang City who use the Shopee platform. A purposive sampling
technique was applied, with the following criteria: (1) respondents aged between 17 and 27
years, (2) residing in Palembang City, (3) having an active Shopee account, and (4) having
been exposed to information or promotional content related to sustainable fashion and
accessories on Shopee. Data were collected through an online questionnaire using a five -
point Likert scale ranging from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). A total of 100
respondents participated in this study.
The research instrument consisted of three constructs: Digital Storytelling,
Environmental Awareness, and Sustainable Consumption Intention. Data were analyzed
using Structural Equation Modeling –Partial Least Squares (SEM -PLS) with the assistance
of SmartPLS software. The analysis comprised two stages: evaluation of the measurement
model (outer model) to assess construct validity and reliability, and evaluation of the
structural model (inner model) to examine the relationships among the constructs and test
the proposed hypotheses. The significance of the path coefficients was assessed using the
bootstrapping procedure with a 5% significance level.

DISCUSSION

The Effect of Digital Storytelling on Environmental Awareness
The results indicate that Digital Storytelling has a positive and significant effect on
Environmental Awareness , supporting H1. This finding suggests that sustainability
messages communicated through engaging digital narratives, visual content, and interactive
media can effectively enhance consumers' awareness of environmental issues. Within the
context of Shopee, digital storyte lling enables consumers to better understand the
environmental value of sustainable fashion products while strengthening their emotional
connection with sustainability -related messages. This result is consistent with Duong
(2026), who argued that storytelling -based sustainability communication strengthens
consumers' emotional attachment and understanding of sustainability values. Similarly, Luo
et al. (2026) found that interactive digital communication increases consumer engagement
with environmental issues, while Elkashif et al. (2026)  demonstrated that sustainability
messages delivered through relevant digital narratives positively influence consumers'
environmental perceptions. Therefore, digital storytelling can be regarded as an effective
communication strategy for enhancing environ mental awareness among Generation Z
consumers.

The Effect of Environmental Awareness on Sustainable Consumption Intention
The findings reveal that Environmental Awareness has a positive and significant
effect on Sustainable Consumption Intention, thereby supporting H2. This indicates that
consumers with greater awareness of environmental issues are more likely to purchase
sustainable fashion and accessories. Environmental awareness encourages consumers to
consider the environmental consequences of their purchasing decisions and motivates them
to support environmentally responsible products. These findings are consistent with Yang
et al. (2024) , who emphasized that environmental values, beliefs, and norms positively
influence sustainable consumption behavior. Likewise, Acikgoz et al. (2026) reported that
consumers' perceptions of sustainability significantly increase their intention to purchase
sustainable fashion products. Consequently, strengthening environmental awareness
represents an important strategy for promoting sustainable consumption among Generation
Z.

The Effect of Digital Storytelling on Sustainable Consumption Intention
The results also demonstrate that Digital Storytelling has a positive and significant
effect on Sustainable Consumption Intention, supporting H3. This finding indicates that
compelling digital narratives not only increase consumers' environmental awareness but also
directly encourage their intention to purchase sustainable products. Storytelling allows
brands to communicate product values beyond f unctional attributes by emphasizing
sustainability, authenticity, and social responsibility. As a result, consumers become more
emotionally engaged with the brand and are more willing to support sustainable products.
These findings reinforce previous studi es suggesting that digital communication strategies
emphasizing emotional engagement and meaningful narratives positively influence
consumers' purchasing intentions toward sustainable products.

The Mediating Role of Environmental Awareness
The mediation analysis confirms that Environmental Awareness significantly
mediates the relationship between Digital Storytelling and Sustainable Consumption
Intention, thereby supporting H4. This finding indicates that digital storytelling influences
sustainable consumption intention both directly and indirectly through increasing
consumers' environmental awareness. The result supports the Stimulus–Organism–
Response (SOR) Theory , in which Digital Storytelling  functions as the stimulus (S) ,
Environmental Awareness represents the organism (O), and Sustainable Consumption
Intention serves as the response (R). In other words, engaging digital narratives first shape
consumers' internal psychological awareness before encouraging sustainable purchasing
intentions. Therefore, environmental awareness acts as a key psychological mechanism
linking digital storytellin g to sustainable consumption behavior among Generation Z
consumers on Shopee.

CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

This study examined the relationships among Digital Storytelling, Environmental
Awareness, and Sustainable Consumption Intention  among Generation Z Shopee users
in Palembang City. The findings demonstrate that Digital Storytelling has a positive and
significant effect on Environmental Awareness, indicating that engaging digital narratives
can effectively increase consumers' awareness of environmental issues. In addition,
Environmental Awareness positively influences Sustainable Consumption Intention ,
