 The growth of digital food delivery services has made it easier for consumers
to seek information and make purchasing decisions. One key source of
information is customer reviews—accounts of previous consumers'
experiences with a product. This study aims to examine the influence of
customer reviews on food purchasing decisions via ShopeeFood and to analyze
this influence through the lens of consumer behavior within the framework of
the Sharia economy. A qualitative approach utilizing the Systematic
Literature Review (SLR) method was employed. Data were gathered by
searching for scholarly articles on Google Scholar covering the period from
2021 to 2026. The selection process yielded 15 articles, which were analyzed
by comparing findings, identifying similarities and differences, and
synthesizing research patterns. The study reveals that customer reviews
influence purchasing decisions and serve as a vital information source for
consumers evaluating food options. Key aspects considered include the
content, clarity, volume, and credibility of the reviews, alongside other factors
such as promotions, price, ratings, food quality, and service. From a Sharia
economic perspective, the use of customer reviews should incorporate
considerations of maslahah and halal status to ensure that purchasing
decisions yield benefits and avoid harm. Thus, while customer reviews assist
consumers in making food choices, they must be utilized critically and in
accordance with Sharia principles.

INTRODUCTION
Digital media is now closely integrated into various aspects of daily life, including how
people meet their everyday needs. Technological advancements enable consumers to
engage in consumption activities through a variety of digital services. The convenience
offered by these services not only makes the ordering process more practical but also
shapes consumer habits regarding product selection and consumption  (Lahagu &
Lahagu, 2024) . Consumer behavior relates to how individuals or groups select,
purchase, use, and evaluate products or services to satisfy their needs (Kotler & Keller,
2012). In the context of digital food ordering services, this behavior is evident in how
consumers search for information and decide which food to purchase. These changes
demonstrate that technological progress influences how people meet their food-related
needs (Aristantia et al., 2025).
Technological advancements have made online food purchasing increasingly easy.
Consumers can browse options, view available information, compare choices, and
place orders via apps without needing to visit the vendor's location. E -commerce
platforms such as ShopeeFood are among the services that facilitate more convenient
consumption. Before making a purchase, consumers may consider factors such as
price, promotions, food variety, app usability, and their overall experience wi th the
service (Resfin et al., 2025). The purchasing behavior of online service users is linked to
considerations such as price, ease of use, customer reviews, food options, and
promotions (Perangin-angin et al. , 2024) . This indicates that the information and
features available within these apps assist consumers in making their purchasing
decisions.

Customer reviews serve as a valuable source of information for consumers prior to
making a purchase. Experiences shared by previous buyers provide insight into the
product, which is particularly helpful for consumers who have not yet tried it. Such
information helps consumers compare product attributes with their own expectations
and needs before making a choice . Shah et al. (2022)found that customer reviews on
digital service applications serve as a factor influencing consumer engagement and
driving repurchase intentions. Agustiningsih and Hartati (2023) also demonstrated
that customer reviews influence purchasing decisions regarding online services. These
findings indicate that customer reviews provide additional information and serve as a
basis for consumer consideration when choosing to use online services.
Consumers make purchasing decisions after weighing various pieces of information
deemed relevant to their needs. When purchasing via digital services, consumers
consider factors that influence their decisions notably customer reviews, which have a
significant impact on purchasing choices. Kotler and Keller (2012) explain that the
purchasing decision process involves five stages: need recognition, information search,
evaluation of alternatives, the purchase deci sion, and post-purchase behavior. These
stages illustrate that consumers engage in a deliberative process before making a
choice, particularly during the information search and alternative evaluation phases.
The use of digital services is also linked to consumer behavior, including tendencies
regarding service usage and order placement  (Nurwani & Muslichah, 2022) . This
indicates that consumer decisions to use digital services are shaped by various
considerations prior to the actual purchase.
Previous research has examined customer reviews as a source of information for
consumers purchasing through ShopeeFood. Experiences shared by buyers help
prospective customers gain insight intoproducts before making their selections. Azaria
and Sagala  (2022) examine the influence of customer reviews on food purchasing
decisions at ShopeeFood . Similarly, Agustiningsih and Hartati  (2023)found that
reviews and ratings assist consumers in evaluating food choices. These findings
demonstrate that the experiences of previous buyers serve as a valuable reference for
consumers before they make a purchase. Two previous studies indicate that customer
reviews can be a fa ctor consumers consider when making a purchase decision.
Previous studies have predominantly focused on the relationship or influence of
customer reviews on purchasing decisions, whereas discussions integrating these
findings within t he perspective of Sharia -compliant consumer behavior remain
limited. Furthermore, the relationship between customer reviews and the principles of
maslahah and halal  status in food selection has not been specifically addressed to a
significant extent. Given this context, this study formulates two research questions: (1)
how do customer reviews influence purchasing decisions via ShopeeFood based on
prior research findi ngs? and (2) how can purchasing decisions via ShopeeFood  in
relation to customer reviews be understood from the perspective of Sharia compliant
consumer behavior?

METHOD
This study employs a Systematic Literature Review (SLR) with a qualitative approach
to examine findings from previous research regarding the influence of customer
reviews on purchasing decisions via ShopeeFood, subsequently interpreting them

through the lens of consumer behavior within the Sharia economy. This approach is
utilized to analyze various prior studies addressing customer reviews, purchasing
decisions, consumer behavior, and Sharia economic principles. The qualitative
approach withi n the SLR framework enables the researcher to examine multiple
existing studies, then systematically compare and synthesize relevant findings
(Kuswandy et al., 2024). The researcher collected scholarly articles via Google Scholar,
covering the publication period of 2021 –2026. Searches were conducted using the
keywords: ShopeeFood, customer review, purchasing decision, Muslim consumer
behavior, maslahah, and halal. The retrieved articles were then screened for relevance
to the research topic and objectives.
Data were collected using a documentation technique, recording details such as the
author and year, research objectives, methodology, subjects or respondents, and key
findings from each relevant article. Data analysis was conducted qualitatively by
comparing and linking research results to identify similarities, differences, and
emerging patterns. The analysis results are presented in the form of a synthesis table
and a narrative description. The researchers then linked these findings to the principles
of maslahah and halal  to examine benefits, needs, potential harm, and the food's
compliance with Sharia requirements in the context of purchasing decisions.

decisions on ShopeeFood in Jakarta. Similar results were reported by Agustiningsih
and Hartati (2023), who found that customer reviews influence food product purchase
decisions on ShopeeFood in the Special Region of Yogyakarta. Ambarwati et al. (2023)
also found that customer reviews have a positive and significant impact on purchase
decisions for "Mie Nyinyir" via ShopeeFood. Latief and Mokhtar  (2026) obtained
similar results among ShopeeFood users in Makassar City. These findings demonstrate

Purchasing Decisions via ShopeeFood from a Sharia Economic Behavioral
Perspective
The consumer behavior perspective within Sharia economics views purchasing
decisions as a process not solely based on desire, but one that also considers needs,
benefits, affordability, and potential risks. Consumers may use customer reviews as
initial information, subsequently comparing them against their personal circumstances
and needs before selecting food. The principle of maslahah guides consumers to choose
products that provide benefits and meet their needs, while avoiding consumption that
could caus e harm (Hamdi, 2022) . Information regarding product shortcomings can
also prompt consumers to exercise greater caution regarding choices that might lead
to loss. Thus, consumer behavior is reflected not only in the act of reading customer
reviews but also in how consumers inter pret, compare, and weigh that information
against the needs and benefits they seek to obtain.
Muslim consumers must als o heed the principles of halal  and tayyib when selecting
food. Surah Al-Ma’idah verse 88, instructs Muslims to consume what is halal and good.
This provision indicates that Muslim consumer behavior is not limited to
considerations of taste and appea l but extends to the product's halal  status, quality,
and safety. Customer reviews can provide preliminary information regarding
ingredients, hygiene, and the experiences of other consumers. However, customer
reviews cannot serve as the sole basis for verifying a product's halal* status; consumers
must still verify halal information through reliable sources. Halal status is a key factor
in the decision-making process for Muslim consumers when selecting food  products
(Lativia et al., 2022). Such considerations demonstrate that Muslim consumers evaluate
potential purchases by aligning product information with their deeply held values and
religious tenets.
Consumers also need to exercise caution when encountering conflicting customer
reviews or unclear product information. The Hadith of the Prophet Muhammad
(PBUH) regarding matter s that are halal, haram, and syubhat teaches Muslims to
abstain from matters that are ambiguous or doubtful. This principle applies when
consumers encounter unverified information, particularly regarding food ingredients

CONCLUSION
A review of 15 articles indicates that customer reviews influence purchasing decisions
made via digital services, specifically ShopeeFood. Customer reviews serve as a source
of information and a factor for consumers to consider prior to making a purchase.
Aspects such as the content, clarity, volume, and credibility of reviews are taken into
account, although purchasing decisions are also linked to other factors —including
promotions, pricing, ratings, food quality, and service.
From a Sharia economic perspective, the use of customer reviews is viewed as part of
the consumer process of seeking and evaluating information before making a
purchase. Consumers can utilize reviews to understand a product's benefits and
drawbacks, subsequently aligning this information with their own needs and financial
means. The principle of maslahah guides consumers to select food that offers benefits
while avoiding choices that could cause harm. Muslim consumers must also consider
the halal and tayyib  nature of the food. When encountering unclear or questionable
information, consumers should seek further details before making a decision.
Therefore, while customer reviews can assist consumers in making food choices, their
use should be accompanied by con siderations regarding maslahah, halal and tayyib
standards, personal needs, purchasing power, and moderation in consumption.
