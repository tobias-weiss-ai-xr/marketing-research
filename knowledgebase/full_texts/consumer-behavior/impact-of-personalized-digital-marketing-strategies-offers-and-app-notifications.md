ABSTRACT:
The rapid growth of quick commerce has changed the way consumers purchase groceries and daily essentials by providing fast and convenient delivery through
mobile applications. Digital marketing strategies such as personalized offers, discount alerts, and app notifications are increasingly used by quick commerce
platforms to attract and influence consumers. This study focuses on the impact of personalized digital marketing strategies, particularly offers and app
notifications,onthepurchasedecisionsofGenerationZconsumersinAndhraPradesh.
The main purpose of this study is to understand how personalized offers and app notifications influence consumers' decisions to make purchases through quick
commerce applications. The study considers Generation Z consumers as the target group and examines their responses to different types of digital marketing
strategies, including personalized discounts, promotional offers, reminder notifications, and product-related alerts. The study also aims to identify which type of
personalizedstrategyhasagreaterinfluenceonpurchasedecisions.
The research will use primary data collected from Generation Z consumers in Andhra Pradesh through a structured questionnaire. The collected data will be
analysedtounderstandconsumerpreferences,responses,andpurchasingbehaviour.

RESEARCHMETHODOLOGY
ResearchDesign
The study follows a descriptive and analytical research design based on a cross-sectional quantitative survey. Primary data were collected once from
respondentsusingastructuredquestionnaire.
Population,SampleandDataCollection
ThepopulationconsistsofGenerationZconsumersinAndhraPradeshwhousequickcommerceapplications.Anon-probabilityconveniencesampling
method was used, and the questionnaire was circulated online through Google Forms. Responses were received between 23 August 2026 and 29
September 2026. A total of 72 responses were received. All 72 responses are used for the respondent profile, and 70 respondents with complete
purchase decision scores are used for the chi-square and ANOVA tests. 70 respondents with complete scores on all three scales are used for the
regressionanalysis.
ResearchInstrument
The questionnaire has two sections. Section A collects the respondent profile and usage pattern (age group, gender, district, occupation, quick
commerceappsused,frequencyanddurationofuse,productspurchased,monthlyspendingandmainreasonforuse).SectionBcontains20statements
measuredonafive-pointLikertscale(1=Stronglydisagree,5=Stronglyagree),groupedintothreeconstructs:
• PersonalizedOffers(PO):6statementsonattention,relevance,discounts,coupons,recommendationsandfrequencyofappuse.
• AppNotifications(AN):6statementsonnoticing,attention,motivationtoopentheapp,promotionalmessages,relevanceandurgency.
• Purchase Decision (PD): 8 statements on the influence of offers and notifications on purchase decisions, purchase intention, unplanned
purchasesandoverallinfluence.
DataPreparationandStatisticalTools
A score for eachconstruct was calculated as the mean of its statements, so every score lies between 1 and5. Reliabilitywas checked withCronbach's
alpha.Forthechi-squaretest,respondentswereclassifiedbytheirPDscoreasLowinfluence(below2.5)orModerate/Highinfluence(2.5andabove);
thistwo-level classificationwas used so thatexpected cell countsremainedadequateina smallsample. To avoidvery smallgroups, agegroups 24-26

5.3 Scope for Further Research
• The sample is small (N = 72; 70 complete cases) and was collected by convenience sampling, so the findings cannot be generalised to all
