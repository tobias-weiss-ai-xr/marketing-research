Abstract
In the fast -paced digital era, time flexibility is an important factor influencing consumer behavior in online
shopping. This study aims to determine the effect of time flexibility on shopping enjoyment and customer visits to
e-commerce platforms. This study uses the Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling approach and the
SOR (Stimulus -Organism-Response) theory to explain the relationships among variables. Data were collected
using a snowball sampling technique. The results show that time flexib ility significantly increases shopping
enjoyment and the frequency of customer visits to e -commerce sites. Users tend to use their free time flexibly to
explore and search for products, which ultimately increases their engagement with the platform. In addition, a high
preference for using mobile devices also strengthens the relationship between time flexibility and online shopping
behavior. These findings recommend optimizing mobile features and personalizing the user experience to
encourage increased shopping activity and sustained customer loyalty.
Keywords: flexible time, shopping enjoyment, consumer visits
1. Introduction
The presence of e-commerce platforms has become an integral part of people's daily lives. With
increasing internet penetration and smartphone adoption, consumers have found it increasingly
easy to visit various e -commerce sites. However, several factors al so reduce consumer
satisfaction with e-commerce sites, such as high user traffic. This can lead to slow access speeds
or even website downtime. Other issues, such as inefficient shipping processes, can result in
delays in delivery. According to a study (Srinivasan, Rutz, & Pauwels, 2015) , Factors
influencing consumer visits to e -commerce sites are the quality of website navigation, page
loading speed, and ease of product search. Customer visits are also closely related to promotions
or discounts offered by the platform, which motivate consumers to visit the platform regularly
(Sari, Indriastuti, & Rahmawati, 2025).
One increasingly visible trend in modern consumer behavior is the increase in free time spent
accessing digital platforms outside of work hours or other primary activities. This free time is
often used by consumers to browse e-commerce sites. With easy internet access, they feel more
relaxed and can spend more time exploring products and making purchases. Research shows
that the time consumers spend reading product information on e -commerce sites has a
significant influence on purchasing decisions. Research shows that more flexible access times
and navigation speed can improve user experience and reduce the negative impact of high
latency (Berkery & Morley, 2020) . Thus, flexible free time allows consumers to visit e -

Besides free time, shopping enjoyment is a key factor driving consumers to visit e -commerce

shopping experience and encourage more visits and transactions.
2. Literature Review
2. 1. Stimulus-Organism-Response Theory (SOR Theory)
The SOR model is a theoretical framework used to understand how external stimuli (S)
influence an individual's internal state or organism (O), ultimately eliciting a specific response
(R). Research (Chan et al., 2017)  On consumer visit resonance on online platforms was
conducted by examining external factors such as website features, marketing strategies, and
product availability, as well as internal factors such as hedonic motivation, impulsivity, and
informational social influence. The situational factor of consumers having free time becomes a
stimulus for consumers' affective and cognitive perceptions, which refers to the buyer's
cognitive and affective emotional state, encompassing the processes of thinking, feeling , and
perception, referred to as the "organism." (Do, Shih, & Ha, 2020).
Organismal factors refer to customers' emotional, affective, and cognitive states, and how real-
time flexibility influences consumer visit behavior on e-commerce platforms (Gao, Chen, Gao,
& Yu, 2022) Affective reactions are emotional responses that arise during interactions with the
surrounding environment, while cognitive reactions are the mental processes of analyzing
information related to a product.
2.2 Flexible Time
Time flexibility in the context of digital marketing and e-commerce refers to consumers' ability
to organize and choose the most suitable time for them to shop, access information, or interact
with brands online. Time flexibility is one of the key factors that increases consumer satisfaction
on e-commerce platforms, because it gives them greater control over their shopping decisions
(Chan et al., 2017) . Consumers tend to prefer digital platforms that allow them to make
purchases at their preferred time, for example, in the evening after work or in between other
activities (Jin & Oriaku, 2013) .(Mukherjee, Yun, & Gershoff, 2025)  emphasized that in the
digital space, time flexibility encourages the formation of individual strategies in managing

2.3 Shopping Enjoyment
A study (Wang & Lee, 2020)  stated that perceived enjoyment is the result of the interaction
between a person's experience and their surrounding environment, leading to a sense of
satisfaction or happiness. The subjective experience of perceived enjoyment is pleasant and
satisfying, and generates positive reinforcement that can strengthen the desire to make
impulsive purchases for self -gratification. Empirical studies consistently show that increased
consumer satisfaction with online shopping is positively correlated with their purch ase
intentions and the development of favorable consumer attitudes (Punuindoong, Syah, &
Anindita, 2020) and (Wen, Prybutok, & Xu, 2011). Consumer behavior can be influenced by
both subjective enjoyment and the emotional components of perceived enjoyment. One
example is how customer satisfaction can modify or enhance the correlation between current
engagement levels and impulse buying tendencies.
H3: The higher the shopping enjoyment, the higher the customer visit rate.
2.4 Customer Visit
In the context of e-commerce, a customer visit can be defined as a visit made by a customer to
a company's digital platform or website to explore the products or services offered. These visits
can be non -transactional, such as browsing products or reading reviews, or transactional,
involving a purchase or other interaction relevant to the buying and selling process (Kepa,
Castander, Rebón, & Alzua-sorzabal, 2015).
With technological advancements, customer interactions with e-commerce have also undergone
significant changes. One recent innovation is the integration of mobile technology and social
media, which allows customers to access e -commerce websites or applicat ions anytime and
anywhere. Mobile commerce and the presence of multiple devices have increased flexibility
and convenience for customers in accessing products or services online (Kandampully &
Zhang, 2015). In the context of this research, the increase in customer visits is examined using
the SOR Model, Web Usage Behavior Theory, and Online Purchase Pathways. The use of the
Stimulus-Organism-Response (SOR) model in the context of increasing customer visits in e-
commerce helps understand how various factors can stimulate customer visits, change their
perceptions and motivations, and ultimately encourage them to visit e-commerce sites.  Figure

3. Research Method
The research was conducted quantitatively, with 119 data points collected from g -forms
distributed via WhatsApp using a snowball sampling technique. The data were screened. The
research was conducted in Indonesia, using the snowball sampling technique. Dat a analysis
used SEM-PLS, due to the data range of 100–200 (Memon et al., 2020). Measurement using a
Likert scale from 1, very dislike to 5, very like.
Flexible time is defined as consumer behavior to shop on e -commerce platforms without time
constraints, supported by indicators of freedom to choose time, availability of free time outside
of work hours, convenience, and personal control over time (Effendi, Akbar, and Murni, 2023;
Moussaoui et al., 2023). Shopping Enjoyment is defined as the pleasure that drives consumers
to shop on e -commerce platforms, expressed by user visits to the e -commerce site within a
specific period, supported by indicators of urg ency to buy, emotional urges, discounts and
promotions, and personal online shopping preferences (Moussaoui et al., 2026) . Visiting
Customer is a user's visit to an e-commerce site within a certain period, with indicators of visit
frequency, visit time, visit duration, and user interaction (Sychov & Bakaev, 2020).

In this research, flexing time serves as a stimulus that prompts consumers to make a customer
visit. When consumers engage directly in shopping platforms, such as chatting with streamers
or discussing products, flexible time stimulates cognitive and emotio nal responses (Do et al.,
2020). Shopping enjoyment, in turn, represents the emotional and cognitive state of consumers
in response to this stimulus. It arises from the enjoyable experience during interaction,
signifying a heightened level of engagement and emotional attachment to the p roducts or
services (Kuciapski, 2020)  and (Lee, Gan, & Liew, 2023). Customer visits, as a response, occur
when consumers, influenced by the stimulus of flexible time and the organism of shopping
enjoyment, make purchase decisions without thorough consideration, driven by emotional
impulses (Marzuki, Abdullah, Bahri, & Kamal, 2016) . This illustrates how the Stimulus -
Organism-Response theory elucidates the connection between flexible time, shopping
enjoyment, and customer visits in e-commerce platforms (Moreno, Fabre, & Pasco, 2022).
5. Conclusion
The contribution of this research is to analyse the relationship between flexible time and
customer visits when consumers view on e-commerce platforms. Customer visit behaviour will
increase because consumers feel enjoyment when deciding to shop, unconscio usly this
behaviour is influenced by S -O-R Stimuli-Organism-Response by influencing the cognitive -
affective - emotional strength of consumers who visit.
The results show that shopping enjoyment can bridge the gap that occurs between flexible time
and customer visit behaviour due to the pleasure of watching the e-commerce platform and the
feeling of happiness when interested in the items on the platform.
It is hoped that businesses in the future will be able to create and attract more visitors by creating
more attractive platforms, better products, and affordable prices, making them feel addicted and
needing to keep visiting the e-commerce platform even if they don't buy anything.
This research has limitations: 52% of participants consistently access the platform, and 44% do
so daily. Next time, this could be done for male customers and specifically for a specific
generation, for example, only Generation Z.
Business implications could focus on improving the quality of platforms and making customers
not only visit the platforms but also increasing visitor traffic, as well as customers feeling
enjoyment when opening the platform, and sales must be more creative  so that customers also
