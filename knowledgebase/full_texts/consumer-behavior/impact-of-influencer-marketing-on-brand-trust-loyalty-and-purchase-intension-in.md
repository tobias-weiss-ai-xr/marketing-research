ABSTRACT
This research is aimed at elucidating the effects of influencer marketing on brand trust, brand loyalty as well as purchase intention
within the fashion industry. Social media influencers are significant in the contemporary digital world in influencing con sumer
decisions, particularly in young consumers. This research aims to examine how influencer credibility and authenticity affect
consumer perceptions and their buying behavior. It is a primary-data-based study, which uses a structured questionnaire to collect
information on 172 respondents, primarily students and young social media users. Statistical tools including percentage analysis,
chi-square test, ANOVA and correlation were used to analyze the data. The results indicate that the influence of influen cer
marketing on brand trust is strong whereas its effect on brand loyalty is moderate and requires real exposure to influencer
promotions. Another important finding was that the credibility and authenticity of the influencer have a positive impact on brand
trust. Nonetheless, a good number of consumers are still neutral, which means that some degree of doubt remains. The study
concludes that influencer marketing works, and brands should pay attention to authenticity and trust to enhance consumer loyalty
and purchase intention.

Keywords: Influencer Marketing, Brand Trust, Brand Loyalty, Purchase Intention, Influencer Credibility, Influencer
Authenticity, Consumer Behavior, Fashion Industry

INTRODUCTION
The fashion industry has undergone a significant change in the mode of communication that the brands have with the consumers.
Traditional advertising like newspapers, magazines and television were the primary modes of advertisement used by companies
to advertise their products in the past. Nevertheless, as digital platforms and social media have been developing at a high pace,
marketing approaches have undergone a transformation. Influencer marketing is one of the strongest instruments of fashion brands
to reach their target customer group today. Influencers with a significant online presence and a following base of followers are
relevant to form consumer opinions and behaviors. Influencers can now be closer and more personal with their audience on
platforms such as Instagram and Tik Tok, making marketing more personal and captivating (Zahran and Aljuhmani, 2025;
Maryati and Utami, 2023).
A shift in one -way communication to two -way communication is one of the major factors contributing to the emergence of
influencer marketing. Influencer content is more natural, relatable, and interactive as opposed to traditional advertisements . In
this age, consumers want to be entertained and authentic with content that is not only informative but also entertaining. The
content made by influencers is based on their personal style and experiences which makes the followers feel closer to them. This
association builds a feeling of trust and familiarity and consumers are more likely to accept product recommendations. This has
prompted brands to be increasingly concerned with selecting influencers that align with their brand image and values, as opposed
to merely spending money on mass advertisements (Azzouzi & Naoui, 2025).
One of the most significant influencer marketing aspects is considered to be trust. The consumers will trust the influencer a nd
thus are likely to trust the products or brands which are being promoted by the influencer. Honesty, expertise and consistency in
content are some of the factors that contribute to this trust. When an influencer is perceived as learned and authentic, thei r
suggestions are perceived as personal suggestions and not as advertisements. It has been found that credibility of the source and
experience are the most powerful aspects of source credibility, which directly influences the perception of the consumers towards
a brand (Low et al., 2025; Sutera, 2023). Thus, influencer marketing is a key to trust that should be developed by the brands that
would like to establish a strong and positive image in the market.
The other significant influencer marketing aspect is authenticity. Today consumers are very sensitive to promotion strategies and
can easily recognize counterfeit or over commercial content. True influencers, with truthful opinions and actual experience, are

more effective in shaping consumer behaviour. Being transparent, including stating sponsored content explicitly, can also be used
to ensure credibility. By being authentic and loyal to their values and personality, the influencers enhance the connection w ith
followers and make their recommendations more effective. This is the reason why brands are paying more attention to long-term
partnership with influencers who will truly match their identity (Malik et al., 2025).
Brand trust is another aspect that is highly influenced by influencer marketing and it is relevant in consumer decision making. As
an influencer advertises a brand on a regular basis and in a positive manner, it assists in creating trust in the consumers. Such
trust minimizes the doubts and boosts confidence in the products of the brand. Research indicates that brand trust tends to serve
as an intermediary between influencer marketing and consumer behavior. Simply put, influencer marketing establishes first a
trust, and then positive results (brand loyalty and purchase intention) are achieved through this trust (Low et al., 2025; Malik et
al., 2025). Even the most popular influencers may not be successful in influencing consumer decisions without trust.
Another valuable consequence of influencer marketing is brand loyalty. When a consumer has confidence in a brand and they
have positive experiences with the brand, they will tend to stick with it and purchase again with the same brand. The influencers
assist in augmenting this devotion by constantly interacting with their fans and marketing the brand in a constant way. Not only
do the loyal customers purchase the brand repeatedly, they also refer others to purchase the brand and this enhances the brand
value even more. Studies indicate that the influencer-based emotional bonds formed by influencer content can greatly enhance
customer loyalty in the fashion sector (Zahran and Aljuhmani, 2025).
Besides trust and loyalty, purchase intention is also directly influenced by influencer marketing. Social media content is extremely
influential in purchasing decisions of many consumers and particularly among young consumers such as Gen Z. Urgent or excited
buying can be motivated by the influence of recommendations, as it generates a sense of urgency or excitement. In addition, upon
the consumers observing the products in real life scenarios being used by the influencers,  it heightens the desire and interest to
purchase the same products. Research has discovered that influencer marketing can have a powerful impact on online shopping
and motivate consumers to visit and buy fashion products (Jabbi et al., 2025; Abdullah et al., 2025).
In spite of its increased significance, influencer marketing has some challenges. The difficulty of preserving authenticity a nd
trust, as time goes by, is one significant challenge. When influencers advertise excessive products or do deceptive advertising, it
will have an adverse influence on their credibility. Also, not every influencer campaign is effective, since its success will depend
on such factors as the suitability of the audience, the quality of the content, and the level of engagement.  This emphasizes the
importance of planning and making the right choice of influencers with the aim of getting the intended outcomes (Azzouzi and
Naoui, 2025).
The study aims at gaining insights into the effects of influencer marketing on brand trust, brand loyalty and purchase intention in
the fashion industry. Other important aspects discussed in the study include the credibility and authenticity of the influen cers,
which are also in the research framework and questionnaire. Through examining these relationships, the study will give a clea r
insight into the dynamics of influencer marketing and how it can be maximized by fashion brands. On the whole, the study is an
addition to the emerging body of research in digital marketing as it shows the psychological and behavioral reactions of consumers
to influencer-based promotions.

LITERATURE REVIEW
According to Khan (2025), Instagram influencers have a very influential  role in influencing consumer buying behavior in the
fashion industry. The research indicates that the influencer assists in raising brand awareness and also attach an emotional bond
on the consumer, which ultimately translates to an increase in purchase intention. Micro and mid-level influencers are also found
to be more effective since people consider them more relatable and trustworthy. The study indicates that audience alignment,
level of engagement as well as content authenticity are highly crucial in shaping consumer behavior. On the whole, the research
demonstrates undoubtedly that influencer marketing can enhance customer relationships and boost sales in the fashion industry
in case it is utilized correctly.
Malik et al. (2025) pay attention to the s ignificance of influencer features in the luxury fashion sector and how they influence
consumer purchasing behavior. The paper concludes that credibility and in particular trustworthiness and experience is a
significant factor in purchase decisions. It goes on to elaborate that usefulness in content assists in creating trust and interaction
among the consumers. Although it is true that attractiveness of the influencers is important, it is not that significant when there is
no ideal fit between the influence r and the audience. The study concludes that to enhance customer loyalty and engagement,
brands must select influencers wisely by emphasizing authenticity and audience fit.
The article by Hamedani et al. (2025) focuses on the influence of influencer credibility on the consumer purchasing behaviour in
the fashion sector. The study shows that all aspects of credibility such as attractiveness, expertise, trustworthiness, and u seful
content have a positive impact on consumer behavior. Interestingly, the study also concludes that demographic factors, such as
age, gender, and income, do not have a significant impact on the effect of these factors. This implies that influencer marketing is
applicable in various categories of persons in a similar manner. The study h ighlights that having credible influencers is very
important for increasing engagement and driving purchase intention.
George et al. (2025) thoroughly review the available literature on the area of influencer marketing in the fashion industry. The
researchers determine that the three factors that are most significant and can affect purchase intention are trust, credibility, and
attractiveness. It further clarifies that theories such as the source credibility theory are usually employed in learning con sumer
behavior in this discipline. The study indicates the existing gaps in the comprehension of influencer marketing in various contexts
and indicates that further research should be conducted. On the whole, the research demonstrates that trust construction via
influencers is a crucial factor to enhance the purchase intention.
Romdonny and Maulana (2025) analyze the impact of influencer marketing on customer loyalty, especially on TikTok in the
fashion industry. The research concludes that the influencer marketing, as well as the promotions and campaigns offered through
social media, positively impact customer loyalty. It also shows that a large part of customer loyalty is attributed to these factors
in conjunction with other factors. The study has demonstrated the need to develop interesting and novel content to sustain interest
and loyalty of customers in a competitive environment.

Srivastava (2025) concentrates on influencer marketing -brand trust connection. It indicates that credibility, authenticity, and
engagement of influencers have a strong impact on consumer perceptions towards a brand. It also elaborates the fact that
transparency and alignment to brand values contribute towards development of trust. Nevertheless, when influencers advertise
too many products or are not authentic, it might lead to a decline in trust. The research paper finds that influencer marketing must
be employed in a wise and strategic manner to establish better and more credible relationships with consumers.
Nair (2024) investigates t he influence of influencers in the fashion industry on the purchase intention. As pointed out in the
research, authenticity, similarity (homophily), and parasocial relationships are some of the factors that affect consumer decisions.
It demonstrates that consumers usually have a sense of personal connection towards influencers, and it makes them more likely
to buy the products that they are recommended. This paper highlights that emotional attachment between influencers and
followers is significant in influencing the purchase behavior.
Deepika and Pranev (2024) examine the success of influencer marketing in various sectors such as fashion. The research
concludes that influencer marketing has a considerable influence on consumer purchasing behavior. It also brings to the fore the
importance of authenticity and credibility in the process of developing consumer trust and loyalty. The study recommends that
brands ought to consider the use of authentic influencers to enhance marketing success.
In their study, Mukherjee and Mukherjee (2025) discuss the impact of influencer marketing on consumer buying behavior in the
online space. The analysis reveals that many of the consumers are persuaded by the influencer recommendations, particularly in
the area of fashion and beauty. It emphasizes that trust, transparency, authenticity, and quality of the content are highly considered
in the development of consumer attitudes. The study also advises consumers to critically assess influencer content before making
decisions, as the effects may not be exactly the same on all consumers.
Kumar (2024) investigates the role of Instagram influencers in affecting consumer behavior through a well-known fashion brand.
The research finds that influencer marketing improves consumer perception and increases brand loyalty. It also demonstrates that
exposure to influencer content has a positive impact on purchase intention. The present study offers evidence to the concept that
social media influencers can be very influential when promoting a brand.
Yesi and Irdawati (2025) discuss the role of brand trust as a mediating variable between the effect of influencer marketing a nd
purchase decisions. The researchers conclude that influencer marketing is not directly related to purchase intention but operates
by creating brand trust. Trust that is built will have a great impact on consumer purchasing. This study shows that trust is vital in
translating the influence effect into real purchases.
The study by Iqbal et al. (2024) focuses on the impact of influ encer credibility and professionalism on quality perception and
purchase intention. The paper concludes that influencer attributes enhance the perception of the consumers about the quality of
the product thereby increasing purchase intention. It also demonstrates that the degree of customer trust mediates this relationship.
This implies that greater trust results in more purchase decisions.
Safare et al. (2025) discuss the effects of influencer marketing on consumer attitudes and engagement. The paper concludes that
trust and purchase intention are strongly predicted by credibility and authenticity. It further reveals that micro-influencers tend to
be more engaging than the celebrity influencers are. The study indicates that more emphasis should be on real r elationships by
the brands as opposed to the number of followers.
The article by Zahran and Aljuhmani (2025) considers the importance of influencer marketing in establishing brand loyalty by
engaging customers. The study concludes that influencer marketing enhances the level of engagement, which subsequently boosts
brand loyalty. It also emphasizes that the most engaged customers are more loyal. This research proposes that emotional bonding
and matching of values are critical in long-term customer relations.
Rahmadi (2023) discusses influencer strategies that can be used to restore brand trust and loyalty. The research concludes that
authenticity has the greatest influence of consumer trust. It also demonstrates that influencer strategies can enhance the trust and
devotion to the existing brands. This study shows the value of authentic and sincere communication in influencer marketing.

Research Gaps
Despite numerous studies on influencer marketing in the fashion industry, the significant gaps remain to be filled. The available
literature primarily concentrates on the influence of the influencer on the purchase intention directly but fails to articula te how
this influence occurs particularly by using brand trust as a mediator. Also, there is a lack of research on the synergistic impact of
influencer credibility and authenticity despite the fact that the two elements in combination have a strong impact o n consumer
perception in practice. It also lacks integrated research that examines brand trust, brand loyalty and purchase intention as a single
research to understand the entire consumer decision making process. Further, a significant portion of the research is carried out
on international grounds with less emphasis on emerging markets such as India where the consumer behavior can vary. The
second gap is that little use is made of structured measurement models which utilize all the key variables as a whole as is the case
with the questionnaire which is utilized in the present study. Hence, the research will help address these gaps by offering a more
detailed and interconnected view of the impact of influencer marketing on consumer behavior within the fashion industry.
HYPOTHESES OF THE STUDY

● H1: Influencer marketing has a significant impact on brand trust.
● H2: Influencer marketing has a significant impact on brand loyalty.
● H3: Influencer credibility and authenticity significantly influence brand trust.

RESEARCH OBJECTIVES
1. To examine the impact of influencer marketing on brand trust, brand loyalty and purchase intention.
2. To study the role of influencer credibility and authenticity in building brand trust in fashion industry.
3. To explore the effect of influencer type (Micro vs. Macro) & trustworthiness on brand trust, loyalty and purchase intension.
4. To analyze variations in influencer impact across Gen Z and Millennial segments.

RESEARCH METHODOLOGY
This section explains the method used to conduct the research on the impact of influencer marketing on brand trust, brand loyalty,
and purchase intention in the fashion industry. It explains the method of data collection, the nature of research design employed
as well as the analysis conducted. This assists in making a clear and systematic understanding of the study.
Research Design
For this study, a descriptive research design was used. Descriptive research is applicable in the study of the current opinio ns,
attitudes and behavior of  consumers. It assisted in this study in the investigation of the impact of influencer marketing on
consumer trust, consumer loyalty, and consumer purchase intention within the fashion sector.
Data Collection
The study used both primary and secondary data sources.
Primary Data: Primary data were gathered with the help of a structured questionnaire via Google Forms. The questionnaire
contained close-ended questions and the questions based on the 5 -point Likert scale. It concentrated on influencer credibility,
authenticity, brand trust, brand loyalty and purchase intention.
Secondary Data: The secondary data were gathered through research papers, journals, articles, websites, and other past studies
on influencer marketing and consumer behavior in the fashion industry. This contributed to developing a robust theoretical
foundation of the research.
Sampling
• Sample Size: 172 respondents
• Target Group: Fashion influencer followers (Gen Z and Millennials) using social media.
• Sampling Method: The convenience sampling method was adopted as it is easy and assists in gathering data within the
available respondents.
• Sampling Area: The research targets primarily the consumers in urban locations.
Tools of Analysis
The collected data was analyzed using Microsoft Excel and basic statistical tools.
The following methods were used:
• Frequency Analysis – to get demographic information about age, gender and income.
• Percentage Analysis – to analyze consumer responses and compare different opinions
• Chi-Square Test – to test the correlation between the nominal variables like influencer type and consumer reactions.
• ANOVA (Analysis of Variance) – used to compare variations in the responses of the various groups, including age groups
or income groups.
• Correlation Analysis – to examine the relationship between variables such as influencer credibility, brand trust, brand loyalty
and purchase intention.
• Charts and Graphs – to effectively present data in a clear and visual way.
These tools helped in presenting the data in a simple and understandable way.
Variables of the Study
• Independent Variables: Influencer credibility, influencer authenticity, influencer type (micro/macro).
• Dependent Variables: Brand trust, brand loyalty and purchase intention.
Limitations of the Study
• The study is based on convenience sampling, so it may not represent all consumers
• The study mainly focuses on urban respondents
• Responses are based on personal opinions, so there may be some bias
• Time and resource limitations may affect the depth of analysis
Ethical Considerations
• Participation in the survey was completely voluntary
• Respondents were informed about the purpose of the study
• Privacy and confidentiality of the respondents were maintained
• The data collected was used only for academic purposes
Summary
The research design employed was a descriptive research design to examine the effects of influencer marketing on brand trust,
brand loyalty as well as purchase intention in the fashion industry. Both primary and secondary data were used. The sample of
150 respondents was chosen on the basis of the convenience sampling. Data was to be analyzed in excel by applying frequency,
percentage and descriptive analysis techniques in order to understand consumer behavior well.

ANALYSIS AND DISCUSSION
The general discussion of the research indicates that the effect of influencer marketing on consumer behavior in the fashion
industry is obvious, and the influence is not always very significant in all situations. The demographic findings indicate that the
majority of the respondents are young (primarily 1924 years), so they are more active on social media and more exposed to the
contents of influencers. Many people (78.5% of the respondents) are followers of fashion influencers and approximately 69.8%
have recently encountered influencer promotion. This is a clear indication that influencer marketing reaches out to young
consumers to a great extent and is an influential factor in their opinion forming.
Influencer credibility and authenticity have ambivalent outcomes when we examine them. Most of the respondents concur that
influencers are educated and to some extent reliable, but still, the number of the neutral responses is very high. It implies that the
consumers have not been convinced completely and still have reservations about influencers. Likewise, the authenticity also has
middle-range outcomes, with some individuals thinking that influencers are authentic, whereas others think that content is staged
or promotional in some cases. It shows that even though the influence of influencers is present, consumers are becoming more
conscious and cautious as they trust them.
The brand trust and loyalty results indicate that both exist but are not strong. Num erous respondents replied neutrally when
questioned about their trusting of brands and their reliance implying that the aspect of trust is still in process. The loyalty is also
having mixed outcomes- some consumers willing to repurchase and recommend brands, some are not attached to any brand. This
is further supported by results of hypothesis testing. As an illustration, influencer marketing influences brand trust strong ly in
most instances, but brand loyalty, the outcome is inconsistent. In particular, it  can be observed that the influence of influencer
promotions is significantly more influential than simple following influencers.
Lastly, the correlation analysis indicates that the influencer credibility and authenticity are positively and significantly correlated
with brand trust (r = 0.237, p = 0.002). It implies that influencers can be viewed as authentic and credible, and in such situations,
consumers tend to believe in the brand that they endorse. In general, the research points to the fact that influ encer marketing is
effective, but its effectiveness is determined by the level of authenticity and credibility that the influencer possesses. Th e real
collaboration is what brands need to pay more attention to and not only the promotional content to establish more trust and loyalty
in consumers.

CONCLUSION
In conclusion, it is evident through this study that influencer marketing has a significant role in influencing the consumer behavior
in the fashion industry particularly in the youth who are deeply engaged in social media. The results indicate that although many
of the respondents follow influencers and are exposed to influencer promotions, trust, loyalty, and purchase intention are moderate
instead of very high. Credibility and authenticity of the influencer were identified to be some of the most significant factors, with
consumers more inclined to trust a brand when the influencer seems authentic, knowledgeable, and honest. Meanwhile, the study
also reveals that consumers are increasingly becoming more conscious and wary and that is why a lot of the responses turn out to
be neutral which means that they are hesitant or doubtful. The hypothesis test also shows that influencer marketing does play an
important role in brand trust, but not entirely in brand loyalty, and only partially based on the actual exposure to influencer content
and not merely on following influencers. Besides, the fact that the influencer authenticity, credibility, and brand trust are
positively and significantly related proves that trust is established with the help of authentic and relatable content. On the whole,
the research findings are that influencer marketing is an effective marketing tool, however, its effectiveness relies on its
authenticity and strategic implementation. To enh ance consumer trust, create loyal and purchase intention in the competitive
fashion market, brands must build long-term relationships with credible influencers and generate honest and engaging content.
