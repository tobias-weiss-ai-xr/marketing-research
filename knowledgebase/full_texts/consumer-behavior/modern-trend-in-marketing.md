# Modern Trend in Marketing

Abstract
Marketing trends have evolved over the last few years owing to many factors, such as
advancement in technology, consumer behavior, and competition level in the
international business environment. Present­day marketing trends are characterized by
digital marketing practices, information management process, social networking, and
personalization of customer experiences. Firms adopt practices like social media
marketing, email marketing, content marketing, and collaborations with influencers to
interact with customers and maintain their engagement in the products/services offered
by the organization. According to Kotler & Keller (2020), inclusion of technology in the
marketing process helps firms identify consumer preferences and develop brand loyalty.
Apart from that, concepts such as green marketing, ethical marketing, and mobile
marketing are significant marketing trends in the contemporary world.
Keywords: Consumer, Digital, Collaboration, Technology, Brand, Convenience,
Significance
Introduction
For as long as there have been businesses, marketing has played a key role in their
functioning. It was designed to promote goods and services and thus satisfy the needs of
consumers. But today, because of technological changes and changes in consumer
behavior as well as global competition, the nature of marketing has undergone
significant transformation (Kotler & Keller, 2020).
Among other things, one of the biggest transformations in marketing has been its
transition to digital marketing. Thanks to the Internet, social media and other
technological changes, it became possible for businesses to interact with consumers
from all over the world on the Internet. As a result, it is much easier for firms to get
feedback and design campaigns targeting specific consumer groups. Digital marketing
has become the main marketing technique used by businesses in order to raise brand
awareness and increase sales (Chaffey & Ellis­Chadwick, 2019).

Modern Trend in Marketing
Yet another marketing strategy gaining popularity is data­driven marketing. Nowadays,
more and more firms rely on the analytics in order to learn about consumers' behavior,
preferences and purchase patterns. Through analyzing large amounts of data, firms may
get a lot of valuable insights related to the development of products, prices, promotions
and distribution of goods. Not only does it make firms' work more efficient but also
decreases risks connected to the trial­and­error approach.
Finally, social media marketing strategy can be seen as one of the most effective ones
nowadays. Using popular platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, Twitter or LinkedIn
firms are able to communicate with consumers and create a positive image of the brand
and its products. Influencers, viral marketing campaigns and user­generated content are
some examples of the marketing strategies implemented via social media.
Moreover, personalization has also turned into an important marketing trend in modern
conditions. Today, consumers expect individualized treatment and services from the
companies, which means that they need personalized recommendations, advertisements
and promotions. It can be achieved through the usage of data analytics, artificial
intelligence technologies and CRM systems.
Besides, modern marketing is concerned about ethics and sustainability. Green
marketing, responsible advertising, and corporate social responsibility campaigns gain
relevance, as more and more consumers are willing to buy products of socially and
environmentally responsible companies. The result is the creation of a good company
image and attracting a loyal consumer base.
Mobile marketing and omnichannel marketing strategies are being developed, as the use
of smartphones and mobile applications increases dramatically nowadays. The business
is able to contact the customer anytime and anywhere using omnichannel marketing
strategy.
Modern marketing trends are very complex and multi­faceted. There are several
tendencies of modern marketing, which influence the effectiveness of marketing
activities positively. These trends are the use of digital platforms, data­driven approach,
active use of social media, personalization of marketing campaigns, ethics, and
omnichannel marketing.

not adequate enough in meeting the requirements and expectations of consumers. The
adoption of modern marketing trends in the form of digital marketing, data­driven
methods, social media marketing, personalization and ethics among others is required
but in a manner that is cost­effective and measurable. Most firms find it difficult to

Literature Review
Over the last few decades, marketing has undergone a rapid development, switching
from old practices like print ads and personal selling to new technologies­based
approaches. As stressed by Kotler & Keller (2020), current marketing is concerned with
more efficient satisfying consumer needs via the use of the Internet and data analysis.
The reason for the emergence of such marketing is changing consumer behavior,
technological development, and global competition.
The digital marketing trend is among the most relevant ones in modern business. In the
era of widespread use of the Internet and mobile technology, businesses have an
opportunity to target global consumers quickly and effectively. As explained by Chaffey
& Ellis­Chadwick (2019), digital marketing involves such strategies as SEO, pay­per­
click advertising, and email campaigns, enabling businesses to interact with customers
more efficiently than before.
Social media marketing has been another innovation that has significantly changed the
ways of communication between businesses and their customers. Platforms like
Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and LinkedIn offer businesses an opportunity to establish
brand loyalty, two­way communication, and successful product promotion. As noted by
Tuten and Solomon (2020), social media marketing helps to create user­generated
content and use influencers to increase the coverage of a brand.
Another vital trend that focuses on the usage of consumer data in making business
decisions and strategies is data­driven marketing. Businesses nowadays take advantage
of big data to analyze their customers' behavior and make strategic decisions. Kumar et
al. (2019) state that companies that apply data­driven approach to their work

demonstrate higher efficiency, better targeting, and lower marketing expenses.
There has been a lot of focus on personalization in marketing over the past few years.
There has been an increase in the need of people to be offered personalized content in
the form of emails, recommendation of products and advertisements based on their
surfing and purchasing habits. Personalization makes customers happy and improves
retention and engagement, as explained by Smith & Chaffey (2020).
Content marketing is the trend where valuable and consistent content is created to attract
a specific set of people. Pulizzi (2019) explains that with content marketing a business
can establish its brand authority and educate its consumers and also improve conversion
rates. Videos, blogs, infographics, and podcasts are examples of content used in
marketing today.
Influencer marketing is a trend through which businesses connect with niche markets
through collaborations with social media influencers. As explained by Freberg (2020), it
builds trust and credibility while also reaching target consumers better than any other
form of advertisement.
Ethical and sustainable marketing has also become quite popular as consumers have
increased consciousness regarding various social and environmental concerns and like
companies that reflect responsibility. As stated by Kotler et al. (2019), green marketing,
social responsibility initiatives of firms, and ethical marketing activities help to develop
brand image and appeal to socially conscious customers.
Mobile marketing is one of the essential components of current trends in marketing. The
increasing usage of smartphones and applications allows organizations to approach
consumers regardless of time and place. There are several techniques associated with
mobile marketing including application marketing, SMS marketing, location­based
marketing, and push notifications (Wedel & Kamakura, 2018).
Omnichannel marketing incorporates several channels including online and offline to
provide a unified customer experience. Companies that engage in omnichannel
marketing ensure consistency of their messages through social media, e­commerce
websites, physical outlets and customer support services. According to Verhoef, Kannan,
and Inman (2017), omnichannel marketing creates satisfaction and loyalty among
customers through convenient and consistent communication of brands.
Another emerging trend in the field of marketing is automation. Automation allows
companies to automate several tasks and save time while increasing efficiency.
Automated marketing tools perform several activities such as management of marketing
campaigns, audience segmentation, sending personalized messages and results
monitoring automatically. Chaffey (2020) highlights that marketing automation not only
saves time but increases effectiveness of marketing activities.

Finally, another modern marketing trend that plays an important role in the future of

Analysis and Discussion
From the trends in modern marketing, it becomes evident how crucial digital marketing
is in ensuring business success. Businesses that have leveraged digital marketing
through the use of websites, social media and email marketing have been able to reach a
larger audience easily. This has been instrumental in making them more visible and
boosting their sales.
Marketing using social media has become very effective because businesses can use it
easily to communicate with their clients and receive instant feedback. As a result of
analyzing the data above, it has been found out that businesses using social media enjoy
more customer loyalty and visibility.
Data­driven marketing has been very useful in helping companies make the right
decisions since they help provide data that informs companies on how to price, market
and position their products.
Personalization plays an important role in contemporary marketing. As a result of
creating personalized ads, recommendations, and offers according to consumer needs,
companies are able to increase customer satisfaction and increase repeat purchases.
Personalization is effective for creating emotions among companies and their
consumers.
A content marketing strategy demonstrates its efficiency when it comes to acquiring
customers and keeping them interested. Companies that create quality information or
entertaining content may be perceived by their target audience as authorities and become
more trusted. Posts, videos, infographics, and podcasts are popular ways to make people
more involved in the process of interaction.
Influencer marketing demonstrates its efficiency as a strategy for targeting niche
audiences. Collaboration with influencers having many followers enables companies to
become credible and reach more people. Engagement from influencer campaigns is
reported to be higher than from advertisements.
Ethical and sustainable marketing is a growing area of concern in contemporary
businesses. The customers favor those companies which are socially responsible and
adopt sustainable marketing policies. Companies using such values in their marketing
campaign improve their reputation and target socially­responsible customers.

Mobile marketing is an essential area in today’s technology­driven world. Companies
use mobile applications, SMS marketing, push marketing, and location­based marketing
to reach their customers easily. It is found from analysis that mobile marketing improves
engagement and customer retention.
Omnichannel marketing makes sure of a cohesive experience of the customers on
various channels. Companies can integrate offline and online channels to offer consistent
interaction with their customers.
Marketing automation allows businesses to automate repetitive activities like email
marketing campaigns, segmenting customers, and social media postings. Marketing
automation allows one to save time, make fewer mistakes, and be efficient, so that
marketing professionals can spend more time thinking about strategy and creating
content.
Artificial intelligence and machine learning allow marketers to change modern
approaches. Through analyzing vast amounts of data, artificial intelligence tools predict
the behavior of clients, personalize user experience, and improve marketing campaigns.
Using artificial intelligence technologies helps companies make quick data­based
decisions which increase the effectiveness and return on investment.
In conclusion, it has been shown that modern marketing trends positively affect the
business performance. Companies which use the approaches of digital marketing,
analytics, social media, personalization, ethics, and artificial intelligence enjoy
competitive advantages. Nevertheless, some challenges include scarcity of resources,
insufficient knowledge, and fast changes of technologies.
Conclusion
Trends in marketing have affected the way companies communicate with their clients.
Transitioning from traditional marketing to digital marketing allows firms to increase the
audience and make wise decisions. Marketing trends such as digital marketing, social
marketing, and content marketing are getting more vital in the business world. Through
data marketing, businesses can study the behavior and purchase history of clients.
Organizations can determine the prices, positioning of products, and promotion thanks to
analysis. Customer satisfaction and loyalty result in the repetition purchases through
personalization due to the information received. Ethics and sustainability of marketing
become increasingly popular in the current competitive environment. People want to
communicate with responsible and ecological organizations, which are open about their
business. Thus, companies employ ethics and sustainability of marketing to improve
their image and appeal to socially oriented customers. These tools assist firms in making
their operations smoother, connecting with the right clients, predicting outcomes, and
