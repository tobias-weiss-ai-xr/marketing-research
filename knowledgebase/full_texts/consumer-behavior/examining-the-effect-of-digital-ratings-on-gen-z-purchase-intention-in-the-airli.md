Abstract

Digital platforms have transformed the way consumers evaluate products and services, making digital
ratings a critical source of information that shapes purchasing decisions. Despite the growing
importance of online ratings, limited research has examined their effects on Gen Z purchase intention
in the airline industry, particularly through the mediating role of perceived ease of use. This study aims
to examine the effect of digital ratings on purchase intention among Gen Z consumers and to assess
the mediating role of perceived ease of use. A quantitative cross-sectional survey was conducted
involving 120 respondents who had previously searched for airline information through digital
platforms. Data was analyzed using PLS-SEM with SmartPLS 4. The results indicate that digital ratings
significantly and positively influence both perceived ease of use and purchase intention. Perceived
ease of use has a significant positive effect on purchase intention and mediates the relationship
between digital ratings and purchase intention. These findings suggest that digital ratings facilitate
consumers’ information processing activities and increase their willingness to purchase airline
services. This study extends theory of TAM by highlighting the role o f digital ratings in shaping
consumer behavior within the airline industry. The findings provide insights for airline companies
seeking to enhance customer engagement and purchase intention through effective management of
digital rating systems and online platforms.

Keywords: Digital Ratings, Purchase Intention, Perceived Ease of Use, Gen Z, Airline Industry

INTRODUCTION
Development of Digital technology has fundamentally transformed consumer decision -
making processes across various industries, including in the airline industry. The existence of online
platforms, mobile applications, travel booking websites, and social media channels has enabled
consumers to access amounts of information before making purchasing decisions (Selem et al.,
2023). Digital ratings have emerged as one of the most influential factors affecting consumer
behavior. Digital ratings represented by numerical scores, star ratings, or aggregated customer
evaluations that provide consumers with indicators of product or service quality based on the
experiences of previous users (Nazifi et al., 2022). Digital ratings are also able to shape consumer
perceptions and purchase decisions. Digital ratings in airline industry play a particularly critical role
due to the intangible nature of airline services. Unlike physical products, airline services cannot be
evaluated before consumption. Evaluation can be done after services gained by customers.
Potential customers often rely on information provided by previous passengers to reduce
uncertainty and perceived risk. Online travel agencies, airline websites, and review platforms such
as TripAdvisor, Google Reviews provide rating systems that allow consumers to assess service
quality, comfort, safety, and customer satisfaction.

Digital ratings serve as informational cues that help consumers compare airlines and make
purchasing decisions (Wongyai et al., 2024). Gen Z, generally defined as individuals born between
the mid-1990s and early 2010s, represents the first generation to grow up entirely within a digital
environment. They were characterized by levels of digital literacy, extensive internet usage, and
strong dependence on online information when making purchasing decisions (Ruangkanjanases et
al., 2022). Gen Z consumers literally trust reviews, ratings, and social media recommendations
before purchasing products or services and digital ratings are one of the crucial determinants of
their decision making. Gen z gains greater purchasing power and becomes a dominant consumer
segment in the travel market, understanding the factors influencing their purchase intention
become essential for airline companies.
Purchase intention refers to an individual’s willingness to purchase a product or service in
the future and recognized as a strong predictor of actual purchasing behavior (Palmieri et al., 2023).
Identifying the mechanisms through which digital ratings influence purchase intention can
therefore provide valuable insights for airline managers seeking to enhance customer acquisition
and competitiveness in digital marketplace. The airline industry must be able to attract and retain
Gen Z customers. Digital ratings positively influence consumer purchase intention. Previous
literature focused on direct relationships between online ratings and consumer behavioral
intentions, often overlooking the role of technology related perceptions that may explain how
digital ratings affect purchasing decisions (Ahn & Lee, 2024). There is a need to explore mediating
variables that can provide a more comprehensive understanding of consumer behavior in digital
environments. Perceived ease of use as mediator in this research. Derived from the Technology
Acceptance Model (TAM), perceived ease of use refers to the degree to which an individual believes
that using a particular system, technology, or platform requires minimal effort (Ly & Ly, 2023).
Consumers may interact with airline websites, mobile applications, booking platforms, and online
review systems. The ease with which consumers can access, interpret, and utilize digital ratings may
significantly influence the effectiveness of these ratings in shaping purchase intentions (Ruswanti
et al., 2020).
Perceived ease of use has been identified as determinant of technology adoption and online
consumer behavior (Mousa et al., 2020). Consumers perceived digital platforms as easy to use, they
absolutely engage with available information, process online reviews efficiently, and develop
positive attitudes toward purchasing. Perceived ease of use may function as an important
mechanism through which digital ratings translate into stronger purchase intentions. Digital
consumers can quickly assess service quality without analyzing lengthy reviews or searching for
additional information (Ngo et al., 2023). Digital ratings may enhance consumers’ perceptions of
platform usability and convenience and finally increase confidence in the purchasing process and
strengthen purchase intentions. Despite the growing significance of digital ratings in online
consumer behavior, empirical studies examining the mediating role of perceived ease of use remain
relatively limited (Habib et al., 2024), particularly within the airline industry. Most previous research
has focused on e-commerce, hospitality, and retail sectors, while the airline context has received
comparatively less attention. This study specifically examines that Gen Z consumers are still scarce,
despite their increasing importance in the global travel market. This gap in the literature highlights
the need for further investigation into the factors influencing airline purchase intention among
digitally native consumers.
This research seeks to address these research gaps by examining the effect of digital ratings
on Gen Z purchase intention in the airline industry while investigating the mediating role of
perceived ease of use. By exploring the Technology Acceptance Model, this research offers a more
comprehensive framework for understanding consumer decision making in digital airline services.
This study contributes to literature in digital marketing and offers practical implications for airline
managers and digital platform developers seeking to optimize online customer experiences and
enhance conversion rates.

LITERATURE REVIEW AND HYPOTHESIS FORMULATION
Digital Ratings
Digital ratings refer to numerical scores or star ratings provided by consumers on digital
platforms based on their experiences with a product or service. Digital ratings have become a crucial
form of electronic word-of-mouth that helps potential customers assess the quality and reliability
of products and services before making purchasing decisions (Gómez-Déniz et al., 2024). Digital
ratings provide valuable information regarding service quality, punctuality, safety, customer
service, and all consumers travel experience in airline industry. Ruangkanjanases et al., (2022)
explain consumers are highly dependent on digital information sources, frequently use digital
ratings to reduce uncertainty and evaluate available alternatives, Gen Z focus on this type of
consumers. The research done by Agus et al., (2020) found that positive digital ratings can enhance
consumer trust and confidence, making them more likely to consider and purchase airline services.
Digital ratings serve as an important informational cue that influences consumers' perceptions and
behavioral intentions.
Perceived Ease of Use
Perceived Ease of Use (PEOU) is a core construct of the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)
developed by Fred Davis (Zaineldeen et al., 2020). It refers to the degree to which an individual
believes that using a particular system or obtaining information requires minimal effort. Perceived
ease of use reflects consumers’ perceptions regarding how easily they can access, understand, and
utilize information during the decision-making process. Digital ratings can improve perceived ease
of use by evaluations in a simple and accessible format, allowing consumers to quickly compare
alternatives without searching for information. According to Ngo et al., (2023) that Gen Z
consumers value convenience and efficiency in online environments, higher perceived ease of use
can facilitate information processing and improve their overall experience when searching for
airline services.
Purchase Intention
Purchase intention refers to a consumer’s willingness to purchase a particular product or
service in the future (Sobiech-Grabka et al., 2022). Purchasing behavior influenced by various
cognitive, emotional, and informational factors. According to Tarkang et al., (2022) that purchase
intention in airline industry reflects to consumers’ readiness to select and buy airline tickets based
on their evaluation of available information and perceived value. Previous studies done by Yang et
al., (2020) have demonstrated that consumers are more likely to develop purchase intentions when
they perceive information as trustworthy, useful, and easy to understand. Digital ratings play a
significant role in shaping purchase intentions because they provide social proof and reduce
perceived risk associated with service purchases (Jadil et al., 2022). Consumers will perceive the
process of obtaining and evaluating information as easy, they are more likely to develop favorable
attitudes and stronger intentions to purchase airline services. Thus, purchase intention serves as
the key outcome variable in this study, reflecting the effectiveness of digital ratings and perceived
ease of use in influencing consumer decision-making.

ratings represent consumers’ evaluations of products or services and are expressed through
numerical scores or star-based systems on digital platforms. Consumers tend to rely on online
evaluations when making purchase decisions, particularly when direct experience with a product
or service is limited. Digital ratings in airline industry provide potential customers with valuable

H1: Digital Rating effect Purchase Intention
Perceived Ease of Use (PEOU) is a key construct in the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)
and refers to the extent to which individuals believe that using a particular system or information
source requires minimal effort. Digital ratings simplify the information search process by providing
evaluations from previous customers. Gen Z consumers prefer quick and convenient access to
information. Digital ratings can significantly improve the ease of obtaining and processing
information related to airline services (Ahn & Lee, 2024). Digital ratings become more transparent
and user-friendly, and consumers are likely to perceive the decision-making process as easier and
more convenient (Zaineldeen et al., 2020).
H2: Digital Rating effect Perceived Ease of Use
The Technology Acceptance Model suggests that individuals are more likely to adopt and
utilize information systems when they perceive them as easy to use. Consumers who experience
convenience and simplicity during the information-search and evaluation process are more likely
to develop favorable attitudes and purchase intentions. When consumers perceive airline-related
information as easy to access, understand, and evaluate, they become more confident in making
purchase decisions. Previous studies done by Krishnan & Koshy, (2021); Habib et al., (2024)
demonstrated that perceived ease of use positively affects behavioral intentions across various
digital environments, including e-commerce, online booking platforms, and travel-related services.
Consumers who perceive digital platforms as easy to use are expected to exhibit stronger purchase
intentions toward airline services (Galindo-Domínguez & Bezanilla, 2021).
H3: Perceived Ease of Use effect Purchase Intention
Digital ratings can directly influence consumers’ purchase intentions, their impact may also
occur indirectly through perceived ease of use (Jain, 2020; Winanti & Fernando, 2024). Digital
ratings provide organized and readily available information that helps consumers evaluate
alternatives more efficiently. The Technology Acceptance Model perceived ease of use serves as an
important mechanism that explains how external information such as digital ratings influence
behavioral intentions. Perceived ease of use mediates the relationship between digital ratings and
purchase intention (Jaiswal et al., 2021) among Gen Z consumers in the airline industry.

in the airline industry. This study focused on Gen Z consumers who had previously searched for
airline-related information through digital platforms such as airline websites, online travel agencies,
social media, and review platforms. A purposive sampling technique was used to select respondents
who met the criteria of being between 18 to 30 years and having experience using digital platforms
to obtain airline information. Data was collected through an online questionnaire distributed via
digital channels, resulting in 120 valid responses. All constructs were measured using multiple items
adapted from previous studies and assessed on a five-point Likert scale ranging from 1 (“Strongly

determination (R²), effect size (f²), predictive relevance (Q²), and mediation effects. The mediating
role of Perceived Ease of Use was examined through the significance of the indirect effect between
Digital Ratings and Purchase Intention.

  The findings reveal that Digital Rating has a positive and significant effect on Purchase
Intention among Generation Z consumers in the airline industry. This result indicates that
consumers increasingly rely on online ratings and evaluations when making purchasing decisions.
Positive digital ratings serve as a signal of service quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction,
thereby reducing uncertainty and increasing consumers’ confidence in purchasing airline services.
This finding is consistent with signaling theory, which suggests that consumers use external
information cues to evaluate products and services before making purchase decisions. The result
also supports previous studies that identified digital ratings as an important determinant of online
consumer behavior, particularly among digitally native generations who actively seek information
through online platforms before making purchases.

The results show that Digital Rating significantly influences Perceived Ease of Use (PEOU),
while PEOU positively affects Purchase Intention. These findings suggest that PEOU plays an
important mediating role in explaining how Digital Ratings influence con sumers’ purchasing
decisions. When airline services receive favorable ratings, consumers tend to perceive the booking
platform and service process as easier to understand and use. This positive perception of ease of
use enhances their intention to purchase. This result is consistent with the Technology Acceptance
Model (TAM), which emphasizes that perceived ease of use is a critical factor influencing users’
behavioral intentions. Therefore, the influence of Digital Ratings on Purchase Intention occurs not
only directly but also indirectly through consumers’ perceptions of ease of use, highlighting the
importance of user-friendly digital platforms in strengthening the effectiveness of online ratings in
attracting Generation Z consumers.
CONCLUSION
Digital Ratings have a positive and significant effect on Purchase Intention among Gen Z
consumers in the airline industry. Consumers tend to have a stronger intention to purchase airline
services when they perceive digital ratings as favorable and reliable. Digital Ratings significantly
influence Perceived Ease of Use (PEOU) and suggest that positive ratings help consumers perceive
airline booking platforms as easier to use. PEOU was found to have a positive and significant effect
on Purchase Intention and plays a significant mediating role in the relationship between Digital
Ratings and Purchase Intention. This finding demonstrates that Digital Ratings not only directly
increase consumers’ purchase intentions but also indirectly enhances purchase intention s by
improving perceptions of ease of use. PEOU serves as an important mechanism through which
Digital Ratings influence consumer decision-making. Airline companies should focus on maintaining
positive digital ratings by encouraging customer reviews and improving service quality. Airline
industry should continuously enhance the usability, simplicity, and convenience of their digital
platforms to strengthen consumers’ perceptions of ease of use. Future research is encouraged to
examine other mediating variables, such as trust, perceived value, or customer satisfaction, and to
expand the study to different industries and consumer segments to improve the generalizability of
this finding.
