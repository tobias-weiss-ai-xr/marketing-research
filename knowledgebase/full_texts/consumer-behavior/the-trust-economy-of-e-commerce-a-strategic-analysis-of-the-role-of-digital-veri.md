Abstract
The aim of this study is to examine the influence of the trust economy in e-commerce in regard to how
the verification of the online transaction using technology, as well as user reviews, affect purchase
decisions and increase consumer trust in the Iraqi e nvironment. In the present research, a descriptive -
analytical method is used. Data collection has been done using a questionnaire with 15 questions based
on a five-point Likert scale divided into two main dimensions. The validity of the instrument has been
verified using face and content validity, while reliability has been determined by means of Cronbach's
Alpha with high consistency. It has been found that trust in e-commerce is rather high, with mean value
of 4.06. The differences have been observed in t he influence of variables, where the variable of user
review was the most influential for purchase decision (mean=4.16), compared with digital verification
mechanism (mean=3.97). It has also been found out that the role of social trust in consumer behavior is
much more important compared to the technical trust and Iraqi consumers use other people's experience
and opinions while making their decisions.
Keywords: Trust Economy, E-commerce, Digital Verification, User Reviews, Purchase Decisions
Introduction
  During the last decades, there has been tremendous development of information and communication
technologies. They have led to the emergence of various new economic activities, especially e -
commerce, which became one of the key features of the digital economy. This type of commerce offers
a broad range of possibilities to facilitate trading and transactions, to eliminate the geographical barriers
and cut down costs, thus increasing the efficiency and competitiveness of the market (Katana, 2009, p.
11) .
However, despite all the advantages of e-commerce, its mass popularity and development are impeded
by the lack of consumer trust in it. This problem is primarily caused by the lack of physical interaction
between the participants of transaction, what leads to the concern about the security of the transmitted
information, credibility of the seller and quality of the products or services. Trust economics turned out
to be the modern scientific paradigm that explains the consumer behavior in the digital world a nd
focuses on the reduction of uncertainties and strengthening of consumer's trust .
Among the most significant aspects that affect consumer's decision and trust there are digital
certification systems that ensure the security of transactions and identification of users and the reviews
of users that represent their actual experience and co ntain valuable information to take into account
while making a purchasing decision and reduce the perceived risk. The combination of all these aspects
makes up the core of trust in e-commerce and affects its sustainability and development. Therefore, the
purpose of this study is to investigate the role of the trust economy in e -commerce through the impact

Research problem   :
  It is obvious that there has been an incredible growth in the sphere of e -commerce over the recent
years. However, such an expansion comes across one of the major barriers, namely the lack of consumer
trust, especially in the context of developing countries like Iraq, where there are issues with information
security, the reliability of payment systems, and the credibility of sellers. Besides, the lack of personal
communication between the participants of the deal makes consumers uncertain about their actions.
Digital verification and users' opinions as technological instruments are known; however, the role of
these elements in the development of trust and making purchasing decisions still needs a thorough
examination. Therefore, this research problem can be stated as follows: How do digital verification and
user opinions affect the development of consumer trust and purchasing decisions in e-commerce in the
context of Iraq ?
Importance of the research:
  The current investigation touches upon an urgent issue – the economy of trust in e -commerce. Trust
is considered to be a crucial factor that determines the success and sustainable development of economic
activity in digital sphere, especially taking into a ccount the fast transition to the use of electronic
marketplaces for buying and selling goods. The current research expands knowledge about the
interaction between digital verification techniques and users' evaluations and their effect on consumers'
trust in the context of e-commerce. The practical significance of the investigation results is highlighted
by the possibility for companies to take into consideration the outcomes of the study when developing
marketing campaigns and increasing consumer trust in order to boost sales and gain a competitive
advantage. At the same time, the contribution of the research to the economic aspect is reflected in the
description of the function of trust in stimulating e-commerce transactions and economic development
in gen eral, especially in developing countries like Iraq. The social value of the research lies in the
analysis of the behavior of digital consumers and their vulnerability in terms of other people's
experiences and opinions. Moreover, the local value of the study is determined by the lack of practical
researches in this field   .

Chapter One : Theoretical Framework of the Research
Section One: The Nature of Trust Economy in E-commerce
  The fast pace of technological development has resulted in significant changes in the organization of
economic activity, where e-commerce became an important component of the modern digital economy.
E-commerce is characterized by the predominant use of electronic means of communication to conduct
transactions. However, such a transformation has faced many difficulties, one of which is the lack of
direct physical interaction between the parties. Thus, there arises the need to develop trust as the most
basic requirement for the success and stability of such transactions. Trust in the digital environment has
become more than the psychological concept, since it is now a valuable resource that defines consumer
behavior and their willingness to use the digital pla tform. The current research aims to describe the
concept of trust economy in the framework of e-commerce. It will be done by describing its nature and
multiple aspects, followed by analyzing the features of e -commerce and their impact on the
development of trust .

First requirement: The concept of the trust economy and its dimensions in the digital environment
A trust-based economy refers to the recent concept that became relevant in relation to the transition
towards the digital economy especially during the development of e -commerce transactions involving
indirect communication between the transacting parties.  As a result, trust becomes an essential
requirement for a transaction. For consumers involved in transactions in the digital space, there are high
levels of uncertainty because it is impossible for them to physically examine the goods and ascertain
the credibility of the seller. Uncertainty leads to reliance on other factors including the reputation of the
platform, the information that is provided and security tools. As a result, trust becomes an important
economic consideration in purchasing decisions (Katana, 2009, p. 11). In this context, trust is not just a
psychological value, but also an economic resource contributing to transaction cost reductions. High
levels of trust minimize the need for additional research and verification, speed up the decision making
process and increase the chances of transaction completion resulting in increased efficiency of the
digital market (Al-Ta'i, 2010, p. 16). It is also possible to make a connection between trust and the legal
regime of e-commerce. Legal regulations, consumer protection and the assurance that consumers have
their rights protected in case of disputes play an important role in the  establishment of trust and
engagement in this type of transactions (Mohammed, 2007, p. 92). It is especially important taking into
account the particularities of electronic agreements. The concept of trust in the digital environment may
be studied from the perspective of several interconnected dimensions. Specifically, the technological
dimension refers to the efficiency of the protection, encryption and data security measures, which serve
as the basis for the technical aspect of trust. The legal dimension refers to the existence of laws that
provide security and define duties and responsibilities. Economic dimension refers to the impact of trust
on the volume of transactions and their efficiency, while the behavioral and marketing dimensions refer
to the attitude of the customer towards the site, being prone to influences such as reputation and past
experience (Abdul Qader, 2002/2003, p. 65). Consumer protection in e -contracting represent s an
essential element of the trust economy since providing consumers with legal guarantees including
information, the right to withdraw from contract, and the right to receive compensation increases their
readiness to enter into transactions (Badr, 2008, p. 26). Thus, the trust economy may be regarded as an
integrated system where technical, legal, economic and behavioral aspects interact in order to overcome
uncertainties of e-transactions   .
The second requirement: The characteristics of e-commerce and its impact on building trust .
   E-commerce is characterized by a number of features that fundamentally distinguish it from
traditional commerce and directly impact the nature of trust-building within it. This type of commerce
operates in a virtual environment where direct interaction bet ween buyer and seller is absent, leading
consumers to rely heavily on the digital information presented. This increases the sensitivity of trust in
this environment, as any lack of information or ambiguity in the presentation can lead to a decline in
trust and consumer hesitation in making decisions (Ahmed, 2009, p. 128). Furthermore, the heavy
reliance on electronic payment systems is one of the most prominent characteristics of e -commerce.
While these systems offer speed and flexibility in completing transactions, they also raise concerns
regarding data security and the potential for fraud. Therefore, the effectiveness and security of these
systems are crucial factors in building trust, as a lack of trust in payment methods can lead consumers
to refrain from completing purchases even if all other conditions are met (Al-Ghadian, 2005, p. 10). On
the other hand, e-commerce is characterized by its ability to reduce operational costs for both businesses
and consumers by minimizing the need for intermediaries and physical infrastructure. However, this
advantage is only truly realized with sufficient trust, as consumers will not be attracted to low prices if
they doubt the product's quality or the seller's credibility. This statement illustrates that trust is a
mediator between economic traits and real outcomes (Al -Zuwaini, 2007, p. 50). Furthermore, e -

commerce traits affect organizational features, which demand developing a new approach of planning
and controlling based on digital technologies. As a result, trust becomes an essential trait not only in

possessing positive sides as high speed, lower costs, and openness, generate an environment that is very
sensitive to trust issues. It makes building trust an essential component of overcoming the balance of
risks and opportunities and sustainable development of this type of commerce   .
Section Two: Digital Verification Mechanisms and User Reviews and Their Role in Enhancing
Trust
In view of the increasing reliance and proliferation of e-commerce, trust building no longer only refers
to theoretical aspects or legislation but involves practical tools and methods that actively build trust
within the digital environment. One such pract ical tool includes digital verification methods aimed at
protecting the transaction process and confirming the identity of participants and user reviews that act
as one of the essential sources of information. All these aspects form the notion of applied digital trust,
which, as opposed to mere promises or legislation, is based on certain practical tools ensuring the
increase of consumers’ confidence. In this context, the current paper explores the role of digital
verification methods in the formation of el ectronic trust and the effect of user reviews on purchasing
decision-making and competitiveness .
First requirement: The role of digital verification mechanisms in building electronic trust
Digital verification mechanisms may be regarded as a cornerstone of trust construction in electronic
commerce. Digital verification mechanisms refer to methods used to guarantee transaction integrity and
verify identity of all the people involved in an ele ctronic transaction as there is no physical contact in
e-commerce that exists in traditional business transactions. When an electronic customer conducts a
transaction, there is a need to confirm that the system is safe and protected from any sort of hacking or
misuse. Therefore, digital verification mechanisms play an important role in reducing risks and
promoting electronic transactions. In essence, e -commerce cannot function without integrated
information systems requiring high levels of security. Any failure of the system leads to an instant loss
of trust, which negatively impacts further transactions conducted electronically (Dimesh, 2011, p. 35) .
Digital verification mechanisms include a range of instruments such as electronic signatures, two-factor
authentication, encryption systems, and security certificates. All these techniques aim at making a
transaction safe, secure, and valid. One of the most important elements among those mentioned above
is the electronic signature that gives the transaction official legal status and can be used to prove
something during disputes. This helps increase trust between parti cipants and adds stability to the
electronic contract. Two-factor authentication allows checking whether it is indeed the user who logs
into the system or makes payments and helps reduce risks of fraud and hacking. Thus, creation and
widespread adoption of  digital verification mechanisms help improve the reputation of electronic
platforms and attract more customers (Siraa, 2014, p. 20).  With regard to economics, digital verification

technologies are essential tools for improving the effectiveness of the e-commerce market. Firstly, they
ensure the reduction of fraud and manipulation, decrease of conflicts between parties and, thus,
minimize the cost of control and litigation. Secondly, they provide accurate data about the identity and
business background of sellers and enable customers to make more informed decisions. Thus, the digital
verification mechanisms bring more economic advantages to the business sphere in addition to security
as they improve the business environment and increase the trust of people to the digital marketplace .
In addition to the improvement of the business environment, the use of digital verification mechanisms
is helpful for the development of the institutional dimension of e-commerce since they signal platforms'
compliance with international standards in the f ield of cybersecurity and data protection and increase
their reputation in the eyes of users and provide them with a competitive advantage. Such organizations
will be able to attract customers as digital verification mechanisms provide safety for users and facilitate
the development of the relationship based on trust between platforms and customers .
The effectiveness of digital verification mechanisms is connected not only with their technical
characteristics but also with the ability of users to apply them correctly which requires certain level of
knowledge. Otherwise, even when there are security me chanisms available, a person may misapply
them and become a victim of fraud. Digital literacy is one of the key aspects of trust building as it
provides people with the opportunity to apply digital mechanisms properly and safely. In such a way,
digital verification mechanisms form the foundation for the creation of electronic trust  .
The second requirement: The impact of user reviews on shaping purchasing decisions and
competitive advantage
   User reviews have become one of the most critical tools for redefining the concept of trust in the
context of e -commerce. These reviews have become one of the key sources of information that
consumers refer to when they make a decision about buying something, especially when they lack any
experience with either the product itself or its seller. Users tend to seek the opinions and experiences of
people who used the products before them in order to eliminate uncertainty and understand how good
the product or its seller is. In such a way, user reviews become one of the key instruments for creating
social trust in the online space. Modern e-marketing makes extensive use of reviews in order to increase
credibility (Abu Rahma, 2009, p. 21) .
The main value of user reviews lies in the authenticity of these opinions and, consequently, high
credibility in comparison with the conventional advertising which tends to be biased. Positive reviews
increase consumers' trust and lead them to purchase products while negative ones decrease the interest
of buyers in the product or service. In such a way, user reviews serve as an efficient tool for controlling
the quality of market, forcing sellers to improve their products in order to maintain their reputati on
online. In such a manner, user reviews can be considered as a kind of unofficial control performed by
consumers (Al-Zuwaini, 2007, p. 50).
  User reviews help in reducing the problem of information asymmetry between the seller and the buyer
as these give consumers additional information not covered in the official description of products. This
information includes actual usability, specifications compliance, delivery speed, and after-sales service.
This promotes the process of decision -making and helps avoid the problems associated with post -
purchase regret. In addition to promoting trust between consumers and companies, the reviews help to
increase market efficiency through the transparency of information (Al-Siriti, 2009, p. 285) .
On the contrary, the positive reviews are extremely important for e -commerce companies since they
help to achieve a competitive advantage. Companies that have a high number of positive reviews will
be better capable of attracting new customers and retaining old ones. The reviews also help in creating
a good digital reputation that is one of the most important intangible resources in the digital economy.

It is the successful company that gathers reviews, analyzes them, and applies the insights obtained from
analysis to improve its product and service in order to increase market competitiveness (Ahmed, 2009,

In addition, reviews can influence consumer trends since they influence the brand image positively or
negatively depending on their number. Thus, the management of reviews becomes one of the important
components of any marketing strategy. This means that t he company will respond to customer's
feedbacks, resolve the complaints, and reinforce positive aspects, thus helping to build long -term
trustful and transparent relations with the customers (Mohammed, 2007, p. 92).
Therefore, user reviews represent one of the most important components of the trust economy in e -
commerce, because they provide an effective mechanism for building social trust, enhance market
transparency, and directly influence purchasing decisions. They  also give institutions the opportunity
to achieve a competitive advantage based on digital reputation and positive interaction with customers

Conclusion   :
E-commerce has emerged as an important part of today's economy, with the digital revolution taking
place at an accelerated pace. But the extent of consumer trust is the key to its success and sustainability.
The goal of this study was to examine the role of trust economy through the lens of digital verification
mechanisms and user reviews, which are one of the most visible factors that plays a role in the world
of online transactions and influences purchase decisions. The study revealed that trust in e -commerce
is not based on a single dimension, but rather is the product of an interaction between technological and
social factors. Digital verification mechanisms are part of the process of creating a secure environment,
and user reviews are a crucial part of uncertainty reduction, thereby creating more credibility. . The
results also showed that consumers in the Iraqi context tend to rely more heavily on the experiences of
others when making purchasing decisions, reflecting the importance of the social dimension in building
digital trust . Therefore, enhancing trust in e -commerce requires adopting integrated strategies that
combine developing security infrastructure and improving user interaction management, thus
contributing to achieving a balance between the technical and social aspects   .
Results
The study reached a number of conclusions, the most important of which are    :
1. There is a high level of trust in e -commerce in the Iraqi environment, where the overall average
reached (4.06    . )
2. Both digital verification mechanisms and user ratings have a significant impact on building
consumer trust   .
3. The impact of user reviews outweighs that of digital verification mechanisms in shaping purchasing
decisions   .
4. The Iraqi consumer relies heavily on the opinions and experiences of other users when making
purchasing decisions   .
5. There is a good level of awareness of the importance of digital security, although some concerns
regarding the entry of personal data persist   .
6. There is a positive relationship between the level of trust and the continued use of e -commerce
platforms   .
7. The digital reputation of platforms plays an important role in attracting customers and enhancing
their loyalty   .
Recommendations
In light of the study's findings, the following recommendations can be made    :
1. The need to strengthen security and digital verification systems in e-commerce platforms to increase
the level of trust among users   .
