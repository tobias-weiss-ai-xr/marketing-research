Abstract
This research examines marketing communication patterns in electronic commerce
(e-commerce) in the big data era that can influence customer online behavior. This
study employs the Information Adoption Model to explain the developed research
framework. Data were collected using a questionnaire distributed to 338 e -
marketplace users in Indonesia selected through a purposive sampling technique .
Data were analyzed using Structural Equation Modeling-Partial Least Square (SEM-
PLS) with a Path Analysis approach by SMART-PLS. This study indicates that
advertisements customized with information about incentives and delivered through
a quality platform are considered as appropriate information, encouraging customer
behavior to click on advertisements. This study recommends that marketers develop
customer communication strategies aligned with the era of big data. To gain positive
customers’ responses or benefit marketers in the big data era, an effective online advertising
strategy should utilize customer profile data and their product traces to create
customized offer information that is delivered  at the right time and benefit the
customers. There are many factors that need to be elaborated regarding online
advertising and consumer behavior. In addition, this study does not classify
customers based on ages, making it difficult to analyze customer behavior based on
generations.
Keywords: advertising, app design quality, incentive, personalization

1. INTRODUCTION
The development of the internet has now greatly influenced individual behavior,
including the behavior on online shopping activities. The tremendous increase in the number
of customers buying online and their spending have motivated many businesses to operate
online (Dhingra et al., 2020). This statement is supported by data released by Tech in Asia
2025 taken from the Ministry of Trade, which shows an increase in e -commerce users in
Indonesia over the past five years, from 2020 to 2024, as shown in the graph below:

Figure 1. Increase in the Number of E-Commerce Users in Indonesia
(Source: Ministry of Trade Data and Information Systems Center, 2024)

The concept of e -commerce that combines elasticity and precision adopts the role of
technology and has the potential to facilitate improvements in business operations (Omar &
Elmansori, 2021). One type of online platform that is more competitive, dynamic and widely
used by customers is the e -marketplace. The use of e-marketplace applications can enable
companies to collect customer profile data and records of product search activities which then
become big data. According to Andriani and Nugroho (2025), the most prominent advantage
of sales activities through online marketplaces is that companies obtain more complete and
accurate customer information so that it can be used to map patterns in designing advertising
strategies. The development of the intern et and digital technology has  made it easier for
retailers to collect information for consumer profiling, this is obtained from online users
increasingly being burdened with requests for personal  data when completing purchases
(Aiello et al., 2020). Many companies are now focusing on implementing personalization
strategies in advertising to provide customers with real experience based on the information
they acquire through customer interaction and usage (Shukla et al., 2021).
Utilization of customer data enables marketers to provide personalized product offers
and recommendations, price discounts, free services, and more relevant  marketing
communications and media content (Ardiansyah et al., 2018; Martin and Murphy, 2017). This
gave rise to a new strategy in communication with customers, namely personalization of
advertising. Therefore, advertisements that are delivered in a customized manner expose
customers to the product offering information they expect. However, according to Tran
(2017), personalized advertising sometimes does make customers unhappy or
uncomfortable, thus leading to negative responses and feelings towards advertisements,
such as avoidance and skepticism.

Providing incentives or financial benefits to customers is expected to reduce feelings of
disturbance due to online advertising. Incentive-based mobile advertising triggers positive
customer attitudes toward mobile advertising; a finding  reinforced by various studies and
markets (Gao and Zang, 2016). This is related to the psychological perspective of customers
who feel benefited by the existence of an incentive offer in an advertisement they receive.
Incentives are the  strongest driving factor for customers and have a significant impact on
their perception of financial benefits and financial management (Le and Wang, 2020).
In addition to sending customized information and sharing financial benefits to
customers, the most important thing in e -commerce activities is the platform used. User
engagement and satisfaction on interactive websites are influenced by interface simplici ty,
content readability, navigational usability, personalized experience, intuitive design, timely
feedback, and excessive customization options (Lun et al., 2024). High customer trust
strengthens the positive effects of website design and usability on cus tomer satisfaction,
while usability weakens satisfaction effects (Guo et al., 2023). Following aesthetic rules in
interface design significantly improves the effectiveness of product recommendations  and
highlights in online fashion stores context (Sulikowski et al., 2022).
Success in online advertising also needs  to consider value based on the ability to
achieve marketing and communication goals (Karamchandani et al., 2021). Perceived value
is associated with customer perceived benefits and trade -offs from online advertising that
affect their response (Lin et al., 2016). The use of value constructs becomes important as a
measure of advertising effectiveness in the case of online advertising (Ha et al.,  2014). The
perception of value in advertising is a marketer's concern to shape customer behavior
because it determines success in e-commerce.
Besides, t he success of e -commerce is also determined by the ability to attract
customers' attention and provoke a reaction to the advertisement by clicking, reading,  or
sharing it with other users (Wu et al., 2018). Several previous studies have examined click
intent based on individual characteristics and personalized targeting based on the customer's
prior knowledge of the product or service (Celli et al., 2016; Lin et al., 2015). Clicking on an
advertisement is an action that reflects the customer's curiosity for more detailed information.
Consumer attitu des, which previously tended to be passive in responding to traditional
advertising, have changed drastically in today's highly connected environment (Divya et al.,
2025). Customer attitudes have become more interactive by providing  feedback to
marketers due to the ease of the latest technology. Therefore, the advertising click
construct can measure customer behavior on exposure to information in the form of
advertisement they receive.
Based on several literature studies , such as research conducted by Liu -Thompkins
(2019) and Mou et al. (2019), the construct of advertising personalization  is relatively new.
This is also supported by findings from Varnali (2019) suggesting exploring  the use of
contextual information to improve the predictive ability of profiling based on online behavioral
data and reflecting customer  responses. Varnali (2019) also mentioned the three-layer
structure of online behavioral advertising that became a reference for research, namely
customer characteristics, personalized advertising, and websites where advertising was
customized based on customer behavior. Then , Yang and Zhai (2022) stated that click
through intention is still a relevant topic to be researched in relation to advertising. In addition,
studies conducted by Kalantari (2017) stat ing that researchers and industry experts are

technology-based communication.
This study aims to examine customer perceptions of personalization, incentives, apps
design quality, advertising value, and advertising click in the e -commerce context. The
Information Adoption Model is used in this study as a powerful lens to explain the
interrelationships of all the constructs  used in this study. The focus of this study is  on
customers in Indonesia to examine their responses to advertisements they encounter in e -
marketplaces, especially their behavioral tendencies to interact with such advertisements.

2. LITERATURE REVIEW
2.1. Information Adoption Model
Information Adoption Model tries to integrate ELM and TAM where individuals will feel
the benefits of information and then process to receive it when  the quality of the information
and the credibility of the source are validated. This is the highlight of the Information Adoption
Model, argument quality captures the extent to which individuals perceive information as
complete, consistent, and accurate, while source credibility refers to the extent to which
individuals per ceive information sources as credible, knowledgeable, and trustworthy
(Sussman and Siegal, 2003). Argument quality and source credibility are the main predictors
in adopting information to investigate whether the information can influence the individual's
perception (Shu and Scott, 2014). According  to Cheung (2014), the Information Adoption
Model needs to consider the characteristics of the webpage search facility and the relevance
of the information to customers when adopting the information.
Wang (2016) found it crucial for companies to understand the various factors that
influence the process of adopting potential customer information, so that companies can take
full advantage of these various factors to promote their products or services online. Therefore,
this study uses Information Adoption Model as a foundation theory to explain customer
behavior in adopting personalized advertising by adding incentives and involving perceptions
of app design quality to determine customer behavior. So far, the Information Adoption Model

2.2. The Relationship between Personalization and Advertising Value
Personalization is the extent to which an e-retailer's website can identify customers to
customize the customer's choice of products, services, and shopping experience (Srinivasan
et al., 2002). Personalized advertising refers to the inclusion of information specific to the
recipient of a marketing message including demographic characteristics, location, personal
interests, purchase history, and online behavioral information about the customer (De Keyzer
et al., 2022; McKee et al., 2024; Yeo et al., 2025), prior knowledge of the customer's
relationship with the product, preferences, attitudes, emotions, goals (Wu et al., 2018),
transactional, behavioral and demographic  data (Taylor et al., 2009). Personalization at the
customer journey level requires a deep understanding of diverse customer needs and
behaviors (Van Nguyen et al., 2024). Personalization refers to the ability of an online
business to customize its services to meet the specific needs and demands of  customers
(Dhingra et al., 2020) based on past and present customer behavior (Baek and Morimoto,
2012).
Meanwhile, Chhabria et al. (2023) in the retail sector found that personalized marketing
has a positive impact on customer satisfaction and loyalty, leading to increased engagement,
retention, and revenue growth. Customers feel that personalization in online advertising gives
value to their activities in browsing product information. Lee et al . (2017) and Aydin (2018)
stated that personalization affects customers' perception  of advertising value. According to
Lee et al. (2017) and Kim and Han (2014), online advertising can be optimized for customers
increasing the value of advertising.  Moreover, personalization on retail websites enhances
the customer experience, thereby encouraging greater focus on favorite products and
increasing the likelihood of a purchase (Lambillotte et al., 2022). This also provides value for
the delivery of online advertising. Therefore, customized advertis ing can increase the value
of product offering information, making it easier for customers to find products that really
match their preferences. Consequently, the first hypothesis is constructed as follows:
H1: Personalization has a positive effect on advertising value.
2.3. The Relationship between Incentives and Advertising Value
There are two types of incentives, namely cash incentives and non -cash incentives.
Cash incentives are defined as gifts worth a currency or currency equivalent, such as gifts or
discounts on future purchases, while non-cash incentives are defined as benefits that do not
have a cash equivalent value, such as information, assistance, or adjustments (Taylor et al.,
2009). Similarly, Zhang et al. (2024) found that promotional incentives can be monetary, such
as discounts, or non-monetary, such as free gifts. Ducoffe (1996) stated that online shopping
incentives including discounts, coupons, and gifts, as well as time -saving benefits such as
priority service, were consistently shown to be positive predictors of customer attitudes and
responses in the context of e-commerce.

Incentives are conceptually considered as a positive reinforcement mechanism that
contributes to explaining the advertising value (Chaihanchanchai  et al., 2026). When
customers see promotions in the form of product bonuses or discounts in advertisements
through their devices, they will seek ways to take advantage of the offer (Kim and Han, 2014).
Meanwhile, Kashyap et al. (2026) perceived that incentives are classified as external stimulus
factors that have been proven to influence advertising value. Le and Wang (2020) added that
if customers are more attracted to incentives, they drive a more significant response to make
a purchase. Therefore, advertising that contains incentives will lead advertising into valuable
information. Therefore, the second hypothesis is formulated as follows:
H2: Incentives have a positive effect on advertising value.

2.4. The Relationship between Apps Design Quality and Advertising Value
The design on the online platform is a collection of elements that are perceived by the
customer on the site of information search and product selection (Ha and Stoel, 2009). The
online platform is the key to the company's success in reaching communication channels with
customers (Chen et al., 2017). In the era of e -commerce, shopping is impersonal and e -
retailers do not know  the customers and cannot see them. However, they can transact with
customers in a digital environment through a capable application or web (Venkatakrishnan et
al., 2023). Pšurný et al. (2022) reinforced that consumers from various segments increasingly
prefer to access e-commerce platforms through websites or applications on digital devices.
Therefore, online retailers should pay attention to e -store operations, including t echnical
solutions that enhance ease of use, to ensure a comfortable shopping experience through
web browsers or applications.
 Therefore, maintaining the quality of the online platform is very important for retaining
customers and encouraging them to  make return visits, which in turn will  foster customer
loyalty. Myunghee and Miyoung (2017) added that an online platform with a great design must
also have a good navigation function allow ing users as potential customers to explore the
platform more thoroughly and prevent them from confusion. Sharma and Lijuan (2015) also
argued that a more visually acceptable website often results in a better quality of service and
can impact on the customer experience which ultimately maintains the long-term relationship
between the service provider and the customer.
Good website design has a positive influence on consumer trust in the e -commerce
environment (Arizal et al., 2024). Website quality significantly influences the perception of
customer value and loyalty within the online shopping experience (Tsao et al., 2016). Fatta,
Musotto, and Vesperi (2016) conducted a meta-analysis and showed that perceived platform
quality has a significant effect on ease of use, usability, and enjoyment, thereby encouraging
the use of online platforms for shopping.  These findings indicate that design quality in
applications, such as online shopping platforms, is an important aspect that requires attention
because it enables customers to comfortably access and adopt information, thereby
enhancing the perceived value a nd acceptance of advertising content.  Therefore, the ease
of access due to good design quality on online shopping platforms will increase the value of
online advertising. Thus, the third hypothesis is structured as follows:

2.5. The Relationship between Advertising Value and Advertising Clicks
Ducoffe (1995) defines advertising value as a subjective evaluation of the relative value
or utility of advertising to customers. Advertising value should also be able to meet customer
expectations because the value of advertising becomes the orientation of promotional
activities (Ducoffe, 1996). According to Ye et  al. ( 2025), t he ability of advertisements to
present product information is viewed as the primary reason why consumers pay attention to
them. Advertising value can predict behavior strongly in the context of online advertising (Ha
et al., 2014).
One of the behaviors in online advertising is clicking on advertising to explore more
detailed information. Zhang and Lee (2022) in social media context  research stated that
social media advertisements often contain links that consumers can click to access further
information. Further click -through is mostly  used in advertising serving for more detailed
information. Advertising click is not only driven by customer responses to specific
advertisements, but also their general attitude towards online advertising in particular (Taylor
et al., 2011). When they click and watch the advertisement, they can get more clues about
the advertised product (Yu et al., 2019).
The higher the click-through intensity will lead customers to a better response to banner
advertisement on the online platform (Yu  et al., 2019). One of them is because product
offering information is considered valuable or beneficial  to customers so that it can direct
customer behavior to click on online advertisement. This indicates that customers' general
liking or disliking of social media  advertisements will be transferred to their attitudes and
behavior towards certain forms of advertising (Zhang & Mao, 2016). Yu et al. (2019) stated
that the click-through intention of customers' advertisement increased one of them because
of visual appeal and quality of information to customers. This is reinforced by Gudipudi et al.
(2023) which states that click -through rates in online advertising improve business
performance and maximize revenue. Therefore, online advertisements perceived as valuable
information by customers can encourage them to seek further product information. Therefore,
the fourth hypothesis is stated as follows:
H4: Advertising value has a positive effect on advertising click.

3. METHODOLOGY
This study applied a quantitative study with an explanatory approach aiming to test the
causal relationship between variables. This study used a purposive sampling technique with
the criteria of respondents being Indonesian citizens, aged at least 16 years, who have made
purchases at least once a year through e -marketplaces, namely Lazada, Tokopedia, or
Shopee. The total respondents in this study were 338 users of e -marketplaces. Meanwhile,
the target respondents in this study were calculated referring to the sample determination
formula by Hair et al., (2021) where 5 to 10 were multiplied by the number of items, resulting
in 10 multiplied by 18 items equaling 180 respondents. Therefore, the number of samples in

4.2. Discussion
The general purpose of online advertising is to convey information to customers, attract
their attention, encourage them to browse and click on advertisements, and ultimately
persuade them to make purchases  (Breuer and Brettel,  2012). This study has examined
several important components of online advertising and identified the interrelationships
between the constructs involved in the developed research model.
The relationship between personalization and advertising value has been approved.
These findings support research from Aydin (2018), Lee et al. (2017), Shukla et al.  (2021).
Advertising value is an important key, so that online advertising can be accepted by
customers (Lee et al., 2017). Dua and Uddin (2022) also perceive that buyer commonly look
for convenience, personalized attention, and fast customer service to solve their problems.

Aljukhadar et al. (2012), Tam and Ho (2005), Ying Ho and Bodoff  (2014), and Zhang et al.
(2011) revealed that personalization strategy can improve the quality of customer decisions
by reducing the excess information presented to customers. The reduction of information that
is considered less relevant to the customer's condition helps customers to focus on the things
they are looking for. Therefore, it is highly recommended  to design an advertising
personalization strategy to be aligned with the value that will be transferred to the customer.
The findings of this study indicate that the more  tailored the advertising to the  customer’s
characteristics, the more valuable the online advertising is.
The relationship between incentive and advertising value has been approved  (Arora
and Agarwal, 2019; Kim and Han , 2014; and Martins et al., 2019). Moreover, Tibrani et al.
(2024) also states that advertising strategies and promotional activities affect human
behavior. Incentives make advertisements more interesting and valuable for customers. This
is because financial benefits can psychologically motivate customers to seek more detailed
information. This finding indicates that advertisements providing greater financial benefits are
more likely to be perceived as valuable by customers because they offer meaningful benefits.
The relationship between apps design quality and advertising value has been approved.
This finding strengthens the research of  Wilson et al. (2019) , Zhou et al. (2009)  and Dua
and Uddin (2022) who found that good design increases the value of an advertisement as it
increases customer satisfaction . Design is one of the factors that provide convenience for
customers to access product information, especially through the advertisements displayed.
An easy -to-understand app design with harmonious navigation, colors, and fonts makes
advertisements acceptable to customers. Good and valuable informational content on sales
websites and an aesthetically pleasing design influence a customer's buying experience and
the desire to shop online (Dua and Uddin, 2022).
Meanwhile, Omar and Elmansori (2021) suggested that an attractive framework is
essential to encourage business practitioners to remain competitive in creating more effective
information-based transactions. Moreover, customers believe that their ability to operate
mobile applications also influences their adoption behavior (Ratten , 2009). Therefore, ease
of application navigation design becomes an important consideration. This finding indicates
that the better the quality of application design, the more customers appreciate
advertisements.
The relationship between advertising value and advertising click has been approved.
This finding is in line with research from Zhang and Mao (2016) that advertisements that are
of value must be informative and entertaining affect the intention of click on advertisements
by customers. In addition, according to Yu et al. (2019) the quality of information also leads
customers to click on advertising. If an advertisement is very valuable information, then the
interest of customers in accessing it will be high. On the other hand, if the information in the
advertisement is considered less valuable for customers, customers will tend to skip it. This
finding indicates that the more valuable the information in the advertising, the higher the
customer's intention to click on an advertisement.

5.1. Research Implications
This research contributes to proving that there needs to be a strategy to develop online
advertising in the big data era. An effective online advertising strategy to get a good response
or benefit marketers in the big data era is to utilize customer profile data and their product
traces to create a customized offer information at the  right time and benefit customers. In
addition, from the customer perspective, it gives them an understanding that the marketers
use their profile data submission to develop a customization strategy. The arrangement of
customized advertisements is attempted to be as minimal as possible not to irritate  them by
paying attention to the information aspects of offerings that have been  adjusted to their
preferences, the accuracy of advertising delivery, and the various kinds of financial benefits
they can get.
The theoretical contribution given through this study is to add research references
related to the use of big data in compiling online advertising on e -commerce, especially e-
marketplace sites. This is because online advertising research is more often done in the
context of social media. In addition, expanding the application of Information Adoption Model
in explaining the phenomenon of online advertising rather than the e-commerce context. This
expansion of context shows that Information Adoption Model is not only relevant in explaining
the information adoption process in the e -commerce context but  can also be used to
understand consumers' tendencies to evaluate the value of advertisements and respond to
them in an e-marketplace environment.
5.2. Limitations and Future Research Agenda
This research still has some shortcomings. First, there are still many factors that need to
be elaborated to become an antecedent of advertising value in the big data era. Second, this
study has looked at online advertising behavior through advertising  click, but this study has
