ABSTRACT

Objectives: This study aims to investigate how ESG  (Environmental, Social and Governance) contributes to
consumer behavior so that they have conscious consumption trend s and how ESG can define patters of consumer
behavior and trend positively in the behavior relationship of consumption.

Theoretical Framework: Contributes to the mapping of the evolution, quantity and journals that published the
list of search strings from 2014 to 2024.

Method: This qualitative study is a Systematic Literature Review that used Parsifal® Software for the process of
choosing and refining articles, which resulted in 81 articles as a final sample. The databases consulted were Web
of Science and Scopus accessed on March 5, 2024. “ESG and Sustainable”, “ESG and consumer behavior”  and
“ESG and conscious consumption” were used as search strings.

Results and Discussion:  Positive relationships are evident between ESG and consumer behavior for conscious
consumption trends, when choosing products and/or services. The implementation of ESG in the company's
business can improve the relationship with consumers of its product or service and lead to robust, conscious and
lasting behavior.

Research Implications: Contributes to the benefits, pillars and how to adopt conscious consumption, in addition
to highlighting the relationships between ESG and conscious consumption and the influence of these factors to
define consumer behavior patterns.

Originality/Value: This study stands out for analyzing the relationship between the adoption of ESG s tandards
and practices and verifying the trend towards conscious consumption arising from the adoption of ESG practices .

Keywords: ESG, Consumer Behavior, Tendencies, Conscious Consumption.

The implementation of ESG in the company's business environment can improve the

growing? Conscious consumption, which takes environmental and social repercussions into

with these factors (Silverman & Barasch, 2023).
To be able to establish standards of sustainable development, greater concern related to
individual consumption, respect for the limits of nature and the need to think and act with others
in mind is necessary (Lee & Roh, 2023). In this way, environmentally conscious consumption
behaviors are daily choices and interfere in decisions to b uy and consume goods, where the
consumer obtains their demands without harming the environment (Sanchez-Sabate & Sabaté,

relationships can allow corporate E SG to positively impact the relationship between behavior

2.1 ESG AND CONSUMER BEHAVIOR

The term Environmental, Social and Governance, more popularly known by the
acronym ESG (or in the free translation into Portuguese: Environmental, Social and
Governance) is recent and appeared in 2005 in the report “Who Cares Wins” – the result of an
initiative led by the Organization of United Nations (UN, 2015). This thinking, despite being
recently baptized, is increasingly permeating companies' decisions about practices,
performance and expected returns from customers and stakeholders (Sergeeva et al., 2023).
ESG is a broad set of issues, ranging from aspects linked to nature, sustainability, to
labor and anti -corruption practices, which justify criteria and care in practices that direct the
role and responsibility of businesses in environmental, social and governance aspects. corporate
(Sergeeva et al., 2023). The real concern with ESG practices is related to concepts and actions
that aim to contribute to a better society and planet, where the economic growth of organizations
and the country itself combine for the success of ESG practices (Liu et al., 2024).
From an ESG perspective, there is an i ncrease in investments by companies in the
adoption of sustainability practices, social and governance practices, which are linked to the
awareness of investors who seek to comply with local, environmental and social regulations
(Khanchel et al., 2023). Concerns about climate change, in this sense, not only impact people's
lives, but also the continuity of organizations, where the effective adoption of ESG practices
can reduce risks, in addition to attracting the attention of new investors and minimizing conflicts
with stakeholders (Liu et al., 2024).
But what has made consumers so aware of the importance of ESG after the report from
ONU (2015) “Who Cares Wins”? Factors such as the unforeseen risks of a pandemic or a future
climate crisis, as two important factors that could substantially affect the global economy, made
investors and policymakers realize the importance of investing in ventures that prioritize ESG,
as society does not it depends only on governments, but also on businesses that are efficient

3 METHODOLOGY

This study with a qualitative approach is a Systematic Review of the Literature on ESG,
consumer behavior and conscious consumption trends. The search strings “ESG and
Sustainable”, “ESG and consumer behavior” and “ESG and conscious consumption” were used.
The search was carried out on March 5, 2024, using the Parsifal® software in the Scopus and
Web of Science databases (Kitchenham & Charters, 2007). As a year of publication factor, the
years 2014 to 2024 were defined. The files were imported with the help of Parsifal® Software
in BibteX format and 748 publications were obtained. After excluding duplicates (583) and
rejected ones (84), as they did not comply with the search strings, did not meet the inclusion
and exclusion criteria and were out of scope, 81 publications remained (Figure 1).
The general quality, inclusion and exclusion criteria were applied during the question
construction process in the Parsifal® Software (Figure 1). In the PICOC plan - Population,
Intervention, Comparison, Result, Context (Population, Intervention, C omparison, Outcome,
Context) the search strings “ESG and Sustainable”, “ESG and consumer behavior” and “ESG
and conscious consumption” were included in Population; In the Intervention the words ESG,
consumption, awareness and consumer; For comparison, a sy stematic literature review,
bibliometrics and case study were included; The Result included review articles, research
articles, tools, methodologies, guidelines and indicators and concluded with the inclusion of the
Context, where the words studies, compan ies, conscious consumption trends and consumer
were included.

4 RESULTS AND DISCUSSIONS

ESG as actions aimed at governance for environmental, economic and social
commitment is in the objectives of product and service business model companies. How ESG
proposes to improve administrative management through actions that take care of relationships
with suppliers, distributors, employees, customers and communities close to the company,
consumers who interact with companies that adopt ESG are choosing them over anothe r
company from the same segment that does not adopt ESG.
ESG can also be understood as the way companies carry out their business, guided by
responsible environmental, social and economic principles, supervised by robust corporate
governance that promotes transparency of results. Liu et al., (2024) pointed out that the real
concern with ESG practices is related to concepts and actions that aim to contribute to a better
society and planet, and when the consumer perceives that a company is following the ESG trend
and practices, starts to look at this company from a different position and consider relationships.
From a practical point of view, a company that makes ESG commitments promotes
business towards sustainability, and this sustainability is perceived by consumers of its products
or services as a factor of choice. The company's behavior establishes a path for consumers to
learn about and initiate trends towards conscious consumption.
Consumer behavior changes frequently, and can be monthly, semi -annually, annually
or for longer periods. Everything will depend on several elements that influence customer
purchasing decisions and shape their behavior, whether in a country, state, city, region or
location around the world. As ESG has in its essence good environ mental and social practices
combined with good governance, which promotes increased profitability and reduced costs,
these practices help to define consumers' choices for companies' products and services. Jain et
al., (2024) showed that the consumer and th e act of consuming are inherent with perceived
choices of good business practices.
What we call consumer behavior is nothing more than a set of practices, habits and
consumption priorities that involve the purchasing process of a person or a group. Trudel
(2019), Zimmermann et al., (2022) and Shevchenko et al., (2023) point out that consumer
behavior is frequently changing, as this depends on variables such as politics and economics,
purchasing power, culture and demography, psychological factors and season ality, which
define the actions that the consumer will take (Table 1).

ESG is an opportunity for companies to differentiate themselves in  business, improve
visibility and align with trends in conscious consumption practices. ESG as a set of standards
and practices establishes guidelines for decision -making in a specific area, process or activity
of companies. All sets of ESG standards and p ractices contribute to promoting and enhancing
company strategies and building strong relationships with consumers of their products and
services.
Consumer behavior trends show that the relationship between the applicability of ESG
by companies and the option to consume consciously are strongly related. These relationships
establish and determine trends for consumption, since consumer decisions are based on
parameters of evaluation, encouragement, rethinking, using, separating, discarding, reducing
and adop ting practices that are behavioral and guide actions. In turn, the benefits that
collaborate with the UN SDGs (2015) interact together to strengthen the pillars, and these shape
the behavior and govern the trends of the conscious consumer.
The study showed  positive relationships between ESG, consumer behavior and
conscious consumption trends. For the consumer, ESG directs the behavior of choices when
purchasing products and services. Therefore, ESG in companies' business environments can
improve relationships with consumers and lead to robust, conscious and lasting behavior.
The results of the Systematic Literature Review show that ESG, consumer behavior and
conscious consumption trends have been growing since 2020. The results indicate that the
relationships between ESG, consumer and conscious consumption are important and
fundamental for companies' businesses. Therefore, managing the relational factors between
ESG and consumers are strategic and essential challenges for companies' businesses.
Aligning ESG s tandards and good practices, with objectives of meeting requirements
that shape consumer behavior, can generate a culture for continuous improvement and establish
cyclical and lasting processes of conscious consumption. In addition to reducing corporate risks,
ESG relationships with consumers can generate business growth and enable greater robustness
for companies.
Companies, in addition to practicing ESG and being aware of consumer behavior, must
be aware of trends, as these trends can be impacted by varia bles that dictate and influence
behavior. Therefore, establishing synchronization of the company's business model with
political and economic variables, purchasing power, culture and demography, psychological
factors and seasonality, is vital for maintaining the company and consumer relationship.
