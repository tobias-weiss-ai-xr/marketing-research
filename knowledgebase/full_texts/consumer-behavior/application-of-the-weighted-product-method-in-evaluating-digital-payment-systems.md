ABSTRACT
Digital payment refers to the electronic transfer of funds or transactions made through digital channels,
replacing traditional cash-based methods. This includes various forms such as mobile money, online banking,
ATM transactions, credit/debit cards, and emerging systems like E-Zwich. Digital payment methods offer
significant advantages, including speed, convenience, and enhanced security. They enable users to conduct
transactions remotely, often using smartphones or computers, and are accessible anytime and anywhere. The
growing preference for digital payments reflects a shift in consumer behavior, with more individuals opting for
mobile and online financial solutions due to ease of use and the increasing reliance on technology in daily
life. As digital infrastructure improves globally, digital payment systems are expected to expand, making
them a key component of the future financial landscape. Their evolution will continue to reshape how people
manage and transfer money, driving a move away from traditional banking and cash- base transactions. This
research on digital payments is important as it highlights the increasing shift toward electronic transactions, a
trend that is transforming how people conduct financial activities globally. It involves data collection from
user surveys, transaction analysis, and case studies to evaluate digital payment trends. ATM Mobile "Online
banking, E-Zwich, and credit/debit card payments. "Evalution parameter: Transaction Speed, Transaction
