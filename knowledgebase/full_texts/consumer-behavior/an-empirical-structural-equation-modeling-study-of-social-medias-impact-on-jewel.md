Abstract
Background and Aims: Digital transformation are profoundly reshaping consumer behavior and
competitive dynamics in the jewelry industry. This study examines how social media is associated
with jewelry purchasing experience through functional value and emotional value, and further
tests the roles of brand identification as a mediating mechanism and brand loyalty as a boundary
condition (moderator).
Methodology: A quantitative survey design was adopted. A total of 483 valid questionnaires were
collected via online platforms to test the structural model using SEM.
Results: Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) results show significant positive associations between
social media dimensions and jewelry purchasing experience, with functional value and emotional
value serving as key mechanisms. The ind irect (mediated) association via brand identification is
statistically significant. In addition, brand loyalty strengthens the association between social media
and the jewelry purchasing experience.
Conclusion: Theoretically, this study integrates social media into the Resource-Based View (RBV)
framework as an intangible strategic resource. It also verifies the applicability of the experience
economy framework in luxury consumption. For practice, the study proposes four strategic paths
for jewelry enterprises: information transparency, immersive experience design, symbolic narrative
communication, and loyalty cultivation. These paths aim to achieve sustainable competitive
advantage.
Keywords: Social Media; Jewelry Purchasing Experience; Strategic Management;  Brand
Identification; Brand Loyalty

Based on this, this study aims to explore the mechanism of how social media influences

strategic levers to maintain a firm's long-term competitiveness? By integrating consumer behavior
theory with strategic management frameworks, this study attempts to bridge the gap between
micro-level consumer psychology and macro-level strategic significance.

Literature Review
1. Social Media and Consumer Experience
Social media has become an important platform influencing consumer purchase decisions.
Kaplan and Haenlein (2010) define social media as "a group of Internet -based applications" that
allow users to create and exchange User-Generated Content (UGC). This characteristic gives social
media the advantages of immediacy, media interactivity, and highly visual communication. Recent
studies show that information dissemination, media interactivity, and visual content play
significant roles in consumer experience (Ashley & Tuten, 2015; Appel et al., 2020).
First, information dissemination on social media may reduce perceived information
asymmetry and uncertainty by enabling access to product details, reviews, and peer discussions.
Such information availability is likely to be associated with more informed evaluations and more
rational decision-making (Chen & Xie, 2008).
Second, media interactivity is often associated with higher trust and emotional value,
because timely responses, two -way communication, and community engagement can make
consumers feel acknowledged and supported. These interactive features are therefore likely to
be associated with a richer emotional experience on social media.

surpasses traditional channels. Its role lies not only  at the functional level but also in the

2. Strategic Management Perspective
In strategic management research, the value of social media can be understood through
various theoretical frameworks.
First, the Resource-Based View (RBV) provides important theoretical support for explaining
the strategic value of social media. Barney (1991) argues that resources that are valuable, rare,
inimitable, and non-substitutable can form a firm's sustainable competitive adva ntage. The user
relationship networks, brand communities, and immersive experiences carried by social media are
precisely such inimitable intangible resources (Teece, 2018).
Second, the Experience Economy theory emphasizes the strategic significance of immersive
and memorable experiences. Pine and Gilmore (1999) point out that with consumption upgrading,
firms no longer just provide products or services. Instead, they create differentiated value by
designing experiences. For jewelry enterprises, social medi a provides an important channel for
achieving immersive storytelling and interactive experiences, thus forming a unique advantage in
the fierce market competition (Schmitt, 2010).
Third, brand strategy theory emphasizes the role of identification and loyalty in competitive
advantage. Aaker (1996) proposed that brand identification can enhance consumer stickiness
through emotional and symbolic value. Recent studies further indicate that social media
interaction significantly strengthens the connection betwee n brand identification and brand
loyalty (Dessart et al., 2016; Islam & Rahman, 2017). For the jewelry industry, brands often carry
identity expression and social symbolism. Social media provides a new strategic path for the
dissemination of this symbolic meaning.
Therefore, from a strategic management perspective, social media is not merely a tactical
marketing tool but also a means of strategic resource allocation. It can help firms gain a
competitive advantage in the digital economy.

Information dissemination strengthens consumers' rational judgment and functional

In the proposed model, information dissemination, media interactivity, and visual content are
theorized to be positively associated with consumers’ value perceptions. These value perceptions
are expected to relate to brand identification and, in turn , to the jewelry purchasing experience
(Chaudhuri & Holbrook, 2001).
Based on this, the following research hypotheses are proposed:
H1: Social media is positively associated with the jewelry purchasing experience.
H2: Social media is positively associated with brand identification.
H3: Brand identification is positively associated with the jewelry purchasing experience.
H4: Brand identification mediates the association between social media and the jewelry
purchasing experience.
H5: Brand loyalty moderates t he relationship between social media and the jewelry
purchasing experience.
The theoretical framework of this study combines the dual perspectives of consumer
behavior and strategic management. It aims to reveal how social media drives strategic
competitive advantage in the jewelry industry through value perception and brand mechanisms.

4. Data Analysis Methods
Data analysis was conducted at three levels:
Reliability and Validity Tests: SPSS 26.0 was used to calculate Cronbach's α to assess
internal consistency. KMO and Bartlett's sphericity tests were performed. Confirmatory Factor
Analysis (CFA) was used to confirm convergent validity and discriminant validity.
Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) Analysis: AMOS 24.0 was used for path analysis to test
hypothesized relationships. Model fit indices (e.g., χ²/df, RMSEA, GFI, CFI, TLI) were evaluated.
Mediation and Moderation Effect Tests: The Bootstrap method (5000 resamples) was used
to calculate 95% confidence intervals. This verified the mediating effect of brand identification
and the moderating effect of brand loyalty.

Conclusion
This study, from a strategic management perspective, explores the mechanism of how social
media influences the jewelry consumer purchasing experience. Using mixed methods and
structural equation modeling analysis, the following main conclusions are drawn:
First, information dissemination, media interactivity, and visual content are associated with
consumers’ functional and emotional value perceptions. These dimensions appear to support
social media’s role as a platform for emotional connection and symbolic meaning construction
in jewelry consumption.
Second, brand identification demonstrates a partial mediating role in the relationship
between social media and jewelry purchasing experience. This suggests that social media–enabled
experiences may translate into stronger brand -related perceptions, which in turn are associated
with higher consumer engagement and longer-term relationship outcomes.
Third, brand loyalty serves as an important boundary condition. The results suggest that
higher brand loyalty strengthens the relationship between social media and jewelry purchasing
experience, consistent with loyalty helping stabilize consumer behavior and reducing
susceptibility to competitive offerings.
In summary, this study reveals how social media, through the combined effects of perceived
value, brand identification, and brand loyalty, reshapes the purchasing experience in the jewelry
industry and creates sustainable competitive advantages for firms.

Discussion
The empirical results of this study confirm that social media significantly influences the
jewelry consumer purchasing experience. Strong effects are observed particularly in functional
value, emotional value, brand identification, and brand loyalty. These findings not only
corroborate previous research (Hudson et al., 2016; Appel et al., 2020) but also provide new
perspectives and an explanatory framework for strategic management.
1. Social Media as an Intangible Strategic Resource
The research suggests that information dissemination, media interactivity, and visual content
constitute critical social media capabilities. Together, they may represent a core pathway for firms
to build competitive advantage by shaping consumers’ value perceptions and brand -related
outcomes. This indicates that social media has unique advantages in reducing information
asymmetry and increasing transparency. According to the Resource-Based View (RBV), for firms to
gain sustainable competitive advantage, they must rely on resources that are valuable, rare,
inimitable, and non-substitutable (Barney, 1991). Social media provides precisely such a resource:
through instant information delivery, precise recommendations, and consumer feedback

2. The Strategic Significance of Immersive Experience and Emotional Value
The effect of media interactivity and visual content on emotional value is significantly
stronger than on functional value. This finding aligns with Pine and Gilmore's (1999) "Experience
Economy" theory, which posits th at modern consumers seek immersive and memorable
consumption experiences. Jewelry, as a product with strong emotional and symbolic meaning,
derives its value not only from material aspects like materials and price but also from identity
recognition, emotional expression, and social symbolism. Through methods like short videos, live
streams, and virtual try -ons, social media helps consumers establish emotional connections in
the digital environment, thereby deepening the consumption experience. This suggests  that
jewelry firms should strategically view social media as a core platform for "experience design,"
not just a sales tool.
3. The Mediating Role of Brand Identification
This study finds that brand identification plays a partial mediating role in the mo del.
Specifically, social media –related experiences are positively associated with value perception,
which is in turn associated with stronger brand identification. According to the consumer-company
identification model by Bhattacharya and Sen (2003), bran d identification enables consumers to
psychologically connect with the brand, thereby enhancing loyalty and word-of-mouth. In jewelry
consumption, brands often represent identity symbols and value propositions. Therefore, firms
should use social media to s trengthen narrative communication and symbolic meaning
construction, thereby enhancing consumers' brand identification.
4. The Moderating Role of Brand Loyalty
Brand loyalty shows a significant moderating effect in the model. This suggests that the
association between social media and jewelry purchasing experience is stronger among consumers
with higher brand loyalty, highlighting the role of relationship maintenance, community
operations, and consumer co -creation. Chaudhuri and Holbrook (2001) point out t hat brand
loyalty implies not only repeat purchase behavior but also consumers' ongoing support for the
brand at the emotional and attitudinal levels. From a strategic management perspective, brand
loyalty is a dynamic capability (Teece, 2018) that can help firms maintain a stable customer base
in a highly competitive and rapidly changing market environment. Therefore, when formulating
social media strategies, firms should focus on cultivating and enhancing brand loyalty through
long-term relationship maintenance, community operations, and consumer co-creation.

Suggestions
Based on the above analysis, the findings of this study provide the following strategic
implications for jewelry firms:
Information-Driven Transparency Strategy. Provide high-quality, reliable information through
social media to reduce consumer decision-making risks and enhance functional value.
Immersive Experience Strategy. Invest in media interactivity and visual content to create
digital "immersive experiences" that meet consumers' emotional needs.
Symbolic Value and Brand Identification Strategy. Use social media to strengthen brand
stories, cultural connotations, and identity recognition, enhancing the psychological connection
between consumers and the brand.
Loyalty Cultivation Strat egy. Utilize social media as a platform for long -term relationship
maintenance. Enhance brand loyalty and amplify its moderating effect through continuous
interaction and value co-creation.
This study not only verifies the multi -dimensional impact of socia l media on consumer
experience but also reveals its competitive value as an intangible resource from a strategic
management perspective. The application of social media in the jewelry industry has transformed
from a mere marketing tool into a strategic ass et. Firms need to synergize efforts in information
transparency, experiential immersion, identity construction, and loyalty cultivation to form a
sustainable competitive advantage.
