ABSTRACT

Advances in digital technology have transformed social media into an essential marketing and transactional
ecosystem that increasingly influences consumer behavior, particularly among Generation Z in Indonesia
where TikTok Shop has become a dominant social  commerce platform driven by algorithmic
personalization and creative content strategies. This research aimed to analyze the effect of TikTok
algorithm personalization and creative content on conversion rates, as well as to examine the moderating
role of the Payday Sale period among Generation Z consumers in Bali. Used a quantitative explanatory
approach with primary data collected through an online questionnaire distributed to 250 Generation Z
respondents in Bali who actively use TikTok and have participat ed in the Payday Sale promotion. Data
were analyzed using SEM-PLS with SmartPLS 4.1 through outer model, inner model, and bootstrapping
evaluations. The findings indicate that TikTok algorithm personalization positively and significantly affects
conversion rates, implying that more personalized content recommendations increase consumers’
likelihood of making purchases. Similarly, creative content significantly enhances conversion rate by
improving user engagement and purchase intention. Furthermore, the Pay day Sale period significantly
strengthens the relationship between algorithm personalization and creative content toward conversion
rate, indicating that promotional momentum amplifies the effectiveness of digital marketing stimuli. In
conclusion, TikTok a lgorithm personalization and creative content are key determinants of conversion
rate among Generation Z consumers in Bali, while the Payday Sale period acts as a reinforcing factor that
enhances both variables in driving purchase conversion behavior on TikTok Shop.

Keywords: algorithm personalization; conversion rate; creative content; digital marketing; Generation Z;
payday sale; TikTok Shop

1. INTRODUCTION

Advances in digital technology have transformed how people interact with social media and have
simultaneously reshaped marketing strategies in the business world. Social platforms are no longer used
merely as communication tools, but have developed into digital marketing and transaction ecosystems
capable of influencing consumer behavior. In Indonesia, social media usage continues to grow
significantly. By early 2025, the number of social media users in Indonesia reached approximately 143
million users, equivalent to 50.2% of the total population, with a growth rate of 2.9% compared to the
previous year. This growth is driven primarily by Millennials and Generation Z, who tend to prefer visual,
fast-paced, and interactive content (Riyanto, 2025). This trend indicates that social media has become an
integral part of modern society’s digital consumption activities.
One of the rapidly growing platforms in today’s digital ecosystem is TikTok, particularly through
its TikTok Shop feature, which integrates entertainment and shopping activities into a single platform.
According to Kemp (2024), Indonesia ranks second globally in TikTok advertising reach for users aged
18 years and older, with a total of 126.8 million users. In addition to having a large user base, Indonesia
also recorded the second -highest global quarterly growth, reaching 19.1%. This data indicates that
Indonesia is a highly promising market for developing TikTok Shop -based digital marketing strategies.
This information is illustrated in Figure 1.1 regarding the global ranking of TikTok advertising reach.
The success of TikTok Shop is strongly influenced by the platform’s ability to personalize content
through the For You Page (FYP) system. TikTok Shop Algorithm Personalization enables users to receive
content and product recommendations that are aligned with their preferences and digital behavior, thereby
increasing user engagement and the likelihood of purchase. In 2024, Indonesia was recorded as the country
with the second -highest TikTok Shop transaction value in the world, with total transactions exceeding
Rp100 trillion (Riyanto & Pertiwi, 2025). This achievement indicates that TikTok Shop is not only popular
in terms of user numbers but also has a tangible impact on online shopping behavior.
Generation Z is one of the most dominant consumer groups in TikTok Shop usage. This
demographic, commonly identified as individuals born between 1997 and 2012, is known for being highly
active on social media and responsive to visual, short-form, and interactive content. Generation Z spends
an average of 89 minutes per day on TikTok, higher than on other social media platforms ( Putri &
Nurhayati, 2024). This finding is also supported by Kemp (2024), which reported that TikTok has the
highest average monthly usage time globally, reaching 23 hours and 5 minutes per month. The high
intensity of TikTok usage creates an immersive and personalized digital environment, causing consumers
to be more frequently exposed to product content relevant to their interests. Under these conditions, the
process from viewing a product to making a purchase becomes increasingly streamlined and has the
potential to increase conversion rate.
Within the TikTok Shop ecosystem, TikTok Shop Algorithm Personalization plays a crucial role
in determining the content and products that appear on users’ feeds. Wang (2022) explains that TikTok’s
recommendation algorithm operates through content-based filtering and collaborative filtering approaches
by considering users’ interaction history, such as likes, comments, watch time, and content -sharing
behavior. Farhannail and Yuniarti (2023) explain that creative content is designed to capture the audience’s
attention and deliver messages in an engaging and entertaining manner. Salsabila and Ajibulloh (2024)
indicate that visual appeal, relevance, and message clarity in TikTok content are key factors driving
consumers’ impulse purchases.
Although TikTok Shop has high engagement potential, not all content exposure consistently leads
to actual purchases. Many businesses still face uncertainty regarding the effectiveness of TikTok Shop
Algorithm Personalization in increasing conversion rate, particularly because intense content competition
on the FYP can easily shift consumer attention. A high number of viewers, likes, or interactions does not
always translate into actual product purchases. This condition shows that marketing success on TikTok
Shop cannot be measured only through exposure or engagement, but also through the  ability of TikTok
Shop Algorithm Personalization and Creative Content to encourage consumers to complete purchase
actions.

The effectiveness of TikTok Shop Algorithm Personalization and Creative Content becomes
increasingly important during specific promotional periods, one of which is the Payday Sale Period. During
this period, TikTok Shop user activity increases significantl y due to promotions such as flash sales, free
shipping, discounts, and live shopping. Zai et al. (2025) state that time-limited promotions can encourage
consumers to make purchasing decisions quickly because of the emergence of fear of missing out
(FOMO). Zhu et al. (2023)  also explain that algorithmic personalization works more effectively during
high-traffic promotional periods because the system can increase the exposure of relevant product content
to users. This phenomenon is also reflected in the Payday Sale Period, which commonly takes place from
the 25th to the 31st of each month, as shown in Figure 1.3. According to Kontan.co.id (2024), during
major promotional periods such as 11.11 and 12.12, which share similar characteristics with the Payday
Sale Period, TikTok users in Indonesia shop up to 1.6 times more than on regular days, wi th average
spending reaching Rp2.3 million per user.
However, increased user traffic during promotional periods does not necessarily result in an
optimal increase in conversion rate. In this situation, the effectiveness of TikTok Shop Algorithm
Personalization and the quality of Creative Content become critical factors in determining whether
consumers only view product content or actually proceed to purchase. The Payday Sale Period is therefore
relevant to examine as a moderating variable because it may str engthen the effect of TikTok Shop
Algorithm Personalization and Creative Content on conversion rate. In other words, promotional
momentum may amplify the influence of personalized product recommendations and creative
promotional messages in encouraging Generation Z consumers to complete transactions on TikTok Shop.
According to Margarena et al. (2023), conversion rate refers to the percentage of digital platform
users who perform specific actions, such as purchasing products, registering accounts, or completing other
activities expected by the company. In digital marketing, conversion rate is an important indicator for
evaluating the success of marketing strategies in converting audiences into actual consumers. In this study,
conversion rate is used to represent the extent to which Generation Z consumers proceed from exposure
to TikTok Shop content toward purchase action. Therefore, understanding the factors influencing
conversion rate on TikTok Sh op is crucial, particularly among Generation Z consumers in Bali who
demonstrate high social media usage and strong responsiveness toward visual and promotional content.
Previous research on TikTok Shop has been extensive, yet several research gaps remain.  Studies
by Mustikasari et al. (2023), Setyawan (2024), and Zulfikar et al. (2025) focused more on the influence of
flash sales, promotions, or content marketing on purchase intention and impulse buying, without
specifically addressing the role of TikTok Shop Algorithm Personalization in driving conversion rate.
Studies by Fitri and Ananta (2025) , Salsabila and Nurmalia (2019) , and Setyawan (2024) have examined
the influence of TikTok’s algorithm on consumer behavior, but have not fully integrated Creative Content
and promotional momentum as moderating conditions. Meanwhile, Putri and Nurhayati (2024) examined
the influence of TikTok’s algorithm and creative content on purchase decisions, but did not consider the
Payday Sale Pe riod as a thematic promotional condition that may strengthen digital marketing
effectiveness. In addition, most prior studies have primarily used purchase intention, purchase decision,
or impulse buying as outcome variables, while research that specificall y positions conversion rate as an
indicator of digital marketing success on TikTok Shop remains relatively limited.
The novelty of this study lies in its focus on conversion rate as the outcome variable, rather than
only purchase intention, purchase decision, or impulse buying as commonly examined in previous TikTok
Shop studies. In addition, this study contributes by positioning the Payday Sale Period as a moderating
variable that strengthens the relationship between TikTok Shop Algorithm Personalization, Creative
Content, and conversion rate among Generation Z consumers in Bali. This study also provides contextual
novelty by focusing on Generation Z consumers in Bali, a segment that is highly active in social media
usage and increasingly exposed to TikTok Shop-based digital promotions. Based on these gaps, this
research offers an empirical contribution by testing the influence of TikTok Shop Algorithm
Personalization and Creative Content on conversion rate and by examining the moderating role of the
Payday Sale Period in strengthening these relationships.

2. THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK AND HYPOTHESIS DEVELOPMENT

2.1. Stimulus Organism Response (SOR) Theory
The Stimulus-Organism-Response (SOR) theory is a framework model that originated in the field
of environmental psychology  and became extensively utilized across digital marketing and consumer
behavior research. This theory states that external stimuli from the environment will affect the internal
psychological state (Organism) of the individual, which ultimately encourages t he emergence of certain
behaviors or actions (Response). In this context, stimuli do not directly cause a response, but must first
go through cognitive and affective processes in the organism. According to Luo et al. (2025) in research
on social commerce, SOR Theory is explained as a framework that connects social commerce features
(Stimulus) such as social recommendations and reviews with customer engagement (Organism), which
ultimately has an impact on purchase intent (Response). The study emphasizes that social features don't
directly drive purchases, but must go through engagement building first.
 SOR theory as a model that analyzes how personalized recommendations and visual appeal
(Stimulus) on short video platforms create a flow experience and hedonistic value (Organism) for users, which
in turn drives actual purchase behavior (Response) Based on the SOR theory, this study positions the TikTok
Algorithm (as a personalized stimulus) and Creative Content (as a creative stimulus) as the Stimulus. This stimulus
will affect the internal psychological state of Gen Z consumers in Bali, such as interests, value perceptions,
and impulsive impulses, which act as Organisms. The result of this internal p rocess is a real action in the
form of purchases, which in this study is measured through conversion rate (Y) as a Response. Furthermore,
the Sale Salary Period (Z) allegedly serves as a reinforcer ( moderator) that influences the strength of the
relationship between the Stimulus and the Organism, by creating a greater sense of urgency and perception
of financial benefits. The description can be visualized in the following conceptual model (Figure 1).

3. RESEARCH METHOD

This research used a quantitative explanatory approach to analyze the influence of TikTok Shop
Algorithm Personalization and Creative Content on Conv ersion Rate among TikTok Shop users, as well
as to examine the moderating role of the Payday Sale Period. The study was conducted in Bali Province
because this region has a high level of internet and social media usage, particularly among Generation Z
(Badan Pusat Statistik Provinsi Bali, 2023). The scope of the study focuses on Generation Z consumers in
Bali who actively use TikTok Shop and have viewed, interacted with, or made transactions during th e
Payday Sale Period on TikTok Shop. The independent variables in this research consist of TikTok Shop
Algorithm Personalization (X1) and Creative Content (X2), while the Payday Sale Period (Z) is used as a
moderating variable and Conversion Rate (Y) as the dependent variable.
The population of this research comprised Generation Z consumers in Bali who actively use
TikTok Shop. Participants were selected using purposive sampling based on specific criteria, including
residing in Bali, belonging to the Genera tion Z age group, actively using TikTok Shop, and having
interacted with or conducted transactions during the Payday Sale Period. Referring to the rule of thumb
proposed by Hair et al. (2019), the minimum sample size in SEM-PLS research should be at least ten times
the total number of indicators. Since this study employed 20 indicators, a minimum of 200 respondents
was required. To improve data adequacy and anticipate incomplete responses, this study  involved 250
respondents.

Data analysis was conducted using Structural Equation Modeling –Partial Least Squares (SEM -
PLS) with SmartPLS 4.1. This method was selected because it allows simultaneous examination of
relationships between latent variables, accommodates moderating effects, and does not require strict data
normality assumptions. The analysis procedure included outer model evaluation, inner model assessment,
and hypothesis testing through bootstrapping techniques. The measurement model evaluation covered
factor loadings, Average Variance Extr acted (AVE), Cronbach’s Alpha, Composite Reliability, Fornell -
Larcker criteria, and Heterotrait -Monotrait Ratio (HTMT). Subsequently, the structural model was
assessed using R-square (R²), Q-square (Q²), and effect size (f²) values. Hypothesis testing was determined
based on a t-statistic value above 1.65 and a p -value below 0.05. Furthermore, simple slope analysis was
employed to examine the moderating role of the Payday Sale Period in the relationship between TikTok
Shop Algorithm Personalization, Creative Content, and Conversion Rate.

needs, the response that arises is an increased tendency to complete a purchase, which is reflected in an
increase in conversion rates. The results of this study are in line with the research ( X. Chen, 2024; Lan,

encourage user interaction, which has implications for marketing effectiveness and increased conversions.

5. CONCLUSION

Based on the research findings, it can be concluded that TikTok algorithm personalization and
creative content have a positive and significant influence on the conversion rate among Generation Z
consumers in Bali. The more effective the TikTok algorithm is in presenting content aligned with users’
interests and behavior, the higher the likelihood of purchase conversion. Similarly, creative content that is
engaging, relevant, and capable of fostering consumer engagement has been proven to make a tangible
contribution to increasing the conversion rate. Furthermore, this study also indicates that the Payday Sale
period acts as a moderating variable that strengthens the influence of algorithm personalization and
creative content on conversion rates, as the promotional momentum of this event amplifies th e
effectiveness of algorithmic recommendations and the appeal of content in driving consumer purchasing
decisions.
However, this study still has several limitations. The variables used are limited to algorithmic
personalization, creative content, and the Payday Sale period, thus not yet covering other factors that may
also influence conversion rates, such as consumer trust, risk perception, service quality, app usability,
transaction security, and customer reviews. Furthermore, this study focuses solely on Generation Z
consumers in Denpasar, so the results cannot yet be generalized more broadly to other regions or
demographic groups. Additionally, since the scope of the study is limited to the TikTok Shop platform,
the results may not necessarily apply to other e-commerce platforms.
Based on these findings, TikTok Shop is advised to continue optimizing its algorithmic
personalization system by improving the relevance and consistency of product content appearing on the
For You Page FYP in line with users’ inte rests and interaction history. Businesses also need to be more
selective in choosing creators or influencers so that the resulting promotional content is more authentic,
relevant, and capable of boosting consumer trust. Additionally, the Gajian Sale program is recommended
to be developed with more varied promotional innovations, such as product bundling, exclusive offers, or
promotions based on user preferences, so that it is not merely a discount event but also a more engaging
shopping experience. For future research, it is recommended to include other relevant variables, expand
the scope of respondents, and test on different e -commerce platforms so that the research results can
provide a more comprehensive and generalizable picture.
