ABSTRACT

The development of digital technology, particularly social media —which has become
widely accessible and continues to grow rapidly —has influenced marketing patterns and
consumer behavior, especially in determining purchasing decisions. This study aims to
measure the effectiveness of affiliate content on the TikTok app in influencing purchasing
decisions for products from the Thanksinsomnia brand. The research employed a descriptive
quantitative approach using a Likert scale through the distribution of questi onnaires to 31
respondents. The results indicate that 45.2% of respondents were interested in purchasing
Thanksinsomnia products after viewing TikTok affiliate content, 43.3% felt confident about
the products after watching such content, and 51.6% stated t hat they had purchased
Thanksinsomnia products. Most respondents perceived affiliate content as providing clear
product information, attractive visuals, and trustworthy reviews from affiliates. Therefore,
TikTok Affiliate can be utilized as a digital promo tional medium capable of increasing
consumer interest while also influencing the purchasing decision -making process for
Thanksinsomnia products.

Keywords: Affiliate Content, TikTok, Purchase Decisions.

INTRODUCTION

In the era of digital technology development —particularly social media, which has a
wide reach and rapid growth—there have been significant changes in marketing patterns and
consumer behavior, especially when it comes to making purchasing decisions. Along with
advances in information technology, various marketing strategies continue to evolve, one of
which is affiliate marketing through the TikTok platform. Affiliate marketing is a digital
marketing method that provides commissions to individuals based on s ales of products
promoted through affiliate links shared on various digital platforms (Karina et al., 2023).
Additionally, TikTok affiliate marketing can be defined as an advertising model in which
companies collaborate with third parties to generate traffic and direct audiences toward the
products being offered (RevoU, 2025).
A long with the growth of affiliate marketing in the digital marketing landscape, social
media platforms have become the primary channels for distributing promotional content,
one of which is TikTok. TikTok is an app that was initially used as an entertainment platform
through the creation of creative video content, but is now also utilized as a business and
promotional tool (Mulyani et al., 2022). TikTok is considered an effective marketing
medium because it has a large user base, enabling products to reac h a wide range of

RESEARCH METHODS

This study employs a descriptive -analytical research design using a questionnaire
distributed via Google Forms to analyze TikTok affiliate content regarding purchasing
decisions for Thanksinsomnia products. The subjects of this study are individuals who have
viewed TikTok affiliate content about Thanksinsomnia products, specifically consumers
aged 17–25 who align with the brand’s primary target market. The study population focused
on TikTok users who are familiar with or have purchased Thanksinsomnia products.
Data was collected using a questionnaire distributed online via social media and the
WhatsApp app to study respondents. The questionnaire was designed using a Likert scale
with five response options: Strongly Agree (SS), Agree (S), Neutral (N), Disagree (TS), and
Strongly Disagree (STS). These options were intended to measure respondents’ reactions to
TikTok affiliate content as a source of information in their purchasing decisions regarding
Thanksinsomnia brand products.
The data obtained from the questionnaire was then analyzed to gain a better
understanding of how affiliate content on the TikTok app influences purchasing decisions
regarding Thanksinsomnia brand products.

opportunities for businesses to market their products online, including through the TikTok
platform. The local fashion brand Thanksinsomnia utilizes TikTok as a promotional medium

through affiliate content can become one of the factors consumers consider when making
purchasing decisions.

that affiliate content on TikTok helped them become familiar with Thanksinsomnia’s

utilized as a digital promotional medium through creative content and affiliate marketing.
Furthermore, respondents also noted that visual quality, presentation style, and
reviews from affiliates can increase their interest in the products. Engaging content is seen
as capable of influencing consumer perceptions before they make a purchase decision. Thus,
TikTok is not only used as a form of entertainment but also serves as an effective digital

CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

The research results show that, out of 31 respondents, it can be concluded that TikTok
affiliate content has a positive influence on purchasing decisions for Thanksinsomnia
products. This is evident from the questionnaire results, which show that 45.2% of
respondents agreed and 41.9% strongly agreed that they were interested in purchasing
Thanksinsomnia products after viewing TikTok affiliate content, while 12.9% of
respondents were neutral. Additionally, regarding the purchase consideration indicator,
32.3% of respondents agreed and 32.3% were neutral about considering a purchase of
Thanksinsomnia products, while 25.8% strongly agreed and 9.7% disagreed. Regarding the
indicator of product confidence, 43.3% of respondents strongly agreed that they felt
confident in Thanksinsomnia products after viewing affiliate content, 36.7% agreed, and
20% were neutral. The research results also showed that 51.6% of respondents strongly
agreed and 25.8% agreed that they had purchased Thanksinsomnia products, while 16.1%
of respondents were neutral and the rest disagreed. These findings indicate that TikTok
affiliate content is capable of increasing consumer interest, consideration, confidence, and
purchase decisions regarding Thanksinsomnia products. Therefore, TikTok Affiliate can be
utilized as an effective digital marketing strategy to increase consumer interest and
purchasing decisions regarding Thanksinsomnia products.
 Future research is expected to examine in greater depth the various factors that may
influence consumer purchasing decisions through TikTok affiliate content, such as users’
lifestyles, preferences for fashion products, social media usage habits, and the level of
consumer trust in affiliates who provide product reviews. In addition, future research is
advised to involve a larger number of respondents and cover a broader research area so that
