  ABSTRACT

The development of digital commerce through social media has transformed
consumer behavior in obtaining information and making online purchasing
decisions. TikTok Shop utilizes live streaming shopping and influencer marketing to
increase consumer interaction and encourage purchase intention. In this context,
consumer trust plays an important role in strengthening consumers’ willingness to
purchase.  This research investigates how live streaming shopping and influencer
marketing contribute to consumers’ purchase intention through the role of
consumer trust among TikTok Shop users in Pekanbaru. A quantitative research
design was employed using a survey approach to obtain primary data from
respondents who had experience using TikTok Shop and satisfied the established
sampling requirements. Respondents were selected through purposive sampling,
and the research instrument was structured using a Likert-scale measurement. The
collected data were subsequently processed with the Statistical Package for the
Social Sciences (SPSS) by applying multiple regression analysis, partial
significance testing, th e coefficient of determination, and mediation analysis to
examine both direct and indirect relationships among the variables. The findings
demonstrate that live streaming shopping significantly enhances consumer trust
and purchase intention. Similarly, influencer marketing contributes positively and
significantly to both consumer trust and consumers’ intention to purchase.
Consumer trust is also found to have a significant positive relationship with
purchase intention. In addition, the mediation analysis confirms that consumer trust
serves as an important mechanism through which live streaming shopping and
influencer marketing influence purchase intention. Overall, the results emphasize
that building consumer trust is essential for maximizing the effectiveness of digital
marketing activities and encouraging purchase intention among TikTok Shop
consumers in Pekanbaru.

2. Literature Review
2.1 Live Streaming Shopping
Live streaming shopping represents a form of
online commerce in which sellers use live video
to showcase products while engaging directly
with potential buyers. Unlike conventional online
shopping, this approach allows consumers to
observe product demonstrations, obtain
explanations about product features, ask
questions, and respond to promotional offers as
the session takes place. The combination of real -
time interaction and purchasing functionality
creates a more engaging shopping experience
and enables c onsumers to receive product -
related information immediately.  This interaction
creates a more dynamic shopping experience
compared with conventional e -commerce, where
consumers generally rely on static product de-
scriptions, images, and reviews  (Ratnawati &
Hanoky, 2023).
Live streaming shopping can reduce consum-
er uncertainty because consumers are able to
obtain more detailed information about products
and communicate directly with sellers or hosts.
The real -time nature of the interaction can also
create a sense of immediacy and social pres-
ence, which may encourage consumers to be-
come more engaged with the products being of-
fered. In the context of TikTok Shop, live stream-
ing shopping is particularly relevant because en-
tertainment, social interaction, product infor-
mation, an d purchasing activities are integrated
within the same digital environment  (R. Akbar et
al., 2025).

2.2 Influencer Marketing
Influencer marketing refers to an approach in
which brands collaborate with social media
personalities who have established credibility and
a strong connection with their followers. Through
their online presence, influencers can introduce
products, share personal opinions or
experiences, and recommend particular brands to
their audience. This strategy enables companies
to reach potential consumers through content that
is often perceived as more relatable and
engaging than conventional forms of advertising.
Influencers can affect consumer perceptions be-
cause their opinions and experiences may be
considered more relatable and credible than con-
ventional advertising (Novita & Laij, 2024).
The effectiveness of influencer marketing is
closely related to several characteristics, includ-
ing credibility, expertise, attractiveness, commu-
nication ability, and the compatibility between the
influencer and the promoted product. When con-
sumers perceive an influencer as knowledgeable,
honest, and trustworthy, recommendations deliv-
ered by the influencer may have a stronger influ-
ence on consumer attitudes and purchasing deci-
sions (Achmad & Sidharta, 2026).
In TikTok Shop, influencer marketing can be
integrated with live streaming activities, product
reviews, demonstrations, and direct recommen-
dations. Therefore, influencers do not merely
function as promotional media but can also be-
come an important source of information and
social influence for consumers.
This study is theoretically supported by the
Stimulus-Organism-Response (S -O-R) frame-
work, which explains how external environmental
stimuli influence an individual's internal psycho-
logical evaluation and subsequently lead to be-
havioral responses. In the context of digital com-
merce, marketing activities can function as exter-
nal stimuli that shape consumers' perceptions,
evaluations, and intentions.
In this study, Live Streaming Shopping and
Influencer Marketing are positioned as stimuli
because both represent external digital marketing
activities encountered by consumers on TikTok
Shop. Live Streaming Shopping provides con-
sumers with product demonstrations, real -time
interaction, product information, and promotional
stimuli, while Influencer Marketing provides prod-
uct recommendations and social information
through influencers.
Consumer Trust is positioned as the organism
because it represents consumers' internal evalua-
tion and confidence developed after receiving
and processing information from live shopping
activities and influencer recommendations. When
consumers perceive the information, sellers,
products, or recommendations as credible and
reliable, their level of trust may increase.
Purchase Intention represents the response
because it reflects consumers' willingness or in-
tention to consider purchasing the promoted
products. Based on this framework, the model
assumes that digital marketing stimuli may influ-
ence purchase intention directly and may also
influence purchase intention indirectly by first
developing consumer trust. Therefore, the S -O-R
perspective provides a theoretical explanation for

2.3 Consumer Trust
Consumer trust represents the level of
confidence consumers place in sellers,
influencers, brands, and digital platforms when
engaging in online transactions. It reflects
consumers’ perceptions that these parties are
trustworthy, act honestly, provide accurate
information, and are able to deliver what they
have promised. A strong sense of trust can
reduce consumers’ uncertainty and make them
feel more comfortable when considering or
making purchases through digital platforms. Trust
is particularly important in online transactions
because consumers cannot directly observe the
seller, physical product, or transaction process
before making a purchase  (Olivia & Widanti,
2026).
Consumer trust can develop through trans-
parent product information, consistent communi-
cation, fulfillment of promises, positive consumer
experiences, secure transaction processes, and
the credibility of information providers. In social
commerce, trust can also emerge through inter-
actions between consumers, sellers, hosts, and
influencers.
High levels of consumer trust can reduce per-
ceived risks associated with online purchasing.
Consumers who believe that the information pro-
vided is reliable and that the seller will fulfill the
transaction are more likely to consider purchasing
the product.

2.4 Purchase Intention
Purchase intention describes the likelihood
that consumers will consider buying a particular
product in the future. This intention generally
develops after consumers gather and assess
relevant product information, compare the
potential benefits, and weigh the possible risks
before making a purchasing decision. Therefore,
purchase intention can be viewed as an early
indication of a consumer’s readiness to proceed
with an actual purchase.  Purchase intention is
commonly regarded as an important indicator of
consumer behavior because it reflects the possi-
bility that consumers will proceed from product
evaluation to an actual purchasing decision
(Fitriningrum, 2025).
In digital commerce, purchase intention can
be influenced by product information, promotional
activities, social interaction, recommendations
from influencers, perceived value, and consumer
trust. In the context of TikTok Shop, engaging live
streaming content and persuasive influencer rec-
ommendations may encourage consumers to
develop stronger intentions to purchase  (Ong et
al., 2024).

2.5 The Relationship between Live Streaming
Shopping and Consumer Trust
Live streaming shopping provides consumers
with opportunities to obtain product information
directly and interact with sellers or hosts in real
time. The ability to ask questions, observe prod-
uct demonstrations, and receive immediate re-
sponses can increase transparency and reduce
uncertainty (Weyna et al., 2026).
When consumers perceive live streaming ac-
tivities as informative, interactive, and credible,
they are more likely to develop confidence in the
seller and the products being offered. Therefore,
better live streaming shopping experiences are
expected to strengthen consumer trust.
H1: Live Streaming Shopping has a positive and
significant effect on Consumer Trust.

2.6 The Relationship between Influencer
Marketing and Consumer Trust
Influencers can become important sources of
information for consumers because they com-
municate products through personal recommen-
dations and social media content. The credibility
and expertise of influencers can influence how
consumers evaluate the information provided
(Andreanto et al., 2025).
When consumers perceive an influencer as
honest, knowledgeable, and credible, the rec-
ommendations delivered by the influencer may
increase their confidence in the promoted prod-
ucts and brands. Therefore, stronger influencer
marketing is expected to contribute to higher
consumer trust.
H2: Influencer Marketing has a positive and
significant effect on Consumer Trust.

2.7 The Relationship between Live Streaming
Shopping and Purchase Intention
Live streaming shopping enables consumers
to observe products directly while receiving ex-
planations and interacting with hosts. These ac-
tivities can provide more complete information
and create an engaging shopping experience.
The combination of product demonstrations,
real-time interaction, limited-time promotions, and
direct communication can stimulate consumer
interest and encourage purchasing decisions.
Therefore, a more effective live streaming shop-
ping experience is expected to increase consum-
ers' purchase intention (Mandala et al., 2025).
H3: Live Streaming Shopping has a positive and
significant effect on Purchase Intention.

2.8 The Relationship between Influencer
Marketing and Purchase Intention
Influencer marketing can influence con-
sumer purchase intention through recommenda-
tions, product demonstrations, reviews, and per-
sonal experiences shared by influencers. Con-
sumers may perceive recommendations from
credible influencers as useful information when
evaluating products (Azzahra et al., 2024).
If an influencer has strong credibility and
communicates product benefits effectively, con-
sumers may develop greater interest in the pro-
moted product. Consequently, effective influencer
marketing is expected to increase consumer pur-
chase intention.
H4: Influencer Marketing has a positive and sig-
nificant effect on Purchase Intention.

2.10 Consumer Trust as an Intervening
Variable
Consumer trust may serve as a bridge
between consumers’ experiences with live
streaming shopping and their intention to make a
purchase. In this role, the level of trust developed
through live shopping interactions can help
explain how such activities ultimately encourage
consumers to consider purchasing a product.
Live streaming shopping may initially provide
consumers with information and interaction that
increase their confidence in the seller or product.
This increased confidence can subsequently en-
courage stronger purchase intention.
Similarly, influencer marketing may influence
purchase intention indirectly through consumer
trust. Credible influencers can strengthen con-
sumers' confidence in the products they recom-
mend, which may subsequently increase their
willingness to purchase (Syahrin et al., 2025).
Therefore, consumer trust is positioned as a
mechanism that explains how live streaming
shopping and influencer marketing can influence
purchase intention.

3.3 Research Location and Time
This research was conducted in Pekanbaru,
Riau Province, Indonesia, focusing on consumers
who use TikTok Shop. Pekanbaru was selected
because of its active social media usage and
digital commerce activities, making it relevant to
the objectives of this study.
The research was conducted from May to

search instruments, questionnaire distribution,
data collection, data processing, and data analy-
sis. The questionnaire was distributed online to
respondents who met the predetermined re-
search criteria.

3. 4 Types and Sources of Data
This study uses quantitative data, consist-
ing of numerical data obtained from respondents'
responses to the research variables: Live
Streaming Shopping, Influencer Marketing, Con-
sumer Trust, and Purchase Intention.
The data sources consist of primary and
secondary data. Primary data were collected di-
rectly from respondents through an online ques-
tionnaire distributed to TikTok Shop consumers in
Pekanbaru who met the research criteria. The
questionnaire used a five-point Likert scale.
Secondary data were obtained from sup-
porting sources, including scientific journals,
books, reports, and relevant publications used to
strengthen the theoretical foundation and support
the research discussion.

3.6 Data Collection Techniques

3.7 Data Analysis Technique
The statistical analysis was performed with
the Statistical Package for the Social Sciences
(SPSS) to assess the proposed relationships
between Live Streaming Shopping, Influencer
Marketing, Consumer Trust, and Purchase Inten-
tion. Several analytical procedures were applied
in stages, beginning with descriptive statistics
and instrument testing, followed by classical as-
sumption testing, regression analysis, hypothesis
testing, and mediation analysis.
Descriptive statistics were first used to pre-
sent an overview of the respondents and summa-
rize the response patterns for each study varia-
ble. The quality of the research instrument was
then evaluated through validity and reliability test-
ing. Pearson Product -Moment correlation was
employed to assess item validity, whereas
Cronbach’s Alpha was used to determine the
internal consistency of the measurement instru-
ments. Before conducting regression analysis,
the dataset was examined through normality,
multicollinearity, and heteroscedasticity tests to
determine whether the underlying regression
assumptions were adequately satisfied.
Multiple linear regression was subsequently
applied to evaluate the direct relationships be-
tween the variables. The analysis examined the
effects of Live Streaming Shopping and Influenc-
er Marketing on Consumer Trust, as well as their
respective effects on Purchase Intention when
Consumer Trust was included in the model. Par-
tial hypothesis testing was performed using the t -
test to determine whether each independent vari-
able made a statistically significant contribution to
the dependent variable. The coeffici ent of deter-
mination (R²) was also examined to indicate how
well the regression model accounted for varia-
tions in the dependent variable.
To investigate the proposed indirect rela-
tionships, a mediation analysis was conducted to
assess the role of Consumer Trust in linking Live
Streaming Shopping and Influencer Marketing
with Purchase Intention. The mediation effect
was evaluated using either the Sobel test or the
PROCESS Macro available in SPSS. All statisti-
cal decisions were made using a 5% significance
threshold (α = 0.05).

Intention. Live streaming activities not only pro-
vide consumers with information and interactive
experiences but can also build trust, which sub-
sequently encourages purchase intention.
Consumers who receive transparent product
information, interact with hosts, and observe
product demonstrations directly may develop
greater confidence in the product and seller. The
trust that develops can subsequently increase
consumers’ willingness to consider making a pur-
chase. Thus, the relationship between Live
Streaming Shopping and Purchase Intention can
occur not only directly but also through Consumer
Trust.

The results indicate that Consumer Trust

5. Conclusion and Recommendations
This study examined the effects of Live
Streaming Shopping and Influencer Marketing on
Consumer Trust and Purchase Intention among
TikTok Shop consumers in Pekanbaru. The find-
ings demonstrate that Live Streaming Shopping
has a positive and significant effect on Consumer
Trust and Purchase Intention. Influencer Market-
ing also has a positive and significant effect on
Consumer Trust and Purchase Intention. In addi-
tion, Consumer Trust has a positive and signifi-
cant effect on Purchase Intention.
The mediation analysis further demonstrates
that Consumer Trust significantly mediates the
relationship between Live Streaming Shopping
and Purchase Intention. Consumer Trust also
significantly mediates the relationship between
Influencer Marketing and Purchase Intention.
These findings indicate that digital marketing ac-
tivities influence purchase intention not only di-
rectly but also through the development of con-
sumer confidence and trust.
The theoretical contribution of this study is to
provide an integrated explanation of how two
digital marketing activities, namely Live Stream-
ing Shopping and Influencer Marketing, influence
Purchase Intention through Consumer Trust. By
positioning Consumer Trust as an intervening
variable, the study provides a clearer explanation
of the mechanism through which digital marketing
activities may shape consumers' behavioral inten-
tions in a social commerce environment.
From a practical perspective, businesses
operating through TikTok Shop should improve
the quality of live shopping by providing clear
product information, demonstrating products di-
rectly, responding to consumer questions, and
maintaining relevant promotional activities. Influ-
encer selection should also consider credibility,
expertise, communication ability, and relevance
to the promoted product. At the same time, busi-
nesses should maintain consumer trust by provid-
ing accurate information, fulfilling promise s, en-
suring transaction security, and maintaining con-
sistency between promotional messages and
actual products.
This study has several limitations. First, the
study involved 96 respondents in Pekanbaru,
which limits the generalization of the findings to
other geographical areas or larger populations.
Second, the study focused on four main con-
structs and therefore did not consider other fac-
tors that may influence purchase intention in so-
cial commerce. Future studies may expand the
sample size, include consumers from other cities
or regions, and examine additional variables or
alternative analytical approaches to provide a
broader understanding of purchase intention in
digital commerce.
