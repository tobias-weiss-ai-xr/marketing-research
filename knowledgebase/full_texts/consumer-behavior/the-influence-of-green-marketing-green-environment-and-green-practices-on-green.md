Abstract

Green consumer behavior has become an important focus in understanding purchasing decisions for
environmentally friendly products amid current environmental issues. The promotion of eco -friendly
vehicles has been widely implemented; however, the situation in Wonosobo indicates that the
adoption of electric motorcycles remains very limited. This study aims to examine the influence of
green marketing, green environment, and green practice on green consumer behavior. The
independent variables in this study are green marketing, green environment, and green practice, while
the dependent variable is green consumer behavior. The data used are primary data and are analyzed
using multiple regression analysis. The results of this study indicate that green marketing, g reen
environment, and green practice have a positive and significant effect on green consumer behavior.

Keywords: Green Marketing, Green Environment, Green Practice, Green Consumer Behavior.

INTRODUCTION
Over the past decades, environmental issues have become central in global development due
to increasing challenges such as climate change, resource depletion, and pollution, which have
strengthened the need for sustainable production and consumption system s. This shift has also
increased public awareness of environmentally responsible behavior, reflected in stronger pro -
environmental values and a growing preference for sustainable products. In this context, Green
Consumer Behavior serves as an important fra mework for understanding how environmental
considerations influence consumer decisions, including the choice of eco -friendly products and
broader efforts to reduce environmental impact and support sustainability (Sholikin, 2024).
According to Mahrinasari MS (2020), Green Consumer Behavior reflects the extent to which
individuals demonstrate awareness and responsibility toward the environmental impacts of the
products they consume. Within this domain, the effectiveness of Green Consumer Behavior  is
shaped by a range of driving factors, one of which is green marketing. Green marketing represents
a strategic approach employed by firms to communicate the sustainability values and ecological
advantages of their products to the market. Its primary objective is to cultivate favorable consumer
perceptions and stimulate purchase intentions by emphasizing environmental benefits and ethical
value propositions (Zaky & Purnami, 2020; Navarro et al., 2014). Furthermore, prior studies in the
marketing and sustainability literature highlight that well-executed green marketing initiatives can

enhance consumer trust, strengthen brand positioning, and ultimately encourage environmentally

The concept of a green environment refers to systematic efforts to create environmental
conditions that support sustainability through efficient resource use and responsible waste
management. It also encompasses the broader external context —both physical a nd socio -
cultural—that facilitates environmentally oriented consumer behavior, including access to
information, institutional support, and ecological infrastructure (Anggraeni et al., 2021). Such
conditions help reduce barriers to green consumption and promote environmental awareness and
pro-environmental norms, making individuals more likely to engage in sustainable consumption
when supported by an enabling environment (Emekci, 2019; Andriana & Arifin, 2024). Additionally,
supportive environmental contexts contribute not only to behavior formation but also to long-term
consistency in sustainable behavior (Steg & Vlek, 2009; Gifford, 2014).
Green practice refers to a set of operational actions carried out by organizations to reduce
environmental impacts through efficient use of resources, such as energy and water conservation,
use of eco -friendly materials, waste reduction, recycling, and the  implementation of sustainable
production and supply chain systems. In sustainability and management literature, green practice
is widely recognized as a key aspect of corporate environmental responsibility that supports
organizational legitimacy and compe titive advantage (Hart & Dowell, 2011; Khan et al., 2021).
When consumers perceive that a company consistently implements strong green practices, it
increases their trust in the company and its products, as transparency in environmental
performance reduces perceived risk and enhances credibility (Suki, 2016). This trust then positively
influences product evaluation and purchase intention, leading to more environmentally responsible
consumer behavior. In addition, green practices also serve as strategic signals that strengthen brand
reputation and build long-term customer loyalty, especially in markets with growing environmental
awareness (Yadav & Pathak, 2017; Khan et al., 2021).
Despite the strong global momentum toward the transition to electric vehicles, the adoption
of Uwinfly electric motorcycles in Wonosobo remains relatively low, indicating limited local market
penetration. Data from the Indonesian Ministry of Energy and Min eral Resources (ESDM) in 2024
show that the total number of battery -based electric vehicles in Indonesia has reached
approximately 133,225 units, of which the majority —around 109,576 units —consists of electric
motorcycles (Shiddiq, 2024). However, this figure is still considerably small when compared to the
millions of conventional motorcycles that continue to dominate the transportation market,
highlighting the slow pace of substitution from fossil -fuel-based vehicles to electric mobility in
Indonesia (Kompas, 2024; Detik Finance, 2024).
Despite the increasing focus on sustainable transportation, electric motorcycle adoption in
Indonesia remains very low, accounting for only around 1% of the total motorcycle market as of
August 2024 (Dio Denanjaya, 2024). A similar condition is observed in Wonosobo, where the use of
Uwinfly electric motorcycles is still very limited and has not achieved significant market penetration.
This situation is influenced by several barriers, such as limited charging infrastructure, higher initial
purchase costs com pared to conventional motorcycles, and insufficient public information and
education regarding the long -term benefits and environmental advantages of electric vehicles
(Moestopo, 2023). Previous studies also show that infrastructure constraints, economic factors, and
low consumer awareness are key obstacles to electric vehicle adoption in Indonesia (Lazuardy et
al., 2024; Rahman et al., 2025). This condition indicates a phenomenon gap between strong policy
support for green mobility and the relatively low l evel of actual adoption at the local level,
particularly in regions like Wonosobo where conventional motorcycles still dominate daily
transportation despite growing environmental awareness.
This study examines the influence of green marketing, green environment, and green
practice on green consumer behavior. The importance of this research is driven by the low adoption
of electric motorcycles in Wonosobo, which shows a gap between national po licies and local

LITERATURE REVIEW AND HYPOTHESIS FORMULATION
This study is grounded in the Theory of Planned Behavior (TPB) introduced by Ajzen (1991),
which posits that individual behavior is not an impulsive action but rather the outcome of a rational
decision-making process influenced by three key determinants: a ttitude toward the behavior,
subjective norms, and perceived behavioral control. Attitude refers to an individual’s positive or
negative evaluation of performing a specific behavior, subjective norms represent perceived social
pressure from significant others, while perceived behavioral control reflects an individual’s belief in
their capability to perform the behavior effectively. This theoretical framework has been widely
validated in explaining environmentally responsible behaviors, including sustainable transportation
choices, green purchasing behavior, and recycling activities (Bamberg & Möser, 2007; Armitage &
Conner, 2001).
In the context of this study, TPB serves as the primary analytical framework to understand
consumer behavior in the adoption of Uwinfly electric motorcycles. A favorable attitude toward
environmentally friendly vehicles, social influence from peers and sur rounding communities, and
strong perceived behavioral control —such as the belief that electric motorcycles are practical,
affordable, and easy to use—are expected to shape consumers’ behavioral intentions, which in turn
influence their actual purchasing and usage decisions. This is particularly relevant in the context of
Wonosobo, where adoption is still limited despite increasing environmental awareness and policy
support for green mobility
Green Consumer Behavior
Green Consumer Behavior in this study refers to the entire consumer decision -making
process, including information search, evaluation of alternatives, purchase, usage, and disposal of
products or services, which is based on awareness and concern for environmental impacts in order
to support ecosystem sustainability (Prothero et al., 2011). This concept emphasizes that
consumption behavior does not stop at the purchase stage, but also includes consumer
responsibility for the environmental impacts throughout the product life cycle, in line with the
perspectives of green marketing and sustainable consumption developed in modern consumer
behavior literature (Kotler & Keller, 2016).
Green Marketing
Green Marketing is a strategic marketing approach that not only focuses on fulfilling
consumer needs and preferences but also integrates sustainability values and environmental
responsibility into all elements of the marketing mix, from product design to promotional activities.
This approach emphasizes company efforts to create added value that benefits both consumers
and the environment through more environmentally friendly marketing practices (Kotler & Keller,
2016). In the context of this study, Green Ma rketing is positioned as a variable that reflects the
extent to which a company implements environmentally based marketing strategies in its business
activities, which can influence consumers’ perceptions and responses toward green products
(Purwanto et al., 2025; Akbarina & Sari, 2024).

Green Environment
Green Environment in this study is defined as the overall external context, both physical and
social, that influences and facilitates Green Consumer Behavior through the provision of relevant
environmental information, adequate green infrastructure, and the formation of social norms and
values that support ecological sustainability. This environment includes physical aspects such as the
availability of waste management facilities and eco-friendly products, as well as social aspects such
as social pressure, habits, and collective environmental awareness that encourage sustainable
consumption behavior (Ajzen, 1991; Hassan et al., 2026). In the Indonesian context, the green
environment is also shaped by government policies and programs that promote waste reduct ion,
recycling activities, and environmental education as efforts to strengthen sustainable consumption
behavior (Amaliah et al, 2023).
Green Practice
Green Practice is a concrete manifestation of sustainability commitment, implemented
through various environmentally responsible operational actions. This practice encompasses all
activities, from production processes, distribution, to consumption, which are designed to minimize
negative environmental impacts, such as emission reduction, efficient resource utilization, and
more responsible waste management. In its implementation, green practice also reflects the
integration of environmentally friendly produ ct and service innovations as part of a company’s
sustainability strategy (Hariadi et al., 2023).
Theoretical Framework
The Relationship between Green Marketing and Green Consumer Behavior
According to Anggoro (2024), green marketing refers to all marketing activities that are
environmentally responsible, aiming to minimize negative environmental impacts. In practice,
green marketing serves as a strategic tool for producers to communicate su stainability values and
the environmentally friendly aspects of their products to the broader public. In line with this, a
study conducted by Putri et al. (2021), which examined the effect of green marketing on green
consumer behavior at Fore Coffee Jakarta, found that green marketing has a positive and significant
influence on environmentally oriented consumer behavior. Thus, it can be assumed that the more
precise and relevant the implementation of green marketing by Uwinfly electric motorcycle
producers, the higher the likelihood of consumers in Wonosobo accepting and purchasing the
product.
H1: Green marketing has a positive effect on green consumer behavior.
The Relationship between Green Environment and Green Consumer Behavior
The Environmentally Responsible Behavior (ERB) theory developed by Hines, Hungerford,
and Tomera, as cited in Sukma (2024), emphasizes the importance of factors such as personal
responsibility and knowledge, both of which are strongly influenced by the env ironment. An
environment that provides easy access to relevant information, supports the availability of eco -
friendly product infrastructure, and is reinforced by pro -environmental social norms significantly
facilitates individuals in adopting green consumption patterns. This is consistent with Sukma (2024),
who found that environmental knowledge has a positive and significant effect on green consumer
behavior, confirming that individuals’ understanding of environmental issues is a key prerequisite
for environmentally oriented behavior. Based on these findings, it can be concluded that a green
environment plays a vital role in facilitating and encouraging green consumer behavior.

RESEARCH METHODS
The type of research conducted in this study is quantitative research. In this study, the
independent variables are green marketing (X1), green environment (X2), and green practice (X3),
while the dependent variable is green consumer behavior (Y). Accordin g to Sugiyono (2019),
population is a generalization area consisting of objects or subjects that have certain qualities and
characteristics determined by the researcher to be studied and then concluded. The population in
this study consists of all consumer s of Uwinfly electric motorcycles in Wonosobo Regency. The
sample in this study initially consisted of 60 respondents; however, the researcher rounded it up to
115 respondents in order to minimize data errors.
Data collection in this study was conducted by distributing questionnaires. The researcher
collected data by providing several written statements to consumers of Uwinfly electric
motorcycles in Wonosobo. The collected data were then analyzed using SPSS version 25. The data
analysis procedures included validity tests, reliability tests, and hypothesis testing.

CONCLUSION
The data analysis results in this study regarding the influence of Green Marketing, Green
Environment, and Green Practice on Green Consumer Behavior among Uwinfly electric motorcycle
consumers in Wonosobo show that the number of respondents in this study w as 115. The data
were then analyzed using SPSS version 25 with the multiple linear regression method, and the
results are presented as follows:
a. Green Marketing has a positive and significant effect on Green Consumer Behavior among
Uwinfly electric motorcycle consumers in Wonosobo. The better the marketing strategy that
emphasizes environmentally friendly products and processes, the greater the likelihood that
consumers will use products that are environmentally friendly and support ecosystem
sustainability.
b. Green Environment shows a positive and significant effect on Green Consumer Behavior
among Uwinfly electric motorcycle consumers in Wonosobo. The better the knowledge and
awareness of environmental protection and nature conservation, the greater the likeli hood
that consumers will use products that are environmentally friendly and support ecosystem
sustainability.
c. Green Practice has a positive and significant effect on Green Consumer Behavior among
Uwinfly electric motorcycle consumers in Wonosobo. The better the implementation of
ensuring environmentally friendly products and supporting sustainability principles in various
