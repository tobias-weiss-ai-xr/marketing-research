INTRODUCTION
The use of metaverse technology has experienced significant growth in line
with its ability to provide immersive and interactive digital experiences for users.
Users' responses and attitudes towards the metaver se are influenced by their
assessment of the benefits obtained, the ease of operating the platform, the
quality of social interaction, and perceptions of the level of engagement and
realism of the virtual environment (Demirci, 2024; Tang et al., 2025). The  user
experience that is perceived as comfortable, fun, and realistic encourages the
formation of positive attitudes, which further increases the desire of users to
return to the platform and strengthens the tendency to make transactions or
purchases of digital products, both for the purposes of avatars and other virtual
activities. Thus, the quality of virtual experiences offered by the metaverse plays
an important role in determining the sustainability of use and purchase decisions
in the digital ecosystem (Demirci, 2024).
The use of metaverse technology continues to increase, including on the
Roblox platform which is now developing into an immersive virtual
environment where users can interact, customize avatars, and make various
digital transacti ons. Through 3D visualization, a high level of interactivity, and
dynamic social activities, Roblox delivers experiences that are close to reality,
triggering the emergence of telepresence, immersive engagement, and the
perception of realism. Ki et al. (20 25) explain that Roblox is one of the  most
evolving transitory forms of the metaverse , where avatar personalization and real -
time social interaction play an important role in increasing consumer
engagement and influencing their attitudes and interest in digital purchases. This
phenomenon indicates that metaverse experiences can shape psychological
processes and user behavior—according to the Stimulus–Organism–Response
(S–O–R) framework —making Roblox the right research object to examine reuse
trends and purchase intentions in virtual spaces.
In the metaverse shopping activity, when the user feels fully immersed in
the virtual environment (sense of immersion) and perceives the digital world as
something very real (perceived reality), the experience becomes an initial trigger
or stimulus within the framework of the S-O-R theory. This stimulus then affects
the psychological state of the user (Organism), resulting in a positive attitude
towards the  future reuse of the metaverse (the attitude to reuse). When the
experience is perceived to be fun, convincing, and realistic, this positive attitude
develops into real behavior (Response), which is the emergence of an intention
to make a purchase in the metaverse (purchase intent). The stronger the sense of
engagement and realism that users feel, the more likely they are to want to return
to the metaverse, which ultimately encourages them to make purchases within
it. These findings are in line with resear ch that explains that the immersive nature
of the metaverse is able to fully attract users and increase their behavioral
tendencies (Tao et al., 2025), as well as other research that states that high
interaction and realism in the metaverse shape user attitudes that then influence
purchase intent (Zhang et al., 2025a).

LITERATURE REVIEW
Stimulus–Organism–Response Theory (S–O–R)
Theory Stimulus –Organism–Response (S–O–R) According to (Kumar,
2025) explains that individual behaviors arise in response to stimuli received
from the environment. In the context of digital consumer behavior, this theory is
used to understand how elements of the digital environment (stimulus) such as
technology, interfaces, or media experiences, affect the internal psychological
state of users (organisms), which then result in behavioral actions or responses
(responses). This model asserts that a person's behavior does not arise
spontaneously, but through an internal mediation process that involves cognitive
and affective reactions.
Variable X1 : Perceived Augmentation
Augmentation refers to the extent to which virtual and physical elements
are harmoniously brought together so that users experience a perception that it
is as if the virtual world and the real world are no longer separate. In the

perception of digital products.
Variable X2 : Perceived Interactivity
Interactivity is the ability of a digital system to enable two -way
communication between users and platforms. In the metaverse, interactivity is
not just limited to clicks or inputs, but includes interactive experiences between
avatars, virtual  objects, and other users simultaneously. The high level of
interactivity creates a sense of control and active participation that increases
satisfaction and emotional attachment to the platform (Kim & Kim, 2021).
According to (Cauberghe, 2010), interactiv ity consists of three main
dimensions: user control, system responsiveness, and two-way communication.
In a metaverse  experience, users can transform digital objects, interact with
others directly, and receive instant feedback. (Jafar, Rana, Muhammad et al., 2025) It
shows that interactivity has a significant influence on the formation of telepresence, because
active interaction makes users feel "present" in the virtual world. Study (Tang et al., 2025) It
also confirms that interactive experiences in the metaverse strengthen users' emotional
engagement, which ultimately increases the purchase intent of digital products.
Variable Y1: Telepresence
Telepresence refers to a psychological state when a person feels present and
interacts in a virtual place, even though they are physically still in the real world
(Steuer, n.d.). This concept is an important foundation in the study of virtual
reality and t he metaverse because it describes the extent to which individuals
experience cognitive and emotional engagement with the digital environment.
According to the findings (Barta et al., 2023) The higher the level of telepresence
perceived, the greater the tendency of the user to treat the virtual environment
as if it were the real world. This can strengthen the sense of emotional
engagement and enrich the overall user experience.
Variable Y2: Sense of Immersion
A sense of immersion is a psychological state when the user feels
completely immersed in the digital experience and loses awareness of the real
world around him. In the metaverse, immersion is created through a combination
of virtual reality technology, 3D visualization, and real -time interaction that
gives rise to a multisensory  experience (Tao et al., 2025).
Variable Y3: Perceived Realism
Perceived realism is the user's perception of the extent to which the virtual
world resembles the real world in terms of visual appearance, interaction, and
authenticity of the experience. In the metaverse, realism results from a
combination of graphic quality, sensory effects, and consistency of interaction
that creates the illusion of the real world (Zhang et al., 2025).
Variable Z1: Attitude to reuse metaverse
Attitude toward reuse indicates the tendency of consumers to return to
using metaverse platforms in the future, while purchase intent indicates the
desire to make real transactions on the platform. Based on (Behavior & Ajzen,

METHODOLOGY
This study uses a quantitative approach with a type of causal research that
aims to analyze the cause-and-effect relationship between the characteristics  of
metaverse technology and consumer behavior on the Roblox platform as a virtual
e-commerce space. The research framework is based on the Stimulus –Organism–
Response (S –O–R) model, which explains that stimuli in the form of perceived
augmentation and perceive d interactivity will give rise to users' psychological
responses in the form of telepresence, a sense of immersion, and perceived
realism. Furthermore, these psychological experiences  affect attitudes towards
reuse and purchase intent in the metaverse environment. The S–O–R model was
chosen because it is able to explain how immersive digital experiences in Roblox
can shape consumer behavior in making virtual purchase activities.
The population in this study is all users of the Roblox platform in Indonesia
who have carried out digital economy activities, such as purchasing virtual items
or transactions using Robux. Given the large population and spread online, this
study uses a non -probability sampling technique with the snowball sampling
method. The techniq ue was chosen because Roblox's user community has a
network that is interconnected and active on various digital platforms.
Determination of the number of samples refers to Roscoe's rule which states that
the minimum sample size is ten times the number of variables studied. With
seven research variables, the minimum number of samples obtained was 70
respondents. However, to increase the strength of statistics, reduce the possibility
of sampling errors, and anticipate incomplete data, the number of samples w as
increased to 83 respondents so that it was considered adequate for the needs of
research analysis.
The data used consists of primary data and secondary data. Primary data
was obtained directly through the dissemination of questionnaires and
interviews t o Roblox users who met the research criteria. The research
instruments were compiled based on six main constructs, namely perceived
augmentation, perceived interactivity, telepresence, sense of immersion,
perceived realism, attitude towards reuse, and purc hase intention in the
metaverse. All indicators are measured using statements that describe the user's

Before the analysis, the research instrument was first tested for  validity and
reliability. The validity test is used to determine the level of accuracy of the
instrument in measuring the research variable, with the criterion that the
statement item is declared valid if the calculated r value is greater than the r of
the table. The reliability test is carried out using the Split Half method, where a
variable is declared reliable if it has a coefficient value greater than 0.297. Data
that have met the requirements for validity and reliability are then analyzed
using path analysis to test the direct and indirect relationships between variables
contained in the research model. The pathway analysis was chosen because it is
a development of  multiple regression that is able to test the causal relationship
simultaneously accordi ng to the Stimulus –Organism–Response (S –O–R)
framework. The entire process of data processing and analysis is carried out with
the help of Analysis of Moment Structures (AMOS) software.

CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
Based on the results of the path analysis in the study "Online Shopping
Beyond Borders: The Metaverse Revolution in E -Commerce and Consumer
Behavior" with the metaverse object Roblox, it can be concluded that the majority
of users have a medium level of en gagement with a frequency of 1 –5 times per
week, where male users tend to be more active than women. The test results show
that the initial model has not been able to explain all causal relationships
optimally because some pathways, particularly those invo lving Sense of
Immersion, are not yet significant. However, after modifications, all structural
relationships become significant, resulting in a more robust, logical, and
theoretically consistent model. Perceived Interactivity has proven to be the most
dominant factor in forming Telepresence  compared to Perceived Augmentation,
while Telepresence acts as the main mediator that channels the influence of
exogenous variables on Perceived Realism and Attitude to Reuse Metaverse. In
addition, Purchase Intentions in the Metaverse are significantly influenced by
both the Attitude to Reuse Metaverse and Perceived Interactivity, which shows
that consumer purchasing behavior in the metaverse environment is formed
through direct and indirect influences. Overall, the results of the goodness of fit
test showed that the improved model met most of the eligibility criteria and was
declared feasible to use to explain the causal relationships between variables in
the context of metaverse-based e-commerce on the Roblox platform.
