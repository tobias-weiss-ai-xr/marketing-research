Abstract. Due to the growth of social media, customized services such as user generated
content, product information and advertisements have become widespread. Social media
uses algorithms to study users' Internet activities and other preferences, thus presenting the
most appropriate information to the users. This trend has changed the way people obtain
information about products and take purchasing decisions. The aim of this paper is to
investigate the effect of social media's recommendation system on consumer's buying
behavior. By reviewing the literature, the study evaluates the advantages and disadvantages
of personalized recommendations. From the positive aspect, it improves the relevance of
information, shortens the user's searching time, arouses the hidden demand and simplifies
the shopping procedure. However, from the negative perspective, personalized
recommendation systems might cause unnecessary purchases, raise worries about the
security of personal information and result in "recommendation fatigue" because of
continuous or repetitive contents. According to these points, this paper puts forward some
suggestions for the platforms, the consumers and the regulatory authorities, such as: the
platforms should improve the protection of user's data while offering high quality
recommendations; the consumers should form reasonable consumption concepts; the
regulatory authorities should set up standards and recommend suitable technologies to
guarantee their proper application.
Keywords: Personalized recommendations, consumer behavior, social media platforms
1. Introduction
Due to the progress of digital technology and the extensive use of social media websites,
personalized recommendation systems have gradually become an important method for linking users
with relevant information. For example, on TikTok and Instagram, algorithms are applied to study
the user's browsing habits, interaction histories and interest preferences, so as to provide precise
content and advertisements. This data-based recommendation system not only affects the way users
acquire information, but also indirectly influences their purchasing decisions.
The combination of individualized recommendations, short video contents and e-commerce
functions assists the users in finding, assessing and purchasing products during the browsing
process, thus reducing the time consumers spend on decision-making. During this procedure, the
consumer's behavior has also changed. For instance, consumers often depend more on the

2. The development status of personalized recommendations and user consumption behavior
on social media platforms
2.1. The development status of personalized recommendations on social media platforms
Along with the rapid development of artificial intelligence, big data and machine learning,
personalized recommendation systems have been widely used in mainstream social media such as
TikTok, RedNote and Instagram. The platform continuously analyzes user behavior data such as
browsing, likes, comments and stays, builds interest portraits, and realizes accurate push of content
and products. At present, personalized recommendation has been upgraded from auxiliary function
to platform core mechanism, which has become a key means to enhance user stickiness and business
transformation [1, 7].
From the perspective of application scale, the recommended technology covers the whole
platform. With content streaming as the main platform for short video, social e-commerce focuses
on product and advertisement recommendation, which has become an important bridge connecting
users and consumption information. The algorithm continues to iterate, from collaborative filtering
to deep learning, and the recommendation is more accurate [8]. The scene is also constantly
expanding, realizing the deep integration of content, advertisements and commodities, and
promoting the natural occurrence of consumption [2, 9].
In terms of effectiveness, personalized recommendation greatly improves information efficiency
and reduces user screening costs. At the same time, it also shortens the consumption decision-

2.2. Current situation of user's consumption behavior
In the context of the popularity of personalized recommendation, users' consumption behavior has
changed significantly. Online consumption habits have become the mainstream: social media has
become the core scene of information acquisition, decision-making influence and even transaction
completion. The "content-driven consumption" model is becoming more and more common [9].
Consumer behavior possesses three characteristics. First of all, the decision-making procedure is
very individualized. People rely on the system for proper suggestions which can decrease the time
spent in searching and comparing and enhance efficiency as well as increase the dependence on
platform [2]. Secondly, the purchase rate has significantly increased. The regular updates allow users
to have frequent contact with the consumption environment and the random and immediate
purchases also contribute to this [3]. Furthermore, impulsive buying is more noticeable. Emotional
materials like short videos and live broadcasts can easily stimulate the desire to buy quickly,
especially among the young people [4, 10]. At the same time, the recommended results are more
appropriate to the users' interests, and it is convenient to build an 'information cocoon', thus forming
a single consuming habit. In summary, personalized recommendation is continually modifying the
consumer behavior and promoting the purchase.
3. Specific impact of personalized recommendation on consumer behavior of social media
platform
3.1. Positive effect
Stimulate consumer demand and promote consumption growth. Personalized recommendation can
not only meet the existing needs of users but also stimulate potential consumer demand to some
extent. Through in-depth analysis of user behavior data, the platform can predict users' interest
trends. And through personalized push and scene content display, guide users to generate new
consumption desires. For example, in the short video platform, goods are often presented in the form
of "lifestyle" or "usage scene", which makes users have the motivation to buy in the entertainment
process [9]. The existing research shows that personalized advertising can enhance impulse buying
behavior by enhancing users' perceived value, such as emotional value and social value [3].
Meanwhile, the accuracy, relevance and timeliness in personalized recommendation will further
promote consumer behavior by stimulating "urge to buy" [4]. As people make purchases quickly,
they show a tendency towards speed and repeated buying. Therefore, personalized recommendations
will improve the users' habit of buying repeatedly. The consumption quantity and frequency will
considerably rise when users are always provided with information about items that match their
preferences.
Enhance the consumption feeling and raise the possibility of customers staying. Personalized
recommendations remarkably improve the consumption feeling by always providing information
and goods that suit the customers' preferences. On the one hand, the good compatibility of the
recommended information will make customers feel more satisfied when browsing, thus improving
the entire experience [2]. On the other hand, personalized recommendations can increase the novelty
and credibility of the information and also increase the positive attitudes of the customers towards
the system and advertisements. This will induce the customers to have more purchasing intentions

3.2. Negative effect
Cause excessive consumption and impulsive consumption. Although personalized recommendation
can promote consumption, its overuse may also lead to an increase in irrational consumption
behavior. The platform constantly pushes high-attractive goods and creates consumption scenes, so
users are easily stimulated to make an instant purchase impulse, which makes them make
consumption decisions without full comparison and rational judgment [11]. Studies show that
personalized recommendation enhances users' impulse buying tendency through emotional
stimulation and value perception. Particularly in the short video and live broadcast environment,
emotional arousal has a significant impact on consumer behavior [10]. In addition, the continuous
push mechanism of the recommendation system will make users repeatedly contact similar products,
thus continuously strengthening their purchase intention and even leading to excessive consumption.
Although this algorithm-based purchasing habit can increase the platform's revenue in the near
future, it may also cause economic burden to the consumers and the difficulty in controlling the
consumption.
Violating users' privacy and decreasing consumer trust. Personalized recommendations depend on
the gathering and examination of user information. Thus, although the accuracy of the
recommendations is improved, there are also privacy risks involved. When users utilize the system,
their browsing habits, interest inclinations and even personal information will be registered and
studied, which might cause worries about the security and privacy protection of the users [5]. From
the consumers' point of view, they compare the "advantages" (e.g., convenience and relevance) with
the "dangers" (e.g., privacy infringement) when using personalized services, a situation called
"privacy calculation". If users consider the privacy risks to be high, their confidence in the system
will decline, which consequently influences their purchasing behavior [6]. Precise recommendations
may make users feel supervised, resulting in a stronger feeling of being intruded upon. Such a bad
experience might make users dislike the system, and reduce their usage frequency, thereby affecting
their long-term consumption.
Generate recommendation fatigue and decrease the desire to accept new information. If the
suggested materials are monotonous or of low quality, it is likely to cause the users' recommendation
fatigue. As the recommendation system usually recommends based on the users' previous actions,
the users may receive the same type of information repeatedly, resulting in a lack of novelty and
interest. Previous studies show that moderate repetition of recommendations can enhance the users'
memory. However, excessive repetition is regarded as a disturbance and causes negative feelings
and aversion in the users [5]. Furthermore, if the recommendation frequency is very high or the
content lacks variety, the users might experience information overload and reduce their attention to
the recommended materials. In addition, annoying recommendations may affect the users' attitude
towards advertisements, leading them to reject the recommended items and thereby lowering their

4. Suggestions for optimizing personalized recommendations and guiding rational
consumption
From the previous analysis, people can see that personalized recommendation improves
consumption efficiency and promotes consumption growth, but it also brings some problems such as
impulsive consumption, privacy risks and recommendation fatigue. Therefore, it is necessary to put
forward targeted optimization suggestions from the platform, users and regulatory levels in order to
achieve a balanced development between personalized recommendation and rational consumption.
4.1. Platform level
Improve the recommendation algorithm and increase the accuracy of the recommendations. Social
media sites should continue to optimize the recommendation algorithm model. At the same time, the
number of inaccurate and repeated recommendations should be reduced to lessen the information
burden for the users. On the one hand, the platform can enhance its ability to recognize users'
preferences by utilizing multiple types of user data to make more appropriate content matches [1].
On the other hand, diversity factors should be incorporated into the recommendation system to avoid
the influence of users' past behaviors. This can prevent the "information cocoon room" phenomenon
and provide users with more different kinds of information [2]. The platform can change the
recommendation strategy to achieve a proper balance between the relevance and novelty of the
recommended results, which will improve the exploration experience and satisfy the users' demands.
Thus, it will be beneficial not only for the users' satisfaction but also for the long-term development
of the platform.
Balanced between the commercial objectives and the user's satisfaction. During the commercial
procedure, the quantities of advertisements and product recommendations should be determined
properly to avoid making the users feel tired or annoyed because of too much promotion. It has been
found that suitable individualized recommendations can enhance the user's participation, but too
many recommendations may result in disturbance and decrease the users' trust and desire to use the
system [5]. Therefore, the system should distribute the content recommendations and commercial
recommendations reasonably to prevent the advertisement contents from occupying too much of the
users' attention. Moreover, efforts should be made to integrate the content and commodity
recommendations better. For instance, the commodity information can be introduced through
relevant content, which will make consumer behaviour more natural. Thus, the business
transformation can be accomplished without deteriorating the user's experience [9].
Improve the privacy protection mechanism and enhance the trust of users. The effectiveness of
personalized recommendation is based on user data, so the platform should attach great importance
to the protection of user privacy. Initially, rules must be established for the acquisition, preservation
and utilization of data, specify the area of data application and prevent the collection of unnecessary
personal information [6]. Secondly, the system should enhance its algorithmic transparency. For
instance, the system can offer a 'Why is this content recommended' choice, so that the users can
know the reason for the recommendation [2]. Furthermore, users need to have more opportunities to
manage their privacy, enabling them to change the recommendation strength or turn off the
personalized recommendation themselves, which can increase the users' confidence in the system.

4.2. User level
Cultivate reasonable consumption habits. People should improve their self-discipline and prevent
impulsive purchasing induced by the overwhelming influence of suggested contents. Since social
media frequently arouses the desire for consumption by means of emotional impact and scene
presentation, one should make rational judgments when encountering suggested contents and make
decisions based on their real needs [10]. One can also enhance the reasonableness of consumption
decisions by postponing the decision, comparing various commodities and fixing the consumption
budget, in order to decrease unnecessary expenditures. This reasonable consumption habit is
beneficial both for personal finance management and for reducing the excessive influence of the
algorithm recommendation on consumption behavior.
Improve the knowledge of privacy protection. People need to take into account the safety of their
information while enjoying the advantages of the individualized suggestions. They should acquire
the usage rules of the system and manage the privacy rights properly. For example, users can turn
off the unnecessary data gathering choices or restrict the individualized advertisement functions [5].
Moreover, users ought to recognize the risk of information leakage and avoid the disclosure of
personal details on the system. By enhancing the knowledge of privacy protection, the possible
dangers resulting from the individualized suggestions can be decreased to a certain degree and a
safer shopping atmosphere can be established.
4.3. Regulatory level
Improving relevant regulatory policies. The government and relevant regulatory agencies should
formulate and improve laws and regulations on personalized recommendation and data use, and
clarify the responsibility boundary between social media platforms and businesses. For example, the
data collection scope of the platform should be standardized, and excessive data utilization behavior
should be restricted. Also, it is important to strengthen the punishment for privacy disclosure and
data abuse. Meanwhile, regulators should strengthen the supervision of personalized advertisements
and recommended content. In this way, false propaganda and misleading recommendations can be
prevented, thus protecting the legitimate rights and interests of consumers. Through institutional
constraints, platform behavior can be standardized to a certain extent, and the negative impact can
be reduced.
Strengthening industry guidance and standardization development. Besides legal supervision,
people should also promote the standardized development of personalized recommendation
technology through industry self-discipline and policy guidance. Relevant institutions can formulate
industry standards and encourage platforms to take into account user experience and rights
protection while pursuing commercial interests. In addition, people should advocate the
development concept of "technology for good". It can guide enterprises to consider social
responsibility in algorithm design and avoid realizing short-term benefits by over-stimulating users'
emotions. Through this multi-party cooperation, people can realize the benign interaction between
personalized recommendation and rational consumption and promote the sustainable development
of the digital economy.

5. Conclusion
This paper uses the method of literature analysis to systematically sort out the influence mechanism
of personalized recommendation of social media on users' consumption behavior. The paper first
analyzes the development status of personalized recommendation and points out that it has become
an important means to connect users and consumer information, which is deeply integrated in
content communication and e-commerce scenarios and has many influences on consumer behavior.
Research shows that personalized recommendation has a significant positive effect. This includes
reducing information cost, shortening decision-making path, stimulating potential demand, and
improving consumption efficiency and user stickiness. However, it also brings negative effects,
including impulsive consumption, excessive consumption, privacy risk and recommendation fatigue,
which weakens users' trust. In this regard, this paper puts forward suggestions from the platform,
users and supervision: Optimize the platform algorithm, balance business and experience, and
strengthen privacy protection; Users enhance rational consumption and privacy awareness;
Supervise and improve laws and regulations, standardize platform behavior, and promote the healthy
development of recommended technologies.
In summary, personalized recommendation indeed is of great value in promoting the development
of the digital economy and improving the convenience of consumption. However, its influence is
twofold. Future studies can further analyze the differences between different platforms and different
user groups with empirical data, in order to understand the long-term impact of personalized
recommendation on consumer behavior more comprehensively. At the same time, how to achieve a
balance between technological innovation and user rights protection will also become an important
direction for follow-up research.
