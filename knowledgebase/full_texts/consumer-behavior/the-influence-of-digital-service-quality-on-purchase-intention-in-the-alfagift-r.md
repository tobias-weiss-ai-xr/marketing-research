 The rapid shift toward digital retail in Indonesia necessitates the integration
of mobile applications to sustain consumer purchase intention, as explored in
this case study of the Alfagift application at PT Sumber Alfaria Trijaya Tbk
in Jakarta. This rese arch evaluates the influence of E -Service Quality
comprising the dimensions of Efficiency, Fulfillment, System Availability,
and Privacy on consumer Purchase Intention, which is analyzed through
Transactional, Referential, and Preferential dimensions. Empl oying a
quantitative research design and structural model assessment, the study
investigates how these digital service quality factors impact user satisfaction
and subsequent consumer behavior. Findings indicate that high-quality digital
service directly c orrelates with increased user engagement and a higher
likelihood of purchase completion within the app. Specifically, Efficiency in
navigation and Fulfillment of delivery are significant drivers of consumer
interest, while System Availability and Privacy r emain critical factors for
facilitating trust. Ultimately, this research provides strategic
recommendations for retail managers to optimize mobile service delivery,
aiming to foster long -term loyalty and maximize sales performance in the
Jakarta market.

INTRODUCTION
The global retail landscape has undergone a seismic shift due to the rapid advancement of internet
technology, which has transformed conventional brick -and-mortar transactions into sophisticated digital
interactions (Kurniadi & Rana, 2023; Tecoalu et al., 2021). In I ndonesia, this evolution is particularly
pronounced, with data indicating that approximately 87 million internet users access e -commerce platforms,
while 90 million have actively engaged in online transactions (Gai et al., 2024). This surge in digital acti vity
has forced traditional retailers to adopt digital transformation strategies to remain competitive and meet the
evolving needs of consumers who increasingly prioritize convenience, speed, and security (Gai et al., 2024;
Kurniadi & Rana, 2023). PT Sumbe r Alfaria Trijaya Tbk, a titan in the Indonesian minimarket sector, has
responded to this shift by launching the Alfagift application, an integrated retail platform designed to
synchronize physical store convenience with digital accessibility (Azmy et al., 2025; Lisnawati et al., 2023).
Despite the growth of the digital economy, e-retailers face significant challenges in retaining customers,
making consumer loyalty and purchase intention vital factors for long-term business success (Kurniadi & Rana,
2023). In the context of the Alfagift application, understanding the determinants of purchase intention is crucial
as consumers' shopping patterns shift toward the online realm (Gai et al., 2024). Purchase intention represents
the initiation of a consumer’s consi deration for a needed product, and in the digital space, this intention is
heavily influenced by the quality of the electronic services provided (Lisnawati et al., 2023; Tecoalu et al.,
2021). Digital service quality, or E-Service Quality, encompasses the entirety of the customer's interaction with
the platform —from initial navigation to the final delivery of the product (Abdillah et al., 2022;
Taufiqurokhman et al., 2024).

In a metropolitan hub like Jakarta, the demands for high -quality digital services ar e intensified by the
high density of internet users and the competitive retail environment (Taufiqurokhman et al., 2024). Consumers
in Jakarta expect digital platforms to be efficient, reliable, and secure (Monica & Triyanto, 2025;
Taufiqurokhman et al., 2 024). However, prior research suggests that increasing service quality does not
automatically lead to customer loyalty or purchase intention; instead, it often requires the mediation of higher
customer satisfaction (Azmy et al., 2025). Specifically, for Al fagift users, it has been discovered that while
information and system quality positively influence satisfaction, service quality alone may not have a
significant direct effect on loyalty without the intervening role of satisfaction (Azmy et al., 2025). Th is
highlights a complex dynamic where technical excellence must translate into a positive psychological state for
the user to catalyze transactional behavior (Azmy et al., 2025; S.A.B & Ariyanti, 2024).
This study aims to investigate the influence of speci fic E -Service Quality dimensions Efficiency,
Fulfillment, System Availability, and Privacy on the Purchase Intention of Alfagift users in Jakarta. By
focusing on these core dimensions, which have been consistently cited as significant drivers of satisfaction and
loyalty in digital settings, the research seeks to provide PT Sumber Alfaria Trijaya Tbk with actionable insights
to optimize its platform (Abdillah et al., 2025; Serrano & Molina, 2025). Furthermore, the study explores how
these technical attribute s foster different facets of purchase intention, including transactional commitment,
referential loyalty, and preferential selection. Through this analysis, the research contributes to the broader
understanding of digital consumer behavior in the Indonesia n retail sector and offers practical
recommendations for enhancing service quality to boost purchase rates and consumer trust (Mulia, 2026; Utami
& Hidayat, 2024).

LITERATURE REVIEW
a. Conceptualizing E-Service Quality
E-Service Quality is a multidimensional  construct that evaluates the functional performance of a
service when customers shop and purchase via electronic media (Abdillah et al., 2025; Serrano & Molina,
2025). In the seminal work by Parasuraman et al., e -service quality was initially examined thr ough
dimensions such as efficiency, reliability, compliance, privacy, responsiveness, compensation, and
contact (Abdillah et al., 2022). For the contemporary digital retail environment, these dimensions are
often categorized into four core attributes that directly impact the user journey:
1) Efficiency: Defined as the ease and speed with which users can navigate the application and
complete transactions (Serrano & Molina, 2025). Efficiency is a key cognitive trigger that influences
user trust and reduces the effort required to achieve a shopping goal (Abdillah et al., 2025; Serrano
& Molina, 2025).
2) Fulfillment: This refers to the platform’s ability to deliver accurately on its promises regarding time,
product condition, and availability (Serrano & Molina, 2025) . Fulfillment is consistently cited as
one of the most valued attributes by consumers, as it represents the tangible result of the digital
service (Abdillah et al., 2025; Serrano & Molina, 2025).
3) System Availability: This relates to the technical reliability of the platform, specifically the absence
of errors, crashes, or downtime (Serrano & Molina, 2025). High system availability is directly
associated with continued usage, as technical failures create significant friction in the consumer
journey (Monica & Triyanto, 2025; Serrano & Molina, 2025).
4) Privacy: This dimension is critical for fostering a perception of security in digital environments
(Serrano & Molina, 2025). It involves the protection of users' personal and financial data, which is
essential for building the trust required to facilitate online transactions (Apriliani et al., 2024;
Serrano & Molina, 2025).

b. Purchase Intention and Consumer Behavior
Purchase intention serves as a foundational concept in consumer behavior studies, representing the
probability that a consumer will proceed with purchasing a specific product or service (Lisnawati et al.,
2023). In the digital retail context, purchase intention is not merely a single action but a multifaceted
behavioral indicator that includes:
1) Transactional Intention: The immediate desire to purchase through the application (Santoso et al.,
2024).
2) Referential Intention: The willingness to recommend the application to others, which acts as
electronic word-of-mouth (Lesmana & Balqiah, 2023; Lisnawati et al., 2023).
3) Preferential Intention: The tendency to prioritize the application over competing retail platforms
(Gai et al., 2024).
Studies have shown that overall e -service quality positively influences purchase intention by
enhancing customer satisfaction (Santoso et al., 2024). In Indonesia, trust plays a pivotal role; consumer
trust in an application has a significant impact on purchasing decisions, as it forms the basis for positive
relationships between consumers and online platforms (April iani et al., 2024). Furthermore, governance
and digital service quality act as direct drivers of purchase intention, while trust can act as a mediator for
other factors like customer reviews (Mulia, 2026).

c. The Mediation Role of Customer Satisfaction
A critical finding in digital retail literature is that the relationship between service quality and
loyalty or intention is often mediated by satisfaction (S.A.B & Ariyanti, 2024; Santoso et al., 2024).
Satisfaction is defined as the accumulation of experiences and the evaluation of product performance
relative to expectations (Purnamasari & Suryandari, 2023, p. 156). For Alfagift users, satisfaction is
determined by perceived value, e-trust, and the e-service quality experienced while utilizing the
program (S.A.B & Ariyanti, 2024). Interestingly, research on the Alfagift application has revealed that
service quality may not automatically raise loyalty in the absence of higher satisfaction (Azmy et al.,
2025). This suggests that the technical dimensions of the application Efficiency, Fulfillment, System
Availability, and Privacy must be high enough to satisfy the user's needs before they manifest as a
concrete intention to buy or remain loyal to the brand (Azmy et al., 2025; S.A.B & Ariyanti, 2024).

d. Digital Retail Trends in Indonesia
The growth of the retail industry in Indonesia is driven by customer behavior motivated by
impulsive needs and the emergence of shopping applications that facilitate ease of transaction (Azmy  et
al., 2025; Gai et al., 2024). E-commerce platforms such as Shopee, Tokopedia, and Lazada have set high
standards for service quality, forcing traditional retailers like Alfamart to enhance their business
operations to meet ever -evolving customer expect ations (Kurniadi & Rana, 2023). In this fiercely
competitive market, building consumer trust through stable technical operations and accurate fulfillment
is the key to long-term success (Kurniadi & Rana, 2023; Monica & Triyanto, 2025).

RESEARCH METHODS
a. Research Design and Approach
This study adopts a quantitative approach, utilizing a descriptive and explanatory causal method
to examine the impact of digital service quality on purchase intention (Monica & Triyanto, 2025; Sarboini
et al., 2025). The explan atory design is intended to elucidate the complex relationships between the
independent variables and the dependent variable (Sarboini et al., 2025).

b. Population and Sampling
The research focuses on Alfagift users residing in the Jakarta metropolitan area,  a region
characterized by high internet usage and a dense concentration of retail outlets (Taufiqurokhman et al.,
2024; Utami et al., 2024). A purposive sampling method is employed to select respondents based on
predetermined criteria, ensuring that they have relevant experience with the Alfagift application (Abdillah
et al., 2022; Lesmana & Balqiah, 2023). The sample consists of 200 respondents, a size that aligns with
statistical recommendations for Structural Equation Modeling and provides sufficient po wer for the
analysis (Azmy et al., 2025; Mulia, 2026; Utami et al., 2024).

c. Data Collection and Instrumentation
Primary data is collected through online -distributed questionnaires (Abdillah et al., 2022;
Taufiqurokhman et al., 2024). The research instrument utilizes a 5-point Likert scale, with scores ranging
from 1 to 5, to measure the following constructs (Taufiqurokhman et al., 2024; Utami et al., 2024):
1) Efficiency: Measured by indicators such as ease of navigation and transaction speed (Serrano &
Molina, 2025; Taufiqurokhman et al., 2024).
2) Fulfillment: Measured by the accuracy of product delivery and order fulfillment (Serrano & Molina,
2025; Utami et al., 2024).
3) System Availability: Measured by technical reliability and the absence of application errors (Serrano
& Molina, 2025; Taufiqurokhman et al., 2024).
4) Privacy: Measured by the perceived security of personal data and payment information (Serrano &
Molina, 2025; Utami et al., 2024).
5) Purchase Intention: Assessed through indicators of transactional, referential, and preferential
intentions (Gai et al., 2024; Lisnawati et al., 2023).

d. Data Analysis Technique
Data analysis is conducted using Structural Equa tion Modeling through the Partial Least Squares
method, facilitated by SmartPLS software (Azmy et al., 2025; S.A.B & Ariyanti, 2024; Taufiqurokhman
et al., 2024). The analytical process involves:
1) Measurement Model Assessment: Evaluating the reliability and validity of the constructs. Reliability
is measured through Cronbach’s alpha and Composite Reliability, both of which must exceed 0.70
(Kurniawan et al., 2025; Pattanasing & Jiraphanumes, 2025). Validity is assessed through
Convergent Validity (Average Va riance Extracted/AVE > 0.50) and Discriminant Validity
(Kurniawan et al., 2025; Pattanasing & Jiraphanumes, 2025; Utami et al., 2024).

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
a. Measurement Model Assessment Results
The results of the Confirmatory Factor Analysis indicate that the measurement model meets the
required standards for reliability  and validity. All latent variables, including Efficiency, Fulfillment,
System Availability, and Privacy, demonstrated Cronbach’s alpha and Composite Reliability values
greater than 0.70 (Kurniawan et al., 2025; Pattanasing & Jiraphanumes, 2025). Furthermore, the Average
Variance Extracted values for each construct were above 0.50, confirming strong convergent validity
(Kurniawan et al., 2025; Pattanasing & Jiraphanumes, 2025). These results ensure that the research
instrument is dependable and that the observed indicators accurately reflect the underlying service quality
dimensions (Kurniawan et al., 2025).

b. Structural Model and Hypothesis Testing
The structural analysis reveals significant relationships between the E-Service Quality dimensions
and Purchase Intention on the Alfagift platform:
1) Efficiency and Purchase Intention: Statistical testing confirms that efficiency has a positive and
significant effect on purchase intention. The ease and speed of navigation within the Alfagift app
act as a cognitive trigger, reducing friction and encouraging transactional behavior (Abdillah et al.,
2025; Serrano & Molina, 2025).
2) Fulfillment as a Dominant Driver : Fulfillment emerged as one of the most valued attributes by
Alfagift users. When the application successfully delivers on its promises regarding product
accuracy and condition, it significantly strengthens the user's intent to repurchase and recommend
the platform (Monica & Triyanto, 2025; Serrano & Molina, 2025).
3) System Availability and Continuous Use: The technical reliability of the Alfagift app is directly
associated with continued usage. Users reported that a stable environment, free from crashes, is
essential for maintaining their preference for the application over competitors (Monica & Triyanto,
2025; Serrano & Molina, 2025).
4) The Critical Role of Privacy: Privacy is pivotal for fostering a perception of security. Users indicated
that feeling safe w hen providing personal and payment information is a foundational requirement
for their purchase decisions (Apriliani et al., 2024; Serrano & Molina, 2025).

c. Discussion
The findings of this study underscore the importance of an integrated strategy in managing digital
service quality to maintain customer loyalty (Fania & Rachmawati, 2025). In the competitive Indonesian
retail landscape, platforms like Alfagift must prioritize technical excellence to build trust and satisfaction
(Kurniadi & Rana, 2023).
One of the most nuanced findings is that while e-service quality significantly impacts satisfaction,
it must be consistently high to drive loyalty. As seen in previous research on Alfagift, increasing service
quality does not automatically lead to loyalty in the absence of high satisfaction (Azmy et al., 2025). This
implies that PT Sumber Alfaria Trijaya Tbk must focus on the user experience as a whole, ensuring that
efficiency and system reliability translate into a content and trusting consumer (Azmy et al., 2025; S.A.B
& Ariyanti, 2024).
Furthermore, the results highlight the "omnichannel" nature of Alfagift. Because the application
bridges physical and digital channels, fulfillment accuracy is paramount. A smooth, comfortable, and
engaging shopping journey is required to move a user from initial purchase intention to long -term
preferential loyalty (Lisnawati et al., 2023). The significant impact of privacy also suggests that as e -
commerce becomes more integral to daily life, retailers must invest heavily in tra nsaction security to
mitigate the risks associated with online shopping (Apriliani et al., 2024; Gai et al., 2024).

CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATIONS

a. Conclusion
This research concludes that Digital Service Quality is a decisive factor in shaping the Purchase
Intention of Alfagift users in Jakarta. The dimensions of Efficiency, Fulfillment, System Availability, and
Privacy collectively influence the consumer's cogn itive evaluation of the platform. Efficiency and
Fulfillment serve as the primary drivers of transactional success, while System Availability and Privacy
act as critical antecedents to trust and long -term brand advocacy. The study confirms that technical
performance, when aligned with user expectations, enhances customer satisfaction, which serves as the
essential bridge to securing preferential and referential intentions in a highly competitive digital market.

b. Recommendations
Based on the findings, the following recommendations are provided for PT Sumber Alfaria Trijaya
Tbk:
1) Enhance Operational Reliability: Continually improve device reliability and application stability to
ensure a frictionless shopping experience. Minimizing crashes during high -traffic p eriods is
essential for maintaining user preference (Monica & Triyanto, 2025; Serrano & Molina, 2025).
2) Optimize Fulfillment Processes: Strengthen the delivery chain to ensure that products consistently
match digital descriptions and reach customers within the promised timeframe. High fulfillment
accuracy is the most effective way to foster repeat purchases (Monica & Triyanto, 2025; Serrano &
Molina, 2025).
3) Strengthen Data Privacy: Invest in advanced security protocols to protect consumer data. Proactively
communicating these security measures can increase user trust and stimulate purchasing decisions
(Apriliani et al., 2024; Serrano & Molina, 2025).
4) Implement Incentive Programs: To boost satisfaction and loyalty, the platform should offer rewards
and persona lized incentives. High -quality digital services, combined with tangible rewards, can
encourage repeat purchases and foster long -term loyalty (Kurniadi & Rana, 2023; Santoso et al.,
2024).
5) Focus on Satisfaction as a Mediator: Management must monitor customer satisfaction levels closely,
as service quality improvements only lead to sustained loyalty when they result in a content user
base (Azmy et al., 2025; S.A.B & Ariyanti, 2024).
