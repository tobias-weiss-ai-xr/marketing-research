Abstract: This exploratory study investigates the impact of EWOM on purchase intention, with brand equity as a mediating
factor in Whiskas cat food products sold on Shopee. With the advent of digitalization, which reconfigures consumer behavior,
EWOM has become one of the strong tools that help influence purchase decisions through customer reviews, ratings, and
feedback. Through the review features and Shopee Video on Shopee's platform, customers get to share experiences aligned with
the principles of EWOM. The study will establish how such interactions affect the brand equity of Whiskas and thereby influence
consumer purchase intentions. Preliminary findings indicate that while Whiskas has managed to retain its leadership in
Indonesian pet food, changes in its brand index invite further investigation into the role of EWOM in maintaining brand loyalty
in the face of increased competition. Furthermore, this research also investigates the moderating role of brand equity in the
relationship between EWOM and purc hase intention. This pilot research contributes to assessing the dynamic effect of digital
consumer engagement on brand performance and has important implications for digital marketing strategy.

Keywords: EWOM, Brand Equity, Purchase Intention.
I. INTRODUCTION
Word-of-mouth promotion is a strategy named in the 1950s, which describes direct interaction among individuals
regarding a brand, product, or service that the recipient perceives as non-commercial. The rise of digital technology significantly
took over communication, hence giving birth to electronic word of mouth, where customers leave their reviews of any good or
service online for a far larger audience.  According to AJ Marketing (2023), WOM ranks as the fourth most popular method of
brand discovery in Indonesia, showing its relevance in digital marketing efforts within the country. Since the 1990s, digital
advancements have also transformed the shopping landscape in Indonesia, shifting from traditional to online shopping methods,
streamlining access and efficiency (Richan, 2024). The act of purchasing and vending things or services via the Internet, known
as online shopping (Turban et al., 2017), is now well-established among Indonesians. Projections from Bank Indonesia predict a
2.8% increase in online sales value, reaching IDR 487 trillion in 2024, with a further 3.3% rise in 2025 (Anggela, 2024). A survey
by Kompas R&D found that 92% of respondents prefer using marketplaces for online shopping, with lower preferences for social
media (5.4%), instant messaging (2.2%), and websites (0.4%)—likely due to a wider range of products, competitive pricing, and
discounts available on marketplaces (Kompas, 2023). In 2023, Shopee led the Indonesian online marketplace sector with 2.3
billion visits, followed by Tokopedia with 1.2 billion, Lazada with 762.4 million, and Blibli with 337.4 million (Ahdiat, 2024).

Indonesia's pet food industry is growing rapidly. According to Euromonitor International , in 2022, the sector achieved a
Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of 24.7% from 2016 to 2021, with a market value of USD 370.6 million in 2021,
making Indonesia the second -largest pet food market in ASEAN, following Thailand (Cacaflyindonesia, 2023). Technological
progress has driven the shift from traditional to online shopping in the pet food sector. Online sales of pet food increased by
26.1% between 2020 and 2024 and are predicted to capture 90.7% of the market (Statista, 2024). In terms of pet ownership, cats
are the most common pets in Indonesia, with 4.80 million owned in 2022, up from 2.15 million in 2016, surpassing dog
ownership, which stood at 737,400 in 2022 (Prasetyo, 2023). Consequently, cat food represents a large portion of the market,
accounting for 66% of the pet food industry (Mordor Intelligence, 2024).

Whiskas, a leading cat food brand, has held the top spot in the Top Brand Award Indonesia from 2015 to 2023. Despite
this, recent data shows a possible decline in its brand index, dropping by 4.5% from 2021 to 2023. In contrast, competitors l ike
Friskies saw a 7.7% rise during the same period (Top Brand Award, 2023), suggesting a shift in the competitive landscape of the
Indonesian cat food market, with Whiskas facing challenges from rising competition. Data from Magpie (2024) revealed that
Shopee had the highest unit sales for Whiskas from January to May 2024, with 299,000 units sold, followed by Tokopedia with
54,000 and Lazada with 16,000. Shopee also offers various features that enhance EWOM's role on its platform. For insta nce,
Shopee’s review system allows customers to leave reviews and ratings, while Shopee Video provides a space for users to engage
through video content. This aligns with Babić Rosario et al. (2016), who described EWOM as the exchange of consumer opinions

II. LITERATURE REVIEW
A) Electronic Word of Mouth (EWOM)
According to Albayrak & Ceylan, 2021, Electronic Word -of-Mouth can be described as comments and reviews from
customers, both positive and negative, concerning certain products or services which are publicly distributed online for many
other consumers' consideration. This idea is close to the concept described by Indrawati in 2017 of earned media, which is when
positive user reviews and recommendations serve effectively for organic brand promotion. Such reviews contain valuable
information because, with EWOM, the key information can be effectively transferred from the sender to the recipient.

B) Brand Equity
A brand represents more than a name and a set of symbols; it is an intangible embodiment of what the product or service's
core identity really is: perceptions, emotions, and experiences in consumers. This identity transforms into brand equity and can
be defined as the "differential impact that brand recognition has on customer responses to the product and its marketing." Br and
equity refers to the degree of a brand's ability to generate customer loyalty and preference. The value of brand equity is cr ucial
in that it provokes favorable attitudes, which determine the consumer behaviors, such as purchase intention. Basically, brand
equity goes beyond the tangible features of the product.

D) Theoretical Framework
The theoretical framework model applied in this work is based on a replication model developed in earlier studies.
According to the authors, a replication model is a conceptual model that  uses constructs from already existing studies without
the inclusion of new variables. The model applied in the study is adopted by Khan et al. (2023), who examined brand equity as
a mediating element in the relationship between customer purchase intention and EWOM. Brand equity serves to become media
for Whiskas cat food p roducts on Shopee . This model is relevant to the current study, which also investigates the relationship
between EWOM and purchase intention. The Elaboration Likelihood Model (ELM) by Petty & Cacioppo (1984) is included in
this study as well. ELM describes how customers proce ss persuasive information, especially when they are exposed to EWOM
in the form of online ratings and reviews. Lee et al. (2008) express that the ELM model identifies central and peripheral rou tes
as the two main ways in which consumers process information. From this theory, some understanding can be derived about how
EWOM is going to influence the decisions of consumers on Shopee, where feedback is so important. This research considers
that EWOM has a two-stage effect on client purchase intentions and brand equity based on the findings of Khan et al. (2023). In
the first stage, EWOM influences purchasing intention positively; in the second stage, EWOM develops brand equity, which
again influences purchase intentions. Therefore, the combination of EWOM and brand equity provides an appropriate framework
for analyzing consumer behavior towards Whiskas products. For that reason, this research has adopted and adapted the proposed
framework to find the function of EWOM and brand equity and its impact on purchase intention in a pet food industry context
in Indonesia.

III. RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
To validate the research model, the author will initially collect data from 30 respondents who are aware of the Whiskas
cat food brand in Indonesia. This preliminary data will be analyzed utilizing IBM SPSS Statistics to conduct validity and
reliability tests. The validity of the questionnaire will be assessed using the “Corrected Item-Total Correlation” (CITC) method,
which identifies the highest correlation coefficient values and excludes items with negative or near-zero correlations, as outlined
by Indrawati (2015). For a measure to be considered valid, its correlation coefficient should be at least 0. 30. Cronbach’s Alpha
will be employed for reliability testing . An item is deemed reliably good if its Cronbach ’s Alpha value exceeds 0.70, according
to Indrawati (2015). The results, as detailed in Table 2, demonstrate that the validity test, performed using the CITC method ,

meets the acceptable reliability threshold. The compiled results are presented in Table 2.

IV. CONCLUSION
The objectives of the research were to assess how brand equity, purchase intention, and electronic word -of-mouth are
influencing Whiskas cat food. A sum of fourteen items from three variables was assessed through the use of IBM SPSS Statistics.
Validity was checked using the Corrected Item-Total Correlation (CITC) method, and reliability was assessed using Cronbach's
Alpha. The results justified that all the measuring devices were valid and reliable and thus could be used for further study. A
total of 30 respondents in Indonesia were given questionnaires. It was found that the model with 3 variables and 14 items was
proven to be fit and thus suitable for use in further research.
