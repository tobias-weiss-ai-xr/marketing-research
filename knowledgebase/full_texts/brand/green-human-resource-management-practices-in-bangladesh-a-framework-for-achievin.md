Abstract
Human resource management (HRM) has evolved into green human resource management
(GHRM) globally by integrating sustainable practices into recruitment, selection, hiring,
training, development, performance appraisal, and employee relation. GHRM practices help
organizations achieve sustainability at the individual, societal, environmental, and
organizational levels.  Effective GHRM practices can enhance organizational outcomes related
to environmental sustainability, corporate social responsibility, employee performance,
organization performance, employee engagement, job satisfaction, organizational citizenship
behavior, employer branding, and organizational identity. Bangladesh, a coastal country, has
recently graduated from a least developed country (LDC) to a developing country.  Therefore,
it is imperative for Bangladesh to address environmental issues in both its business
operations and managerial practices. Accordingly, this study evaluates the status of GHRM
practices among the companies operating in Bangladesh.  The authors developed a
structured green human resource management (GHRM) index based on existing literature

Introduction
Sustainability in product designing, production human resource hiring, recruitment,
selection, training, development, performance appraisal, compensation management,
organizational culture, and values are as relevant as the world is now acting in the manner of
sustainability.  Now business organizations are called for practicing sustainable human
resources management. Green human resource management is an emerging concept of
management which explains the association of environment-friendly initiative practices of an
organization with the human resource department to achieve long-term sustainable
growth (Likhitkar & Verma, 2017). Globally, there is a growing standing of integration of
environmental management and human resources management as green human resources
management (Sharma & Gupta, 2015). In each and every sector of human resource
management, practicing eco-friendly and sustainability is green human resource
management (GHRM). For the betterment of the individual, society, natural environment,
and the business; policies, practices, and systems that influence the organization's green and
sustainable implementation of resources are GHRM (Saha et al., 2020). GHRM is the process
of the utilization of human resource policies in order to ensure sustainable use of
environmental resources within the organization for the achievement of environmental
sustainability. Eco-friendly HR practices and the preservation of knowledge capital are the
basic elements of GHRM (Sharma, 2016). In other words, inside the business, to endorse the
sustainable uses of resources using HRM policies is GHRM (Sharma & Gupta, 2015). There is
always a strong relationship between human resource management and organizational
environmental performance (Uddin, 2018). GHRM has significant positive influences on green
in-role, extra-role service behavior, and green knowledge sharing. Additionally, green
knowledge sharing significantly mediates between green human resource management and
green service behavior (Al Mamun, 2019). Green human resource management has an
influence on job analysis, recruitment, selection, induction, performance appraisal, rewards
management, and overall organizational environmental sustainability (Uddin, 2018). The
green service behavior of employees can be influenced by green human resource
management through green knowledge sharing (Al Mamun, 2019).  For a few decades, green
human resource management has appeared as a catchphrase but in an organizational context
least devotion has been given (Uddin, 2018). Going green and eco-friendly environment can
reduce operational costs. Employees’ engagement can be achieved through GHRM (Saha et
al., 2020). GHRM, employee green behavior in the workplace, and organizational

Why GHRM Matters?
Human resources of the organization have considered as the most important and prominent
contributors to organizational performance. Due to global warming and climate change,
integration has been seen globally in managing the human resources in the light of
sustainability and green policies and practicing such as green hiring, recruitment, and
selection, green training and development, green performance appraisal, green
compensation management, and green employees relation on order to achieve sustainability.
Sustainability has a direct influence on competitiveness, reputation, and talent retention.
GHRM can help in the organizational process, management change, and cultural stewardship.
To create a sustainable culture, the GHRM of a corporation can play a significant role (Das &
Sing, 2016). Priority should be given to social equity, health, wellness, and well-being which
are the state of art in green human resource management (Amurutha & Geetha,
2020). GHRM practices have positive influences on sustainable growth and higher employee
retention in the organization (Likhitkar & Verma, 2017). GHRM can facilitate employees’
collective engagement in environment protection. Collective commitment to change
environmental management can be played mediating role in the relationship between GHRM
practices and collective voluntary behaviors towards the environment (Pinzone et al.,
2016). In organizational citizenship behavior toward the environment, there is a positive
indirect effect of GHRM through green employee empowerment (Hameed et al., 2020). In
the relationship between customers’ pressure and regulatory stakeholders on environmental
issues and environmental performance, green hiring, green training, green involvement,
green performance management, and green compensation played mediating role (Guerci et
al., 2016). On environmental performance; green recruitment and selection, green training
and development, and green compensation management have significant positive
associations (Yusoff et al., 2020). Green competence building practices, green motivation
enhancing practices, and green employee involvement practices can positively influence the
organizational citizenship behavior towards environment and environmental
performance (Anwar et al., 2020). Employees’ sustainable engagement can be achieved
through the practices of GHRM (Yap Peng Lok & Chin, 2019). Employees’ engagement can be
facilitated by GHRM through the practices of green recruitment and selection, green
employee relations, green induction, and green training and development (Singh & Pandey,
2020). Job satisfaction of the employees can be mediated by GHRM (Ahmad & Umrani,
2019). Employees’ organizational citizenship behavior can be influenced by an organization's
green culture and green values (Hooi et al., 2021). GHRM has a significant positive association
with organizational citizenship behavior toward the environment and environmental values
are playing a moderating role in the effects of GHRM on organizational identity and

Literature Review
Sustainability is now every there, which leads to converting human resource management
into green human resource management. GHRM is catching the attention of researchers
globally.  Numerous studies have been done on this hot topic.

Environmental Sustainability and Green Human Resource Management
Environmental sustainability is now a catching attraction as it has importance in every aspect
of the environment (Sharma, 2016). GHRM has been considered the most commonly used
conceptualized theory used in human resource management in developing organizational
performance (Saha et al., 2020). Using employees’ edge in order to promote and maintain
sustainable business practices and creating awareness which is helping organizations to
operate in an environmentally sustainable is GHRM (Rani & Mishra, 2014). By supposing
environmental-related problems in management philosophy, human resource policies and
practices, and training and implementation of laws regarding environmental safety, the
GHRM can play an important role (Rani & Mishra, 2014).  There is a significant positive
association between GHRM practices and collective voluntary behaviors towards the
environment (Pinzone et al., 2016).  Corporate social-environmental performance has a
strong association with financial and marketplace success (Das & Sing, 2016).  There is a
positive indirect effect of GHRM on organizational citizenship behavior towards the
environment through green employee empowerment and individual green values can be
played a moderated role in the positive relationship between green employee empowerment
and organizational citizenship behaviors towards the environment (Hammed et al.,
2020). Additionally, organization-person interaction can foster the employees’ engagement
in environmental initiatives (Ababneh, 2021). Organizational citizenship behavior towards
the environment significantly mediated the association between corporate social
responsibility and green human resource management practices. Additionally, Organizational
citizenship behavior toward the environment significantly affects the sustainable

Green Human Resource Management and Organizational Performance
Sustainability is now a very critical issue in business globally (Das and Sing, 2016).  In
comparison with the economic and environmental areas of sustainability; social sustainability
has been the minimum explored area. There is a meditational role of employee green
behavior in the workplace on the association between GHRM and organizational
sustainability (Amurutha & Geetha, 2020). Sustainability can be positively influenced by
green recruitment and green training but green analysis, green job description, green
selection, green performance assessment, and green reward have no direct influence on
sustainability (Yong et al., 2020).  Green hiring, green training, green involvement, green
performance management, and green compensation played a mediating role in the
relationship between pressure (both from customers and regulatory stakeholders) on
environmental issues and environmental performance (Guerci et al., 2016). Green
recruitment and selection, green training and development, and green compensation
management have significant positive associations with environmental performance but
green performance appraisal has not had such meaningful influences (Yusoff et al.,
2020). Green competence building practices, green motivation enhancing practices, and
green employee involvement practices have meaningful positive influences on organizational
citizenship behavior towards the environment leading to bear positive impact on
environmental performance (Anwar et al., 2020). There is a direct relationship between
GHRM and employees' sustainable engagement (Yap Peng Lok & Chin, 2019). Green
recruitment and selection, green employee relations, green induction, and green training and
development are the prominent dimensions impacting employees’ engagement (Singh &
Pandey, 2020). Employees’ engagement can be played a mediating role in the relationship
between GHRM practices and individual green behavior (Ababneh, 2021). There is a
mediating role of GHRM and psychological safety on employees’ job satisfaction (Ahmad &
Umrani, 2019). Psychological safety and GHRM have indirect effects on employees’ job
satisfaction (Moin et al., 2021). Organizational environmental culture has a direct positive
association with GHRM and GHRM is positively associated with organizational environmental
performance. Finally, GHRM positively influences the employees’ job satisfaction and
meaningfulness (Shafaei et al., 2020).   GHRM is significantly associated with green citizenship
behavior and green culture. Additionally, green culture and green values played a significant
role in green organizational citizenship behavior (Hooi et al., 2021). There is a significant
positive association between GHRM and organizational citizenship behavior towards the
environment and environmental values moderate the effects of GHRM on organizational
identity and organizational citizenship behavior towards the environment (Liu & Guo,
2020).  There is a significant positive association between GHRM and employers branding and
this association is mediated by green employees’ empowerment and moderated by a green
psychological climate (Kivinda et al., 2021). GHRM has a positive influence on employees’
eco-friendly behavior and organizational identification (Ribeiro et al., 2022).

Methodological Approached
Al Mamun (2019) used partial least square methods to analyze the employees’ perception of
365 frontlines. The grounded theory approach has been used in examining the association
between GHRM and organizational sustainability (Amurutha & Geetha, 2020). A novel multi-
model approach has been implemented under three sections as an initial seven factors
structure, interpretive structural modeling, and structural equation modeling to assess the
state of –the art green HRM systems (Gholami et al., 2016). Paper–pencil survey has been
used to assess the association between GHRM practices and employees' environmental
performance (Hammed et al., 2020).  Multi response survey has been used to confirm the
hypothesized mediation model in the study of translating stakeholders’ pressure into
environmental performance through mediating role played by GHRM practices (Guerci et al.,
2016). The partial least square structural equation modeling has been used to assess the
association between GHRM practices and environmental performance  (Yusoff et al.,
2020). Quantitative research design along with partial least square modeling has been used
to assess the relationship between GHRM practices on organization citizenship behavior
towards the environment and environmental performance (Anwar et al., 2020). The
quantitative cross-sectional study design was adapted to analyze the impacts of GHRM on
employees’ engagement  (Singh & Pandey, 2020).   To fill the gap in addressing GHRM
practices in Bangladesh, this study has been initiated. Thus, objectives of study are:

i. Evaluating the Green Human Resource Management (GHRM) practices by the
organization operating in Bangladesh.
ii. Identifying the reasons for bad GHRM practices and providing suggestions that help
the concerned authority and policymakers in preparing guidelines for adoption with
climate change.
iii. Developing a theoretical model of  Green Human Resource Management
(GHRM) practices for the achievement of sustainability.
iv. Assessing the possible outcomes can be derived from good practices of Green Human
Resource Management (GHRM)
v. Improvement of the companies’ ethical, sustainable and eco -friendly management
practices (in-house and outhouse) for the companies and adoption of climate change.

Materials and Methods
Dataset Development
To complete the study , data on GHRM practices were collected from 187 companies across
ten sectors, namely  Banking, Cement, Ceramics, Engineering, Food & Allied, Fuel &
Power,  IT, Jute, Paper & Printing, and  Pharmaceuticals & Chemicals sectors. The Annual
reports, websites , and other publicly available reports of the selected companies were
thoroughly examined to assess their GHRM practices. The disclosed information was then
compared with the pre-developed GHRMP index.

Ability–Motivation–Opportunity (AMO) theory and a systematic review of the literature ,
Faisal (2023) identified six key dimensions: green recruitment and selection, green training
and development, green compensation management, green performance management,
green employee empowerment and participation, and green employee relations. Drawing on
the above literature authors has developed a Green Human Resource Management Practices

Content Analysis and Coding Framework
Content analysis is described as a methodology using a series of procedures to get precise
conclusions from texts (Smith, 2022). The content analysis approach has traditionally been
used to examine texts and documents, aiming to measure content according to established
categories in a systematic and reproducible manner (Bryman & Hardy, 2009).The content
analysis approach is used to discern qualities and quantify information in a text by
categorising it based on defined criteria, allocating each information unit to a category, and
tallying the total occurrences and frequencies within each category (Data & Silverman, 2011).
Content and theme analyses have been extensively used to examine the extent of disclosing
certain topics in business annual reports (Rashid et al., 2025; Uddin et al., 2025; Islam et al.,
2025). This research measures the extent of green human resource management practices
index (GHRMPI) in the corporations' annual reports. The researcher manually compiled the
information using the annual reports of selected businesses. The designated indexed items
received a score of 1 for correct disclosure and 0 for nondisclosure. The main aim of this
research is to conduct a content and theme analysis of the annual reports of the chosen firms.
Corporate organizational green human resource management practices indexes (GHRMPI) to
methods are evaluated on a scale from 0 to 1.

Conclusion
Green Human Resource Management (GHRM) has become an integral part of the modern
business organizations because of its contribution sustainable development, the corporate
image, market growth, employers’ identification, employees’ organizational citizenship
behavior, employee’s engagement, environmental performance, and organizational
performance. Growing global awareness of environmental sustainability has encouraged
organization to adopt environmentally responsible management practices. Consequently,
the corporate sector has shifted its focus from solely maximizing profits to promoting
environmental conservation, preserving biodiversity, maintaining ecological balance, and
encouraging responsible consumption and production.  Accordingly, GHRM has emerged as
a key business strategy by integrating environmental considerations into human resource

functions, including green of hiring, recruitment, selection, training and development,
performance appraisal, and employee relation. Therefore, this study focuses on the green
human resource practices adopted by the companies operating in Bangladesh. To achieve
this objectives, annual reports and other publicly available documents of 187 companies from
ten sectors namely, Banking, Cement, Ceramics, Engineering, Food & Allied, Fuel & Power,
IT, Jute, Paper & Printing, and Pharmaceuticals & Chemicals were comprehensively
evaluated. The findings identified 26 Green Human Resource Management Practices
(GHRMP), including Green Hiring, Recruiting, and Selection; Green Training and
Development; Green In-Role Service Behavior; Green Extra Role Service Behavior; Green
Knowledge Sharing; Green Performance Appraisal; Green Rewards Management; Green
Psychological Climate (Go Green and Think Green); Green Compensation Management;
Green Employees’ Relation; Green Involvement; Sustainable Culture; Green Analysis; Green
Job Description; Green Competence Building Practices; Green Motivation Enhancing
Practices; Green Employees’ Involvement; Employees Sustainable Engagement; Green
Employees’ Retention; Sustainable Out Look; Preservation of Knowledge Capital; Talent
Development for Greening Organizations; Designing the Green Job; Top Management Follow
the Green Modeling; Information Sharing Techniques and Empowerment; and Management
of Green Parameter and Performance. Overall, the findings reveal that the level of  GHRMP
disclosure among the companies operating in Bangladesh remain very low, indicating that
GHRM practices have yet to be widely incorporated into corporate reporting.

Contribution of the Study
Firstly, this study  extends the existing literature on G reen Human Resource Management
(GHRM), with particular emphasis on green hiring, green recruitment, green selection, green
training, green development, green performance appraisal, and green  employee relation.
Secondly, the findings of this study broaden the scope and depth of GHRM by highlighting its
role in achieving environmental sustainability from organizational perspective. Thirdly, to the
best of the authors’ knowledge, this study is the first and most comprehensive attempt to
measure the green human resource practices in context of Bangladesh. The findings provide
a foundation for developing conceptual framework   for effective GHRM practices. Fourthly,
the findings encourage and promote the adaptation of G HRM practices to support
environmentally sustainable business operations. Fifthly, this study represents the first
comprehensive attempt to identify the key components of GHRM that contribute to
environmental sustainability.  Sixthly, the findings of this study can serve as a foundation for
developing organizational GHRM policies and practices. Finally, this study provides valuable
insights for future researchers, professionals, policymakers, and practi tioners by offering a
broader perspective on the role of GHRM in achieving environmental sustainability.
