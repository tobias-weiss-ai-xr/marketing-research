ABSTRACT
In the digital service economy, online customer reviews are
crucial for consumer trust, brand credibility, and corporate
reputation. For service -based industries like automotive
dealerships, Google Reviews have become a strategic tool
rather than a passive feedback channel, requiring active
management. This study explores the role of Google Reviews at
PT Nasmoco Bahtera Motor and examines how reward -based
strategies, Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
practices, and corporate reputation influence review
performance. It specifically investigates the impact of non -
monetary rewards on customer motivation, the integration of
review management within CRM, and how reputation
strengthens trust and feedback behavior. Using a qualitative
case study approach, data were collected via semi -structured
interviews, direct observation during an internship,
documentation of review activities, and internal records, and
analyzed through thematic analysis. Findings indicate that
review performance relies not only on service q uality but also
on effective reward systems, personalized CRM, and a strong
corporate reputation, which enhances credibility and turns
feedback into long-term relational value.

INTRODUCTION
 PT Nasmoco Bahtera Motor was established on April 15, 1961, initially operating under
the name PT Ratna Dewi Motor, before undergoing a corporate rebranding to PT New Ratna
Motor in 1973. As part of the official distribution network of PT Toyota Astra Motor, Nasmoco
became the first authorized Toyota dealership in Central Java and the Special Region of
Yogyakarta (DIY). This early establishment positioned the company not only as a pioneer in
regional automotive distribution but also as an organization with long-standing experience in

 The inauguration of the Nasmoco Mlati Branch on April 16, 1988, marked a significant

 As the automotive industry has shifted toward a more customer -centric and digitally
mediated market environment, PT Nasmoco Bahtera Motor has continuously adapted its
business strategies. The company’s core operations now extend beyond vehicle sales to include
comprehensive after -sales services, such as maintenance, repairs, and the distribution of
genuine Toyota spare parts. These service interactions generate frequent and recurring
touchpoints with customers, making the company particularly exposed to cus tomer feedback
mechanisms, including online reviews and digital evaluation platforms (PT Nasmoco Group,

 Currently, Nasmoco Group operates 22 branches, 14 Sales and Service Points (SSP), and
2 Toyota Outlet Service Stations (TOSS) across Central Java and Yogyakarta. This wide operational
network increases the visibility of the company in digital search environments, especially on
location-based platforms such as Google Maps. Consequently, Google Reviews have become a
critical interface through which customers express their service experiences, evaluate service

 In response to digital transformation and changing consumer behavior, PT Nasmoco

strategies. Online customer feedback, particularly through Google Reviews, functions not only as

reputation, service credibility, and competitive positioning. This condition underscores the
relevance of Customer Relationship Management (CRM) practi ces, reward -based engagement

LITERATURE REVIEW
Google Reviews as Digital Customer Feedback
Online reviews influence consumer attitudes, trust formation, and purchase intentions
through social proof mechanisms (Zhu & Zhang, 2020). Google Reviews, integrated within search
and mapping systems, are particularly influential due to their immediacy and accessibility (Filieri
et al., 2021). In service industries where quality is assessed post -consumption, reviews reduce
uncertainty and shape expectations (Walsh & Beatty, 2021).

Beyond influencing customers, Google Reviews function as managerial feedback tools.
They provide real-time, unsolicited insights into service strengths and weaknesses (Trainor et al.,
2021). When systematically monitored and responded to, reviews support service improvement
and relational trust-building.

Reward/Gift Strategies in Online Review Contexts
Reward mechanisms are managerial tools designed to stimulate customer participation
in feedback activities (Xie et al., 2021). In digital review environments, incentives are most
effective when non -coercive and framed as appreciation rather than exchange (Mazhar et al.,
2022).
Research indicates that incentives primarily influence review volume rather than review
valence (Kim & Tang, 2020). Ethical framing and transparency are critical in preserving
authenticity (Babić Rosario et al., 2020). Thus, rewards function as participation triggers rather
than determinants of content positivity.

Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
CRM is a strategic approach focused on long -term relational value creation through
systematic customer interaction management (Buttle & Maklan, 2022). In digital contexts, CRM
extends to managing online feedback, personalized follow -ups, and multichannel
communication (Chatterjee et al., 2021).
Effective CRM increases customer engagement and discretionary behaviors such as
providing online reviews (Hollebeek et al., 2022). Online feedback can therefore be interpreted
as an outcome of relationship quality rather than isolated digital activity.

Corporate Reputation
Corporate reputation represents accumulated stakeholder perceptions regarding
organizational reliability and credibility (Fombrun et al., 2020). Reputation functions as a
cognitive shortcut that influences customer expectations and trust (Veloutsou & Delgado -
Ballester, 2020).
Reputation interacts dynamically with online reviews. Positive reputation amplifies
favorable feedback and mitigates isolated negative reviews, while weak reputation increases
vulnerability to criticism (Park & Nicolau, 2020). Reputation thus operates as both antecedent
and outcome of digital feedback performance.

METHODS
Research Design
This study employs a qualitative case study approach to explore managerial processes
and customer perceptions related to Google Review management at PT Nasmoco Bahtera Motor
Mlati Branch. A qualitative inquiry is appropriate because it enables an in -depth understanding
of the contextual, relational, and digital feedback dynamics within the natural organizational
setting, allowing the researcher to capture nuanced perspectives from both internal
stakeholders and customers (Creswell & Poth, 2021). The case st udy method is particularly
suitable for examining contemporary phenomena in real -life contexts, providing rich insights
into managerial practices, customer engagement strategies, and reputation management
mechanisms.

Sampling
A purposive sampling technique was employed to select participants who could provide
relevant, detailed, and diverse perspectives regarding Google Review management. The
respondents include four internal stakeholders (Customer Relation Coordinator, Sales Executive,
Service Administration, Digital Marketing staff) and four customers who had interacted with the
dealership and contributed online reviews. This approach ensures that the data reflect both
managerial intentions and customer experiences, enhancing the validity and richness of the
findings.
RESULTS
Google Reviews as Strategic Feedback Platform
The findings confirm that Google Reviews function as a primary digital reference point for
customers. Review ratings significantly influence visitation decisions, especially in location -based
searches. During the observation period, structured engagement initiatives increased the branch
rating from 4.4 to 4.7.
This improvement supports the argument that online reviews are dynamic and
manageable assets (Kietzmann & Canhoto, 2021). Active encouragement, timely responses, and
professional communication contributed to enhanced digital perception. Thus, Google Reviews
operate as both evaluative indicators and relational interfaces.

Reward/Gift as Participation Trigger
Reward strategies were implemented as appreciation gestures. Findings indicate that
small non -monetary gifts reduced post -service inertia and increased review participation.
Customers perceived rewards positively when framed transparently.
Consistent with Kim & Tang (2020), incentives influenced participation volume but not
review honesty. Authenticity was maintained because customers retained autonomy over
content. Therefore, reward strategies strengthen engagement without substituting service
quality.

CRM AS RELATIONAL DRIVER
CRM practices—including personalized follow -ups, coordinated responses, and structured
service records —enhanced customer satisfaction and willingness to provide feedback.
Customers who experienced attentive communication were more inclined to leave reviews.
This aligns with relationship marketing theory suggesting that discretionary behaviors
emerge from perceived relational value (Buttle & Maklan, 2022). Google Review performance can
thus be interpreted as an outcome of effective CRM capability.

Reputation as Reinforcing Mechanism
The branch’s status as an authorized Toyota dealer created baseline trust. Reputation
increased customer confidence in publicly sharing experiences. However, it also elevated
expectations for service consistency.
Consistent with signaling theory (Park & Nicolau, 2020), reputation amplified both positive
experiences and potential dissatisfaction. Reputation therefore reinforces CRM and reward
effects while necessitating consistent service delivery.

CONCLUSION
This study demonstrates that Google Review performance is a relational and strategically
manageable outcome shaped by integrated managerial practices. At PT Nasmoco Bahtera
Motor, improvement in review ratings resulted from structured engagement initiatives rather
than isolated digital activity.
Reward or gift strategies function as participation triggers that increase review volume
without compromising authenticity. CRM practices serve as the foundational driver translating
service quality into digital feedback. Corporate reputation acts as a reinforcing mechanism
amplifying both engagement effectiveness and customer expectations.

LIMITATION
It This study focuses on a single branch within one automotive network. Future research
may adopt quantitative designs or comparative multi -branch analyses to test causal
relationships.
