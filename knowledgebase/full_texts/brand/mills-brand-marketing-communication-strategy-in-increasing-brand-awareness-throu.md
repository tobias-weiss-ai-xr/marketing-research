	 Background:	This	 study	 analyzes	 Mills'	 marketing	 communication
strategy	in	increasing	brand	awareness	through	Instagram	social	media.
Method:	A	 qualitative	 case	 study	 design	 was	 employed	 because	 the
research	focuses	on	communication	processes,	strategic	meanings,	and
audience	engagement	in	a	real-life	digital	context.	Data	were	collected
through	in-depth	interviews	with	key	informants,	observation	of	Mills'
Instagram	 content,	 and	 documentation	 of	 posts,	 captions,	 visual
elements,	 and	 audience	 interactions.	 The	 data	 were	 analyzed	 through
data	 reduction,	 data	 display,	 and	 conclusion	 drawing,	 supported	 by
source	triangulation	and	member	checking.
Results:	The	 findings	 show	 that	 Mills	 uses	 Instagram	 as	 a	 strategic
communication	 infrastructure	 by	 integrating	 structured	 content
planning,	consistent	visual	identity,	influencer	and	athlete	collaboration,
active	 audience	 interaction,	 and	 performance-based	 evaluation.	 These
elements	strengthen	brand	awareness	by	increasing	repeated	exposure,
reinforcing	 recognition,	 supporting	 recall,	 and	 building	 associative
positioning	in	the	sports	apparel	market.
Conclusion:	The	 study	 provides	 practical	 benefits	 for	 sports	 apparel
brands	 that	 need	 to	 manage	 digital	 communication	 consistently	 and
theoretical	benefits	for	integrated	marketing	communication	studies	in
social	media	environments.

INTRODUCTION
The	integration	of	digital	technologies	has	transformed	marketing	communication.	Social
media	 platforms	 enable	 direct	 audience	 interaction	 and	 coordinated	 messaging	(Kotler	 et	 al.,
2022;	Kotler	 &	 Keller,	 2016)	.	The	 rapid	 development	 of	 digital	 technology	 has	 transformed
marketing	 communication	 practices,	 particularly	 through	 the	 emergence	 of	 social	 media	 as	 a
dominant	 communication	 channel.	 Platforms	 such	 as	 Instagram	 enable	 companies	 to	 interact
directly	with	audiences,	produce	more	dynamic	messages,	and	build	participatory	communication
environments.	In	this	context,	social	media	is	no	longer	limited	to	information	dissemination;	it
functions	 as	 a	 strategic	 space	 where	 consumers	 encounter	 brand	 narratives,	 evaluate	 visual
identity,	 respond	 to	 promotional	 messages,	 and	 participate	 in	 communication	 through	 likes,
comments,	shares,	stories,	and	direct	messages.	These	characteristics	make	social	media	a	central
medium	 for	 contemporary	 marketing	 communication	 because	it	 allows	 brands	 to	 inform,
persuade,	remind,	and	interact	with	audiences	in	the	same	communication	ecosystem	(Abbas,	et
al.,	2021;	Yuefei	et	al.,	2024).
Instagram	 is	 especially	 relevant	 for	 brands	 operating	 in	 markets	 that	 rely	 on	 visual
identity	 and	 lifestyle	 representation.	 The	 platform	 provides	 a	 combination	 of	 feed	 posts,	 reels,

both	visual	and	narrative	forms.	For	sports	apparel	brands,	Instagram	offers	the	opportunity	to
present	 products	 not	 merely	 as	 functional	 clothing,	 but	 also	 as	 symbols	 of	 performance,	 team
identity,	 national	 pride,	 lifestyle,	 and	 consumer	 aspiration.	 The	 effectiveness	 of	 such
communication	depends	on	whether	the	brand	is	able	to	maintain	message	consistency,	visual
coherence,	and	interaction	quality	across	different	content	formats	(Bulmer	et	al.,	2024;	Wibowo
et	al.,	2024).
Within	 this	 digital	 environment,	 brand	 awareness	 becomes	 a	 fundamental	 objective	 of
marketing	communication	strategy.	Brand	awareness	refers	to	the	extent	to	which	consumers	can
recognize	and	recall	a	brand	in	a	particular	category.	Recognition	occurs	when	audiences	are	able
to	identify	a	brand	after	being	exposed	to	its	name,	logo,	product,	or	visual	cues,	while	recall	occurs
when	the	brand	comes	to	mind	when	consumers	think	about	a	product	category	or	consumption
situation.	 Social	 media	 can	 support	 both	 processes	 because	 audiences	 repeatedly	 encounter
brand-related	 content	 through	 their	 timelines,	 algorithmic	 recommendations,	 influencer	 posts,
and	interactions	with	other	users	(Aaker,	1996;	Efendioğlu	&	Durmaz,	2022).
Previous	 studies	 have	 shown	 that	 social	 media	 marketing	 activities	 contribute	 to
consumer	response,	brand	equity,	and	purchase	intention	in	apparel	and	other	consumer	markets.
Abbas	et	 al.	(2021)	and	Yuefei	 et	 al.	(2024),	 for	 example,	 indicate	 that	 social	 media	 marketing
activities	 influence	 consumer	 responses	 through	 brand	 equity.	Efendioğlu	and	Durmaz	 (2022)
show	that	social	media	advertising	perception	influences	advertising	value,	brand	awareness,	and
brand	association.	Other	studies	emphasize	that	Instagram	communication	can	build	awareness
through	 content	 marketing,	 storytelling,	 endorsement,	 and	 audience	engagement	(Prasojo	 &
Aliami,	2024;	Rahardani	&	Sanjaya,	2022;	Wibowo	et	al.,	2024).	These	findings	suggest	that	social
media	is	not	merely	a	promotional	tool,	but	an	important	strategic	component	in	the	formation	of
brand	meaning	Studies	on	consumer	reach	and	social	media	content	also	indicate	that	audience
feedback,	brand	image,	and	continuous	digital	exposure	can	support	awareness	development	in
younger	and	digitally	active	markets	(Vinay,	2023;	Wasik	et	al.,	2025;	Zanuarizqi	et	al.,	2025).
However,	 many	 previous	 studies	 tend	 to	 examine	 communication	 elements	 separately.
Some	studies	focus	on	content	marketing,	while	others	discuss	influencer	collaboration,	audience
engagement,	 platform	 features,	 or	 advertising	 perception.	 This	 fragmented	 approach	 provides
important	 insights,	 but	 it	 does	 not	 fully	 explain	 how	 these	 elements	 interact	 as	 a	 coordinated
communication	system.	In	practice,	brands	do	not	build	awareness	through	one	element	alone.
Awareness	is	formed	through	the	cumulative	interaction	of	strategic	planning,	media	selection,
message	 consistency,	 visual	 identity,	 endorsement,	 engagement,	 and	 evaluation.	 Therefore,	 an
integrated	 marketing	 communication	 perspective	 is	 needed	 to	 understand	 how	 social	 media
strategies	 systematically	 support	brand	 awareness	 formation	(Butkouskaya	 et	 al.,	 2024;
Kalogiannidis	et	al.,	2022;	Nabila	&	Wibowo,	2022).
Integrated	 Marketing	 Communication	 (IMC)	 emphasizes	 the	 coordination	 of	 messages
across	communication	channels	to	create	a	unified	brand	image.	In	digital	communication,	IMC
becomes	 increasingly	 important	 because	 audiences	 encounter	 brand	 messages	 through	many
touchpoints	at	the	same	time.	Message	inconsistency	may	weaken	brand	identity,	while	consistent
and	coordinated	communication	can	strengthen	brand	associations	and	audience	perception.	IMC
also	 highlights	 the	 importance	 of	 aligning	 communication	 objectives	 with	 audience	 targeting,
content	planning,	media	usage,	and	evaluation	mechanisms.	In	other	words,	IMC	is	not	only	about
using	many	channels,	but	about	ensuring	that	every	communication	element	supports	the	same
strategic	direction	(Butkouskaya	et	al.,	2024;	Palevska,	2023).
The	 sports	 apparel	 industry	 provides	 a	 relevant	 context	 for	 examining	 these	 issues
because	competition	in	this	sector	is	increasingly	shaped	by	digital	visibility,	brand	community,
and	 consumer	 engagement.	 Sports	 apparel	 brands	 must	 communicate	 product	 qua lity,
performance	value,	emotional	identity,	and	social	relevance.	A	brand	that	succeeds	in	maintaining
digital	visibility	may	become	more	familiar	to	audiences,	but	visibility	alone	is	not	sufficient.	The
brand	also	needs	to	establish	distinctive	visual	codes,	consistent	messages,	credible	associations,
and	active	interaction	so	that	audiences	can	recognize	the	brand	and	associate	it	with	a	particular
category	or	lifestyle	(Pertiwi	&	Sigit,	2024;	Wasik	et	al.,	2025).
The	 sports	 apparel	 market	 also	 has	a	 strong	 symbolic	 dimension.	 Consumers	 do	 not

with	global	brands	that	already	have	strong	recognition,	international	athlete	endorsements,	and
long-established	brand	communities.	Local	or	national	sports	apparel	brands	therefore	need	to
use	digital	media	strategically	to	create	visibility,	credibility,	and	distinctiveness.	Instagram	can
help	reduce	this	gap	because	it	allows	a	brand	to	appear	frequently	in	the	audience's	daily	media

Awareness	is	more	likely	to	emerge	when	digital	communication	is	planned	consistently,	when
visual	identity	remains	recognizable,	and	when	collaboration	choices	support	the	desired	brand

explained	 how	 a	 national	 sports	 apparel	 brand	 integrates	 those	 elements	 within	 the	 same
Instagram	communication	system.	A	qualitative	case	study	is	therefore	useful	because	it	can	show
how	 strategic	 planning,	 content	 categories,	 visual	 repetition,	 collaboration,	 engagement,	 and
evaluation	operate	together	in	everyday	brand	communication.	By	focusing	on	Mills,	this	study
seeks	to	provide	a	contextual	explanation	of	how	brand	awareness	is	built	through	coordinated
Instagram	 practices	 rather	 than	 through	 isolated	 promotional	 actions	 Related	 studies	 on
marketing	communication	practice	further	show	that	communication	programs	need	to	be	read
through	strategic	coordination,	not	only	through	isolated	promotional	actions	(Amin	&	Priansah,
2019;	Andriani	et	al.,	2023).
Mills	 is	 a	 national	 sports	 apparel	 brand	 that	 actively	 uses	 Instagram	 as	 a	 primary
communication	 channel.	 The	 brand's	 Instagram	 activities	 include	 product-focused	 content,
campaign	 information,	 athlete	 and	 influencer	 collaborations,	 interactive	 features,	 and	 visual
storytelling.	 These	 activities	 provide	 an	 appropriate	 case	 for	 exploring	 how	 a	 local	 or	 national
sports	 apparel	 brand	 manages	 Instagram	 communication	 to	 strengthen	 brand	 awareness.	 The
case	is	also	important	because	sports	apparel	brands	face	strong	competition	from	both	global
brands	and	local	competitors.	Therefore,	understanding	how	Mills	constructs	awareness	through
Instagram	 can	 offer	 practical	 insights	 for	 brand	 managers	 and	 theoretical	 insights	 for	 digital
marketing	communication	scholarship.
The	 research	 problem	 addressed	 in	 this	 article	 is	 how	 Mills	 implements	 marketing
communication	strategies	through	Instagram	to	increase	brand	awareness.	The	study	is	guided
by	the	assumption	that	brand	awareness	is	not	built	through	isolated	promotional	activities,	but
through	an	integrated	communication	process.	The	analysis	therefore	focuses	on	the	relationship
between	platform	utilization,	content	planning,	visual	identity,	influencer	collaboration,	audience
engagement,	 and	 performance	 evaluation.	 By	 examining	 these	 dimensions,	 the	 study	 seeks	 to
explain	how	Instagram	functions	as	a	strategic	communication	ecosystem	for	brand	awareness
formation.
The	novelty	of	this	study	lies	in	its	integrated	perspective.	Instead	of	treating	Instagram
content,	 endorsement,	 engagement,	 and	 evaluation	 as	 separate	 variables,	 this	 article	 organizes
them	 into	 a	 coherent	 framework	 of	 Instagram-based	 marketing	 communication.	 The	 study
contributes	 theoretically	 by	 enriching	 the	 literature	 on	 IMC	 and	 social	 media	 marketing	 in	 the
context	of	brand	awareness.	It	contributes	practically	by	offering	recommendations	for	brands
that	aim	to	build	awareness	through	consistent	and	adaptive	social	media	management.

METHOD
This	study	employed	a	qualitative	research	approach	with	a	exploratory	case	study	design.
The	qualitative	approach	was	selected	because	the	research	seeks	to	understand	communication
processes,	meanings,	and	strategic	practices	in	a	real-life	digital	context	rather	than	to	measure
variables	 statistically.	 A	 case	study	 design	 is	 appropriate	 because	 the	 research	 focuses	 on	 a
specific	 case,	 namely	 the	 marketing	 communication	 strategy	 of	 Mills	 through	 Instagram.	 This
design	 allows	 the	 researcher	 to	 explore	 how	 strategic	 communication	 elements	 are	 planned,
executed,	 evaluated,	 and	 interpreted	 within	 the	 brand's	 digital	 communication	 environment
(Lim,	2025;	Salim	&	Syahrum,	2012)	Recent	qualitative	methodology	literature	also	emphasizes
that	case-based	inquiry	is	useful	for	evaluating,	refining,	and	expanding	theory	when	the	research
object	involves	contextual	and	process-oriented	phenomena	(Abidin	et	al.,	2023;	Fife	&	Gossner,
2024).
The	research	object	is	Mills'	Instagram	account	as	a	communication	channel	used	to	build
brand	awareness.	The	selection	of	this	object	was	based	on	the	brand's	active	use	of	Instagram
features,	including	feed	posts,	reels,	stories,	live	sessions,	and	direct	interaction	with	audiences.
Mills	 was	 also	 selected	 because	 it	 represents	 a	 national	 sports	 apparel	 brand	 that	 needs	 to
compete	in	a	digital	market	characterized	by	high	visual	exposure	and	strong	brand	competition.
The	 object	 is	 therefore	 relevant	 for	 examining	 how	 social	 media	 communication	 supports
recognition,	recall,	and	associative	positioning	in	the	sports	apparel	industry.
The	subjects	of	the	study	were	selected	using	purposive	sampling.	Informants	consisted
of	 key	 individuals	 who	 were	 directly	 involved	 in	 Mills'	 marketing	 communication	 activities,
including	the	Head	of	Brand	Activation	and	the	Marketing	Manager.	Supporting	perspectives	were
also	 obtained	 from	 audiences	 or	 consumers	 who	 followed	 Mills'	 Instagram	 account.	 These
informants	 were	 selected	 because	 they	 possessed	 relevant	 knowledge,	 professional
responsibility,	experience,	and	understanding	of	the	brand's	communication	practices.	Purposive
sampling	 was	 used	 to	 ensure	 that	 the	 data	 came	 from	 individuals	 who	 could	 provide	 rich	 and
relevant	 information	 about	 the	 research	 focus	(Saraan	 et	 al.,	 2025).	 The	 study	 involved	 8
informants:	2	managers	(Head	of	Brand	Activation,	Digital	Marketing	Manager)	and	6	consumer
informants	(age	18-35,	regular	Instagram	engagement	minimum	3	posts/month,	familiarity	with
Mills	≥6	months).
Primary	 data	 were	 collected	 through	 in-depth	 interviews.	 The	 interviews	 were	 used	 to
explore	 strategic	 objectives,	 content	 planning,	 message	 development,	 platform	 management,
collaboration	decisions,	audience	engagement	practices,	and	evaluation	mechanisms.	Secondary
data	were	collected	through	observation	of	Instagram	content	and	documentation.	Observation
focused	on	feed	posts,	reels,	stories,	captions,	visual	identity,	interaction	patterns,	and	the	use	of
Instagram	 features.	 Documentation	 included	 archived	 content,	 screenshots	 of	 interactions,
campaign	 materials,	 and	 relevant	 supporting	 literature.	 The	 combination	 of	 these	 techniques
enabled	 the	 study	 to	 capture	 both	 managerial	 perspectives	 and	 observable	 communication
practices.
The	 interview	 procedure	 was	 semi-structured.	 The	 questions	 were	 organized	 around
several	themes:	communication	objectives,	target	audience,	content	planning,	Instagram	feature
selection,	 visual	 identity	 management,	 collaboration	 with	 athletes	 or	 influencers,	 engagement
practices,	and	evaluation	indicators.	This	format	allowed	the	researcher	to	maintain	consistency
across	 informants	 while	 still	 giving	 space	 for	 additional	 explanations	 that	 emerged	 during	 the
interviews.	 The	 managerial	 informants	 were	 asked	 to	 describe	 not	 only	 what	 strategies	 were
used,	but	also	why	those	strategies	were	selected	and	how	they	were	evaluated	in	practice.
Instagram	 observation	 was	 conducted	 by	 examining	 visible	 communication	 patterns
rather	 than	 by	 focusing	 on	 isolated	 posts.	 The	 observation	 emphasized	 recurring	 content
categories,	 consistency	 of	 visual	 elements,	 caption	 style,	 use	 of	 platform	 features,	 audience
interaction	 patterns,	 and	 the	 presence	 of	 athlete	 or	 influencer	 collaborations.	 The	 observed
materials	were	then	grouped	into	thematic	categories	so	that	the	analysis	could	identify	how	each
content	 type	 contributed	 to	 brand	 exposure,	 recognition,	 and	 category	 association.	 Publicly
available	 Instagram	 content	 was	 treated	 as	 documentation	 of	 brand	 communication	 practice,
while	 interview	 data	 were	 used	 to	 clarify	 the	 strategic	 reasoning	 behind	 those	 observable
practices.	Observation	period:	January-June	2024	(6	months).	Stratified	sampling	of	150	posts	by

Data	analysis	followed	the	interactive	model	of	Miles	and	Huberman,	consisting	of	data
reduction,	data	display,	and	conclusion	drawing.	Data	reduction	was	conducted	by	selecting	and
focusing	 interview,	 observation,	 and	 documentation	 data	 that	 were	 relevant	to	 the	 research
question.	 Data	 display	 was	 conducted	 through	 narrative	 descriptions,	 thematic	 organization,
tables,	and	visual	mapping.	Conclusions	were	drawn	by	identifying	patterns	related	to	strategic
platform	utilization,	content	categorization,	visual	consistency,	influencer	collaboration,	audience
engagement,	 and	 performance	 evaluation.	 The	 interpretation	 was	 then	 linked	 to	 marketing
communication,	IMC,	social	media	marketing,	and	brand	awareness	concepts	(Miles	et	al.,	2014;
Miles	&	Huberman,	1994).
To	 ensure	 credibility,	 this	 study	 applied	 source	 triangulation	 by	 comparing	 interview
findings	 with	 Instagram	 observation	 and	 documentation.	 Member	 checking	 was	 conducted	 by
confirming	 relevant	 interpretations	 with	 informants	 to	 reduce	 misunderstanding	 and	improve
accuracy.	 Prolonged	 engagement	 with	 the	 research	 object	 was	 also	 carried	 out	 through
continuous	 observation	 of	 Instagram	 activities.	 These	 procedures	 were	 used	 to	 improve	 the
trustworthiness	of	the	findings	and	to	ensure	that	the	analysis	reflected	the	actual	communication
context.
Triangulation	was	carried	out	by	comparing	three	forms	of	evidence.	First,	interview	data
were	 used	 to	 identify	 managerial	 explanations	 regarding	 planning	 and	 evaluation.	 Second,
Instagram	observation	was	used	to	examine	whether	those	explanations	were	reflected	in	visible
content	practices.	Third,	documentation	was	used	to	support	the	analysis	through	archived	posts,
campaign	materials,	captions,	and	interaction	evidence.	When	the	same	pattern	appeared	across
these	sources,	it	was	treated	as	a	stronger	finding.	When	a	pattern	appeared	only	in	one	source,	it
was	interpreted	more	cautiously	and	presented	with	qualifying	language.
The	 analysis	 also	 considered	 the	 limitation	 of	 using	 social	 media	 metrics	 as	 evidence.
Engagement	rate,	reach,	impressions,	and	follower	growth	are	useful	for	understanding	exposure
and	interaction,	but	they	do	not	fully	represent	internal	consumer	awareness.	Therefore,	these
metrics	were	not	treated	as	direct	proof	of	brand	awareness	in	a	psychological	sense.	Instead,	they
were	 interpreted	 as	 indicators	 of	 communication	 visibility	 and	 audience	 response	 that	 can
support	 awareness	 formation	 when	 combined	 with	 consistent	 visual	 identity	 and	 repeated
message	 exposure.	 This	 cautious	 interpretation	 helps	 maintain	 objectivity	 in	 presenting
qualitative	findings.

Results
Instagram	as	a	Coordinated	Communication	Hub
Across	interview	and	observation	data,	Instagram	emerged	as	the	main	communication
hub	used	by	Mills.	Rather	than	relying	on	one	feature	only,	the	brand	combines	feed	posts,	reels,
stories,	live	sessions,	and	direct	interaction	to	keep	communication	continuous.	Feed	posts	are
primarily	used	to	display	products	and	maintain	a	stable	visual	identity,	while	reels	and	stories
are	used	for	more	dynamic	updates,	campaign	visibility,	and	audience	interaction.
This	 pattern	 is	 closely	 linked	 to	 planned	 communication	 routines.	 The	 Head	 of	 Brand
Activation	explained	that	"consistency	in	posting	and	visual	alignment	is	deliberately	maintained
to	 ensure	 audience	 familiarity	 with	 the	 brand	 identity"	 (Head	 of	 Brand	 Activation,	 interview).
Observation	data	also	show	that	posting	is	scheduled	through	a	monthly	editorial	calendar	and
distributed	at	engagement-relevant	times.	These	findings	indicate	that	Instagram	is	treated	as	a
managed	communication	environment	rather	than	a	space	for	incidental	posting.
A	more	detailed	reading	of	the	data	shows	that	the	editorial	calendar	functions	as	a	control
mechanism.	It	helps	the	brand	decide	which	content	should	appear	as	product	information,	which
content	should	support	a	campaign,	and	which	content	should	be	used	to	stimulate	interaction.
This	 arrangement	 reduces	 the	 possibility	 of	 fragmented	 communication	 because	 each	 post	 is
linked	to	a	broader	communication	plan.	In	observation,	the	same	logic	can	be	seen	in	the	way
product	visuals,	campaign	captions,	and	interactive	features	appear	in	a	repeated	but	not	identical
pattern.
The	main	contribution	of	this	theme	is	repeated	exposure.	By	appearing	regularly	through
multiple	 Instagram	 features,	 Mills	 maintains	 familiarity	 with	 its	 name,	 product	 visuals,	 and
communication	style.	The	key	evidence	for	this	first	theme	is	summarized	in	Table	2.
In	 short,	 the	 first	 theme	 shows	 that	 Instagram	 serves	 as	 the	 communication	 base	 from
which	other	strategic	practices	are	carried	out.

Athlete	Collaboration	Strengthens	Category	Association
The	third	theme	concerns	the	role	of	collaboration	with	athletes	and	other	relevant	public
figures.	Interview	data	and	observed	posts	indicate	that	collaboration	is	used	to	expand	reach	and
to	place	the	brand	within	a	clear	sports	context.	Collaborative	posts	do	not	only	show	the	product;
they	also	connect	the	product	with	athletic	performance,	sports	routines,	and	credible	users	This
pattern	 is	 in	 line	 with	 digital	 endorsement	 logic	 in	 which	 relevant	 public	 figures	 can	 increase
visibility	and	strengthen	message	credibility	(Amin	&	Priansah,	2019;	Andriani	et	al.,	2023).
Observation	 data	 suggest	 that	 posts	 featuring	 athletes	 tend	 to	 attract	 more	 visible
interaction	 than	 standard	 catalog	 posts.	 Informants	 also	 reported	 that	 athlete-related	 content
usually	receives	stronger	audience	attention	and	is	useful	for	campaign	refinement.	This	does	not
mean	that	collaboration	replaces	other	content	types.	Instead,	it	works	alongside	regular	product
and	campaign	content.
This	pattern	was	also	reflected	in	the	way	informants	discussed	performance	review.	The
Marketing	Manager	indicated	that	athlete-related	content	is	often	used	as	a	reference	for	future
campaign	planning	because	it	tends	to	create	stronger	interaction	than	ordinary	catalog	content.
The	statement	is	important	because	it	shows	that	collaboration	is	not	evaluated	only	as	a	branding
accessory.	It	becomes	part	of	a	learning	process	in	which	the	brand	identifies	what	type	of	content
attracts	attention	and	what	type	of	association	strengthens	the	brand's	position.
The	 main	 descriptive	 pattern	 in	 this	 theme	 is	 category	 association.	 Through	 athlete
collaboration,	 Mills	 becomes	 easier	 to	 associate	 with	 sports	 performance	 and	 professional
sporting	 environments.	 This	 makes	 the	 brand	 more	 salient	 within	 the	 sports	 apparel	category
because	the	audience	encounters	the	brand	together	with	category-relevant	figures.
Thus,	the	third	theme	shows	that	collaboration	functions	as	evidence-based	positioning
support,	not	merely	as	decorative	endorsement.

Discussion
This	study	asked	how	Mills	uses	Instagram	to	build	brand	awareness.	The	results	show
that	the	answer	lies	in	the	integration	of	four	practices:	Instagram	is	treated	as	a	communication
hub,	 content	 is	 organized	 into	 consistent	 categories,	 athlete	 collaboration	 builds	 category
association,	 and	 evaluation	 guides	 adaptation.	 Figure	 2	 synthesizes	 these	 relationships	 into	 an
integrated	framework.
These	 findings	 support	 the	 integrated	 marketing	 communication	 perspective.	 IMC
emphasizes	 that	 communication	 effectiveness	 depends	 on	 consistency	 across	 message	 design,
media	use,	and	audience	orientation.	In	the	present	study,	the	integration	does	not	primarily	occur
across	 many	 platforms;	 it	 occurs	 within	 one	 platform	 that	 contains	 multiple	 features	 and
interaction	spaces.	This	extends	earlier	IMC	discussions	by	showing	that	feed	posts,	reels,	stories,

familiarity.
Compared	with	previous	research,	the	present	study	confirms	the	importance	of	content
consistency,	endorsement,	and	audience	engagement,	but	it	also	addresses	a	gap	in	the	literature.
Earlier	studies	often	isolated	these	elements	by	examining	content	marketing,	endorsement,	or
platform	 interaction	 separately	(Abbas,	 et	 al.,	 2021;	Efendioğlu	 &	 Durmaz,	 2022;	Rahardani	 &
Sanjaya,	2022).	The	current	findings	suggest	that	these	elements	are	more	meaningful	when	read
as	an	interconnected	process.	Table	3	clarifies	how	each	empirical	theme	connects	to	theory	and
practical	implications.
The	 results	 also	 provide	 a	 more	 nuanced	 view	 of	 Instagram	 performance.	 Not	 all	 posts
appear	to	contribute	in	the	same	way.	Routine	product	posts	may	generate	less	visible	interaction
than	collaborative	posts,	yet	they	remain	important	because	they	sustain	visibility	and	reinforce
visual	continuity.	This	negative	case	is	important	because	it	shows	that	lower-interaction	content
is	not	necessarily	ineffective;	its	role	may	be	supportive	rather	than	attention-maximizing.
Practically,	the	findings	suggest	that	brand	managers	should	evaluate	Instagram	not	only
by	 output	 volume	 or	 follower	 growth,	 but	 by	 the	 interaction	 among	 platform	 use,	 content
structure,	collaboration,	and	feedback	loops.	For	local	or	national	brands	such	as	Mills,	this	means
that	 competitive	 advantage	 in	 social	 media	 can	 be	 developed	 through	 disciplined	 consistency,
relevant	collaborations,	and	adaptive	planning	rather	than	through	promotional	intensity	alone.
Theoretical	implications	can	be	drawn	from	the	way	this	study	positions	Instagram	as	a
site	of	internal	integration.	IMC	is	often	explained	as	coordination	across	several	media	channels,
such	 as	 advertising,	 public	 relations,	 sales	 promotion,	 and	 digital	 media.	 The	 present	 findings
suggest	 that	 integration	 is	 also	 needed	 within	 one	 platform	 because	 Instagram	 itself	 contains
different	communication	spaces.	A	feed	post,	a	reel,	a	story,	a	live	session,	and	a	direct	message	do
not	 operate	 in	 the	 same	 way.	 Each	format	 has	 its	 own	 rhythm,	 audience	 expectation,	 and
communicative	function.	When	these	formats	are	connected	by	the	same	strategic	direction,	they
can	collectively	support	brand	awareness.
The	 study	 also	 contributes	 to	 the	 discussion	 of	 brand	 awareness	 by	 showing	 that
recognition	 and	 recall	 are	 supported	 by	 different	 but	 connected	 practices.	 Recognition	 is
strengthened	through	repeated	exposure	to	visual	cues,	product-centered	content,	color	tone,	and
layout	consistency.	Recall	is	strengthened	when	the	brand	is	repeatedly	associated	with	a	specific
category,	 such	 as	 sports	 performance,	 athlete	 credibility,	 or	 national	 sports	 identity.	 This
distinction	 is	 useful	 because	 a	 brand	 may	 be	 visible	without	 being	 strongly	 associated	 with	 a
category.	 For	 Mills,	 the	 combination	 of	 visual	 continuity	 and	 athlete-related	 association	 helps
connect	visibility	with	category	meaning.
The	practical	implication	for	social	media	managers	is	that	content	evaluation	should	be
multidimensional.	 A	 post	 with	 high	 interaction	 may	 be	 valuable	 for	 reach	 and	 salience,	 but	 a
lower-interaction	 post	 may	 still	 be	 valuable	 for	 continuity	 and	 recognition.	 Therefore,	 brands
should	not	eliminate	routine	product	posts	only	because	they	generate	fewer	visible	responses.
Instead,	 they	 should	 define	 the	 role	 of	 each	 content	 type	 within	 the	 broader	 communication
system.	Product	posts	maintain	visibility,	campaign	posts	create	narrative	direction,	collaborative
posts	increase	category	relevance,	and	interactive	posts	strengthen	audience	participation.
For	local	or	national	sports	apparel	brands,	the	findings	also	suggest	the	importance	of
selecting	collaboration	partners	carefully.	Collaboration	should	not	be	based	only	on	popularity
or	follower	count.	It	should	be	based	on	compatibility	between	the	collaborator's	public	image
and	 the	 brand's	 desired	 association.	 In	 the	 sports	 apparel	 category,	 athletes	 and	 sport-related

The	 integrated	 framework	 developed	 in	 this	 study	 therefore	 contributes	 both

CONCLUSION
This	study	concludes	that	Mills'	marketing	communication	strategy	through	Instagram	is
systematically	planned,	integrated,	and	consistently	implemented	to	enhance	brand	awareness.
Instagram	 is	 used	 not	 merely	 as	 a	 promotional	 platform,	 but	 as	 a	 strategic	 communication
infrastructure	 that	 supports	 repeated	 exposure,	 audience	 interaction,	 message	 delivery,	 and
performance	evaluation.	The	strategy	is	implemented	through	structured	content	planning,	visual
identity	 consistency,	 influencer	 and	 athlete	 collaboration,	 and	 adaptive	 evaluation	 based	 on
Instagram	insights.
The	 findings	 indicate	 that	 consistent	 messaging	 and	 visual	 identity	 contribute	 to	 brand
recognition,	 while	 athlete	 collaboration	 and	 platform	 interaction	 strengthen	 associative
positioning	 and	 brand	 salience.	 Brand	 awareness	 is	 therefore	 formed	 through	 a	 synergistic
process	 rather	 than	 a	 single	 communication	 activity.	 The	 coordination	 between	 platform
utilization,	 message	 structuring,	 visual	 coherence,	 collaboration,	 and	 evaluation	 creates	 a
comprehensive	 communication	 system	 that	 supports	 recognition	 and	 recall	 in	 the	 competitive
sports	apparel	market.	Theoretically,	this	study	contributes	to	digital	marketing	communication
literature	by	proposing	an	integrated	Instagram	marketing	communication	framework	for	brand
awareness	 formation.	 The	 framework	 shows	 that	 social	 media	 communication	 should	 be
understood	as	a	system	of	interconnected	dimensions	rather	than	isolated	tactics.	Practically,	the
findings	suggest	that	brands	should	manage	Instagram	through	clear	planning,	consistent	visual
identity,	 relevant	 collaboration,	 active	 engagement,	 and	 regular	 performance	 review.	 Future
studies	 are	 encouraged	 to	 test	 this	 framework	 quantitatively,	 compare	 different	 brands,	 and
examine	how	audience-generated	content	influences	brand	awareness	over	time.
