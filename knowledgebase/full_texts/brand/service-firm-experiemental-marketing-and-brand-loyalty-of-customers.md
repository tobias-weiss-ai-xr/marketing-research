Abstract
The study examined experiential marketing influence on brand loyalty of customer among
service firms in Cross River State. It was motivated by the increasing challenges faced by
service firms in maintaining customer loyalty amidst growing competition, changin g
customer preferences, and declining customer retention rates. The s et objectives include
determining the influence of customer engagement, brand experience, emotional connection,
event marketing, and customer participation on customer loyalty among servi ce firms in the
study environment. In the study, survey research design was us ed. Descriptive statistics and
multiple regression analysis w ere employed to present and analyze data, and test the
hypotheses at 0.05 level of significance using the SPSS. Findings revealed that customer
engagement, brand experience, emotional connection, event marketing, and customer
participation all have significant positive influences on customer loyalty among service firms
in Cross River State. Emotional connection has the greatest influence on  customer loyalty.
Based on these outcome, it was recommended that service firms should strengthen customer
engagement strategies, improve brand experiences, develop emotional connection
programmes, organize experiential marketing events, and encourage active customer
participation in order to enhance customer loyalty and long -term organizational
sustainability.

Keywords: Service Firms, Experiemental Marketing, Brand Loyalty, Customer Engagement,
Brand Experience, Emotional Connection, Event Marketing.
Introduction

customer-driven, compelling service firms to adopt innovative marketing approaches capable
of creating memorable consumer experiences and sustaining customer loyalty. T raditional
transactional marketing strategies that focused primarily on product promotion and sales
stimulation are gradually giving way to experiential or experimental marketing approaches
that emphasize emotional engagement, interactive communication, an d customer
participation. According to Fatoki and Fatoki (2020), experimental marketing represents a

The approach focuses on allowing consumers to “feel” the brand through sensory, emotional,

In service-oriented economies such as Nigeria, customer loyalty has become a major driver of

particular service brand despite competing alternatives. According to the authors, loyal
customers not only engage in repeat patronage but also become advocates of the organization
through positive word-of-mouth communication and brand recommendation.
Experimental marketing is one of  the most effective mordern  approaches for improv ing
customer loy alty because it creates emotional and psychological attachments between
customers and brands. One major proxy of experimental marketing is customer engagement,
which reflects the degree to which consumers adopt organizational offerings and
communication platforms. Fatoki and Fatoki (2020) argued that engaged  customers are more
satisfied, and usually develop long-term relationships and trust with service firms. Similarly,
brand experience constitutes another important dimension of experimental marketing beca use
customers tend to retain brands that provide enjoyable, memorable, and satisfying
experiences. Positive brand experiences stimulate customer satisfaction and reinforce repeat
patronage behaviour.
Another important element of experimental marketing is e motional connection, which
involves the creation of emotional bonds between customers and service providers.
According to Ogwezzy -Ndisika, Oguntola, Ojenike, Tejumaiye, Ibraheem and Olunifesi
(2023), emotional attachment significantly influences consumer d ecision-making and loyalty
behaviour because customers often patronize brands with which they share psychological
affinity and emotional satisfaction. Event marketing also represents a strategic experiential
tool through which firms organize promotional ev ents, roadshows, exhibitions, sponsorships,
and interactive campaigns aimed at creating direct customer involvement with the brand. The
scholars emphasized that such activities increase brand visibility, customer interaction, and
memorable experiences capable of strengthening customer loyalty.
Furthermore, customer participation enables consumers to become active contributors in
service creation and delivery processes. Maduka (2016) observed that modern consumers
increasingly desire personalized experiences  and opportunities to co -create value with
organizations, thereby enhancing their level of attachment to a  brand. This participatory
process strengthens customer satisfaction and encourages long -term relational commitment
between consumers and service firms.

 Although several studies have examined experiential marketing and customer behaviour in
developed economies, limited empirical attention has been devoted to examining how
customer engagement, brand experience, emotional connection, event marketing, and
customer participation influence customer loyalty among service firms in Cross Ri ver State.
This study, thus , is meant  to bridge this gap in knowledge  by examining the relationship
between service firm experimental marketing and customer loyalty in Cross River State,
Nigeria.
The service sector in Cross River State has witnessed increasing competition arising from the
rapid expansion of banking, hospita lity, telecommunications, transportation, healthcare,
tourism, and other customer -oriented businesses. This competitive business environment has
significantly altered consumer expectations, attitudes, and purchasing behaviour, thereby
making customer reten tion one of the most difficult challenges confronting service firms.
Customers are increasingly becoming more informed, selective, and experience -driven in
their patronage decisions. Consequently, many service firms now struggle with declining
customer tru st, weak emotional attachment, low repeat patronage, and high customer
switching behaviour despite huge investments in conventional advertising and promotional
campaigns.
The inability of many service firms to maintain long -term customer relationships has
continued to affect profitability, market share, and organizational sustainability. Traditional
marketing approaches that primarily focus on product promotion and transactional exchanges
appear insufficient in addressing the contemporary demands of consume rs who seek
memorable, interactive, and emotionally satisfying service experiences. As observed by
Umoren et al (2022), experiential brand activations significantly influence customer loyalty,
customer sentiment, and repeat engagement in highly competitive  markets. However, many
service firms in Cross River State have not adequately integrated experimental marketing
dimensions such as customer engagement, brand experience, emotional connection, event
marketing, and customer participation into their strategic operations.
Furthermore, empirical studies on experimental marketing in Nigeria have largely
concentrated on manufacturing firms and urban commercial centres, with limited attention
devoted to service firms operating within Cross River State. This has created a contextual and
empirical gap regarding the extent to which experimenta l marketing strategies affect
customers’ brand loyalty to the service sector. It is against this backdrop that this study seeks
to investigate the relationship between service fi rm experimental marketing and customer
loyalty in Cross River State, with emphasis on customer engagement, brand experience,
emotional connection, event marketing, and customer participation.

hospitality, insurance, telecommunications, healthcare, transportation, education, and
consulting services to customers. Unlike manufacturing firms that offer physical products,
service firms emphasize customer interactions, experiences, and relationship management as
core drivers of value creation. Modern service economies increasingly depend on customer

Concept of Experiential Marketing
Experiential marketing refers to a strategic marketing approach t hat creates memorable and
emotionally engaging experiences capable of influencing customers’ perceptions, attitudes,
and purchasing behaviors. The concept emerged from the experience economy theory
developed by Pine and Gilmore, which emphasizes that custo mers seek memorable
experiences rather than mere products or services.
Experiential marketing focuses on sensory stimulation, emotional appeal, customer
participation, and interactive engagement. Schmitt (2019) explained that experiential
marketing enables firms to establish stronger emotional relationships with consumers through
sensory, affective, behavioral, and relational experiences. Modern consumers prefer brands
that provide immersive and personalized experiences capable of satisfying both functional
and emotional needs.
Recent studies indicate that experiential marketing positively influences customer
satisfaction, brand attachment, trust, and loyalty. Rashid et al. (2021) found that experiential
value significantly enhances customer loyalty in coffe e shop services. Similarly, Zha et al.
(2024) established that sensory brand experiences involving visual, auditory, tactile,
olfactory, and taste dimensions significantly improve customer attachment and loyalty.

Customers’ Brand Loyalty
This is when customer show consistent demand, commitment, and repeated patronage toward
a brand or organization over time. It encompasses and shows  through their emotional
attachment, advocacy, and positive word -of-mouth communication for the brand. It is one of
the most valuable assets of service firms because loyal customers contribute to profitability,
reduced marketing costs, and organizational sustainability.
Recent literature identifies customer satisfaction, service quality, emotional attach ment, trust,
and experiential value as major determinants of loyalty. So et al. (2025) conducted a meta -
analysis inv olving 153 studies and found that  customer loyalty is s trongly affected by
perception of service quality and perception of values to derive d from the brand, leading to
customer satisfaction. Similarly, Ahmad et al. (2022) observed that positive online customer
experiences improve customer loyalty through customer engagement and value co -creation,
and that customers are more loyal to firms tha t actively involve them in value creation
processes.
In contemporary service markets, loyalty extends beyond repeated purchases to include
emotional commitment, social advocacy, and customer participation. Modern consumers
increasingly support brands that align with their personal values and emotional expectations.
Consequently, service firms now focus on relationship -building and experiential strategies
capable of strengthening emotional and psychological bonds with customers.
Customer Engagement
Customer engagement refers to the cognitive, emotional, and behavioral interactions between
customers and brands across various touchpoints. It involves customers’ active participation
in brand-related activities, communications, and experiences.
Customer engagemen t has emerged as a strategic tool for enhancing customer loyalty and
organizational competitiveness. Engaged customers tend to exhibit higher trust, emotional
attachment, advocacy behavior, and repeat patronage. Rohit et al. (2025) argued that
customer eng agement is strongly influenced by service marketing mix elements such as
product quality, employee interactions, and service experiences. Digital technologies and
social media have transformed customer engagement processes by enabling interactive
communication, personalized content, and user -generated participation. Mazeli (2025) found
that social media engagement tools such as interactive content, real -time communication, and
visual storytelling significantly improve customer loyalty in Nigerian online retail stores.

Brand Experience
Brand experience refers to the sensations, emotions, perceptions, and responses evoked by

Emotional Connection
Emotional connection refers to the psychological bond and affective attachment customers
develop toward brands or organizations. Emotional connections influence customers’
attitudes, trust, commitment, and loyalty behaviors. Research indicates that emotionally
connected customers exhibit stronger loyalty and advocacy behaviors than customers whose
relationships are purely transactional. Emotional branding strategies enable firms to appeal to
customers’ feelings, values, aspirations, and identities.
Recent studies  reveal that emotional loyalty has become increasingly important in
modern competitive markets. Contemporary consumers prefer brands that demonstrate
authenticity, empathy, social responsibility, and cultural relevance. Reports indicate that
emotional brand equity significantly influences customer retention and engagement in highly
competitive industries. Service firms create emotional connections through personalized
interactions, empathy, storytelling, memorable experiences, and community -building
initiatives. Emotional attachment enhances customer trust and reduces customers’ likelihood
of switching to competitors.

Guiding Theoretical Standpoint
The study is hinged on the Relationship Marketing Theory by Robert M. Morgan and Shelby
D. Hunt, 1994. The theory emphasizes the establishment, nurturing, and sus tenance of long-
term relationships between organizations and customers through trust, commitment,
communication, and mutual value creation (Morgan & Hunt, 1994).

advantage when they focus on building enduring relationships rather than merely engaging in
transactional exchanges. According to the theory, customer trust and commitment are the two
major variables that determine the strength and continuity of business relationships (Morgan
& Hunt, 1994). When customers perceive that firms consistently provide value, personalized
interactions, emotional satisfaction, and reliable services, they develop stronger attachment
and loyalty toward the organization (Salem, 2021).
The theory buttresses this experiential marketing activities such as customer engagement,

Summary and Gap in Literature
The reviewed literature demonstrates that experiential marketing significantly enhances
customer loyalty through customer engagement, emotional connection, brand experience,
event marketing, and customer participation. Existing studies consistently show that
memorable and emotionally engaging experiences improve customer satisfaction, attachment,
and retention.
However, despite extensive global studies, limited empirical attention has been given t o the
combined effects of experiential marketing dimensions on customer loyalty among service
firms in developing economies, particularly Nigeria. Most existing studies focus on
hospitality, retail, or digital platforms without adequately examining broader service sectors.
Furthermore, many studies examine individual experiential variables independently rather
than integrating customer engagement, emotional connection, event marketing, and customer
participation within a unified framework. This creates a co ntextual and conceptual gap that
necessitates further investigation.

 Research Methodology
Research Design
This study adopted the survey research design. The design was considered relevant for
generating data from respondents concerning experimental marketing and customer loyalty
among service firms. The survey design also facilitates the generalization of findings  from
collected and analyzed quantitative data.

The Study Area
The study was conducted among selected service firms in Calab ar. The area was selected
because of the increasing presence of service -oriented businesses and the growing
competition among firms in sectors such as banking, hospitality, telecommunications, and
retail services.

The Study Population
The population of t he study comprised customers of selected service firms in Calabar, Cross
River State. The target population included active customers who regularly interact with the
selected firms and are knowledgeable about their experiential marketing practices. The
population figure is estimated at 257,000 service firms customers in the study area.

Sampling Process
A sample size of 400 respondents was determined for the study using the Taro Yamane
formula for finite populations. The purposive sampling technique was ado pted in the study.
Purposive sampling was used to select relevant service firms, while simple random sampling
was employed in selecting respondents from the customer base of the firms.

Sources of Data
Primary sources of data were utilized in the study. The data were obtained through the
administration of structured questionnaires to respondents, while secondary data were
sourced from textbooks, journals, conference papers, internet materials, and related academic
publications.

Instrument for Data Collection
The major instrument for data collection was a structured questionnaire titled “Experimental
Marketing and Customer Loyalty Questionnaire (EMCLQ).” The questionnaire was divided
into two sections. Section A captured respondents’ demographic characteristics, while
Section B contained items relating to experiential marketing variables such as customer
engagement, brand experience, emotional connection, event marketing, customer
participation, and customer loyalty.

The Instrument Validity
The instrument was subjected to face and content validity by experts in marketing and
research methodology. Their observations and corrections helped to improve the clarity,
relevance, and adequacy of the questionnaire items.

Summary of Findings
The findings of the study revealed that experiential marketing dimensions significantly
influence customer loy alty among service firms in Cross River State. Specifically, customer
engagement, brand experience, emotional connection, event marketing, and customer
participation were found to have positive and statistically significant effects on customer

Recommendations
1. Service firms should strengthen customer engagement strategies through personalized
communication, interactive digital platforms, and prompt customer feedback
mechanisms in order to improve customer loyalty.
2. Management of service firms should invest in creating positive and memorable brand
experiences by improving service quality, environmental ambience , and customer
service delivery.
3. Service organizations should develop emotional connection strategies such as
empathy-driven services, storytelling, and customer appreciation programmes to
enhance customers’ emotional attachment to their brands.
4. Firms should regularly organize experiential and event marketing activities including
promotional events, customer forums, live experiences, and community -based
programmes to deepen customer relationships and brand visibility.
5. Service firms should encourage active c ustomer participation through co -creation
activities, online interactions, loyalty programmes, and customer involvement in
