Abstract
The sports apparel industry plays an important role in economic development and the sports
industry, particularly among small and medium -sized enterprises (SMEs) that face intense competition.
Brand creation and logistics management processes are considered strategic factors that influence business
competitiveness. This study aimed to review and synthesize literature related to brand creation, logistics
management processes, and the competitive advantage of sports apparel businesses in the context of
Chonburi Province. A systematic literature review methodology was employed, using reliable academic
documents from both domestic and international sources, which were selected according to criteria aligned
with the objectives of the study. The literature review revealed that brand creation is related to competitive
advantage in terms of differentiation and customer loyalty, whereas logistics management processes are
related to competitive advantage in terms of cost and operational speed. In addition, the integrat ion of
brand creation with logistics management processes can strengthen sustainable competitive advantage.

Introduction
 The sports apparel industry is an industry that plays a significant role in economic development
and the sports industry, as it is connected to promoting exercise, health, and sports tourism, as well as creating
added value for the country's textile and apparel industry ( Watchravesringkan et al., 2013) Sports apparel
businesses not only meet functional needs but also serve as important tools for creating brand image, value,
and differentiation, which affect the business's long-term competitiveness (Belch & Belch, 2018)
 However, sports apparel businesses in Thailand, particularly small and medium -sized enterprises
(SMEs), face intense competition from international brands, rapid changes in consumer behavior, and
resource constraints in capital, technology, and management capabilities. This necessitates Thai
entrepreneurs to develop strategies that can create differentiation and sustainably enhance
competitiveness (Watchravesringkan et al., 2013).
 Additionally, operational challenges, particularly in logistics and supply chain management
processes, are critical factors affecting the efficiency and performance of sports apparel businesses. Issues
related to costs, delays in product delivery, and inconsistencies between brand communication and product
delivery may impact customer satisfaction and business image in the long term (Mentzer et al., 2001 ;
Christopher & Peck, 2004).
 Although previous research has extensively studied concepts of branding and logistics management
processes, few studies have systematically linked branding and logistics management processes to competitive
advantage in sports apparel businesses, especially in spatial contexts. Research focusing on sports apparel
businesses in Chonburi Province, an area with potential in industry, tourism, and sports activities, remains limited.
This indicates a lack of integrated understanding regarding the roles of brandin g and logistics management
processes on competitive advantage in such contexts (Porter, 2008).
 From the literature review, gaps remain in studies linking the relationship between branding and
logistics management processes to competitive advantage of sports apparel businesses in the context of
Chonburi Province, an area with potential for sports ind ustry development. Therefore, this study aims to
review and synthesize literature related to branding, logistics management processes, and competitive
advantage to explain the conceptual relationships among these factors and provide a foundation for
developing knowledge and strategic approaches appropriate for sports apparel businesses in the context
of Chonburi Province.
 Chonburi Province possesses strong potential in terms of economic development, sports tourism,
and logistics systems, as it is part of the Eastern Economic Corridor (EEC). In addition, the province has
continuously developed its infrastructure and promoted  sports-related activities, thereby facilitating the
operation of sports -related businesses, including sports apparel enterprises. Therefore, synthesizing the
concepts of branding and logistics management for enhancing the competitive advantage of sports apparel
businesses in the context of Chonburi Province is important for advancing knowledge and practical
applications (Kamonmarl et al., 2023)

Literature Review Method
 This article aimed to synthesize literature related to the relationship between branding and logistics
management processes and competitive advantage of sports apparel businesses, focusing on explaining the
roles of these variables in the context of small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), as well as conceptual
 This article adopted  a systematic literature review approach, utilizing academic sources from
recognized databases including Google Scholar, Thai Journals Online (TCI), and Scopus, to cover both
international and Thai research contexts.
 Keywords used for searching include “brand creation”, “branding”, “logistics management”,
“supply chain management”, “competitive advantage”, and “sports apparel”. The review focuses on
documents published between 2015 and 2025 in both Thai and English to ensure relevance to
contemporary competitive contexts.
 The selection of literature was based on the following criteria: (1) relevance to the main variables,
namely branding, logistics management processes, and competitive advantage; (2) credibility of publication
sources such as academic journals, books, and t heses; and (3) clarity of conceptual frameworks or findings
that can be theoretically synthesized.
 The selected literature was analyzed using a narrative synthesis approach to identify key concepts,
patterns, and relationships among variables reported in previous studies. This article emphasizes conceptual
explanation rather t empirical testing.

 Branding in Sports Apparel Business
 Concepts and Theories
 The relationship between branding and competitive advantage is clearly explained in competitive
strategy research, particularly Porter's (2008 ) concept proposing that differentiation is a key mechanism in
creating competitive advantage. Creating a brand with clear identity and market positioning (Brand Identity
and Brand Positioning) enables organizations to present value different from competitor s and reduce
sensitivity to price competition (Aaker, 1991; Belch & Belch, 2018).
 Branding research also indicates that Brand Loyalty and Customer Experience play important roles
in creating a stable customer base, which directly affects Customer Lifetime Value and supports long -term
competitive advantage, especially in highly competitive industries such as the sports apparel industry (Belch
& Belch, 2018).
 Yanpitak (2023) examined brand value creation in online marketplace businesses and found that
brand value is a key factor in enhancing competitiveness. Important dimensions include brand awareness,
brand image, credibility, and customer loyalty. Effective digital communic ation and customer engagement
contribute to stronger brand value and support sustainable competitive advantage in the online
marketplace environment.

branding connected to local identity and economic contexts can help reduce resource constraints and

Seven Components of Branding
 Branding is divided into seven main aspects: (1) Brand Identity, (2) Brand Image, (3) Brand Awareness,
(4) Brand Loyalty, (5) Brand Communication, (6) Customer Experience, and (7) Brand Positioning.

Related Research
Belch & Belch (2018) explains that integrated marketing communication plays a significant role in
creating brand message consistency, which helps enhance brand awareness and clear image in consumers'
minds, affecting value perception and long -term purchase decision behavior. F urthermore, the strategic
application of innovative marketing communication tools is essential to effectively enhance brand power.
By creating differentiation and increasing brand recall, these tools enable brands to maintain a competitive
advantage, especially in highly volatile and competitive markets.

Logistics Management Processes in Sports Apparel Business
Concepts and Theories
 Logistics management is a process involving planning, implementing, and controlling the
movement and storage of goods, services, and related information from origin to consumption points to
meet customer requirements efficiently and at the lowest cost (Mentzer et al., 2001). Logistics management
processes involve various dimensions including demand planning, material sourcing, production, delivery,
returns, and operational support, all of which are interrelated and directly impact overall supply chain
efficiency.
 In competitive strategy contexts, efficient logistics management processes can create competitive
advantage in cost reduction (Cost Leadership) and rapid responsiveness (Speed and Agility). According to
Porter ( 2008 ), cost leadership is a key mechanism in creating competitive advantage, especially for
businesses operating in markets with intense price competition. Reducing operational costs, improving
distribution efficiency, and minimizing inventory can help businesses  offer products at more competitive
prices than competitors.
 Klan Khamson (2023) investigated the selection of logistics service providers for e -commerce
businesses and found that the key dimensions of logistics management include transportation, data
management capabilities, service levels, and warehouse management. These factors sign ificantly influence
operational efficiency, cost reduction, and the ability to respond to customer demands, ultimately leading
to the creation of competitive advantage. Furthermore, the effective integration of logistics components
enhances organizational competitiveness and supports sustainable business growth.

Six Main Logistics Management Processes
Logistics management processes consist of six main processes:
1. Customer Demand Planning - Forecasting and analyzing customer needs to plan production
and inventory appropriately.
2. Material Sourcing - Procuring raw materials and necessary resources from suppliers with
appropriate quality and costs.

Competitive Advantage
 Concepts and Theories
 Competitive advantage is the capability of an organization to create more value for customers than
competitors, enabling sustainable profitability and market position (Porter, 2008 ). According to Porter's
framework, competitive advantage can be created through three main strategies: (1) Cost Leadership,
creating advantage by offering products or services at lower costs than competitors; (2) Differentiation,
creating advantage through presenting unique products or services that customers perceive as different and
valuable; and (3) Focus Strategy, concentrating on specific customer groups or market segments.
 Dovbischuk (2022) examined the innovation -oriented dynamic capabilities of logistics service
providers in rapidly changing and uncertain business environments. The study found that organizations need
to enhance logistics flexibility and adaptability to cope with technologic al changes and evolving customer
demands. These capabilities contribute to improving operational performance and sustaining long -term
competitive advantage.

Three Dimensions of Competitive Advantage
      Competitive advantage consists of three main dimensions:
1. Low Production Cost - Capability to reduce operational costs and present products at
competitive prices through efficient resource management, production process improvement, and cost
reduction in supply chains.
2. Differentiation from Competitors - Capability to create uniqueness and differentiation
customers can perceive, whether in product design, quality, innovation, or brand image.
3. Rapid Response - Capability to respond quickly to market demand changes, reduce production
and delivery times, and efficiently adapt to environmental changes.
Conceptual Framework
 The conceptual framework derived from the literature synthesis suggests that the relationships
among branding, logistics management processes, and competitive advantage in sports apparel businesses
can be conceptually explained. A proposed framework consists of three main components:
1. Branding consisting of seven components: Brand Identity, Brand Image, Brand Awareness, Brand
Loyalty, Brand Communication, Customer Experience, and Brand Positioning.
2. Logistics Management Processes consisting of six main processes: Customer Demand Planning,
Material Sourcing, Production, Product Delivery, Product Returns, and Operational Support.
3. Competitive Advantage consisting of three dimensions: Low Production Cost, Differentiation
from Competitors, and Rapid Response.

Discussion
 This article reviews and synthesizes literature related to branding, logistics management processes,
and competitive advantage in sports apparel businesses. The findings from the literature indicate that
competitive advantage does not arise from a single factor, but from the integration of ma rketing strategies
and operational processes.
 The findings from the literature suggest that branding plays a significant role in creating competitive
advantage through differentiation and customer loyalty. Brands with clear identity, consistent
communication, and perceived value can enhance differenti ation and reduce price sensitivity. Meanwhile,
logistics management processes contribute to competitive advantage through cost efficiency and
operational responsiveness.
 Furthermore, the findings from the literature indicate that the integration of branding and logistics
management processes is a key mechanism for achieving sustainable competitive advantage. When these
factors are aligned, they enhance both emotional and f unctional value, creating advantages that artificial
to imitate.
Theoretical Benefits
 Theoretically, this study contributes to expanding knowledge on competitive advantage by
presenting integrated explanations of relationships between branding and logistics management processes,
which helps fill gaps in research that typically studies each factor separately. Such synthesis helps elevate
competitive advantage explanations from component perspectives to more systemic perspectives.
Practical Benefits
 Practically, study findings indicate that sports apparel business entrepreneurs, especially small and
medium-sized enterprise entrepreneurs, should prioritize developing branding alongside consistently
improving logistics management processes. Investing in  branding alone or focusing solely on logistics
efficiency may not be sufficient for creating long-term advantages; rather, integrating both aspects will help
enhance competitiveness, reduce resource constraints, and support sustainable business growth.
Recommendations for Entrepreneurs
1. Develop brand identity connected to local contexts and create clear differentiation.
2. Create integrated brand communication systems consistent across both online and offline
channels.
3. Improve logistics processes to reduce costs and increase speed of response.
4. Integrate brand promises with product and service delivery to create consistent experiences.
5. Develop data analysis capabilities to connect marketing data with logistics management.

in Chonburi Province areas. Additionally, furth er studies should examine moderating or mediating factors

Conclusion

explained through the integrated relationships between branding and logistics management processes,
serving as both a conceptual framework and a practical approac h for developing strategies and improving
business operations. Integrating both factors is considered for enhancing sustainable competitive advantage
for SME entrepreneurs in the sports apparel industry, particularly in the context of Chonburi Province, which
has strong potential for sports industry and sports tourism development.
