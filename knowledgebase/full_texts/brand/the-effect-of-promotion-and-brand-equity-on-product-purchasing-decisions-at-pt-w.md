ABSTRACT
This study aims to examine the effect of promotion an d brand equity on product purchasing
decisions at PT Weigo Houseware Medan. The problem addressed in this study is the decline in
consumers and sales from 2021 to 2025, which indicates that purchasing decisions require
further analysis through relevant marketing factors. This research used an associative
quantitative approach. The population consisted of 1,273 consumers during January to June
2025, and 96 respondents were selected using the Slovin formula. Data were collected through
questionnaires and anal yzed using validity tests, reliability tests, classical assumption tests,
multiple linear regression, t -test, F -test, and coefficient of determination with SPSS 24. The
results show that promotion has a positive and significant effect on purchasing decisions with a
regression coefficient of 0.334, t -value of 3.846, and significance of 0.000. Brand equity also has
a positive and significant effect with a regression coefficient of 0.485, t -value of 5.912, and
significance of 0.000. Simultaneously, promotion a nd brand equity significantly affect purchasing
decisions with an F -value of 83.526 and significance of 0.000. The adjusted R square value of
0.635 indicates that 63.5% of purchasing decisions can be explained by promotion and brand
equity. Brand equity is the dominant variable in the model.

INTRODUCTION

Purchase decisions are a critical issue in retail marketing, as they reflect consumer responses to
products, brands, promotional messages, and the overall shopping experience. In the houseware market,
consumers look beyond mere product functionality; they also weigh factors such as product information,
brand reputation, ease of acquisition, and confidence in the quality of items intended for household use.
Consequently, companies must gain a deeper understanding of the factors that most effectively drive
consumers to make a purchase.
PT Weigo Houseware Medan is a company that markets household products. The houseware
market is extensive, serving families, individuals, and small business operators alike. However, it is also
highly competitive, with numerous brands offering similar products through physical stores, digital
catalogs, social media, and online marketplaces. This competitive landscape necessitates that companies
strengthen their marketing communications and brand identity to ensure their products are not only visible
but also trusted by consumers.
Preliminary observations indicate a decline in both the number of consumers and sales figures.
The customer base fell from 41,280 in 2021 to 36,410 in 2025, while sales dropped from IDR
3,185,000,000 in 2021 to IDR 2,739,200,000 in 2025. These data suggest  that consumer purchase
decisions are not currently robust. This decline should not be viewed merely as a drop in sales figures;

LITERATURE REVIEW

Promotion serves as a marketing communication tool. It conveys information about a product —its
benefits, offers, and the reasons why a consumer should choose it. Kotler and Armstrong (2018) explain
that promotion communicates product benefits and persuades  target customers to make a purchase. In
the context of houseware products, clear promotion helps consumers understand a product's utility before
buying. Conversely, ineffective promotion may leave consumers unfamiliar with a product, even when it
aligns with their needs.
Brand equity also plays a significant role in purchasing decisions. Kotler, Keller, and Chernev
(2022) define brand equity as the added value attributed to a product or service, derived from consumer
responses to the brand. A strong brand makes it easier for consumers to recognize, recall, and trust a
product. When it comes to household goods, consumers often base their choices on past experience,
reputation, and perceived quality. Therefore, brand equity acts as a factor that reinforces purchasing
decisions.
Previous research indicates that both promotion and brand equity are linked to purchasing
decisions. Permana (2017) as well as Njoto and Sienatra (2018) found that promotion has a positive
impact on purchasing decisions. Similarly, studies by Khafid and Pr adana (2022), Tresna, Chan, and
Herawaty (2021), and Pottu, Massie, and Roring (2022) demonstrate that brand equity influences
purchasing decisions. Furthermore, Maulana and Santoso (2024) identify both promotion and brand
equity as relevant variables for explaining purchasing decisions in the retail sector.
The research gap in this study lies in its specific context and the phenomena being examined.
Much of the existing literature focuses on products such as food, apparel, motorcycles, or digital services.
This study, by contrast, centers on a houseware company in Medan that has experienced a decline in
both its consumer base and sales over a five-year period. This study also replaces the price variable from
the research by Sudirman, Lie, Efendi, and Wijaya (2017) with promotion, as pre -survey findings indicate
that the appeal of promotions is a more prominent issue.

METHODS

This study employs a quantitative approach with an associative research design. A quantitative
approach was selected because the research data consist of questionnaire scores that are processed
statistically. An associative design was used because the study aims to examine the influence of
independent variables on a dependent variable. The independent variables are promotion (X1) and brand
equity (X2), while the dependent variable is the purchasing decision (Y).
The research was conducted at PT Weigo Houseware, Medan. The study population consists of
1,273 consumers who made purchases between January and June 2025. The sample size was
determined using the Slovin formula with a 10% margin of error, resulting in 96 respondents.
Respondents were selected based on the following criteria: having purchased a product at least once,
being at least 17 years old, being aware of or having seen the company’s promotions, and being able to
evaluate their product purchasing experience.
The research data comprise both primary and secondary data. Primary data were obtained from
respondents' responses to a questionnaire, while secondary data were gathered from company records,
books, journals, and relevant prior research. The research instrument utilizes a 5 -point Likert scale,
ranging from "strongly disagree" to "strongly agree." The questionnaire was developed based on
indicators for the variables of promotion, brand equity, and purchasing decision.
Data analysis was conducted in several stages. The first stage involves data quality testing,
consisting of validity and reliability tests. An item is considered valid if its calculated r-value is greater than
0.30, and an instrument is considered reliable if its Cronbach’s Alpha coefficient is greater than 0.70. The
second stage involves classical assumption testing, which includes tests for normality, multicollinearity,
and heteroscedasticity. The third stage consists of multiple linear regression analysi s, t-tests, F-tests, and
the determination of the coefficient of determination.

Discussion on the Influence of Promotion on Purchasing Decisions
The results show that promotion has a positive and significant effect on purchasing decisions. This
finding means that promotional activities carried out by PT Weigo Houseware Medan can influence
consumers in choosing products. Promotion helps consumers obtain information about product benefits,
product availability, and purchase offers. The positive coefficient indicates that improvement in promotion
can increase purchasing decisions.
The findings are consistent with th e role of promotion in marketing theory. Kotler and Armstrong
(2018) explain that promotion communicates product value and persuades consumers to buy. For
houseware products, consumers need information that is practical, clear, and easy to remember.
Promotional messages that explain product function, durability, price advantage, and usage benefits can
help consumers compare alternatives before buying.
The result also supports previous studies by Permana (2017) and Njoto and Sienatra (2018), which
found that promotion has a positive effect on purchasing decisions. In this study, the effect of promotion
was significant, but its beta value was lower than brand equity. This indicates that promotion remains
important, yet consumers do not rely only on promotional messages. Consumers also need brand
confidence before deciding to buy.
The implication for PT Weigo Houseware Medan is clear. The company needs to design promotion
that is more consistent and more specific to consumer needs. The company can use social med ia

content, digital catalogs, display promotions, product demonstrations, and limited promotional offers. The
message should not only state that the product is available. It should explain product benefits and
reasons why the product is suitable for household needs.

Discussion on the Influence of Brand Equity on Purchase Decisions
The results show that brand equity has a positive and significant effect on purchasing decisions.
Brand equity had a regression coefficient of 0.485 and a standardized beta value of  0.547. These values
indicate that brand equity was the dominant variable in the model. Consumers were more likely to make
purchasing decisions when they recognized the brand, perceived the product as good quality, and
remembered the brand easily.
This finding is relevant to the concept of brand equity. Kotler, Keller, and Chernev (2022) explain that
brand equity reflects the added value given by a brand to a product. Tjiptono (2017) also emphasizes that
brand equity can add or reduce value for customers an d companies. In this study, brand equity helped
consumers build confidence in products sold by PT Weigo Houseware Medan. Strong brand equity
reduces consumer doubt because the brand becomes a sign of quality and reliability.
The finding supports studies by Khafid and Pradana (2022), Tresna et al. (2021), and Pottu et al.
(2022), which found that brand equity influences purchasing decisions. Consumers tend to choose brands
that are known and believed to have good quality. In the houseware market, this tenden cy is reasonable
because products are used repeatedly in daily household activities. Consumers usually prefer products
that appear reliable and safe to use.
The practical implication is that PT Weigo Houseware Medan should strengthen its brand identity
and brand association. The company can improve brand visibility in stores and digital channels, use
consistent visual identity, provide quality guarantees, and highlight product strengths. The company
should also encourage positive consumer experience because  experience can strengthen brand recall
and recommendation behavior.

Discussion of Simultaneous Effects
The simultaneous test shows that promotion and brand equity together have a positive and
significant effect on purchasing decisions. The F -value of 83.526 and significance value of 0.000 confirm
that the two variables form a meaningful model. This result indicates that purchasing decisions are
shaped by both information exposure and brand strength. Promotion attracts attention and delivers
product information. Brand equity builds trust and strengthens consumer preference.
The Adjusted R Square value of 0.635 shows that the model has a strong explanatory power.
However, 36.5% of purchasing decisions are explained by other variables outside this study. This means
that PT Weigo Houseware Medan should not rely only on promotion and brand equity. The company also
needs to pay attention to price competitiveness, product quality, store location, service quality, product
availability, and consumer satisfaction. These va riables can be included in future research to produce a
broader model.
This finding supports the view that marketing strategy should be integrated. Promotion that is
attractive but not supported by strong brand equity may only create short -term attention. Conversely, a
strong brand that is not promoted consistently may fail to reach new consumers. PT Weigo Houseware
Medan needs to combine both. Promotion should communicate brand value, while brand equity should
make promotional messages more credible.

Managerial Implications
First, the company needs to improve promotional content. Promotional messages should be simple,
direct, and focused on consumer benefits. Product information should include function, material,
durability, price, and usage examples. This approach can reduce uncertainty and make consumers more
confident in comparing products.
Second, the company should strengthen the brand through consistency. Product labels, store
displays, social media posts, and sales communication should use a consistent identity. Consistency
helps consumers remember the brand more easily. The company can also develop customer testimonials
and product education content to reinforce perceived quality.
Third, the company should build recommendation behavior. The study shows that recommendation
remains a weak aspect in the preliminary findings. The company can develop loyalty programs, after -
sales service, and customer follow -up. Satisfied consumers can become informal promoters who
strengthen brand equity through word of mouth.

Fourth, the company should evaluate promotional channels. If consumers are more active on social

Limitations and Directions for Future Research
This study has several limitations. First, the research model only used two independent va riables,
namely promotion and brand equity. The Adjusted R Square value of 0.635 shows that the model has
strong explanatory power, but there are still other variables that may influence purchasing decisions.
Price, product quality, service quality, store atmosphere, product availability, and customer satisfaction
may also shape consumer decisions in the houseware market.
Second, the respondents were limited to consumers of PT Weigo Houseware Medan during the
January to June 2025 period. The results therefo re explain consumer behavior in one company context
and one observation period. Future research can expand the object to several houseware retailers in
Medan or compare offline and online purchasing channels. A broader sample can provide a more general
picture of consumer decision patterns.
Third, the data were collected through questionnaires, so the analysis depended on respondent
perceptions. Future research can combine survey data with interview data, sales transaction data, or
digital promotional engagement data. This combination can strengthen the empirical explanation because
consumer perception can be compared with actual purchasing behavior.
Future research may also use a more comprehensive marketing model. Researchers can test the
role of customer satisfaction as a mediating variable between promotion, brand equity, and repurchase
decisions. Researchers can also test whether digital promotion strengthens the relationship between
brand equity and purchasing decisions. This direction is relevant becau se consumers increasingly obtain
product information through social media and digital catalogs.

CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
Conclusions
This study concludes that promotion has a positive and significant effect on purchasing decisions
for products at PT Weigo Houseware Medan. The result is supported by a t -value of 3.846, which is
greater than the t -table value of 1.986, and a significance value of 0.000. This means that better
promotional activities can improve consumer pur chasing decisions. Clear, attractive, and consistent
promotion helps consumers understand product value and encourages purchase action.

Brand equity also has a positive and significant effect on purchasing decisions. The result is supported by
a t -value of 5.912, which is greater than the t -table value of 1.986, and a significance value of 0.000.
Brand equity is the dominant variable in this study because it has the highest regression coefficient and
standardized beta value. This means that consumers place strong consideration on brand recognition,
perceived quality, brand recall, and confidence in the product before making a purchase.
Promotion and brand equity simultaneously have a positive and significant effect on purchasing
decisions. The F -value of 83.526 is greater than the F -table value of 3.09, and the significance value is
0.000. The Adjusted R Square value of 0.635 indicates that 63.5% of purchasing decisions can be
explained by promotion and brand equity, while 36.5% is explained by other factors outside the model.
These results show that PT Weigo Houseware Medan needs to integrate promotional strategy and brand
strengthening to increase consumer purchasing decisions.

Recommendations
The practical recommendation of this study is that PT Weigo Houseware Medan should improve
promotional quality through social media, digital catalogs, product demonstrations, and attractive offers.
The company also needs to strengthen brand equity through consistent brand identity, improved product
quality, better service, and p ositive customer experience. Future researchers are advised to include
additional variables such as price, product quality, service quality, location, and customer satisfaction to
explain purchasing decisions more comprehensively.
