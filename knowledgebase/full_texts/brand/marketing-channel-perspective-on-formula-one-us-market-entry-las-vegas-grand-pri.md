Abstract

This

paper

analyzes

Formula

One's

entry

into

the

US

market

through

the

lens

of

marketing

channels.

Formula

One

is

described

as

a

global

media

and

entertainment

property

that

licences

promotional

rights

to

local

promoters

and

operates

a

contractual

vertical

marketing

system

through

The

examines

F1’s

historical

barriers,

strategy

shifts,

and

transformation

into

a

self-promoted

corporate

vertical

marketing

system

in

Las

Vegas.

The

research

uses

qualitative

pattern

matching

across

official

files,

sports

business

literature,

and

peer-reviewed

papers

to

evaluate

how

Liberty

Media

captured

100%

of

revenue,

increased

credibility

across

promoters,

demonstrated

commitment

to

local

authorities,

and

overturned

its

previous

brand

image

by

adopting

a

new

structure.

The

is

described

as

Contractual

Vertical

Marketing

System

(Contractual

VMS),

Corporate

Vertical

Marketing

System

(Corporate

VMS),

and

Horizontal

Marketing

System

(HMS).

This

research

also

finds

that

Liberty

Media

mitigated

fixed-cost

risk

and

operational

liabilities

through

a

multi-use

commercial

strategy.

This

case

unique

on

sports

management

applications,

highlighting

Contractual

VMS

and

Corporate

VMS

while

HMS

It

a

potential

for

modern

sports

business
