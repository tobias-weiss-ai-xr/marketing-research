ABSTRACT
This study examines  the influence of brand equity, brand awareness, brand loyalty, and
brand trust on customer loyalty among clients of PT Gesit Digital Solution, a business-to-
business digital marketing agency in Jakarta, Indonesia. Data were collected from 79 client
companies and analyzed using partial least squares structural equation modeling. The
results show that brand loyalty and brand trust each have a positive and significant effect
on customer loyalty, while brand equity and brand awareness do not. Together, the fou r
constructs explain 77.8 percent of the variance in customer loyalty, with brand loyalty
exerting the strongest influence, followed by brand trust. These findings suggest that in a
business-to-business digital agency context, customer loyalty is shaped mo re by clients'
emotional attachment to the brand and confidence in its reliability than by brand image or
awareness. The study contributes to the marketing literature by showing that the relative
influence of brand -related constructs on customer loyalty va ries between business -to-
business and business -to-consumer contexts. Practically, digital agencies seeking to
strengthen client retention should prioritize building trust and loyalty through consistent
service quality, transparent communication, and reliab le long-term cooperation, rather
than relying primarily on brand-image strategies. Future research should extend this study
to a larger sample of digital agencies and examine additional predictors such as customer
satisfaction and relationship quality.

INTRODUCTION
The creative and digital marketing industries in Indonesia have grown
considerably in recent years (Hadiyati et al., 2024; Lubis et al., 2025) . Companies
increasingly rely on digital platforms and social media rather than conventional
marketing channels to build and sustain relationships with consumers  (Saikia &
Bhattacharjee, 2024). This shift has given rise to numerous digital agencies that
provide services such as social media management, content strategy, and campaign
planning. As competition am ong these agencies intensifies, digital agency
companies must create distinctive value to retain clients and cultivate long -term
loyalty. Customer loyalty is therefore a critical determinant of business continuity.
Loyalty is understood as a customer's sustained commitment to continue purchasing
and engaging with a preferred product or service despite competing alternatives and
situational pressures to switch (Bae & Kim, 2023) . In the service sector, and
particularly among digital agencies, customer loyalty carries added weight because
the client relationship is ongoing and depends heavily on trust between the two
parties. Firms that succeed in retaining loyal clients tend to secure steadier revenue
streams, expand their business networks, and benefit from client referrals.
In the current digital era, customer loyalty depends not only on service quality
but also on the strength of a company's brand. One factor shaping customer loyalty
is brand equity, which reflects the value and associations that consumers  attach to
a brand and that, in turn, shape their perceptions of a product or service  (Parris &
Guzmán, 2023). A firm with stronger brand equity tends to enjoy higher levels of
client trust and confidence. For a digital agency, brand equity may be expres sed
through its reputation, the quality of its work, the caliber of its collaborative
relationships, and its professional standing among clients  (France et al., 2025) .
Alongside brand equity, brand awareness also influences customer loyalty. Brand
awareness refers to a client's capacity to recognize and recall a brand within a given
service category, a capacity that social media engagement has been shown to
strengthen substantially (Ibrahim et al., 2025). Elevated brand awareness increases
the likelihood that a firm will be sele cted when clients require digital marketing
services. Within an industry populated by numerous competitors, agencies that
build strong brand awareness are more readily recognized and recalled by
prospective clients, which is why sustained visibility throug h social media,
portfolio presentation, and other communication strategies is essential  (Bergkvist
& Taylor, 2022).
Brand loyalty is a further determinant of customer loyalty. It describes a
client's commitment and continued inclination to engage the same brand over time,
an outcome closely associated with the quality of experience the brand delivers
(Chung & Welty Peachey, 2021). Clients who are loyal to a brand are less inclined
to switch providers even when competitors offer more attractive pricing or
additional services. For a digital agency, brand loyalty manifests as continuity of

PT Gesit Digital Solution operates within this competitive digital agency

level by 2024. Interviews conducted with the company's management team suggest
that this fluctuation may be linked to several factors, including service quality,
timeliness of content production, the quality of delivered content, service pricing,
and competitive pressure from other digital agencies offering comparable services.
These factors correspond closely to brand trust, brand loyalty, and overall brand
strength, all of which affect a firm's ability to retain clients. This pattern suggests
that customer loyalty within the digital agency sector is shaped by a range of factors
linked to brand strength. Companies whose clients are satisfied and confident in-
service quality tend to sustain long-term cooperation, whereas clients who perceive
unmet expectations are more likely to shift toward competitors. Firms must
therefore u nderstand the determinants of customer loyalty in order to sustain
durable business relationships.
Prior studies examining the influence of brand equity, brand awareness, brand
loyalty, and brand trust on customer loyalty have produced mixed findings; some
report significant positive effects, while others find inconsistent or non -significant
relationships (Hanaysha, 2022). Much of this research has concentrated on the fast-
moving consumer goods (FMCG) and broader consumer goods sectors, leaving
customer loyalty within the digital agency industry comparatively underexamined.
Comparable gaps are evident in adjacent digital -service contexts, where trust and
continued engagement with a platform have similarly been shown to shape client
retention (Antonio & Kurniawan, 2025; Davin & Ariyanti, 2025). Building on this
gap, the present study examines the influence of brand equity, brand awareness,
brand loyalty, and brand trust on customer loyalty at PT Gesit Digital Solution. The

LITERATURE REVIEW
Brand Equity
Brand equity is a central concept in marketing that captures the value and
associations connected to a brand, its name, or its symbol, and that shapes how
consumers perceive a product or service, ultimat ely raising or diminishing that
product's value in the market (Parris & Guzmán, 2023). Aaker (2015) identifies four
dimensions through which brand equity is expressed: brand awareness, the
consumer's ability to recall a brand within a given product categ ory; brand image
or brand association, the consumer's perception of a brand's attributes and the
impressions that position it in the consumer's mind; perceived quality, the
consumer's assessment of a branded product's quality; and brand loyalty, the
tendency of consumers to repurchase a branded product because of satisfaction with
its prior performance. This four -dimensional framework remains foundational to
brand equity research and is retained here as grand theory underpinning the study's
conceptualization of brand equity.
Brand Awareness
Brand awareness is a consumer's capacity to recognize or recall a brand as
belonging to a particular product or service category, and this capacity has been
shown to strengthen substantially through active social media engagement (Ibrahim
et al., 2025) . It represents the simplest and earliest stage of the broader brand
recognition process, since a consumer must first be aware that a brand exists before
more complex associations can form. (Kotler & Keller, 2021)  describe brand
awareness as unfolding across four levels of consumer recall. Top of mind refers to
the brand a consumer names first and without prompting when asked to recall any
brand in a category. Brand recall describes a consumer's ability to name a brand
when prompted with the relevant product category. Brand recognition, the
minimum level of awareness, occurs when a consumer identifies a brand correctly
once it is presented, and it plays a decisive role at the point of purchase. Unaware
brand marks the lowest level of the awareness hierarchy, in which the consumer has
no knowledge that the brand exists at all (Raffaelli et al., 2024).
Brand Trust
Brand trust arises from customer confidence in a brand and reflects an
understanding, built through direct or indirect experience, that leads  customers to
believe a brand will act in their interest (Cardoso et al., 2022). It has similarly been
described as a form of accountability that customers grant to a brand once repeated
interaction confirm that the brand consistently meets expectations r egarding

product or service suitability, with this trust often mediating the relationship
between overall brand experience and the loyalty it produces  (Bae & Kim, 2023) .
Four indicators typically capture brand trust in this context  (Cardoso et al., 2022).
Trust itself denotes the confidence a customer develops through the act of
purchasing. Reliability reflects the customer's sense that the brand will consistently
perform as expected. Honesty concerns the customer's belief that the brand deals
fairly and transparently with those who use it. Safety refers to the assurance a
customer receives that a product or service will not expose them to unnecessary
risk.
Brand Loyalty
Brand loyalty describes a customer's positive disposition, ongoing
commitment, and continued intention to purchase from a particular brand over time
(Koranti & Wicaksana, 2021) . This relationship carries particular weight in
business-to-business contexts, where the accumulation of consistent service
experiences over an extended engagement pe riod has been found to be a stronger
driver of brand loyalty than brand familiarity alone  (Raddats et al., 2024) .
According to  Aaker (2015), brand loyalty operates across four dimensions.
Cognitive loyalty stems from product quality, cost considerations, a nd customer
knowledge that together shape brand preference. Affective loyalty reflects the
satisfaction and enjoyment a customer associates with a brand, encompassing the
positive or negative emotional reactions the brand evokes. Conative loyalty
captures the customer's desire to repurchase as a behavioral response to a favorable
attitude toward the brand, driven by a sense of attachment that motivates continued
patronage (Ladeira et al., 2025) . Behavioral loyalty describes the regularity with
which a customer purchases from a pa rticular brand, reflecting a preference to
satisfy needs through that brand rather than switching to alternatives.
Customer Loyalty
Customer loyalty is a customer's sustained commitment to continue
purchasing or supporting a preferred product or service ov er time, even when
situational pressures or competitors' marketing efforts create opportunities to switch
(Bae & Kim, 2023) . According to Kotler and Keller (2021), customer loyalty can
be assessed through three indicators: repeat purchase, which reflects  a customer's
continued willingness to buy from the same brand; retention, which reflects
resistance to switching toward competing brands; and referrals, which capture the
extent to which a customer recommends the brand to others.

Hypotheses
Building on the conceptual framework outlined above, this study proposes
that brand equity, brand awareness, brand loyalty, and brand trust each exert a
positive influence on customer lo yalty. Specifically, it is hypothesized that brand
equity has a positive influence on customer loyalty (H1), that brand awareness has

RESEARCH METHODOLOGY
This study employs a quantitative approach with a descriptive correlational
design, using a cross -sectional survey to examine relationships among the
constructs of interest at a single point in time (Hunziker & Blankenagel, 2024). The
population comprised 98 active service -user companies of PT Gesit Digital
Solution in the Jakarta area during 2024, from which 79 companies were selected
as the sample using purposive sampling based on established cooperation with the
company and sufficient experience with its digital marketing services to provide
informed assessments of brand equity, brand awareness, brand loyalty, brand trust,
and customer loyalty.

Data were collected th rough a structured questionnaire completed by a

The data were analyzed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation

hypothesized relationship.

The Influence of Brand Equity on Customer Loyalty
The results of this study indicate that the first hypothesis, which proposed that
brand equity influences customer loyalty, could not be accepted. Brand equity was
found to have no significant influence on customer loyalty among the service -user
companies of PT Gesit Digital Solution. Although brand equity is theoretically
understood to strengthen consumer perceptions of value and brand preference, in
the context of a business-to-business digital agency, customer loyalty appears to be
shaped more strongly by service quality and direct cooperative experience. The

The Influence of Brand Awareness on Customer Loyalty
The results of this study indicate that the second hypothesis, which proposed
that bran d awareness influences customer loyalty, could not be accepted. This
indicates that service-user companies' awareness of the PT Gesit Digital Solution
brand did not significantly drive their loyalty. In the B2B digital agency industry,
brand awareness appe ars to function mainly as an initial point of introduction to
potential clients rather than as a determinant of whether a cooperative relationship
endures. Service-user companies gave greater weight to the quality of work output,
communication, and respons iveness than to their recognition or recall of the
company's brand.
This result differs from the findings of  Ibrahim et al. (2025), who reported a
positive effect of brand awareness on customer loyalty in a business -to-consumer
setting, but is consistent with the finding of Binari (2023) that brand awareness does
not significantly affect customer loyalty. This divergence suggests that the
influence of brand awareness depends heavily on industry characteristics and
customer behavior. In digital marketing se rvices specifically, customer loyalty
appears to form more through positive service experience and the fulfillment of
cooperative expectations than through brand familiarity or visibility alone.

the PT Gesit Digital Solution  brand, the greater its tendency to continue using the
company's services, sustain cooperation, and recommend the company to others.

CONCLUSION
This study examined the influence of brand equity, brand awareness, brand
loyalty, and brand trust on customer loyalty among 79 service -user companies of
PT Gesit Digital Solution, a business -to-business digital marketing agency in
Jakarta, using a quantitative approach with partial least squares structural equation
modeling. The results show that brand loyalty and brand trust each exert a positive
and significant effect on customer loyalty, while brand equity and brand awareness
do not, with the four constructs jointly accounting for 77.8 percent of the variance
in customer loyalty. These findings indicate that in this business -to-business
context, customer loyalty is driven primarily by clients' emotional and behavioral
attachment to the brand and their confidence in its reliability, rather than by broader
brand image or brand recognition.
Theoretically, this study contributes to the marketing literature by showing
that the dimensions of Aaker's brand equity framework do not carry equal wei ght
in a business -to-business service setting, and that brand loyalty and brand trust
function as the more decisive antecedents of customer loyalty when the underlying
relationship is one of ongoing professional cooperation rather than a single
consumer tr ansaction. Practically, the findings suggest that PT Gesit Digital
Solution, and comparable digital agencies, should prioritize strategies that build
trust and loyalty directly, such as transparent project management, consistent
service delivery, responsiv e communication, and long -term relationship -building
initiatives, rather than relying primarily on brand -image campaigns to retain
business clients.
This study is subject to several limitations. The sample was confined to 79
client companies of a single d igital agency in South Jakarta, which limits the
generalizability of the findings to other firms, regions, or industries. The
quantitative design, while suited to testing the hypothesized relationships, did not
capture the emotional and relational nuances underlying clients' loyalty and trust in
the depth that a qualitative or mixed -methods approach could provide. The study
also examined only four predictors of customer loyalty; other plausible influences,
such as customer satisfaction, relationship quality, and service pricing, fell outside
its scope. Future research could address these limitations by sampling across
multiple digital agencies and industries, incorporating qualitative methods to
explore the mechanisms underlying brand trust and brand loyalty , and testing
additional predictors such as customer satisfaction and relationship quality within
the same conceptual framework.
