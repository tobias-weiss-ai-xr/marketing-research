Zifan Wu

Abstract. The "GuoChao" is currently a major trend in China. Consumers are gradually
linking local brands with Chinese culture, national pride, modern design, and confidence in
local original products. This article mainly explores the establishment of Hongxing Erke's
brand image in the national trend sportswear market, as well as its impact on consumers'
perception and purchase intentions. The research is based on a literature review and
quantitative questionnaire survey. A total of 206 valid responses were received, with 1
incomplete response removed. Finally, 205 valid questionnaires were retained for
descriptive and summary analysis. The research shows that Hongxing Erke has a high brand
awareness and a unique national trend identity. Consumers typically link the brand with low
prices and social responsibility. But this knowledge has not yet entirely transformed into
purchase and recommendation, i.e. the quality of the product, the design and the user
experience need to be further improved. The research suggests that Guochao identity, fitting
prices and social responsibility generate attention and emotional value, but not
automatically generates long-term commitment. Hongxing Erke must improve product
quality, design, cultural authenticity, and consumer experience to convert its high awareness
into repeat purchase and higher recommendation intention.
Keywords: Hongxing Erke, national trend, brand image, consumer perception, purchase
intention
1. Introduction
Guochao has become one of the biggest trends in China in recent years. Guochao is not about
buying Chinese made stuff. It is related to Chinese culture, local identity, modern design and
consumers' rising faith in domestic companies. According to iiMedia Research, the Guochao
economy is a consumer movement focused on Chinese culture, Chinese brands, local industries,
modern design, and innovative business strategies [1].
The Guochao trend is most evident in the sportswear sector. Many Chinese consumers used to
choose international athletic companies such as Nike and Adidas, thinking that they were more
professional and fashionable. But Chinese athletic labels such as Li-Ning, Anta, Xtep and Hongxing
Erke are becoming popular. According to Fidelity International, the Chinese sportswear market has
recorded retail sale value of RMB 386 billion [2]. McKinsey also discovered that some of the

Hongxing Erke was established on June 8, 2000. According to its official website, the company
has grown from a footwear business to a broader sportswear brand, offering shoes, clothing, and
accessories [4]. The brand is generally associated with low prices, domestic identity and social

and many of its products were bought up quickly in this period [5]. However, being temporarily
popular online does not always translate to long-term success. A sportswear brand still needs to have
good design, reliable quality, brand positioning, and good consumer experience.
This is an important matter because of the local consumption cultural meaning. According to the
official website of Hongxing Erke, the company has grown from a footwear enterprise to a
comprehensive sports brand covering shoes, clothing, and accessories [4]. This brand is usually
associated with low prices, local identity, and social responsibility. In 2021, Hongxing Erke's
charitable donations attracted widespread attention on the internet and social media, and many of its
products were sold out quickly [5]. However, the short-term online popularity does not always
translate into long-term success. A sports brand still needs to have excellent design, reliable quality,
clear brand positioning, and good consumer experience.
This is particularly important because it involves the deep meaning of local consumption culture.
According to BizBeat of the National University of Singapore, for many Chinese consumers,
especially young people, purchasing domestic brands has become an emotional and cultural
behavior, which can express identity, national pride, creativity, and self-confidence [6]. This also
explains why "national trend" brands can quickly gain attention. At the same time, it highlights the
value of authenticity: consumers may reject those that seem overly mechanical or false in cultural
promotion. The significance of this study lies in exploring how local Chinese sports brands can use
the "national trend" (Guochao) to build consumer trust and enhance market position. It provides
important suggestions for Hongxing Erke and other local brands on how to convert high brand
awareness into purchase intention, repeat purchase, word-of-mouth recommendations, and long-term
loyalty.
This study is mainly conducted from five aspects: local identity, price, product design, perceived
quality, and social responsibility. The study adopts two methods: one is the literature analysis
method, by analyzing academic papers, industry reports, enterprise materials, and market reviews, to
explore the construction of Guochao brands and consumer behavior; the other is online
questionnaire survey, collecting first-hand data from consumers on brand perception, purchase
experience, brand impression, Guochao perception, and recommendation willingness. This paper
first reviews previous research results, then introduces the questionnaire survey method, presents the
research results, analyzes the implications discovered, and finally reaches a conclusion and proposes
future research directions. The main research purpose is to evaluate Hongxing Erke's brand image
construction in the Guochao sports clothing industry and determine the gap between its brand
perception and actual purchase behavior. The core research question is: How does Hongxing Erke
build its brand image in the Guochao sports clothing industry, and how does this image affect
consumers' perception and purchase intention? The study points out that Hongxing Erke's brand
image is mainly shaped through national identity, low-price positioning, and social responsibility,
but the brand still needs to further improve in product design, perceived quality, and user experience.

2. Literature review
Based on current research and market assessment, "national trend" is a cultural movement and
represents a shift in consumer behavior. According to iiMedia Research, Chinese style, traditional
aesthetics, and cost-effectiveness are important reasons for consumers to choose domestic trend
brands [1]. This means that people purchase national trend products not only because of their
"Chinese" attributes, but they also consider design, price, quality, and practical value. NUS BizBeat
stated that local consumption is increasingly becoming a way to express personal identity,
confidence, and national pride, and cultural relevance and authenticity are becoming important
factors in promoting brand loyalty [6]. These materials reveal the emotional basis behind national
trend, but they do not directly measure the awareness, purchase history, or recommendation
intentions of Hongxing Erke consumers.
Li Cao and Xu studied the impact of brand nationality on Chinese consumers' brand attachment,
brand trust, and purchase intentions [7]. Through three empirical studies, they found that domestic
identity, political stance, national contribution, market performance, and national culture have a
positive impact on purchase intentions. Brand identity and brand trust also play independent and
chain mediating roles. This study provides strong support for analyzing the domestic identity and
social contribution of Hongxing Erke. However, this research focuses on general issues of domestic
brands and does not focus on a specific sportswear brand or compare brand awareness with actual
purchase experience.
Diniso studied how brand cognition, brand image, and perceived quality affect the brand
satisfaction and brand affection of Generation Y consumers [8]. The study found that these three
dimensions have direct and indirect effects on satisfaction, ultimately influencing consumers'
emotional attachment to the brand. This research shows that brand cognition is only one component
of brand assets; image, quality, and satisfaction must work together to build a stronger relationship.
The limitations of this study lie in that it only targets Samsung smartphone users in South Africa,
rather than Chinese sportswear consumers. However, the brand asset framework of Hongxing Erke
helps to understand the difference between "understanding Hongxing Erke" and "choosing to
purchase". Zhang, Liu, Zhang, and Pang studied the relationship between consumer perceived value
and repeat purchase intentions [9]. Their research found that there is a correlation between
functional value, hedonic value, cognitive value, and social value, and perceived value has a positive
impact on repeat purchase intentions. This study is based on online reviews of reciprocal
accommodation, rather than reviews in the sportswear field, so the results cannot be directly
generalized. However, this research provides important insights for current research: when
consumers feel value in product performance, usage experience, novelty, and social connection, they
are more likely to make repeat purchases.
Industry information also indicates that Hongxing Erke is in a highly competitive environment.
According to "Interface News", national trend sports brands are constantly rising, and brands like Li
Ning and Anta have occupied a stable position [5]. McKinsey also pointed out that Chinese
consumers are becoming increasingly cautious and pragmatic, with income and actual value
increasingly influencing their purchase decisions, rather than just confidence [3]. The results show
that competitiveness is still reliant on product design, quality, technology, communication and
consumer experience even if attention is generated by domestic identity.
Overall, previous studies explain important parts of the research problem, including cultural
identity, brand ethnicity, brand equity, perceived quality, and repeat purchase value. However, the
reviewed literature does not provide a consumer-level case study of Hongxing Erke that combines

3. Methodology
The study employed a quantitative online questionnaire to examine consumer awareness and
perceptions of Hongxing Erke. The reason for choosing the questionnaire is that all respondents
could answer the same structured questions, which is suitable for the study to compare brand
awareness, purchase experience, Guochao perception, and recommendation intention through the
numerical data. Additionally, it was a practical method of obtaining a relatively large number of
responses in a short period of time.
Recent brand research has applied more sophisticated quantitative methods. Li, Cao, and Xu [7]
employed three empirical studies and a chain mediation model, Diniso used structured
questionnaires and structural equation modeling, and Zhang et al. used text mining, sentiment
analysis and structural modeling [8, 9]. They are useful for testing relationships and mediation. The
purpose of the present study is exploratory, and this study aims to identify the main patterns of
Hongxing Erke's brand awareness, image and consumer response rather than to prove a causal
model, hence a simpler descriptive design is adopted.
3.1. Questionnaire design
The questionnaire was designed based on the main research question, which is how Hongxing Erke
builds its brand image in the Guochao sportswear market and how consumers respond to this image.
It consisted of 14 closed-ended questions and one optional open-ended question. No usable
responses were obtained to the open-ended question, so the final analysis was confined to the
closed-ended items.
The first section collected demographic variables such as gender, age and current identity or
occupation. The study did not gather information on the region, so it is not possible to compare
consumers from different provinces or cities. The second section examined general sportswear
purchase frequency, to ensure that the respondents had relevant product experience.
The third part is about brand awareness and consumer experience of Hongxing Erke. Variables
included whether respondents were aware of the brand, how they learned about the brand, overall
brand impression and whether they purchased the brand's products. The fourth part measured
perceptions related to Guochao, such as whether the respondent's thot that Hongxing Erke was a
Guochao brand, what characteristics of Guochao they thot were important, whether the support for
domestic products increased their willingness to purchase, and whether the identity of domestic
brands improved favorability.
The last section measured intention to recommend and possible areas for brand improvement.
Improvement options included product design, product quality, Chinese cultural elements,
interaction with younger consumers, social media promotion, spokesperson selection, social
responsibility and offline store experience.
The questionnaire was dominated by nominal and ordinal categorical variables rather than a
multi-item Likert scale. Most of the questions were single answer questions. Attitude questions used
ordered response options such as very willing, willing, neutral, unwilling and very unwilling. Some
questions permitted multiple answers, so respondents could select more than one communication

3.2. Data collection and sample
The questionnaire was circulated online. Received a total of 206 response records. The last record
had only a few answers and was discarded as incomplete. The valid questionnaires were 205. For the
communication-channel question, 198 respondents answered because seven respondents had never
heard of Hongxing Erke.
Of the 205 valid respondents, 119 (58.0%) were male and 86 (42.0%) were female. In terms of
age, 85 respondents were aged 18–25 (41.5%), 81 were aged 26–40 (39.5%), 26 were aged 40–50
(12.7%) and 13 were over 50 (6.3%). The major identity group was working adults (56.1%),
followed by freelancers (17.6%), students (10.2%), other identities (10.2%), and athletes (5.9%). In
addition, 93.7% had bought sportswear or sports shoes at least rarely. This demonstrates that the
majority of the respondents had experience in the product category.
3.3. Data analysis tools
Responses were organized in Microsoft Excel and analyzed with descriptive statistics. The main
measures were frequency counts and percentages. Qualtrics explains that descriptive statistics are
used to show how survey data are distributed, while inferential statistics are used to make wider
predictions or test hypotheses [10]. Descriptive analysis was therefore suitable because the research
aimed to summarise the main patterns in awareness, buying behaviour, brand perception, and
purchase intention.
For single-choice questions, each category was divided by the 205 valid responses. For the
communication-channel question, percentages were calculated from the 198 respondents who
answered it. For other multiple-choice questions, percentages were calculated from the 205 valid
respondents, and totals could exceed 100%. The results were presented through response numbers
and percentages to identify Hongxing Erke's main brand strengths, weaknesses, and the gap between
awareness and purchase.
4. Results
The survey collected 205 valid responses. The sample was mainly composed of young and middle-
aged consumers: 41.5% were aged 18–25 and 39.5% were aged 26–40. Most respondents also had
sportswear purchasing experience. A total of 38.0% reported buying sportswear or sports shoes
frequently, 41.0% bought them occasionally, 14.6% bought them rarely, and only 6.3% had never
bought them.
The respondents had a very high awareness about the brand Hongxing Erke. In all, 96.6% had
heard of the brand and only 3.4% had not. Among the 198 respondents who answered the question
about the information channel, the most common channel was recommendations from friends
(54.0%). 49.5% of respondents selected social media (Douyin and Xiaohongshu), 49.0% news
events, 46.5% offline stores and 43.9% television advertisements. The percentages add up to more
than 100% since this was a multiple-choice question.
Overall, the respondents' impressions reflected that the image of Hongxing Erke was mainly
related to price and social responsibility. 32.7% of the most numerous groups opted for low and
suitable prices. Strong social responsibility was the choice of another 31.2%. Only 19.0% selected

Recommendation intention was weaker. Only 32.2% were willing to recommend Hongxing Erke
to friends or family, 42.4% were neutral, and 25.4% were unwilling. For brand improvement, the
most selected option was inviting suitable spokespersons at 46.3%, followed by strengthening
Guochao cultural elements at 45.4%, improving offline store experience at 43.9%, strengthening the
social responsibility image at 42.9%, increasing social media promotion at 42.4%, improving
product quality at 42.0%, improving product design at 39.5%, and increasing interaction with young
consumers at 38.0%.
5. Discussion
The results show that Hongxing Erke is a very well-known brand. Most of the respondents are
familiar with this brand, indicating that it has already established a significant position in the hearts
of consumers. At the same time, the data also indicates that brand awareness should not be the sole
goal of brand building. Diniso pointed out that brand awareness, brand image, and perceived quality
can affect consumers' satisfaction and emotional attachment [8]. This is a different product and
belongs to a different country, but this foundation helps explain why merely high exposure is not
sufficient for Hongxing Erke.
The biggest challenge for Hongxing Erke lies in the gap between awareness and action. 96.6% of
the respondents are familiar with the brand, but only 37.6% have purchased it at least once. The
willingness to recommend is also very limited, with only 32.2% willing to recommend, and the
proportion of neutral attitudes is 42.4%. The results indicate that Hongxing Erke has achieved
recognition but has not yet formed a strong consumer relationship. Satisfaction and product-based
trust for the brand need to be increased to convert awareness into purchase, repeat purchase and
positive word of mouth.
The brand is strong in Guochao identity, that's for sure. Most respondent's thot Hongxing Erke is
a Guochao sportswear brand, and domestic identity is the most selected Guochao characteristic.
Additionally, 66.9% were willing or very willing to buy domestic sportswear because of support for
local products. Li, Cao and Xu found that domestic identity, national culture and national
contribution had a positive influence on purchase intention through brand attachment and brand trust
[7]. This is in line with the perspective that the Chinese identity and social contribution of Hongxing
Erke can create symbolic meaning and emotional support.

The NUS commentary also explains this result. It argues that buying local has become a way of
self-expression and national pride for many Chinese consumers, especially the younger generation
[6]. Therefore, Hongxing Erke will leverage the "Guochao" to establish a sense of cultural identity
and emotional connection with consumers.
However, the same source also indicates that if the brand story appears predictable or deliberately
designed, consumers may experience aesthetic fatigue. Therefore, Chinese traditional cultural
elements should be integrated into Hongxing Erke's products in a modern and authentic way, rather
than simply adding traditional symbols to the products. The most common description of Hongxing
Erke was that it was cheap. This can attract price sensitive consumers and maintain a great price
performance position. But price alone is not a guaranty of repeat purchasing [9]. Zhang and
coworkers found that perceived value positively affected repeat purchase intention, and value was
composed of functional, hedonic, epistemic, and social relationship aspects. Hongxing Erke gains
functional value from comfort, durability and sports performance; hedonic value from attractive and
fashionable design; epistemic value from innovation and new product experience; and social value
from Guochao identity and social responsibility.
Another strong element in the brand image is social responsibility. This was the main impression
for almost a third of respondents, and 42.9% felt that the brand should build on this image. The
donation of Hongxing Erke shows that social contribution can obtain large-scale public support [5].
Li, Cao and Xu also identify national contribution as a dimension of brand ethnicity which may
affect attachment, trust and purchase intention [7]. However, this support may be even driven. The
brand should keep taking responsible actions continuously and relate it to reliable products instead
of using social responsibility to replace product value.
Product-related perceptions remain the biggest weakness. While 46.8% of the respondents
indicated that reliable quality is an important feature of a Guochao brand, only 19.0% selected
quality and design as their main impression. This difference suggests that consumers have quality
expectations for Guochao brands, but not so much for Hongxing Erke. Diniso's findings show that
perceived quality is an important component of satisfaction and emotional attachment [8]. Zhang et
al. show that functional and hedonic value contributes to repeat purchase intention [9]. Therefore,
Hongxing Erke needs to upgrade both the actual performance of the product and the communication
of quality, technology, comfort, durability and design.
The improvements in the choices indicate that consumers expect better products and better
communication at the same time. Having the right spokesperson can increase the attention, but the
spokesperson should match the brand's value and target market. "Don't use stronger cultural
elements as decoration, but pair it with contemporary fashion. Social media can communicate
product technology, user experience, and brand stories, while offline stores can enhance product
trial, display, service, and the overall atmosphere of the brand. These measures help Hongxing Erke
to shake off its image that is solely characterized by low prices and donation activities. Overall, the
national trend image of Hongxing Erke has brought attention, emotional value and cultural
significance. Its domestic identity and social responsibility help consumers identify and support the
brand. However, long-term success depends on whether these advantages can be balanced with
modern product design, its functional value, user satisfaction and the achievement of the experience.
Therefore, the brand must maintain its accessibility position and domestic identity, while creating
more distinct product advantages, converting awareness into purchase behavior and loyalty. The
discussion should be read with the limitations of the study. A cross-sectional on-line survey and
descriptive statistics were used in the study. It did not gather regional data or test statistical
relationships between identity, quality, trust and purchase intention. Therefore, the results present

6. Conclusion
This study aims to investigate the brand image building of Hongxing Erke in the Guochao
sportswear market. Based on the literature review and 205 valid responses to the questionnaire, the
study found that the brand has a very high awareness and a clear Guochao identity. Its main positive
associations are low prices, social responsibility and domestic identity. But awareness is much
higher than actual purchase and quality and design are not yet the brand's strongest consumer
associations.
The main finding is that Guochao provides Hongxing Erke cultural meaning and emotional
support, but Guochao identity alone cannot generate long-term loyalty. The brand needs to give
greater value to the products for the consumers to buy again. It needs to have greater quality, more
comfort, functionality and design, more innovation in the product, better shop experience and better
communication. These product attributes should be complemented, not replaced, by social
responsibility and cultural narrative.
The present work is of practical and theoretical value. In the case of Hongxing Erke, it describes
the gap between knowing and buying/recommending. As for the research of Guochao brands, it is
suggested that, beside brand awareness, perceived quality, purchasing experience and
recommendation intention, cultural identity should also be taken into consideration. It also provides
a consumer-level case to complement larger studies of local brand recognition and perceived value.
Future research should involve a larger and geographically more diverse sample and document
region of respondents. It may put Hongxing Erke in the same league as Li-Ning, Anta, Xtep and
worldwide sportswear brands. Researchers can also develop multi-item scales for brand trust,
cultural identification, perceived quality, satisfaction and loyalty and use regression or structural
equation modeling to examine the links among them. Longitudinal studies would be particularly
helpful to see if the attention from these donation events or Guochao campaigns translate into long-
term purchasing behaviors.
