Abstract
This project aims to provide comprehensive support to Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs), specifically focusing
on the business partners Cakealamanja and Rujak Jambu Cristal. This initiative is realized through active participation in the
Polbeng Business Expo Chapter II, with the primary objective of optimizing social media marketing strategies to enhance both
brand image and digital competitiveness. The project encompasses a series of practical programs, inc luding the creation of
professional business profile videos, engaging promotional content, and informative digital product catalogs. Additionally, i t
involves structured promotional management across social media, the integration of the Quick Response Code  Indonesian
Standard (QRIS) digital payment system, and intensive mentoring for business owners in preparing standardized financial
reports.Methodologically, this project was systematically executed through three primary stages: project preparation
(encompassing observation, needs analysis, and planning), project implementation (focusing on marketing execution and the
exhibition), and project completion (evaluation and comprehensive reporting). The findings reveal that social media marketing
significantly e levates brand visibility, audience engagement, and consumer awareness for both MSMEs. The strategic
distribution of creative promotional content across platforms like Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook successfully captured
public attention and increased dire ct consumer traffic.Furthermore, the adoption of the QRIS payment system substantially
improved transaction efficiency, speed, and security during the exhibition, effectively meeting the demands of modern
consumers. Managerially, the assistance provided in  structured financial reporting empowered the MSME owners to
transparently understand their business performance, thereby aiding in future financial decision -making. In conclusion, the
Polbeng Business Expo Chapter II served as a highly effective platform for advancing MSMEs through a combination of digital
marketing and direct business assistance. Ultimately, this project not only delivered a measurable positive impact on MSME
sustainability and economic growth but also significantly strengthened the colla borative synergy among students, business
owners, and the broader community.
Keywords: Social Media Marketing, Digital Marketing, MSMEs, Polbeng Business Expo, Product Promotion.
1. Introduction
Indonesia's identity is deeply rooted in its abundant cultural and culinary diversity, which serves as a massive draw
for both domestic and international tourists  [1]. This regional uniqueness can be strategically promot ed through
cultural events, such as culinary exhibitions, which act as dynamic platforms to elevate local arts and cuisine.
Analytically, these exhibitions represent more than just entertainment; they function as a strategic catalyst for
preserving cultura l heritage while simultaneously generating economic traction by exposing local richness to a
broader, enthusiastic audience [2].
To actively introduce this culinary diversity to the community in Bengkalis, students initiated the Polbeng Business
Expo. This event is specifically designed to provide a stage for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)
to showcase their creative power and non -culinary or culinary products  [3]. From an analytical perspective, the
Expo is not merely a promotional event but a  vital empowerment ecosystem that bridges the gap between local
entrepreneurs and the public, directly facilitating MSME development and fostering stronger community-business
relationships [4].
The structural importance of MSMEs cannot be overstated, as they are community -driven initiatives that play a
critical role in reducing the unemployment rate in Indonesia[5]. Their ability to absorb labor combined with small

economic landscapes [6].
Within the broader MSME ecosystem, the culinary sector is uniquely dominant and consistently in high demand
among business practitioners. This sector is highly attractive due to low barriers to entry, relatively low production
costs, and a constantly growing market  [7]. A deeper analysis reveals that food and beverage enterprises thrive
because they align perfectly with the modern lifestyle, which prioritizes convenience and efficiency; therefore,
culinary MSMEs possess i mmense potential for sustainable growth provided they maintain product quality,
affordability, and continuous innovation [8].
To remain competitive in this modern landscape, MSMEs must leverage rapid advancements in digital technology,
particularly through online marketing  [9]. Implementing digital marketing strategies  such as social media
utilization and search engine optimization  allows these businesses to expand their reach far beyond local
boundaries into regional and national markets. Analytically, the transition to digital marketing is no longer optional
but a fundamental survival strategy, as it drastically lowers the cost of customer acquisition while maximiz ing
visibility and direct consumer engagement [10].
Alongside digital marketing, the modernization of payment systems is a crucial variable for MSME success,
highlighted by the implementation of the QRIS digital payment standard. Designed by Bank Indonesia, QRIS
facilitates quick, safe, and efficient transactions through mobile QR code scanning, reducing the reliance on cash.
The deeper analytical significance of QRIS lies in its role in driving financial inclusion; by streamlining the
checkout process, it removes transactional friction, builds consumer trust, and fundamentally professionalizes the
operational capacity of small vendors[11].
The project specifically focuses on empowering Cakealamanja, a local MSME founded in 2020 that produces
innovative food and beverages, notably its flagship natural date milk (Susu Kurma Hanin)  [12]. Dates offer
immense health benefits, containing essential nutrients like vitamin A, potassium, and antioxidants that aid
digestion and improve immune health. Analytically, Cakealamanja represents the resilient and adaptive nature of
modern local businesses; their survival since 2020 and their proactive, though under -optimized, use of Instagram
and WhatsApp highlight a strong foundational potential that is ripe for targeted digital scaling  [13].
To provide a diversified product landscape at the expo, the project also integrates Rujak Jambu Cristal, an MSME
established in 2021 that sells fresh crystal guava fruit salad  [14]. Crystal guava is highly valued for its crunchy
texture, natural sweetness, and high vitamin C and digestive fiber content, which boost immunity and protect
against free radicals. From a market analysis standpoint, including Rujak Jambu Cristal creates a strategic product
contrast against typical heavy culinary foods, catering to health-conscious consumers and showcasing the creative
versatility of local ingredient processing [13].
The core research problem identifies how the Polbeng Business Expo Chapter II was executed for these two
specific MSMEs, with highly structured operational objectives  [15]. The specific goals include creating business
profile and promotional videos, digital product catalogs, executing social media campaigns, implementing QRIS,
and generating fin ancial reports. Analytically, these objectives represent a comprehensive blueprint for digital
transformation; by providing these specific deliverables, the project transitions the MSMEs from traditional
operational models into modernized, digitally literate enterprises capable of sustained commercial tracking [16].
Ultimately, the implementation of this project  scheduled for May 20 to 22,  2025, at the State Polytechnic of
Bengkalis carries profound, multi -layered significance. It directly aids entrepreneurs in developing superior
marketing strategies, provides the author with practical entrepreneurial experience, and serves as a future academic
reference. Analyzing its broader impact reveals a highly symbiotic relationship: the project not only elevates the
market presence of the MSMEs but also actively cements the State Polytechnic of Bengkalis's reputation as an
institutional pillar dedicated to real-world local economic development.

3.5 Project Reporting
This section presents the comprehensive logbook documenting the social media marketing activities executed for
the Cakealamanja and Rujak Jambu Cristal MSMEs throughout the 30-day pre-event promotional campaign. This
logbook serves as a structured record of daily operational tasks, detailing the content publication schedules, media

The performance analysis of promotional videos and digital catalogs for the MSMEs Cakealamanja and Rujak
Jambu Cristal reveals distinct, consistent roles for TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook in shaping brand perception
and audience reach. Across both businesses and content types, TikTok emerged as the prima ry engine for
expanding brand awareness, consistently driving the highest view counts and overwhelmingly attracting new
audiences, with non-follower reach ranging from 90% to 100%. Meanwhile, Instagram proved highly effective at
sustaining community engagement, securing the highest interaction rates across the board from existing followers.
In contrast, Facebook offered limited additional exposure, functioning purely as a minor, complementary
supporting channel for both brands.
Based on a 30-day performance analysis of promotional videos and digital catalogs for the MSMEs Cakealamanja
and Rujak Jambu Cristal, each social media platform plays a distinct, complementary role in expanding audience
reach and shaping brand perception. TikTok stands out as the most powerful tool for driving broad brand awareness
and capturing new audiences, generating the highest view counts and non-follower exposure, particularly through
its digital catalogs. Conversely, Instagram excels at sustaining community interaction and deep ening follower
engagement through video content, while Facebook provides limited exposure, functioning purely as a secondary
supporting channel. Ultimately, strategic execution of videos and catalogs on TikTok and Instagram demonstrates
strong potential for significantly accelerating overall brand visibility.

4. Conclusions
The Polbeng Business Expo Chapter II successfully provided Cakealamanja and Rujak Jambu  Cristal MSMEs
with valuable opportunities to expand their market reach and directly engage with the public, heavily supported
by the author's role as Tenant Field Coordinator in delivering promotional materials and implementing QRIS
digital payments. Fina ncially, both businesses demonstrated excellent health and profitability during May 2025,
accumulating significant net profits and maintaining a stable, liability -free capital structure funded entirely by
owner equity. In terms of digital marketing perform ance, the analysis concluded that TikTok serves as the most
effective platform for capturing new audiences through videos and catalogs, while Instagram remains essential for
cultivating follower engagement, and Facebook provides limited, supplementary expo sure. For future event
optimization, it is recommended that the expo organizing committee establish a clearer division of labor based on
competency, implement professional management training, and carefully evaluate strategic locations and weather-
favorable timing to maximize product visibility. Concurrently, the MSMEs are advised to center their digital
promotion strategy on TikTok to capture new audiences while consistently leveraging Instagram to sustain current
customer engagement and Facebook as a minor supporting channel. Furthermore, maintaining a consistent output
of high -quality, visually appealing videos and digital catalogs is strongly urged to continuously enhance
promotional effectiveness and reinforce a strong brand image.
