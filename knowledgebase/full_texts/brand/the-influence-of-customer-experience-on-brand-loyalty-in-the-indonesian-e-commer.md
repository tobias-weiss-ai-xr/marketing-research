 This study examines the influence of customer experience on brand loyalty
within the Indonesian e -commerce industry, focusing on key factors such as
website usability, personalized service, and post -purchase support. Using a
quantitative approach, data were collected from 600 online shoppers across
various e -commerce platforms in Indonesia. Structural Equation Modeling
(SEM) was applied to analyze the relationships between customer experience
dimensions and brand loyalty. Findings indicate that a positive customer
experience significantly enhances brand loyalty, with personalized service
and user-friendly website design being the most influential factors. The results
suggest that e -commerce platforms can build stronger customer loyalty by
improving usability and offering tailored experiences that meet individual
customer needs. These insights provide valuable implications for e-commerce
businesses seeking to retain custome rs in an increasingly competitive digital
landscape.

INTRODUCTION

The rapid growth of e -commerce has transformed the retail landscape globally, with Indonesia
emerging as one of the fastest -growing e-commerce markets in Southeast Asia. Driven by increasing internet
penetration, widespread smartphone usage, and a digitally-savvy population, Indonesia’s e-commerce industry
is projected to continue its robust growth, reaching substantial market size in the coming years (Kemp, 2021).
Amid this growth, e-commerce businesses face intense competition, making brand loyalty a critical factor for
sustaining long -term success. In an environment where consumers can easily switch between platforms,
fostering brand loyalty has become a strategic priority, as loyal customers are more likely to make repeat
purchases, recommend the platform to others, and serve as brand advocates (Yasa et al., 2020).
A key determinant of brand loyalty in e-commerce is the customer experience, defined as the sum of
all interactions a consumer has with a brand across the entire customer journey —from browsing to post -
purchase support (Lemon & Verhoef, 2016). Research suggests that positive customer experiences foster
emotional connections and satisfaction, which are essential for building loyalty (Homburg et al., 2017). In the
Indonesian e -commerce context, where consumers have high expectations for convenience, speed, and
personalization, providing a seamless and engaging customer experience is crucial. Dimensions of customer
experience, such as website usability, personalized service, and post -purchase support, have been shown to
influence customer perceptions and, subsequently, their loyalty intentions (Klaus & Maklan, 2013).
Website usability, which includes intuitive navigation, fast load times, and a visually appealing layout,
is essential for retaining customers. Studies show that usability can significantly impact customer satisfaction
and loyalty by enhancing the ease of use and accessibility of the platform (Grewal et al., 2019). Personalized
service, such as tailored product recommendation s and targeted communication, also plays a central role in
customer experience, as it allows brands to connect with consumers on a personal level, fostering a sense of
individual recognition and relevance (Leroi -Werelds et al., 2014). Additionally, effecti ve post -purchase

Despite the importance of customer experience in shaping brand loyalty, limited research has focused

in a competitive digital market.

METHODOLOGY

Research Design

This study uses a quantitative research design to investigate the influence of customer experience on brand
loyalty in Indonesia’s e -commerce industry. A survey-based approach was adopted to collect data on key
factors of customer experience, including website usability, personalized service, and post -purchase support,
and their impact on brand loyalty. Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) was used to analyze  the relationships
between these variables, as SEM allows for comprehensive examination of complex relationships and the
effects of multiple factors on a single outcome variable (Hair et al., 2019).

Sample and Participant Selection

The target population for this study includes Indonesian e -commerce customers who actively shop on
major platforms such as Tokopedia, Shopee, Lazada, and Bukalapak. Purposive sampling was employed to
ensure participants had sufficient experience with e -commerce platforms. The f inal sample consisted of 600
respondents, a sample size considered robust for SEM analysis (Kline, 2016). Participants were diverse in
terms of age, gender, and e-commerce platform preference, reflecting the general Indonesian e-commerce user
demographic.

Data Collection

Data were collected through an online questionnaire distributed via social media channels and email
invitations. The questionnaire was divided into four main sections: demographic information, website usability,
personalized service, post-purchase support, and brand loyalty. Each construct was measured using a five-point
Likert scale (1 = strongly disagree to 5 = strongly agree), adapted from validated scales in prior customer
experience and brand loyalty research (Grewal et al., 2019; Klaus & Maklan, 2013).
1. Website Usability: Items assessed ease of navigation, page loading speed, and overall user -
friendliness of e-commerce platforms (Leroi-Werelds et al., 2014).
2. Personalized Service: Questions measured the perceived relevance of personalized recommendations,
customized communications, and offers based on user preferences (Bolton et al., 2018).
3. Post-Purchase Support: This section evaluated the efficiency of customer service, ease of returns, and
satisfaction with after-sales support (Homburg et al., 2017).
4. Brand Loyalty: Items in this section measured intentions to make repeat purchases, willingness to
recommend the platform, and emotional attachment to the brand (Yasa et al., 2020).

Data Analysis

The data were analyzed using Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) with Smart PLS software. The analysis
process included the following steps:

3. Moderation Analysis: To explore the potential moderating role of demographic factors (e.g., age,
platform preference) on th e relationship between customer experience factors and brand loyalty,
subgroup analysis was performed. Bootstrapping with 5,000 resamples was used to ensure robustness
of the moderation results (Preacher & Hayes, 2008).

Results and Discussion
Results

The analysis using Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) revealed significant relationships between
customer experience dimensions —website usability, personalized service, and post -purchase support —and
brand loyalty among Indonesian e-commerce users. The key findings for each factor are outlined below:

1. Website Usability and Brand Loyalty

The study found a strong positive relationship between website usability and brand loyalty (β = 0.47, p <
0.001). Respondents indicated that user -friendly website design, qu ick load times, and intuitive navigation
significantly influenced their loyalty to the platform. High usability levels not only made shopping convenient
but also built trust in the platform, as consumers felt confident navigating it without friction. These results align
with previous research indicating that website usability enhances customer satisfaction and retention by
creating a seamless shopping experience (Grewal et al., 2019). In Indonesia’s competitive e-commerce market,
platforms with optimized usability are more likely to build loyal user bases by meeting consumer expectations
for easy and fast online shopping.

2. Personalized Service and Brand Loyalty

Personalized service also demonstrated a strong impact on brand loyalty (β = 0.52, p < 0.001). Respondents
who received tailored product recommendations, customized offers, and personalized communications
reported higher levels of loyalty to the platform. Personalized service helps create a sense of individual
recognition, which fosters emot ional attachment and enhances customer satisfaction. This finding supports
previous studies that emphasize personalization as a critical component in developing loyalty among digital
consumers, who appreciate being recognized as individuals rather than just users (Leroi-Werelds et al., 2014).
Indonesian consumers appear to respond positively to platforms that engage them with relevant and
individualized interactions, suggesting that personalization can serve as a key differentiator in the crowded e -
commerce market.

3. Post-Purchase Support and Brand Loyalty

Post-purchase support was also positively related to brand loyalty, though to a lesser degree than website
usability and personalized service (β = 0.38, p < 0.01). Efficient customer service, hassle -free returns, and
reliable after-sales support encouraged repeat purchases and increased customer retention. Respondents noted
that effective post-purchase support provided reassurance and built trust in the brand, particularly in addressing
potential issues that might arise after a transaction. This finding is consistent with studies that highlight post -
purchase support as essential for fostering loyalty, as it strengthens the overall customer experience by
demonstrating commitment to service beyond the sale (Bolton et al., 2018). In Indonesia’s e -commerce
landscape, platforms that invest in efficient post -purchase processes can enhance customer satisfaction and
foster long-term loyalty.

Discussion

These findings illustrate that customer experience dimension s—website usability, personalized service,
and post-purchase support—each play a unique role in fostering brand loyalty within Indonesia’s e-commerce

industry. While all three factors are positively associated with loyalty, their varying impacts offer insights into

Website Usability as a Foundational Element

Website usability emerged as a foundational factor for loyalty, as it directly impacts customers’ ease of
navigation, shopping convenience, and trust in the platform. This suggests that Indonesian e -commerce
platforms should prioritize optimizing usability features, such as quick load times, intuitive design, and mobile
compatibility, to meet consumers’ expectations for fast and easy shopping. The  emphasis on usability aligns
with findings from Grewal et al. (2019), which highlight that a seamless and efficient shopping experience is
crucial in competitive e -commerce environments. Platforms that invest in usability enhancements can build
stronger relationships with users, as convenience is essential in encouraging repeat use and loyalty.

Personalized Service as a Key Differentiator

The significant effect of personalized service on brand loyalty suggests that personalization can be a
powerful differentiator for e -commerce platforms in Indonesia. By providing tailored recommendations,
targeted promotions, and individualized communications, platforms can create an emotionally resonant
shopping experience that fosters loyalty. Indonesian consumers, like those in other digital markets, appreciate
personalized interactions, as they reinforce a sense of individual recognition and relevance (Leroi -Werelds et
al., 2014). E -commerce platforms can leverage data analytics to refine personalization  strategies, using
customer behavior and preferences to enhance engagement and create a unique experience that drives loyalty.

Post-Purchase Support and Brand Trust

Although post -purchase support had a slightly weaker impact on brand loyalty than usabili ty and
personalization, it remains an essential element in fostering loyalty, especially in building brand trust. Effective
post-purchase support assures customers of the platform’s reliability, which can encourage repeat purchases
and long -term retention.  As highlighted by Bolton et al. (2018), customer service that extends beyond the
transaction stage is crucial in building trust, particularly in addressing any issues with orders or returns. For
Indonesian e -commerce platforms, investing in quality custom er service, efficient returns processes, and
consistent after -sales support can enhance the overall customer experience and solidify loyalty, ensuring
consumers feel supported throughout their shopping journey.

Practical Implications

The findings offer p ractical implications for e -commerce managers and marketers in Indonesia. First,
website usability should be prioritized as it serves as the foundation of the customer experience. Efforts to
improve navigation, speed, and mobile responsiveness can significantly enhance loyalty. Second, personalized
service offers a competitive advantage by fostering deeper customer connections; therefore, platforms should
invest in data-driven personalization techniques to engage users effectively. Finally, improving post -purchase
support processes, including customer service and return policies, can build brand trust, further strengthening
customer loyalty.

Conclusion

This study demonstrates that customer experience factors —specifically website usability, personalized
service, and post -purchase support —are pivotal in driving brand loyalty within the Indonesian e -commerce
industry. Each factor contributes uniquely to customer retention, with usability and personalization having the
strongest effects. These insights suggest that Indonesian e -commerce platforms seeking to enhance loyalty
should focus on creating seamless, individualized, and supportive shopping experiences. Future research could
explore other potential drivers of loyalty, such as pricing strategies and social media engagement, to provide a
more comprehensive understanding of loyalty determinants in the Indonesian digital marketplace.
