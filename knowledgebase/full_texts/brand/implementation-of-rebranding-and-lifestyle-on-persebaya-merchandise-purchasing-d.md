 Abstract: This study aims to analyze the influence of
rebranding and lifestyle on purchasing decisions of
Persebaya merchandise through brand image as a
mediating variable on AZA Wear consumers at Persebaya
Store Sidoarjo. The method used is quantitative with
purposive sampling technique, involving a sample of 127
respondents. Data were processed using SmartPLS 4.1.1.2
through a series of tests including validity and reliability
tests, outer model evaluation (convergent validity,
discriminant validity, composite reliability, and
Cronbach's Alpha), inner model evaluation (R-Square and
path coefficient), and hypothesis testing. The results of the
study show: 1) Rebranding has a positive and significant
effect on Brand Image. 2) Lifestyle has a positive and
significant effect on Brand Image. 3) Rebranding has a
positive and significant effect on Purchasing Decisions. 4)
Lifestyle has a positive and significant effect on Purchasing
Decisions. 5) Brand Image has a positive and significant
effect on Purchasing Decisions. 6) Brand Image
significantly mediates the effect of Rebranding  on
Purchasing Decisions. 7) Brand Image significantly
mediates the effect of Lifestyle on Purchasing Decisions.
Keywords: Brand Image,
Purchasing Decision, Lifestyle,
Rebranding

Introduction
The sports industry today is not only developing as an entertainment and competition sector,
but also has great economic potential  (Mutohir et al., 2024) . The Indonesian sports sector's aggregate
economic value in 2024 reached IDR 39. 45 trillion, or approximately 0.19% of national GDP. This
demonstrates the sports industry's growing contribution to the Indonesian economy, including through
the consumption of various sports products and merchandise.
This development has also increased t he potential of the sports merchandise market. Sports
products featuring club identities no longer serve merely as supporting attributes but also as part of a
consumer's lifestyle and identity expression. The global licensed sports merchandise market is projected
to reach US$36.36 billion by 2024.

This phenomenon is also evident in Persebaya Surabaya merchandise, which has a Bonek
supporter base with a strong emotional attachment to the club. Official Persebaya merchandise serves
not only as an attribute to show support but also as a part of the supporters' identity and pride. One brand
that plays a role in providing this official merchandise is AZA Wear, which produces various products
such as home, away, alternate, and goalkeeper jerseys that emphasize the club's identity, historical value,
and symbolize supporter pride (AZA WEAR, nd).
Changing merchandise design and identity is increasingly important as consumers consider not
only product functionality but also its sui tability to their lifestyle. Rebranding can update a brand's
identity to better align with consumer developments and needs  (Andriani & Anandita, 2019) , while
lifestyle reflects activities, interests, and opinions that can influence product choices, including sports
apparel (Nam et al., 2017). This suitability can form a brand image through consumer perceptions and
associations with the brand  (Arba'in & Ika Purwanti, 2025) , which can further influence pur chasing
tendencies (Nabilah & Zulaikha, 2024) . However, research integrating rebranding and lifestyle on
purchasing decisions through brand image as a mediating variable, particularly on Persebaya
merchandise, is still limited. Therefore, this study aims to analyze the influence of rebranding and
lifestyle on purchasing decisions for Persebaya merchandise thr ough brand image as a mediating
variable among AZA Wear consumers at the Persebaya Store in Sidoarjo.
Previous research shows that brand image, lifestyle, and consumer engagement play a role in
purchasing decisions for sports products  (Arba'in & Ika Purwanti, 2025) . found that brand image
influences the decision to purchase Persela Store merchandise, while  (Fahri Rehansyah, 2023) shows
that brand image and lifestyle influence the purchasing decisions of Adidas sports footwear. In line with
that, (Sadewa et al., 2025)  found a positive influence of brand image on the decision to purchase
Ortuseight sports shoes, while (Noviany & Rakhmawati, 2025) confirms that emotional attachment and
symbolic meaning contribute to the purchase of sports merchandise. However, research integrating
rebranding and lifestyle i nto purchasing decisions through brand image as a mediating variable,
particularly for Persebaya AZA Wear merchandise, is still limited. This gap is the one this study aims
to fill.
This study attempts to fill this gap by analyzing how Rebranding and Lifes tyle influence
Purchasing Decisions through Brand Image as a mediating variable, on Aza Wear consumers at
Persebaya Store Sidoarjo.

Literature review
Rebranding
Rebranding is part of the branding process that aims to create a new position and image in the
minds of consumers for an existing brand, so that a clear differentiation is formed both for consumers
and compared to competitors (Sunyoto & Jamal, 2024). Refers to(Laurentia Vionita Hendarjoa, 2025),
rebranding is measured through four indicators:
1. Repositioning
2. Renaming
3. Redesigning
4. Relaunch.

Lifestyle
(Hadi et al., 2022) states that lifestyle is a person's lifestyle pattern that can be observed through
the activities, interests, and opinions the y hold in their daily lives. Lifestyle is measured using three
indicators:
1. Activity
2. Interest
3. Opinion

Brand Image
(Sunday Ade Sitorus et al., 2022) , explains that brand image is the result of r einterpreting all
perceptions of a brand formed through information and past consumer or customer experiences. In line
with this,(Salsabila & Pradana, 2023)states that brand image is a view or belief stored in the consumer's
memory regarding a particular brand  (Rani Anjariani, Sundjoto, 2025) . Brand Image is measured
through three indicators:
1. Corporate image
2. Product image
3. User Image

Buying decision
(Budiatmo, 2021) defines purchasing decisions as the process of consumers understanding their
needs and desires for a product, based on various available sources of information, by setting purchasing
goals and evaluating alternatives before finally making a decision to purchase. According to  (Vika
Ulfiana Latifah, 2023) Purchasing decisions are measured through four indicators:
1. Product Selection
2. Brand Selection
3. Determining the Place of Purchase
4. Decisions Concerning the Timing of Purchases

Method
Research Design
This study used a quantitative survey approach to obtain data from respondents through
questionnaire distribution. The main objective of this study was to examine the influence of rebranding
and lifestyle on purchasing decisions for Persebaya merchandise through brand image as a mediating
variable.

Population and Sample
This study involved consumers of Aza Wear merchandise at the Persebaya Store in Sidoarjo.
The sampling technique used was non -probability sampling with a purposive sampling approach.
Respondents were required to reside in Sidoarjo, have previously purchased Aza Wear merchandise,
and be aged between 17 and 35 years. The final sample size was 127 respondents, determined using the
Lemeshow formula and an indicator-based approach.

Data collection
Data were collected through a structured questionnaire distributed online via Google Forms.
Each construct was measured using indicators adapted from validated previous research, using a five -
point Likert scale ranging from "Strongly Disagree" to "Strongly Agree."

Data Analysis Techniques
Data were analyzed using SEM -PLS with SmartPLS 4.1.1.2, which included: (1) validity and
reliability testing; (2) outer model evaluation (convergent validity, discriminant validity, composite
reliability, Cronbach's Alpha); (3) inner model evaluation (Coefficient Determination, F-Square, Model
Fit, HTMT, Fornel Lacker); and (4) hypothesis testing through bootstrapping, with significance assessed
at t-statistics > 1.96 and p -values < 0.05 according to Hair et al. (2019) in their research. (Damayanti
Lestaluhu, La Mohammat Saleh, 2024).

Discussion
This section discusses and interprets the results of hypothesis testing by linking them to the
theories and previous research used in the study.
Rebranding of Brand Image.Rebranding has a positive and significant effect on brand image
with a coefficient of 0.571, a t-statistic of 8.116, and a p-value of 0.000. This path is the strongest direct
influence in the model. These findings indicate that changing brand identity, updating visual appearance,
and strengthening brand positioning can create a more positive perception in the eyes of consumers.
These results are in line with(Ridho Ilviendri, 2021).
Lifestyle on Brand Image.Lifestyle has a positive and significant effect on Brand Image with
a coefficient of 0.336, a t -statistic of 3.868, and a  p-value of 0.000. Consumer lifestyle plays a role in
shaping how consumers view a brand, as brands that are perceived to align with consumers' activities,
interests, and outlook on life will more easily build a positive image. This finding is in line with (Okky
Dwi Cahyono, Piji Pakarti, Amalia Nur Chasanah, 2025).
Rebranding on Purchasing Decisions. Rebranding has a positive and significant effect on
purchasing decisions with a coefficient of 0.368, a t -statistic of 3.724, and a p -value of 0.000. Brand

Conclusion
 This study found that rebranding and lifestyle were positively and significantly associated with
brand image and purchasing decisions for AZA Wear merchandise. Brand image also had a significant
mediating role in the relationships between rebranding, lifestyle, and purchasing decisions. However,
this study was limited by the use of pur posive sampling and respondents restricted to Sidoarjo, which
may limit the generalizability of the findings. Future research could involve respondents from other
regions and examine additional variables, such as product quality, price, and digital promoti on.
Practically, AZA Wear should strengthen its brand positioning and develop marketing strategies that
align with the lifestyle and identity of Persebaya supporters to support a positive brand image and
purchasing decisions.

Suggestion
 Aza Wear management is advised to continue strengthening its rebranding strategy by
emphasizing its positioning as Persebaya's official, modern merchandise that aligns with the supporter's
identity. This can be achieved through a wider product range, active digital promotions, and campaigns
that highlight the emotional value, pride, and lifestyle of supporters to strengthen the brand image and
drive purchasing decisions.
 For future researchers, it is recommended to add other variables, such as product quality, price,
digital promotions, or consumer loyalty. Furthermore, the scope and research area could be expanded to
include supporters in other regions or other football club merchandise brands to broaden the research
results and enrich the study of consumer behavior in the sports merchandise industry.
