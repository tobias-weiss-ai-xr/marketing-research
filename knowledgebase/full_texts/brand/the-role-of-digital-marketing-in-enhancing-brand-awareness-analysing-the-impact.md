This study examines the impact of digital marketing strategies including
Social Media Marketing (SMM), Search Engine Optimisation (SEO), and
Personalisation on consumer engagement and brand equity in digital
environments. This research aims to analyse the contribution of each of these
strategies towards an enhanced customer interaction and a heightened
customer-perceived brand value. A quantitative method was undertaken
using a self -administered online questionnaire, which was sent through
convenience sampl ing. Statistical analyses on the collected data were
performed to identify the strength as well as the direction of the relationship
between variables. Personalisation emerged as the most important driver for
consumer engagement, followed by SEO, which fac ilitated improved
consumer engagement through building brand visibility and credibility. As
compared to SMM, this study found no statistically significant direct effect of
SMM on either the level of consumer engagement or the level of brand equity.
Given its limitations, such as exploratory study and a geographically narrow
sample, its findings cannot be applied to the general population. In practice,
marketers are encouraged to tailor the personalised digital experience and
SEO techniques for better engagement as well as brand trust. The study makes
original contributions by comparing how effective these strategies are
relative to each other and suggests actionable recommendations on how to
better align a digital brand position.

1. Introduction
Over the last decade, digital marketing has gone through an incredible transformation due to the
rapid growth of internet access and mobile device use. Global digital advertising spending, currently
worth around US$522.5 billion in 2021, is estimated to re ach US$740 billion by 2025, significantly
exceeding other marketing channels (Granados et al., 2025; Baki, Jaradat and Haider, 2024). Social
media platforms now host over 4.8 billion users, with consumers spending an average of 2.5 hours
daily engaging with digital content (Kemp, 2023; Mahmood, 2025). Today, having an online presence
is equivalent to market relevance. Three areas of digital marketing segment themselves, namely
Social Media Marketing (SMM), Search Engine Optimization (SEO), and Personalizati on, become

Digital marketing strategies including social media marketing, SEO, and personalization are widely

data that can demonstrate that these strategies are effect ive in producing desired outcomes (Nipa

marketing investments and bra nd equity (Seo and Park, 2018). Therefore, this study aims to
investigate the role played by social media marketing (SMM) on consumer engagement and brand
equity in the digital market. Furthermore, the study intends to examine how search engine
optimization (SEO) can improve consumer engagement and help the building of brand equity through
improved brand visibility and trust. The other goal is to determine the effect of personalized digital
marketing on consumer involvement and the role it plays in creating  brand equity through enjoying
customized, applicable brand experiences. Finally, the study attempts to offer strategic
recommendations to enhance digital marketing strategies to optimize for maximum engagement
with consumers as they interact with brands across digital channels.
Several important research questions are addressed in this study. First, it investigates the role that
social media marketing plays in generating consumer engagement and brand equity in digital arenas.
Secondly, it aims at clarifying the role played by SEO  in increasing consumer interaction and its role
in the creation of brand equity through increasing brand visibility and credibility. Third, the research
studies how personal digital marketing modifies the level of consumer engagement and develops the
form of brand equity by way of customized brand interactions. Finally, the study attempts to
determine which strategically produced interventions in digital marketing can maximize consumer
engagement and increase brand equity on digital channels.
This study is crucial for marketers, advertisers and business strategists to better understand how to
allocate digital spend better using empirical evidence. The research provides a practical decision -
making framework through identifying which digital stra tegies most influence brand performance.
Additionally, it extends academic literature by advancing the relationship previously overlooked in
the quantitative marketing literature between the digital activity metrics and the brand perception
outcomes (Iqbal et al., 2024; Sharif et al., 2026).

2.1 Social Media Marketing and Brand Equity
Social Media Marketing (SMM) is an essential approach for growing the engagement of consumers
as well as increasing the brand portfolio. Oluwaseyi et al. (2025) investigates how platforms like
Instagram and Facebook help add to brand equity through fostering User Engagement, which aids so
much in building brand trust and brand loyalty. According to them, brands that use authentic,
personalized content with their consumers are able to enhance emotional connection to the
customers they interact with, increasing brand loyalty and ultimately brand equity. The study stresses
the significance of the content engaged with and the social media emotional connection that gives
rise to brand equity.
Research further investigates the role of user -generated content (UGC) in brand visibility and brand
recognition, user -generated content (UGC) through social media, and brand visibility and brand
recognition (Lariba, 2023; Talaue, Kalanther and Bitanga, 2026). According to the study, encouraging
UGC thus helps build the trust necessary for brands as an element of brand equity. The study finds
social media engagement typically boosts brand loyalty but also warns that excessive
commercialization of UGC can tu rn consumers off, possibly damaging brand impression. For this
reason, the engagement authenticity is important to establish and maintain the brand equity. The
emphasis in both studies is placed on how social media can significantly involve consumers throu gh
trust and emotional engagement to improve brand equity. Yet longitudinal research measuring the
lasting impact of SMM on brand loyalty and equity is lacking. In addition, the effectiveness of SMM
strategies differs concerning regional and cultural differences.
2.2 Search Engine Optimization (SEO) and Consumer Engagement
Enhanced visibility of a brand largely increases its consumer engagement and brand equity through
Search Engine Optimization (SEO). A study conducted by Berman and Katona (2017) shows how
higher SEO rankings on search engine result pages (SERP) directly tr anslate to traffic in the website,
which subsequently shows to have a greater interaction with the brand. Furthermore, they
discovered that brands that ranked higher in search results were seen as more credible and reliable
and enhanced their brand trust and increased brand equity. This is then complemented by Hasibuan
and Najmudin (2024), who explore the role that content quality plays in magnifying the impact of
SEO on consumer engagement. Through regression analysis, they demonstrate that SEO alone could
result in driving traffic, however, high -quality and relevant content leads to consumers actively
engaging with the traffic. Additionally, their research reveals that while SEO helps you increase brand
visibility, SEO improves brand visibility, but only w hen it is conjoined with engaging content.
However, they recommend adding SEO integration with other content marketing strategies to obtain
the maximum benefit of SEO to enhance user engagement and brand equity. This study reinforces
the relevance of SEO in boosting the visibility, trust, and engagement of the brand. But there is a lack
of understanding regarding how various SEO strategies influence long -term brand loyalty across

2.3 Personalization and Its Impact on Consumer Engagement
Personalization is the cornerstone of today’s modern digital marketing and is equally relevant for
improving consumer engagement and brand equity. Sani et al. (2024) study the linkage between
personalized product recommendations and consumer loyalty in e -commerce. According to their
study, tailored experiences, including personalized recommendations, were associated with more
repeat purchases and stronger emotional attachment to the brand. Personalized interactions enable
brands to address individual consum er needs and build consumer loyalty, which is a valuable
contribution to the brand equity. In addition, Sharma, Patel, and Gupta (2022) further investigate the
significance of personalized email marketing on consumer engagement. The results of their A/B
testing of their personalized and non -personalized email campaigns found that they experienced a
huge increase in their open rates and click-through rates when the content was tailored to individual
consumer preferences. The results of the study highlight the value of personalization when aiming to
make consumers more engaged. Further the enhanced personalization with AI on the social media
improves customer awareness, use experience and purchase intention (Beyari and Hashem, 2025;
Sarwar and Malik, 2025). But it also brings to light a crucial issue related to data privacy. The growing
wariness of consumers over how their data is being used could represent a challenge to the brands
that want to use personalized marketing strategies. The positive effects of per sonalization on
consumer engagement and brand equity are emphasized in the two studies. Nevertheless, the issue
of data privacy concerns is still an underexplored area of study. Future research is needed that will
examine how privacy regulations and consum er trust affect the effectiveness of personalized
marketing strategies in improving brand loyalty and equity.
2.4 Strategic Recommendations for Optimizing Digital Marketing Practices
Brands should integrate social media marketing, SEO, and personalization into one single digital
marketing strategy to maximize consumer engagement and brand equity. Kushwaha et al. (2020)
advise that businesses should be posting the same and authentic con tent on various platforms to
attract consumers. However, as their study determined, integration of both SEO strategies with
personalized marketing and social media interaction significantly boosts brand visibility, improves
consumer engagement, and increas es brand loyalty which is also found from the study of Moiz &
Hussain (2026). By using each of these strengths to their capacity, this multi-faceted approach helps
maximize consumer engagement. Likewise, Shukla et al. (2024) assert the importance of using data-
driven strategies to leverage real-time consumer insights to maximize marketing. Their survey-based
research shows that combining real -time personalization with SEO optimization strategies has
extremely high consumer engagement and brand loyalty. But they warn that overloading consumers
with too much content can result in brand fatigue and decreased consumer satisfaction. These
studies offer strategic guidelines for optimally running digital marketing. But both studies show a gap
in understanding how c onsumer feedback should be used to refine marketing strategies. Other
research might examine how brands could better incorporate real -time feedback to continually
adjust their digital marketing approaches and thereby enhance brand loyalty in time.

2.5 Conceptual Framework
Based on this, a conceptual framework for this literature review was developed, which coupled social
media marketing (SMM) and search engine optimization (SEO) as key drivers to consumer
engagement, thereby increasing the brand equity. Kushwaha et al. (202 0) further claim that these
strategies continue to work together synergistically to foster emotional connections through SMM,
to make the brand more visible through SEO, and to personalize consumer experiences. This
interaction mediates the relationship between the use of these strategies and brand loyalty, and as
such leads to the generation of long -term brand value. Brand visibility in search engines is ensured
by SEO, SMM builds emotional ties and trust, while personalization tailors experiences to indiv idual
consumers, making them feel valued and connected (Singh, 2024). Therefore, the framework
suggests that when these elements are joined together, they synergies to strengthen both brand
trust and brand loyalty. However, there is another gap in research that focuses on investigating how
these strategies interact across different industries, and how these interactions can be optimized to
ensure sustained brand success.
2.6 Literature Gap
Although considerable work has been conducted on the separate effects of SMM, SEO, and
personalization, there are some remaining gaps in existing research. Almost all of the existing studies
only take on individual strategies without proper system integrat ion of SMM, SEO, and
personalization. As suggested by Kushwaha et al. (2020), the combined impact of these strategies on
consumer engagement and brand equity remains underexplored. The previous research largely
focuses on the short-term effects on consumer engagement and brand equity, while limited attention
is paid to their long-term effects on brand loyalty and sustained brand equity. According to research
by Berman and Katona (2017) and Hasibuan and Najmudin (2024), further longitudinal research that
sheds light on SEO and personalization’s long-term impact on consumer loyalty is needed. Moreover,
sector-specific research is lacking. While e -commerce is studied extensively in digital marketing
literature, the industries of healthcare, finance, and educati on have been underrepresented. As
pointed out by Shukla et al. (2024), real-time personalization and SEO strategies can differ massively
across other sectors and warrant further research. Additionally, the concerns regarding data privacy
and personalization are underexplored. Sani et al. (2024) and Sharma et al. (2022) note consumers’
concerns about data usage as challenges to personalized marketing strategies. It is worthwhile to
investigate the ethical implications of using personal consumer data for pers onalized marketing in
future research. This literature review shows that personalization, SMM, and SEO are necessary to
improve consumer engagement and, at the same time, increase brand equity. Each of these
strategies on its own represents a source of brand success; together, they have the most potential to
develop brand loyalty and sustain equity. Their combined effects and ethical concerns should be
explored in future research.

In this research, the role of digital marketing strategies, namely social media marketing, SEO and
personalization in increasing brand awareness was studied through quantitative research approach

relationships (Benoit et al., 2018).
The survey design used in the research was cross sectional survey design that involved administering
of the structured questionnaire to respondents at one point of time. The reason for choosing this
design is that it offers a straightforward snapshot of how digital marketing strategies affect customer
behavior and perception. Furthermore, it is a quick way to find associations of variables in one point
in time and can therefore be used in studies where associations and patterns in consumer responses
are stu died (Dong et al., 2022).  This was similar to the recommendations on how to assess the
consumer interaction with the digital marketing strategies such as social media campaigns, search
visibility, and personalized content (Dong et al., 2022).
For statistical analysis and comparison of sampling variation, the sample size of 100 participants was
deemed great enough. The respondents were the ones who were knowledgeable about digital
marketing strategies particularly those who used social media, se arch engine, and personalized
marketing; hence purposive convenience sampling technique was used in selecting the participants.
The reason this technique was useful was to target people who were able to provide informed
responses regarding their own digita l marketing practices. The sample included participants of
different age groups, genders and income levels to have a more spread representation of consumer
experience and perceptions.
In order to maintain the credibility of the sample, participants were also required to have had some
exposure to digital marketing, including receiving personalized online content, interacting with a
social media campaign, or discovering a brand from a sea rch engine. The criterion was in place to
ensure that the responses were not based upon phone insights of real consumers, but both offline
and online.
The survey was conducted using an online survey tool to efficiently collect data about the participants
experience and perceptions towards digital marketing strategies. The statements regarding social
media usage, SEO visibility and trust, personalized con tent and their synergistic effect on consumer
engagement and brand equity were included in the form of Likert scale in the questionnaire. The
indicators used to measure the engagement behavior were interaction frequency, emotional
connection and brand recall (Dong et al., 2022; Talaue, 2026), which are in accordance with the digital
consumer behavior frameworks.
Quantitative data collected were analyzed using SPSS (Statistical Package for the Social Sciences),
version 30. It has been chosen SPSS, which has an excellent capability in quantitative analysis and to
see the relationship between the multiple variables ( Kumar, 2019). The analysis started with
descriptive statistics in order to give an overview of participant responses, and then correlation

moderate relationships, which implies that personalization does have a meaningful, though not
strong, effect on how consumers interact with digital content.   Moreover, there is a statistically

means of attracting consumers’ interaction, and that brand equity may be affected by other
mediating factors beyond the scope of this study.
4.2 Summary of Findings
This study’s findings provide significant knowledge on how various digital marketing techniques, i.e.,
social media marketing, search engine optimization (SEO), and personalization, influence consumer
engagement and brand equity. The results of correlation  tests showed that personalization has the

strongest and most statically significant relationship with consumer engagement (r = 0.638, p < 0.01),
indicating that when brands customize content to a consumer’s preference, engagement levels
increased significantly. In line with this, Sani et al. (2024 ) and Sharma, Patel, and Gupta (2022)
concurred that consumers are attracted to personalized experiences, which in turn lead to higher
levels of consumer loyalty and interaction. Moreover, SEO had a significant but moderate relationship

The results of regression analysis revealed that SEO is the most influential predictor of social media

predictor of social media return. Interestingly, social media marketing itself did not lead to a
significant direct relationship with either consumer engagement or brand equity as postulated by

emotional and trust building relationship with consumers. Moreover, there was no significant direct
relationship in any digital marketing strategy to brand equity, implying tha t there may be other
factors besides digital campaigns that affect brand value. Overall, these results confirm the
importance of personalization and SEO in achieving engagement as well as the conditional nature of
social media, perhaps even indirectly.
4.3 Discussion
The purpose of this study was to examine how social media marketing, search engine optimization
(SEO), and personalization lead to consumer engagement and brand equity. Results clearly indicate
which strategy is more effective. According to Sani et al. (2024) and Sharma, Patel, and Gupta (2022),
personalization proved to be the most effective tool to increase consumer engagement by offering
personalized recommendations or emails. The personalization and engagement show a strong
positive correlation (r = 0.6 38, p < 0.01) suggesting that the consumers interact more with brands
which understand their individual needs. Emotional connections are the foundation of sustained
engagement, and personalized experiences create them. Although, as pointed out by Sharma et  al.,
the issue of data privacy is now sharply emerging, and future marketing strategies will need to find a
way to balance personalization with ethical use of data.
It was also found that SEO was a significant predictor of consumer engagement, however less so than
personalization. The result of the correlation (r = 0.216, p < 0.05) supports the findings of Berman
and Katona (2017) that better search rankings improve c onsumer trust and interaction by building
brand credibility. Furthermore, the regression outcomes demonstrated that SEO is the most powerful
predictor of social media marketing effectiveness, with the regression coefficient equaling 0.440 (p =
0.000), which implies that SEO improves not only visibility in search engines but also other digital
strategies indirectly. This backs Hasibuan and Najmudin’s (2024) conclusion that SEO can only achieve
its full potential when combined with high quality and relevant c ontent. Any brand that invests in
SEO and also produces quality content will be more likely to convert views into active engagement,
so SEO should be regarded as part of the foundations of any digital strategy.

On the contrary of what is predicted and reported in the literature, social media marketing did not
have a statistically significant relationship with consumer engagement or brand equity. Oluwaseyi et
al. (2025) and Lariba (2023) state that social media is  important in developing emotional brand
connections and trust, which contradicts with this study’s results, which indicate that simply having
the social media presence or being active on the platforms is not enough to drive engagement. The
saturation and over commercialization of social platforms could have become so that it results in
content fatigue and consumer fatigue to respond. This notion is supported by Lariba’s warning
against a UGC overuse that could drive away consumers. Therefore, social media may need more
appropriate and authentic content strategies to fully leverage its effect on engagement and brand
perception.
It also found that there was no statistically significantly correlation between any of the digital
marketing strategies and brand equity, which was another unexpected finding. The present study
disagrees with Oluwaseyi et al. (2025), Berman and Katona (201 7), and Sani et al. (2024) that digital
strategies such as social media, SEO, and personalization contribute to brand recognition and loyalty,
as brand equity could be influenced by other factors that are not directly measured by social media,
SEO, and per sonalization. In all likelihood, these elements (product quality, customer service, long
term brand consistency and offline experiences) play a much larger role in building brand equity than
digital tactics alone. What is needed is a more comprehensive, in tegrated approach to building a
brand that goes beyond simply digital outreach.
From a strategic perspective, the findings support the opinion by Kushwaha et al. (2020) and Shukla
et al. (2024) that the digital marketing strategies should not work in isolation. While, personalization
and SEO had very respective effects on consumer eng agement, their combined usage is likely to
achieve consistent and sustainable results. In addition, the observed relationship between SEO and
social media (r = 0.411, p < 0.01) validates Kushwaha et al.’s argument for cross channel synergy
between social media, SEO, and personalization. Finding this, it actually backs up the idea that SEO
can drive traffic to not just websites, but social platforms as well and when combined thoughtfully,
can increase the effectiveness of a social campaign. Shukla et al (202 4) cautions that however the
challenge is still on how to avoid brand fatigue by creating excessive of content.
These findings therefore imply that digital marketing strategies should be tuned not only to platform
features, but also to consumer expectations. For example, personalization should be executed with
transparency and respect for data privacy, and, in SEO, the content rather than the algorithm should
drive the search. While social media is absolutely key for visibility, it’s a very different story than it
was back in 2008, and to continue to be effective requires more focus on authenticity, on storytelling,
and on building community. Shukla et al. (2024) recommend that brands should not rely too much
on any one strategy and take a balanced, data driven, and feedback -oriented approach to digital
marketing.
Finally, this study provides evidence -based insights of how social media marketing, SEO and
personalization work differently. Furthermore, it confirms that all three strategies are possible,
however, personalization is the strategy that can help contribute  to the highest levels of consumer
engagement, while SEO remains foundational in boosting digital efforts. Brand equity on the other

5. Conclusion
The conclusions of this research demonstrate that personalization is the most effective approach to
improve consumer engagement. The results showed a significant positive effect (r = 0.638, p < 0.01),
suggesting that tailored experiences lead to emotional bonds with consumers, leading to better
consumer interaction and brand loyalty. Though its impact is modestly positive (r = 0.216, p < 0.05),
SEO increases brand visibility, which increases consumer trust and interaction. The findings of this
study, howeve r, indicated a less direct and statistically insignificant influence of social media
marketing on consumer engagement and brand equity. Although social media platforms are
extensively utilized, the result indicates that SMM may not have such a direct influence on the brand
equity as is anticipated due to the other influencing factors, such as content quality, platform type,
or consumer preferences. Moreover, the application of these strategies did not seem to significantly
affect brand equity, which means that brand equity is affected by much more than digital marketing.
5.1 Limitations
This study adds valuable information to digital marketing strategies, though there are limitations. The
research employed a cross-sectional survey design and is therefore unable to establish the long-term
effects of SMM, SEO, and personalization on brand e quity and consumer loyalty. This is reported by
many as a common limitation in social media marketing research, one that captures only the
snapshot of the behavior of the consumer at a given point in time (Thoumrungroje, 2018).
Furthermore, it was conducte d with a small sample size (100 participants) that might not be
representative of the entire consumer population (Vozzi et al., 2021). Like the sample, it also
consisted purely of digitally engaged individuals, who were not included in the sample because t hey
did not have limited exposure to digital marketing, making the results biased. Finally, this research
did not explore regional and cultural differences that might affect these strategies. Thus, findings
may not be generally applicable in other global markets.
5.2 Future Implications
Longitudinal studies should be conducted in the future to study the long -term effects of digital
marketing strategies on brand equity and customer loyalty. Thus, the study would be able to delve
deeper into the nuances and relations between SMM, SEO, and personalization in the context of how
they shape perceptions of the brand over an extended period. However, this research also pays
attention to the differences in the effectiveness of these strategies in the sector, since e -commerce
may not be fully representative for all industries (Mueller and Swoboda, 2025; Sarfaraz, 2025). Future
research could emphasize other sectors, including healthcare, financial, and educational sectors, that
may need a different approach in digital marketing. Finally, it also warr ants further exploration of
data privacy concerns and how they impact personalized marketing strategies. Personalization is
