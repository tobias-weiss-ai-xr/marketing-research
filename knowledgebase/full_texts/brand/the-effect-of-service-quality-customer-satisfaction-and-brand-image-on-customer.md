ARTICLE INFO ABSTRACT
Article History: This study examines the effects of service quality, customer satisfaction, and
brand image on customer loyalty among Garuda Indonesia passengers at
Soekarno-Hatta International Airport. A quantitative causal design was
employed, with primary data collected through online question naires from
100 respondents selected via accidental sampling. The data were analyzed
using multiple linear regression with SPSS. The results indicate that
service quality (t = 5.696, sig = 0.000), customer satisfaction (t = 3.221, sig
= 0.002), and brand image (t = 3.972, sig = 0.000) each have a positive and
significant effect on customer loyalty. Furthermore, the simultaneous test
(F = 28.983, sig = 0.000) confirms that all three variables collectively
influence customer loyalty. The R-square value of 0.475 indicates that these
variables explain 47.5% of the variance in customer loyalty, while the
remaining 52.5% is influenced by other factors outside the model. These
findings suggest that Garuda Indonesia should prioritize service quality
enhancement, particularly in staff responsiveness and service consistency,
while strengthening its premium brand image to foster long-term customer
loyalty. This study contributes empirical evidence to the service marketing
literature in the context of full-service airlines in Indonesia.

INTRODUCTION
Air transportation plays a strategic role in supporting national connectivity, economic
integration, and tourism development, particularly in an archipelagic country such as Indonesia
(Raihan et al., 2024).  The airline industry is highly competitive, as full -service carriers and low-
cost carriers compete not only to acquire customers but also to retain them. In service marketing,
long-term profitability depends increasingly on relational strategies that maintain existing
customers, because customer retention is generally more efficient than continuous ly acquiring
new customers (Kotler & Keller, 2021).
Customer loyalty represents a strong commitment to repurchase or reuse a preferred
service consistently in the future despite situational influences and competing marketing efforts

However, Garuda Indonesia faces challenges in maintaining customer loyalty. Data from

This pattern indicates a weakening of brand strength and suggests that service quality, customer
satisfaction, and brand image have  not been managed optimally to sustain customer trust and
loyalty.
Service quality is a comprehensive customer evaluation of service excellence based on the
gap between expectations and perceived performance (Cesariana et al., 2022). In a full -service
airline, customers expect high performance in reliability, responsiveness, assurance, empathy,
and tangible facilities. Service quality is therefore considered a direct predictor of loyalty (Wirtz
& Lovelock, 2022). Complaints related to check-in queues, distant gates, malfunctioning facilities,
limited staff friendliness, and service discipline at Terminal 3 indicate that service delivery may
not always meet passenger expectations (Anggelina & Naipospos, 2023).
Customer satisfaction is another important antece dent of loyalty. It reflects customers’
affective and cognitive evaluation after comparing the actual service experience with their prior
expectations (Tjiptono & Chandra, 2020). When service performance meets or exceeds
expectations, customers are more li kely to repurchase, recommend the service, and resist
competitors. Previous airline research also shows that satisfaction contributes to the formation of
customer loyalty (Sutapa & Pradana, 2022).
Brand image refers to the set of beliefs, ideas, and impres sions associated with a brand
(Kotler & Keller, 2021). In the airline industry, brand image is formed through service experience,
corporate reputation, safety perceptions, and marketing communication. A strong image as a
safe, trustworthy, and premium airl ine can generate emotional attachment and support loyalty
even when customers encounter minor service failures (Rachim et al., 2023; Safwati et al., 2022).
Conversely, inconsistent service experiences may weaken the image and accelerate customer
switching.
Previous studies have examined the influence of service quality, customer satisfaction,
and brand image on customer loyalty, but predominantly within other airlines or service
industries, such as low-cost carriers and e-commerce platforms (e.g., Tirtayasa et al., 2021; Tannus
& Fransisca, 2022), rather than a national full -service carrier like Garuda Indonesia. Moreover,
most of these studies were conducted prior to or without explicit consideration of the shift in
customer expectations and travel behavior following the COVID-19 pandemic, a period marked
by heightened sensitivity to service reliability, health -related assurance, and brand trust. In

Theoretical Framework
Service Quality
Service quality is the abi lity of a service provider to fulfill customer needs and
expectations accurately and consistently. It is evaluated by comparing the service expected by
customers with the service they actually perceive. In this study, service quality is measured
through reliability, responsiveness, assurance, empathy, and tangible evidence (Handyana et al.,
2022). High service quality creates positive experiences, strengthens trust, and encourages
customers to continue using the same airline.

Customer Satisfaction
Customer satisfaction is the feeling or evaluation that arises after customers compare their
actual experience with prior expectations. Performance below expectations produces
dissatisfaction, performance that meets expectations produces satisfaction, and performance that
exceeds expectations produces high satisfaction (Kotler et al., 2022). The indicators used in this
study include repurchase intention, positive word -of-mouth, the creation of a favorable brand
image, and purchase decisions within the same company.

Brand Image
Brand image is a set of perceptions and associations embedded in customers’ memories
regarding a particular brand. A positive image can increase confidence, preference, and
emotional attachment, whereas a negative image can discourage continued use. The brand image
construct in this study is measured through strength, favorability, and uniqueness (Marcellia &
Fairliantina, 2023).

Customer Loyalty
Customer loyalty is a long -term commitment to continue purchasing or using a  brand’s
products and services despite competing offers. Loyal customers make repeated purchases, use
other services offered by the same company, recommend the company to others, and demonstrate
resistance to competitors’ promotional attractions (Amin, 2023).

RESEARCH METHOD
Research Design and Data Source
 This study employed a quantitative causal research design to examine the effects of
service quality, customer satisfaction, and brand image on customer loyalty. The research was
conducted from September to December 2025 at Soekarno -Hatta International Airport (CGK),
Tangerang, Banten. Primary data were collected through a structured online questionnaire
distributed using Google Forms. The questionnaire used a five-point Likert scale ranging from 1
(strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).

Population and Sample
The population consisted of Garuda Indonesia customers who used flight services at
Soekarno-Hatta International Airport, but the exact population size was unknown. The study
used non -probability accidental sampling, in which respondents were selected when they
encountered the researcher and met the eligibility requirements. A quota of 100 respondents was
established. Respondents had to be over 17 years old and had to have used Garuda Indonesia
services more than once (Sugiyono, 2020).

Variables and Measurement
Service quality (X1) was measured using ten items representing reliability,
responsiveness, assuran ce, empathy, and tangible evidence. Customer satisfaction (X2) was
measured using eight items related to repurchase, word -of-mouth, brand image, and purchase
decisions. Brand image (X3) was measured using six items representing strength, favorability,
and uniqueness. Customer loyalty (Y) was measured using eight items covering repeat
purchases, cross-buying, referrals, and resistance to competitors.

Data Analysis
Data analysis was performed using IBM SPSS Statistics. The procedures included
respondent and variable descriptive analysis, Pearson product -moment validity testing,
Cronbach’s alpha reliability testing, the Kolmogorov –Smirnov normality test, multicollinearity
testing through tolerance and Variance Inflation Factor (VIF), the Glejser heteroscedasti city test,
multiple linear regression, partial t -tests, a simultaneous F -test, and the coefficient of
determination. Statistical significance was evaluated at the 5% level (Ghozali, 2021).

Discussion
The Effect of Service Quality on Customer Loyalty
 The t-test results show that service quality has a positive and significant effect on customer
loyalty, with a t -value of 5.696 and a significance value of p < 0.001.  This result means that
improvements or deficiencies in service quality directly influence customers’ willingness to use
Garuda Indonesia again. Customers are particularly sensitive to punctuality, staff friendliness,
cabin comfort, ease of check -in, clari ty of information, and the speed of complaint handling.
When these elements are delivered consistently, customers are more likely to repurchase and
recommend the airline. The result is consistent with Akbar and Perkasa (2025), Tirtayasa et al.
(2021), Bism ala (2022), and Tannus and Fransisca (2022), who found that service quality
contributes to customer loyalty.
Garuda Indonesia should therefore maintain consistent service quality at every customer
touchpoint, from check-in to in-flight service. Frontline employees require continuous training to
provide responsive and empathetic service. On-time performance should also remain a priority,

The Effect of Customer Satisfaction on Customer Loyalty
Customer satisfaction has a positive and significant effect on customer loyalty, as
indicated by a t -value of 3.221 and a significance value of 0.002. This finding confirms that
customers who are satisfied with cabin service, flight comfort, punctuality, staff friendliness, and
reservation convenience are more likely to continue choosing Garuda Indonesia, avoid switching
to competing airlines, and provide positive recommendations. This result is consistent with
Sebayang and Perkasa (2026), Aisha and Kurnia (2024), and Ratnawati et al. (2022), who
concluded that customer satisfaction positively and significantly strengthens loyalty.
The finding indicates that customer satisfaction must remain a managerial priority.
Positive flight experiences reduce perceived  risk in future purchase decisions and create
confidence that the same service benefits will be obtained again. Accordingly, Garuda Indonesia
needs to maintain personal attention and staff courtesy, while improving customers’ perceptions
of safety, professionalism, and service consistency.

The Effect of Brand Image on Customer Loyalty
Brand image has a positive and significant effect on customer loyalty, with a t -value of
3.972 and a significance value of 0.000. Customers perceive Garuda Indonesia as a national airline
with a reputable, premium, safe, and high -quality image. A positive perception generates trust,
pride, and comfort, creating an emotional attachment that reduces customers’ responsiveness to
competing offers. This result supports Indrasari an d Perkasa (2026), Rachim et al. (2023), and
Safwati et al. (2022), who found that brand image plays an important role in strengthening
customer loyalty.
 Because brand image is reinforced by actual customer experience, Garuda Indonesia must
maintain servic e consistency across domestic and international routes. A gap between the
premium image and the service received can weaken trust. Conversely, a consistent experience,
distinctive service, and clear brand communication can strengthen the airline’s position  as a
preferred national carrier.

CONCLUSION AND SUGGESTION
Conclusion
 Based on the data analysis, service quality has a positive and significant effect on customer
loyalty among Garuda Indonesia customers at Soekarno -Hatta International Airport. Customer
satisfaction also has a positive and significant effect on customer loyalty, indicating that
customers who receive satisfactory flight exper iences are more likely to repurchase and
recommend the airline. Brand image has a positive and significant effect on customer loyalty,
confirming that a strong, trusted, and distinctive airline image supports continued customer
preference. Simultaneously, service quality, customer satisfaction, and brand image significantly
affect customer loyalty. The model explains 47.5% of the variation in loyalty, while the remaining
52.5% is influenced by other factors outside the study. Therefore, Garuda Indonesia sho uld
strengthen staff responsiveness, maintain service professionalism and facility quality, improve
the consistency of the customer experience, and reinforce its distinctive premium image to sustain
long-term customer loyalty.

Suggestion
Based on these fi ndings, Garuda Indonesia should intensify staff training to strengthen
responsiveness and empathy at key touchpoints such as check-in and boarding, establish periodic
monitoring of service quality and customer satisfaction, ensure consistency between its premium
brand positioning and the actual service experience across domestic and international routes, and
redesign loyalty programs such as GarudaMiles to offer more relevant, personalized benefits. For
future research, since this study's model explains only 47.5% of the variation in customer loyalty,
subsequent studies are encouraged to incorporate additional variables such as price perception,
promotional programs, prior flight experience, trust, and emotional attachment, expand the
sample beyond Soekarno-Hatta International Airport, and apply longitudinal designs to capture
how loyalty evolves over time.
