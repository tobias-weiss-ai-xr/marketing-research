ABSTRACT
In the digital era, customer relationship management (CRM) has undergone a
significant transformation with the advent of advanced technology that facilitates more
effective interactions between companies and customers. Digital CRM allows companies to
collect, analyze and utilize customer data in real-time, thereby offering a more personalized
and relevant experience. Technologies such as artificial intelligence, big data and predictive
analytics have played an important role in optimizing CRM strategies. Through the use of
chatbots, automated personalization, and data analysis, companies can increase customer
satisfaction and brand loyalty. Implementing digital CRM not only helps in understanding
customer needs and preferences, but also enables companies to respond quickly and
appropriately to changes in customer behavior. This study explores how digital technology has
changed traditional CRM approaches and provided competitive advantages for companies. In
addition, this research also highlights the challenges faced in implementing digital CRM,
including issues of data privacy and information security. In conclusion, technology integration
in CRM is the key to improving customer experience in the digital era, making interactions with
customers more effective and efficient. Thus, companies that successfully adopt digital CRM
can create stronger and more sustainable relationships with their customers.
Keywords: Customer Relationship Management, Digital Era, Technology

INTRODUCTION
The development of digital technology has brought significant changes in various
aspects of life, including in the business world. One area that is experiencing a major
transformation is Customer Relationship Management (CRM). In this digital era, CRM has
evolved from just a customer data management system into a sophisticated platform for
building more personal and efficient relationships with customers. This transformation is driven
by advances in information and communications technology, as well as the increasing need
for companies to provide superior customer experiences in order to increase customer
satisfaction and loyalty.
According to Kotler (2020), CRM is a strategic approach that integrates business
processes and technology to understand customer needs, provide superior value, and improve
long-term relationships with customers. As digital technology has developed, CRM has also
transformed to become more interactive and integrated, allowing companies to interact with
customers through various channels, such as social media, email, mobile applications and
websites.

Technology has played a very important role in changing the way companies interact
with customers. With the help of technology, companies can collect and analyze customer data
in real-time, which can then be used to make better business decisions and provide a more
personalized experience to customers. One example of technology that has had a big impact

Benefits of Digital CRM Implementation
Implementing digital CRM provides various benefits for companies. First, digital CRM
allows companies to collect customer data from various sources, such as purchase
transactions, interactions on social media, and customer service history. This data can then be
analyzed to gain deeper insight into customer needs and preferences. According to Brown
(2020), companies that use digital CRM can increase the effectiveness of their marketing
campaigns by providing more relevant and personalized content to customers.
Second, digital CRM helps companies improve operational efficiency. By automating
routine tasks, such as managing customer data and sending emails, companies can save time
and resources. This allows marketing and customer service teams to focus on more strategic
tasks and provide added value for customers. For example, chatbots powered by AI can answer
customer questions quickly and accurately, thereby reducing the workload of customer service
teams and increasing customer satisfaction (Zhang, Følstad, & Bjørkli, 2023).
Third, digital CRM allows companies to provide consistent customer experiences across
multiple channels. By integrating multiple communication channels, such as email, social
media, and mobile apps, companies can ensure that customers receive a uniform, high-quality
experience at every touchpoint. According to Gao (2021), multichannel integration in digital
CRM can increase customer loyalty and strengthen long-term relationships with customers.
Challenges in Digital CRM Implementation
Although digital CRM offers various benefits, its implementation also faces several
challenges. One of the main challenges is the issue of data privacy and security. In collecting
and storing customer data, companies must ensure that the data is properly protected and not
misused. According to a survey conducted by Henein (2020), data security is one of the main
concerns for companies adopting digital CRM. Companies must comply with data privacy
regulations, such as Europe's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and implement strict
security measures to protect customer data.
Apart from that, another challenge is system integration. Companies often have a
variety of different systems for managing customer data, such as sales, marketing, and
customer service systems. Integration between these systems can be complex and require
significant investment in technology and human resources. According to Witell (2020),

METHODS
This research uses a qualitative descriptive approach to explore how digital technology
can improve customer experience through customer relationship management (CRM). This
approach was chosen because it allows researchers to gain a deep understanding of complex
and dynamic phenomena, and provides flexibility in collecting and analyzing data. The data in
this research was collected through various sources, including literature studies, in-depth
interviews, and document analysis.
A literature study was conducted to identify key concepts, theories, and previous
findings that are relevant to the research topic. Literary sources used include books, journal
articles, industry reports, and other reliable publications. The aim of the literature study is to
build a strong theoretical foundation and identify research gaps that can be filled by this study.
In-depth interviews were conducted with experts and practitioners in the field of CRM
and digital technology. Participants were selected based on their expertise and experience in
implementing digital CRM in various industrial sectors. These interviews were conducted face-
to-face and via teleconference, depending on participants' availability and preferences.
Interview questions were designed to explore participants' views on the benefits, challenges,
and best practices of using digital technologies to improve customer experiences. These
interviews were recorded with the participant's permission and the transcripts were analyzed
thematically.
In addition, document analysis was carried out to complement the data obtained from
literature studies and interviews. The documents analyzed include company annual reports,
case studies, news articles and other relevant publications. Analysis of this document helps in
understanding the context of digital CRM implementation in various companies and industrial
sectors.
Data collected from various sources was analyzed using a thematic analysis approach.
The analysis process begins with reading and marking the data to identify key themes that

DISCUSSION
The Influence of Digital Technology on CRM
This research shows that digital technology is playing a critical role in changing the
customer relationship management (CRM) landscape. With technological advances such as
artificial intelligence (AI), big data analytics, chatbots, automatic personalization, and cloud
computing, companies can manage relationships with customers more efficiently and
effectively. The data obtained shows that the use of digital technology in CRM not only
increases operational efficiency but also provides a more personalized and satisfying customer
experience.
Artificial intelligence, for example, has made it possible to automate many routine tasks
in CRM, such as answering customer questions via chatbots and providing relevant product
recommendations. This is in line with the findings of Smith (2019), which shows that AI can
improve operational efficiency and customer satisfaction. AI allows companies to automate
processes that previously required human interaction, thereby reducing response times and
improving the quality of customer service.
Improved Customer Experience
Digital technology allows companies to provide more personalized and relevant
customer experiences. Big data analytics enables in-depth analysis of customer preferences
and behavior, which can be used to devise more targeted marketing strategies. Automated
personalization allows companies to tailor their messages and offers according to customers'
individual needs. According to McKinsey research (2019), effective personalization can increase
customer satisfaction by up to 75%, as shown in this research data.
A more personalized and relevant customer experience can also increase customer
loyalty. Amazon case studies show that using big data and AI to analyze customer purchasing
history and provide relevant product recommendations can increase customer loyalty by 15%
(Forbes, 2020). Similarly, Starbucks uses mobile apps and geolocation technology to offer
customized promotions, which has increased customer satisfaction by 12% (McKinsey, 2019).
Challenges in Implementing Digital Technology in CRM
Although there are many benefits to be gained from using digital technology in CRM,
implementation is not without challenges. The main challenges identified in this research are
data privacy and security issues. The collection and analysis of large amounts of customer
data raises concerns about data privacy and security. Gartner (2021) reports that 55% of
companies consider data security to be the main challenge in implementing digital CRM.
Companies must ensure that customer data is properly protected and complies with data
privacy regulations, such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe.
Apart from data privacy and security issues, system integration is also a big challenge.
Many companies have a variety of different systems for managing customer data, such as
sales, marketing, and customer service systems. Integrating these systems so they work

Implications for Business Practices
The findings of this study have several important implications for business practice.
First, companies should consider adopting digital technologies in their CRM to improve
operational efficiency and customer experience. Technologies such as AI and big data analytics
can provide valuable insights into customer behavior and preferences, which can be used to
devise more effective and personalized marketing strategies.
Second, companies must pay attention to data privacy and security issues in
implementing digital CRM. Given the significant concerns about data privacy, companies must
ensure that they comply with applicable data privacy regulations and implement stringent
security measures to protect customer data. In addition, system integration must be well
planned to avoid problems that may arise from using multiple different systems.
Research Limitations and Recommendations for Further Research
Although this research provides valuable insight into the influence of digital technology
on CRM, there are several limitations that need to be noted. First, this study uses a qualitative
descriptive approach, which may not provide broad generalizations. Further studies with a
quantitative approach could provide more representative data and stronger generalizations.
Second, this research is limited to several key technologies in CRM, such as AI, big
data analytics, chatbots, automatic personalization, and cloud computing. Other technologies
that may also be influential, such as the Internet of Things (IoT) and blockchain, have not
been discussed in depth. Future research could explore the impact of these technologies on
CRM and customer experience.
This discussion confirmed that digital technology has a very important role in improving
customer relationship management and customer experience. Despite facing several
challenges, companies that successfully implement this technology can gain significant
competitive advantages, such as increased operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and
customer loyalty. The Amazon and Starbucks case studies provide concrete examples of how
digital technology can be used effectively in CRM. Therefore, companies looking to improve
their CRM in the digital era should consider using this technology while addressing the
challenges that may arise.

automatic personalization, and cloud computing have had a significant impact on CRM. These
