Technology Enabled Services (ITeS) sector. Conventional recruitment approaches are

structured onboarding to enhance workforce quality and organizational competitiveness.

supports sustainable workforce development and long -term business performance (Yu et al.,
2022; Collins & Martinez -Moreno, 2022). This study examines how talent acquisition practices

2.3 Technology -Enabled Selection and
Onboarding: Beyond Hiring Efficiency :
Digital transformation has redefined
employee selection by enabling organizations
to adopt technology -driven assessment
methods that improve recruitment quality and
decision-making. Artificial intelligence,
online competency assessments, structured
virtual interviews, and data -driven evaluation
tools have enhanced the accuracy,
consistency, and transparency of selection
processes while reducing recruitment time
and administrative bias. Consequently,
organizations increasingly regard selection
effectiveness as a strategic mechanism for
identifying candidates whose competencies
align with long-term organizational objectives
rather than merely filling immediate
vacancies (Nikolaou, 2021; Yu et al., 2022).
Recent research suggests that selection
effectiveness should be evaluated not only
through hiring outcomes but also through
applicants' perceptions of procedural fairness,
transparency, and organizational
professionalism. Recruitment processes
perceived as objective and technology -
enabled strengthen organizational credibility,
improve candidate acceptance decisions, and
contribute to a positive employer image.
Conversely, inconsistent or poorly designed
selection procedures may negatively influence
applicants' organizational perceptions,
regardless of employment opportunities
offered (Collins & Martinez -Moreno, 2022;
Nikolaou, 2021).  Similarly, onboarding has
evolved beyond a routine orientation activity
into a strategic process that facilitates
employee adjustment, organizational
socialization, and early performance
development. Digital onboarding platforms,
virtual learning systems, and structured
induction programmes enable organizations to
integrate new employees more effectively
while enhancing engagement and reducing
uncertainty during organizational entry.
Effective onboarding therefore complements
recruitment and selection by ensuring that
newly hired employees successfully transition
into organizational roles and culture (Klein &
Polin, 2012; Bauer, 2010). Although selection
effectiveness and onboarding have received
increasing scholarly attention, existing studies

2.4 Towards an Integrated Digital Talent
Acquisition Framework
The growing complexity of digital labour
markets suggests that talent acquisition can no
longer be understood through isolated
recruitment activities. Instead, organizations
increasingly require an integrated approach in
which recruitment strategy, employer
branding, selection effectiveness, and
onboarding function as complementary
practices that collectively influence workforce
quality and organizational competitiveness.
Recent Human Resource Management
literature emphasizes that these dimensions
reinforce one another and create greater
organizational value when implemented as a

independent HR initiatives (Yu et al., 2022;
Tumasjan et al., 2020).  From a strategic
perspective, organizations that successfully
integrate digital recruitment technologies with

consistent recruitment experiences, improve
organizational credibility, and enhance

sustainable competitive advantage in
knowledge-intensive industries (Barney,
1991; Collins & Martinez-Moreno, 2022).

4.2 Sampling and Data Collection
The target population comprised employees
working in Indian ITeS organizations who
had direct experience of the organization's
recruitment and entry process. A purposive
sampling technique was adopted because
respondents were required to have sufficient
experience of digital recruitment, selection, or
onboarding practices to provide an informed
assessment. The instrument therefore
measured employee perceptions of the digital
hiring and entry experience rather than HR
practitioner evaluations of organizational
capability.
4.3 Measurement of Variables
The proposed research model consists of one
endogenous construct, Digital -Era Talent
Acquisition, explained through four
exogenous constructs:
⚫ Recruitment Strategy (RS)
⚫ Employer Branding (EB)
⚫ Selection Process Effectiveness (SPE)
⚫ Onboarding Practices (OP)
All constructs were measured using multiple
reflective indicators on a five-point Likert
scale ranging from 1 = Strongly Disagree to 5
= Strongly Agree, cons istent with established
Human Resource Management research (Hair
et al., 2022).

No personally identifiable information was
collected, and the data were analysed
exclusively for academic purposes.

6. Discussion
The present study examined the influence of
recruitment strategy, employer branding,
selection process effectiveness, and
onboarding practices on Digital -Era Talent
Acquisition (DETA) within Indian
Information Technology Enabled Services
(ITeS) companies. The empirical findings
indicate that all four proposed dimensions
significantly contribute to strengthening
digital talent acquisition, thereby supporting
the multidimensional perspective of
contemporary talent acquisition advanced in
recent Human Resource Management
literature.
Among the examined predictors, Employer
Branding emerged as the st rongest
determinant of Digital-Era Talent Acquisition.
This finding suggests that organizations with
a credible employer image, transparent
communication, and a compelling employee
value proposition are better positioned to
attract and engage high -quality talent in
competitive digital l abour markets. The result
is consistent with Collins and Martinez -
Moreno (2022), who reported that credible
recruitment messages significantly influence
applicants' job pursuit intentions, and with
Tumasjan et al. (2020), who demonstrated
that employer branding str engthens
recruitment effectiveness by improving
organizational attractiveness and recruitment
efficiency. In the Indian ITeS sector, where
skilled professionals frequently evaluate
multiple employment opportunities
simultaneously, a strong employer brand
appears to function as a strategic differentiator
rather than merely a communication tool.
The findings further demonstrate that
Recruitment Strategy exerts a significant
positive influence on Digital -Era Talent
Acquisition. Organizations adopting
structured recruitment planning, technology -
enabled sourcing, and systematic workforce
planning are more capable of identifying
candidates whose competencies align with
organizational requirements. This finding
supports the argument of Yu et al. (2022) that
strategic talent acquisition extends beyond
vacancy filling and should be viewed as an
integrated organizational capability that
creates long -term value through effective
human capital acquisition. The result also
reinforces the principles of the Resource -
Based View, which emphasize that
sustainable competitive advantage depends

7. Conclusion
This study examined the influence of
Recruitment Strategy, Employer Branding,
Selection Process Effectiveness, and
Onboarding Practices on Digital -Era Talent
Acquisition (DETA) in Indian ITeS
companies. The findings indicate that all four
dimensions significantly contribute to
strengthening digital talent acquisition,
confirming that talent acquisition has evolved
into a strategic organizational capability
rather than a traditional recruitment function.
Among the examined factors, Employer
Branding emerged as the strongest predictor,
followed by Recruitment Strategy,
Onboarding Practices, and Selection Process
Effectiveness, highlighting the importance of
an integrated approach to attracting and
engaging skilled professionals.
The study contributes to the Human Resource
Management literature by proposing and
validating an integrated framework for
digital-era talent acquisition within the Indian
ITeS context. Unlike previous studies that
examined these practices independently, the
present research demonstrates their collective
contribution to effective talent acquisition,
thereby extending the application of the
Resource-Based View (Barney, 1991) and
supporting recent research on strategic talent
acquisition (Yu et al., 2022).  From a practical
perspective, the findings suggest that ITeS
organizations should strengthen employer
branding, adopt technology -enabled
recruitment strategies, implement transparent
selection processes, and develop structured
onboarding programmes to enhance talent
acquisition outcomes. Such integrated
practices can improve organizational
attractiveness and support long -term
workforce competitiveness.  The study is
limited by its cross -sectional design and its
focus on the Indian ITeS sector. Future
research may employ longitudinal designs,
examine additional organizational factors, and
compare digital talent acquisition practices
across industries and countries to enhance the
generalizability of the findings.
