Abstract:
Advertising has evolved dramatically, moving from traditional media to a dynamic, data
-

driven digital landscape. This paper
explores the shift from print and broadcast ads to the rise of digital platforms that offer precise targeting and real
-

time perf
ormance
analytics. Key trends such as personalization, influencer marketing, video content, and programmatic advertising are shaping
the future
of the industry. Additionally, the growing emphasis on social responsibility and brand purpose reflects changing

consumer expectations.
Alongside these opportunities, challenges like ad fatigue, privacy concerns, and ethical practices present new hurdles for ad
vertisers. This
paper discusses the evolution, current trends, and the future trajectory of advertising in
the digital age.

Keywords:
Digital advertising, personalization, influencer marketing, programmatic advertising, social responsibility, video content,
consumer privacy, brand purpose, advertising trends
