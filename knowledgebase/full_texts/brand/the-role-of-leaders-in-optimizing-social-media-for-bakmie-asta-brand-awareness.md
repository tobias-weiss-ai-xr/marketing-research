INTRODUCTION
The Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSME) sector plays a crucial
role in supporting stability, job creation, and national economic growth in
Indonesia (Lubis & Salsabila, 2024). However, in the current era of globalization
and the digitalization of trade, the competitive challenges faced by local
businesses are becoming increasingly intense (Hakim et al., 2024). These
conditions require MSME to continuously adapt to advancements in information
technology in order to sustain their businesses (Arianto & Sofyan, 2022). Shifts in
consumer behavior now dominated by digital activities have positioned digital
marketing platforms, particularly social me dia, as promotional tools that are
considered far more efficient, responsive, cost-effective, and capable of reaching
a broad market share (Syaharullah et al., 2021).
The main problem frequently faced by most MSME operators, particularly
in the local culin ary sector, is a limited understanding of digital marketing
strategies, which results in low brand awareness among potential customers
(Talib & Sari, 2024). This phenomenon is clearly evident at Bakmie Asta, a
handmade noodle business operating in the Food  Court area of Pasar Santa in
South Jakarta. Although it has great potential and generates satisfactory net
operating profits, the outlet’s location deep within the market makes Bakmie
Asta less visible to the public from the outside (low visibility). Befo re this
management project began, Bakmie Asta’s digital marketing efforts were not
being managed optimally; digital promotions were limited to an Instagram
account that was rarely updated, and the business lacked interactive channels on
other popular platforms such as TikTok and Facebook.
In response to these challenges, this intervention project titled “Social
Media Management” was carried out as one of the prerequisites for graduation
from the Bachelor of Management program (Firdausi & Sulistyorini, 2023) . This
study aims to optimize the management of social media accounts (Instagram,
TikTok, and Facebook) to boost brand awareness for the Bakmie Asta MSME.
The management of this project was structured using the Waterfall model of the
System Development Lif e Cycle (SDLC) framework, and its success was
measured through strict team performance indicators in the form of Key
Performance Indicators (KPI) (Abidin & Firdaus, 2025). Through this integrative
approach, the project’s outcomes are expected to make a practical contribution to
expanding digital exposure, fostering interactive engagement, and increasing
sales conversions and the competitiveness of local culinary MSME in the digital
realm.

LITERATURE REVIEW
Social Media and Digital Marketing for MSME
Social media is a digital platform that allows users to interact, share content, and
build communities in an online space  (Talib & Sari, 2024). In a business context, social
media serves not only as a means of communication but also as a strategic tool for
building long -term relationships with customers and strengthening brand image
(Obermayer et al., 2022)
System Development Life Cycle (SDLC)
The System Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is a systematic framework
fundamentally used to build, develop, and maintain a system, ensuring that it
operates strictly in accordance with expected objectives. While originally rooted
in software engineering and information systems, the SDLC framework has been
increasingly adapted into digital marketing and social media management. In
this context, SDLC provides a rigorous, step -by-step approach to proj ect
execution, enabling a social media management team to transition from an
unstructured, ad -hoc content creation process into a highly organized and
measurable digital campaign (Suryana & Yulianti, 2021).
Within the various SDLC approaches, the Waterfall model is particularly
distinguished by its emphasis on sequential, downward -flowing work phases.
The fundamental principle of the Waterfall model is that each phase must be
completely fulfilled, documented, and approved before the team can procee d to
the subsequent phase. This linear progression prevents overlapping tasks,
minimizes miscommunication among team members, and ensures that the
strategic foundation is solidly built before execution begins. As applied in this
social media management project, the Waterfall model consists of five definitive
stages (Abidin & Firdaus, 2025).
1. Planning: Involves foundational preparations such as team formation,
task allocation based on specific expertise, resource management, and the
creation of a comprehensive project timeline.
2. Analysis: Focuses on gathering empirical data, including market analysis,
competitor benchmarking, and identifying current content trends to
define the strategic direction.
3. Design: Translates the analytical findings into tangible creati ve assets,
involving the creation of visual identities, content templates, and mockups
using tools like Canva and CapCut.
4. Implementation: The execution phase where the designed strategy is
brought to life through scheduled content uploads, active community
engagement, running paid advertisements, and executing collaborations.
5. Maintenance: The ongoing phase of conducting routine evaluations,
monitoring digital metrics, and making necessary strategy adjustments
based on content performance.
The integration of the SDLC Waterfall model is highly crucial for Micro,
Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSME) like Bakmie Asta. It provides several
critical advantages for project management. First, it establishes a structured
workflow that enforces intern al team discipline, ensuring that high -volume
content production aligns precisely with predetermined editorial calendars.

Second, the linear nature of the Waterfall model facilitates strict documentation
and progress tracking at every single stage. This sy stematic tracking makes the
framework highly compatible with quantitative evaluation tools such as Key
Performance Indicators (KPI), allowing project leaders to objectively measure
output, assess team effectiveness, and validate whether the digital marketi ng
efforts are continuously aligned with the ultimate goal of boosting brand
awareness.
SWOT Analysis, STP, and the AIDA Model
SWOT analysis is used to identify the strengths, weaknesses,
opportunities, and threats that influence a business’s social media management
strategy (Suirono, 2021). The STP (Segmenting, Targeting, Positioning) strategy
is used to determine relevant audience groups and position a brand differently
from competitors (Handayani et al., 2023). Meanwhile, the AIDA model
(Attention, Interest, Desire, Action) explains the psychological stages consumers
go through when responding to marketing content, ranging from capturing
attention to driving a purchase (Kurniawati et al., 2022). These three theoretical
frameworks are used in an integrated  manner in formulating the social media
content strategy for the Bakmie Asta MSME.
Key Performance Indicator (KPI) in Social Media Management
KPI are used as quantitative measurement tools to assess a team’s
performance in a project, including social media  management projects. KPI
measurements include indicators such as the number of published posts,
follower growth, the number of likes, and the number of views, which are
compared to targets set at the beginning of the period (Rachmawati & Abma,
2022).

METHODOLOGY
This study employs a mixed -methods approach integrated with an
implementation-based project management framework. The research was
conducted over an eight -month period, from May through December 2025,
focusing on Bakmie Asta MSME located in the  Pasar Santa Food Court, South
Jakarta. The research team was structurally organized based on specific
functional roles to ensure comprehensive execution, including project leadership,
visual content creation, and community engagement management.
Data coll ection techniques in this study consisted of primary and
secondary data gathering. Primary data were obtained through direct field
observations, in -depth interviews with the MSME owner regarding business
constraints, and the extraction of digital engagemen t metrics directly from the
analytics dashboards of Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook. Secondary data were
collected through the review of historical transaction reports and relevant
literature. The collected data were then analyzed using a descriptive mixed -
method analysis: qualitative data were synthesized to formulate STP and SWOT
strategies, while quantitative data regarding content reach and audience
engagement were analyzed descriptively by comparing the actual realization
against the predefined Key Per formance Indicators (KPI) targets using
spreadsheet software.

The project implementation procedure follows the steps in the Waterfall
SDLC life cycle:
1. Planning & Analysis Phase: Includes team formation, initial field
observations, in-depth interviews with the partner (Mr. Aditya Agustino
Hermawan), review of historical transaction reports, signing of a
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU), and conducting STP, SWOT, and
competitor analyses.
2. Design Phase: Developing content pillar plans, writing copy, designin g a
content editorial calendar in Google Spreadsheet, and creating visual
mockups for social media feeds using Canva and CapCut based on the
official Bakmie Asta logo color palette (red and white).
3. Implementation Phase: Scheduled publication of short -form video and
static image content on Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook, running paid
social media ads, collaborating with food vloggers, and conducting
periodic monitoring of digital engagement metrics.
4. Maintenance & Evaluation Phase: Conducting weekly and mo nthly
performance reviews, compiling quantitative evaluation reports based on
the team’s Key Performance Indicators (KPI) framework, and
coordinating regularly with the SME owner to ensure the sustained
quality of the digital campaign

DISCUSSION
Discussion is to interpret and describe the significance of the findings in
light of what was already known about the research problem being investigated,
and to explain any new understanding or fresh insights about the problem after
taking the findings into consideration.
The implementation of a structured work system based on the SDLC
framework has proven to enhance the team’s internal work effectiveness. In the
first quarter, the process of adapting to the new workflow resulted in content
publication falling short of the full target (61 out of a target of 72 pieces of content,
or 85%). This w as because the team was still in the phase of aligning
communication and finalizing revisions to the mockup design with the partner
owner (Mr. Aditya Agustino Hermawan).
However, entering the second and third quarters, the team’s work
discipline improved d ramatically, with content upload rates exceeding
operational targets (reaching 126% and 156%). Allya Putri, the content creator,
was able to mass -produce creative content using Canva and CapCut thanks to
the editorial calendar neatly organized on a cloud s torage platform (Google
Drive), ensuring that the account’s content variety remained consistently strong.
The quantitative data in Table 4 -6 highlights the striking differences in
strategic impact between paid advertising (social media ads) and organic growth.
Content published through Instagram Ads and Facebook Ads has proven
successful in instantly boosting an account’s reach and views on a large scale.
This phenomenon is reflected in the surge in view metrics on the Instagram
platform in June (12,155 views) and July (21,455 views) when the promotional
budget was intensively allocated. The use of paid ads was  effectively able to
capture the attention of new audiences outside the Pasar Santa area in a short

period of time. However, after the ad budget ran out, there was a fluctuating
downward trend in view metrics in the following months.
In contrast, an organ ic content strategy reflects a much more stable,
healthy, and long-term growth pattern. Although new accounts (such as TikTok
and Facebook) generated relatively low organic viewership figures in May, the
team’s consistency in posting high -quality visual co ntent such as videos of
handmade noodle -making, signature chili oil seasoning, and highlighting the
hygiene of ingredients gradually helped build a trustworthy brand image in the
minds of the audience. The combination of leveraging trending audio on TikTok
and strategically crafting regional culinary hashtags successfully secured stable
audience engagement metrics (likes and views) by the end of the year (TikTok
maintained a stable average of 4,110 views in the third quarter).
A critical issue identified in  the final performance evaluation of this
project is the low conversion rate of views into new followers. Based on the data
in Tables 4 –6, follower growth across all three platforms experienced a sharp
stagnation toward the end of the third quarter, with t he Instagram account
gaining only single -digit numbers of new followers (+7 and +6), while TikTok
averaged +5 new followers. This situation stands in stark contrast to the view
metrics, which remained exceptionally high (Facebook reached 1,351 views and
TikTok reached 4,110 views in December).
These empirical findings indicate that high exposure to digital
information does not necessarily correlate directly with the formation of a loyal
digital community willing to commit to following an account. Modern audiences
tend to act as passive viewers; they enjoy creative videos that appear on their For
You page but are reluctant to visit a profile and click the “Follow” button.
This strategic evaluation underscores the need to revise the AIDA model
in its final st age (Action). Khoerudin, the community engagement coordinator,
recommends tactical interventions such as including much more persuasive Call
to Action (CTA) instructions in the video captions, releasing a series of
interconnected content, and organizing di rect promotional programs (such as
offering price discounts or free fried dumplings on -site to customers who can
prove they have clicked the “Follow” button on Bakmie Asta’s official social
media accounts). Through this combined approach to operational man agement
based on theoretical data, social media interactions are expected to foster the
growth of a loyal digital community and drive an increase in actual sales revenue
for local culinary businesses.
Based on the evaluated metrics, the empirical findings strongly support
the formulated hypotheses. First, the structured implementation of the SDLC
Waterfall model was proven to significantly enhance the operational capabilities
of the team, evidenced by the KPI achievement rates exceeding 100% in the
second a nd third quarters. Second, the data confirms, as paid social media
advertising generated an immediate, albeit temporary, surge in visibility
(peaking at 21,615 views on Instagram in July). Lastly, the sustained performance
on TikTok, which maintained over 4,000 average views through consistent,
quality postings without ad spend, validates by demonstrating that organic
strategies yield more resilient and long-term brand awareness growth

CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
This study concludes that optimizing soc ial media management through
the System Development Life Cycle (SDLC) Waterfall model is highly effective
in establishing a structured, documented, and measurable digital strategy for
MSMs. The integration of Key Performance Indicators (KPI) enabled a rigo rous
evaluation of the team's operational effectiveness. The findings reveal a distinct
dichotomy between promotional strategies: paid advertising (social media ads)
acts as a powerful catalyst for immediate, short -term surges in audience reach,
whereas an  adaptive organic content strategy focusing on visual aesthetics,
digital trends, and emotional product narratives provides stable and sustainable
long-term growth in brand awareness.
The managerial implication of this research indicates that local culinar y
MSMEs must dynamically combine both approaches; utilizing paid ads for initial
market penetration and sustaining audience retention through consistent organic
engagement. Furthermore, to address the challenge of passive audience behavior
(high views but low conversion to followers), businesses must strengthen the
'Action' phase of the AIDA model by integrating more compelling Call to Actions
(CTAs) and direct promotional incentives.
Despite its positive outcomes, this project has limitations. The observation
was confined to a single culinary MSME over an eight-month period, which may
not fully represent the digital dynamics of other business sectors. Therefore,
future research is recommended to apply this SDLC -based digital marketing
framework across a wider variety of MSME sectors and for a longer duration to
test the consistency and scalability of the strategy.

ADVANCED RESEARCH
While this study successfully demonstrates the effectiveness of
implementing the System Development Life Cycle (SDLC) Waterfall model and
Key Performance Indicators (KPI) in optimizing social media management for
Bakmie Asta, several limitations must be acknowledged to guide future scholarly
investigations. First, the empirical observation was confined to a single culinary
Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprise (MSME) operating within a specialized
food court setting in South Jakarta over an eight -month evalua tion window,
which limits the generalizability of the findings across other business domains.
 Therefore, future research should extend this framework across diverse
MSME sectors —such as fashion, services, or manufacturing  and broader
geographical locations to evaluate the cross-industry applicability and scalability
of the SDLC  based digital marketing workflow. Additionally, given that the
rigid, sequential nature of the Waterfall model presented minor challenges
during real-time social media trend updates , subsequent studies could explore
adaptive project management methodologies, such as Agile or Scrum, to assess
whether iterative sprint cycles provide greater flexibility in responding to rapidly
changing social media algorithms. Finally, since this study  identified a notable
gap between high video view metrics and lower follower/sales conversion rates
in the final stage of the AIDA model, future research should incorporate
advanced financial and analytical metrics  such as Customer Acquisition Cost
