Abstract:

This

on

in

The

and

and

business

Social

to

between

2015

and

The

The

contribute

to

long-term

customer

relationships

and

enhancing

sustainable

competitive

advantages

in

the

digital

economy.

The

rapid

of

digital

technology

has

transformed

the

global

economic

environment

and

significantly

changed

the

The

the

The

to

Organizations

to

but

consumers.

As

are

changing

and

increasingly

One

Social

real

Platforms

Unlike

This

to

The

Consumers

the
