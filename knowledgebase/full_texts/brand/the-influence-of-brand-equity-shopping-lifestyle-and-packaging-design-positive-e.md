  ABSTRACT

The rapid growth of the creative economy in Pekanbaru  has intensified market
competition and encouraged businesses to understand consumers’ impulse b uying
behavior. This study examines the effects of brand equity, shopping lifestyle, and
packaging design on impulse buying, with positive emotion serving as a mediating
variable among consumers of creative economy MSMEs in Pekanbaru City. The
study employ ed a quantitative associative -causal approach. Data were collected
through questionnaires distributed to 170 respondents selected using purposive
sampling. The data were analyzed using the Structural Equation Modeling –Partial
Least Squares (SEM-PLS) method. The results indicate that brand equity, shopping
lifestyle, and packaging design have positive and significant effects on impulse
buying. Positive emotion also positively and notably influences  impulse buying.
Moreover, positive emotion mediates the rela tionships between brand equity,
shopping lifestyle, packaging design, and impulse buying behavior. These findings
suggest that strengthening brand equity, understanding consumers’ shopping
lifestyles, and developing attractive and functional packaging can generate positive
emotional responses that encourage spontaneous purchase decisions. Therefore,
creative economy MSMEs in Pekanbaru should enhance their brand value, adapt
to changing consumer lifestyle trends, and develop innovative packaging strategies
to increase impulse buying and strengthen market competitiveness.

2. Literature Review and Hypothesis
Brand equity : this concept describes the
added value consumers assign to a product as a
result of their perceptions , experiences, and as-
sociations with  a particular brand  (Lamorena et
al., 2022). Strong brand equity can increase con-
sumer trust, perceived quality, and product differ-
entiation, thereby encouraging consum ers to
make spontaneous purchases  (Susetya et al.,
2024). In addition, shopping lifestyle reflects con-
sumers’ patterns in allocating time, money, and
attention to shopping activities as part of their
daily lifestyle and self -expression(Dewi and Adi,
2023). Consumers with a high shopping life style
tend to follow trends, prefer famous brands, and
seek enjoyable shopping experiences, which may
increase their t endency to engage in impulse
buying behavior (Liantifa and Siswadhi, 2022).
Packaging design performs functions that
extend beyond merely protecting a product ; it
conveys product quality, uniqueness, and brand
identity (Kamariah, 2023) . Attractive packaging
can capture consumers’ attention, create positive
perceptions, and stimulate emotional responses
that encourage spontaneous purchases
(Ramadhina and Mugiono, 2022) . For locally
produced handicrafts, packaging design serves
as an essential marketing attri bute that shapes
consumers' initial perceptions of the product. The
visual appearance of the packaging often
influences how consumers evaluate the
perceived quality of a product , uniquene ss, a s
well as overall value prior to making a purchase

Impulse buying behavior can be described as

experienced throughout the shopping process.
(Wahyuningrum, 2025) . The likelihood of

purchasing decisions with minimal prior plannin g
(Thios et al., 2025) . In creative economy prod-
ucts, especially handicrafts, consumers may pur-
chase products not only for functional purposes
but also because of aesthetic value and emotion-

3. Analysis Method
This study adopted a quantitative approach
with an associative –causal research design to
investigate the influence of brand e quity,
shopping lifestyle, and packaging design on
impulsive purchasing behavior, with positive
emotion acting as a mediator among consumers
of creative economy MSMEs in Pekanbaru City.
The study population comprised consumers who
had previously purchased products offered by
creative economy MSMEs in the city. A sample of
170 respondents was selected using a non -
probability purposive sampling strategy, targeting
individuals with prior purchasing experience of
local handicraft products, including woven
textiles, batik, rattan handicrafts, and
embroidered products. A structured questionnaire
employing a five -point Likert scale was used to
gather data related to respondents’ perceptions
of the research variables. The data analysis was
performed using Partial Least Squares–Structural
Equation Modeling (PLS -SEM) utilizing
SmartPLS 4.0 , as this analytical approach
facilitates the simultaneous evaluation of
measurement and structural mod els in analyzing
relationships among the proposed constructs.
Partial Least Squa res–Structural Equation
Modeling (PLS-SEM), performed using SmartPLS
4.0 software , was employed to analyze the
research data. This method was selected due to
its ability to analyze complex relationships among
latent constructs simultaneously and to evaluat e
both direct and indirect effects, including
mediation effects, within the proposed structural
framework (Hair et al., 2022) . The data analysis
process included a thorough assessment of both
the measurement and structural models . The
measurement model assessment included tests
of convergent validity, discriminant validity, AVE,
Cronbach’s Alpha, and Composite Reliability to
assess construct validi ty and reliability, while the
structural model was analyzed based on R², f²,
and hypothesis significance testing using
bootstrapping.
