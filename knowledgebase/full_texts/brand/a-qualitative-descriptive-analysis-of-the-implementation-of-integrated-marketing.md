Abstract
Purpose: This study aims to analyse the implementation of integrated marketing communication (IMC) for the Aerostreet brand in the
context of digital ecosystem-based marketing communication.
Methodology: We employs a descriptive qualitative a pproach. Data were collecte d through interviews with purposively selected key
informants and direct observation of Aerostreet’s marketing communication activities. This study involved a total of 4 informants from
Aerostreet : the Brand Director, Marketing Team, CBO Secretary, and Social Media Team Manager.
Results: The findings indicate that Aerostreet’s IMC implementation has evolved into an adaptive and sales-performance-oriented
digital ecosystem. The communication strategy  is primarily dominated by digital channe ls, especially TikTok, which functions as  a
sales conversion hub through live commerce, affiliate marketing, and data-driven advertising, contributing to a 300% increase i n
monthly sales compared to offline channels. Instagram, meanwhile, plays a more significant role in brand image development. The
study also reveals differences in perceptions between short-term performance orientation and long-term brand identity building, as well
as between adaptation to digital trends and communication consis tency. Although multiple communication channels have been utilised
simultaneously, the integration remains operational and has not yet fully unified the brand’s messages and meaning.
Applications: Based on the research findings, although there is currently a shift in IMC activities towards online platforms, the study
suggests that the use of certain offline strategies, such as sa les promotion, is still considered effective for engaging consum ers with the
brand; consequently, it is hoped that the shift towards digital promotion by the Aerostreet brand will not directly undermine t hese
offlinebased IMC approaches. Furthermore, a balance is also requ ired between data-driven approaches to drive conversions and ef forts
to build sustainable brand equity.
Keywords: Aerostreet, integrated marketing communication, marketing, TikTok, Instagram
Introduction
Information technology has transformed the world into a seemingly borderless space, enabling people to access
information more easily (Asongu et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2026). According to Itani et al. (2020), social media is
currently a major marketing communications trend. Social media can be defined as an online platform that enables users
to participate, share information and establish communication within a virtual space. In this era, social media is no longer
solely used for entertainment; it is beginning to penetrate the economic sphere, with platforms such as TikTok and
Instagram leading the way. TikTok is a social media platform that allows users to express themselves through short
videos. Users can utilise video features complete with music and effects, with durations ranging from 15 seconds to 10
minutes. TikTok enables its users to act as both communicators and recipients of communication (Aiger et al., 2026).
Launched in 2010, Instagram has grown into a popular social media platform with a significant user base
worldwide. In Indonesia, it is one of the most frequently used platforms after TikTok, with over 92 million active users in
January 2025. Of this total, around 53.5% are women and 46.5% are men (NapoleonCat, 2025). It offers features such as
Direct Messages, live streams, Instagram Stories, Reels, co mments, hashtags, editing tools and the Explore page. These
can be used to communicate, share content and connect with audiences worldwide (Birkun, 2024). The marketing
industry has undergone significant transformation in recent years, with the emergence of new technologies and the spread
of the internet changing how companies operate to meet consumer expectations (Osorio-Andrade et al., 2025). Social
media has also transformed how companies and consumers interact by providing new channels for communication,
engagement, and brand promotion (Kelm & Ibrahim, 2023).
The onset of the COVID-19 pandemic in early 2020 created unprecedented challenges for manufacturing firms
across the globe. Declining consumer purchasing power, driven by mass layoffs and mobility restrictions, significantly
reduced market demand during this period (Duffett & Thomas, 2024). At the same time, society became increasingly

dependent on digital and online-based services for education, employment, and the fulfilment of daily necessities. These
conditions indirectly pressured companies to rapidly adjust their business strategies and enhance the quality and
accessibility of services offered to consumers (Bragg et al., 2026). The digitalisation growth of consumer behaviour has
further accelerated the transition towards digital platforms in various aspects of economic activity (Pick et al., 2025).
This shift has reshaped the ways consumers interact with brands, search for information, evaluate available alternatives,
and make purchasing decisions. In response to these changes, digital marketing has become an essential strategic
approach, enabling firms to distribute information more efficiently while reaching increasingly targeted consumer
segments (Tsai et al., 2026).
Various approaches such as affiliate marketing, online advertising, content marketing, search engine marketing
(SEM), mobile marketing, email marketing, social media marketing, search engine optimisation (SEO), and analytics
have become the primary tools for effectively reaching consumers (Almoqbel et al., 2025). Its characteristics-which
include a broad yet segmented reach, cost-efficiency, high interactivity, and the ability to measure performance in the
real timefundamentally distinguish digital marketing from traditional marketing (Wu et al., 2025).
Aerostreet is an Indonesian local fashion brand offering  a diverse range of products, from casual footwear to
various other fashion lines. The brand has successfully established itself as a provider of competitively priced, high-
quality products, enabling it to reach a broad market segment and compete amidst the dominance of global brands
(Rusdianto & Saputro, 2025). However, the increasingly competitive dynamics of the digital market require Aerostreet
not only to rely on product excellence but also to optimise adaptive and integrated marketing communication strategies.
Failure to respond appropriately to changes in consumer behaviour risks reducing sales performance and weakening the
brand’s market position (Ismaeel et al., 2025). Therefore, the implementation of integrated marketing communication
(IMC) is crucial as a strategic approach to integrating various communication channels in order to enhance the
effectiveness of marketing messages, strengthen brand equity, and drive sales growth (Butkouskaya et al., 2024).
A previous study has examined the application of IMC in the context of digital marketing, particularly via social
media. Rosita et al. (2023) demonstrated that the implementation of IMC for the Erigo brand via Instagram contributed to
strengthening the brand image and supporting the achievement of marketing objectives. These findings are in line with
those of Pebrianti & Nuswantari (2024), who found that IMC activities on Pelita Air’s Instagram were able to create
value and increase the company’s profits through various interactions built with the audience. Meanwhile, Wirobuwono
& Wahid
(2024) emphasise that a TikTok-based digital marketing strategy, through promotional campaigns, can drive a significant
increase in sales, particularly through the use of interactive short-form video content. Recent studies also reinforce the
importance of integrated marketing communication in the digital age. However, research by Butarbutar et al. (2023)
indicates that the strategic implementation of integrated IMC can enhance brand performance through message
consistency and synergy across communication channels. Furthermore, the findings of Da Silva et al. (2025) emphasise
that the success of IMC in a digital environment is heavily influenced by a company’s ability to manage the customer
experience in a unified manner across various touchpoints. Another study by Kushwaha et al. (2020) also found that the
use of social media as part of an integrated communication strategy has a positive impact on customer engagement and
purchasing decisions.
Nevertheless, various previous studies still reveal a number of conceptual and empirical limitations. Most studies
tend to focus on the use of a single social media platform in isolation or within the context of global brands, and thus do
not fully represent cross-platform integration. Furthermore, the approaches used are generally tactical in nature,
emphasising short-term indicators such as engagement and sales growth, without thoroughly examining the strategic
synergy between communication channels. Yet, as emphasised by Da Silva et al. (2025), IMC emphasises holistic
integration, message consistency, and the creation of a sustainable brand experience across various consumer
touchpoints.
In Indonesia, research on the implementation of IMC for local brands, particularly those utilising social media
platforms such as Instagram and TikTok simultaneously, remains relatively limited. Most previous studies have not
adequately accounted for domestic market dynamics and the characteristics of local audiences, particularly Generation Z,
who have a strong preference for visual content and short videos. This situation highlights a research gap, namely the
suboptimal exploration of cross-platform marketing communication strategy integration within the context of local
brands operating in a dynamic digital ecosystem. Therefor e, this study offers a novel approach by analysing the
implementation of IMC for the Aerostreet brand through the optimisation of Instagram and TikTok as part of an
integrated marketing
communication strategy, aimed at strengthening brand image whilst enhancing sales performance.
This study employs an exploratory qualitative approach to gain a deeper and more contextual understanding of IMC
practices. This approach allows for analysis not only of the visible forms of marketing communication but also of the
meaning, strategies, and integration between the communication elements utilised by the brand. Based on this, the
research question is: how is IMC implemented in the promotion of Aerostreet products on digital platforms?. This study
aims to identify and analyse IMC elements on Instagram and TikTok, as well as evaluate their integration in shaping
effective digital promotion strategies. In practical terms, this study is expected to provide strategic recommendations for

business practitioners in optimising IMC integration to enhance reach, engagement, and consumer loyalty. Theoretically,
this study contributes to enriching the study of marketing communication, particularly regarding the implementation of
IMC by local brands within a dynamic digital ecosystem.
Methods
This study utilizes a qualitative descriptive approach supplemented by observation and purposive interviews. According
to
Moleong, as cited in Malahati et al. (2023), observation involves the examination of various events or phenomena related
to the research objectives, to be conducted through direct observation of Aerostreet’s promotional activities across
various communication channels, such as social media, advertisements, and marketing events. Meanwhile, interviews
involve data collection through questions focused on a questionnaire prepared by the researcher and approved by relevant
experts. Primary data collection techniques involve in-depth purposive interviews conducted with primary sources
involved in the promotion and sales process, this study involved a total of 4 informants from Aerostreet : the Brand
Director, Marketing Team, CBO Secretary, and Social Media Team Manager. Each interview session lasted
approximately 15 to 20 minutes to explore the implementation of IMC, as well as interviews with consumers to
investigate the extent to which they recognise and understand the Aerostreet brand. Meanwhile, secondary data was
obtained from observations of social media activity and promotional campaigns.
Data analysis, as outlined by Miles & Huberman in Saleh (2023), comprises data reduction, data presentation, and
drawing conclusions. The data reduction stage aims to filter and focus on relevant information from the interviews and
observations regarding the Aerostreet brand. Prioritised data includes the implementation of IMC as a promotional
medium via the social media platforms TikTok and Instagram for the Aerostreet brand among consumers, whilst
irrelevant data is set aside. Subsequently, the data presentation stage is carried out to organise the results of data
reduction into an easily understandable format. Data is presented by displaying the results of the analysis, which illustrate
the implementation of IMC as a promotional medium via the social media platforms TikTok and Instagram for the
Aerostreet brand. The final stage involves drawing conclusions, where the analysed findings are used to provide an
overview of the implementation of IMC as a promotional medium via the social media platforms TikTok and Instagram
for the Aerostreet brand, as well as the factors influencing its success, and recommendations for the development of
future marketing strategies.
Data credibility is etablished through triangulation techniques, including source triangulation, by cross-referencing
information from various data sources to ensure consistency and validity. Furthermore, there is methodological
triangulation, which utilises various data collection techniques such as observation, interviews, and documentation.
Finally, temporal triangulation is used to ensure the accuracy of the research results (Fikri et al., 2025). Through this
study, the researchers hope to provide new insights for creating more effective marketing communication strategies in the
future.
Results
Performance-Based Digital Ecosystem
The results of the study indicate that Aerostreet’s marketing communication practices are driven by a primary focus on
sales, fuelled by viral-based promotional strategies for digital content. In this context, marketing is no longer viewed
merely as the act of conveying a message, but rather as a mechanism for rapidly generating traffic, engagement and
conversions through widespread exposure on digital platforms. In terms of the target audience, the findings indicate that
the primary market being targeted is the teenage demographic, characterised by responsiveness to trends, price
sensitivity, and high levels of digital content consumption. This situation implies the adoption of a light-hearted, trend-
following, and generally informal communication style. Meanwhile, in terms of communication channels, marketing
activities are centred on digital platforms, particularly TikTok and Instagram, which function not only as channels for
message distribution but also as spaces for interaction and transactions.
The intensity of digital channel usage indicates that marketing communication takes place within a fully digitalised
ecosystem. In terms of timing, Aerostreet’s promotional activities are no longer periodic but occur continuously, adapting
to specific moments such as viral trends, twin-date campaigns, or payday periods. This signifies a shift towards a strategy
based on momentum and rapid responsiveness. The TikTok-based online sales promotion approach is driven by demands
for efficiency and effectiveness, where campaign performance can be directly measured through engagement, click-
through rate (CTR) and conversion rate (CVR). Engagement reflects the level of audience interaction with promotional
content, such as the number of likes, comments, shares, or other responses indicating consumer engagement. Meanwhile,
CTR indicates the percentage of users who click on a promotional link compared to the total number of users who view
the advert, thus serving as an indicator of the advert’s effectiveness in capturing the audience’s attention. CVR, on the
other hand, describes the percentage of consumers who take an action in line with the marketing objective after clicking

4142
the advert, such as making a purchase. Consequently, marketing decisions are increasingly determined by actual data
rather than rigid long-term planning.
Overall, the functioning of this ecosystem is experimental and iterative, as reflected in the informant’s statement:
“Technically, we’ve been doing it, but in practice it’s really a case of learning by doing as we shift from offline to
online.” (Brand Director)
The results of the observation reinforce these findings by showing that Aerostreet’s communication strategy is
implemented flexibly and is constantly being adjusted based on content performance and audience response on digital
platforms. Consequently, the marketing communication practices carried out can be understood as an iterative, data-
driven, and performance-oriented system.
Shift in Brand Identity and Promotional Direction
Our research findings further indicate that Aerostreet’s digital transformation has not only impacted its communication
strategy but has also led to a shift in its brand identity. Initially, Aerostreet was known as a local footwear brand with a
positioning that emphasised affordability and product functionality. However, this identity has shifted towards a brand
that is more closely aligned with digital culture, trends, and the lifestyles of today’s youth. The research findings indicate
that Aerostreet’s marketing communication practices have undergone a significant shift from offline channels to digital
channels. This shift is driven not only by changes in consumer behaviour but also by considerations of economic
efficiency in managing marketing activities. The findings indicate that offline marketing activities, such as setting up
booths in shopping centres and participating in events, require relatively high operational costs, yet these are not matched
by commensurate revenue levels. This situation has prompted the company to evaluate the effectiveness of offline sales
channels in supporting sales performance. This is reflected in the informant’s statement:
“Offline activities often end up being a money-losing… the costs simply don’t justify the results.” (Brand Director)
The decision to reduce the intensity of offline activities is based on the logic of efficiency and resource optimisation.
Digital platforms are seen as better able to deliver measurable results and performance at a lower cost, particularly
through data-driven digital promotional mechanisms. Nevertheless, the research findings also indicate that offline sales
activities have not been entirely abandoned, but rather have undergone a repositioning of their function; in practice,
offline channels are still utilised selectively, particularly to support specific strategies such as selling products via flash
sales to reduce
unsold stock in warehouses, and as a means of introducing new products to the public.
This shift is evident in the direction of promotions (where the promotion is heading), which increasingly
emphasises entertainment-driven content, virality, and audience emotional engagement, rather than merely conveying
product information. Findings from interviews and social media observations indicate that TikTok has become the
dominant channel for driving sales conversions through live commerce, affiliate marketing, and performance-based
advertising.
“Right now… TikTok is the most effective.” (Brand Director)
“TikTok is quite influential… live sales are also consistent on TikTok.” (CBO Secretary)
Findings indicate that promotional activities are not only managed internally by the marketing team but also involve
various external parties such as affiliates, content creators, and key opinion leaders (KOLs). This signifies a
decentralisation of communication, where brand messaging is no longer entirely controlled by the company but is also
shaped by the digital creator ecosystem. This finding is reinforced by observations showing that Aerostreet actively
utilises TikTok features such as live streaming, the shopping cart, and trend-based content to drive direct purchases.
Conversely, Instagram is primarily used as a medium for brand image building and engagement:
“Instagram… is for branding… whereas for commercial purposes, it’s more about TikTok.” (Brand Director)
Through observation, it is evident that content on Instagram tends to be more visually curated, focusing on product
aesthetics, storytelling, and audience interaction via comments and the Stories feature. This differentiation indicates a
clear division of functions, yet it has not yet been fully accompanied by cross-channel narrative integration, thereby
creating room for fragmentation in the brand experience.
Role Integration within the Digital Ecosystem
A synthesis of the research findings indicates that the implementation of marketing communications for the Aerostreet
brand has evolved into a system involving the integration of various roles-both internal and external-within a single
performance-based digital ecosystem. In terms of media structure, TikTok was found to dominate as the central hub for
communication orchestration, integrating communication, distribution and transactional functions within a single
platform. Meanwhile, other channels such as Instagram play a role in building brand image and emotional connection
with the audience. From a communication strategy perspective, the practices employed are dominated by data-driven
advertising and sales promotion, such as discounts, double-date campaigns, live streaming, and affiliate marketing:
“We use Ads, live streams, affiliate marketing… vouchers, and discounts too.” (CBO Secretary)
“Bundling… payday offers, double-date campaigns.” (Marketing Team)

Conversely, other communication functions such as public relations (PR), personal selling, and direct marketing
remain relatively limited and are not yet systematically structured:
“PR isn’t 100 per cent… it’s more about collaboration.” (Brand Director)
“There’s no urgency for personal selling yet.” (Brand Director)
From a decision-making perspective, all these communication activities are heavily influenced by performance data
and digital trends:
“There’s CTR, CVR, engagement…” (Brand Director)
“Content changes every day… different days, different stories.” (CBO Secretary)
“Whatever’s trending… that’s what we use.” (Content Manager)
This situation indicates that the communication integration currently taking place remains at an operational level,
but has not yet fully achieved strategic integration that unifies the brand’s message, meaning and experience in a more
consistent manner.
Discussion
Research findings indicate that digital platforms are considered more efficient because companies can monitor consumer
behaviour in real time and adjust their communication strategies based on the performance of the content produced.
However, Salim et al. (2022); Osorio-Andrade et al. (2025); Wu et al. (2025) state that marketing communication now
functions not only as a sales tool, but also as a mechanism for building brand equity. Consequently, the dominance of a
performance-oriented approach in this case indicates a reduction in the functions of IMC, shifting from its original sales
and branding focus to a greater emphasis on commercial dimensions. Nevertheless, the functional differentiation between
TikTok as a commercial channel and Instagram as a ‘branding’ medium has revealed fragmentation in marketing
communication practices. Multi-channel usage should be accompanied by consistency in messaging and experience
across touchpoints (Rehman et al., 2022). Meanwhile, at Aerostreet, the integration remains coordinative rather than
integrative. This means that each channel operates according to its own logic and objectives, without any orchestration of
a unified brand narrative.
Aerostreet’s decision to cease offline activities reflects the dominance of efficiency-driven logic in its marketing
communication strategy. Digital channels are prioritised as they can deliver faster, measurable, and scalable performance.
This approach aligns with digitalisation trends that emphasise efficiency and real-time data-driven optimisation
(OsorioAndrade et al., 2025). However, previous research indicates that a holistic customer experience, including offline
interactions, also plays a crucial role in building an emotional connection with the brand (Salim et al., 2022). The
findings of this study show that the implementation of Aerostreet’s integrated marketing communication (IMC) has
undergone a fundamental transformation triggered by the in efficiencies of an offline-based marketing communication
approach. These findings are reinforced by the results of an interview with the Brand Director, who stated that a number
of regular monthly events held throughout 2024 to 2025 tended to show a trend of 20–30% losses on the total event
organisation costs.
Changing preferences have prompted Aerostreet to adjust its marketing communication strategy by shifting the
focus towards a more adaptive, measurable approach based on digital platforms that are more adaptable and
performanceoriented. This is underpinned by the Brand Director’s statement revealing that sales on online platforms over
a single day equate to the transaction value of offline event sales over a week. Furthermore, digital promotion costs have
been shown to increase monthly sales performance by 300%. These research findings are supported by the findings of
Ginting & Harahap (2022), who state that digital marketing communication carried out through the integration of
promotional elements creates message consistency whilst strengthening Aerostreet’s brand image, which ultimately
boosts sales performance.
Safitri et al. (2022) explain that in IMC, marketing communication strategy is understood as the process of
integrating various promotional elements, such as advertising, sales promotion, public relations, and personal selling,
which are carried out in a coordinated manner to create message consistency whilst strengthening the brand image in a
unified way. Thus, sales promotion remains superior for fashion products, particularly for brands such as Zara, as studied
by Liang (2024), and Uniqlo by Yu & Yuenyong (2024); for example, through offline sales promotion involving the
direct distribution of discount vouchers and vouchers during events, which enhance brand image and public relations-
elements of IMC that Aerostreet is now beginning to abandon. However, research findings on Aerostreet indicate a shift
in IMC implementation towards a more digital and ecosystem-based approach.
The communication strategies implemented no longer merely emphasise coordination between promotional tools,
but also rely on cross-platform integration, the utilisation of audience data, and real-time responses to consumer
interactions. This situation indicates that IMC practices in the digital era are evolving to become more flexible and
dynamic, as companies are required to constantly adapt their communication strategies based on content performance,
changes in audience behaviour, and rapid market trends. Conceptually, this shift represents a transition from tactical
integration to strategic integration, consistent with the findings of Kushwaha et al. (2020), but with distinct digital
characteristics, namely the dominance of algorithmic logic and performance metrics.

The research findings also identified that the IMC strategy for the Aerostreet brand is dominated by a performance
marketing approach, which includes the use of (Butkouskaya et al., 2024): platform-based paid advertising, live
commerce, affiliate marketing, momentum-based promotional campaigns (flash sales, double dates, payday). The
domination of TikTok as the primary channel, serving both as a communication medium and a transactional space,
demonstrates a strong orientation towards performance-oriented communication. These results align with developments
in digital marketing that emphasise the use of metrics such as CTR, CVR, and engagement rate as the basis for
decisionmaking (Wu et al., 2025). In line with this, Aerostreet’s Brand Director revealed that the company’s digital
campaign performance showed a CTR of around 4.8%, a CVR of 3.1% and an engagement rate of 9.4%. These
achievements indicate that the digital communication strategy implemented is not only effective in capturing the
audience’s attention, but also in fostering consumer engagement whilst directly driving purchasing decisions.
Research findings indicate that Aerostreet’s marketing communication strategy possesses a higher level of
adaptability to the dynamics of digital trends and the logic of platform algorithms. Aerostreet’s adaptability is viewed as
a key source of competitive advantage, as it enables the company to respond directly to market changes and maximise
every opportunity for visibility and interaction with the audience. Consequently, IMC at Aerostreet no longer operates
within the framework of traditional communication integrati on; rather, it has evolved into a performance-based digital
communication ecosystem that is more adaptive, efficient, and output-oriented, although it still faces challenges in
achieving fully integrated strategic alignment.
Conclusion
This study shows that the implementation of integrated marketing communication (IMC) for the Aerostreet brand has
evolved into a performance-oriented marketing communication system based on a digital ecosystem. The communication
strategy is dominated by digital channels, particularly the TikTok platform, with a primary focus on achieving
conversions and sales through a data-driven approach and adaptation to trends. Although various social media platforms
are utilised for simultaneous communication, the integration remains operational in nature and has not yet fully unified
the brand’s messages and meaning consistently. This situation reflects a divergence in perspectives between a short-term
performance orientation and the need to build a long-term brand identity.
Based on the research findings, although there is currently a shift in IMC activities towards online platforms, the
researchers’ analysis suggests that the use of certain offline strategies, such as sales promotions, is still considered
effective for engaging consumers with the brand. Consequently, the shift in Aerostreet’s preference towards digital
promotion is not expected to directly undermine these offlin e-based IMC approaches. Furthermore, a balance is required
between datadriven approaches to drive conversions and efforts to build sustainable brand equity.
