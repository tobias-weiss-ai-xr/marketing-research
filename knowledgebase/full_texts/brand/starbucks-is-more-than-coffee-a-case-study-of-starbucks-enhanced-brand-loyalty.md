Abstract
Starbucks is more than just a coffee; it is the brand that provides an experience not just to stimulate our brain
cells but to also rejuvenate our hearts and soul. The brand ensures that the aroma of freshly ground coffee
beans is potent enough to elicit a sensory reaction from its customers. The pleasant lighting and the green
and yellow store interiors create visuals that are a sight to sore eyes. The ‘sound of Starbucks’ is the soothing
music; that ears will fall for. The texture and comfort of the armchairs offered in every Starbucks store mixed
with the pleasant environment are highly tranquilizing. The store even stopped serving breakfast with eggs
and cheese sandwiches because that would alter the smell of fresh coffee. Starbucks uses a sensory marketing
model to give customers the authentic Starbucks experience that shapes up customers’ emotions and memories
and builds everlasting personal relationships with them. The purpose of this research paper is to examine the
relationship between Sensory Marketing strategy and psychological tricks the brand uses to attract customers.
Surprisingly, many other covert psychological factors shape the success of the brand. These factors are, how-
ever, latent and are being performed by the customers naively without realizing that the brand is compelling
them to demonstrate specific buying behavior. Decoy Effect, FOMO (Fear of Missing out), Cheerleader effect
or Combo, Cashless effect i.e., Menu without Dollar Sign, Charm Pricing, and Endowment Effect are some of
the major Psychological Tricks that Starbucks Uses to Market “Their Coffee Brand” and do a thriving busi -
ness. The market landscape has no longer confined itself to the same pack of marketing strategies adopted by
companies it requires continuous innovation to withstand today’ s competitive world. Companies are spending
billions of dollars to understand the psychology and behaviour of their customers.  Marketing in the current
times is perceived through senses and psychology which are way beyond other conventional tools. The study
will use structure equational modelling to investigate the relationship between sensory marketing strategy
constructs and the concept of brand psychological tricks based on data collected from 422 Starbuck custom -
ers across UAE, India, UK, Oman, Australia, and Malaysia. The findings will offer additional insights into
the food and beverages business managers have the right kind of strategy which is most impactful for their
business. The multi-sensory brand experience and the concept of psychological marketing tricks can be well
understood. This research opens opportunities for managers to identify emotional/psychological linkages in
differentiating, distinguishing, and positioning a brand as an image in the human mind. This research opens
opportunities for managers to identify emotional/psychological linkages in differentiating, distinguishing, and
positioning a brand as an image in the human mind.

Introduction and Background
Sensory marketing strategies, such as the use of aromas, music,
and visual elements in Starbucks stores, aim to engage consum-
ers' senses and create a unique experience. These sensory cues
evoke positive emotions and enhance the overall brand experi -
ence for customers. Psychological brand tricks, such as brand
storytelling and emotional appeals in marketing campaigns, in -
fluence consumers' perception of the Starbucks brand, creating a
sense of connection and loyalty. For instance, Starbucks empha-
sizes its commitment to sustainability and ethical sourcing, ap -
pealing to consumers' values and creating a positive brand image
[1]. By appealing to sight, smell, touch, and sound, Starbucks
creates a sensory-rich environment that enhances the overall
customer experience and contributes to brand loyalty [2].
Consumer buying behaviour also plays a crucial role in enhanc-
ing Starbucks brand loyalty. The Theory of Experiential Market-
ing suggests that when consumers have positive and memorable
experiences with a brand, they are more likely to develop an
emotional attachment and become loyal customers. Starbucks
has successfully created a unique and immersive coffee shop ex-
perience, encouraging customers to spend time in their stores,
try new products, and develop a sense of belonging to the Star -
bucks community [3]. Researchers can assess the level of brand
loyalty towards Starbucks by examining factors such as repeat
purchase behaviour, frequency of visits, and customer retention
rates [4].
By employing sensory marketing strategies, psychological brand
tricks, and understanding consumer buying behavior, Starbucks
has been able to cultivate a loyal customer base. The Theory of
Experiential Marketing explains how creating memorable and
positive experiences, appealing to the senses, and connecting
emotionally with customers contribute to enhanced brand loy -
alty. Starbucks' attention to sensory details, such as the aroma of
coffee, visual appeal, music selection, and comfortable store am-
biance, all contribute to a sensory-rich experience that enhances
customers' overall brand experience and fosters loyalty [5].
In addition to sensory marketing strategies, researchers can ex -
plore customer associations with the Starbucks brand, including
quality, sustainability, convenience, and social responsibility.
Positive brand associations enhance customer loyalty by cre -
ating a favourable brand image and influencing perceptions of
offerings. Perceived quality, brand loyalty, brand image, and
brand trust are dimensions that researchers can examine to gain
insights into customer loyalty and decision-making [6]. Under -
standing these dimensions of brand equity can help Starbucks
and other companies in the coffee industry develop effective
strategies to enhance brand loyalty and strengthen customer re -
lationships.
By creating positive experiences, appealing to the senses, and
building a strong brand image, Starbucks has successfully built
a loyal customer base and established itself as a leader in the cof-
fee industry. By examining these dimensions of brand equity in
the research study, researchers can gain a deeper understanding
of how they influence customer loyalty and decision-making.
This knowledge can help Starbucks and other companies in the
coffee industry to develop effective strategies to enhance brand
equity and strengthen customer relationships [7].
Research Statement
The research aims to explore the concept of brand loyalty in the
context of Starbucks, going beyond its association with coffee.
The study will investigate how Starbucks has successfully en -
hanced brand loyalty among its customers, examining the strat -
egies and factors that contribute to this phenomenon. By under-
standing the drivers of enhanced brand loyalty, this research,
seeks to provide insights into building and sustaining strong
customer loyalty in the competitive market.
Research Scope
The scope of this research will focus on Starbucks as a case
study to gain a comprehensive understanding of how the compa-
ny has managed to go beyond its core product of coffee and cre-
ate a strong brand loyalty among its customers. The study will
examine various aspects, including the brand identity, customer
experience, marketing strategies, and customer perceptions. It
will also consider the impact of Starbucks' social responsibility
initiatives and the role of technology in enhancing brand loyalty.
The research will primarily be based on qualitative data analysis,
including interviews, surveys, and content analysis.

Literature Review
In the research study on "Starbucks is more than Coffee," var -
ious dimensions of brand equity were explored to understand
their influence on customer loyalty and decision-making. Brand
awareness, which refers to the extent to which customers are
familiar with and recognize the Starbucks brand, plays a signif -
icant role in influencing customer loyalty. Higher brand aware -
ness can lead to increased customer loyalty as it creates a sense
of familiarity and trust. Brand associations, which are the mental
connections and perceptions that customers have about a brand,
also contribute to brand loyalty. The aroma of freshly brewed
coffee, the visual appeal of carefully crafted beverages, the tac -
tile experience of holding a Starbucks cup, and the ambient mu-
sic in stores all contribute to a sensory-rich environment. These
elements create positive associations and enhance the overall
customer experience, which can lead to increased brand loyalty
[8].
Sensory marketing strategies, psychological brand tricks, and
consumer buying behavior are key factors that influence brand
loyalty for Starbucks. Sensory marketing involves engaging
consumers' senses to create a memorable and immersive brand
experience. Starbucks excels in this aspect by appealing to mul-

Methodology
Data Collection
To ensure robust and valid findings, this research employed
both primary and secondary data collection methods. Primary
data was gathered through a carefully designed questionnaire
distributed via email and WhatsApp, targeting specific sample
groups in the UAE, India, Malaysia, Oman, Australia, and the
UK. The survey, hosted on Google Forms, ensured valid and
relevant questions aligned with the research objectives. Sec -
ondary data collection involved consulting online resources
like websites, journal articles, research papers, and books. This
enriched the literature review, strengthened the analysis of spe -
cific concepts, and guaranteed that all aspects of the conceptual
model were thoroughly explored [16].
Data Analysis
This research adopted a quantitative approach, utilizing a ques -
tionnaire to gather data. The findings are presented through
visually appealing tabulated data, displaying accurate percent -
ages and statistical data for easy interpretation. This technique
offered several advantages: first, it enabled the researcher to
gather opinions from a wider sample size, enhancing the gen -
eralizability of the results, and thus, boosting the study's cred -
ibility.
Second, data collection was swift and efficient, with immedi -
ate responses received through online platforms like Google
Forms. However, a limitation inherent to quantitative ques -
tionnaires is the lack of in-depth participant feedback or expla -
nations behind their choices due to the nature of pre-defined,
closed-ended questions. As such, while quantitative research
provides valuable insights and broad-scale data, it may not yield
nuanced understanding of underlying motivations or individual
perspectives [17].
Sample Size
Due to time constraints and COVID-19 restrictions affecting
participant outreach, this research employed a sample size of
422 individuals. While this falls slightly above the 385 rec -
ommended by the Rao soft sample size calculator for a 90%
confidence level, 10% margin of error, and a population size of
20,000 with 50% response distribution, it still provides valid
and statistically significant results. The full questionnaire used

Conclusion and recommendation
Implications of this Research
The research study "Starbucks is more than coffee: a case study
of Starbucks enhanced brand loyalty" has important implica -
tions for both academic and practical realms. The findings of
the study provide valuable insights into the factors that contrib -
ute to enhanced brand loyalty and shed light on the strategies
employed by Starbucks to achieve this. Firstly, the study high -
lights the significance of brand experience in fostering brand
loyalty. It demonstrates that Starbucks has successfully creat -
ed a unique and immersive experience for its customers, go -
ing beyond the product itself. This implies that organizations
can enhance brand loyalty by focusing on creating a holistic,
customer-centric experience that goes beyond the functional
attributes of the product or service. Secondly, the study under -
scores the importance of customer engagement and relationship
building. Starbucks has effectively utilized various channels,
such as social media and loyalty programs, to engage with cus -
tomers and cultivate a sense of community. This suggests that
organizations can strengthen brand loyalty by actively engaging
with customers, listening to their feedback, and creating op -
portunities for interaction and participation [24]. Moreover, the
research highlights the role of corporate social responsibility
(CSR) in enhancing brand loyalty. Starbucks' commitment to
ethical sourcing, environmental sustainability, and community
involvement has resonated with customers and contributed to
their loyalty. This implies that organizations can build stronger
brand loyalty by aligning their values and actions with social
and environmental causes. Additionally, the study emphasiz -
es the importance of consistent branding and brand image.
Starbucks has successfully maintained a strong and consistent
brand identity, which has contributed to customer loyalty. This
suggests that organizations should invest in building and man -
aging a strong brand image that aligns with their target audi -
ence and values [25].
Practical Implications
Practically, the findings of this research have implications for
marketing and branding strategies. Organizations can learn
from Starbucks' success in creating a differentiated and immer -
