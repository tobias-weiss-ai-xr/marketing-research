Abstract. With the continuous development of subculture, the value of IP has gradually
changed from a single work to a diversified product and content ecology, so that the cultural
significance, brand identity and symbol capital carried by IP can be continuously
accumulated. This paper takes the three core IPS of Mobile Suit Gundam, Kamen Rider and
Dragon Ball under Bandai as the research object, discusses how subcultural communities
promote the capital appreciation of IP symbols through circle recognition, fan participation
and consumption behavior, and further analyzes the impact of brand building and brand trust
on the long-term value operation of IP. Using the method of literature analysis and multi
case analysis, the study focuses on the sub cultural community, circle identity, brand
building, consumer purchase intention and brand trust, to analyze how consumers form their
identity with IP and brand in the process of long-term content contact, community
interaction and symbol consumption. The research aims to sort out the value-added path of
Bandai IP from content dissemination, community identity to product consumption and
brand value accumulation, and further explore how enterprises can strengthen consumer
stickiness through continuous IP operation, realize the long-term transformation of IP
cultural value and commercial value, and provide reference for the continuous operation and
value promotion of IP brands in the context of subculture.
Keywords: Subculture, IP value-added, circle consumption, community marketing
1. Introduction
This study focuses on the development of global cultural industry and IP economy. Animation IP
has gradually changed from a single content output to a comprehensive cultural brand with multi-
cultural ecology such as games, toys, films and exhibitions. Especially in Japan, the animation
industry has been an important part of the cultural industry and has formed a complete commercial
chain. For animation IP, it has long valued the quality of works, but it also valued the cultural
identity and consumer behavior formed by the fan community. After decades of playing classic IP
such as Mobile Suit Gundam, Kamen Rider and Dragon Ball, it still maintained high commercial
value, continued to launch new dramas and products, held exhibitions and strengthened the identity
of fans through online communities, so that IP gradually evolved into a symbol asset with cultural

2. Literature review
In recent years, the path of capital appreciation of IP symbols under the sub cultural community has
gradually become a hot issue in the field of marketing and cultural industry. With the continuous
development of secondary meta culture, special photo culture and trendy play culture, the value of
IP has not only come from the work itself but also depends more on the symbolic capital formed by
fan community, brand operation and cultural identity. This paper aims to sort out the relevant
literature on Subculture community, symbolic capital, IP value-added path and brand building, and
provide a theoretical basis for subsequent research.
First, in the study of subculture community and circle identity, some studies Pointed out that
ACG fans are first fascinated by a specific text in the ACG cultural circle through their personal
viewing behavior. This fascination has turned from unconsciousness to consciousness. In this
process, fans actively seek ways to seek self-identity and begin to understand the wider ACG
cultural circle. Finally, fans will participate in the fan community, become deep readers of ACG
cultural circle, and build a complete identity. In the process of building self-fans' identity, fans'
initiative has been reflected. ACG fan community also has long-term vitality and development
potential due to the initiative of fans [2]. This kind of circle recognition not only enhances the
stickiness of fans, but also further improves brand loyalty, providing a stable foundation for the
long-term operation of IP.
Seungyeon Ha & Seongcheol Kim point out, in symbolic consumption contexts such as fandom
and collectibles, merchandise value also encompasses emotional and symbolic meanings, reflecting
enjoyment, attachment, and identity expression beyond utilitarian or quality-based evaluations [3]. It
can be seen that in the IP consumption scenario, commodity value has broken through the evaluation
dimension of practicality and quality, and emotion and symbolic connotation have become the
driving forces of IP value growth that cannot be ignored. IP therefore constantly realizes the
appreciation of symbolic capital.
In addition, existing studies suggest that, "Firstly, by collaborating with multiple brands and
platforms, IP can expand its influence and further enhance user stickiness and activity through the
fan economy. Secondly, promptly responding to market feedback and utilizing social media

3. Research method
Existing literature shows that the value of cultural and entertainment intellectual property is not only
limited to its functional or commercial characteristics, but also closely related to symbolic meaning,
community relations and consumer identity. Pronay and Hetesi argue that symbolic consumption is
based on the assumption that consumption is more than functional problem solving, because
products and brands have significant meanings and can be utilised as symbols within a cultural
ecosystem [5]. This view is very important for entertainment IP. Products derived from IP, such as
hand-made products, models, cards and character peripherals, may carry cultural significance
beyond their practical functions. Therefore, the value of IP products stems in part from the common
meaning given to them by consumers and communities.
The important thing is, propose that the symbolic meaning of a brand should be examined within
the context of specific brand communities rather than in general. They further distinguish different
types of brand communities according to their main cohesive force, with brand subcultures
representing communities in which members are more committed to each other than to the brand
itself .This provides a useful theoretical basis for investigating the subculture community around
Mobile Suit Gundam, Kamen Rider and Dragon Ball [5]. In these communities, shared knowledge,
cultural references, consumption practices and community participation may have an impact on the
symbolic meaning of these intellectual property rights "Brand community" further explains how
these common meanings form social organizations [6]. Define a brand community as a specialised,
non-geographically bound community based on a structured set of social relationships among
admirers of a brand. They identify shared consciousness, rituals and traditions, and a sense of moral
responsibility as key characteristics of brand communities. These characteristics show that repeated
participation in activities related to virtual IP can transform individual consumption into collective
response, thereby enhancing community identity.
In addition, McAlexander, Schouten, and Koenig conceptualise brand community as a network of
relationships involving the customer, brand, firm, product, and other customers, and argue that
marketers can strengthen brand communities by facilitating shared customer experiences It shows

4. Research results
The value-added of Bandai's main intellectual property (IP) is not only realized through enterprise
marketing and product development. On the contrary, IP value is continuously built through the
interaction between enterprise management and fans. Recent research on online brand communities
shows that value creation can be generated through the interaction between consumers and between
consumers and brands, meaning that consumers can also actively participate in value creation rather
than remaining passive recipients of corporate marketing Consumer and brand value formation,
value creation and co-creation in social media brand communities This view is applicable to works
such as Mobile Suit Gundam, Kamen Rider and Dragon Ball. In these works, products, media
content and the fan community interact, thus expanding the symbolic value of the IP [8].
4.1. Mobile Suit Gundam
Gundam mobile warrior presents a product oriented IP value creation model. Bandai extended the IP
from the original animation content to Gundam models, games, activities and other derivatives.
However, the value of these products is not limited to their functional uses. Taking Gundam model
as an example, it requires consumers to assemble, refit, collect and display, thus creating
opportunities for continuous interaction between consumers and IP.
Transform consumers from passive buyers to active participants. Found that brand community
value can encourage consumers to participate in value co-creation, particularly when consumers
perceive social and experiential value within the community [9]. Therefore, GAODA's value
creation can be understood as such a process: enterprise product development provides a material
basis for fans' participation, while fans' activities further enhance the cultural and symbolic
significance of products.
4.2. Kamen Rider
Kamen Rider shows another form of intellectual property value appreciation, which is mainly
related to the long-term continuity of the brand, consumers' memory and brand identity. Unlike
short-term entertainment products, Kamen Rider continues to launch new series, new characters and
new products while maintaining the connection with historical IP identity. This enables the company
to attract new consumers and retain existing fans. Who found that consumers' perceptions of brand
heritage are influenced by the continuity between a brand's past and present, while changes that
disrupt perceived continuity can weaken perceptions of brand heritage. The importance of
continuity is supported [10]. This is particularly important for the IP of Kamen Rider. Old fans can
connect new products with childhood memories, while young consumers can establish a new sense
of identity with contemporary characters.
4.3. Dragon Ball
Dragon Ball shows a more media focused and globally distributed IP value creation model. The IP
has been extended to the fields of animation, games, set exchange cards, competitions, handmade

4.4. Cross case discovery
These three cases show different IP value creation mechanisms, but they have a common process.
Enterprise management provides new products, media content and consumption opportunities, while
fans turn these resources into cultural significance through participation and community interaction.
Therefore, the overall path of IP value creation can be summarized as: "Enterprise IP
management → product/media extension → fan participation → community interaction → symbolic
meaning → long-term IP value" This shows that IP value is not only created by the company and
directly transferred to consumers, but is constantly reconstructed through the interaction between
corporate strategy and fan groups. Recent research on social media brand communities similarly
argues that value formation involves both brand–consumer and consumer–consumer interactions,
allowing consumers to actively participate in value creation [8].
5. Discussion
The results show that the creation of IP value stems from the interaction between Enterprise IP
management and fan community participation. Enterprises lay the foundation for IP development
through content, products, cross media expansion and marketing, while fans enhance the symbolic
value of IP through consumption, interaction and community participation. However, excessive
commercialization, product quality problems and the conflict between corporate decisions and fans'
expectations may weaken community participation and have a negative impact on the long-term
value of IP. Therefore, while maintaining the continuity and identity of mature IP, enterprises need to
strike a balance between commercial development and the cultural expectations of fans. Overall,
these three cases show that the creation of IP value is a continuous process: enterprise management
→ fans' participation → community interaction → symbolic value → long-term IP value, in which
fans' feedback will also affect the subsequent enterprise strategy. In the future, IP management
should focus on sustainable development and combine business innovation with product quality,
cultural continuity and meaningful fan participation. Future research can also compare more
entertainment IP and enterprises to explore whether this mode of value creation by enterprises and
fans has wider applicability.
6. Conclusion
Based on the analysis of the three core IPS of Bandai, including Bandai, masked knight and
Longzhu, it can be found that the continuous appreciation of IP value does not simply depend on the
original content or the number of derivative products, but is based on the joint effect of the long-
term operation of the enterprise and the stable development of the subculture community. First,
through continuous content marketing, product development, cross media communication, offline
activities and brand building, enterprises maintain a high degree of market exposure and cultural

activity of IP and continue to extend the life cycle of IP through different forms of products and
consumption scenarios. In this process, the enterprise not only provides physical goods, but also
continues to strengthen the cultural symbols and brand awareness of IP, to promote the
transformation of IP from a single work to a brand asset with sustainable commercial value. Second,
the subculture community is an important foundation for the long-term accumulation of IP value.
GAODA, masked knight and dragon ball all have relatively stable fan groups. Consumers form
circle identity through watching works, purchasing products, collecting, communicating and
participating in offline activities, making IP gradually become an important symbol for consumers to
express interest, identity and cultural attribution. Therefore, the appreciation of IP is the result of the
interaction between enterprise operation ability and community identity: enterprises create
continuous value input through marketing and brand operation, while fan communities further
expand the cultural influence of IP through participation, dissemination and consumption, and
finally form the continuous accumulation of symbolic capital. But this value-added model also has
some drawbacks. With the increasing commercialization of IP, excessive reliance on new product
development, limited commodities and high-frequency marketing may lead to consumer fatigue and
weaken the original cultural value of IP. At the same time, the decline in product quality control,
high prices, the proliferation of piracy and the deterioration of the purchase experience may damage
consumers' trust in the brand. When the circle identity and brand trust formed by fans for a long time
are destroyed, the stability of the community will also decline, and further affect consumers'
purchase intention and IP life cycle. Therefore, the long-term appreciation of IP is not simply to
pursue the expansion of business scale, but to establish a long-term balance between the continuous
operation of enterprises, the stable identity of the community and brand trust, which is also an
important reason why the three core IP of Bandai can continue to maintain market value.
