Abstract
This study to analyze the influence of Customer Relationship Marketing and Brand Image
on Consumer Loyalty at Aulia Toothpick Chips in Jambi City. This study employed a
quantitative method involving 120 respondents selected using random sampling. Data were
collected via a questionnaire using a Likert scale and analyzed using multiple linear
regression. The results indicate that, collectively, C ustomer Relationship Marketing and
Brand Image have a significant effect on Consumer Loyalty, with a coefficient of
determination (R²) of 60.3%. The remaining 39.7% is influenced by other factors not
discussed in this study. Partially, Customer Relationship Management (CRM) has a positive
and significant effect on Consumer Loyalty. This is evidenced by the t -test, which shows a
significance value for the CRM variable of 0.001 < 0.05. Furthermore, Brand Image has a
significant effect on Consumer Loyalty. Mea nwhile, the results of the F -test, which showed
a significance value of 0.000 < 0.05.

Brand Image
Customer trust in a brand can be built through the influence of the brand image it self.
Brand image refers to how customers view the products being offered. It encompasses their
perceptions and views of those products. If a company offers high-quality products that meet
customer needs, customers’ views of the brand will naturally be positive. Brand image is a
response that often comes to mind when consumers think of a particular brand; therefore,
companies must create brand awareness for their pro ducts or services to attract consumers
by producing high-quality products that establish a positive brand image with characteristics
distinct from those of competitors —so that consumers recognize the unique value of the
company’s offerings (Putri et al., 2022).
Meanwhile, (Kotler et al., 2019) define brand image as a product or service possessing
characteristics that distinguish it from other products or services. Brand image distinguishes
it from other goods or services that aim to fulfill the same needs. (Kotler et al., 2019) explain
that brand image provides benefits to businesses in the following ways: (1) making product
handling or tracking easier; (2) helping to keep inventory records and accounting documents
organized; and (3) providing legal protection to businesses for features.
The following indicators, as stated by Kotler et al. (2019), help develop brand image
through brand associations:
1. Consumers’ memory of details and feelings toward a product after using it is known as
the favorability of brand association (favorability of brand association).
2. Customers’ perceptions of products they have used and found useful form the basis of the
strength of brand association.
3. The unique characteristics or differences that attract customers are known as brand
association idiosyncrasies.

Loyalty
Loyalty is when customers keep coming back and have a positive view of the company.
Customer loyalty is the primary focus of a company’s marketing strategy (Tjiptono et al.,
2017). Therefore, MSMEs need to create product s with their own unique characteristics,
useful value, and quality that can satisfy consumers. This will help companies compete and
build customer loyalty.
There are four distinct types of consumer loyalty, as described by (Kotler et al., 2019).
These incl ude: a) No loyalty, where customers rarely return to the same place —even a
second time. Generally, they constantly switch between locations; b) Apparent loyalty,
where buyers purchase products out of habit. Due to the element of habit. Usually, they use
specific products or have certain feelings associated with them. Consumers are unwilling to
switch to other retailers; c) Latent loyalty: Customers of this type are unlikely to make repeat
purchases. Situational factors influence whether someone buys something or not; d) Loyalty:
Consumers take great pride in the products they use. They even mention them to their
relatives and acquaintances.
The following indicators are used to measure the variables identified by (Rangkuti, 2020):
1. Behavioral metrics, which take into account consumers’ actual purchasing habits.
2. Substitution cost measurement (also known as switching cost measurement), which
measures brand loyalty by calculating the cost of switching brands.
3. Measuring consumer satisfaction, which specifically c ompares consumer loyalty to a
brand with customer happiness or dissatisfaction with that brand.
4. Measuring brand affinity, which specifically measures consumer loyalty in terms of brand
preference, trust, feelings of friendship, or respect.
5. Measuring commitment, which refers to how customers relate to the product.

(2023). The results of this study in dicate that there is a positive and significant
relationship between customer relationship marketing and customer loyalty.
2. Brand Image and Consumer Loyalty
 Brand Image has a significant influence on Consumer Loyalty. This is indicated by a

conducted by Karwini et al. (2021). The results of this study indicate that brand image

consumer loyalty toward Aulia Toothpick Chips.
