 The rapid development of social media has made political communication
increasingly strategic in enabling political figures to build their personal
branding through direct, interactive, and sustained engagement in the
digital sphere. This study aims to ana lyze public perceptions of Gus
Muhaimin’s political communication through social media in shaping his
political image. The research employed a descriptive -exploratory
qualitative approach within a constructivist paradigm using a case study
method. Data wer e collected through non -participant observation of Gus
Muhaimin’s social media content on Instagram, TikTok, X, and YouTube,
semi-structured interviews with informants selected using purposive and
snowball sampling techniques, and digital documentation. Da ta were
analyzed using the Miles and Huberman interactive model, including data
reduction, data display, and conclusion drawing, complemented by
thematic analysis. The findings indicate that public perceptions of Gus
Muhaimin’s political communication refl ect a consistent communication
strategy implemented across multiple social media platforms through the
integration of visual, verbal, and personal narrative elements, resulting in a
coherent political image. The most prominent communication elements
contributing to his personal branding include his religious -pesantren
identity, the high visibility of his digital content, and an empathetic image
toward ordinary citizens. Furthermore, visual narratives and storytelling
play a central role in shaping public pe rceptions by portraying him as a
religious, people -oriented, humanistic political figure who is closely
connected with younger generations. The study also reveals that this
communication strategy has considerable potential to strengthen emotional
engagement with digital audiences. However, it continues to face challenges
related to maintaining authenticity, ensuring consistency in the substance of
political messages, and addressing public sensitivity toward excessive
political image-building.

INTRODUCTION
The rapid development of social media has significantly transformed political
communication in Indonesia. Digital platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, X, and
YouTube enable political figures to establish more direct, interactive, and personalized
communication with the public than conventional media (McNair, 2003; Nasrullah,
2020). In this context, social media no longer functions merely as a channel for
disseminating political information but has become a strategic instrument for building
public image, strengthening political identity, and fostering emotional connections
with the public. Consequently, personal branding has become an essential aspect of

The urgency of this study has increased alongside the growing influence of

the dissemination of electronic word-of-mouth, all of which have implications for the
image and public perception of political candidates (Nurnisya & Kasmani, 2025) . At
the same time, social media requires authenticity, message consistency, and

represents an interesting political figure to investigate because he combines a strong
religious identity, extensive political experience, and a digital communication strategy
that targets diverse segments of society. His communication activities across multiple
social media platforms demonstrate consistent efforts to build a political image
through visual content, narratives, audience interaction, and the communication of
public policy issues (Putra & Rochmaniah, 2024).
This phenomenon indicates that the personal branding of political figures is not
developed spontaneously but rather through well-planned communication strategies.
The consistent use of visual content, storytelling, audience engagement, and the
management o f multiple social media platforms reflects the application of the
concepts of social media marketing, political communication, and personal branding
in constructing a political image (Fill, 2013; Kotler & Keller, 2016; Schultz et al., 1995).
Therefore, research on personal branding through social media is important for
understanding how digital communication strategies are employed to establish
political identity and maintain public trust.
Previous studies have demonstrated the significant role of social media in
political communication. Abid et al. (2025)  explained that political social media

(2025) found that social media marketing influences candidate image and voting
intention through content quality and message credibility. Meanwhile, Pratiwi et al.
(2025) emphasized that Instagram serves as a strategic platform for developing
political identity and emotional closeness with the public, whereas Nurnisya &
Kasmani (2025)  concluded that social media contributes to the development of
personal branding, increased public engagement, and the formation of public
sentiment during the 2024 Indonesian Presidential Election.

LITERATURE REVIEW
Integrated Marketing Communication Theory
Integrated Marketing Communication (IMC) is a communication concept that
emphasizes the importance of integrating multiple communication channels to deliver
consistent, clear, and effective messages to target audiences. Schultz et al. (1995)
explain that IMC coordinates all communication activities to establish a strong
organizational image and foster sustainable relationships with audiences. In the
context of this study, IMC provides the theoretical foundation for understanding how
various social media platforms are strategically integrated to develop the personal
branding of Gus Muhaimin Iskandar.

Political Communication Concept
Political communication refers to the process through which political actors
convey political messages to the public with the objective of enhancing public
understanding, influencing public opinion, and gaining political support. According
to (McNair, 2018), political communication encompasses all forms of communication
conducted by political actors, as well as communication concerning political activities
disseminated through various media. In this study, the concept of political
communication serves as the foundation for analyzing how Gus Muhaimin Iskandar
communicates political messages through social media.

Personal Branding Concept
Personal branding is the strategic process of developing and managing an
individual's identity, character, and reputation to establish a distinctive public image.
(Montoya & Vandehey, 2008) argue that effective personal branding is built upon
consistency, differentiation, and authenticity, enabling individuals to create a strong
and memorable perception in the minds of their audiences. This concept is employed

Political Communication Concept
In the digital era, political communication has evolved into a more interactive
communication process through the utilization of digital media and social networking
platforms. (Cangara, 2009) explains that political communication media function not
only as channels for disseminating information but also as instruments for building
political image, encouraging public participation, and strengthening relationships
between political actors and the public. Accordingly, this study considers social media
as a political communication space that facilitates the development of Gus Muhaimin
Iskandar's personal branding.

Political Marketing Communication Concept
Political marketing communication refers to the application of marketing
principles within political activities to build political image, communicate political
values, and establish relationships with voters.  Lees-Marshment (2001)  argues that
political marketing communication focuses on understanding public needs,
developing relevant political messages, and selecting appropriate communication
channels to enhance communication effectiveness. This concept is applied in the
present st udy to analyze Gus Muhaimin Iskandar's communication strategies in
fostering closer relationships with the public through social media.

Political Integrated Marketing Communication (Political IMC) Concept
Political Integrated Marketing Communication (Political IMC) refers to the
application of Integrated Marketing Communication principles within political
communication through the integration of messages, media, and communication
activities to establish a s trong and consistent political image. According to  (Ghiuta,
2009), Political IMC enables political actors to deliver coherent messages across
multiple communication channels, thereby enhancing credibility, public trust, and
long-term relationships with society. This concept provides the theoretical basis for
understanding the integration of digital communication strategies employed by Gus
Muhaimin Iskandar across various social media platforms.

interact with various forms of content in a rapid and interactive manner.  (Kaplan &

METHODS
This study employed a qualitative approach within a constructivist paradigm
using a case study method to gain an in -depth understanding of Gus Muhaimin
Iskandar's political communication strategy in developing his personal branding
through social media. Th e constructivist paradigm was adopted because it views
social reality as a product of meaning constructed through social interactions and
experiences, while the case study method enables researchers to investigate a
contemporary phenomenon comprehensively within its real-life context (Creswell &
Creswell, 2018). Accordingly, this study focused on Gus Muhaimin Iskandar's political
communication practices across Instagram, TikTok, X, and YouTube.
The research utilized both primary and secondary data sources. Primary data
were collected through non-participant observation of social media content and semi-
structured interviews with informants selected using purposive sampling, comprising
individuals who had experience accessing or interacting with Gus Muhaimin
Iskandar's social media content. The number of informants was determined based on
the principle of data saturation. Secondary data were obtained from digital
documentation, including social medi a posts, screenshots, online news articles, and
other relevant supporting documents. The research instruments consisted of
observation guidelines, interview protocols, and documentation forms designed to
ensure systematic data collection aligned with the research objectives (Patton, 2015).
Data were analyzed using the interactive model proposed by Miles and
Huberman, which consists of data reduction, data display, and conclusion drawing
and verification. Furthermore, thematic analysis was employed to identify key themes
related to social media marketing, political communication, and personal branding in
the construction of Gus Muhaimin Iskandar's political image. To ensure the
trustworthiness of the findings, data validity was established through source
triangulation and methodological triangulation by comparing evidence obtained from
observations, interviews, and documentation, thereby enhancing the credibility of the
research findings (Lincoln & Guba, 1985).

FINDINGS AND DISCUSSION
Findings
Assessment of Gus Muhaimin Iskandar's Political Communication through Social
Media in Building His Political Image
The interview findings indicate that the majority of informants perceived Gus
Muhaimin Iskandar's political communication across social media platforms,
including Instagram, TikTok, X, and YouTube, as being implemented consistently.
Informants from diverse  backgrounds, including youth, university students, santri,
Islamic teachers, private -sector employees, homemakers, micro, small, and medium
enterprise (MSME) owners, and civil servants, generally reported that although each
platform employed a distinct communication style, the core messages portraying Gus
Muhaimin as a religious figure, a young Nahdlatul Ulama leader, a politician who is
close to the public, and an advocate for younger generations were consistently
conveyed. The variation in communication styles across platforms was perceived as
an adaptation to platform characteristics without altering the central political identity
being projected.
In addition to message consistency, the findings reveal that the use of visual
symbols, recurring narratives, and platform -specific content formats strengthened
Gus Muhaimin's political image in the perception of the informants. Most participants
considered these communication strategies effective in establishing his image as a
humanistic, people -oriented, and easily recognizable political figure. Nevertheless,
several informants, particularly those from professional backgrounds, observed that
some content especially on TikTok placed greater emphasis on emotional appeal than
on policy substance. Overall, the findings suggest that Gus Muhaimin Iskandar's
political communication strategy has successfully established a consistent political
image, although greater emphasis on substa ntive policy -oriented content is still
needed to effectively engage more critical and rational audience segments.

The Role of Visual Branding and Storytelling in Shaping Public Perceptions
The interview findings indicate that visual branding and storytelling play a
significant role in shaping public perceptions of Gus Muhaimin Iskandar through
social media. Informants perceived that the use of religious symbols, Nahdlatul Ulama
and pesantren  identities, as well as narratives about his personal life and social
concern, reinforced Gus Muhaimin's image as a religious, humanistic, and people -
oriented political figure. However, several informants, particularly professionals and
university students, argued that visual branding and storytelling should be supported
by more substantive content to enhance public trust.

Challenges and Opportunities of Social Media Marketing, Politic, and Personal
Branding in Building the Personal Branding of Political Figures
The interview findings reveal that the implementation of Social Media
Marketing, Politic, and Personal Branding in developing Gus Muhaimin Iskandar's
personal branding faces several challenges, including maintaining message
consistency, preserving authenti city, and adapting communication strategies to
diverse audience characteristics. At the same time, informants identified opportunities
to strengthen personal branding through the reinforcement of his religious identity,
more human -centered communication, r elevant storytelling, and the integration of
digital communication with direct community engagement.

CONCLUSIONS
This study demonstrates that the Social Media Marketing, Politic, and Personal
Branding strategy implemented by Gus Muhaimin Iskandar across multiple social
media platforms has successfully established a consistent political image as a
religious, people-oriented leader who is closely connected with younger generations.
The integration of communication messages, consistency of narratives, and strategic
utilization of various digital platforms have strengthened an authentic and
recognizable personal brand. Th ese findings indicate that integrated digital political
communication plays a significant role in shaping public perceptions and the political
image of political figures in the era of social media.
The findings also reveal that Gus Muhaimin Iskandar's personal branding is
supported by the eight fundamental elements of personal branding, with
specialization, visibility, and goodwill emerging as the most dominant dimensions.
Furthermore, visual brandin g and storytelling contribute significantly to fostering
emotional engagement and reinforcing positive public perceptions, although their
effectiveness varies across audience segments. Religious communities and grassroots
audiences tend to respond more pos itively to emotionally oriented communication,
whereas professionals and university students place greater emphasis on content that
is substantive, evidence-based, and policy-oriented.
This study further identifies several challenges in implementing Social Media
Marketing, Politic, and Personal Branding, including maintaining message
consistency, preserving authenticity, creating content differentiation, and adapting
communication strate gies to the characteristics of diverse audience segments.
Nevertheless, substantial opportunities remain to strengthen personal branding
through more human-centered communication, the integration of digital and offline
communication, and the development of  more adaptive, substantive, and audience -
oriented content.
Based on these findings, future research is recommended to adopt quantitative
approaches to examine the influence of Social Media Marketing, Politic, and Personal
Branding on the personal branding of political figures by employing methods such as
Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) or large -scale survey research. From a practical
perspective, political actors and communication teams should maintain message
consistency, strengthen authenticity, develop audience-specific content, and integrate
digital communic ation with offline engagement to establish a more effective,
sustainable, and trustworthy personal branding strategy.
