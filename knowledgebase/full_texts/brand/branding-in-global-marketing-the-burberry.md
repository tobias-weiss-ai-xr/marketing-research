Abstract

In today's globalized world, it is crucial for capital to have a presence in
international markets in order to maintain its existence. Therefore,
companies need to gain a competitive advan tage by adapting to the
volatile competitive environment of global markets. At this point,
globalization has become a significant force in enabling businesses to
increase their capacities and direct themselves towards international
markets by eliminating international borders. For businesses that
thoroughly analyze global markets, establishing a strong brand in their
own national markets first, and then entering global markets, is an
effective approach in terms of global branding strategies. Thus, global
brands that provide significant advantages to businesses will now be
among the most important symbols of our rapidly changing world.
This study examines the relationship between the expected benefits
from global brands and the level of global brand awareness within the
context of Burberry's globalization. The study employs a qualitative
research method: descriptive and explanatory case analysis. The reason
the research was limited to the Burberry brand is that, according to the
Interbrand report, it is among the world's "100 Most Valuable Global
Brands". In this context, this study also identifies the principles behind
Burberry's global brand building.

2.1. Globalization
The concept of globalization is one of the most debated concepts, particularly in
academic and economic circles. The term globalization, derived from the English
word "globe," means belonging to the entire globe (Gezgin, 2005, s.  11).
Researchers who argue that globalization has offered very positive results and
contributions in all areas suggest that globalization is essentially an economic
concept rather than a political one. Held and Mc Grew (2008) defined
globalization as the unrestricted movement of mo ney, services, and goods in
the new world, and therefore, equality achieved through competition between
global markets and global companies.

The concept of globalization entered the literature when Marshall McLuhan
(2001) first used the term "Global Village" in his book "Communication

2.3. Reasons Why Brands Expand into International Markets
When companies achieve success in the local market, expanding into different
markets can become attractive to them. Essentially, this situation stems from
the fact that the global economy wants companies to expand into foreign
markets. Another benefit of global expansion is that it allows businesses to
mitigate risks by increasing their market share in other countries when the
economic or political situation in their local market is unstable (Terpstra &
Sarathy, 2001, s. 245-249).

With the advancement and increasing power of technology, companies seeking
growth and development have found it necessary to expand internationally. By
differentiating their marketing principles, companies have created broad and
innovative tactics. Companies that want to differentiate themselves from their
competitors and stand tall against their rivals have chosen to change and build a
brand. As a result of globalization, multinational companies are investing

a. Research Method
The ready -to-wear industry, one of the first industries to experience
globalization, has enabled the formation of retail within the ready -to-wear
industry through the developments it has achieved (Kanoğlu &Öngüt, 2003, s.

saw an increase in production volume and a noticeable concentration of brands
due to rising demand (Hilger, 2008, s. 9 -10). By the twenty -first century, we see
the establishment of fashion houses that are pioneers of today's fashion. During
this period, the growth of the fashion market and the resulting increase in
consumer demand also boosted the production and trade of ready -to-wear
clothing worldwide. With globalization, smaller businesses in the garment
industry have merged to create fewer but larger companies, thereby
accelerating the globalization of the garment sector (Hilger, 2008).

This study also aims to identify the principles behind building a global brand,
considering the global scale of the Burberry brand. Within this scope, the
methodology of the study was carried out using descriptive and explanatory
case analysis, which is a qualitative research method. Regarding the Burberry
brand, Interbrand's reports from 2017 -2025 were examined, and digitally
available data was used. Consequently, this study examined the relationship
between the benefits expected from global brands and the  level of global brand
awareness within the context of Burberry's globalization.
