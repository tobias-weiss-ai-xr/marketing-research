  ABSTRACT
Advertising is a crucial marketing tool that influences customer awareness, attitudes,
and purchasing decisions in the highly competitive automobile industry. This study
examines the impact of advertising on customers of Hero MotoCorp, focusing on how
various advertising strategies affect consumer behavior, brand recognition, purchase
intention, customer satisfaction, and loyalty. The research evaluates the effectiveness
of Hero MotoCorp's advertising campaigns across television, digital media, social
media, print media, and outdoor promotional platforms. The study aims to understand
how advertising influences customers' perceptions of product quality, performance,
affordability, and brand image. Data collected from customers are analyzed to
determine the re lationship between advertising exposure and buying behavior. The
findings are expected to provide valuable insights into the effectiveness of advertising
in strengthening Hero MotoCorp's market presence, enhancing customer engagement,
and maintaining long -term customer relationships in the evolving two -wheeler
industry.

RESEARCH GAP
Although several studies have explored advertising effectiveness and consumer
buying behavior in the automobile sector, limited research has specifically examined
the impact of Hero MotoCorp's advertising strategies on customer perceptions and
purchasing decisions in the contemporary digital environment. Most existing studies
focus on general automotive advertising or compare multiple vehicle manufacturers
without providing an in -depth analysis of Hero MotoCorp's promotional activities
across both tradition al and digital media channels. Furthermore, there is a lack of
recent research investigating how social media advertising, influencer marketing,
digital campaigns, and customer -centric promotional strategies influence brand
preference, purchase intention, and customer loyalty toward Hero MotoCorp products.
Therefore, this study seeks to fill this gap by comprehensively analyzing the impact of
advertising on customer behavior and brand perception, thereby contributing to a
better understanding of effective m arketing practices in the Indian two -wheeler
market.

Consumers go through the AIDA Model's (Attention, Interest, Desire, and Action)
phases before deciding to buy, which lend credence to the study. Ads for Hero
MotoCorp, in this model , initially pique consumers' interest through clever copy,
celebrity endorsements, digital marketing, and other forms of promotion. After getting
people's attention, ads pique their interest by showcasing the product's advantages,
such as its low price, cu tting-edge technology, safety features, and fuel economy. In
the end, these messages motivate buyers to buy Hero MotoCorp automobiles by
generating desire. As a result, ads play a key role in shaping consumers' preferences
and ultimately their purchasing decisions.
Consumers, according to the Consumer Decision -Making Theory, which is another
major theoretical framework, research products thoroughly before settling on a
purchase. People are less likely to be confused and more equipped to weigh their
options when they see helpful information about products and services in ads. Ad
frequency, message substance, media outlets, emotional appeal, and brand image are
some of the aspects that the framework believes impact customer perceptions and buy
inclinations. He ro MotoCorp hopes that its advertising would increase product
knowledge, happiness, and loyalty through positive and convincing messaging. As a
result, the theoretical framework links the efficacy of advertisements (the independent
variable) to the results  of consumer behaviour (the dependent variables), including
brand recognition, purchase intent, customer happiness, and brand loyalty. Hero
MotoCorp's advertising efforts can be examined using this framework to determine
their impact on client attitudes and purchasing behaviour.
