Abstract
The objective of this study is to describe the application of innovation strategy, digital
marketing and resources orchestration capacity in building competitive advantage
and sustainable business performance in endek SMEs in Bali. The population of this
study was owner of endek SME firm in Bali. The sample of endek SME business
owners was purposively selected and had 151 participants. The data were evaluated
using descriptive analysis. The study shows that the innovative strategies applied by
endek SMEs in  Bali are categorized as high. Likewise, their digital marketing
strategies are classified as good and the level of resources orchestration competence
is likewise classified as high. In addition, their competitive advantage is classified as
high and their sustainable business performance is classified as high. Therefore, it is
crucial for Bali's endek SME business owners to actively improve the implementation
of innovation strategies, digital marketing, and resource orchestration capabilities to
strengthen their competitive advantage and to reach higher levels of sustainable
business performance.
Keywords: innovation strategy, digital marketing, resources orchestration capability,
competitive advantage, sustainable business performance.

INTRODUCTION
Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) have a critical role to assist
economic growth and job creation in Indonesia. MSMEs in Bali not only become the
driver of the regional economy but also an important aspect of cultural preservation
and the growth of creative industries based on local wisdom. One product that has
economic and cultural worth is Endek cloth. Endek handicraft is one of Bali's creative
industries which has the potential to be developed into a flagship product with a
wider market. Howeve r, the increasingly dynamic market conditions compel Endek
MSMEs not only to maintain the traditional value of their products but also to adapt
to the changing wants of consumers, technical innovations, and increased
competition.
This problem may be seen from the shifting sales turnover of Endek MSMEs in
Bali. Preliminary interviews with numerous Endek MSME business owners in Bali,
such as Sriwidhi in Klungkung and Aiswarya in Denpasar, showed that the average
annual sales turnover  objective was about IDR 1.5 billion, but the actual sales were

131

around IDR 1 billion. This circumstance represents a gap between the targets of
business performance and the actual performance. The decrease or variation in sales
turnover can also effect the profitability of the business and the ability of MSMEs to
run t he business on a long term basis. It is vital to study this topic since MSME
sustainability is not only determined by its ability to create sales in a certain period,
but also the ability to maintain growth, profitability and business continuity in the
long run. This condition can be affected by several things like customer preferences,
increased competition, the fit of corporate strategies with target markets, innovation
capabilities, marketing effectiveness and so on. firm strategy and resource availability
are critical elements in influencing MSME firm performance based on previous
research (Yasa et al., 2023). Innovation strategy was also shown to contribute to the
improvement of the business performance of Endek MSMEs through the ability of
MSMEs to respond to changes in the business environment (Telagawathi et al., 2022).
These findings imply that the ability to innovate in goods, production processes, and
business models is increasingly vital for Endek MSMEs to keep their products relevant
in the market.
Innovation strategy can be a strategic means to create competitive
advantage. The invention of products helps MSMEs to design, theme, quality and
variation of products according to the changes in consumer needs. Innovation in
production processes can enhance efficiency and quality and innovation in business
models can assist MSMEs establish new ways to provide and create value to
customers. Research on MSMEs has also shown that there is a relationship between
innovation and competitive advantage. Studies of  the handicraft sector in Bali have
highlighted innovation as a key source of business success (Yasa et al., 2020; Guntara
et al., 2024).
The rise of digital technology also opens potential for Endek MSMEs to
increase their market through digital marketing. MSMEs can reach consumers
beyond their geographical borders using social media, internet marketing, digital
customer interactions and di gital promotion. Yasa et al. (2022) demonstrated that
digital marketing has an effect on MSME business performance, Rahmayanti et al.
(2024) demonstrated that digital marketing strategy has an effect on the association
between digital orientation and susta inable business performance. These findings
suggest that the transformation of marketing through digital media can be a source
of competitive advantage for MSMEs.
But the capacity to create and apply digital marketing does not mean that you
will have sustained commercial performance. MSMEs should also be able to manage
and integrate, merge and make optimum use of the resources available. The
Resource Orchestration view implies that a firm’s advantage is not just based on the
possession of resources, but also on the firm’s capacity to arrange, bundle and
leverage resources to produce value. This view is particularly pertinent to MSMEs as

132

business owners need to make the most use of the internal and external resources
available to them due to resource restrictions.
Resource Orchestration Capability is vital in the setting of Endek MSMEs
because business owners need to integrate multiple resources, combine internal and
external resources, and to mobilize resources in an effort to grasp market
possibilities . Such capa bilities may enhance the efficiency of innovation strategies
and digital marketing, and hence create advantages that are more difficult for
competitors to copy. Therefore, Resource Orchestration Capability is not only
considered as resources management but  a strategic capability to transform
resources into competitive advantage. \
Competitive advantage is therefore positioned as a key mechanism linking the
different strategic capabilities to sustained company performance. MSMEs with
differentiated products, better quality and strong distinctive value are more likely to
be in a bette r position to face competition. Competitive advantage is an important
variable to explain the marketing success of handcraft MSMEs in Bali (Guntara et al.,
2024). This conclusion supports the notion that the capacity to generate competitive
advantage is a crucial process by which a firm’s resources and plans are turned into
commercial outcomes.
Empirical studies in the past still have room for additional inquiry. Studies on
innovation have proven a favorable association with corporate performance.
However, some of the studies still view innovation as a direct determinant of
performance without a thorough explanation of the creation of competitive
advantage. The research on digital marketing also showed favorable benefits on firm
performance but the processes via which digital marketing activities generate
sustainable performance still need to be f urther explored. Moreover, research that
combines innovation strategy, digital marketing, and resource orchestration
capabilities in a single model to explain competitive advantage and sustainable
company performance, especially among Endek handcraft MSMEs  in Bali, is still
lacking.
The construction of the research model is based on the research gap. The
current study investigated the direct influence of innovation strategy, digital
marketing, and resource orchestration capabilities on sustainable company
performance. However, the stu dy considers competitive advantage as a mediating
mechanism. This technique can illustrate the examination of how the innovation
strategy, digital marketing and resource orchestration capacity are converted into
competitive advantage and subsequently contribute to sustainable business success
in the long run.
This study is unusual as it integrates three strategic capabilities, namely
innovation strategy, digital marketing and resource orchestration capability, to
explain the establishment of competitive advantage and sustained business

133

performance among Endek MSMEs. This study also expands the application of
Resource-Based View, Dynamic Capability Theory and Resource Orchestration Theory
viewpoints inside the framework of a creative sector based on local wisdom. With
this background, thi s study intends to present an overview of the innovation
strategy, digital marketing, and resource orchestration capability in establishing
competitive advantage and sustainable business performance of Endek MSMEs in
Bali.

RESEARCH METHOD
This study was categorized as qualitative research with descriptive analysis due
to the research challenge's nature. This study compiles the responses of Endek MSMEs
in Bali about innovation strategy, digital marketing, competitive advantage, capability
to orchestrate resources, and sustainable business performance.
An investigation of Endek MSME in Bali was carried out. Offline distribution of
the questionnaire was the initial step. Results from 30 participants were used to confirm
the data's validity and reliability. All indicators have correlation values more than 0.30,
according to the validity test results. All of the variables had Cronbach's Alpha values
more than 0.60, indicating high reliability.
Subsequently, the data collection was augmented by distributing the
questionnaire directly (offline) and through other WhatsApp groups using a Google
Form. The formula for determining the number of samples provides that 160
respondents should be collected,  which is 10 times the number of indicators (16).
Nevertheless, the analysis was extended to 151 respondents who met the criteria. We
used descriptive analysis to look at the data.

CONCLUSION AND IMPLICATIONS FOR RESEARCH
Based on the results of the descriptive study, it can be stated that the
innovation strategy followed by Endek MSMEs in Bali was on average rated in the
high category consisting of product innovation, manufacturing process innovation,
and business model innovation. Good appraisal was also given to the digital
marketing activities of MSMEs Endek in Bali. In the very good category . This implies
that in the future digital marketing needs to be enhanced . Similarly, the resource
orchestration capability should be progressively improved from the high to the super
high level by strengthening the abilit y to integrate resources, combine internal and
external resources, and deploy resources to exploit business opportunities. These
conditions are the basis for enhancing competitive advantage, as evidenced by
product differentiation, improving product quality and product uniqueness. All these
elements lead to more sustainable business performance.  The present study
presents an overview of innovation strategy, digital marketing, resource
orchestration capabilities, competitive advantage, and sustainable business
performance of Endek MSMEs in Bali. Thus, the results can be used as a basis for
designing strategies to increase the competitive advantage and sustainable business
performance of Endek MSMEs in Bali.
