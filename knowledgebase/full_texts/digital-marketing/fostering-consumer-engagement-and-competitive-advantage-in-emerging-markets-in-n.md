Abstract
This study examined the effect of disruptive market innovation on consumer engagement and
competitive advantage in emerging markets in Nigeria. The specific objectives ascertained the
effects of digital platforms, mobile-based promotions, social media engagement, innovative service
delivery, and technology-driven analytics on consumer engagement and competitive advantage in
selected emerging markets in Nigeria. The study was anchored on Disruptive Innovation Theory
and the Resource-Based View. A survey design method was adopted, with data collected from the
respondents. The sample size was determined using Yamane’s formula. The reliability of the
research instrument was tested using Cronbach’ s alpha, and the validity of the measuring
instrument was established. Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) was used to test the formulated
hypotheses. The findings revealed that digital platform has a positive significant effect on
consumer engagement and competitive advantage in emerging markets in Anambra State, Nigeria.
The study found that mobile -based promotion has a positive significant effect on consumer
engagement and competitive advantage in emerging markets in Anambra State, Nigeria. The
findings revealed that social media engagement has a positive significant eff ect on consumer

Introduction
The Nigerian marketing and business environment has become increasingly dynamic and
competitive that prompted firms to adopt technological advancement and innovative strategies to
survive. Evidently, consumer behaviour is evolving and developing rapidly wi th the introduction
of technological advancements and the proliferation of digital platforms. Obviously, new
technologies appear to displace established practices thereby forcing firms to look for survival
strategies. In Nigeria, this reality is evident as companies struggle to maintain relevance in volatile
markets. However, recent studies have demonstrated on the importance of disruptive innovation
in shaping firm competitiveness. For instance, the work by Anetoh et al. (2021; 2023)
demonstrated that disr uptive innovation strategies are critical for firm survival and growth in
Nigeria’s GSM and SME sectors. Similarly, the work by Etuk et al. (2023) observed that digital
transformation reshaped service marketing strategies and enhanced customer engagement. Akpan
et al. (2022) confirmed that disruptive innovations improved marketing performance among online
marketers in Uyo, while Abereola and Ogundele (2024) emphasized that product and marketing
innovation is a vital tool for competitive advantage in Nigeria.
Globally, disruptive innovation has been linked to consumer engagement and competitiveness.
The Resource-Based View (Barney, 1991) suggests that firms with strong internal resources, such
as managerial competence and technological infrastructure, are bette r positioned to adopt
disruptive strategies. Nigerian evidence supports this: Anukwe et al. (2025) showed that social
media marketing strengthened customer engagement among SMEs, while Morah and Brownb
(2025) highlighted the importance of data -driven marke ting strategies in optimizing audience
engagement. Despite these advances, Nigerian firms still face challenges such as infrastructural
deficits, regulatory barriers, and managerial limitations. Ishola (2024) argued that innovation is the
secret to business success in Nigeria, particularly in volatile markets, while Novatia (2024) stressed
the importance of resilience and agility in navigating Nigeria’s dynamic business environment.
These insights underscore the need for empirical studies that assess how di sruptive marketing
innovations affect consumer engagement and competitive advantage in Nigeria.

Regrettably, disruptive innovation has been recognized as a major driver of competitiveness in
many advanced countries, but in Nigeria, its adoption remains slow and uneven. Firms often
struggle with infrastructural challenges such as poor internet connect ivity, unreliable electricity,
and regulatory bottlenecks, which limit their ability to fully embrace new marketing strategies
(Novatia Consulting, 2024). As a result, consumer engagement is often weak, brand loyalty is
fragile, and firms find it difficult to sustain competitive advantage. This is particularly worrying in
emerging markets like Nigeria, where consumer expectations are rapidly evolving due to the
spread of mobile technology, social media, and digital platforms. Ishola (2024) maintained that
innovation is the secret to business success in Nigeria, especially in volatile markets, yet many
organizations lack the managerial competence to implement disruptive strategies effectively.
Extant empirical studies have shown that there are varying  findings in relations to disruptive
innovation studies. For instance, Etuk et al., (2023) showed that digital transformation reshaped
service marketing strategies and improved customer interaction. Abereola and Ogundele (2024)
emphasized that product and marketing innovation is essential for competitive advantage. Akpan
et al. (2022) found that mobile‑based innovations improved marketing performance among online
marketers. Despite these findings, many firms still face difficulties in implementation, with
adoption varying widely across industries.
Recent research continues to confirm these challenges. Anetoh et al., (2025) demonstrated that
disruptive innovation is critical for sustaining competitiveness in Nigeria’s GSM industry, but
adoption remains inconsistent.  Thus, Nnanna‑Ohuonu et al. (2024) found that small businesses
leveraging digital disruption, especially mobile promotions and social media markets, achieved
stronger consumer engagement, yet many firms lacked the infrastructure to scale these strategies.
Similarly, Ido gun and Agbonifoh (20 25) emphasized that customer ‑centric digital marketing
strategies are essential for competitive advantage in Nigeria’s emerging markets, but many
organizations continue to depend on traditional approaches. Thus, the consequences of failing to
adopt disruptive marketing innovations are  significant. Without effective consumer engagement,
firms struggle to build loyalty and advocacy, which are critical for long ‑term survival. Without
competitive advantage, firms cannot differentiate themselves from rivals or sustain profitability in
volatile markets. This situation undermines not only firm performance but also broader economic
development of Nigeria especially in Anambra State of Nigeria (Bari et al., 2022).
Therefore, there is a pressing need to investigate how Nigerian firms are adopting disruptive
marketing innovations, the measurable effect of these innovations on consumer engagement and
competitive advantage, and the organizational challenges that arise. By focusing on five key
variables digital platforms, mobile promotions, social media engagement, innovative service
delivery, and technology‑driven analytics. This study seeks to provide comprehensive evidence on
how disruptive strategies can reshape marketing outcomes in Nigeria. Therefore, this study seeks
to fill a critical gap by examining the effect of disruptive market innovations namely; digital
platforms, mobile promotions, social media engagement, innovative service delivery, and
technology-driven analytics on consumer eng agement and competitive advantage in Nigerian
firms. By employing Structural Equation Modeling tool, the study is an attempt to provide robust
empirical evidence in the context of emerging markets in order to contribute to knowledge.

Objectives of the Study
The main aim of this study is to examine the effect of disruptive market innovation on consumer
engagement and competitive advantage in selected emerging markets in Anambra State, Nigeria.
The specific objectives of this study are to:
1a: Ascertain the effect of digital platform on consumer engagement.
1b: Assess the effect of digital platform on competitive advantage.
2a: Investigate the effect of mobile-based promotion on consumer engagement.
2b: Evaluate the effect of mobile-based promotion on competitive advantage.
3a: Establish the effect of social media engagement in fostering consumer engagement.
3b: Assess the effect of social media engagement in enhancing competitive advantage.
4a: Investigate how innovative service delivery affects consumer engagement.
4b: Examine how innovative service delivery affects competitive advantage.
5a: Determine the effect of technology-driven analytics on consumer engagement.
5b: Ascertain the effect of technology -driven analytics on competitive advantage in selected
emerging markets in Anambra State, Nigeria.

Research Questions
1. To what extent do digital platforms affect consumer engagement and competitive advantage
in selected emerging markets in Anambra State, Nigeria?
2. How do mobile-based promotions affect consumer engagement and competitive advantage in
selected emerging markets in Anambra State, Nigeria?
3. How does social media engagement improve consumer interaction and competitiveness in
selected emerging markets in Anambra State, Nigeria?
4. To what degree does innovative service delivery affect consumer engagement as well as
competitiveness in selected emerging markets in Anambra State, Nigeria?
5. To what dimension do technology -driven analytics affect consumer engagement as well as
competitiveness in selected emerging markets in Anambra State, Nigeria?

Globally, Christensen (1997) introduced the concept of disruptive innovation, showing how

Digital Platforms
Digital platforms such as websites, mobile applications, and online portals enable firms to interact
directly with consumers, offering convenience and accessibility. Etuk et al., (2023) highlighted
that digital transformation reshaped service marketing str ategies in Nigeria, making consumer
interaction more efficient. Similarly, Nkpurukwe et al. (2025) maintained that digital marketing
practices significantly enhance competitiveness in e-commerce firms like Jumia Nigeria. Miracle
Eze (2024) confirmed that social media and mobile platforms are the primary engagement tools in
Nigeria compared to South Africa, showing the centrality of digital platforms in emerging
economies. Nigerian banks such as GTBank, Fidelity and Zenith Bank have also leveraged mobile
apps and online portals to improve customer service and loyalty, demonstrating that digital
platforms are now indispensable for consumer engagement and competitive advantage.

Mobile Promotions
Mobile promotions include SMS campaigns, app-based discounts, and mobile advertising, which
allow firms to reach consumers instantly and personally. Akpan et al. (2022) found that mobile -

Ohuonu et al. (2024) confirmed that small businesses leveraging mobile promotions achieved
stronger consumer engagement, though infrastructural challenges limited scalability. Hanaysha
(2017) emphasized the role of mobile promotions in in fluencing consumer purchase decisions,
showing their relevance across different contexts. In Nigeria, telecom firms like MTN and Airtel
frequently use mobile promotions such as bonus airtime and discounted data bundles to attract and
retain customers, prov ing that mobile promotions are very powerful tool for sustaining
competitiveness

while Morah and Brownb (2025) emphasized the role of data -driven strategies in optimizing

Innovative Service Delivery
Innovative service delivery includes e -commerce systems, personalization, and product -service

Technology-Driven Analytics
Analytics and data-driven strategies are increasingly shaping marketing decisions, enabling firms
to understand consumer behavior and tailor strategies effectively. Morah and Brownb (2025)
demonstrated that predictive analytics enhanced engagement, while Ishola (2024) argued that
innovation and analytics are essential for business success in Nigeria’s volatile markets. Novatia

indispensable for competitive advantage.

Consumer Engagement
Consumer engagement is the depth of interaction and emotional connection between customers
and a brand, reflected in loyalty, advocacy, and repeat patronage. It goes beyond transactional
relationships to include active participation, trust, and emotional attachment. In Nigeria’s emerging
market, where consumers are increasingly affected by digital tools, engagement has become a
critical determinant of firm success. Morah and Brown (2025) showed that data-driven marketing
strategies, including predictive ana lytics and tailored content delivery, significantly enhanced
audience engagement. Contributing, Anukwe et al. (2025) demonstrated that social media
marketing strengthened consumer engagement among SMEs in South-East Nigeria. Onamusi and
Adenekan (2020) found that customer engagement capability directly improved market share s in
FMCG firms, proving that engagement is not just communication but a driver of competitiveness.
Notably, practical examples abound in Nigeria. E -commerce firms such as Jumia and Konga
depend heavily on consumer engagement through interactive campaigns, personalized
recommendations, and loyalty programs. Banks such as Access Bank and GTBank and so on use
mobile apps and social media platforms to maintain constant interactions with their  numerous
customers, thereby fostering trust and loyalty. These instances show that consumer engagement is
both a marketing strategy and a competitive weapon in Nigeria’s volatile markets.

Competitive Advantage
Competitive advantage refers to a firm’s ability to outperform rivals by offering superior value.
Peteraf and Barney (2003) define it as the economic value created when buyers perceive greater
benefits compared to costs. This advantage can be achieved thro ugh differentiation, cost
leadership, or innovation. Abereola and Ogundele (2024) emphasized that product and marketing
innovation is a critical tool for competitive advantage in Nigeria. Adefulu et al. (2021) found that
strategic marketing practices foster innovation culture, helping firms remain ahead of competitors.
Ajube & Emueje (2024) showed that innovation management practices in brewery firms
significantly enhanced competitive positioning. In Nigeria, competitive advantage is often linked
to the abi lity to leverage technology and innovation. For example, fintech firms such as
Flutterwave have differentiated themselves by offering secure, fast, and user -friendly payment
solutions, while FMCG firms like Nestlé Nigeria have sustained advantage through p roduct
diversification and customer -centric innovations. These cases demonstrate that competitive
advantage in Nigeria is increasingly tied to disruptive marketing innovations that align with
evolving consumer expectations.

Theoretical Review
Disruptive Innovation Theory
Disruptive Innovation Theory as propounded by Christensen (1997) states that new entrants
challenge incumbents by offering affordable, accessible solutions. This theory has been widely
applied in marketing and management studies. In Nigeria, Anetoh et al. (2025) applied the theory
to demonstrate how disruptive strategies sustain competitiveness in volatile markets, particularly
in the GSM industry.

Resource-Based View (RBV)
The Resource-Based View as coined by Barney (1991) emphasizes that firms with strong internal
resources such as managerial competence, technological infrastructure, and innovative capacity
are better positioned to adopt disruptive strategies. Nigerian scholars like Anetoh (2023) have used
RBV to explain how SMEs leverage internal strengths to adopt disruptive innovations. However,
the two theories are relevant to the present study since all the study variables originated and also
anchored on them.

Discussion of Findings
Digital platform significantly and positively affects consumer engagement. This finding supports
Etuk et al. (2023), who reported that digital transformation reshaped service marketing strategies
in Nigeria. Christensen (1997) found that disruptive technol ogies redefine consumer access and
competitiveness, which resonates with the Nigerian context. Mobile promotions significantly and
positively affect competitive advantage. This aligns with the findings of Akpan et al. (2022), who
demonstrated that mobile-based disruptive innovations improved marketing performance among
online marketers. It also echoes Hanaysha (2017), who highlighted the role of promotions in
consumer purchase decisions in Malaysia. Social media engagement had the strongest effect on
consumer engagement. This corroborates Anukwe et al. , (2025), who found that social media
marketing strengthened customer engagement among SMEs.
Similarly, Morah and Brownb (2025) emphasized that data-driven social media strategies optimize
audience interaction. Innovative service delivery was positively associated with competitive
advantage. This supports Abereola and Ogundele (2024), who argued t hat product and service
innovation is a critical tool for competitive advantage in Nigeria. The result also aligns with the
Resource-Based View (Barney, 1991), which emphasizes leveraging internal resources to sustain
competitiveness. Analytics and technol ogy-driven strategies significantly affect both consumer
engagement and competitive advantage. This reflects the growing importance of data in marketing.
Novatia Consulting (2024) stressed resilience as well as  agility in Nigeria’s dynamic business
environment, while Ishola (2024) argued that innovation and analytics are the secret to business
success. The results highlight the importance of integrating multiple disruptive strategies rather
than focusing on isolated innovations. This study therefore contributes to both theory and practice
by demonstrating that disruptive marketing innovations are not only relevant but essential for firms
seeking sustained consumer engagement and competitive advantage in Nigeria.

Conclusion and Recommendations
The findings demonstrate that disruptive marketing innovations are critical drivers of consumer
engagement and competitive advantage in Nigeria. The results reinforce the Disruptive Innovation
Theory (Christensen, 1997) and the Resource-Based View (Barney, 1991), showing that firms that
adopt innovative strategies and leverage internal resources are better positioned to thrive in
competitive markets. This study supports Anetoh (2019, 2021, 2023), who argued that disruptive
innovation adoption must be studied holistically, considering both internal resources and external
market dynamics. The evidence confirms that integrating multiple disruptive strategies rather than
focusing on isolated innovations is essential for sustained competitiveness.
Based on the findings, the following recommendations were made:
1. Firms should invest in digital platforms to enhance consumer engagement and competitive
advantage of developing economies.
2. Mobile promotions should be strategically deployed to strengthen competitive advantage.
3. Social media engagement must be prioritized, given its strong effect on consumer
interaction.
4. Innovative service delivery should be continuously improved for enhanced consumer
engagement and competitive advantage.
5. Analytics and technology -driven strategies should be sustained and integrated into
marketing operations.
