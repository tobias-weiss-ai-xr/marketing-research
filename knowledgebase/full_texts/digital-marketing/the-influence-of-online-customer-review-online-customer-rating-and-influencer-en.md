This study examines the influence of online
customer review, online customer rating, and
influencer endorsement on the purchase decisions
of Garnier facial cleanser consumers on Shopee in
DKI Jakarta, motivated by Garnier's comparatively
small market share (around 0.85%) amid the
rapidly growing beauty and personal -care sales
value on Shopee. A quantitative survey was
conducted with 170 Garnier consumers in DKI
Jakarta selected through purposive sampling, and
data were analyzed using Partial Least Squares
Structural Equation Modeling (PLS -SEM) with
SmartPLS 4.0. The results indicate that online
customer review, online c ustomer rating, and
influencer endorsement each have a positive and
significant effect on purchase decisions, with online
customer review exerting the strongest influence,
followed by influencer endorsement and online
customer rating. The three predictors jointly
explain 74.2% of the variance in purchase decisions,
indicating substantial explanatory power. These
findings extend the e -commerce consumer
behavior literature and offer practical guidance for
Garnier in strengthening its digital marketing
strategy on Shopee.

INTRODUCTION
Technological development has reshaped the landscape of consumer
behavior, particularly in the context of online shopping. Society's shopping
habits have shifted from face-to-face transactions in offline stores toward online
transactions, driving a significant increase in competition among marketplace
platforms. The growing number of internet and social media users, together with
intensifying competition among e -commerce platforms, indicates that online -
based purchasing decisions in Indonesia are increasingly dominant (Imamatuz
Zakiyah et al., 2024).
Data on internet users in Indonesia show a consistent upward trend from
2018 to 2024. In 2018, internet users were recorded at 106 million, growing 24.6%
year-on-year; this trend continued with 128 million users (20.7%) in 2019, 146
million (13.7%) in 2020, 169 million (16.5%) in 2021, 183 million (7.7%) in 2022,
184 million in 2023, and 185.3 million (0.8%) by January 2024. Although the
growth rate has declined year o ver year, the overall direction remains positive,
and this expansion has strongly driven the growth of the digital industry,
particularly the e -commerce sector, which has become one of the pillars of
Indonesia's digital economy (Ahdiat, 2024). Online shopping activity is itself one
of the reasons internet usage continues to rise (Kadek et al., 2022).
Indonesia's economy continues to grow above the global average, a
development inseparable from the role of entrepreneurs who have capitalized on
the country's large population as a potential market for various products,
including skincare (Rahmawati & Puspita, 2024). Online shopping behavior has
become a trend and tendency of Indonesian society, prompting businesses to
safeguard their sustainability (Hamdan et a l., 2023). Appearance plays an
important role in boosting self -confidence, making skincare products
increasingly popular and sought after (Fahruddin & Aminah, 2025). Skincare has
become a primary need across demographics, with the beauty industry
continuously innovating new products for men and women, from teenagers to
adults, causing consumers to become more selective in choosing brands based on
various considerations (Intan Tasia et al., 2024).
Regarding the beauty and personal -care sales value across fiv e e -
commerce platforms in Indonesia (January –October 2024), Shopee dominates
with a 60% market share, far ahead of other platforms, reflecting the strength of
its ecosystem in marketing, active users, and transaction convenience. TikTok
Shop ranks second w ith 29%, leveraging live -streaming features and influencer
promotion; Tokopedia trails at 6.1%; Lazada holds 4.7%; and Blibli records only
0.2% (Muhamad, 2025). The wide variety of skincare products in Indonesia —
from local brands to American, European, J apanese, and Korean brands —
demonstrates strong public enthusiasm for cosmetics, one of which is Garnier
(Ponto et al., 2023).
Garnier was founded by Alfred Amour Garnier in Blois, Paris, in 1904,
initially focusing on natural hair -care products before expanding into skin care,
guided by the belief that nature provides the best self -care solutions
(Garnier.com). Amid intensifying competition in the facial-care industry, Garnier
must prioritize the development of more effective, innovative, and consumer -

LITERATURE REVIEW
Stimulus-Organism-Response (S-O-R) Theory
The S-O-R theory states that an individual's behavior is determined not
only by external stimuli but also by internal processes within the organism.
Stimulus refers to anythi ng originating from outside the individual words,
images, sounds, situations, or social experiences while organism encompasses
internal psychological processes such as emotion, perception, and motivation,
and response is the resulting behavior or reaction after the organism processes
the stimulus it receives (Armawan et al., 2025). In the digital context, S -O-R
theory is widely used to understand social media and e-commerce user behavior:
an advertisement on a phone screen is a stimulus, but whether a person becomes
interested, ignores it, or feels disturbed is determined by internal conditions, and
users' emotional responses to digital stimuli substantially determine the success
of marketing communication (Armawan et al., 2025). The theory comprises three
main components: Stimulus (S) — an external factor that triggers a specific
individual response; Organism (O) — internal psychological aspects such as
emotion, attitude, personality, belief, and interpretation that shape how a
stimulus is processed; and Response (R) — the resulting behavioral reaction from
the interaction between stimulus and organism.
Marketing Management
Marketing management involves undertaking processes that support
organizational or business objectives, encompassing planning, direction, pricing,
distribution, and promotion (Suhairi et al., 2023). It is also described as the art
and science of identifying target markets and acquiring, retaining, and growing
customers through creating, delivering, and communicating superior custo mer
value (Rahayu et al., 2021). According to Misra et al. (2024), marketing's
objectives include informing (promotion) to attract consumers, creating
purchases, creating repeat purchases through satisfied and loyal customers,
indirectly creating employmen t, and building mega -brands whose loyal
customer base ensures continued market attention.
E-WOM
Word of Mouth (WOM) can be spread not only through direct oral
communication but also through social media, in which case it is termed
electronic word of mouth (e-WOM), typically expressed through product
reviews, recommendations to other consumers, or simply sharing experiences
(Ayunita et al., 2021). WOM is a form of promotion consisting of mouth-to-mouth
recommendations about a product's positive attributes, d efined as
communication by consumers who have made a purchase and describe their
experience with a product or service (Anggi Muninggar et al., 2022). While WOM
traditionally refers to direct, face -to-face communication between consumers,
rapid technologica l development has encouraged consumers to share product
information online, increasing the incidence of e-WOM (Saragih et al., 2022).

Online Customer Review
Online customer review can be defined as p eer-generated product
evaluations uploaded on online retailer or third -party websites user -generated
information from those who have purchased a target product, containing
information and recommendations about the product from the consumer's point
of view (Rahayu et al., 2021). It is also described as a form of electronic word of
mouth (e -WOM) that allows consumers to view previous consumers'
descriptions or reviews of a product, a company's service, and the producer itself
(Hasanah & Kosasih, 2022), and as  positive or negative messages conveyed by
consumers who have used a company's product or service, reaching many
individuals or institutions through the internet (Saragih et al., 2022). Online
customer review comprises three dimensions: perceived usefulness (the extent to
which consumers believe reviews are useful and beneficial), perceived
enjoyment (comfort and pleasure felt when seeking information through
reviews), and perceived controlling (the degree of control consumers feel over
their actions) (Sals ia et al., 2024), operationalized through nine indicators:
usefulness, improved effectiveness, benefit, attractiveness, enjoyment, comfort,
importance of information, self -control, and necessity of information (Salsia et
al., 2024).
Online Customer Rating
Online customer rating is an assessment customer give through symbols,
typically stars; Indonesian marketplaces generally use a five -point rating scale,
where five stars indicates high satisfaction and one star indicates high
dissatisfaction (Pratama Putra et al., 2021). Rating plays a very important role in
purchase decisions by providing customer satisfaction, and marketplace rating
schemes can influence consumer purchase decisions (Sukirman et al., 2023); it
also functions as feedback from consumers to sellers after a purchase, expressed
on a one -to-five-star scale according to product condition (Mokodompit et al.,
2022). Online customer rating comprises three dimensions helpfulness (ratings
are highly helpful for online purchases), representativeness (re presenting
consumer satisfaction through star ratings), and purchase decision (satisfied
consumers give high ratings) (Salsia et al., 2024) operationalized through nine
indicators: purchase, trustworthiness, usefulness, satisfaction, assessment,

Influencer Endorsement
Endorsement is a form of support or recommendation from influential
individuals, such as celebrities or influencers, for a product or service for
promotional purposes, commonly conducted through social media and
advertising to increase brand awareness and consumer trust and drive sales, with
the influencer being a key element in this process (Asmiyatun et al., 2025;
Armawan et al., 2025). Three factors a re considered effective in influencer
endorsement: celebrities' ability to capture consumer attention and create buzz
on social media; celebrities' popularity and credibility, which increase consumer
trust in the endorsed product or brand; and fans' tenden cy toward high loyalty
to their idols, making them more likely to purchase products endorsed by those
idols (Yanuar Erika Putri et al., 2025). Influencer endorsement comprises three
dimensions visibility, credibility, and attractiveness (Ulinnuha et al., 2 024)
operationalized through eight indicators: popularity, attractiveness, broad
influence, expertise, trustworthiness, likeability, familiarity, and similarity
(Ulinnuha et al., 2024).
Purchase Decision
Purchase decision is the final decision a consumer makes to buy a good or
service after weighing various considerations, reflecting how far marketers have
succeeded in marketing a product to consumers, and is influenced by financial
and economic conditions, technology, politics, culture, product, price, pla ce,
promotion, physical evidence, people, and process (Marlius & Jovanka, 2023).
According to Kotler et al. (2024), four major factor groups shape purchase
decisions: cultural (culture, subculture, social class), social (reference groups,
family, roles and status), personal (age and life-cycle stage, occupation, economic
condition, lifestyle, personality and self-concept), and psychological (motivation,
perception, learning, beliefs and attitudes). Purchase decision comprises three
stages consumers undergo purchase conviction (confidence and satisfaction
sufficient to purchase), information search (recommending the product to
others), and post -purchase behavior (repurchasing the product) (Salsia et al.,
2024) operationalized through seven indicators: purchas e conviction,
information quality, consumer perception, information planning, repurchase,
purchase satisfaction, and providing recommendations (Salsia et al., 2024).

Hypothesis Development
Online Customer Review and Purchase Decision.Syafitri et al. (2023) show
that online customer review has a positive and significant effect on purchase
decision, a finding reinforced by Widjaya and Aodi (2024) and Kadek et al. (2022).
However, Yanuardi Wisnu Murti and Awan Santosa (2025) and Wijaya et al.
(2023) found no significant effect, indicating that customer reviews do not always
determine purchase decisions. Logically, well -written reviews reduce
consumers' perceived risk by offering a realistic picture of a product's quality,
benefits, and prior users ' experience, thereby strengthening the confidence
needed to proceed with a purchase.
H1: Online customer review has a positive and significant effect on purchase
decision.
Online Customer Rating and Purchase Decision. Widjaya and Aodi (2024)
and Kadek et al. (2022) found that customer ratings positively and significantly
affect purchase decisions, a pattern also confirmed by Novitasari and Maulana
(2022) for both review and rating. Conversely, Wijaya et al. (2023) found that
rating does not significantly a ffect purchase decisions, suggesting that its
influence may depend on other factors such as consumer demographics or
product type. From a rational standpoint, a numerical star rating functions as a
quick, aggregated signal of product quality that reduces consumers' search effort
and reinforces their confidence before purchasing.
H2: Online customer rating has a positive and significant effect on purchase
decision.
Influencer Endorsement and Purchase Decision. Chindy Ariesca  et al.
(2025) show that influencer endorsement has a positive and significant effect on
purchase decisions, a finding corroborated by Asmiyatun et al. (2025), Muhani
and Sabillah (2022) for celebrity endorsers, and Bintang Gorafli Wibowo and
Andriansyah Bachtillah Putra (2024). An influencer's popularity, credibility, and
attractiveness create an image -transfer effect in which consumers associate the

H3: Influencer endorsement has a positive and significant effect on purchase

Conceptual Framework
Based on the theoretical review and hypothesis development above, the
conceptual framework positions online customer review (X1), online customer
rating (X2), and influencer endorsement (X3) as independent variables
hypothesized to positively and significantly affect purchase decision (Y) as the
dependent variable, tested simultaneously in a single structural model using
PLS-SEM.

METHODOLOGY
This stu dy employed a quantitative research design. According to
Creswell and Creswell (2023), quantitative research is an approach for testing
objective theories by examining the relationships among measurable variables,
so that numerical data can be analyzed usi ng statistical procedures. The study
was conducted from March 2025 to February 2026 in DKI Jakarta, covering its
five administrative cities (Central, North, West, South, and East Jakarta), focusing
on Garnier as the object of research within the beauty and personal-care industry.
The population comprised residents of DKI Jakarta who had purchased
Garnier facial cleanser products on Shopee, with an unidentified exact
population size. Given the unknown population size, sample size was
determined using the formula proposed by Hair et al. (2021), which recommends
a minimum sample of five to ten times the number of indicators used in the
research instrument. With 34 indicators, the minimum required sample was 5 ×
34 = 170 respondents; accordingly, 170 respondents  were selected using a non -
probability, purposive sampling technique based on the following criteria: (1)
had purchased Garnier facial cleanser at least once on Shopee, (2) had used
Garnier facial cleanser at least once, (3) resided in DKI Jakarta, (4) wer e
male or female, and (5) were between 17 and 45 years of age.
Primary data were collected through a structured online questionnaire
distributed via Google Forms, using a five-point Likert scale (Strongly Agree = 5,
Agree = 4, Neutral = 3, Disagree = 2, St rongly Disagree = 1) to measure each
indicator of online customer review, online customer rating, influencer
endorsement, and purchase decision (Sugiyono, 2023).
Data were analyzed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation
Modeling (PLS-SEM) via Smar tPLS 4.0, a causal -predictive structural modeling
approach used primarily to maximize the explained variance of dependent
constructs, and particularly useful for theory development, prediction, and
contexts with limited sample size or non -normal data distr ibution (Hair et al.,
2021). The analysis proceeded through evaluation of the measurement model
(outer model) comprising convergent validity (outer loadings ≥ 0.70 and Average
Variance Extracted ≥ 0.50) and discriminant validity (assessed through cross -
loadings and the Heterotrait–Monotrait Ratio/HTMT, with thresholds of < 0.85
for conceptually distinct constructs or < 0.90 for conceptually similar constructs)
followed by reliability testing using Composite Reliability and Cronbach's Alpha

DISCUSSION
The Effect of Online Customer Review on Purchase Decision (H1)
The findings confirm that online customer review has a positive and
significant effect on the purchase decisions of Garnier facial -cleanser consumers
on Shopee (O = 0.476, T = 7.859, p = 0.000), supporting H1. This indicates that the
better the quality of reviews consumers provide, the higher the tendency of other
consumers to make a purchase decision. The outer -loading results show that
OCRE1 reflecting reviews' ability to provide the information consumers seek
before purchasing recorded the highest loading (0.817), making it the most
dominant indicator of the construct, while OCRE2.5 ("I feel happy reading online
reviews") recorded the lowest loading (0.740), still above the 0.70 threshold.
Descriptively, respondents scored highest on reviews' informational usefulness,
reinforcing that consumers primarily use reviews as an information source that
helps them gauge product quality, benefits, and other users' real experience —
consistent with Syafitri et al. (2023), Wi djaya and Aodi (2024), and Kadek et al.
(2022).

The Effect of Online Customer Rating on Purchase Decision (H2)
Online customer rating was also found to have a positive and significant
effect on purchase decision (O = 0.216, T = 2.981, p = 0.003), supporting H2 and
indicating that the higher a product's rating, the greater consumers' confidence
to purchase. OCRA3.1 ("the rating helps me determine my purchase choice")
recorded the highest outer loading (0.854), while OCRA3.4 ("the rating reflects
consumer satisfaction with the product") recorded the lowest (0.770), still
meeting the validity criterion. Descriptively, respondents indicated that product
ratings help them decide and provide a picture of consumer satisfaction levels.
Although rating exerted the smallest effect among the three predictors consistent
with the comparatively lower trust respondents placed in ot her consumers'
ratings during descriptive analysis the result aligns with Widjaya and Aodi
(2024), Kadek et al. (2022), and Novitasari and Maulana (2022), while diverging
from Wijaya et al. (2023), who found no significant effect, suggesting rating's
influence may be more context-dependent than that of reviews.
The Effect of Influencer Endorsement on Purchase Decision (H3)
Influencer endorsement was found to have a positive and significant effect
on purchase decision (O = 0.363, T = 6.168, p = 0.000), suppo rting H3. This
indicates that using influencers as a promotional medium can strengthen
consumers' interest and confidence in purchasing. EI4.3 ("the influencer has
broad social influence") recorded the highest outer loading (0.838), showing that
the breadth of an influencer's social reach contributes most to shaping consumer
perception, while EI4.4 ("the influencer has expertise in recommending the
product") recorded the lowest loading (0.784), still above the minimum
threshold. Descriptively, respondents r ated the influencer's broad social media
influence highest, while trust in the specific product information conveyed by the
influencer scored lowest mirroring the preliminary survey finding that
influencers attract initial interest more effectively than th ey build deep
informational trust. These results are consistent with Chindy Ariesca et al. (2025),
Asmiyatun et al. (2025), Muhani and Sabillah (2022), and Bintang Gorafli
Wibowo and Andriansyah Bachtillah Putra (2024).

CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
Based on the analysis and discussion above, three conclusions can be
drawn. First, online customer review has a positive and significant effect on the
purchase decisions of Garnier facial -cleanser consumers via the Shopee
marketplace; the better the quality o f consumer reviews, the higher other
consumers' tendency to purchase, as reviews serve as an important information
source describing product quality, benefits, and real usage experience. Thus, H1
is accepted. Second, online customer rating has a positive a nd significant effect
on purchase decision; a high rating increases consumer confidence in choosing
to purchase, although its effect is the smallest among the three independent
variables, positioning rating as supporting rather than primary decision
information. Thus, H2 is accepted. Third, influencer endorsement has a positive
and significant effect on purchase decision; influencers with credibility,
attractiveness, popularity, and broad social media reach increase consumer trust
in the product and encourage purchase decisions. Thus, H3 is accepted.

Several practical recommendations follow from these findings. All
measurement items met the validity threshold (outer loading > 0.70);
nevertheless, several items recorded comparatively lower loadings and are
recommended for refinement in both future research and Garnier's managerial
practice. For online customer review, item OCRE2.5 ("I feel happy reading
Garnier's online reviews on Shopee") recorded the lowest loading (0.740); future
studies should develop this  item to more comprehensively capture the
attractiveness of review content and ease of understanding, while at the
managerial level Garnier should optimize the quality of product information
available on the marketplace by better managing consumer reviews and
encouraging buyers to share clear, informative post -purchase experiences. For
online customer rating, item OCRA3.4 ("the rating reflects consumer satisfaction
with the product") recorded the lowest loading (0.770); future research should
refine this it em to better capture consumer trust in the rating system and its
consistency with product quality, while Garnier should maintain consistent
product quality and purchasing experience across the marketplace so that ratings
more objectively reflect actual pro duct conditions. For influencer endorsement,
item EI4.4 ("the influencer has expertise in recommending the product") recorded
the lowest loading (0.784); future research should add items capturing
influencers' experience in the beauty field, credibility to ward the promoted
product, and ability to educate consumers clearly, while Garnier should give
particular attention to selecting and managing influencer partnerships based on
fit between the influencer's characteristics, the product's image, and the target
consumer profile. Finally, for purchase decision, item KPB1.4 ("the information I
obtain influences my perception of the product") recorded the lowest loading
(0.728); future research should refine this item to better capture the quality of
information received, the level of trust placed in it, and the extent to which it
shapes purchase confidence.

ADVANCED RESEARCH
This study is subject to several limitations that offer directions for future
research. The sample was drawn using non-probability purposive sampling
limited to 170 Garnier consumers within DKI Jakarta, which constrains the
generalizability of the findings to other regions or to the broader national
consumer population. The cross -sectional design also captures consumer
perceptions only at a single point in time, without accounting for changes in
preference over a longer period. Future research is therefore encouraged to
employ probability sampling techniques with a larger and more geographically
diverse sample, to exten d the model with additional variables beyond online
customer review, online customer rating, and influencer endorsement (such as
price, brand image, e -trust, or brand ambassador effects, which prior studies
suggest also shape purchase decisions for compara ble products), to examine
potential mediating variables such as trust or purchase intention, and to refine
the measurement items identified above particularly those related to review
enjoyment, rating credibility, influencer expertise, and the information -to-
