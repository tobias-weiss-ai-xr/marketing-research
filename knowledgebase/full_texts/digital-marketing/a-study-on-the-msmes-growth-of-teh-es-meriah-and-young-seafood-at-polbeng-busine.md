Abstrak
Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) play a vital role in supporting sustainable local economic growth, creating
jobs, and improving community welfare. This study aims to analyze the development of MSMEs through the strategic
integration of digital marketing and structured financial management, focusing on the specific case studies of Teh Es Meriah
and Young Seafood during the POLBENG Business Expo Chapter II. This project was conducted at the State Polytechnic of
Bengkalis in May 2025 using a pract ical, project-based learning approach to successfully bridge academic theory with real -
world business challenges.The key project activities included producing engaging business profile videos, creating targeted
digital marketing content, designing professi onal product catalogs, implementing QRIS as a modern digital payment system,
and preparing systematic bookkeeping and financial reports before and during the intensive expo event. The research team
gathered reliable primary data through direct field observ ation, documentation, and real -time financial transaction
records.The results demonstrate that digital marketing strategies, supported by highly attractive promotional media,
significantly increased consumer interest, target market reach, brand awareness, and overall sales performance. Furthermore,
the implementation of QRIS greatly improved transaction efficiency, reduced human error, and enhanced financial recording
accuracy. Ultimately, this comprehensive integration of digital marketing and disciplined financial management provides a
scalable, highly effective model that positively contributes to sustainable MSME growth, operational independence, and
competitiveness in today's rapidly evolving digital economy.
Keywords: MSMEs, Digital Marketing, Financial Management, QRIS.
1. Introduction
In the current era of globalization, economic development serves as a primary benchmark of a government's
success in managing a nation [1]. Economic growth is an urgent priority because it directly correlates with rising
household incomes, poverty reduction, and the overall improvement of community welfare. Within the Indonesian
economic landscape, Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) act as th e backbone of the national
economy. These enterprises possess a highly strategic and critical role, not only in generating local employment
but also in maintaining economic resilience during global financial fluctuations  [2].
As times change and market competition intensifies, MSME owners are constantly demanded to be more creative
and adaptive in executing their business processes  [3]. To survive and thrive, these businesses cannot rely solely
on traditional methods; they must actively upgrade their sales systems and continuously innovate their product
offerings to align with shifting consumer preferences. Fortunately, Indonesia’s rich abundance of raw materials
provides a highly favorable environment, making product development and supply chain management relatively
accessible and cost-effective for local entrepreneurs who wish to diversify [4].
Recognizing the vital need for platforms that bridg e academia and industry, the International Business
Administration Study Program at Bengkalis State Polytechnic (Polbeng) is organizing a large -scale event called
the Polbeng Business Expo [5]. Centered at the campus location on Jalan Bathin Alam, Sungai Alam, Bengkalis,
this expo is designed to serve as a strategic business center where local entrepreneurs can showcase their brands.
To foster community engagement and attract a diverse crowd, the event will also host various supporting activities,
including educational competitions for kindergarten and preschool children  [6].

In today's digital-first market, implementing traditional marketing is no longer sufficient to secure a competitive
edge. Digital marketing, which involves the strategic utilization of electronic data and modern applications to plan,
price, promote, and distribute products, has become an absolute necessity for modern MSMEs  [7]. By leveraging
these digital tools, businesses can create mutually beneficial exchanges that satisfy both individual customer
desires and organizational goals, allowing them to reach a much broader audience and build stronger brand equity
during high-profile events like the upcoming expo [8].
The first MSME selected as the object of this research is "Es Teh Meriah," a local beverage brand situated on Jalan
Lembaga. This business specializes in serving traditional tea-based drinks enhanced with refreshing ice and a wide
variety of flavors, such as fresh lemon and orange, which are highly favored by Indonesian consumers  [9]. With
its unique brand identity and refreshing product lineup, Es Teh Meriah is positioned as an exceptionally appealing
business concept, especially in a bustling, crowded environment like the Polbeng Business Expo where visitors
are constantly seeking refreshing beverages [10].
The second business under study is "Young Seafood," a popular culinary enterprise located on Jalan Antara Ujung.
This brand offers an appetizing selection of seafood -based snacks, including savory fishcakes (otak -otak),
dumplings, and other seafood variants, served either baked or deep-fried to perfection. By focusing on high-quality
raw materials, attractive presentation, and customizable portion sizes, Young Seafood has successfully built a loyal
customer base among local snack lovers, making it an excell ent candidate to demonstrate the power of targeted
promotional strategies at the expo [11].
This research focuses specifically on how customized digital marketing strategie s can be designed and executed
for both Es Teh Meriah and Young Seafood to drive customer interest and maximize sales at the Polbeng Business
Expo. The author chose these two specific MSMEs to help them gain wider recognition within the Bengkalis
community while boosting their profitability [12]. Ultimately, the success of this project is expected to yield mutual
benefits, paving the way for the author’s academic success while generating a positive, tangible economic impact
on the business owners and the wider community [13].
To ensure the study remains highly structured and focused, two primary business challenges have been identified
regarding the growth and operational management of Es Teh Meriah and Young Seafood during the event. The
first challenge addresses how these MSMEs can sys tematically operate, manage, and structure their day -to-day
financial statements [14]. The second challenge focuses on how to run and optimize the actual business operations
of these MSMEs efficiently, ensuring smooth workflow, inventory management, and customer service throughout
the duration of the exhibition [15].
To address the identified chall enges, the project outlines several specific, measurable objectives designed to
upgrade the operational and marketing capabilities of both businesses. Specifically, the project aims to create
professional company profile videos and produce 60 digital marke ting videos (30 for each business) to enhance
their online promotional campaigns. Additionally, the author will design 60 comprehensive product catalogs,
compile structured bookkeeping reports before the expo, prepare detailed final financial statements, and implement
QRIS digital payment systems to facilitate seamless, cashless transactions [15].
This project carries immense value for all involved stakeholders, offering the author a chance to apply academic
knowledge in a real -world setting, providing MSMEs with operational innovations, and strengtheni ng Polbeng’s
reputation as an institution that supports local economic development. This three -day collaborative project is
scheduled to take place during the even semester in May 2025. The entire event will be officially hosted in the
highly accessible an d strategic courtyard of the Commercial Administration Building at Bengkalis State
Polytechnic [16].

3.5. Project Reporting plan
The Polbeng Business Expo Chapter II, themed "Local Innovation, Global Impact," was successfully held over
three consecutive days from May 20 to 22, 2025, to foster cross -sector synergy among educational institutions,
industry, and MSMEs while developing the sustainable creative economy. Serving as a strategic platform to
showcase local products to enthusiastic visitors and s tudents, the lively event officially commenced with a
traditional welcoming dance performed by students from the International Business Administration program. This
cultural performance set a respectful and hospitable tone, warmly receiving invited guests,  MSME participants,
and all attendees to an event dedicated to driving local innovation with a global impact.
The Expo faced several operational challenges, including spatial constraints that hindered layout optimization and
limited promotional efforts that resulted in lower external participation. Delayed sponsorship proposals reduced
potential funding, while grouping MSME tenants by mentors rather than product types led to redundant offerings
and unfair internal competition. Furthermore, schedu ling conflicts with lecture hours and placing competitions
away from the main stage reduced the overall effectiveness and visitor appeal of the event.
To overcome these challenges in future Expos, the committee plans to secure more strategic locations, int ensify
promotional activities, and accelerate the submission of sponsorship proposals. Operationally, MSMEs will be
grouped by product type to prevent redundancy, event schedules will be adjusted to avoid class conflicts, and all
competitions will be centr alized on the main stage. These improvements will be backed by stronger internal
communication and team management.
To increase event visibility and public awareness, the committee actively collaborated with the media by sending
direct invitations and offe ring interview opportunities. This media coverage effectively highlighted the Expo's
objectives, the MSME products, and the various student competitions. As a result, the event successfully
strengthened its public image, establishing itself not just as a business exhibition, but as a recognized platform for
community empowerment.
A comprehensive and transparent financial report was compiled to track all monetary transactions, comparing the
initial budget against actual realizations. The total revenue  sourced from committee dues, registration fees, and
sponsorships amounted to IDR 90,509,000. Meanwhile, total operational expenses across all divisions (including
events, equipment, security, consumption, and side competitions) reached IDR 85,558,435. The projec t
successfully concluded with a remaining surplus of IDR 4,950,565, which was distributed equally among the

The research on the MSMEs Teh Es Meriah and Young Seafood at the Polbeng Business Expo Chapter II
concludes that b oth profile catalogs and digital marketing significantly influence visitors' purchasing decisions,
especially when combined. The implementation of supporting business tools, such as digital records and the QRIS
payment system, further aided their operation al management. While the expo served as a strategic platform that
provided essential non -financial benefits like expanded brand visibility for both MSMEs, the financial impacts
varied. Teh Es Meriah experienced a significant revenue increase due to good pr oduct exposure, whereas Young
Seafood faced a decline caused by intense competition and suboptimal booth placement.  Based on these findings,
it is recommended that the MSMEs continuously update their promotional catalogs and digital marketing content
while maintaining systematic financial records. The expo organizers are advised to hold the event regularly and
provide specialized training in digital marketing and financial management to optimize benefits. Furthermore, the
State Polytechnic of Bengkalis is encouraged to keep supporting student involvement in real -world business
