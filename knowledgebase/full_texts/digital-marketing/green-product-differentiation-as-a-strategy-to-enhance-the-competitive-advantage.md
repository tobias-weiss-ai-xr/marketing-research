ABSTRACT

Waste-based handicraft SMEs face increasing challenges in sustaining competitive advantage despite
the growing demand for environmentally friendly products. This study aims to formulate a sustainable
product differentiation strategy for KJL Art & Craft through an analysis of its internal and external
business environment using the Internal Factor Evaluation (IFE), External Factor Evaluation (EFE),
and Internal–External (IE) matrix. A descriptive case study approach was employed, with data collected
through semi-structured interviews, observation, documentation, digital media review, and literature
analysis. The results show an IFE score of 3.41 and an EFE score of 2.71, positioning the business in
Cell IV (Grow  and Build) of the IE matrix. These findings indicate strong internal capabilities and a
moderate ability to respond to external opportunities and challenges. The recommended strategy
focuses on sustainable product differentiation by leveraging recycled ma terials, distinctive product
designs, and artisan craftsmanship, supported by green digital marketing, product innovation, market
development, and internal capacity enhancement. This study contributes practical strategic
recommendations for waste -based han dicraft SMEs seeking to strengthen their sustainable
competitive advantage in an increasingly competitive market.
Keywords: SMEs, Sustainable Competitive Advantage, Product Differentiation, Green Digital
Marketing, IE Matrix.

INTRODUCTION

The role of small and medium -sized enterprises
(SMEs) in the Indonesian economy has become
increasingly significant, with the number of SMEs
reaching 30.19 million as of 31 October 2025.
West Java recorded the largest number of SMEs,
with approximately 5.4  million enterprises.
According to Biro DTI Kementerian UMKM
Republik Indonesia (2025), SMEs play a crucial
role in driving economic growth, creating
employment opportunities, and preserving local
products.
Despite their significant role, SMEs face
complex challenges arising from both internal and
external business environments. These
challenges primarily relate to human resources,
marketing capabilities, particularly digital
marketing capabilities, access to capital, product-
related issues, infrastructure, and various other
constraints. In response to these challenges, a
study found that SMEs have the capability to
create product variations, products innovations,
and branding strategies. The study also revealed
that SMEs actively market their products, remain
open to adopting new approaches, and are
oriented toward sustainable product development
(Retnaningsih et al., 2024).
The ability to compete effectively is essential
for SMEs. Various marketing strategies have
been shown to enhance competitiveness and
increase sales performance. Several studies
have demonstrated that the use of digital
platforms can minimize marketing cha llenges
faced by SMEs, as reported by Partini and
Mulyani (2024), while also contributing to
increased sales, as evidenced in the study by
Partini (2024). Furthermore, product designs that
align with market trends, incorporate ergonomic
features, and are p roduced efficiently have been
found to expand market segments and increase
sales revenue by up to 50% (Maryam et al.,
2025).

KJL Art & Craft SMEs also face competition in
the creative and handicraft industry. Established
in 2019, KJL Art & Craft is an enterprise located
in Wantilan Village, Cipeundeuy District, Subang
Regency, West Java, Indonesia.  It was initiated
by a creative community, one of whose members
is Mr. Yoyo Taryono. The enterprise operates in
the creative industry by processing and utilizing
waste materials to produce artistic, functional,
and economically valuable products. Its primary
raw materials consist of discarded PVC pipes and
wood. Initially producing decorative lamps made
from PVC pipes, the enterprise has since
expanded its product portfolio to include a wide
range of other creative products. Various
marketing initiatives, particularly digital marketing,
have been implemented through social media and
participation in numerous exhibitions. The
enterprise serves both B2B and B2C markets
domestically and in several international markets,
although domestic demand remains its primary
market. Its current produc tion capacity reaches
approximately 500 units per month. KJL adopts
an environmentally friendly concept based on the
3R principles (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle) and
possesses competitive strengths in handmade
products, distinctive designs, and customized
services tailored to customer needs. In addition to
achieving profitability, KJL Art & Craft SMEs also
generate social and environmental benefits
through waste utilization and community
empowerment. These characteristics are
described in Facebook Kabisa Jalma Le utik
(2025), a blog authored by Nurtyalndi (2023), and
the Instagram account bahyono_kjl (2023).
Although KJL Art & Craft SMEs have undertaken
various efforts, including participation in regional,
national, and international events, they have not
yet achieved a sustainable competitive advantage
in either domestic or international markets.
Creativity in handicraft products remains an
important factor, even in traditional handicraft
industries that are considered part of ancestral
heritage (Fetzer, 2025).
Creativity is an important element in product
innovation to strengthen competitiveness. It is
also a key component of a product differentiation
strategy. Differentiation represents the third
generic strategy within Porter’s Generic
Strategies framework. Ac cording to Porter, as
cited in David (2011), differentiation is a strategy
that offers products or services that are unique
compared with competing products within the
same industry. However, since the handicraft
industry is often closely linked to cultural heritage,
it is important for SMEs operating in this sector to
address sustainability aspects (Harsanto et al.,
2026). These sustainability aspects are
increasingly perceived as important. For instance,
several national media outlets have highlighted
sustainability issues, particularly those related to
waste reduction activities. Moreover, in
developing countries, practical implementation
remains far from the expectations set by global

KJL Art & Craft SMEs are highly relevant in

The increasing awareness of sustainability issues

A study explains that green digital marketing

sustainability. In relation to KJL Art & Craft SMEs,
this is considered relevant as it can also be
adopted in the form o f a green digital marketing
strategy (Wangsa, 2026).
Another study explains that differentiation
strategy can enhance competitive advantage.
This study also emphasizes product design
uniqueness and added value, which are highly
relevant for KJL Art & Craft SMEs, particularly in
relation to environmentally fr iendly products as
part of their brand image (Sulastri et al., 2025).
The importance of the differentiation strategy and
uniqueness is not only relevant for SMEs but also
for large-scale technology companies (Arts et al.,
2021). According to David (2011), superior
differentiation must be unique, and such
uniqueness should be difficult for competitors to
imitate, while also being accompanied by other
competitive advantages.
According to Barney (1991), the Resource -
Based View (RBV) theory argues that internal
performance plays a more decisive role in
strengthening competitive advantage compared
to external factors. These internal resources
consist of three main categories, namely physical
resources, human resources, and operational
resources. Furthermore, Barney (1991) explains
four empirical indicators of such resources,
namely valuable, rare, imperfectly imitable, and
non-substitutable (VRIN). The underlying
assumptions of th is theory are that strategic
resources are heterogeneously distributed across
firms and remain relatively stable over time.
In addition to the RBV concept, strategy
formulation becomes more appropriate when a
company is able to analyze both its internal and
external environments using the Internal Factor
Evaluation (IFE), External Factor Evaluation
(EFE), and Internal -External (IE) Matrix
developed by David (2011).
If a strategy based on the Resource -Based
View (RBV) concept is applied to SMEs, in this
study specifically KJL Art & Craft SMEs, by
adopting a differentiation strategy and green
digital marketing, it is expected that SMEs will
achieve sustainable competit ive advantage by
considering both their internal and external
environmental conditions.

Previous studies have extensively discussed
SME development strategies using the
differentiation concept; however, in Indonesia,
there are still limited studies that integrate this
concept with the Resource -Based View (RBV),
green digital marketing, and su stainability, along
with an analysis of internal and external factors.
Existing studies have mostly focused on
examining causal relationships between
variables. In addition, research on KJL Art & Craft
SMEs as an enterprise that uses waste as its
main raw material remains limited; therefore,
further research is needed to provide
recommendations that are aligned with its
business characteristics.
Based on these issues, an important question
arises regarding how the internal and external
strategic factors of KJL Art & Craft SMEs can be
systematically identified and analyzed to
formulate an appropriate green product
differentiation strategy. Furtherm ore, it is
necessary to determine how this strategy can
strengthen the sustainable competitive advantage
of waste -based handicraft SMEs in an
increasingly competitive business environment.
To address this research problem, the objective
of this study is to  analyze internal and external
factors using the IFE, EFE, and IE matrixes as a
basis for recommending a green product
differentiation strategy to strengthen the
competitive advantage of KJL Art & Craft SMEs.

METHOD

This study uses a descriptive approach and a
case study of KJL Art & Craft SMEs, located in
Subang Regency, West Java. The SME uses
industrial waste as its main raw material. The
researcher formulates its development strategy.
The population in this study consists of
individuals who understand the operations and
history of KJL Art & Craft SMEs. The data
collection techniques include semi -structured
interviews, direct observation, documentation of
business activities, digital new spapers, social
media, and literature studies on KJL Art & Craft
SMEs, strategic management, differentiation,
green digital marketing, competitive advantage,
and SWOT analysis.
The instruments used in this study include
interview guidelines, observation sheets,
documentation records, and assessment sheets
listing internal and external strategic factors.
The data analysis technique begins with the
identification of internal and external factors.
Internal factors consist of strengths and
weaknesses, while external factors consist of
opportunities and threats. The next step involves
assigning weights and rat ings to obtain the total
scores for each internal and external factor. The
weights and ratings are determined by the
researcher based on the analysis and synthesis
of data obtained from interviews, observations,

RESULTS and DISCUSSION

Based on the results of interviews conducted and
various related literature, the author identified at
least five key opportunities and four main threats
as part of the external factors. After inputting the
data and performing scoring to assess the level of
influence on the company and its ability to
respond, the total EFE (External Factor
Evaluation) score obtained was 2.71. This
indicates that KJL Art & Craft SMEs are a ble to
respond to their external environment fairly well.
Meanwhile, the IFE score was 3.41. The IFE
score reflects the position or response of KJL Art
& Craft SMEs to internal factors. A score of 3.41
indicates that the internal condition of KJL Art &
Craft SMEs is relatively strong. Six strengths and
four weaknesses were identified. This position is

Based on the IE matrix presented in Table 2,
the position of KJL Art & Craft SMEs is located in
cell IV. Referring to David (2011), this cell falls
within quadrant 1 (cells I, II, and IV), which
represents the grow and build category. Each
quadrant is asso ciated with appropriate types of
strategies.
The strategy that is relevant to these
analytical results is an intensive strategy. Within
the intensive strategy, there are three strategic
options, namely market penetration, market
development, and product development. Product
development strategy inclu des differentiation
strategy.
The results of the analysis of internal factors
in the form of strengths indicate that KJL Art &
Craft SMEs have unique products made from
waste materials based on the 3R principles
(Reduce, Reuse, Recycle), maintain active social
media channels, have enga ged in export
activities, possess skilled human resources or
artisans, establish cooperation with the
government, and actively participate in various
SME exhibition events. These internal strengths
are difficult for competitors to imitate and are
consistent with Barney’s (1991) Resource-Based
View (RBV) theory, which explains that a firm’s
competitive advantage is stronger when it is
based on valuable, rare, and non -substitutable
internal resources.
Based on these findings, the appropriate
recommendation is that KJL Art & Craft SMEs

In response to external factors, particularly

This approach is aligned with the concept of green

The existing export experience can also be
strengthened by expanding market networks,
participating more frequently in international
exhibitions, and optimizing the use of global
marketplaces.
In addition to opportunities, KJL Art & Craft
SMEs also face several external factors classified
as threats, including an abundance of handicraft
products in the market, changing consumer
preferences, declining purchasing power, and the
emergence of similar imported products offered at
lower prices. These external factors indicate that
a cost leadership strategy based on low prices will
be increasingly difficult for KJL Art & Craft SMEs
to implement. The appropriate strategic choice is
to maintain competiti veness through product
differentiation by offering products with
uniqueness and added value that are difficult for
competitors to imitate, in line with Porter’s (1985)
theory.
Based on the above discussion, the results of
the internal and external factor analysis using the
IFE, EFE, and IE matrices indicate that a
differentiation strategy is the most appropriate
alternative for KJL Art & Craft SMEs. Unique
product design, the use of waste materials as raw
inputs, handmade production processes, the
availability of customized product services, and a
product image strengthened through green digital
marketing as a sustainable brand are expected to
expand market reach while simultaneo usly
enhancing competitive advantage.

CONCLUSION

In The results of the analysis using the IFE, EFE,
and IE matrices indicate that the company is
positioned in Cell IV (grow and build), suggesting
that an intensive strategy is the most appropriate
strategic option. The findings also show that KJL
Art & Craft SMEs have a strong internal condition
and a relatively good ability to respond to external
environmental changes. A sustainability -based
differentiation strategy is recommended as the
primary strategic direction, supported by product
uniqueness derived from waste materials, skilled

green digital marketing, market expansion, and
strengthening internal capacity are required to

The limitations of this study lie in its case
study approach, which focuses on a single SME;
therefore, the findings cannot be generalized to all
handicraft SMEs. In addition, the weighting and
rating in the IFE and EFE matrices were
determined based on the researcher’s analysis of
interview results, observations, documentation,

degree of subjectivity in the assessment process.
Future research is expected to involve a
larger number of SMEs as research objects or
conduct comparative analyses across regions or
creative industry sectors. The combination of

future studies is expected to test the effectiveness
of implementing differentiation strategies in

competitive advantage.
