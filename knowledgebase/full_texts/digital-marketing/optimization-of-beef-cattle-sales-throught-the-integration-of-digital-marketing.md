ABSTRACT
This study aims to analyze the optimization of beef cattle sales through the integration of digital marketing strategies
and a pre -order system, compare sales performance, and evaluate supply chain efficiency and business financial
feasibility at PS Tunas Muda, Tu ban Regency. The research method used was descriptive quantitative with a data
collection period from December 2025 to January 2026. Data analysis used the Independent T-Test to compare sales
volume and financial feasibility analysis using the indicators of R/C Ratio, B/C Ratio, and Break Even Point (BEP).
The results showed a significant increase in sales volume from 14 heads (conventional) to 23 heads (digital pre-order)
with a significance value of 0.015 (p < 0.05). Financial analysis recorded a profit o f IDR 47,129,646 with an R/C
Ratio of 1.08 and a B/C Ratio of 0.08. The pre -order system successfully shortened the supply chain, resulting in a
farmer’s share of 100% with a marketing margin of 0%. The integration of pre -order-based digital marketing
effectively increased sales volume and created economic efficiency by eliminating the role of intermediaries in
livestock distribution.

Keywords: Digital marketing, Pre-order, Beef cattle, Sales volume, Financial feasibility.

INTRODUCTION
The beef cattle farming sector in Tuban Regency is a strategic pillar of the East Java economy, with a livestock
population reaching 239,263 heads in 2023. This great potential is often hindered by inefficiencies in the conventional
marketing chain dominated by middlemen or br okers. The traditional system creates information asymmetry that
weakens the bargaining position of farmers, where profit margins are enjoyed more by intermediaries than by the
main producers.
The shift in consumer behavior toward a digital ecosystem in Tuban, with internet penetration reaching 60.08%,
opens opportunities for supply chain de -intermediation. The adoption of technologies such as WhatsApp Business
and Facebook is not merely a trend, but an urgent necessity to build digital trust through transp arency in livestock
specifications. In the conventional system, price uncertainty and stock risk burden farmers. In contrast, the pre-order
system offers market certainty through initial payment commitments and transparency of livestock visual data.
This study evaluates the implementation of the pre-order system at PS Tunas Muda as a model for transforming
community agribusiness. With a Down Payment (DP) mechanism of 20–30% and formal contracts (MOU), farmers
can optimize capital and stock management. The main focus of this study is to provide empirical evidence on whether
the integration of digital marketing can have a statistically significant impact on sales volume and improve financial
efficiency through the elimination of intermediary margins.

RESEARCH METHOD
Research Design
This study used a descriptive quantitative approach to measure the phenomenon of transition from conventional
marketing to digital marketing based on a pre -order system. This method allows precise comparative analysis of
business performance variables.

Time and Place of Research
The research was conducted at PS Tunas Muda, Tasikmadu Village, Palang District, Tuban  Regency. Primary
data collection was carried out during the period of December 2025 (conventional marketing) to January 2026 (pre-
order-based digital marketing).

Research Target/Subject
The research subject was the fattening business unit of PS Tunas Muda , focusing on superior beef cattle
commodities such as Simmental, Limousin, Ongole Crossbreed (PO), and Madura cattle.

Research Procedure
The research procedure included:
1. Identification of production costs and initial sales volume,
2. Implementation of digital content strategies (education, entertainment, promotion),
3. Application of the pre-order system with MOU and DP mechanisms,
4. Recording post-intervention sales data, and
5. Evaluation of financial feasibility.

Instruments and Data Collection Techniques
Primary data were collected through in -depth interviews with business owners and direct observation of
engagement activities on Facebook and WhatsApp Business. Research instruments included interview guidelines and
recording sheets for sales volume and operational costs.

Data Analysis Technique
Data were analyzed using the Independent T-Test through SPSS software to test the significance of differences
in sales volume. Financial feasibility analysis was conducted by calculating production costs, revenue, profit, R/C
Ratio, B/C Ratio, and Break Even Point (BEP).

An in-depth analysis of the B/C Ratio value (0.08) indicates that although this business is profitable, the obtained
margin is relatively thin. This is due to the dominance of variable costs, which reached 97% of total production costs,
especially for feeder cattle purchases and feed. Digital marketing is effective in solving sales volume problems, but
net profit efficiency remains highly vulnerable to feed price fluctuations in the market.

The digital pre -order strategy successfully shortened the distribution chain, allowing farmers to fully enjoy
consumer prices (Farmer’s Share 100%). Consumer trust was built through visual content showcasing the physical
performance of superior cattle. Video uploads of Simmental cattle weighing up to 1,150 kg or muscular Limousin
cattle through Facebook Pages provided tangible proof of quality to prospective buyers. This was reinforced by the
MOU mechanism and a 20–30% DP , which institutionalized trust between both parties.

CONCLUSION
Digital transformation through the pre -order system at PS Tunas Muda has proven t o be an effective de -
intermediation instrument in the beef cattle agribusiness. Statistically, this system significantly increased sales
volume (p = 0.015) and economically achieved a Farmer’s Share of 100% with an R/C Ratio of 1.08. Although sales
volume increased, the profit margin (B/C 0.08) remains vulnerable to high variable production costs. Therefore,
consistency in uploading educational content and engagement management must be accompanied by feed
procurement efficiency to improve long-term profitability.
