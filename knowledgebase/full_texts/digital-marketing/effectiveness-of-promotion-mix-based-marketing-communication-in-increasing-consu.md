ABSTRACT

Marketing communication has become an essential strategy for improving customer
engagement and maintaining competitiveness in the automotive service industry. This study
aims to evaluate the effectiveness of marketing communication implemented by Planet Ba n
Pademangan Branch, North Jakarta, from the perspective of consumers. A quantitative
descriptive research design was employed using a saturated sampling technique involving 37
customers. Primary data were collected through structured questionnaires using a five -point
Likert scale, while supporting data were obtained from company documents and relevant
literature. The study assessed four dimensions of the promotion mix, namely direct marketing,
sales promotion, advertising, and personal selling. Data were analyzed  through tabulation and
the calculation of the average score for each indicator using the Stanford Labovitz method. The
findings reveal that the overall effectiveness of marketing communication achieved an average
score of 3.99, indicating a Good  level of effectiveness. Among the four dimensions, sales
promotion recorded the highest performance, with attractive tire purchase promotions obtaining
the highest score (4.22), followed by personal selling. Conversely, telephone -based customer
service communication received the lowest score (3.59), suggesting the need for service quality
improvement. Overall, the integration of promotion mix strategies has effectively supported
customer communication and strengthened positive consumer perceptions. The stu dy
recommends enhancing telephone communication quality and optimizing digital marketing
channels, particularly WhatsApp and Instagram, to improve customer experience, strengthen
long-term relationships, and increase marketing communication effectiveness.

Keywords: Marketing Communication Effectiveness; Promotion Mix; Direct Marketing; Sales
Promotion; Advertising; Personal Selling

THEORETICAL STUDIES

Marketing Communication
Marketing communication is a key strategic element in marketing that works to build
relationships between a company and its customers by sharing information, shaping
perceptions, and persuading people about the products and services offered. According to
Kotler and Keller (2022), marketing communication isn’t just aimed at boosting sales; it’s also
about creating customer value, building a brand image, and maintaining long-term relationships
with customers. Digital transformation has changed marketing communication from a one-way
communication pattern to an interactive two -way communication. Companies now utilize
various digital channels such as social media, mobile applications, websites, and marketplaces
to create a more personalized customer experience. S hankar et al. (2022) explain that the
digitalization of marketing communication has shifted the company's focus from message
delivery to creating customer experience through continuous digital interactions. The study
indicates that the effectiveness of mar keting communication is influenced by the company's
ability to integrate technology, content, and customer interactions.

Integrated Marketing Communication (IMC)
 Integrated Marketing Communication (IMC) is a strategic approach that integrates all
marketing communication activities so that a company can deliver a consistent message across
all customer touchpoints. Belch and Belch (2024) state that IMC is a process o f planning,
implementing, and evaluating marketing communications carried out in a coordinated manner
in order to achieve optimal communication impact. The development of IMC research over the
past five years shows a paradigm shift from merely media integr ation to the integration of
customer experience. Meng Qi et al. (2025), through bibliometric analysis, found that IMC
research is evolving towards digital media integration, customer engagement, brand equity,
artificial intelligence, and omnichannel communication as dominant themes for future research.
Jamira (2026) added that IMC in the digital era is no longer only oriented towards
message consistency, but also towards the synchronization of all communication channels,
resulting in a seamless customer experience. The integration of communication with
omnichannel strategies becomes an important foundation in enhancing the effectiveness of
digital marketing. Rana and Mandagie  (2026) demonstrated that IMC is a strategic capability

Promotion Mix
Promotion mix is a combination of various marketing communication tools used by a
company to achieve marketing objectives effectively. According to Kotler and Armstrong
(2023), the promotion mix consists of advertising, sales promotion, personal selling, p ublic
relations, and direct marketing. The development of digital technology has caused the
promotion mix to no longer be seen as a standalone activity, but rather as a communication
system integrated with digital media, customer analytics, and customer re lationship
management (CRM). This integration enables companies to deliver messages that are more
personal, relevant, and targeted. Research by Silaningsih and Setiawan (2026) indicates that the
implementation of all elements of the promotion mix in an integrated manner is able to enhance
the effectiveness of marketing communications and support the company's sales performance.
These findings reinforce the importance of coordination among the elements of the promotion
mix compared to partial application.

Direct Marketing
 Direct marketing is direct communication between a company and its customers through
media such as WhatsApp, telephone, email, and digital applications. According to Kotler and
Keller (2022), direct marketing allows companies to obtain feedback quickly, ma king
communication more personal and effective. Ismaeel et al. (2025) found that direct marketing
is the most influential component of IMC on purchasing decisions because it allows companies
to convey relevant information according to customer needs. Perso nal communication
increases trust and accelerates the consumer decision-making process.

Marketing Communication Effectiveness

success in delivering marketing messages so that the information can be received, understood,
and elicit responses in accordance with the company's objectives. In a qualitative approach, the
effectiveness of marketing communication is not only viewed from the increase in sales
volume, but also from the clarity of the message, consumer understanding, customer responses,
quality of interaction, trust, and the suitability of the communication media used. This approach
allows researchers to gain an in -depth understanding of how a company plans, delivers, and
evaluates its marketing communication.
 Mustaqim and Endarti (2024) used a qualitative approach to analyze  the effectiveness
of marketing communication through the product knowledge of marketing personnel. The study
indicated that mastery of product information, the ability to explain product benefits, and the
quality of interactions by marketing personnel play a role in building consumer trust. Research
data were obtained through interviews and the review of various supporting sources. The study
demonstrated that the effectiveness of marketing communication can be understood through
the experiences, understand ing, and behaviors of the parties involved in the communication

RESEARCH METHODS

This study uses a quantitative approach with a descriptive method, aimed at describing
the level of effectiveness of marketing communication implemented by Planet Ban
Pademangan Branch, North Jakarta, based on consumer perceptions. The quantitative approach
was chosen because this study focuses on measuring respondents' responses to marketing
communication activities through a questionnaire instrument structured systematically.
According to Sugiyono  (2022), quantitative research is a research method based on the
philosophy of positivism to examine a specific population or sample using research instruments
and statistical data analysis to depict the phenomenon being studied.   The research location
was conducted at Planet Ban Pademangan Branch, North Jakarta. The research object focused
on the effectiveness of marketing communications implemented by the company through four
dimensions of the promotion mix, namely direct marketing, sales promotion, advertising, and
personal selling. These four dimensions were chosen because they are the main communication
media used by Planet Ban in conveying product information, building relationships with

consistency of service to customers.
The sales promotion dimension received the highest rating compared to the other
dimensions. All indicators fell into the good to very good category, with scores ranging from

of 4.22, making it the only indicator in the very good category. These findings indicate that
consumers responded very positively to the promotional programs offered by Planet Ban. The
promotional programs have been proven to increase consum er appeal and encourage
purchasing decisions. Promotions for app payments, engine services, and spare parts also
received good ratings, indicating that the promotional strategies have successfully enhanced
consumers' positive perception of the company.

DISCUSSION

The research results indicate that Planet Ban's marketing communication has been carried
out effectively, with an average score of 3.99. These findings support the concept of Integrated
Marketing Communication (IMC), which states that the success of market ing communication
is greatly influenced by a company's ability to integrate various communication media so that
the messages received by consumers become consistent (Kotler & Keller, 2022; Belch & Belch,
2024). The success of the sales promotion dimension indicates that providing short -term
incentives remains a highly effective strategy in the automotive retail industry. Promotional
programs such as tire purchase discounts, service promotions, and payments via ap plications
are able to enhance perceived value, thereby encouraging consumer interest in making
purchases. This finding aligns with the research by Rana and Mandagie  (2026), which states
that integrated promotions are capable of improving purchasing decisions through the
enhancement of brand equity and customer satisfaction.
In the dimension of direct marketing, communication via WhatsApp received higher
ratings compared to communication via telephone. This condition indicates a change in
consumer behavior , where they prefer digital communication media because it is more
practical, flexible, and allows information to be conveyed in writing. This finding supports the
research of Ismaeel et al. (2025), which explains that digital media -based direct marketing is
one of the most effective components of marketing communication for enhancing the
relationship between companies and customers. Research findings also indicate that advertising
through Instagram has been able to provide information that is both communic ative and
attention-grabbing for consumers. The development of social media has transformed corporate
communication patterns from one -way communication into interactive communication.
Therefore, social media functions not only as a promotional tool but also as a medium to build
engagement with customers. These findings are consistent with the research of Shankar et al.
(2022), which explains that the effectiveness of digital marketing is greatly influenced by a
company's ability to create customer experiences through digital media.
The personal selling dimension scored highly because sales personnel were considered
capable of explaining products clearly and providing a positive experience for consumers. This

CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

Conclusion
The results of this study indicate that the marketing communication activities
implemented by Planet Ban Pademangan Branch, North Jakarta, have been categorized as
effective. This is reflected in the overall average score of 3.99, which falls within the Go od
category. The findings show that the four dimensions of the promotion mix, namely direct
marketing, sales promotion, advertising, and personal selling, have generally been well
received by consumers.
Among all indicators, the highest score was obtained by the indicator of attractive tire
purchase promotion, with a score of 4.22, categorized as Very Good. This result confirms that
sales promotion, particularly tire purchase promotions, is the most effec tive marketing
communication activity in attracting consumer interest. In addition, personal selling also
showed strong performance, especially in providing added value to the consumer experience
through direct interaction with sales staff.
 Advertising through Instagram was also considered effective because it was perceived
as communicative, informative, and capable of attracting consumer interest. Meanwhile, direct
marketing through WhatsApp received a better response than communication thr ough
telephone. The lowest score was found in the indicator of providing informative service
information through telephone, with a score of 3.59, although it remained within the Good
category. This finding suggests that telephone-based communication still requires improvement
in terms of clarity, consistency, and completeness of information.
Overall, the implementation of an integrated promotion mix has supported the
effectiveness of marketing communication at Planet Ban Pademangan Branch. However,
continuous improvement is still needed, particularly in direct communication channels, to
ensure that customers receive consistent, clear, and responsive information across all media.

Recommendations
Planet Ban Pademangan  Branch is advised to maintain and strengthen sales promotion
programs, particularly tire purchase promotions, because this activity obtained the highest
consumer response. Promotional programs should be developed regularly with clear terms,
relevant benefits, and attractive communication materials to encourage repeat purchases. The
company should improve the quality of telephone -based customer service by preparing
communication guidelines, service scripts, and standard operating procedures. Employees who
handle telephone communication should also receive training in product knowledge,
communication skills, and complaint handling so that information can be delivered more clearly
and consistently.
The use of WhatsApp and Instagram should be optimized through faster responses,
informative content, visual promotions, service reminders, and interactive communication.
These digital channels can also be integrated with customer databases to support more
personalized marketing communication. Sales personnel should continue to receive training
because personal selling has proven to provide added value to the consumer experience.
Employees need to be encouraged to explain product benefits objectively, identi fy customer
needs accurately, and build trust without applying excessive sales pressure.
For future research, it is recommended to involve a larger number of respondents and
expand the research location to several Planet Ban branches. Further studies may also include
variables such as customer satisfaction, customer engagement, purchase intent ion, and
customer loyalty to obtain a more comprehensive understanding of the impact of marketing
communication effectiveness.
