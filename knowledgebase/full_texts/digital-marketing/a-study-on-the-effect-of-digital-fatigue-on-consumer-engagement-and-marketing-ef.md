Abstract
The widespread use of digital technology has changed the way people communicate, shop, and interact with brands.
Consumers now spend their daily lives on social networking ecosystems, and mobile applications, where they regularly
encounter advertisements, notifications, and oth er promotional content. While these social networking ecosystems
platforms make it easier for businesses to reach their target audience, constant exposure to online information can
sometimes leave consumers feeling mentally tired and less interested in engaging with digital content.
This study examines the effect of digital fatigue on consumer engagement and marketing effectiveness. It looks at the
main reasons behind digital fatigue, and how that fatigue shapes the way people respond to marketing content online.
To do this, a structured questionnaire was given to 100 respondents . Statistical tools such as Chi -Square, ANOV A,
Correlation, and Regression is used to analyze the data . The analysis points to information overload, excessive
advertising, frequent notifications, and prolonged screen exposure as the principal contributors to digital fatigue. The
study points to one clear lesson, communication that's relevant and not excessive does a lot more for engagement and
marketing performance than communication that's just frequent.
Keywords: Digital Fatigue, Consumer Engagement, Marketing Effectiveness, Attention Economy, Information
Overload, Digital Marketing, Purchase Intention.

Digital platforms have become a normal part of everyday life. People use social media, websites, and mobile apps to
communicate, shop, find information, and spend their free time. As a result, they are exposed to a constant stream of
advertisements, emails, notifications, and other online content.  While these platforms offer convenience, too much
exposure to digital content can leave people feeling mentally tired. This digital fatigue may reduce their interest in

RESEARCH GAP
While reviewing earlier studies while going through past research, a pattern showed up: these topics, digital marketing,
engagement, how individuals interact with social media being exposed to too much information at once , tend to get
studied separately rather than as a connected whole  However, a ve ry few people have done  how digital fatigue
influences consumer engagement and marketing effectiveness at  the same time. Most of the available research has
focused on these areas individually, making it hard to understand their overall connection. In recent years, the use of
digital platforms has grown rapidly in India, but research on this topic in the Indian context remains limited. Therefore,
it is important to explore how digital fatigue affects consumers' interactions with digital marketing a nd how it
influences the effectiveness of marketing efforts in the present digital environment.

IV. METHADOLOGY
This study was carried out to gain a better understanding of people's current views and behaviours in the digital space.
It looked at how feeling mentally tired from constant online activity, known as digital fatigue, affects consumers and
how digital mark eting captures and holds their attention. Information for the study was gathered through a
questionnaire from 100 regular internet users selected using the convenience sampling method.
The responses were analysed using percentage analysis, Chi -square test,  One-Way ANOV A, correlation, and
regression. These techniques helped in identifying the connections between different factors and understanding how
digital fatigue influences consumers' involvement with brands, their reactions to marketing efforts, and their purchase
decisions. The study also shed light on the main reasons behind digital fatigue and explained how these experiences
shape consumer behaviour in today's increasingly digital world.

FINDINGS
• Information overload was identified as the main reason for digital fatigue. The large amount of content availa ble
online can make it difficult for consumers to focus on or process information effectively.
• Excessive advertisements and frequent notifications were also found to contribute to feelings of online exhaustion.
Continuous interruptions can reduce consumers' attention and make online experiences less enjoyable.
• The results suggest that consumers experiencing higher levels of digital fatigue are less likely to respond positively
to digital marketing campaigns. Many tend to ignore promotional messages or p ay less attention to marketing
communications.
• Consumer engagement has a positive influence on marketing outcomes. Respondents who regularly interacted with
brand content showed a greater interest in products and a stronger intention to make purchases.

SUGGESTION
 Brands should avoid giving consumers too much information at one time. Content that is simple, useful, and easy to
follow is more likely to gain attention and keep consumers interested.
 Frequent advertisements and notifications can become annoyi ng for users. Reducing unnecessary promotional
messages may make the online experience more comfortable and enjoyable.
 Companies should focus on building long-term relationships with consumers by providing valuable and meaningful
content rather than concentrating only on promotional activities.
 Maintaining a proper balance in digital communication is important. Sharing more information may lead to a loss of
interest, while a balanced approach can help keep consumers engaged.
 Consumer feedback should be reviewed regularly. Understanding what consumers think and expect can help them
for future use and better improvement.

The study indicates that digital fatigue is becoming widely experienced by consumers who spend a considerable
amount of time on digital platforms. Constant exposure to online content, advertisements, notifications, and
promotional messages can make users feel exhausted and less interested in engaging with marketing-related content.
The analysis suggest that when consumers experience higher levels of fatigue, their attention towards online marketing
activities tends to decrease.
The analysis also indicates that consumers who frequently engage with a brand's content tend to show greater curiosity
and interest in what that brand has to offer. This highlights that meaningful engagement remains an important factor in
shaping purchasing behaviour. Rather than relying on constant promotions, businesses can benefit from delivering
content that consumers find valuable and relevant to their everyday needs. Adopting a more considerate communication
approach can strengthen brand –consumer relationships, sustain audience interest, and enhance the effectiveness of
marketing efforts over time.
