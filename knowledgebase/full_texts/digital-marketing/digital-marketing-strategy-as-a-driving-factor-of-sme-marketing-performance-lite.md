Abstract.
Digital transformation has transformed the marketing landscape of Micro, Small, and Medium
Enterprises (MSMEs), particularly in re aching consumers, building brands, and increasing sales. In an
increasingly competitive environment, the use of digital media is no longer merely an additional
promotional tool but has evolved into an integral part of marketing strategy. This study aims to  analyze
the role of digital media in marketing.digital marketing strategyas a driving factor for MSME marketing
performance through an approachliterature review. The study was conducted on scientific literature that
discusses digital marketing strategies,digital marketing capabilities, social media, marketplace,customer
engagement, market orientation, and MSME performance, with an emphasis on publications from 2022–
2026. The study results show that digital marketing strategies contribute to marketing perfo rmance by
increasing market reach,brand awareness, customer interaction, consumer acquisition and retention, and
utilization of market information. Social media,content marketing, marketplace, anddigital
advertisingDigital marketing has become the primary tool used by MSMEs. However, the success of this
strategy is determined not only by the use of technology, but also by the MSME's ability to understand
consumers, produce relevant content, utilize data, innovate, and integrate digital activities with marketing
objectives. Therefore,digital marketing strategyIt needs to be viewed as a strategic capability, not just a
promotional activity, because its effectiveness is determined by the ability of MSMEs to transform digital
resources into value for consumers and improve marketing performance.
Keywords: Digital Marketing Strategy, Digital Marketing Capabilities, Marketing Performance, MSMEs
and Customer Engagement.

I. INTRODUCTION
The development of digital technology has transformed the way companies conduct m arketing
activities. Consumers increasingly rely on the internet, social media, marketplaces, and various digital
platforms to search for information, compare products, communicate with sellers, and even conduct
transactions. These changes have created a m ore open marketing environment while increasing competition
among businesses (Wu et al., 2024; Elkhoudary et al., 2026).
These changes present both opportunities and challenges for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises
(MSMEs). On the one hand, digital tech nology enables MSMEs to reach a wider consumer base without the
need for a large physical marketing network. On the other hand, the increasing number of businesses using
digital platforms has intensified competition for consumer attention (Hazzam et al., 2 026; Lestari e t al.,
2025).
In these conditions, digital marketing is no longer simply understood as the activity of uploading
products to social media. Digital marketing strategy encompasses the process of selecting target markets,
determining platforms, developing con tent, building interactions with consumers, managing customer
relationships, and evaluating marketing results (Wu et al., 2024; Hazzam et al., 2026; Jaya et al., 2026).
Therefore, the success of digital marketing depends heavily on the ability of MSMEs to integrate technology
with marketing objectives.
Research by Wu, Botella Carrubi, and Blanco González Tejero (2024) shows that digital marketing
strategies are related to SME performance and can be explained through a digital perspective. Resource-
Based View (RBV) and Dynamic Capabilities The study also demonstrated the importance of innovation,
proactivity, and managerial skills in supporting the relationship between digital marketing strategy and
performance.
In the Indonesian context, research o nsocial media marketingResearch on MSMEs also shows that
social media utilization is linked to MSME performance. These findings demonstrate that social media

II. LIBRARY REVIEW
Digital Marketing Strategy
Digital marketing is a marketing activity that uses digital technology and channels to deliver value to
consumers and achieve marketing objectives. As it develops, digital marketing functions not only as a
promotional medium but also as a means of communication, transactions, information gathering, and
customer relationship management (Wu et al., 2024; Elkhoudary et al., 2026).
Digital marketing strategyDigital marketing can be understood as the process of planning and managing
various digital marketing activities to achieve specific marketing objectives. This strategy includes selecting
digital channels, determining consumer targets, developing content, engaging customers, u sing digit al
advertising, and measuring results.
In MSMEs, this strategy is generally realized through:
 Social media marketing, to build communication and interaction with consumers;
 Content marketing, to provide information and build product appeal;
 Marketplace/e-commerce, to expand market access and facilitate transactions;
 Digital advertising, to reach target consumers more specifically; and
 Customer relationship management, to maintain relationships with customers.
Recent studies show that the digital m arketing s trategies of MSMEs are mostly developed through
social media, marketplaces,content marketing, SEO, and digital communications.
MSME Marketing Performance
Marketing performance is the level of success a company has in achieving its marketing obje ctives. In
MSMEs, marketing performance is not only indicated by increased sales but can also be seen in customer
growth, market expansion, and customer acquisition. brand awareness, customer interaction, and the ability
to retain consumers (Jaya et al. 2026).
According to Octaviani et al. (2026), relevant marketing performance indicators in the context of
MSMEs include:

 Sales growth;
 Growth in the number of customers;
 Expansion of market reach;
 Improvementbrand awareness;
 Improvementcustomer engagement;
 Increased repeat purchases; and

Resource-Based View And Dynamic Capability
According to Wu et al. (2024), PerspectiveResource -Based Viewexplains that a company's competitive
advantage can be built through valuable and difficult -to-imitate resource s and capabi lities. In digital
marketing, technology isn't the only source of competitive advantage. The ability to produce content,
understand consumers, manage data, and build customer relationships are all strategic resources.
Meanwhile, according to On gkowijoyo et al. (2025), the perspectiveDynamic Capabilityemphasizes a
company's ability to adapt resources and strategies to environmental changes. This perspective is relevant for
MSMEs because digital trends, consumer behavior, and platform characteristics can change rapidly.
Therefore, it's not enough for MSMEs to simply have a digital account. They need to be able to read
market changes, capitalize on digital opportunities, and continuously adapt their marketing strategies.

III. METHODS
Types of research
This re search uses an approach literature review using a qualitative descriptive analysis method. This
approach was used to identify and synthesize the results of previous research on digital marketing strategies
and MSME marketing performance.
Literature Sources
Literature was obtained from national and international scientific articles relevant to the research topic.
The search was conducted using the following keywords:
 Digital Marketing Strategy;
 Digital Marketing Capabilities;
 WEPerformance;
 MSME Marketing Performance;
 Social Media Marketing MSMEs;
 Digital MarketingMSMEs; and
 Customer Engagement SMEs.
Prioritized literature is publications from 2022 –2026, especially empirical research and Systematic
Literature Review latest.
Literature Selection Criteria
The literature was selected based on several criteria, namely:
 Discussing MSMEs, MSMEs, or SMEs;
 Has a Direct Relationship With Digital Marketing;
 Discussing Marketing Performance or Business Performance;
 Published in a Verifiable Journal or Academic Source; and
 Has relevance to research objectives.
Literature that only discusses digital transformation in general without any connection to marketing and
performance is not the main focus.

Analysis Techniques
The analysis was carried out by grouping the r esults of pr evious research into main themes, namely
digital marketing strategies, market expansion, brand awareness, customer engagement, digital capabilities,
market orientation, and marketing performance. Furthermore, the research results were compared t o identify
patterns of relationships between digital marketing strategies and MSME marketing performance

Digital Marketing Strategy for MSMEs
Based on the results of the study, digital marketing strategies in MSMEs are developing through the use
of various digital channels, especially social media, marketplaces, content marketing, and digital advertising.
Social media is one of the easiest channels to use because it allows MSMEs to communicate directly with
consumers. Meanwhile, marketplaces make it easier to expand markets and conduct transactions. Content
marketingused to provide information and build brand perception, while digital advertising allows MSMEs
to reach consumers based on certain characteristics.
However, utilizing v arious platfo rms does not automatically indicate that MSMEs have an effective
digital marketing strategy. A strategy can only be considered effective if each channel is used with a clear
purpose and supports each other.
For example, social media can be use d to buildawareness And engagementThe marketplace is used to
generate transactions, while WhatsApp Business can be used to maintain customer relationships. This
integration allows each platform to serve distinct yet mutually supportive functions.
A systematic study of digital marketing for MSMEs in Indonesia shows that social media, e -commerce,
SEO, and digital collaboration are important instruments in expanding market reach and increasing consumer
engagement.
Thus, the main problem for MSMEs does not lie in the number  of platforms used, but in the
appropriateness of platform use according to marketing objectives.
Digital Marketing Strategyin Expanding Market Reach
One of the main contributions of digital marketing to marketing performance is its ability t o expand
market reach (Khan & Kansal, 2026; Tatik & Setiawan, 2025). Conventional marketing tends to rely on
business location and distribution networks. Digital marketing reduces these limitations because products can
be displayed to consumers outside the MSME's geographic area.
Marketplaces allow products to be discovered by consumers who previously wouldn't have access to
them. Social media also allows MSME content to be shared with other users, allowing information to grow
organically.
Expanding market reach is important because the greater the number of consumers who know a product,
the greater the opportunity for MSMEs to acquire new customers.
However, market reach shouldn't be viewed as an end in itself. Reach must translate into customer

Brand awarenessis one of the important outcomes of digital marketing activities (Kanthi et al., 2026).
Consumers tend to be more likely to consider p roducts they ar e familiar with than products they are

consistency can help shape consumer perceptions of the brand.
Content marketingalso plays a crucial role. Informative and relevant content can help consumers not
only learn about a product but also understand its value. Therefore, MSMEs should not u se all content as
direct advertising. Educational content, information, testimonials,storytelling, product usage demonstrations,
and customer experiences can be used to build rapport with consumers.

Digital marketing enables MSMEs to reach consumers outside their operational areas.

Digital interactions enable MSMEs to build closer communication with customers.
4. Increased Customer Acquisition.
Easy-to-find product information increases the chances of new consumers making a purchase.
5. Increased Customer Retention.
Communication through digital media allows MSMEs to maintain relationships with consumers after
transactions.

Market reach,awareness, engagement, and customer relationships can ultimately increase the chances of a
transaction occurring.

Factors That Determine the Effectiveness of Digital Marketing
According to Lestari et al. (2025) and Kanthi et al. (2026), although digital marketing offers significant
opportunities, the results achieved by each MSME can vary. These differe nces are influence d by several
factors.
1. Digital Literacy
Digital literacy determines the ability of MSMEs to select and utilize digital platforms. Low literacy
often limits technology use to basic activities.
2. Content Quality
Content is a crucial part of digital communication. It must be relevant, engaging, consistent, and aligned
with the characteristics of your target consumers.

V. CONCLUSION
Based on the results literature review, it can be concluded that digital marketing strategy is one of the
factors that can encourage increased marketing performance of MSMEs. Digital marketing strategies provide
opportunities for MSMEs to expand market reach, increase brand awareness, build customer engagement,
acquire new customers, and retain existing customers.
The most widely used st rategies include social media  marketing, content marketing , marketplace,
anddigital advertising However, the presence of multiple digital channels does not automatically translate
into better performance. Their effectiveness depends on MSMEs' ability to integrate technology with market
needs and marketing objectives.
Literature shows that digital marketing capability is a crucial factor in explaining this success. MSMEs
that are able to understand consumers, produce relevant content, manage customer relationships, utilize data,
innovate, and adapt strategies to market changes have a greater chance of achieving better marketing
performance.
Thus, digital marketing needs to be positioned not merely as a promotional tool, but as a business
strategy and capability that supports the creation of customer value and improves the marketing performance
of MSMEs.
For MSMEs, digital marketing implementation needs to be directed toward measurable goals, not just
increasing the number of followers or po sting frequency. MSMEs n eed to choose platforms based on their
consumer characteristics, produce relevant content, build engagement, and use data to evaluate marketing
results.
For the government and institutions supporting MSMEs, digitalization programs s hould not only
provide a ccess to technology, but also improve digital literacy, content creation skills, data analysis, and
managerial skills of MSME actors.
