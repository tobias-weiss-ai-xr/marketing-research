Abstract
Marketing covers all tools and processes in reaching the right audience for products or services. Marketing strategy plays an
important role in the success of businesses. In a world that changes over time, the concept of marketing has been subjected t o
certain changes with the change in customer wants and needs. In the digitalizing world, it has become inevitable for most
businesses to adopt a digital marketing strategy. Especially for businesses that want to gain competitive advantage, it is critical
to choose the right digital marketing strategy. Since Multi -Criteria Decision Making (MCDM) methods adopt an analytical
perspective, they improve decision quality and are a powerful method for selecting the right strategy. This study reviews the
scientific literature focusing on Multi -Criteria Decision Making (MCDM) methods used in the selection of digital marketing
strategies and evaluates existing approaches. In this direction, 8 studies conducted between 2019 and 2025 were reached. The
studies were categorized in terms of the application sector, the Multi-Criteria Decision Making method used, and the selected
digital marketing strategy. According to the findings, social media marketing and viral marketing emerged as the primary
digital marketing strategies. Sw ara was selected as the most widely used method. The reasons for the prominence of these
strategies include low cost, short-term results, measurability and ease of analysis, the potential to create brand awareness, the
opportunity for interaction and feedback, and high reach. Studies have generally used a single method, whereas stronger results
can be obtained by using mixed methods.
Keywords: Digital Marketing, Digital Marketing Strategy Selection, Multi-Criteria Decision Making.

1. Introduction
Marketing 1.0 emerged with the industrial revolution. Marketing 1.0 is a product -oriented
approach. Businesses have adopted strategies to achieve higher production capacity. In the Marketing
2.0 approach, consumer wants and needs started to gain importance . Marketing strategies focused on
gaining consumer satisfaction. With Marketing 3.0, it is a human-centered approach that sees consumers
as people with emotions. In the Marketing 4.0 perspective, digital tools and technologies came to the
forefront and wer e integrated into marketing strategies. In Marketing 5.0, artificial intelligence
supported marketing has emerged. Marketing 6.0 offers consumers immersive and personalized
experiences through data-driven and technological advances. As in the development o f marketing, the
impact of technological advances in every field is increasing day by day.
Although the use of digital marketing strategies has been widely discussed in the marketing
literature, a systematic synthesis of how Multi-Criteria Decision Making (MCDM) methods have been
applied to digital marketing strategy selection has not yet been u ndertaken. Existing applications of
MCDM to this problem remain scattered across sector -specific studies (e.g., tourism, textile,
manufacturing) without a unifying synthesis of the methods used, the strategies prioritized, or the
methodological patterns across sectors. This study addresses this gap by systematically reviewing and
comparatively analyzing the existing literature on MCDM applications in digital marketing strategy
selection, thereby providing researchers and practitioners with a consolidated re ference point and
identifying methodological gaps, such as the limited use of hybrid MCDM approaches, for future
research.
2. Literature Review
Technology is changing the way people live at a high rate. In addition, the time people spend
on digital media is increasing. For example, 75% of the population in Turkey are internet users. In terms
of the number of internet users, Turkey ranks 31st in the world. For this reason, marketing activities are
now largely carried out in the digital environment. Digital marketing has become even more popular
today (Yüksel, 2020).
Digital Marketing is the whole of marketing activities carried out with the use of digital tools in
the digital environment. Especially for businesses that want to gain competitive advantage, it is critical
to choose the right digital marketing strategy. The choice of digital marketing strategies varies according
to the sector in which businesses operate, the target audience to be reached, the mission and vision, the
product offered to the market and the competitive conditions in the market (Utlu, 2023: 175). As a result
of the literature review, it is seen that various and widespread digital marketing strategies are used to
help businesses reach their target audiences quickly (Gibson, 2018:13 -15). In this study, 10 different
digital marketing strategies, including content marketing, search engine marketing (SEM), blogs, online
advertising, social media marketing, viral marketing, e -mail marketing, text messaging, affiliate
marketing and Pay Per Click (PPC), were discussed (Utlu, 2024).
Content marketing: According to the Content Marketing Institute, “content marketing is the
marketing and Business Process of creating and distributing relevant and valuable content to attract,
acquire and engage a clearly defined and understood target audience - with the goal of driving profitable
customer action” (Content Marketing Institute, 2015). According to Rowley (2008), content marketing
is a method by which a firm analyzes, identifies and satisfies consumer wants through the use of content
to generate profit. Information is an important element of marketing. Through content marketing, firms
can learn more about customers and potential customers. In addition, one of the most important elements
of content marketing is to transfer the right information to the target audience.

Businesses have to promote themselves, get their products or services accepted in the market
and gain consumer trust. Strong brands use storytelling to promote their values and communicate who
they are. In fact, content marketing is an effective tool used not only to tell the company's story but also
to build trust. In short, firms will gain consumer trust when they effectively use content marketing and
storytelling to tell their stories (Denning 2011: 47).
Search Engine Marketing (SEM) is a type of marketing that aims to rank high in search engines
and gain more visibility. Search engine marketing is a method that consists of comprehensive tools such
as internet advertising, performance analysis and website optimization, and aims to directly reach the
target audience (Scott, 2009: 254). In other words, search engine marketing aims to rank high organically
by making the necessary arrangements on the website.
Blogs: Blogs are essentially internet diaries. In this diary, blog owners share articles, videos or
images related to their fields of interest. Authors convey their views on a subject to users, while business
owners see these platforms as a tool through which they carry out their marketing activities (Utlu, 2024:
343). There are five different methods of blog marketing. First, bloggers use their own blogs to share
their opinions and make recommendations about products or services without any profit. Second,
products are sent to bloggers for free and promoted without any specific instructions or directions. Third,
bloggers are given gift vouchers or paid to make product recommendations (Patterson, 2012). Fourth,
bloggers can increase traffic by directing user s to a specific website or point of sale and receive
remuneration in return. Fifth, bloggers earn money from the banners, links or advertisements they share
in relation to the articles they write. In this step, the marketing message of businesses is clearl y
communicated to consumers (Liljander & Gummerus, 2015).  Thanks to all these effective methods,
blogs are highly preferred by businesses today.
Online advertising or display advertisements: advertisements of products or services appear as
banners, banners or pop -ups on websites with a high number of visitors. Consumers can access the
details of the advertisements by clicking on these banners (Elde n, 2015: 540). In display advertising,
consumers' interests, demographic characteristics or the digital traces they leave in the digital
environment increase businesses' ability to reach the right audience and increase advertising recycling
rates (Kotler e t al., 2021). In short, online advertising increases brand awareness and helps to
communicate the brand message effectively.
Social Media Marketing: social media is widely used by businesses. Businesses use social media
platforms to promote themselves and their products and effectively manage customer relationships.
Major brands such as Dell, IBM and Burger King make extensive u se of social media tools to conduct
their marketing activities (Saravanakumar and SuganthaLakshmi, 2012). The advantages of social media
marketing are fast and effective as well as free membership.
Viral marketing: Viral marketing is also known as buzz marketing or word-of-mouth marketing,
which enables the rapid spread of messages, information and content among consumers. In other words,
the marketing of products or services by consumers influencing each other is known as viral marketing
(Subramani & Rajagopalan, 2003). Social media tools accelerate word -of-mouth marketing by
facilitating communication between consumers (Miller & Lammas, 2010). In short, consumers trust the
comments of their peers mo re than advertisements, so they evaluate consumer comments on digital
media before purchasing products or services. This method is advantageous for marketers as it is low -
cost. Also, care is taken to ensure that there are many reviews about a product.
E-Mail Marketing: Today, e -mail has become a fundamental element of normal and business
life. Therefore, it is used in marketing to communicate with customers and promote products (Gunelius
2018). According to Mailchimp (n.d.): "Email marketing is a form o f direct marketing that uses email

3. Methodology
In this study, studies using multi-criteria decision-making method in digital marketing strategy
selection were systematically examined and classified. A literature review was conducted covering the
years 2019-2025. Google Scholar open access database was used. The following keywords were used
in Turkish and English:
“Digital Marketing Strategy”
“Digital Marketing Strategy Selection”
“Multi-Criteria Decision Making”
The studies obtained as a result of the review were examined in terms of the use of method and
subject integrity in digital marketing strategy selection and 8 studies were considered for analysis. Some
criteria were taken into consideration in the selection of the reviewed studies.
- To have evaluated, ranked or selected digital marketing strategies
- To be using one of the Multi-Criteria Decision Making methods
- Publication of the research to be after 2019
- Publication in an international conference or peer-reviewed journal
Scopus, Web of Science, and ScienceDirect were also searched using the same keywords during
the review process. However, these databases did not return the specific set of studies matching the
inclusion criteria of this review, whereas all studies meeting the criteria were accessible through Google
Scholar, which also offers broad, cross -disciplinary coverage of both journal articles and conference
proceedings (such as IEEE and ICFNDS conference proceedings). For this reason, Google Scholar was
adopted as the primary database for the present review.

The review period was set to begin in 2019 because the earliest identified study applying a
Multi-Criteria Decision Making method specifically to digital marketing strategy selection (Leung &
Mo, 2019) was published in that year; the review was extended through 2025 to capture the most recent
developments in the field.
The study selection process followed the identification, screening, eligibility, and inclusion
stages recommended by the PRISMA guidelines (Page et al., 2021). In the identification stage, two
keyword-combination searches were conducted in Google Scholar: “Digital Marketing Strategy” AND
“Multi-Criteria Decision Making” (157 records) and “Digital Marketing Strategy Selection” AND
“Multi-Criteria Decision Making” (13 records), yielding a total of 170 identified records. Because
Google Scholar ranks results by relevance and results diverge sharply from the review topic beyond the
first pages, the 60 most relevant records were screened at the title and abstract level; the remaining 110
records were excluded as clearly unrelated to the scope of this review. Duri ng screening, 3 duplicate
records were identified and removed, leaving 57 unique records for full -text eligibility assessment.
Applying the inclusion criteria listed below, 49 records were excluded (e.g., not applying an MCDM
method specifically to digital marketing strategy selection, not published in a peer-reviewed journal or
conference proceeding). As a result, 8 studies met all inclusion criteria and were retained for the final
analysis. This process is summarized in the PRISMA flow diagram presented in Figure 1.

In this study, 8 studies using Multi -Criteria Decision Making method in digital marketing
strategy selection were analyzed. The studies were categorized in terms of application area,
methodology, publication year, MCDM method used and prominent marketing strategies.
The publication year of the studies reached is 2019 -2025 and there was a concentration
especially in the period 2021-2024. This situation has emphasized the need for decision support systems
and analytical methods in the field of marketing in recent years.  In the distribution of multi -criteria
decision making methods used, Swara method was the most preferred method with 3 studies. The second
most common method is TOPSIS with 2 studies. AHP, Fuzzy AHP, ANP and SWARA + COPRAS
mixed methods have 1 study each. In the distribution of the studies according to the sector, there are 3
studies in general digital marketing, 2 in Tourism, 1 in Textile, 1 in ICO (Crypto Finance) and 1 in
production/manufacturing.
In the ranking of digital marketing strategies, social media marketing (highlighted in 5 studies),
Viral Marketing (highlighted in 2 studies), SEO/SEM (Search Engine Marketing), Email Marketing,
and Website Effectiveness were the remaining digital marketing strategies, each highlighted in only one
study. This distribution shows that Social Media Marketing is of critical importance among digital
marketing strategies.
Although Social Media Marketing and Viral Marketing dominate the findings, a critical
comparison of the reviewed studies reveals that the methodological choice appears to be influenced by
sector context rather than a single superior technique. In tourism-related studies, SWARA and its hybrid
extension (SWARA-COPRAS) were preferred, likely because SWARA's expert -weighting procedure
suits the qualitative, experience-driven nature of tourism decision-making (Esmaelnezhad et al., 2023;
Nebati, 2022). In contras t, studies addressing general digital marketing or manufacturing contexts
favored TOPSIS or AHP -based approaches, which rely more heavily on quantifiable performance
criteria (Saçan & Eren, 2022; Erik & Kuvvetli, 2025). This divergence suggests that no sin gle MCDM
method has emerged as a universal standard for digital marketing strategy selection; rather, method
choice reflects the availability of expert versus quantitative data within each sector.
A further point of divergence concerns methodological rigor: only one of the eight studies
(Nebati, 2022) combined two MCDM methods (SWARA-COPRAS), while the remaining seven relied
on a single technique. This is consistent with the broader MCDM literature,  which argues that hybrid
approaches reduce the sensitivity of results to the weighting assumptions of any one method
(Esmaelnezhad et al., 2023). The prevalence of single-method studies in this sample therefore points to
a methodological gap: current appl ications of MCDM to digital marketing strategy selection have not

5. Conclusion
In this research, studies that used Multi -Criteria Decision Making method in the selection of
digital marketing strategies were systematically analyzed. As a result of the analysis, Swara method was
selected as the most preferred method. Since the Swara method is weighting based on expert opinion, it
can create a flexible structure. Studies have generally used a single method, whereas stronger results can
be obtained by using mixed methods. Social media marketing and viral marketing were the prominent
digital marketing strategies in the studies. The reasons for the prominence of these strategies are low
cost, short time to results, measurability and ease of analysis, potential to create brand awareness,
interaction and feedback opportunities, and high reach capacity. It is known that consumers spend most
of their time on Social Media. Moreover, Social Media provides opportunities to communicate directly
with brands and comment on products or services. Viral marketing and Social Media provide high
returns at much lower costs than traditional advertising.
 Thanks to these advantages, Social Media marketing and Viral marketing strategies have
become attractive for businesses. Social Media marketing can be measured and analyzed, and Viral
marketing is seen as effective marketing strategies by businesses thank s to the ability of campaigns to
spread instantly or products to go viral quickly. Therefore, Social Media marketing advertising
expenditures are increasing more and more. In short, the change in consumer behavior with the digital
transformation and the de velopment of technological infrastructure have enabled these strategies to
come to the forefront both in literature and in practice. As a result of this research, it is recommended
that companies prioritize social media to strengthen customer interaction and increase brand awareness.
This study is subject to several limitations that should be acknowledged. First, although Scopus,
Web of Science, and ScienceDirect were also queried during the search process, the review ultimately
relied on Google Scholar as the primary database, since i t was the source that returned the specific set
of studies meeting the inclusion criteria; a protocol that combines and deduplicates results from multiple
databases within a single, uniformly applied search strategy would nonetheless offer stronger
methodological rigor for future reviews. Second, because search results were ranked by relevance, only
the 60 most relevant records were screened at the title and abstract level, which may have excluded a
small number of eligible studies ranked lower in the search results. Third, the review yielded a relatively
small final sample of eight studies, which limits the generalizability of the conclusions and precluded a
quantitative meta-analysis; as the review reflects the literature available as of 2025, studies publ ished
after this date are not included. Finally, the majority of the reviewed studies applied a single MCDM
method rather than hybrid approaches, which constrains the extent to which methodological robustness
could be compared across studies.
Future research could address these limitations in several ways. Subsequent reviews could adopt
a fully integrated multi -database search protocol, combining and deduplicating results from Scopus,
Web of Science, and ScienceDirect alongside Google Scholar within a single systematic search strategy,
to further strengthen coverage and replicability. Extending the review period beyond 2025 and applying
a full screening of all identified records, rather than a relevance-ranked subset, would further strengthen
methodological rigor. Given the limited use of hybrid MCDM approaches identified in this review,
future empirical studies are encouraged to test combined methods (e.g., SWARA -COPRAS or AHP -
TOPSIS) for digital marketing strategy selection and to validate thes e methods in real organizational
settings across a wider range of sectors beyond tourism, textile, and manufacturing. Finally, as the
