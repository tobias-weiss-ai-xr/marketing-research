Digital marketing is a widely accepted term that encompasses all online
marketing strategies, regardless of whether they are focused on attracting customers
known as inbound or pushing messages outwards known as outbound including in
the field of garment, textile and fashion. The current distinction between marketing
techniques and strategies is not made, as digital outbound strategies endeavor to
present marketing messages directly to as many people as possible within the online
space regardless of their r elevance or desirability. In contrast, inbound strategies
utilize digital marketing tools, such as online content, to engage target audiences by
offering valuable resources that guide them to websites. Digital marketing not only
facilitates the identificat ion of trends and behavioral patterns before potential
consumers make purchasing decisions, thus aiding in the design of effective
marketing campaigns and strategies, but it also highlights the limitations of
traditional offline marketing which struggles t o track consumer interactions with a
brand prior to sales engagements. Today, social networks play a crucial role in
swiftly and efficiently disseminating information or content, and consequently, in
the realms of advertising and brand development. By stra tegically launching paid
campaigns on social platforms with significant follower bases, businesses can
enhance their reach and boost sales. Furthermore, effective online performance
demands the integration of classical marketing principles, positioning int ernet
marketing as a means of transferring products or services from providers to
consumers, utilizing the internet as a promotional and sales channel165.
A rapidly growing technique in marketing is the utilization of social media,
which provides a platform for consumers to exchange text, images, audio, and video
amongst themselves or with businesses. Marketers can also participate in or create
an online community, thereby informing or enticing consumers to engage in various
activities, effectively conducting discreet marketing and lead generation.
Additionally, social media encompasses social networks internet spaces designed
for mutual connection and communication among users who are potential customers,
making them valuable for marketing objectives including the elements social media
such as of Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter and others166 167 168 169.
Although marketing specialists propose specific strategies and tactics in their
marketing plans, it is crucial to note that producers or entrepreneurs, along with those
responsible for marketing, must possess adequate knowledge and insights about the
utilization and methodologies of each marketing technique. This knowledge is
essential for making well-informed choices and suggesting precise business actions.
