Abstract
Digital marketing has revolutionized the way businesses promote their products and
communicate with consumers in the modern business environment. The rapid growth of
internet penetration, smartphone usage, social media platforms, and e -commerce has
significantly changed consumer purchasing behaviour, making digital marketing one of the
most effective tools for increasing product awareness, customer engagement, and sales
performance. In the consumer electronics industry, where technological advancements and
intense market competition continuously influence consumer preferences, digital marketing has
become an essential strategy for attracting customers and strengthening brand loyalty. LG
Electronics India, one of the leading manufacturers of consumer electron ics and home
appliances, extensively utilizes digital marketing strategies including search engine
optimization (SEO), search engine marketing (SEM), social media marketing, email marketing,
influencer marketing, content marketing, online advertising, video marketing, and e-commerce
promotions to reach a wider audience and improve customer experiences. These digital
platforms enable the company to provide personalized product recommendations, promotional
offers, product demonstrations, customer reviews, and  after-sales support, thereby enhancing
consumer confidence and encouraging purchase decisions. Digital marketing also allows
organizations to measure campaign performance using real -time analytics, customer
engagement metrics, conversion rates, and return  on investment, enabling continuous
improvement of marketing strategies. The primary objective of this study is to examine the
impact of digital marketing on the sales of consumer electronics products at LG Electronics
India by analyzing consumer awareness , online engagement, purchase intention, customer
satisfaction, and the effectiveness of digital promotional activities.
Keywords: Digital Marketing, Consumer Electronics, LG Electronics India, Online
Advertising, Consumer Behaviour, Social Media Marketing, Customer Engagement.

IV. FINDINGS
The study reveals that digital marketing has
a significant positive impact on the sales of
consumer electronics products at LG
Electronics India. The analysis indicates that
the majority of consumers are highly aware
of the company's digital marketing
campaigns, demonstrating the effectiveness
of online platforms in reaching potential
customers. Social media marketing, search
engine optimization, online advertisements,
influencer collaborations, email marketing,
and video marketing have successfully
enhanced product awareness and consumer
engagement. The findings further show that
digital marketing plays a crucial role in
influencing consumer purchase decisions by
providing detailed product information,
customer reviews, comparison features,
promotional offers, and personalized
recommendations. Respondents reported that
digital platforms make it easier to evaluate
product specifications, compare prices, and
make inf ormed purchasing decisions. The
research also found that social media
platforms such as YouTube , Instagram,
Facebook, and X significantly contribute to
brand visibility and customer interaction
through product demonstrations, live
sessions, customer testimonials, and
influencer reviews. Online promotional
campaigns, including festive discounts,
cashback offers, exchange programs, EMI
facilities, and exclusive online deals, were
identified as major factors encouraging
consumers to purchase LG consumer
electronics products. The study additionally
indicates that digital marketing improves
customer satisfaction by offering convenient
access to product information, responsive
customer support, easy online purchasing
options, and after-sales service.
