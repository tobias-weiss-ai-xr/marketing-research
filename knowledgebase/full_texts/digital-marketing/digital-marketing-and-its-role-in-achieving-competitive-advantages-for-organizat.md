1. INTRODUCTION
Many factors have led to changes in the global economy, most notably the emergence of technological advances,
especially in long-distance communication, the information technology revolution, and cybersecurity development.
This has led to many economies adapting to and using these technologies, which provide speed and efficiency and
shift the basis of how sales firms compete and secure competitive advantages that allow them to service their target
markets effectively [1] [2]. These advantages relate to the capacity of economic institutions to use advanced
technologies in business management and marketing, particularly following the rise of the use of computers and
information in business management. The importance of the information also grows with its utilization [3].
In fact, it is the key motivator of the financial and business sector, and so, e-services have emerged.
ABSTRACT:
This paper discusses the application of digital marketing in the realization of competitive advantages to organizations
with a specific reference made to the private banks in Sulaymaniyah City, Iraq. The study would employ a quantitative
cross-sectional study design to examine the association between the digital marketing practices and the dimensions
of competitive advantage, which include efficiency, service quality, modernization and innovativeness, and customer
responsiveness. A structured questionnaire was used to collect data by administering the questionnaire to employees
and managers working in banks, and a total of 87 valid responses were obtained. The SPSS software was used in
statistical analysis, where the reliability analysis, descriptive statistics, correlation, and regression analysis were
applied. The reliability findings showed that it had good internal consistency (Cronbach's Alpha = 0.898). The results
indicate that the effect of digital marketing on competitive advantage is significant and statistically significant (R² =
0.593, p < 0.001). Moreover, digital marketing enhances operational efficiency, service quality, innovation, and
responsiveness to customers in banks to a large extent. The study concludes that having digital marketing techniques
will lead to improved performance within organizations and competitive positioning within the banking industry. The
findings have practical implications for the banking institutions that are aiming at sustainable competitiveness in the
business environment, which is becoming more digital.

1.1 The Importance of the Research
Nowadays, the success or failure of any banking institution in the market is determined by the ability of the
institution to understand and analyze the situation of the environment of the institution and to respond and adapt to the
information of the environment [11], [12]. Meeting customer needs and expectations with the right efficiency and
quality is the key to the success, growth, and development of the banking institution [13]. To meet this, the institution
needs to understand the nature of the competition in the market and the strategies adopted by the competitors, to
enhance its strategic competence to implement marketing strategies that help to gain new customers, retain existing
assets, and meet the customers' needs to increase its competitive advantage [14], [15].
Iraqi private banks are operating in a more competitive and digital environment where customers are demanding
faster services, digital availability, and personalized communication. But Iraqi banks still face challenges in areas like
technology adoption, customer engagement, and marketing modernization. Therefore, the importance of understanding
the role of digital marketing in improving competitive advantage has become strategically important for enhancing
banking performance and sustainability.

2. LITERATURE REVIEW
2.1 Concept of Digital Marketing
Digital marketing is defined as any marketing process that uses digital media, social media, websites, email, and
search engines to communicate with consumers and other stakeholders. This method enables companies to reach their
audiences by means of effective targeting, affordable methods, and quantifiable outcomes, which makes it an essential
part of contemporary business operations [22].
Through digital marketing, companies can go beyond the local or regional market, achieve greater customer
interaction, and marketing efficiency due to analytics and real-time feedback. Studies show that online channels can help
organizations tailor their marketing channels to a specific group of customers, and this enhances brand awareness and
loyalty among the customers. In spite of the fact that these benefits are not clearly brought out, they offer a well-grounded
theoretical framework on how digital marketing helps in enhancing competitive advantage in the current business world
[23].

2.2 Digital Marketing and Competitive Advantage
Competitive advantage is the capability that an organization has to beat its competitors in providing more value to
its customers, either in terms of possessing special resources, operating efficiently, or offering differentiated products. In
the current dynamic markets, the concept of digital marketing is establishing itself as a marketing tool that has the capacity
to enhance the position of a firm [24].
As an example, the study of the Egyptian public sector organizations revealed that marketing is a major factor in the
attainment of sustainable competitive advantage through improved organizational responsiveness and customer
responsiveness [25]. On the same note, research within service-based organizations has indicated that there are positive
correlations between different factors of digital marketing- customer attraction, communication, engagement, and
sustainability, and general competitive performance [26].

2.3 Empirical Evidence on the Relationship
       Many empirical studies emphasize the beneficial influence of digital marketing on the creation of competitive
advantage in various industries:
Banks and Financial Services: The study on the Iraqi banks shows that digital marketing practices can enhance the
competitive advantage by enhancing the quality of service, increasing speed to service delivery, and improving customer
interactions. Companies with good digital marketing practices in the banking sector are more likely to record better
organizational performance in competitive markets [23].
Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs): Research on SMEs indicates that incorporating digital marketing in a
larger digital transformation project can help firms to have a better response time to market needs and customer
expectations, which increases competitiveness in a company [27].
Manufacturing Firms: This study, carried out in the manufacturing industry, found that digital marketing
competencies are an intermediary between modernization and competitive advantage, with digital marketing
demonstrating that innovative activities are turned into actual successes in the market [28].
Service Organizations: In service-related institutions, digital marketing has a significant effect on their
competitiveness and customer involvement, which is seen as the most powerful dimension in this justification [29].
In addition, studies conducted among private banks in Sulaymaniyah city and Iraq indicate that digital marketing can
play a role in the faster, more efficient, and dependable operational responsiveness, and this strengthens the competitive
advantage [8].

2.4 Dimensions of Competitive Advantage Enhanced by Digital Marketing
Studies reveal that digital marketing reinforces several features of competitive advantage:
Operational Efficiency: Digital channels help in streamlining the communication process and workflow, hence
lessening the response time and lowering the cost of operation [30].
Service Quality: The delivery of the service is more precise, reliable, and consistent with the help of the Online
communication and feedback systems [31].
Modernization and Innovation: The digital spaces encourage the use of innovations in operating businesses and
modernization in customer services [32].
Customer Responsiveness: The use of real-time communication using the digital channel enhances customer
satisfaction, customer loyalty, and retention [8].

2.5 Theoretical Frameworks
The Resource-Based View (RBV) of the firm is often applied in the explanation of the possible utilization of digital
marketing resources as strategic resources, which generate competitive advantage. The digital marketing capabilities
suggested by RBV help companies to create value, differentiate their services/products, and improve the management of
customer relations. These are essential to the achievement of competitive advantage in the local markets and in the global
markets [28].

2.6 Theoretical Frameworks Development
Considering digital marketing capabilities using the Resource-Based View (RBV) is seen as a strategic intangible
resource that can create a sustainable competitive advantage. RBV theory suggests that firms may outperform their rivals
because of valuable, rare, inimitable, and well-organized resources [28].

Digital marketing in this study is defined as a strategic competency that helps to improve the process efficiency,
quality of the service, innovation, and customer responsiveness of operations. These dimensions put together constitute
a competitive advantage.

Based on this, the conceptual framework suggests that:

Digital Marketing is related to Competitive advantage.
Digital Marketing is related to Efficiency
Digital Marketing is related to Service Quality
Digital Marketing is related to Modernization/Innovation
Digital Marketing is related to Customer Responsiveness.

The main and sub-hypotheses of the study are based on these relationships.

2.7 Summary
Comprehensively, the literature shows that digital marketing is not merely a tool, but a strategic resource that enables
organizations to react to the dynamic needs of customers, enhance their performance, and achieve a competitive
advantage in dynamic market environments. The practical studies in various fields and regions continue to support the
importance of incorporating digital marketing in corporate strategies to attain a competitive advantage.
While most previous research shows that digital marketing has a positive impact on the competitiveness of
organizations, literature indicates some conceptual and contextual weaknesses. Some of the studies exclusively
concentrate on customer satisfaction, online promotion or social media engagement without considering other areas of
competitive advantage like operational efficiency, modernization or customer responsiveness. Moreover, most empirical
studies have been carried out in developed economies and technologically well-developed banking systems, making it
difficult to extend the results to the emerging markets like Iraq. Furthermore, the digital marketing literature has tended
to focus on the digital marketing concept as a standalone marketing activity rather than as a strategic organizational
competence that can be used to generate sustainable competitive advantage. Hence, the importance of conducting more
research to explore the multidimensionality of digital marketing in developing financial sectors in which technology is
in constant change and a competitive environment.

dependent and independent variables to examine the relationship between digital marketing and its role in achieving

3.6 Justification of the Methodology
The study will use a quantitative cross-sectional research design to examine the influence of digital marketing on the
realization of competitive advantages of organizations. The quantitative method is assumed to be the right one since
the study is expected to quantify and analyze the connection between digital marketing practices and competitive
advantage through the numerical data that will be received from respondents. A cross-sectional design is suitable
because the data will be obtained at one point in time, and this will help the researcher to explore the existing
organizational practices and perceptions in an effective manner without the need to observe them over a long period
of time. Moreover, this methodology allows the application of statistical analysis methods, including correlation and
regression analysis, to identify the level of strength and significance of associations among variables. The chosen
methodology is both economical and time-saving and is applicable in the study of organizational phenomena in a
real business setting.

3.8 Ethical Considerations
This research follows the current research ethics in order to guarantee the safety of the participants and the involved
organizations. The study will involve complete voluntary participation of the research subjects, and the study participants
will be made aware of the research purpose before collecting data. All the participants are informed and give informed
consent before filling out the questionnaire. Bank employees' and managers' anonymity and confidentiality are preserved
in the strictest sense, and no personal or organizational information is revealed. The participants will be assured that their
responses will be utilized only for academic purposes and will not affect their professional standings and positions in
their organizations. In addition, the respondents will have the right to drop out of the participating process at any time
without repercussions. Data collected is stored safely inaccessible to any other party except the researcher.

The results of this study would support the significant role of digital marketing in improving the competitive advantage
of private banks in Sulaymaniyah from an empirical perspective.

Correlation analysis showed that digital marketing and competitive advantage, as well as their dimensions, were
positively and strongly correlated. The correlations show that increased use of digital marketing activities is related to
increased effectiveness, service quality, modernization/innovation, and customer responsiveness. Correlation does not

Overall, the findings of this study confirm that digital marketing has a positive and statistically significant impact on
competitive advantage in banks. The results demonstrate that digital marketing significantly enhances competitive
advantage, efficiency, service quality, modernization/innovation, and customer responsiveness, with all regression
models showing strong to moderate positive relationships and high statistical significance (Sig. = 0.000). Additionally,
the reliability analysis (Cronbach’s Alpha = 0.898) indicates high internal consistency of the measurement instrument,
supporting the validity of the results. Therefore, it can be concluded that adopting and strengthening digital marketing
strategies plays a crucial role in improving overall bank performance and sustaining competitive advantage.

The results showed that digital marketing was more effective in the modernization/innovation and customer
responsiveness dimensions than in efficiency and service quality dimensions. This could be attributed to the fact that
digital marketing technologies directly improve customer interactions, communication speed, and innovation in banking
services. However, other organizational aspects like employee training, internal processes, and technological
infrastructure can also influence the operational efficiency and service quality.

The results are aligned with the previous studies that were conducted by [23] and [33] In the banking sector in Iraq, they

same way, [34] and [35] Conducted studies in manufacturing and service companies, respectively, and also found that
digital marketing improves competitiveness by contributing to innovation and customer engagement.

This study, however, builds on the previous studies by testing the explanatory power of digital marketing for several
dimensions of competitive advantage at once in the context of private banks in Sulaymaniyah. This gives evidence at a
local level from an emerging economy context, which is not yet explored in the literature on digital marketing.

5. CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATIONS
5.1 Conclusion
This study is an affirmation of the role of digital marketing in strengthening the competitive advantage in private
banks in Sulaymaniyah. The results show that digital marketing is as much about engaging customers as it is
modernization, responsiveness, and operational improvement. The study also highlights the growing importance of
digital transformation in emerging banking sectors such as Iraq. The results offer both theoretical and practical
implications for banking institutions aiming for sustainable competitiveness in a digitally changing environment
The analysis also indicated that digital marketing is an effective way of ensuring operational efficiency as it boosts
the process of communication and lessens the time-to-service delivery. Moreover, digital marketing has a positive
impact on the quality of the services due to the improvement of communication with customers and the availability
of banking services. The research results also verify that online marketing leads to modernization and innovation,
as it promotes the use of modern and sophisticated technological tools and digital technologies by banks. In addition,
digital marketing was identified to significantly increase the level of customer responsiveness, whereby banks were
able to understand and act accordingly to customer needs and expectations.
The study adds to the RBV literature by empirically proving that digital marketing capability acts as a strategic
intangible resource that gives significant contribution to the dimensions of competitive advantage in the banking
sector. The results add to the growing evidence base on a digitally transforming economy in an emerging market
environment, demonstrating that digital skills are critical for sustainable competitiveness.
