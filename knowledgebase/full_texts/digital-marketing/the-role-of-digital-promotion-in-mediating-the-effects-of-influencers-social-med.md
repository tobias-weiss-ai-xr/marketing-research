Promotion in the tourism sector plays a crucial role in
introducing destinations, building a positive image,
and increasing tourists' intention to visit. However,
many tourism destination managers have yet to
implement prom otional strategies optimally. This
study aims to examine the effects of influencers, social
media, and tourism events on digital promotion and
their implications for the competitiveness of
Sangkanika Edugarden Agrotourism. A quantitative
research design was employed using purposive
sampling to select 132 respondents who had visited
Sangkanika Edugarden Agrotourism. Data were
collected through a structured questionnaire using a
five-point Likert scale during April –May 2026 and
analyzed using path analysis with IBM SPSS Statistics
26. The findings indicate that influencers, social
media, and tourism events have positive and
significant effects on digital promotion. Furthermore,
digital promotion has a positive and significant effect
on destination competitiveness. The results also
reveal that digital promotion mediates the effects of
influencers, social media, and tourism events on
competitiveness. These findings suggest that
enhancing destination competitiveness depends not
only on the effective utilization of  influencers, social
media, and tourism events but also on the ability to
integrate these three marketing strategies through
effective digital promotion. This study contributes to
the development of digital marketing strategies for
agrotourism destinations.

INTRODUCTION
The rapid advancement of digital technology has transformed marketing
practices within the tourism industry. Today, tourists increasingly rely on
digital media to obtain information, compare destinations, and make travel
decisions. This shift requires destination managers to adopt more effective and
adaptive promotional strategies. Digital promotion has emerged as one of the
most effective approaches for enhancing the visibility, attractiveness, and
competitiveness of tourism destinations in an increasingly competitive market
(Angliawati & Ratnasari, 2024).
Promotion in the tourism sector serves to introduce destinations, build a
positive image, and stimulate tourists' intention to visit (Huwae et al.,2024).
Various digital promotional tool s, including social media, influencers, and
tourism events, have proven to be effective communication channels for
enhancing audience engagement and increasing the exposure of tourism
destinations. However, the effectiveness of these promotional tools may vary
across destinations, highlighting the need for further empirical investigation.
One of the tourism destinations recognized for its uniqueness and
popularity among visitors is Sangkanika Edugarden Agrotourism, more widely
known as Sangkanika Yellow Dra gon Fruit Agrotourism, located in
Sangkanurip Village, Cigandamekar District. The destination offers an
educational tourism concept by integrating yellow dragon fruit cultivation,
fruit-picking activities, local culinary experiences, and organic farming
education for visitors. To provide an overview of the research site, supporting
data on the growth in the number of visitors to Sangkanika Edugarden
Agrotourism are presented in Figure 1.

Based on the visitor data from June to November, the number of tourist
visits to the agrotourism destination exhibited fluctuating trends over time. This
pattern is characterized by recurring increases and decreases across the
observation period, indicating that visitor arrivals have not yet reached a stable
level. These fluctuations may be attributed to various factors, including
accessibility, transportation availability, supporting facili ties, and seasonal
conditions. Furthermore, a preliminary survey conducted in July 2025 involving
15 visitors to Sangkanika Edugarden Agrotourism identified insufficient digital
promotion as one of the primary concerns expressed by respondents. This issue
requires serious attention from destination managers, as inadequate digital
promotion may negatively affect the destination's sustainability by reducing
tourists' intention to revisit, which could ultimately lead to a decline in visitor
numbers. A persiste nt decrease in tourist arrivals may, in turn, threaten the
economic viability of the agrotourism business. Therefore, improving digital
promotion should become a strategic priority for destination managers and
other stakeholders through the implementation of both theoretical insights and
practical marketing approaches to strengthen the long -term sustainability and
competitiveness of the destination.
Previous studies have demonstrated that digital promotion strategies
play a crucial role in enhancing the a ttractiveness and competitiveness of
tourism destinations. The utilization of social media through engaging and
consistently delivered visual content has been shown to increase tourists'
intention to visit (Lubis  et al., 2022 ; Maulana et al., 2024). Furthermore,
influencers who possess high credibility and are closely aligned with the
characteristics of a tourism destination can enhance audience engagement and
foster positive perceptions of the destination among potential tourists
Pettersen-Sobczyk, 2023). Moreover, the organization of tourism events and
active community participation in digital content creation contribute to
strengthening experience -based promotion while enhancing the organic
visibility of tourism destinations. (Sumarna et al., 2025). Overall, digital
marketing through social media has become a key factor influencing tourists'
interest, travel decision -making, and behavioral intentions when selecting
tourism destinations. (Afren, 2024).
Based on the observed phenomenon and the inconsistencie s found in
previous studies, this research focuses on analyzing the effects of influencers,
social media, and tourism events on digital promotion and their impact on the
competitiveness of Sangkanika Edugarden Agrotourism. This study offers
novelty by simultaneously examining the influence of these variables in
shaping the effectiveness of digital promotion in an agrotourism destination
using a path analysis approach. The findings are expected to provide a
theoretical contribution by enriching the literatur e on digital marketing and
tourism, particularly regarding the roles of influencers, social media, and
tourism events in enhancing the effectiveness of digital promotion.
Furthermore, this study is expected to serve as a practical reference for
agrotourism managers in formulating more effective promotional strategies to
improve tourism competitiveness.

LITERATURE REVIEW
Theoretical Background
Agrotourism is a form of tourism that utilizes agricultural resources as
tourist attractions, including agricultural  landscapes, local culture, and
agricultural products, thereby contributing to the improvement of community
welfare (Meilani et al., 2023).
Influencer Theory
Influencers are individuals who have the ability to influence the
perceptions, attitudes, and behaviors of audiences through the content they
share on social media. In destination marketing, influencers serve as
communicators who can enhance the image and attractiveness of tourism
destinations because they are perceived as credible, knowledgeable, and  closely
connected with their followers (Rosalina et al., 2025). Through firsthand
experiences shared in the form of visual content, influencers can build trust and
stimulate people's intention to visit a tourism destination. A study by
Muhammad et al (2023) found that the use of influencers in tourism promotion
contributes to increasing tourists' intention to visit through the dissemination of
information and testimonials that are perceived as more authentic than
conventional advertising. According to Putri et al. (2024), the effectiveness of
influencers in promotional activities is influenced by several factors, including
credibility, authenticity, attractiveness, expertise, and congruence with the
brand or tourism destination being promoted.
Social Media Theory
Social media is a digital communication platform that enables the rapid,
interactive, and widespread dissemination of information to the public. In the
tourism sector, social media serves as an effective promotional tool for
introducing tourism dest inations, building destination image, and enhancing
tourist engagement through various forms of digital content (Goretti et al.,
2024). According to Kaplan and Haenlein (2010), the primary characteristics of
social media are its participatory and interactive nature, enabling users to
contribute to marketing activities through comments, reviews, content sharing,
and user -generated content. The effective utilization of social media can
enhance the visibility of tourism destinations and influence tourists' int ention
and decision to visit. Furthermore. Peters et al. (2013) stated that the
effectiveness of social media can be assessed based on several aspects, including
responsiveness, content consistency, information relevance, and the optimal use
of platform features.
Tourism Events Theory
Tourism events are planned activities designed to create memorable
visitor experiences, encourage public participation, and enhance the image of
tourism destinations. In the context of agrotourism, such events may include
harvest festivals, agricultural workshops, educational farming activities, and
fruit-picking experiences (Šušić & Mojić, 2014). According to Getz and Page
(2019), tourism events are a strategic promotional tool that strengthens
destination image, fosters visitors' emotional engagement, and creates
memorable experiences. Furthermore, Fariz and Setyo (2021) found that

Digital Promotion Theory
Digital promotion refers to the use of various digital channels and
technologies, such as social media, search engines, online content, and digital
advertising, to enhance visibility, build destination image, attract potential
visitors, and encourage tourists' decisions to visit a destination (Sumaia Afren,
2024). In the context of agrotourism, digital promotion enables destination
managers to showcase agricultural activities, educational programs, and the
unique attribute s of a destination through engaging visual content and
storytelling, thereby increasing its attractiveness to tourists (Suniyyah et al.,
2024). According to Kotler et al. (2024), digital promotion provides
opportunities for two -way communication between destination managers and
the public, making marketing activities more effective, interactive, and efficient
than conventional promotional methods. Mahendra and Utami (2024) stated
that the effectiveness of digital promotion can be evaluated based on content
quality, visual appeal, reach and engagement, message clarity, and the
effectiveness of digital advertising.
Competitiveness Theory
Competitiveness refers to the ability of a tourism destination to attract
and retain visitors through the quality of its products, uniqueness, services,
innovation, and marketing strategies. According to Dwyer and Kim (2010),
destination competitiveness is influenced not only by physical resources but
also by marketing strategies, destination image, and sustainability. In the
context of agrotourism, competitiveness is strengthened by unique agricultural
activities, educational experiences, direct interaction with nature and local
farmers, and effective digital promotion. Lesmana et al. (2022) found that
community-based agrotourism management enhances farmers' welfare while
improving the competitiveness of tourism destinations in the educational
tourism market.

METHODOLOGY
  This study employed a quantitative research design (Fauziah et al., 2025),
to analyze data collected from respondents during April –May 2026. The study
examined the effects of Influencers, social media , and Tourism Events on the
Competitiveness of Sangkanika Edugarden Agrotourism, with Digital
Promotion serving as the mediating variable. The study population comprised
all visitors to Sangkanika Edugarden Agrotourism. The sample was selected
using an accidental sampling technique (Ammbiya et al., 2025; Prasetyo et al.,
2023). A total of 132 respondents participated in the study, satisfying Hair et
al.'s recommendation of 5 –10 respondents per indicator. With 22 measurement
indicators, the required sample size ranged from 110 to 220 respondents,
indicating that the sample used was adequate (Sofia et al., 2023). This study
employed 22 measurement indicators; therefore, based on Hair et al.'s
recommendation, the required sample size ranged from 110 to 220 respondents.
The sample of 132 respondents met this requirement. Primary data were
collected through a questionnaire administered to the respondents, while
secondary data were obtained from relevant literature, documents, and other
supporting sources (Sugiyono, 2019). Data were collected using a structured
questionnaire with a five -point Likert scale distributed through Google Forms
(Septyanto et al., 2025; Wachdijono, 2022). The independent variables consisted
of X1 (influencers), X2 (social media), and X3 (tourism Events). X1 (influencers)
was measured using five indicators, namely  influencer credibility, authenticity,
attractiveness, expertise, and brand relevance. X2 (social media) was measured
using four indicators, including responsiveness, content consistency, content
relevance, and the utilization of social media platforms. X3 (tourism events)
was measured using four indicators, namely event attractiveness, activity
diversity, event publicity, and visitor involvement.
  The intervening variable in this study was Y1 (Digital Promotion), which
was measured using five indicators: content quality, visual attractiveness, reach
and engagement, clarity of promotional messages, and digital advertising
effectiveness. The dependent variable was Y2 (Competitiveness), which was
measured using four indicators: destination image, tourism product
attractiveness, destination uniqueness, and visitor growth. All indicators were
measured using a five-point Likert scale, ranging from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5
(strongly agree) (Jaeroni & Wachdijono, 2023; Jihan Nabilah et al., 2023).

 The novelty of this study lies in the development of an empirical model
that integrates influencers, social media, and tourism events as determinants of
digital promotion while examining the mediating role of digital promotion in
enhancing the competitiveness of agrotourism destinations. Unlike previous
studies, which generally focused only on the direct effects of individual
marketing variables on promotion or visit decisions, this study demonstrates that
the effectiveness of influencers, social media, and tourism events in improving
destination competitiveness can be optimized when integrated through an
effective Digital Promotion strategy. In addition to providing empirical evidence
in the context of Sangkanika Edugarden Agrotourism, this study also enriches
the literature on digital marketing in the agrotourism sector by emphasizing that
Digital Promotion serves as a strategic mechanism linking va rious marketing
activities to improved destination competitiveness.
 Furthermore, the results showed that digital promotion mediated the
relationship between influencers, social media, and tourism events and the
competitiveness of Sangkanika Edugarden Agrotourism. These findings indicate
that improving the quality of influencers, optimizing social media, and
organizing tourism events would be more effective in enhancing destination
competitiveness when supported by effective digital promotion. Therefore,
digital promotion plays an important role in strengthening the effectiveness of
various marketing strategies to enhance the competitiveness of tourism
destinations. These findings are consistent with the studies of Lubis et al. (2022)
and Sumaia Afren (2024), which explained that digital promotion plays a role in
strengthening the influence of marketing strategies on improving the
competitiveness of tourism destinations.

CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
Based on the findings of this study, it can be concluded  that Influencers,
social media, and Tourism Events have positive effects on the Digital Promotion
of Sangkanika Edugarden Agrotourism. Furthermore, Digital Promotion has a
positive effect on destination Competitiveness. The findings also demonstrate
that Digital Promotion serves as a mediating variable in the relationships
between Influencers, social media, and Tourism Events and Competitiveness.
These results indicate that improving the competitiveness of Sangkanika
Edugarden Agrotourism depends not only on optimizing the use of Influencers,
social media , and Tourism Events, but also on effectively integrating these
strategies into a comprehensive Digital Promotion strategy. Therefore,
strengthening Digital Promotion is essential for enhancing the competitiveness of
agrotourism destinations.
Based on the findings of this study, the management of Sangkanika
Edugarden Agrotourism is recommended to optimize its Digital Promotion
strategy by collaborating with credible Influencers, maintaining consistent and
informative Social Media management, and organizing innovative Tourism
Events that are effectively promoted through digital platforms. These efforts are
expected to enhance the effectiveness of digital promotion while strengthening
the competitiveness of the destination.

FURTHER STUDY
This study has several limitations, highlighting the need for further
research to deepen the understanding of the roles of influencers, social media,
and digital promotion in the context of agrotourism. Future studies are
encouraged to extend the research model by incorporating additional variables
that may influence digital promotion, such as electronic word -of-mouth (e -
WOM), content quality, brand image, engagement, and visit intention. In
addition, larger sample sizes and broader respondent coverage are
recommended to improve the generalizability of the findings. Future research
may also employ alternative analytical approaches, such as Partial Least Squares
Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM), or conduct comparative studies across
different tourism destinations to provide a more comprehensive understanding
of the effectiveness of digital promotion. Furthermore, more specific
investigations into the roles of individual social media platforms, including
Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube, are recommended to identify the
most effective platforms for promoting tourism destinations.
