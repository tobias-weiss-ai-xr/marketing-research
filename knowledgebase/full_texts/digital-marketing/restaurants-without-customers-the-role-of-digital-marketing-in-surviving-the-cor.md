Introduction. Talking about restaurants means talking primarily about tourism,
entertainment, leisure and recreation, which means talking about cultural exchange and learning
about the cultural heritage of each region and each country. It is a culture of the last decade, a
luxury service that is indispensable at the same time. As we know, the coronavirus pandemic
forced many restaurant owners to close their establishments during the rapid and widespread
outbreak of the pandemic and during the period known as quarantine, which meant separating
groups of people from each other and restricting the movement of those who had been exposed
or might be exposed to the disease, so that they could be monitored to check whether they had
contracted the disease.Therefore, continuing to interact with customers despite the pandemic or
other crises is a challenge in itself and a struggle for survival, but at the same time an
opportunity to prove their existence in another form on a virtual level.
Digital marketing for restaurants achieves remote presence, which is a necessity, so it
must start today rather than tomorrow. This is the marketing philosophy of seizing
opportunities, turning every threat into a marketing opportunity, and achieving success by
taking advantage of it.

Digital marketing is an essential tool for creating interaction and continuous
communication with customers, maintaining constant awareness of their needs and
developments, and enabling the customisation of offers. If customer satisfaction is a source of
profit, then customer loyalty is the basis for survival and continuity. This has given rise to
concepts related to relationship marketing, which focuses on establishing strong interactive
relationships and developing ideas that build long-term mutual trust. This is where value is
created for restaurant owners, which is the basis of the modern concept of marketing, which
now focuses on value rather than product characteristics. For restaurants, this value has come
to mean the need for a relationship based on integrity, credibility and security in order to ga in
customer trust and loyalty.
Recent literature has increasingly addressed the intersection of digital marketing and
crisis management. According to [Vaillat, 2012], digital marketing is defined as "the use of
digital means to carry out marketing activities, with digital means referring to modern means
of communication that have developed with the advent of information technology and the
internet." [Dave & Chadwick , 2014] further define digital marketing as "a field for achievin g
marketing objectives through digital technology, such as computers, tablets, mobile phones,
and other platforms." [Gastaud, 2017] provides a comprehensive taxonomy of digital marketing
tools, which include websites, search engines, social media platforms, email, mobile
applications, and interactive television. These tools form the operational foundation upon which
digital marketing strategies are built. [LEVY, 2017] argue that the process of planning a digital
marketing strategy is no different from planning a traditional marketing strategy, as they share
the same objectives. However, the fundamental difference lies in the means and tools used to
achieve those objectives. Their work emphasizes the three pillars of strategic planning for
marketing activities: segmentation, targeting, and positioning, adapted to the digital context.
Digital marketing uses digital technologies to attain goals and improve performance in
hospitality companies especially in restaurants [Singh et al., 2022]. Regarding the restaurant
industry, [Durocher, 2017] notes that restaurants represent a balance between goods an d
services, with the offering being 50% goods and 50% services, or "hybrid." The characteristics
of restaurant services—including the physical environment, overall atmosphere, and interactive
relationship between service provider and customer —have been well documented. More
recently, [Al-Ani, 2021] has explored digital marketing and search engine optimization
strategies applicable to small businesses, including restaurants. The work on restaurant
marketing met and crisis-specific marketing has begun to address the practical application of
digital tools during the COVID-19 pandemic hods  [Bedingfield, 2020]. Contactless payments,
QR code menus, robots, delivery applications, and high-tech cleaning devices serve as tools for
service innovation and minimize risks and interactions [Esposito et al., 2022], tСО МШЦЩКЧв’s
website and the application of digital delivery are considered primary tools that help
communicate and sell on the internet. Reports from Algeria [Yildiz, 2020]   and other Arab
media [ALAraby, 2026] have documented the specific impact of the pandemic on Algerian
restaurants, noting complete closures from 23 March 2020 to 31 August 2020 and significant
financial losses for restaurant owners. The COVID-19 pandemic made it necessary for
restaurants to use digital technology as survival strategies and as a means of being resilient,
thus hastening the digital transformation of businesses [Esposito et al., 2022; Fainshtein et al.,
2023].
Despite the growing body of literature on digital marketing and on the impact of
COVID-19 on the restaurant industry, several important gaps remain unresolved. First, while
digital marketing tools and strategies have been described in general terms, there is a lack of
systematic application of these tools to the specific context of restaurants during a prolonged

Research Methodology . In this study, we used a descriptive analytical approach in
order to understand and cover all elements of the study, describe them theoretically, and analyze
their content—that is, analyze what lies behind the concepts and theoretical content to draw
various logical conclusions that help us find answers and solutions to the problem and,
consequently, either reject or accept the research hypothesis.
The research methodology encompasses several key components. First, the study
employed content analysis of secondary data sources, including government reports, industry
association statements, and media coverage of restaurant closures and recovery efforts during
the pandemic period. Second, the research utilized comparative analysis to examine the impact
of the pandemic on restaurants across different regulatory environments—countries with strict
lockdown measures compared to those without enforced lockdowns. Third, the study analysed
specific digital marketing strategies implemented by restaurants during the pandemic,
categorising them into tactical approaches and evaluating their effectiveness based on
documented outcomes.

Conclusions and Directions for Further Research. The actual gathering of
individuals in real life may cause diseases, transmit infections, and lead to excessive spread and
inability to control them. This is why the digital marketing slogan in times of crisis is
particularly relevant: "There is no social distancing on the internet; everyone communicates,
watches, chooses, and supports." From this research, the following conclusions can be drawn:
First, digital marketing represents a fundamental shift from traditional marketing practices and
has become an imperative for organizations of all types. For restaurants specifically, digital
marketing achieves remote presence, which is a necessity rather than an option. Second, the
COVID-19 pandemic had a devastating impact on the restaurant industry globally, including in
Algeria where complete closures lasted over five months, causing significant financial losses.
The phenomenon of "coronavirus phobia" exacerbated the crisis beyond governmental
restrictions. Third, digital marketing tools – including websites, search engines, social media
platforms, and email – provide practical mechanisms for restaurants to maintain continuous
interaction and communication with customers during crises. These tools enable restaurants to
understand customer needs, customize offers, and build long-term mutual trust. Fourth, specific
crisis-oriented digital strategies – such as sharing success stories, using user-generated content,
providing behind-the-scenes access, sharing recipes, reaching new audiences through emerging
platforms like TikTok and Pinterest, and demonstrating social solidarity – have proven effective
in maintaining customer relationships during lockdowns. Fifth, the study confirms its
hypothesis: the adoption of digital marketing strategies does have a significant impact on the
fate of restaurants and their relationships with customers. Customer satisfaction may be a source

of profit, but customer loyalty – built through continuous digital engagement – is the basis for

Future research should address several promising directions. First, empirical studies
measuring the quantitative impact of specific digital marketing strategies on restaurant revenue,
customer retention, and post-crisis recovery rates would strengthen the findings of this
theoretical and descriptive study. Second, comparative research examining the differential
effectiveness of digital marketing strategies across countries with varying levels of digital
infrastructure – particularly comparing Algeria with European and Asian countries – would
provide valuable insights for policy development. Third, longitudinal studies tracking
restaurants that adopted digital marketing during COVID-19 and their long-term performance
compared to those that did not would further validate the hypothesis. Fourth, the development
of a practical, low-cost digital marketing toolkit specifically designed for small and medium-
sized restaurants in developing countries represents an important applied research direction.
Finally, future research should explore how digital marketing can prepare restaurants for other
types of crises beyond pandemics, including natural disasters, economic recessions, and
geopolitical disruptions. As the study has shown, creating attractive content, improving online
presence, helping the surrounding community, and always preparing to start again are actions
that increase respect and appreciation for restaurants and achieve greater brand awareness –
objectives that every restaurant strives to accomplish.
