Introduction
Digital transformation has changed the way consumers discover, evaluate, and
remember brands. In an increasingly digitalized marketing environment, value creation no
longer occurs only at the point of sale but across a series of digital touchpoints, including search
engines, social media, customer reviews, and user -generated content. Kannan and Li (2017)
explain that digital technologies broaden marketing touchpoints while enabling firms to build

Gulung Jenebora Souvenir Center is a local culinary business in Balikpapan established
in 2012, with rolled snacks developed as its primary product identity. The business serves local
consumers and tourists through several sales outlets in Balikpapan. The nature of the souvenir
business requires a brand to be easy to find and remember because tourists often make purchase
decisions within a short period. Under these conditions, a digital presence should do more than
display product information; the brand must also create consistent, relevant, and recognizable
content across platforms.
Brand awareness refers to consumers' ability to recognize and remember a brand when
encountering a particular product category. In the context of social media marketing, engaging,
interactive, informative, and trend-related activities can improve consumers' brand knowledge
(Cheung et al., 2020). Bilgin (2018) also shows that social media marketing activities are
associated with b rand awareness, brand image, and brand loyalty. In the Indonesian context,
Silfitri and Hermawan (2023) found that social media marketing contributes to the formation
of brand awareness for local brands. Therefore, the quality of a digital presence is an important
prerequisite for ensuring that local brands are not only visible but also embedded in consumers'
memory.
The effectiveness of digital marketing is strongly influenced by content quality and the
intensity of interaction. Dabbous and Barakat (2020) found that social network content quality
and brand interactivity can enhance brand awareness and purchase intention. At the strategic
level, Li et al. (2021) emphasize that social media marketing strategies should integrate
interaction and social connectedness as marketing resources rather than merely treating social
media as a channel for message distribution. Engagement behaviors such as liking,
commenting, sharing, and creating content can also strengthen relationships between
consumers and brands (Dolan et al., 2019; Cheung et al., 2021).
Although previous studies have extensively examined the effects of social media
marketing on brand equity and consumer responses, evaluations of local culinary MSMEs still
require a more contextual assessment of each d igital channel. Studies of brands with a local
customer base need to address two practical issues: which channel is currently most effective
in creating brand exposure, and whether that exposure has developed into consumers' ability to
recall the brand spo ntaneously. These questions are important because a large number of
followers does not necessarily indicate strong brand recall; message consistency, experience
quality, trust, and engagement also shape brand equity (Ebrahim, 2020; Hafez, 2021; Zollo et
al., 2020).
Based on this gap, this study aims to analyze the digital marketing practices of Gulung
Jenebora Souvenir Center on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and Google Maps; identify the
level of consumer brand awareness; and formulate digital strategy prior ities that can move the
brand from recognition toward brand recall and top -of-mind awareness. The findings are

Research Methods
This study employed a descriptive quantitative design supported by digital observation.
The approach was used to describe digital marketing practices and the level of brand awareness
without testing causal relationships between variables. The study was conducted at Gulung
Jenebora Souvenir Center in Balikpapan, with observations focused on Instagram, Facebook,
TikTok, and Google Maps. Data were collected from October to November 2025,  allowing
survey data and traces of digital activity to be analyzed within the same observation period.
The study involved 50 respondents selected using purposive sampling. Respondents
were required to have purchased Gulung Jenebora products or interacted with its digital content,
followed or accessed the business's social media accounts, been over 17 years old, actively used
digital media, and been willing to complete the questionnaire. The instrument used a 1-5 Likert
scale. Digital marketing indicators i ncluded content quality, posting intensity and message
consistency, interaction features, and the use of social media and marketplaces. Brand
awareness indicators included brand recognition, brand recall, top of mind, and brand
knowledge.
Primary data were obtained through questionnaires, while secondary data were derived
from observations of digital channels and platform -level metrics, including follower counts,
posting frequency, views, likes, comments, and customer reviews. The data were analyzed
descriptively using frequencies, percentages, and comparisons of performance across
platforms. Survey findings were then descriptively triangulated with digital observation results
to determine the main channels creating brand exposure and assess whether consumer
awareness remained predominantly at the recognition stage or had progressed toward recall and
top-of-mind awareness.

Result and Discussion
The survey results show that 82% of respondents had recognized Gulung Jenebora
before making a purchase, 68% first learned about the product through Instagram, and 74%
considered visual content in the form of product photos or videos highly influential to purchase
interest. These findings position Instagram as the primary digital touchpoint in the brand
recognition process. The visual dominance of food-related content reinforces the role of social
media as an initial stimulus connecting product appearance with the brand name. This pattern
is consistent with Dabbous and Barakat (2020), who emphasize the role of content q uality in
building brand awareness, and Cheung et al. (2020), who show that entertainment, interaction,
trendiness, and eWOM enhance brand knowledge.
Observation of the digital channels revealed clear differences in performance. Instagram
had approximately 11.4 thousand followers and the highest publication frequency. Facebook
had around 3,100 followers and continued to generate interaction with promotional content,
although its posting frequency was lower. TikTok had a smaller follower base of approximately
429 followers, yet several production -process videos received thousands of views. Google

When interpreted according to the stages of brand awareness, these findings indicate
that Gulung Jenebora's digital exposure is relatively strong in terms of recognition but is not
yet sufficient to ensure spontaneous recall. Consumers can relatively easil y associate product
appearance and visual content with the brand when provided with a stimulus, while indicators
of brand recall still require strengthening. This pattern is consistent with Bilgin (2018), who
shows that social media marketing activities are associated with brand awareness, and Seo and
Park (2018), who found that social media activities can strengthen brand equity and customer
responses. Fetais et al. (2023) also emphasize that social media marketing activities can increase
loyalty through b rand-equity mechanisms, meaning that recognition should be viewed as an
initial stage that must be developed into a stronger brand relationship.
Differences in performance across platforms show that audience size alone is not
sufficient for evaluating success. Instagram performs strongly because of its higher Story and
Feed frequency, supported by visual content that fits the characteristics of food products.
Facebook still generates responses to promotional content, but visual consistency and response
speed need improvement. Dolan et al. (2019) explain that content characteristics influence
forms of user engagement, while Cheung et al. (2021) show that the consumption, contribution,
and creation of user content can expand consumer engagement with brands. Th erefore,
improving performance requires not only increasing publication frequency but also using

TikTok offers opportunities to expand reach because several production-process videos
received more views than the account's follower count. This potential can be developed through
short videos featuring the production process, behind-the-scenes activities, brand-origin stories,
product ASMR, and customer content, while consistently displaying the Gulung Jenebora name
and visual identity. Collaboration with local creators or food reviewers can be implemented
selectively to accelerate penetration of new audiences. Through a meta -analysis, Barari et al.
(2026) show that influencer  effectiveness is shaped by mechanisms such as credibility and
attractiveness; therefore, collaborator selection should consider audience fit and authenticity
rather than follower count alone.
Google Maps functions primarily at the search and pre-visit evaluation stages. A rating
of approximately 4.2 out of 5 indicates relatively positive perceptions, but the limited number
of reviews means that the strength of social proof has not yet been maximized. The business
can therefore encourage customers to provide reviews after purchase, update product and store
photos, and respond professionally to every review. These improvements should be aligned
with consistent brand names, addresses, operating hours, contact numbers, and visual identities
across all channels. Kannan and Li (2017) identify the management of digital touchpoints as an
important part of the consumer journey; therefore, consistency of information across channels
can reduce friction when consumers search for and evaluate a brand.
Strategically, Gulung Jenebora should manage each channel according to its function:
Instagram as the primary channel for brand recognition and community maintenance, TikTok
as a channel for expanding reach through short -form video, Facebook as a channel for
information and customer conversations, and Google Maps as a channel for local search
visibility and credibility. Zeqiri et al. (2025) show that social media marketing can strengthen
brand awareness, engagement, and purchase intention in emerging markets. The experiential
value consumers receive through social media can also contribute to brand equity (Zollo et al.,
2020), while trust is an important mechanism linking social media activities with brand equity
and loyalty (Ebrahim, 2020; Hafez, 2021). Thus, the ultimate goa l of digital strategy is not
merely to increase exposure but to build consistent brand associations so that recognition
develops into recall and top-of-mind awareness.

Conclusion and Recommendation
This study found that digital marketing at Gulung Jenebora Souvenir Center primarily
supports brand recognition, with Instagram serving as the main channel for initial brand
discovery. TikTok shows potential for reaching broader audiences through production-process
videos, while Facebook supports promotional communication and Google Maps provides local
search visibility and customer reviews. However, the findings indicate that brand recognition
has not necessarily developed into spontaneous brand recall or top-of-mind awareness.
Based on these findings, Gu lung Jenebora may prioritize consistent visual identity,
short-form video content, more responsive customer interaction, user -generated content, and
customer reviews on Google Maps. These recommendations should be implemented and
evaluated to determine the ir contribution to brand awareness rather than assumed to produce
improvements automatically.
