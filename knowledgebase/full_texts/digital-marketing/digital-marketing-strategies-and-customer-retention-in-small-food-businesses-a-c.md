ABSTRACT :
This study explores the role of digital marketing activities on customer retention at a small food enterprise called Smoked Out BBQ in Vijayawada, Andhra
Pradesh. Datawere gatheredthroughaGoogle Form and76responses wereincluded inthe study. This paperaddressesthe characteristicsofthe respondents,use
of digital platforms, perception of the content on social media, perceived usefulness of digital information, influence of promotions and reviews, engagement,
satisfaction,revisit intentionandrecommendationintention.Instagramemerged asthe mostuseddigitalplatform whereasoffersanddiscountswere the preferred
activities for revisiting the business. The study revealed positive perspectives of respondents towards the digital-marketingactivities. A Pearson-product moment
correlation of a composite digital-marketing measurement constructed from Q10–Q17 and a composite measurement for retention/recommendation developed
from Q18–Q19 revealed a positive association (r= 0.882, p < 0.001). This finding supports the hypothesised relation at the level ofstatistical association, but no
causal inferences should be drawn. This paper concludes with a set of suggestions for improving the content of the social-media posts, promotions, reviews,
engagementandloyalty-buildingdigitalcommunications.

Introduction of the Study
Digital marketing is increasingly serving as a mechanism of communication and engagement for small food enterprises. Through social media
platforms, online reviews, food-delivery applications, promotional offers and digital conversations, restaurants engage with customers outside their
premise.Forsmallbusinesses,thesedigitalavenuescanbeimportanttoraiseawarenessandencouragerepeatvisitsandrecommendations.
The current study was conducted with Smoked Out BBQ in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh. It aims to understand the customers’ perspectives of the
business’sdigital-marketingactivitiesandhowtheseactivitiesareassociatedwiththeirintentiontorevisitandrecommendtherestaurant.Byexploring
the respondents’ answers, thisstudy identifies the digital channels that customers are familiarwith, the content andoffers that they findappealing and
thefactorsthatcontributetotheirdecisiontoreturntothebusiness.
The paper is significant because food enterprises can increase customer retention if the factors identified are considered. Although food quality, taste,
pricingandserviceareintegraltotherestaurantexperience,digitalmarketingcanstrengthencustomerrelationshipsbeforeandafterthepointofsale.
Research Gap
The supplied literature demonstrates that previous research has explored restaurant social-media adoption, social-media strategies, digital marketing
and business growth, social-media monitoring, hospitality social-media influence, brand trust and loyalty, and Facebook-based customer engagement.
Those studies, however, primarily focus on engagement, awareness, business growth or brand loyalty, rather than directly investigating customer
retentioninasmallIndianfoodbusiness.
Another gap relates to contextual factors. Some of the reviewed studies were conducted abroad or focused on restaurant chains, whereas the Indian
studyin the supplied literature examined digital marketing at a broader SME level rather thanspecifically connecting digital marketing activities with
retention in a local food business. The present study aims to address this gap by analysing customers of a specific small food business in Vijayawada
andcombiningdigital-marketingperceptionswithsatisfaction,revisitintentionandrecommendationintention.

Research Methodology
Research Design
The study follows a descriptive and analytical cross-sectional survey design. It describes respondent characteristics and perceptions and examines the
associationbetweendigital-marketingperceptionsandretention-relatedresponses.
Sample Design
The study uses 76 valid survey responses. A non-probability convenience/voluntary response approach is treated as the practical sampling method
because the data were obtained through a Google Form. The sample should not be interpreted as statistically representative of all customers in
Vijayawada.
Data Collection
Primarydatawere collectedthroughastructuredGoogleFormcontainingdemographicquestions,multiple-choicequestionsondigital-mediaexposure
andcustomerbehaviour,5-pointLikert-scalestatements,satisfactionandrevisitmeasures,andopen-endedquestions.
Questionnaire Preparation
The questionnaire moves from respondent profile to digital awareness and usage, perceptions of digital communication, behavioural influence,
retention indicators, and open-ended feedback. Likert-scale statements use a five-point response structure where higher values represent stronger
agreement.
Data Analysis
The analysis uses frequency counts, percentages, Likert-scale means,descriptive interpretation and Pearson correlation. Graphs are provided for every
survey question. Open-ended responses are summarized descriptively. The statistical association is interpreted cautiously because the design is cross-
sectionalandthesampleisnon-probabilitybased.

Hypothesis Testing and Interpretation
The digital-marketing perception index based on Q10–Q17 has a mean of 4.04/5, while the retention/recommendation index based on Q18–Q19 has a
mean of 4.21/5. Pearson correlation between the two indices is r = 0.882, p < 0.001. Therefore, the null hypothesis is rejected for this exploratory
associationtest andthealternativehypothesis issupported. Theresult means thathigherdigital-marketingperceptionscores arestatistically associated
with higher retention/recommendation scores in this sample. It does not prove that digital marketing causes retention because the study is cross-
sectional,thesampleisnon-probabilitybased,andotherfactorscanaffectcustomerbehaviour.

Conclusion
ThestudyindicatesthatdigitalmarketingisanimportantcomponentofthecustomerrelationshipforSmokedOutBBQ.Respondentsreporthighlevels
of awareness through social media, positive perceptions of digital information, and substantial influence from offers and online reviews. Instagram is
the dominant digital platform in the sample, while discounts and offers are the most frequently selected digital activity for encouraging revisits. The
strong positive correlation between the digital-marketing perception index and the retention/recommendation index suggests that stronger digital
experiencesareassociatedwithstrongerretention-relatedresponsesamongthesurveyedcustomers.
At the same time, the findings showthat digital marketing should work inconjunction with the fundamental restaurant experience. Food quality, taste
and value are major return reasons. An integrated approach of good food and service alongside consistent digital communication, attractive content,
crediblereviews,personalizedoffersandloyalty-orientedengagementarethereforeindicatedbythesurvey.
The studyhas limitations: the sample consists of 76voluntary respondents, the survey is cross-sectional and self-reported, and the correlation analysis
identifiesassociationratherthancausation.Futureresearchcanuselargerprobability-basedsamples,longitudinaldataandobjectivebusinessindicators
suchasrepeat-purchaserecords,campaignredemptionandcustomerlifetimevalue.
