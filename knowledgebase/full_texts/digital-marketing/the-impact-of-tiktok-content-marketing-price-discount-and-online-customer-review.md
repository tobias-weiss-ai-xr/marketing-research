This study aims to analyze the influence of Content Marketing, Price Discount,
and Online Customer Reviews on TikTok on Impulse Buying among consumers’
of Gokana  Ramen & Teppan at Metropolitan Mall Bekasi. The research
employed a quantitative method using a non -probability sampling technique
through a purposive sampling approach, with the sample size determined using
the Lemeshow formula. Data were collected throu gh questionnaires distributed
to 100 respondents, utilizing a Likert scale as the measurement instrument. The
data were analyzed using multiple linear regression with the assistance of IBM
SPSS Statistics version 29. The results indicate that Content Marketing and Price
Discount have a positive and significant effect on Impulse Buying, while Online
Customer Reviews do not have a significant effect. Price Discount emerged as
the most dominant variable influencing Impulse Buying. Simultaneously, the
three ind ependent variables have a positive and significant effect on Impulse
Buying. It can be concluded that attractive digital marketing strategies and
beneficial price promotions are capable of increasing consumers’ impulse buying
behavior.

1. Introduction
The food and beverage (F&B) industry continues to grow alongside increasing consumer needs and changes
in modern lifestyles. This development has encouraged the emergence of various culinary services and food
ordering applications that are becoming increas ingly popular [1]. As a result, competition among business
operators has intensified, requiring companies to continuously innovate in attracting consumer attention [2].
According to data from the Indonesian (Badan Pusat Statistik), the number of restaurant s in Bekasi City
increased by 9.16% in 2024. This growth was particularly significant in South Bekasi District, where the
number of restaurant businesses rose from 665 to 776 units. This condition indicates that South Bekasi not
only has the highest number of culinary businesses but also serves as one of the main contributors to the growth
of the culinary sector in Bekasi City [3] . The increasing level of competition encourages business owners to
implement appropriate, focused, and well -planned strategies to maintain their market position and business
sustainability amid dynamic market conditions [4]. Furthermore, businesses need to differentiate their products
or services from those of competitors. In the purchasing decision -making process, consumers tend to rely on
various sources of information, including recommendations from friends and family as well as online reviews
[5].

Gokana Ramen & Teppan is one of the leading Japanese restaurant brands in Indonesia, operating
numerous outlets across various cities, including Bekasi. Although the brand has maintained its position as
one of the top ramen restaurant brands in Indonesia, Gokana experienced fluctuations in its Top Brand Index
(TBI) during the 2022 –2024 period. Its TBI score declined from 13.6% in 2022 to 11.1% in 2023, before
slightly increasing to 11.9% in 2024. These fluctuations may indicate challenges related to marketing
effectiveness and changing consumer preferences in the digital era. Understanding the factors that influence
consumers’ purchasing decisions is therefore essential for maintaining competitiveness and strengthening
brand positioning in the market.
Previous studies have suggested that impulse buying behavior may be influenced by Content Marketing,
Price Discount, and Online Customer Review [8], [9], [10]. Engaging content marketing can attract consumer
attention and stimulate purchase interest [8]. Price discoun ts provide economic benefits that may encourage
consumers to make immediate purchases without prior planning [11]. Meanwhile, online customer reviews
help consumers obtain information regarding product quality based on the experiences of other users, which
may influence their purchasing decisions [12].
However, previous studies have reported inconsistent findings regarding the influence of these variables
on impulse buying. Several studies found that Content Marketing has a positive and significant effect o n
Impulse Buying, including studies conducted at the G -Walk culinary tourism destination [13], among
UMSIDA students [14], on Jiniso fashion products among Generation Z consumers in Surabaya [15], on Gen-
Z fashion products through TikTok Shop in Surabaya [8], and on qomeshop fashion products in Kediri [16].
In contrast, studies involving TikTok Shop users in university environments [17] and TikTok Shop
marketplace users in Semaran g [18] reported that Content Marketing does not have a significant effect on
Impulse Buying.
Similar inconsistencies have also been identified in studies examining Price Discount. Price Discount was
found to have a positive and significant effect on Impuls e Buying in TikTok Shop marketplaces [9], TikTok
Shop products in Pekanbaru [19], Shopee marketplaces [20], Galeri Batik Sampit [21], and among Shopee
users from university student groups [22]. Conversely, studies conducted at Muara Mall Ternate [23], among
supermarket consumers in Irian City [24], while another study found that Price Discount does not significantly
influence Impulse Buying [25].
Likewise, Online Customer Review was found to positively and significantly affect Impulse Buying
among Shopee users in Madiun City [10], Shopee marketplace consumers’ [26], [27], [28], and TikTok Shop
Live users [29]. Nevertheless, other studies reported that Online Customer Review does not significantly affect
Impulse Buying [22], [30].
The inconsistent findings re ported in previous studies indicate that the relationships between Content
Marketing, Price Discount, Online Customer Review, and Impulse Buying require further investigation.

2. Methods
2.1 Grand Theory
Consumer behavior  theory explains how individuals, groups, and organizations select, purchase, use, and
evaluate products, services, ideas, or experiences to satisfy their needs and desires [31]. Furthermore, consumer
behavior theory describes the decision -making process, including information search, alternative evaluation,
purchase decisions, and product usage, which are influenced by cultural, social, personal, and psychological
factors [32]. In this study, Consumer Behavior Theory explains that consumers’ purchasing dec isions are
influenced by external marketing stimuli. Content Marketing, Price Discount, and Online Customer Review
serve as external stimuli that shape consumers’ perceptions and evaluations, which may encourage Impulse
Buying. Therefore, Consumer Behavior  Theory provides the theoretical foundation for the proposed
relationships among the research variables.
2.2 Impulse Buying
Impulse Buying is a purchasing behavior that occurs spontaneously without prior planning, triggered by a
strong urge or specific stimuli encountered during the shopping process, which encourages consumers’ to make
an immediate purchase  [33], [34]. According to Consumer Behavior Theory, impulse buying occurs when
consumers’ respond to external marketing stimuli that influence their emotions and purchase decisions, leading
to unplanned buying behavior [32].
2.3 Content Marketing
Content Marketing is a marketing process that involves creating and distributing engaging and relevant content
through various media channels to attract attention and build audience engagement  [35], [36]. From the
perspective of Consumer Behavior Theory, engaging content functions as an external stimulus that attracts
consumers’ attention and shapes their purchase decisions, thereby increasing the likelihood of impulse buying
[32].
2.4 Price Discount
Price Discount is a reduction from the regular price offered by sellers to consumers’ under certain conditions
or during specific periods in order to attract purchase interest and provide consumers’ with greater perceived
value [37], [38].Consumer Behavior Theory suggests that price discounts increase consumers’ perceived value
and create a sense of urgency, which can encourage spontaneous purchasing decisions [32].
2.5 Online Customer Review
Online Customer Review refers to consumers’ reviews that contain their experiences, opinions, and
evaluations of a product or service shared through di gital media. These reviews can be accessed by other
consumers’ and are often used as an important source of information in making purchasing decisions  [39],
[40]. Based on Consumer Behavior Theory, online customer reviews provide information that shapes
consumers’ evaluations and reduces purchase uncertainty, thereby increasing the likelihood of impulse buying
[32].

2.7 Research Design and Sampling
This study adopted a quantitative research design. The target population comprised consumers of Gokana
Ramen & Teppan at Metropolitan Mall Bekasi who owned a TikTok account, had purchased G okana
promotional vouchers through TikTok Deals, and had redeemed the vouchers at the Metropolitan Mall Bekasi
outlet. Respondents were recruited directly at the outlet during April 20 –25, April 27 –29, and May 1 –4.
Purposive sampling was applied under a no n-probability sampling approach, with incomplete responses and
respondents who did not meet the criteria excluded from the analysis. The sample size was determined using
the Lemeshow formula for an unknown population, with a 95% confidence level (Z = 1.96) , maximum
estimated proportion (p = 0.5), and 10% precision (d = 0.1), resulting in 96.04 respondents and rounded to 100
respondents. Data were collected using a questionnaire containing 23 statement items measured on a five-point
Likert scale. The data we re analyzed using validity and reliability tests, classical assumption tests, multiple
linear regression, t-test, F-test, and the coefficient of determination (R²) using IBM SPSS Statistics version 29.

3.13 Discussion
This study was conducted to investigate the effects of Content Marketing, Price Discount, and Online Customer
Review on Impulse Buying amon g consumers’ of Gokana Ramen & Teppan Metropolitan Mall Bekasi. The
results reveal that Content Marketing and Price Discount significantly affect Impulse Buying, while Online
Customer Review does not have a significant impact.
The significant effect of Content Marketing supports the findings of [8], [13], [14], [15], [16]. which reported
that engaging digital content can stimulate spontaneous purchasing behavior. Content distributed through
TikTok provides information and entertainment simultaneously, makin g consumers more interested in
products and encouraging unplanned purchases.  Furthermore, Price Discount was found to positively and
significantly influence Impulse Buying. This finding is supported by previous studies conducted by [9], [19],
[20], [22]. A ttractive discounts encourage consumers to make immediate purchase decisions because they
perceive greater economic benefits from promotional offers. In this study, Price Discount was identified as the
strongest determinant of Impulse Buying, indicating that promotional pricing strategies remain highly effective
in attracting consumers’ and increasing spontaneous purchases.
In the context of this study, respondents purchased Gokana promotional vouchers through TikTok Deals
and redeemed them at the Gokana Ramen & Teppan Metropolitan Mall Bekasi outlet. Therefore, the significant
effects of Content Marketing and Price Discount indicate that engaging promotional content and attractive
discount offers on TikTok Deals can effectively encourage consumers to make spontaneous purchase decisions
before redeeming the vouchers at the restaurant. In contrast, Online Customer Review showed a positive but
insignificant relationship with Impulse Buying. Similar findings were reported by [22], [30], suggesting that
customer reviews are not always the main consideration in making impulsive purchase decisions. Consumers’

creative TikTok content and maintaining attractive price promotions, as these factors have proven to encourage
Impulse Buying. Although Online  Customer Review does not significantly affect consumers’ spontaneous

changes in consumer behavior over time. Future research is recommended to incorporate other variables, such
as electronic word of mouth, brand image, or sales promotion , and to involve broader research objects and
locations to obtain more comprehensive findings.

4. Conclusion
This study has several limitations. First, the research was conducted at a single Gokana Ramen & Teppan
outlet, namely Metropolitan Mall Bekasi, and used purposive sampling, which limits the generalizability of
the findings to other outlets or consumer groups. Second, the study used self -reported questionnaire data, so
the results may be influenced by respondents’ perceptions and understanding of t he questions. Third, all
variables were measured using the same questionnaire, which may result in common-method bias. Fourth, the
cross-sectional design only reflects consumer responses during the research period and does not capture
changes in impulse buying behavior over time. Future research is recommended to involve multiple outlets or
different research objects, consider additional variables, and use longitudinal or qualitative approaches to
obtain a broader understanding of impulse buying behavior. This study was conducted for academic purposes,
