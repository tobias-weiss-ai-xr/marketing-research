Abstract. With the rapid development of the global pet economy, the cognition of pet health
management by pet owners has changed from simple feeding to precision nutrition. The goal
of this study is to investigate the impact of precision nutrition marketing strategies on pet
food consumer decision-making. This study applies quantitative analysis and qualitative
case studies to analyze how Royal Canin creates brand differentiation advantages by
satisfying breed-specific and life-cycle needs. The report explores Royal Canin's "nutrient-
first" philosophy and its digital platforms, including interactive tools such as health
calculators and breed-specific guides. Digital tools are built to close the information gap
between the brand and pet owners. The research showed that a science-based precision
nutrition system can significantly increase consumer brand loyalty and perceived value. The
results show that contemporary digital marketing converts buying into long-term advisory
relationships. In addition, the research explores the penetration logic of this model in
different regional markets and provides some theoretical references for the market
positioning and strategic decision-making of global pet food companies under a complex
economic environment.
Keywords: Precision nutrition, pet food, consumer behavior, marketing strategies, brand
value
1. Introduction
The global pet industry is experiencing a radical transformation from traditional commodity-based
consumption to sophisticated data-driven health management in the digital age. As pet owners
continue to consider their animals as part of the family, the market for nutrition that goes beyond just
survival and into "precision nutrition"—a model designed to meet the specific physiological and
life-stage needs of individual animals—is expanding [1].
This research studies the effectiveness of the precision nutrition marketing strategies on consumer
behavior, with a special emphasis on the Royal Canin brand as a main case study. The author, a
student at the School of Computer Sciences, Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM), adopts a
multidisciplinary approach, merging software engineering principles, including data modeling and
analytical visualisation, with traditional marketing research frameworks.

2. The evolution of digital marketing in the pet nutrition industry
2.1. The paradigm of precision nutrition: from commodity to customized intervention
Precision nutrition is an emerging concept for companion animals, moving away from the traditional
mass market formula approach to specific, individualized interventions. It's a reflection of the
increasing understanding of how what we feed can influence the long-term physiological outcomes
of our pets, moving well beyond merely supplying the basic macronutrients to meeting the specific
metabolic, behavioral and life-stage needs of each animal [2].
Industry leaders such as Royal Canin have embraced this paradigm, and at the core is the
"nutrient-first" philosophy. Here we put science ahead of marketing trends so that formulas are
developed to meet specific biological requirements of certain breeds and health conditions.
"Research into companion animal health is evolving and the industry is focusing more on digestible,
highly bioavailable ingredients to avoid chronic disease and enhance quality of life" [3, 4]. This has
led to precision nutrition moving from a scientific concept to a market-led imperative for companies
that need to base product portfolios on hard physiological data rather than consumer whims.
2.2. Digital marketing as a driver of consumer engagement and trust
As the shift towards precision nutrition is driven by the significance of information transparency and
professional endorsement, digital platforms are indispensable for the interaction of brands with
consumers. Digital marketing here is not just a promotional tool but an important interface to deliver
scientific value and consumer trust [5]. For brands like Royal Canin, the digital ecosystem is a
knowledge center that guides pet owners through the complexities of breed-specific needs and life-
stage health requirements.
This digital-first approach allows brands to bring the abstract nature of nutritional science into a
tangible consumer application. Brands can move from transactional to long-term advisory
relationships by offering personalized content, including digital breed-health guides and interactive
dietary calculators. Such digital interventions are critical for addressing the "information
asymmetry" often experienced in the pet food market, empowering consumers to make data-backed
decisions based on their pets' unique physiological profiles. Moreover, the ability of these platforms
to measure and respond to user behavior highlights the effectiveness of a data-driven strategy where
the quality of digital engagement is directly related to brand loyalty and the perceived value of
precision nutrition.
3. Case study: quantifying brand resonance and strategic dynamics
3.1. Digital metrics as a gateway to brand resonance
A review of the performance metrics for April 2026 on royalcanin.com offers an empirical look at a
thriving digital ecosystem. In this time the platform has accumulated 3.6 million total visits, making
it more than just a transactional hub; it is a primary knowledge repository for precision nutrition [6].

3.2. The shift in brand-consumer power dynamics
Data-driven marketing technologies have changed the power dynamic between pet food brands and
consumers forever, from one-way influence to two-way engagement. Historically, brands held the
informational asymmetry, controlling the story around nutritional quality. However, the rise of
digital platforms and tailored analytical tools has empowered pet owners to transition from passive
recipients to active and informed decision-makers.
Interactive health calculators, breed-specific data visualization and customized feedback loops
are among the technical drivers that have democratized access to specialized veterinary and
nutritional insights. This technological shift means brands like Royal Canin must shift to a more
transparent and advisory-centric marketing strategy [7]. In this new paradigm, the "brand power" is
no longer limited to market reach, but to the brand's ability to be a trusted digital partner providing
actionable, evidence-based solutions.
Furthermore, this technology-driven engagement creates a feedback loop in both directions.
Consumers create data through their interactions—such as usage patterns and health-related
questions—that helps inform the brand's R&D and precision nutrition strategies. Consequently, the
power paradigm has shifted to a symbiotic relationship where the brand provides scientific authority,
and the consumer provides the granular data to fine-tune that science. The shift points to a critical
strategic imperative: companies should now focus on building meaningful digital ecosystems rather
than traditional mass advertising, because the power to dominate the market now lies with those
who can best translate complex nutritional data into user-centric value [8-10].
4. Conclusion
This research has studied the paradigm change that has occurred in the pet food industry, where
precision nutrition has moved from a scientific theory for specialists to a market-led imperative. The
effectiveness of current nutrition brands is closely related to their capacity to use digital platforms as
information centers for scientific education. This study analyses the digital marketing techniques of
Royal Canin.
The data indicates that the use of digital marketing technology has shifted the balance of power
between brands and pet owners. Brands have become advisory partners, who through data-driven
interactions and personalized digital ecosystems, make the purchase an informed decision based on
value. High interaction metrics and global traffic reach prove that digital engagement is a key
feedback loop for brands to develop their precision nutrition solutions based on user needs.

This research gives an overview analysis of the digital marketing methods within the paradigm of
precision nutrition. However, several limitations exist. The empirical approach relies mostly on
publicly available web traffic data for a certain period of time, which may not capture the entire
complexity of offline consumer behavior or long-term purchasing habits. Moreover, the use of
secondary data sources means that following research should use primary data, such as large-scale
consumer surveys or long-term behavioral monitoring, to further corroborate these findings.
Looking ahead, the use of AI-powered data presents a promising area for precision nutrition
marketing. As data processing develops, machine learning algorithms could be used in future
research to further define the precise association between tailored digital interaction and health
outcomes in companion animals. These changes are likely to result in more advanced, automated
marketing strategies where firms will be able to foresee and meet consumer requirements better than
ever before.
