 Abstract: This study aims to analyze the competitive
environment and formulate development strategies for the
Bakso Meletup micro, small, and medium enterprise
(MSME) using Porter’s Five Forces framework. This
research employed a descriptive qualitative approach, with
informants selected purposively, including the business
owner, employees, suppliers, and consumers. Data were
collected through observation, interviews, a nd
documentation and analyzed descriptively. The findings
indicate that rivalry among existing competitors is high,
driven by the large number of similar culinary businesses
in Palembang. The threat of substitute products is also
high, as consumers can eas ily access alternatives such as
chicken noodles, seblak, cilok, and cimol at relatively lower
prices. Buyer bargaining power is moderate, because
consumers can switch to cheaper alternatives, although
Bakso Meletup’s distinctive menu and bulk -purchase
options help maintain customer interest. Supplier
bargaining power is high, primarily because the business
depends on a single supplier for its main raw material,
beef, giving the supplier considerable  influence over raw
material prices. Based on these findings, the recommended
strategies include strengthening product differentiation,
improving service quality, optimizing digital marketing,
building customer loyalty, and developing alternative
supplier relationships to support business competitiveness
and sustainability.

Keywords: Competitive Strategy,
Digital Marketing, Porter's Five
Forces, Small and Medium
Enterprises, Strategic

very large, but at the same time it also increases the intensity of competition so that business actors are
required to be able to develop appropriate business strategies to maintain the sustainability of their
businesses.
Despite its promising prospects, MSMEs in the culinary sector still face various challenges that
can impact business performance. In February 2026, annual inflation reached 4.76 percent, with the
food, beverage, and tobacco group experiencing a 3.51 percent price increase. The ris ing prices of
various commodities, such as chicken, rice, shallots, and eggs, as key ingredients in food products, have
led to increased production costs, potentially depressing business profits (National Statistics Agency,
2026). In addition to pressures on production costs, rapidly  changing culinary trends have also
intensified competition among businesses, necessitating business development strategies that can
enhance competitiveness.
Bakso Meletup, a culinary MSME in Palembang, also faces this problem. Bakso Meletup has
been opera ting since 2018 and offers a variety of products and has opened branches. However, its
financial records are still kept simply using a cash book that only records daily income and capital
deductions without more detailed records. In terms of marketing, despite utilizing social media platforms
like Instagram and TikTok, as well as delivery services like GoFood, ShopeeFood, and GrabFood,
digital marketing has not been optimal due to difficulties in determining content ideas, promot ional
designs, and utilizing social media as a marketing tool. This situation presents a challenge amidst the
growing number of meatball businesses in Palembang, which increases market competition.
Business development efforts not only require improvements in production, finance, marketing,
and human resources, but also require an understanding of the industry's competitive structure through
Porter's Five Forces analysis. This analysis allows busines s actors to identify competitive conditions
based on the s trength of competition between competitors, the threat of new entrants, the threat of
substitute products, the bargaining power of buyers, and the bargaining power of suppliers, so that
strategies are more appropriate to the business conditions faced. Previous research shows that high buyer
bargaining power and competition between business actors demand product differentiation and
improved service quality (Sahi et al., 2024), while the threat of subs titute products and new entrants
encourages the importance of innovation and brand strengthening (Pratama et al., 2025). Furthermore,
Porter's Five Forces analysis has been proven to determine strategic direction through differentiation,
service, and promotion (Antoni & Timothy, 2024).

from the aspects of production, finance, marketing, and human resources, analyze the condition of
business competition using Porter's Five Forces, and formulate business development strategies that are
in accordance with the conditions of the Bakso Meletup business. This research is important because it

Research methods
This study employed a qualitative appro ach with a descriptive method. Qualitative research is
a method used to understand a phenomenon in depth based on perceptions of the object under study,
with the analysis results presented descriptively (Sahir & Afni, 2021). The study was conducted at the
Bakso Meletup MSME located on Jalan Residen H. Amaluddin, Sukamaju, Sako District, Palembang
City, South Sumatra. The study focused on analyzing business development strategies based on
entrepreneurial aspects, including production, finance, marketing, hum an resources, and Porter's Five
Forces analysis.
The research instrument used interview guidelines, observation sheets, and documentation as
tools to obtain research data. The data used consisted of primary and secondary data. Primary data was
obtained dir ectly through interviews with business owners, raw materia l suppliers, employees, and
consumers of Bakso Meletup. Next, the data was analyzed descriptively through the stages of data
reduction, data presentation, and drawing conclusions. The analysis was c arried out by examining the
condition of MSMEs based on as pects of production, finance, marketing, and human resources, then
continued with Porter's Five Forces analysis which includes competition between competitors in the
industry, the threat of new entr ants, the threat of substitute products, the bargaining po wer of buyers,
and the bargaining power of suppliers.
The research subjects were determined using purposive sampling, which selects informants
based on specific considerations and characteristics th at align with the research objectives (Sari et al.,
2023). The research informants consisted of the owner of the Bakso Meletup MSME as a key informant
because he has in -depth knowledge of business conditions, including production processes, finance,
marketing, human resources, and business competition. In additio n, raw material suppliers and
consumers were selected to obtain information on supplier and buyer bargaining power, while
employees served as supporting informants to strengthen and compare informat ion obtained from key
informants. Informants were selected based on the criteria of understanding the conditions of the Bakso
Meletup MSME, being directly involved in business activities, and being willing to provide objective
information regarding product quality, service, and factors influencing purchasing.
The research procedure began with determining the location and focus of the research on the
Bakso Meletup MSME. The next stage involved data collection through observation, in -depth
interviews, and doc umentation to obtain information related to the business c onditions. The data
obtained were then classified and analyzed based on aspects of production, finance, marketing, human
resources, and Porter's Five Forces to identify competitive conditions, opportunities, and threats facing
the business. The results of the analysis were then used as a basis for formulating a business development
strategy for the Bakso Meletup MSME, then compiled into research results, conclusions, and
recommendations.

managing finances and marketing of Bakso Meletup, and is responsible for business development and

2. Production
 The production department at the Bakso Meletup UMKM is responsible for preparing and
ensuring the availability of raw materials, carrying out the production process into finished products,
maintaining taste quality, storing raw materials and production results.

Porter's Five Forces Analysis
          Porter's Five Forces analysis is used  to examine the level of competition and strength of an
industry through five forces. This analysis helps business owners understand the competitive landscape
they face and provide appropriate strategies.Ramli and Kartini (2022),There are several factors from the
five forces, namely competition within the same industry, the threat of new entrants, the threat of
substitute products, buyer bargaining power, supplier bargaining power with the following indicators:
1. Competition Between Competitors in the Same Industry
         Industry growth to show how the condition of a business is devel oping. Based on data sources
using spreadsheet extensions and information from Google Maps, the number of Meatball MSMEs in
Palembang is 107 and other Meatball MSMEs that are not yet registered on Google Maps shows a lot
of meatball culinary competition th at becomes a competitor for Bakso Meletup so that competition is
getting tighter. Bakso Meletup's revenue in 2025 has increased compa red to the previous year. The
current increase in material prices affects the purchase of raw materials for making meatball s, because
the rising price of raw materials causes less profit. Bakso Meletup diverts it by frequently participating
in events such as Car Free Night (CFN), food bazaars at concerts, and opening a food court.
         Fixed costs are costs that a business must routinely incur regardless of sales, whether high or low.
Based on interviews, Bakso Meletup's routine costs include raw materi als, rent, electricity, water,
internet, and employee costs. The largest expense comes from purchasing raw materials. Despite these
fixed costs, Bakso Meletup remains profitable, as evidenced by its annual revenue increase.
Having a unique product compared  to competitors can be a business advantage. Based on
interviews, the owner of Bakso Meletup differentiates himself by offering a variety of meatball dishes,
including mozzarella meatballs, tendon meatballs, and popping meatballs, with a choice of red and broth
broth. Furthermore, Bakso Meletup maintains its signature flavor and quality. This strategy ensures the
product's distinctive character, with a varied menu that attracts and retains customers.
Barriers to exit are obstacles that business owners consider when considering discontinuing their
operations, even if sales are declining and competition is intensifying. Interviews revealed that barriers
to exit for the Bakso Meletup business are high, as the business has already  invested personal funds to
open. Furthermore, the Bakso Meletup business is a primary source of income, so it must be prepared
to sustain it, regardless of the circumstances.

2. Threat of New Entrants
The threat of new entrants represents a barrier to entry for new players in an industry, either low
or high. Low barriers, such as low capital requirements, readily available technology, and a simple
licensing process, mean more businesses can enter the industry. Conversely, high barriers, such as high
capital requirements, brand advantages, technological expertise, and difficult regulations, mean fewer
businesses can enter the industry.(Murhadi, 2024).
Capital requirements are the costs incurred to open a business. Based on interviews, the initial
capital required by business owners to open their busi nesses was relatively small, at Rp21,750,000,
making it possible for many newcomers to open similar businesses.
Based on research results, the Bakso Meletup business is unique compared to other meatball
businesses, offering a varied menu and prioritizing q uality and taste. However, despite its uniqueness,
meatballs are easily copied, especially by newcomers, as evidenced by several meatball businesses that
already offer variations like Bakso Meletup.
Access to distribution relates to how business owners rea ch consumers to sell their products.
Based on interview results,To anticipate the upcoming explosion of meatballs among stall sellers in the
food industry, they are anticipating this by intensifying marketing, distribution and promotional strategy
activities such as Distributing sales directly to consumers via WA, Instagram, TikTok, and business
premises, as well as indirectly distributing through intermediaries via resellers.
Government policies are regulations that business actors must comply with to run a  business.
Based on Government Regulation (PP) Number 28 of 2025 regulates the Implementation of Risk-Based
Business Licensing (PBBR), The scope of the implementation of PBBR includes: basic requirements;
Business Licensing (PB ); Business Licensing to Supp ort Business Activities (PB UMKU); norms,
standards, procedures, and criteria; OSS system services; supervision; evaluation and policy reform;
funding; resolution of problems and obstac les; and sanctions (Audit Agency of the Republic of
Indonesia, 2025).
Based on the interview results, Bakso Meletup already has a business permit in the form of a

Porter's Five Forces Analysis
1. Competition between Competitors in the Same Industry
       The threat of competition from competitors in the same industry at Bakso Meletup is  considered
high. This finding is in line with Ramli and Kartini (2022) ,The higher the level of competition of a
company, the higher the profitability of the industry, but the company's profitability decreases.
       The growth of the Bakso Meletup industry poses a high threat. The large number of meatball
businesses around the business location makes competition increasingly fierce, and rising ingredient
prices impact profits. The threat of fixed costs for Baks o Meletup is moderate. Bakso Meletup incurs
fixed costs such as raw materials, employee salaries, electricity, water, rent, and internet costs, which
are all incurred monthly.
Product differentiation at Bakso Meletup poses a low threat due to its uniquenes s compared to
other competitors, giving consumers a reason to choose Bakso Meletup. Product differentiation
encourages consumers to consider purchasing due to the uniqueness and quality of the product offered.
Meanwhile, exit barriers pose a high threat. This is because Bakso Meletup is the business owner's
primary source of income. This makes it likely that the business owner will remain resilient despite
facing challenges and intense competition.Wicaksono, (2025)explains that the higher the exit barriers,
the greater the tendency of business owners to continue operating so that the number of competitors
increases.
Competition among meatball businesses in the area is quite high. Therefore, Bakso Meletup
implements a differentiation strategy by maintaining the uniqueness of its product compared to
neighboring meatball businesses. Furthermore, it is necessary to improve service, maintain consistent
taste, and maintain customer loyalty, as this business is the primary source of income for the owner.

2. Threat of new entrants
The threat of new entrants into the industry depends heavily on how easy or difficult it is for new
businesses to enter. The easier it is, the more difficult the competition will be because many  new
businesses will open similar businesses.(Magretta, 2021). Based on the analysis results, the threat level
of entry of new competitors in Bakso Meletup is classified as medium, because Bakso Meletup has a
medium level of intensity in product differentiation, low intensity in distribution channels, and low
intensity level in government policy factors.
The high capital requirement poses a high threat because the capital requirement is not substantial,
allowing new entrants to easily enter the industry. Product differentiation represents a moderate threat.
Bakso Meletup has unique product characteristics that distinguish it from other meatball competitors,
but these characteristics can still be easily imitated by other businesses.
       Regarding access to distribution channels, the threat level is low. Bakso Meletup distributes its
products directly to consumers via social media, rather than through resellers. Bakso Meletup has a large
customer base, so it will take time for newcomers to build a similar network.
       Furthermore, government policies indicate a low threat level. Bakso Meletup has a business license,
is halal certified, and already owns the trademark. These conditions are advantages for Bakso Meletup,
but regulations pose a barrier to potential new entrants.
       The strategy to face the threat of new entrants is to strengthen the brand image through promotions
that explain the advantages and uniqueness of Bakso Meletup, and strengthen relationshi ps with

Conclusion
This study shows that the internal conditions of the Bakso Meletup  MSME in the aspects of
production, finance, marketing, and human resources still have opportunities to be developed to increase
business competitiveness. Porter's Five Forces analysis shows that the level of competition among
culinary business actors is relatively high, supported by the large number of similar businesses, the ease
of entry of new competitors, and the high bargaining  power of consumers in determining product
choices. On the other hand, good relationships with suppliers and product character istics that have a
distinctive taste are strengths that can be utilized to maintain the business's position in the market. Based
on these conditions, the business development strategy is directed at improving product quality,
optimizing digital marketing, improving financial management, strengthening human resources, and
product and service innovation so that the business is able to adapt to changes in the business
environment and maintain a competitive advantage.
This study still has limitations because it  was conducted on a single research object, so the
results obtained cannot describe the condition of all MSMEs in the culinary sec tor. In addition, the
analysis focused on the internal conditions of the business and the competitive structure based on Porter's
Five Forces, so it has not integrated other strategic analysis approaches. Therefore, further research is
recommended to invol ve more research objects, expand the scope of the research area, and combine
Porter's Five Forces with other strategic analysi s methods to obtain more comprehensive
recommendations. Practically, the results of this study can be a consideration for the owner of the Bakso
Meletup MSME in developing a more effective business development strategy, as well as serve as a
reference for other culinary MSMEs in increasing competitiveness, strengthening marketing, and
developing their businesses sustainably.
