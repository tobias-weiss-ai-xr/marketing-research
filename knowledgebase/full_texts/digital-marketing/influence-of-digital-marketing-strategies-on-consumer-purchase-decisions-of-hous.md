Abstract
This study investigated the influence of digital marketing strategies  on consumer purchase of
household products in Delta State, Nigeria.  The specific objectives were to investigate the
influence of social media; email marketing \; online adverts, and website search engines on
consumer purchase decisions of household products in Delta State . The study adopted a
descriptive research design using a survey research method. The population of the study
comprised the consumers of household products in Delta State. The sample size was 382
consumers which was derived using the Cochran (1963) formula. A structured questionnaire was
used as an instrument of data collection for the study.
Findings of the study indicated that the social media; email marketing, online adverts, and website
search engines influence consumer purchase of household products in Delta State. Therefore, the
study concludes that digital marketing strategies have significant influence on consumer purchase
decisions of household products in Delta State , and recommended amongst others that business
owners should leverage continual digital marketing development and regularly appraise the
effectiveness of the marketing tools employed.

Keywords: Digital Marketing , Consumer Purchase Decision, Household Products, website
marketing, email advertisement.

2. LITERATURE REVIEW
Digital Marketing
Digital marketing uses technology to become involved with customers by distributing information
online through various digital channels. The concept of digital marketing is defined as the sale and
purchase of information, products, and services through a computer or internet network (Rao &
Ratnamadhuri, 2018). The capacity to contact the targeted customer via social media and search
engines is the most significant benefit that digital marketing has over traditional marketing (Sinha
& Singh, 2018).  As a result of digital marketing, products promotion has become an increasingly
important component in the new digital age. Digital marketing took advantage of the existence of
technology by providing online content, and it connects with the consumer through digital
channels. The biggest advantage that digital marketing has over traditional marketing is the ability
to reach target customers using search engines and the lower cost compared to traditional
marketing (Agus et al., 2021). In the modern world, digital marketing is essential in increasing and

Consumer Purchase Decisions
Consumer purchase decision involves the psychological processes that consumers go through in
recognizing needs, finding ways to solve these needs and making a purchase decision on whether
to buy or not. (Silverman, 2019). Consumer purchase is the process of completing a transaction
after a selection has been made by the consumer.  Consumer purchase is the  process by which an
individual obtain the goods and services required to fulfil its basic needs and wants in the most
timely and cost-effective manner. Consumer purchase starts with the consumer making decisions
because the actual purchase. Consumer purchase  decision is some considerations made by the
consumer before the actual purchase is made. Consumer purchase decision is the thought process
that leads a consumer from identifying a need, generating options, and choosing a specific product
and brand.
Understanding consumer purchase behavior is pivotal for effective marketing, as it helps
companies tailor their strategies to meet the precise needs and preferences of their target markets.
This behavior is influenced by cultural, social, personal, and psy chological factors, which shape
the decisions consumers make about what, when, and how to buy products (Qureshi, et al. 2015).

Theoretical Framework
This study is anchored on the Technology Acceptance Model. The technology acceptance model
(TAM) postulates that “individual’s behavior is influenced by their perceived usefulness and ease
of use of a particular technology. In the context of digital marketing, individuals’ behavior towards
a particular product or service is influenced by their perception of the usefulness and eas e of use
of the digital marketing tools used to promote that product or service”.
This theory is considered suitable for this study because it can help in understanding the complex
relationship between digital marketing and consumer purchase behavior. The theory of technology
acceptance model provide insight into various factors that influence consumer buying behavior
towards digital marketing. Organizations can use this knowledge to develop effective digital
marketing strategies that align with their customers’ buying decisions.

Empirical Review
A number of empirical studies have been conducted on the relationship between digital marketing
and consumer buying decision with divergent results. For instance, Ogbu and Gbandi  (2025).
study investigated the influence of brand page content value on consumer engagement in online
brand communities. A cross -sectional survey research design was utilized, and the population
consisted of followers of the official brand pages of three Nigerian  online firms (Jumia Nigeria,
Konga Nigeria, and Payporte) on Facebo ok and Instagram. A total of 480 respondents were
sampled, with 455 respondents properly completing and returning the questionnaire. Descriptive
statistics including frequency distribution, mean and standard deviation, correlation, and multiple
regression analysis were employed to analyze the data collected from the questionnaire. The study
found that brand page content dimensions such as information value, originality value, and
relational value positively and significantly impact consumer engagement in Ni geria. However,
entertainment value and economic value do not significantly impact consumer engagement. The
study recommends that brand managers of the investigated e -commerce firms (Jumia Nigeria,
Konga Nigeria, and Payporte) and other related firms shoul d provide information on their goods
and services to customers in a reliable and timely manner to facilitate consumer engagement

Managers and Junior Staff were selected using the Taro Yamanes Sample size determination. A
research questionnaire was used in gathering data from the respondents. The Resource Based View
Theory was the theory adopted in this study. Descriptive and inferential statistics, including
regression, were employed. The findings reveal that social media usage positively impacts

Gap in Literature
It is clear that empirical research examining the relationship between digital marketing tactics and
customer purchasing decisions yields inconsistent findings, which may be explained by variations
in the analysis's location, secto r, and methodology. There are not  many empirical research in
Nigeria that examine how digital marketing tactics affect consumers' decisions to buy.
Furthermore, there is a lack of agreement on the subject due to the large number of differing
opinions and conclusions. Additionally, no previous study examined how digital marketing tactics
affect customer purchases, particularly for household goods in Delta State. Therefore, it was
hypothesised that this study will close this research gap.

5. CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATIONS
This study focused on the influence of digital marketing strategies  on consumer purchase of
household products in Delta State. The data generated from the respondents were subjected to
empirical analysis. Findings of the study indicated that the social media; email marketing, online
adverts, and website search engines influence consumer purchase of household products in Delta
State. Therefore, the study concludes that digital marketing strategies have  significant influence
on consumer purchase of household products in Delta State. In line with the findings of the study,
it is recommended that business owners should leverage the creative capabilities of their workforce
for continual digital marketing development and regularly appraise the effectiveness of the tools
employed. Additionally, business owners and managers are advised to engage in continuous digital
marketing efforts that align closely with customer needs and to incorporate robust feedback
mechanisms to refine customer experience continually.
This study provided valuable insights into the effect of digital marketing strategies on consumer
purchase decision of household items in in Delta State. However, its scope was limited to three
metropolitan areas of the state, suggesting the need for broad er geographical research to better
understand the dynamics at play across different locations. Future research could extend to other
states to assess if similar patterns emerge or if regional differences significantly influence digital
marketing's effectiveness.
