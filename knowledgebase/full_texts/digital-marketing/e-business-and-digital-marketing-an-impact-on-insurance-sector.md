Abstract:

The

technology

of

e-business

and

digital

marketing

changed

the

world

of

industries

and

the

same

applies

for

insurance.

This

paper

studies

the

e-business

and

digital

marketing

activities

for

the

insurance

industry,

and

how

digital

platforms

can

change

business

models

for

the

better.

With

e-business

integrated

into

insurance,

we

have

helped

customers

get

in

touch,

streamline

operations

and

enabled

new

customer

service

channels.

Digital

marketing

strategies

like

SEO,

social

media

marketing,

personalized

email

campaigns

have

also

brought

in

more

customers

for

acquiring,

loyal

and

purchasing.

This

paper

also

explains

what

obstacles

insurance

companies

face

when

deploying

these

technologies

such

as

data

privacy,

regulation

compliance,

and

changing

customer

expectations.

Through

case

studies,

industry

developments

and

real

data,

the

paper

reveals

why

digital

innovation

is

so

vital

to

growth,

competition

and

sustainability

in

the

insurance

industry

today.

1.

INTRODUCTION

Insurance

Business

in

India

India’s

insurance

market

is

in

a

transformation

mode

due

to

heightened

awareness,

regulations

and

strong

growth.

These

developments

are

changing

the

game,

and

they

have

implications

for

insurers.

This

is

a

time

for

some

businesses

to

reinvent

and

preserve,

for

others

it

is

a

time

for

responding

to

the

changing

world

in

order

to

stay

alive

and

thrive

in

the

long

run.

A

new

trend

in

Indian

insurance

industry

is

that

the

government

has

announced

FDI

(Fermandised

direct

investment)

of

up

to

74%.

It’s

positioned

the

sector

to

appeal

more

to

global

investors

and

afforded

insurers

the

capital

they

need

to

pursue

aggressive

growth

and

innovation

plans.

The

other

major

sector

disruptor

is

digital

transformation

that

completely

rewrites

the

way

companies

operate

and

manage

their

business.

As

more

people

access

the

internet

(Indians

have

429

million

internet

users

today

vs

829

million

by

2021,

which

represents

17.9%

increase

per

year)

the

influence

of

digital

technologies

on

the

insurance

industry

will

increase

with

each

passing

year.

Today,

the

insurance

companies

invest

a

lot

of

money

in

digital

technologies

to

make

business

more

efficient,

cost

less

and

manage

more

effectively.

Industry

estimates

state

that

digital

functions

can

save

20-30%

of

non-life

insurance

products

and

15-20%

of

life

products

in

costs.

Aside

from

the

cost

savings,

digital

transformation

provides

insurers

with

opportunities

to

collaborate,

innovate,

provide

personalized

services,

and

delight

their

customers.

Not

only

is

the

digital

revolution

reshaping

the

way

insurance

products

are

sold,

marketed

and

provided,

but

it

also

offers

opportunities

for

insurers

to

keep

up

with

changing

consumer

tastes

and

expectations.

With

more

and

more

consumers

turning

to

the

online

platforms

for

their

insurance,

the

ability

to

rethink

e-business

and

digital

marketing

models

will

be

key

to

growing

your

business,

enhancing

customer

satisfaction,

and

having

an

edge

in

the

rapidly

evolving

insurance

market.

1.2

POSTING

OF

WEB

AGGREGATORS

IN

DISTRIBUTION

web

aggregators

have

made

distribution

of

insurance

products

so

different.

The

internet

in

insurance

started

off

as

a

result

of

comparison

and

research

platforms

that

made

it

convenient

for

people

to

shop

around

and

make

their

choice.

When

Apna

Insurance

and

BimaDeal

were

launched

in

2005,

it

was

the

era

of

digitalization

in

insurance.

Then

came

Policybazaar,

Zibika,

Fintact,

MyInsuranceClub

and

Insuring

India,

all

over

the

years,

and

they

changed

the

way

consumers

deal

with

insurance.

These

aggregators

opened

up

an

alternative

marketplace

for

insurance

products.

In

2013,

the

Insurance

Regulatory

and

Development

Authority

of

India

(IRDAI)

published

web

aggregator

rules

which

made

it

easier

for

web

aggregators

to

be

registered

and

formalised.

As

of

March

2021,

the

registration

of

web

aggregators

rose

from

11

in

2013

to

22

showing

that

digital

platforms

are

taking

over

the

sale

of

insurance

products.

These

aggregators

are

now

multi-insurance

product

portals

providing

product

information,

benefits,

pricing,

claims

processing

and

multiple

insurance

carrier

comparisons.

2.

INSURERS

ENJOY

NEXT

GENERATION

OF

FACTORS

TO

TAILOR

THEIR

CUSTOMERS

1)

Simplicity:

Insurers

are

working

on

making

customer

interaction

simpler.

They

have

to

account

for

customer

demographics,

language

preferences,

and

communications

to

keep

insurance

data

available

and

accessible.

Video-

and

product

information

in

the

native

languages,

for

example,

improves

understanding.

Easy

communication

is

crucial

for

not

confusing

customers

and

developing

trust

with

them.

2)

Customer

Centricity:

Insurance

companies

need

to

be

more

customer

centric

with

less

IVR

and

network

communication

barriers.

To

make

the

entire

process

frictionless

and

user-friendly,

we

should

be

able

to

mix

channels

including

social,

financial,

digital

storage

and

health

trackers.

The

multi-channel

solution

allows

customers

to

build

more

relationship

and

communication

with

us.

3)

Personalization:

Insurance

companies

are

becoming

personal

marketers

using

data

from

customers

to

create

personalized

products

and

services.

Insurers

are

able

to

tailor

solutions

based

on

customer

behaviour

to

enhance

customer

experience.

Personalization

also

goes

for

conversations,

where

every

interaction

should

be

a

fit

for

the

customer.

4)

Digitization:

Insurers

adopt

digital

marketing

to

automate

the

processes.

The

move

to

digital

channels

for

communication,

claims

and

document

management

helps

streamline

the

operations

and

is

also

in

keeping

with

customer

demands

for

speed

and

convenience.

With

digital

business

process

implementation,

the

paperwork

gets

automated

and

customer

experience

gets

improved.

MOBILE

TECHNOLOGY

IN

LIFE

INSURANCE

Mobile

Technology

—

the

next

big

opportunity

for

life

insurers.

There

is

a

fast

mobile

migration

of

consumer

habits

and

companies

should

be

ready

for

it

if

they

are

to

keep

up.

Mobile

technologies

aren’t

a

novelty,

they

are

becoming

a

part

of

the

insurance

business.

Insurance

companies

are

stuck

with

how

to

use

these

technologies

to

serve

their

customers

better

and

to

deliver

more

services.

Traditionally,

insurance

industry

sold

goods

using

manual,

hand-held

approaches.

But

the

internet

and

mobile

technologies

are

changing

the

way

insurers

and

customers

interact.

Researchers

have

found

that

the

majority

of

new

life

insurance

customers

start

by

getting

a

quote

or

info

on

the

web,

before

visiting

an

agent

to

buy.

This

is

a

clear

signal

of

the

importance

of

digital

platforms

for

insurance

sales.

With

mobile

technology,

insurance

companies

have

an

opportunity

to

speed

up

selling

by

equipping

agents

with

devices

for

engaging

presentations

and

customers.

For

insurers,

they

have

to

be

very

careful

about

the

mobile

devices

and

platform

for

which

they

should

choose.

Cheap

and

cross-device

apps

that

run

on

multiple

devices

are

getting

in.

These

apps

allow

agents

to

move

about

and

be

in

touch

with

clients

and

be

able

to

find

marketing

materials

much

more

easily.

Insurers

can

also

use

mobile

to

hire

and

keep

younger

sales

staff,

offer

a

better

customer

experience,

and

eliminate

error.

Then

there

are

mobile

apps,

which

will

be

capable

of

easing

the

ever-growing

pressure

of

compliance

for

insurers

through

simplified

operations

and

improved

data

protection.

DECISION

POLICYMAKERS

The

gender,

age,

income,

education,

and

so

on

all

influence

insurance

investment

decisions.

But

policymakers

must

first

be

aware

of

insurance

coverage.

The

online

tools

help

educate

the

investor

potential

so

they

can

make

a

smart

decision

about

insurance.

These

platforms

explain

policies

in

detail

to

investors

so

that

they

can

make

decisions

regarding

their

advantages

and

disadvantages.

Digitalization,

although

having

a

number

of

benefits

—

in

time,

perhaps,

but

also

in

expenses

(i.e.,

internet-based

transactions

rather

travel),

is

not

without

its

problems.

Data

security

issues

and

personal

data

theft,

be

thought

about.

Even

so,

opportunities

for

consumers

and

insurers

in

the

digitalization

of

the

insurance

market,

which

brings

growth

and

innovation

to

align

with

market

needs.

FACTORS

AFFECTING

DIGITAL

INSURANCE

ADOPTION

Digital

insurance

will

adopt

—

there

are

several

things

that

influence

whether

people

will

accept

the

digital

change.

Digital

insurance

primarily

adopted

for

convenience,

safety,

investment

opportunities,

and

savings

through

digital

platform.

These

are

the

points

that

are

covered

below.

1.

Awareness
:

The

main

hurdle

in

the

adoption

of

digital

insurance

across

India

is

awareness.

Digital

is

needed

but

most

consumers

and

insurers

don’t

understand

what

digital

technologies

can

do

for

them.

In

comparison

to

other

nations,

the

infamy

for

innovative

new

products

in

the

insurance

domain

is

quite

minimal

in

India.

This

knowledge

gap

is

further

exacerbated

by

poor

financial

literacy

that

can

discourage

people

from

taking

up

digital

insurances.

2.

Feedback

and

Security:

Failing

to

trust

can

make

or

break

digital

insurance

adoption.

The

consumers

must

have

a

trust

that

their

information

and

purchases

are

safe

when

dealing

with

digital

mediums.

–

Customer

is

hesitant

to

go

digital

if

they

do

not

feel

trust

or

security

in

digital

services.

Even

customers

are

difficult

and

clumsy

in

their

use

of

digital

tools,

and

it

can

be

very

dissuading.

Thus,

insurance

companies

should

also

focus

on

easy-to-use

features

and

high

levels

of

security

to

secure

customer

trust.

Also,

constant

updates

and

enhancements

in

digital

services

to

respond

to

changing

customer

demands

and

expectations.

CONCLUSION

Digital

marketing

and

e-business

in

insurance

industry

has

rewritten

the

rules

of

the

game

when

it

comes

to

communication

with

clients.

It’s

created

a

marketplace

where

marketing,

technology

and

customer

service

are

united.

Insurance

companies

are

already

progressing

in

using

digital

channels

for

a

better

customer

experience,

by

providing

their

services

via

mobile

apps,

social

networks

and

websites.

Now

that

the

mobile

phones,

tablets

and

other

handheld

devices

have

become

ubiquitous,

individuals

use

them

to

shop

for

prices,

search

products,

and

buy

insurance

from

anywhere

at

any

time.

But,

users

are

not

just

averse

to

a

frictionless,

quick

process

from

their

insurance

company

as

they

might

have

from

their

favorite

apps,

Google

or

internet

store.

Digital

revolution

has

changed

the

business

paradigm,

and

more

so

as

web

aggregators

are

introduced

into

the

distribution

chain.

The

effects

of

web

aggregators

have

been

ambivalent,

but

they

have

been

useful

for

consumers

shopping

for

insurance

quotes

and

finding

information.

Furthermore,

more

customers

are

now

reaching

out

to

the

insurer

via

mobile

apps,

chatbots,

and

company

websites

to

make

service

calls

or

claim

inquiries.

E-business

and

digitization

changed

the

value-added

service

providers

of

insurance

industry.

These

changes

transformed

how

insurance

policies

are

promoted,

customer

service

is

conducted

and

after-sales

care

is

offered.

Answers

on

time

and

timely

claim

resolution

are

the

new

priorities

of

insurance

companies

in

today’s

market.

As

insurance

industry

becomes

more

digitalized,

insurers

need

to

focus

on

applying

these

tools

in

order

to

gain

customer

loyalty

and

stay

ahead

of

the

curve.
