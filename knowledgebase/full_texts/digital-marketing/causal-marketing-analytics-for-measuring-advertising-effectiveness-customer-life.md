Abstract:
Causal marketing analytics seeks to distinguish incremental commercial effects from
correlations created by targeting, customer self-selection, seasonality, platform optimization
and channel overlap. This research book develops an integrated framework for measuring
advertising effectiveness, customer lifetime value and omnichannel performance through
causal research design, statistical inference, machine learning, scalable data engineering and
accountable decision governance. The analysis connects randomized and quasi-experimental
designs with potential-outcomes reasoning, time-series counterfactuals, heterogeneous
treatment effects, survival and value models, and policy-constrained optimization. It further
treats identity resolution, event instrumentation, feature dependence, model drift and
reproducibility as part of the estimand rather than as downstream engineering details. A
unifiedmeasurementarchitectureisproposedinwhichexposure,intervention,customerstate,
channel sequence and value realization are represented across a governed lifecycle from data
capture to decision deployment. Ten research themes organize the supplied literature around
causal identification, experimentation, customer value, omnichannel attribution, uncertainty,
predictive generalization, streaming inference, trustworthiness, global transferability and
governance. Five chapters then advance foundations, statistical modeling, machine learning,
scalable analytics operations, and sectoral-policy horizons. The resulting monograph is
designedforresearchers, practitionersandpolicymakers whorequire marketingmeasurement
systems that are interpretable, reproducible, operationally scalable and suitable for cross-
marketdecisionmaking.
