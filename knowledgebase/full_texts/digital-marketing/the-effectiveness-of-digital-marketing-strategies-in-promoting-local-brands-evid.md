ABSTRACT
Digital Marketing is now a critical method of creating awareness, obtaining customers, informing about specials and advertising brands within the Restaurant &
Café Industry. This research will explore how Digital Marketing can be utilized to advertise Local Restaurant & Café Brands in Hyderabad, Telangana. A
Quantitative, Descriptive and Cross-Sectional Approach was used by administering a Structured Questionnaire via Google Forms. There were 75 usable
responses in total. The Questionnaire included Demographic Characteristics, Social Media Usage, Restaurant Discovery, Preferred Digital Content, Online
Reviews, Advertisements, Discounts, Influencers, Google Search/Maps, Food Delivery Apps, Behavioral Responses and Overall Perceived Effectiveness. Data
was analyzed using Frequencies, Percentages, Mean Scores for 12 Five-Point Likert Statements, an Exploratory Composite Analysis and a One-Sample T-Test
for Overall Effectiveness. Findings indicate that Instagram was the most commonly used Social Platform (61.3%), while Videos/Reels and Food Photographs
werethemostappealingContentFormats(30.7%each).

1. Introduction of the Study
The use of social media, search platforms, online reviews, food delivery apps, and short-form video has changed how consumers find and assess
restaurants and cafés. Digital channels allow local brands to reach consumers directly while also providing them with the opportunity to showcase
products, share promotions, receive reviews, and create a recognizable brand identity. Restaurants and cafés are especially well-suited for digital
marketing because they can visually communicate food photos, short videos, reviews, location details, and promotional offers quickly. However, the
successofadigitalmarketingstrategywill dependon whatconsumerssee andhowtheirdigitalexperienceaffectsdiscovery, trust,trial,purchase, and
recommendation. This study looks at consumers in Hyderabad, Telangana, and studies their digital media habits and views on restaurant and café
promotions. Rather than assuming that all digital channels have equal impact, this study compares respondents' self-reported experiences using social
media,reviewsandratings,discounts,influencers,videos/reels,GoogleSearch/Maps,deliveryapps,andonlineads.
2. Research Gap
Digital marketing has been found to be positively associated with restaurant visibility, competitiveness, customer behavior, purchaseintentions and/or
engagement; however, there is very little research available from the perspective of consumers regarding digital marketing in Hyderabad. In addition,
previous studies have varied by location, target audience and outcomes, which makes it difficult to use the results of those studies to make inferences
aboutrestaurantandcaféconsumers inHyderabad.There isalsoamajorgapintermsofhowcustomersinteractwithmultipledigitaltouchpoints.For
example,acustomermightseearestaurantonsocialmedia,readreviews,findtherestaurant'slocationonGoogleMaps,lookatphotos,noticeanoffer
and then order through a delivery app. By studying all of these interactions together we can gain a better understanding of the customer journey.

5.2 Sample Design
The final dataset includes 75 valid responses. This study used a non-probability convenience sample because there was no evidence that the Google
Formsresponseswerechosenfromarandomsamplingframe.Therefore,theresultswillbepresentedasdescriptiveevidenceofthesurveyparticipants.
5.3 Data Collection
Primary data was gathered using a structured questionnaire. The questionnaire contained demographic questions; digital usage questions;
platform/content questions; 12 five-point Likert scale items (Questions 11-22); behavioral questions; strategy preference questions;
trial/recommendationintentionquestions,andanoveralleffectivenessratingquestion.
5.4 Questionnaire Preparation
Thequestionnairewas dividedintofivesections:respondentprofile, digital-mediausage, digitaldiscoveryandcontent,digitalmarketing's impact,and
behavioral/overallefficacy.Afive-pointLikertscalewasusedtoassesstheLikertstatements.
5.5 Data Analysis Techniques
For each categorical question, frequencies and percentages were determined. For the 12 Likert-scale questions, means were computed; in addition, a
summary descriptive score was created by averaging the scores from questions 11-22. An exploratory one-sample t-test was conducted on the overall
effectiveness question. Question 7 was a multiple-response question; therefore, the number of times each platform was mentioned was counted
individuallyandpercentagesmayexceed100%.
5.6 Reliability of Likert Items
The 12 Likert items in questions 11-22 yielded an exploratory Cronbach's alpha of 0.822; which indicates that there is a high degree of internal
consistencyforthissample.
However,whilethisreliabilityestimateisindicativeofconsistency,itdoesnotprovideevidenceofeithervalidityorgeneralizability.

especially helpful during the discovery phase; food photos and Reels provide visual content thatmakes a restaurantmore visible. Reviews and ratings
providesocialproofasconsumerscanseewhatotherconsumershaveexperiencedpriortomakingtheirowndecision.Onlineoffersandadsseemtobe
