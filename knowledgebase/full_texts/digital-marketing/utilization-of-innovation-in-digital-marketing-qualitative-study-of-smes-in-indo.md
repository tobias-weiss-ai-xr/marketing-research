1. Introduction

Digital marketing has become the main focus of global business strategy, especially amidst the rapid development of
information technology. According to Danuri (2019), global digital advertising spending is estimated to reach more than 500
billion US dolla rs, reflecting a significant shift from traditional to digital marketing (Soelaiman & Utami, 2021 a). SMEs, the
backbone of the economy in many countries, including Indonesia, are challenged to adapt to these changes. Although many
SMEs in Indonesia are starting to implement digital marketing strategies, only approximately 40% use digital platforms to
market their products (Sobarna, 2021). Many SMEs do not yet understand how to implement effective digital strategies, and
they face challenges in accessing technology and competent human resources (Fachrunnisa et al., 2020).
Facts about specific problems in Indonesia show that although internet use among SMEs continues to increase, many
still rely on traditional marketing. Research by Indra (2023) shows that a lack of knowledge about digital marketing is the main
obstacle for SMEs in adapting to market changes. In addition, many SMEs have not utilized the full potential of social media,
even though social media has proven effective in reaching a wider range of consumers (Nurfarida et al., 2021). Therefore, it is
important to explore how SMEs can utilize innovations in digital marketing to increase their competitiveness.
Several previous studies have shown that adopting digital marketing can improve business performance. Fatimah (2021)
reported that using information technology in marketing can increase SMEs' operational efficiency and market reach (Sugiyanti

Abstract This study investigates the adoption of digital marketing innovations by Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in
Indonesia, focusing on the Jabodetabek area. Utilizing a qualitative case study approach, data were collected through
interviews, observations, and questionnaires with SME owners and managers. Findings indicate that 80% of respondents
perceive social media as a significant factor in increasing product visibility, yet many SMEs struggle to fully optimize digi tal
marketing due to technical knowledge limitations. Questionnaire results reveal that 65% of SMEs report a positive impact
of digital marketing on sales growth, while observations highlight that businesses implementing advanced strategies—such
as paid advertising and influencer collaborations—experience better sales performance than those relying solely on organic
reach. The study underscores key barriers to digital marketing adoption, including a lack of expertise, resource constraints,
and limited strategic planning. Additionally, the research suggests that SMEs require structured training and policy support
to enhance their digital marketing capabilities. By analyzing the challenges and opportunities faced by SMEs, this study
provides valuable insights for practitioners and policymak ers in formulating strategies to improve digital literacy, increase
market competitiveness, and drive sustainable business growth. The findings highlight the urgent need for capacity-building
initiatives, such as government -supported digital literacy progr ams and industry partnerships, to bridge the gap in SMEs'
digital marketing proficiency. Moreover, the study contributes to the broader discourse on digital transformation by
demonstrating how marketing innovation can enhance business resilience in an incr easingly digital economy. Future
research could explore comparative analyses between regions or sectors to provide a more comprehensive understanding
of SMEs' digital marketing adoption. Overall, this research offers a critical perspective on the role of d igital marketing in
fostering SME competitiveness and serves as a foundation for developing more effective support mechanisms tailored to
the needs of small businesses in Indonesia.

2. Materials and methods

2.1. Types of research

This research uses a qualitative approach with a case study research design. A qualitative approach was chosen because
the focus of the research was to understand the phenomenon in depth, namely, the use of innovation in digital marketing by
SMEs in Indonesia. Through this approach, researchers can explore the experiences, views, and practices of SMEs adopting
digital marketing innovations.

2.2. Location and Research Subjects

This research was conducted in several SMEs in the Jabodetabek area, Indonesia. The choice of this location was based
on the high concentration of SMEs in the area and the accessibility of conducting interviews and observations. The research
subjects included SME owners and managers who had implemented digital marketing in their business strategy. Researchers
select approximately 10--15 SMEs as samples, with the criteria that these SMEs have been operating for at least 2 years and
have used digital media in marketing.

2.3. Research instrument

The instruments used in this research were semistructured  interview guidelines and observation sheets. The interview
guide will contain open-ended questions to elicit in-depth information about the digital marketing strategies implemented, the
challenges faced, and the innovations that SMEs use. Observation shee ts record digital marketing practices directly, including
how to use social media, websites, and other digital marketing tools.

2.4. Data collection techniques

Data are collected through three main techniques:
1. Interview: Semistructured interviews with SME owners and managers will be conducted. The aim of these interviews
is to collect qualitative information about their experiences using digital marketing and the innovations implemented.
2. Observation: Researchers will make direct observations of digital marketing practices carried out by SMEs. These
observations will help researchers understand the context and application of the strategies implemented.
3. Documentation study: Researchers will also collect related documents, such as marketing reports, promotional
materials, and relevant sales data, to support the data obtained from interviews and observations.
Using this qualitative research method, we hope that this research can provide an in-depth understanding of how SMEs
in Indonesia utilize innovation in digital marketing and the factors that influence the adoption of these strategies.

4. Discussion

4.1. Interpretation of Interview Data

The interview results show that most SMEs have adopted digital marketing, especially through social media. As many
as 80% of the respondents felt that the use of social media had significantly increased the visibility of their products. The se
findings show that social media plays an important role in SME marketing strategies, but not all SMEs utilize the  potential of
digital platforms optimally.
Some SMEs, such as SME A and SME B, reported high levels of satisfaction with the use of digital marketing, mainly due
to increased sales and ease of communication with customers. Other SMEs, such as SME C, are still in the learning stage to
maximize the use of social media. This shows that although digital marketing is well received, there is a knowledge gap among
SMEs, as identified by Irawan and Umboh (2023), who mentioned a lack of technical knowledge as one of the main challenges.

4.2. Analysis of Questionnaire Results

The questionnaire results reveal that 65% of respondents perceive digital marketing as having a significant impact on
sales growth, while 70% plan to further enhance their use of digital media. These findings reinforce the idea that SMEs are
increasingly acknowledging digital marketing as an essential business strategy. However, while digitalization enables greater
market reach and customer engagement, many SMEs struggle with resource constraints and the abilit y to navigate complex
marketing tools.
Compared to prior research, these results support Fatimah’s (2021) findings, which suggest that digital adoption can
improve SMEs’ operational efficiency. However, unlike prior studies that focused on quantitative analysis, this study highlights
SMEs’ qual itative experiences and challenges in adopting digital innovations. While Adam (2022) demonstrated that social

Moreover, the results show a clear distinction between SMEs that have fully embraced digital marketing innovations

A study by Sugiyanti et al. (2022) found that SMEs that invest in social media advertising achieve better customer

sales improvements. However, SMEs that struggle with digital marketing implementation cite lack of expertise and financial
constraints as major obstacles, a finding consistent with Indra et al. (2023), who emphasized that digital literacy remains a
critical challenge for SMEs.
Additionally, the questionnaire results highlight that SMEs view digital marketing as a long-term investment, with many
planning to increase their reliance on digital strategies. This supports Vernia’s (2017) argument that continuous training an d
support programs are essential for SMEs to sustain digital marketing success.
While prior studies primarily focused on the benefits of digital marketing adoption, this study adds a new dimension by
examining SMEs’ self -reported barriers and motivations. The findings suggest that despite the benefits of digital marketing,
challenges such as limited financial resources and lack of technical expertise persist. Future studies should explore how
targeted interventions, such as government-sponsored digital training programs, can address these challenges to enable more
SMEs to harness the full potential of digital marketing.

4.3. Evaluation of the observation results

Observations reveal that SMEs employ diverse digital marketing strategies, ranging from simple organic social media
content to advanced tactics like SEO, paid advertisements, and influencer partnerships. SMEs that invest in paid advertising
and influencer collaborations report better sales performance than those relying solely on organic content. This is consistent
with Ariescy et al. (2021), who found that innovative digital marketing strategies enhance customer retention and brand
visibility.
In comparison with prior research, this study reinforces the findings of Vernia (2017), who argued that digital literacy
significantly impacts the effectiveness of digital marketing. SMEs that receive proper training and develop structured digita l
marketing strategies tend to outperform those that adopt a trial-and-error approach. Similarly, our study highlights that SMEs
leveraging Google Ads, SEO, and email marketing demonstrate a higher rate of engagement and conversion, supporting Umboh
and Aryanto’s (2023) conclusions on the effectiveness of digital tools in enhancing SME performance.
Contrastingly, research by Rokhmah and Yahya (2020) suggested that many SMEs struggle with adapting to digital
transformation due to limited financial resources. While our findings acknowledge this challenge, they also indicate that SMEs
that strategically allocate resources to digital marketing reap significant benefits. This contrasts with earlier work that focused
more on barriers rather than on the practical strategies SMEs implement to overcome those barriers.
Moreover, research by Sugiyanti et al. (2022) emphasized that SMEs engaging in paid digital advertising see an increase
in brand awareness and customer engagement. This study corroborates those findings but adds that the effectiveness of digital
marketing strategies also depends on SMEs’ ability to analyze market trends and optimize their campaigns accordingly. SMEs
that continuously refine their strategies based on analytics and customer feedback tend to achieve better results than those
who adopt static digital marketing approaches.
Another point of contrast lies in the role of influencer marketing. While Indra et al. (2023) found that influencer
collaborations provide limited long -term benefits due to fluctuating engagement levels, this study suggests that influencer
partnerships, when combined with other strategies like paid advertising and content marketing, can yield sustained results.
SMEs that diversify their digital marketing efforts rather than relying on a single method tend to see more consistent growth .
These findings underscore the importance of structured digital marketing training programs and financial support
initiatives for SMEs. Given that resource allocation remains a major challenge, policymakers should consider offering grants
and subsidies to help SMEs invest in professional digital marketing services. Future research should explore the impact of long-
term digital marketing training on SME growth to determine best practices for sustainable digita l transformation.

4.4. Comparison with Previous Research

The results of this research are in line with Fatimah's (2021) findings, which state that the use of information technology
can increase the operational efficiency of SMEs. However, this research highlights that despite increasing technology adoption,
there are still obstacles to understanding and implementing effective strategies. This is also supported by research by Adam
(2022), which revealed that although SMEs using social media exper ienced increased sales, challenges in managing content
and marketing strategies remained.

4.5. Practical implications

The practical implications of this research show that SMEs need to receive training and guidance in using digital
marketing tools effectively. The government and related institutions are advised to hold workshops and seminars that focus
on developing SMEs' digital capacity. With the right knowledge, SMEs can be better at implementing digital marketing
innovations and increasing their competitiveness in the market.

4.6. Research limitations

Limitations in this study include the relatively small number of respondents, which may not represent Indonesia's entire
population of SMEs. In addition, this research focuses only on SMEs in the Jabodetabek area, so the results may not be
generalizable to other areas. Further research should involve more respondents from various regions and industrial sectors to
obtain a more comprehensive picture of SMEs' use of innovation in digital marketing.

5. Conclusions

By utilizing innovation in digital marketing, SMEs in Indonesia have great potential to increase their competitiveness.
This research shows that despite challenges in adoption, awareness of the importance of digital marketing is increasing among
SMEs. Further support is needed to help SMEs take advantage of existing opportunities and overcome their obstacles.
