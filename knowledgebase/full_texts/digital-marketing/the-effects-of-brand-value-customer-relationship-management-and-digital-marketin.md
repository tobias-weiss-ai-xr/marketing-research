ABSTRACT

Increasing competition in the hospitality
industry requires hotels to strengthen
marketing strategies to maintain sales
performance. This study examines the
individual and simultaneous effects of
brand value, customer relationship
management (CRM), and digital marketing
on sales performance at Alltrue Hotel
Palangka Raya, Indonesia. A quantitative
explanatory approach was employed using
questionnaire data from 81 respondents
selected through purposive sampling.
Multiple linear regression was used to test
the hypotheses. The results indicate that
brand value has a positive but statistically
insignificant relationship with sales
performance (B = 0.812, p = 0.085). In
contrast, CRM (B = 0.356, p = 0.011) and
digital marketing (B = 0.521, p  < 0.001)
demonstrate significant positive
relationships. The three variables are jointly
significant (F = 153.646, p < 0.001),
explaining 85.69% of the variation in
perceived sales performance. These
findings highlight the importance of
strengthening customer relationships and
optimizing digital marketing to support hotel
performance. Although brand value is not
individually significant, maintaining a
positive brand image remains relevant to
the hotel's marketing strategy. The findings
should be interpreted considering the
limited sample and perception -based
measurements.

Marketing; Hotel; Sales

INTRODUCTION

The hospitality industry operates in a highly competitive environment, requiring hotels to
integrate service delivery with effective marketing strategies and customer engagement.
Marketing performance in the hospitality sector is closely associated with ho w
businesses communicate value, manage customer relationships, and utilize digital
platforms to reach potential customers (Kee et al., 2024; Palazzo et al., 2021). Therefore,
hotel sales performance should be understood not only as the outcome of transactions
but also in relation to consumer perceptions, customer relationships, and information -
search behavior.

Sales performance is an important indicator of hotel business performance, reflecting the
extent to which consumer interest translates into actual purchases  (Wibisono et al.,
2025). Despite receiving favorable customer evaluations, hotels may experience
difficulties converting consumer awareness and positive perceptions into sales,
particularly in increasingly competitive markets. Understanding the marketing factors
associated with sales performance is therefore essential for developing appropriate
strategies and maintaining business competitiveness.

Brand value represents the value associated with a brand, as reflected in consumers'
perceptions, trust, brand image, loyalty, and perceived quality  (Sinollah & Masruro ,
2019). In the hospitality industry, brand -related perceptions are particularly relevant
because hotel services are intangible, and consumers often rely on brand reputation and
previous experiences when evaluating accommodation alternatives. Previous research
has highlighted the relevance of hotel brand equity to consumer responses and
purchase-related intentions (Huang et al., 2022).

Customer relationship management (CRM) refers to systematic efforts to establish,
maintain, and strengthen customer relationships through effective communication,
service responsiveness, complaint handling, and continuous engagement. In the
hospitality ind ustry, CRM plays an important role in maintaining positive customer
experiences and encouraging long -term relationships. Previous studies have identified
associations between CRM, customer satisfaction, and loyalty, providing a theoretical
foundation for e xamining its relationship with hotel sales performance ( Bintarto et al.,
2021; Palazzo et al., 2021).

Digital marketing has become an important component of hotel marketing strategies as
consumers increasingly rely on online platforms to search for information, compare
alternatives, and evaluate accommodation services. Social media, hotel websites, online
advertising, and other digital channels enable hotels to communicate with potential
customers and promote their services. Previous studies have emphasized the relevance
of digital marketing to hotel booking decisions, consumer engagement, and purchasing
behavior (Faradila, 2023; Lok et al., 2024; Rahayuningrat et al., 2024). Digital marketing
has also been associated with consumer behavior and customer loyalty in broader
business contexts (Mukhtar et al., 2023).

Alltrue Hotel Palangka Raya provides a relevant setting for examining these marketing
factors. The hotel has reportedly received customer review scores exceeding 8.9 across
several digital platforms, indicating generally positive customer evaluations. Howe ver,
despite these favorable assessments, its annual revenue reportedly declined from
approximately Rp1.2–1.3 billion to Rp900 million following the establishment of a modern
shopping mall in the surrounding area. This decline was particularly relevant to the Food

LITERATURE REVIEW

Theoretical Foundation
This study adopts a marketing perspective that considers sales performance in relation
to consumer perceptions, customer relationships, and responses to marketing
communication. Brand value reflects consumers' evaluations of a hotel brand, customer
relationship management (CRM) represents the processes used to establish and
maintain customer relationships, and digital marketing encompasses communication
activities conducted through digital platforms. In the hospitality industry, these
dimensions are relevan t throughout the consumer decision -making process, from
information searching and evaluating alternatives to purchasing decisions and repeat
patronage (Huang et al., 2022; Palazzo et al., 2021).

Accordingly, brand value, CRM, and digital marketing are examined as distinct but
complementary predictors of sales performance. Their individual and simultaneous
relationships with sales performance provide the theoretical basis for the present study.

Brand Value
Brand value refers to the value associated with a brand based on consumers'
perceptions and evaluations. In this study, brand value is examined through four
dimensions: brand image, brand trust, brand loyalty, and perceived quality. These
dimensions reflect consumers' perceptions of the hotel brand, confidence in its reliability,
willingness to maintain relationships with the brand, and evaluations of its overall quality.

Brand value is particularly relevant in the hospitality industry because consumers
frequently consider reputation, previous experiences, and perceived service quality
when selecting accommodation and related services. Favorable brand perceptions may
strengthen consumer confidence and influence purchase-related decisions. Angela and
Kesumahati (2023) identified relationships between brand equity, customer satisfaction,
and purchase intention, while Imbayani et al. (2025)  examined the influence of brand
image and digital marketing on purchasing decisions.

Although previous studies suggest that brand -related factors are associated with
consumer purchasing behavior, their effects may differ across business settings and
consumer groups (Rahmanwali, 2024). Therefore, the relationship between brand value
and sales performance requires further examination within the context of Alltrue Hotel
Palangka Raya.

Customer Relationship Management
Customer relationship management (CRM) is a strategic approach to establishing,
maintaining, and strengthening long -term relationships with customers. CRM
emphasizes effective communication, service responsiveness, complaint handling, and
continuous custom er engagement. In the hospitality industry, these activities are
particularly important because direct interactions between customers and service
providers contribute to the overall service experience.

Quality of Service
Service quality refers to the extent to which service delivery meets customer
expectations (Kesumahati et al., 2025) . In the hospitality industry, service quality is
commonly associated with reliability, responsiveness, assurance, empathy, and tangible
aspects of service delivery. Previous studies have identified relationships between
service quality, customer satisfacti on, and loyalty in hospitality settings ( Anabila et al.,
2022; Han et al., 2021).

Service quality is relevant to the business context of Alltrue Hotel Palangka Raya
because the hotel has reportedly received favorable customer evaluations despite
experiencing a decline in revenue  (Fairuz et al., 2021 ). This situation suggests the
importance of distinguishing positive customer perceptions from actual sales
performance. However, service quality is considered a contextual factor in the present
study and is not included as an independent variable in the re gression model.
Consequently, its direct effect on sales performance is not statistically examined  (Jaya
Saputra & Djumarno, 2021).

Product Development
Product development refers to the creation of new products or the improvement of
existing products and services to respond to changing market needs. In the hospitality
industry, product development may involve improving facilities, introducing new service
packages, expanding complementary services, or creating differentiated guest
experiences. Cooper (1986) highlighted the relevance of product innovation strategies
to business performance.

Within the context of Alltrue Hotel Palangka Raya, product development may represent
a managerial consideration for responding to competition and changing customer
preferences. Nevertheless, product development is not included as an independent
variable in the present regression model. Therefore, its direct relationship with sales
performance cannot be determined from the findings of this study.

Digital Marketing
Digital marketing refers to marketing activities conducted through digital technologies
and internet-based communication channels. In this study, digital marketing is measured
through four indicators: social media, website marketing, online advertising, and ease of
access to information. These indicators represent the hotel's digital communication
activities and consumers' accessibility to information about its services.

Previous Studies and Hypotheses Development
Previous research provides an empirical foundation for examining the relationships
between brand value, CRM, digital marketing, and sales performance. Studies on brand-
related factors have identified associations with consumer perceptions and purchasing
decisions (Angela & Kesumahati, 2023; Imbayani et al., 2025 ). Research on CRM has
emphasized its relevance to customer satisfaction, loyalty, and marketing performance
(Bintarto et al., 2021 ; Hikmawati et al., 2017 ). Meanwhile, studies on digital marketing
have highlighted its relationship with consumer engagement, booking decisions, and
purchase-related behavior (Faradila, 2023; Lok et al., 2024).

Although these studies demonstrate the relevance o f marketing -related factors to
consumer behavior, their relationships with sales performance may differ depending on
market conditions, consumer characteristics, and business contexts. Therefore, the
present study examines the individual and simultaneous e ffects of brand value, CRM,
and digital marketing on sales performance at Alltrue Hotel Palangka Raya.

The Effect of Brand Value on Sales Performance
Brand value reflects consumers' perceptions of a brand and may influence their
confidence and purchasing decisions. Favorable brand image, trust, loyalty, and
perceived quality may encourage consumers to consider a hotel when selecting
accommodation or related services. Previous studies have demonstrated relationships
between brand -related perceptions and purchase -related outcomes ( Angela &
Kesumahati, 2023 ; Imbayani et al., 2025 ). Based on this theoretical and empirical
foundation, the following hypothesis is proposed:

H1: Brand value has a significant effect on sales performance at Alltrue Hotel Palangka
Raya.

engagement. These activities are relevant to customer satisfaction, loyalty, and repeat
patronage in the hos pitality industry. Previous research has identified relationships
between CRM and customer -related outcomes ( Bintarto et al., 2021 ; Palazzo et al.,
2021). Accordingly, the second hypothesis is formulated as follows:

H2: Customer relationship management has a significant effect on sales performance at

The Effect of Digital Marketing on Sales Performance
Digital marketing enables hotels to communicate with potential customers, improve

H3: Digital marketing has a significant effect on sales performance at Alltrue Hotel

The Simultaneous Effects of Brand Value, Customer Relationship Management,
and Digital Marketing on Sales Performance
Brand value, CRM, and digital marketing represent different but complementary aspects
of hotel marketing strategies  (Masruroh et al., 2023 ). Brand value reflects consumers'

channels.

These variables may contribute to different stages of the consumer decision -making
process. Brand value may influence initial brand evaluations, digital marketing may
facilitate information searching and consideration, and CRM may support customer
interactions and long -term relationships. Examining these variables simultaneously
provides an understanding of their collective relationship with hotel sales performance.
Based on the theoretical foundation and previous empirical studies, the fourth hypothesis
is formulated as follows:

H4: Brand value, customer relationship management, and digital marketing
simultaneously have a significant effect on sales performance at Alltrue Hotel Palangka
Raya.

RESEARCH METHOD

Research Design
This study employed a quantitative approach with an explanatory research design to
examine the individual and simultaneous effects of brand value, customer relationship
management (CRM), and digital marketing on sales performance at Alltrue Hotel
Palangka Raya, Indonesia. A cross-sectional survey design was applied to collect data
on respondents' perceptions of the research variables during the study period. The
quantitative approach was considered appropriate because the study aimed to evaluate
the statistical relationships between three independent variables and one dependent
variable through multiple linear regression analysis.

Population and Sampling Technique
The population of this study consisted of approximately 500 customers of Alltrue Hotel
Palangka Raya. The initial sample size was estimated using Slovin's formula with a
nominal margin of error of 5%, resulting in a target of approximately 222 respondents.
However, the final dataset consisted of 81 valid questionnaire responses, which were
included in the statistical analysis.

The study employed purposive sampling, a non-probability sampling technique in which
respondents were selected based on their relevance to the research objectives. The
selection focused on individuals with experience using the hotel's services, including
accommodation and other services such as food and beverage offerings. This approach
was intended to obtain responses from individuals who could provide relevant
evaluations of the hotel's marketing activities and service-related experiences.

Although the initial sample size was calculated using Slovin's formula, the final sample
was smaller than the estimated target. Furthermore, because respondents were selected
through purposive sampling rather than probability sampling, the nominal 5% margin of
error cannot be interpreted as the achieved sampling precision. Accordingly, the findings
should be interpreted with consideration of the sample size and sampling technique.

Data Collection Procedures
Primary data were collected using a structured questionnaire administered to
respondents associated with Alltrue Hotel Palangka Raya. The data collection process
was conducted over approximately three months and concluded in April 2026. The
questionnaire was designed to obtain information regarding respondents' perceptions of
brand value, CRM, digital marketing, and sales performance.

The questionnaire consisted of 28 measurement items, with seven items representing
each research variable. Responses were measured using a five -point Likert scale,
ranging from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). This measurement approach
enabled respondents to indicate their level of agreement with statements related to the
research variables.

This study examined three independent variables and one dependent variable. The
independent variables were brand value (X1), CRM (X2), and digital marketing (X3),
while sales performance (Y) served as the dependent variable.

relationship with sales performance (B = 0.356, t = 2.582, p = 0.011). This finding
indicates that higher CRM scores are associated with higher sales performance scores
when the other independent variables are held constant. Accordingly, H2 is supported.
Similarly, digital marketing demonstrates a positive and statistically significant
relationship with sales performance (B = 0.521, t = 4.927, p < 0.001). This result suggests
that higher digital marketing scores are associated with higher sales performance scores,
controlling for the other predictors. Therefore, H3 is supported.

Overall, the partial regression results indicate that CRM and digital marketing are
significant positive predictors of sales performance, whereas brand value does not
demonstrate a statistically significant individual relationship. Although brand value has a
positive regression coefficient, the available evidence is insufficient to conclude that it
has a statistically significant effect on sales performance at Alltrue Hotel Palangka Raya.

DISCUSSION

The Effect of Brand Value on Sales Performance
The results indicate that brand value has a positive but statistically insignificant
relationship with sales performance at Alltrue Hotel Palangka Raya (B = 0.812, t = 1.739,
p = 0.085). Since the significance value exceeds 0.05, H1 is not supported. This finding
suggests that although respondents may hold favorable perceptions of the hotel's brand,
these perceptions are not significantly associated with sales performance after
controlling for CRM and digital marketing.

Brand value encompasses brand image, brand trust, brand loyalty, and perceived quality
(Amelia Rizky et al., 2025). These dimensions may contribute to how customers evaluate
a hotel, but favorable brand perceptions do not necessarily translate into purchasing
behavior. In the context of Alltrue Hotel Palangka Raya, the reported positive customer
evaluations alongside declining revenue illustrate the possibility that brand -related
perceptions alone may be insufficient to sustain sales performance in a competitive
environment.

Previous studies have identified relationships between brand -related factors and
consumer purchasing decisions ( Angela & Kesumahati, 2023 ; Imbayani et al., 2025 ).
However, the present finding indicates that the relationship between brand value and
sales performance is not statistically significant in this particular regression model.
Differences in research settings, consumer characteristics, and measured outcome s
may contribute to variations across studies.

Therefore, while maintaining a positive brand image remains relevant to hotel marketing,
the findings do not provide sufficient statistical evidence that brand value independently
predicts sales performance at Alltrue Hotel Palangka Raya.

The Effect of Customer Relationship Management on Sales Performance
The results demonstrate that customer relationship management (CRM) has a positive
and statistically significant relationship with sales performance (B = 0.356, t = 2.582, p =
0.011). Since the significance value is below 0.05, H2 is supported. This finding indicates
that higher CRM scores are associated with higher sales performance scores when
brand value and digital marketing are held constant.

The Effect of Digital Marketing on Sales Performance
The regression results indicate that digital marketing has a positive and statistically
significant relationship with sales performance (B = 0.521, t = 4.927, p < 0.001).
Therefore, H3 is supported. This finding suggests that higher digital marketing scores
are associated with higher sales performance scores after controlling for brand value and
CRM.

Digital marketing enables hotels to communicate with potential customers through social
media, websites, online advertising, and other digital channels. These platforms provide
opportunities to promote services, communicate offers, and facilitate access to
information. In the hospitality industry, accessible and relevant digital information may
support customers during the evaluation and purchasing process  (Misidawati et al.,
2023).

This finding is consistent with previous research highlighting the relevance of digital
marketing to hotel booking decisions and consumer purchasing behavior (Faradila, 2023;
Imbayani et al., 2025; Rahayuningrat et al., 2024). The results reinforce the importance
of digital communication in supporting marketing-related outcomes within the hospitality
sector.

In the context of Alltrue Hotel Palangka Raya, the significant relationship between digital
marketing and sales performance suggests that the hotel may benefit from improving the
accessibility, consistency, and relevance of its digital promotional activiti es.
Strengthening its online presence may help the hotel communicate its offerings and
reach potential customers in an increasingly competitive market.

Although digital marketing has the highest reported t-value among the three predictors,
this result alone does not establish that it is the most influential variable. Such a
comparison would require appropriate standardized effect estimates.

Theoretical and Practical Implications
The findings provide empirical evidence regarding the relationships between brand
value, CRM, digital marketing, and perceived sales performance in a local hospitality
business setting. Theoretically, the results indicate that marketing -related variables do
not necessarily demonstrate equivalent statistical relationships with sales performance.
While CRM and digital marketing are significant positive predictors, brand value is not
statistically significant after accounting for the other variables in the model.

Practically, the findings suggest that hotel management should strengthen c ustomer
communication, improve complaint -handling processes, and maintain long -term
customer relationships. Management may also consider optimizing social media
activities, website information, and online advertising to improve customer access to
hotel offerings.

Nevertheless, the findings should be interpreted within the study's methodological
limitations. The analysis was based on 81 respondents selected through purposive
sampling, and sales performance was measured through questionnaire responses rather
than ind ependently verified financial records. Consequently, the results demonstrate
statistical associations within the sample and should not be interpreted as definitive
evidence of causal effects or actual revenue changes.

CONCLUSION

This study examined the effects of brand value, customer relationship management
(CRM), and digital marketing on sales performance at Alltrue Hotel Palangka Raya. The
findings indicate that brand value has a positive but statistically insignificant relationship
with sales performance, whereas CRM and digital marketing demonstrate significant
positive relationships. Collectively, these variables have a significant relationship with
sales performance, highlighting the importance of customer relationship pract ices and
digital marketing activities in supporting hotel business performance.

LIMITATION
This study has several limitations. First, the use of purposive sampling and a relatively
small sample from a single hotel limits the generalizability of the findings to other
hospitality settings. Second, the cross -sectional research design restricts the ability to
establish causal relationships among the variables. Third, sales performance was
measured through respondents' perceptions rather than actual financial records.
Additionally, the study focused exclusively on brand value, customer relationship
management, and digital marketing, without examining other potentially relevant factors,
such as service quality, product development, pricing strategies, and market competition.
Future research is encouraged to involve larger and more diverse samples, inclu de
multiple hotels, incorporate objective sales indicators, and examine additional variables
to provide a more comprehensive understanding of hotel sales performance.
