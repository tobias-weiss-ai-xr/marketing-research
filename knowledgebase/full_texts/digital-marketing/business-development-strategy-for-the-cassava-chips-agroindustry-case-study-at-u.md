ABSTRACT
This study aims to analyze the development strategy of the cassava chips agroindustry at UD Komodo in
Sikumana, Kota Kupang. The research method used in this study is SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses,
Opportunities, Threats) to identify internal and external factors that influence the development of the cassava
chips agroindustry at UD Komodo. Data were collected through in -depth interviews with the business owner and
employees directly at the research site, as well as through literature studies from relevant sources. The results
show that UD Komodo has strengths in good product quality, affordable prices, a strategic location, capable
management, and adequate labor. However, it has weaknesses in marketing and distribution channels. The
opportunities include a large potential local market and consumer interest in snack products, while the main
challenges are competition from similar businesses and increasing raw material prices. Based on this analysis,
several business development strategies were formulated, including product diversification by offering various
flavors and types of cassava chips to meet consumer preferences; expansion of distribution channels by utilizing
various distribution channels such as cooperation with shops and minimarkets as well as online sales through
marketplaces; and the development of digital marketing by utilizing various social media channels to promote
products and interact with consumers. These strategies are expected to enhance the competitiveness of UD
Komodo, expand market share, and increase business income.
Keywords: Cassava chips, development strategy, UD Komodo

INTRODUCTION
 Indonesia is one of the countries with considerable natural resource potential that can be further
developed to support national economic growth. Among these resources, the agricultural sector plays an
important role because it provides food, raw materials, employment opportunities, and income for a large
proportion of the population. The agricultural sector consists of several subsectors, including food crops,
horticulture, livestock, and plantations, which contribute to the development of rural economies and agribusiness
activities. The availability of abundant agricultural commodities also creates opportunities for the development of
value-added processing industries, particularly agroindustries based on local agricultural products.
 Agriculture is one of the sectors that can serve as a foundation for developing promising agribusiness
enterprises because agricultural products can be processed into various food products with higher economic value.
The development of agricultural processing activities is particularly important because agricultural products are
generally characterized by perishability, seasonality, and relatively low added value when marketed in raw form.
Processing agricultural commodities into finished or semi -finished products can therefore improve product
durability, increase market value, expand marketing opportunities, and generate additional income for business
actors. In this context, the industrial sector also plays a strategic role in the Indonesian economy because it
contributes significantly to production, employment, and national income. Since 1991, the industrial sector has
surpassed the agricultural sector in its contribution to the formation of Indonesia's gross domestic product (GDP),
indicating the increasing importance of processing activities in economic development.
 Agroindustry refers to economic activities that use agricultural products as raw materials and transform
them into products with greater utility and economic value. Agroindustry is also an integral part of the agricultural
industrial system, beginning with primary agricultural production, followed by processing or transformation
activities, distribution, and finally consumption. The development of agroindustry is expected to strengthen the
linkage between the agricultural and industrial sectors, increase farmers' income, create employment, and
encourage the growth of local economies. Small -scale agroindustries are particularly important in rural and
regional development because they often utilize locally available raw materials and involve local labor.
 However, the development of small -scale agroindustries is often constrained by various internal and
external factors. Internal constraints may include limited capital, inadequate production facilities, low levels of
technology utilization, limited managerial capacity, weak human resource management, and inefficient marketing
practices. External factors may include changes in consumer preferences, increasing competition, fluctuations in
raw material availability and prices, technological development, and changing market conditions. Therefore, an
appropriate business development strategy is required to enable agroindustrial enterprises to respond to these
challenges and improve their competitiveness.
 One important aspect in developing contemporary agroindustrial businesses is the utilization of digital
technology and social media for marketing activities. Social media has increasingly become an important
marketing instrument because it enables businesses to communicate directly with consumers, promote products at
relatively low cost, strengthen brand awareness, and reach markets beyond their immediate geographical areas.
For small and medium enterprises, the use of social media may provide an opportunity to overcome limitations in
conventional marketing channels. Purwidiantoro et al. (2016) emphasized that social media plays an important
role in business activities and can become a strategic component in business development.
 One agricultural commodity with considerable potential for agroindustrial development is cassava.
Cassava is a versatile food crop that can be processed into various products, including flour, fermented foods,
snacks, and chips. The processing of cassava into value -added products can increase its economic value and
provide additional business opportunities for local agroindustries. Cassava chips, in particular, are relatively easy
to produce and have broad consumer acceptance because they can be offered in various flavors and packaging
formats. Nevertheless, the sustainability of cassava -processing enterprises depends not only on production

 UD Komodo is one of the local agroindustrial enterprises that processes cassava into cassava chips. The
business produces three flavor variants, namely original, savory salty, and balado. Based on information obtained
from the owner of UD Komodo, the savory salty variant was the most preferred flavor among consumers in 2024.
This indicates that the enterprise has market potential and opportunities for further product development.
However, the development of UD Komodo has not yet reached its optimal level. Several problems remain,
including limitations in raw material procurement, inadequate production buildings, labor management, limited
use of cassava slicing machines, insufficient packaging technology, and business management that has not
functioned effectively.
 In addition to production -related constraints, marketing is another important challenge faced by UD
Komodo. The business has not yet fully utilized digital marketing channels to expand its market reach. In the
current competitive environment, relying only on conventional marketing may limit the ability of a small
agroindustry to attract new consumers and strengthen its market position. Online marketing has become
increasingly important because it allows businesses to reach broader consumer segments, increase product
visibility, support sales growth, and develop brand identity more efficiently (Anugrah, 2020). Therefore,
improving digital marketing activities may become one of the strategic opportunities for strengthening the
competitiveness of UD Komodo.
 Previous studies have discussed agroindustrial development strategies, small business management, and
the role of digital marketing in supporting business performance. However, business development strategies are
highly dependent on the specific internal conditions and external environment faced by each enterprise.
Differences in production capacity, resources, market access, technology adoption, and managerial capabilities
may result in different strategic priorities. Therefore, a strategy that is appropriate for one agroindustrial enterprise
may not necessarily be applicable to another. This creates a need for a specific analysis of the internal strengths
and weaknesses as well as the external opportunities and threats faced by UD Komodo.
 A comprehensive assessment of these factors is important for determining appropriate and realistic
development strategies. Identifying internal and external factors can provide information regarding the current
position of the enterprise and the factors that may support or inhibit its future development. Such analysis may
also assist business owners in determining priority actions related to production improvement, marketing
expansion, technology adoption, business management, and market development. Accordingly, the findings of
this study are expected to provide practical recommendations for improving the sustainability and competitiveness
of the cassava chips business at UD Komodo.
 Based on the above considerations, this study aims to identify the internal and external factors affecting
the cassava chips business of UD Komodo and to formulate appropriate strategies for its business development.
The results of this study are expected to contribute to the development of small -scale cassava agroindustries and
provide practical insights for local food -processing enterprises facing similar production, management, and
marketing challenges.

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
UD Komodo is an industry that does not only produce cassava chips. The UD Komodo Home Industry began
operating in Kota Kupang in 2015, owned by Mrs. Hasana Tardji as the owner of the UD Komodo Home
Industry. The cassava chips products produced by UD Komodo are very affordable and consist of three flavor
variants: balado, savory salty, and original. The labor structure at UD Komodo consists of the business owner
(Owner) and workers who carry out their respective duties to ensure production efficiency.
The production process and marketing strategy were analyzed in order to provide more comprehensive
recommendations regarding the strategies to be used in developing the cassava chips business of UD Komodo.
With the method used in this study, it is expected to provide a sufficiently clear picture of the challenges/threats
and opportunities in the development of the UD Komodo cassava chips business in Kota Kupang.
Rating of Internal Factors in the IFAS Matrix Analysis (Internal Factor Analysis Summary) and External
Factors in the EFAS Matrix Analysis (External Factor Analysis Summary)

fundamental point that is very important in the process of making a clear strategic decision. Thus, this statement
indicates that the process of assigning weights and ratings is not merely subjective, but is also supported by data
and actual conditions within the company. This will greatly assist efforts to improve capability and ensure that the
strategies formulated are appropriate to the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats faced by a business
at present and in the future. The IFAS matrix is used to analyze internal factors through strengths and weaknesses,
while the EFAS matrix is used to analyze external factors through opportunities and threats (Kurniawan &

Alternative Strategies
SO Strategy
This strategy is implemented by utilizing existing strengths to seize opportunities.
Maintaining the quality of cassava chips by obtaining quality raw materials in order to increase consumer
purchasing power toward cassava chips.
Maintaining the selling price of cassava chips at a low price so as to increase the market and help improve the
family economy, as well as establishing cooperation with partners.
Utilizing this strategic location to expand market reach and improve managerial skills in order to produce higher -
quality cassava chips.
ST Strategy
The strategy carried out is to utilize all strengths to avoid threats that may affect the development of the
cassava chips business.
Maintaining quality raw materials even when raw material prices are high; UD Komodo must make every effort
to face this challenge.

CLOSING
Conclusion
1. Internal and external factors have a significant influence on the development of the cassava chips business at
UD Komodo. The internal factors consist of strengths, namely quality products, strategic location, managerial
skills, low prices, and adequate labor, and weaknesses, namely never having participated in exhibitions, business
management that has not been well organized such as the absence of business evaluation, the absence of a clear
organizational structure, promotional strategies that are still simple, and limited equipment. Meanwhile, the
external factors consist of opportunities, namely market demand, technological development, improving the
family economy, cooperation with partners, and government support, as well as threats faced, namely the large
number of competitors and expensive raw materials.
2. Alternative strategies that can be implemented by UD Komodo in developing its cassava chips business include
maintaining quality, maintaining selling prices and the existing location, and further improving managerial skills
so that quality cassava chips products can continue to be produced in order to increase market demand and the
family economy.
Suggestions
The suggestions that can be proposed by the author are as follows:
1. The owner of the cassava chips business at UD Komodo needs to implement strategies to improve promotion
and should also try to participate in available exhibitions in order to promote the processed products to the wider
community.
2. The government should pay more attention to and provide support for home -based entrepreneurs to grow and
develop by providing training, mentoring, access to financing, business permits, and halal labels for existing
products.
3. For researchers, further research is needed on the development of the business being studied, such as digital
marketing strategies or product innovation, and the importance of an in -depth understanding of the research
object, including the type of business, target market, and business environment.
