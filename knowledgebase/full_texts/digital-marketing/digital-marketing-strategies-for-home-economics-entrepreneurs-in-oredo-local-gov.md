Abstract

The study assessed digital marketing strategies among Home Economics Entrepreneurs in Oredo  Local Government Area,
Edo State. Digital marketing has become an important tool for business visibility and customer engagement. The study
adopted the descriptive cross -sectional survey design. Three research questions guided the study. The population fo r the
study was 386 registered Home Economics Entrepreneurs in Oredo. A proportionate stratified random sampling technique
was used to determine the sample size of 216 entrepreneurs for the study. The instrument for data collection was a
structured questio nnaire validated by experts, while the reliability coefficient of 0.78 was obtained using the Cronbach
Alpha reliability technique. Data was analyzed using descriptive statistics. The results showed that WhatsApp marketing,
Facebook marketing and Instagram  marketing were the most widely adopted digital marketing strategies, while search
engine optimization, paid online advertising and influencer marketing were the least adopted. The study also showed that
overall utilization of digital marketing strategies was high, while implementation was hindered by frequent power outages,
poor internet connectivity and high internet data costs. The study concludes that digital marketing adoption among Home
Economics entrepreneurs is skewed toward low -cost social media pl atforms, constrained by infrastructural and financial
barriers. It is recommended that government and stakeholders improve power supply, subsidize internet costs, and provide
targeted digital marketing training to enhance utilization of more advanced strat egies.

Keywords: Digital Marketing Strategy, Adoption, Utilization, Implementation Challenge

Introduction

The rapid advancement of information and
communication technologies has fundamentally
transformed the way businesses operate across the
world. Traditional marketing approaches that relied
heavily on face -to-face interactions, print media, radio
and televis ion have increasingly given way to digital
marketing strategies that leverage internet -based
platforms to reach broader audiences, engage
customers and enhance business performance. Digital
marketing encompasses the use of digital channels such
as social m edia platforms, websites, search engines,
email marketing, e -commerce platforms, mobile
applications and online advertising to promote
products and services while building long -term
customer relationships (Peter, & Dalla, 2020). The
increasing accessibilit y of smartphones, affordable
internet services and social networking sites has made
digital marketing an indispensable business strategy for
entrepreneurs, particularly owners of micro, small, and
home-based enterprises (Jadhav, Gaikwad, & Bapat,
2023).

In developing countries such as Nigeria, digital
marketing has gradually become an important driver of
entrepreneurial development. Nigeria has experienced
remarkable growth in internet penetration, mobile
phone ownership, and social media usage over the past
decade, creating enormous opportunities for
entrepreneurs to market their products and services
online. Studies indicate that Nigerian SMEs increasingly
utilize information and communication technology
(ICT), social media platforms, e-commerce applications,
and digital payment systems to improve operational
efficiency and business performance (Agamalafiya,
Inobemhe, & Santas,  2025). Gender also appears to
influence patterns of engagement with digital
marketing content. Aso gwa, Okeke, Gever  & Ezeah.
(2020), found that women in Nigeria spent more time
engaging with social media advertisements than men
and were more likely to use the sharing option,
indicating differences in the way male and female users

engage with social media marketing content.  Digital
technologies have also enhanced financial inclusion,
business resilience, and market expansion among
entrepreneurs operating in different sectors of the
Nigerian economy (Adah & Ezekiel, 2024).

Digital marketing has become particularly significant for
Home Economics Entrepreneurs whose businesses
largely revolve around food production, catering
services, fashion designing, tailoring, interior
decoration, event planning, childcare services, textile
production, crafts, home management products and
other household -related enterprises. These businesses
depend heavily on customer awareness, product
visibility, and effective communication with existing and
potential customers. The integration of digital
marketing enables Home Eco nomics Entrepreneurs to
showcase products through social media platforms such
as Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, and
YouTube while utilising online marketplaces and
websites to reach customers beyond their immediate
geographical locations (Meludu, 2 020; Nair & Aithal,
2023). Evidence from various empirical studies suggests
that entrepreneurs adopt diverse digital marketing
strategies depending on business size, available
resources, technological competence and target
markets, including social media m arketing, content
marketing, email marketing, influencer marketing,
search engine optimisation, online advertising, mobile
marketing and e -commerce platforms (Peter et al.,
2020; Bashir & Dangongola, n.d.).

Despite the increasing opportunities associated with
digital marketing, its adoption among many Nigerian
entrepreneurs remains relatively low compared to
developed economies. Several studies have attributed
this situation to poor digital literacy, inadequa te
technological skills, limited financial resources,
unreliable internet services, high cost of data
subscriptions, poor electricity supply, cybersecurity
concerns, and weak digital infrastructure (Eze, Chinedu-
Eze, Okike & Bello., 2020; Eze, Chinedu-Eze, Awa, &
Alharthi., 2021). Other barriers include lack of digital
marketing knowledge, inadequate government support,
regulatory challenges, online fraud, and the difficulty of
measuring the effectiveness of digital marketing
investments (Rath, 2024). These  constraints often limit
entrepreneurs' ability to maximize the full benefits of
digital marketing technologies.

Oredo Local Government Area, Edo State, has witnessed
a growing number of Home Economics Entrepreneurs
engaged in catering services, baking, f ashion design,
event decoration, hairdressing, interior decoration and
other home -based enterprises. Like entrepreneurs in
other parts of Benin City, Home Economics
Entrepreneurs in Oredo operate in an environment
where digital platforms provide opportunit ies for
product promotion, customer engagement and market
access. Evidence from Benin City shows that digital
marketing strategies such as social media, email and
search engine marketing are significantly related to SME
performance (Agbonifoh & Ozolua, 2023), while studies
in Edo State have also reported positive effects of social
media business strategies on SME performance (Izuagie
& Umole, 2025). However, existing studies have largely
focused on SMEs generally, rather than specifically
examining the digi tal marketing strategies adopted by
Home Economics Entrepreneurs, the extent of their
utilization, and the challenges encountered in
implementing them. Thus, there appears to be limited
empirical evidence on digital marketing strategies
among Home Economics Entrepreneurs in Oredo Local
Government Area, Edo State.

It is against this background that this study examined
digital marketing strategies among Home Economics
Entrepreneurs in Oredo, with a view to identifying the
strategies adopted, determining the extent to which
they are utilised, and examining the challenges
encountered in their implementation. The findings are
expected to contribute to the growing body of
knowledge on digital entrepreneurship while providing
useful information for entrepreneurs, educators,
policymakers and other stakeholders interested in
promoting sustainable home economics enterprises in
the digital economy.

Methodology

Design of the Study
This study adopted a descriptive survey research design.
This design was considered appropriate because it

Area of the Study

The study was carried out in Oredo Local Government
Area, Edo State, Nigeria. Oredo is a semi -urban
community situated in the south senatorial district of
Edo State. Oredo  was selected because of its growing
concentration of Home Economics Entrepreneurs
involved in food processing and catering, fashion and
textile production, cosmetology, event planning and
household product manufacturing, representing a semi-
urban setting typical of many Nigerian communities
where digital marketing adoption among small
entrepreneurs is still evolving.

Population of the Study
The population comprised all registered Home
Economics Entrepreneurs operating within Oredo Local
Government Area, Edo State. Based on records obtained
from the Oredo Local Government Area Commerce
Department and the National Association of Small-Scale
Industrialists (NASSI) Oredo chapter, a total population
of 386 active Home Economics Entrepreneurs was
identified, eng aged in food processing and catering,
fashion and textiles, cosmetology, event planning and
household product manufacturing.

Sample and Sampling Technique
The sample size for the study is 216 and it was
determined using the Taro Yamane formula for a finit e
population, n = N / [1 + N(e)2], where N (386) is the
population and e (0.05) is the margin of error. This
yielded a sample size of 196, which was increased by
10% to allow for possible non -response, giving a final
sample size of 216. A proportionate str atified random
sampling technique was adopted, whereby the
population of 386 registered entrepreneurs was divided
into five strata based on business sub-sector, thereafter,
entrepreneurs were selected from each stratum using
simple random sampling in propo rtion to stratum size,
as shown in Table 1.

Instrument for Data Collection
A self -structured questionnaire, developed based on
the study objectives and relevant literature, served as
the instrument for data collection. It comprised four
sections: Section A elicited demographic data (age,
gender, educational qualificati on, years of business
operation and business sub-sector); Section B identified
the specific digital marketing strategies adopted using
a Yes/No response format; Section C determined the
extent to which digital marketing strategies were
utilized on a four-point Likert scale (Very High Extent =
4 to Very Low Extent = 1); and Section D examined the
challenges faced in implementing digital marketing
strategies on a four-point Likert scale (Strongly Agree =
4 to Strongly Disagree = 1). Face and content validity
were established through review by experts in Home
Economics, Entrepreneurship, and a Psychometrician,
while reliability was established using Cronbach's alpha
on a pre -test of 20 questionnaires administered in
Oredo yielding a coefficient of 0.78.

Method of Data Collection
A well -structured questionnaire was administered
personally to Home Economics Entrepreneurs across
the five identified sub -sectors in Oredo , following the
necessary permissions from the Local Government Area
Commerce Department and the leadership of the NASSI
Oredo chapter. Data collection was conducted by the
researcher with the assistance of two trained research
assistants; questionnaires w ere administered during
business hours at the entrepreneurs' respective
business locations and retrieved on the same day to
ensure a high response rate. The exercise lasted
approximately three weeks, after which all retrieved
questionnaires were screened f or completeness and
accuracy before analysis. A total of 207 questionnaires
returned from the 216 administered questions,
representing a response rate of 95.8%. Data were
analysed using SPSS version 27.0; descriptive statistics
(frequencies, percentages, and mean scores, with a cut-
off mean of 2.50) summarised the socio -demographic
profile and study variables, while the Chi -square test of

Discussion of Findings

Findings from Research Question 1 shows that
WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram were the m ost
widely adopted digital marketing strategies among the
entrepreneurs. This aligns with Lawal, Salisu, & Bappa -
Yaya. (2022), who established that social media
platforms serve as primary marketing channels for
women micro -entrepreneurs in Nigeria, and wit h
Ilodigwe (2024) and Gwadabe (2021), who similarly
identified Facebook and social media platforms as the
most commonly used marketing tools among Nigerian
entrepreneurs and SMEs. The same table also shows
that SEO, paid advertising, and e -commerce marketing
recorded the lowest adoption rates, a finding that
contrasts with the growth potential of advanced digital
marketing tools emphasised by Bashir and Dangongola
(n.d.) and is consistent with Peter and Dalla Vecchia
(2020), who found that small businesses significantly lag
behind larger enterprises in adopting sophisticated
digital marketing tools due to limited resources,
technology constraints and lack of expertise.

In Table 4, the high overall extent of digital marketing
utilization (grand mean = 2.8), driven largely by digital
payments and direct messaging for customer
communication, corroborates Burhanudin, Yusnaini,
Khamisah & Kartasari. (2022) and Gao, Siddik, Khawar,
Hamayun, Masukujjaman & Alam.  (2023), who found
that e -commerce and digital marketi ng tools
significantly helped home -based businesses improve
product value, sustain competitiveness, and enhance
financial performance. The same table further shows
that social media analytics, paid advertising and
interactive content recorded the lowest ut ilization
scores, reflecting Gwadabe (2021) and Omotosho
(2023), both of whom observed that Nigerian SMEs and
small-scale entrepreneurs were not leveraging
advanced digital tools despite general awareness of
social media platforms. This suggests that while  Home
Economics Entrepreneurs in Oredo have embraced
basic digital marketing practices, deeper and more
strategic utilization remains limited.

Findings from Table 5 showed that frequent power
outages, poor internet connectivity, and high internet
and advertising costs were the leading implementation
challenges. This strongly aligns with Olarinde Idem, &
Obieze (2024), who identified electricity o utages, high
data costs, and inadequate digital infrastructure as
primary impediments to e-commerce growth in Nigeria,
and with Cueto, Frisnedi, Collera, Batac & Agaton,.
(2022) and Nursanti and Nurhayati (2024), who
identified poor internet infrastructure , limited digital
skills and socio -cultural constraints as key barriers to
digital entrepreneurship. The same table also shows that
the challenge of algorithm changes and online
competition is consistent with Bashir and Dangongola
(n.d.), while concerns ab out online fraud align with
Joseph, Miri, & Mswaka. (2025), who identified trust -

Conclusion

The study concludes that Home Economics
Entrepreneurs in Oredo have embraced di gital
marketing as an important tool for promoting their
businesses, with the majority utilizing digital marketing
strategies to a high extent, particularly through readily
accessible social media platforms such as WhatsApp,
Facebook, and Instagram. Howeve r, the adoption of
advanced digital marketing strategies, including search
engine optimization, paid online advertising, email
marketing, website marketing, and e -commerce
platforms, remains relatively low. Despite the high level
of utilization, entrepreneurs continue to face significant
challenges, notably frequent power outages, poor
internet connectivity, high internet and advertising
costs, inadequate financial resources and limited access
to digital marketing training and support.

Recommendations

1. Home Economics Entrepreneurs should expand their
use of digital marketing beyond basic social media
platforms by adopting advanced strategies such as
search engine optimization, website marketing,
email marketing, e -commerce platforms and paid
online advertising to improve business visibility and
profitability.
2. Government agencies and relevant
entrepreneurship development bodies should
organize regular training, workshops, and digital
literacy programmes to equip Home Economics
Entrepreneurs with practical skills in modern digital
marketing techniques.
3. Financial institutions, government intervention
agencies and microfinance organizations should
provide affordable loans, grants and financial
support targeted at small -scale entrepreneurs to
enable inve stment in digital marketing
technologies and services.
4. Government should improve digital infrastructure by
ensuring more reliable electricity supply, expanding
broadband internet coverage and reducing the cost
of internet services.
5. Entrepreneurship s upport organizations, business
associations, and professional bodies should
establish mentorship programmes and digital
marketing support centres offering continuous
guidance on content creation, customer
engagement and analytics.
6. Institutions offering Home Economics programmes
should integrate practical digital marketing, e -
commerce and digital entrepreneurship courses
into their curriculum.
7. Home Economics Entrepreneurs should regularly
monitor social media analytics, customer
engagement metrics and market trends to evaluate
and improve the effectiveness of their digital
marketing activities.
