significantly to food security, employment generation, and rural development. The continuous availability of
quality agricultural inputs, including certified seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, planting materials, farm equipment,
and other essential farm supplies , is necessary to support agricultural productivity and sustainable crop
production. Efficient agricultural supply operations therefore play an important role in strengthening the
agricultural value chain by ensuring that these inputs are procured, stored, d istributed, and delivered to end
users in a timely and reliable manner.

Abstract. Crop agricultural supply enterprises play a vital role in
supporting agricultural productivity and food security by ensuring the
continuous availability and efficient distribution of quality agricultural
inputs to government agencies, agricultural cooperatives, retailers, and
farmers. Their operational effectiveness influences business
sustainability and the overall agricultural value chain. This study
assessed the crop agricultural supply operations of LM Arenas Agri -
Products Corporation, a medium -sized agricultural supply enterprise
in Manaoag, Pangasinan. Specifically, it evaluated the corporation's
organization and management, technical operations, distribution and
marketing, and financial performance; identified operational factors
limiting business performance; and developed strategic direc tions to
improve operational efficiency, competitiveness, and long -term
sustainability. A descriptive case study research design was employed
using structured interviews with the Chief Executive Officer, direct
observation of business operations, documenta ry analysis, and
examination of audited financial statements covering 2023 –2025.
Financial performance was analyzed through vertical analysis,
horizontal analysis, and profitability ratios, including Gross Profit
Margin, Net Profit Margin, and Return on As sets. Findings revealed
that the corporation has established a functional organizational
structure, standardized operational procedures, efficient technical
operations, and a relationship-based business-to-business distribution
network that has supported its continued operations for seventeen
years. Financial results showed that net sales increased by 61.47% in
2024 before declining by 29.49% in 2025, while profitability improved
through effective cost management. Despite these strengths,
operational limitations were identified in employee development,
manual inventory systems, warehouse and logistics capacity,
dependence on government procurement, limited digital marketing
initiatives, and the absence of formal budgeting and cash flow
forecasting. The study provides practical strategic directions that may
strengthen operational performance and sustainable business growth
among agricultural supply enterprises in the Philippines.

Literature Review
Crop Agricultural Supply Operations
Agricultural supply operations involve the coordinated procurement, storage, inventory management,
transportation, and distribution of agricultural inputs required by farmers and institutional buyers. Effective
supply operations ensure product availability , timely delivery, efficient logistics, and customer satisfaction
while supporting the continuity of agricultural production. Studies on supply chain management emphasize
that the integration of procurement, warehousing, distribution, and logistics contrib utes to improved
organizational performance and operational efficiency (Chopra & Meindl, 2019; Christopher, 2022; Monczka
et al., 2020).  In agricultural enterprises, operational effectiveness depends on the ability to coordinate
suppliers, maintain adequate inventory, manage warehouse facilities, and deliver products efficiently to
customers. Heizer et al. (2023) emphasized that efficient o perations management improves productivity and
organizational responsiveness, particularly in businesses that rely heavily on inventory movement and
logistics. Similarly, agricultural supply enterprises must continuously adapt their operational systems to
changing customer demand, technological developments, and supply chain challenges to maintain
competitiveness and long-term sustainability.

Organization and Management
Organization and management play a critical role in establishing efficient business operations through
clearly defined organizational structures, administrative policies, employee development, leadership, and
performance management. Daft (2021) explained t hat effective organizational structures promote
accountability, coordination, and efficient decision -making by clearly defining functional responsibilities
within an organization. Likewise, Armstrong and Taylor (2023) highlighted the importance of employee
development, performance evaluation, succession planning, and workforce capability in sustaining
organizational growth and improving business performance.  For medium -sized enterprises, strong
organizational management contributes to operational consistency by ensuring that employees understand
their duties, business procedures are standardized, and managerial decisions are implemented effectively.
These prac tices provide the foundation for maintaining operational stability while supporting continuous
organizational improvement.

Technical operations encompass procurement, receiving and inspection, warehousing, inventory
management, quality control, order processing, loading, and product distribution. These activities directly
influence the efficiency with which agricultural supplies move from suppliers to customers. Christopher (2022)
emphasized that logistics integration is essential in improving supply chain responsiveness and ensuring
efficient product movement across distribution networks.  Inventory management remains particularly
important because inaccurate inventory records and limited warehouse capacity may affect product availability
and operational efficiency. Richards (2021) discussed the importance of effective warehouse management i n
improving storage utilization, inventory accuracy, and cost efficiency. Heizer et al. (2023) further emphasized
that the adoption of modern operational technologies strengthens inventory visibility and improves decision -
making within supply chain operati ons. These studies indicate that digital inventory systems and improved
logistics practices can enhance the operational capability of agricultural supply enterprises.

Distribution and Marketing
Distribution and marketing determine how agricultural supply enterprises reach customers, maintain
business relationships, and expand market opportunities. Relationship marketing is particularly relevant in
business-to-business settings because sustained exchange relationships can support cooperation, customer
retention, and long -term business performance. Recent scholarship continues  to apply and extend the
commitment-trust perspective in contemporary relationship marketing. Badrinarayanan and Ramachandran
(2024) reviewed relationship marketing research through the commitment -trust framework and emphasized
its continuing relevance across supplier, buyer, lateral, and internal relationships. Similarly, Chen et al. (2022)
found that relationship commitment and trust remain important mechanisms in relationship marketing,
particularly in digitally mediated customer relationships.  Contemporary marketing literature likewise
emphasizes the increasing importance of digital marketing in expanding customer reach and strengthening
business competitiveness. Kotler et al. (2022) explained that traditional relationship marketing can be
complemented by digital marketing strategies that improve customer engagement and market visibility.
Chaffey and Ellis-Chadwick (2022) similarly highlighted the role of online marketing platforms in improving
communication, customer interaction, and market accessibility. These studies suggest that agricultural supply
enterprises may strengthen their competitiveness by combining relationship -based marketing with
appropriate digital marketing initiatives.

Financial Performance
Financial performance reflects an enterprise's ability to utilize organizational resources efficiently
while maintaining profitability and long -term financial sustainability. Financial statement analysis provides
important information regarding operational  performance by examining trends in sales, profitability, and
resource utilization. Brigham and Ehrhardt (2022) explained that profitability indicators such as Gross Profit
Margin, Net Profit Margin, and Return on Assets provide useful measures for evaluating operational efficiency
and financial management.  Vertical and horizontal analyses further assist organizations in identifying
financial trends and evaluating changes in financial performance across reporting periods. Through these
analytical tools, business managers are able to assess revenue movements, cost management effectiveness,
profitability improvement, and overall organizational growth. Financial analysis therefore complements
operational assessment by providing quantitative evidence that supports strategic decision -making and
resource planning.

Methodology
Research Design
This study employed a descriptive case study research design to assess the crop agricultural supply
operations of LM Arenas Agri-Products Corporation, a medium-sized agricultural supply enterprise located in
Barangay Matolong, Manaoag, Pangasinan. The study adopted the descriptive case study approach because it
enables an in-depth examination of a single organization within its actual business environment and provides
a comprehensive understanding of organizational practices, operational processes, and business performance
(Yin, 2018). Principles of descriptive qualitative inquiry were likewise applied in examining the corporation's
management systems and operational practices through interviews, observations, and documentary analysis
(Creswell & Creswell, 2018).  The research focused on four operational dimensions: organization and
management, technical operations, distribution and marketing, and financial performance. These operational
areas were examined to determine the corporation's strengths, identify factors limiting business performance,
and formulate strategic directions for operational improvement and long-term sustainability.

The study was conducted at LM Arenas Agri -Products Corporation located in Barangay Matolong,
Manaoag, Pangasinan. The corporation is engaged in the distribution and marketing of agricultural supplies,
including certified seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, planting materials, farm equipment, and other agricultural
inputs. It serves government agencies, local  government units, agricultural cooperatives, retailers, and
individual farmers throughout Region I and nearby provinces.  The research locale was selected because the
corporation has maintained continuous business operations since its establishment in 2009 and possesses
organizational records, operational documents, and audited financial statements necessary for conducting a
comprehensive case study on agricultural supply operations.

Research Participants
The key informant of the study was the Chief Executive Officer (CEO)/President of LM Arenas Agri -
Products Corporation. The CEO served as the principal informant because of his comprehensive knowledge of
the corporation's organizational structure, managerial practices, proc urement activities, operational
procedures, marketing strategies, logistics operations, and financial management.  Additional operational
evidence was obtained through direct observation of the corporation's business facilities and day -to-day
operations, including warehouse activities, inventory handling, procurement procedures, product receiving,
distribution activiti es, loading operations, and logistics practices. Documentary records were likewise
examined to validate and supplement the information obtained from the interview and observations.

Research Instrument
The study utilized both primary and secondary sources of data. Primary data were gathered through a
structured interview guide and an observation checklist prepared by the researcher. The structured interview
guide was designed to obtain information relevant to the study objectives, particularly regarding organizational
management, technical operations, distribution practices, marketing strategies, operational challenges, and
business improvement initiatives. The observation checklist was used during the ex amination of warehouse
operations, inventory management practices, procurement procedures, logistics activities, and other daily
business processes.  Secondary data were obtained from company documents and organizational records,
including administrative policies, operational reports, inventory records, procurement documents, marketing
information, and audited financial statements covering the years 202 3, 2024, and 2025. These documentary
sources provided objective evidence for evaluating the corporation's operational practices and financial
performance.

Data Gathering Procedure
Prior to data collection, permission to conduct the study and access company records was obtained
from the management of LM Arenas Agri -Products Corporation. Upon approval, the researcher conducted a
structured interview with the Chief Executive Officer to  gather information regarding the corporation's
organizational management, technical operations, distribution and marketing practices, financial
management, and operational challenges.  Direct observation was subsequently conducted within the business
premises to examine actual operational activities involving procurement, receiving and inspection,
warehousing, inventory monitoring, loading, product distribution, and customer service proc esses.
Documentary analysis was also performed using relevant organizational records and audited financial
statements to validate interview responses and provide supporting operational and financial evidence. The
collected qualitative information from inte rviews, observations, and documentary review was organized
according to the four operational dimensions of the study.  Financial data from the audited financial statements
were analyzed using vertical analysis, horizontal analysis, and profitability ratio analysis, specifically Gross
Profit Margin, Net Profit Margin, and Return on Assets, to evaluate the corporation's fina ncial performance
during the three -year period. The results were then synthesized and interpreted to identify operational
strengths, business limitations, and strategic directions for improving organizational efficiency and long-term
sustainability.

Conclusion
This study assessed the crop agricultural supply operations of LM Arenas Agri-Products Corporation,
a medium-sized agricultural supply enterprise in Manaoag , Pangasinan, by examining its organization and
management, technical operations, distribution and marketing, and financial performance. The findings
demonstrated that the corporation has established functional organizational structures, standardized
operational procedures, reliable distribution practices, and prudent financial management that have enabled
the enterprise to sustain its operations and maintain profitability despite fluctuations in annual sales.  The
study revealed several operational constraints that may affect long -term competitiveness and organizational
sustainability, including limited employee development programs, continued reliance on manual inventory
systems, warehouse and logistics limitations, dep endence on government procurement activities, limited
digital marketing initiatives, and the absence of formal budgeting and cash flow forecasting systems. These
findings demonstrate that while the corporation possesses strong operational foundations, cont inuous
improvement is necessary to enhance efficiency, adaptability, and long -term business resilience.  By
integrating organizational management, technical operations, distribution and marketing, and financial
analysis within a single descriptive case study, this research contributes practical evidence to the limited body
of case-based literature on crop agricultural supply operations in the Philippines. The findings provide useful
managerial insights that may assist agricultural supply enterprises in improving operational practices,
strengthening strategic decision-making, and supporting sustainable agribusiness growth.

Recommendations
Based on the findings of the study, the following recommendations are proposed to strengthen the
operational performance and long -term sustainability of LM Arenas Agri -Products Corporation and provide
practical guidance for similar agricultural supply ente rprises. First, the corporation should strengthen its
human resource management by establishing formal employee development programs, regular skills training,
performance evaluation systems, and succession planning initiatives. Providing comprehensive employee
welfare and development opportunities may improve employee competence, motivation, retention, and
organizational continuity.  Second, the corporation should modernize its technical operations through the
gradual adoption of a digital inventory management system integrated with warehouse monitoring and
delivery tracking. Digitalization may improve inventory accuracy, operational e fficiency, product monitoring,
and decision-making while reducing reliance on manual recording procedures. Third, warehouse and logistics
capability should be enhanced through improved storage utilization, preventive maintenance of delivery
vehicles, and long -term planning for warehouse expansion as operational demand increases. Strengthening
logistics infrastructure may improve delivery reliability and support the efficient movement of agricultural
supplies during peak operational periods. Fourth, the corporation should diversify its market opportunities by
strengthening digital marketing initiatives while maintaining its existing relationship-based business strategy.
Developing an official corporate website and establishing professional soc ial media platforms may improve
market visibility, expand customer reach, and reduce dependence on government procurement activities and
institutional buyers.  Finally, management should institutionalize formal financial planning practices by
