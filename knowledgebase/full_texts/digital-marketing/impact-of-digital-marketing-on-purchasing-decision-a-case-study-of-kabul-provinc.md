ABSTRACT

Digital marketing has become an essential strategy for businesses looking to influence customer behavior, improve
communication, and boost sales. This study looks at how digital marketing affects customer purchasing decisions in electronic
stores in Kabul province, Afghanistan. The study focuses on six key digital marketing dimensions: social media marketing,
online advertising, email marketing, search engine optimization (SEO), pay-per-click advertising (PPC), and mobile marketing.
The study's goal is to determine how digital marketing strategies influence consumer purchasing decisions in the Afghan
electronic market. A structured questionnaire with a five-point Likert scale was used to collect primary data from respondents.
Customers in Kabul Province, Afghanistan, who used electronic products were the target population. Using Cochran's formula,
a sample size of 384 respondents was calculated and chosen using stratified random sampling. Statistical analyses were carried
out using SPSS software, which included descriptive statistics, normality testing, linearity testing, and simple linear regression.
The study's conclusions showed that consumer purchasing decisions are significantly and favorably impacted by digital
marketing. Digital marketing explained 50.7% of the variance in customer purchasing decisions (R² = .507), and the regression
analysis showed a strong positive relationship between digital marketing and purchasing decisions (R = .712). The significant
impact of digital marketing on consumer purchasing behavior was further confirmed by the standardized regression coefficient
(β =.712, p <.001). The study suggests that future researchers look into other Afghan provinces and industries and look into how
each aspect of digital marketing affects consumer buying habits.

Keywords- Digital Marketing, Purchasing Decision, Social Media Marketing, Online Advertising.

II. LITERATURE REVIEW

Digital marketing
Digital marketing is a type of marketing that
uses digital channels to promote products or services and
reach out to current or potential customers (Key, 2017).
While digital marketing is present in channels that do not
require the internet, this feature extends beyond internet
marketing (Kulova & Mihaylov, 2018). Mobile phones
(Short Message Service—SMS and Multimedia Message
Service—MMS), search engine marketing, social media
marketing, display advertising, and other forms of digital
media are all examples of digital marketing
(Suryawardani & Wiranatha, 2017). Digital marketing
has a broad definition that encompasses all of its
activities in digital environments. It is also defined as
assisting marketing activities through the use of
technology and developing information that is
compatible with customer needs (Chaffey, 2013). Early
approaches to digital marketing defined it as a reflection
of traditional marketing, tools, and strategies on the
Internet (Constantinides, 2014). At the same time, the
digital world's characteristics and suitability for
marketing have resulted in the development of new sales
channels, languages, and formats (Chaffey, 2019).
Furthermore, digital marketing has emerged as a new
trend that combines mass marketing and personalization,
or adapting to the customer, to achieve marketing
objectives (Wind & Rangaswamy, 2001; Behera,
Gunasekaran, Gupta, Kamboj, & Bala, 2020). This
approach replaces traditional marketing methods
(Piñeiro-Otero & Martínez-Rolán, 2016). The evolution
of technological devices and the internet has resulted in
the emergence of marketing thought via the internet
(Gedik, 2020). These developments have also resulted in
the emergence of the digital marketing concept, which
makes global marketing more measurable, user-centered,
and accessible and in which producers and consumers
interact constantly. Digital marketing is also known as
"online marketing," "internet marketing," or "web
marketing" in the literature. Although the term "digital
marketing" has grown in popularity over time, it appears
that the terms "online marketing" in the United States
and "web marketing" in Italy are more widely used.
After 2013, digital marketing became the most
commonly used term in England and around the world
(Kaur & Sandhu, 2017). When we look at some of these
definitions, Chaffey (2013) defines digital marketing as
the use of technologies to assist marketing activities in
collecting and developing data that meets customer
needs. Digital marketing is a type of marketing that
makes use of electronic devices such as game consoles,
smartphones, personal computers, and cell phones while
also involving stakeholders (Nathan Research Inc.,
2019).

Email marketing has evolved over time to embrace new
technological opportunities in data analysis, targeting,
and automation, making it a more personalized and
precise tool than ever before (Sathish et al., 2022).

III. RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

Digital marketing and its six strategies (email
marketing, social media marketing, online advertising,
SEO, pay-per-clicks, and mobile marketing) are
measured using a 33-item measuring tool (3-5 items for
each latent-variable). They are used by Mahalaxmi &
Ranjith (2016), Fazla Rabby (2022), Tasnim Alhelali
(2023), Fahim Iftikhar & Imran Khan (2017), Fathima
Shahina & Vilani Sachitra (2020), Naveena.R (2022),
Motunrayo Oladele Ajao (2022), Kamal Pant (2018),
and Ala' Atef Alkarablieh & Hamza Salim Khraim
(2015). The measuring tool is based on a 5-point scale
presented as the Digital Marketing Response Scale: 1
(SD), 2 (D), 3 (N), 4 (A), and 5 (SA): Strongly Disagree,
Disagree, Neutral, Agree, and Strongly Agree.
Consumer Purchasing Decision (dependent variable):
Tasnim Alhelali (2023) used a 5-item questionnaire to
assess consumers' purchasing decisions. Furthermore, to
avoid incomplete responses, the questionnaire was
designed to accept only responses that answered all
questions. The data was completely collected and tested
from February 18 to May 5, 2026. Furthermore, a
quantitative research approach (positivism). The
sampling method is random (stratified), and the sample
consisted Kabul province residents who used electronic
tools. The appropriate sample size for a 95% confidence
level and 5% margin of error, calculated using Cochran's
formula, was approximately 384 respondents. Statistical
analyses were carried out using SPSS software version
27, which included linearity testing, normality testing,
descriptive statistics and simple linear regression.

IV. RESULT AND DISCUSSION

Linearity Test
Prior to performing regression analysis, the
assumption of linearity between digital marketing and
customer purchasing decisions was tested using ANOV A
for linearity. The results showed a significant linear
relationship between the two variables (F (1,113) =
440.726, p < .001). Furthermore, the deviation from
linearity was determined to be statistically insignificant,
F (269, 113) = 1.172, p = .167. The insignificant
deviation from linearity suggests that the relationship
between digital marketing and customer purchasing
decisions is linear rather than non-linear. As a result, the
assumption of linearity was met, confirming the
appropriateness of using simple linear regression
analysis in this study. These findings indicate that
changes in digital marketing practices are proportionally
related to changes in customer purchasing decisions.

V. DISCUSSION

The primary goal of this study was to look into
the impact of digital marketing on customer purchasing
decisions in electronic stores in Kabul province,
Afghanistan. The results showed that digital marketing
has a strong and statistically significant positive impact
on customer purchasing decisions. The regression
analysis found that digital marketing accounted for
50.7% of the variance in purchasing decisions (R² =
.507). The standardized beta coefficient (β =.712, p
<.001) confirmed the strong positive relationship
between the variables. These findings suggest that
customers who are exposed to effective digital marketing
activities are more likely to make purchases. The
findings of this study are consistent with previous
research, which has identified digital marketing as a
critical factor influencing modern consumer behavior.
For example, Maha and Ranj (2016) found that digital
marketing improves customer engagement and
influences purchasing behavior through online platforms
and social media communication. Similarly, Rai (2018)
concluded that digital marketing strategies raise
customer awareness and encourage consumers to shop
more efficiently. The current study supports these
findings by demonstrating that digital marketing
practices in Kabul's electronic stores have a significant
impact on consumer decisions. Furthermore, the findings
are consistent with the arguments of Tham et al. (2019),
who emphasized that digital marketing increases brand
value and purchase intention through interactive
communication between consumers and businesses.
Consumers in Afghanistan are increasingly turning to
social media platforms, online advertisements, mobile
applications, and search engines for information before
purchasing electronic products. As a result, customers'
purchasing behavior improves as they become more
informed and confident in their decisions. The
descriptive statistics also revealed high mean values for
both digital marketing (M = 4.1762) and purchasing
decisions (M = 4.3396), indicating that respondents have
a positive attitude toward digital marketing activities.
These findings suggest that consumers in Kabul province
are becoming more accepting of digital technologies and
online marketing communications. This could be
explained by the rapid rise in internet usage,
smartphones, and social media platforms in Afghanistan
in recent years. Businesses are gradually shifting from
traditional marketing methods to digital channels
because it allows them to reach a larger audience at a
lower cost and faster. Another important interpretation
of the results is based on the Theory of Reasoned Action
(TRA), which served as the study's theoretical
foundation. Individual attitudes and subjective norms
influence people's behaviors, according to TRA.
According to this study, digital marketing activities such
as social media marketing, online advertising, and email
marketing can positively shape customers' attitudes
toward electronic products and influence their intention
to purchase. When consumers see appealing
advertisements, positive reviews, and promotional
campaigns online on a regular basis, they develop
positive perceptions of products and become more likely
to buy. Thus, the findings provide empirical support for
the use of TRA to explain consumer purchasing behavior
in the digital environment. Ng Sweeney et al. (2020)
support the findings, claiming that social media
engagement boosts customer loyalty and strengthens
business-consumer relationships. In the current study,
many respondents seemed to value the convenience and
accessibility provided by digital platforms. Electronic
stores that actively use social media pages, targeted
advertisements, and online communication tools are
better able to capture customer attention and establish
trust. This trust encourages customers to make more
confident purchasing decisions. Similarly, the study
supports the findings of Ukaegbu (2013), who
discovered that online advertising significantly
influences customer attitudes, beliefs, and perceptions of
products. In Kabul's electronic market, online
advertisements may make it easier for customers to
compare prices, evaluate product features, and identify
available brands than traditional advertising methods.
This increased access to information reduces uncertainty
and improves customer decision-making processes. The
regression analysis yielded a strong positive coefficient
(B = 0.707), indicating that increased digital marketing
effectiveness leads directly to increased customer
purchasing behavior. This suggests that businesses that
invest in digital marketing campaigns can get better
customer responses and sales results. Digital marketing
tools enable businesses to communicate directly with
customers, personalize promotions, and build long-term
customer relationships. These benefits render digital
marketing more effective than many traditional
promotional methods. In addition, the study adds to the
limited literature on digital marketing in Afghanistan.

VI. CONCLUSION

This study looked at how digital marketing
affected customer purchasing decisions in electronic
stores in Kabul province, Afghanistan. The findings
demonstrated that digital marketing has a significant and
positive impact on customer purchasing behavior.
Effective digital marketing strategies strongly influence
consumer purchasing decisions (β =. 712, p < .001).
Furthermore, digital marketing accounted for 50.7% of
the variation in customer purchasing decisions,
highlighting its importance in shaping modern consumer
behavior. The study also discovered that respondents had
generally favorable attitudes toward digital marketing
activities such as social media marketing, online
advertising, email marketing, search engine optimization
(SEO), mobile marketing, and pay-per-click advertising.
These findings indicate that digital platforms have
emerged as effective tools for attracting customers,
improving communication, raising product awareness,
and influencing purchasing decisions in the Afghan
market. The findings are consistent with previous
research, which has shown that digital marketing
increases customer engagement, raises brand awareness,
and positively influences purchasing intention. The study
lends support to the Theory of Reasoned Action (TRA),
which states that customer attitudes and intentions have
a significant influence on behavior. In this study, digital
marketing positively influenced customers' attitudes
toward products and encouraged purchasing decisions.
