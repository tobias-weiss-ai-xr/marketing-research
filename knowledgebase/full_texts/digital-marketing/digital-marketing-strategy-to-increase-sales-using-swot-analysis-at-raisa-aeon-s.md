Abstract:  The purpose of this study is to analyze the Digital Marketing strategy in
increasing sales at RAISA Aeon Store BSD Tangerang with a SWOT analysis
approach which experienced a decline in revenue due to increasingly tight
Company competition. The place of this research was conducted at RAISA
Aeon Store BSD Tangerang located at Jl. BSD Raya Utama, Pagedangan,
Pagedangan District, Tangerang Regency, Banten 15339. The research method
uses a qualitative descriptive method with data collection techniques including
observation, interviews, and documentation which are then analyzed using
SWOT analysis. The results of the analysis show that the IFAS value is 0.91 and
EFAS 0.87, the position of the SWOT matrix is in an aggressive strategy in
quadrant I. This strategy shows that RAISA Aeon Store BSD has high internal
strengths and considerable external opportunities, but has not been utilized
optimally in the face of increasingly tight competition, so it is neces sary to
strengthen and develop digital marketing strategies to increase sales.

Keywords:  Digital Marketing, Marketing Strategy, Sales, SWOT

1. Introduction
The rapid development of digital technology has significantly transformed business activities
in Indonesia, particularly in the retail sector. The shift from conventional marketing methods
to digital marketing has changed the way companies interact with consumers. Previously,
marketing re lied on brochures, banners, and printed media. However, in the digital era,
companies utilize social media, websites, and digital communication platforms to reach
customers more effectively and efficiently. This transformation not only affects promotional
strategies but also influences consumer purchasing behavior.
Digital marketing has become an important strategy in increasing company sales.
According to Kotler (2016), marketing is a social and managerial process through which
individuals and groups obtai n what they need and want by creating and exchanging products
of value. In the digital context, marketing activities are carried out through online platforms
such as WhatsApp, Instagram, and websites that allow businesses to communicate directly with
customers. Based on Hootsuite (2024), WhatsApp is the most widely used social media
platform in Indonesia, reaching 90.9% of internet users. This condition shows that WhatsApp
can be an effective tool for implementing digital marketing strategies.
In developing  effective marketing strategies, companies need to evaluate internal and
external factors. One strategic tool that can be used is SWOT analysis, which analyzes
Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats. Through SWOT analysis, companies can

improving company performance.

2. Literature Review
2.1 Digital Marketing
According to Heinze and Huertas (2021), digital marketing strategy involves planning,
developing, and implementing marketing activities that utilize digital technology and social
media to achieve business goals such as increasing sales and brand awareness. According to
(Pramudita & Suharyati, 2024) Digital Marketing is an effort made by companies to utilize
digital media or the internet so that consumers can receive information about t he products or
services offered, so that ultimately the company's sales increase.

2.3 SWOT Analysis
According to Mujito (2023:43), SWOT analysis is a method used to identify internal
factors (strengths and weaknesses) and external factors (opportunities and threats) that affect a
company's access. This helps companies evaluate their strategies and determine what actions
to take to strengthen their market position. According to Virgo Simamora (2022:27) Analysis
of strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats or the abbreviation SWOT is an a nalysis
carried out to determine the current situation faced by the Company concerning external
factors, namely opportunities and threats, as well as internal factors, namely strengths and
weaknesses.

3. Research Method
According to Sugiyono (2020:9), quali tative research is a method used to examine natural
object conditions, where the researcher acts as the key instrument, data collection techniques
are carried out through triangulation (combination), data analysis is inductive in nature, and
qualitative research results emphasize meaning rather than generalization.
This research was conducted at Raisa AEON Store BSD, located at Jl. BSD Raya Utama,
Pagedangan, Tangerang, Banten. Supporting informants are individuals who provide additional
information to com plement the analysis and discussion in qualitative research. The total
number of informants in this study was 20 people, consisting of 1 key informant and 19
supporting informants. The key informant was the Assistant Manager of Raisa AEON Store
BSD because the Assistant Manager has comprehensive knowledge regarding the company’s
digital marketing strategies and overall operational activities. Meanwhile, the supporting
informants consisted of 1 Group Leader, 1 Assistant Group Leader, 4 employees, 3 personal
shopper, and 10 customers who were considered to understand the implementation of digital
marketing and service processes at Raisa AEON Store BSD.
According to Sugiyono (2020:105), there are four data collection techniques commonly
used in qualitative research, namely observation, interviews, documentation, and triangulation
(a combination of observation, interviews, and documentation). In this study, data were
collected through direct observation of digital marketing activities, semi-structured interviews
with informants, documentation of sales data for the period 2022 –2024, and triangulation to
ensure data validity. The data were then analyzed using SWOT analysis by identifying internal
factors (strengths and weaknesses) and external factors (opportunities and threats), which were
further evaluated using IFAS and EFAS matrices to determine the company’s strategic position
and formulate appropriate digital marketing strategies to increase sales.

Matrix IFAS (Internal Factor Analysis Summary)
After a company's internal strategic factors have been identified, an IFAS table is prepared
to formulate these internal strategic factors within the company's strengths and weaknesses.
The steps are as follows:
a. Determine the factors that constitute the company's strengths and weaknesses in column 1.
b. Determine factor weights on a scale from 1.0 (most important) to 0.0 (unimportant),  based
on the influence of each factor on the company's strategic position. (The sum of these
weights should not exceed a total score of 1.00).
c. Calculate a rating (in column 3) for each factor on a scale from  4 (outstanding) to 1 (poor)
based on the factor's influence on the company's condition.
d. Then, multiply the weights in column 2 by the rating in column 3 to o btain the weightings
in column 4.
e. Sum the weighted scores (in column 4) to obtain a weighted score for the company's
response to each of the factors. Good supplier support. Internal strategic planning. This total
score can be used to compare the company with other companies in the same industry group.

5. Conclusion

implemented by Raisa AEON Store BSD plays an important role in supporting sales
performance, particularly through the utilization of WhatsApp Business and various
promotional programs such as discounts, cashback, and free delivery services. However, sales
data from 2022 –2024 indicate fluctuating performance, where sales targets were not
consistently achieved in the last two years. The SWOT analysis shows that Raisa AEON Store
BSD is positioned in Quadrant I (agg ressive strategy), indicating strong internal capabilities
and favorable external opportunities. Therefore, the appropriate strategy is to optimize strength
