ABSTRACT
Digitalization has transformed marketing practices in Islamic banking by enabling more
interactive, personalized, and customer-oriented communication. In this context, digital marketing
has become increasingly important for strengthening customer relationships and customer loyalty
amid growing competition in the banking industry. This study aims to examine how digital
marketing contributes to customer loyalty, identify challenges in its implementation in Islamic
banking, and determine effective digital marketing strategies for creating customer loyalty. A
Systematic Literature Review (SLR) was conducted using the Preferred Reporting Items for
Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses (PRISMA) framework. Literature was retrieved from
Scopus using the keyword “digital marketing customer” and managed with Watase UAKE. From
105 identified articles, 10 studies met the final inclusion criteria and were analyzed using qualitative
thematic synthesis. The findings indicate that digital marketing strengthens customer loyalt y
through engagement, satisfaction, digital experience, personalization, trust, and Sharia-oriented
branding. Key implementation challenges include uneven digital literacy, security and privacy
concerns, information transparency, Sharia compliance, and com petitive pressure. Effective
strategies involve informative digital content, responsive digital services, responsible AI and data
analytics, and consistent Islamic branding. The study concludes that digital marketing is most
effective when integrated with customer experience, service quality, trust, and Sharia values.

Keywords: Customer Loyalty, Digital Marketing, Islamic Banking, Marketing Strategy.

INTRODUCTION
The advancement of digital technology has fundamentally transformed marketing
practices across industries, including Islamic banking. Digitalization has shifted
marketing from conventional, one-way promotion toward data-driven, interactive, and
personalized approaches that enable organizations to communicate with customers and
create value more effectively. In the banking sector, digital marketing has consequently
become an important strategic instrument for strengthening customer relationships,
improving service experiences, and sustaining competitiveness. The development of
contemporary marketing strategies increasingly depends on the integration of digital
technologies with customer-oriented activities, making digitalization an essential
component of modern marketing practice (Merisavo, 2008; Valmai et al., 2026).
Changes in customer behavior accompany this transformation . Customers
increasingly rely on digital platforms to search for information, interact with service
providers, and conduct financial transactions, creating greater expectations for
convenience, speed, accessibility, and personalized experiences. Digital ma rketing
therefore extends beyond promotional activities to facilitate continuous communication
and customer engagement. Evidence indicates that digital marketing can strengthen
customer loyalty by improving interactions, customer value, and relationship quality

(Mohammad, 2022; Nuseir & El Refae, 2022; Listiorini, 2025). In banking, digital
marketing communication through social media and other digital channels can also
strengthen trust and loyalty by enabling more accessible and responsive customer
interactions (Reyhanrafif et al., 2024; Souza et al., 2025).
Customer loyalty is particularly important in the banking industry because retaining
existing customers is essential amid increasingly competitive financial markets. Loyalty
reflects customers’ willingness to continue using banking services, conduct repeat
transactions, and recommend services to others (Kim et al., 2024). Previous studies by
Pranata et al. (2023), Kamal et al. (2025), and Gargun (2025) have demonstrated that
digital marketing can contribute positively to customer loyalty across different contexts.
Studies in banking have linked digital marketing strategies with loyalty through customer
satisfaction, while research in other sectors has hi ghlighted the roles of customer
experience, engagement, and digital interaction in strengthening loyalty. In Islamic
banking, digital marketing is also increasingly associated with customer loyalty,
particularly through digital services, brand image, and S haria-oriented marketing
strategies (Firdaus, 2024; Margahayu et al., 2025; Rabbani et al., 2026).
Despite these developments, existing research remains fragmented. Much of the
literature focuses on whether digital marketing affects customer loyalty or examines
specific digital channels and mediating mechanisms, such as satisfaction, customer
experience, or engagement (Petroccione et al., 2025; Shajrawi, 2025). Other studies by
Al-Adwan et al. (2024) and Alawiyah and Rosyada (2025) emphasize the broader
adoption of digital marketing and its contribution to customer value or business
performance. However, limited attention has been given to understanding digital
marketing as an integrated strategic practice in an Islamic banking context, particularly
by simultaneously examining its contribution to customer loyalty, the challenges
encountered during implementation, and the strategies that can be most effective in
strengthening loyalty. This gap is important because Islamic banks must reconcile
technological innovation and customer expectations with Sharia principles and the
specific characteristics of Islamic financial services (Fuady, 2025; Rianita & Fasa, 2025).
This study introduces an integrated perspective by examining digital marketing not
only as a means of promoting banking services but also as a strategic mechanism for
creating and sustaining customer loyalty while identifying implementation challenges and
effective strategic approaches in Islamic banking. The study aims to examine how digital
marketing contributes to enhancing customer loyalty, identify the challenges faced in its
implementation in Islamic banking, and determine the digital marketing strategies that
are most effective in creating customer loyalty. The findings are expected to contribute
theoretically by extending understanding of digital marketing and customer loyalty in
Islamic banking and practically by providing strategic insights for dev eloping more
effective, customer-oriented, and sustainable digital marketing practices.

LITERATURE REVIEW
Digital Marketing
Digitalization has transformed marketing practices from conventional, one -way
communication toward more integrated, interactive, and customer-centered approaches.
Apaza-Chullunquia et al. (2025) define digital marketing as the use of digital platforms
and technologies to reach customers, facilitate communication, and establish long-term
relationships through personalized and measurable interactions. The development of
social media, websites, mobile applications, data analytics, and artificial intelligence
enables organizations to identify customer needs, personalize marketing communication,
and respond more rapidly to changing market conditions (Shajrawi, 2025; Alwreikat &
Awamleh, 2026). Unlike traditional marketing, digital marketing facilitates real-time two-
way communication, allowing organizations to obtain customer feedback and develop
more targeted marketing strategies (Nuseir & El Refae, 2022 ; Rahayu, 2024).
Digitalization has therefore expanded the role of marketing beyond promotion toward

The strategic value of digital marketing is particularly evident in service industries,
including banking, where continuous customer interaction is essential. Digital channels
allow banks to communicate information efficiently, provide personalized services, and
strengthen customer engagement through accessible and responsive interactions. Based
on Al-Adwan et al. (2024) and Gargun (2025), data-driven digital marketing also enables

Sharia-oriented values. Digital marketing strategies should therefore not only emphasize

Islamic principles (Al-Adamat et al., 2023; Samudra & Tamamudin, 2025). Effective
digital marketing represents an integrated strategic process that connect s digital
technology, customer interaction, and value creation.

Customer Loyalty
Customer loyalty represents a customer’s commitment to continue using an
organization’s products or services, conduct repeat transactions, and recommend the
organization to others (Kim et al., 2024). Loyalty is strategically important in banking
because retaining existing customers contributes to long-term relationship development
and reduces dependence on continuous customer acquisition. Previous studies by Apaza-
Chullunquia et al. (2025) and Mahfouz et al. (2025) indicate that customer loyalty can be
strengthened through customer satisfaction, trust, engagement, service quality, and
positive customer experiences. In the digital environment, these dimensions increasingly
depend on the quality of interactions provided through digital channels. Personalized
communication, accessible services, relevant information, and responsive digital
experiences can strengthen customers’ perceptions of value and encourage continued
relationships with financial institutions (Mohammad, 2022; Harshini et al., 2024). Thus,
customer loyalty is increasingly understood as a multidimensional relationship outcome
rather than merely repeated purchasing behavior.
The relationship between digital marketing and customer loyalty becomes particularly
important in Islamic banking because customers evaluate financial institutions through
both functional and value-based considerations. Digital marketing can strengthen loyalty
by facilitating customer engagement, building trust, communicating relevant information,
and creating consistent experiences across digital touchpoints (Sang, 2023; Reyhanrafif et
al., 2024). In Islamic banking, however, loyalty may also depend on cus tomers’
perceptions of Sharia compliance, institutional credibility, and the consistency between
Islamic values and digital service delivery (Firdaus, 2024; Rabbani et al., 2026).
Therefore, creating strong customer loyalty in Islamic banking requires digital marketing
strategies that combine technological convenience, personalized communication, service
responsiveness, trust, and a consistent Sharia-oriented identity. This perspective positions
digital marketing not simply as a promotional instrument but as a strategic mechanism
for developing sustainable customer relationships.

RESEARCH METHODS
This study employs a Systematic Literature Review (SLR) approach to
comprehensively examine the utilization of digital marketing in enhancing customer
loyalty in Islamic banking. The SLR method was selected due to its ability to provide a
systematic, transparent, and structured synthesis of previous research findings, thereby
generating a deeper and evidence-based understanding. The SLR process in this study
follows the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta -Analyses
(PRISMA) guidelines, which consist of several key stages: identification, screening,
eligibility, and inclusion. The entire literature selection process was supported by the
Watase UAKE tool to improve the accuracy and efficiency of literature management.

During the identification stage, articles were retrieved from the Scopus database using
the keyword “digital marketing customer.” The initial search yielded 105 articles relevant
to the research topic. An initial screening process was then conducted to exclude articles
that did not meet the criteria, including five articles published outside the specified period
of 2016–2026 and 26 articles that were not classified within reputable journal categories
(Q1–Q4). At this stage, no duplicate records or articles without abstracts were identified.
Following this initial filtering, 74 articles proceeded to the screening stage. During this
phase, a more detailed assessment of topic relevance and content alignment with the
research focus was conducted, resulting in the exclusion of 52 articles that did not
specifically address the relationship between digital marketing and customer loyalty in a
relevant context.
In the eligibility stage, 22 articles were selected for full -text review. However, 15
articles were not retrievable and therefore could not be included in further analysis. As a
result, seven articles met all inclusion criteria and were deemed suitable for  analysis.
Additionally, this study incorporated three articles from other sources, which were also
evaluated and considered relevant. Thus, a total of 10 articles were included in the final
analysis. Data analysis was conducted using a qualitative approach through thematic
synthesis techniques, involving the identification, categorization, and interpretation of
key findings from the selected studies. This process aimed to generate a structured
understanding of how digital marketing is leveraged to enhance customer loyalty,
particularly within the context of Islamic banking. The literature identification, screening,

Contribution of Digital Marketing to Customer Loyalty
Digital marketing plays a crucial role in enhancing customer loyalty through its ability
to facilitate more interactive, personalized, and continuous communication between
banks and customers. Digitalization has transformed marketing practices from
conventional communication toward more integrated and customer-oriented approaches.
Through digital channels such as social media, websites, and mobile banking applications,
banks can deliver relevant information and respond to customer needs more quickly.
Digital marketing therefore functions not only as a promotional tool but also as a means
of building continuous customer relationships. Kobets et al. (2024) explain that
digitalization has transformed contemporary marketing strategies by expanding the use
of digital technologies to create more responsive customer interactions. In the banking

sector, this transformation is increasingly important because customers expect
convenient, accessible, and responsive services through digital channels.
Digital marketing also strengthens customer engagement by enabling two -way
communication. Customers can interact with banks, provide feedback, obtain
information, and access services without being restricted by conventional communication
channels. Tubalawony et al. (2024) demonstrate that social media marketing can
strengthen brand loyalty and intention to use digital banking services. Kamyabi et al.
(2025) similarly emphasize that sustainable digital marketing strategies can strengthen
customer engagement and brand promotion when digital communication is implemented
consistently. These findings indicate that the effectiveness of digital marketing depends
not only on the availability of digital platforms but also on the ability of banks to use these
platforms to create meaningful interactions with customers.
The utilization of digital technologies also enables faster, more convenient, and more
efficient service delivery, thereby increasing customer satisfaction, which serves as an
important foundation for loyalty formation. Nuseir (2016) shows that digital marketing
capabilities can support organizational performance through customer relationship
management. Al-Adwan et al. (2024) further highlight the importance of digital marketing
and electronic service quality in banking customer relationsh ips. This suggests that
customers evaluate digital marketing not only through promotional content but also
through the quality of the digital experience accompanying such communication. When
digital information is relevant and supported by convenient services, customers are more
likely to perceive greater value from their interactions with the bank.
Customer satisfaction and experience therefore represent important mechanisms
connecting digital marketing with customer loyalty. Apaza-Chullunquia et al. (2025)
identify satisfaction as an important element linking digital marketing strategies and
customer loyalty among bank customers. Harshini et al. (2024) also demonstrate the
importance of modernized digital marketing strategies in improving customer experience
toward customer loyalty. Kim et al. (2024) further explain that customer loyalty in
banking is influenced by multiple factors, including satisfaction, trust, and customer
experience. Accordingly, digital marketing contributes to loyalty when it consistently
creates positive experiences across different stages of the customer relationship.
Digital marketing also enables data-driven service personalization, allowing banks to
provide products, information, and communication that are more closely aligned with
customer needs. Alwreikat and Awamleh (2026) emphasize the role of data -driven
strategic orientation and digital marketing transformation in creating customer value. In
banking, the ability to understand customer preferences and behavior can make digital
communication more relevant and strengthen the perceived value of interactions.
Listiorini (2025) similarly highlights the role of digital marketing strategies in developing
customer loyalty in the digital economy. Thus, great customer loyalty is not formed solely
through repeated transactions but also through consistent, relevant, and value -driven
experiences throughout the customer journey.
Within Islamic banking, however, the contribution of digital marketing to customer
loyalty must also consider Sharia values. Customers may evaluate not only convenience
and service quality but also the transparency, ethical character, and Sharia compliance of
the bank. Syahfitri (2026) emphasizes the importance of Sharia marketing strategies in
enhancing customer loyalty in Islamic banks. Rabbani et al. (2026) also demonstrate the
relevance of digital marketing, Islamic financial literacy, and brand image to customer
loyalty at Indonesian Sharia Bank. Therefore, digital marketing in Islamic banking needs
to combine technological convenience with the institutional values that distinguish
Islamic financial services.

Challenges in Implementing Digital Marketing in Islamic Banking
Islamic banking faces several challenges in implementing digital marketing. One
primary challenge is the uneven level of digital literacy among customers. Differences in
digital capability can affect customers’ ability to access information, use mobile banking

digital literacy may experience difficulties in navigating digital platforms, which can
reduce the benefits expected from digital transformation. Therefore, digital marketing
strategies need to be accompanied by clear communication and customer education to
ensure that digital services remain accessible to different customer groups.
Another challenge concerns customer trust in digital services, particularly regarding
data security, privacy, transaction reliability, and information transparency. Banking
customers provide sensitive financial information and therefore require confidence that
their data and transactions are adequately protected. Judijanto et al. (2024) identify trust
as an important factor in customer loyalty within the banking industry, while Reyhanrafif
et al. (2024) highlight the role of digital marketing communication in strengthening trust
and loyalty in Indonesian banking. Consequently, digital marketing communication
needs to be supported by reliable services and transparent information. Attractive
promotional content alone cannot create sustainable loyalty when customers remain
uncertain about security and service reliability.
Trust also has a distinctive dimension in Islamic banking because it is closely related
to Sharia compliance and institutional credibility. Digital marketing activities must
therefore be transparent, ethical, and consistent with Islamic principles. Samudra and
Tamamudin (2025) highlight the trends and challenges associated with Sharia-compliant
digital marketing in Indonesia’s financial sector. This means that Islamic banking needs
to ensure that digital promotional messages, product information, and custom er
communication accurately represent the characteristics and principles of Islamic banking.
Any inconsistency between marketing communication and actual service practices could
weaken customer trust and negatively affect loyalty.
Competitive pressure represents another challenge. Conventional banks and other
financial institutions continue to develop digital services with increasingly sophisticated
technologies and user-oriented features. Islamic banks must therefore adapt to
technological developments while maintaining their distinctive Sharia identity. Ulfa and
Fasa (2024) and Valmai et al. (2026) emphasize the importance of adapting Islamic
banking marketing strategies to the digital era. The challenge is consequently not simply
to adopt new technologies but to integrate them into a value proposition that combines
convenience, efficiency, trust, and Sharia compliance.

Effective Digital Marketing Strategies for Creating Customer Loyalty
Islamic banking needs to implement integrated and sustainable digital marketing
strategies. One effective approach is to optimize social media and digital platforms as
channels for communication and customer education. Informative content concerning
Islamic financial products, digital banking procedures, security, and Sharia principles can
increase customer understanding and trust. Sang (2023) demonstrates that social media
marketing can contribute to brand loyalty in digital banking, while Mukhtar et al. (2023)
highlight the relationship between digital marketing, consumer behavior, and loyalty.
Digital content should therefore not focus exclusively on promotional messages but also
provide practical information that creates value for customers.
Islamic banking also needs to develop user-friendly and responsive digital services,
particularly mobile banking applications that are easy to use and equipped with features
relevant to customer needs. Ease of access and convenience contribute to positive
customer experiences and can support loyalty. Lauren and Handrian (2023) demonstrate
the relevance of digital banking services and service quality to customer loyalty. Digital
marketing and digital service delivery should therefore be managed as interconnected
components. Effective promotion may attract customers, but convenient and reliable
digital services are necessary to maintain their long-term relationship with the bank.
The use of artificial intelligence and data analytics can further support personalization
and responsiveness. AI-based technologies can help banks understand customer
preferences, provide relevant recommendations, and respond more quickly to customer
needs. Shajrawi (2025) highlights the mediating role of customer experience in the

Strengthening Islamic branding within digital marketing is another strategic priority.
Islamic branding should emphasize transparency, product compliance, ethical

digital services, customer relationship management, and Islamic values reinforce one
another. Through informative content, responsive digital services, data -driven
personalization, appropriate technological innovation, and consistent Islamic branding,
Islamic banking can strengthen long-term customer relationships and develop sustainable
customer loyalty.

DISCUSSION
The findings indicate that digital marketing has evolved into a strategic instrument for
building strong customer loyalty, particularly in the increasingly digitalized banking
industry. Digital marketing is no longer positioned merely as a promotional medium but
has become a mechanism for developing long-term relationships through personalized,
responsive, and continuous communication. Digital platforms such as social media,
websites, and mobile banking applications enable banks to provide relevant information,
facilitate two-way communication, and respond more quickly to customer needs. These
findings are consistent with Kobets et al. (2024), who highlight the transformation of
marketing strategies through digitalization, and Sang (2023), who demonstrates the role
of social media marketing in strengthening loyalty in digital banking. The findings further
indicate that digital marketing contributes to loyalty through customer engagement,
satisfaction, and digital experience. Apaza-Chullunquia et al. (2025) identify satisfaction
as an important element connecting digital marketing strategies with customer loyalty,
while Harshini et al. (2024) emphasize the contribution of modernized digital marketing
strategies to customer experience and loyalty. Thus, customer loyalty is not established
solely through repeated transactions but through consistent and value-driven experiences
across digital touchpoints.
Data-driven personalization further strengthens this relationship by enabling banks to
provide information and services that better correspond to customer needs. Alwreikat and
Awamleh (2026) emphasize the role of digital marketing transformation and data-driven
orientation in creating customer value, while Shajrawi (2025) highlights the importance
of AI-supported personalization and customer experience in strengthening brand loyalty.
Within Islamic banking, this relationship also depends on trust and Sharia  values.
Customers evaluate not only convenience and service quality but also transparency,
ethical communication, and Sharia compliance. Firdaus (2024) and Rabbani et al. (2026)

be optimized not only for promotion but also for customer communication and education
through informative content concerning Islamic financial products, digital services,
security, and Sharia principles. Sang (2023) and Mukhtar et al. (2023) support the

experience, personalization, trust, service quality, and Islamic values. Digital marketing
is therefore most effective when treated not as a standalone promotional activity but as
part of an integrated customer relationship strategy that creates sustainable value and
strengthens long-term competitiveness.

CONCLUSION
This study finds that digital marketing contributes to great customer loyalty in Islamic
banking by strengthening customer engagement, satisfaction, digital experience,
personalization, trust, and communication across digital touchpoints. The findings show
that social media, websites, mobile banking, data analytics, and artificial intelligence can
support more relevant, responsive, and continuous customer interactions. However,
implementation faces challenges related to uneven digital literacy, data securit y and
privacy, transaction reliability, information transparency, Sharia compliance, and
competitive pressure from conventional banks. The findings imply that Islamic banking
should treat digital marketing as an integrated strategic function rather than a standalone
promotional activity. Digital communication should be combined with customer
education, user-friendly digital services, responsible personalization, reliable servi ce
delivery, and consistent Islamic branding to create sustainable customer relationships and
strengthen competitiveness.
