 Abstract: This research is motivated by the increasing use
of digital distri bution channels and social media in the
development of culinary MSMEs, while Cireng Isi Gembul
still faces delivery constraints, limited platform reach, and
digital promotion optimization. This study aims to analyze
distribution constraints, the effectiven ess of distribution
channels, and the role of digital platforms in reaching the
target market. The study used a descriptive qualitative
approach. Six research informants, consisting of one
owner, two employees, and three customers, were selected
based on t heir involvement and experience in business
activities. The research instrument was an interview guide
with the researcher as the main instrument, supported by
observation and documentation. Data were analyzed
descriptively qualitatively using SWOT analysis and source
triangulation. The results showed that ShopeeFood,
GoFood, and GrabFood supported transactions and
product distribution, although obstacles remained in
communication, network connectivity, service radius, and
delivery delays. Based on the experiences and perceptions
of the informants, TikTok was perceived as the most
effective platform for reaching new consumers, while
Instagram primarily functioned as a product information
and promoti onal medium. ShopeeFood, GoFood, and
GrabFood functioned as transaction and distribution
channels. In conclusion, the SO strategy can be applied by
optimizing digital marketing, product innovation, and
distribution expansion.

Introduction
Indonesia's increasingly dynamic economic developments are encouraging Micro, Small, and
Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) to adapt to market changes, technological advancements, and consumer
needs. These conditions require businesses to develop appropriate business and marketing strategies to
maintain business continuity and increase competitiveness . Strategy is a long -term plan to achieve
specific goals, making marketing strategy a crucial component in designing various activities aimed at
achieving marketing ob jectives (Effendi et al., 2022: 52). In the culinary business context,

market reach and facilitate consumer product acquisition.
This phenomenon is also seen at Cireng Isi Gembul in Jagakarsa, South Jakarta, which is  a
home-based culinary business with cireng products with a distinctive taste and various  variants. This
business has utilized technological developments through the use of social media Instagram and TikTok
as digital marketing tools and ShopeeFood, GoFood , and GrabFood as distribution channels. The
implementation of this strategy has shown po sitive developments as reflected in the increase in sales
turnover during the period 2021 to 2025. Sales turnover increased from Rp50,985,500 in 2021 to
Rp121,662,500 in 2022, Rp268,720,000 in 2023, Rp398,504,000 in 2024, and reached Rp510,250,500
in 2025.  However, the achievement of this turnover has not fully reached the target each year, so
evaluation and optimization of the implemented strategy are still necessary. On the other hand, customer
reviews on ShopeeFood, GoFood, and GrabFood show a high level  of satisfaction with an average
rating of 4.8 to 5.0, especially regarding taste, product filling, texture, condition of the product upon
receipt, promotions, price, and delivery speed.
The first research problem relates to the distribution process, speci fically product delivery to
consumers. Although the use of ShopeeFood, GoFood, and GrabFood has facilitated the ordering and
delivery process, Cireng Isi Gembul still faces a dependency on app -based delivery services. This
condition can cause communication barriers between sellers, drivers, and customers, delays in delivery,
network or app disruptions when orders come in, and increased workloads when the number of orders
increases. Dependence on third parties also means that business owners lack full contro l over the
delivery process until the product is received by consumers. This is crucial because the quality of food
products can be affected by the delivery process and time.
The second issue relates to the effectiveness of distribution channels in reachin g consumers
broadly. Distribution channels are the means used to deliver products from producers to end users
through retailers or other intermediaries. In practice, distribution channels have a direct impact on sales
and therefore need to be managed effec tively and efficiently. At Cireng Isi Gembul, the use of
ShopeeFood, GoFood, and GrabFood has helped consumers make purchases without having to visit the
store and has contributed to increased transactions. However, distribution reach is still influenced b y
the service radius of each platform. Therefore, the increase in sales does not fully indicate optimal
market reach. This situation highlights the need to assess the effectiveness of the distribution channels
used to determine the extent to which they are able to reach consumers broadly.
The third problem relates to optimizing digital marketing to reach the target market. According
to Kingsnorth (2022), as cited by Denny Astanto et al. (2025), digital marketing is a strategic approach
that coordinates various digital channels to create a consistent and valuable customer experience, with
consumers at the center of digital marketing activities. Furthermore, digital market ing through social
media allows users to present themselves, interact, collaborate, share  information, and form virtual
relationships with other users (Chakti, 2022). Cireng Isi Gembul has utilized Instagram and TikTok in
its digital marketing activities, but its implementation still faces obstacles such as promotions that have
not been carried out optimally and consistently and social media content that is still less interesting and
varied. The results also show differences in function between platforms: T ikTok is more effective in
building brand awareness and reaching new target markets, Inst agram functions as a product
information medium, while ShopeeFood acts as a transaction channel. These conditions indicate that
each platform has a different function,  so it is necessary to determine the most effective platform for
reaching the target market according to business needs.
Based on these phenomena and problems, this study aims to determine the obstacles faced in
the distribution process, especially those related to product delivery, determine the effectiveness of the
distribution channels used by Cireng Isi Gembul in reaching consumers widely, and determine the most
effective digital platforms in reaching the target market. This research is important becaus e the right
distribution channel and digital marketing strategies are needed to optimize business operations,
increase distribution efficiency, expand marketing reach, and support the increase in competitiveness
of Cireng Isi Gembul in the digital era. The  novelty of this study lies in the integrated study of

Research methods
The research entitled "Digital Marketing and Distribution Channel Strategy for Cireng Isi Gembul
in Jagakarsa, South Jakarta" uses a descriptive qualitative approach to obtain a systematic, factual, and
in-depth picture of the phenomenon of digital marketing and distribution channel strategies on the
research object. A qualitative approach is used to understand phenomena b ased on the conditions,
experiences, and activities of the research subjects, not through  statistical calculations. According to
Sugiyono (2023), a qualitative approach focuses on an in -depth understanding of a phenomenon by
paying attention to the context and experiences of research participants. The research was conducted at
the Cireng Isi G embul shop located on Jl. Srengseng Sawah, RT 011/RW 09, Gg Buntu No. 33,
Jagakarsa District, South Jakarta, 12640, with a focus on activities related to digital marke ting and
distribution channel strategies.
The research instrument used interview guidelines with the researcher as the main instrument, and
was supported by observation and documentation to obtain data related to distribution channel strategies
and digital marketing. Distribution channel indicator s include transportation systems, product
availability, timeliness, and adequacy of quantity, while digital marketing indicators include the level
of attraction, optimization of curiosity, level of commitment, and level of affinity. Data collection was
carried out through observation, interviews, and d ocumentation, then analyzed descriptively
qualitatively through the compilation and interpretation of data in the form of words, images, interview
results, field notes, and documentation. Furthermore, the data  was analyzed using a SWOT analysis
that includes Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats to identify the internal and external
conditions of Cireng Isi Gembul and determine strategies that are appropriate to business conditions.
This qualitative research does not use a population and sample in the sense of quantitative research,
but uses units of analysis and research informants directly related to the research focus. The research
analysis unit includes subjects and activities related to the dist ribution channel strategy and digital
marketing at Cireng Isi Gembul in Jagakarsa, South Jakarta. The research informants numbered 6
people, consisting of Alda Alfionita Zachra as the business owner, Cucu Sumiati and Nuryati as
employees, and Sherly, Laras ati, and Kafka as customers. The selection of t hese informants was
adjusted to their involvement and experience in business activities, distribution processes, services, use
of digital platforms, and experience in obtaining Cireng Isi Gembul products so th at the information
obtained can support the research focus.
The research procedure was carried out in stages, starting from proposal preparation, proposal
submission, proposal seminar, proposal revision, guidance, instrument development, data collection,
data analysis, and the submission and implementation of the thesis defense. After the instruments were
developed, the researcher collected data through observation, interviews, and documentation of
activities related to the distribution channel strategy and digital marketing of Cireng Isi Gembul. Data
obtained from business owners, employees, and customers were then analyzed descriptively and
qualitatively based on the research focus, namely obstacles in the distribution process, especially
product delivery, the effectiveness of distribution channels in reaching consumers widely, and the most
effective digital platforms in reaching the target market. Data were then grouped based on Strengths,
Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats factors to be compiled into a SWOT matrix. While the validity
of the data was checked through source triangulation by comparing information from each informant
or research source (Sugiyono, 2021:369).

Research Discussion
1. Obstacles in the distribution process, especially product delivery
These obstacles indicate that Cireng Isi Gembul  is highly dependent on third parties, namely
applications and drivers, so that sellers do not have full control over product quality when it reaches
consumers. The longer the delivery distance, the greater the risk of product quality decline and customer
dissatisfaction. This condition is in line with the findings of Arief P. Kumaat et al. (2024) who
emphasized that the success of distribution channels is largely determined by the management skills and
distribution te chniques of business actors, so technic al and communication obstacles need to be
overcome through clearer operational standards. The study assesses that this obstacle is a normal risk
that occurs in culinary businesses that rely on third -party application-based distribution, so proactive
communication with consumers and more systematic order management are needed to minimize its
impact.

Conclusion
Based on the research results, the digital distribution and marketing channel strategies of Cireng
Isi Gembul in Jagakarsa, South Jakarta have contributed to increased sales and expanded market reach.
Distribution chan nels through ShopeeFood, Go Food, and GrabFood are considered quite effective
because they provide convenience for transactions and delivery to consumers, although they still face
obstacles such as dependence on drivers, limited service radius, network disr uptions, and the risk of
changes in product quality during delivery. From a digital marketing perspective, TikTok is the most
effective platform in reaching new consumers and increasing brand awareness, while Instagram
functions more as a product informati on medium and a delivery se rvice platform for transactions. A
SWOT analysis shows that Cireng Isi Gembul has strengths in the form of distinctive flavors, product
variety, affordable prices, loyal customers, and the use of various digital platforms, with the SO strategy
being the most appropriate strategy to capitalize on market opportunities.

not quantitatively measure the contribution of each platform to sales. Future research is recommended

mixed-methods approach to measure the effectiveness of each distribution channel and digital platform
based on sales indicators, consumer reach, interaction, and conversion rates. Practically, Cireng Isi
