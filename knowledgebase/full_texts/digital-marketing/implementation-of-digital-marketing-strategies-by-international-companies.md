Abstract. The author analyzes the theoretical and practical aspects of implementing digital marketing
strategies by international companies. A comparative analysis of the world's largest non -alcoholic
beverage manufacturers – The Coca-Cola Company and PepsiCo – is conducted. It is demonstrated that
modern international companies diversify their digital strategy types and develop unique hybrid models.
Keywords: marketing strategy, MNCs (multinationals), digital marketing, emotional branding, data-
driven marketing.

The majority of prominent international companies utilize digital marketing
strategies as a primary lever for globalization. Based on practical analysis, experts from
Southern New Hampshire University (SNHU) identify eight core types of digital
marketing strategies [1]. At the same time, comparing the theoretical classification of
digital marketing strategies with the actual practices of international companies
indicates that no multinational corporation (MNC) relies on a single type; instead, they
combine and customize them.
In the context of analyzing digital marketing strategies, it is appropriate to
examine companies operating within the same industry. For instance, let us consider
the non-alcoholic beverage giants, The Coca-Cola Company and PepsiCo.
In the 2000s, the international enterprise The Coca -Cola Company adopted a
strategy based on the " One Brand" principle. The core idea behind this strategy is to
promote all the company's products under the single corporate Coca-Cola brand as part
of the creative marketing campaign "Taste the Feeling" [2].
Evolving from traditional to digital advertising, the company preserves the
foundational concept of its marketing strategy: focusing on the long-term strategic goal
of strengthening brand equity by building universal psychological and emotional
connections on a global scale. The entirety of The Coca -Cola Company's marketing
strategy is rooted in the concept of Emotional Branding, the activation of everyday
consumer behavior patterns, the intensification of target audience engagement through
digital communication channels, and the enhancement of brand loyalty by integrating
the principles of Sustainable Development [3; 4].
Concurrently, it is worth noting that the operational execution of marketing
activities is being modernized through Big Data analytics, the deployment of Artificial

Intelligence (AI) tools, and a differentiated approach to local creative content
(Glocalization). The channels for marketing strategy promotion have also shifted. Over
the past two years, advertising content has been distributed via social media platforms,
including TikTok, X (T witter), and Instagram. Such advertising serves as a prime
example of Social Media Marketing (SMM) strategy implementation.
The company also incorporates elements of Influencer Marketing by publishing
video content and initiating themed contests that invite young consumers to upload
creative short videos or images related to Coca -Cola. This strategy helps optimize
advertising costs and deepens the emotional bond between the brand and its consumers
through online interaction, thereby fostering the loyalty of existing users while
simultaneously attracting new customers.
As part of its Influencer Marketing execution, the company also collaborates with
other brands, such as PopMart and Labubu, which have enjoyed viral popularity and
powerful digital advertising. Coca-Cola partnered with them to launch limited-edition toys,
successfully capturing younger consumer segments [5]. This business-to-business (B2B)
co-branding has generated yet another unprecedentedly brilliant advertising approach
A different methodology is maintained by PepsiCo, which actively employs
digital marketing to enhance consumer loyalty, personalize communications, and drive
e-commerce development. Its digital marketing strategy is fundamentally anchored in
direct communication with consumers.
PepsiCo's digital marketing functions as a multi-component system – a unique hybrid of:
- content marketing: the Gatorade brand creates video series featuring athlete training
sessions, motivating the audience to associate its products with an active lifestyle;
- SMM: during major sporting events, the company launches interactive videos,
contests, and flashmobs on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube;
- data-driven marketing: consumer preferences, primary distribution channels,
and marketing priorities are developed based on massive datasets gathered from social
networks, mobile applications, and online stores;
- mobile marketing: the smartphone serves as the primary hub for communicating
with the enterprise;
- influencer marketing: musicians, athletes, TikTok bloggers, and streamers are
engaged to promote products;
- omni-channel marketing: seamlessly integrating customer interaction channels
(e.g., a digital video advertisement includes a clickable link or a QR code to obtain a
discount coupon that can be instantly redeemed at a physical retail store);
- e-commerce marketing: driving sales through Amazon, Walmart, Carrefour, and
other global marketplaces [6; 7; 8; 9; 10].

The defining characteristic of the company is its definitive transition from mass
marketing to highly personalized, one-on-one consumer interaction.
Conclusions. An analysis of the digital marketing strategy execution by the
world's largest non-alcoholic beverage manufacturers demonstrates that marketing
effectiveness is determined not by the selection of a specific tool, but by the
management's Dynamic Capabilities to balance the deep-seated mental and emotional
demands of the target audience with the end-to-end digitalization of business processes.
A comparative analysis of the digital marketing strategies of these international
producers highlights the efficiency of designing unique, customized marketing
frameworks that synthesize all established strategic types.
