Abstract: Digital marketing allows social enterprises to expand market reach, yet strong online
interaction does not always lead to actual purchases. This study examines the gap between
advertising clicks and purchase conversion on the BERIDAMPAK platform managed by
Yayasan Ciptakarya Baik Indonesia. The objectives are to analyze the implementation of
digital marketing, identify barriers to sales conversion, and formulate improvement strategies.
A descriptive qualitative approach was applied through observation, inte rviews,
documentation, and analysis of sales-forecasting and audience-interaction data. The findings
show that social media, digital advertising, and Meta Ads improve product exposure, audience
reach, interaction, and access to product-trend information. However, sales performance
remains suboptimal because product information is not sufficiently persuasive, audience
targeting is inaccurate, prices do not always match perceived value, promotional displays are
less attractive, and pre-order duration is too long. The novelty lies in identifying the click-to-
purchase conversion gap and integrating content quality, targeting accuracy, pricing,
promotional appeal, and pre-order duration as practical determinants of sales conversion.

Keywords: Digital Advertising, Digital Marketing, Meta Ads, Product Sales, Social Media

INTRODUCTION
The development of the internet has changed communication patterns, information -
seeking behavior, and consumer decision-making, while also shifting marketing strategies from
conventional approaches toward digital channels. For organizations that sell products through
online platforms, digital marketing is not merely a promotional tool; it also enables interaction,
product-information delivery, audience targeting, and the movement of prospective customers
toward purchase decisions (Suharto & Suhendri, 2022; Santoso, 2020). BERIDAMPAK is a
social-enterprise platform developed by Yayasan Ciptakarya Baik Indonesia that combines
social initiatives with e-commerce activities. In marketing its products, the platform uses social
media, digital advertising, and Meta Ads. These channels provide opportunities to reach wider
audiences, establish two-way communication, and obtain information about audience
responses and product trends. Digital marketing can also improve promotional efficiency when

However, the effectiveness of digital marketing cannot be assessed solely from
impressions, interactions, or clicks. Sales growth occurs only when audience interest is
converted into transactions. BERIDAMPAK sales-forecasting data show an upward pattern
that remains inconsistent. In November, for example, the recorded increase was only
approximately 12.5% compared with the preceding period. This condition indicates a gap
between initial audience interest and realized sales. The research gap lies in the limited
understanding of why digital interactions do not consistently progress to purchases in the
specific context of the BERIDAMPAK social-enterprise platform. Previous studies have
emphasized the benefits of digital marketing for market expansion and sales improvement,
whereas this study focuses specifically on the conversion point from click to transaction by
examining audience-targeting accuracy, product-information quality, price, promotional
presentation, cost efficiency, and pre-order duration (Gumilang, 2019; Syukri & Sunrawali,
2022; Arfan & Hasan, 2022).
The state of the art of this study is an integrated analysis of the click -to-purchase
conversion gap on BERIDAMPAK rather than a simple evaluation of social-media and Meta
Ads usage. The study positions content quality, targeting accuracy, pricing, promoti onal
appeal, and the pre-order process as interconnected factors that determine whether digital
interaction can be transformed into sales.  Accordingly, this study aims to analyze the
implementation of digital marketing on the BERIDAMPAK platform in increasing product
sales, identify the factors that prevent advertising interactions or clicks from progressing to
purchases, and formulate marketing improvements that are more accurately targeted, attractive,
efficient, and conversion-oriented.

METHODS
This study used a descriptive qualitative design to provide an in-depth description of
digital marketing implementation in increasing product sales on the BERIDAMPAK platform.
The approach did not focus on statistical hypothesis testing, but rather on understanding social-
media strategies, digital advertising, Meta Ads, and the factors influencing sales conversion.
The research object was the digital marketing activity for products marketed through
BERIDAMPAK, which is managed by Yayasan Ciptakarya Baik Indonesia. The analytical
focus covered interactivity, informativeness, personalization, product -trend monitoring,
pricing accuracy, marketing-cost efficiency, and sales improvement. Data were collected
through observation, interviews, documentation, and analysis of sales data. Observation
covered social-media content, published advertisements, the use of Meta Ads, and audience
responses. Interviews were used to obtain informants' expl anations of digital marketing
practices. Documentation included sales-forecasting data, product-promotion information, and
audience-interaction data such as advertising clicks. Data were analyzed descriptively by
organizing findings from observations, interviews, documentation, and sales information to
identify patterns of digital marketing implementation, conversion barriers, and possible
strategic improvements. Findings from the different sources were compared to obtain a
coherent description of the relationship between digital marketing activities and sales
conditions.

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
1.  Digital Marketing Implementation on the BERIDAMPAK Platform
The observations, documentation, interviews, and sales -data analysis show that
BERIDAMPAK implements digital marketing through social media, digital advertising, and
Meta Ads. Social media functions as the main channel for communicating product information,
promotions, and offers, while also enabling interaction through comments and direct messages.
Digital advertising is used to broaden reach and monitor audience responses, whereas Meta
Ads supports targeting based on selected audience characteristics.
Research informants explained that digital media are used because they are considered
more effective than conventional marketing for reaching prospective customers. Digital
interaction also accelerates information delivery and responses to customer inquiries. However,
the click data indicate that initial interest does not always result in transactions, meaning that
performance assessment should focus on conversion rather than engagement alone. Digital
marketing provides four main operational benefits: interactivity, faster information delivery,
personalization, and product-trend monitoring. These benefits can help the organization
understand audience interest and design more relevant communicati on. This finding is
consistent with the view that digital media can expand market reach and strengthen customer
relationships (Gumilang, 2019; Syukri & Sunrawali, 2022).

2.  Barriers to Converting Digital Interaction into Sales
The findings indicate that digital marketing has not yet optimized sales performance.
Advertising can generate clicks, but not every click develops into a purchase. The identified
barriers include product information that is not sufficiently persuasive, inaccurate audience
targeting, selling prices that may not match perceived consumer value, less attractive
promotional displays, and excessively long pre-order periods.
Pricing accuracy is important because customers compare price with expected benefits,
quality, and purchasing power. When perceived value is not proportional to price, prospective
customers may postpone or cancel a purchase. Marketing-cost efficiency should therefore also
be assessed in relation to sales outcomes. High advertising costs without adequate conversion
indicate the need to evaluate content, targeting, and promotional offers. The previous pre-order
period of up to one month may reduce purchasing urgency and increase the risk that interested
customers abandon their intention to buy. The source manuscript recommends shortening the
pre-order period to approximately three to seven days and providing a clear delivery estimate.

3.  Improvement Strategies to Increase Sales Conversion
Strategic improvement should prioritize conversion rate rather than clicks alone. The
organization can compare the number of advertising clicks, product-page visits, orders, and
completed transactions to identify where potential customers are lost. A/B testing of images,
videos, headlines, prices, offers, and calls to action can help determine which promotional
material produces stronger responses and better conversion.
Product information should also be strengthened by clearly presenting product benefits,
quality, prices, testimonials, authentic photographs, the social impact of purchases, and
straightforward ordering procedures. Meta Ads targeting should be aligned with prospective
customer characteristics so that advertising expenditure becomes more efficient and campaigns
do not stop at engagement without transactions. The findings emphasize that digital marketing
is not merely a content-publication activity. Its effectiveness depends on the interaction
between content quality, targeting accuracy, price, promotion, purchasing convenience, and
order-fulfillment speed. Accordingly, digital marketing evaluation should combine interaction
data with sales data.

4.  Practical Implications for Digital Marketing Performance Measurement
The findings suggest that BERIDAMPAK should shift digital marketing evaluation from
attention metrics toward transaction metrics. Clicks remain useful as an indicator of initial
interest, but they are insufficient for determining marketing success unless f ollowed by
product-page visits, orders, and completed transactions. Performance evaluation should
therefore map the stages from advertisement exposure, click, product consideration, order
creation, and transaction completion. Conversion-rate measurement can show the proportion
of audiences who actually purchase after interacting with advertising. In this study, this need
arises because clicks were observed without a proportional increase in sales. Linking
interaction and transaction data would help distinguish whether the main problem occurs in
targeting, content quality, product trust, pricing, or the ordering process.
Promotional-cost evaluation is also necessary to assess efficiency. Advertising
expenditure should be compared with sales results so that the organization can determine
whether promotional spending produces adequate returns. The source manuscript recommends
adding indicators such as click-through rate, conversion rate, and return on advertising spend
in future research because these measures can provide a more quantified view of digital
marketing effectiveness. In content management, A/B testing can be applied to images, videos,
headlines, price offers, and purchase calls to action. This approach allows the organization to
compare alternative materials and retain the version that generates stronger responses and
conversion. It can also help determine whether messages about product benefits and the social
impact of purchasing are persuasive enough.
The purchasing experience should be treated as part of conversion strategy. A long pre-
order period can reduce urgency and increase the likelihood that prospective customers cancel
their intent to buy. Shorter pre-order periods, transparent delivery estimates, authentic product
images, testimonials, benefits, prices, and ordering procedures should therefore be integrated
into the conversion strategy. The novelty of this study is the identification of a conversion gap
rather than an exposure problem. The study integrates five practical determinants - product-
information quality, audience-targeting accuracy, price, promotional appeal, and pre-order
duration - as simultaneous intervention points that must be managed if BERIDAMPAK is to
translate digital interaction into more tangible sales improvement.

CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
Conclusion
Digital marketing on the BERIDAMPAK platform through social media, digital
advertising, and Meta Ads has supported product introduction, wider audience reach, stronger
interaction, faster information delivery, and product -trend monitoring. However, sales
improvement remains suboptimal because interactions and clicks are not consistently
converted into transactions. The main barriers are insufficiently persuasive product
information, inaccurate audience targeting, prices that may not fit consumer perceptions, less
attractive promotional presentations, and excessively long pre-order periods. Therefore, digital
marketing effectiveness should be evaluated on the basis of sales conversion and supported by
improvements in content, targeting, pricing, promotion, and the purchasing process.

Limitations
This study used a descriptive qualitative approach and focused on a single platform, so
the findings cannot yet be generalized to all social enterprises or small and medium -sized
businesses. The available quantitative information consisted mainly of sales forecasts and
interaction data, while actual sales, advertising expenditure, click-through rate, conversion rate,
and return on advertising spend were not comprehensively available. Consequently, the

relationship between digital marketing and sales improvement cannot be interpreted as causal
or statistically inferential.

Recommendations
BERIDAMPAK is advised to routinely monitor conversion rate, conduct A/B testing for
Meta Ads content, strengthen product information, refine audience targeting, evaluate price
suitability, and shorten the pre-order period to approximately three to seven days with clear
delivery estimates. Future studies should incorporate actual sales, advertising costs, click -
through rate, conversion rate, and return on advertising spend, and may use quantitative or
mixed-method designs to test strategy effectiveness more rigorously.
