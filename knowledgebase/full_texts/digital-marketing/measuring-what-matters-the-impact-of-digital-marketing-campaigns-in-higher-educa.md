Abstract

This research investigates the strategic evolution of the global education sector, with a particular
emphasis on the Philippines, where Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) are increasingly
embracing commercial and marketing strategies. This transformation is driven by heightened
competition and the urgent necessity for HEIs to improve their visibility, branding, and institutional
reputation in the digital age. While digital and cyberspace marketing allow institutions like National
University-Dasmariñas to expand their outreach, there is a notable lack of research focusing on the
specific local challenges faced by Philippine HEIs in utilizing digital marketing to connect with
prospective students and their parents. Guided by Facet Theory, this qualitative study seeks to
cultivate a localized and detailed understanding of digital marketing practices within the Philippine
context. Its primary objectives are threefold: to explore the digital marketing tools and strategies
employed by HEIs such as National University -Dasmariñas; to evaluate their effectiveness in
attracting and retaining parents by analyzing their influence on decision-making processes; and to
assess parents’ perceptions of these digital initiatives, including their views on credibility and
usefulness. Through purposive sampling, the research conducts semi -structured, in -depth
interviews with prospective parents at National University -Dasmariñas to uncover barriers and
opportunities in the implementation of effective digital marketing strategies. The findings of this
study provide actionable recommendations to enhance digital marketing frameworks for HEIs,
thereby improving institutional outreach and competitive positioning in the Philippines. These
insights contribute to the advancement of Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) 4 Quality
Education and SDG 17 Partnerships for the Goals, by promoting more effective engagement
between educational institutions and their key stakeholders.

Introduction

The global education sector, including the Philippines, is undergoing a significant transformation a
itincreasingly incorporates commercial and marketing practices in response to rising competition and evolving
expectations from students and parents. Educational institutions now face challenges s uch as fluctuating
demand, intense global competition, and reductions in financial aid, prompting a strategic shift from relying
solely on academic excellence to actively employing marketing strategies to enhance visibility and appeal to
prospective studen ts and stakeholders. This shift emphasizes branding, institutional image, and reputation
building, alongside effective communication and outreach efforts, treating students and parents as informed
consumers who make deliberate, data -informed choices (Daoud , M. K., et al., 2023; Calma, A., & Dickson -
Deane, C., 2020).

Rationale and Motivation
The exponential marketing approaches primarily leveraging cyberspace and digital media -have enabled
institutions, such as National University Dasmariñas, to reach and interact with their audiences more effectively,
demonstrating benefits and adapting to the dynamic demands of informed students and parents (Nwankwo, S.,
& Gbadamosi, A., 2021; Ghosal, I., Prasad, B., & Kaur, K., 2020). Analyzing this shift is highly releva nt in
light of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), especially SDG 4 (Quality Education) and SDG 17
(Partnerships for the Goals), to ensure education systems remain responsive, inclusive, and sustainable in an
era of digital transformation (Durrani, N. et al., 2023). This study contends that understanding and responding
to the challenges and opportunities presented by commercial and exponential marketing in higher education is
critical for Philippine institutions to remain competitive, build effective bran ding, and improve stakeholder
engagement in a rapidly evolving, technology-driven educational landscape.

Purpose and Problem Statement
Therefore, the primary purpose of this study is to provide insight and localized understanding of the impact of
commercial and exponential marketing practices on Philippine higher education institutions (O'Brien, C., 2025).
Using qualitative methods, the research seeks to investigate parent behavior and institutional initiatives within
this competitive, technology -driven context, aiming to help HEIs refine their marketing strategies for more
effective recruitment and retention (Ijaz, M., & Khan, S., 2021; Harbi, A. M., & Ali, M. M., 2022; Uddin, M.,
& Islam, M. R., 2022).

Literature Review

The Strategic Shift in Higher Education Marketing
The global education sector is undergoing a significant transformation, increasingly incorporating commercial
and marketing practices in response to rising competition and evolving stakeholder expectations (Calma, A., &
Dickson-Deane, C., 2020). Historically, institutions relied primarily on academi c excellence, but they now face
challenges such as fluctuating demand and intense global competition, prompting a shift toward active branding
and reputation building. This evolution treats students and parents as informed consumers who make deliberate,
data-informed choices regarding their education (Hackett, P., (2022); Nwankwo, S., & Gbadamosi, A. (2021);
Ijaz, M., & Khan, S. (2021).

The Critical Role of Digital Platforms and Branding
Digital marketing transforms the education sector by expanding access and improving brand visibility. Research
identifies social media platforms and institutional websites as the most critical channels for information
dissemination and interaction (Pawar, 2024) . These digital tools are essential for shaping student satisfa ction,
managing institutional image, and supporting recruitment and retention efforts. Effective digital marketing
significantly influences the overall “student experience” by enhancing engagement and institutional reputation
(Benchekroun et al., (2024); Mai To et al. (2022); Ijaz & Khan (2021).

Influence on Engagement and Enrollment Decisions
Digital marketing is increasingly viewed as the primary and most effective way to attract and influence students,
often surpassing traditional marketing methods in i t is ability to capture the attention of high -quality applicants.
Studies show that institutions utilizing effective digital strategies report significantly higher engagement and
enrollment rates. These strategies are most effective when they target the en tire enrollment journey, influencing
the decision-making process from the initial stage of awareness to the final point of enrollment (Ijaz & Khan
(2021); Nwankwo & Gbadamosi (2021); Rajkumar et al. (2022); Kusumawati (2018).

Trust, Credibility, and Parental Perceptions
For marketing initiatives to be successful, they must prioritize credibility and transparency. Prospective parents
and students judge digital content based on its perceived usefulness and the trustworthiness of the institution.
Innovations in digital marketing, such as tailored platforms, multimedia content, and interactive sessions, foster
stronger institutional connections and provide a competitive advantage. Conversely, a lack of transparency or
irrelevant content can serve as a barrier to engagement (Edwards, A. L., & Omilion-Hodges, L. M. (2022); Calma,
A., & Dickson-Deane, C. (2020); Mai To, A., Mindzak, M., Thongpapanl, N., & Mindzak, J. (2022).

Strategic Frameworks and Localized Challenges
The use of comprehensive frameworks, such as Facet Theory, allows HEIs to structure complex social phenomena
into defined components like stakeholders, digital dimensions, and outcome measures. In the Philippine context,
HEIs face specific challenges, including the need for ongoing technological adaptation and the transfer of digital
marketing knowledge to staff (Valdiviezo Sir et al. (2024). To remain competitive, institutions must align their
digital strategies with overall institutional goals and utilize advanced analytics to track efficiency and return on
investment (ROI) (Hackett, P. (2022); Valdiviezo Sir et al. (2024); Bhati, P., & Mangal, M. (2025); Kumar, P.,
& Modi, R. K. (2025).

Methodology

This study adopted a qualitative research design, which is suitable for exploring and understand ing complex
phenomena in depth. Qualitative methods allow for a nuanced investigation of “how” universities like National
University Dasmariñas react and how digital marketing impacts parents' perceptions and choices

The research was conducted at the Nati onal University Dasmariñas. The population for the study comprised
prospective parents whose children are entering the School of Tourism Management as students. Focusing on
parents provides insights into their digital habits and decision -making. A purposiv e sampling technique was
employed to select participants who can provide meaningful insights into the effectiveness of digital marketing.
This included ten (10) parents whose children have gone through the enrollment process and enrolled at the
institution

The primary tool for collecting data was semi-structured, in-depth interviews. This technique allows for exploring
perceptions and views in depth, revealing conceptions about a research area and providing open access to
participants' thoughts and feeling. Questions were open-ended, flexible, and relevant to participants' experiences.
Interviews were conducted with the identified prospective parents to align with the study's primary objectives and
population focus.

Theme 2: Effectiveness in Driving Engagement, Inquiry, Application, and Enrollment

Theme 3: Student/Parent Perceptions, Behaviors, Trust, and Engagement

highlights the strategic impact of digital marketing on Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) in the Philippines:
The study concludes that digital marketing has become a strategic necessity for Philippine HEIs to remain
competitive in a techno logy-driven educational landscape. As parents and students transition into informed
consumers making data -driven choices, institutions must shift from a sole reliance on academic excellence to
actively managing their institutional image and branding through digital channels.

The key findings leading to this conclusion include:

1. Platform Effectiveness: Social media and institutional websites are identified as the most critical channels for
information dissemination, interaction, and shaping the overall student experience. These platforms are essential
for expanding institutional reach and improving brand visibility.Superiority Over Traditional Methods: Digital

their competitiveness in an increasingly global and technology-driven educational landscape.
