ABSTRACT
This study analyzes strategies for optimizing the YouTube
channel as a digital marketing medium at PT. Tourezia  Cakra
Inspira. The study was motivated by the fact that the utilization
of the YouTube channel has not yet been optimized. YouTube
is currently used only as a digital portfolio archive for the
company, resulting in low view counts and audience
engagement. This study aims to identify the current utilization
of the YouTube channel, understand the obstacles and
challenges faced, and discover strategies for optimizing video
content so that this channel can be transformed into a digital
marketing channel. This study employs a qualitative approach
using a descriptive method. Data collection was conducted
through observation and in -depth interviews with the Digital
Marketing Division and Creative Design Division. The results of
the study indicate that the obstacles faced include a lack of
keyword research, high video content production costs, and an
inconsistent upload schedule.

INTRODUCTION
According to Kingsnorth (2022), digital marketing is the strategic application of digital
technology and media integrated with traditional communication methods to achieve marketing
and business objectives (Kingsnorth, 2022). This involves managing a company's online presence,
such as through websites, mobile apps, and social media, in line with interactive digital
communication techniques (Ye et al., 2024) . To support this marketing effectiveness, companies
utilize various digital platforms as intermediaries directly with consumers  (Fattah et al., 2025) .
Some examples of commonly used platforms include Facebook, Google, Instagram, TikTok, and
YouTube (Parker et al., 2024).
Social media has now become a key pillar of digital marketing. According to Chaffey & Ellis
Chadwick (2019), social media is a category of digital media that facilitates two -way interaction
and audience participation through content creation and real -time information exchange
(Chaffey & Ellis -Chadwick, 2019). This means that a social media presence can provide a space

Among these digital platforms, YouTube serves as an effective marketing medium due to

In practice, various digital platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube
have been utilized by businesses to support digital marketing activities  (Purnama et al., 2026) .
PT. Tourezia Cakra Inspira, a tourism company, has used YouTube as a platform to store video
documentation of client activities (portfolios). These videos serve as a form of accountability to
clients after the event, not specifically produced for marketing content. Therefore, YouTube is
limited to serving as a portfolio archive, not as marketing content that provides reviews and
experiences.
Indications of this suboptimal YouTube function were evident during the author's
internship at PT. Tourezia  Cakra Inspira and his involvement in the Digital Marketing Division,
particularly while serving as the person in charge (PIC) of YouTube for a month. The author
realized that PT. Tourezia Cakra Inspira's YouTube channel was not being utilized optimally.
Ideally, YouTube serves as a marketing medium that can attract audiences or potential clients
through video content uploaded to YouTube. In reality, PT. Tourezia  Cakra Inspira's YouTube
channel only functions as an archive of the company's digital portfolio, resulting in PT. Tourezia
Cakra Inspira has experienced stagnation, which is evident from the low number of views and
minimal audience interaction on the majority of uploaded videos.
 Based on field findings, three main factors were identified that contributed to the
suboptimal performance of PT. Tourezia  Cakra Inspira's YouTube channel. First, a lack of
keyword research relevant to audience search intent, making the company's video content
difficult to find in search engines. Second, the high cost of video content production hindered PT.
Tourezia Cakra Inspira in designing marketing video content. Third, an inconsistent content
upload schedule limited audience exposure, making it difficult for the YouTube channel to reach
a wider audience.
However, optimizing the company's YouTube channel is feasible due to its potential for
development. This is supported by research by Koponen et al. (2024) which found that social
presence through video is important because it has been shown to be associated with various
positive communication outcomes, such as attraction and persuasion  (Koponen et al., 2024) .
According to Salonen et al. (2024), digital content marketing is a "pull" strategy, where companies
provide valuable content to solve customer problems, rather than simply promoting company
offerings (Salonen et al., 2024).
There is a gap between YouTube's role as a marketing platform and the reality of its
implementation at PT. Tourezia  Cakra Inspira is the reason the author is researching this topic.
As a digital marketing platform, YouTube video content is designed with an audience orientation.
This approach indicates that a YouTube channel is not simply a place to upload raw
documentation, but rather a medium for presenting videos with educational, informative, and

LITERATURE REVIEW
Uses and Gratifications Theory (UGT) emphasizes that audiences are active in selecting and
using media to meet various needs such as information, entertainment, self -identity, and social
interaction (Katz et al., 1973) . In the context of digital media like YouTube, audiences have full
control over content consumption, so the success of a video is largely determined by its ability to
meet cognitive, affective, personal integration, social integration, and escapism needs. The
application of this theory in research shows that the low performance of PT. Tourezia  Cakra
Inspira's YouTube channel is caused by content that is still focused on internal documentation
and is not oriented to audience needs, resulting in less interest and minimal interaction.
Therefore, channel optimization needs to be carried out with an audience -based approach
through understanding preferences, creating relevant and interesting content, and consistent
uploads to increase engagement and make YouTube an effective marketing medium.
Digital marketing is the application of digital media, data, and technology integrated with
traditional communications to achieve marketing objectives (Chaffey & Ellis-Chadwick, 2019), and
involves data processing, ethical considerations, and the use of various channels such as social
media and search engines to reach a broad and efficient audience. This strategy is used in
various sectors, including B2B, which demands accuracy, scalability, and personalized services
due to more complex decision -making processes. Furthermore, digital marketing also plays a
crucial role in building and maintaining an organization's reputation, where consistent
innovation can strengthen a company's image,  while a lack of initiative can damage it (Kumar et
al., 2022) . In the context of this research, optimizing PT. Tourezia Cakra Inspira's YouTube
channel as a B2B company is a strategic step to transform the platform's function from a passive
portfolio to an active marketing medium capable of building professionalism and trust with
potential clients.
According to Chaffey & Ellis -Chadwick (2019), digital platforms act as key intermediaries
that bridge interactions between companies and consumers, particularly through large
ecosystems such as FAMGA, which control digital infrastructure and influence the effectiveness
of marketing communications  (Chaffey & Ellis -Chadwick, 2019). This is in line with Kumar et al.
(2022), who stated that digital platforms also function as transactional networks that increase
cost efficiency and support the management of long -term relationships with business partners
(Kumar et al., 2022) . In the context of this research, YouTube, as part of the Google ecosystem,
plays a strategic role as a visual communication medium for companies. Therefore, optimizing
YouTube is not limited to just uploading videos, but also utilizing digital platforms to increase
marketing effectiveness, so that it can become a medium capable of reaching a broad audience
and strengthening business relationships.
According to Salonen et al. (2024), digital content marketing represents a shift from a
direct sales approach to one focused on helping, providing insight into the customer decision -
making process so that companies can present relevant and solution -oriented content (Salonen
et al., 2024) . This approach emphasizes that marketing is no longer focused on the product

METHODS
This research uses a qualitative approach with a descriptive method  (Sugiyono, 2023). The
unit of analysis is divided into research subjects and objects. The research subjects involved
internal parties of PT Tourezia Cakra Inspira who are authorized and directly involved in
managing digital media, especially the YouTube channel, namely the Digital Marketing Division
(Haris Aji Hogantoro) and the Creative Design Division (Ahmad Fathur Rohman). Meanwhile, the
research object focuses on the company's YouTube channel as the digital unit being analyzed.
Primary data collection was conducted thr ough observation and interviews, while secondary
data was obtained from analytical documentation of the @tourezia.media  YouTube channel,
which includes graphs of the number of views, watch time, and audience interaction before the
implementation of the optimization strategy.
In this qualitative research, validity testing was conducted using five techniques: data
triangulation, peer debriefing, researcher self -reflection (clarifying bias), detailed procedural
recording, and transcript checking  . The data analysis technique used was qualitative descriptive
analysis, which was conducted through several stages, including data reduction, data
presentation, and conclusion drawing (Creswell & Creswell, 2022).

RESULTS
Data Description
Based on data from the sources, the author conducted in -depth interviews with two
parties responsible for managing PT Tourezia Cakra Inspira's video content: Haris Aji Hogantoro,
Digital Marketing Executive, and Ahmad Fathur Rohman, Creative Design Executive.
The interview with Haris Aji Hogantoro  took place in a comfortable office meeting room
and lasted 30–60 minutes. The interview revealed that YouTube is viewed as a strategic platform
for building long-term branding through the company's video portfolio. He also emphasized the
importance of implementing SEO and keyword research, as well as utilizing YouTube Shorts to
increase viewership. He also outlined plans to develop the channel through collaboration with
influencers, storytelling, and paid advertising to expand audience reach.
Meanwhile, the interview with Ahmad Fathur Rohman took place in the office living room
and lasted 30 –40 minutes. He viewed YouTube as a portfolio tool to convince clients, with a
content strategy focused on video after movie tailored to client needs. Contrary to previous
views, he considered low viewership not a major issue because the channel is not focused on
reaching a broad audience.

The second theme concerns budget limitations and content availability, which depend

content types and planning. However, constraints related to budgeting and inconsistent portfolio
content production hinder the development of a structured content plan. Therefore, a strategic
development plan is needed to support the optimization of the YouTube channel.

DISCUSSION
Analysis of the Conditions of Utilization of the @tourezia.media YouTube Channel
The research results show that the @tourezia.media YouTube channel has not been
optimally utilized because it only functions as a portfolio platform without any content planning.
However, content planning is crucial for maintaining consistent interactions and building
relationships with audiences (Chaffey & Ellis -Chadwick, 2019). Furthermore, passively managing
digital platforms as static showcases fails to optimize algorithms or generate engagement
(Cartwright et al., 2022).
The findings also indicate differing internal perspectives: AF views YouTube as a portfolio
platform for easy access and client engagement, while HA views it as a potential marketing
channel, particularly through features like YouTube Shorts to increase reach and cost efficiency.
Nevertheless, both agree that the channel's primary function remains as the company's portfolio
platform.

Analysis of Inhibiting Factors in Optimizing the @tourezia.media YouTube Channel
Based on interviews, several major obstacles emerged in managing the @tourezia.media
YouTube channel. First, the high cost of producing YouTube content compared to other
platforms is a constraint, primarily due to the company's limited budget. Second, SEO
implementation is suboptimal, particularly in keyword research and metadata management

audiences.

Analysis of @tourezia.media YouTube Channel Optimization Strategy
Based on the analysis, optimization of the @tourezia.media YouTube channel can be
achieved through the implementation of a targeted and sustainable strategy. Optimizing
YouTube content, such as using keyword -rich titles, managing metadata, and increasing user
interaction, has been shown to increase visibility, customer trust, and a company's professional
image (Giankos & Giannakopoulos, 2025) . Therefore, a YouTube channel serves not only as a
portfolio but can also be developed into an effective digital marketing tool.
The first strategy that can be implemented is producing content that is genuine or
authentic and relevant to the audience. Content that showcases real -life experiences in line with
the company's character will more easily attract attention and build rapport with the audience.
Furthermore, this strategy is relatively cost -effective because it can utilize existing
documentation or simple productions. This approach also aligns with the concept of digital
content marketing, which emphasizes informative value an d audience engagement as effective
ways to generate leads (Salonen et al., 2024).

CONCLUSION
Based on the research results, the utilization of the @tourezia.media YouTube channel is not
optimal because it only functions as a portfolio archive to convince potential clients, which
results in low number of views and audience interaction due to lack of SEO optimization, no
target views, and inconsistent uploads due to dependence on events and high production costs.
However, the strategy of reworking videos into the YouTube Shorts format has proven to be an
effective solution with minimal costs. Therefor e, this channel needs to be optimized as a digital
marketing medium by overcoming obstacles such as lack of keyword research, high production
costs, and inconsistent uploads through the creation of authentic and relevant short content, as
well as improving video metadata to comply with the YouTube algorithm. This research also
shows that YouTube has the potential to be used effectively as a digital marketing medium in the
B2B sector in the tourism and event organizer industries.

SUGGESTION
Companies are advised to consistently implement a content calendar even when there are
no events, for example by reworking portfolio videos into YouTube Shorts, and optimizing
keyword research and SEO on video titles, descriptions, and tags, including exploring
collaborations with influencers to expand audience reach. From a managerial perspective, PT.
Tourezia Cakra Inspira needs to align differing perspectives across divisions by establishing clear
standards, targets, and evaluation indicators, and implem enting Standard Operating Procedures
(SOPs) for YouTube management so that the channel can function optimally as a digital
marketing medium. Meanwhile, further research can explore the relationship between YouTube
Shorts interactions and B2B sales conversions, as well as test the effectiveness of storytelling
strategies and influencer collaborations in a broader context.
