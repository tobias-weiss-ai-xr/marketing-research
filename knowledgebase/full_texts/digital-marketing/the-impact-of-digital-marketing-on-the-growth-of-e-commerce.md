ABSTRACT:
Digital marketing has become an important way for e-commerce businesses to create awareness, engage customers and encourage online purchases. This study
examines the impact of selected digital marketing activities on the perceived growth of e-commerce applications, focusing on social media advertisements,
influencer recommendations, online advertisements, promotional messages and digital offers. A descriptive research design was used, and primary data were
collected through a structured questionnaire from 52 respondents in Chennai. Percentage analysis, weighted average analysis, a chi-square test and multiple
regressionanalysiswere usedtoanalyse the responses.Instagram wasthe mostfrequently reportedplatform exposingrespondentstoe-commerce advertisements
(65.4%), while video advertisements attracted the most attention (65.4%). Customer reviews were the most selected social-media content type (32.7%), and
honest reviews were the leading factor making influencer recommendations convincing (50.0%). The chi-square test did not show a statistically significant
association between gender and e-commerce app usage frequency (p = 0.232). The regression model explained 69.1% of the variation in perceived overall e-
commerce growth; market reach and business expansion had significant positive coefficients in the reported model. The findings indicate that relevant digital
contentandcustomer-focusedmarketingmaysupporte-commercegrowth,whiletheresultsshouldbeinterpretedinlightofthelimitedsamplesize.

INTRODUCTION
Digitalmarketingusesdigitalchannelssuchassocialmedia,searchengines,websites,mobileapplications,emailandonlineadvertisementstopromote
products and services. Compared with traditional marketing channels, these tools allow businesses to communicate with customers quickly and to
observeresponses throughengagementandothermeasurableindicators.Asinternetaccess andsmartphoneusehaveexpanded,customers increasingly
search for products, compare prices, read reviews and make purchases through online platforms. E-commerce refers to the buying and selling of
products and services through electronic platforms. Mobile shopping applications provide product information, customer reviews, payment options,
order tracking and delivery services in one place. Competition among e-commerce businesses makes it important to attract new customers, retain
existing users and maintain a visible digital presence. Digital marketing supports these objectives through social media advertisements, influencer
recommendations, online video and search advertisements, promotional messages and offers such as discounts, cashback, coupons and free delivery.
These activities can create awareness and influence customer interest, visits and purchase decisions. However, customers may respond differently
depending on their needs, trust, usage patterns and perceived value. The present study examines customer responses to selected digital marketing
activities and their relationship with perceived e-commerce growth. The study uses questionnaire responses from Chennai and considers customer
adoption,purchaseactivity,marketreach,businessexpansionandoverallperceivedgrowth.
INDUSTRY CONTEXT AND SIGNIFICANCE
Thee-commerce industry connects onlinemarketplaces, direct-to-consumerwebsites, mobile shoppingapplications,digitalpayment systems, logistics
providersanddigitaladvertisingservices.Improvementsininternetconnectivity, mobiletechnology andpaymentfacilities havemadeonlineshopping
more accessible. Customers can compare alternatives across platforms, so e-commerce businesses need to communicate product value and maintain
customer engagement. Digital marketing is closely connected with this environment. Social media helps businesses introduce products; influencers
providedemonstrationsandrecommendations;onlineadvertisementscangeneratevisits;andapp
notifications, email or SMS can communicate new products and promotional offers. Businesses can also examine response data to understand which
messages attract attention and adjust campaigns accordingly. Understanding these responses is useful for identifying the digital touchpoints customers
notice and the activities they report as influencing their shopping. The study therefore provides a small-sample, customer-response view of digital
marketingandperceivede-commercegrowthinChennai.

RESULTS
The survey results show that the sample was predominantly young, with 67.3% aged 20–25 years, and that students formed 71.2% of respondents.
Instagramwasthemainreportedsourceofexposuretoe-commerceadvertisements(65.4%).Videoadvertisementsattractedthemostattention(65.4%),
whilecustomerreviewswerethemostselectedsocial-mediacontenttype(32.7%).Honestreviewswerethemostfrequentlyselectedreasonforfinding
influencerrecommendationsconvincing(50.0%).
A majority of respondents reported purchasing a product after seeing an online advertisement (61.5%) and visiting an e-commerce app after an
influencer recommendation (65.4%). Discount offers were the most selected app message and digital offer category, although several other types of
promotional communication also received responses. These results suggest that respondents encounter multiple forms of digital marketing during
onlineshopping.
Forperceived growth measures, 59.6%agreedor strongly agreedthate-commercewas experiencingsignificantgrowth inthe currentmarket. Thechi-
square test found no statistically significant association between gender and e-commerce app usage frequency (p = 0.232). The multiple regression
model explained 69.1% of the variation in perceived overall growth. Market reach and business expansion were statistically significant positive
predictorsinthereportedmodel.
