Abstract: Social media represent modern carriers of communication, a promotion channel for
businesses, and allow brands to connect with their audience through engaging content, discussions, and
targeted ads. The goal of the research is to determine to what extent social media accounts Facebook
and Instagram are properly managed in order to achieve maximum effects in the promotion of traditional
farms in Vojvodina. The selected traditional farms are the carriers of the offer of rural tourism , and
nurture tradition, the autochthonous way of life and work on the farm, with a modern approach to
tourists. This study applies a quantitative content analysis of social media accounts.  The use of
Facebook and Instagram accounts by owners of traditional farms is present, but their effectiveness is
not always satisfactory. On the example of the processed sample, we can conclude that Facebook
accounts, although they have a larger base of followers, do not achieve a significant level of interaction,
which reduces their promotional potential. On the other hand, Instagram accounts show a slightly
higher level of activity and a higher engagement rate, which makes them a more effective communication
channel. The dominance of a small number of successful accounts and posts indicates the need to
improve the digital marketing strategy, create a strategic plan that would include regular publication,
greater focus on effective content formats and consistency in communication with the public.
Keywords: social media marketing, digital engagment, audience interaction, Facebook and Instagram
platforms, rural tourism.

1. Introduction

The use of digital technology has enabled an easy and fast way of providing information in
tourism. Even more, it made the communication between the offer holders and the consumers
interactive. An increasing number of tourists use online information and co mmunication
technologies (ICT) when researching a destination, planning a trip, booking and purchasing
tourist services, as well as communicating their own impressions of the destination/tourism
experience. Accordingly, tourism companies are developing the ir own digital marketing
strategies in order to increase the recognition of their brand. By implementing a well -defined
strategy, businesses can ensure consistent messaging, optimize resource allocation, and
effectively engage their target audience.
A digital marketing strategy sets the framework for creating and distributing content across
digital channels such as websites, landing pages, social media, email, and mobile apps (N. N.,
2025). Digital marketing enables precise user targeting, easily meas urable results and lower
promotion costs. Micro and small businesses can achieve excellent results even though they
have low budgets for marketing, so the classic media of communication (press, radio,
television) are increasingly losing their importance. W ith the correct choice of channels and
focus on local targeting, it is possible to achieve excellent results with minimal investment
(Videnov Digital, 2025). Businesses must maintain competitiveness by staying abreast of the
most recent trends and technologies in digital marketing (De Alwis, 2023: 4).

traditional farms in V ojvodina. Traditional farms were chosen as an important segment of the

2. Traditional Farms as Carriers of the Offer in Rural Tourism of Vojvodina

Traditional farms represent unique residential and economic units of the Pannonian region,
which are an important segment of the rural tourism offer in V ojvodina. Traditional farms were
mostly built in the second half of the 19th and early 20th century. They were built far from the
villages, and for the needs of the peasants to be close to their arable fields during agricultural
work. Over time, they grew from temporary settlements into permanent places of residence and
in some places they were grouped into a long street (so -called ’šor’). Traditional farms are
characterized by a traditional way of life, economy and housing with preserved cultural,
customary and folklore elements (Ćurčić and Bjeljac, 2008).
A small number of traditional  farms have survived to this day, especially in their original
function (Dragićević, 2007). One way to revive traditional farms in V ojvodina is certainly to
activate them in the field of tourism. In recent years, placed in a new context of tourism, ecology
and sustainable development, traditional farms have experienced a strong tourist expansion and
are becoming interesting for city dwellers. The appearance, equipment, atmosphere and some
daily activities bring the experience of living in the old, tradition al way of an isolated rural
estate (Ćurčić and Pavlović, 2011). Traditional farms provide an authentic rural experience in
the modern tourist offer of V ojvodina. Well-preserved elements of tradition that are placed
through original offer packages have a strong effect on attracting tourists. The touris t offer of

Tourist destinations are attaching ever -greater importance to food given its ability to attract
visitors, enhance travel experiences and achieve differential positioning as culinary destinations
(Martin et al., 2021). Gastronomy, in addition to preserved nature and traditional lifestyle, is a
very important attractive attribute in agricultural tourism. V ojvodina cuisine is characterized by
a large number of various dishes made from vegetables, dough, meat and fish, and fruit is used
in fresh or processed fo rm (jam, compote) or in the form of juices, liqueurs, wine and brandy
(Ćurčić, 2025). The gastronomy of V ojvodina developed on the basis of the influence of the
people who immigrated in the past and who brought their own rules for food preparation. In
V ojvodina, there is a wealth of culinary knowledge and mutual influence of Serbian, German,
Hungarian, Romanian, Slovak, Ruthenian, Croatian, Ukrainian, Macedonian, Turkish cuisine
(Kalenjuk et al., 2022). On the traditional farms, the offer mostly consists of local specialties,
made from locally produced food. Visitors to the traditional farms are able to observe or even
participate in the preparation of dishes according to traditional techniques and homemade
recipes, the meal is slow with several dishes served in a certain order (soup, boiled meat and
tomato sauce, baked meat and salad, cakes) (Banić Grubišić and Antonijević, 2013). In order to
adapt to the needs of tourist demand in the gastronomy segment, in 2020 one facility in
V ojvodina became the first holder of the HALAL certificate in rural tourism in Serbia, and
previously it also became the holder of the HACCP food safety system (Ćurčić, 2025).
The complex offer on traditional farms was created by a combination of natural and cultural
factors of the local environment. Market experience and consumer reactions and demands have
led to the development of an offer that can meet consumer expectations  in the best way . In
addition to the basic offer (accommodation, food), all entrepreneurs have developed an
additional offer as well as educational content for children and adults. Additional offers include:
agricultural work, feeding domestic animals, selling souvenirs, ethnic exhibition, children's
playground, carriage ride, horse riding, bicycle riding, collecting fruits from nature, hunting,
fishing, excursions, sports. Educational content includes: workshops of old crafts, painting on
glass and silk, workshops of making paintings from straw, making souvenirs, learning folklore,

Rural tourism in Serbia, as a priority form of tourism development, receives strong support
through various sources of funding: favorable credit lines and grants from the Ministry of
Tourism and Youth and the Ministry of Agriculture, certain cross -border cooperation projects
(IPA program), as well as other international projects, which enables the development of
complementarity between agriculture and tourism (e.g. IPARD II program in 2020)

allocated around €1,270,000 in grants for the reconstruction and additional equipment of
existing hospitality facilities for accommodation in rural tourism (N1info, 2024). At that
competition, 20 projects were approved for entrepreneurs from V ojvodina, half of which were
projects carried out by traditional farm owners.

effective solutions and strong communicative connectivity. Social media fosters direct
communication between destinations and prospective tourists, facilitating the shar ing of
personalized and visually rich content that appeals to consumer preferences (Kim et al., 2017).
Studies suggest that social media marketing is effective in increasing destination awareness and
shaping positive perceptions that encourage travel inten t (Stylidis et al., 2017). Recent studies
highlight that interactive features such as live streams, influencer collaborations, and virtual

Platforms like Facebook and Instagram enable destinations to share visually engaging content
that stimulates interest and provides real-time, user-centered perspectives on travel experiences
(Hudson and Thal, 2013). In this context, this study explores the effectiveness of the traditional
farm's Facebook and Instagram social media accounts.
This study applies a quantitative content analysis of social media accounts. Content analysis is
widely recognized as an appropriate method for examining digital marketing practices because
it enables the systematic collection and evaluation of publicly available data generated through
online communication. The research focuses o n assessing the effectiveness of social media
promotion of traditional farms in Vojvodina by analyzing their official Facebook and Instagram
accounts. The study combines descriptive statistical analysis with a comparative approach,
allowing the identification of differences between social media platforms, content formats, and
observation periods.
The research sample consists of 15 traditional farms operating in Vojvodina that actively
participate in rural tourism. The sample was selected using purposive sampling, with the
inclusion criterion being the existence of at least one official Facebook and /or Instagram
account used for promotional purposes. The selected farms represent providers of
accommodation, gastronomy, recreation, and cultural experiences based on the traditional rural
lifestyle of Vojvodina. Although the sample does not include all traditional farms in the region,
it encompasses some of the most recognizable and active representatives of rural tourism,
providing an adequate basis for comparative analysis.
Data were collected using the ContentStudio analytics platform 1, a software solution designed
for monitoring and analyzing social media performance. The platform provides access to
publicly available performance metrics and enables standardized extraction of indicators from
Facebook and Instagram accounts.
Two observation periods were included in the analysis: the previous 12 months and the previous
30 days. The data intersection was made on April 5th, so the data included in the analysis were
published until the mentioned date, in the periods from 5th of March until 5th of April 2026,

The collected data were analyzed using descriptive statistical methods. For each indicator, total
values, arithmetic means, minimum and maximum values, as well as the percentage
contribution of maximum values to the overall results, were calculated. These measures enabled
the assessment of variability among the analyzed profiles and the identification of dominant
performers.
The analysis was conducted at two levels. First, Facebook and Instagram were compared
regarding account activity, audience size, engagement, and engagement rate. Second, different
Instagram content formats (image, reels, and carousel posts) were compared i n order to
determine which type of content generates the highest level of audience interaction. Finally, the
results for the two observation periods (12 months and 30 days) were compared to identify
differences between long -term and recent communication pe rformance. The obtained results
served as the basis for assessing the current state of digital marketing practices among
traditional farms and for formulating recommendations aimed at improving social media
communication in rural tourism.

5. Discussion

The findings indicate that traditional farms in V ojvodina have recognized the importance of
social media as a promotional channel, as the majority of the analyzed businesses maintain
official Facebook and Instagram accounts. However, the results also reveal that merely having
a presence on social media does not necessarily ensure effective digital communication.
Considerable differences were observed in account activity, audience engagement, and posting
consistency, suggesting that social media are still no t managed strategically by a large
proportion of the analyzed traditional farms.
Analysis of activity and engagement on Facebook shows that the level of posting is low, with
an average of 2.90 posts per month. Although the total engagement reaches relatively high
values, the distribution of the results is extremely uneven, as a small number of pages generate
most of the interaction. The average level of engagement is low, which indicates the limited
effectiveness of the content in stimulating audience response. These results suggest that
Facebook, although it has a larger follower base,  does not achieve a significant level of
interaction, which reduces its promotional potential.
On the other hand, Instagram shows a slightly higher level of activity and a higher engagement
rate, which makes it a more effective communication channel. The average number of posts is
higher than on Facebook, and user interaction is more intense, althou gh overall engagement
values are lower. However, even on this platform there is a pronounced unevenness of results,
with a small number of profiles achieving a dominant part of the total engagement. This pattern
indicates a lack of uniform strategy and a different level of digital development among farms.
A detailed analysis of Instagram content, according to the types of posts, further sheds light on
the causes of such results. In the long -term period of 12 months, carousel posts achieve the
highest average engagement and reels the highest degree of engage ment, which confirms that
dynamic and visually rich content has the highest potential to attract users' attention. In contrast,
image posts recorded the weakest results, even though they were used relatively often. This
discrepancy between frequency and ef fectiveness indicates insufficient strategic content
planning.
In the short-term period of 30 days, a general decline in all engagement indicators was observed.
Although the carousel still performs best, the differences between the formats are significantly
smaller and the engagement rate is low and consistent. Such r esults indicate a reduced

6. Conclusion

The service sector plays a major role in raising the quality of life in the countryside and the
smooth functioning of rural households. A developed service sector reduces the difference
between urban and rural lifestyles, functionally improves the life of rural communities and
influences the attraction of new villagers (Ćurčić et al., 2021). The functional development of
rural tourism in V ojvodina includes the economic adaptation of farms to tourist demand and the
merging of tradition and modern information  and communication technologies. The choice of
promotional tools and communication channels plays an important role in better market
positioning of the traditional farm. Therefore, offer holders on traditional farms recognize and
increasingly apply social media in promotion.
Rural tourism facilities have greater opportunities to reach interested customers through social
media using little financial outlay (Wojciechowska -Solis, 2025). The researchers determined
during the sample selection that some farms do not have their own website (or the website is
not active), but that is why they have an account on one, two or less often more social media.
Through research, we found that in the promotion of farms, Facebook and Instagram are
dominantly used as social media, while other media are rarely used (YouTube, G+, X, LinkedIn,
Tripadvisor). The choice of Facebook and Instagram platforms is expected given the popularity

The power of social media communication with tourism consumers reflects on previous
