ABSTRACT
This study aims to: (1) identify internal and external factors in the utilization of  social media at
UMKM Sambal Nyala Rasa Yakusa; (2) analyze the constraints faced in digital marketing; and (3)
formulate social media utilization strategies to increase sales. This research uses a descriptive qualitative
approach with a case study method. Data were obtained through observation, interviews, and
documentation, and were then analyzed using IFAS, EFAS, and SWOT matrices to determine the strategic
position of the business. The results show that UMKM Sambal Nyala Rasa Yakusa  has relatively strong
internal conditions. The main strengths include comprehensive product information and an easy ordering
process. However, several weaknesses remain, including inconsistency in content uploads, suboptimal
promotional creativity, and limited marketing reach. From the external perspective, the increasing use of
social media and the development of digital technology provide major opportunities for business
development. Based on the results of the strategic position analysis, the UMKM is positioned in Quadrant I
(Strength-Opportunity), indicating that the appropriate strategy is an aggressive strategy by maximizing
internal strengths to utilize external opportunities through improving content quality, increasing
promotional intensity, and strengthening interaction with consumers in order to encourage sales growth.

Keywords: social media, IFAS, EFAS, SWOT analysis, utilization strategy, Micro, Small, and Medium
Enterprises (MSMEs).

INTRODUCTION
The development of information technology has brought major changes to people's lives, particularly
in communication patterns, consumer behavior, and economic activities. The presence of social media has
made a significant contribution to reshaping people's behavior, including culture, ethics, and social norms.
Almost all levels of society across various age groups use social media as a means to obtain and
disseminate information (Cahyono, 2016). In the business context, social media has developed into one of
the effective marketing media, especially for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (UMKM).
UMKM have an important contribution to the Indonesian economy; however, this sector still faces
various constraints such as limited capital, low quality of human resources, limited access to technology,
and weak marketing strategies (Tulus Tambunan, 2002 in Anita Febrianty , 2023). The utilization of social
media is one of the strategic alternatives to overcome these limitations. Digital marketing is considered
more effective because it can expand market reach and facilitate consumers in obtaining information and
conducting online transactions (Juliana et al., 2020; Purwana E.S. et al., 2017). However, many UMKM
have not yet utilized social media optimally in their marketing activities (Herdiyani et al., 2022).
Kabupaten Ende has great potential for UMKM development, especially in household business sectors
such as processed food and local crafts. Increasingly easy internet access and the rising use of social media
create opportunities for entrepreneurs to utilize digital platforms as promotional media, build brand identity,
and increase sales. However, most UMKM in Kabupaten  Ende have not optimally utilized these
opportunities due to limited knowledge in managing social media, the absence of a directed digital
marketing strategy, and inconsistent promotional content. Data from BPS (2024) show that an average of
64.96% of the population of Kabupaten  Ende has accessed the internet. In addition, there are 24,728
business units registered as UMKM, but only around 43 UMKM actively use social media for marketing.
This indicates that the use of social media remains very minimal compared with the existing digital access
potential.
UMKM Sambal Nyala Rasa Yakusa is one of the 43 UMKM in Kabupaten  Ende that utilizes social
media in marketing activities. This home -based industry produces various local chili sauce variants with
distinctive regional flavors and quality raw materials, such as Sambal Ikan Asap Ende, Sambal Ijo Ikan
Asap, Sambal Baby Cumi, and Sambal Teri Medan. In its marketing efforts, this UMKM uses Instagram,
Facebook, and WhatsApp as tools for promotion and sales. However, the utilization of social media still
faces several problems, such as limited human resources in creating creative promotional content and an
unstable internet network. This condition needs to be analyzed through a SWOT approach to formulate
more effective marketing strategies. Based on the background described above, the objectives of this study
are to identify internal and external factors in the use of social media at UMKM Sambal Nyala Rasa
Yakusa, analyze the challenges faced in online marketing, and design social media utilization strategies to
increase sales.
METHODS
This research uses a descriptive qualitative approach with a case study method at UMKM Sambal
Nyala Rasa Yakusa, located in Kelurahan Mautapaga, Kecamatan Ende Timur, Kabupaten  Ende. The
research was conducted from November to December 2025 with the aim of analyzing the utilization of
social media in increasing sales through a SWOT analysis approach. Respondents in this study were
determined using the accidental sampling technique, namely sample selection based on anyone who was
coincidentally encountered by the researcher and considered to meet the research criteria. Respondents
consisted of the UMKM owner, workers, and consumers encountered while purchasing products.
The data used included primary and secondary data. Primary data were obtained through non -
participant observation, in -depth interviews, and documentation related to production activities and
promotion through social media. Secondary data were obtained from books, journals, and relevant literature
to support the theoretical foundation of the study. Data analysis was carried out using descriptive
qualitative analysis through the stages of data reduction, data presentation, and conclusion drawing.
Furthermore, SWOT analysis was conducted by preparing IFAS and EFAS matrices to identify strengths,

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
UMKM Sambal Nyala Rasa Yakusa utilizes three social media platforms, namely Facebook,
WhatsApp, and Instagram, as media for product promotion, communication, and sales. The selection of
these platforms is based on their ease of use and wide user reach.
The results show that Facebook is the most dominant social media platform used by consumers. A
total of 14 respondents (63.64%) stated that they used Facebook to obtain information and purchase
products, while 8 respondents (36.36%) used WhatsApp. No respondents used Instagram as the main
medium for purchasing.
Facebook is utilized through business page features, product photo uploads, and Facebook Reels to
increase product visibility and appeal. Meanwhile, WhatsApp functions as a follow -up communication
medium and a direct transaction channel between the business actor and consumers. Instagram is used as a
supporting medium through feed, story, reels, and direct message features; however, its utilization has not
been optimal, as indicated by the low number of followers and inconsistent upload frequency.
This finding is in line with Munawaroh (2023), who stated that Facebook has a large number of users
in Indonesia and therefore has potential as an effective marketing medium. In addition, Sugiyantoro et al.
(2022) noted that WhatsApp is widely used in small business networks because of its convenience and
efficiency.

Another strength possessed by UMKM Sambal Nyala Rasa is its attractive photo display and ease of

weaknesses that hinder the effectiveness of digital marketing strategies through social media (Sano et al.,

effectiveness, sales, and business competitiveness in the local market (Roqybah  et al., 2025). Therefore,
UMKM Sambal Nyala Rasa needs to optimize its internal strengths, particularly those related to the clarity
of product information, service quality, and ease of ordering. In addition, improvements are also needed in
the aspects of content consistency and creativity as well as expansion of promotional reach so that
marketing strategies through social media can operate more effectively and sustainably.

Opportunity factors have the largest contribution in the EFAS analysis, indicating that the external
environment provides significant support for business development. Social media is considered capable of
enabling more people to recognize Sambal Nyala Rasa, thereby increasing potential market reach. In
addition, increased social media account activity has the potential to attract more consumers, because more
regular interactions and posting frequency can increase the attention and interest of prospective buyers.
Reels and story content also have great potential to attract new customers, along with the increasing user
interest in short and attractive visual content. The growing use of social media in society also opens broader
opportunities for increasing product sales. Furthermore, promotional and discount programs carried out
through social media have the potential to stimulate consumer purchase interest and increase sales volume.
The EFAS analysis also indicates several external threats that need to be considered by UMKM
Sambal Nyala Rasa. Competition with similar chili sauce products is a fairly significant threat, considering
the many business actors with similar products who also use social media as a marketing medium. In
addition, an unstable internet network can hinder promotional activities and communication with
consumers through social media. Constraints in the product delivery process also have the potential to
reduce consumer interest in making online purchases, especially among consumers who prioritize speed
and security in delivery.
Overall, the total EFAS score of 1.14 shows that UMKM Sambal Nyala Rasa has considerable external
opportunities to develop its business through the utilization of social media. These opportunities can be
optimally utilized to overcome various existing threats, especially by improving digital marketing
strategies, strengthening product advantages, and improving distribution systems and customer service.
Thus, this external condition provides a strong basis for UMKM Sambal Nyala Rasa to formulate
appropriate and sustainable business development strategies.
Constraints in the Utilization of Social Media
The utilization of social media by UMKM Sambal Nyala Rasa Yakusa in digital marketing activities
has not yet fully run optimally because it still faces several constraints, both internal and external. These
constraints include limited human resources, low creativity in developing promotional content, and
technical barriers in the form of an unstable internet network.

The main constraint faced is limited human resources (HR). The business owner still experiences
difficulties in generating creative ideas for digital marketing content, such as product photos, production
process videos, and attractive promotional designs to be published through social media. This condition
indicates that digital marketing skills and professional social media management remain relatively limited.
In fact, the quality of human resources is one of the important factors in business development, because
competent human resources have the ability to improve business performance and adapt to technological
developments, including the implementation of digital marketing strategies. This is in line with the opinion
of Indra et al. (2024), who stated that human resource competence plays an important role in increasing the
competitiveness of UMKM through the utilization of digital technology.
In addition to limited human resources, another prominent constraint is the low level of creativity in
developing social media content. The uploaded content still tends to be monotonous, lacks variety, and has
not followed current digital marketing trends, such as the use of short videos, reels, and product
storytelling. As a result, the level of consumer engagement with the published content remains relatively
low. In fact, content creativity is an important element in attracting consumer attention and building
purchase interest. The study by Miryam and Antonio (2022) shows that attractive social media content
design through quality visuals and relevant message variations can significantly increase customer
engagement.
The next constraint comes from technical factors, particularly the unstable quality of the internet
network. The network condition in Kabupaten  Ende, which tends to be inconsistent, causes delays in
uploading promotional content, so content publication schedules cannot always be carried out on time. In
addition, limited internet connectivity also affects the smoothness of communication between the business
actor and consumers, especially in responding to messages or orders received through social media and
instant messaging applications. In some conditions, power outages also worsen communication access,
resulting in less than optimal  customer service. This is in line with the opinion of Huda and Munandar
(2021), who stated that network quality and digital infrastructure support are important factors in the
implementation of digital marketing by UMKM, because unstable connectivity can hinder the effectiveness
of communication and business marketing performance.

The Quadrant I position indicates that the business has more dominant internal strengths than
weaknesses and greater external opportunities than threats. This condition shows that the UMKM is in a
favorable position and has great potential to be developed through an aggressive strategy (SO), namely
utilizing internal strengths to optimize market opportunities.
The main strengths lie in the ease of access to product information, visual content quality, ease of
ordering, and quick admin response. Meanwhile, external opportunities are supported by the increasing use
of social media and the potential of visual content to reach a wider range of consumers.
Based on the combination of internal and external factors, four alternative strategies were obtained as
follows:
1. SO Strategy (Strength-Opportunity)
This strategy focuses on utilizing internal strengths to optimize external opportunities, including:
a. Optimizing the consistency and quality of social media content (photos, video reels, and stories) to
take advantage of the increasing use of social media in reaching more consumers.
b. Strengthening interaction with consumers through quick responses, publication of testimonials, and
promotional and discount programs to increase purchase interest and customer loyalty.
c. Optimizing the use of paid advertising features and market segmentation on social media to expand
reach and attract new customers in a more targeted manner.
2. ST Strategy (Strength-Threat)
This strategy aims to use internal strengths to face external threats, such as:
a. Maintaining consistent chili sauce product quality as an effort to face competition with similar
products.
b. Utilizing content advantages and fast service to differentiate Sambal Nyala Rasa Yakusa products
from competitors.
c. Using social media to build a strong brand image so that the product remains attractive to consumers.
d. Providing responsive service to retain consumers amid increasingly intense market competition.

3. WO Strategy (Weakness-Opportunity)
This strategy utilizes external opportunities to overcome internal weaknesses, namely:
a. Increasing the consistency and creativity of social media content.
b. Optimizing social media account activities routinely.
c. Utilizing various social media features (such as stories, reels, and marketplaces) to cover the
limitations of conventional promotion.
d. Completing location and purchasing information on social media.
e. Combining content variation with promotional and discount programs.
4. WT Strategy (Weakness-Threat)
A defensive strategy to minimize weaknesses and avoid threats, including:
a. Increasing the consistency and quality of social media content.
b. Preparing promotional content that is simple but clear and informative.
c. Clarifying location and purchasing information on social media.
d. Optimizing relevant and easily understood content variations for consumers.
e. Providing clear and realistic shipping information.
CLOSING
Conclusion
The internal and external factors affecting the utilization of social media at UMKM Sambal Nyala
Rasa Yakusa are as follows: a. Internal factors; UMKM Sambal Nyala Rasa Yakusa  has strengths in the
ease of access to product information through social media, attractive photo displays, clear price and
ordering information, and quick admin responses. However, its weaknesses lie in content consistency and
creativity that have not been optimized, limited promotional reach, unclear video quality, incomplete
location information, and insufficient variety of promotional content. b. External factors; UMKM Sambal
Nyala Rasa Yakusa has opportunities in the form of increasing social media use by the community and the
opening of a wider market through digital marketing. On the other hand, the threats faced include

management, both in terms of upload schedules and content quality, as well as to improve digital marketing
skills through training so that social media utilization becomes more effective in increasing sales. For other
UMKM actors, this research is expected to serve as a reference in preparing digital marketing strategies
through social media to expand market reach and increase competitiveness. Furthermore, for the
government and related institutions, it is expected that digital marketing coaching and training programs
can be strengthened to support UMKM development. Meanwhile, for future researchers, it is recommended
to develop further studies by adding variables, analytical methods, or broader research objects in order to
