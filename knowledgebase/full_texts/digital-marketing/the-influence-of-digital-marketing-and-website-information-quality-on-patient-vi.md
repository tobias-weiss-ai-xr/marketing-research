Abstract: The development of digital technology encourages hospitals  to utilize digital
marketing and websites as a means of communication with the public and the delivery of
service information. This study aims to analyze the influence of digital marketing and the
quality of hospital website information on patient visits  interest, both partially and
simultaneously. Sampling was carried out using non -probability sampling with purposive
sampling techniques. The study respondents were 92 people who knew Maranatha Hospital
and had seen or accessed the hospital's website and/or digital media. Data was analyzed using
multiple linear regression. The results showed that digital marketing and website informatio n
quality had a significant effect on patient visit interest. These findings show that the quality of
website information h as a stronger role in encouraging patient visits interest. Therefore,
hospitals need to integrate digital marketing strategies with the provision of accurate, complete,
relevant, up-to-date, clear, and trustworthy website information.

Keywords: Digital Marketing, Patient Visit Interest, Website Information Quality.

INTRODUCTION
The development of digital technology has changed the way people obtain informatio n
and interact with various organizations, including healthcare organizations. In Indonesia,
internet usage continues to increase. The number of Indonesian internet users in 2024 will
reach 221.56 million people with a penetration rate of 79.5%, an increase from 78.19% in the
previous year. Data also shows that 72.78% of Indonesia's population will have access to the
internet by 2024 and 68.65% of the population already has a mobile phone (Haryanto, 2024;
Badan Pusat Statistik, 2025). This condition shows the increasing public access to digital
information sources. In the context of health services , these changes also affect the process
of prospective patients in getting to know, compare, and consider hospitals before using
services. Prospective patients can obtain information about doctors, types of services,
facilities, schedules, locations, registration procedures, and other information through various
digital channels without having to come directly to the hospital. Thus, the ability of hospitals
to form and maintain interest in patient visits becomes increasingly related to how hospitals
present and communicate in the digital environment (Chen & Wang, 2021).

Patient interest is one of the important aspects in the sustainability of hospital services
because it describes the tendency of the community to choose and use the services offered.
This interest is not formed suddenly but can be influenced by the process of seeking
information, assessment of the hospital, experiences and recommendations from other parties,
as well as perceptions of the hospital's ability to meet service needs. In perspective Theory of
Planned Behavior, the intention to perform a behavior (behavioral intention) is a factor that
precedes actual behavior and is formed by attitudes towards behavior, subjective norms, and
perceived behavioral control (Ajzen, 1991). In the context of hospitals, visit interest can be
understood as the tendency of prospective patients to choose or use services after evaluating
various information and considerations they have. Therefore, efforts to understand the factors
that can shape patient visit interest are important in hospital management.
One of the factors that can be related to the patient's interest in visits is digital marketing.
The development of digital media allows hospitals not only to convey information about
services, but also to build  communication and interaction with the community. Through
websites, social media, search engines, apps, and other digital channels, hospitals can
introduce services, provide health education, build brand awareness, as well as increasing
engagement with po tential patients (Setyawati et al., 2024). The effectiveness of these
activities became relevant to patient visit interest because social media marketing proven to
have a positive and significant effect on patient visit intention, either directly or through brand
awareness (Hariyanti et al., 2023).
Several studies have shown a relationship between digital marketing and patient visit
interests. Pramesti et al. (2024) showed that interactive elements and incentive programs in
digital marketing had a sig nificant relationship with visit interest. Hariyanti et al. (2023)
shows that social media marketing affects patient visit intention, with brand awareness as one
of the mechanisms related to the formation of interest in visits. Hairiah et al. (2026) research
also found a relationship between digital marketing and customer revisit intention in hospital
patients, either partially or together with the quality of service and price. The findings provide
the basis that digital marketing can be one of the factors related to the patient's tendency to
visit or reuse hospital services.
In addition to activities digital marketing in general, website information quality is
another aspect that can play a role in the process of considering prospective patients. Website
an official hospital can be one of the sources of information that provides information about
hospital profiles, doctors, types of services, facilities, schedules, registration procedures, and
other supporting information. For prospective patients, the existence of information alone is
not necessarily sufficient if the information is inaccurate, incomplete, irrelevant, or not
updated. The quality of information is important because prospective patients need to obtain
information that can be understood and tru sted when evaluating alternative health services.
Thus, website information quality can be part of the prospective patient's initial experience
before direct service contact occurs (Shaygani et al., 2023; Theresia & Syahrir, 2025).
Research on quality websites the hospital shows that the information and quality aspects
website related to how hospitals deliver and promote services through the digital
environment. The quality of websites can be evaluated through accessibility, experience,
marketing, and technology, and found differences in quality in several dimensions based on
hospital characteristics. Shaygani et al also found a relationship between quality website
hospitals as well as information about services and facilities with a share of medical tourism
at the provincial level. The study shows that websites are one of the marketing instruments
that can be used to convey information about hospital services to potential service users.
Meanwhile, Gunawan et al found that the quality of website and the quality of service affects
patient satisfaction, which is further related to the formation of the hospital's image (Gunawan
et al., 2026; Nurfikri et al., 2023; Shaygani et al., 2023).

METHOD
This study uses a quantitative approach with the type of explanatory research and cross-
sectional design. The sampling method used is non -probability sampling with techniques
purposive sampling. The research sample is the community or prospective patients who know
Maranatha Hospital and have seen or accessed website official and/or digital media of
Maranatha Hospital. Respondents must be able to understand and fill out the questionnaire and
be willing to be r espondents. The determination of the number of samples refers to Green
(1991) for regression analysis, namely N ≥ 50 + 8m, with m being the number of independent
variables. This study has two independent variables so that N ≥ 50 + 8(2) = 66 respondents. To
anticipate incomplete data or not meeting the analysis criteria, the target number of respondents
is set at 85 respondents (Green, 1991).
The definition of variable operationalization is prepared based on previous research
concepts and instruments. Variables digital marketing (X1) is Respondents' perception of the
effectiveness of hospital marketing activities through various digital media in conveying
information, communicating, and promoting services and measured using the concept social
media marketing activities (SMMA) consists of entertainment, interaction, trendiness,
customization, and electronic word of mouth (e-WOM) (Kim & Ko, 2012; Wijaya et al., 2021).
The variable of website information quality (X2) is Respondents' perception of the quality of
information available on website hospital in meeting the information needs of users, this refers
to the concept WebQual, with a focus on dimensions information quality, including aspects of
accuracy, completeness, relevance, up-to-date, and ease of understanding information (Barnes
& Vidgen, 2002; Natasia et al., 2023). The variable of patient visit interest (Y) refers to the
concept of behavioral intention in Theory of Planned Behavior (Ajzen, 1991), which in this
study describes the tendency or desire of r espondents to visit with indicators in the form of
transactional, referential, preferential, and exploratory interests. All indicators were measured
using a scale Likert five levels, i.e. 1 = strongly disagree to 5 = strongly agree (Ajzen, 1991;
Saadi & Junadi, 2025). Data analysis was performed using multiple linear regression with the
help of SPSS (Ghozali, 2021).

Discussion
Digital marketing had no significant effect on patient visit interest . Nevertheless, a
positive regression coefficient showed that the relationship digital marketing to patient visit
interest has a positive direction. Thus, the increase in activity digital marketing tends to be
followed by an increase in visitor interest, but the increase is not statistically strong enough to
be statistically significant. Digital marketing can serve as a mediu m to introduce hospitals,
build awareness, convey promotions, as well as reach a wider community. However, in the
context of health services, a person's decision to visit a hospital is a decision that can involve
other considerations, such as health servic e needs, trust in the hospital, previous experience,
quality of service, cost, location, and recommendations from others. Pramesti et al. (2024) show
that not all aspects of digital marketing are significantly related to the interest of visits.
Interaction and incentive programs are influential, while cost and site design are insignificant.
However, these results differ from the research of Theng & Saputra (2023) which found that
digital marketing has a positive and significant effect on the decision of patient visits (p=0.001).
These differences can be caused by the characteristics of the respondent, the hospital, the form
of digital marketing, as well as the research variables used. Meanwhile, Utami et al. (2024)
show that digital marketing Related to revisit intention. This shows that the influence of digital
marketing on visiting behavior can be indirect and influenced by other factors, such as hospital
image, patient experience, and electronic word of mouth. So, this study shows that it is not
enough for hospitals to just increase activities digital marketing, but it is necessary to pay
attention to how the activity provides the information that the potential patient really needs.
Digital marketing should be directed not only to increase the frequency of promotions, but also
to direct the public to service information that is complete, relevant, easy to understand, and
trustworthy. This is important because of the value of significance digital marketing. In this
study, 0.052 is very close to the limit of 0.05, so there is a tendency for a positive relationship

Based on the results, website information quality has a positive and significant effect on
patient visit interest. This means that the better the qualit y of information on the hospitals, the
higher the interest of respondents in making visits. These findings show that website hospitals
not only function as a medium for delivering information but can also be a source of
consideration for prospective patients in choosing health services. Information that is complete,
clear, relevant, easy to understand, and trustworthy can help prospective patients understand
the services available and reduce uncertainty before deciding. Zhang & Kim (2022) through a
meta-analysis of 147 studies that showed a strong relationship between the quality of web -
based health information and intention to use health information systems (r=0.58). Aspects of
ease of understanding information, navigability, and presentation website also a ffect users'
perception of information quality. In this study, website information quality  has an average
score of 4.0598, the highest compared to digital marketing (3.9855) and interest in visits
(3.9493). In addition, the value of standardized beta by 0. 611 is higher than digital marketing
by 0.292. This shows that website information quality is a comparatively stronger variable in
explaining visitation interest. Pian et al. (2022 also shows that internet users in assessing the
quality of health informati on consider various criteria, including the characteristics of the
source, the content of the information, and the aspect of information presentation. The research
emphasizes the importance of the user's ability to assess whether health information found
online can be trusted and used. This supports the results of this study that the quality of
information is not only related to the existence of information on the website, but also how that
information is perceived and used by users. In the context of hospi tals in Indonesia, Theresia
& Syahrir (2025) also emphasizes the importance of quality website and user experience in the
development of hospital digital services. The research on type C and D hospitals in East Java
shows that the quality of the design web site and user experience is an important factor in the
development of hospital digital services. From the results of the discussion above, website
information quality is a factor closer to the consideration process of prospective patients.
Digital marketing can attract the attention of the public, while websites with quality informatio n
help prospective patients understand services and assess their suitability for their needs.
Digital marketing and website information quality simultaneously had a significant effect
on visitor interest. These results show that the two variables can be seen as a series of digital
communication that complement each other. Digital marketing plays a role in reaching and
attracting the attention of the public, while websites provi de more complete information to
support the consideration process of potential patients. Thus, the effectiveness of hospital
digital communication does not only depend on promotional activities, but also on the quality
of information available after the pu blic is interested in seeking more information. These
findings are in line with Theng & Saputra (2023) who show that digital marketing, word of
mouth, and the quality of service simultaneously has a positive and significant effect on the
patient's decision to visit the hospital This shows that the decision of a patient visit can be
influenced by several factors simultaneously. The results of this study need to be understood
together with the partial test, namely digital marketing has a significance of 0.052 , while the
quality of website information is 0.001. This means that even though digital marketing is not
partially significant at the level of 5%, the two variables together still produce a significant
model, with the quality of website information showing a stronger partial influence. Based on
the overall results, hospitals should integrate digital marketing with the provision of quality
website information. Digital marketing can be used to attract the attention of the public, then
the website provides complete, accurate, relevant, easy-to-understand, and trustworthy service
information so that it can support the formation of interest in visits.

CONCLUSION
Based on the results, digital marketing and website information quality  simultaneo usly
have a significant effect on patient visit interest. The findings of the study show that the
provision of complete, accurate, relevant, clear, easy -to-understand, and trustworthy website
information needs to be the concern of hospitals in supporting the formation of interest in visits.
Although digital marketing does not show a partial significant influence at the level of 5%,
these activities can still play a role as a medium to attract public attention and direct prospective
patients to obtain further information thro ugh the hospital website. Therefore, hospitals need
to integrate digital marketing strategies with website information quality management so that
digital communication can support the consideration process of prospective patients more
effectively.
