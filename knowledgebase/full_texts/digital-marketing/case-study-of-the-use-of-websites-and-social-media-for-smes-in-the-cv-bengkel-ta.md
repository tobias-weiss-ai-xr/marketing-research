Abstract
SMEs or Small and Medium Enterprises are small and medium -sized
businesses that have an important role in the pace of economic
development in Indonesia. One of the MSMEs that has existed for a
long time in Jakarta is the Small Industry Village (PIK). The S mall
Industrial Village (PIK) used to be famous and visited by many visitors,
before it was eroded by the changing times. The digital era is indeed
impossible to avoid, MSME actors must be able to maximize the
benefits of digital development. One of the bag craftsman MSMEs that
has taken advantage of digital developments is the Central Bengkel
Tas. Digital marketing is used to achieve marketing goals through the
application of technology and digital media. There are 6 elements in
digital marketing , namely interactivity, intelligence, individualization,
integration, industry restructuring, and independence of location . The
research method used is a qualitative method. The type of research
used in this study is descriptive. In conducting this study, the
researcher collected various data from various sources using
interview techniques, non -participant observations, and
documentation. The resource person interviewed by the Researcher
was the owner of the Central Bengkel Tas in the Small Industrial
Village. The results of the research obtained by UKM Central Bengkel
Tas have implemented several elements of digital marketing in  its
website. Central Bengkel Tas has implemented two -way
communication, regularly changed the content, and expanded its
market. However, Central Bengkel Tas is not active in its social media
and marketplace. The conclusion of this study is that the
implementation and implementation of SME Digital Marketing Central
Bengkel Tas is carried out through the website. UKM Central Bengkel
Tas also said that the website is one of the tools to market its products
and also promotions. Central Bengkel Tas is inactive in its social media
and marketplace due to a lack of human resources that makes the
owner manage the production part and also the website at once.

INTRODUCTION
Medium Business is an independent  productive economic enterprise, carried out by an
individual or business entity that is not a subsidiary or branch of a company that is owned,
controlled, or is part either directly or indirectly with a Small business or large business with a
total net worth or annual sales result The asset criteria is 500 million – IDR 10 billion and the
turnover criterion is >IDR 2.5 billion – IDR 50 billion. The number of employees ranges from tens
to hundreds.
Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in Indonesia can be said to be growing very fast. In
mid-2016, the number of SMEs in Indonesia has reached 57.9 million. SMEs in Indonesia itself
have a fairly important role for national economic development, especially  in economic growth
and reducing the unemployment rate, as well as encouraging the acceleration of regional
development. SMEs are one of the important parts of the economy of a country or region,
including in Indonesia.
The SME sector has proven to be resilient, when there is an economic crisis, only the SME
sector survives the economic collapse in Indonesia, while the larger sector actually goes bankrupt
at that time. Based on a report by the Ministry of Cooperatives and Small Enterprises (KUKM) in
2013, the contribution of SMEs to the creation of national foreign exchange through non -oil and
gas exports increased by Rp 50.35 trillion or 30.09%, which is Rp. 189.46 trillion or 18.17% of
the total value of national non -oil and gas exports. Statistics show that small business actors in
Indonesia are able to absorb a workforce of 100,096,270 people or 93.04% of the total absorption
of the existing workforce.
One of the SMEs that has existed for a long time in Jakarta is the Small Industry Village
(PIK) located in the Pulogadung area, East Jakarta. According to Jakarta Open Data of the DKI
Jakarta Provincial Government (2019, accessed in May 2019) there are 667 small industries and
as many as 25 percent of them are traders. The PIK area itself has existed since the 1970s. The
products are produced directly at that location, then sold directly in stores there.
 There are various types of small industries engaged in the area, one of which is bag
craftsmen. This area used to be famous and visited by many visitors, before being eroded by the
changing times. The digital era is indeed impossible to avoid. Therefore, SMEs must be able to
maximize the benefits of digital development.
One of the bag crafts SMEs that has taken advantage of digital developments is the Central
Bengkel Tas. Central Bengkel Tas is a small business or local business that has been established
since the year. This business focuses on making bags with the right design, selection of the right
raw materials, efficient, and professionally carried out workmanship. Central Bengkel Tas has at
least 15 superior bag craftsmen. Central Bengkel Tas is also one of the SMEs that has started using
the website since 2010.

digital-based marketing has evolved from the beginning of marketing activities of goods and

Industry Restructing
Disintermediation and reintermediation are key concepts of industrial restructuring that
should be considered by companies developing digital strategies and should be explored.
Disintermediation itself is the elimination of intermediaries such as distributors or brokers who
previously connected the company to its customers. Instead of going through traditional
distribution channels, which have some type of intermediary (such as distributors, wholesalers,
or agents), companies can now handle each customer di rectly, for example via the internet. One
important factor is the decrease in the cost of serving customers directly. This model is very
closely related to direct sales. Business -to-consumer or B2C is now mediated by electronic
commerce as a bridge function between buyers and manufacturers. Disintermediation is initiated
by consumers who are affected by high market transparency. Buyers know the supply price
directly from the manufacturer so consumers prefer to buy goods directly to the manufacturer
rather t han through a distributor with the aim of being able to spend less than through a
distributor.Buyers have now cut out middlemen (wholesalers and retailers) to buy directly from
manufacturers, and pay less. Buyers can choose alternatives to buy from wholesa lers. To
illustrate, a typical B2C supply chain originally consisted of four or five entities (in order):
Supplier-Manufacturer-Wholesaler-Retailer-Buyer but now as it is argued that technologies
including the internet have modified the supply chain due to  market transparency become:
Supplier-Manufacturer-Buyer Contents. The term was originally applied to the banking industry
around 1967 and then applied more generally to 'cutting the middleman' in trade. The most
prominent example of successful disintermediation is Dell, which sells many systems that go
directly to consumers – thus bypassing traditional retail chains, and which has managed to create
brands, well known by customers, profitable and with sustained growth. Disintermediation has
become an important strategy for many retailers to lower prices by reducing the n umber of
intermediaries between suppliers and buyers. The loss of the intermediary as a distributor does
not mean the loss of media between producers and consumers to make transactions. In addition
to disintermediation, it is also known as reintermediation. Reintermediation is the reintroduction
of intermediaries between consumers and producers. The term reintermediation applies mainly

RESEARCH METHODS
This study uses a research design using research with a descriptive qualitative approach.
The descriptive qualitative approach aims to research a phenomenon that occurs in real life
experienced by the research subject and describes in a systematic, factual , and accurate manner
something that is the object of research. Descriptive qualitative research emphasizes the issue of
depth (quality) of data, not the quantity of data, which means that research does not need many
informants because digging for information is not many informants who are asked but how deep
the information is dug up. The data produced in the descriptive qualitative method can be in the
form of interview transcripts, field notes, photographs, videotapes, and other official documents.
According to Bogdan and Taylor (Moleong, 2023:4), qualitative methodology is defined as
a research process that produces descriptive data in the form of written or oral data from people
and behaviors that are observed, then directed to the setting and indi viduals as a whole, then
examined each part one by one, presenting directly the essence of the relationship between
researchers and informants. The meaning of the above meaning is that qualitative methods will
ultimately produce data in the form of written  or oral data obtained from the informants being
studied.
Definition of descriptive research as explained by Sukmadinata Descriptive research is a
form of research aimed at describing existing phenomena, both natural phenomena and man -
made phenomena. Phenomena can be in the form of forms, activities, characterist ics, changes,
relationships, similarities, and differences between one phenomenon and another. (Sukmadinata,
2013:72)
This study uses a descriptive qualitative approach because it is to explore the reality that
occurs in the research area of the application and use of digital marketing of SME Central Benkel
Tas through the website which will then be outlined in a row of sentences as found by researchers
in the field.

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
The researcher conducted observations and interviews with resource persons who
owned the Central Bengkel Tas. UKM who also held the website of the Central Bengkel Tas and

by Central Bengkel Tas through a website with a digital marketing  concept that has elements
consisting of interactivity, intellegence, individualisation, integration, industry restructing , and
independence of location. (Chaffey, 2022:37).
The result of this study is how to utilize  the interactivity  element of digital marketing of
SME Central Bengkel Tas through the website. The application of interactivity is carried out by
marketing and promoting products owned through  the  Central Bengkel Tas website. The Central
Bengkel Tas website is also managed quite well such as updating the website appearance,

This 2-way communication also minimizes errors in product manufacturing. If there is a

 The Central Bengkel Tas website also does not implement a subscription system where
