This study aims to analyze low -budget digital marketing strategies
implemented by Fazia Beauty in Metro City, Lampung. This research
employed a qualitative approach using a qualitative survey method and
a case study design. The informants consisted of 12 p articipants,
including the owner, two employees, four long -term consumers, three
new consumers, one prospective consumer/follower, and one university
practitioner in marketing or entrepreneurship. Data were collected
through semi-structured interviews, open-ended surveys, Instagram and
WhatsApp Business observations, documentation, and literature review.
The data were analyzed through data reduction, data display,
verification, and thematic analysis. The findings show that Fazia Beauty
uses Instagram for pr omotion, education, testimonials, and awareness,
while WhatsApp Business plays a stronger role in consultation, follow -
up, ordering, and repeat purchases. Relevant low -budget strategies
include organic content, product education, authentic testimonials, word
of mouth, referrals, storytelling, customer communities, content
calendars, basic bundling, and customer retention. These strategies have
the potential to support consumer trust and sales opportunities, but their
effectiveness needs to be tested through consistent implementation

INTRODUCTION
Advances in digital technology have
transformed the way businesses introduce products,
communicate with consumers, and build market
trust. In the context of beauty products, digital
marketing serves not only as a promotional medium
but also as a tool for e ducation, personalized
communication, sharing testimonials, and building
consumer trust. Beauty products are sensitive in
nature because they relate to skin condition, safety,
product suitability, and user experience. Therefore,
digital marketing strategies for beauty products must
prioritize information, credibility, and long -term
relationships with consumers.
Digital marketing can be understood as the use
of technology and digital media to support marketing
activities, build relationships with customers, and
create value for consumers (Chaffey & Ellis -
Chadwick, 2022). Social media is a key channel
because it enables two-way communication between
brands and consumers. Kaplan and Haenlein (2010)
explain that social media provides a space for users
to participate, share information, and interact. In
beauty product marketing, social media can be used
to showcase pro ducts, build awareness, educate
consumers, and share customer experiences.
Instagram is a particularly relevant social media
platform for beauty products due to its visual and
interactive nature. On Instagram, business owners
can make use of Reels, Stories, product photos,
testimonials, and educational content. However,
using Ins tagram does not always directly result in
sales. For local brands with limited promotional
budgets, Instagram more often serves as a platform
for raising awareness, strengthening brand image,
and acting as an initial entry point before consumers
engage in further communication through more
personal channels.
In addition to Instagram, WhatsApp Business
also plays a crucial role in the digital marketing of
small and medium -sized businesses. WhatsApp
Business allows business owners to display product
catalogs, answer customer questions, follow up with
customers, and maintain post-purchase relationships.
From a relationship marketing perspective, the
relationship between a brand and consumers is built
not only through transactions but also through trust,
commitment, communication, and ongoing service
(Morgan & Hunt, 1994). Thus, WhatsApp Business
can be a relevant platform for beauty products, as
consumers often require personalized explanations
both before and after a purchase.
Fazia Beauty is a local beauty brand located on
Jl. Alamsyah RPN, Metro City, Lampung. Its
product offerings include day cream, night cream,
facial wash, moisturizer, and serum. Fazia Beauty
uses Instagram (account: @faziabeauty.id) and
WhatsApp Business a s its digital marketing
channels. Strategies implemented have included
product promotions, educational content, customer
testimonials, communication via WhatsApp, and the
use of local influencers at various times. However,
Fazia Beauty still faces challeng es in digital
promotion, particularly budget constraints when it
comes to hiring brand ambassadors, public figures,
or running large-scale paid promotions.
These challenges highlight the importance of
low-cost digital marketing strategies. A low -cost
strategy does not mean promotions are carried out
without planning; rather, it emphasizes optimizing
the resources the brand already possesses, such as
organic c ontent, product education, authentic
customer testimonials, word of mouth, referrals,
customer databases, customer communities,
storytelling, and customer retention. This strategy is
relevant for local beauty brands because not all
businesses have the fina ncial capacity to follow the
promotional models of major brands.
Previous research on digital marketing for
beauty products has largely focused on social media
marketing, influencer marketing, electronic word of
mouth, brand trust, and purchase intention. Several
studies have shown that social media, influencers,
and co nsumer engagement can influence the
intention to purchase local cosmetic products
(Azizah, Gunawan, & Sinansari, 2021; Lim &
Kesumahati, 2022). However, most of these studies
employed a quantitative approach and focused on

testing relationships between variables. This study
takes a different approach by using a qualitative
methodology to understand low -cost digital
marketing strategies employed by local beauty
brands in the region.
Based on this background, this study aims to
analyze low-cost digital marketing strategies at Fazia
Beauty in Metro City, Lampung. The research
focuses on the digital strategies that have been
implemented, promotional challenges with limited
budgets, consumer perceptions of digital content and
communication, and low -cost strategies that can be
applied to support opportunities for increased sales.

METHODS
Using qualitative survey methods and case
studies. A qualitative approach was used because this
study aims to gain an in-depth understanding of Fazia
Beauty’s experiences, perceptions, challenges, and
digital marketing strategies. Creswell and Poth
(2018) e xplain that qualitative research is used to
understand the meanings constructed by individuals
or groups regarding a social or practical issue. In this
study, the issue under examination is how Fazia
Beauty manages low -cost digital marketing amid
limited promotional budgets.
A qualitative survey method was used to explore
the diversity of informants’ perspectives on Fazia
Beauty’s digital marketing. Jansen (2010) explains
that qualitative surveys are used to explore the
diversity of experiences, perspectives, or behaviors
within a specific group, rather than to produce
statistical generalizations. In addition, this study also
employed a case study approach because it focused
on a single research subject, namely Fazia Beauty in
Metro City, Lampung. Case studies are relevant for
understanding contemporary phenomena in real -life
contexts (Yin, 2018).
The research was conducted at Fazia Beauty,
located on Jl. Alamsyah RPN, Metro City, Lampung.
The research and observation period ran from
January to May 2026. There were 12 research
informants selected using purposive sampling. The
informants consisted of  one owner, two employees,
four long -time customers who had been using the
products for at least one year, three new customers
who had been using the products for less than one
year, one prospective customer or follower who had
not yet made a purchase, and  one university
practitioner in the field of marketing or
entrepreneurship. The informant codes used were O,
K1, K2, KL1 –KL4, KB1 –KB3, CK, and P. These
codes were used to maintain the confidentiality of the
informants’ identities. The informants in this st udy
were real individuals, not fictional characters.
Data were collected through semi -structured
interviews, open -ended qualitative surveys, social
media observation, documentation, and literature
review. Interviews were conducted with the owner,
employees, and practitioners. Open -ended surveys
were administ ered to existing customers, new
customers, and prospective customers. Observations
were made on the Instagram account
@faziabeauty.id and the use of Fazia Beauty’s
WhatsApp Business account. The documentation
used included Instagram content, anonymized
customer testimonials, the WhatsApp Business
catalog, and promotional or bundling materials, if
available.
Data analysis was conducted through data
reduction, data presentation, drawing conclusions,
verification, and thematic analysis. Miles,
Huberman, and Saldaña (2020) explain that
qualitative data analysis is conducted through data
condensation or reduction, data presentation, and the
drawing and verification of conclusions. Thematic
analysis is used to identify thematic patterns in
qualitative data (Braun & Clarke, 2006). Data
validity is ensured through source triangulation and
methodological triangulation. Source triangulation is
performed by comparing information from the
business owner, employees, existing customers, new
customers, prospective customers, and practitioners.
Methodological triangulation is conducted by
comparing data from interviews, open -ended
surveys, observations, documentation, and literature
reviews.

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
Digital Marketing Strategies Implemented by
Fazia Beauty
Research findings indicate that Fazia Beauty has
utilized Instagram and WhatsApp Business as its
primary digital marketing channels. Instagram is
used to promote products, share skincare
information, showcase testimonials, and build
rapport with customers and potential customers. The
content used includes Reels, single images or
product photos, Stories, and educational content on
facial skincare and the proper use of skincare
products. WhatsApp Business is used as a more
personalized communication channel, primarily for
orders, consultations, after -sales service, product
reservations, and repeat purchases.
These findings indicate that Fazia Beauty’s
digital marketing is not only focused on product
promotion but also on customer education and
communication. From a digital marketing
perspective, these activities align with the concept
that digital channels can  be used to create value,
build customer relationships, and manage the
consumer experience (Chaffey & Ellis -Chadwick,
2022). For beauty products, the consumer experience
begins with viewing content, reading testimonials,
asking questions via WhatsApp, tryi ng the product,
and ultimately considering a repeat purchase.
The owner of Fazia Beauty stated that digital
promotions will be aimed at educating women to
choose products that are healthy and safe for the long
term. This statement indicates that Fazia Beauty’s
marketing is not only focused on sales but also on
building consumer awareness regarding product
safety and quality. This is important because beauty
products come into direct contact with the skin and
often raise concerns about compatibility and long -
term effects.
“Fazia will consistently run campaigns —
whether on Instagram or WhatsApp —to show
women how they can prioritize choosing products
that are healthy and safe for the long term.”

Instagram as a Platform for Awareness and
Education
Instagram plays a vital role in introducing Fazia
Beauty to consumers. According to the owner and
employees, Instagram serves as a platform to
promote products, share educational content, and
attract potential customers. However, direct
interaction with co nsumers occurs more frequently
via WhatsApp than on Instagram. Thus, in the
context of Fazia Beauty, Instagram is best
understood as a platform for awareness and
education, rather than as the primary channel for
transactions.
These findings align with the concept of social
media marketing, which positions social media as a
space for communication and consumer engagement
(Tuten & Solomon, 2021). Instagram allows Fazia
Beauty to showcase product visuals, educational
content, and customer testimonials. However,
Instagram’s effectiveness cannot be measured solely
by the number of posts or visual views, but also by
its ability to encourage consumers to seek further
information.
“Instagram is quite effective at attracting
attention, although Instagram followers have not yet
significantly used or purchased Fazia Beauty
products. The content is just as engaging as other
Instagram content in the same product category. It’s
just that Fazia doesn’t yet have a wide range of
product categories.”
This statement indicates that Instagram has
played a role in building brand awareness but has not
yet fully functioned as a driver of sales conversions.
This is understandable because the process of
purchasing beauty products often requires a higher
level of confidence compared to regular consumer
goods. Consumers don’t just look at the content’s
visuals; they also consider other users’ experiences,
product safety, skin compatibility, and
recommendations from people close to them.
Educational content is a relevant strategy for
Fazia Beauty because it addresses consumer
concerns. Content on facial skin care, how to use
skincare products, the skin’s recovery process, and
safe product usage can help potential customers

926

better understand the products. From a digital
content marketing perspective, content that provides
informational value can build engagement, trust, and
a positive perception of the brand (Hollebeek &
Macky, 2019).
WhatsApp Business and Relationship Marketing
WhatsApp Business serves as a personal
communication channel between Fazia Beauty and
consumers. The primary feature used is the product
catalog. Additionally, WhatsApp is used for pre -
purchase consultations, product orders, after -sales
service, follow -ups after product use, and repeat
purchases. Common questions from consumers
relate to skin conditions, skin reactions after using
products, the duration of the skin recovery process,
and complaints regarding product use.
From a relationship marketing perspective,
communication via WhatsApp Business can
strengthen the relationship between the brand and
customers. Morgan and Hunt (1994) explain that
trust and commitment are key elements in marketing
relationships. At Fazia B eauty, these relationships
are built through personal, responsive, and ongoing
communication.
“WhatsApp is typically used by existing
customers for orders and repeat purchases, or to
reserve products out of concern that they might run
out.”
This statement indicates that WhatsApp
Business has the potential to support customer
retention. Although this study did not statistically
measure the relationship between WhatsApp and
increased sales, the data suggests that WhatsApp
plays a role in mainta ining communication with
existing customers and fostering opportunities for
repeat purchases. Thus, WhatsApp Business can
serve as a relevant, low -cost strategy for Fazia
Beauty—especially when managed through a
customer database, product catalog, and ethi cal
follow-up practices.
Post-purchase follow-ups are also a crucial part
of beauty product marketing strategies. Fazia Beauty
checks in on how customers are using the products
after some time has passed. This activity serves not
only to gather testimonials but also to demonstrate
care for consumers. In the context of relationship
marketing, post -purchase service can enhance the
customer experience and foster loyalty.
Testimonials, Word of Mouth, and Consumer
Trust
Customer testimonials are one form of electronic
word of mouth used by Fazia Beauty. Testimonials
are collected via WhatsApp after customers have
used the products. Fazia Beauty requests permission
from customers before using their testimonials.
Customers’ identities and their eyes are blurred to
protect their privacy and ensure the focus of the
testimonials remains on the condition of their facial
skin.
Electronic word of mouth can influence
consumer perceptions because it stems from the
experiences of other users (Hennig -Thurau,
Gwinner, Walsh, & Gremler, 2004). In the beauty
industry, testimonials play a crucial role because
potential customers often se ek evidence of others’
experiences before deciding to make a purchase.
However, this study shows that digital testimonials
are not the only factor influencing consumer
confidence. Word of mouth from close contacts —
such as friends, family, or coworkers —is a ctually
more powerful in driving the decision to try a
product.
“The reason I decided to try and use Fazia
products was because I saw a coworker whose skin
used to look unhealthy but now looks healthy —not
just glowing.”
These findings show that real -life experiences
shared by people close to us are more
convincing than typical digital promotions. As
a result, word -of-mouth and customer referral
strategies are highly relevant for Fazia Beauty.
Trust is built not only throu gh Instagram
content but also through social proof stemming
from consumers’ personal relationships.
“Alhamdulillah, Fazia’s products are excellent,
and the longer I use them, the healthier and
more radiant my skin feels. However, using

927

these products does require patience because it
takes time for the skin to repair damaged skin
cells.”
This statement suggests that consumer trust
develops gradually through the experience of using a
product. In brand trust theory, trust is related to
consumers’ belief that a brand can meet their
expectations and provide reliable value (Chaudhuri
& Holbrook , 2001; Delgado -Ballester & Munuera -
Alemán, 2005). At Fazia Beauty, informants also
linked the trust factor to the perception that the
products have BPOM approval and are safe for long-
term use.
However, testimonials still need to be managed
ethically. Fazia Beauty must not present testimonials
as a guarantee of the same results for all consumers.
Results from using beauty products can vary due to
skin condition, history of previous product use,
consistency of use, and other individual factors.
Therefore, testimonials should be positioned as
consumer experiences, not absolute claims.
Challenges of Low-Cost Digital Marketing
Fazia Beauty’s main challenge in digital
marketing relates to budget constraints that limit its
ability to run large -scale campaigns, such as those
involving brand ambassadors or well -known public
figures. Additionally, Fazia Beauty faces challenges
related to limited human resources, a lack of fresh
content ideas, and the need to manage its Instagram
and WhatsApp Business accounts more effectively.
“Generally, the beauty industry requires high
promotional costs. As a result, many beauty products
on the market emerge and go viral because they’re
backed by high -cost promoters. However, this
doesn’t last long if it isn’t supported by good, high -
quality products.”
This statement reflects an awareness that going
viral does not always equate to brand sustainability.
Fazia Beauty has chosen not to rely on discounts or
price cuts because it wants to maintain its positioning
as a premium-quality product at an affordable price.
This strategy is crucial because discount -based
promotions risk diminishing the perceived value of
the product if they do not align with the brand’s
positioning.
Fazia Beauty has also worked with three
local influencers at different times. However,
according to research data, the results have not
significantly boosted sales. The use of local
influencers has been more effective in raising
awareness among certain seg ments of the
public—particularly women —about Fazia
Beauty. These findings suggest that local micro -
influencers can be a viable alternative, but they
must be selected carefully. The number of
followers is not a sufficient primary indicator;
audience fit, cr edibility, communication style,
and proximity to the target market are more
important factors to consider.
Barriers to Purchase Among Potential
Consumers
Potential consumers still have reservations
before purchasing Fazia Beauty products.
These reservations stem from past negative
experiences with beauty products, comparisons
with other trending brands, and the lack of
endorsements from major public figures  or
cosmetic doctors.
“It takes a fair amount of courage to decide to
try a new product, especially after having used
beauty products from various brands with
unsatisfactory results. However, hearing
testimonials from close friends who have
already used the product significantl y boosts
confidence and encourages people to give
Fazia a try.”
This statement reinforces the finding that word -
of-mouth from close acquaintances is a key factor in
building trust among potential customers. Potential
customers need not only engaging content but also
information that can reduce perceived risk. In the
context of consumer behavior, decisions to purchase
beauty products often involve information -seeking

and risk assessment. Therefore, Fazia Beauty needs
to strengthen its educational content, authentic
testimonials, product safety explanations, and
consultative communication.
Formulation of a Low -Cost Digital Marketing
Strategy
Based on the results and discussion, a low -cost
digital marketing strategy tailored for Fazia Beauty
encompasses several aspects. First, optimizing
organic Instagram content. Instagram content should
focus not only on product promotion but also on
education, storytelling, testimonials, and interactive
content. A weekly content calendar can be used to
ensure consistency and reduce reliance on
spontaneous ideas.
Second, educational content on beauty products
needs to be strengthened. Content can cover how to
care for facial skin, how to use skincare products, the
skin recovery process, realistic expectations for
results, and the importance of choosing safe
products. This strategy is relevant because many
consumers ask about their skin condition, how their
skin responds to products, and how long the skin
recovery process takes.
Third, authentic customer testimonials and
word-of-mouth need to be managed more
systematically. Digital testimonials can be used to
strengthen social proof, while word -of-mouth from
existing customers can be channeled through a
simple referral program. Referrals are better suited to
Fazia Beauty’s situation because recommendations
from people close to the customer are proven to be
more convincing to potential customers than
promotions from unknown sources.
Fourth, WhatsApp Business needs to be
optimized as a channel for customer follow -up and
retention. Fazia Beauty can use its customer database
to segment potential customers, new customers,
existing customers, and customers likely to place
repeat orders. Ho wever, follow -ups must be
conducted ethically to avoid disturbing customers.
Fifth, local brand storytelling can serve as a
differentiation strategy. Fazia Beauty can highlight
the brand’s journey, the value of its healthy and safe
products, customer experiences, and its connection
with local consumers. Storytelling doesn’t have to be
direct promotion; instead, it can take the form of
narratives that build emotional connection and trust.
Sixth, a customer community can serve as a
long-term alternative. The community can be used
for light educational content, product usage tips,
skincare reminders, and sharing experiences.
However, the community must be managed carefully
so it does not turn  into an overly aggressive
promotional space.
Seventh, basic bundling packages can be
implemented without undermining price positioning.
Since Fazia Beauty does not want to use a discount
strategy, bundling can be positioned as a basic
skincare package —for example, day cream, night
cream, and facial wash. This bundling can help new
customers understand basic product combinations
without positioning the brand as a discount brand.
These strategies cannot be guaranteed to
increase sales before being tested through
implementation. However, they have the potential to
boost trust, customer engagement, repeat purchases,
and sales opportunities. Their effectiveness can be
measured through indicators such as the number of
inquiries via WhatsApp, increased Instagram
engagement, the number of customer testimonials,
the number of repeat customers, and the number of
new customers acquired through referrals.
Research Contribution
This study contributes to the field of digital
marketing research on local beauty brands.
Theoretically, this study demonstrates that social
media marketing, relationship marketing, electronic
word-of-mouth, and brand trust can be used in an
integrated man ner to understand low -cost digital
marketing strategies. Instagram plays a role in
raising awareness and providing education,
WhatsApp Business plays a role in personal
communication and customer retention, while
testimonials and word -of-mouth play a role in
building trust.
Practically, this study offers insights for Fazia
Beauty and other local beauty brands, showing that
limited promotional budgets do not always pose an
insurmountable obstacle. Local brands can optimize

929

their available resources through organic content,
education, personalized service, authentic
testimonials, loyal customers, referrals, storytelling,
and customer retention. These strategies are more
realistic than attempting to replicate the high -cost
promotional models of major brands.

CONCLUSION
This study concludes that Fazia Beauty’s digital
marketing strategy relies on Instagram and
WhatsApp Business. Instagram is used for
promotion, education, testimonials, and brand
awareness, while WhatsApp Business plays a greater
role in consultations, fol low-ups, orders, and repeat
purchases. These two channels complement each
other in fostering communication between Fazia
Beauty and its customers.
Fazia Beauty’s main challenge is a limited
budget for large -scale promotions, such as brand
ambassadors, public figures, or intensive paid
promotions. Additionally, Fazia Beauty faces
limitations in human resources, a lack of fresh
content ideas, and the n eed for more effective
management of its Instagram and WhatsApp
accounts.
Consumer perceptions indicate that trust in Fazia
Beauty is built through product usage experience,
product safety, BPOM status (according to
informants), WhatsApp customer service, customer
testimonials, and recommendations from close
contacts. Digital te stimonials help build trust, but
word-of-mouth from friends, family, or coworkers
has a stronger influence in encouraging potential
customers to try the products.
Low-cost digital marketing strategies that Fazia
Beauty can implement include optimizing organic
Instagram content, educational content, authentic
customer testimonials, word of mouth, customer
referrals, WhatsApp Business for follow -ups and
repeat orders,  local brand storytelling, customer
communities, a weekly content calendar, basic
product bundles, and retention of existing customers.
These strategies have the potential to build consumer
trust and drive sales opportunities, but their
effectiveness still  needs to be tested through
consistent implementation.
This study has limitations because it focuses on
only one local beauty brand and uses a qualitative
approach. Future research could involve more local
beauty brands or use a mixed -methods approach by
incorporating quantitative data, such as Instagram
engagement, the number of WhatsApp clicks, the
number of repeat orders, or changes in sales before
and after the strategies are implemented.
