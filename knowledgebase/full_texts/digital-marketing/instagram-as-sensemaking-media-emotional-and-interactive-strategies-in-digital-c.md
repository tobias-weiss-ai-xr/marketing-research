Abstract
This study aims to understand how Instagram is used as a sensemaking medi um in digital
communication. Referring to Karl Weick's sensemaking theory (1995), this study explores seven
dimensions of sensemaking: identity construction, retrospective, enactment of sensible environments,
social interaction, ongoing communication, extracted cues, and plausibility. A descriptive qualitative
approach was used, with data collected through interviews with two informants, namely the Owner
and the digital marketing admin. Data analysis was carried out using NVivo software. The results of
the study show that Instagram is not only used as a visual promotional tool, but also as a participatory
space to build meaning with customers. The communication strategies implemented involve
emotional narratives, visual consistency, two -way communication, and data -based decision making.
Collaboration between strategic and technical actors within the organization creates adaptive,
relevant, and sustainable communication practices. This study concludes that sensemaking is an
effective framework for understandin g the dynamics of digital communication on social media, as
well as providing practical contributions to the development of communication strategies in the
competitive digital era.

Keywords: Content Strategy, Digital Marketing Communication, Interactive Communication,
Sensemaking, Social Media

INTRODUCTION
The development of information and communication technology has changed the way
individuals and organizations interact with consumers to create meaning, especially through
social media  (Sidharta et al ., 2024) . Social media not only functions as a channel for
distributing information, but has transformed into a complex social interaction space, where
individuals and organizations shape meaning through fast and dynamic communication  (Brill
et al., 2022) . One of the most widely used platforms in digital marketing communication
strategies today is Instagram (Kis et al., 2024). platform offers visual and interactive features
that allow brands to build more personal and dynamic relationships with audiences
(Wulandari et al., 2025).
Two-way communication through comments, direct messages, and interactive
features such as polls and Q&A, allows companies to respond to consumer needs and
expectations in real time. In this context, digital communication is not just about delivering
messages, but rather a sensemaking process, namely how individuals create meaning from the
messages, symbols, and interactions they encounter (Weick, 1995). Emotional and interactive

Many organizations now use Instagram not only as a promotional medium, but also as
a strategic space to build meaning and strengthen emotional connections with audiences
(Kumar & Masvood, 2023) . Instagram has evolved into a digital communication platform
that combines the power of visuals, personal narratives, and real -time interactions, allowing

ecosystem of meaning where audiences participate in building an understanding of brand
values and identity.
However, the rapid dynamics in the era of digital disruption often create uncertainty,
ambiguity, and the need to continuously adapt. This is where the role of sensemaking
becomes crucial. As explained by Weick (1995), sensemaking is the process by which
individuals or organizations interpret complex realities through the creation of understandable
and shared meanings (Weick, 1995; Le Bris, 2021; Petersen, 2022). ). In the context of digital
marketing communications, sensemaking becomes an important mechanism for business
actors to read market signals, understand consumer responses, and navigate digital platform
algorithms and trends more strategically (Combe & Carrington, 2015).
By sensemaking, organizations not only react to change but also create a framework
of meaning that allows audiences to understand and respond to brand messages emotionally
and participatively. This process involves analyzing digital data (insight), reading visual
narratives, and interpreting consumer interactions that ultimately underlie valuable, adaptive,
and sustainable decision -making (Vickers & Bennett, 2024) . Therefore, it is important to
understand how visual strategies, personal narratives, and interactive responses implemented
by brands on Instagram shape the sensemaking process in the minds of audiences.
This study aims to explore how Instagram is used as a sensemaking medium in digital
communication, emphasizing the emotional and interactive strategies used by business actors,
as well as their impact on consumer perception and loyalty. This study is not only important
for expanding theoretical understanding of social media as a meaningful communication
space, but also provides practical contributions to the development of more humanistic and
participatory digital marketing strategies.

RESEARCH METHOD
This study uses a qualitative descriptive method that aims to gain an in -depth understanding
of how Instagram is used as a sensemaking medium in building an emotional and interactive
digital communication strategy. This approach was chosen because it is in line with the
research objectives that emphasize the exploration of meaning, narrative, and communication
practices that are formed dynamically and contextually in the daily life of the organization
(Creswell, 2018). This search not only focuses on the content of the message conveyed, but

Data collection was conducted through in -depth interviews with three main informants who
have strategic roles in managing the Millen Florist Nusantara Instagram account, namely the

researchers used the NVivo application as an aid in the thematic coding process. This

communication and digital interaction is at the core of the organization's communication

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
Digital communication through Instagram has undergone a transformation from just a
promotional medium to an interactive space that allows for the sensemaking process, which is
a process in which organizations and audiences together build an understanding of reality,
brand identity, emotional values, and social relations formed through commu nication
(Pınarbaşı & Kırçova, 2021). platform is no longer just a place to disseminate information or
display products, but has become an integral part of a communication strategy that
emphasizes participation, interaction, and collective meaning creation (Mefalopulos, 2008).
Millen Florist Nusantara consciously utilizes the features available on Instagram such as
visual feeds, stories, reels, polls, a nd direct messages to create an emotional, personal, and
continuous communication experience. This strategy is key in building and maintaining
customer loyalty, where the interactions that are established are not one -way, but reflective,
responsive, and based on value proximity.
Within the framework of the sensemaking theory developed by Weick (1995), one of
the main dimensions that appears dominant in Millen Florist's communication practices is
identity construction, namely how an organization's identity i s formed through a process of
interaction and interpretation that continues to develop. Brand identity is not only present
through a logo or visual design alone, but through a narrative that is built consistently and
touches on the emotional aspects of customers' lives. Millen Florist's digital marketing admin
explained that the strategy implemented not only displays products visually, but also includes
the story behind the moment of using the product:
"We focus on visual consistency and storytelling, so we  don't just upload
photos of flowers, but we give stories behind the moments, such as
anniversary flowers or condolences. So there is an emotional touch that
makes customers feel close" (Marketing).
Narratives like this show how brand identity is not only defined by products, but by
emotional experiences that can be understood by customers personally. Meanwhile, the
retrospective dimension in sensemaking theory (Weick, 1995) is also clearly visible in Millen
Florist Nusantara's digital communication practic es. Retrospective sensemaking refers to the
process of building me aning based on past experiences where individuals or organizations
reflect on what has happened and then interpret it as a basis for making relevant decisions in
the present. In this context, organizations do not only move instinctively or reactively, but

The ongoing dimension refers to the understanding that the process of meaning
formation is not fi nal, but rather takes place continuously and dynamically. Meaning is not
produced from a single moment, but is formed continuously through repeated interactions,
experiences, and interpretations over time. In the context of Millen Florist Nusantara's digital
communication, this principle is clearly reflected in the pattern of content production and
distribution that is carried out routinely and consistently through Instagram. Content is not
created randomly or occasionally, but is designed as part of an ong oing strategy that is
continuously updated based on responses and changes in audience preferences. The digital
marketing admin explained that consistency is the main principle in social media
management. Every week, the team creates an upload schedule that considers the best time
based on audience activity insights.
"We post at least 3-4 times a week. For the hours, we adjust them based on
insight, usually in the afternoon or evening after work hours" (Marketing).
The statement indicates that decisions in c ommunication do not only follow intuition,
but are based on data that is continuously monitored and analyzed periodically. This supports
the idea that sensemaking is a living process that requires constant attention and involvement.
In fact, in practice, seemingly simple daily content is actually part of continuous interpretive
work where organizations try to understand changes in audience tastes, platform dynamics,

In addition, the extracted cues dimension describes how individuals or organizations

designing actions. In the context of digital communication, these clues can be data,
interaction patterns, or audience responses to the content presented. Millen Florist Nusantara

and formulate more targeted content strategies. The digital marketing team not only produces
content routinely but also ensures that each content has emotional and visual relevance that
matches the profile of their followers. The digital marketing admin explained that the
majority of Millen Florist's followers are young women who like aesthetic things and are
sensitive to emotional touches.
"Our followers are mostly women aged 20 -35 who like aesthetic things.
That's why our content is made feminine, soft, and full o f meaning"

This statement shows that the decision -making process in compiling content is based

This data becomes strategic raw material for determining the style of caption language, visual

By understanding these preferences, Millen Florist is able to create content that is not only
visually appealing, but  also has high emotional resonance for the target audience.
Furthermore, this process shows how the organization interprets small signals from the

communication carried out is not only based on visual creativity, but also on sensitivity to the
dynamics of the ever -changing audience. By focusing content on the most active and
responsive segmentation, Millen Florist not only increases engagement, but also strengthens
the brand image as an entity that cares, is relevant, and is close to customers.
In the sensemaking theory according to Weick (1995), one important dimension is
plausibility, namely the tendency of individuals or organizations to act based on what is
considered reasonable rather than demanding absolute accuracy or absolute certainty. In the
highly dynamic world of digital communication filled with uncertainties such as algorithm
changes, interaction fluctuations, or rapidly shifting audience trends, organizati ons are
required to be able to interpret situations quickly and responsively  (Weick, 1995 ). This is
where the principle of plausibility becomes very relevant. Instead of waiting for complete data
or in -depth research results, Millen Florist Nusantara often makes decisions based on
experience and strategic intuition that are considered the most logical and appropriate to the
context at that time. This can be seen from the statement of the owner of Millen Florist who
emphasized the importance of improvisation and flexibility in dealing with declining
interactions.

To gain a comprehensive understanding o f how Instagram is used as a sensemaking
medium by Millen Florist Nusantara, this study combines the theoretical approach of the
sensemaking concept by Weick (1995) with empirical data from in-depth interviews with two
key informants from Millen Florist Nusantara: the owner and admin of digital marketing.
Through the process of coding and thematic analysis, it was understood that the two
informants contributed in a complementary manner. Owners tend to play a role in the
strategic aspects and emotional value  of the brand, while admins emphasize more on
technical implementation and daily content management on Instagram.
Table 1 contains four main elements: first, the main nodes or major themes that reflect
the seven dimensions of sensemaking; second, the main focus that explains the specific
aspects of the observed digital communication strategies; third, the roles in sensemaking that
describe the theoretical function of each theme based on Weick's framework; and fourth, the
informant contributions, which summa rize the quotes, views, or actual practices provided by
each informant on each dimension. The structure of this table presents a holistic picture of
how meaning is constructed, managed, and maintained in Millen Florist's digital
communication.
The sensemaking process in Millen Florist's digital communication involves not only
the formulation of effective messages, but also the integration of visual meaning, social
interaction, and adaptation to platform dynamics. In the analysis of each theme, it was found
that each sensemaking dimension is applied in real terms in the organization's social media
practices. For example, the enactment of sensible environment dimension is reflected in a
visual strategy that arouses emotions, while extracted cues are applied through reading
followers' insights and demographics. Meanwhile, identity construction is carried out by
presenting the brand in the customer's personal moments, and ongoing communication is
realized through a consistent content schedule. Even in situations that require improvisation,
such as a decrease in interaction, the team applies plausibility in decision making by holding
giveaways or small campaigns. Evaluation of content performance and social relations built
through DM and comments also show that the retrospective evaluation and social interaction
dimensions are actively carried out.
From all these findings, it can be concluded that the use of Instagram by Millen
Florist Nusantara is not only based on marketing strategies, but involves a complete
sensemaking practice both from the organizational and consumer side. This process involves
combining emotional narratives, utilizing digital data, social engagement, and communication
continuity, thus creating a digital space that is more than just a promotional platform.
Instagram becomes an arena for forming shared meaning, where brand identity, emotional
values, and customer engagement are managed in a strategic and long -term valuable digital
communication ecosystem.

CONCLUSION
This study shows that Instagr am not only functions as a social media for visual promotion,
but has developed into a strategic space for organizations in building meaning with
customers, especially through the sensemaking approach developed by Weick (1995). In the
context of Millen Florist Nusantara, the use of Instagram as a digital communication medium
involves seven dimensions of sensemaking, namely: identity construction, retrospective,
enactment of sensible environments, social, ongoing, extracted cues, and plausibility. These
seven dimensions are actually applied in the practice of managing digital content and
