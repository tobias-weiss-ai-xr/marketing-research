Abstract
This original paper is a research into the impact of digital content marketing on online purchasing behavior of University students in Ho Chi
Minh City. An empirical study was designed to test the effect of content marketing on e-commerce platform (Shopee, TikTok, and Lazada) on
participants’ online purchasing intention via the mediating effect of perceived technological innovativeness and perceived value. A valid sample
of 265 respondents was analyzed by SPSS. PLS-SEM was used to examine the direct and indirect relationships. As a result, all six hypotheses
were supported, confirming the positive impact of marketing content and the two mediators. The finding sheds light on the underexplored
aspects in existing literature of online shopping behavior by confirming the impact of newness and innovativeness of online platforms as well as
the effect of content marketing in information fulfilment. Current paper contributes to both theory and practice, giving managerial implication for
e-commerce digital marketing industry.
Keywords:Digital content marketing; online purchasing behavior; perceived technological innovativeness; perceived value; Stimulus-
Organism-Response.
Introduction
The literature on consumer behavior has proved its
maturity over the last two decades with remarkable
publications (i.e., [ 1–3]). Accordingly , contemporary
theory has been grounded on online shopping be-
havior as a consequence of e-commerce development
and digitalization world-wide. T o date, industry 5.0
adoption and smart phone popularity in industrialized
cities facilitate consumer’s online behavior for either
valuable utilities of online products and services or
innovative technology. Besides, digital marketing has
played an undeniable role in influencing consumer’s
shopping behavior, including intention to purchase,
decision process and buying behaviors. In this respect,
there are sufficient research evidence showing the
emerging positive effect of online marketing: Virtual
reality marketing, digital marketing public relation
factors, digital content marketing, AI technology ,
influencer marketing, etc. However, it is unclear in the
literature as regards the generalized impact of digital
marketing content on a specific group of consumers in
a different context. Thus, this paper aims at University
students’ online shopping behavior in Ho Chi Minh
City for insightful research and empirical study. There
are a couple of reasons for this research target.
First, Ho Chi Minh City (HCM City) is a financial
hub of Vietnam, an internationally recognized emerg-
ing economy. In the context that Vietnam is imple-
menting digitalization program nationwide (749/QĐ-
TTg), HCM City takes the lead in motivating and
fostering the citizens to engage in digital world.
Regardless digital necessity , owning a smart phone
and shopping online via phone have become not
merely a trend in smart cities like HCM, but also
an everyday habit. The city ensures every household
has at least one smart phone in the second quarter of
2026 (https://dost.hochiminhcity.gov .vn). Among the
habitant groups, University student group is one active
consumer group in mobile shopping apps (i.e., Shopee,
TikT ok, and Lazada) thanks to the app convenience,
product variety and utilities [ 4, 5]. The value of this
research target is two-folded. Studying these potential
consumers’ perceptions helps the digital marketers
know how to build the right marketing message and
develop better marketing content on these apps to
generate customer satisfaction and positive purchasing
process. Practically , the research implies a motivation

Theoretical background
Consumer’s online purchasing behavior
Online purchasing behavior can be defined to encom-
pass online affects demonstrating a consumer’s active
or passive intent towards a product/service. In the
literature, the types of online purchasing behavior
were analyzed and measured in diverse angles to
include following behaviors:
•Find the products that best suit their needs [9]
•Online purchasing intention [6]
• Seek recommendations, reviews, and endorse-
ments from influencers whom they perceive as
authentic and relatable [10]
• Merchandise selection and purchase decision [ 11]
•Changes in buying habit and preferences [12]
•Satisfied with and loyal to online shopping [13]
•Online repurchase intention [14]
• Environmentally friendly shopping behavior [ 15]
Over the past decade, digital platforms connecting
brands with consumers have impacted greatly on
purchasing behaviors. The factors having positive
effect confirmed in quantitative studies include the
following:
•Digital marketing public relations [11]
•Digital content marketing [16]
•Virtual reality marketing [17]
•Digital marketing credibility [18]
• Digital advertising on mobile phone, email, social
media
• AI driven-personalization technology and cus-
tomer engagement advertisement [20]
•The influence of Instagram influencers [21]
• The relevance and personal interest in a promoted
product [22]
•Green consumption value [23]
Stimuli-Organism-Response theory
SOR theory (model) is written by Robert Woodworth
[24]. The model added self-driven mediators (organ-
ism) including an individual’s biological factors and
psychological factors. For whichever applied science,
SOR proves the reaction of every individual to any
source of stimuli has to deal with the individual’s
inwardness or inference process.
Since the pioneering theory by Robert Woodworth
[24], SOR has been developed and applied in various
fields [ 25], including Environmental psychology by
Albert Mehrabian and James A. Russell [ 26], Lead-
ership and management theories, Cognitive process
to decision making, Purchasing behavior theory by
Michael J. Houston and Michael L. Rothschild [ 27].
For the long-standing generalizability of the theory
over more than 90 years and the credibility of the
theory in explaining a consumer’s feelings and behav-
iors, current paper adopts SOR to scope consumer’s
online reactions and response to digital stimuli on
e-commerce platform or apps (i.e., Shopee, TikT ok,
and Lazada).

Research Model and Hypotheses
Digital content marketing and perceived technology
innovativeness
Content marketing is conceptualized as to create and
deliver meaningful, appropriate, and consistent con-
tent to have the attention of targeted consumers and to
retain their interest for marketed product/service. In
digital world, content marketing not merely provides
information about products/services but also con-
tributes to brand building, representing a company’s
innovation level and applied technology ability.
Consumer’s perceived technology innovativeness
reflects the degree to which a consumer evaluates
a brand name or a digital platform on leading in-
novation, trend responsiveness, and new technology
applicability. According to diffusion of innovation
theory by Rogers [ 28], consumer’s perception of
innovation plays an important role in developing
consumer’s positive attitude and response towards
companies and their products in the context of fast-
paced technology. Research in information system
implies that consumer’s perceived innovation has a
significant impact on consumer’s attitude and behavior
in the interaction with digital platform [ 29]. In digital
marketing, content shows creativity. Thus, the fact
that a company stays up to date with technology
and new trend will help their consumers increase
knowledge about the innovation level of the brand.
T o students, the consumer group having active inter-
action with new technology and digital marketing,
effective content marketing can enhance brand image
to technology innovator. From the above assumption,
this paper hypothesizes:
Hypothesis 1 (H1): Digital content marketing has a
positive impact on students’ perceived technology
innovativeness.
Digital content marketing and perceived value
Perceived value is defined to be overall evaluation by
consumers on the benefits per cost of using a prod-
uct/service. This perceived value represents not only
perceived functional value, but also emotional aspects
and psychological aspects during consumption. In
modern marketing, content marketing helps in making
and bringing value to consumers. Those contents
provide useful information which are designed to be
easy to understand, entertaining to help consumers to
reduce searching time and cost, increase their knowl-
edge and have positive experience during buying
decision process. Many publications pointed out that
perceived value is a multi-faceted concept, comprising
of functional value, emotional value and social value
and being grandly influenced by marketing activities
[1, 30]. Content marketing has an impact through
supplying information, creating positive emotions and
reinforcing brand image among consumer community.
T o students, the consumer group having limited
financial status and being sensitive to value and
price, digital content marketing has a special effect on
developing their perception of value.
Hypothesis 2 (H2): Digital content marketing has a
positive impact on students’ perceived value.
Perceived technological innovativeness and online
purchasing intention
Consumer’s online purchasing intention is their level
of readiness to buy prior to purchasing behavior on e-
commerce platform. Intention to buy is considered the
important precedent of actual buying behavior. As per
theory of innovation diffusion, those individuals who
have high awareness of technological innovation tend
to accept and use new quicker products/services [ 26].
In e-commerce, consumer’s perceived technological
innovativeness of trademark or platform help them
to reduce their uncertainty and their perceived risk
in online order and transaction. Previous studies
also showed consumers’ awareness of innovation
leads to positive attitude towards digital platform,
urging intention to get and buy products/services
online. T o students, the consumer group being familiar
with digital technology , their perceived technological
innovativeness can have a strong impact on their
intention to buy.
Hypothesis 3 (H3): Student’s perceived technological
innovativeness has a positive impact on students’
online purchasing intention.

Perceived value and online purchasing intention
Perceived value is considered one of the most impor-
tant determinants of consumer’s purchasing intention.
When consumers feel beneficial from the cost, they
are more likely to buy [ 30]. In this respect, studies
on consumer behavior affirmed that perceived value
is in a close relation with purchasing intention and
customer’s loyalty [1]. In online shopping context, per-
ceived value is derived from either prices or utilities,
from emotional experience and social benefits/value
brought about by a product/service. T o students,
perceived value seems to be more important due to
their income limitation and their value consideration
prior to placing the order.
Hypothesis 4 (H4): Students’ perceived value has
a positive impact on students’ online purchasing
intention.
Mediating effect of perceived technological innova-
tiveness and perceived value
The logic of SOR model implies that the stimulus
from external environment do not affect directly
consumer behavior, but indirectly via the individual’s
awareness state and psychological internalization.
Current paper assumes that content marketing plays
a role of stimuli, meanwhile, perceived technological
innovativeness and perceived value reflect student’s
state of awareness towards marketed product/service.
In a deeper sense, the digital content is purposefully
designed by the marketer to catch views quickly and
provide essential information about the product in
the first place. Therefore, this stimulus has a powerful
effect in inviting a student-shopper to read more
about the advertising message. Additionally , modern
content marketing on the apps creates meaningful
engagements with users [ 31], making them trust and
stay. New technology is AI-driven application and
interactive servant functions on the apps, including
customer reviews, user comments, direct conversa-
tion between the buyer and the seller, and buying
policies benefiting customer’s needs. Experiencing the
shopping time, a student-shopper will consequently
feel satisfied with the interesting functions and with
product/service information which are easy to access.
Especially to gen Z students, this source of affection is
expected to be not only new tech and trendy but also
attractive offer, because they are low-income smart
phone user.
Furthermore, having contact with content market-
ing probably changes the degree to which students
evaluate the innovativeness and the value of the brand
and the product, indirectly influencing their online
purchasing intention. Taken together, current paper
posits that the two variables, perceived technological
innovativeness and perceived value, are mediators in
the influencing relationship between digital content
marketing and online purchasing intention.
Hypothesis 5 (H5): Student’s perceived technological
innovativeness mediates the relationship between
digital content marketing and students’ online
purchasing intention.
Hypothesis 6 (H6): Student’s perceived value me-
diates the relationship between digital content
marketing and students’ online purchasing intention.
Method And Sample
Data collection
A quantitative study was conducted in February -
March 2026 to survey University students in Ho Chi
Minh City about their shopping behaviors on Shopee,
TikT ok, and Lazada to test the research model. A
questionnaire in Google Form was sent to invitees
via emails, social media and learning groups. The
invitees were introduced about the study objectives,
the ethics of current research project and the ethics
of data collection that ensure anonymity of their
answer as well as their identity. Before the survey ,
the participants were explained about each concept,
variable, and statement. The participants joined volun-
tarily and were asked to rate their answer on 5-Likert
scale from (1) totally disagree to (5) totally agree to
questions about digital content marketing, perceived
technological innovativeness, perceived value, online
purchasing intention.
There were 300 replied forms and the final valid
sample includes 265 respondents who already ex-
perienced shopping on those e-commerce platform
or apps. Following the inverse square root method
by Kock and Hadaya [ 32] to calculate the smallest
sample size in PLS-SEM, the valid sample should be
at least 155. The popular sample size in PLS-SEM
ranges from 150 to 200. Thus, current study sample of
265 is credible to achieve a statistical power of 80%.
Table 1 below represents the demographical statistics
of the sample.
Measurement
Current paper adopts previous empirical studies’
measurements with minor modifications (Table 2.1–2.4
below). SPSS was used to input the data and analyze
the data. T o test the valid of the measurement, PLS-
SEM was used to examine the internal consistency re-
liability - Cronbach’s Alpha > 0.7, composite reliability ,
convergent validity , and discriminant validity [ 33, 34].
Next, to test the hypotheses, multicollinearity test
using variance inflation factor (VIF) was conducted,
coefficient of determination (R2) and path coefficient
(f2) were evaluated. The structural model assessment
results indicate that all VIF values for the predictor
constructs associated with each endogenous latent
variable are below 5, the threshold proposed by Hair
et al. [ 34]. Consequently , empirical evidence does not
reveal any serious issues regarding collinearity within
the structural model.

Discussion
Digital content marketing has a positive impact on
students’ perceived technology innovativeness.
Table 4 shows Digital content marketing (CM) has a
statistically significance and strong effect on Perceived
technological innovativeness (TI) ( β = 0,594; p-value
< 0,05). This result signifies if students have frequent
interaction with online message of a product/service
being digitalized marketed on e-commerce platform,
they are likely to evaluate those platform to be modern,
trendy , able to innovate. This result is consistent with
previous studies about the crucial role of content on
developing the awareness of consumers. In this regard,
some profound findings by Holliman and Rowley [ 35],
Lou and Xie [ 36] indicated that content marketing
is not merely informative but also contributive to
defining a brand image and the level of innovation
being perceived by consumers. Current paper assumes
that online content marketing is an indicator of the
platform’s technology capability which consumers
can evaluate upon. On one hand, content marketing
plays a role in dealing with retaining a shopper
and motivating this one to pursue this offer. This
is the reason why some marketing strategists adopted
entertaining techniques, for example, virtual reality of
a product [ 17], advertisement by a showbiz star – the
influencer [ 10], amusing icons and music. Relatedly ,
current study confirmed a platform’s technology
capability mainly by its modernness and continuous
improvement. This measurement is different from pre-
vious studies (i.e., [ 39, 40]) using TAM – Technology
Acceptance Model which examined technology in the
aspect of perceived usefulness, perceived ease-of-use,
and readiness. The meaning of current paper’s content
marketing is to satisfy a shopper’s demand for new
tech and high tech which need to be improved along
with customer survey and market survey. On the other
hand, the purpose of this content marketing strategy
is to serve a shopper’s shopping time, including read-
ing activity , searching activity , and order processing
activity. Therefore, an improvement may be requested
for technical issue, function advancement, safety , or
speed-up. These dimensions are still underexplored
by current literature of online shopping and are room
for future studies.
Digital content marketing has a positive impact on
students’ perceived value.
Table 4 shows Digital content marketing (CM) has
a strong effect on Perceived value ( β = 0,621; p-
value < 0,05). This result entails that digital content
being designed creatively and suitably is able to foster
students’ perception about the benefits, worthiness of
product/service and their positive experience while
shopping. This result is consistent with previous
studies by Zeithaml [ 30] and Sweeney and Soutar [ 1].
The authors stated that perceived value is formed by
consumers’ consideration on benefit and cost. Indeed,
in online platform, content marketing clarifies the
benefit of product usage, reduces uncertainty and
supports decision making, leading to increased level of
perceived value by consumers, especially students who
care more about cost and innovation information. In
this respect, future studies are encouraged to examine
the impact of financial status as control variable of
student’s perceived purchase benefit.
Student’s perceived technological innovativeness has
a positive impact on students’ online purchasing
intention.
H3 is confirmed ( β = 0,312; p-value < 0,05), adding
proof to the assumption that if students find the
platform/apps modern, innovative, high tech, they are
likely to choose online shopping and prioritize online
shopping in near future. This result is consistent with
the theory of diffusion of innovation [ 26], which posits
that awareness about the innovativeness and moder-
nity of technology is an important factor pushing the
intention to accept and use a product/service. In e-
commerce context, high technology factor is a key to
building customer trust and reducing consumer’s per-
ceived risk. Because of the nature of online marketing
creating uncertainty about the product quality and the
brand credibility , a shopper inherently perceives high
tech as cyber security. Moreover, function innovation
and policy innovation also increase shopper’s certainty
about the product/the delivery. One example is when
active communication function is enabled, a shopper
feels psychologically safe with this seller because
the seller is accessible at any time and can stay
connected. Another example is when payment options
are diversified, a shopper feels more at ease to choose
whichever suits them the most (i.e., Pay at delivery
on Shopee, Pay by credit card on TikT ok).

students’ online purchasing intention.

with previous studies by Zeithaml [ 30] and Pavlou
[37] in which the authors share the same viewpoint
on perceived value as one of important predictors
for actual purchasing behavior in online context. This
result sheds light on existing literature of consumer’s

of the technological innovativeness of the brand or
their product/service. This result can be explained
further by SOR model chain of effect which links the
information signal in content marketing to consumer’s
intention behaviors via consumer’s interest about the
technology. The fact that this mediator effect was
not statistically strong in current study is probably
because of the alteration of students’ perceived risks in
online shopping in Vietnam which are found in recent
publications to be the inhibitor [ 7, 8]. Because studies
on technological innovativeness and technology inno-
vation as mediators were missing in the literature of
online consumer behavior, it is too early to conclude
about the prediction possibility of this variable. Prior
to this study , only one study [16] was found to examine
technology innovation as mediator, however, this one’s
approach is different from current study’s approach.
While Alnasser and Alsadoun [ 16] measured technol-
ogy innovation by the participant’s self-report about
their personal interest for new technology , current
study measured technological innovativeness by the
participant’s evaluation about online platform’s new
technology and their ability to innovate continuously.
For those reasons, more empirical studies are needed
to either consolidate current study’s mediation effect
or replicate the study.
Student’s perceived value mediates the relationship
between digital content marketing and students’
online purchasing intention.
Table 5 also confirmed Perceived value as a mediator.
The effect was positively humble ( β = 0,237; p-value
< 0,05). This result is logic from the perspective
of consumer psychology theory , such that content
marketing helps consumers recognize the benefits
(utilities), save time and cost in searching, and gain
shopping experience. The exist of further consideration
or hesitation during information evaluation can be
explained by the other moderators. For example, the
quality of product and information [ 41], promotion
information or perceived trustworthiness [6]. Perceived
value has been proved over decades as mediator and
moderator in the field of consumer behavior, including
both customer satisfaction and customer loyalty. How-
ever, in contemporary literature of digital marketing,
no studies examined perceived value as shopper’s real
benefit. Thus, current’s study positive result is a good
signal for further research. Current paper encourages
researchers in the field to investigate perceived value
as regards quality and personal aspects, because the
shopping trend is towards sustainable consumption
[3] and personalization [42].
Conclusion
This original research is set out to test a SOR model
in the context of online shopping. The main purpose
of the empirical study is to survey University students
in Ho Chi Minh City for their perceptions towards
advertisement message from the sellers on e-commerce

examined on digital content marketing. The SOR
model was validated, and all six hypotheses were
supported. This paper has both practical contribution
and theoretical contribution. Practically , current paper
presumes that students in Ho Chi Minh City are
stereotype consumer group. On one hand, they are
at low/limited income and therefore sensitive to
value information regarding price, product quality ,
utilities, promotion. On the other hand, they are high
tech-oriented generation and likely to be attracted

significant results on their positive perceptions and
purchasing intention prove their interest for the prod-

behavior and repurchasing behavior at a suitable
timing. Theoretically , by analyzing the interplay of
digital content marketing and technological inno-

smart functions, ...), current research shows more
empirical evidence of contemporary conceptual model
of consumer behavior. Furthermore, the researched
SOR model is not limited to explaining student’s on-
line shopping behavior in HCM City , current paper’s
model extends SOR literature to the degree that dis-
covers the most important determinants of a shopper’s
intention to purchase and buying behavior. The logic
is consumers sharing same demographic character-
istics (age, income, digital technology-orientation) as
students are likely to have same online purchasing
behavior tendency. Therefore, current paper’s model
is possibly used by future studies to predict its effect
in a new national context and/or for a new consumer
group. Brief, current paper contributes a SOR model
of consumer’s online behavior to academic commu-
nity , recommending practical application on digital
marketing industry and shopping apps development.
The following is research implication for practitioners
and researchers.
Improve quality and information in content market-
ing
Research results imply content marketing affect
positively consumer’s awareness and support their
decision-making process with valuable information.
Enterprises should develop content marketing in the
manner that both digital concept and advertisement
message show useful, clear and valuable information
to product/service seekers. In other words, content
marketing should be built with the purpose of making
the targeted consumers understand product’s features,
usage, and benefits via product description, product
review , usage instruction or product benchmarking.
In so doing, reliable and full information will help
reduce uncertainty and increase trust in digital plat-
form/brand. Future research is encouraged to examine
the quality of information in digital shopping apps,
especially the credibility of information to bridge
the literature gap in students’ perceived risk and
uncertainty in online shopping.
Increase the attractiveness and creativity of content
marketing
Research results also point out the power of content
marketing in making consumers feel interested while
interacting with digital information. This implies
the enterprises should invest in content design to
satisfy the criteria of consumer attraction. T o student-
consumers, the active users on social media and digital
platforms, content marketing needs to be appealed
creative, lively , easy to access, and diversified. Some
trendy practices include short video, storytelling mar-
keting, entertaining content, and interactive content
to increase the attractiveness level and the spillover
effect of the informative message. When the content
becomes attractive and easier to access, consumers
have the likelihood to spend more time on searching
more information about the product, forming intention
to buy. Because of the existence of students’ hesitation
in decision process, future research is needed to
investigate some moderation relationship for SOR
model.
Encourage new technology adoption in e-commerce
platform/apps
The findings on the influencing effect of student’s
perceived technological innovativeness reassure about
this consumer group’s concern for technological inno-
vation issues. Therefore, for e-commerce enterprises,
it’s worth investing in innovative technology which
helps consumers in online experiencing process. T o
date, the adoption of AI in product suggestions,
chatbot in conversation with customers, personalized
content marketing and consumer data analysis tools
all improve the effectiveness of customer interaction.
Given literature gap in AI application, future research
can testify the effect of AI functions and emotional
aspects in robot-human interaction.
Increase customer’s perceived value while shopping
online
The results show greater effect on students’ perceived
value (PV) and that this variable is a stronger mediator.
This finding implies the students’ dependence on the
benefits or utilities of getting a product to make buying
decision. In other words, besides the satisfaction about
technology issues, the students are more likely to
rely on value evaluation for their purchase. Thus,
enterprises should focus on improving the content
in the manner that give informative value regarding
price, product itself, and benefits. In fact, knowing
students’ psychological needs while shopping online,
some brands targeting student consumers choose to
offer good quality products at reasonable price. At
the same time, they try to improve delivery speed
and build clear policy on product return to ease their
buying decision. Besides, practitioners can optimize
user experience on their website or shopping apps to
help students perceive the valuable utilities of having
the products. This helps consumers feel the benefits

the tendency is higher for them to get the products

Build content marketing strategy in longer term to
retain customer loyalty
T o conclude, digital content marketing is not merely
for pushing online purchasing behaviors in short term,
but also contributive to long term relationship building
with customers. Overall, enterprises should see digital
content marketing as a long-term strategy , and not
an advertising tool only. Table 5 shows a strong total
indirect effect ( β = 0,422) of the two mediators in
the correlation between digital marketing content and
online purchasing intention. This finding implies a
powerful combined effect of consumer’s perceived
technological innovativeness and perceived value in
the prediction of their buying decision or loyalty to the
brand/shopping platform. T o adopt this SOR model
into effect, practitioners can put in quality informa-
tion satisfying the targeted consumers’ demand or
expectation on what matter to them the most. For
example, putting in educational content, knowledge
sharing and experience sharing. This application will
further build a good brand image and trustworthiness
in the mind of their consumers.
