Abstract

Digital marketing offers micro and small enterprises opportunities to expand market reach, increase consumer
engagement, and strengthen competitiveness. Some wooden batik business owners in Krebet Hamlet, Bantul Regency,
already use social media; however, not all have optimized digital media as a marketing channel. This stud y aims to
analyze the effect of digital marketing on income. The study employed a quantitative survey approach using purposive
sampling. The sample consisted of 57 wooden batik micro and small enterprise (MSE) owners in Krebet who use social
media and the Shopee marketplace and are members of the Sido Katon Cooperative in Krebet Hamlet. Data were
collected through questionnaires, observation, and a literature review. Simple linear regression analysis was used to
determine the magnitude of the effect of digital marketing on business income. The results show that digital marketing
affects business income. This study is expected to provide empirical evidence that implementing digital marketing can
increase the income of MSE owners by expanding market access, im proving promotional effectiveness, strengthening
customer relationships, and reducing marketing costs. A limitation of this study is that it analyzes only one variable,
namely digital marketing, as a determinant of income, whereas other variables such as b usiness capital, skills,
education, and knowledge may be examined in future research. The findings are expected to provide a basis for local
governments, academics, and business owners to formulate human resource capacity -building strategies through the
development of digital marketing competencies to support the sustainability of micro and small enterprises.

INTRODUCTION
Digital transformation has become one of the most influential forces shaping the business
world over the past decade. The growth of the internet, social media, mobile devices, and various
digital platforms has changed how consumers search for information, compare products, make
transactions, and post reviews of purchased products. These changes have encouraged businesses
to adopt more adaptive marketing strategies by using digital technology as part of efforts to
improve competitiveness and business sustainability.
Digital marketing is a form of m arketing innovation that enables companies and
microenterprises to reach consumers more broadly without being constrained by space and time.
Various digital media, such as social media, marketplaces, websites, instant messaging applications,
and search engines, provide substantial opportunities for businesses to introduce products, build
customer relationships, and increase sales volume. Compared with conventional marketing, digital
marketing offers advantages in cost efficiency, ease of measuring promotional performance, more
precise market segmentation, and the ability to reach consumers at both national and international

In Indonesia, digitalizing micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) has become a
priority for national economic development. The government continues to promote digital
transformation through various training, mentoring, and digital ecosystem development programs
for business owners. Nevertheless, gaps remain in the level of digital technology adoption among
MSEs. Many business owners still face constraints such as low digital literacy, limited ability to
create marketing content, insufficient understandin g of digital marketing strategies, and
suboptimal use of e-commerce platforms and social media.
Krebet Hamlet in Bantul Regency, Special Region of Yogyakarta, is a handicraft center with
substantial potential for development through digital marketing. Kreb et Hamlet is known as a
wooden batik handicraft center that produces a variety of high -artistic-value products, such as
masks, statues, household items, and souvenirs marketed to both domestic and international
tourists. Krebet wooden batik products are di stinctive because they combine traditional batik
techniques with wood as the medium, resulting in products with both cultural and high economic
value.
Despite this considerable potential, some wooden batik MSEs in Krebet Hamlet still rely on
conventional m arketing through tourist visits, exhibitions, and distributor networks. This
dependence on traditional marketing patterns results in a relatively limited market reach and
makes sales highly sensitive to economic conditions, tourist volumes, and shifts in c onsumer
behavior. This situation became particularly evident during the COVID-19 pandemic, when tourism
declined and directly affected sales of handicraft products.
The shift in consumer behavior toward digital transactions presents both a challenge and
an opportunity for wooden batik MSEs. Consumers now obtain more product information through
social media, marketplaces, and search engines before making purchases. Therefore, business
owners' ability to use digital marketing is an important factor that can d etermine product
marketing success. The use of social media platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and
WhatsApp Business, as well as marketplaces such as Shopee and Tokopedia, has the potential to
increase product visibility, expand customer networ ks, and strengthen consumer trust through
attractive and interactive information.
Several previous studies have shown that digital marketing is positively associated with
improved business performance. As'adi and Sularsih ( 2022) found that digital transformation
contributes to increased MSME income by improving marketing effectiveness and expanding
market access. Firmansyah et al. (2022)  also reported that digital transformation has a po sitive
effect on business performance, particularly through increased operational efficiency and a greater
ability to reach consumers. In addition, Hadiono et al. (2023)  explained that digital marketing
innovation can improve business competitiveness through the optimal use of digital technology.
Nevertheless, research on the effect of digital marketing on the income of wooden batik
MSEs in Krebet Hamlet remains relatively limited. Most previous studies focus on MSMEs in
general, whereas culture -based handicraft businesses face distinct challenges, particularly in
marketing, market segmentation, and the development of product added value. Therefore, research
is needed that specifically examines the relationship between digi tal marketing and increased
income in the wooden batik handicraft center of Krebet Hamlet.
This study aims to analyze the effect of digital marketing on the income of wooden batik
MSEs in Krebet Hamlet, Bantul Regency. In this study, digital marketing is viewed as the use of social
media as a communication channel that enables business owners to build direct relationships with
consumers. Platforms such as Instagram, WhatsApp, and TikTok provide opportunities to increase
brand awareness, promote products, and build customer loyalty through the delivery of engaging
content.

LITERATURE REVIEW

Digital Marketing
The development of information technology has shifted the marketing paradigm from
conventional approaches to digital  marketing. Digital marketing is the process of marketing
products or services through digital media to reach a wider range of consumers, build long -term
relationships, and increase customer value. The use of the internet, social media, search engines,
mobile applications, and electronic commerce platforms enables businesses to conduct marketing
activities more effectively, efficiently, and measurably.
According to Kotler et al. (2021), digital marketing is part of the evol ution of marketing,
integrating digital technology with human interaction to create more personalized customer
experiences. In the Marketing 5.0 era, technology serves not only as a communication tool but also
as a means of creating value through data, artificial intelligence, and marketing automation.
Chaffey and Ellis -Chadwick (2022)  explains that digital marketing is the use of digital
technology to acquire customers, maintain customer relationships, and increase custom er value
through integrated digital communication. This concept emphasizes the importance of using
multiple digital channels in an integrated manner to ensure marketing strategies achieve optimal
performance.
Ryan (2021)  states that digital marketing is the process of building two -way
communication between companies and consumers through digital media. Unlike traditional
marketing, which is one -way, digital marketing gives consumers opportunities to provide direct
feedback, thereby creating more interactive relationships.
In the context of micro enterprises, digital marketing plays an increasingly important role
because it can help overcome limitations in promotional budgets. Social media, marketplaces, and
communication appli cations enable business owners to introduce their products to a broader
market at relatively low cost compared with conventional media.

Dimensions of Digital Marketing
The measurement of digital marketing in this study draws on the concepts developed by
Chaffey and Ellis -Chadwick (2022) , Ryan (2021) , and Kotler et al. (2021) , which include the
following key dimensions.
1. Social Media Marketing
Social media serves as a communication channel that enables business owners to build
direct relationships with consumers. Platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, TikTok,
and YouTube provide opportunities to increase brand awareness, promote products,
and build customer loyalty by delivering engaging content.
2. Marketplace
A marketplace is a digital platform that connects buyers and sellers within an electronic

trading system. Using marketplaces such as Shopee and Tokopedia can expand
marketing reach, increase consumer trust, and facilitate transactions.
3. Content Marketing
Content marketing is a strategy for delivering valuable, relevant, and engaging
information to build long -term customer relationships. High -quality content can
increase consumer engagement and encourage purchasing decisions.
4. Website
A website serves as a business's digital identity by providing information about
products, the business profile, and customer communication channels. An informative
and easily accessible website can enhance business credibility.
5. Digital Communication
Digital communication through applications such as WhatsApp Business, email, and
social media enables business owners to provide faster, more responsive, and more
personalized service, thereby increasing customer satisfaction.

MSE Income
Income is one of the primary indicators of business success. According to Sukirno (2019),
income is the revenue a business receives from the sale of goods or services over a specific period.
An increase in income signals growth in business activities and reflects the effectiveness of
marketing and operational strategies.
From a marketing management perspective, income is influenced not only by product
quality but also by a business's ability to reach markets, build customer relationships, and increase
consumer loyalty. Thus, an effective marketing strategy is a key factor in increasing business
income.
For micro enterprises, income is often measured using several indicators, namely:
1. Growth in sales turnover;
2. Increase in the number of customers;
3. Increase in transaction frequency;
4. Increase in business profit; and
5. Income growth over a certain period.

Relationship between Digital Marketing and Income
Digital marketing enables business owners to expand their markets without geographical
limitations. Through digital media, products can reach consumers in different regions and even
abroad. In addition, digital marketing makes it easier for business owners to obtain information
about consumer behavior so that promotional strategies can be adjusted to market needs.
According to Kotler et al. (2021) , digital technology improves marketing effectiveness by
making communication with customers faster, more personalized, and more interac tive. Chaffey
and Ellis -Chadwick (2022)  adds that digital media enable companies to measure promotional
effectiveness in real time, allowing marketing decisions to be made more accurately.
For wooden batik MSEs in Krebet Hamlet, digital marketing is expected to increase the
number of customers through broader promotion, create more transaction opportunities, and
increase business income. Digital marketing also helps reduce dependence on tourist visits because
products can be marketed online to consumers in various locations.

RESEARCH METHOD
This study used a quantitative survey with purposive sampling. The sample comprised 57
wooden batik MSE owners in Krebet who use social media and the Shopee marketplace and are
members of the Sido Katon Cooperative in Krebet Hamlet. Data were collected via questionnaires,
surveys, and a literature review. Simple linear regression was used to assess the magnitude of the

Research Design
This study used a quantitative approach with a survey method. The quantitative approach
was selected because the study aims to test the effect of digital marketing on the income of wooden
batik micro and small enterprises (MSEs) in Krebet Hamlet, Bantul Regency, using statistical
hypothesis testing. The survey method was used to collect primary data from respondents through
a questionnaire developed based on the indicators of the research variables.

Research Location
The study was conducted in Krebet Hamlet, Sendangsari Village, Pajangan District, Bantul
Regency, Special  Region of Yogyakarta. Krebet Hamlet was selected as the research location
because it is a wooden batik handicraft center that has developed into a creative tourism
destination and has considerable potential for digital marketing.

Population and Sample
The study population comprised all wooden batik MSME owners actively operating
businesses in Krebet Hamlet. Based on data from the artisan group and the local government, the
study included 57 MSME owners.
The sampling technique used was purposive sampling, a method of selecting samples based
on specific criteria so that respondents matched the study's objectives. The respondent criteria
were as follows:
1. Own a wooden batik business that is still actively operating.
2. Have operated the business for at least one year.
3. Have used or are familiar with digital media as a means of product promotion or sales.
4. Are willing to participate as research respondents.
5. Are MSMEs registered with the Sido Katon Cooperative.
Because 57 people met these criteria, all of them were included in the research sample.

Types and Sources of Data
The study used two types of data:
1. Primary data were obtained through questionnaires and field observations.
2. Secondary data were obtained from scientific books, national and international journals,
government reports, publications from relevant institutions, and documents relevant to the
study.

Data Collection Techniques
The data collection techniques consisted of:
1. Survey to obtain an overview of business activities and the implementation of digita l
marketing.
2. Questionnaire, the primary instrument for collecting data on respondents' perceptions of
the research variables.
3. Literature review conducted by examining various sources related to digital marketing and
business income.

Data Analysis Techniques
The data analysis tools used to test the hypothesis included simple linear regression, a t -
test for partial testing, and the coefficient of determination (R²) to assess the goodness of fit of the

Operational Definition of Variables
This study uses one independent variable and one dependent variable.

1. Independent Variable (X): Digital Marketing
Digital marketing encompasses marketing activities that use digital media to promote
products, build customer relationships, and increase sales. The digital marketing
indicators, based on Chaffey and Ellis -Chadwick (2022) , Kotler et al. (2021) , and Ryan
(2021), include:
1. Use of social media.
2. Use of online marketplaces.
3. Quality of digital content.
4. Use of websites or other digital media.
5. Intensity of digital communication with customers.

2. Dependent Variable (Y): Income
Income is the total revenue earned by MSME owners from the sale of wooden batik
products over a specific period. Income indicators include:
1. Increase in sales turnover.
2. Increase in the number of customers.
3. Increase in transaction frequency.
4. Increase in business profit.
5. Growth in business income.

CONCLUSIONS
In digital marketing, almost all MSMEs use social media platforms such as Instagram and
WhatsApp, along with substantial use of the Shopee marketplace. The majority of respondents gave
positive assessments of the digital marketing variable, indicating that  digital marketing was
considered 62.4% effective in increasing MSME income in the area. Assessments of the income
variable also showed positive results.
Simple linear regression analysis shows that the digital marketing variable (X) has a
positive and significant effect on MSME income (Y). A regression coefficient of 0.624 indicates that
each 1% increase in digital marketing use is associated with a 0.624-unit increase in MSME income.
The partial test (t -test) yielded a significance value of 0.020 —lower than the 0.05 threshold —so
the null hypothesis (H0) was rejected and the alternative hypothesis (H1) was accepted. This
confirms that digital marketing has a significant effect on MSME income in Krebet, Pajangan, Bantul.

LIMITATIONS & FURTHER RESEARCH
A limitation of this study is that it examines only one digital marketing variable affecting
business income, whereas other variables, such as business capital, human resource skills,
education, and knowledge, may be examined in future research. The findings ar e expected to
provide a basis for local governments, academics, and business owners to formulate human
resource capacity -building strategies through digital marketing development to support the
sustainability of micro and small enterprises.
