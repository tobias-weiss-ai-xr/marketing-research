Abstract
The event organizer industry in Bali is becoming increasingly competitive, requiring companies to continuously build
and maintain customer loyalty. This study aims to analyze the effects of service quality, digital marketing, and unique
selling proposition (USP) on customer loyalty and to examine the mediating role of brand trust at Energy Event ID. This
study employed a quantitative approach with a causal associative research design. The population consisted of all
Energy Event ID customers from 2023 to 20 25, totaling 313 customers. A sample of 176 respondents was determined
using the Slovin formula and selected through simple random sampling. Data were collected through online
questionnaires and analyzed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Mode ling (PLS-SEM) with SmartPLS 4.1
software. The study was grounded in the Stimulus -Organism-Response (SOR) Theory, where service quality, digital
marketing, and USP acted as stimuli, brand trust as the organism, and customer loyalty as the response. The res ults
indicate that brand trust was found to be the most influential variable affecting customer loyalty, with the highest path
coefficient value of 0.410. Furthermore, service quality, digital marketing, and USP have positive and significant effects
on brand trust. Brand trust also has a positive and significant effect on customer loyalty. The mediation analysis reveals
that brand trust partially and complementarily mediates the effects of service quality, digital marketing, and USP on
customer loyalty. The  R-square value of customer loyalty is 0.732, indicating that 73.2 percent of the variance in
customer loyalty can be explained by the proposed model, while the remaining 26.8 percent is explained by other factors
outside the model.
Keywords: Service Quality; Digital Marketing; Unique Selling Proposition; Brand Trust; Customer Loyalty; Event

1. Introduction
The event organizer (EO) industry has experienced significant growth in recent years, driven by the increasing demand
from individuals and organizations for various types of events. This growth is reflected in the rising number of
companies offering similar services, resulting in increasingly intense comp etition (Moko, 2019; Prihatin, 2021). Bali's
prominence as a world -renowned tourism destination has positioned the region as a strategic location for hosting a
wide range of events, including conferences, exhibitions, concerts, corporate gatherings, and la rge-scale international
events. One of the event organizer companies actively managing both national and international events is Energy Event
ID. The company has been operating for approximately 15 years and has established collaborations with numerous
corporate clients across Indonesia and internationally. Its extensive experience in managing diverse types of events has
positioned Energy Event ID as one of the well-recognized event organizer companies in Bali. Energy Event ID primarily
specializes in organizing corporate events.

The customer base of Energy Event ID is predominantly composed of corporate clients, including national and
multinational companies, brands and private enterprises, government institutions, as well as organizations operating
within the meetings, incentives, conferences, and exhibitions (MICE) industry.
Within the event management service industry, customer loyalty is conceptually regarded as a strategic asset that
determines long -term business sustainability. This condition encourages companies not only to focus on increasing
sales but also to establish long-term relationships with customers by enhancing customer satisfaction and delivering
superior customer experiences (Fitriani & Asniwati, 2023). The issue has become increasingly complex due to rapid
digital technological advancements that have signific antly transformed consumer behavior. Service quality represents
a fundamental element in building customer trust and loyalty. High service quality has been shown to exert a direct
positive influence on brand trust and customer loyalty (Mariana et al., 2021 ; Ng et al., 2024). Moreover, increasingly
intense market competition requires companies not only to rely on service quality and digital marketing but also to
establish clear differentiation through a Unique Selling Proposition (USP).
This phenomenon indicates a discrepancy between theoretical expectations and actual business practices.
Theoretically, service quality, digital marketing, and USP are expected to foster brand trust, which subsequently
enhances customer loyalty. However, in  practice, these relationships are not always consistent. Aggressive digital
marketing may elevate customer expectations beyond what the company can realistically deliver, inconsistent service
quality can immediately undermine customer trust, and the absen ce of meaningful differentiation may encourage
customers to switch to competing service providers.

Although numerous previous studies have examined the influence of service quality, digital marketing, and Unique
Selling Proposition (USP) on customer loyalty, most of these studies have been conducted separately and have not
integrated all variables into a comprehensive research model. Furthermore, the role of brand trust as a mediating
variable in the relationship between service quality, digital marketing, and USP and customer loyalty has also produced
mixed findings. Several studies have found that bran d trust plays a strong mediating role in building customer loyalty
(Ng et al., 2024; Ningrum et al., 2021), while other studies have shown that not all digital marketing activities are able
to directly enhance customer trust or customer decisions (Wulandari et al., 2025). These differences in findings indicate
inconsistencies in previous research results that require further investigation.
This study will also employ the Stimulus–Organism–Response (SOR) Theory as its theoretical foundation. This study is
expected not only to provide a theoretical contribution to the development of marketing management knowledge but
also to offer practical implications for companies in formulating more effective marketing strategies, improving service
quality, and strengthening differentiation through the Unique Selling Proposition (USP) to build customer trust and
loyalty sustainably amid the increasingly competitive event organizer industry.
2. Research methods
This study was conducted at Energy Event ID, a leading event organizer company in Bali that focuses on organizing
various local, national, and international events. The exogenous variables in this study act as the stimulus (S) within the
Stimulus–Organism–Response (SOR) Theory framework. The endogenous variable (Y) examined in this study is
Customer Loyalty (Y). The mediating variable is Brand Trust (M).
The population of this study consists of all customers of Energy Event ID during the 2023 –2025 period, with a total
population of 313 customers. The sample in this study comprises a portion of Energy Event ID customers who meet
specific criteria, namely cu stomers who have used the company's services at least once during the 2023 –2025 period.
Based on the Slovin formula, the sample size obtained was 175.6, which was then rounded up to 176 respondents, as
the sample size cannot be expressed as a decimal.
The sampling technique used in this study was probability sampling with the simple random sampling method. The data
collection method employed in this study was a survey method, which is a primary data collection technique conducted
by distributing questio nnaires to respondents who were customers of Energy Event ID. To examine the relationships
among the variables in this study, Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) based on Partial Least Squares (PLS) was
employed.

Based on Table 2, the research results show that digital marketing, service quality, unique selling proposition (USP),
and brand trust influence customer loyalty in Energy Event ID. The higher the quality of digital marketing implemented,
the better the quality of service provided, and the stronger the company's uniqueness and differentiating value, the
higher customer loyalty to Energy Event ID. These results support the research of Xu et al. (2025), which found that
personal interactions and the quality of service facilities have a positive effect on customer loyalty.
The results also show that digital marketing influences brand trust. This finding indicates that informative, interactive,
and consistent digital marketing activities can increase customer trust in Energy Event ID. Customers who receive clear
and transparent information through digital media tend to have greater confidence in the company's credibility. These
results support the research of Mesquita (2024), which proved that digital marketing has a direct, positive and
significant effect on customer loyalty. An optimal digital marketing strategy can create customer satisfaction throughout
the information search process and through service use, ultimately driving customer loyalty. Similar findings were also
presented by Putri (2021), who stated that improving digital marketing strategies significantly increases customer
loyalty.
Service quality has been shown to influence brand trust, demonstrating that professional, responsive service that meets
customer expectations can increase customer trust in a company. The better the service quality perceived by customers,
the greater their confidence in Energy Event ID's capabilities and integrity. This study aligns with research conducted
by Nina Rosalina (2009), which found that a unique selling proposition influences customer loyalty.
A Unique Selling Proposition (USP) has also been shown to influence brand trust. This demonstrates that Energy Event
ID's unique advantages and characteristics can create positive perceptions and increase customer confidence in the
company. The uniqueness of the service offered is a crucial factor in building customer trust amidst the increasingly
competitive event organizer industry. This study's findings are also supported by Aprilyani and Syah (2023), who stated
that in a high-risk industry, service quality is the most effective stimulus for increasing customer trust.
Brand trust has been shown to influence customer loyalty, demonstrating that the higher the level of customer trust in
Energy Event ID, the higher the customer loyalty. This established trust encourages customers to continue using the
company's services, recommend it to others, and establish long-term relationships with the company. The results of this
