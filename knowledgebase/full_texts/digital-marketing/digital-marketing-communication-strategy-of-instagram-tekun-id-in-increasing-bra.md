The purpose of this study is to explain the
implementation of digital marketing
communication strategies carried out by Tekun by
utilizing the @tekun.id Instagram account through
the AISAS (Attention, Interest, Search, Action, and
Share) model. The researc h method employs a
descriptive qualitative approach, utilizing data
collection techniques such as observation,
documentation, in-depth interviews, and literature
review. The object of research is the Instagram
account @tekun.id, with seven informants and
three sources to explore and analyze digital
marketing strategies in depth. The results pointed
out that Tekun succeeded in increasing brand
awareness through the application of the AISAS
model. At the attention phase, Tekun attracts
audiences with structur ed content and various
media that convey religious messages. Meanwhile,
in the interest phase, partnering with local
influencers and creating content that aligns with
the company's brand image helps to spark interest.
In addition, in the search phase, Teku n prioritizes
making information easily accessible through
website links and the use of relevant hashtags. At
the Action stage, clear instructions and exclusive
promotions encourage purchase, while at the Share
stage, focus on product quality, incentives f or
positive testimonials, and handling negative
feedback are done constructively.

INTRODUCTION
The marketing world system has changed recently due to the information
technology revolution. This change affects the marketing communication
methods that were originally carried out by distributing brochures and
advertising in the mass media, now integrated in the digital world. This
phenomenon includes a move from the physical environment to the digital
world, from offline to onl ine interaction, from real space to cyberspace,
accompanied by changes in the mindset and behavior of members and users
(Panggabean et al., 2024). This paradigm shift is very important, because without
this change, the effectiveness of digital marketing co mmunications cannot be
fully realized (Suwatno, 2018).
Marketing communication is an approach that involves various
disciplines, integrating models and concepts from communication science with
marketing. This approach results in a new field of study known as marketing
communication. Marketing communication activities involve the use of a
communication approach with the intention of sending company information to
the intended target market (Priansa, 2017; Akbar, 2023). According to Diniati et
al (2022). Comp anies utilize digital media to distribute information, enabling
their target market to access, comprehend, and effectively use this information.
Rachmadi (2022) explains that digital marketing channels continue to increase by
137%, and 500% of brands have now also used digital marketing. The percentage
is anticipated to keep increasing as a greater number of individuals engage and
dedicate more time to online activities. In his research, Rahmat (Rahmat, 2021)
concluded that the use of online advertising in Marketing 4.0 is an effective way
to communicate with consumers. In fact, the benefits of using online advertising
as a marketing strategy help develop several industrial sectors.
The primary focus of this research is the transition of the Covid -19
pandemic to an endemic phase, which has significant implications for the
existing marketing communication framework. Additionally, the shift from
traditional marketing methods to digital approaches has become essential in the
current landscape. Consequently, the researchers aim to explore how @tekun.id
adapts to these evolving circumstances and identifies effective marketing
communication strategies to enhance brand awareness for its products and the
company as a whole.

is a model designed to approach target audiences effectively by observing
evolving behavioral changes, especially in connection with advances in internet

technology. During the a ttention phase, consumers focus on the products,
services, or ads that are available. In the meantime, the interest phase reveals the
consumer's curiosity about the product, prompting the urge to seek additional
information regarding it (search). Once they  have collected information,
consumers will carefully assess the product and decide on their course of action
(Action) (Sugiyama et al, 2010).
 By discussing product details with others or posting them online,
consumers turn into information distributors ( sharing) (Meilyana, 2018). The
AISAS model comprises five dimensions, one of which is attention, representing
the focus that potential customers direct towards products as influenced by
electronic channels. Secondly, interest, which is the curiosity genera ted by a
product. This interest is seen as passive, meaning that if a consumer is interested
in an advertisement, service, or product, they will inevitably take the initiative to
seek out more information about it. Of course, if consumers are interested in  an
advertisement, service, or product, they will naturally seek out additional
information about it. Search is the stage in which consumers actively seek
information and inquire about the product. Action refers to the phase of physical
engagement with the  product through making a purchase. Finally, share,
indicating the phase in which consumers spread information by offering
suggestions about the items they have purchased to others (Norma et al, 2022; Ji,
2022).

LITERATURE REVIEW
Digital marketing communication has transformed the way organizations
build relationships with consumers by integrating digital platforms into
marketing communication activities. Social media, particularly Instagram,
provides organizations with opportunities to disseminate information, present
visual content, interact directly with audiences, and monitor consumer
responses. Conceptualize social media marketing strategy as a set of strategic
activities that organizations develop through soc ial media platforms to
communicate and create value with target audiences (Li et al, 2021) . In this
context, Instagram is not merely a promotional channel but also functions as an
interactive communication environment in which brands can develop content,
encourage audience engagement, and strengthen relationships with consumers.
Previous research confirms that Instagram has become an important medium in
digital marketing because it can function as a marketing communication channel,
increase brand awareness, stimulate consumer engagement, and provide data
that can support marketing decision-making (Watajdid et al., 2021). Research on
marketing communication s trategies through Instagram also demonstrates that
the use of informative and educational content, interactive communication,
publicity, promotional activities, and audience engagement can contribute to
increasing brand exposure and awareness (Utomo et al, 2024) . Therefore, the
effectiveness of digital marketing commun ication through Instagram can be
understood not only from the frequency of promotional content but also from
how strategically the brand manages content, interaction, visual identity, and
audience engagement.
Brand awareness represents an important compone nt of brand equity
because it reflects consumers' ability to recognize or recall a brand in particular
situations. Aaker (1991) explains brand awareness as the ability of a potential
buyer to recognize or recall that a brand belongs to a particular product category.
Similarly, Keller (1993) positions brand awareness within customer-based brand
equity through two major dimensions, n amely brand recognition and brand
recall. In the context of Instagram, these dimensions can be strengthened through
consistent visual identity, relevant and informative content, repeated brand
exposure, and active interaction with followers. Recent studies  examining
Instagram-based marketing have found that strategic use of visual content,
Instagram features, and audience interaction can support the development of
brand awareness (Susanti et al, 2024; Setya et al., 2024) . Accordingly, the
Instagram account @Tekun.id can be examined as a digital marketing
communication c hannel through which the organization constructs brand
recognition and recall among its target audience. This perspective provides a
theoretical basis for analyzing how content strategy, message delivery, visual

qualitative approach. The model used is Dentsu's AISAS by paying attention to
the dimensions of Attention, Interest, Search, Action and Share (Sugiyama et al,
2010). Here, in -depth interviews, documentation, literature reviews, and
observation were used as data-collection techniques. The main data was obtained
from interviews with five main informants who are Tek un employees and two
supporting informants who are Tekun consumers, and three resource persons
consisting of two practitioners and one academic, supported by direct
observation and literature review to obtain information related to the
implementation of th e AISAS model in the digital marketing communication
strategy @tekun.id on Instagram. The main data was obtained from interviews
with five main informants who are Tekun employees and two supporting

AISAS model in the digital marketing communication strategy @tekun.id on

 After collecting enough data as a basis for research, the next step is to

RESEARCH RESULT AND DISCUSSION
The results of this study indicate that the application of the AISAS model
(Attention, Interest, Search, Action, and Share) by Tekun in digital marketing
communication strategies is effective in increasing brand awareness with a
holistic approach and focuses on several key dimensions. The application of the
AISAS (Attention, Interest, Search, Action, Share) model in digital marketing
communication strategy by Tekun.id aims to increase brand awareness through
Instagram. The AISAS model aims to assist busine sses in engaging with
audiences that have evolved as a result of technological advancements.
 Tekun's application of the AISAS model (Attention, Interest, Search,
Action, Share) in its digital marketing communication strategy through
Instagram @Tekun.id shows an integrated and effective approach in increasing
brand awareness. This research reveals how eac h element of the AISAS model
contributes to building strong brand awareness among audiences. The following
is an in-depth review of Tekun's strategy on each element of AISAS
Attention
 Attention is a crucial first step in building brand awareness. In a
competitive market, attracting audience attention is a challenge. Tekun
implements several strategies to attract audience attention, as follows:

1. Well-designed and responsive plan: Tekun develops a regular content upload
schedule, which ensures consistency and regularity in message delivery. With
good planning, Tekun can capitalize on specific moments to capture the
audience's attention.
2. Media Variety: Instagram's content includes not onl y images but also video
and audio. This variety helps to capture the attention of audiences who have
different preferences in consuming content.
3. Instagram as a Dakwah (Religious Propagation) Media: With strong religious
branding, Tekun utilizes Instagram n ot only for product promotion but also
to spread religious messages. This strengthens the emotional connection with
the audience.
4. Event Sponsorship: Sponsoring events and participating in food bazaars
helps Tekun attract more attention, which is then displayed on Instagram.
5. Storytelling and Interactive Content: Content that tells the journey of the
product and company, as well as interactive content such as quizzes, opens
up communication opportunities between Tekun and its followers.
6. Holistic, Creative and Responsive Approach: This approach ensures that all
aspects that affect brand awareness are considered, creating a consistent and
relevant brand experience.
 With these strategies, Tekun succeeded in capturing the audience's
attention and building an emot ional connection that can increase customer
loyalty. This planned and responsive approach is also in line with the findings
that consistency in content planning and media variety increases brand appeal
and relevance.
Interest
 After attracting attention, the next step is to arouse the audience's interest.
Tekun employs several strategies to achieve this, as follows:
1. Displaying Religious Content Matches the Company's Image: The content
presented reflects Tekun's religious image, which attracts customers not only
through the products but also the values.
2. Avoiding Controversial Content: Tekun actively avoids content that may
cause controversy, such as SARA (Religious, Ethnic and Racial Issues) and
pornography, to maintain a positive image of the company.
3. Collaborating with Local Influencers: Collaborating with local influencers
boosts engagement and expands access to a more specific target market.
4. Focusing on Product Benefits and Advantages. Every content created
emphasizes the benefits and advantages of the product, so that customers can
choose products according to their needs.
5. Not Comparing Products with Other Brands. Tekun carefully decides to
concentrate on their own brand and offerings, steering clear of comparisons
that might result in disputes.
 Tekun also applies the concept of a timeline of attention -grabbing
scenarios by changing the message at regular intervals. Surprise elements in the
content, such as color branding changes, help maintain audience interest. With
this approach, Tekun succeeds in generating customer interest and maintaining
content relevance.

Search
 The search element covers the various ways in which audiences seek more
in-depth information about the products or services offered. In this context,
Tekun implements several eff ective strategies to make it easier for audiences to
find information:
1. Link in Instagram Bio: Tekun places a link to its official website in its
Instagram bio. This makes it easy for followers to find more information about
Tekun's products and store locations. This way, audiences can quickly access
the information they need without having to look elsewhere.
2. Interesting Caption and Description: Every content uploaded by Tekun is
accompanied by an engaging and curious caption. By using questions or
statements that spark curiosity, Tekun encourages followers to slide or read
further, thus increasing engagement with the content.
3. Trend Utilization. Tekun consistently keeps up with current trends in content
and product development: For example, when a certain pro duct goes viral,
Tekun strives to present a similar product with their signature modifications.
This not only makes their content relevant, but also catches the attention of
audiences who are looking for information on the latest trends.
4. Exclusive Promotions and Content Teasers: Tekun offers exclusive
promotions of an urgent nature and short content teasers. This way, they
create curiosity among followers, encouraging them to seek more information
about the upcoming product.
5. Highlight and Hashtag Features: Tekun utilizes the highlight feature on
Instagram to manage product-related information regularly. In addition, the
consistent use of hashtags such as #selalutekun and #sahabattekun makes it
easier for followers to connect and seek more information about the products
presented.
6. Interactive Content: Tekun also presents interactive content, such as quizzes
and Q&As, which not only attract attention but also encourage followers to
actively participate. This creates a more immersive experience  for the
audience and increases their likelihood of seeking more information.
 Tekun facilitates audiences to find information by placing links to
websites in Instagram bio, using intriguing captions, and following trends.
Interactive content such as quizz es also encourages audiences to seek more
information.
Action
 After catching attention and generating interest, the next step is to
encourage the audience to take the expected action, such as doing a purchase or
registration. Tekun employs several strategies to drive this action:
1. Clear Instructions: Tekun consistentl y provides clear and unequivocal
instructions on each of its interactive content. This way, followers can easily
follow the steps required to perform the desired action, such as making a
purchase or participating in a contest.
2. Consistent Hashtags and Taglines. Consistent use of hashtags helps followers
to tag and find related content easily. It also strengthens Tekun's brand
identity on social media.

3. Special Campaigns on Specific Events. Tekun capitalizes on the momentum

Tekun can encourage new customers to make purchases.
4. Application of the FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) Technique. The FOMO

Share
 The Share element emphasizes the importance of encouraging audiences
to share posi tive experiences, which can strengthen marketing and extend the
reach of the message. According to the studies conducted by researchers, to
enhance brand awareness during the Share stage, Tekun implements the
following strategies:
1. Focusing on Product and S ervice Quality: Tekun puts product and service
quality as the top priority. By providing a pleasant shopping experience,
customers are more likely to share their positive experiences on social media.
This not only increases customer satisfaction but also encourages them to tag
@tekun.id in their posts, which directly extends the reach of the brand's
message.
2. Taking advantage of Word of Mouth: Tekun regularly harnesses the power
of word of mouth (WOM) by creating a domino effect of customer
testimonials. Pos itive experiences shared by customers can spark interest
from other audiences, thus increasing brand awareness organically. This
strategy is particularly effective in building trust among potential customers,
as recommendations from friends or family are o ften more trusted than
traditional advertising.
3. Giving incentive for Positive Testimonials: Tekun diligently incentivizes
customers who provide positive testimonials. This way, customers feel
valued and motivated to share their experiences. These incentive s can be
discounts, gifts, or attractive loyalty programs, which in turn can increase
customer engagement and encourage more people to share their positive
experiences.
4. Doing Direct Communication to Handle Negative Testimonials: Tekun also
employs a direct communication strategy to address negative testimonials. By
responding openly and constructively to customer complaints, Tekun not
only improves the experience of dissatisfied customers but also demonstrates
their commitment to good service. This approach  can turn a negative
