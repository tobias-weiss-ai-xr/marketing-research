  Abstract
With the digitalisation of global trade, the old ways of communicating that are
offline trade fairs, static emails and printed product catalogues are unable to
maintain continuous cooperation among buyers and suppliers due to  a lack of
timely information updates and delayed feedback. Social media live -streaming
platforms, including Facebook Live, Instagram Live and TikTok Live, have begun
to use live streaming for a long time and are now often employed by cross -border
enterprises to keep in touch with consumers. The object of this paper is the
construction of trust in real time during live broadcasts, and based on the
stimulus-organism-response (SOR) model and relationship marketing theory, it
studies how live streaming affects  the stable loyalty of customers in global trade
cooperation. Based on industry data and typical cases of B2B foreign trade, this
paper will examine the three internal mechanisms that promote buyer trust
through live interaction, summarize four operational  live-streaming customer
retention models, identify the main problems of cross -cultural barriers,
homogeneous content and disconnected post -live services, and put forward
corresponding improvement plans. Based on the above study, timely
communication with foreigners can be initiated to build rapport and increase the
frequency of such contact; thus, the sense of switching costs for importers will be
reduced, motivating them to be more willing to buy and engage in subsequent
trade, extending the period of coo peration among these enterprises and their
overseas partners for small and medium-sized export enterprises.

1. Introduction
Restructuring of the world's supply chains and the spread of digital infrastructure
have changed the whole system of international trade cooperation. For decades,
cross-border suppliers have only been able to contact foreign buyers through offline
exhibitions twice a year and emails. The previous channels are far from people, have

2. Theoretical Framework and Concept Definition
2.1. Relationship Marketing Theory
Berry (1983) proposed the idea of relationship marketing, that is to say, through
closer contact with customers, one aims to cultivate a sense of connection among
them rather than focus on a single transaction. Stable customer loyalty is based on
the trust built through long -term use and satisfaction with services, not just a low
price. Foreign buyers have high switching costs in the cooperation of global trade
due to problems such as supplier inspection, sample testing and production trial [1].
Traditional offline and email communication have been lacking regular interaction;
therefore, we will start using live -streaming on social media to build trust with
suppliers through frequent contact and lay the groundwork for longer cooperation.

2.2. Stimulus-Organism-Response (SOR) Model
SOR is the theoretical framework that will be used in this paper. External Stimuli (S)
are all the dynamic elements in the live -streaming process of social media, such as
on-site factory demonstrations and live Q&A, etc. Organism variables (O) are the
internal psychological perceptions of overseas buyers, and mainly refer to the trust
and emotional connection they feel after viewing the live-stream. The two indicators
of stable customer loyalty in global trade are the dependent variables (R):
behavioural loyalty (such as repeated purchase, long -term framework contract and
an increase in procurement quantity) and attitudinal loyalty (voluntary supplie r
recommendation, resistance to alternative manufacturer). The above model can
help us understand why people remain and buy after seeing live -streaming
advertisements.
2.3. Core Concept Definition
Live-streaming on social media for foreign trade cooperatio n: Export enterprises
abroad can organise professional live-streaming events to give B2B buyers a look at
the factory, demonstrations of product technology, customised schemes and
post-order case studies, etc., to attract new customers and keep existing cooperation
partners.

Real-time Construction of Trust in Live-streaming: The main research content of this

3. Mechanisms of Live Streaming Cultivating Stable Buyer Loyalty
Social media live-streaming is synchronous and has an appealing visual effec t; thus,
it can reduce the problem of information asymmetry to a certain extent, build trust
and an emotional bond with consumers gradually, and finally cultivate a long -term,
stable relationship between the brand and consumers. The three kinds of Running
Mode are as follows:
3.1. Real-time Live Interaction Eliminates Information Asymmetry and
Establishes Initial Trust
The main reason that foreign trade cooperation cannot be realised in our country is
the lack of information. The short communication windows at trade fairs and
ambiguous written emails do not allow buyers to independently verify the scale of

not have cultural bias from fixed written materials; thus, it is more trustworthy for
buyers.
3.2. Scheduled Live Operations Deepen Emotional Attachment and Form
Attitudinal Loyalty
Customer loyalty in global trade cooperation is now based on emotional ties built
over a long time and not only on product advantages. The old way of foreign trade
communication is to announce the introduction of new products or promotions,
with no daily contact. Social media live-streaming has set a fixed broadcast time, and
there will be a weekly technical-sharing live session and a monthly factory open-day
live tour. Over time, continuous contact with the brand will also help the public feel
closer to it.
After every live-streaming event, the host will invite the buyers who took part in the
live-stream to join an exclusive social group, post the play link of the live -stream
promptly, and answer frequently asked questions. The New Closed -Loop Interactive
Model has prolonged the Life of Mobile Homes. Many long -term cooperative buyers
have developed the habit of watching live streams and have actively put forward
product optimisation suggestions during the stream. The two -way value co-creation
process has generated a sense of belonging among the consumers, and they are
willing to continue purchasing products from the original company[3].

4. Four Replicable Live-streaming Modes for Customer Retention
Based on the actual operating cases of B2B live -streaming by manufacturing
exporters on Facebook and TikTok, four mature live -maintenance models centred
on the construction of real -time trust have been summarized for global trade
cooperation.
4.1. Factory Live Tour Mode (New Buyer Trust Cultivation)
This way can help potential buyers and newly added buyers with low confidence.
The company has organized weekly open days at the raw material warehouse,
automatic production line, quality control laboratory and finished goods warehouse.
Professional technical hosts can answer viewers' questions in real time about the
production quality and foreign certifications of the products, and they will also
arrange a one -on-one video call with the main buyer during the live broadcast.
Recorded live footage will be sent to the buyer after the broadcast as supplementary
qualification materials. Hardware, machinery and furniture export factories have
used this way to reduce buyers' desire to visit other suppliers.
4.2. Themed Technical Live Mode (D eepen Cooperation with Existing
Clients)
Biweekly technical live -streaming sessions are for mid - and long-term cooperative
buyers, and in order not to be a sales pitch, they will focus on presenting the
professional advantages. Live themes are product upda tes, changes in foreign
standards and after -sales service instructions. Home appliance exporters have
started live-streaming the process of disassembly and fault repair to train the sales
force of foreign distributors through recorded videos. Continuous te chnical support

4.3. Cross-cultural Festival Live Mode (Strengthen Emotional Bonds)
According to the main festivals in the target export countries for our company,

Live-streaming is the first place for interaction; social media private groups are used

live-streaming and industry analysis re ports, will be posted in the group often by
the operator. All unanswered questions from the live room will be tracked and
answered for the targeted buyer within one working day, and the optimization
results will be presented in the next live stream. The 24-hour continuous interaction
loop will not be interrupted after the end of the live broadcast.
5. Major Restrictions on Live Streaming Building Buyer
Loyalty
Although social media live -streaming has the advantage of attracting overseas
clients for the retention of export enterprises, many operational problems still need
to be addressed before stable long-term cooperation in global trade can be achieved.
5.1. Cross-cultural Communication Barriers Weaken Trust
Transmission
There are many differences in value, i nformation demand and cultural taboos
among all countries. Buyers in Europe and America are more concerned about
objective test data and after-sales service cases in live-streaming; buyers in Asia pay
more attention to the scale of the factory and the qual ifications of the company
(International Digital Trade Institute, 2025). Many domestic live -streamers have
borrowed sales scripts from other areas, focused too much on offering short -term
low prices, and thus failed to meet the demand of local consumers fo r a good
environment and excellent service [4]. Mistakes in cross -cultural expression may

make the buyer distrust us. In addition, the live host lacks a reserve of professional
vocabulary and has poor oral fluency; therefore, when translating industrial
technical terms during the Q&A, there may be distortions and misunderstandings of
the information.
5.2. Homogenized Live Content Causes Buyer Fatigue
Many export enterprises have started the single -product display live -streaming
mode without differentiating themes. Repeatedly introducing the product and its
basic information makes the long -term cooperative buyer lose interest. Most
enterprises are only looking to increase the number of short -term live -streaming
orders and do not have a tiered content design for potential buyers, short -term
clients and long -term framework partners. As there are no factory tours, technical
sharing and cross -cultural exchange live -streaming topics, the audience has low
stickiness and cannot form an emotional conne ction with the brand that makes
them want to keep trading.
5.3. Disconnection Between Live Commitment and Post -live After-sales
Service
Trust formed during the live broadcast will rapidly disappear if the promises made
live cannot be realised in actual trad e. Some hosts make too many promises of
customised modification and fast -delivery service to attract orders during the
live-streaming period, but the production and after -sales departments are unable to
meet the demand. When buyers have problems with the q uality of goods or delays
in delivery after live -streamed purchases, the slow reply from customer service
makes them unhappy. Many enterprises have separated the live -operation team
from the after -sales department and are not communicating. Live streamers are
unable to promptly notify after-sales staff of the actual requirements of buyers; thus,
the interactive -service chain is interrupted and harm is done to the long -term
development of trade cooperation.
5.4. Lack of Refined Live Data Operation
Most small and medium-sized exporters have only used live -streaming for the main
sales of their products and have been unable to make full use of the back -end data
provided by the platform for customer segmentation and management. They cannot
distinguish among the po tential buyers, transactional clients and loyal framework
users based on live audience data; all these groups are shown the same live content
and post-live maintenance plan. Without data support, the company will be unable
to provide personalised live-match services that motivate buyers to stay and will not
be able to show them their choices have become more binding.

Based on the four restraining factors mentioned above, this paper puts forward
some specific improvement plan s to help export enterprises make better use of
live-streaming and build a stable customer group for international trade
cooperation.
6.1. Build Cross-cultural Professional Live Host Teams
Hire and train local experts who are good at the language and know t he industry
well. Pre -live cross -cultural training can identify the different information
requirements, cultural taboos and certification issues in various markets, and thus
multiple live scripts can be developed for Europe, America, Southeast Asia and the
Middle East. In the process of the live broadcast, professional real -time translation
tools will be employed to accurately translate technical terms and avoid errors
caused by cross-cultural communication that could harm the foundation of trust.
6.2. Construct Layered, Differentiated Live Content Systems
Three levels of live themes have been set for different segments of buyers to avoid
content uniformity. The aim of the factory tour live session is to reduce the mistrust
of new buyers; the purpose of the b iweekly technical sharing live broadcasts is to
strengthen the demand alignment of short -term cooperative clients; and regularly
held cross -cultural festival live sessions will maintain the long -term framework of
buyers and increase their emotional connect ion. Set a regular schedule for the live
broadcast (weekly, biweekly, monthly), save all the replay videos, and let buyers
watch them later. The main direction of change in live operation is to shift from
price promotion to professional value output and em otional communication to
improve the stickiness of the audience.
6.3. Realize Unified Linkage Between Live Frontend and After -sales
Backend
Only one channel for the demand transmission of live -streaming groups and
after-sales service will be set up. All buy er comments, demand suggestions and
complaint intentions recorded during the live stream will be forwarded to the
relevant after-sales staff on the same day. Strictly speaking, hosts should not make
delivery and compensation promises that exceed the produc tion capacity of the
enterprise. Complete buyer demand tracking files will be generated, and after
resolving the problems raised by buyers, after -sales staff will notify them of the
handling results through private social media messages to form a closed -loop live
interaction and order execution and after -sales follow-up process to keep the trust
of buyers stable.

7. Conclusion
With the digita lisation of cross -border cooperation in trade, social media
live-streaming has overcome time and information barriers. Based on the actual
construction of trust in the live -streaming environment, it is hoped that continuous
trust and emotional connections among manufacturers and overseas buyers will be
formed to achieve stable two -dimensional customer loyalty (attitude and
behaviour). The three stages of its internal operating process are to reduce
information asymmetry by having synchronous on -site live in teractions to build
initial trust, regularly conduct multi -level live broadcasts to strengthen the
emotional connection with buyers, and use refined live data operation to increase
the switching cost for buyers and encourage them to purchase again. The fou r
replicate live -maintenance modes of export enterprises to keep the interest of
overseas clients in the daily life and facilities of workers are factory live tours,
technical-sharing live sessions, cross -cultural festival live broadcasts and
live-community closed-loop operations.
Currently, there are problems such as cross -cultural communication barriers,
standardized live -streaming content, a lack of connection between live -streaming
and after -sales service, weak data operation capabilities, etc., which r educes the
loyalty-building effect of live -streaming. Export enterprises need to build
cross-cultural professional live -streaming host teams, create a system of various
types of multi-level live-streaming content, guarantee good communication between
the l ive-streaming department and after -sales service, begin collecting data, and
optimize the management of buyers. Only after learning the basic operating
principles of real -time live trust construction will the manufacturer be able to
transform sporadic temp orary transactions into continuous and stable global trade
cooperation and enhance the long -term competitiveness of its products in the
international market.
Most of the B2B manufacturing export enterprises are included in this research, but
some are not covered. Further research can be carried out in the field of
cross-border service trade, and quantitative questionnaire data will be used to
examine the correlation between the frequency of live interaction and the
repurchase rate of buyers to add to the study results.
