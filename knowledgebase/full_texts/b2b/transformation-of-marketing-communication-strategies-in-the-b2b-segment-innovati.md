Purpose. The purpose of the study is to analyze the transformational processes in marketing communication strategies
within the B2B segment under the conditions of economic digitalization, as well as to identify mechanisms for
integrating the principles of behavioral economics and innovation management to enhance the effectiveness of
interaction between business entities. Methodology. The research methodology is based on the systematization of
theoretical approaches to marketing communications, the analysis of current marketing trends, and the synthesis of
best practices of B2B companies using a comparative analysis of traditional and digital interaction channels. Results.
The results of the study include the identification of key directions for the transformation of communication strategies
(from mass messaging to personalized data -driven multichannel interactions) and the substantiation of the role of
artificial intelligence technologies, content marketing, and account -based marketing (ABM) in improving the
performance of B2B communications. Additionally, a conceptual model for the form ation of marketing
communication policy has been substantiated, based on the synergy of innovative tools, AI, and the experience
economy concept, which allows for the creation of added value and a memorable customer experience in negotiation
processes. The  importance of transitioning to evidence -based marketing and utilizing business -oriented metrics
(MQL, SQL, ROI, LTV) to justify marketing budgets in the context of digital transformation is emphasized. Scientific
novelty. The scientific novelty lies in th e systematization of transformational changes in B2B marketing
communications and the identification of the relationship between the level of an enterprise's digital maturity, the
implementation of innovative management models, and the effectiveness of com munication strategies in the context
of irrational consumer behavior. Practical significance. The practical significance is determined by the possibility of
applying the developed approaches to form an effective marketing communication policy for industria l enterprises
and service companies, specifically for mitigating cognitive biases in B2B negotiations and improving strategies for
promoting innovative products in the conditions of a war economy and the post-war recovery of Ukraine.
Keywords: B2B marketin g; marketing communications; marketing; digital transformation; content marketing;
artificial intelligence; CRM; omnichannel; innovation management; behavioral economics
Statement of the problem
The modern business environment is undergoing radical changes  under the influence of digitalization,
globalization and transformation of consumer behavior. In the B2B ( business-to-business ) segment, these
processes are of particular importance, since purchasing decisions are made collectively, the transaction
cycle is long, and the cost of each contact (significantly higher than in the consumer segment. Under such
conditions, traditional approaches to marketing communications (exhibitions, personal meetings, direct
marketing) are gradually losing their effectivenes s and giving way to digital tools based on data and
automation.
However, the transformation of B2B communications is not just about moving from offline to online
channels. It is about a qualitatively new paradigm of interaction: personalization at the leve l of a specific
company and decision-makers (DMOs), the transition to an account-based strategy marketing (ABM), the
use of artificial intelligence (AI) to analyze intent signals, and the development of omnichannel ecosystems
where every touchpoint generat es measurable business results. This creates new demands on marketing
strategies and marketer competencies.
At the same time, most Ukrainian B2B companies remain in the early stages of digital marketing
transformation. The lack of integrated CRM systems, l ow level of analytical maturity, lack of qualified
personnel and limited budgets complicate the implementation of modern communication strategies. This
highlights the need for systematic research into transformation processes in B2B marketing

Formulation of the purpose of the article
The purpose of the article is to study transformational processes in marketing communications strategies

economy.

Presentation of the main research material
Marketing communications in the B2B segment have traditionally been distinguished from the
consumer market by greater rationality, longer decision -making cycles , and a higher role for personal
relationships. The classic model of B2B communications involve d the dominance of personal sales,
participation in exhibitions and conferences, direct marketing, and the industry press [1, 16]. However, the
rapid development of digital technologies, the spread of social networks, and the transformation of the
buyer's role have radically changed this landscape.
A key driver of the transformation has been the changing behavior of B2B buyers. According to Gartner
research [5], today's buyers spend only 17% of their time in the buying cycle on direct communication with
suppliers - the rest is spent on independent research in the online space. This means that marketing
communications must be present at every stage of the buyer's research journey ( Customer Journey): from
problem awareness to final supplier selection. Consequ ently, businesses must shift their focus from
aggressive sales pitches to creating high -value educational content that establishes authority long before
the first sales call. This strategic omnipresence ensures that a brand remains a top -of-mind contender by
providing the right information at the precise moment a potential client identifies a pain point. A
comparative analysis of traditional and digital B2B communication channels is presented in Table 1.

The most notable trend in the transformation of B2B communications is the development of account -
based strategies. marketing (ABM). Unlike traditional marketing aimed at a broad audience, ABM involves
personalized communications for specific target companies and key decision-makers [6]. The technological
basis of ABM is the integration of CRM systems, marketing automation platforms (MAP) and AI tools for
analyzing customer intent [2, 17]. According to expert research, companies using A BM demonstrate a
significantly higher ROI from marketing investments compared to other approaches [6].
The above comparison shows that digitalization has not only expanded the list of available channels,
but also changed the very logic of their functioning. For a systematic understanding of these processes, the
authors have developed a conceptual model of the transformation of marketing communications in the B2B
segment (Fig. 1), which is based on the integration of innovation management tools and behaviora l
economics principles [21, 23].
It is based on the synergy of innovation management, which is responsible for technological renewal of
business processes, and behavioral economics, which allows for a deeper understanding of irrational aspects
of decision-making by corporate buyers (CPP) [21, 23]. The presented model demonstrates that modern
B2B communications are evolving from linear information to a complex ecosystem of customer experience
management. At the center of this model is not a product, but a sp ecific association of decision -makers
whose path to purchase ( Customer Journey ) is adjusted through mechanisms of "nudging" and leveling
cognitive biases.
In the context of transformation, B2B marketing communication is transforming from one -way
advertising to an intelligent dialogue, where the company uses IT technologies to “accompany” the client
even before the first call. Instead of mass mailings, businesses use artificial intelligence to analyze the
digital footprints of buyers, creating a personaliz ed information field for each target company through
expert articles, video cases , and professional networks ( LinkedIn ) [19, 22]. The key change is that

At the same time, the role of content marketing as a strategic tool for B2B communications is growing.
It is no longer enough to simply communicate about a product or service; - the modern B2B buyer expects
deep industry expertise and useful content from the supplier. According to research by Content Marketing
Institute [7, 11], the vast majority of B2B marketers actively use content marketing, and the most effective
formats are considered to be case studies, analytical reports, and video content [10]. Key trends in marketing
communications in the B2B segment are systematized in Table 2.
An important component of the transformation of B2B communications is social media, and LinkedIn
in particular . This platform has become the main digital platform for professional interaction: according to
LinkedIn Marketing Solutions [8], the vast majority of leads generated in social media for the B2B segment
fall on this network. The platform's functionality allows not only to promote expert content, but also to
carry out precise targeting by position, industry, company size and other relevant parameters [15, 19].
Artificial intelligence is pl aying a significant role in the transformation of B2B communications. AI
technologies are used to personalize communications at scale, analyze signals of buyer intent, automate
email marketing, and predict scoring [ 21 ] McKinsey & Company research [9, 14]  shows that companies
that actively implement AI and “ super -agency ” tools in the workplace demonstrate significant revenue
growth and reduced operating costs due to higher targeting accuracy and automation of routine tasks.

coverage are giving way to business -oriented indicators: the number of qualified leads (MQL, SQL), the
cost of acquiring a customer (CAC) and its lifetime value (LTV). The transition to evidence -based
marketing based on end -to-end analytics is a necessary condition for justifying marketing budgets in
modern conditions [19, 21]. A comparison of the classic and transformed models of B2B marketing

The growth of video content consumption determines its active use in the B2B environment: from
product demonstrations and webinars to educational content that forms the intellectual leadership of the
supplier. The Wyzowl study [10] demonstrates the significant direct  impact of video on purchasing
decisions in the professional segment, with short video content remaining the most effective , providing the
highest level of audience engagement.
Thus, the transformation of marketing communications strategies in the B2B seg ment is characterized
by a transition from mass single-channel messages to personalized , data-driven multichannel interactions.
The key drivers of this transformation are the digitalization of B2B buyer behavior, the development of AI
and marketing automation technologies [2, 17], as well as increasing demands for measurability and proven
effectiveness of each marketing tool within the new experience economy [21, 23].
Conclusion
The transformation of marketing communications in the B2B segment is a multidimensional process
that encompasses technological, organizational, and strategic dimensions. The analysis conducted allows
us to formulate the following key conclusions.
First, changing B2B buyer behavior (increasing online self -research, collective decision-making, and
greater information literacy) necessitates a reorientation of marketing communications around buyer needs,
rather than supplier capabilities. This requires companies to build a strong digital presence and
systematically produce useful content.

Secondly, the account -based strategy marketing is the most promising approach for B2B companies
with a limited pool of target customers or a long deal cycle. Implementing ABM requires technological
maturity, coordinated marketing and sales work, and investment in data and analytics.
Third, artificial intelligence and marketing automation are transforming from a competitive advantage
to basic B2B marketing hygiene. Companies that do not invest in these technologies risk falling
significantly behind their competitors in both communication efficiency and speed of response to market
signals [24].
Fourth, measuring the effectiveness of marketing communications through business-oriented metrics is
becoming a prerequisite for strategic marketing management. Marketing in the B2B sphere must prove its
contribution to the company's revenues, not just to brand awareness.
Prospects for further research include the development of methodological tools for assessing the digital
maturity of B2B marketing of Ukrainian enterprises, research into the specifics of the transformation of
communication strategies for various subsegments of the B2B market, as well as studying the features of
implementing AI tools in marketing in the context of a war economy and post-war recovery.
