Abstract:

This

study

aims

to

of

(ADS)

at

PT

Pink

Services

Indonesia.

The

include

failure

to

sales

targets

and

limited

sales

A

descriptive

approach

was

through

observation,

interviews,

and

The

analytical

integrates

the

SOSTAC

model

(Situation,

Objectives,

Strategy,

Tactics,

Action,

Control)

with

Segmentation-Targeting-Positioning

(STP),

Account-Based

Marketing

(ABM),

and

Customer

(CRM).

Analysis

Spradley's

qualitative

(domain,

taxonomy,

componential,

and

cultural

theme

identified

a

internal

culture

and

the

B2B

The

improve

and

strengthen

with

STP

more

market

while

CRM

supports

customer

retention

and

service

quality.

This

strategic

has

potential

to

increase

sales,

build

brand

awareness,

and

improve

overall

operational

effectiveness.

The

study

concludes

that

a

digital

and

priority-account-based

marketing

approach

can

enhance

the

competitive

advantage

of

ADS

products

in

the

market.
